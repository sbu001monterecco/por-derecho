## 3. DP 1956 attribution architecture

The strongest controlling method is:

> information available to AC → concrete insolvency/professional duty → AC act or omission → information supplied/withheld from the Mercantile Court → institutional result → AC implementation/maintenance/non-correction → corrective measure available → action/inaction → advantage to another actor → incremental patrimonial/procedural harm to LPB.

Preserve four categories:

- **A — direct AC conduct**;
- **B — judicial result materially conditioned by AC input/calculation/recommendation/omission**;
- **C — judicial result implemented, maintained or left uncorrected by AC**;
- **D — autonomous judicial conduct with no evidential bridge to AC; exclude from the AC allegation and keep only as context.**

The filing is intentionally not the battleground against the judge. It should, however, preserve the **judicial interface** so that judicial approval cannot automatically absorb the AC's own factual inputs, calculations, recommendations, execution, restoration duties or accounts. Conversely, AC involvement does not establish judicial criminal liability.
