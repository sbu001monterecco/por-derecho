# Explain-First filing summary template — ES / EN

Use this only as the first-read layer. Replace every bracketed field with source-controlled matter-specific content.

## ES — RESUMEN PARA EL LECTOR

### QUÉ HA OCURRIDO, POR QUÉ IMPORTA Y QUÉ SE SOLICITA

**Objeto inmediato.** [Una frase: qué decisión, omisión, contradicción, documento, competencia o actuación concreta motiva este escrito.]

**Qué ocurrió.** [2–4 frases con la secuencia mínima necesaria. No reconstruir aquí toda la historia.]

**Punto decisivo.** [La distinción, contradicción o cuestión no resuelta que el lector no debe perder.]

**Por qué corresponde a este órgano.** [Conectar el punto anterior con la función, competencia o decisión pendiente del destinatario, sin ampliar artificialmente su competencia.]

**Anclajes documentales mínimos.** [Normalmente 2–5 fuentes/actos/referencias decisivas. Identificar qué demuestra cada una y qué no demuestra.]

**Qué se solicita.** [Lista o frase corta de actuaciones finitas: preservar, identificar, producir, incorporar, remitir, motivar, resolver, corregir, etc.]

**Límite de prueba.** [Una frase si es necesario: qué alegación, autoría, enlace, intención, recepción o consecuencia sigue abierta/no acreditada.]

## EN — READER SUMMARY

### WHAT HAPPENED, WHY IT MATTERS, AND WHAT IS REQUESTED

**Immediate issue.** [One sentence defining the concrete decision, omission, contradiction, document, competence issue or act that triggers this filing.]

**What happened.** [2–4 sentences containing only the minimum sequence needed for orientation.]

**Decisive point.** [The distinction, contradiction or unresolved question the reader must not miss.]

**Why this recipient is concerned.** [Connect the issue to the recipient's actual function, competence or pending decision without artificially enlarging it.]

**Minimum documentary anchors.** [Normally 2–5 decisive sources/acts/references. State what each proves and what it does not prove.]

**What is requested.** [Short finite actions: preserve, identify, produce, incorporate, refer, reason, determine, correct, etc.]

**Proof boundary.** [One sentence where needed identifying any allegation, authorship, linkage, intent, receipt or consequence that remains open/unproved.]

---

## Final first-page gate

Before finalisation confirm:

- A competent first-time reader can understand the immediate issue without reading the annexes first.
- Unexplained acronyms have been removed.
- File/proceeding references are limited to those needed for orientation.
- Fact, official outcome, allegation, inference, contrary material and gap are not collapsed.
- The recipient's competence is explained, not assumed.
- The requested action is finite and operational.
- The summary does not attempt to prove the whole case.
- Detailed facts, law, chronology, evidence and annexes follow after the summary.
