# Costa Papagayo — Deep Scan & Publication-Routing Protocol

**Purpose:** a two-stage, continuity-preserving research task for the Costa Papagayo / Sun Park / LPB nexus.  
**Status:** public-safe coordination protocol. It contains no private correspondence, personal data, legal advice or untested allegation. It is not a pleading, legal conclusion or public-page copy.

## Decision rule

Do not make a public page merely because a proceeding, email or allegation exists. First create one **private master proceedings and evidence register**. Then route each item as one of:

1. **No public mention** — private, privileged, personal, incomplete, unverified or unfairly prejudicial material.
2. **A proposition within the bilingual Costa Papagayo transparency hub** — where the record is source-safe but only supports a bounded documentary question.
3. **A dedicated bilingual page** — only where the record has a distinct public-interest question, sufficient primary sources, a clear scope and a correction route.
4. **An institutional request page** — where the useful public action is identification/preservation/disclosure by a competent public custodian rather than an assertion of fault.
5. **A linked proceedings index entry** — where a source-safe procedural fact gives readers necessary context but does not warrant narrative treatment.

Every public statement must immediately identify its source category and status: **verified fact / party assertion / inference / contrary material / open question**.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- Concurso 36/2012 concerns **Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. (LPB)** and its estate. Do not describe the whole Sun Park complex, its hotel operation, communities, Matkator, Aweswell, third-party units, common elements, licences, staff, bookings, systems, goodwill or all revenues as insolvent or under the administrator concursal's authority without source-specific proof.

- Treat alleged false authority, false documentation, “capture”, undisclosed contact, agreement, concealment, fraud and criminality as **hypotheses to test**, never as established facts.
- A contact, shared recipient, overlapping commercial interest, information possession, a procedural appearance or a public filing does not by itself prove coordination, intent, validity, invalidity or liability.
- Keep all mailbox content, legal advice, personal data, private attachments and untested submissions out of this public repository and the public website. They may appear only as confidential leads in the private evidence register.
- No direct merits allegation to a counterparty. Preserve/access requests must be separated from public communications and court steps must proceed through counsel/procurador where appropriate.

## Search method

1. Search with exact, alternative and date-bounded families. Begin with:
   - `"ETJ 370/2013"`
   - `"monitorio 1438/2012"`
   - `"Costa Papagayo"`
   - `"Costa Papagayo" "administrador concursal"`
   - `"Costa Papagayo" "Comunidad de Propietarios"`
   - `depuradora`, `"Parcela G"`, `"finca 8241"`, `"Luchy Playa Blanca"`
   - the confirmed court, entity and representative names extracted from native sources.
2. Paginate each result family to exhaustion; record the query, date range, result count, reviewed count and exclusions.
3. Shortlist by legal relevance and read complete threads where surrounding context changes meaning. For every attachment, identify original filename, message date, sender/recipient role, MIME type, pages/metadata, hash if available, and whether it is native, scanned, forwarded, duplicated or derived.
4. De-duplicate variants without deleting provenance. A later PDF, OCR text, copied excerpt or counsel summary is not a substitute for the native order, filing, certificate, account record or email.
5. Search File Library and current uploads independently. Treat file search snippets as leads; read the original source before relying on content.
6. Reconcile every item against the Prompt A proceedings register. If a new proceeding/reference appears, add it as “unverified lead” until the native record is obtained.
7. Search for contrary material: authority grants, notices, ratifications, payment explanations, procedural orders, appeal outcomes, account entries, corrections and denials. Record absence carefully: “not located in the sources searched,” never “does not exist.”

## Verification tests

For each material event, complete:

`PERSON/ENTITY → CLAIMED CAPACITY → AUTHORITY SOURCE → CONTACT/FILING → INFORMATION OR DOCUMENT → RECIPIENT/CUSTODIAN → USE/DECISION → DISCLOSURE → IN-CONCURSO EFFECT → EXTRA-CONCURSO EFFECT → BENEFIT/LOSS → CONTRARY EXPLANATION → STATUS`

Apply the following gates separately:

- **Authority gate:** statutes/constitution, convocation, agenda, attendance, quotas, voting, minutes, certification, power, transmission, ratification and challenge.
- **Proceedings gate:** full docket, pleadings, signed resolutions, service, appeal processing, finality, deposit/accounting and payment proof.
- **Concursal gate:** identification of estate asset/credit, disclosure to administrator concursal/Mercantile Court, accounting treatment, authorisation and effects limited to LPB.
- **Extra-concursal gate:** identify the owner/right holder and evidence of any effect on non-LPB property or operations. Do not assume the effect.
- **Communication gate:** differentiate direct evidence of contact from inferred contact; differentiate information request, notice, negotiation, agreement and implemented arrangement.
- **Publication gate:** only publish a bounded proposition when native/official source quality and public interest justify it, personal data is handled, contrary material is fairly represented, and an update/correction route exists.
