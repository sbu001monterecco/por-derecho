## Controlling evidential rule
A status of Received establishes receipt/registration in the record available to us. Standing alone it does **not** establish acceptance/validation of allegations; a finding that an offence occurred; identification of a suspect or criminal responsibility; opening of police diligencias/investigation; assignment to UDEF, Policía Judicial, UCO or another specialist unit; actual preservation/seizure/securing of evidence; interviews, third-party requests, an atestado or report; referral to Fiscalía/court; closure; or a merits decision.

A Rejected status must not be described as substantive police rejection of the criminal allegations unless the underlying rejection document establishes that. It is presently treated as a registration/processing status.

## What is not yet evidenced
As of this consolidation, no reviewed/publicly identified document is sufficient to state that the January receipts resulted in a named investigating unit, police diligencias/reference number, atestado, witness interviews, third-party production requests, actual evidence-preservation measure, referral to Fiscalía/court, reasoned closure, or another substantive police decision.

This is deliberately **not** phrased as “the police did nothing.” Absence of located/published evidence of subsequent action is not proof that no subsequent action occurred.

**Verifiable question:** after the documented receipts, what body/unit materially received and processed each file, what assessment was made, what preservation action — if any — occurred, and what subsequent referral, investigation, closure or other action — if any — is documented?

## Public-site rule
Use three layers: (1) what was requested; (2) what the registry proves; (3) what remains unverified. Prefer “not yet evidenced/documented in the material available to us” over “was not done.”
