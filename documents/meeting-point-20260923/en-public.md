## STATEMENT OF THE MATTER

### First. Purpose and precise procedural route

This submission identifies information and documents concerning the Sun Park complex at **calle Janubio 3, Playa Blanca, Yaiza**, the presentation known as “Lava Verde” and the “Club Sei Lanzarote” project publicized by Meeting Point/FTI. Gil requests **inclusion of the complete submission and the annexes actually supplied in originating file 357/2024, and inclusion or documentary association with the linked plan-approval proceeding 93/2025, as appropriate to the status of each, with referral to the competent judicial decision-maker and a record of the decision and any circulation ordered**. He asks the court office to identify where the original is held and where its reference or copy appears, maintaining one documentary index. Separately, he requests information about the proceedings’ status and limited access to the identified acts. If a request has no available procedural route, he asks for a separate, reasoned decision and an opportunity to remedy any legally remediable defect. The alleged facts may support civil, insolvency, other non-insolvency and, if the relevant elements are met, criminal routes, but **each claim requires its own competent body, representation and procedure**. This notification concerns the possible reflection of rights and risks in the three debtors’ documents, without abandoning other available routes.

**Principal request in these proceedings.** Gil seeks a decision on including the communication in the record and, through the appropriate route, on the requests for reports, circulation and referral to the Public Prosecutor set out in the relief sought. His independent request for information and access is not a prerequisite to those decisions. He asks that any copy referred to the General Council of the Judiciary (CGPJ), a judicial governing body or another authority be identified as such, together with a decision on the requests addressed to this court. The account and its documentary index are intended to remain together; separate decisions on each request reflect their different legal bases and make the treatment of each ascertainable.

**The substantive issue placed before this body is alleged concealment of commercialization already carried out.** Gil maintains that the private actors and the persons linked to Meeting Point’s leadership individually identified in the submission advanced the commercial exploitation of Sun Park from 2018 outside the required corporate and judicial oversight. The alleged clandestine conduct concerns concealment of the legal basis for the availability offered, the agreements, their actual participants, consideration and risks. The presentations to third parties described in sworn judicial testimony and the subsequent retail distribution form part of that sequence, preserving the date and scope of each item of evidence.

**One factual core, two affected settings.** In **36/2012**, the allegation concerns control and value of the productive unit, its operation, opportunities for improved offers and recovery, and the rights of owners and creditors. In **Meeting Point’s 2024–2025 restructuring**, it concerns which contracts, income, obligations, litigation, warnings and possible recovery actions should have appeared in the corporate, financial and judicial information, and what was actually provided to AUREN and the court. The sequence extends to the Spanish and German estates and creditors identified in each relationship.

**The proposed verification must follow both information and money.** The recovered 2024 notices and the responses of compliance personnel and insolvency administrators establish the knowledge and actions that each document actually describes. Gil asks that these be used to identify who knew each item, who had the duty and capacity to transmit it, what each communicated or omitted, and how that was reflected in the versions of the plan and its annexes. The allegation of deliberate concealment is therefore tied to verifiable documents, people, companies, dates and decisions, alongside contrary evidence and matters still unresolved.

**Specific connection with this court’s function.** The communication places three linked documentary questions before the custodian of **357/2024 and 93/2025**: **which Meeting Point entity contracted for, planned or commercialized rights concerning Sun Park/Lava Verde/Club Sei; how those rights, their income, costs, claims and contingencies were described in the restructuring and financial documentation; and who now holds the documents and received warnings following the sale and merger**. Their alleged relevance to LPB, Matkator, the productive unit and public funding must be established through those relationships, rather than through a bare list of actors or proceedings. The alleged irregularities are raised as possible criminal and non-insolvency conduct as well as for their financial effects. Gil asks the commercial court to act within its documentary jurisdiction and assess any legally appropriate referral to the Public Prosecutor.

This is not a new notice of the opening of negotiations under Article 585 of the consolidated Insolvency Act (**TRLC**): that power belongs to the debtors. Nor does this submission seek recognition of a debt, annulment of acts in other proceedings, judicial recusal or the opening of a criminal investigation. Possible confidentiality of parts of the file, requirements for procedural representation and the absence of recognized creditor status are expressly left to the appropriate decision.

The proposed appearance is **predominantly as an informant**, also stating the claimed financial interest. Articles 1–3, 16, 35 and 36 of **Law 2/2023** are invoked only to the extent that its substantive scope, the context in which the information was obtained and the lawful reporting route apply, taking account of the exclusions in Article 35.2. The use of “whistleblower” does not establish protected status for all the information. Neither the claimed informant capacity nor presentation to a court alone creates party status or replaces procedural requirements. **Directive (EU) 2019/1937**, Germany’s **Hinweisgeberschutzgesetz** and UK protected-disclosure law may be relevant to communications and relationships within their respective personal, material and territorial scopes; they do not themselves expand standing in this Spanish proceeding. Gil requests proportionate handling of personal data and a traceable record of any decision on the submission.

**Identification and documentary unity.** The two-document package identifies both proceedings. If it is registered separately in each so that it can be located, the second entry is intended as a copy for the record and a link to the first, not a duplicate independent claim or a request to consolidate the proceedings. Gil asks the court office to cross-reference the entries and determine the proper procedural destination.
