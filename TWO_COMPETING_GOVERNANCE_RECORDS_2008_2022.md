## 1. Naming rule

This is the controlling evidential title for the Sun Park governance/minutes reconstruction from 2008 through the 2022 transition.

The repository should **not** use “capture”, “hijack”, “fake community”, “bogus actas” or equivalent language as the evidential section label. Those formulations may appear only as an expressly attributed allegation or as a hypothesis to be tested against the primary record.

The public-safe and court-safe label is therefore:

> **Two Competing Governance Records: Comunidad, Exploitation and the Disputed Majority (2008-2022)**

## 2. Express criminal attribution; mechanisms tested, not assumed

The repository records Por Derecho's express allegation that the Community organ
and its documentary outputs were criminally instrumentalised. The following is
the principal mechanism to test:

> From 2011 onward, a numerically minority Owners’ Community governance line may have acquired effective decision-making control through the attribution of Community debt to LPB, the resulting exclusion of LPB from voting, and the later use of those resolutions to generate or propagate authority over debt, records, litigation, access, security, operation or related matters.

The **attribution of that criminal case thesis to Por Derecho is a repository
conclusion**. Whether the mechanism occurred as alleged, whether a particular
document was false or unauthorised, and whether a named person completed an
offence are not adjudicated conclusions. They must be proved or narrowed act by
act, actor by actor and resolution by resolution.

The fact that a numerical minority participated or voted does **not by itself** establish invalidity. The controlling questions are the applicable voting rules, the legal and economic basis of any debt used to remove voting rights, the convocation/proxy/majority requirements for the particular resolution, and the limits of the Owners’ Community’s authority relative to CEXP, the operator, registered owners and the tourism framework.

## 3. The two records to reconstruct

### Record A — Owners’ Community governance/debt line from 2011 onward

Audit every minute and linked act for:

- ownership and participation on the meeting date;
- persons present and represented;
- proxies;
- debt attributed to each owner;
- owners treated as non-voting and the stated basis;
- first/second call and required majority;
- offices elected or ratified;
- accounts, budgets and debt certificates;
- litigation authority;
- security/access/technical/operational decisions;
- later reliance before courts, the insolvency administrator, administrations, counterparties or investors.

This is the line in which the alleged **criminal-instrumentalisation mechanism**
is to be tested, not presumed as an adjudicated finding.

### Record B — original / majority / exploitation-side line from 2008 onward

Reconstruct separately:

- the pre-2008 constitutional/title baseline;
- the 29 April 2008 CEXP formation/approval record;
- the 26 May 2008 exploitation/CEXP instruments;
- the July 2008 and 2008–2010 majority/accounting baseline;
- later LPB/Gil Owners’ Community meetings;
- separate CEXP meetings and appointments.

A CEXP acta must never be silently relabelled as an Owners’ Community acta, and vice versa.

## 4. Why “disputed majority” is the correct evidential concept

The core question is not simply “who owned more?” It is whether the majority ownership position retained voting effect at each decision point and, if it did not, why.

The working causal proposition is:

`operator / CEXP / hotel bears common operating expenditure → Owners’ Community attributes quotas/debt to LPB → LPB is treated as in arrears → LPB’s large ownership position is excluded from voting → the remaining eligible voters become the apparent voting majority → resolutions generate further debt, offices, certificates, claims or authority`.

Each arrow requires proof.

## 5. 26 April 2016 as a high-value bridge document

The 77-page digitally extracted act and annex package is a particularly important bridge because the minute itself records:

- **89.727%** of participation present or represented;

- Carlos Saavedra Rodríguez representing Simon Peter Thompson, Miguel Hernández Lorenzo representing Juan Carlos Fernández Cox, Elena Santos Ramos representing Víctor Fernández Cox, and Patricia Domínguez representing LPB and Mariano Palacios Fernández;
- incomplete 2010–2015 accounting information;

Those facts do not establish unlawfulness. They make the relationship between **economic allocation, arrears status and governance power** a finite documentary question.

## 6. Three compliance gates remain mandatory

For every acta or purported exercise of authority, keep three separate tests:

1. **Owners’ Community / LPH / statutes** — convocation, attendance, proxies, debt, vote exclusion, majority, act-book and challenge history.
2. **CEXP / exploitation contract / private title** — who contractually held operation, integrated maintenance, costs, keys, reception, employees, supplies and revenue; and what authority existed to terminate, assign or replace those rights.
3. **Canary tourism / unidad de explotación** — who held the enabling title to exploit tourist accommodation and whether any act purported to fragment or replace the operating structure.

Do not collapse a defect in one plane into automatic invalidity in another.

## 7. Chronology lock

Do not describe the evidence as proving a single hotel-wide physical eviction in 2011.

The controlled chronology remains:

- **February–June 2011:** governance/debt/voting rupture;
- **2011 onward:** desahucio and related litigation over units/rights;
- **2011–2018:** continuing governance, debt, record, access and operational conflict;
- **7 June 2018:** strongest presently documented physical-control/access event affecting the hotel operation.
