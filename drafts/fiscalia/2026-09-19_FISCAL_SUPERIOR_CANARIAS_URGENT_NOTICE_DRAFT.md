## 3. Institutional route

EOMF art.13.2 assigns ordinary inspection of territorial Fiscalías to the Fiscal Superior, without prejudice to FGE Inspection. EOMF art.22.4 gives the Fiscal Superior territorial representation and jefatura; art.22.7 places Provincial Fiscal Chiefs hierarchically under the Fiscal Superior; art.22.9 and art.25 provide coordination/instruction functions within the legal hierarchy. RD305/2022 arts.11–16 regulate Inspection Fiscal and ordinary/extraordinary inspection.

These provisions make the Fiscal Superior an appropriate recipient for dated knowledge, preservation, attribution and review within powers. They do not prove prior knowledge of DP1901.
