## 4. Objective competence

LOPJ article 73.3.b assigns to the Sala de lo Civil y Penal del TSJ the instruction and trial of criminal causes against judges, magistrates and members of the Ministerio Fiscal for offences committed in office within the Autonomous Community, unless Supreme Court competence applies. Official text: https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1985-12666#asetentaytres

If DP1901 actually decided alleged prevaricación by Alberto López Villarrubia for judicial acts in office, Plaza 6 had to address why it was objectively competent, or identify an inhibition, remittal or other competence act. The response must not assert invalidity conclusively until the initiating document and certified docket are produced.

Require answers to:

1. Was Alberto López Villarrubia the denunciado in the corpus decided?
2. What document created DP1901 and what object did it record?
3. What legal basis permitted Plaza 6 to decide the judge-related merits?
4. Was Article 73.3.b considered?
5. Was anything sent to or returned by the TSJ?
6. What inhibition, remittal, accumulation, reassignment or segregation act exists?
