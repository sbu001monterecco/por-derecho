## I. Object

This recurso challenges the 14-Sep Auto insofar as it orders sobreseimiento libre y archivo without resolving procedural identity, provenance and objective competence. It does not ask this court to decide E.G.745 or ask the Fiscal Superior to revoke the Auto.

## II. Supervening record

The controlled transcription says DP1901 began by a complaint for alleged judicial prevaricación, records the 29-Jul Fiscal archive request and orders free dismissal. The signed Fiscal report, author, assignment, corpus, native Auto and notification metadata remain unproduced.

The June/July source closure also controls separate Ref.24 judge-related and Ref.21 private-actor filings; 9-Jul ATLANTE; stamped private-actor material under DP1901/Plaza 6; July private-actor mapping; and the 12-Jul DP1901/DIP2 providencia. This establishes a material identity collision, not its direction.

## III. First ground — identity and provenance

The court must identify the initiating document, Decanato entry, reparto, recorded object and accused, destination of Ref.21, destination of Ref.24 and its supplement, 9-Jul electronic incorporation, corpus sent to Fiscalía, and every joinder/reassignment/segregation.

An unidentified mixed corpus cannot safely acquire finality over two autonomous complaints.

## IV. Second ground — competence

LOPJ article 73.3.b gives the Sala de lo Civil y Penal del TSJ instruction and trial competence for in-office criminal causes against judges/magistrates in the Autonomous Community, unless Supreme Court competence applies.

Ref.24 was expressly directed to that route and DIP2 recorded the same framework. If DP1901 decided alleged prevaricación by Alberto López Villarrubia, the Auto must explain Plaza 6's objective competence or produce the inhibition/remittal/competence act.

Request express identification of person, acts, competence rule, TSJ transmission/return and any route-preserving act.

## V. Third ground — Fiscal report not produced

The Auto relies on a 29-Jul report that is not before the complainant. Produce the signed report, author, Fiscalía, assignment, index, complete corpus, reasoning, visado/instruction/review and treatment of Ref.21, Ref.24, DIP2 and the 11-Mar correction. If disclosure is lawfully limited, provide a reasoned certificate of existence, authorship, object, corpus categories and handling.

## VI. Fourth ground — private-actor complaint

If the Auto is judge-only, certify the current destination and status of Ref.21 and its 9-Jul material. If it decides Ref.21, give private-actor-specific analysis of persons, acts, classifications, evidence, contrary evidence and competence. Generic prevaricación reasoning cannot silently dispose of an autonomous private-actor complaint.

## VII. Fifth ground — DIP2 and appeal-history discrepancy

DIP2's 6-Mar decree recorded Article 73.3.b and aforamiento. The 2-Mar ATLANTE no-appeal premise is contested by the registered 11-Mar correction and later RPL3304/2025 and RPL3319/2025 records. Treat this as a source-to-premise issue requiring reconstruction, not automatically as deliberate falsehood or innocent error.

## VIII. Sixth ground — sobreseimiento libre

The Auto invokes Article 637.2 and Article 779 while using language concerning lack of a minimum objective evidential basis. Explain why the facts are legally incapable of constituting an offence, rather than merely insufficiently supported at the present investigative stage. This is material while identity, competence, report and corpus remain open.

Preserve native Auto, notification metadata, docket/index, Decanato/reparto, association history, Ref.21/Ref.24 routing, 9-Jul ingestion, DIP2 transfer/correction, 29-Jul report and electronic retention/access history where lawful. No deletion is alleged.
