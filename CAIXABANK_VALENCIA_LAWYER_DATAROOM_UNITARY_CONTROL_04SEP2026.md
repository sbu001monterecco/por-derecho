## Key unitary findings published

### A. Delay / standing
The public FAQ explains that the recovery attempt predates 2023. The AC formally told the insolvency court in January 2021 that he had accepted bringing a Bankia damages action because success could benefit the estate. The 2023 record documents a later refusal to grant procedural authority. This explains the chronology without deciding limitation, prescription or subsidiary standing, all of which remain live CaixaBank defences.

### B. Defence-source provenance
The strongest current proposition is not merely that CaixaBank and the AC use similar language. CaixaBank's defence expressly files/relies on AC/insolvency material to support standing, preclusion/finality and scope arguments. CaixaBank also requested the AC's testimony in Valencia, with Aweswell later adhering. This is a concrete source-provenance and witness question; it is not by itself proof of coordination or collusion.

### C. Split-credit / split-package thesis
The public visual distinguishes:
- integrated 2008–2010 economic package: mortgage + floor + swap + refinancing + second facility + pledge + accounts;
- upstream banking/product responsibility route: Caja/BFA/Bankia → CaixaBank;
- downstream asset/credit route: Bankia → SAREB → PH122 → CAM;
- insolvency/AC gateways between the two.

### D. OB REM structural mirror
The 2018–2019 node explains that the registry wording reproduced in Aweswell's opposition described linked properties and the distributed mortgage as one integrated loan structure for alienation/payment allocation. LPB, represented by the AC, and CAM later declared the OB REM link extinguished for a direct sale. The initial Registry suspension and later validation dispute are published as a controlled transaction requiring authority, estate-benefit, conflict, valuation and accounting analysis.

### E. Functional-agent / enabling-gatekeeper allegation
The public FAQ and OB REM pages expressly attribute to Por Derecho the allegation that the Insolvency Administrator disloyally instrumentalised his institutional position and functionally enabled the CAM/Acosta Matos perimeter to the detriment of LPB and its perimeter.

`Agent` is expressly controlled as a functional investigative hypothesis, not a proven contractual-agency or criminal-concert fact. Any legal conclusion requires actor-specific proof of duty/power, knowledge, act/omission, intent where required, causation, loss, benefit and any necessary instruction/concert.
