## Material work product now preserved

The canonical file preserves the following durable decisions and analytical framework:

1. **Atlantic House thesis:** a discreet, Canarian-rooted, internationally connected private-membership ecosystem built around trust, repeated interaction and cultural fit rather than a literal copy of any one foreign enclave or club.
2. **Core distinction:** expensive, exclusive, socially selective and strategically useful are not synonymous.
3. **Primary design metric:** relationship density rather than celebrity density.
4. **Network principle:** **the membership graph is the asset.**
5. **Benchmarking architecture:** residential enclaves, golf/country clubs, city/social clubs, yacht clubs, UHNW/family-office networks, founder networks and hybrids; compare both Indian Creek similarity and practical strategic value.
6. **Canary feasibility position:** conditional GO for validation, not GO for heavy capital deployment.
7. **Geographic architecture:** Tenerife · Gran Canaria · Lanzarote, with reciprocal relationship corridors into Madrid, London, Mallorca/Sotogrande, Monaco and Israel rather than automatic physical expansion.
8. **Founding 50 model:** deliberately mixed cohort spanning established Canary family businesses, international owners/residents, capital, hospitality/property, founders, professionals, culture/science, next generation and international connectors.
9. **Validation threshold:** test whether approximately 30–40 genuinely desirable founding participants can be assembled credibly through trusted introductions without public advertising.
10. **Admission principle:** wealth alone does not buy entry; nomination → conversation → committee consideration → participation period → membership decision.
11. **Asset-light sequence:** prove the community first through dinners, sailing, golf, culture, salons, introductions and retreats; property follows validated community, not the reverse.
12. **Scale discipline:** no business-model requirement to grow to 500–1,000 members; excessive scale is treated as a threat to the product.
13. **Cultural red-team:** avoid a 'Canary billionaires club', imported status theatre, transactional networking, property-sales-funnel dynamics, token local participation and celebrity-driven selection.
14. **Next decision gate:** Founding 50 simulation plus membership-graph/density analysis before major property or capital commitments.
