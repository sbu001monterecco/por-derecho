# Multi-method forensic stack

**Control:** PD-TRUTH-METHOD-STACK-20260925-01

`method_stack.py` is the reusable fail-closed adjunct for proposition-level ACH, statement–evidence consistency, Verifiability, Wigmore evidence mapping, FATF financial rails, FCA expected-control evidence, PSFA/PEACE discipline, FBI-style linguistic probe cues and NIST/ENFSI validation boundaries. It accepts `pd.forensic.v1` and produces a private proposition matrix plus public aggregate. It never auto-promotes a cue to falsehood, knowledge, intent, guilt or liability and prohibits numeric truth/lie/credibility scores without validated method/reference data.

Regression: `python -m unittest recovery_tests.test_method_stack -v`.
