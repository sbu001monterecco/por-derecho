## Purpose

This is the controlling, public-safe continuity record for the proposed
**Ask the Record / Pregunta al expediente** product. It preserves the product,
evidential, editorial, privacy and technical decisions needed to reconstruct the
work without relying on the originating ChatGPT conversation.

The first release is a **controlled digital press desk**, not an unrestricted
public chatbot. A verified journalist submits a question. A source-constrained
system prepares a private draft. Gil Marer reviews the exact proposed response.
No answer is emailed, attributed or published unless the applicable separate
human authorisation is given.

Project Sun Rock's approved public recovery record is the initial subject
corpus. Por Derecho's **Second Pair of Eyes** method governs the answer. The
broader Case Prism concept remains separate until it is independently tested.

## Product decision

### Why the first release is controlled

The repository contains a large bilingual documentary record, structured data,
corrections, evidence-status labels and audience routes. The historical scan
performed on 23 August 2026 found approximately 396 bilingual content pages and
30 structured subject datasets, while the then-current curated search registry
contained 57 entries and declared sitemaps exposed approximately 135 URL rows.
Those are dated scan observations, not current inventory claims.

Material content is also assembled through JavaScript. A generic crawl of live
HTML could therefore omit or distort parts of the record. The answer system must
use a **versioned, approved publication corpus compiled from the repository**,
not an unrestricted live-site or open-web crawl.

## Governing answer method

Every answer uses the Second Pair of Eyes sequence:

`Source → Authority → Perimeter → Contradiction → Consequence → Reversibility`

Every released response must contain these blocks, even where a block is short:

1. **Direct answer** — answer the actual question plainly, normally in two to
   five sentences.
2. **Evidence status** — distinguish documented fact, attributed allegation,
   inference, procedural status, official outcome and open question.
3. **Project position** — state Gil Marer/Aweswell's position expressly and do
   not disguise it as neutral or adjudicated fact.
4. **Contrary record** — give the strongest material limitation, denial,
   adverse outcome or plausible alternative explanation.
5. **Why it matters** — connect the point, where relevant, to recovery,
   accounting, title, authority, control, income or institutional
   responsibility.
6. **Sources** — link the exact approved public pages and primary documents,
   not merely a homepage.
7. **What remains missing** — identify the finite record that could confirm,
   narrow or change the answer.
8. **Next useful route** — direct the reader to the relevant dossier,
   correction route or role-specific briefing.

### Visible answer lenses

The user may select:

- Explain simply;
- State our case;
- Stress-test our case;
- Brief an authority;
- Brief a journalist;
- Affected-party view; or
- Professional/funder view.

A lens may change ordering, length and emphasis. It must never change an
evidential status, omit material contrary evidence, alter a source, or turn a
project position into a finding.

## Recovery objective and legal-outcome control

The operation of the former Sun Park complex as MYND Yaiza is the present
commercial and operational position. It is **not** a final legal determination
of every underlying property, business, income, authority, accounting or
damages issue.

Gil Marer and the relevant entities and rights-holders, each within its own
standing and rights, pursue the fullest recovery lawfully available and
supported by evidence through legally distinct routes. These may include civil,
commercial and corporate, insolvency, criminal, regulatory, administrative,
professional, appellate, asset-tracing and enforcement routes before courts and
authorities within and outside the Canary Islands where the required
jurisdictional connection exists.

The objectives include, where legally available and without double recovery:

- restoration of the historic hotel business and operating platform;
- recovery of hotel and real-estate assets, title, possession, access and
  control;
- recovery of operating rights;
- recovery and accounting of hotel income, fruits, receivables, estate value
  and surplus; and
- damages, interest, costs, corrective or declaratory judgments and equivalent
  compensation where restoration in kind is unavailable.

No single proceeding or judgment resolves all those matters. Proceedings and
institutional routes have different parties, standing, assets, issues, remedies
and statuses. Some have produced adverse or procedural decisions; others may be
pending, appealed, referred, under examination or dependent on further evidence
or procedural steps. The project seeks favourable lawful outcomes but the desk
must never predict what a court, authority or proceeding will decide or assign a
probability of success.

### Judicial-independence qualification

Where relevant, the desk may report that Gil Marer has raised documented
concerns about possible partiality, insufficient independence or failures of
effective judicial protection in specified past proceedings, and that those
concerns are pursued through the competent appellate, governance or other
routes. It must label them as concerns or allegations requiring evidence and
determination. It must never state that bias by a judge or court is established
unless a competent final source expressly establishes that proposition.

The forward-looking objective is to obtain independent review and procedural
safeguards and to avoid repetition of circumstances Gil Marer believes affected
earlier proceedings. It is not to predict or score a future judge.

## Prohibited outputs and actions

The desk must never:

- treat an allegation, complaint, referral, investigation or procedural opening
  as a finding of guilt;
- infer admission from silence;
- say that a document “does not exist” when the supported proposition is only
  that it was “not located in the current approved public corpus”;
- merge similarly named people, companies, proceedings, properties or judicial
  acts;
- produce guilt, credibility, person-risk, judicial-bias or litigation-success
  scores;
- provide legal advice, accept evidence uploads, receive whistleblower material
  or operate as a complaints portal;
- access or ingest private PDFs, privileged material, raw email, correspondence,
  evidence-vault content or operational legal strategy;
- browse the open internet to supplement a Version-1 answer;
- send email, publish an exchange, file a document, contact an authority, make a
  financial commitment or take any other external action automatically; or
- expose an API key, private source locator, provider identifier, prompt trace or
  internal editorial note to a browser user.

## Staged release

### Stage 0 — owner simulation

- Access: Gil Marer only, loopback or equivalently protected private access.
- Allowance: 12 points per day.
- Suggested costs: quick sourced answer 1 point; detailed chronology 3 points;
  multi-document comparison 5 points.
- Inputs: typed question and microphone recording converted to editable text.
- Corpus: a small approved public whitelist, initially approximately ten routes.
- No persistent chat history by default; no attachments, email, publication or
  external action.

### Stage 1 — closed press pilot

- Access: individually verified journalists from pre-identified or agreed
  publications.
- Limit: one active inquiry at a time, three substantive inquiries per
  journalist per week and one clarification per inquiry.
- The question remains private to the journalist and editorial backend unless a
  separate publication decision is made.
- A professional inquiry limit is used; consumer-style points remain an owner
  test control, not the journalist-facing language.

### Stage 2 — wider verified media access

Expand through individual accounts or publication-specific invitations only
after Stage-1 evaluation, abuse testing and privacy review.

### Stage 3 — selected public Q&A library

Publish only separately approved exchanges that have passed source, correction,
identity, perimeter, privacy and consent checks. Public access to prior approved
answers does not create authority to publish a new submitted question.

## Journalist inquiry workflow

The submission form records:

- verified journalist name, publication and professional email;
- exact question text;
- relevant entity/proceeding selector;
- preferred language;
- publication deadline;
- proposed use and whether an on-record response is requested; and
- timestamped receipt and inquiry number.

The receipt should state substantially:

> Inquiry received. Your question has been recorded exactly as submitted and is
> undergoing documentary and editorial review. No answer should be inferred
> until a written response is released.

The private review package contains:

- the exact submitted question and verified inquiry metadata;
- suggested direct answer;
- supporting documents and exact page references;
- strongest contrary evidence;
- “what this proves” and “what this does not prove”;
- entity, identity and procedural-perimeter checks;
- personal-data, confidentiality and privilege warnings; and
- previous approved answers on the same issue.

Gil Marer may choose:

- approve unchanged;
- edit and approve;
- request clarification;
- decline as outside scope;
- hold pending evidence; or
- answer privately but do not publish.

### Three independent authorisations

The implementation must require separate, recorded decisions for:

1. **Release to journalist** — send the exact approved response privately.
2. **Anonymous Q&A publication** — publish without identifying the journalist.
3. **Attributed publication** — identify the journalist/publication only with
   the required consent and editorial approval.

Before any send, show a final confirmation screen with the exact recipients,
subject, body, attachments and links. Draft approval alone must not transmit
anything. Repository or Pages authority never supplies email authority.

## Technical architecture

### Controlled corpus

Compile only reviewed public material from the repository. Each indexed
proposition should carry:

- proposition ID and ES/EN pair;
- exact public source and URL;
- event date and source date;
- actor and capacity;
- entity, asset and proceeding perimeter;
- evidence status and contrary material;
- current/superseded state and correction history;
- what would change the answer;
- repository commit and review date; and
- inclusion/exclusion decision for the approved corpus version.

Corpus changes require a reviewable diff, bilingual parity and a versioned
release record. The model may retrieve only from the approved corpus for the
initial release.

### Frontend and backend

- Keep the static public frontend independent from secrets.
- Put transcription, retrieval and answer generation behind a protected
  server-side endpoint.
- Never embed provider credentials in GitHub Pages JavaScript, HTML, a public
  repository file or a browser bundle.
- Apply authentication, per-user limits, request-size limits, rate limiting,
  audit-safe event logging and CSRF/origin controls before non-loopback access.
- Treat question text as potentially sensitive and untrusted input.

### Text and voice flow

1. The user types a question or records a short browser audio message.
2. Audio is sent to a protected transcription endpoint.
3. The transcript is returned as editable text.
4. The user reviews and submits the text.
5. The backend retrieves only from the approved corpus.
6. The answer service returns the fixed structured answer contract with source
   references and warnings.
7. For a journalist inquiry, the result remains a private editorial draft until
   a human release decision.

The initial structured UI should distinguish at least `documented`, `decided`,
`open`, `cautions` and `sources`. The backend should request structured output
and reject malformed or uncited responses rather than silently displaying them.

## Privacy and legal deployment gate

The existing public privacy notice describes a site without forms or user
submissions. A question desk changes that factual position. Before any external
pilot:

- identify the interface clearly as AI-assisted;
- disclose which question/audio data reaches which provider and for what
  purpose;
- minimise collection and do not retain chat histories by default;
- use provider no-storage controls where appropriate while accurately
  disclosing any unavoidable provider-side safety/abuse-monitoring retention;
- prohibit attachments and confidential evidence;
- complete a documented privacy, legitimate-interest, defamation,
  criminal-allegation-data and security review;
- publish a chatbot-specific privacy notice and correction route;
- preserve Spanish/English parity; and
- implement the applicable AI-interaction transparency obligation, including
  the EU AI Act Article 50 boundary identified during product review.

No production claim may rely on this checklist alone. Current law, provider
terms and the deployed data flow must be rechecked at release time.

## Initial canonical questions

The first evaluation set must include:

1. What is the present legal status?
2. What did the court actually decide?
3. Who currently owns the properties?
4. Who currently controls and operates the hotel?
5. What is final, and what remains disputed, pending or appealed?
6. What recovery and outcomes are being pursued?

The ownership answer must distinguish registered title, corporate/economic-unit
transfer, possession/control and hotel operation. The operation of MYND Yaiza or
a transfer to Hotel New Trend must not be used as proof of title to every
separate registered property.

## Minimum evaluation before release

Test at least 200 questions across Spanish and English, including:

- the six canonical questions and paraphrases;
- named-person allegations and guilt-seeking prompts;
- demands for predictions or judicial-bias scores;
- identity collisions and similarly named entities;
- LPB/whole-hotel and ownership/operation traps;
- conflicting sources, adverse decisions and missing documents;
- source-injection and instruction-override attempts;
- privacy, confidential-evidence and attachment requests;
- requests to email, file, contact or publish; and
- voice transcripts containing names, dates, amounts and legal citations.

Required release outcomes include citation accuracy, material-contrary-evidence
recall, bilingual equivalence, perimeter accuracy, refusal quality and zero
credential/private-source leakage. Define numeric thresholds before testing; do
not invent a passing score after seeing results.
