# ICAM / CCACM — Cuatrecasas institutional-examination control

**Control date:** 25 August 2026  
**Publication class:** public-safe procedural and professional-governance control  
**Scope:** ICAM 434/26, CCACM 193/2026, ICAM 1487/26, RE-007622/26 / 1566/26, related ETJ 163/2020 acts and the Cuatrecasas professional-firm question

## Core conclusion

Institutional support means support for an independent, reasoned examination—not support for a party or for a predetermined sanction.

The strongest defensible formulation is not that Cuatrecasas has committed firm-wide misconduct. It is that identified later acts and steps carried out under the firm's name in pursuit of the credit it claims justify eight finite questions capable of confirming, narrowing or eliminating the professional-conduct hypothesis.

The examination must separate:

1. **individual conduct** — dated, authored or personally communicated acts;
2. **partner decision or supervision** — instruction, authorisation, supervision, ratification or strategic approval; and
3. **professional-firm responsibility** — only where the Article 128 threshold of concurrent or directly imputable responsibility is established.

## Procedural gate

CCACM formally opened 193/2026. On 26 June 2026 it said a supplemental writing would be joined to the file, but also invoked Article 118.1 of Law 39/2015 and rejected an open-ended sequence of replies or a request that CCACM transfer that writing back to ICAM.

**Operating consequence:** do not create a rolling supplementation cycle. Before any later filing:

- inspect the complete current CCACM file;
- identify an express institutional request or a genuinely supervening, dated and essential fact;
- explain why it could not have been produced earlier;
- keep the communication finite and tied to the pending appeal;
- preserve the respondents' hearing and defence rights; and
- do not use the appeal to introduce a new complaint.

## Minimum issue in 193/2026

CCACM can resolve the appeal without deciding the debt, the merits of ETJ 163/2020, damages or criminal liability.

The minimum issue is whether the 434/26 archive was premature because it:

- fixed the entire professional-conduct object at 2019;
- did not separately test whether identified 2024–2026 steps were purely automatic or reflected new professional choices;
- did not obtain a minimum explanation of client / payer / debtor attribution, former-client conflict, secrecy or the firm's decision chain; and
- may therefore have reached limitation before adequately delimiting the relevant acts.

Under Article 119 of Law 39/2015, CCACM can uphold, partly uphold, dismiss or reject the appeal. Where a formal defect prevents a merits determination, retroaction is available.

## Three responsibility levels

### Individual

- **Íñigo de Luisa:** prior professional relationship and knowledge, personal LinkedIn message, joint CCACM submissions. Do not attribute ETJ filings without proof of instruction, authorisation or ratification.
- **Juan Antonio Ruiz:** 23 June 2025 letter and joint CCACM submissions. Do not attribute LinkedIn or ETJ authorship without proof.
- **Teresa Morenés:** electronic signer of identified ETJ filings. Signature proves action, not the upstream instruction or approval chain.

### Partner decision or supervision

Identify who instructed, authorised, supervised or ratified:

- the transition from advice to own-account recovery;
- opposition to suspension or expert verification;
- the request for adjudication at 70%;
- the reiteration of adjudication; and
- the reservation of assignment of the remate to a third party.

Partner status alone is not proof.

### Professional firm

The professional firm is subject to professional regulation, but firm responsibility is not automatic. It requires evidence of concurrent responsibility, action for the firm's account and benefit through administrators or instructed representatives, or directly imputable conduct.

The proper inquiry is case-specific:

- client and matter identification;
- former-client conflict analysis;
- secrecy and information access;
- team separation and information barriers;
- recovery and adjudication approvals;
- ethics / risk / compliance or management escalation; and
- preservation of engagement, billing, conflict, DMS and decision records.

## Strongest defence

The following defence must be represented fairly:

1. the note and cambiario were instantaneous 2019 acts and are time-barred;
2. the 23 June 2025 letter was ordinary defence;
3. later enforcement was an automatic consequence of the existing process;
4. Gil, as Matkator's administrator, knew of the litigation;
5. cordial 2021 communications undermine later repudiation;
6. no confidential-information use or connected assignee has been proved;
7. ETJ acts belong territorially in Tenerife; and
8. no firm responsibility follows merely from individual action.

The framework survives that defence because it seeks no sanction without evidence. It asks what finite evidence can confirm or eliminate the disputed bridge.

## Eight finite questions

1. Who was the client, instructor, beneficiary, invoiced entity, expected payer, note issuer and judicial debtor?
2. When and for what matters did the engagement end?
3. What former-client conflict analysis was performed before recovery and enforcement?
4. What prior information was available, to whom, and under what barriers?
5. Who instructed, authorised or supervised each 2024–2026 ETJ step?
6. What review preceded the 70% adjudication request and assignment reservation?
7. Was ethics, risk, compliance or management consulted, and is there a record?
8. What documents provide the exculpatory account?

## Constructive professional-firm inspiration

Cuatrecasas' current public governance page says its Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct applies to partners, lawyers and staff; describes subscription every three months, information-security policies and compliance structures; and provides an Ethics Communication Channel.

This is not evidence that any control failed. It supports an institutionally neutral request:

> Please show how the firm's published governance architecture was applied to this matter.

A documented answer may exonerate the firm or narrow the concern.

The SRA Code of Conduct for Firms is a non-binding comparator only. Its methodological value is governance, systems, controls, records and manager accountability. It must never be presented as Spanish authority.

## Public/private boundary

Public pages may describe procedural status, distinct bodies, responsibility levels, the strongest defence, finite questions, the exculpatory gate and the legal availability of professional-firm regulation and preliminary information.

Do not publish full private emails, privileged lawyer-client material, current legal-team advice, a claimed assignee identity without primary proof, collusion or conspiracy as fact, a claim that `RECIBIDO` means institutional support, or firm responsibility without the Article 128 threshold.
