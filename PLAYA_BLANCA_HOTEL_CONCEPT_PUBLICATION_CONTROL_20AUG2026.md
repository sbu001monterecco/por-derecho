## Purpose

Publish a bilingual, carefully qualified future-development concept for Playa Blanca, municipality of Yaiza, southern Lanzarote, inside Project Sun Rock's **Future / Futuro** route.

The public treatment is deliberately broader than the confidential transaction perimeter. It identifies Playa Blanca, Yaiza and the development thesis while withholding the exact private site, parcel, ownership, sellers, broker, price and transaction terms.

## Implemented public proposition

- 100% hotel and non-residential.
- Indicative one-bedroom hotel-suite, inward-resort and outward local-services concept.
- Playa Blanca and Yaiza are identified; the exact private site, parcel, ownership and transaction perimeter are not.
- The visual language is original, stylised destination-poster work and is not represented as survey-verified architecture.
- The page explains proximity to the town and ferry harbour cautiously: a short drive is stated; walking/cycling routes and exact times remain subject to access and survey verification.
- Supercor Exprés / Supercor is identified only as a preferred target study. No operator affiliation, negotiation or commitment is represented.
- Destination indicators are contextual evidence of Playa Blanca/Lanzarote market depth, not a forecast of this project's viability or return.

## Strategic narrative implemented

The page links the development concept to Aweswell's confidence in Playa Blanca while preserving the Sun Park/MYND Yaiza recovery record as a separate matter.

The controlling proposition is:

> Recovery and reinvestment can proceed in parallel. The willingness to originate, capitalise and build again in Playa Blanca is presented as evidence of confidence in the destination, not as a factual, legal or transactional merger with Sun Park/MYND Yaiza.

The page does **not** represent this concept as:

- compensation for Sun Park;
- a replacement asset already offered or discussed by an authority;
- a project known to, endorsed by or under consideration by Yaiza, the Cabildo, the Canary Islands Government or another public authority;
- an approved or licensed project;
- a controlled or acquired site;
- a committed hotel, supermarket or financing arrangement.

## Separation controls

- The Sun Park/MYND Yaiza recovery record remains separate.
- The connection is confidence in Playa Blanca and willingness to invest again, not a factual or legal merger of sites, owners, files or claims.
- No public-authority awareness, dialogue, support, approval or consideration of this concept is represented.
- Existing Yaiza, Cabildo and regional engagements are linked only as separate institutional records and evidence of long-term engagement with place.
- No complaint, evidence, regulatory matter or non-disposable public-law duty is offered for withdrawal or exchange.
- The page is not a public investment offer, data room, planning application or claim that site control has been secured.
