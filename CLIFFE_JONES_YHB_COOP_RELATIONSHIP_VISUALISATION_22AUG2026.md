## 5. Six proof bridges

Before vicarious or derivative responsibility is asserted as fact, the record must test:

1. **Agency or employment:** authorised or apparent mandate.
2. **Operational control:** publication, booking code, CRM, merchant flow and correction power.
3. **Commissioning or supply:** access, text, images, hospitality, referral terms or payment.
4. **Adoption or ratification:** adoption, retained benefit or refusal to correct after adequate notice.
5. **Coordinated purpose:** communications and timing showing a common objective rather than parallel conduct.
6. **Connected benefit and causation:** enquiries, bookings, commissions, reputation or value and the challenged conduct's causal contribution.
