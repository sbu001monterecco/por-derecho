## 1. 20 January 2017 — genuine texto definitivo modification comparator

**20 January 2017 is a genuine `texto definitivo` modification event.** The Community-credit family demonstrates that formal statutory machinery existed and was actually used to alter the definitive texts.

This comparator must be used to test later disputed treatment. It is relevant to authority, capacity, procedural opportunity, differential treatment, knowledge and possible `fraude concursal` / `fraude procesal` hypotheses. It does not by itself establish deceit, capture, intent or criminal liability.

## 2. 8 February 2018 — change of creditor is not change of credit

The Promontoria → Construcciones Acosta Matos succession is a fundamentally different legal event from an amount increase.

The primary order states that the assignment changes creditor identity **but not amount**. The located special-privilege figures are:

- mortgage loan 1: **€8,194,877.88**;
- mortgage loan 2: **€857,373.81**;
- recognized fixed special-privilege total: **€9,052,251.69**;
- plus contingent enforcement costs without their own fixed quantified amount in that order.

The permanent visual and analytical rule is:

# CHANGE OF CREDITOR ≠ CHANGE OF CREDIT

and, where the source wording is being explained:

# 8 FEBRUARY 2018 — “NOT CHANGE THE AMOUNT”

An unchanged amount does not establish lawful authority, lawful representation, valid standing, truthful provenance, or lawful procedural effect. The 8-Feb event must therefore be read both as a creditor-succession/final-text event and through the separate **AUTHORITY BEFORE ARITHMETIC** rule.

## 5. Five-column credit ontology — permanent separation

Every amount on the lender/CAM/final-text track must be assigned to exactly one or more expressly justified columns. Never collapse them into one undifferentiated “credit” figure.

| Column | Question | Examples of evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **RECOGNITION** | What amount/classification was admitted or recognized in the definitive texts? | definitive creditor list, final-text modification order |
| **ACCRUAL** | What interest/cost component lawfully accrued after recognition, to what date and within what security ceiling? | loan terms, interest statements, LC art. 59 analysis, mortgage limits |
| **LIQUIDATION / PAYMENT** | What payoff or liquidation amount was used for payment, plan execution or rival-bid threshold? | certificates, liquidation-plan orders, payoff calculations |
| **ADJUDICATION / SET-OFF** | What amount was actually credited, compensated or set off in adjudication/deed/implementation? | adjudication order, deed, accounting, Registry bridge |
| **MODIFICATION** | Was the definitive inventory/list itself formally altered? | statutory application, contradictory route where required, signed modification order, corrected list/inventory, finality |

**Numerical continuity is not procedural authority.** Similarity between **€13,165,832.36** in June 2018 and approximately **€13,168,082.02** later does not, by itself, prove a lawful modification of the definitive texts.

### Permanent final-text gate

> **No change to the texto definitivo is to be recorded in Por Derecho unless the repository identifies the application, statutory gateway, contradictory procedure where required, judicial resolution ordering the modification, exact before/after entry, date of finality and resulting corrected inventory or creditor list. A later debt certificate, AC calculation, liquidation-plan order, clarification, deed recital or accounting statement is not to be treated as an amendment merely because it contains a different number.**
