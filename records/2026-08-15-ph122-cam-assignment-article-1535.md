# Evidential record — PH122/CAM assignment, disputed debt and effective judicial protection

**Record date:** 15 August 2026  
**Assignment date:** 20 October 2017  
**Matter:** LPB / Concurso 36/2012 / mortgage credits assigned by Promontoria Holding 122 B.V. to Construcciones Acosta Matos, S.A.

## Evidential proposition

Before the assignment, LPB had placed the balance and enforceability of the financing package in concrete and quantified dispute. The complete assignment deed, its schedules, allocated price and debt calculation were not made effectively available to LPB before procedural effect was sought. A later judicial order required CAM to produce the complete deed, exact price, payment date and costs.

The combined sequence supports investigation of whether opacity and divergent debt figures deprived LPB of an effective opportunity to object, invoke Article 1535, challenge creditor substitution and protect the insolvency estate. It also supports scrutiny of an objective appearance of favourable treatment, institutional bias and deficient effective judicial protection.

## Primary record matrix

1. **24 June 2016 — Cristo A. Suárez Pimentel legal report.** Identifies allegedly incorrect balances, duplication, uncredited payments or partial mortgage releases, challengeable clauses, interest and swap issues.
2. **12 July 2016 — formal notice to the insolvency administration.** Quantifies approximately €2,874,076.19 excess; identifies €603,508.80 in alleged undeducted releases and possible €850,000 double counting; requests correction and authority to bring proceedings.
3. **15 May 2017 — PH122 reconsideration filing.** PH122 actively opposes the composition and seeks liquidation.
4. **20 October 2017 — PH122/CAM assignment.** Complete deed, schedules, allocated price and calculation remain required.
5. **12 January 2018 — order in DP 1041/2017.** CAM ordered to produce the complete deed, exact price, payment date and transaction costs.
6. **8 February 2018 — insolvency order.** Rejects Article 1535 under a restrictive approach but records that substitution cannot alter amount and identifies €857,373.81 and €8,194,877.88, plus contingent enforcement costs.

## Responsibility questions

### Promontoria Holding 122 B.V.
What balance was sold, what adjustments were made, at what allocated price, and why was the complete deed not supplied to LPB before procedural consequences were sought?

### Construcciones Acosta Matos, S.A.
Why was voluntary complete production not made? What debt figure did it acquire and assert? Did it know of the 2016 objections and the final insolvency figures? What benefit flowed from delayed disclosure?

### Insolvency administration
What verification was performed? How were conflicting figures reconciled? Why was substitution supported without disclosure of the essential instrument to LPB? What was done to preserve LPB's claims and equality of arms?

### Insolvency court
Why was complete production to LPB declined or delayed while verification by the insolvency administration was presumed? How were the conflicting figures, direct interest of the buyer, adversarial hearing and effective judicial protection addressed?

## Cumulative hypothesis requiring investigation

Prior quantified dispute + opaque assignment + divergent figures + supported substitution + delayed or denied disclosure + lost opportunity to object may have manufactured a civil appearance of an undisputed debt and enabled that appearance to be used against redemption, funded exit and protection of LPB's estate.

## Legal limitation

Article 1535 Civil Code defines litigiosity from the answer to the claim concerning the credit. The material dispute is documented. Strict qualification still requires the pre-20 October 2017 judicial pleading and responsive act for each loan. This record does not declare criminal guilt. It preserves correction, reply and the presumption of innocence.
