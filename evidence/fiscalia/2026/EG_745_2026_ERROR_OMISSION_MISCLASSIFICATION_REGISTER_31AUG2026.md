# E.G. 745/2026 — error, omission and misclassification register — 31 August 2026

## Control rule

This register is a review/audit instrument, not a declaration of illegality or criminality. Each entry is classified as one of:

- **SOURCE-PROVED DISCREPANCY** — two controlled sources can be compared directly;
- **REVIEW GROUND / ALLEGED MISCLASSIFICATION** — a legal/procedural contention requiring determination;
- **OMISSION REQUIRING EXPLANATION OR PRODUCTION** — the located decision does not expressly answer or expose the relevant item;
- **UNRESOLVED EVIDENTIAL QUESTION** — the answer depends on institution-held material not yet produced;
- **PROJECT-SIDE CONTROL GAP** — our own unresolved source/procedure issue and not attributable to the Ministerio Fiscal without further evidence.

The official Decree's adverse outcome, silence, sequence or non-production does not by itself establish bad faith, obstruction, coordination, prevarication or criminality.

## D. Potential legal/procedural classification issues to preserve for counsel

These are **not established legal errors**. They are questions for the reposición/direct-review route and counsel control.

1. Whether invocation of Article 66.1(c) without an exposed Article 68 cure analysis is legally sustainable in the circumstances.
2. Whether the Decree adequately motivates why a structured 12-request filing with six named proceedings is nevertheless `confusa e incoherente`.
3. Whether the Decree lawfully required the filer to supply personal attribution that the filing expressly asked Inspection to reconstruct from institution-controlled records.
4. Whether a global archive could dispose of distinct preservation, source-fixing, traceability, file-production and referral requests without individualized treatment.
5. Whether the administrative file sufficiently records what evidence was considered and what institutional checks were undertaken before archive.
6. Whether the notification mechanism/date and the stated review routes/deadlines are correctly fixed for the chosen challenge route.

## F. Public strategy

The public strategy is to make each contested proposition testable:

**source → official characterization → identified discrepancy/omission → production request or review ground → filing receipt → institutional response → unresolved gap/correction**.

Do not publish a non-response as obstruction. Publish it only as the procedural fact that a defined request was filed on a defined date and no response has yet been located as of a defined control date.
