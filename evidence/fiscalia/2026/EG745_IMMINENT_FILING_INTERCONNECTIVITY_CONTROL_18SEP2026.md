## 4. Inspection's own identity-finding mechanism

The current Reglamento del Ministerio Fiscal matters directly:

- **Article 163:** a complaint/queja concerning prosecutorial conduct is to be sent to Inspección with the related antecedents/documents held by the receiving office.
- **Article 164.1.b:** where indications may exist but the elements required for direct disciplinary initiation are not sufficiently established, Inspección **may** open diligencias informativas.
- **Article 167.1 and 167.3:** those diligencias may perform the indispensable checks to test plausibility and **determine the identity of the presumed author or authors**, including requesting documents or other appropriate steps.

Official source: https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2022-7184

This does **not** establish that Inspección was legally obliged to open diligencias informativas in E.G. 745. It does make it necessary, if absence of final personal attribution was decisive, to explain why the Article 164/167 mechanism was not appropriate on the facts presented.
