## Public-source labels

Use these labels or their clear equivalent on public pages and controlled records:

- **PRIMARY-SOURCE FACT** — directly shown by a preserved native source or controlled receipt.
- **PROCEDURAL FACT** — docket/notification/filing status supported by a reliable procedural record.
- **OUR ALLEGATION** — attributed contention, not an adjudicated fact.
- **INFERENCE / HYPOTHESIS** — analytical proposition requiring testing.
- **UNRESOLVED EVIDENTIAL QUESTION** — evidence needed before verification.
- **INDEPENDENT DETERMINATION REQUIRED** — matter for the competent institution/court.
