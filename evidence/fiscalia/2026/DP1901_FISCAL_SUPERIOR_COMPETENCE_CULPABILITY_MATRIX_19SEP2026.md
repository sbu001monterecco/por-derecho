## Post-notice rule

Repeated conduct after a contradiction has been formally notified must be analyzed separately from the initial event. A later actor is not automatically responsible for the original intake/reparto event, but a distinct responsibility hypothesis arises where the actor:
- knew or objectively had the contradiction before him/her;
- had power/duty to verify or correct;
- nevertheless repeated/propagated the premise or produced additional prejudice;
- and lacks a sufficient lawful explanation.
