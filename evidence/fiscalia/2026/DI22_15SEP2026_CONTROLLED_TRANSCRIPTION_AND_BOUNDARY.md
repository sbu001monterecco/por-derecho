## Controlled transcription / substantive content

The decree records that writings presented by **Gil Marer on 20, 26 and 27 August 2026** reiterate prior writings already resolved and notified. It orders those writings to be joined to the preprocedural file and states that the previously agreed archive is to remain in place.
