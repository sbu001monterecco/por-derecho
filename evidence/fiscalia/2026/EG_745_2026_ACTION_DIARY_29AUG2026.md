## Status vocabulary

- **DRAFTED:** text exists but has not been approved for filing.
- **APPROVED:** authorised for filing, but no filing proof located.
- **FILED:** supported by a preserved sent/registry/judicial receipt or equivalent.
- **ACKNOWLEDGED:** recipient acknowledgement preserved.
- **AWAITING RESPONSE:** filed/acknowledged and response remains outstanding.
- **SUPERSEDED:** replaced by a later controlled version without erasing history.
- **OUTSTANDING:** required action remains open.

## Deadline boundary

The one-month reposición calculation currently used in the repository is a **controlled working deadline**, not a substitute for counsel verification. It uses 26 August 2026 as the located notification date and rolls the corresponding Saturday 26 September date to Monday 28 September. The legally effective notification date and any special rule must be checked before filing. The direct contentious-administrative deadline is intentionally not converted into an asserted final date here.

## Closure rule

Every filing closes only when the repository records: (1) exact filed bytes/version; (2) destination; (3) timestamp; (4) receipt/reference; and (5) any acknowledgement. Until then, the status remains no higher than `APPROVED / OUTSTANDING`.
