## Controlled conclusion

The 29 July DP 1901 archive request is significant because it converts one formerly open node into a dated prosecutorial act. It does not eliminate the need to prove author, knowledge, reasoning, corpus, causal effect and lawful alternative. It should be read together with — not used to blur — the older DI 248, DI 113, DI 22, DIP 2 and Calificación records.


## Imminent-filing interpretation

The response should not be framed as a demand that Inspection accept a global capture conclusion. The finite test is whether materially adverse treatment was asymmetric and whether that asymmetry produced a de facto protective effect for the private-actor perimeter without equivalent actor-specific scrutiny. If Inspection disagrees, the strongest answer is the underlying file: who examined what, which diligences were performed, how later/distinct facts were treated, and what lawful explanation accounts for each archive/referral.
