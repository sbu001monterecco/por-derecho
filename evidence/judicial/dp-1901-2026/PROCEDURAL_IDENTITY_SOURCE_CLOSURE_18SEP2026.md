## Evidential rule

This record separates:

1. **stamped presentation evidence** — proves physical presentation/label/content shown on the face of the source;
2. **official ATLANTE/court records** — prove the fields actually displayed by the official source;
3. **contemporaneous correspondence** — proves what was being reported/understood at the time, but is not a substitute for a certified reparto/index;
4. **later court acts** — prove how the court later characterised DP 1901.

No source below, alone or in combination, proves intentional misrouting, improper joinder, prosecutorial misconduct, judicial prevarication or coordination.

## Chronology that materially narrows the collision

### 18 June — two different complaint packages existed from inception

The two stamped presentation covers establish separate packages:
- daily reference **22**: Administrador Concursal complaint;
- daily reference **24**: judge/judicial-conduct complaint addressed to the TSJC Civil/Criminal Chamber and expressly framed as denuncia/notitia criminis.

This excludes any reconstruction in which references 22 and 24 were merely alternate labels for one filing.

### 25 June — a third autonomous complaint was filed, while the judge supplement remained separately identified

The stamped private-actor cover establishes a new autonomous five-private-actor filing under daily reference **21**.

The separate 13-page stamped supplement expressly says it is to be joined to the **18 June daily-reference-24 judge complaint**. This excludes treating the supplement as an amplification of daily reference 21 or 22.

A contemporaneous email sent at **11:23 on 25 June to Procuradora María Díaz Vecino** records that Gil had just filed a **third, autonomous and separate private-actor complaint under daily reference 21**, attached its stamped cover for tracking, and separately filed the dependent Ref. 24 supplement. The same email reports Ref. 24 still at Decanato and not yet remitted. This is contemporaneous evidence of the filing identity and Gil's understanding on the filing day; it is not a substitute for the court's certified electronic reparto log.

### 9 July — private-actor material was physically tendered under DP 1901 / Plaza 6

The ATLANTE image officially displays:
- DP 1901/2026 — Plaza 6 — Gil Marer as denunciante;
- DP 1956/2026 — Plaza 1 — Gil Marer as denunciante.

The ATLANTE image itself does not label either proceeding by defendant or subject.

Separately, the stamped 9-Jul cover proves that an amplification/documentary contribution concerning the private-actor lane was physically presented **for DP 1901/2026, Plaza 6**.

Contemporaneous correspondence to the procuradora and counsel describes the operational understanding at that time as:
- 1901: CAM/private/Community complaint;
- 1956: AC complaint.

The 9-Jul email to counsel is more specific than the earlier summary: it records `1901: Contra CAM y otras partes de la Comunidad de Propietarios` and `1956: Contra la AC`. A same-day email to the procuradora records that Gil had returned personally to the Decanato, tendered the CAM/private-actor amplification and was supplied an ATLANTE copy showing the two already-opened matters. These are contemporaneous party/counsel communications and therefore evidence of the operational understanding then being acted on; they remain distinct from a certified initiating/reparto record.

### 10–12 July — contemporaneous court-contact account and the signed providencia diverge in subject characterisation

A contemporaneous 12-Jul email to counsel reports that Gil had returned to **Juzgado de Instrucción n.º 6** regarding “la denuncia contra CAM y la CP” identified as 1901/2026; staff reportedly confirmed a providencia existed, would be made available once signed and would give traslado to Fiscalía.

The signed paper providencia now re-photographed on 19-Sep identifies the same DP 1901/NIG/IUP, is electronically signed by Luis Francisco Galván Mesa on 12-Jul-2026, and formally sends the admission question to Ministerio Fiscal for five days **in relation to DIP 2/2026**. Gil states that a paper copy was made available informally by the court office while he was following DP 1901 as the CAM/private-actor matter; no formal service/receipt is inferred from that account.

The email chain is contemporaneous evidence of the filing identity and operational mapping, not an official electronic reparto certificate. Read together, the 25 June email to Procuradora María Díaz Vecino, the stamped Ref. 21 cover, the 9 July private-actor tender under DP 1901 and the 9 July correspondence expressly recording `1901: Contra CAM y otras partes de la Comunidad de Propietarios` materially corroborate Gil's firsthand statement that Ref. 21 became DP 1901. The signed providencia controls as to its own 12 July text. What remains uncertified is the court's internal electronic creation/association event trail and the later mechanism by which the Ref. 24 judge material came to be treated within DP 1901.

### 14 September — the later Auto expressly characterises DP 1901 as alleged judicial prevaricación

The 14-Sep Auto states that DP 1901 was opened on Gil Marer's complaint for alleged prevaricación, records a 29-Jul Fiscal report seeking archive, and orders sobreseimiento libre/archive.

## What remains unproved

- daily reference 21 generated DP 1901 and daily reference 24 was later routed/merged into it;
- daily reference 24 generated DP 1901 and the private-actor 9-Jul material was mistakenly tendered into it;
- a formal joinder/reassignment/segregation occurred but its act has not yet been produced;
- another administrative/reparto explanation reconciles the sources.

Therefore the canonical state is:

**REF21_TO_DP1901_CONTEMPORANEOUSLY_CORROBORATED — OFFICIAL_REPARTO_AND_REF24_ASSOCIATION_HISTORY_OUTSTANDING.**
