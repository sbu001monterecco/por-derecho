**Public layer:** hashes, redacted substantive text and neutral source cards only  
**Private layer:** exact source binaries, signatures, verification codes, contact data and confidential material

## Source-status grammar

- **Holding:** what an official act decided.
- **Recital/observation:** what an official act reports or observes without necessarily deciding.
- **Party assertion:** a party's position, not an adjudicated fact.
- **Expert opinion:** the attributed opinion of the report author/commissioning side.
- **Open:** the needed source has not been located or authenticated in this corpus.

### `MAT-008` / `C36-JUD-2021-02-24-001` — preservation/inspection reposición

- **Source:** official electronic copy, SHA-256 `3b02a944ae5becb154bdc2109935d060de80bb9e862f10ab055582696e5ee40f`, 4 pages.

The Auto itself noted that such a request might make sense in a possible responsibility action against CAM or the AC.
