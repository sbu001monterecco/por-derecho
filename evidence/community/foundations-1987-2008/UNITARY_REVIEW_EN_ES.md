### 1. Read the cost provisions together

The May 2008 contract puts operating costs before distribution of the remainder (clause X) and assigns operation-derived expenses to CEXP during the agreement (XII). Clause VII also requires separate accounting of prior Montelanza rights and obligations and a joint transitional committee. Clause XIII distinguishes ordinary maintenance from extraordinary investment requiring the stated authority.

**Result:** identify the actual payer, invoice party, operating purpose, period, authorisation and claimant. A payment by another project entity may support the collective hotel operation without becoming a payment from a CEXP bank account or automatically assigning the payer's rights. Neither an isolated Pink-only account nor an undifferentiated CEXP-everything account is sufficient. This does not concede the validity of the separately disputed LPB–Pink agreement. Source: CEXP compilation pp11–14.

### 2. Management, continuity and authority are separate tests

CEXP articles 5 and 16 permit external management and delegation. A manager or a different payer does not itself extinguish the collective structure. But the permission is not proof of an actual later mandate. Read it with the contract's duration and property-transfer restrictions, the subscribing-owner perimeter and the missing authority annex. The annual renewal, indefinite operating cession and older regime's maximum-duration provision concern different instruments; none should silently decide all later authority. Sources: CEXP pp1–7,9–10,14; 1987 article6.

### 3. Apply the same controls to our own side

CEXP article19 prescribes books, annual accounts and advance member access. Governance, delegation and extraordinary-spending controls apply to the relevant project-side office-holders too. Paying hotel costs does not establish that every requested record was delivered. A missing item in the held corpus is not proof it never existed. Useful professional work does not answer every alleged decisive failure; an adverse outcome does not make every professional act wrongful.

The AC's 2019 report includes an operating-side counter-account; do not call it wholly absent. The question is its verification, classification and treatment. The private review supplies exact source anchors and competing explanations; it does not convert an inference into a bank trace or a disputed conclusion into proved knowing falsity.

### 5. Join acts without inventing an edge

A judicial record must be examined for actual receipt, issue, decision and effect; the AC's knowledge does not automatically become the judge's or a prosecutor's personal knowledge.

Deben conservarse pagador, destinatario de factura, finalidad, período, autorización y titular del derecho.
