The joined findings

Costs: CEXP clauses X and XII support the collective operating account. Clause VII requires separate legacy Montelanza accounting; XIII distinguishes extraordinary investment. Preserve payer, invoice party, operating benefit, authority and claimant.

Management: articles 5 and 16 permit delegation; they do not prove each later mandate. Different duration and transfer clauses must retain their own objects and conditions.

Our side: article19 requires books, accounts and member access. Useful professional work and operating contributions do not answer every delivery or protection question.

Later use: do not call a quoted counter-account absent, a suspected foreign receipt a bank trace, or a recorded authorisation complete implementation. The full review tests the AC report and later-use hypotheses without replacing the actual sources.
