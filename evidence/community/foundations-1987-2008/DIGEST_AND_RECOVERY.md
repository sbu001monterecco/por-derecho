### Read the provisions together / Lectura conjunta

**Costs:** clauses VII, X, XII and XIII distinguish old Montelanza obligations, operating costs, distribution and extraordinary investment. They do not make every later payment one undifferentiated expense or identify a later payer automatically.

**Duration and transfer:** the statutes' one-year/renewal provisions, the contract's indefinite transfer and its clause XV restrictions are retained together. No editorial amendment resolves their interpretation.

**Authority:** the contract invokes a 21-May-2008 board agreement. The eight contract pages are held; that authority annex is not included in the carrier.

**Costes:** VII, X, XII y XIII separan obligaciones anteriores, gastos operativos, remanente e inversión. No se atribuye automáticamente todo pago posterior a una sola persona o derecho.

**Duración/cesión:** se conservan juntas las reglas anuales estatutarias, la cesión indefinida y la cláusula XV, sin corregir su interpretación editorialmente.

## 5. Publication and evidence boundary

A digest, full held-page transcription, source facsimile, authenticated original, annex-complete package, valid authority and later legal effect are different states. No missing instrument is filled with a plausible reconstruction. Public transcriptions omit only labelled personal identifiers, home addresses and autograph images. Original private copies are not modified. Facsimile derivatives are prepared separately; publication/deployment is reported only after actual remote readback. No statement of universal mandate, current enforceability, misappropriation or criminal guilt follows automatically from this source reader.
