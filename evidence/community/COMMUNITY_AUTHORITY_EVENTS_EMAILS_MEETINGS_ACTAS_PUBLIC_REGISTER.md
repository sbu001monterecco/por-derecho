**Cut-off:** 28 August 2026

**Status:** canonical public-safe event/source control; **all 19 located ACTA/minutes control-copy families plus one separately classified non-ACTA RICPE notice/agenda package digitised and prepared in the current repository worktree; reviewed merge, deployment and live readback remain pending; source certification/completion remains in progress**

**Scope:** Owners' Community, CEXP, meeting notices, ACTAs, direct communications, implementation records, challenges and downstream reliance relevant to the Sun Park authority/debt/vote/exploitation chain.

## 1. Controlling purpose

This ledger is the single source-controlled index for reconstructing each material event as a chain:

`notice / agenda` → `meeting / attendance / proxies` → `signed ACTA / resolution` → `implementation / communication` → `challenge / institutional reliance`.

It prevents three recurring errors:

1. treating a located filename, later recital or summary as the signed original;
2. treating a structured digest as a complete transcription; and
3. treating source discovery as proof of delivery, validity, performance, reliance or legal effect.

As at this cut-off, all **19 located ACTA/minutes control-copy families** and the **one located non-ACTA RICPE notice/agenda** have generated public-safe digitisation packages prepared in the current repository worktree: page-sequenced public-redacted text or explicit full-page redaction markers, rendered text-edition PDF/pages, raster-only irreversibly redacted source facsimile, redacted source-page image gallery, provenance, redaction log and integrity manifest. Reviewed merge, Pages deployment and live readback are not yet claimed. The 20 packages represent **412 source pages**, **448 text-edition pages**, **20 source facsimiles**, **20 text-edition PDFs** and **860 current JPEG page images** (412 source-gallery images plus 448 text-edition previews). The separately retained superseded layer contains **209 legacy WEBP previews** and is not folded into that current count. This is completion of derivative generation for the located control copies, not certification: every OCR remains uncertified and not manually verified line by line; received copies are not presented as official minute books or certified originals. The unredacted native sources and variants remain separately controlled and outside the public repository.

The standalone 20-Nov-2018 ACTA remains referenced only in later material and unlocated. The five-page president's statement referenced by the located 10-Aug-2012 ACTA is now located at pages 66–70 of the 155-page 10-Apr-2014 notarial package; the separate 2012 objection writing remains unlocated. The located RICPE source is a received notice/agenda dated 11-Feb-2022 that attributes a proposed 11/12-Mar-2022 corporate shareholders' call to the RICPE board through its secretary; it is **not an ACTA and does not independently prove authorship, actual issuance, service, recipient knowledge, occurrence, quorum, votes or outcome**. Its recital of a 29-Dec-2021 shareholder resolution supports a separate gap event, not a reconstructed meeting or minutes. Non-location is not non-existence.

## 2. Evidence and publication codes

### Evidence status

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `located-package-digitised-public` | machine status for a located control-copy package whose page-sequenced public-redacted text/markers, rendered text PDF/pages, raster-only redacted source facsimile, source-page gallery and manifest exist; it does not certify OCR, authenticity, family completeness or legal validity |
| `P1` | native or signed primary document located |
| `P2` | primary-document copy or attachment located; certification/original-book comparison open |
| `D1` | direct contemporaneous email, notice, delivery or meeting record |
| `R1` | later recital, pleading, analysis or reconstruction; not a substitute for the primary source |
| `A1` | attributed party allegation; not an adjudicated fact |
| `J1` | judicial/administrative act, limited to what that act decides or records |
| `O` | open or unresolved |

### Publication status

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `PUB-FULL-PENDING` | complete public-safe transcription and redacted facsimile are not yet both available |
| `PUB-TEXT-EDITION-PARTIAL` | a page-sequenced public-redacted OCR text edition and rendered PDF/previews exist, but manual line verification, source facsimile and/or source-family reconciliation remain open |
| `PUB-DIGITISED` | the located control copy has page-sequenced public-redacted OCR text or explicit page-redaction markers, a text-edition PDF/pages, raster-only redacted source facsimile, redacted source-page images and integrity manifest; this does not certify the OCR, source authenticity or family completeness |
| `PUB-DIGEST` | structured redacted digest exists; it is not a full line-by-line transcription |
| `PUB-INDEX` | public-safe metadata/source index only |
| `PRIVATE` | native body is retained only in controlled source custody pending review |
| `PRIV-REVIEW` | confidentiality or legal-privilege review is required before any body text is published |
| `MISSING` | referenced source has not been isolated in the searched universe |

## 8. Public/private and privilege decision rule

For each source, preserve two deliberately separate objects:

1. **private preservation master:** native binary or native export, full headers/revisions, SHA-256, acquisition metadata and access control; and
2. **public derivative:** verified text, page references, neutral redactions and a redacted facsimile whose redaction is burned in and re-extracted to confirm that hidden text is absent.

Do not publish:

- signatures, DNI/NIF, private addresses, telephone numbers or personal email addresses;
- owner-by-owner debt/bank/payment rows unless lawfully necessary and separately reviewed;
- bank account data, tax-reserved records or confidential corporate/personnel data;
- raw meeting audio or unverifiable speaker labels;
- full lawyer/client, PwC or litigation-strategy communications without privilege/confidentiality review; or
- allegations of fraud/crime as findings.

Indexing a private email by date, subject family, functional capacity, attachment name and stable opaque ID does not waive privilege and does not authorise publication of its body.
