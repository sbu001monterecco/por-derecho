## Controlled finding

The one-page communication dated 27 February 2018 records the insolvency administrator of Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. asking the President of the Sun Park Owners’ Community to convene a General Meeting so that the Community could consider hiring a security service. The stated purposes were to prevent access by unauthorised persons and to prevent deterioration and improper use of common and privately owned areas.

The communication is material to the pre-7 June chronology and to the documentary route by which security was placed before the Community. It must not be expanded beyond its wording.

## What the source directly supports

1. A direct communication by the named insolvency administrator, expressly invoking that capacity.
2. A request directed to the Community presidency to convene a General Meeting.
3. A proposed agenda purpose: adoption of an agreement to hire security.
4. The stated objectives: preventing access by unauthorised persons and preventing deterioration/improper use.
5. A stated assertion that LPB held the majority of participation quotas in the Community.

## What the source does not establish by itself

- who qualified as an “unauthorised person”;
- the finca-by-finca or operational perimeter of any security mandate;
- authority to break locks, replace cylinders, place chains or seize keys;
- authority to exclude workers, guests, owners or persons exercising rights over non-LPB assets;
- transfer of title, possession, hotel operation or whole-complex control;
- the validity, attendance, voting or scope of a later Community resolution;
- the identity, contract, payer, instructions or conduct of any security provider;
- that later acts were ordered by the insolvency administrator;
- a criminal agreement or final legal characterisation.
