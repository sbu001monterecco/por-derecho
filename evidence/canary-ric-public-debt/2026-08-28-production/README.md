# Canary Treasury production — public document room

**Control:** `PD-CANARY-RIC-PUBLIC-DEBT-DOCROOM-20260831-01`
**Scope:** eight files, 734 source pages, delivered following Treasury Resolution 154/2026
**Public form:** eight searchable, public-redacted OCR derivatives; eight page-sequenced Spanish OCR transcripts; eight redaction logs; a complete 734-page partitioned JSON index; and a hash manifest.

Each public PDF, including the two-page sharing exhibit, is stored byte-for-byte as independently encoded JSON-contained Base64 chunks because the authenticated repository connector cannot transmit large binary blobs directly. The short JSON segments keep the transport auditable by repository privacy controls. The bilingual viewer reconstructs the PDF in the browser, verifies its exact byte count and SHA-256, and only then enables display and download. This is a transport representation, not a content reduction: every public-derivative byte is present in the repository.

The native files remain in protected custody. They are not silently replaced by these derivatives. OCR is not certified line by line, the access-delivery derivative does not preserve the native digital signature, and any material quotation should be checked against the displayed page and the retained native source.

## Unitary findings

1. The 2022 borrowing-authorisation request expressly contemplated loans, bonds and a possible RIC debt issue. Its first three pages describe possible RIC terms and an Article 27.4.D.4 investment destination. This corpus does **not** establish execution, subscription, settlement or allocation of a 2022 RIC issue.
2. The 2024 AFI contracting memorandum says RIC debt could reduce the Canary Community's financing burden because the investor's financial-tax return permits a lower coupon. It also defines work on issue design, financial-tax scenarios, prospectus, placement, IBERCLEAR/AIAF and order allocation.
3. The production documents the public RIC issues made in 2024 and 2025. It contains no located named occurrence of Acosta, Hotel New Trend, MYND or RICPE. That search result is not proof that no connection exists in records outside this tranche.
4. The 2024/2025 public issues post-date the December-2022 and June-2023 HNT participating loans and cannot be their original source. No public-bond-to-HNT allocation is established.
5. The 2025 published initial minimum allocation of EUR 80,000 and an operational reference to EUR 100,000 remain to be reconciled.
6. The signed delivery act describes staged production. Later material promised after review and dissociation remains open.
