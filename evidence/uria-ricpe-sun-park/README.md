## Event register

### 28 April–4 May 2015 — creditor / HAYA / Uría communications and counter-evidence

A native email thread records contemporaneous reports that HAYA had asked for case material to be passed toward **Uría Menéndez in Madrid** and that **Javier González Guimaraes-Da Silva** was involved in a communication about continuing the proceeding. Those are **documented reported communications**, not the native HAYA instruction, Uría engagement or Javier González communication.

The same thread contains material counter-evidence. On **30 April 2015**, Cuatrecasas reported after speaking with Uría that Uría said it **did not yet have instructions to take any action on behalf of HAYA**. Further correspondence records that the local enforcement lawyer confirmed acting for **SAREB rather than Bankia**. These points must remain visible because they prevent the 2015 evidence from being collapsed into an assumed HAYA retainer or a simplified creditor identity.

Publication status: **DOCUMENTED REPORTED COMMUNICATIONS + CONTRADICTORY/QUALIFYING EVIDENCE; CLIENT / MANDATE / TITLE REMAIN OPEN**.
