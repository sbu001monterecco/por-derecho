## What the thread establishes at source level

Juan Tomás Parrilla wrote to Daniel Jiménez that he had spoken with the Administrador Concursal about recognition of his fees and those of LPB's procuradora. In Parrilla's own email he stated that:

- the AC accepted recognition of an **€80,000 + IGIC crédito contra la masa** for the insolvency application and LPB legal representation;
- the procuradora had communicated fees around **€20,000 + IGIC**, which Parrilla said the AC was also willing to recognise absent opposition;
- concerning payment, Parrilla wrote that the AC **“me abonaría a mi ahora 25.000 euros y a la procuradora 15.000 euros”**, with the balance to remain recognised in the insolvency proceeding pending payment;
- he asked Daniel Jiménez for approval before formally communicating the fees to the AC.

Daniel Jiménez then proposed to the clients a €95,000 formulation: €80,000 legal fees plus €15,000 procuradora, with €25,000 to Parrilla, €15,000 to the procuradora, and €55,000 to be reimbursed to the clients if/when collected. He later stated that the agreement should be conditional on Parrilla and the procuradora continuing to work in the concurso until completion, otherwise it should be renegotiated.

Patricia's contemporaneous response requested copies of the communications rather than leaving the arrangement verbal between Parrilla and the AC, and expressly raised concern about a **potential resignation immediately afterwards**.

## Evidential boundary

This thread strongly documents a **proposed/communicated recognition and immediate-payment arrangement** and the client's contemporaneous concern about a possible subsequent resignation.

It does **not, by itself, prove that the €25,000 was actually transferred**, from which account it would have been paid, the authority and accounting basis for any payment, or that any payment caused a litigation act/omission or the end of representation.

Therefore the current proposition is:

`AC_FEE_RECOGNITION_AND_PROPOSED_25K_PAYMENT_DOCUMENTED__EXECUTION_CAUSATION_NOT_YET_PROVED`

Do not convert the words “me abonaría” into “paid” without bank, ledger, invoice, receipt, AC report or equivalent execution evidence.
