# Por Derecho — “El Segundo Par de Ojos” implementation record

**Date:** 19 August 2026  
**Status:** generic public prototype implemented on a dedicated branch before any application to the live legal matter.

## Purpose

This implementation deepens the Por Derecho public-interest initiative without increasing the visual or narrative dominance of Por Derecho on the live Project Sun Rock case pages.

The architecture follows the rule:

> **Por Derecho becomes deeper, not louder.**

The live legal and evidential matter remains separate. No “Second Pair of Eyes” checkpoints have been added to Sun Park or other live case pages in this implementation.

## Locked methodology

The core sequence is:

1. Source
2. Authority
3. Perimeter
4. Contradiction
5. Consequence
6. Reversibility

The prototype uses controlled, non-adjudicative alert language. It does not label a person or decision “illegal”, “criminal” or “wrong”.

Human response options are:

- Verify further
- Narrow the action
- Proceed with reasons
- Escalate through the competent route

A source-backed warning can be overridden, but the prototype preserves the warning and the human reason.

## Two-icon grammar

- `second-pair-eyes.svg`: master methodology mark — two eyes plus a small balance.
- `record-review.svg`: micro-marker — document plus eye/check; used beside a legally significant documentary reconciliation point.

The micro-marker means “second documentary check required”. It does **not** mean that an infringement exists.

## Generic-first safeguard

The first demonstrator uses a synthetic mixed-ownership insolvency property scenario. It includes both:

- a material inconsistency requiring further review; and
- a resolved-alert example in which additional evidence removes the concern.

This is designed to demonstrate that the method can clear a concern rather than act as an accusation engine.

## Institutional accuracy

“Supervision of supervision” is presented through separate lanes:

- insolvency/judicial route;
- prosecutorial route, where legally applicable; and
- professional/institutional route.

The pages expressly state that:

- the prosecution service is not an appellate court;
- professional oversight does not replace judicial remedies;
- an alert is not a breach;
- silence or an absent visible response does not itself prove misconduct; and
- the tool does not create a new “super-authority”.

## Formation and partnership boundary

The pages consistently state that Por Derecho is an initiative in formation:

- it is not yet a registered foundation;
- it does not provide legal advice;
- it does not receive complaints or evidence from the public;
- it does not claim university accreditation, endorsement or partnership;
- it is not a deployed judicial decision system; and
- responsibility for decisions remains human.

## Future gate

No application to the live matter should occur until the generic prototype has been reviewed against at least these tests:

1. A judge, prosecutor or insolvency practitioner can read it without perceiving automated accusation.
2. The method can conclude that an initial concern is not supported.
3. AI visibly remains subordinate to human judgment.
4. A neutral visitor understands the method quickly.
5. Any later live-case checkpoint uses the same pre-existing rules and links back to this neutral methodology.
