**Date:** 19 August 2026  
**Scope:** repository-wide and website-wide conceptual review of the Administrador Concursal (“AC”) loyalty/conflict/independence question, the pre-CAM Monte Lanza/Molina/Community architecture, possible adoption by the Acosta Matos perimeter, and the act-specific consequences for LPB and other right-holders.  
**Branch:** `chatgpt/ac-constructive-agency-sitewide-review-20260819`

## 1. Review question

The review was run against this controlling proposition:

> **At what point, if any, did powers entrusted to the insolvency administration to administer and protect LPB and the estate begin functioning in practice as a mechanism that advanced, protected or implemented the objectives of an adverse private perimeter?**

The review deliberately does not begin with CAM. It asks whether there is a continuity:

`2008 sale/operation → fracture → Monte Lanza/Molina/Community predecessor perimeter → AC knowledge/acts/omissions → CAM entry/adoption → credit/control → June 2018 → liquidation/adjudication → HNT/MYND → RICPE/RIC/public support`

This is a forensic dependency model, not a finding of agency, collusion or criminal agreement.

## 3. Repository architecture review

The prior canonical model was L0–L5 and focused primarily on a loyalty breakpoint.

This review upgrades it to four independent thresholds:

1. `LOYALTY_BREAKPOINT`
2. `CONFLICT_BREAKPOINT`
3. `INDEPENDENCE_BREAKPOINT`
4. `CONSTRUCTIVE_AGENCY_THRESHOLD`

It also upgrades the scale to L0–L6:

- L0 neutral/loyal adequately explained;
- L1 isolated adverse effect;
- L2 repeated same-beneficiary/adverse-perimeter effect;
- L3 conflict/independence warnings;
- L4 strong functional alignment;
- L5 functional/de facto adverse-agency hypothesis — knowing implementation of adverse objectives using administrator powers;
- L6 direct concerted-action evidence.

L5 is deliberately separated from L6 so “functional agency” does not silently become “criminal collusion.”

## 4. New power-source control

Canonical question:

> **Was a power entrusted to the AC for LPB/the estate used to produce an outcome an adverse private actor could not itself lawfully produce?**

Mandatory companion questions:

- what was the source/purpose of the power?
- whose problem did it solve?
- whose legal theory/information did it adopt?
- who requested or anticipated the act?
- what independent verification occurred?
- who gained immediately/downstream?
- what did LPB/the estate receive?
- what neutral alternative existed?
- was conflict disclosed / abstention or enhanced supervision considered?

## 5. Legal-source review

Official BOE sources confirm the legal-duty anchor used for publication:

- historic Ley 22/2003 art.35: diligence of an orderly administrator and loyal representative; judicial supervision;
- current TRLC art.80: diligence in the interest of the insolvency and impartiality/independence.

The review preserves temporal discipline: historic acts are assessed under applicable historic law; current provisions are used for institutional continuity/current context, not retroactive merits.

## 6. Constructive / functional agency terminology decision

Internal label:

`CONSTRUCTIVE_FUNCTIONAL_ADVERSE_AGENCY_HYPOTHESIS`

Public ES:

`hipótesis de agencia funcional / de facto a favor del perímetro adverso`

Public EN:

`functional / de facto adverse-agency hypothesis`

Reason:

- captures the user's reverse-engineering theory;
- forces analysis of practical function rather than formal labels;
- avoids falsely asserting a standalone named Spanish insolvency-law doctrine;
- keeps formal agency, conflict, invalidity, liability and criminal agreement as separate legal questions.

## 7. Website-wide implementation strategy

A global dynamic module remains the safest and most maintainable mechanism for the cross-cutting question because:

- it guarantees ES/EN consistency;
- it can tailor the question to the route type;
- it avoids hand-copying a high-risk allegation into dozens of static pages where wording can drift;
- dedicated pages retain the complete evidential/legal qualification;
- future corrections can be propagated in one controlled source.

The module is loaded through the already site-wide CAM-pattern loader and now applies route-specific treatments across Community, AC, CAM, retracto, takeover, judge/institutional, recovery, downstream finance and insolvency pages; non-core substantive routes receive a compact global question. Search/privacy/update/book utility routes remain excluded.

## 9. Strongest current public proposition

> **There is a unitary forensic question whether powers entrusted to the insolvency administration for LPB ceased, at some point, to serve the debtor/estate independently and began functioning as a mechanism implementing objectives of a pre-existing adverse private perimeter and its possible economic successor. The record must identify any loyalty, conflict and independence breakpoint and determine its consequences act by act.**

This is stronger than “the AC made bad decisions” but narrower and safer than “the AC was CAM's agent.”

## 10. Prohibited regressions

Do not publish as established fact without new evidence:

- that constructive/functional agency is a standalone Spanish-law doctrine;
- that the AC was a formal or secret agent/lawyer for Molina-family interests or CAM;
- that CAM was legally the successor of the predecessor perimeter merely because of economic continuity;
- that AC/CAM collusion is proved;
- that all acts after a breakpoint are automatically null;
- that the AC personally instructed/drafted/filed the DP1041 withdrawal;
- that the AC personally extinguished Article 1535.
