## 1. Controlling legal question

The two procedural families are legally distinct but factually capable of interacting.

- **Track A:** private-owner possession/exploitation litigation concerning 18 units, ending in AP Las Palmas Judgment 89/2014.
- **Track B:** later insolvency-administration litigation concerning the LPB–Monterecco/Pink agreement, rent/termination, possession/keys, enforcement and calificación.

The correct criminal-law question is not whether the outcomes point in the same practical direction. It is whether a **specific false or deceptively incomplete proposition, document, evidential characterisation or procedural state** was knowingly generated in one route, transmitted or reused in another, relied upon by the relevant decision-maker, and caused a legally relevant patrimonial or control effect.

Use this proof chain:

`ORIGIN → EXACT PROPOSITION/DOCUMENT → ACTOR/CAPACITY → KNOWLEDGE ROUTE → ADOPTION/USE → RELIANCE → DISPOSITION/EFFECT → BENEFIT/HARM → CONTRARY EVIDENCE → LAWFUL ALTERNATIVE`.

No criminal conclusion may be drawn unless the applicable offence elements are separately satisfied.

## 2. Non-criminal duty baseline

### Administration concursal

TRLC Article 80 requires diligence, efficiency in the interest of the concurso, and impartiality/independence. Articles 94–99 govern civil liability for unlawful acts/omissions or breaches of duty causing loss. Article 100 treats serious breach of diligence and breach of impartiality/independence as grounds for separation.

These duties are a baseline for the lawful counterfactual. Civil breach, negligence, poor judgment or an adverse litigation position is not automatically criminal.

Official source: https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2020-4859

### Procedural good faith

LEC Article 247 and LOPJ Article 11.2 require procedural good faith and permit rejection of manifest abuse or procedural fraud.

Official sources:
- https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2000-323
- https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1985-12666

### Ministerio Fiscal

Constitution Article 124 and the EOMF place the Ministerio Fiscal under legality and impartiality/objectivity duties. A fiscal position is therefore analysed independently: source received, proposition adopted, contrary material, legal reasoning and later correction or review.

Official sources:
- https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1978-31229
- https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1982-837

## 3. Criminal-law element matrix

### 3.1 Estafa procesal — CP Article 250.1.7

Potential relevance arises only where there is:
1. manipulation of evidence or analogous procedural deception;
2. sufficient causal deception in the proceeding;
3. the required knowledge/intent and fraud purpose;
4. error in the judge or tribunal;
5. a resolution caused by the error;
6. economic prejudice;
7. actor-specific attribution.

An inaccurate allegation, disputed interpretation or adverse legal argument is not by itself procedural fraud.

**Potential evidence targets:** title/authority documents, key/possession evidence, debt/certification inputs, monetary worksheets, proof of concealment or manipulation, and the precise judicial reliance.

**Current state:** serious investigative hypothesis only where the element chain can be closed.

Official Penal Code: https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1995-25444

### 3.2 Documentary falsity / knowing use — CP Articles 390, 392, 393, 395, 396

The analysis must distinguish:
- false factual assertion;
- altered essential document;
- simulated document;
- false attribution of participation or declarations;
- knowing presentation/use of a false document.

For private actors, Article 392 refers to the first three forms in Article 390.1; mere untruthful narration is not automatically Article 392 falsity. Article 395 applies the same first-three-form structure to private documents with purpose to prejudice.

**Priority evidence targets:** 2011 debt/vote schedules, Community certificates/proxies, May-2012 key/notarial evidence, 2017 possession/key instruments, 2018 access instructions, rent/execution calculations, and deed/Registry authority recitals.

**Current state:** document-by-document only.

### 3.3 Administración desleal — current CP Article 252; temporal-law control required

Potential relevance exists where a person empowered to administer another's patrimony exceeds those powers and causes patrimonial loss.

Mandatory controls:
- exact act date;
- statutory version then in force;
- exact patrimony affected;
- source of management powers;
- excess of powers;
- causation and damage;
- required mental element.

The hotel as an economic unit must not be confused with the legal ownership of each asset.

### 3.4 Insolvencias punibles — CP Article 259 et seq.

Potential relevance can arise from concealment/damage/destruction of estate assets, unjustified dispositions or debts, below-cost transactions lacking economic justification, or simulation/recognition of fictitious credits.

The decisive boundary is the estate: LPB's insolvency estate was not automatically every asset, unit or right connected to Sun Park.

**Current state:** open; asset + act + insolvency + intent must be mapped.

### 3.5 Participation and omission

Responsibility does not transfer through professional, institutional, creditor, family or corporate proximity.

For every alleged participant test:
- authorship/co-authorship;
- inducement or necessary cooperation;
- complicity;
- for omission, a specific legal duty, practical capacity, knowledge and causal equivalence.

Never infer one actor's knowledge from another actor's knowledge without a proved communication or source route.

### 3.6 Judicial prevarication — CP Articles 446–449

This can be assessed only resolution by resolution.

Required table:
`RESOLUTION → LEGAL/FACTUAL PREMISE → CONTRARY CONTROLLING MATERIAL → PROVED JUDICIAL KNOWLEDGE → OBJECTIVE INJUSTICE → SUBJECTIVE ELEMENT`.

An adverse ruling, later reversal, factual error or acceptance of a party's argument is not itself prevarication.

### 3.7 Organización / grupo criminal — CP Articles 570 bis/ter

This is a second-order hypothesis only after underlying crimes and the required structure/concert/criminal purpose are evidenced. Recurrent relationships and aligned economic interests do not suffice.

**Current state:** not established; never use as a shortcut.
