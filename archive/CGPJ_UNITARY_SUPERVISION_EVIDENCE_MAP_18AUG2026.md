# CGPJ Unitario — Evidence / Publication Map

Date: 18 August 2026
Scope: DI 169/2026 · Alzada 286/2026 · Concurso 36/2012 · Commission/Inspection visitor journey

## Controlling public thesis

The visitor journey must not be organised as a list of adverse rulings. It is organised as:

`known risk → direct judicial knowledge OR Administrator knowledge/reporting duty → available supervisory power → identifiable response → patrimonial consequence → reversibility → later corrective opportunity`

The public question is whether judicial supervision of the Insolvency Administration and protection of the insolvency estate can be reconstructed without gaps before each difficult-to-reverse step. This is not a request for the CGPJ to recalculate debt, reverse adjudication, declare crime, or substitute jurisdictional appeals.

## Three knowledge levels

1. **Direct judicial knowledge** — filed submissions, hearings, rulings reproducing allegations, reports and communications incorporated into the case.
2. **Administrator knowledge / reporting duty** — facts known or requiring verification by the Insolvency Administration. Do not attribute automatically to the judge; identify reporting/incorporation or later direct notice to the Court.
3. **External/later context** — RICPE/CNMV, tax, funds, later operation, public-authority tracks. Use for continuity/consequence, not historical judicial knowledge unless incorporation is proved.

## Cross-cutting tests

### Insolvency estate / extra-insolvency perimeter
LPB was the insolvent debtor; Sun Park as a whole was not. Test both:
- whether LPB property/rights were sufficiently protected;
- whether insolvency-derived authority produced material effects beyond LPB over Matkator, third parties, operating rights or other independent titles.

### Irreversibility
For each nucleus record:
- risk;
- first verifiable knowledge;
- available control;
- actual response;
- next patrimonial step;
- point at which correction became materially/registrally/economically harder;
- later opportunity to correct.

### Community → Administrator → Court information quality
Where contested Community acts, accounts, debts, certificates or authority narratives entered the insolvency, identify the Administrator's verification and reporting and the judicial response. Do not assume invalidity merely because the underlying private governance was disputed.

## LPAM / appearance-of-impartiality module
Keep separate from the seven supervisory nuclei.
Do not state intimate friendship, influence, partiality, corruption or responsibility as established.
Ask only whether the individualised allegations and finite official sources warrant checking or a specific reason why checking is unnecessary.

## Adverse record first
Always show before Por Derecho's objections:
- DI 169/2026 archived 14 May 2026;
- Promotor maintained archive 10 July 2026;
- appeal/subsequent filing does not equal reversal or acceptance;
- registry routing does not equal substantive examination.

## Publication boundaries

Public:
- dates, case numbers, signed/public rulings already used on site;
- source provenance and verification propositions;
- alternative lawful explanations;
- missing-document questions;
- institutional competence map.

Source-gated/private unless separately cleared:
- full legal-adviser email bodies;
- private phone numbers and unnecessary personal data;
- professional-secret or litigation-strategy content;
- unredacted personal witness information;
- raw material whose authenticity/provenance is not yet controlled.

## Institutional output model
A reasoned CGPJ response may lawfully disagree with every Por Derecho inference. The site should make it possible to distinguish:
- purely jurisdictional merits;
- disciplinary issues within the Promotor/Commission route under LOPJ art. 608;
- court-functioning matters within Inspection competence;
- LAJ/court-office matters outside judge-discipline allocation;
- criminal relevance reserved to competent prosecution/court routes.

The website should never imply that referral, inspection, reopening, or verification itself proves misconduct.
