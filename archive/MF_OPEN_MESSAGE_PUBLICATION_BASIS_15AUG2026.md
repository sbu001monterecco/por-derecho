# Ministerio Fiscal Open Message — Publication Basis

Updated: 15 August 2026

## Purpose

This note records the evidential basis and publication discipline for the public ES/EN open-message pages. It is not itself a criminal allegation. Future ChatGPT maintainers must update this note when primary evidence materially changes the public thesis.

## Controlling public thesis

The public message should not principally argue that Fiscalía was never informed. The stronger and better-supported question is what the Ministerio Fiscal did **after repeated notice**, whether later evidence was assessed as later evidence, and whether fragmented territorial/superior/central/specialist routes ever became a cumulative institutional reassessment.

The core public question is:

> When the direction of the allegations reversed, did the direction of prosecutorial scrutiny reverse too?

The second is:

> Was evidence arising after an archive examined as genuinely new evidence when it was materially new, or repeatedly routed through inherited decisional frames?

## 2013–2014 primary-origin sequence

### December 2013 — DI 273/2013 / Fiscalía querella

Primary scanned querella now available in the evidential corpus. It shows the Ministerio Fiscal itself filing a criminal querella against Gil Marer and Patricia Domínguez, signed by Tomás Fernández de Páiz on 27 December 2013. The prosecutorial theory concerned the LPB/operator economic relationship and possible patrimonial prejudice, including corporate-offence and asset-concealment characterisations then relied upon.

Public-safe significance: by December 2013 Fiscalía had adopted an affirmative criminal frame in which Gil/Patricia were suspected wrongdoers. This establishes an early institutional frame; it does not establish later bias.

### March 2014 — counter-notice to Fiscalía de Arrecife

Two extensive complaints by Gil Marer / Patricia Domínguez were addressed to the Arrecife economic-crime prosecution office. They presented a materially different causal model involving Community governance, majority-owner voting exclusion, disputed Community debt attributed to LPB, hotel-wide operating costs, unidad de explotación, tourism structure, access/keys and alleged minority-owner interference.

These documents are important primarily as **notice evidence** and as maps to underlying primary annexes. Their highly accusatory wording is historical allegation, not proof of the allegations.

### May–June 2014 — Fiscalía 39/2014 archive

A scanned Fiscalía document dated 8 May 2014 records that the complaint was studied and then archived on the basis that the matters were in part excessively inconcrete/general and in part matters for the civil jurisdiction, including Community-agreement validity/nullity issues. The document is signed by María Inmaculada Martín-Comas Fernández. A later oficio of **23 June 2014** notified the archive and the possibility of reiterating the claims before the competent investigating court.

Public-safe significance: within months of the 2013 querella, Fiscalía received and archived a substantial counter-narrative from the persons it had prosecuted. This makes comparative investigative treatment a legitimate research question. It does not itself prove discriminatory treatment.

## Key comparative insight

The 2013 prosecution theory and 2014 counter-narrative can be presented as competing explanations of LPB/Sun Park economic deterioration:

- **2013 prosecution theory:** whether value was improperly displaced from LPB through the operator/related-party structure, rent/consideration and related transactions, prejudicing LPB/creditors.
- **2014 counter-theory:** whether Community/minority-owner governance, voting exclusion, disputed debt, operating-cost allocation and interference with unitary exploitation caused or materially aggravated the economic weakness.

The current corpus does not yet identify a single institutional document demonstrating that Fiscalía comparatively tested and reconciled both causal models.

## 2018 role reversal

By 2018, while the earlier criminal route remained active, Gil/Aweswell became complainants concerning the insolvency administrator, CAM and other actors. DI 248/2018 is the critical measurable comparison file.

Do not assert that requested diligences were not performed until the complete certified file establishes what was actually done. Public wording should say that requested-versus-performed investigative correspondence remains to be reconstructed.

## Publication discipline

The live page must distinguish:

- RECEIVED from EXAMINED;
- REFERRED from INVESTIGATED;
- ARCHIVED from VALIDATION OF ALL ANTECEDENT CONDUCT;
- RELATED FACTS from IDENTICAL FACTS;
- INSOLVENCY CONTEXT from AUTOMATIC INCLUSION OF ALL PROPERTY/RIGHTS;
- TEMPORAL SEQUENCE from CAUSATION;
- INSTITUTIONAL KNOWLEDGE from PERSONAL KNOWLEDGE OF EVERY NAMED PROSECUTOR.

## Capture / instrumentalisation

Do not publish capture, collusion, deliberate protection, corruption or personal affinity as established facts absent independent evidence.

A publication-safe analytical formulation is that early credibility allocation, inherited frames, territorial return and fragmentation may create **functional instrumentalisation** even without a conscious agreement or improper purpose. This must remain an analytical question unless stronger evidence emerges.

## Live-page design objective

The public page is an **interactive institutional audit with a formal open letter attached**, not a long accusation with evidence appended. Attention flow:

question → 60-second chronology → role reversal → competing causal models → knowledge/response/consequence → routing → concessions → ten answerable questions → public thesis → formal letter → Justice Map.
