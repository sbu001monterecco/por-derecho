## 1. Controlling allegation

Project Sun Rock makes the following **strong, substantiated allegation**:

> The same physical and economic hotel — Hotel Sun Park, later presented and operated as MYND Yaiza — and materially overlapping assets, works, project expenditure, value, employment outputs and operating capacity acquired multiple simultaneous or successive financial and institutional lives while a substantial part of the relevant property perimeter remained inside Concurso 36/2012 and other integrated property remained outside that concurso. The documentary record justifies investigation of double or triple funding, repeated qualification of the same underlying project, incomplete cross-disclosure and serial institutional reliance.

This is not limited to the unremarkable proposition that a development may lawfully combine different sources of finance. The allegation is that the **same qualifying base** may have been reused or re-described across different gates:

- the same hotel or materially overlapping property perimeter;
- the same refurbishment or substantially overlapping works;
- the same invoices, eligible costs or project expenditure;
- the same employment creation or operating headcount;
- the same valuation uplift, availability, collateral or development value;
- the same physical operating unit despite legally fragmented title;
- the same economic output while different institutions received incomplete or differently framed accounts of ownership, court protection, earlier finance and other support.

The allegation must be tested **asset by asset, work by work, invoice by invoice, job by job, funding instrument by funding instrument and euro by euro**.

### Publication status

Public wording may say that Project Sun Rock **alleges** double/triple funding and multiple financial lives on the basis of the assembled documentary record. It may identify the concrete anchors and explain why the allegation is substantiated. It must not say that a court, regulator or audit authority has already found subsidy fraud, double funding, criminal intent, false certification or unlawful receipt unless a competent primary decision expressly establishes that proposition.

## 2. The unitary object of analysis

Sun Park must be analysed on two planes at once.

### Legal fragmentation

The controlled July 2021 RICPE certification described a 262-property perimeter:

- **54 CAM**;
- **190 LPB**;
- **18 third-party**.

It also recorded future/conditional CAM ownership, an unsigned LOI and that full due diligence had not begun. The 190 LPB properties represent approximately 72.5% of the 262-property count. Counting properties is not automatically the same as calculating economic ownership, voting rights, built area or value, but it demonstrates that the predominant identified property count was not then CAM property.

LPB alone was the debtor in Concurso 36/2012. Matkator and other third-party property did not become part of LPB’s insolvency estate merely because it was physically or operationally integrated into Sun Park.

### Economic and operational unity

The hotel was nevertheless presented, refurbished, financed, marketed and later operated as an integrated hotel product. Unity of operation does not erase separate title. Separate title does not erase the economic reality that a financing, works package, valuation, employment promise or operating projection may concern the hotel as a whole.

The correct audit question is therefore not simply “which company received each sum?” It is:

> What precise assets, works, costs, rights, jobs, revenues and value supported each financing or public-support decision, and how did each decision exclude or account for the parts belonging to LPB, Matkator or other owners?

## 6. Missing RICPE project-admission and control chain

The current record is incomplete unless it reconstructs the normal RICPE process for admitting, approving and funding a new project and compares that process stage by stage with Sun Park.

### Mandatory chain

`originator → sponsor → preliminary screening → promoter eligibility → title/availability → valuation → legal DD → technical DD → financial DD → related-party/conflict screening → investment committee → board approval → conditions precedent → waiver/exception → drawdown → monitoring → remediation/correction`.

For every stage identify:

1. the rule, policy or prerequisite;
2. the document CAM/HNT supplied;
3. the date compliance was required;
4. the responsible checker;
5. the information available to that checker;
6. any exception, waiver, deferral or conditional approval;
7. the person/body authorised to grant it;
8. whether the exception was recorded and disclosed;
9. how the project crossed into the next stage;
10. what happened when the July 2021 certification documented fragmented title and incomplete maturity.

### Three permitted classifications

Every departure must be classified as one of:

- **documented authorised exception** — a competent person/body knowingly waived or deferred a requirement;
- **control failure** — the requirement was unmet and no valid waiver is identified, but the project progressed;
- **unresolved evidential gap** — the current record cannot yet determine compliance or waiver.

“Blind eye” is not an evidential category. Deliberate tolerance or conscious disregard requires proof of knowledge, duty, ability to act and intentional inaction. The investigation should identify those elements rather than use rhetoric as a substitute.

### Specific prerequisites requiring proof

- promoter identity, experience and financial capacity;
- ownership or legally sufficient control of the proposed assets;
- treatment of LPB’s court-protected properties;
- treatment of Matkator/third-party properties;
- signed LOI or other acquisition basis;
- complete legal, technical and financial DD;
- valuation adequate for the actual financing structure;
- planning, works and operating permissions;
- authority of the Comunidad and owners;
- related-party/conflict declarations;
- abstentions and independent review;
- investment-committee and board minutes;
- conditions precedent to commitment and drawdown;
- investor/CNMV correction when material assumptions changed;
- monitoring after award/title/public support.

The core governance allegation is not merely that CAM may have failed a prerequisite. It is that the project appears to have obtained multiple institutional lives despite documented deficiencies, requiring identification of the persons and bodies that allowed each progression.

## 7. Double/triple-funding tests

The audit must distinguish lawful blended finance from prohibited or undisclosed overlap. The following tests are mandatory.

### Test 1 — same invoice

Was the same invoice, contractor certificate, payroll cost or purchase document charged, reimbursed, capitalised or certified under more than one instrument?

### Test 2 — same work package

Were materially identical works — rooms, pools, reception, restaurants, common areas, plant, energy, accessibility, digital systems or other refurbishment — used to justify the Comunidad amount, RICPE financing, regional incentive and FEDER support?

### Test 3 — same eligible cost

Did more than one application treat the same gross project cost as available without netting other aid, reimbursements, grants, contributions or finance where required?

### Test 4 — same employment output

Were the same employees, contracts, FTEs or payroll periods counted toward both the 60-job commitment and the alleged 86-job RICPE basis? Were jobs gross or net, new or maintained, group-wide or hotel-specific?

### Test 5 — same property/value/collateral

Was the same property, including LPB or extraconcursal property, presented as available, owned, collateralised or value-supporting in more than one transaction without consistent disclosure of title and earlier commitments?

### Test 6 — same equity contribution

Was money originating in one subsidised/financed layer represented elsewhere as sponsor equity or unencumbered contribution? This requires bank-source tracing; chronology or common parties alone does not prove recycling.

### Test 7 — same tax/investment qualification

Were the same investment expenditure and project facts used for RIC suitability, private investor communications, regional incentives and FEDER certification? If so, what anti-cumulation rules and declarations were applied?

### Test 8 — same projected uplift

Was the same post-refurbishment value uplift monetised repeatedly through financing, valuation, subsidy or later platform/track-record representations?

### Test 9 — non-disclosure across gates

Did each institution receive a complete schedule of:

- all prior and concurrent finance;
- all grants and public support;
- all owner/Community contributions;
- title fragmentation and litigation;
- the concurso status;
- related parties;
- previous project withdrawal and incomplete DD;
- conditions, security and repayment obligations?

### Test 10 — institution-specific knowledge and action

For every RICPE director, committee member, adviser, AC, court actor, public authority, fund controller and beneficiary representative, prove separately:

`duty/power → information received → knowledge → decision/omission → consequence`.

Do not infer shared intent merely because documents moved through a chain.

## 8. Court-protection and extraconcursal questions

### Concurso plane

The audit must establish what the AC and Mercantile Court knew, when they knew it and what authority they exercised concerning:

- the November 2018 Comunidad refurbishment/finance allegation;
- 2020 RICPE investor presentation;
- the July 2021 RICPE certification and CAM filing;
- project reactivation;
- title/adjudication/deed sequence;
- private/RIC finance;
- regional incentive and FEDER-linked project;
- current exploitation and value.

The fact that a court act later enabled or recorded title does not retroactively answer earlier project, possession, works, finance or disclosure questions.

### Extraconcursal plane

Matkator and third-party property must have a separate ledger identifying:

- title and Registry status;
- physical use and access;
- works performed;
- financing/valuation treatment;
- rooms/services/revenue generated;
- Community treatment;
- consent, compensation or exclusion from eligible-cost/property schedules.

The same hotel can therefore contain both court-protected LPB assets and extraconcursal assets. A unitary economic project cannot use that unity to erase legal boundaries, while later actors cannot use legal fragmentation to deny that the same hotel/project underpinned multiple financial applications.
