## 1. Controlling conclusion

> **The connection is not zero, and it is not the same money.** Canary public debt apt for RIC and RICPE are parallel methods for materialising the same statutory RIC incentive. The direct Acosta Matos destination-side connection is documented through RICPE Series F/G and Hotel New Trend / MYND Yaiza. No allocation or source-of-funds bridge from the 2024 or 2025 public bonds to HNT or another Acosta Matos entity has been established.

This conclusion replaces either of the following fragmented formulations:

- `there is no connection` — too broad because both routes operate within the same RIC policy architecture; and
- `the Government raised the bond money for RICPE / Acosta Matos` — unsupported because issuer, recipient, instrument, chronology and use of proceeds differ.

## 2. Why the Government raises the money

The public issue performs two simultaneous functions:

1. it gives natural and legal persons, including qualifying RIC taxpayers, a public-debt instrument capable of materialising RIC under the applicable conditions; and
2. it gives the Canary Autonomous Community debt finance for listed public investment in infrastructure, equipment, environmental improvement and protection and, for the 2025 issue, certain protected-housing rehabilitation for rental.

The Government is not raising money to fund RICPE. It is borrowing for public investment through a security that also qualifies as a RIC materialisation asset. The investor receives a debt claim; the Government receives financing; the RIC tax policy links the private tax benefit to territorial investment.

Any person could subscribe under the public Orders. RIC eligibility of the bond does not establish that every subscriber used the bond to materialise a RIC allocation.

## 7. 2025 minimum-allocation anomaly

The published 2025 Order states an initial minimum allocation of EUR 80,000. A separate operational draw/proration record refers to a EUR 100,000 minimum. Possible explanations include a drafting carry-over, a later operational rule or an error in the record. No explanation is selected without the version/authority/communication and actual allocation ledger.

This discrepancy does not itself prove unequal treatment or an improper allocation.

## 8. Public-debt versus ordinary-debt separation

The `OPERACIONES` files include ordinary long-term loans and private placements used for distinct debt-management purposes, including maturities and reimbursements. Those operations are not silently recategorised as RIC-eligible public issues.

The canonical website must maintain:

- public RIC bond issues;
- ordinary debt operations;
- private placements;
- RICPE private materialisation;
- regional incentives; and
- any FEDER/ERDF component

as legally separate lanes connected only where source-specific evidence establishes a bridge.
