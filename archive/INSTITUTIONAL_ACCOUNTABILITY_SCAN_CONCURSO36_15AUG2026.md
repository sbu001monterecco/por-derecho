## High-value verified primary propositions

### 26 June 2018 order

The Mercantile Court No. 1 order, signed by Judge Alberto López Villarrubia, records the CAM/dación/Aweswell dispute, decides to defer the dación and suspends asset-realisation operations “in the form proposed by the insolvency administrator” until September. It also orders testimonies requested by prosecutors.

This proves judicial knowledge of that dispute and a suspension decision. It does **not**, standing alone, establish the full material scope of any prior access/control action. The underlying AC proposal referenced by the order remains a priority source because it defines what “in the form proposed” meant.

### 24 October 2019 order

The scanned primary order records notification of an `ob rem` unlinking/sale carried out by the insolvency administrator and concludes that the transfer/sale occurred without the necessary prior judicial authorisation. The court therefore states that it is not appropriate to authorise and/or validate the unlinking and sale after the fact.

This is a strong institutional-control fact. It does **not**, by itself, prove criminal conduct, universal nullity of all later acts, or the legal consequence for every downstream asset. Those consequences require separate proceeding/title analysis.

## Public-message discipline

The correct public object for the judiciary is the **court/institution/proceeding**, not a personalised visual target aimed at an individual judge. A judge's name may be used only to attribute a signed public order where the primary source supports it.

For the AC, the current evidence supports a dedicated evidence-first subhub. A later formal open message may be justified after completion of the act-by-act matrix, but the site should first make the underlying duty/knowledge/act comparison independently legible.

For AP Section 4, the evidence supports a dedicated hub now. It does **not** support a personal open message to the chamber members. The useful public question is completeness and corrective capacity: what is actually on appeal, what record is before the chamber, and what later evidence can procedurally be considered.

For the LAJ, do not publish personal allegations. First complete a matrix of `presented → incorporated → notified → certified/testimoniado → transmitted → received` and identify any concrete discrepancy from primary procedural records.

## Strongest reasonable institutional defences

### Insolvency administrator

The AC had statutory functions, operated within a court-supervised procedure and could reasonably rely on the liquidation plan, court decisions and information then available. Error, a disputed exercise of power, or an act later found to lack prior authorisation does not establish corrupt intent or collusion.

### Mercantile Court

The court decides questions within the insolvency proceeding and is not required to resolve every collateral criminal, civil, corporate or third-party issue in each order. Public analysis must therefore distinguish `known` from `decided` and `outside scope` from `ignored`.

### AP Section 4

An appellate chamber is not a de novo investigation. Review is bounded by the appealed orders, grounds, transmitted record and procedural rules for later evidence. The site should ask whether the record is complete for the issue actually under appeal, not demand that the chamber investigate the entire Sun Park history.
