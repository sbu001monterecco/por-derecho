## Source hierarchy and non-overwrite rule

1. Registry history and public conveyance deed.
2. Judgment/court-file primary material.
3. Primary private contract or instrument.
4. Contemporaneous owner schedule/accounting record.
5. Later derivative schedule.
6. Attributed statement or reconstruction position.

A later or higher-ranked source may explain an earlier conflict, but the earlier record remains preserved with its date and source. Conflicting unit/owner maps remain parallel until the transfer, numbering or source error is actually resolved.

## Instrument boundaries

- An **option to purchase** is not a completed sale, conveyance or registered transfer.
- A **touristic-exploitation cession** may identify an owner and create representation rights, but it does not by itself establish who sold the unit.
- A source column reading **DIRECT** is reproduced as a source field only; it is not treated as proof that LPB was seller.
- A representative, lawyer or Community officeholder is not an owner by reason of that role.
- An owner is not adverse/dissident merely because the owner is not LPB or Matkator.
