# AC professional isolation and attrition — canonical evidential record

**Canonical ID:** PD-AC-PIA-20260922-01  
**Adopted:** 22 September 2026  
**Scope:** Concurso 36/2012 · Administrador Concursal · former lawyers · procedural representatives · professional advisers · commercial/operating partners · financing/investment counterparties  
**Public status:** analytical framework and attributed allegation; not an adjudicated finding of wrongdoing.

## Canonical formulation

Gil Marer alleges a sustained course of conduct involving **institutional pressure, interference with independent professional relationships, economic and informational leverage, strategic isolation, and professional/commercial attrition** around persons and organisations advising, representing, financing, operating with, or otherwise supporting Gil Marer, Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. (LPB), AWESWELL LIMITED and the wider Sun Park perimeter.

The allegation is not based on personality, tone or ordinary professional disagreement. The testable question is whether different mechanisms repeatedly appeared at points where independent professionals or commercial counterparties were challenging, scrutinising or potentially limiting decisions or positions of the Administrador Concursal (AC).

This formulation replaces non-institutional shorthand such as “thug”, “bully” or “mob-like” in repository and public drafting. Source quotations or an expressly attributed speaker may retain such wording only when evidentially necessary.

## Canonical mechanism taxonomy

1. **Direct mandate displacement** — termination, venia, abstention demands, filing restrictions or other acts affecting an existing professional mandate.
2. **Professional or regulatory escalation** — Bar, disciplinary, judicial, criminal, insurance or reputational exposure connected with the professional’s work.
3. **Economic leverage** — control, recognition, timing or proposed payment of professional fees or other estate-funded economics capable of affecting incentives.
4. **Intermediation and access** — obtaining access to incoming advisers through outgoing advisers or other intermediaries, particularly where the new mandate concerns scrutiny of the AC or connected actors.
5. **Information and control asymmetry** — differential access to documents, estate information, operational access, funds or decision channels.
6. **Relationship disruption** — acts or circumstances capable of weakening trust, continuity, independence or willingness to continue acting.
7. **Alternative-pathway impairment** — acts or omissions alleged objectively to impair an independent legal, commercial, operating, financing, restructuring or exit route.
8. **Cumulative isolation / attrition** — the possible aggregate effect of otherwise distinct mechanisms over time.

No event is promoted from one mechanism to another without its own source and causal analysis.

## Four analytical rings

- **Ring 1 — Legal representation:** lawyers and law firms acting for Gil, LPB, AWESWELL LIMITED or related entities.
- **Ring 2 — Procedural and professional support:** procuradores, experts, accountants, valuers, investigators and other advisers.
- **Ring 3 — Commercial and operating partners:** operators, prospective operators, transaction advisers and collaborators.
- **Ring 4 — Capital and investment counterparties:** lenders, investors, financing intermediaries and restructuring/exit counterparties.

Presence in a ring does not imply victim status, adverse status, complicity or common purpose. Each actor remains individually attributable.

## Contemporaneous anchor events

### Cristo Suárez Pimentel — 25 January 2018
The controlled record documents an AC communication stating an intention to report the matter to the Bar, terminating Cristo’s LPB professional relationship, seeking venia, requiring abstention, warning of judicial action and instructing the procurador not to file further work without the AC’s express consent.

**What this establishes:** direct displacement of a defined mandate and use of institutional/professional escalation.  
**What remains open:** the legal scope and justification of the AC’s authority in liquidation, whether a Bar complaint was actually made, and later causal consequences.  
**Non-transfer rule:** Cristo continued work elsewhere in the wider Sun Park perimeter; termination of one LPB mandate is not total project withdrawal.

### Juan Tomás Parrilla Suárez — January 2019 and May–June 2020

The controlled chronology now separates **non-filing**, **professional rationale**, **fee administration** and **transition causation** instead of treating them as one inference.

- A 4 January 2019 draft sought judicial intervention, Fiscalía referral and reservation of AC separation/responsibility actions. A controlled Drive copy is explicitly labelled **NO PRESENTADA**, and a later project-side handover email also described it as not filed. Certified court/LexNET proof remains the decisive filing-status source.
- Parrilla's direct 9 January 2019 email is mandatory counterevidence: he said he wanted the deed before advancing a substantive nullity/fraud challenge, opposed a precipitous broader filing at that stage and proposed the narrower step of asking the court to order production of the deed. Rosa Gual agreed with that scope.
- The client-side substitution was communicated on 31 May 2020 as a **mutual loss of confidence** after an approximately eight-year relationship. Parrilla accepted the venia, briefed successor counsel and stated that his fee liquidation had to be communicated to the Administrador Concursal.
- On 1 June 2020 incoming counsel Daniel Jiménez contemporaneously reported that the AC said Parrilla's and the procuradora's invoices would be paid from concurso funds and praised Parrilla's work.
- On 18 June 2020 Jiménez separately recorded that his team had broken off negotiations with Parrilla and the AC because he perceived an attempt to condition the successor team's work on fee payment, and said he did not want his own fees to depend on the AC's view.

**What this establishes:** a source-linked economic/intermediation sequence, a real non-filing/protective-action question and a direct successor-counsel report of perceived conditioning during the representation transition.

A controlled AC liquidation-operations report itself expressly lists **“Juan Tomás Parrilla 26750 jul-20 Honorarios abogado concursada”**, with the same entry repeated in a later AC report. That proves the AC reported the amount/month/concept; it does not close the invoice, authority, estate-ledger or matching-bank-debit chain.

**What it does not establish:** bribery, corrupt inducement, criminal knowledge, collusion, judge participation, an unlawful payment, or that AC conduct was the sole cause of Parrilla's replacement. A properly authorised estate payment and Parrilla's stated professional judgment remain material lawful explanations. The invoice, tax, authority, estate-ledger, bank-debit and certified-docket records remain priority closure items.

### Joaquín Ruiz de Infante / Alas team — February–September 2020

### Daniel Jiménez / SLJ — incoming-counsel access
The new mandate contemplated scrutiny of the AC and possible AC/CAM action. The record documents a request for contact relayed through outgoing counsel and subsequent contact with the incoming lawyer, plus a later CAM-lawyer contact request channelled through the AC.

**What remains open:** whether any later CAM call occurred, what was communicated, and whether any contact caused the later resignation. Jiménez contradicted several AC positions and his resignation also cited strategy, client direction and unpaid fees.

### ONA / Clubotel and the funded-exit ecosystem — 2018–2019
The wider record supports testing the same mechanism outside lawyer turnover. Contemporary material records warnings that separating operational elements would damage both debt-payment/exit and future competitive sale routes. The later 24 October 2019 order is treated as independent structural corroboration that the sale should have been unitary and that excluding operating installations impaired realistic third-party improvements.

**Boundary:** later judicial structural corroboration does not itself prove the AC’s earlier intent, concert, criminal purpose or counterfactual certainty that a funded exit would have closed.
