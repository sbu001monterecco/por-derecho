## 4. Digitisation standard for every ACTA event

A future completion pass must preserve four linked layers:

`PRE-MEETING NOTICE / INVITATION / AGENDA`
→ `ATTENDANCE / PROXIES / OWNERSHIP-COEFFICIENT SNAPSHOT`
→ `SIGNED ACTA / RESOLUTIONS / VOTE`
→ `POST-MEETING IMPLEMENTATION / EMAILS / BUROFAX / LITIGATION / AC COMMUNICATION`

For every ACTA, create an ACTA X-RAY record containing:

1. exact filename and source-native ID;
2. date/body/type of meeting;
3. convenor and claimed authority;
4. notice method/date;
5. attendees and proxies;
6. unit/coefficient ownership represented;
7. excluded owners/votes and stated reason;
8. voting denominator and arithmetic;
9. each resolution separately;
10. signatories and certification chain;
11. referenced debt/accounting basis;
12. referenced exploitation/statutory agreement;
13. later persons/institutions relying on the ACTA;
14. legal/procedural challenge and outcome, if any;
15. evidence classification and limitations.
