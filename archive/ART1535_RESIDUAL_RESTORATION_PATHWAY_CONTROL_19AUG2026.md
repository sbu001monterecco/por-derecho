## 1. Strategic role

The Article 1535 workstream is not to be treated as a historical footnote merely because the credit was later consumed, transformed or followed by adjudication, dación, CAM→HNT succession, refurbishment, financing or hotel operation.

Its controlled strategic role is **upstream reserve pathway**:

> If the PH122→CAM credit position was a juridical/economic bridge into CAM's later creditor standing, liquidation leverage and eventual hotel-related position, the unresolved Article 1535 question attacks the bridge upstream rather than arguing only about downstream title.

The internal shorthand may be **the ace / joker / reserve card**. Publicly, use **reserve restoration pathway**, **upstream reserve right**, or **residual Article 1535 pathway**.

## 2. What the statute does — and does not do

Article 1535 CC provides that, where its technical conditions are met, the debtor may **extinguish** a sold litigious credit by reimbursing the assignee the price paid, qualifying costs and interest on the price. It is not technically a mechanism by which the debtor buys the credit and becomes the assignee.

The statutory nine-day period runs from the point at which the **assignee demands payment**. The present file must therefore not equate mere knowledge of the assignment or mere deed disclosure with the statutory trigger without source-specific legal analysis.

Supreme Court jurisprudence applies Article 1535 strictly. STS 728/2020 identifies temporal and material requirements and excludes certain block/succession transfers. STS 10-May-2021 treats the nine-day period as **caducidad**, not ordinarily interruptible by preservation activity that does not itself constitute effective exercise.

Accordingly, Por Derecho does **not** publish any of the following as already established:

- that Article 1535 certainly applied to the PH122→CAM assignment;
- that the right is unquestionably alive today;
- that a criminal case automatically suspends the nine-day period;
- that LPB may simply “buy back the credit” today;
- that later title is automatically void if Article 1535 is ultimately available.

## 3. What must not be intellectually closed without proof

The current record does not justify treating the Article 1535 position as extinguished merely because later events occurred.

Before any responsible conclusion that the position is legally exhausted, the record must establish at least:

1. the qualifying pre-assignment controversy, credit by credit;
2. whether the PH122→CAM transfer satisfies the statutory transfer requirements rather than an excluded bulk/succession category;
3. the exact assignment price/economics applicable to the relevant credits;
4. the earliest act capable of constituting a valid assignee demand for payment;
5. service/receipt of that act by the correct debtor;
6. whether the nine-day period therefore ever began and, if so, when;
7. whether any historical act constituted timely effective exercise;
8. the legal effect of the 25-Jan-2018 AC termination of Cristo's specific formal LPB mandate and the later claimant-side withdrawal of DP 1041;
9. whether literal statutory exercise remains possible; and
10. if literal exercise is no longer possible, what restoration, restitution, nullity/ineffectiveness, unjust-enrichment, tracing or damages consequence may remain.

**Control rule:** legal extinguishment is a proposition to prove. It is not inferred from downstream complexity or from the disappearance of the original credit as an accounting item.

Hypothesis: no qualifying demand for payment validly started the nine-day period, or another legally sufficient reason leaves literal exercise open.

Status: `OPEN — TRIGGER FORENSICS P0`.

### B. Restoration of a frustrated Article 1535 position

Hypothesis: literal present exercise is disputed or mechanically impossible, but LPB's effective opportunity was frustrated through acts later found ineffective, unlawful, disloyal, procedurally defective or incompatible with effective judicial protection.

Possible consequence to research: restoration of the economic/juridical position LPB would have occupied had Article 1535 been effectively exercisable at the relevant time.

Status: `OPEN — LEGAL THEORY REQUIRES ADJUDICATION`.

### C. Equivalent-value / downstream restitution

Hypothesis: the original credit has been consumed, satisfied or transformed into later assets/rights/value, so literal extinguishment can no longer be mechanically performed.

Possible consequences to test act by act:

- tracing into downstream value;
- restitution/accounting;
- unjust enrichment;
- ineffectiveness/nullity of dependent acts where legally available;
- damages measured against the lost position;
- criminal civil-liability consequences if predicate criminal conduct is eventually established.

Status: `OPEN — REMEDY DEPENDS ON CAUSATION, STANDING AND DOWNSTREAM PROTECTIONS`.

## 5. Bridge-removal counterfactual

Every major downstream dossier should be capable of running this model:

`PH122 credit → CAM assignment → CAM creditor recognition → later creditor leverage → adjudication/dación → CAM/HNT title/economic position → financing/refurbishment → MYND operation`

Then remove the upstream bridge:

`assume effective Article 1535 exercise → reimburse original CAM acquisition price + statutory additions → assigned credit extinguished → remove that creditor position → recalculate standing/leverage → recalculate adjudication/dación → recalculate CAM→HNT → identify which downstream rights survive independently`.

This is a **counterfactual causation test**, not a statement that every downstream act would necessarily fall.

## 6. Protected-trigger doctrine — public and internal boundary

Internal strategic objective: do not allow an unresolved Article 1535 position to be accidentally destroyed by provoking a fresh act that may arguably start a nine-day caducity period before counsel has mapped the trigger history and protection strategy.

Before any new direct demand to CAM for the deed/economics, counsel should determine:

1. whether a historical trigger already occurred;
2. what act currently could qualify as a demand for payment;
3. whether independent-source disclosure should be preferred;
4. what substantive proceeding could support precautionary relief;
5. whether declaratory/cautelar protection is available before a fresh trigger;
6. what emergency filing protocol applies if CAM makes a demand for payment.

Publicly, do **not** call this an established “right to freeze the nine-day clock.” Use:

> Por Derecho contends that an unresolved statutory position should not be rendered practically useless by a fresh triggering act before a competent court can determine its existence, historical treatment and appropriate remedy. The availability of declaratory or precautionary protection must be tested by counsel before any act capable of initiating an irreversible statutory period.

Criminal prejudiciality under Article 40 LEC is not an automatic tolling rule. It may be relevant where a criminal case investigates facts underlying the civil claim and the criminal determination may decisively affect the civil outcome. Any suspension/preservation argument must therefore be constructed specifically rather than assumed.
