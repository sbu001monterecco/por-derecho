# ART1535 DAY ZERO PRESERVATION MULTITRACK CONTROL

## Controlling proposition

No party should obtain an irreversible nine-day Article 1535 forfeiture merely by asserting that the clock has started. Before expiry can be responsibly asserted, the relevant credit, qualifying transfer, assignee, debtor, demand for payment and receipt must be identified and proved. The public shorthand is: **prove Day Zero before asserting Day Nine**.

This is deliberately narrower than saying that criminal or insolvency proceedings automatically freeze Article 1535. They do not.

## Current statutory anchors

- Civil Code Article 1535: sold litigious credit; reimbursement of price/costs/interest; nine days from the assignee's demand for payment.
- Civil Code Article 1536: statutory exceptions.
- LO 1/2025 Article 7: qualifying negotiation request interrupts prescription or suspends caducity under the statute's conditions.
- LEC Article 40: criminal prejudiciality requires an existing criminal cause investigating facts underpinning the civil claim and a potentially decisive criminal determination.

## Evidence rule

Any claimed trigger must be logged as:

`credit -> transfer -> transferor -> assignee -> price -> payment date -> debtor -> demand for payment -> recipient -> receipt date/time -> attachments -> legal trigger status`

Classification: `YES / NO / UNCERTAIN`.

## Anti-trigger protocol

- Preserve the complete communication and metadata.
- Do not inadvertently concede assignment validity, assignee status, amount, trigger sufficiency or expiry.
- Calculate the shortest conservative date while the true dies a quo remains disputed.
- Consider urgent pre-action interim relief before negotiation where appropriate.
- Consider LO 1/2025 Article 7 MASC preservation where legally available.
- Map any Article 40 LEC criminal nexus fact by fact.
