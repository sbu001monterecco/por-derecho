## 1. Direct physical handover — controlling witness statement

Gil Marer states as a matter of direct personal knowledge that he **personally handed over the relevant key copies** at Sun Park shortly after becoming involved at the hotel and assuming the Presidency of CEXP.

He states that:

1. he had recently become involved on the acquisition/ownership side around Luchy Playa Blanca and the Sun Park project;
2. the Presidency of CEXP had been passed to him by the prior office-holder; the prior office-holder is not to be named on the public website merely for this explanatory point;
3. persons attending from or on behalf of the local court arrived at Sun Park accompanied by a notary;
4. they showed papers indicating that they were entitled to receive the keys for the affected rooms;
5. Gil obtained the relevant copies and **personally handed them over**;
6. he did so **as President of CEXP**;
7. he understood the act as simple compliance with the court-imposed position, with no additional concession or significance beyond complying;
8. the keys handed over were copies, not a symbolic delivery of the hotel or an admission that Monterecco possessed or tourist-operated the affected units.

This is classified as **DIRECT WITNESS STATEMENT**. It must not be downgraded to a vague later recollection. Public attribution should remain explicit — “Gil Marer states…” — unless and until the primary notarial/receipt record independently corroborates the proposition.

## 2. Maintenance-copy context

Gil explains why the operating/maintenance side could hold copies even where a particular apartment was locked and not being used by the hotel.

Sun Park contains physically interdependent units. An incident in one unit can affect another vertically or elsewhere in the same block. A water leak in an upper unit, for example, can damage the unit below and potentially affect a wider part of the building. Effective complex maintenance may therefore require emergency access to a unit that is otherwise closed or outside active hotel use.

Accordingly:

`maintenance-key copy`

must not automatically be converted into:

`tourist exploitation`

or

`legal possession of the unit`.

The existence of a maintenance copy is compatible with a practical need to prevent damage to adjoining units, common services and the wider complex.

Gil does **not** assert that emergency maintenance entry actually occurred in every or any affected unit after the locks were changed. His evidence is narrower: if emergency access was required, it would have been maintenance access to cure the incident, not hotel guest use or commercial exploitation.

## 3. Compliance despite disagreement

Gil states that when the new ownership/management side arrived, he intuitively believed the adverse ruling was wrong. He later came to believe, after understanding more of the underlying history, that the Arrecife court had been presented with a materially distorted account of the rights and obligations attaching to the relevant units — in particular, treating the dispute as though it concerned ordinary holiday apartments rather than units embedded in a hotel participation/exploitation structure.

Gil presently alleges that the adverse side may have obtained the judicial intervention through false pretence or material misrepresentation of that structure.

**Classification:** `GIL MARER PRESENT ALLEGATION / WORKING HYPOTHESIS — NOT AN ADJUDICATED FINDING`.

This allegation must be tested against the originating claim, annexes, horizontal-division title, CEXP formation/participation documents, tourism/exploitation regime, the complete hearing record, judgments and later appellate evidence. It must not be published as established fraud merely because Gil holds that view.

The contemporaneous-conduct point is separate and strong: **despite believing the ruling was wrong, Gil states that he and his side deliberately respected the court order and chose to contest it through legal means rather than by self-help.**

## 4. Post-lock-change non-use through the hotel platform

Gil gives a categorical first-person operational statement concerning the affected adverse-party units after the locks were changed and through **7 June 2018**:

> **Not one of those affected apartments was used for a single hotel night through our hotel platform.**

This is classified as **DIRECT WITNESS / OPERATIONAL STATEMENT** and should be attributed to Gil pending corroboration from PMS, room inventory, booking, housekeeping, revenue, reception and channel records.

The statement means:

- no guest-night use through the hotel platform is asserted for the affected lock-changed units during that period;
- no commercial hotel exploitation of those units by Gil's operating side is admitted;
- any possible maintenance access, if an emergency required it, would have been limited to fixing the maintenance problem and preventing damage;
- maintenance intervention must not be conflated with hotel use, guest occupation or commercial exploitation.

## 5. Entity-capacity rule

The physical act described by Gil is:

**Gil Marer personally → acting as President of CEXP → handing over CEXP maintenance copies in compliance with the court/notarial attendance.**

It is not, without separate evidence:

- an act by Monterecco Sun Park, S.L.;
- proof that Monterecco held or controlled those keys in its own capacity;
- proof that Monterecco possessed the affected units;
- proof that Monterecco tourist-operated the affected units;
- an act by LPB, Pink Canary Services or every other entity with which Gil was associated.

Natural-person overlap must not collapse distinct legal capacities.

## 6. Contrary contemporaneous source — preserve, do not erase either side

A contemporaneous private communication under custody states that Gil had already left Lanzarote and that a security guard would make the physical delivery.

That source is material contrary evidence and must remain preserved. It creates a source conflict requiring reconciliation. It does **not** justify erasing or replacing Gil's direct witness statement about an act he says he personally performed.

Correct evidential treatment:

- `DIRECT WITNESS STATEMENT: Gil personally delivered the copies as CEXP President`;
- `CONTRARY CONTEMPORANEOUS SOURCE: private communication indicates a security guard would deliver`;
- `PRIMARY TIE-BREAKER REQUIRED: notarial act + signed receipt + attendance/capacity record + key schedule + any physical-delivery record`.

Possible explanations for the discrepancy must not be invented. The primary record should determine whether plans changed, whether more than one delivery/action occurred, whether the message was inaccurate, or whether the witness statement requires qualification.
