## Case identity and result

- Court: Juzgado de Primera Instancia n.º 5 de San Cristóbal de La Laguna.
- Proceeding: Procedimiento Ordinario 344/2013.
- Decision: Sentencia 41/2014, 18 February 2014, seven pages.
- Claimant: J&A Garrigues, S.L.P.
- Defendants: Gil Marer and Patricia Domínguez Montelongo.
- Defendants' counsel: Juan Tomás Parrilla Suárez.
- Principal claimed: EUR 63,441.67 plus interest and costs.
- Outcome: claim dismissed in full; first-instance costs imposed on Garrigues.

This proceeding concerns Garrigues's fee/guarantee claim. It is not the separate LPB-Monterecco/Pink advice, contract-validity or later insolvency-classification lane. Common actors and documents do not merge those legal matters.

- **9 May 2012:** Garrigues requested payment of a EUR 9,450 invoice for a challenge-of-resolutions/interim-measures matter.
- **17 May 2012:** Gil stated that HAVAVIDA T&T, S.L. transferred EUR 9,450 to Garrigues and described the funding/return arrangement.
- **10 June 2013:** the earlier HAVAVIDA/Garrigues exchange was forwarded to Parrilla; a bank copy was said to have been sent separately.
- **24 November 2013:** Gil wrote a detailed history of the Garrigues engagement, dispute and wish for an amicable resolution.
- **9 December 2013:** follow-up on that proposed resolution.
- **19 February 2014:** Gil sent the judgment and requested a meeting; a Garrigues recipient replied that the earlier escalation had not reached him and that he would review the files.
- **20 February 2014:** acknowledgement/follow-up.
- **28-30 May 2020:** client-side reconciliation states the La Laguna case was budgeted at EUR 2,500, EUR 1,250 was paid initially and EUR 1,250 followed; Patricia recorded that Parrilla would retain the costs and apply them to an unpaid balance.
- **11-12 June 2020:** Parrilla's own reconciliation says Gil and Patricia paid EUR 1,250 in the La Laguna Garrigues proceeding and that, after winning with costs, a EUR 1,250 amount was applied to the insolvency proceeding.

These summaries preserve the evidential propositions without publishing full private communications. They do not establish bank settlement, privilege waiver or acceptance of every participant's characterisation.
