**Classification:** the request and LPB's legal position are source-supported; the litigious-credit classification was LPB's legal contention, not a judicial finding.

### 19 December 2017 — Preliminary Proceedings 1041/2017

A final order of the Court of First Instance No. 2 of Las Palmas de Gran Canaria allowed LPB's preliminary measure seeking production and a complete copy of the 20-Oct-2017 assignment deed, the precise price, payment date and transaction costs. The court found the measure appropriate to the stated purpose and found just cause and legitimate interest, subject to security.

**Limit:** admission of the preliminary measure does not itself prove unlawful withholding, final non-compliance, wrongdoing or the merits of any later Article 1535 action.

Primary-source retrieval: `Auto admite Diligencia (LPB Tanteo y Retracto - Proc 1041) 19DIC17.pdf`, Drive id `1_n8VvVPEFfC37fJ4c3wdbpgiVLLlPR3P`.

### 8 February 2018 — Commercial Court order

The primary Commercial Court No. 1 order in Concurso 36/2012 records:

- LPB argued that the mortgage credits were litigious and Article 1535 should apply;
- the court rejected that argument, reasoning that the credits were recognised in the final insolvency texts and that mortgage enforcement, on its reasoning, did not itself make the credits litigious;
- the court accepted LPB's separate amount objection insofar as assignment changes the creditor but not the amount recognised in the final texts;
- Construcciones Acosta Matos, S.A. was recognised as holder of **€857,373.81** and **€8,194,877.88**, plus contingent mortgage-enforcement costs without a separate amount;
- the order provided an appeal route.

Primary-source retrieval: `8. Modificacion Textos Definitivos PRIV ESPECIAL (SIN INTERESES) 8FEB2018.pdf`, Drive id `1jwG4inqC8HFOdLbU3RdPY3PKj6SzKA7f`.

## Article 1535 legal boundary

The official BOE Civil Code text states that a credit is considered litigious from the point the claim relating to it has been answered. Therefore:

- a quantified commercial/accounting dispute is not automatically enough;
- mortgage enforcement alone is not published as sufficient to satisfy every technical requirement;
- the exact pre-20-Oct-2017 claim, opposition or incident and responsive pleading/act must be identified credit by credit before the site describes Article 1535 status as established.

The public page must state both LPB's position and the 8-Feb-2018 adverse ruling with comparable prominence.

## Prohibited shortcuts

Do not publish as established fact that:

- CAM deliberately concealed the deed or price;
- PH122 or CAM deliberately inflated the debt;
- the court or insolvency administration favoured CAM;
- Article 1535 indisputably applied on 20-Oct-2017;
- any Article 1535 right was definitively lost by unlawful conduct;
- the 19-Dec-2017 preliminary order proves liability;
- a debt discrepancy proves fraud.

Old PR #19 language using "deliberate opacity", "favouritism/bias" or a "manufactured" undisputed debt is **not controlling** and must not be revived without stronger source-specific proof.
