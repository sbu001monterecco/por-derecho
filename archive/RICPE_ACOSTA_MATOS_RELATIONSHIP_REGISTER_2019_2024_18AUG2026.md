# RICPE × ACOSTA MATOS — RELATIONSHIP RECONSTRUCTION REGISTER

**Scope:** 2019–2024, with 2022–2024 intensive follow-through  
**Status:** evidential reconstruction in progress  
**Public-site purpose:** prevent the 11-Nov-2020 webinar from being misread as a stand-alone event  
**Method:** facts / documented representations / inference / missing evidence kept separate

## 1. Controlling proposition

The evidential unit is **not one webinar**.

The 11 November 2020 RIC Private Equity webinar is a particularly useful public snapshot of a relationship that the preserved material itself describes as broader and already operational: project origination, analysis begun months earlier, fiscal and legal work, due diligence, external risk review, investor commitments, negotiation and formalisation, technical validation of drawdowns, quarterly monitoring and institutional contacts.

Accordingly, the repository and public page must reconstruct a **relationship network and sequence of interactions**, not merely quote one presentation.

The controlling question is:

> Who introduced whom, who arranged or convened each interaction, which fiscal/legal/technical or other adviser prepared each step, what documents circulated, which investors participated, which public authority or notarial officer took part in a relevant act or closing, and what knowledge reached each RICPE/CAM decision-maker, from 2019 through 2024?

## 3. Why the relationship necessarily extends beyond the webinar

The source contains multiple internal pointers to earlier, parallel and later workstreams:

### 3.1 Analysis pre-dated the webinar

In the Q&A, Enrique Guerra says the analysis had begun in **February 2020**. That places the working relationship months before the 11-Nov-2020 broadcast and creates a retrieval obligation for the preceding introductions, meetings, mandates, information requests, data-room access, investment analysis and project-selection process.

### 3.2 Project-side participation was structural, not incidental

José Acosta is presented as both Director General of Construcciones Acosta Matos and a RIC Private Equity board member/consejero. He says the group had placed three tourism-renovation projects in RICPE's portfolio and later identifies Sun Park as one of the projects.

This is not consistent with treating the webinar as the beginning and end of the interaction. It points to a project-originator / partner / governance relationship requiring chronology.

### 3.3 Fiscal advisers and fiscal files were part of the process

Murli Kessomal is presented as **Asesor Fiscal y Vicesecretario de RIC PRIVATE EQUITY**. The webinar describes fiscal/administrative reports and a fiscal filter before board consideration.

For Sun Park, the evidential task is to identify the actual fiscal advisers, consultations, meetings, instructions, files, reservations and reports, and distinguish general RIC procedure from project-specific work.

### 3.4 Lawyers and formalisation were part of the process

Enrique Guerra states that the lawyers handling the process were **Uría Menéndez, Madrid**, and expressly places project study, analysis, negotiation and formalisation in the cost/process chain.

For Sun Park, retrieve the engagement letter, named team, instructions, calls/meetings, transaction versions, closing checklist, contracts/deeds and any title/insolvency/third-party reservations.

### 3.5 Due diligence was described as a multi-professional exercise

The webinar describes:

- internal RICPE financial due diligence / investment analysis;
- **Pricewaterhouse** legal and tax due diligence of the company and especially the project;
- an external risk-control function identified as **Intermoney**;
- a technical architect monitoring works and validating invoices/drawdowns;
- insurance advisory as part of the wider control perimeter (AON is mentioned as an example, not established here as Sun Park's actual adviser).

The public record must therefore ask not simply what RICPE said, but what each professional actually received, checked, qualified, reported and circulated for Sun Park.

### 3.6 Other investors were part of the relationship

The webinar describes investor commitments and third-party investors accompanying the promoter, while José Acosta also describes his own experience as a RICPE investor.

The reconstruction must therefore include investor introductions and investor-facing meetings, what information was supplied before commitments, capital-call timing, and whether project descriptions changed between investor cohorts or later documents.

### 3.7 There were other institutional events around the webinar

The preserved source records Enrique Guerra referring to a separate RICPE institutional act attended by the President of the Canary Islands Government and an AEAT representative. The compilation also preserves a programme for a separate **“Reinicio del Turismo en Canarias”** event dated 9-Nov-2020.

These references must be reconciled from primary invitations, attendee lists, calendars and contemporaneous communications. They are important precisely because they show why a single-webinar chronology is inadequate.

### Important evidential caveat on notaries

The webinar source itself **does not identify a particular Sun Park notary**. It proves that negotiation/formalisation formed part of the described RICPE process; it does not prove which notary or protocol was used for Sun Park.

Therefore the notarial lane is mandatory but must remain **unattributed until primary records are found**: deeds, powers, protocol numbers, drafts, notarial appointments, closing emails, attendance records and documents delivered to the notary.

## 5. Event-register schema

Use permanent IDs:

`RICMEET-YYYY-NNN`

Each event/contact entry must contain:

- exact date or bounded date window;
- event type;
- arranger / introducer / convenor;
- attendees;
- organisations and capacities;
- location or channel;
- agenda / purpose;
- documents supplied before/during/after;
- representations made;
- questions raised;
- decisions / commitments;
- follow-up action;
- evidential source;
- confidence level;
- whether Sun Park-specific or general RICPE relationship evidence.

An interaction can be registered even where the formal invitation is missing, but it must be labelled **inferred event** and the inference must be stated.

## 6. “Work backwards from the document” rule

Do not search only for the words `meeting` or `reunión`.

If a source says:

- “following our meeting” — locate the meeting;
- “as agreed” — locate the agreement path;
- “we have completed due diligence” — locate the mandate, data room, report, qualifications and recipients;
- “commitment signed” — locate the investor/project meeting and approval path preceding it;
- “formalisation” — locate lawyers, drafts, deeds, powers, notary/closing records and executed documents;
- “quarterly monitoring” — locate the monitoring pack, reviewers, issues, decisions and subsequent action;
- a later prospectus changes a project description — locate the internal event or information that generated the change.

Every material document should be treated as a possible pointer to an earlier or later interaction.

Its cover states that an interview with Enrique Guerra was published on 30-Nov-2021 on the channel of **San Telmo Abogados y Economistas** and preserves the referenced video URLs.

Classification at this stage:

- **proved by the preserved compilation:** existence of a documented public/professional San Telmo ↔ Enrique Guerra interaction as presented in that compilation;
- **not proved by that fact alone:** that San Telmo arranged the Sun Park investment, advised RICPE on Sun Park, transmitted Sun Park information, or that any specific San Telmo professional had a Sun Park decision-making role.

It should therefore be used as a **relationship lead requiring matter-specific tracing**, not as a shortcut to a conflict conclusion.

Required follow-up: original post/video metadata, host identity, invitation/arranger, any accompanying article or promotion, contemporaneous emails, and any evidence connecting the same professionals to a Sun Park-specific act.

## 9. Evidence standard

Use:

- **E1** primary contemporaneous evidence;
- **E2** official/public record;
- **E3** contemporaneous secondary evidence;
- **E4** later recollection/compilation;
- **E5** inference.

Do not convert proximity into conflict, a general adviser role into a Sun Park-specific mandate, or formalisation language into a named notarial act without the underlying source.

Conversely, do not exclude an interaction from the register merely because only the surrounding documentary footprint survives. Preserve it as an inference or missing-evidence entry.
