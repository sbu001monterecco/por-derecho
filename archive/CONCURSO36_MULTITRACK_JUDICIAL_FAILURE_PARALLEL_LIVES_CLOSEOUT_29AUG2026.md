## Purpose

Create a reader-facing, evidence-controlled matrix that places party filings and warnings directly beside located court/LAJ/prosecutorial treatment and the legal/economic life that continued afterward.

The publication does **not** declare that the judiciary, Ministerio Fiscal or any public authority formed part of a criminal plan. It tests whether perimeter failure, inadequate protection, accounting failure, contradictory traceability, procedural non-merits outcomes or adverse reframing had the practical effect of allowing challenged private positions to continue acquiring additional legal/commercial lives.

## Six failure/control tests

1. Perimeter failure.
2. Preventive/protective failure.
3. Reconciliation/accounting failure.
4. Traceability/contradiction failure.
5. Merits-access failure.
6. Institutional-neutralisation failure.

The sixth is deliberately an **effect test** first. To upgrade to criminal capture/shielding, the record must prove a specific act/omission, competence/duty, knowledge, legally required intent/purpose, coordination where alleged, causal effect, beneficiary/harm and contrary evidence.
