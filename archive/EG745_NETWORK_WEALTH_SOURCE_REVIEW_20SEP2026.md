# E G 745 network and economic benefit source review

20 September 2026. Research supporting the substantive response; no filing or determination of liability.

## What the evidence permits us to ask

Gil Marer alleges that connected private, professional and institutional interventions have enabled the Acosta Matos perimeter to obtain and retain the Sun Park operating platform and associated benefits, and to resist investigation and restitution. The intended inquiry encompasses the original acquisition, control before title, subsequent exploitation and financing, and the use of resources and influence to protect the resulting position. His allegation of illicit origin and continuing deployment of wealth must remain explicit. A financial investigation must establish which assets, gains, substitutions, transfers, expenditures and interventions actually bear that connection.

Three records should remain distinct and linked: loss allocated to each injured right-holder; gain or advantage allocated to each recipient; and present assets potentially available under an identified legal remedy. A common director, later commercial success or a retained lawyer does not by itself prove an unlawful transfer or obstruction. Conversely, a missing direct rent transfer does not dispose of non-cash benefits, financing capacity, guarantees, introductions, mandates or the use of the acquired platform to secure later opportunities.

The strongest response identifies concrete fiscal acts, the corpus available at each date, the alleged omission or misclassification, its potential protective effect and the requested investigation or competent referral. It should not ask Inspección Fiscal to treat the entire network as already convicted or to exercise asset-seizure powers it does not possess.

## Historic loss calculations and the updated board document

The 29-page February 2024 board annex was read in full. The separately recovered 27-page annex dated 20 April 2026 was checked for its version, opening and loss schedules. It repeats several historical figures and periods; its later date does not convert them into a fresh expert assessment through September 2026.

The board documents claim EUR22,225,355.37 loss of income, EUR5,556,577.55 rent and GBP14,271,400 for a wider UK distribution scenario. They attribute different contractual and economic bases to those sums. These are claims in the group's own records. They must not be totalled automatically, treated as court awards, or conflated with the value of the hotel, financing raised by another party or the other party's gross turnover. Separate overlap, owners, periods, counterfactual occupancy, costs, signed-contract conditions and expert methodology.

The wider UK scenario identifies travel/distribution arrangements and a five-year calculation. It supports the existence of a broader asserted business case beyond rent. Native negotiations and contractual status must determine what was binding, conditional, unsigned or merely contemplated at each date.

The historic board annex also contains a strong version of an alleged intermediary-payment account. Newly examined earlier correspondence distinguishes a reported commission figure from speculation over its recipient. That provenance must not be silently upgraded into a proven payment to the administrator. Request the engagement, invoice, payer, payee, bank trail, accounting treatment and related communications. The recovered administrator's deposition cannot supply an answer to a question the court disallowed.

The board annex's legal interpretations of mortgage-liability ceilings, debt extinction, auction consequences, successor liability and recoverable damages are the directors' positions. The present response should rely on the actual instruments, decisions and applicable legal tests; their appearance in an accounts annex is not independent legal verification.

## Direct conduct and professional custody

The four-page March 2018 telephone-transcript document contains a custody certificate followed by a conversation about obtaining access, presenting an entry as unauthorised if the owners appear, a community agreement, future security and possible maintenance work. The transcript uses generic interlocutor labels. Its filename does not independently authenticate the speaker's identity. Preserve the original audio, certified electronic matrix, transcription history and attribution evidence. This is a specific source inquiry into access, authority and conditional benefits, not a substitute for voice identification.

The 149-page June 2016 meeting transcript was examined at its opening, not read in full in this lane. It explicitly mixes timestamped dialogue and bracketed editorial comments. Comments must never be quoted as something a recorded participant said. The opening discusses examining actual payments, who paid and on whose behalf, while distinguishing the operating community from the owners' community. Audio comparison and source-page attribution remain necessary for any longer quotation.

The email and public-site reviews add the actual administrator's deposition, contemporaneous maintenance/access instructions, professional due-diligence roles and later corporate positions. Those records must be read together with denials, conditions and alternative explanations. The object is to determine the individual contribution and knowledge behind a connected sequence, including any misuse of authority or information, rather than to infer liability from a contact list.

## Proposed substantive formulation

“Denuncio la posible instrumentalización coordinada de un perímetro privado, profesional e institucional en beneficio y protección del perímetro Acosta Matos. Solicito que se examine cómo el control material, la apariencia de legitimidad, la explotación, la financiación y el tratamiento sucesivo de las denuncias pudieron contribuir a obtener, consolidar y defender una posición patrimonial y comercial que considero de origen ilícito. La investigación debe abarcar la riqueza, los rendimientos, las sustituciones patrimoniales y los recursos empleados para sostener esa posición, individualizando actos, fechas, conocimiento, deberes, beneficiarios y trazas documentales. La referencia a una red expresa la conexión funcional que denuncio y que debe contrastarse; no sustituye la determinación de la conducta de cada interviniente.”

“La relevancia económica excede la titularidad aislada de determinadas fincas: comprende la explotación, la clientela, los canales de comercialización, las oportunidades de financiación, los contratos, las garantías y los derechos de cada entidad afectada. Pido reconstruir separadamente el perjuicio de los titulares, los beneficios de los receptores y los bienes actualmente alcanzables por cada vía legal, evitando duplicidades. La tutela solicitada incluye la preservación de la prueba y, cuando proceda, la remisión concreta al órgano competente para investigar los flujos o solicitar medidas patrimoniales.”
