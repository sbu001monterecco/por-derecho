## 1. Controlling proposition

The Edgeworth proceedings are a useful comparator at the level of professional-liability issue families, procedure and communication discipline. They are not evidence of the Sun Rock / Aweswell allegations, a merits precedent, a finding of repeated conduct, a joint case or a bridge between legal entities.

**Current Sun Rock / Aweswell status:** no UK legal action is being pursued. The formerly explored UK provider route is closed and no UK firm is currently instructed for such proceedings. The civil and professional-liability work remains Spain-led. A possible SRA notification, if separately justified, is a non-court regulatory question and must not be described as UK litigation.

The public formulation is:

> Parallel questions may exist; duty, evidence, causation, loss and procedural control remain independent.

## 2. Public case record

The approved judgment dated 2 May 2025 identifies:

- **Case:** `BL-2024-000237`; `[2025] EWHC 1014 (Ch)`.
- **Claimant:** Edgeworth Capital (Luxembourg) S.à r.l.
- **Defendant:** Cuatrecasas Gonçalves Pereira LLP.
- **Pleaded dispute:** a €213 million professional-negligence and breach-of-retainer claim concerning an alleged failure to advise on the effect of two early-2019 Spanish Supreme Court insolvency-interest decisions before a June 2019 transaction.
- **Opposing position:** the defendant denies the claim.
- **Decision actually made:** specified amendments were permitted and the strike-out application was to be dismissed subject to a compliant statement of truth.
- **Decision not made:** no merits determination on duty, breach, causation or loss.

A 20 June 2026 secondary report says an April 2026 costs and case-management conference occurred and the case was moving toward disclosure. Until the sealed order or an equivalent primary procedural record is added, that later status must remain expressly attributed to secondary reporting.

## 3. Partial-overlap rule

Potentially useful overlap is confined to:

1. professional-negligence / breach-of-retainer analytical structure;
2. a potentially related early-2019 Spanish Supreme Court issue, without asserting identity with Edgeworth's two Decisions unless the exact judgments and Sun Rock/Aweswell documentary intersection are proved from primary files;
3. a lost-opportunity causation model; and
4. the need to identify the precise retainer, entity, advice duty, counterfactual and claimant-specific loss.

Non-overlap is controlling for:

- claimants and standing;
- defendant entities and personnel;
- transaction, assets and counterparties;
- principal adverse actors;
- governing law, jurisdiction and limitation;
- procedural posture;
- evidence and disclosure obligations; and
- quantum.

No proposition may use the Edgeworth case to prove pattern, propensity, institutional culpability, common intent, knowledge, breach, causation or loss in the Sun Rock record.

## 5. Future-cooperation gate

No new cross-matter exchange or communication is authorised by this control. Before information moves, require:

1. independent merits review for each claimant;
2. identity, authority and beneficial-control checks;
3. conflict checks for parties and advisers;
4. jurisdiction-specific privilege and disclosure advice;
5. written scope, purpose, consent, confidentiality, use and exit terms;
6. data minimisation and lawful-transfer review;
7. separate control of pleadings, witnesses, experts, media, funding and settlement; and
8. a logged, document-level record of what was shared and on whose authority.

A label such as “common interest” or “cooperation protocol” must never be treated as creating privilege or authority by itself.

## 7. Entity and regulatory firewall

The LLP identified in the Edgeworth judgment cannot be treated as responsible for another mandate because the same international brand or a London office appears. Every civil, insurance or SRA proposition must identify the exact legal person, retainer, office, regulated status, work, invoice, act, governing law and alleged breach.

The SRA route remains secondary and jurisdiction-dependent. It must not be presented as a substitute for civil causation, a damages forum, or proof that a group entity is regulated.
