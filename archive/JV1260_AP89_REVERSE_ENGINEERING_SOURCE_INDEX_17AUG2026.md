## Governing evidential rule

A later judgment's recital is evidence of what the court recorded and decided; it is not automatically independent proof of every upstream factual proposition. Where an original pleading, ACTA, registry note, notarial act or contemporaneous record exists, that primary object controls the reconstruction of its own contents.
