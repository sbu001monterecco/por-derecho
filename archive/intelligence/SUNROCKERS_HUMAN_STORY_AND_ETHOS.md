# Sun Park / SunRockers human story and ethos — source-led book direction

**Status:** public-safe working synthesis · 15 August 2026

## Why this file exists

The book and promotional architecture must not reduce Sun Park to a hotel, a credit or a legal dispute. The connected Gmail and Google Drive archive preserves a distinct social/hospitality history: Sun Park Living, the SunRockers community, longer stays, resident participation, shared activities and the idea that later life can remain adventurous, social and purposeful.

This file records that narrative direction while separating primary/historical material from later analytical summaries and from any disputed claim concerning present-day MYND Yaiza.

## Historical working description

A later self-archive email summarising earlier public and internal material described Sun Park Living (2012–2018) as a community proposition aimed at people over 50 / grown-ups, based on longer stays and participation in activities and workshops. Examples listed there include art, music, walking, gardening, small markets, reading and community events.

Because that email is itself a later analytical summary, the book should use it as a research map and return to the original brochures, community documents, photographs, programmes and contemporaneous public material for publication-grade detail.

## The human narrative

The strongest book framing is:

> **Before Sun Park became a case file, it was a place people came to live, return, make friends and begin again.**

The book should recover:

- what Playa Blanca felt like during the Sun Park years;
- why some guests stayed longer or returned;
- who the SunRockers were and how they described themselves;
- friendships and social rituals;
- resident-led or participative activities;
- staff/guest relationships;
- photographs, dances, walks, markets, creative work and ordinary daily life;
- the role of sunshine, outdoor life and Lanzarote itself;
- the business idea behind longer-stay grown-up hospitality;
- what was lost or interrupted when operational control changed;
- what survived in memories, relationships, images and later business ideas.

This material should precede the densest legal chapters so readers understand **what was at stake before being asked to understand how it was lost or transformed**.

## Sun Park → MYND Yaiza

The physical continuity is narratively powerful: the location at the centre of the old Sun Park history is today operated/marketed as MYND Yaiza.

Safe publication rule:

- it is appropriate to show the same physical place across time;
- it is appropriate to compare hospitality concepts where source material supports the comparison;
- similarities in creative activities, experiential hospitality or guest participation do **not** by themselves prove intellectual-property transfer, copying, unlawful appropriation or legal succession of the original SunRocker concept;
- any such legal/economic thesis must be proved separately.

The book can therefore juxtapose **place and experience** without overclaiming ownership of ideas.

## Adam J. Jackson and The Ethos

A 26 January 2023 email from Gil Marer to a professional contact was explicitly headed around the “Project Ethos of Sun Rock” and shared:

- a Sun Park Living video;
- the Sun Park Living YouTube channel; and
- Adam J. Jackson's book **`The Ethos: Because life is better together`**.

The book was published by Third Age Publishing in 2017. Public bibliographic/synopsis material describes a story about a man approaching 60 who encounters a community and rediscovers friendship, purpose, opportunity, new dreams and the possibility that later life can become richer rather than smaller.

This is a philosophically relevant reference because the SunRocker proposition similarly emphasised community, participation and a fuller later chapter of life.

### Attribution boundary

Do not state or imply that Adam J. Jackson:

- created Sun Park Living or the SunRockers concept;
- endorsed `Reason to Believe`;
- endorses Gil's legal allegations;
- is involved in Fundación Por Derecho or Havidia;
- has authorised use of his intellectual property beyond ordinary reference/criticism/commentary.

The safe formulation is that `The Ethos` was an **explicit philosophical reference used by Gil to explain the Sun Rock ethos**, and that its published themes resonate with the community/later-life idea.

Avoid reproducing substantial text from Jackson's book. Summarise themes and attribute them.

## Private editorial feedback — internal lesson, not public endorsement

Private 2023 correspondence with a trusted reader reinforced several editorial rules: explain the case clearly and concisely; prefer simple English and short sentences; use exact facts, sums, dates, parties and contracts rather than loaded labels; make the story understandable to non-lawyers; address contrary guest/resident accounts with evidence; and reconnect with former Sun Park residents and guests.

The correspondence itself should remain private unless publication consent is obtained. It should not be used publicly to imply endorsement by the correspondent.

**Book/editorial rule derived from it:** if a reader cannot understand the human story and the core factual proposition before seeing the detailed evidence, the book has failed regardless of how much evidence exists.

## Promotion implications

The marketing should show **people and place before paperwork**.

Lead emotional promise:

> **Before it was a legal battle, it was a place people belonged.**

Supporting lines:

> **Sun Park. Playa Blanca. The SunRockers. The same place now known as MYND Yaiza. One history still being reconstructed.**

> **The evidence explains what happened. The book remembers what mattered.**

Visual priorities for future cleared assets:

1. Sun Park / Playa Blanca atmosphere;
2. historical community life and activities where consent/publication rights permit;
3. SunRockers dancing/social/creative imagery;
4. then the transformed present-day physical asset;
5. documentary evidence as a secondary visual layer, not the emotional foreground.

## Memory collection programme

Create a voluntary, consent-based route for former guests, SunRockers, staff and collaborators to contribute:

- memories;
- photographs;
- old brochures/programmes;
- newsletters;
- activity schedules;
- guest stories;
- corrections or contrary accounts.

Every contribution should carry source, date/provenance where available, permission/publication status and whether it is a personal recollection versus contemporaneous documentary record.

A right to disagree is essential. Former guests who had negative experiences or financial disputes should not be excluded merely because their account is adverse.

## Core principle

**The legal case explains why the history matters. The SunRockers explain why the reader should care.**
