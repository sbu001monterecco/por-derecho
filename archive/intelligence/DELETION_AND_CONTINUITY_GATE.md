# Conversation deletion and continuity gate

## Purpose

This checklist defines when a source ChatGPT conversation may be treated as non-essential to project continuity. It does **not** guarantee that every sentence from a conversation has been reproduced. The objective is preservation of material reusable intelligence, decisions, architecture and pointers to underlying evidence while respecting privacy and privilege.

## Do not delete a source thread until all applicable gates pass

### Gate A — reusable intelligence preserved

Confirm the repository contains the thread's material reusable:

- objectives and theses;
- major factual/research questions;
- evidential methodology;
- architecture and schemas;
- decisions already made;
- planning assumptions and confidence levels;
- resource/token strategy;
- publication/legal boundaries;
- book/brand architecture;
- Foundation/Havidia/commercial boundaries;
- execution plan, decision gates and KPIs;
- unresolved questions and next actions.

### Gate B — source dependency preserved

Where the thread relied on primary documents, email, Drive/OneDrive files or other evidence, the handover must identify the relevant corpus/source class or durable repository record. It is not necessary or appropriate to copy restricted material into a public repository.

### Gate C — public/private separation

Confirm that continuity was **not** achieved by publishing:

- privileged advice or litigation strategy;
- private personal information;
- confidential or restricted correspondence;
- protected whistleblower/source information;
- unredacted private audio;
- reserved tax or other legally restricted material;
- unsupported allegations.

Private-source work remains recoverable through the relevant connected private source or a controlled archive, not through public GitHub.

### Gate D — fresh-thread test

Start a new ChatGPT thread and instruct it to read:

1. `archive/intelligence/START_HERE_CHATGPT.md`;
2. the five linked continuity files;
3. the relevant public website/depository pages;
4. connected private sources only as required by the actual task.

Test whether the new thread can accurately explain, without access to the old conversation:

- One Asset. One History.;
- the evidence/source/proposition architecture;
- the current corpus/token estimates and their caveats;
- the staged processing strategy;
- the 90-day / 12-month plan;
- Reason to Believe's role;
- Fundación Por Derecho's intended public-interest role;
- Havidia's distinct intended commercial role;
- why the project does not claim proprietary AI;
- the publication firewall;
- what remains unresolved.

### Gate E — repository state is durable

Confirm the preservation files are on `main`, not only an unmerged working branch, and can be retrieved by another thread through the GitHub connector or public GitHub Pages where applicable.

### Gate F — deployment verified

Confirm the public Knowledge Project pages load and show the intended current statements. Confirm the Por Derecho section visibly links the upcoming book and the continuity page/link if one is deployed.

## Material excluded by design

The public continuity pack preserves **intelligence**, not every private source or every conversational utterance. Exact private-source material should remain in its lawful source system and be retrieved when needed.

## Current limitation

The continuity pack was reconstructed from the conversation context available to the model and the durable decisions/work products identified in the thread. ChatGPT does not expose a guaranteed machine-readable export of every omitted/skipped historical turn to this repository workflow. Therefore the final deletion recommendation must be based on a **material-intelligence audit plus a fresh-thread recovery test**, not a claim of byte-for-byte conversation replication.

## Deletion threshold

Recommended threshold before telling the user a thread is safe to delete:

- **Critical reusable intelligence gaps: 0**
- **Material architecture/decision recovery: ≥99%**
- **Private/restricted omissions: intentional and source-recoverable**
- **START_HERE retrieval test: pass**
- **Files merged to `main`: pass**
- **Public deployment verification: pass**

Only after these gates pass should the project describe the old thread as non-essential for continuity.
