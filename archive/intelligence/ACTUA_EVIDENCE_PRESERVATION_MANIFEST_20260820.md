## Preservation rule
This manifest distinguishes three classes and must not collapse them:
1. **IN-REPOSITORY PRESERVED** — substantive findings, corrections, evidence-object ledgers, source identifiers, publication safeguards and open questions are stored in this repository.
2. **NATIVE-SOURCE PRESERVED BY PROVENANCE** — original evidence remains in its native Gmail / Google Drive / LexNET source and is identified here and in the linked repository digests. A repository pointer is not the same thing as a byte-for-byte evidential copy.
3. **OPEN / NOT YET ACQUIRED** — evidence sought but not yet obtained. Absence must not be filled by inference.
