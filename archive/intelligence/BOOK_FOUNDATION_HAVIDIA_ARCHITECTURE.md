# Reason to Believe / Fundación Por Derecho / Havidia architecture

## Brand / programme family

The present architecture distinguishes six connected but non-identical components:

### ONE ASSET. ONE HISTORY.
The evidential demonstrator: the continuous history of Sun Park and the rights/economic interests around it.

### REASON TO BELIEVE / RAZÓN PARA CREER
The human narrative and publishing project.

### THE KNOWLEDGE PROJECT
The methodology and experiment: converting fragmented records into an auditable, reusable knowledge system.

### FUNDACIÓN POR DERECHO
The planned public-interest institutional pathway.

### AMERICAN AI JUSTICE
The international research/outreach challenge: whether AI can help legal systems preserve and reconstruct institutional memory.

### HAVIDIA LLC
The intended US commercial and future-technology pathway, subject to documented rights, governance and actual product/service development.

## Unifying visual idea

A **continuous line** can connect the family: asset → documents → decisions → institutions → horizon. It represents continuity of history and rights despite fragmentation.

## Reason to Believe

### Working title

`Reason to Believe / Razón para creer` remains a working title. Alternative naming can continue dynamically.

### Current cover proposition

Preferred hierarchy:

**REASON TO BELIEVE**  
**ONE ASSET. ONE HISTORY.**  
**GIL MARER**

The Spanish/English editions should use the same master artwork with edition-specific typography. Authentic Spanish documentary fragments can remain subtly present in the English edition because the story is anchored in Spain.

### Narrative role

The repository documents. The book explains the human and institutional journey.

The book should include the lived experience: years of uncertainty, business decisions, Patricia and close collaborators, legal meetings, mistakes, setbacks, institutional encounters, moments of discovery, financial/economic pressure, the arrival of modern AI as a research tool, the emergence of Por Derecho, and the fact that the outcome is still developing.

The book should be written while preserving the distinction between:

- established documentary fact;
- first-instance/adverse findings and appellate status;
- party allegations;
- evidence-based inference;
- unresolved questions.

### Foundation relationship

Public-safe formulation:

> **Reason to Believe / Razón para creer is a publishing and educational project associated with Fundación Por Derecho. The evidence repository reconstructs the documentary history; the book tells the human and institutional story behind it.**

This formulation does **not** itself assign copyright, royalties, translation, audio, film/documentary or other commercial rights to the Foundation.

A book purchase and a Foundation donation are separate transactions. If any royalty allocation to the Foundation is later adopted, publish the exact documented arrangement rather than implying it.

## Fundación Por Derecho

### Intended public-interest role

- legal literacy and civic education;
- access to justice;
- responsible organisation of complex legal records;
- legal/institutional memory;
- research and institutional-improvement proposals;
- academic/student collaboration;
- transparent evidential methodology;
- responsible AI research in justice-related information systems;
- pro bono/referral networks where legally and operationally appropriate.

### Foundation boundary

The Foundation should not be presented as:

- owner/funder of private recovery claims merely by association;
- an investment vehicle;
- a law firm;
- a conduit for Havidia/private-company investment;
- an automatic owner of book, corpus, IP or software rights.

Donations/grants should support documented public-interest purposes and governance.

## Havidia LLC

### Intended role

US-facing commercial / future technology pathway for exploring:

- complex-case reconstruction;
- evidence and provenance intelligence;
- title/credit/NPL diligence;
- institutional-memory audits;
- professional research services;
- future software or integrations actually developed;
- strategic technology/AI partnerships;
- appropriate investment and commercial pilots.

### Mandatory transparency

Havidia does **not** presently claim to have developed a proprietary foundation AI model. The programme relies predominantly on ChatGPT and other available tools.

Potential proprietary value must be described accurately and only where rights exist: lawfully usable corpus/data, methodology, structured knowledge, prompts/workflows, product design, integrations, code/software actually developed, brands and contracted rights.

### Required rights register

Before claiming ownership, document:

- book copyright and author rights;
- translation/audio/film/documentary rights;
- corpus/data rights by source/entity;
- website and structured-knowledge rights;
- methodology/workflow rights;
- trademarks/names;
- future code/software;
- licenses between Gil, relevant private entities, Foundation and Havidia;
- confidentiality/data-protection constraints.

## Foundation × enterprise bridge

Public-interest work and commercial work can be complementary without being financially or legally interchangeable.

| Function | Fundación Por Derecho | Havidia LLC |
|---|---|---|
| Public research | Core | May support/consume lawfully |
| Donations | Yes, once legally enabled | No |
| Grants | Public-interest grants | Separate commercial grants where applicable |
| Education | Core | Professional training/products may be separate |
| Open methodology | Possible/core | May implement subject to rights |
| Commercial services | Limited by legal purpose/governance | Core potential |
| Equity investment | No | Possible |
| AI/compute credits | Public-interest allocation | Commercial allocation |
| Private recoveries | Not Foundation property by default | Not Havidia property by default |
| Case evidence | Governed by source/data rights | Only where lawfully available/authorised |

## Audience journey

Public UX developed in the originating work:

**Understand** — One Asset. One History.  
**Investigate** — Follow the Evidence / Title / Credit / Money / Decisions.  
**Challenge** — Test Our Case; show adverse evidence and alternative explanations.  
**Belong** — Por Derecho identity based on effective rights and evidence, not agreement with every conclusion.  
**Own / Support / Build** — book, donation, research, compute, partnership or investment through the correct channel.

Trust and evidence must precede conversion.

## Revenue/resource separation

### Publishing/media
Book sales, audiobook, translation and adaptation rights where lawfully owned/licensed.

### Foundation
Donations, grants, founding patrons, academic/public-interest support and compute donations/credits subject to legal setup.

### Havidia
Commercial pilots, services, strategic partnership, investment, future licensing/software.

### Private business/recovery
Investor diligence, funding, settlement/recovery strategy, asset or interest transactions. Keep separate from Foundation fundraising.

Do not double-count value or describe unrealised claims as revenue.
