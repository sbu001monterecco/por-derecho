# Integrated thread handover — 15 August 2026

## 1. Working thesis

The current programme is built around the proposition **One Asset. One History.** Sun Park has one continuous economic and documentary history even though that history has been divided across insolvency, enforcement, property, corporate, tax, administrative, regulatory, professional and criminal processes. The project seeks to reconstruct that history without erasing the legal boundaries between proceedings or converting allegations into facts.

A core concern is fragmentation: the same asset, credit, property rights, operating business, decisions and institutional knowledge can be atomised into separate files so that no individual decision-maker sees the whole sequence. The response is not to assert coordination merely because outcomes align; it is to reconstruct chronology, authority, knowledge, causation and consequences from traceable sources and test competing explanations.

## 2. Governing evidential posture

Maintain three primary classes throughout substantive work:

1. Verified fact supported by a primary or identified source.
2. Evidence-based inference / working hypothesis.
3. Matter requiring further evidence or reconciliation.

Public work adds explicit classes for party allegation, procedural status, contradiction, correction and document gap.

Do not describe a formal judicial or administrative act as economically or institutionally neutral merely because it occurred through a formal process. Analyse its origins, legal scope, information available, operation and consequences. Equally, do not state criminality, capture, bias or coordination as established unless the evidence and competent findings support that conclusion.

## 3. Central asset and rights reconstruction

The repository should treat the physical/economic Sun Park asset as a persistent object across time. It should separately map:

- title to LPB properties;
- Matkator and other extra-insolvency properties;
- the mortgage credit and every transfer/succession;
- Community/CEXP authority and voting/representation;
- possession, access and practical control;
- hotel operation, bookings, income and exploitation;
- valuations and project representations;
- works, licences and technical records;
- public incentives / RIC / financing;
- judicial and administrative decisions;
- institutional knowledge and notice;
- consequences for each economic interest.

Two proposed canonical top-level objects are:

- `SR-A001` — Sun Park / underlying asset.
- `SR-C001` — LPB mortgage credit.

## 4. High-value questions

The knowledge base should be able to answer, with sources and uncertainty visible:

- Who owned each relevant property at each material date?
- Who owned the mortgage credit economically, legally and procedurally at each stage?
- Which exact entity acquired the credit from the PH122/Cerberus perimeter?
- Which entity initiated, continued or benefited from enforcement?
- What documentation supported every substitution, assignment, succession or appearance?
- What authority existed for control, access, works, possession and exploitation before whole-title consolidation?
- What did the insolvency administrator, judges, prosecutors, public authorities, professional advisers, investors and counterparties know, and when?
- What did each judgment/order actually decide, and what did it not decide?
- Which facts are adverse to the project's thesis?
- What contemporaneous document could falsify or materially change a present conclusion?
- What happened to each economic interest after each decision?

## 6. Book thesis — Reason to Believe / Razón para creer

Working title: **Reason to Believe / Razón para creer**. The title remains dynamic and may change during development.

Core narrative proposition: Spain's rule-of-law institutions, tutela judicial efectiva and protection of citizens' property/economic interests can and should work better. The book explores whether a person can reconstruct a fragmented history and force institutions to confront the whole record even before the final outcome is known.

The book should not be a legal textbook or merely a corruption exposé. The evidence repository answers **what happened and how we know**. The book answers **what it was like to live through it, what the institutional experience reveals, and why there is still reason to believe change is possible**.

Preferred cover hierarchy developed in this thread:

- `REASON TO BELIEVE`
- `ONE ASSET. ONE HISTORY.`
- `GIL MARER`

Visual identity: one continuous line running through asset, documents and horizon, representing the unbroken history beneath institutional fragmentation. Same underlying artwork can serve English and Spanish editions, with edition-specific typography. Authentic Spanish documentary language may remain subtly visible in the English edition.

The book should be associated with Fundación Por Derecho as a publishing/educational project, without implying copyright, royalties, translation, audio, film or other rights have been assigned unless formally documented.

## 7. Fundación Por Derecho

Planned public-interest pathway. Intended mission includes legal literacy, access to justice, legal/institutional memory, research, education, methodology, academic/student participation, and responsible uses of AI for understanding complex legal records.

The Foundation is not to be presented as a route for financing private litigation or private recoveries. Donations/grants must remain distinct from investment in Havidia or private economic interests.

Governance work still required includes: funding transparency, independence, donor non-interference, conflicts, data rights, publication independence, corrections/right of reply, and relationship with Havidia/private interests.

## 8. Havidia LLC

Havidia is the intended US commercial/future-technology pathway. It should **not** be presented as having developed proprietary AI. Present value may lie in lawfully controlled corpus/data rights, methodology, structured knowledge, workflows, product design, integrations and future technology or services actually developed.

Potential commercial applications discussed include complex-case reconstruction, evidence intelligence, credit/title provenance, NPL diligence, institutional-memory audit and related high-value professional services. Commercial demand should be tested before major software engineering investment.

IP/data ownership must be documented before public claims that Havidia owns the corpus, methodology, book rights or technology.

## 9. American AI Justice

Working international/outreach proposition: **Can AI help justice remember?** The US-facing strategy seeks attention, compute/AI credits, legal-tech expertise, academic collaboration, journalists/podcasts, philanthropic support and commercial partners. It relies on American legal-tech/AI capability as a potential external source of tools and scrutiny, not on a claim that the project has already built proprietary technology.

## 10. Knowledge Project

The Knowledge Project is the bridge between the evidential case, Foundation, book and commercial future. Its mission is to turn a fragmented archive into an auditable, reusable knowledge base.

Public principles:

- AI is a tool, not a witness, judge or source.
- The underlying document is the evidence.
- Adverse evidence and alternative explanations are preserved.
- The objective is not maximal token consumption; it is verified reusable knowledge per token spent.
- A public site should show what is known, disputed, missing and corrected.

## 11. Audience architecture

The project is intended for multiple audiences without forcing all of them into a campaign identity:

- judges, prosecutors and public authorities;
- lawyers, insolvency and enforcement specialists;
- Canary Islands and Spanish administrative/legal audiences;
- students of law and public administration;
- academics in rule of law, integrity, corruption, public administration and legal technology;
- journalists and investigative media;
- international comparative-law audiences;
- US AI/legal-tech entrepreneurs and researchers;
- donors, philanthropists and grant-makers;
- investors/strategic partners where appropriate;
- readers of Reason to Believe;
- affected citizens who may recognise the fragmentation problem.

A visitor journey developed in this thread is: **Understand → Investigate → Challenge → Belong → Own/Support**. Trust must precede conversion.

## 12. Revenue/resource pathways

Keep distinct:

- publishing/media: book, audiobook, translations, documentary/rights;
- Fundación Por Derecho: donations, grants, academic/public-interest funding, compute support;
- Havidia: commercial pilots, partnerships, services, investment, future licensing/software;
- private economic interests: recovery, settlement, financing, investor diligence and asset/interest transactions.

Do not describe unrealised litigation/recovery claims as revenue. Do not conflate a book purchase, Foundation donation, Havidia investment or private recovery transaction.
