## Reason to Believe — controlling correction

The flagship must NOT be treated as a SunRockers/community book.

Canonical intellectual formula:

`ONE ASSET · ONE HISTORY · ONE MEMORY`  
`UN ACTIVO · UNA HISTORIA · UNA MEMORIA`

Controlling thesis: a citizen can live one continuous economic/factual controversy while the Spanish legal/institutional system divides it among proceedings, courts, prosecution, insolvency, enforcement, registries and public administrations. The book tests whether the rule of law — experienced principally through the Canary Islands — can remember the complete history, protect effective rights and correct legally relevant failure.

Sun Park / MYND Yaiza is the principal documented case study. The SunRockers human/community material belongs primarily to Book 3 and should appear in Book 1 only as a limited cross-reference explaining what the asset/place was and why it mattered.

The page language should preserve: `many proceedings should not mean many realities`; `remember → connect → hear → protect → review → correct`.

## Technical lessons preserved

1. A successful GitHub Pages build is not pixel-level visual verification.
2. Static HTML cover references and static Open Graph metadata are authoritative; do not rely on JavaScript cover substitution.
3. Cache-bust changed global assets when a stale browser can reproduce an old bug.
4. Do not force square artwork into portrait `object-fit: cover`; do not desaturate/darken approved covers; do not overlay duplicate titles on printed cover art.
5. Do not enlarge tiny/compressed web derivatives and call them high resolution. Preserve/use proper masters.
