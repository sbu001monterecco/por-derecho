## Primary-source findings
1. ACTÚA identifies Construcciones Acosta Matos S.A. as requester and treats the 262 registry properties as one functional unit.
2. The original report states actual situation/occupation is unknown; do not turn model assumptions into a factual zero-occupancy claim.
3. ACTÚA's own calculation applies 20 years physical age and 57.14% physical depreciation and selects approximately EUR9.776m as net-replacement value.
4. The 13 substantive photographs on pages 16–21 are mixed. Only three are clear apartment interiors and none is visibly identified by apartment, finca, block, owner or access provider.
5. This falsifies/weakens the prior strong Project formulation that the visible ACTÚA appendix was largely/wholly a negative Blocks4–5 photographic sample. The surviving issue is representativeness/extrapolation.
6. ACTÚA's 3 April counter-report says it could not access the interior of 100% of the complex and relied in part on general exterior images in concluding the complex was deficient.
7. ACTÚA says there were `at least 61` unrefurbished units `conforme se nos informa`: the number is now directly found in ACTÚA, but ACTÚA expressly attributes it to supplied information. Provider and exact schedule remain unknown.
8. ACTÚA expressly defends 57.14% depreciation for the TOTAL functional unit rather than finca-by-finca depreciation.
9. CAM formally filed the ACTÚA/GESVALT valuation-dispute package into the insolvency court record on 17 April 2018. This proves transmission and CAM's litigation position, not the merits or causal weight assigned by the AC/court.
