## 2. Central documentary question

RICPE's audited 2023 accounts distinguish:

- **Series F — Hotel MYND Yaiza:** 253 shares · **€1,598,849.32**.
- **Series G — MYND Yaiza Employment Creation:** 785 shares · **€4,974,853.78**.

The same controlled accounting record reports:

- Canary Islands Government idoneidad on **1 December 2022**;
- a first participatory financing/investment of **€1,598,849.32** in December 2022;
- hotel opening after reform/transformation on **16 December 2022**; and
- the later employment phase in 2023.

Therefore the unresolved public-file questions are:

1. Did **Decree 224/2022** and the **AEAT binding report of 17 November 2022** cover Series F, Series G or both?
2. Did another, amended, supplementary or parallel application/report/idoneidad exist?
3. If Series F was refurbishment/project financing at the point of opening after reform/transformation, what exactly did €1,598,849.32 finance: future works, unpaid invoices, prior eligible expenditure, permitted reimbursement, bridge financing, supplier liabilities or another category?
4. How were Series F/G reconciled with any other public aid, incentives, employment commitments or financing layers?

None of these questions is itself a finding of fraud, double financing, tax irregularity or illegality.

Article 15 requires the application to identify the project entities, describe the project/RIC eligibility, state the implementation period and describe the financial instruments; it also requires a binding AEAT report.
