### Evidential boundary of the decision

The decision establishes institutional consideration and a decision not to initiate fiscalisation. It does **not** establish:

- that the submitted evidence was true or false;
- that public funds or instruments were correctly or incorrectly used;
- that RIC treatment was correct;
- that there was or was not overlapping financing;
- civil title, insolvency validity or criminal responsibility;
- what documents were independently verified;
- the issue-by-issue competence partition;
- the complete content of the Área de Comunidad Autónoma report.

### Key procedural and competence analysis

The thread developed the following controlled propositions:

1. A private person cannot compel exercise of statutory fiscalisation initiative; the response therefore does not claim such a right.
2. Article 10 of the Audiencia's Regulation appears to provide a complaint-processing route involving Plenary treatment, appropriate verifications, an Area report and a final Plenary decision. The complete procedural record remains to be obtained.
3. The decision says **`la mayoría de los hechos`**, not all facts, were outside the Audiencia's remit. The unidentified remainder is a legitimate clarification question.
4. Public subsidies, credits, guarantees, aid, expenditure, eligible costs, payment, accounting, monitoring and public-control records must be distinguished from individual tax liability, insolvency validity, civil title and criminal responsibility.
5. `Judicializado` is the correct Spanish term. A pending judicial proceeding may justify coordination or avoidance of concrete duplication, but it is not automatically a substitute for external public-finance audit.
6. DP `1901/2026` was used only as a bounded example: the controlled July 2026 order concerned a Fiscalía report on admission; it did not itself audit subsidies, public credit, guarantees, eligible expenditure, sources and uses, certifications or public financial-control records.
