# One Knowledge Programme — 90-day execution / 12-month horizon

## Governing objective

Turn information already accumulated across Gmail, Drive, OneDrive, ChatGPT/library files, the website and legal/investment work into a **verified reusable knowledge asset** supporting legal strategy, the evidential site, Reason to Believe, Fundación Por Derecho, journalism/academia, US outreach, resource generation and appropriate commercial work.

**Do not spend significant additional tokens rediscovering information already possessed. Every major processing pass should leave behind persistent source-linked knowledge.**

## Operating flow

```text
RAW CORPUS
→ DEDUPLICATION + VERSION CONTROL
→ CANONICAL SOURCES
→ ATOMIC PROPOSITIONS
→ ENTITIES / EVENTS / PROCEEDINGS / ASSETS
→ TITLE / CREDIT / MONEY / KNOWLEDGE / DECISION / CONSEQUENCE LEDGERS
→ COUNTER-CASE + MISSING EVIDENCE
→ PUBLICATION FIREWALL
→ WEBSITE / BOOK / LEGAL / JOURNALISM / ACADEMIA / INVESTOR / FOUNDATION / COMMERCIAL OUTPUTS
```

## Days 1–14 — Evidence Factory

- establish canonical schema;
- instantiate `SR-A001` and `SR-C001`;
- canonicalise ~250 highest-value sources;
- build first 750–1,000 propositions;
- identify ~50 core entities;
- skeleton master chronology;
- missing-document register;
- adverse-evidence register.

Priority order: key court decisions, concurso, title/registry, credit transfers, Bankia/SAREB/PH122/CAM chain, valuations, dación/adjudication, Arrecife, Matkator, Community/CEXP, AEAT/Pink, key contemporaneous email, adverse decisions, active proceedings.

### Gate 1 — Day 14

Does the schema work in practice? Repair before mass ingestion if not.

## Days 15–30 — One Asset. One History

Build six rails:

- Follow the Title;
- Follow the Credit;
- Follow the Money;
- Follow the Control;
- Follow the Decisions;
- Follow the Knowledge.

For every material decision: **DECIDED / NOT DECIDED / EVIDENCE RELIED UPON / WHAT HAPPENED NEXT**.

Target by Day 30: ~500 canonical sources, 1,500+ propositions, 100 entities, all principal proceedings mapped, first complete master timeline, first credit-history graph and first judicial-decision graph.

### Gate 2 — Day 30

Can a new reader follow title, credit and decisions coherently from source-linked records?

## Days 31–45 — Public evidential interface

Public navigation should progressively expose:

- One Asset. One History.;
- Explore the History;
- Follow the Credit;
- Follow the Title;
- Follow the Money;
- Follow the Decisions;
- Who Knew What?;
- Test Our Case;
- What We Still Don't Know.

Use visible evidential statuses. No automated guilt/capture scoring.

### Gate 3 — Day 45

Does the interface increase credibility and comprehension rather than merely increasing volume?

## Days 31–60 — Knowledge Project

Develop the public section covering:

- corpus;
- AI transparency;
- evidence method;
- knowledge map;
- progress dashboard;
- staged token/resource challenge;
- research partners;
- separate support/build/invest pathways.

### Gate 4 — Day 60

Are Fundación Por Derecho and Havidia/private interests sufficiently separated in public description and governance planning?

### Days 1–30

- treatment;

- source map;

### Days 31–60

- each material factual passage linked internally to canonical propositions/sources.

### Days 61–90

- publishing/KDP positioning;
- Spanish/English production system;
- book landing page and founding-reader concept.

The book must contain the human experience and meaning that the website deliberately does not duplicate.

## Fundación Por Derecho — 90-day track

Develop mission and governance sufficient for a visitor to understand:

- why it exists;
- why the problem is larger than Sun Park;
- what research/education/public-interest activity it intends to support;
- what donations would support;
- what they would not support.

Governance documents to develop: independence charter, funding policy, private-benefit firewall, donor non-interference, Havidia relationship, correction/right-of-reply, data/privacy, conflict rules.

## Havidia — 90-day track

Create a rights/beneficiary register for book/media, methodology, data, structured knowledge, names/brands, future software and commercial services. Do not assume ownership before documentation.

Test near-term service propositions such as complex-case reconstruction, evidence/provenance intelligence, credit/title/NPL diligence and institutional-memory audit. Validate willingness to pay before major proprietary software development.

## American AI Justice / US outreach

Position as an international challenge: **Can AI help justice remember?**

Seek appropriate AI/compute credits, engineering, academic review, legal red-team input, journalism/podcast attention, philanthropy and commercial partnerships.

Initial outreach should prioritise a relatively small number of high-fit contacts rather than mass campaigning.

## Resource programme

- 10M-token proof phase;
- 25M cumulative expansion;
- 50M usable-system milestone;
- up to 100M only if measured residual gaps justify it.

At each gate measure yield before spending further.

## Weekly operating cadence

Every week should produce:

1. Evidence — canonical sources/propositions.
2. Publication — one meaningful site/depository improvement.
3. Narrative — book/public communication progress.
4. Resources — at least one funding/partner/commercial action.

Suggested scorecard:

```text
New canonical sources:
New verified propositions:
Contradictions resolved:
Open gaps:
Tokens/credits consumed:
Reusable knowledge generated:
Website modules improved:
Book chapters/words:
New supporters:
Partner conversations:
Revenue/donations (realised only):
```

## What not to do

Do not spend the programme generating dozens of new standalone “master reports”, repeatedly scanning entire corpora, publishing every old allegation, claiming proprietary AI, mixing Foundation/public-interest finance with private commercial/recovery finance, or treating token consumption as success.

**The bottleneck is conversion of accumulated intelligence into canonical, reusable, legally disciplined knowledge.**
