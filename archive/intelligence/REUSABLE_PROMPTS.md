# Reusable master prompts

These prompts preserve the reusable operating logic developed in the originating work. They are starting points, not substitutes for source verification.

## 1. Master corpus and token economics audit

### Objective

Determine as accurately as technically possible:

- how much information the project possesses;
- where it is stored;
- how much is duplicated;
- how much is genuinely unique;
- approximately how many language-model tokens the corpus represents;
- how many model-processing tokens are realistically required to transform it into a persistent, auditable knowledge system;
- what processing should not be repeated;
- the minimum economically efficient future AI budget;
- how structured knowledge can support publishing, donations/grants, commercial work, investment, partnerships or private transactions.

### Mandatory correction

Do not describe the project as having developed proprietary AI unless primary evidence establishes that. The project currently relies predominantly on ChatGPT and other third-party tools. Distinguish the underlying AI technology from lawfully controlled corpus/data, human expertise, prompts/workflows, methodology, chronology, classifications and structured knowledge.

### Sources to audit

Where technically accessible, count/sample:

1. Gmail;
2. Google Drive;
3. ChatGPT Library/conversation files;
4. OneDrive / SharePoint;
5. GitHub website/repository;
6. existing documentary depository/archive;
7. any additional connected storage discovered.

Do not double-count the same document because it appears as email attachment + Drive copy + Library artifact + repository copy.

### Inventory

For each source determine, where possible:

- total items/files/messages/attachments;
- total bytes/GB;
- date range;
- file-type distribution;
- text-bearing versus binary content;
- relevant versus unrelated corpus.

### Deduplication

Estimate separately:

- exact duplicates;
- near duplicates;
- version families;
- email/attachment duplication;
- Drive/Library duplication;
- AI-output duplication;
- signed/unsigned and draft/final versions;
- translations/localisations.

Report both **raw item count** and **estimated unique knowledge-bearing items**.

### Classification

Classify unique material into primary evidence, judicial, administrative, banking/NPL, corporate, accounting, property/registry, tax, tourism/planning, legal advice, correspondence, witness/personal account, analytical work product, AI-generated analysis, public sources/media, duplicate/low-value.

Assign processing priority P0–P5.

### Token estimation

Do not calculate tokens from GB alone. Prefer actual extracted character/word counts; otherwise use statistically defensible samples by file type. Report low/central/high estimates for raw corpus, unique corpus, P0/P1 corpus, email, documents and derivative/duplicate material.

### Processing scenarios

Calculate:

**A — Brute force:** every unique source read fully once. Upper baseline only.

**B — Hierarchical ingestion:** metadata → deduplicate → classify → extract facts/entities/dates/amounts → propositions → summary/holdings/limits → citations → canonical ledgers.

**C — Search-first intelligence:** retrieve only material required to resolve known propositions/gaps; persist the result so original sources are reread only when verification/new questions require it.

Recommend the most efficient scenario and explain assumptions.

### Existing-work audit

Search existing chronologies, reports, spreadsheets, forensic work, legal submissions, website pages, handovers and knowledge packs. Estimate how much is already extracted, structured, cross-linked, requires verification, or remains genuinely unprocessed. Do not spend tokens recreating existing work.

### Future token budget

Estimate requirements for:

- initial canonical ingestion;
- evidence verification;
- book research;
- website/depository population;
- continuous new-document ingestion;
- monthly intelligence updates;
- international/US research;
- investor/donor diligence;
- journalist/research packs.

Report ranges and confidence; estimate the reduction achievable through deduplication, caching, search and persistent summaries.

### Final dashboard

Report:

- raw files/messages;
- estimated unique sources;
- storage;
- raw and unique textual token equivalents;
- P0/P1 tokens;
- % already mapped;
- % remaining;
- brute-force versus optimised processing requirement;
- ongoing monthly requirement;
- top processing priorities;
- redundant processing areas;
- missing data sources;
- confidence level for each number.

Finish by answering: **How much knowledge do we actually have? How much is already structured? How much additional AI processing is realistically necessary? What is the cheapest reliable completion path? What resource request can be defended to a third party? What economic/public-interest outputs can the completed knowledge base support?**

Core KPI: **verified reusable knowledge per million tokens spent.**

## 2. One Asset / One History investigation prompt

Treat the asset as having one continuous physical, economic and documentary history despite fragmentation across proceedings and institutions.

For every material event ask:

- What exact asset/right existed immediately before the event?
- Where/how was it held and recorded?
- Who had title, authority, possession, economic benefit and information?
- Which document authorised or evidenced the transition?
- What changed immediately afterwards?
- What first-, second- and higher-order consequences followed?
- Which later decision-maker relied on the resulting state?
- Was an unresolved premise carried forward as if already settled?
- What evidence contradicts the preferred interpretation?
- What document would most strongly falsify or change the present conclusion?

Maintain throughout:

1. verified fact supported by primary/identified source;
2. evidence-based inference / working hypothesis;
3. matter requiring further evidence/reconciliation.

Separately attribute allegations. Never convert repetition into proof. For every court/administrative decision identify **what it decided, what it did not decide, evidence relied upon, appellate/current status, and downstream consequence**.

Build/update the Title, Credit, Money, Knowledge, Custody, Decision, Power, Consequence, Contradiction, Counter-case and Missing-document ledgers.

## 3. Public depository publication red-team prompt

Before publishing a page/proposition, test:

- precise source chain and pinpoint;
- evidential status;
- whether it is a fact, inference, party allegation or unresolved question;
- strongest contrary evidence/alternative explanation;
- current procedural/appellate status;
- whether naming an individual is necessary and proportionate;
- privacy, privilege, whistleblower and confidentiality restrictions;
- whether wording describes evidence/conduct rather than declaring unadjudicated culpability;
- whether the statement could be misunderstood as an official finding;
- what would require correction/update later;
- whether the public page links back to the underlying canonical source/proposition rather than becoming its own source.

Prefer legally disciplined formulations such as: “the record establishes X; it does not by itself establish Y”; “Gil Marer alleges…”; “this is an evidence-based inference requiring independent determination”; “the current public record does not resolve…”.

## 4. Reason to Believe cover-generation brief

Create a premium international nonfiction book cover for **REASON TO BELIEVE**, subtitle **ONE ASSET. ONE HISTORY.**, author **GIL MARER**.

Concept: one physical place carrying many conflicting versions of its history while the underlying evidence remains one continuous reality. Show a sophisticated Atlantic/Canary Islands coastal setting with a substantial white resort/hotel that is not copied from a specific real hotel. Embed extremely subtle documentary/evidential textures: architectural plans, registry geometry, ledger lines, dates, document edges and connecting traces.

The strongest visual device is **one continuous fine line** travelling through the asset, documents and toward the horizon, representing the unbroken factual history beneath changing ownership, credit, institutions, proceedings and narratives.

Avoid judges, gavels, handcuffs, money piles, red-string conspiracy boards, villains, flames, chess pieces or sensational corruption-thriller imagery. Avoid generic AI/technology aesthetics.

Mood: intelligent, calm, determined, credible, slightly haunting and ultimately hopeful. Prestigious investigative nonfiction / literary memoir. Strong Amazon-thumbnail legibility. The Spanish edition should use the same master artwork with translated typography; authentic Spanish documentary fragments may remain subtly visible in both editions.

Central emotional message: **Many versions were created. There was only one history.**
