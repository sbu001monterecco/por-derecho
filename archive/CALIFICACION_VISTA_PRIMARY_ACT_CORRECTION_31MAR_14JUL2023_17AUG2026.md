## 4. 14-Jul-2023 judicial act

The primary source is a **Providencia del Magistrado-Juez Alberto López Villarrubia dated 14-Jul-2023**, electronically signed by Alberto López Villarrubia on 14-Jul and by LAJ Águeda Reyes Almeida on 15-Jul. The copy carries `NOTIF. 18/07/23`.

Stable ID: `C36-JUD-2023-07-14-001`.

It **does not concern Gil Marer's own remote intervention**. It addresses a request that **LPB's lawyer Luis Miguel López Gómez** intervene in the 25/26-Jul Vista by Webex and refuses that request, requiring the lawyer to appear physically at the court.

This corrects earlier shorthand that described the 14-Jul act as denying Gil Marer remote participation.

## 5. Relationship between the two acts

The 31-Mar act expressly authorised Gil Marer's affected-person declaration by Webex. The 14-Jul Providencia refused Webex participation only for LPB counsel Luis Miguel López Gómez. There is therefore no contradiction between Gil appearing remotely in the supplied video and the 14-Jul Providencia.

Any repository text treating Gil's remote video appearance as something to reconcile with a 14-Jul refusal must be corrected.
