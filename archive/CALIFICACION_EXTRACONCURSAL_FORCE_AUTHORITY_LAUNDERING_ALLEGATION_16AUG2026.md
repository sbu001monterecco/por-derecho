## 1. Gil Marer's allegation — wording lock

`PARTY ALLEGATION / CRIMINAL CHARACTERISATION NOT ADJUDICATED`

Gil Marer alleges:

> **CAM and separately identified actors in the Acosta Matos / Community-security perimeter obtained and maintained control of Sun Park in 2018 illegally, fraudulently, without whole-hotel judicial authority and through force, exclusion, security and access mechanisms that were functionally clandestine or imposed against the existing possession/operation.**

Gil further alleges:

> **The decisive institutional wrong was not simply that a creditor entered before title. It was that authority belonging to the LPB insolvency process was converted into a practical bridge to control property, access, business and rights outside LPB's insolvency estate; that private control was then progressively normalised or re-described through AC, prosecutorial and judicial acts; and the resulting institutional narrative was later used against Gil/Pink in the calificación while the private-actor control/benefit side was not subjected to equivalent scrutiny.**

Gil calls that alleged mechanism:

> **concursal-authority laundering / circular self-legitimation** — a limited insolvency authority is invoked to enable or tolerate private material control; the resulting control is then treated as factual normality; later institutional acts rely on that normality and on each other; and those later acts are then capable of being cited as apparent validation of the original control.

This terminology describes **Gil Marer's allegation and analytical theory**. It is not a judicial finding that any actor committed fraud, usurpation, coercion, procedural fraud, disloyal administration or prevaricación.

## 2. Documentary facts that make the allegation materially testable

### 2.1 LPB was the insolvent debtor; the whole hotel was not

`DOCUMENTED / CONTROLLING PERIMETER`

Concurso 36/2012 concerned **Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. (LPB)** and its estate. It did not automatically absorb:

- the whole mixed-ownership Sun Park complex;
- Matkator S.L. property;
- property of other owners;
- every CEXP right;
- every Community power or common element;
- the hotel business/platform as an indivisible legal object merely because LPB was the majority owner.

Accordingly:

> **AC authority over LPB did not itself create authority to transfer, surrender, deliver, exclude, possess, operate or dispose of non-LPB property or third-party rights.**

This is the central extraconcursal boundary.

### 2.2 CAM was already a secured-credit holder before the control event

`DOCUMENTED`

The controlled credit chronology records:

- PH122 → CAM assignment route dated **20 October 2017**;
- Commercial Court order of **15 February 2018** recognising CAM as holder of the two relevant mortgage-credit amounts.

Credit status did not itself confer ownership or whole-hotel possession.

### 2.3 7 June 2018 is the controlled de facto material-control threshold

`EVIDENCE-BASED / CR-018 CONTROLLED`

Current repository control treats **7 June 2018** as a source-supported threshold of de facto material/access/operational control. Full practical possession of the hotel as an operating whole remains **Gil Marer's allegation/inference**, and exact finca-by-finca reach remains open.

The takeover dossier records acts including forced access, locks/chains/cylinders, security and exclusion. Those facts must remain separated actor by actor.

### 2.4 No reviewed whole-hotel possession order has been located for 7 June

`UNRESOLVED / HIGH-VALUE NEGATIVE CORPUS FINDING`

No reviewed judicial order delivering **whole-hotel legal possession** to CAM on or before 7 June 2018 has been located in the controlled corpus.

This does **not** prove that no authorising document can exist. It creates a finite production question:

> **Produce the exact order, deed, mandate, consent or other legally sufficient authority operative on 7 June 2018, identify its parties and asset perimeter, and explain how it authorised each act of access, exclusion, locks/security, possession, works or hotel-wide control.**

### 2.5 AC's 31 July 2018 judicial declaration is a critical knowledge/authority bridge

`DOCUMENTED SOURCE AS PRESENTED ON THE PUBLIC TAKEOVER DOSSIER`

The controlled takeover page records the AC's 31-Jul-2018 judicial declaration as follows:

- he denied ordering the breaking of the coded access;
- he admitted access;
- he acknowledged a forced door;
- he acknowledged a general authorisation to the Community;
- concerning broken locks used to execute the access, he said in substance that he **"lo da por bueno"**.

The same public dossier records that the AC had requested the Community security point while representing LPB and that his authority did not itself extend to CEXP or extra-concursal fincas.

This does not prove that the AC personally ordered every act or gave every physical key. It is materially stronger than a passive-knowledge theory: it is evidence supporting inquiry into **authorisation, ratification, scope and failure to restore**.

### 2.6 User allegation concerning keys

`PARTY ALLEGATION / SOURCE COMPLETION REQUIRED`

Gil Marer specifically alleges that the AC's acts/authorisations resulted in private actors obtaining the practical keys/access mechanisms for the hotel and that this went beyond any legitimate LPB-only insolvency authority.

Do **not** publish as a verified quotation or fact that the AC literally "handed CAM the keys" unless the original acta, testimony, security instruction, key ledger or witness evidence establishes that physical delivery. The stronger source-supported formulation is presently:

> **Gil alleges that the AC used or allowed LPB's concursal position and the Community-security mechanism to give CAM/perimeter actors effective access/key/security control over a hotel whose entire property and operation were not in the concurso.**

## 3. Civil-possession frame — why force/clandestinity matter independently of creditor status

The Spanish Civil Code provides a strong issue-identification frame:

- **Article 441:** possession cannot be acquired violently while an existing possessor opposes; a person asserting a right to deprive another of possession must seek assistance from the competent authority.
- **Article 444:** merely tolerated acts, and acts executed clandestinely and without the possessor's knowledge or with violence, do not affect possession.
- **Article 446:** a possessor is entitled to be respected and, if disturbed, to seek protection or restitution through procedural law.

Therefore the legal issue is not answered by saying `CAM was a creditor` or `the Community had security powers`.

The finite issue is:

> **Were the acts of forced access, exclusion, lock/security control and practical possession authorised by a legally sufficient title against each affected possessor/right-holder, or were private actors required to obtain judicial/competent-authority assistance rather than impose control materially?**

Gil's use of `clandestine`, `forcible`, `unauthorised` and `illegal possession` is a **legal/factual allegation to be tested against these predicates**.

## 4. Criminal hypotheses — element-by-element only

The project does not convert the 2023 offence catalogue into findings. The following are **finite hypotheses** generated by the documented control/perimeter problem.

### 4.1 CAM / private actors — alleged usurpation / coercion

`PARTY ALLEGATION / LEGAL HYPOTHESIS`

Potentially relevant Criminal Code provisions include:

- **Article 245:** occupation/usurpation of another's immovable property/right, including occupation without due authorisation in the statutory cases;
- **Article 172:** coercion where a person, without legitimate authority, uses violence to prevent another doing what the law permits or compels conduct against their will.

Proof questions:

1. Which exact property/right was possessed by whom immediately before the act?
2. Which actor entered, excluded, changed locks, instructed guards or controlled access?
3. What authority was invoked?
4. Was the affected possessor/owner/operator opposed or excluded?
5. Was there violence/intimidation or other coercive conduct within the statutory meaning?
6. What utility/benefit and damage followed?

### 4.2 Private/institutional chain — alleged procedural fraud

`PARTY ALLEGATION / HIGH THRESHOLD`

Criminal Code Article 250.1.7 defines procedural fraud around manipulation of evidence or analogous procedural fraud that causes judicial error and a prejudicial resolution.

Gil alleges a possible form of **institutional/procedural instrumentalisation** if evidence proves that:

- the limited LPB/Community authority was materially misrepresented as authority over the whole hotel or third-party rights;
- that mischaracterisation was put before a court/Fiscal or used in proceedings;
- contrary title/control facts were withheld, distorted or rendered legally invisible;
- the institutional decision-maker was thereby caused to adopt a materially false premise; and
- a prejudicial judicial result followed.

A wrong legal argument, adverse order or incomplete record is not enough. The statutory fraud/manipulation and causal judicial error must be proved.

### 4.3 AC — alleged administration desleal / excess of entrusted authority

`PARTY ALLEGATION / DP1956-TYPE HYPOTHESIS`

Post-2015 Criminal Code Article 252 is relevant only if evidence proves entrusted powers over another's patrimony, infringement/excess in those powers and patrimonial harm.

Gil alleges that the AC's role must be tested at two boundaries simultaneously:

1. **within LPB:** whether he protected rather than impaired LPB's estate, operation, rescue and claims;
2. **outside LPB:** whether he purported to authorise, ratify or lend institutional cover to acts affecting Matkator/third-party/CEXP/business rights he had no power to administer.

The second issue is not automatically Article 252 because the non-LPB asset may not be part of the patrimony entrusted to the AC. It may instead establish **overreach, participation, causation or evidence of misuse of office/authority** depending on the act and offence proved.

### 4.4 Judge — alleged prevaricación through knowing legitimisation

`EXPRESS PARTY ALLEGATION / ARTICLE 446 THRESHOLD`

Criminal Code Article 446 requires a judge or magistrate to knowingly issue an unjust judgment/resolution.

Gil's strongest allegation is **not** that failure to stop the 7-Jun event by itself proves prevaricación. It is:

> **If the certified record proves the Judge knew (a) only LPB was in concurso, (b) no whole-hotel possession had been judicially delivered to CAM, (c) material control had nevertheless been acquired through forced/security/access mechanisms, (d) third-party/extraconcursal rights were affected, and (e) the AC's limited authority was being relied upon as the bridge — then any later resolution or judicial finding that knowingly treated that private control as legitimate, irrelevant or attributable back against Gil without confronting those facts is alleged by Gil to form part of a knowingly unjust judicial mechanism.**

The element test remains:

`EXACT RESOLUTION → CONTRARY LAW/FACT → PROOF BEFORE JUDGE → REASONABLE EXPLANATION → KNOWING INJUSTICE → MATERIAL EFFECT`.

### 4.5 Fiscal — alleged circular institutional instrumentalisation

`PARTY ALLEGATION / NO AUTOMATIC OFFENCE LABEL`

The controlled record already documents:

1. Gil/Aweswell complained in **DI 248/2018** against the AC/CAM perimeter;
2. Fiscal Ricardo de Mosteyrín signed the adverse calificación opinion on **12 March 2019**, relying on the AC accusation package;
3. the later **7 May 2019 DI 248 archive** expressly invoked that `este Ministerio` had already treated the insolvency as culpable and Gil as responsible, including for alleged failure to collaborate with the AC.

Gil alleges this created a **circular self-legitimation loop**:

> `AC frame about Gil → Fiscal endorses AC frame → Fiscal institution uses its own endorsed frame when deciding Gil's complaint against AC/CAM → complaint against the source-side actors loses credibility because the complainant has already been institutionally characterised as culpable/non-cooperative`.

The documented sequence supports a serious **institutional anchoring/circularity/asymmetric-credibility** question. It does not prove personal collusion between the individual prosecutors, corrupt motive or a criminal offence.

Gil's stronger criminal-instrumentalisation allegation is that the Fiscal position helped transform a disputed concursal narrative into **institutional cover** for the private-control trajectory while failing to independently test the extraconcursal boundary and the source-side AC/CAM conduct. That allegation requires evidence of what each Fiscal personally had before them and what investigative duties/options applied.

→ `PRIVATE CONTROL CONTINUES / PROJECT, WORKS, COMMERCIALISATION AND LATER FINANCE DEVELOP`

→ `AC'S CALIFICACIÓN ACCUSATION SHIFTS BLAME TOWARD GIL/PINK`

→ `FISCAL ENDORSES THE AC ACCUSATION`

**Control rule:** each arrow is independently provable or disprovable. The chain is an allegation architecture, not proof of an agreement among all actors.

## 6. Why the hotel-not-in-concurso point changes the entire calificación analysis

The sentence **"LPB was in concurso; the whole hotel was not"** must be prominent in every relevant public and internal page.

### 6.1 It changes the authority question

The AC was not universal administrator of Sun Park. A power to act for LPB could not silently become a power to surrender third-party possession or hotel-wide access.

### 6.2 It changes the causation question

Once private actors controlled access/security/operation, post-event deterioration, lost revenue, failed rescue, inspection failure and inability to produce operational material cannot be attributed to Gil/LPB without a **capacity/control counterfactual**.

### 6.3 It changes the victim/offender narrative

Gil alleges that the calificación inverted the evidential lens:

- conduct of Gil/Pink was treated as the source of insolvency aggravation;
- the alleged forced displacement of control and the AC/private-actor authority bridge were not subjected to equivalent causal scrutiny;
- a person alleging dispossession/overreach was later characterised through non-collaboration and rent narratives that helped undermine his credibility when seeking investigation of the control event.

This is an **alleged victim/offender inversion**, not a finding that Gil committed no concursal breach or that every adverse finding is false.

### 6.4 It changes third-party culpability analysis

Former Ley Concursal Article 166 did not make every private actor an accomplice. But if an act grounding culpability was materially caused or enabled by a third party, the record must show whether third-party participation was tested and individualised rather than treated as invisible background.

### 6.5 It creates extraconcursal harm even if some concursal acts were lawful

A lawful act concerning LPB could still produce unlawful or compensable effects outside the estate if it was used beyond its perimeter.

Therefore:

> **The validity of a concursal act inside LPB does not automatically validate its extra-concursal projection onto Matkator, other owners, CEXP/business rights or the whole hotel operation.**

The physical hotel was an economic platform involving customers, bookings, staff, common areas, data, goodwill, operator proposals and the ONA/financed-exit route. Gil alleges forced control damaged or displaced this platform outside the narrow LPB estate analysis.

Audit direct foreign-investor/shareholder harm independently from LPB: rescue opportunity, financing, control, subsidiary value, costs and any cause of action belonging to Aweswell itself.

### Current fruits / benefit

Current MYND/HNT/CAM/RICPE/public-finance layers must remain entity-separated. The question is not to treat all current hotel value as damages; it is to trace **which current use, income, finance or value depends on which disputed asset/right/control bridge**.

## 12. Non-overstatement locks

- `LPB in concurso` **≠** `whole hotel in concurso`.
- AC authority over LPB **≠** authority over Matkator/other owners/the entire hotel business.
- creditor rights **≠** whole-hotel possession.
- Community security powers **≠** universal eviction/possession/title powers.
- forced door / locks / exclusion evidence **≠** automatic conviction for usurpation or coercion.
- no whole-hotel order located **≠** proof no authority document can exist.
- AC authorisation/ratification evidence **≠** proof he personally ordered every physical act.
- institutional circularity **≠** proof prosecutors personally colluded.
- judicial adoption of adverse findings **≠** Article 446 prevaricación without proof of knowing injustice.
- chronology + later benefit **≠** conspiracy.
