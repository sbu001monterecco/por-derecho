## 2. Evidence grammar

Each entry uses one or more of the following statuses:

- **VERIFIED PRIMARY DOCUMENT** — content read from the located ACTA/instrument itself;
- **CONTEMPORANEOUS THIRD-PARTY SOURCE** — independent contemporary publication, useful for what was publicly represented;
- **PARTY ALLEGATION** — a litigant/project position, not an adjudicated fact;
- **ADVERSE EVIDENCE / PROCEDURAL STATUS** — material that weakens or limits the project thesis and must remain visible;
- **EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE** — connection supported by multiple records but not itself a formal finding;
- **OPEN / UNRESOLVED** — primary evidence or reconciliation still required.

A later act or institutional response does not retroactively validate the originating ACTA. Conversely, a challenged ACTA is not automatically invalid, fabricated or criminal merely because it was challenged.
