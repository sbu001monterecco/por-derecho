## Controlling evidential position

### VERIFIED FACT / DIRECT SOURCE

The attachment Javier Romera sent on 20 January 2025 as `AUTO CONCURSO CULPOSO.pdf` is **Sentencia 163/2023, dated 28 September 2023**, in the calificación of **Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U., Concurso 36/2012**.

It is materially adverse. It must not be minimised. But the controlled correction is:

- it is a **sentencia**, not an auto;
- the debtor/subject is **LPB**, not the mixed-ownership Sun Park complex as a whole;
- the judgment itself provides an appeal route and ties the disqualification period to finality;
- preserved records establish appeals already filed before the January-2025 media exchange; and
- the judgment did not adjudicate the main Meeting Point/FTI commercialisation, mixed-ownership, Matkator, RIC/RICPE, public-incentive or FEDER questions raised in the proposed media investigation.

### EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE

The **physical transmitter remains unresolved**, but the response provenance is no longer properly described as a neutral “sender unknown” question.

The strongest controlled inference is:

> **The judgment was supplied or procured as part of the CAM/Acosta Matos response channel initiated by Laura.**

That inference rests on the sequence established before and at delivery:

1. a contemporaneous pre-delivery record attributed to Laura/CAM a request to wait until Monday while judicial material was supplied;
2. on 17 January Romera directly confirmed that he had been asked to wait until Monday for judicial orders;
3. the 19-January memorialisation, written before delivery and expressly tied to Romera WhatsApp audios, recorded the same promised-document sequence; and
4. on Monday 20 January Romera confirmed that the judicial document had arrived and linked it to the communicated decision not to publish.

### UNRESOLVED

Still unresolved are:

- who physically transmitted the attachment;
- the transmitting account;
- the exact accompanying message sent upstream to Romera;
- whether LPAM personally transmitted it;
- whether a lawyer, adviser, CAM representative, insolvency administrator or another intermediary transmitted it;
- whether any intermediary acted under a specific personal instruction from LPAM rather than on behalf of the CAM-side response generally;
- the exact authenticated words spoken by Laura/CAM and Meeting Point in the underlying calls;
- and what independent legal/editorial verification elEconomista performed before deciding not to publish.
