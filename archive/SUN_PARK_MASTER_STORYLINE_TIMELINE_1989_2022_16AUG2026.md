## 1. What this source is — and what it is not

The Annex is a **party-authored analytical presentation**, not a neutral court judgment, registry certificate, expert report or institutional finding. It is valuable because it preserves a dense, contemporaneous reconstruction of the project’s own understanding as of January 2023 and collates images, screenshots, extracts, dates, names, diagrams, public marketing material, court excerpts and a proposed causal narrative.

Use it for:

- **story architecture** — which events the project regarded as connected and why;

Do **not** use the Annex itself to establish as fact that any person committed `acoso inmobiliario`, `estafa`, `fraude procesal`, `usurpación`, `apropiación indebida`, `blanqueo`, `pacto comisorio`, `destrucción de pruebas`, `coacciones`, `prevaricación` or any other offence or unlawful act. ## 2. The reader-facing story in one minute

Sun Park began as a **single operating hotel/product built from legally separate units and common/operating infrastructure**. From the late 1980s through 2008 it was presented and operated as one hotel. After the 2008 ownership/restructuring phase, the ownership, exploitation, Community governance and cost-sharing structure became progressively contested. Gil's current root-cause position is that incomplete/frustrated performance by a seller/minority perimeter associated, in specific acts, with Montelanza, S.L. and Molina-linked owners began the causal chain. That is a party theory, not a collective breach or liability finding. The documented structural vulnerability lies in divided titles, voluntary CEXP participation and unresolved seller/unit completion. The Annex’s related thesis that minority/dissident owners ceased participating while continuing to use hotel infrastructure must be proved actor by actor and **must not be treated as the sole established cause of LPB’s insolvency**.

LPB entered **Concurso 36/2012** in June 2012, yet the hotel did not simply disappear as an economic unit: the Annex depicts Sun Park as continuing to operate through 2018, with a living guest/community proposition, press coverage, commercial agreements and a functioning hotel environment. That distinction matters. **The company was in concurso; the physical hotel and mixed-ownership operating platform remained an economic reality.**

A pre-existing Community/dissident apparatus concerning debt, voting, administration, keys, possession and litigation existed before Acosta Matos. The current repository now controls the stronger, more disciplined proposition that CAM did not simply arrive in 2022: the first specifically dated physical-incursion lead presently located is **2 October 2017 (Q4)**, followed by further access/project activity. CAM also became a defined-unit owner and acquired/claimed the privileged-credit position on a separate chronology. The exact assignment date is internally inconsistent in the Annex and remains a primary-source reconciliation point.

The decisive practical threshold is **7 June 2018**. The Annex devotes extensive visual material to the replacement of the access regime, private security, exclusion, condition changes and later works. Current repository control requires this to be expressed as **de facto material/access/operational control**, not an adjudicated transfer of whole-hotel legal possession. Gil Marer alleges that the cumulative effect amounted to full practical possession of Sun Park as an operating whole. No reviewed order granting CAM possession/title of the whole mixed-ownership hotel has been located.

From 2018 onward, the **same physical location and project imagery** appear through a sequence of public identities and commercial/investment propositions: **Sun Park → Hotel Lava Verde → Club Sei Lanzarote / Meeting Point–FTI → Sun Park as a RIC Private Equity project → MYND Yaiza / Hotel New Trend / Canarian Hospitality**. The Annex’s strongest narrative value is showing that these are not isolated stories: access/control, redevelopment, hotel marketing, investor presentation, title, financing and later operation occurred on overlapping timelines.

Formal adjudication/title concerning the LPB perimeter arrived only in **January–February 2022**, after years of alleged/de facto control, works, project presentation and commercial preparation. Later in 2022 the investor/prospectus identity changed from `Sun Park / Construcciones Acosta Matos` to `MYND Yaiza / Hotel New Trend`, while the hotel was prepared for reopening and public/tax-finance layers converged around the project. The public website should therefore explain **three different thresholds** rather than collapsing them: **control before title; project/commercialisation before adjudication; formal title/finance/operation later**.

The unresolved question is not merely “who owned Sun Park?” It is a chain of distinct questions: **who owned what; who controlled what in practice; who had authority to change access or works; who presented the hotel to operators/investors; what was disclosed about mixed ownership and the concurso; what public/private finance was approved and on what factual basis; what value, income, rights or remedies were lost or transferred; and which institutions had which evidence at which time.**

## 3. Core narrative spine for the website

Future public pages should be capable of reconnecting the following ten beats without turning chronology into proof of criminal causation.

### Beat 1 — A hotel can be one business while legally fragmented

**1989–2008.** The Annex depicts a 262-unit productive hotel structure: 220 apartments plus 40 commercial premises and 2 solariums, operated as one hotel/business despite unit-level ownership. See Annex pp. 3–11 and p. 123.

**Website point:** explain early that **unity of exploitation ≠ unity of title**. This single distinction makes later disputes over Comunidad, LPB, Matkator, other owners, operator, title and works much easier to understand.

### Beat 2 — The conflict is about governance, exploitation and cash flow before it is about liquidation

**2008–2012.** The Annex presents a split between participants and dissidents, competing exploitation arrangements, non-participation in common expenses and alleged parallel use of hotel infrastructure. Gil now locates his alleged originating private cause in incomplete/frustrated 2008 performance by an actor-specific Montelanza, S.L./Molina-linked seller/minority perimeter. The 22-Jun-2011 ACTA is the distinct debt/vote/authority matrix node; Bankia enforcement and the scheduled auction are the immediate documented 2012 trigger. These are separate causal layers, not substitutes for each other. See pp. 12–13 and Declarations 005–008.

**Control rule:** the Annex’s `boycott → negative results → concurso` chain is a **party causation thesis**, not an established judicial finding. The public insolvency page must separately test seller/finca obligations and defences, structural vulnerability, the June-2011 governance mechanism, bank debt, liquidity, asset values, swap/financing, owner interference, operating results, costs, Garrigues advice and the parties' own decisions. Gil authorises naming Garrigues and requests its testimony, but the first presently verified activity is 28-Mar-2012 and confidential/privileged source bodies remain review-controlled. His written self-attribution supports attribution of the text; it does not authenticate a voice.

### Beat 3 — Concurso did not mean the economic hotel disappeared

### Beat 10 — The current case is patrimonial and institutional, not merely historical

The Annex ends by connecting operating loss, investor finance, RIC mechanics, public statements and the reopened MYND Yaiza product. See pp. 128–135.

The current repository expands this into three distinct planes:

- **concursal** — LPB estate/value/income/payment/remedy;

- **cross-border/transnational** — Aweswell/foreign-investor value, capital, costs, financing and recovery.

Public finance also remains layered: **RICPE/private RIC capital → RIC fiscal/idoneity → regional incentives → FEDER → current exploitation**. Never infer double funding, subsidy fraud or EU-budget loss merely because multiple finance layers converge.
