**Scope:** 2008 ownership/exploitation architecture → disputed 2011 Community-authority rupture → pre-concurso eviction/operator litigation → 2012 concurso → debt/vote/office-holder continuity → 2016 institutional knowledge → 2018 material-control shift → 2019 downstream reliance/calificación → 2020–2022 finance/title/project formalisation → 2022+ operation, public funding/finance, institutional reliance and current harm.

## 1. Controlling proposition — do not fragment the case

Future Project Sun Rock analysis must not break the factual sequence into self-contained labels such as:

- `civil Community dispute`;
- `contractual operator dispute`;
- `desahucio`;
- `concurso`;
- `liquidation`;
- `security/access incident`;
- `creditor enforcement`;
- `calificación`;
- `RIC / fund / public-finance issue`;
- `title/adjudication`;
- `hotel reopening / MYND operation`;
- `present damages`.

Those may be separate legal or procedural tracks, but the repository must test whether they are **functionally connected parts of one authority–asset–control–value chain**.

The Project's unitary allegation is that an initially limited minority/non-participating perimeter may have **manufactured, assumed, captured, instrumentalised or perpetuated authority over the Community apparatus**, then used or benefited from that authority through documents, debt/voting mechanisms, litigation, access, possession, concursal processes, creditor relationships, project finance, title events and later operation. The allegation expressly includes the possibility of **criminal as well as civil, contractual, concursal, administrative and patrimonial consequences**.

This is a **PARTY ALLEGATION / INVESTIGATIVE MODEL**, not an adjudicated criminal finding. Every suspected criminal link requires actor-specific proof of act, authority, falsity or illegitimacy where alleged, knowledge/intent, use/adoption, benefit or consequence, causation and the relevant legal element.

The repository must therefore do two things simultaneously:

1. **refuse artificial fragmentation** where documents show functional continuity; and
2. **refuse guilt-by-association or chronology-only reasoning** where actor-specific proof is absent.

### View B — Top-down reverse engineering

Start with the present observable result and trace backwards:

`WHO OPERATES / CONTROLS / BENEFITS NOW?`

← `WHAT TITLE, CONTRACT, LICENCE, FUNDING, SECURITY OR COMMUNITY AUTHORITY IS INVOKED?`

← `WHAT COMMUNITY ACTA / DEBT / OFFICE-HOLDER / AC OR COURT AUTHORITY WAS INVOKED?`

← `DID IT DEPEND UPON THE DISPUTED 2011 AUTHORITY CHAIN?`

← `WHAT OWNERSHIP / PARTICIPATION / CONTRACTUAL BASIS EXISTED IN 2008–2011?`

Reverse engineering is mandatory because later formalities can otherwise visually or analytically wash backwards over earlier defects. **Later title, later investment, later operation, later licence, later judicial reliance or later institutional recognition do not by themselves prove that the earlier authority used to reach them was lawful.**

### View C — Actor / authority / knowledge matrix

For every material actor, reconstruct:

`ROLE / CAPACITY`

→ `SOURCE OF POWER OR DUTY`

→ `WHAT THEY KNEW, AND WHEN`

→ `DOCUMENT OR ACT THEY MADE / SIGNED / USED / ADOPTED / IGNORED`

Do not merge different actors merely because they benefited from the same outcome. Conversely, do not isolate actors into separate silos where evidence shows knowledge, adoption, instruction, certification, implementation or reliance across the same chain.

### View D — Offence / causation matrix

Where the Project alleges criminal significance, do not start with a crime label and fit facts into it. Reverse the process:

`EXACT DOCUMENT / ACT / OMISSION`

→ `WHO HAD LEGAL CAPACITY TO DO IT?`

→ `WHAT WAS REPRESENTED AS TRUE OR AUTHORISED?`

→ `WHAT PRIMARY MATERIAL SUPPORTS OR CONTRADICTS THAT REPRESENTATION?`

→ `WAS THE DEFECT MERELY CIVIL/FORMAL OR POTENTIALLY DELIBERATE?`

→ `WHAT EVIDENCE EXISTS OF KNOWLEDGE / INTENT / COORDINATION / CONCEALMENT / REPETITION AFTER NOTICE?`

→ `WHO USED OR ADOPTED THE RESULT?`

→ `WHAT PROPERTY, VOTE, OPERATOR RIGHT, POSSESSION, CREDIT, MONEY, SECURITY, PROCEDURAL POSITION OR PUBLIC/PRIVATE FUNDING CONSEQUENCE FOLLOWED?`

→ `WHAT INDEPENDENT CAUSAL LINK CAN BE PROVED?`

→ `WHAT OFFENCE OR OTHER LEGAL THEORY, IF ANY, IS THEN WORTH TESTING?`

This is the required prosecutorial direction of travel.

## 3. The unitary event spine

### 3.1 2008 — lawful baseline and structural vulnerability

The first question is not who later won a dispute. It is what the legal/economic structure actually was before the disputed rupture:

- fragmented unit title;
- participating/compliant majority ownership;
- non-participating or dissenting minority ownership;
- Comunidad de Propietarios function;
- Comunidad de Explotación / CEXP function;
- operator/exploitation contracts;
- allocation of maintenance, utilities and hotel-operating costs;
- notice, voting and representation rules;
- transfer/restructuring documents involving Monte Lanza / Multimatrix / LPB and the owner perimeter.

A later authority claim must be tested against this baseline. The baseline is not a presumption that every majority act was valid; it is the legal/evidential starting point.

### 3.2 2011 — disputed authority rupture

Treat 2011 as the first major control node, not merely an old ACTA.

The Project alleges a transition from:

`OWNERSHIP / PARTICIPATION / CONTRACTUAL AUTHORITY`

into a contested alternative:

`SELF-ASSERTED COMMUNITY AUTHORITY`

→ `OFFICE-HOLDER / ADMINISTRATOR STATUS`

→ `BOOKS / BANK / RECORDS / DOCUMENT CUSTODY`

→ `DEBT ATTRIBUTION`

→ `VOTE EXCLUSION / DENOMINATOR CONTROL`

→ `CERTIFICATION / REPRESENTATION`

→ `LITIGATION / ACCESS / LATER INSTITUTIONAL USE`.

The potentially penal significance is not “minority owners held a meeting.” It is whether any person **knowingly created, signed, certified, used or perpetuated a false or unauthorised institutional reality and used it to obtain consequences that the true ownership/contractual structure would not have permitted**.

### 3.3 2011–2012 — JV 1260/2011 as the first major downstream authority test

The pre-concurso eviction/operator proceeding must be read immediately after the 2011 authority rupture.

Documented layer:

- there was pre-concurso eviction/operator litigation;
- the 23-Feb-2012 source family records an existing action against the then operator and its expansion against Monterecco Sun Park after the exploitation/operator change;
- this predates the opening of Concurso 36/2012.

Unitary investigative question:

**Who claimed authority to terminate, evict, displace, replace or control the operator, and what valid ownership, Community, CEXP, contractual or delegated source gave that claimant the power to do so?**

The Project alleges that this proceeding may have been an early externalisation of the disputed 2011 authority apparatus. That link must be proved, not assumed.

The proceeding must therefore be reverse-engineered through:

- claimant identity;
- represented body;
- authority mandate;
- parent ACTA;
- owner/coefficient basis;
- contractual termination basis;
- alleged non-payment/use premise;
- classification of the hotel/unit use relied upon;
- court rulings on standing and merits;
- what the court did **not** decide;
- operational and cashflow consequences.

### 3.4 Incoming management / Monterecco / Pink — correction without self-disqualification

The incoming operating side's early good-faith use of Community/maintenance-type invoicing must remain visible.

The correct analytical treatment is:

- **admission:** the mechanism was used;
- **correction:** the Project now accepts that the mechanism was incorrect or legally conflated in the governance/exploitation structure;
- **context:** the practical problem of some owners occupying/renting/using units while consuming hotel/common services may still have existed;
- **non-convalidation:** the incoming operator's mistake does not retrospectively validate the disputed 2011 Community organ, office holders, debt certificates, vote exclusions or later acts;
- **investigative value:** later actors who rely on those invoices must identify the independent legal basis for their own authority rather than saying “the incoming operator did something similar.”

This admission strengthens the unitary analysis because it separates genuine operational confusion from alleged deliberate capture or instrumentalisation.

### 3.5 6 June 2012 — Concurso 36/2012 as a new legal layer, not a reset

The concurso must not erase what happened before it.

Always ask:

- what cashflow/solvency position existed immediately before filing;
- what part was attributable to banking/Bankia enforcement;
- what part, if any, was attributable to operator interference, fragmented exploitation or Community conflict;
- what assets were LPB's estate and what assets belonged to Matkator or third parties;
- what authority the AC actually acquired;
- what authority remained with owners, Community, operator/CEXP or third parties;
- which pre-concurso claims/documents the AC later accepted, rejected, used or failed to investigate;
- whether later concursal decisions inadvertently or knowingly amplified a disputed private-authority structure.

The penal/prosecutorial question is not “the concurso was criminal.” It is whether identifiable actors used, adopted, enabled or failed to confront specific allegedly false/unauthorised instruments or acts, with the knowledge and legal duty necessary for individual responsibility.

### 3.6 2016 — knowledge and persistence after warning

2016 is a knowledge/notice checkpoint.

For each relevant actor ask:

- what was communicated;
- what source was supplied;
- whether the Community authority/debt/operator issue was expressly raised;
- what response followed;
- whether the disputed chain was independently verified;
- whether the same documents or office holders continued to be accepted after notice;
- whether continued reliance after notice materially changes the inference available about knowledge or intent.

Do not convert receipt into agreement or knowledge into criminal intent. But do not treat repeated post-notice reliance as analytically identical to an innocent first encounter.

### 3.7 2018 — authority becomes physical control

The 7-Jun-2018 control shift is a convergence node.

The analysis must integrate:

- creditor/lender position;
- Community authority invoked;
- AC communications/authorisation;
- security company action;
- locks, keys, chains, access and exclusion;
- exact LPB / Matkator / third-party / common-element perimeter;
- ongoing hotel operation and revenue impact;
- criminal proceeding/archival context;
- what the court did and did not determine about title and authority.

Reverse-engineering question:

**What exact chain of documents and authority converted a minority/creditor/private-actor position into practical control of a mixed-ownership hotel before the later 2022 formal-title events?**

The Project's allegation of forcible, clandestine, fraudulent or criminal takeover remains an allegation unless independently established. The source-supported physical-control change must not be diluted merely because the criminal case was provisionally archived.

### 3.8 2019 — institutionalisation and Calificación

2019 must be tested as the point where private-authority products may have acquired institutional weight.

For each ACTA, debt certificate, Community assertion, AC report proposition, Fiscal submission or judicial conclusion ask:

`WHAT SOURCE WAS USED?`

→ `WHO CREATED IT?`

→ `WHAT AUTHORITY DID THAT CREATOR CLAIM?`

→ `WAS THE 2011 PARENT CHAIN TESTED?`

→ `WAS CONTRARY MATERIAL AVAILABLE?`

→ `WHAT PROPOSITION DID THE INSTITUTION ADOPT?`

→ `WHAT CONSEQUENCE FOLLOWED FOR LPB / GIL / PINK / ASSETS / LIQUIDATION / CALIFICACIÓN?`

The Project's stronger allegation is a possible **authority-laundering / reciprocal-validation mechanism**: a disputed private document or organ is treated institutionally as authoritative, and that institutional reliance is then cited back as proof that the private authority was valid. This is an investigative hypothesis. It must be broken into each adoption step and cannot be asserted merely from circular appearance.

### 3.9 2020–2021 — pre-title project, finance and representation

The same reverse-engineering method applies to RIC/RICPE, project finance, due diligence, ownership representations, valuation, public or private funding, and hotel redevelopment before formal title consolidation.

Ask:

- what was represented as owned, controlled, free of claims or available for redevelopment;
- on what date;
- by whom;
- to whom;
- what due-diligence source supported it;
- whether the representation depended upon the 2018 material-control position or disputed Community/concursal authority;
- whether contrary title, litigation, Matkator/third-party ownership or concurso material was known;
- what funds, approvals, benefits or project milestones followed.

Do not collapse legitimate financing and alleged misrepresentation into one claim. Prove the representation, knowledge, reliance and consequence separately.

### 3.10 2022 — formal-title thresholds are late and exact

Treat adjudication, dación, mortgage cancellation and CAM→HNT formalisation as separate legal events with exact finca/perimeter effects.

Core rule:

**2022 title may change rights from its legally effective date and within its exact perimeter; it does not automatically cure or validate disputed Community authority, possession, operator displacement, access, finance representations or acts performed in 2011–2021.**

The reverse-engineering question is whether any 2022 title event depended procedurally or economically upon earlier contested acts and, if so, what legal consequence follows. Dependency does not automatically invalidate title; it identifies the chain that must be tested.

### 3.11 Post-2022 → present — benefits, reliance, harm and recovery

The current position must be decomposed by level:

- LPB / PropCo asset loss or impairment;
- hotel OpCo/exploitation loss;
- Pink/Monterecco operational loss;
- Matkator/third-party property effects;
- Aweswell/UK/international platform value and lost opportunity;

Do not state a single global damages number unless the components are independently modelled and double counting is removed.

## 4. The prosecutorial chain-of-proof table

Every major suspected act must ultimately be capable of being reduced to this table:

| Element | Required question |
|---|---|
| **Actor** | Who personally or corporately acted? |
| **Capacity** | In what legal/professional/institutional capacity? |
| **Power/duty** | What source gave power or imposed a duty? |
| **Instrument** | What ACTA, certificate, invoice, pleading, email, order, deed, report, valuation, funding statement or omission is in issue? |
| **Representation** | What fact/authority/title/debt/status was asserted or implied? |
| **Objective source test** | What primary material supports or contradicts it? |
| **Knowledge** | What did the actor know, and when? |
| **Intent / consciousness** | What evidence, if any, supports mistake, recklessness, deliberate use, concealment or persistence after warning? |
| **Use/adoption** | Who relied upon, transmitted, certified, implemented or repeated it? |
| **Benefit / effect** | What vote, control, possession, credit, money, title, litigation position, funding, licence or commercial benefit followed? |
| **Causation** | Did this instrument materially cause the consequence? |
| **Counterfactual** | What likely happens if the disputed instrument/authority is removed? |
| **Legal significance** | Civil invalidity, concursal effect, administrative issue, professional breach, possible criminal theory, or none? |
| **Evidence status** | VERIFIED / PARTY ALLEGATION / EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE / OPEN / CORRECTED |

No actor should be publicly accused of a particular offence merely because several table cells look suspicious. The table exists to identify what still must be proved.

## 5. Bidirectional causation test

### Forward test

For each early act:

**If this act were legally/factually removed, which later outcomes would still independently occur?**

This prevents overclaiming causation.

### Reverse test

For each later outcome:

**What minimum earlier chain had to exist for this outcome to occur, and is each link independently lawful/proved?**

This prevents later formalisation from concealing the origin of control.

### Convergence test

Where multiple tracks converge on the same asset or result — Community, concurso, creditor, security, title, fund, public authority, operator — ask whether the convergence is:

1. independent and lawful coincidence;
2. lawful reliance on a valid common source;
3. negligent or uncritical reciprocal reliance;
4. coordinated adoption/use;
5. evidence of a common plan or deliberate instrumentalisation.

Only primary evidence and actor-specific knowledge can justify movement from categories 1–3 toward 4–5.

## 8. Counter-evidence and falsification discipline

A prosecutorial model must actively search for evidence that could defeat the Project's thesis.

For each key node, search for:

- a valid independent mandate;
- a properly convened majority;
- a contract expressly authorising termination or fees;
- a court ruling actually deciding the disputed authority issue;
- an independent source of possession/control;
- a lawful basis for debt/vote exclusion;
- actor evidence of mistake rather than intent;
- evidence that a later actor performed genuine independent due diligence;
- a lawful source of title/control that breaks the alleged dependency chain;
- evidence that the same economic outcome would have occurred without the disputed act.

If such evidence exists, it must narrow the allegation. **A model that cannot be falsified is not a reliable prosecutorial model.**
