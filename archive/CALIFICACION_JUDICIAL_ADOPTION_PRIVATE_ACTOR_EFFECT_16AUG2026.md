# CALIFICACIÓN — JUDICIAL ADOPTION → PRIVATE-ACTOR EFFECT / DP 1901 BRIDGE

**Concurso Ordinario 36/2012 · Sentencia 163/2023 · 16 August 2026**  
**Status:** `PUBLIC FIRST-PERSON ALLEGATION / JUDICIAL FINDINGS VERIFIED / PRIVATE-ACTOR INTENT-AND-BENEFIT BRIDGE OPEN`

## Purpose

Preserve the distinction between an allegation made by the Administrador Concursal and a proposition that Judge Alberto López Villarrubia expressly adopted as a finding in Sentencia 163/2023, and connect that distinction — without collapsing separate proceedings — to Gil Marer's allegation that the resulting judicial narrative operated in favour of the Acosta Matos private-actor perimeter.

This record supplements the calificación radical-transparency ledgers and the separate €3,032,010.34 fabrication/personal-gain allegation.

## 1. Judicial adoption is a separate accountability event

### DOCUMENTED — Fundamento Cuarto

Sentencia 163/2023 does not merely recite the insolvency administrator's rent/Pink theory. It expressly states as judicial findings that:

- Pink Canary Services operated Sun Park from February 2012 to November 2015 `en connivencia con LPB`;
- it did so `sin pagar renta alguna` to LPB;
- Gil Marer incurred at least gross negligence, if not intent, because of the alleged omission in pursuing rent;
- Pink incurred intent or at least gross negligence in failing to pay the stipulated rent; and
- the Court considered proved a causal nexus between the non-pursuit/non-payment of rent and aggravation of LPB's insolvency.

These propositions therefore ceased, for the purposes of the first-instance judgment, to be merely AC allegations: the Judge selected and adopted them as adjudicative findings and used the surviving culpability grounds in the operative judgment.

### DOCUMENTED — operative consequences

The same judgment declares LPB's insolvency culpable, declares Gil Marer an affected person, imposes ten years' disqualification from finality, declares Pink Canary Services an accomplice, removes specified creditor rights and imposes €3,032,010.34 damages against Gil plus the stated interest consequences. The judgment remains appealed.

## 2. Important internal `connivencia` tension — keep the modules distinct

In Fundamento Cuarto, the Court says it considers `connivencia con LPB` proved in the rent/operation branch.

Later, in Fundamento Sexto, while rejecting the separate alzamiento theory based on the frustrated execution against Pink and suspected English receipts, the Court says that the reference to that conduct having occurred with the debtor's `connivencia` is a mere allegation without evidential corroboration.

This is a serious internal coherence issue but must not be overstated as a word-for-word contradiction without qualification. The two passages arise in different legal modules and may concern a different temporal/conduct proposition. The correct public question is:

> **What exact facts, period and evidence made `connivencia` judicially proved in Fundamento Cuarto while a `connivencia` proposition in Fundamento Sexto was held uncorroborated, and what did each conclusion contribute to the operative outcome?**

## 3. Contrary record identified on appeal

`APPEAL ALLEGATION / SOURCE-CHECK REQUIRED`

The Pink/Patricia appeal attacks the statement that Pink never paid rent. It says rent was initially paid, that this appears in LPB's accounting, and that after the ERE Pink made partial payments and continued to bear maintenance/preservation costs. It also attacks the profitability presumption and the causal reasoning.

The appeal is not proof that those propositions are true. The decisive knowledge test is now finite: obtain the certified filed accounting/payment exhibits and establish what exact material was before the Judge before he signed the opposite factual finding.

If the certified docket shows actual rent payments and proves that the contrary material was before the Judge, that would materially strengthen Gil Marer's allegation of knowing judicial falsehood. If it does not, the allegation must be narrowed accordingly.

## 4. Gil Marer's private-actor-effect allegation

`PARTY ALLEGATION — GIL MARER`

Gil Marer alleges that the AC/Fiscal/Judge chain did not merely harm him and Pink inside the sixth section. He alleges that it **judicialised a materially false allocation of responsibility** that operated criminally in favour of private actors, including the Acosta Matos perimeter, by:

1. concentrating the causal narrative on Pink/Gil — no rent, connivencia, gross negligence/possible intent, causal aggravation;
2. converting selected private/AC propositions into signed judicial findings carrying institutional authority;
3. producing a portable adverse judgment capable of being invoked outside the calificación itself; and
4. thereby, he alleges, helping shift scrutiny, liability and economic prejudice toward Pink/Gil while private actors pursuing control, title, financing, operation and later benefit from Sun Park/MYND occupied a materially different evidential position.

This allegation is **not** recorded as a finding that CAM, HNT, MYND, RICPE, JDAM, LPAM or any other private actor procured the judgment, directed the Judge, paid any public actor, or committed a particular offence. Those propositions require actor-specific proof.
