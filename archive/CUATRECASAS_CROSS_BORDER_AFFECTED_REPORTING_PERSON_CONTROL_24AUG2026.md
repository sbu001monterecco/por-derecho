## 8. Remate, claim-assignment and single-satisfaction control

Do not collapse the following into one generic `sale`:

1. assignment of the invoice, pagaré, cambiario or enforcement claim;
2. cession of the remate or adjudication position to a third party;
3. adjudication to Cuatrecasas followed by a later transfer of the asset; and
4. any participation, recovery-sharing, nominee, referral, success-fee, recourse or side arrangement that allocates value or control without formally transferring the whole claim or asset.

The controlled ETJ record establishes a concrete procedural possibility, not a completed third-party transfer: Cuatrecasas's 10-February-2026 filing reiterated adjudication at 70% and expressly reserved cession of the remate to a third party. The latest located act, signed 28 July 2026, admitted Matkator's reposición against the 19-June order. No located record identifies an assignee or proves completed adjudication, cession, registration or possession.

Every future Billing/Collections/Risk draft must therefore request, for each contemplated or completed route:

- the exact right or asset transferred, date, instrument and procedural basis;
- the assignee/transferee category and identity to the extent lawfully disclosable, including whether it was a client, former client, prospective client, affiliate, referral source, co-investor, counterparty, connected person or wholly independent third party;
- the consideration paid or payable, funding route, source-of-funds/beneficial-owner control and every side agreement, rebate, indemnity, recourse or contingent amount;
- the date, scope and result of the former-client/conflict/confidentiality review and the institutional decision-maker, without demanding privileged advice;
- the date and scope of KYC/AML review **where legally applicable**, recognising the litigation-related limits and non-disclosure rules in Law 10/2010;
- every court appearance, decree, payment proof, Registry act, delivery of possession and later transfer;
- a negative certification if no assignee was contemplated, contacted, negotiated with, instructed, selected or used; and
- one single-satisfaction ledger applying every invoice, WIP amount, pagaré, VAT item, interest, cost, payment, set-off, EUR 77,840 adjudication credit, cession consideration, overprice, recovery and remaining claim exactly once.

The request may seek a non-privileged certification, chronology, document index and reason/category for withholding. It must not assert an entitlement to raw KYC files, privileged legal advice, protected third-party personal data or information whose disclosure is prohibited.

The currently effective wording of LEC article 647.3 addresses identification of cedent/cessionary, proof of a priced cession, debtor credit for the full remate/adjudication amount and treatment of overprice. Current counsel must determine the version and transitional rule applicable to each act in this 2024-origin auction.
