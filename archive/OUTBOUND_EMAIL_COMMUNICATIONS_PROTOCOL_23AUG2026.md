# Project Sun Rock / Por Derecho — Outbound Email Communications Protocol

**Control date:** 23 August 2026  
**Status:** canonical outbound-communications protocol  
**Scope:** continuing recipient-specific email outreach, supplements, corrections, follow-ups, preservation requests, media approaches and institutional/compliance communications  
**Controlling send gate:** `EMAIL_SEND_FINAL_AUTHORIZATION_RULE.md`

## 1. Purpose

This protocol governs how Project Sun Rock / Por Derecho outbound emails are researched, personalised, drafted, evidence-checked, translated, supplied with attachments and links, approved, sent, verified and followed up.

It does **not** itself authorise any email to be sent.

The repository, website and mailbox now contain a family of related communications to journalists, editors, public authorities, regulators, professional bodies, auditors, compliance functions, investors, transaction participants, companies, witnesses and other relevant recipients. These communications should share a recognisable evidential and narrative backbone while remaining genuinely tailored to the recipient's role, existing knowledge, prior correspondence and realistic ability to act.

The objective is **consistency without mechanical templating**.

## 3. Mandatory source scan before drafting

Before preparing an important new email or follow-up, inspect the current state in both directions:

`REPOSITORY / WEBSITE → RECENT SENT → RECENT RECEIVED → PRIOR THREAD → CURRENT CORRECTIONS → CURRENT DRAFT`

Do not prepare material correspondence solely from conversational memory or an older email.

At minimum determine:

- who the recipient is and why they are relevant;
- what role, competence or journalistic/professional interest makes the approach appropriate;
- what has already been sent to that person or institution;
- what they have replied, acknowledged, redirected, disputed or left unanswered;
- what evidence has changed since the last communication;
- whether a correction has superseded any earlier wording;
- what realistic action is now being requested; and
- which language, attachments and links are justified for this particular recipient.

Search for evidence adverse to the Project Sun Rock position as deliberately as supporting evidence.

For every proposed email, the Gmail portion of this scan is governed by `archive/PRE_SEND_GMAIL_PERSON_OUTLET_HISTORY_GATE_23AUG2026.md`. Search the entire accessible corpus independently for the named person and the outlet/employer/organisation; include names, variants, addresses, domains, relevant colleagues/fallbacks, all header roles, quoted/forwarded content, replies, bounces, redirects and other-channel notifications. Exhaust every continuation token and read relevant threads. Until both halves are complete and the collision is classified:

**SEND STATUS: BLOCKED — PERSON/ORGANISATION GMAIL HISTORY GATE INCOMPLETE.**

## 4. Communication-family model

Treat outbound correspondence as a **family of related communications with a common evidential spine and recipient-specific branches**.

Do not maintain one frozen master email and copy it mechanically to everyone.

For each recipient answer:

`WHO → WHY THIS PERSON → WHAT THEY CAN REALISTICALLY DO → WHAT THEY ALREADY KNOW → WHAT WE HAVE ALREADY SENT → WHAT HAS CHANGED → WHAT WE WANT FROM THEM NOW`

Classify the intended message as one of:

- first approach;
- evidential update;
- correction / supersession;
- follow-up;
- preservation request;
- request for comment / right of reply;
- institutional notification;
- compliance / regulatory supplement;
- witness / source approach;
- journalist / editor pitch;
- response to an inbound email; or
- escalation / re-routing after bounce, OOO or redirect.

The message's ask must follow from the recipient's actual role. Do not ask a journalist to perform a regulator's function or a regulator to behave like a newsroom.

Unless the recipient-specific subject requires a narrower treatment, the wider Project Sun Rock story may be explained through the following analytical spine:

**Sun Park's fragmented legal / ownership / exploitation structure**  
→ alleged hotel mobbing / `acoso hotelero` / interference and governance pressure  
→ financial and operational pressure  
→ credit / control transition  
→ material access, possession/control and works questions

→ Sun Park → MYND Yaiza  
→ regional incentives  
→ EU / FEDER-related funding or control questions where specifically sourced  
→ current exploitation, income and value  
→ accountability, restitution and recovery.

This is an **analytical narrative spine**, not a licence to state every link as proved causation.

Every material proposition must remain identifiable as:

- **VERIFIED FACT**;
- **PARTY / PROJECT ALLEGATION**;
- **EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE**;
- **UNRESOLVED QUESTION**; or
- **CORRECTED / SUPERSEDED**.

Never convert chronology, association, notice or downstream benefit into proof of conspiracy, fraud, intent, criminal participation or causation without the evidence required for that proposition.

## 6. Recipient-specific drafting skeleton

Adapt this skeleton rather than reproducing it verbatim.

### A. Why I am writing to you

Open with a short personalised explanation of why the recipient's role, publication history, institutional competence, previous involvement, custody position or documented relationship makes the matter relevant.

Avoid generic praise or flattery.

### B. What the matter is

Explain in two or three paragraphs why the issue is not merely an isolated dispute or merely Concurso 36/2012.

Use the public-interest, institutional, regulatory, evidential, professional or journalistic frame appropriate to the recipient.

Preserve the distinction between LPB's concursal perimeter, hotel exploitation, Matkator / third-party property, Community / CEXP structures and other legal persons.

### C. Why it matters now

Identify what has materially changed, for example:

- a newly located primary source;
- a correction;
- direct commercial evidence;
- a new filing or proceeding;
- an institutional acknowledgment or response;
- a newly authenticated attachment;
- current investment / funding / incentive information;
- a new public dossier; or
- a contradiction now capable of independent verification.

Do not manufacture urgency merely to increase response rates.

### D. Strongest evidential anchors

Prefer three to five independently checkable anchors over a catalogue of accusations.

For each significant source apply:

`WHAT IT PROVES → WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVE → WHY IT MATTERS`

Where a graphic or source map is used, make clear that it is a visual index / explanatory derivative and does not itself prove every relationship depicted.

### E. Recipient-specific question / ask

End with a finite, realistic request, for example:

- whether a journalist would examine the documentation;
- preservation of specified records;
- confirmation of receipt;
- identification of the competent office;
- explanation of a documented representation;
- comment / right of reply;
- reconciliation of two identified records; or
- confirmation whether a named document entered a supervisory, professional or compliance file.

### F. Source access

Where appropriate include:

- the Por Derecho / Project Sun Rock website;
- the most relevant exact dossier, not only the homepage;
- the controlled San Telmo / RICPE webinar location;
- relevant primary public sources; and
- any recipient-specific evidence page.

### G. Standing and closing

Where relevant and accurate, identify the sender as a directly affected / interested party and, where legally supportable in the particular context, as an `alertador` / reporting person.

Do not overstate statutory status, legal protection or representative capacity.

## 7. Language protocol

### Spanish

Use Spanish as the normal default for:

- Canary Islands and Spanish journalists;
- Spanish authorities and courts where email communication is appropriate;
- Spanish professional bodies;
- Spanish companies, investors and compliance teams; and
- recipients whose established correspondence is Spanish.

The Spanish should read as professional native Spanish rather than literal English syntax.

### English

Use English where it is the recipient's normal working language, the recipient is international, the established thread is in English, or an international investor / media / professional audience is being addressed.

### German

Use German for German authorities, German media and German professional/compliance recipients where it materially improves comprehension and seriousness.

Where continuity or a mixed recipient set justifies bilingual treatment, an approved architecture may be:

**German original → complete Spanish translation**

or another language order justified by the actual recipient set.

Do not create sentence-by-sentence hybrid emails.

### Translation parity

No translated version may become materially stronger or weaker than the controlling source-language version without an explicit, justified reason.

Names, dates, legal status, allegations, evidential caveats and asks must remain equivalent across language versions.

## 8. Attachment protocol

Every proposed email with attachments must have an explicit **Attachment Manifest** before final approval.

For each attachment record:

`filename → language → source/type → reason for inclusion → evidence status → version/date → size if known → primary source or explanatory derivative`

Do not attach a file merely because it appeared in another message.

### Reusable visual/source family

Where relevant and current, consider the appropriate language version of:

- PwC 2016 knowledge-point / source map;
- Sun Park / RICPE / San Telmo role/source map;
- controlled webinar/source presentation; and
- any currently verified recipient-specific visual.

These are explanatory source maps / visual indexes, not independent proof of every relationship shown.

### Journalists

For a first approach, prefer a compact, high-value package over a document dump. Normally use the smallest set that allows independent understanding and verification.

### Authorities / regulators / professional bodies

Prioritise the actual filing, signed decision, receipt, official document or primary source necessary for the requested action.

### Auditors / compliance / custodians

Prioritise records relevant to knowledge, preservation, instructions, conflicts, due diligence and custody.

### Witnesses / factual sources

Avoid argumentative bundles that may lead or contaminate recollection. Seek their own records, recollection and preservation first.

## 9. Link Manifest

Before final approval list every outbound link separately and verify:

- exact destination;
- language;
- relevance;
- whether the page is currently public;
- whether the page has inherited current corrections; and
- whether the link resolves.

Classify each link as:

- **WEBSITE HUB**;
- **SPECIFIC DOSSIER**;
- **PRIMARY PUBLIC SOURCE**;
- **VIDEO / WEBINAR**; or
- **INSTITUTIONAL SOURCE**.

A material change to the link package after approval resets approval.

## 10. Evidence and legal-publication discipline

Before finalising an important email:

- search for contradictory or exculpatory evidence;
- disclose material adverse judgments and their current appeal/finality status where relevant;
- preserve alternative explanations;
- preserve source limitations and provenance gaps;
- distinguish absence from the located corpus from proof that no response/action existed;
- distinguish receipt from examination;
- distinguish referral/routing from merits determination;
- distinguish later benefit from causation; and
- distinguish institutional knowledge from personal knowledge.

Do not present `FEDER fraud`, `fraudulent RIC materialisation`, `collusion`, `conspiracy`, `criminal takeover`, judicial prevarication or equivalent as established fact unless a competent source establishes the proposition.

Where the repository supports a serious allegation, it may be stated firmly **as an allegation**, with its evidential status and strongest contrary explanation preserved.

## 11. Version and correction control

Treat every outbound communication as a versioned evidential object.

Maintain internally where practical:

- `COMMUNICATION_ID`;
- recipient / role;
- topic;
- language;
- message type;
- version;
- draft date;
- source cutoff;
- attachment manifest;
- link manifest;
- approval status;
- Gmail draft ID where created;
- sent message ID where sent;
- response status; and
- next action.

If a material factual correction is discovered:

1. update the canonical correction/source controls first where appropriate;
2. identify earlier recipients materially affected by the error;
3. prepare a targeted correction / supersession;
4. do not silently rewrite communication history; and
5. do not send the correction without a new final-authorisation checkpoint.

A correction should state precisely what was wrong, what replaces it and what remains unchanged.

## 12. Mandatory email state machine

The normal operating states are:

**READ / RESEARCH ONLY**  
→ **PREPARED**  
→ **PERSON + ORGANISATION GMAIL HISTORY GATE PASSED**
→ **GMAIL DRAFT CREATED**  
→ **DRAFT READ-BACK VERIFIED**  
→ **EXACT OUTBOUND PACKAGE PRESENTED TO USER**  
→ **USER FINAL APPROVAL**  
→ **EXACT APPROVED DRAFT SENT**  
→ **SENT COPY READ-BACK VERIFIED**  
→ **RESPONSE MONITORING / FOLLOW-UP**

Until **USER FINAL APPROVAL**, never invoke a send, resend, forward or self-email action.

Preparation uses draft operations only.

Where a Gmail draft has been reviewed and approved, prefer sending that **exact reviewed draft by its draft ID** rather than reconstructing the email through a new send action.

If anything material changes after approval—To, Cc, Bcc, subject, body, attachment, attachment version or link—return to the approval stage.

## 14. Post-send verification

Immediately after an authorised send, read the actual sent message and verify it against the approved package.

Check:

- SENT status;
- sender;
- every To / Cc / Bcc recipient;
- exact subject;
- substantive body;
- required website links;
- required source / video / webinar links;
- exact attachment count;
- exact filenames;
- attachment language / version; and
- message/thread identifier.

Do not state “sent correctly” merely because a send operation returned without an obvious error.

If the sent package differs materially from the approved package, **do not automatically send a correction**. Report the discrepancy and wait for instructions.

A failed or uncertain send remains **NOT VERIFIED / NOT SENT AS INTENDED** until resolved.

## 15. Incoming-response protocol

Classify every new inbound message relevant to an outbound campaign as one of:

- **SUBSTANTIVE RESPONSE**;
- **ACKNOWLEDGMENT / TICKET CREATED**;
- **REQUEST FOR INFORMATION**;
- **REQUEST FOR CORRECTION**;
- **OUT OF OFFICE**;
- **ALTERNATIVE CONTACT / REDIRECT**;
- **DELIVERY FAILURE / BOUNCE**;
- **LEGAL / PRESERVATION RESPONSE**;
- **HOSTILE OR DISPUTED RESPONSE**; or
- **NO ACTION REQUIRED**.

Then act according to that class.

An OOO containing an alternative address is **not authority to resend or forward**.

A ticket acknowledgment should normally preserve the established thread/reference for later supplements.

A bounce requires address verification before any proposed new send.

A substantive reply requires reading the full thread and updating recipient-specific context before drafting a response.

## 16. Recipient-list discipline

Large CC lists must not substitute for recipient analysis.

Before including each addressee, identify why that person or mailbox belongs in **To**, **Cc** or **Bcc**, and whether inclusion creates unnecessary disclosure, confidentiality, privilege, harassment, reputational or data-protection risk.

Do not infer knowledge, responsibility, agreement or association merely because somebody was copied on a prior email.

Where a recipient should be excluded—temporarily or permanently—record the reason in the working package so the exclusion is not accidentally reversed by reuse of an older recipient list.

## 17. Continuous campaign intelligence

Do not maintain a frozen “master email.” Maintain a **master communication architecture plus current evidence state**.

Before every significant outreach:

`LATEST REPOSITORY`  
`+ LATEST WEBSITE`  
`+ LATEST SENT EMAILS`  
`+ LATEST RECEIVED EMAILS`  
`+ RECIPIENT HISTORY`  
`+ CURRENT CORRECTIONS`  
`= CURRENT PERSONALISED DRAFT`

The common story should become more coherent as evidence develops, while each recipient receives only the material reasonably relevant to their role.

## 18. Privacy, privilege and repository boundary

The public repository may contain this protocol, public-safe communication architecture, sanitised event records and public-source references.

Do **not** publish to public GitHub merely for convenience:

- private email addresses that are not already appropriately public;
- private email bodies;
- privileged legal communications;
- confidential source details;
- native Gmail message identifiers if they create unnecessary exposure;
- private attachment locators;
- personal phone numbers;
- signatures / identity documents;
- restricted evidence; or
- material whose publication would breach an applicable privacy, professional or legal restriction.

The mailbox and controlled evidence systems remain the source of truth for private correspondence.

**consistent without being templated;**  
**personalised without drifting factually;**  
**strong without overstating proof;**
