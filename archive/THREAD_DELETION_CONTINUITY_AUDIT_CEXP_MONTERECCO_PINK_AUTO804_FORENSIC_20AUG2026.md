## 4. Controlling public-safe findings and corrections

### 4.1 CEXP provenance is not proved exclusivity

Among the reviewed materials, CEXP has the strongest located collective, participating-owner-derived foundational provenance: a participating-owner resolution and a direct Montelanza, S.L.–CEXP transfer are both located. That does **not** establish lawful exclusivity, a universal mandate, registry status, authority over every unit/common area or uninterrupted operation.

### 4.2 The 1 February 2012 instrument and real Pink operation must be confronted

A signed CEXP–Monterecco notice dated 1 February 2012 says the activity was transferred/subrogated and asks counterparties to treat Monterecco as the new operator. Monterecco/Pink also had a real 159-unit/477-bed administrative and operational position. The sustainable gap is the competent CEXP/owners-body authority for that act and the scope it could convey, especially common areas and third-party units.

Monterecco Sun Park, S.L. and Pink Canary Services, S.L. are the same Spanish legal person after a name change, not successor Spanish companies. That person is separate from the UK Monterecco/Aweswell company.

### 4.3 Order 804/2018 does not prove total abandonment or operator title

Order 804/2018 is final as an appellate order affirming provisional dismissal of a criminal investigation. It is not a civil, tourism or tax trial finding the lawful operator. Its conditional compression of a May 2017 email cannot safely be presented as a final finding that Pink totally abandoned the complex or only wound down old reservations.

The original May 2017 record distinguishes cessation of new commercialisation from continuing existing stays, supplies, services, maintenance, preservation and transition before formal handover. The order is also adverse evidence: it did not recognise CEXP's asserted possession at that time. Both aspects must be disclosed.

### 4.4 No proved Bankia or Garrigues direction

Severe financial, enforcement, liquidity, insolvency and governance pressure is documented. The reviewed record does not prove that Bankia or Garrigues ordered the incorporation or use of Monterecco/Pink. Crisis reliance, incomplete facts and a mistaken or unproved perimeter assumption are stronger sustainable propositions than express coercion.

### 4.5 Credit/product separation remains an investigated allegation

The mortgage-credit position travelled through SAREB and Promontoria into the CAM perimeter; the disputed financial-product claim remained against CaixaBank in Valencia PO 1859/2023. No located ruling establishes that this separation was unlawful or criminal. Intent, legal accessory status, assignment scope, notice, defence effects and harm remain evidence targets.

The primary quantified 12 July 2016 notice to the insolvency administrator is located and is the specific pre-2018 notice hinge for disputed Community authority, accounting, debt and voting issues. Native burofax, annex and receipt reconciliation remains partial and must not be overstated.
