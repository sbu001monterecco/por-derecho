## 1. Controlled conclusion

The located record supports examining whether the 2013 Fiscalía querella adopted an affirmative criminal frame materially supplied by the administrador concursal (AC) against the incoming Gil Marer / Patricia Domínguez perimeter. That frame used an economic, Community and receivable architecture formed partly during 2008–2011, before their assumption of control, while directing criminal suspicion toward the 6 February 2012 operating arrangement and alleged non-payment/non-collection through September 2013.

Gil alleges that this was an inversion of causation and a misuse of the criminal route. The documentary record makes that allegation serious and testable. It does not presently establish that the fiscal knowingly filed a false querella, acted under private capture or committed a criminal offence.

It would be inaccurate to state that every operative allegation concerned conduct before Gil and Patricia arrived. The source itself centres important branches on 2012–2013 conduct. The correct audit is therefore **actor-specific and temporal**, not a blanket pre-arrival defence.

## 3. Transformation audit: source uncertainty → accusation

The AC's reproduced 18 September 2013 complaint used qualified propositions, including that payment was not known to him, that the UK/Spanish corporate relationship was presumed, that the receivable had not been verified and that prejudice would exist *if* the alleged circumstances were so. The 27 December querella converted that material into an official accusatory act and asked the court to investigate it.

That conversion is not unlawful merely because it occurred: a querella may present a hypothesis and request evidence. It does create finite verification questions:

1. Which AC propositions were independently checked during DI 273/2013?
2. Which annexes supported the corporate-control assertion and Patricia's precise capacity?
3. Was absence of knowledge of payment treated as evidence of non-payment?
4. Was the EUR 737,338.85 receivable reconciled with Community participation, source ledgers and later treatment?
5. What evidence existed before the low-rent suspicion was advanced, given that the querella itself requested a market-rent expert?
6. Which facts were attributed to Gil personally, Patricia personally, LPB, the UK company and the Spanish operator?

## 10. Legal characterization control

- The 2013 querella is an official accusatory act, not a judgment and not proof of its factual hypotheses.
- Objective treatment can be audited under the Ministerio Fiscal's constitutional/statutory functions without asserting a crime by an individual fiscal.
- Criminal attribution to a fiscal requires actor-specific conduct, knowledge/intent, applicable offence elements and evidence. Institutional persistence or an evidential error does not automatically establish prevaricación, falsedad, coacción or corrupt capture.
- Article 36.2 of Law 2/2023 entered into force in 2023. It does not retroactively convert the 2013 querella into a prohibited reprisal. Any post-entry act must be assessed separately for protected-informant status, qualifying communication/public disclosure, disadvantage and causal connection.
