### Inference

- The statements may have informed the replacement assessment issued after April 2022. The complete reliance trail is not yet controlled.

- Pink may argue that assistance, maintenance, reservations, guest attention, payments or contracts in its name do not automatically establish legal substitution of the Operating Community or comprehensive taxable operation of the whole complex.
- That argument requires period-, unit-, function-, account- and income-specific proof. The diligence alone does not resolve it.
