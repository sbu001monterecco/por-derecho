Gil's allegation is preserved at full attributed strength subject to these source controls:

1. **Entity:** the exact vehicle is **Promontoria Holding 122 B.V. (PH122)**. `Promontoria Holding 1112`, `Promotoria`, `PH11` and similar variants are not used for this chain unless a different primary source establishes a separate entity.
2. **Capacity:** Haya is a servicer/interlocutor, not a proved holder. Cerberus is context unless a primary ownership, control, mandate, instruction or ratification bridge is proved.
3. **Two lanes:** `Caja Insular→BFA→Bankia→CaixaBank` is the corporate/product-liability lane; `Bankia→SAREB→PH122→CAM` is the mortgage-asset lane.
4. **EH 90/2012:** the located initiating pleading is the second-facility claim for €857,373.81 plus €257,000 provisional; it does not prove that both loans were independently enforced or fully recovered there.
5. **Protocol 870:** €839,441 + €109,512.11 + €329,564.92 equals €1,278,518.03 exactly. The €30 question is the later movement from €839,441 to €839,411, not an internal Protocol 870 arithmetic defect.
6. **Protocol 457:** its six printed components total €13,065,186.68. The further €102,895.34 is explained by the source-supported movement from €3,079,104.66 default interest to the €3,182,000 mortgage cap, yielding €13,168,082.02. The legal-function and day-by-day bridges remain open.
7. **Separate assets:** Protocol 457 implements the 159-finca mortgaged-apartment dación. The €400,000 route concerns a separate 31-finca locales/pools branch and must not be added mechanically to the deed consideration or used as proof of EH satisfaction.
8. **NPL economics:** a discounted NPL transfer or recovery above transfer price is not itself fraud or unjust enrichment.
9. **Notarial proof:** an authentic deed can accurately record disputed or false party statements without proving instrument alteration or notarial guilt.
10. **Criminal proof:** civil, banking, accounting, concursal, enforcement, notarial or Registry error is not automatically criminal conduct.
11. **Historical criminal-law boundary:** legal-person criminal liability began on 23-Dec-2010 and exists only for offences for which the Code expressly provides it. CP 130.2 preserves or transfers an otherwise applicable legal-person responsibility in its listed corporate events; it does not retroactively criminalise the 2008 or May-2010 conduct, transfer a natural person's guilt or intent, or allocate ordinary civil/product liability. The exact CP 250.1.7, 390/392/393 and pre-/post-1-Jul-2015 CP 259–261 elements remain actor-, document-, purpose- and date-specific legal tests under the canonical control.
