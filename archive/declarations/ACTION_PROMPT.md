# Standard cross-thread action prompt — Por Derecho declarations

Use this instruction in any thread where a first-person account, testimony, voice-message account, contemporaneous recollection or document-led personal explanation should become a permanent Por Derecho declaration.

## Executable instruction

**Turn the account above into a formal Statement of Truth / Declaración de Veracidad for the permanent Por Derecho evidence repository. Treat this as an execution task, not merely a drafting task.**

1. **Recover the complete source first.** Use the current conversation, connected files/library, email or repository material as needed. Do not rely on a partial summary when the underlying account, transcript, audio-derived working transcript, declaration, email or attachment is available.

2. **Preserve the declarant’s first-person account faithfully.** Do not add facts merely because they fit the theory of the case. Where the declarant is a private person whose identity is reserved in the public repository, preserve the legal identity in the private/native evidence record but use the approved public descriptor in GitHub and website text.

3. **Apply the public privacy rule before naming the file.** Read `../ANONYMISATION_AND_LPAM_NAMING_CONTROL_17AUG2026.md`. For the witness from Gil Marer's personal and business perimeter, use `Witness from Gil Marer's perimeter — identity reserved` / `Testigo del perímetro de Gil Marer — identidad reservada`. Do not expose the legal name in a public filename or index merely for narrative convenience.

4. **Do not confuse the anonymised witness with Laura Patricia Acosta Matos (LPAM).** LPAM is a different person and is not anonymised when material. On first relevant reference use `Laura Patricia Acosta Matos (LPAM)`; thereafter `LPAM` or the full name. Never use `Patricia` alone as ambiguous public shorthand.

5. **A separate legal adviser is role-described publicly.** Where the relevant LPAM source chain refers to the other private person previously identified by first name, describe that person as `one of Gil Marer's legal advisers at the relevant time` / `un asesor jurídico de Gil Marer en aquel momento`.

6. **Classify every material proposition by evidential level**, making clear whether it is:
   - **(a) personal knowledge / direct observation or recollection**;
   - **(b) a statement attributed to a third person** — record that the declarant remembers hearing it, without treating the truth of the third party’s statement as personally proved;
   - **(c) knowledge derived from identified documents**;
   - **(d) reasonable inference, concern or working hypothesis**;
   - **(e) a matter requiring verification**.

7. **Do not strengthen allegations beyond the evidence.** Do not silently convert suspicion into fact, corporate conduct into personal conduct, an entity into an individual, later benefit into earlier knowledge, or absence of evidence into evidence of absence.

8. **Do not weaken a material first-person recollection merely because it is serious.** Preserve what the declarant actually says, but attach the correct evidential limitation and identify the independent records or witnesses that could confirm or refute it.

9. **If any material passage cannot responsibly be stated as true at the level in which it is expressed, mark it visibly as `REQUIERE ACLARACIÓN` or restate it at the correct evidential level.** Do not invent certainty and do not stop the entire task because one point remains unresolved.

10. **Use dates correctly.** If converting a new account into a declaration, date the declaration today. If archiving an already dated declaration or contemporaneous statement, preserve its original declaration date and record today separately as the archive date.

11. **Make the declaration actionable.** After the factual statement, add an **“Apéndice operativo — acciones derivadas”** when useful. Translate the declaration into finite actions: documents to preserve or obtain; custodians/date ranges; people to interview separately; registers/metadata/accounting; contradictory or exculpatory explanations; and objective criteria capable of confirming or disproving each hypothesis. The appendix must not convert hypotheses into facts.

12. **Signature is not a prerequisite for use.** A declaration may be archived and used as a dated repository statement, evidence map, preservation trigger, investigative lead, citation or supporting exhibit even when unsigned. State that status transparently. If a signed, ratified or institutionally filed version later becomes available, preserve it as the authenticated controlling copy in the appropriate private/evidence layer while retaining the public derivative for provenance.

13. **Add it to GitHub, not just to the chat.** Work in `sbu001monterecco/por-derecho` and:
    - read `archive/declarations/README.md`, `INDEX.md`, `STATEMENT_TEMPLATE.md` and the anonymity control;
    - inspect the actual existing declaration numbers;
    - allocate the **next sequential archive number** rather than assuming one;
    - create a privacy-compliant public filename (for an anonymised Gil-perimeter witness: `archive/declarations/NNN_WITNESS_GIL_PERIMETER_TEMA_AAAAMMDD.md`);
    - update `archive/declarations/INDEX.md` in the same change;
    - preserve hashes and REGAGE/RedSARA references where available without unnecessarily exposing private filenames/identifiers;
    - never overwrite or silently rewrite a material prior declaration: supplement or version it transparently.

14. **Use the protected-branch workflow.** Create or refresh a dedicated branch from current `main`, commit the declaration and index/rule changes, open a pull request, review the diff for evidential overstatement and privacy leakage, and merge when repository permissions and checks permit. After merge, verify the declaration and `INDEX.md` on `main`.

15. **Return an execution report.** State declaration number and public filename; public declarant descriptor and subject; whether it is signed, unsigned, ratified or presented; any `REQUIERE ACLARACIÓN` items; hashes or filing references preserved; PR number/merge status; and the next concrete evidential actions.

16. **Apply the voice-to-text protocol whenever an oral source is involved.** Read `archive/declarations/VOICE_TO_TEXT_STATEMENT_OF_FACT_AND_TRUTH_PROTOCOL_25AUG2026.md`; record V0–V4 source/version state and S0–S4 speaker-attribution grade; keep speaker boundaries, corrections and missing segments visible; and run the applicable clarification queue. Text attribution is not voice authentication, and one person cannot ratify another person's words.

17. **Run a named-person/entity OSINT check before linking actors.** Apply `archive/OPEN_SOURCE_INTELLIGENCE_NAMED_PERSON_ENTITY_PROTOCOL_25AUG2026.md`; use exact names, dated capacities and direct sources; search contrary material and homonyms; distinguish verified, alleged, inferred and not-located relationships; and never transfer knowledge, intention or liability through association.

18. **Keep private mailboxes and source bodies out of public GitHub.** Use opaque source IDs and aggregate counts only where material. Full mailbox acquisition requires the account holder's authorisation and the custody workflow; do not request a password, publish a private address or treat a mirrored mailbox subset as complete.

## Non-negotiable integrity rule

**The declaration must be useful without pretending to be stronger than it is.** Preserve serious testimony faithfully, preserve uncertainty faithfully, make both actionable, and do not confuse public anonymisation with alteration of the underlying evidence.
