# Gil Marer statement of fact and truth — ONA funded exit

**Date of adoption:** 26 August 2026  
**Declarant:** Gil Marer  
**Matter:** Sun Park / Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. / Concurso 36/2012  
**Declaration ID:** `PD-DECL-GM-ONA-20260826-01`

## Adoption

I, **Gil Marer**, adopt the following as my statement of fact and truth, to the best of my present knowledge, recollection and belief and subject to correction by the native documents and certified court record.

I state that ONA, acting in the Sun Park transaction through the relevant ONA/Clubotel legal-person perimeter, was not introduced as an unrelated rival buyer seeking to displace Luchy Playa Blanca or our stakeholder group. ONA's proposed operating and acquisition role was part of a wider coordinated alliance designed to obtain the funds and procedural route necessary to pay or consign the recognised insolvency liabilities, conclude Concurso 36/2012, return LPB to ordinary corporate capacity and restore professional, unitary operation of the hotel.

The ONA letter of intent, business-plan work and transaction development existed before 7 June 2018. By 6 June 2018 the Clubotel/ONA hotel-industry lease and its suspensive architecture had been signed. Carlos Sanz, our co-adviser through Pledge Investments, had already approached the Insolvency Administrator in April 2018 for the offer conditions, debt/bid information and timing. The AC did answer, but did not provide the substantive information sought and instead directed the interested transaction perimeter to personarse and proceed through the insolvency court.

I state that the events of 7 June 2018 did not occur before an embryonic or imaginary project. They occurred against a developed ONA-led operating, acquisition, due-diligence, valuation, financing and security package. I allege that the material-control takeover and the conduct of the adverse private perimeter impaired the hotel, access, information, income, possession and security assumptions on which that package depended.

The work did not simply stop after 7 June. Daniel Irigoyen took the integrated exit route to the judge and the Insolvency Administrator in June 2018. In September 2018 Carlos Sanz, Daniel Irigoyen, Cuatrecasas, Juan Tomás Parrilla and our side coordinated the work necessary to fix the debt figure and implement an exit. Daniel reported that the judge had been told that financing was approved to leave the concurso by payment or consignation to all creditors. The contemporaneous drafting record also shows that ONA was within the strategy even though a subtle reference to ONA was removed from one proposed filing.

Clubotel La Dorada later sought formal personación through Daniel Irigoyen. I regard that effort as the procedural continuation of the same alliance: ONA/Clubotel needed standing and information to help convert the operating/acquisition role into an executable exit. I understand that the formal pleading described Clubotel's acquisition interest; I do not claim that the pleading, standing alone, expressly promised that Clubotel itself would pay the texto definitivo. The wider creditor-payment purpose is established by the combined transaction and exit correspondence.

## My criminal allegation

I allege that the funded exit, the hotel business and the underlying security package were knowingly frustrated or diverted through a continuum of acts and omissions by the adverse private actors and through the active or enabling conduct of the Insolvency Administrator and the Magistrate-Judge.

My allegation against the private perimeter is that parallel community, creditor, possession and operating control was used to interfere with the project, take or retain material control, degrade the asset and its income/security package and position the Acosta Matos perimeter and associated interests to obtain later benefit.

My allegation against **Francisco de Borja Rodríguez-Batllori Laffitte**, as Insolvency Administrator, is not limited to rejecting a commercial proposal. I allege that after he had been approached and then repeatedly informed of a developed exit route, he failed or refused to produce or procure the transparent debt and procedural outputs needed for payment, failed to protect the hotel and funded-exit security perimeter, and later opposed Clubotel's access/personación in a way that assisted the neutralisation of the exit.

My allegation against **Alberto López Villarrubia**, as Magistrate-Judge, is that he had notice of the ONA-led package and later of financing said to be approved for all-creditor payment or consignation, but did not provide effective protection or a reproducible procedural route, did not preserve the asset and security package and allowed the adverse control structure to prevail. I allege that those omissions and acts require criminal investigation together with the private-actor and AC conduct.

I make these allegations separately against each person. I do not transfer knowledge, intent or responsibility between people merely because of a family, corporate, professional or institutional relationship. The evidence must establish for each person the act or omission, capacity, knowledge, transaction dependency affected, causal effect, intent if alleged, benefit and contrary explanation.
