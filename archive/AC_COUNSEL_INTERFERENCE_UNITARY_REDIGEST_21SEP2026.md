## Unitary pattern — and its limit

The evidence supports a **recurrent-pressure / continuity-interference hypothesis**, not a universal “every lawyer was forced out” conclusion:

1. direct mandate termination and Bar threat (Cristo);
2. client-lawyer fee/payment and intermediation during a loss-of-trust transition (Parrilla);
3. potential personal criminal exposure linked to the central defence pleading (Joaquín / procurator / Alas environment);
4. contact and “peace” access to incoming counsel before protective actions were complete (Jiménez);
5. Administrator-controlled dependencies affecting standing, information, money and implementation across later mandates.

The mechanisms can be connected for prosecutorial examination because they occur in the same Concurso and around the same estate/control conflict. **Responsibility, intent and causation do not transfer from one episode or professional to another.**
