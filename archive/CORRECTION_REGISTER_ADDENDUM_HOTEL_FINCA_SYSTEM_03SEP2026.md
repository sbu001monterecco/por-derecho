# CORRECTION REGISTER ADDENDUM — HOTEL FINCA SYSTEM / TITLE-ON-TITLE INJURY

**Addendum ID:** `PD-CR-ADD-HOTEL-FINCA-20260903`  
**Date:** 3 September 2026  
**Status:** controlling addendum; read with `archive/CORRECTION_REGISTER.md` and the earlier 8584 addendum

## CR-HFS-001 — scope

**Old/incomplete:** finca 8584 treated as the complete asset perimeter.  
**Corrected:** 8584 is the immediate ETJ object and worked example. The asset-classification rule applies at minimum to both source-locked Matkator titles, 8584 and 8588, to any later-identified Matkator title, and title-by-title to the entire hotel denominator.

## CR-HFS-002 — asset class

**Old/incomplete:** each finca treated as a freestanding apartment or ordinary parcel.  
**Corrected:** each finca is a legally distinct title compartment carrying exclusive, common-element, quota, governance, owner-authority, possession, revenue, evidence and remedy dimensions inside a regulated unitary hotel operation.

## CR-HFS-003 — hotel unity

**Old/incomplete:** unitary hotel operation used either to collapse all titles into one owner or to deny all cross-title consequences.  
**Corrected:** operational unity does not merge ownership; separate ownership does not make each title an autonomous hotel business or erase a common economic/remedial event.

## CR-HFS-004 — acquisitions and “consent”

**Old/incomplete:** every deeded acquisition treated as legally and evidentially closed, or every Acosta acquisition treated as automatically unlawful.  
**Corrected:** classify each transfer as verified consensual, consensual but disputed as to validity/disclosure/authority/pressure, alleged non-consensual integration, judicial disposition, corporate/productive-unit representation, unresolved, or common/operational asset. A valid acquisition may still matter to cumulative control; relevance is not criminality.

## CR-HFS-005 — title-on-title injury

**Old/incomplete:** “finca on finca violence” left undefined or read as a statutory offence.  
**Corrected:** use the controlled analytical term “title-on-title injury / lesión jurídica título-sobre-título” for the alleged deployment of title, common-element, governance, operator, procedural, Registry or regulatory control to deprive another title of identity, access, utility, revenue, voice or remedy. It is not a standalone penal type.

## CR-HFS-006 — perimeter injury

**Old/incomplete:** injury confined to Matkator and the immediate title.  
**Corrected:** map direct Matkator injury and derivative/interconnected injury to Aweswell, LPB, Concurso 36/2012, other owners, creditors, the hotel productive system and regulatory/public-finance records, without collapsing legal persons or estates.

## CR-HFS-007 — claimant’s estafa-procesal position

**Old/incomplete:** the repository described only possible future criminal relevance or stated that no completed estafa was alleged.  
**Corrected:** the claimant maintains an alleged completed estafa procesal in the La Laguna Cuatrecasas/Matkator proceedings. Classification is `PARTY_POSITION / ALLEGED_COMPLETED_OFFENCE`, not an adjudicated finding. Public language must preserve both halves.

## CR-HFS-008 — conspiracy / enablement / concealment

**Old/incomplete:** generic references to conspiracy, enablement or concealment without legal role separation.  
**Corrected:** particularise alleged agreement and resolution for conspiracy; translate enablement into authorship, inducement, necessary cooperation or complicity; reserve encubrimiento for qualifying post-offence conduct by a non-participant; and identify any autonomous concealment/regularisation act separately.

## CR-HFS-009 — La Laguna / Las Palmas bridge

**Old/incomplete:** either merge the proceedings or treat them as unrelated.  
**Corrected:** preserve separate identities while mapping the alleged instrumental chain from La Laguna monetary/enforcement acts to title disposition, impaired restitution, continued hotel-control benefit, and possible weakening or normalisation of evidence, claims, value and recovery in Concurso 36/2012.

## CR-HFS-010 — “fraude concursal”

**Old/incomplete:** omnibus label without candidate offence or act.  
**Corrected:** identify the exact alleged concursal procedural fraud, insolvency offence, document offence, obstruction, misappropriation, disloyal administration, prevaricación or other route, actor by actor and act by act.

## CR-HFS-011 — denominator discipline

**Old/incomplete:** extrapolation from 8584 treated as proof about every hotel title.  
**Corrected:** distinguish a class-level proposition from evidence for 8584, evidence for 8588, evidence for a defined subset and a hotel-wide hypothesis requiring the complete title denominator.

## CR-HFS-012 — governing sequence

All successor work must use:

`title classification → physical/operational mapping → consent/authority → money/benefit → procedural act → injury/remedy → cross-module propagation`.
