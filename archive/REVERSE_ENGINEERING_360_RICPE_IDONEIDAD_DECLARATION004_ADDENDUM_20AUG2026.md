## 1. New evidence-status rule

The 360° method must distinguish **the thing that creates a bridge** from **a person's recollection or understanding of that bridge**.

Therefore:

> **A source-derived witness declaration may prove or support knowledge, recollection, notice, chronology or the provenance of a question. It does not replace the primary legal, administrative, banking, accounting or technical record that creates or proves the underlying bridge.**

This rule applies equally to evidence favourable and adverse to Por Derecho.

## 6. Strongest lawful reconciliation scenarios

The 360° reconstruction must preserve at least these scenarios until the primary file resolves them:

1. **Single-file scenario:** one idoneidad process and one final act lawfully covered both phases/instruments.
2. **Separate-authorisation scenario:** Series F and Series G were covered by separate applications, reports, resolutions or other competent acts.
3. **Amendment/extension scenario:** the original process was later modified, enlarged, corrected or supplemented.
4. **Prior-cost/reimbursement scenario:** Series F lawfully reimbursed eligible expenditure already incurred or discharged outstanding supplier/bridge obligations.
5. **Alternative-documentary-basis scenario:** another lawful documentary basis explains the sequence and use of funds.

The fact that a scenario is possible does not prove it. The fact that the public file is incomplete does not disprove it.

## 7. Criminal-forensic threshold

The 360° record may investigate whether later formalisation or financing relied on incomplete or inaccurate premises. It may not jump from timing discomfort to criminality.

For any criminal theory, prove separately:

`exact proposition → exact actor → knowledge/intent → act/use → recipient → dependency/error → benefit → prejudice → causation → contrary evidence`.

A witness's concern, recollection or question is not a substitute for any of those elements.
