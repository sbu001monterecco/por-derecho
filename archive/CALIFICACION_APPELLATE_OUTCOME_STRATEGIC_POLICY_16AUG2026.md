This policy is a **decision rule for strategy, accountability and future investigation**. It must never substitute for the exact legal effect of the appellate judgment.

## 2. Binary strategic rule

### Bucket A — CLEAN EXONERATION

The Audiencia Provincial is excluded from Gil's adverse institutional-accountability perimeter only if its judgment produces a substantively clean exoneration.

For this policy, `clean exoneration` means an appellate result that removes the materially adverse culpability case against Gil rather than merely narrowing, redistributing or re-labelling it. Expected indicators include:

- reversal of the materially adverse culpable-classification findings attributable to Gil;
- removal of the personal/patrimonial consequences based on those findings;
- no preservation through alternative reasoning of materially equivalent factual or causal blame;
- no ambiguous formulation that grants relief while retaining the adverse narrative as institutional fact;
- no fragmentation leaving the central documentary contradiction materially unresolved; and
- a disposition/reasoning combination fairly describable as substantive exoneration rather than procedural or quantum relief alone.

A clean exoneration would not erase historical questions concerning the lower court, AC, Fiscalía or private actors, but it would materially change the strategic treatment of the Audiencia Provincial itself.

### Bucket B — ALL OTHER OUTCOMES

For Gil's strategic purposes, **every outcome short of clean exoneration is treated as insufficient and institutionally adverse**.

This includes:

- full confirmation of Sentencia 163/2023;
- partial confirmation / partial reversal;
- reduction of damages, disqualification or other consequences while materially adverse findings remain;
- reversal of some grounds while one or more core culpability/causation findings survive;
- procedural nullity or remand without substantive exoneration;
- a mixed result that allows all sides to claim partial success;
- fragmentation that avoids confronting the central contradiction as a whole;
- compartmentalised reasoning that leaves actor-specific knowledge, causation or material inconsistencies unresolved; or
- formally favourable language that leaves a materially equivalent adverse institutional narrative intact.

Gil may describe such a result internally as **strategically equivalent to ratification** of the lower-court adverse frame.

### Mandatory wording control

`Strategically equivalent to ratification` does **not** mean that the Audiencia Provincial legally affirmed every finding, rationale or consequence below.

Every future analysis must separately state:

1. what the AP legally affirmed;
2. what it reversed;
3. what it modified;
4. what it annulled or remitted;
5. what it did not decide; and
6. how Gil treats the result strategically.

Do not say `the AP ratified the lower court` if the operative judgment is mixed, partial, remittal-based or otherwise legally different from full affirmance.

## 3. Consequence for the AP accountability perimeter

### Bucket A

The AP is not treated as an adverse institutional actor in the Calificación outcome chain, absent separate evidence unrelated to the merits/outcome itself.

The judgment becomes corrective evidence relevant to the lower-court judgment, the AC/Fiscal accusation package, damages caused before correction, and institutions/private actors that relied on Sentencia 163/2023.

### Bucket B

Gil intends to continue the dispute and is prepared to scrutinise the Audiencia Provincial itself within every legally competent route potentially available.

This does **not** create an automatic civil, criminal, disciplinary or regulatory allegation against the appellate judges.

Before the AP or a member of the panel is included in a specific liability allegation, the same evidence-first threshold applies:

`exact appellate proposition → contrary primary evidence/law → proof it was procedurally before the court → reasonable alternative explanation → material effect → applicable jurisdiction/remedy → actor-specific knowledge or other required mental element where relevant`.

An adverse or unsatisfactory appellate judgment is not, by itself, evidence of prevaricación, corruption, collusion, regulatory breach or civil liability.

The strategic decision is that a mixed/partial/fragmented result will **not be treated as institutional closure or vindication**.

## 4. Civil, criminal, regulatory and further-remedy separation

Gil's preparedness to continue on civil, criminal and regulatory grounds must remain jurisdictionally separated.

For every proposed action after the AP judgment, future work must independently determine:

### Further judicial remedies

- what further remedy is legally available under the law applicable on the date of decision;
- which grounds survive and which deadlines apply; and
- what parts of the AP judgment are final, modified, remitted or still challengeable.

The binary strategic classification must never cause loss of a useful partial legal victory or a procedural deadline.

### Criminal

- identify the exact proposition/conduct alleged to meet an offence;
- prove the required knowledge/intent rather than disagreement, error or adverse interpretation; and
- identify the competent body for the particular actor and conduct.

### Civil / patrimonial

- identify the legally recognised cause of action or state-liability route;
- identify actionable act, damage, causal bridge and any finality/exhaustion requirement; and
- separate losses attributable to first instance, appeal, private-actor use and other acts.

### Regulatory / institutional

- identify the competent regulator/governance body for the actor and subject matter;
- distinguish professional, court-functioning, judicial-discipline, public-funding, securities/investor-disclosure and other remits; and
- do not use a regulator as a substitute appellate court.

Multiple routes do not permit their legal elements to be collapsed into one allegation.

## 5. Middle outcomes must still be harvested

A middle outcome is strategically Bucket B, but every favourable legal/evidential holding must still be preserved and used.

> **Strategically adverse does not mean legally worthless.**

If the AP rejects a culpability ground, removes an award, finds indefensión, criticises AC/Fiscal reasoning, rejects causation, narrows a period, recognises document/account production or identifies lower-court error, those holdings remain valuable even if the overall strategic result is Bucket B.

The project must never suppress favourable appellate evidence to preserve a binary narrative.

## 6. Public communications rule

Before judgment, Por Derecho should not state as fact that the AP will rule adversely because of `institutional` or `political` reasons.

Gil considers a fully clean exoneration unlikely for institutional/political reasons. That is **Gil's expectation/hypothesis**, not an evidenced finding about the motives, independence or future conduct of the panel.

Publication-safe pre-judgment formulation:

> **For my own strategic purposes, only a substantively clean exoneration will close the appellate-accountability question. Any mixed, partial or fragmented outcome will be analysed for its exact legal effect, but I will not treat it as institutional closure.**

Publication-safe Bucket-B formulation after judgment:

> **The legal effect of the Audiencia Provincial's judgment must be stated exactly: what it affirmed, reversed, modified and left undecided. Strategically, however, I do not regard a mixed or partial result as resolving the documentary contradictions that remain. I will continue to pursue the legally competent routes on their own evidence and legal thresholds.**

Do not state that institutional or political pressure caused a future result unless independently proved.

## 7. The AP remains legally and evidentially material

The eventual AP decision may be strategically non-dispositive while remaining legally and evidentially highly material. Its functions include:

1. identifying which first-instance propositions survive judicial scrutiny;
2. testing the AP's treatment of the documentary contradictions;
3. identifying favourable holdings even inside an overall adverse result;
4. determining finality/exhaustion and available next remedies;
5. assessing whether institutions/private actors must correct reliance on Sentencia 163/2023; and
6. creating a new actor-specific reasoning/knowledge record if later accountability questions are legitimately raised.
