## 3. What “truth inversion” means here

The thesis does **not** use `false` as a synonym for `every underlying datum is fabricated`.

A culpability proposition can be materially false, misleading or overbroad even where one raw datum is true if one or more of the following occurs:

1. the wrong actor is treated as having had practical control or capacity;
2. a receivable owed **to** LPB is transformed into culpability **of** LPB/Gil without a reciprocal recovery audit of the debtor and AC;
3. a formal/documentary deficiency is expanded into a global narrative of obstruction or absence;
4. a commercial/operating event is stripped of the hotel’s actual costs, governance, inventory, access and financing constraints;
5. a possible counterfactual (`if X had been collected, insolvency would have ended`) is treated as causation without probability, timing, enforceability, cost or solvency analysis;
6. later loss/control is attributed to a person without first identifying what that person still controlled after the material-control threshold;
7. positive agency — viability, financing, operating, protection, recovery and exit work — disappears from the causation story;
8. an adverse institutional frame is later reused against the same complainant when procedural roles reverse.

The finite test is:

`RAW FACT → OMITTED CONTEXT → POWER / CAPACITY → ACTOR → ACT / OMISSION → CAUSATION → CULPABILITY → INSTITUTIONAL REUSE → WHAT DISAPPEARED FROM SCRUTINY`.

## 4. What “diversion” means here

`Diversion` is first used as a description of **function/effect**, not as proof of subjective motive.

The public page may state that an allegation **diverted scrutiny** where the record shows that framing the event against Gil/LPB/Pink/Patricia displaced a concrete, testable question about:

- a third-party debtor;
- Community debt, voting or governance;
- AC recovery/protection decisions;
- practical control/access/security;
- a bank/creditor-side act;
- title versus material possession;
- operator economics;
- asset/value preservation;
- financing/normalisation/exit;
- the provenance or use of an institutional proposition.

To say the diversion was **deliberately designed** to protect a person, conceal wrongdoing or secure a private benefit requires additional actor-specific knowledge and purpose evidence.

## 7. Three strongest inversion examples already source-controlled

### 7.1 Receivables inversion

The €737,338.85 CEXP branch is the clearest completed example.

The AC advanced a construction that, because LPB was majority participant in CEXP, LPB effectively owed itself much of the receivable. Sentencia 163/2023 accepted expert evidence that the amount was a valid LPB receivable and rejected that self-debt construction.

That makes the public question legitimate and finite:

> How did a right of collection held by LPB become evidence of culpability against Gil before the underlying debtor, legal basis, collectability, enforcement and AC recovery position were fully reconciled?

This does not excuse a director’s duty to pursue valid claims. It shows why the direction of the debt matters.

### 7.2 Cooperation/accounting inversion

The adverse AC report itself reproduces Gil’s 30-Jan-2018 cooperation response and records substantial accounting material already supplied. Judgment 163/2023 also distinguishes material accounting from the narrower statutory-books and support-document findings.

Therefore public wording must separate:

- `some material was late/missing/insufficient`;
- `specific statutory book not proved/supplied`;
- `supporting documents for specified receivables not supplied`;

from the much broader propositions:

- `there was no accounting`;
- `there was no cooperation`;
- `the AC could not know what caused insolvency because of Gil’s obstruction`.

The strongest accusation is not that no deficiency ever existed. It is that narrower defects were allegedly expanded into a materially broader culpability frame despite contrary material inside the same administration/report.

### 7.3 Procedural-role inversion / circularity

The controlled DI 248 chronology shows Gil/Aweswell asking Fiscalía to investigate the AC/CAM perimeter. The 12-Mar-2019 adverse Fiscal calificación position preceded the 7-May-2019 DI 248 archive, and the latter expressly invoked the Ministerio Fiscal’s already-adverse classification position when disposing of the complaint.

This supports a **circularity / anchoring / frame-inheritance question**:

`COMPLAINANT ASKS TO INVESTIGATE AC/CAM → ADVERSE CALIFICACIÓN POSITION AGAINST COMPLAINANT → SAME INSTITUTION LATER RELIES ON THAT POSITION WHEN ARCHIVING COMPLAINT`.

It does not by itself prove collusion, corrupt purpose or personal coordination.

## 8. The hotel-system causation requirement

Any allegation tied to operation, rent, loss, deterioration, access, financing or exit must use one non-fragmented model:

`HOTEL ECONOMY → COMMUNITY GOVERNANCE / DEBT / VOTING → UNIT INVENTORY → OCCUPANCY / REVENUE → COSTS / SET-OFFS → LPB/PINK CASH → BANKING / CREDIT PRESSURE → AC KNOWLEDGE / ACTION → SECURITY / ACCESS → MATERIAL CONTROL → FINANCE / OPERATOR / EXIT CONDITIONS → RECOVERABILITY → SPECIFIC INSOLVENCY EFFECT`.

For consequences at or after 7 June 2018 add:

`FORMAL DUTY / POWER → ACTUAL ACCESS / CONTROL / CAPACITY → ACT / OMISSION → CONSEQUENCE → CAUSATION`.

CR-018 controls. 7 June 2018 is a source-supported de facto material/access/operational-control threshold, not a title date or proven whole-hotel legal possession by CAM.

## 13. Evidence-before-actor rule

For any high-risk public accusation use:

`DISPUTED PROPOSITION → CONTRARY PRIMARY SOURCE → DATE → PROOF BEFORE AC → PROOF BEFORE FISCAL → PROOF BEFORE JUDGE → LATER ACT → KNOWLEDGE GRADE → INFERENCE / ALLEGATION`.

Allowed knowledge grades:

- `PROVED BEFORE ACTOR`;
- `STRONG TRANSMISSION INFERENCE`;
- `INSTITUTIONALLY AVAILABLE ONLY`;
- `NOT YET PROVED`.

Institutional possession is not automatically personal knowledge.

## 14. Strongest-defence discipline

The page should remain difficult to dismiss by stating the strongest defence before the accusation of intent.

- **AC strongest defence:** some delays, missing material and collection failures were real. **What remains:** whether those narrower facts were materially amplified despite contrary material and despite AC duties/knowledge.
