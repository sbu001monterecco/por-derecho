## Primary procedural corrections

### 1. The 4 June 2018 Auto is not classified as an incidente concursal

The primary order states that it clarifies the 16 April 2018 liquidation-plan approval and records non-appealability of the clarification under LEC art. 214.4. Its substantive reach remains open to legal challenge/audit, but misclassifying it as an incident would hide the route-substitution question.

### 2. The 4 June Auto is not recorded as a formal definitive-text modification

The primary 8 February 2018 definitive-text order states that creditor assignment changes identity, not amount, and that the amount must remain what the definitive texts recorded. It records special privilege of:

- €857,373.81; and
- €8,194,877.88;
- total €9,052,251.69;
- plus contingent enforcement costs without their own fixed amount.

On 4 June, the same judge relied on two CAM balance-fixing instruments dated 17 May and used:

- €11,887,314.33; and
- €1,278,518.03;
- total €13,165,832.36;
- plus continuing default interest to the extent covered by the respective mortgage security.

The public issue is therefore a legal/procedural reconciliation, not a numerical assumption.

Former LC art. 59 remains a serious alternative-lawful explanation for secured-interest accrual within the respective guarantee. Accrual/enforceability and formal definitive-list modification are not collapsed.

### 3. Definitive-text modification has a proof gate

Por Derecho will not record a lawful change merely because a later certificate, AC calculation, plan/clarification order, testimony, deed, set-off or accounting entry contains another figure.

Required chain:

`application/request → statutory ground → AC report → transfer/opposition → incidente if required by the applicable route → signed order expressly modifying → exact before/after entry → service/remedy/finality → corrected list/inventory/implementation binding`.

### 4. Community-fee family corrected

The primary order currently controlled is the 20 January 2017 **Article 97 bis definitive-text modification**. LPB objected/requested correction, the AC report was favourable, it was transferred, no opposition is recorded in the located order, and the judge expressly modified the definitive texts concerning the Community's contingent ordinary pre-insolvency fee credit.

This is a strong comparator for the change gate. It is **not** relabelled as an `incidente concursal` unless a separate primary incidental docket/claim/admission/decision is recovered.

### 5. True incidentes concursales currently primary-verified

This is a located denominator, not the certified court docket.

- **Piece 7 / 2012 — LPB v Garrigues**: `Pz Incidente concursal impugnación inventario/lista de acreedores (Art 96.4)`. Judgment 1 April 2016 dismissed LPB's attempt to exclude the recorded Garrigues credit; no costs due doubts recorded by the court.
- **Piece 8 / 2012 — AC v LPB + Monterecco Sun Park, S.L.**: `Pz Incidente concursal Resolución contrato por incumplimiento (Art 62 LC)`. Judgment 24 November 2015 granted termination for non-payment, vacating relief and €2,733,923.64 plus interest/future rent/costs.

Proposed/draft later incidents are not counted until a filing/admission/docket is source-bound.

## Dual-lens governance

Every material act is now reviewed in two passes.

### A. Dry law / black-letter classification

Classify the act under the historical law actually governing it. Former LC art. 192 made the incident a residual route for questions for which the Ley Concursal supplied no other procedure.

### B. Adversarial misconduct hypothesis testing

For judge, AC and relevant private actors test:

- legal authority and procedural route;
- possible route substitution;
- knowledge/notice;
- consistency with prior acts/decisions;
- evidentiary/calculation asymmetry;
- contradiction/equality of arms;
- review/remedy effect;
- protection of estate, productive unit and third-party/extraconcursal perimeter;
- beneficiary/harm;
- implementation/causation;
- repetition/cumulative pattern;
- lawful counterexplanations and corrective/protective counterweights;
- subjective element where criminal classification requires it.

No benign-regularity shortcut and no crime-by-outcome shortcut are allowed.
