## Readiness

The record is sufficiently reconstructed to proceed with the requested unitary analysis, while preserving four evidence states: established documentary fact; reasonable inference; allegation requiring proof; unresolved/contrary evidence.

### 1. Arrecife → Las Palmas chain
JV 1260/2011 and AP Las Palmas Sentencia 89/2014 form a material minority-unit possession/exploitation branch. AP 89/2014 is adverse evidence: it found possession/operation affecting 18 claimant units and relied on key/relationship evidence. It must not be enlarged into a ruling that LPB owned the whole hotel, CEXP never held collective rights, Pink validly succeeded to every CEXP/owner right, every minority unit had the same status, Matkator/third-party assets belonged to LPB, or later CAM whole-hotel authority was established.

### 2. Montelanza/Molina / CEXP / Community
Sun Park was one economic hotel over divided legal titles. The 2008 structure placed LPB as a major property owner and CEXP as an owners-derived exploitation/governance vehicle, with non-LPB units remaining outside LPB and participation not universal. The Project's attributed Montelanza/Molina root-cause theory still requires actor/finca/obligation/performance/defence proof.

### 3. Pink legal/economic position
Monterecco Sun Park, S.L. and Pink Canary Services, S.L. are the same Spanish company, NIF B76564517, after the 2013 name change. Pink had a real factual operating/administrative position for a defined period/perimeter. Operation does not itself prove valid succession to every CEXP/owner right or universal whole-hotel authority.

### 4. Pink rent branch
The 6-Feb-2012 LPB–Monterecco/Pink agreement must be analysed as a full economic package. Controlled terms include €24,575/month net rent plus €33,915.29/month of LPB Community charges assumed by the operator: €58,490.29/month fixed burden before IGIC, plus wider operating costs. The unresolved legal issue is the extent to which LPB alone could confer the operative rights required for the integrated hotel given CEXP/owner authority and divided titles.

### 5. Adverse judgment branch
Sentencia 163/2023 records €2,733,923.64 as the amount attributed to the 24-Nov-2015 Pink judgment and later adopts a €3,032,010.34 damages figure. It split the Pink theory: it rejected the proposition that entry into the 2012 contract was culpably irrational merely because rent was below the mortgage instalment, but made materially adverse first-instance findings on later non-payment/non-enforcement, gross fault/causation and Pink complicity. Those findings are under appeal. The exact euro bridge from contract → 2015 judgment → 2016 execution → €3,032,010.34 remains open.

### 6. AC knowledge
The AC possessed and litigated the full agreement, so his knowledge of the contract economics is directly reconstructable. His 17-Jan-2014 action, 2015 judgment, 2016 execution, 11-Feb-2019 calificación report, 2025 removal opposition and 17-Sep-2026 appellate opposition are key knowledge/adoption checkpoints. The April-2019 Fiscalía expert cannot prove what the AC knew in February 2019.

### 7. Judge knowledge
