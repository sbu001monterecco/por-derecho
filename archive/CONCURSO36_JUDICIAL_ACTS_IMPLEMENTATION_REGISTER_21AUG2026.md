## Controlling question

Do not compress this track into a single proposition such as “the 26 January 2022 adjudication order”. The forensic object is the **whole judicial-act family and its implementation chain**:

> **what was requested → what the court actually ordered → conditions and asset/credit perimeter → what was implemented → what document proves implementation → what difference remains unresolved.**

A formal judicial or notarial act is not treated as economically or evidentially self-executing. Conversely, a documentary difference is not by itself proof of nullity, fraud, bad faith, surplus or criminal responsibility.

## Locked reading rules

1. **LPB estate ≠ whole Sun Park complex.** Never allow an insolvency order affecting LPB assets to become shorthand for title or possession over Matkator or third-party property.
2. **Recognised insolvency credit ≠ mortgage liability ≠ competitive threshold ≠ debt stated as consideration for the dación ≠ Registry value ≠ possible surplus.** Each transition requires its own legal/documentary bridge.
3. **Authorisation ≠ implementation.** Every order that authorises or permits an act must be paired with the downstream filing, deed, mandamiento, Registry entry, bank/ledger treatment and/or final accounts that show what actually occurred.
4. **The two 26 January 2022 Autos are clarifications, not the original adjudication.** Both complete signed copies are located and tie separately to the two 15 October 2021 Autos; neither makes a new award or transfers title. Deed 457 contains an internal 22/26-January recital discrepancy. Preserve that discrepancy as a notarial reconciliation target, not as proof of a distinct 22-January judicial act.
5. **A later deed or Registry entry does not retrospectively answer earlier authority, competition, credit-classification or non-convalidation questions unless the curing title is identified.**
6. **Adverse material remains visible.** A source that weakens the Project Sun Rock thesis must be incorporated at the same evidential rank as a favourable source.

## Three linked ledgers to maintain

### A. Order → Asset

For every act, identify:
- LPB finca(s) actually affected;
- mortgage status and rank;
- whether the asset is inside or outside LPB's estate;
- Matkator / third-party exclusion where applicable;
- deed and Registry implementation;
- any physical/operational-control consequence that must not be confused with title.

### B. Order → Money

Track each amount by **function**, not merely by number:
- recognised insolvency credit;
- ordinary/subordinated/special-privilege classification where proved;
- mortgage liability cap;
- accrued interest calculation;
- third-party competitive threshold;
- €400,000 separate premises/pools line;
- Community-fee condition;
- debt stated in deed 457 as consideration;
- cash, tax, costs, cancellation and possible remanente.

### C. Order → Implementation

For each judicial act, maintain:
- applicant and relief requested;
- evidence before the court;
- exact operative wording;
- conditions;
- notification/service;
- appeal/finality/status;
- required downstream step;
- actual downstream evidence;
- discrepancy, if any;
- proposition status: VERIFIED / INFERENCE / ALLEGATION / OPEN / CORRECTED.
