## 2. Executive evidential synthesis

The ten-source set materially improves the historical record in five directions.

### 2.1 The operator/control history is not a single uninterrupted role

The documents now support a much more precise, adversarial chronology:

1. **23-Dec-2011:** minority-owner plaintiffs themselves alleged that the Comunidad de Explotación del Complejo Sun Park had possessed/exploited their 18 units since July 2008 and controlled the keys, while they contested the legal basis for that possession.
2. **23-Feb-2012:** the same litigation family informed JPI nº4 that there had been a change in exploitation and expanded JV 1260/2011 against **Monterecco Sun Park, S.L.** as the new exploitation company.
3. **24-Mar-2014:** Gil/LPB and Patricia/Pink pleaded that **Pink Canary Services, S.L. was the renamed Monterecco Sun Park, S.L.** and described an operator/subrogation chain; this is party evidence and must be tested against company-registry and Tourism records.
4. **12-Sep-2018:** Gil/Aweswell's Fiscalía complaint gives a later and materially more nuanced role allocation: after litigation brought by the AC, Pink is said to have delivered possession to LPB; CEXP was reactivated to fill the operational gap; Pink thereafter remained a **booking/reservations manager**. That proposition is incompatible with any simplistic statement that Pink held full operational possession/control continuously through the whole 2012–2018 period.
5. **2-Aug-2018 DP 1132 pleadings:** Matkator appears expressly in the represented-party/control narrative. Matkator must remain separate from LPB, CEXP and Pink.

The correct repository model is therefore **period-by-period capacity**, not a single global label such as “operator”, “possessor” or “controller”.

### 2.2 DP 1132/2018 is now anchored by official procedural records, not only later recollection

TS-07 establishes that an Arrecife investigating court received a complaint on 23-Apr-2018 and opened/processed DP 1132/2018. TS-08 then preserves a 1-Aug-2018 providencia showing the procedural roster and referral to Fiscalía, plus LexNET receipts for two 3-Aug-2018 filings opposing reform appeals.

This strengthens **procedural existence, timing, party-status and retrieval**. It does not establish that the investigated parties committed the alleged offences.

### 2.3 The May-2019 Fiscalía archive is a mandatory adverse source

TS-09 is not a minor footnote. It is the official prosecutorial disposition of DI 248/2018 then before Fiscalía. The decree archives the investigation because Fiscalía considered the material insufficient to disclose a criminal offence and stated that the complainants could reiterate before the competent investigating court under article 773 LECrim.

Any repository, public page, complaint or Calificación narrative relying on DI 248 must disclose this disposition accurately. It is **not an acquittal**, not a judicial merits judgment, and not a finding that every underlying factual event did not happen. It is nevertheless an adverse official assessment of the criminal case as presented to Fiscalía at that time.

The decree also specifically weakens any direct “commission to AC” inference from the 24-Jan-2018 email: Fiscalía recorded that the email contained an `intermediación/comisiones 3%` calculation in the credit-assignment context, but noted the absence of a recipient reply and that the email did not mention the AC. Transaction-level proof remains necessary.

### 2.4 The anti-convalidation record before the 24-Oct-2019 Auto is now more exact

TS-10 identifies the 28-Nov-2018 instrument as a deed before Notary Manuel Guzmán Ramos, **protocol 2.150**, and records LPB's 8-May-2019 opposition to the AC's written request 1760/2019 to ratify the OB REM unlinking and subsequent sale. TS-03/CF-10 supplies Aweswell's 13-May-2019 parallel opposition and detailed economic linkage.

These pleadings remain party positions. The later signed **24-Oct-2019 Auto**, already controlled elsewhere in the repository, is the stronger judicial source for the proposition that the court would not convalidate the transaction and that the earlier partial-suspension approach was erroneous in light of the unitary architecture.

### 2.5 The 2011/2014 pair is valuable precisely because the positions are adversarial

TS-04 is the minority-owner eviction case. TS-05 is a later LPB/Pink criminal complaint attacking that litigation and the minority-owner conduct. Neither should be used as an adjudicated truth source. Read together, however, they provide a high-value map of:

- who claimed physical control;
- what each side said the tourism/unitary-exploitation rules required;
- which administrative and judicial proceedings already existed before the June-2012 insolvency;
- which contracts, minutes, Tourism files, notarial acts and judgments should now be retrieved.

That is highly relevant to Calificación causation because it proves the **existence of a pre-concurso operational/legal conflict record**, while leaving responsibility and legal effect to stronger sources.

## TS-04 — 23-Dec-2011 minority-owner desahucio por precario, JV 1260/2011

**Source class:** adverse-party initiating pleading; scanned copy; not a judgment.

The plaintiffs were a group of owners of 18 bungalows/units in Sun Park. They sued the Comunidad de Explotación del Complejo Sun Park seeking recovery of possession by a verbal eviction action.

Material propositions made **by those plaintiffs** include:

- the Comunidad de Explotación had, according to them, taken control of the keys from July 2008 and had thereafter possessed and tourist-operated their apartments;
- the plaintiffs denied that the operating community had a sufficient title from them and said they had rejected joining the operating community;
- they relied on Canarian tourism regulation and asserted that the tourism operator needed habilitating titles covering the establishment;
- they said that on 25-Mar-2010 they complained to Tourism and referred to **expediente sancionador 92/11**;
- they stated that the sanctioning proceeding had been brought to an end and that notification occurred on 4-Oct-2011, while also saying they had not obtained access to the final decision and therefore sought judicial production of it;
- they relied on notarial demands of 25-Feb and 3-Mar-2010 for delivery of keys; the pleading reproduces a notarial account in which the then complex director refused delivery and stated that all apartments were affected to tourist exploitation;
- they sought eviction and costs.

### Evidential value

This source is particularly useful as an **adverse admission/position about factual control**: before LPB's June-2012 insolvency, the minority-owner plaintiffs themselves were pleading that the Comunidad de Explotación was in physical/tourist operation of their units.

It does **not** prove that such occupation was unlawful, that the plaintiffs were wrong about title, that the operating community had valid title, or that the tourism administration agreed with either side.

### New retrieval targets

- full JV 1260/2011 docket and judgment/outcome;
- full annex set to the 23-Dec-2011 demand;
- final resolution and complete expediente **92/11**;
- 25-Feb/3-Mar-2010 notarial instruments;
- the 3-Jan-2011 private-detective report cited by the plaintiffs;
- any 2010 Tourism complaint and responses.

## TS-09 — Fiscalía DI 248/2018 archive, 7-May-2019 decree / 16-May oficio

**Source class:** official adverse prosecutorial disposition; scanned official record.

This source must be carried in every future DI 248 treatment.

### What Fiscalía did

The scanned decree states that, after studying the complaint material, Fiscalía did not consider the complained facts to disclose a criminal offence and ordered the investigation archived under article 773.2 LECrim / the Fiscalía statute. It expressly advised that the claim could be reiterated before the competent investigating court.

An accompanying oficio dated/signed electronically on 16-May-2019 communicates the archive.

### Prosecutorial reasoning that must not be omitted

The decree, in substance:

- characterized the dispute with the AC as substantially involving disagreements with concursal decisions subject to judicial supervision and the ordinary concursal-remedy route;
- noted the existence of sub judice disputes and DP 1132/2018;
- referenced the then-open Calificación section and Fiscalía's own culpability position;
- treated CAM as a recognized privileged creditor and stated, as Fiscalía's premise, that property had consequently been allocated in dación en pago;
- addressed the commission allegation and found the relied-on email insufficient to connect a commission to the AC.

### Mandatory limitations

Do **not** translate the archive into any of the following:

- “the court found no wrongdoing”;
- “the allegations were false”;
- “CAM/AC were acquitted”;
- “all underlying facts were judicially validated”.

The decree is a prosecutorial archive at that stage, not an acquittal or a final judicial merits judgment.

Conversely, do not hide or minimize it. It is a material adverse official assessment and belongs in any fair presentation of the 2018–2019 criminal-reporting history.

3. Fiscalía's statement about a dación is its 2019 characterization/premise. It must not override the later signed concursal record concerning suspension, non-convalidation, tendering and the ultimate 2022 title chain.
