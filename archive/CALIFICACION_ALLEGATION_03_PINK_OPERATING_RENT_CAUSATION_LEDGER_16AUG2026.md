## Status

**CONTROLLING DEEP-AUDIT SUBLEDGER FOR ALLEGATION 03.**

This allegation must be split into two legally and evidentially different propositions:

1. **03-A — entry into the 6-Feb-2012 operating agreement was itself culpably irrational / aggravating.**  
   **Result:** Sentencia 163/2023 expressly rejects that proposition on the AC's pleaded rent-versus-mortgage reasoning. The original contract and the now-recovered Fiscalía rent expert materially reinforce the need to treat the whole economic package rather than rent alone.

2. **03-B — rent was not collected / Pink did not pay / Gil failed to pursue payment and termination, aggravating insolvency.**  
   **Result:** materially adverse first-instance finding. Sentencia 163/2023 finds gross fault, causation and Pink complicity on this branch. Those findings are appealed. The actual operating-profit/cash premise and the quantum chain remain materially unresolved and must not be inferred from the adverse judgment more broadly than it says.

3. **03-C — civil validity, CEXP authority and Gil's current total-nullity *ab initio* position.**
   **Result:** not adjudicated by the 03-A economic-rationality ruling. Declaration 009 supersedes Gil's earlier total/partial alternative and records his primary position that the 6-Feb-2012 LPB–Monterecco contract was wholly void *ab initio* because LPB could not independently convey or bypass CEXP's owners-derived whole-hotel mandate. The contrary case remains mandatory: the 1-Feb CEXP–Monterecco notice, LPB's own-unit rights, the contract's severability clause, Pink's real operation/registration, competent-organ authority, third-party/common-area scope, later resolution/rent judgments, restitution and preclusion/res judicata. This is a party legal position, not a current finding of nullity.

The strongest defensible accountability conclusion is therefore **not** `the whole Pink allegation was fabricated`. It is:

> **the AC's attack on the economic rationality of entering the contract was expressly rejected, while the later non-payment branch was upheld on a record in which actual operating income/profitability was not reconstructed and the monetary sequence from contractual rent to €2.733m and €3.032m remains unreconciled.**

That conclusion does not decide 03-C. Economic rationality for calificación, civil validity/authority, factual operation, contractual payment, resolution and restitution are different questions.

# 2. Exact AC accusation — do not collapse its components

The AC used the 6-Feb-2012 agreement under Art. 164.1 LC as a reason why the insolvency was allegedly generated/aggravated by dolo or culpa grave.

The adverse narrative included these propositions:

- LPB transferred the tourism operation of its Sun Park properties to Spanish Monterecco/Pink;
- contractual rent was **€24,575/month**;
- Pink also had to bear LPB's Community charges, quantified at **€33,915.29/month**;
- the €24,575 rent was lower than the monthly mortgage amortisation figure invoked by the AC;
- therefore, according to the AC, the agreement could not enable LPB to service the mortgage and its other creditors;
- the AC said no rent had been paid;
- the AC had reported the matter to Fiscalía and later required Gil to sue;
- the AC itself ultimately brought the contractual-resolution/rent action.

### Control point

The accusation mixed two separate questions:

`Was entering the agreement itself economically culpable?`

and

`Was later non-payment / non-enforcement culpable?`

Sentencia 163/2023 did **not** answer those two questions the same way.

# 3. What the original 6-Feb-2012 contract actually required

The original signed contract is annexed to the AC's own 2013/2014 incident demand.

It is not economically described by the €24,575 rent alone.

## Fixed monthly burden stated in the contract

- net contractual rent to LPB: **€24,575.00/month**;
- LPB Community maintenance quota assumed by the operator: **€33,915.29/month**;
- combined fixed burden before IGIC: **€58,490.29/month**;
- annualised fixed burden before IGIC: **€701,883.48/year**.

## Operator-side burdens also allocated by the contract

The operator assumed the operating machinery and expenses associated with hotel exploitation, including the contractually described supply, staffing/personnel, maintenance/repair/replacement, regulatory, commercial/marketing and other operating functions/costs.

### Evidential consequence

The AC knew the contract's complete economic architecture no later than his own resolution action, because he attached the agreement and pleaded its rent and Community-charge provisions.

A comparison of only:

`€24,575 rent` **vs** `mortgage instalment`

is therefore an incomplete economic description of what LPB obtained/avoided under the agreement. That does not prove that the arrangement was optimal, nor does it excuse any unpaid contractual rent. It means only that the whole bargain must be tested rather than a single payment line.

# 5. Sentencia 163/2023 expressly rejects the contract-entry culpability theory

This is the decisive correction to the first half of Allegation 03.

The judgment records the AC theory that the agreed monthly rent was below the mortgage payment and therefore the contract itself aggravated insolvency.

The court then accepts LPB's answer on that specific point. Its reasoning is that, if the criticism is merely that the rent was insufficient to cover the monthly mortgage instalment, **entering the agreement itself does not appear to be an antijuridical act deserving calificación reproach**.

### Controlled conclusion

`agreement entered at rent below mortgage instalment → therefore culpable/aggravating act`

was **rejected by the first-instance judge himself**.

This must now be prominent on the public page. The adverse later non-payment finding must not be allowed to retroactively rewrite the separate judicial result on contract entry.

### Classification

`CONTRACT-ENTRY CULPABILITY THEORY — REJECTED BY SENTENCIA 163/2023`

# 6. Recovered Fiscalía rent expert — ME-004 resolved, but with a critical limitation

## What the expert was actually asked to do

Determine an **average market rent for all LPB properties**.

## What evidence the expert did not receive

The report expressly records that requested operational source material was not supplied, including original/source financial-operating material and tour-operator contracting needed to reconstruct actual hotel economics.

Therefore the expert used an **alternative modelling / market-comparison method** rather than a reconstruction of Pink's actual 2012–2015 accounts and cash.

## Modelled results

Using 188 assets and historical/market assumptions, the report models approximately:

- gross monthly income: **€124,851.74**;
- monthly expenses: **€87,950.13**;
- monthly operating result: **€36,901.61**;
- modelled operating result: **€196.29/property/month**;
- contractual rent: **€130.72/property/month**;
- modelled pre-tax operator margin after rent: **€65.57/property/month**;
- modelled after-tax margin: **€45.90/property/month**.

The report separately annualises the combined fixed contractual rent + Community charge:

**€701,883.48/year**, or approximately **€3,733.42/property/year**,

and compares that with a national fixed-hotel-rent range, concluding that the agreed price sat within the average/reasonable range considered by the expert.

## What this proves

It materially supports the proposition that the **pricing structure was not obviously abusive or economically irrational merely because €24,575 rent was below the mortgage instalment**.

## What this does NOT prove

It does **not** establish:

- Pink's actual 2012–2015 room revenue;
- actual occupancy/ADR by month;
- actual profit/loss;
- actual bank cash available to pay LPB;
- that all Pink revenue was consumed by maintenance;
- that no rent was due;
- that rent was in fact paid;
- that the later judgment/execution amounts were wrong.

### Correction to the defence record

The Pink appeal describes the Fiscalía expert more strongly, effectively using it as proof that actual operating income merely covered maintenance and that Pink obtained no profit. The recovered primary expert does **not** establish that proposition. It is a modelled market-rent exercise undertaken precisely because complete actual operating documentation was unavailable.

# 8. Profitability / ability to pay — the judgment itself reveals the evidence gap

The judgment discusses defence expert material concerning operating expenses. It identifies, among other matters, a figure of approximately **€414,697.10** in maintenance costs for 2012–2015 and excludes an earlier personnel-period figure from the relevant contract period.
