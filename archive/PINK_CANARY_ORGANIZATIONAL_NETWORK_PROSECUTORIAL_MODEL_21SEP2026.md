## 4. Organization-level questions

The system review asks:

1. Did the same proposition recur through multiple actors?
2. Did later actors independently verify it?
3. Did one actor have contrary evidence another actor did not?
4. Did the proposition change wording or scope in transit?
5. Did a limited proposition become broader after repetition?
6. Did later legal/economic acts depend on the broadened form?
7. Did multiple actors benefit from the same propagated proposition?
8. Is there evidence of instruction, agreement, coordination or simply parallel reliance?
9. What innocent institutional explanation fits the same sequence?

## 7. Coordination scale

Do not jump directly from “multiple aligned acts” to conspiracy.

Use a scale:

0. NO LINK SHOWN  
1. COMMON SUBJECT ONLY  
2. DOCUMENT/INFORMATION CONTACT  
3. MUTUAL KNOWLEDGE  
4. COORDINATED ACT  
5. SHARED OBJECTIVE EVIDENCED  
6. OFFENCE-SPECIFIC COMMON PLAN EVIDENCED.

Every edge must be assigned only to the highest level actually proved.

## 8. Institutional-capture / instrumentalisation test

A potential capture/instrumentalisation pattern requires proof of:

- an organ/process with legal authority;
- a proposition or mechanism introduced into it;
- actor knowledge of limits/contradictions;
- repeated use;
- practical decision/effect;
- advantage/disadvantage;
- proof excluding ordinary lawful administration.

No label of “capture” is conclusive without this chain.
