## Mission

Optimise the Calificación track so that it performs three functions simultaneously:

1. accurately explain what happened in the calificación proceedings;
2. test, document by document, the AC/Fiscal/Judge allegations, findings, contradictions, appeal and accountability questions; and
3. tell the wider factual story of what Gil Marer, LPB, Aweswell, Pink Canary, Matkator and the relevant Group interests **did and continue to do** to preserve and recover the business, hotel interests, assets, income, rights, causes of action and damages.

The Calificación page must support the wider Por Derecho website, and the wider website must supply the context necessary to understand the Calificación.

## Controlling strategic principle

**Recovery first. Accountability in service of recovery. Punishment only where independently justified.**

Do not publicly rely on adjectives such as `heroic`, `courageous` or `relentless` as evidence. Make determination and persistence emerge from the verified chronology of actions.

## Public-interest frame

Allow the case to illuminate broader rule-of-law questions without pretending disputed allegations are already established:

- Can insolvency distinguish a debtor company from a mixed-ownership hotel?
- Can culpability analysis distinguish failure from genuine but unsuccessful rescue work?
- What happens when an AC allegation becomes a prosecutorial position and then a judicial finding?
- What safeguards apply when an appealed first-instance judgment travels into media, regulatory or commercial contexts?
- How should investors/operators/public bodies distinguish title, material control and economic presentation?
- How does an injured party obtain correction where institutions each see only part of the chronology?

## Evidential controls

Use only:

- `VERIFIED FACT`;
- `PARTY ALLEGATION`;
- `EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE`;
- `UNRESOLVED`;
- `CORRECTED / SUPERSEDED`.

Never turn:

- appeal into reversal;
- complaint into proof;
- filing/receipt into merits acceptance;
- judicial error into prevaricación;
- institutional possession into personal knowledge;
- economic benefit into corrupt benefit;
- chronology into causation;
- group relationship into common intent.

Do not weaken a documented contradiction merely to sound neutral.
