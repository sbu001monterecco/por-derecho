## Reverse-engineered order of analysis — criminal first

For each material event run this order before narrative drafting:

1. **Offence-neutral facts:** actor, date, act/omission, document, source, recipient, use, reliance, effect, alleged benefit and strongest non-criminal explanation.
2. **Knowledge and intent:** exact contrary notice, receipt, personal engagement, practical capacity, repetition, concealment/coordination evidence and mistake-to-intent level.
3. **Offence elements:** identify the statutory candidate only after steps 1–2; mark each element `PROVED / SUPPORTED / OPEN / CONTRADICTED / NOT APPLICABLE`.
4. **Criminal procedure:** competent route, current status, finality, limitation/preservation issue, admissible new-evidence bridge and what the route cannot decide.
5. **Civil/concursal consequences:** nullity, restitution, asset recovery, accounting, damages, limitation and interim preservation. Do not wait for criminal success where a civil clock or asset-preservation need is live.
6. **Regulatory/disciplinary/protection routes:** competence, receipt, status, protection and non-merger boundary.

Apply these specific controls:

- CP Articles 446–449 require the actor-specific judicial act/omission and statutory mental element. Appeal grounds, adverse reasoning or later reversal do not alone prove prevaricación, refusal to judge or malicious delay.
- CP Article 464 requires its own violence/intimidation or retaliatory-act elements and a person acting in a proceeding. Do not map the elEconomista episode or Law 2/2023 retaliation onto Article 464 by label alone.
- Deception/property, procedural-fraud or disloyal-administration hypotheses require their distinct representation, knowledge, reliance/disposition, entrusted power, profit/benefit, harm and causation elements as applicable. Do not infer them from document use or structural benefit alone.
- Law 2/2023 supplies protection and rebuttable-presumption rules only when its predicates are satisfied. It does not itself create a criminal offence.
- A provisional dismissal is not a conviction, acquittal or universal merits finding. Any reopening/new-evidence theory must identify the exact new proposition, element and competent procedural act.
