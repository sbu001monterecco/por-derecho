## INTERFERENCE CLASSIFICATION

Classify each episode as one of:

- `D1 DIRECT DOCUMENTED MANDATE ACT`
- `D2 DIRECT DOCUMENTED PROFESSIONAL PRESSURE`
- `S1 STRUCTURAL AUTHORITY / INFORMATION / STANDING CONSTRAINT`
- `I1 INDIRECT PRIVATE-ACTOR EFFECT`
- `J1 DOCUMENTED AC / PRIVATE-ACTOR CONVERGENCE`
- `M1 MIXED CAUSATION`
- `O1 ORDINARY PROFESSIONAL EVENT`
- `OPEN`

Preserve `threatened`, `filed`, `admitted`, `investigated`, `decided`, `upheld`, `archived`, `appealed` and `final` as distinct states. Do not describe a threat as a filed complaint, a filing as an upheld complaint, or institutional receipt as examination of the merits.

For `J1`, require a documentary bridge: communication, instruction, transaction, shared filing, coordinated chronology with independent support, or another source proving connection. Common benefit, temporal proximity or similar rhetoric is not enough.

## CAUSATION TEST

Score **workstream impact** and **exit / reduction / non-personation causation** separately on the existing C0–C5 scale.

For every episode build:

`POWER / CAPACITY → VERIFIED KNOWLEDGE → ACT / OMISSION → PRACTICAL EFFECT → LAWYER RESPONSE → HANDOVER / EXIT EFFECT → COUNTERFACTUAL → STRONGEST ALTERNATIVE → BENEFICIARY → REMEDY / OPEN REQUEST`.

Ask:

1. Would the workstream probably have become harder, slower, more expensive or procedurally vulnerable without the identified act?
2. Would the engagement probably have ended, narrowed or failed to formalise without that act?
3. Does a contemporaneous lawyer statement attribute the result to the act?
4. Are fees, strategy, confidence, conflict, health, capacity, client instruction or another factor better supported?
5. Did the lawyer continue after the event?
6. Was the AC exercising an ordinary statutory function, and if so, what issue survives that defence?

No C4/C5 exit claim may be published without direct contemporaneous evidence and a recorded contradiction review.

### Joint or convergent conduct

Publish only the proved connection. If the record shows only parallel or mutually reinforcing effects, say exactly that and classify the joint-purpose proposition as open.

### Courts, bar associations and institutions

Keep judicial, disciplinary, regulatory and private actors separate. Distinguish jurisdiction, competence, receipt, examination, decision and appeal. Ordinary adverse rulings or disciplinary routing are not proof of coordination.
