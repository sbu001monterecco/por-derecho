## Mission

Run a **unitary, non-fragmented, reverse-engineered reconstruction** of the entire ONA/Sun Park transaction from the earliest commercial workstream to the latest known consequences, and then backwards from the present-day asset/control/financing structure to the original funded-exit package.

The page must answer one central question:

> **What exactly was the ONA-led operational, acquisition, financing and security package; what legal/procedural conditions did it depend on; what happened to each dependency before, during and after 7 June 2018; and what evidence would be required to prove or disprove that specific acts or omissions frustrated, impaired or diverted that funded exit and its underlying security package?**

Do not answer that question with rhetoric. Build the evidentiary bridge actor-by-actor, asset-by-asset, date-by-date and condition-by-condition.

## 12. Publication / defamation controls

Before any merge or deployment, run a sentence-level audit for named living persons and companies.

For every adverse sentence ask:

1. Is this a documented act, a party allegation, an inference or an official finding?
2. Is the subject the correct legal person?
3. Is the source contemporaneous and attributable?
4. Have contrary evidence and official outcomes been included where material?
5. Does the wording imply criminal guilt beyond what the source supports?
6. Can the same evidentiary point be stated more precisely and more strongly without overclaiming?

Use phrases such as:

- “the reviewed record documents…”
- “the contemporaneous correspondence records…”
- “LPB/Oswell alleges…”
- “this chronology raises the causal question whether…”
- “the present record does not by itself establish…”
- “proof still required…”

Avoid categorical criminal labels absent an adjudicated basis.

## Core analytical principle

**The objective is not to make the allegation sound stronger. The objective is to make the evidence chain stronger.** Where the evidence supports a serious inference, articulate it precisely. Where an element is missing, expose the missing element and identify the document, witness, account, court record or financial reconciliation most likely to resolve it.
