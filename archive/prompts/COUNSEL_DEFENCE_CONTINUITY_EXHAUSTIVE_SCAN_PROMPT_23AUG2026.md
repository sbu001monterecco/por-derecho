## Required analytical method

For every professional, construct a separate row:

`CODE → FULL IDENTITY → FIRM → CLIENT → PROCEEDING → MANDATE → WORK PRODUCT → AC POWER/DUTY → AC ACT/OMISSION → SOURCE → IMMEDIATE EFFECT → SUBSTITUTION/WITHDRAWAL → STATED REASON → CAUSATION GRADE → STRONGEST CONTRARY EVIDENCE → OPEN GAP`

Use only these causation grades:

- **DIRECTLY DOCUMENTED** — source expressly connects AC conduct and effect;
- **STRONG SEQUENCE; CAUSATION UNRESOLVED** — sequence is established but causal bridge is absent;
- **CLIENT/PROFESSIONAL REASON DOCUMENTED** — stated reason points elsewhere;
- **NO AC LINK LOCATED**;
- **CONTRADICTED / CORRECTED**.

## Anti-overclaim rules

- Do not convert authorisation to bring a complaint into proof that it was filed or upheld.
- Do not convert chronology into causation.
- Do not suppress a lawyer’s own stated reasons for withdrawal.
- Do not describe client substitution as involuntary withdrawal.
- Do not infer that non-payment was an AC decision without the invoice/request/response chain.
- Do not merge LPB’s counsel with counsel for Gil, Aweswell, Matkator or Pink.
- Preserve legal privilege. Do not publish advice, strategy, weaknesses, limitation analysis or confidential work product.
- A lawyer’s appearance does not imply wrongdoing or agreement with Gil’s allegations.
