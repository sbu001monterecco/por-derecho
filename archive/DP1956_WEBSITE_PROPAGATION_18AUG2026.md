## Public hierarchy decision

DP 1956/2026 is **not a homepage priority**.

It belongs under the insolvency-administrator / justice / institutional-accountability layer because the website's first-order public hierarchy remains:

`asset → title → control → financing → operation/value → recovery → institutional/accountability routes`.

Criminal, disciplinary and professional-liability routes are important but secondary to the asset-recovery story and should not displace it visually.

## Public wording

Preferred status:

> **Sobreseimiento provisional · el estado puede ser jurídicamente reexaminado si aparecen nuevos elementos relevantes; cualquier reapertura depende de los requisitos legales y de una decisión judicial.**

English:

> **Provisionally dismissed · later reassessment may be legally possible if materially new evidence emerges; any reopening depends on the applicable legal requirements and a judicial decision.**

Do not use:

- `closed` without the provisional qualifier;
- `acquitted`;
- `free dismissal`;
- `appeal pending`;
- `reopening pending`;
- `reopening expected`.

## Privilege firewall

The public website must not state or imply why any litigation decision was made, what counsel advised, what tactical risk was assessed, or what outcome/timeframe counsel expected. Public legal propositions are sourced to the LECrim/BOE and public FGE doctrine.

## Professional-firm cooperation framing

RSM and Grant Thornton are not asked to `support` DP 1956/2026, DP 1901/2026, ICALPA, CNMV or any public-funds authority.

The public test is:

> **identify the correct professional-responsibility entity → preserve relevant records → cooperate with any lawful, properly framed request from the competent body.**

Where DP 1956 is named, the wording is conditional: cooperation if a future lawful request arises or if the proceeding's provisional status changes.
