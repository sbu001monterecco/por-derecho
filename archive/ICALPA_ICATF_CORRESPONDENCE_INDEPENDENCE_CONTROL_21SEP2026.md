## 4. Regional rapporteur safeguard

The living Consejo Canario draft now asks that:

- any rapporteur/instructor be conflict-cleared in advance;
- relevant professional/economic/institutional relationships be checked and documented;
- the person have sufficient independence, continuity, time and institutional backing to carry the matter to a reasoned outcome; and
- any call, informal approach, representation or material contact outside the file by an interested/connected person be logged and channelled into the formal record.

This is a preventive traceability safeguard, not an allegation that any specific call or pressure has occurred or will occur.
