## 1. Controlling proposition

> **LPB was the debtor. CEXP was outside the insolvency estate. Its economic value was not outside the insolvency analysis.**

The group’s central valuation case is wider than an allegation that LPB’s individual registered properties were undervalued. The case is that Concurso 36/2012 did not adequately identify, reconstruct, value and integrate LPB’s legally and economically attributable interest in the unified Sun Park hotel productive unit organised through the Comunidad de Explotación del Complejo Sun Park (CEXP).

That attributable interest may include, depending on the controlling primary documents and applicable law:

- LPB participation and contractual exploitation rights;
- property/inventory contributed to the operating structure;
- receivables and income entitlements;
- operating contracts and dependencies;
- maintenance, staff, utilities and service economics;
- goodwill, distribution and going-concern uplift attributable to LPB’s estate property;
- rescue/refinancing capacity created by a functioning productive unit; and
- claims arising from impairment, obstruction, displacement or loss of those rights.

## 2. Essential perimeter correction

Do **not** describe CEXP as the registered owner of the whole hotel merely because the hotel operation was organised through it.

Preferred formulation:

> **The collectively participated hotel productive unit was articulated and operated through CEXP while title remained divided property by property.**

Mandatory distinction:

`REGISTERED TITLE ≠ CEXP PARTICIPATION ≠ EXPLOITATION AUTHORITY ≠ PRODUCTIVE-UNIT VALUE ≠ OWNERSHIP OF EACH CASH FLOW`

The valuation exercise is therefore not to bring all CEXP or third-party assets into LPB’s estate. It is to identify LPB’s estate value and claims in, through or against the operating structure and to measure the going-concern effect of that structure on LPB’s own property.

## 4. Insolvency Administrator headline control

The AC accountability page must no longer treat CEXP productive-unit value as a secondary accounting question.

The controlling AC audit is:

> **NOTICE → POWER/DUTY → CEXP MATERIAL RECEIVED → VALUE IDENTIFIED → VALUATION METHOD → PROTECTION/PURSUIT → LIQUIDATION USE → CONSEQUENCE FOR LPB → REASONED RESPONSE**

It requires answers to four clusters:

1. **Knowledge:** what the AC knew about CEXP provenance, LPB participation, hotel operation, Community debt/voting conflict, operator arrangements, income and maintenance costs.
2. **Power and responsibility:** what was an estate right, what was extraconcursal, and what indirectly affected estate market/going-concern value.
3. **Valuation and rescue:** what methodology was used and how ACTÚA, GESVALT, operating income, rescue and refinancing evidence were reconciled.
4. **Reciprocity in calificación:** if rent/non-collection and operator economics are used adversely, upstream constraints on income and the AC’s own knowledge/decisions must be examined on the same record.
