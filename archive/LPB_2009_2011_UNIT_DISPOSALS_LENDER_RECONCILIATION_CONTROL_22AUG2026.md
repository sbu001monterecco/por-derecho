## Controlled proposition

Contemporaneous LPB accounting and asset material support a finite population of twelve apartment disposals between 2009 and early 2011: six recorded in 2009, four in 2010 and two in early 2011. The 2010 impairment material describes 171 apartments at acquisition and ten sales by the end of 2010; the 2012 inventory records 159 apartments. The journal entries contain property-specific sale, cash-account and mortgage-reduction/subrogation postings. They do **not** prove that the original lender received a cash payment, accepted a subrogation, released a mortgage, credited the enforcement balance, or calculated Mortgage Execution 90/2012 incorrectly.

## Required transaction-level bridge

For each of the twelve identified disposals, reconcile:

1. finca/unit and recorded owner/title immediately before and after completion;
2. sale deed, price, completion statement and actual payment route;
3. mortgage balance, novation/subrogation/release and lender acceptance;
4. LPB journal, cash-account and long-term-debt entries;
5. lender account/loan ledger and security release history; and
6. the debt certificate, liquidation and property schedule used in Mortgage Execution 90/2012.

Only that bridge can establish whether a particular completion affected lender principal, security, interest, default, enforcement or a later balance. It must remain separate from the LPB insolvency estate, Matkator title/use/revenue questions, CEXP governance and whole-hotel operation.
