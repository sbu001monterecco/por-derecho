## CR-DP-001 — Do not call the judicial filing “Control 24”

**Supersedes:** shorthand in older repository records that calls the judicial-conduct track `Control 24` as though that were the proceeding name.

**Correct formulation:**

> the separate criminal complaint presented on 18 June 2026 concerning specified judicial resolutions/conduct, together with its later ampliación, both registered or linked by the Decanato under the daily reference n.º 24.

The daily reference `24` is **not** a NIG, Diligencias Previas number, formal criminal-case number or proof of judicial opening. Until a primary judicial record supplies a formal reference, do not write `DP 24`, `procedure 24`, `case 24` or `Control 24` as a procedural identifier.

**Classification:** CORRECTED / PROCEDURAL SOURCE CONTROL.

## CR-DP-002 — Three routes must remain separate

The controlling actor/perimeter split is:

1. **DP 1956/2026** — AC-specific track: the Insolvency Administrator’s own knowledge, entrusted powers, verification duties, acts/omissions, factual inputs, recommendations, implementation/non-correction, patrimonial consequence, liquidation reporting and accounting.
2. **DP 1901/2026** — private-actor track: alleged culpability of JDAM, LPAM, CAM / Acosta Matos entities and other private actors identified there, including authority, title, control, Community machinery, debt, access, works, transaction/project representations and benefit.
3. **Judicial-accountability routes** — separate criminal complaint + ampliación under the Decanato daily reference n.º 24, and separately DI 169/2026 / Alzada 286/2026 for the institutional/disciplinary-supervision dimension.

Common evidence may be used for different legal purposes. **One route does not prove another and separation must not become artificial factual fragmentation.**

**Classification:** CONTROLLING PROCEDURAL FRAME.

## CR-DP-003 — DP 1956 must not import private culpability from DP 1901

DP 1901 material may be used in DP 1956 only to answer actor-specific questions about the AC, for example:

- what the AC received or knew;
- what duty or power was engaged;
- what he verified or failed to verify;
- what he communicated, recommended or omitted before the Mercantile Court;
- what result he implemented, maintained or failed to correct;
- what he recorded in liquidation reports/accounts;
- and what incremental patrimonial consequence is attributable to his own conduct.

Do not write that private wrongdoing automatically becomes AC wrongdoing, or that AC involvement automatically proves the private actors’ intent.

## CR-DP-004 — Judicial approval does not absorb the AC’s own acts; AC involvement does not prove judicial criminality

Use the following control sequence:

> AC information/input or omission → judicial record/decision opportunity → judicial act or omission → AC implementation/non-correction/accounting → patrimonial effect.

This sequence is for **causal attribution**, not automatic concertation.

A judicial resolution does not by itself erase the AC’s responsibility for factual premises, calculations, professional recommendations, implementation, custody, restoration or accounting. Conversely, an AC report/recommendation does not by itself establish that a judicial resolution was criminally unjust.

## CR-DP-005 — CAM’s 2018 works: correct the wording

A primary CAM filing reviewed in the source corpus states, in substance, that CAM owned 54 apartments and that in 2018 it began **interior works in several apartments of its own**, under an asserted municipal licence, because it expected the complex to enter into operation; it maintained that LPB’s units were not affected.

Do **not** state as a direct admission that CAM confessed to beginning the hotel’s complete or “integral refurbishment” in 2018 unless a separate primary source says so.

The stronger evidence-based inference is narrower:

> the limited-unit explanation must be reconciled with CAM’s whole-complex acquisition strategy, the offer’s reliance on the economic/tourism unity of the complex, Community/security/common-installation activity, later RICPE presentation and the eventual integrated hotel project.

That may support investigation of a pre-existing **project-level expectation of control/reopening**. It does not by itself prove a guaranteed or predetermined adjudication.

**Classification:** VERIFIED SOURCE FACT + EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE / NO PREDETERMINATION FINDING.

## CR-DP-006 — Unity of exploitation is not unitary ownership

CAM’s January 2018 offer invoked the Canary Islands tourism framework and the principle of unity of exploitation as part of the economic logic of the offer.

Do not convert that principle into a legal rule that one person had to own every registry unit or that one commercial flag/brand was legally mandatory. Preserve the narrower proposition:

> Sun Park’s useful tourism operation was understood and represented as an integrated establishment requiring unitary exploitation, while registry ownership could remain fragmented.

The evidential question is why CAM invested in limited-unit works linked to expected reopening of an integrated complex, what expectation of control/acquisition supported that investment, and what communications or approvals existed.

## CR-DP-008 — Liquidation-accounting bridge: different figures perform different legal functions

Do not flatten or migrate figures automatically between these categories:

- recognised insolvency credit;
- mortgage-security ceiling;
- principal actually outstanding;
- remuneratory or moratory interest;
- amount a third-party bidder had to improve;
- payoff/redemption figure;
- amount of credit actually extinguished;
- sale/cash component;
- total economic consideration;
- compensation/condonation;
- expenses;
- possible surplus due to the estate.

The current forensic control requires a reproducible bridge through the milestones:

> textos definitivos → CAM offer / plan approved 16 April 2018 → AP order of 23 July 2019 and the third-party improvement threshold → legally relevant 2019 Supreme Court interest doctrine → 2021 competitive process → amount actually extinguished → separate EUR 400,000 component → 21 February 2022 deed / registry effects → possible surplus → liquidation reports / final rendition of accounts.

The absence of that bridge in the currently accessible corpus is **not proof that it never existed**. The investigation/reconciliation question is whether contemporaneous records exist, whether they are legally and arithmetically reproducible, when they were prepared, and what the AC reported to the court and estate.

## CR-DP-009 — Continuous reporting and final rendition must be distinguished

Do not say that a complete final rendición de cuentas was necessarily due at every intermediate point in liquidation.

The safer legal-accounting frame is:

- continuing documentary traceability and periodic/quarterly liquidation reporting during the liquidation phase;
- preservation of ledgers, bank support, calculations and legally differentiated treatment of operations as they occurred;
- and formal final rendition of accounts at the legally corresponding stage.

Later correction or accounting may reduce continuing harm and may become evidence. It neither retroactively erases an earlier act/omission nor proves earlier guilt by itself.
