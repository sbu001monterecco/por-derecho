# P18 — SUN PARK / COMUNIDAD CROSS-ISLAND PROCEEDINGS INTELLIGENCE

**Lanzarote · Gran Canaria · Tenerife**  
Reusable agentic protocol · Version 1 · 15 August 2026

## Mission

Reconstruct and continuously maintain the complete procedural intelligence of every **currently located Sun Park / Comunidad / LPB / Aweswell / Matkator / Pink–Monterecco / CAM–HNT–MYND related proceeding or institutional file** arising in or materially connected with Lanzarote, Gran Canaria and Tenerife.

This protocol supplements:

- `archive/P17_JUDGE_LAJ_COMMUNICATIONS_INTELLIGENCE_CONCURSO36_15AUG2026.md`, which remains controlling for judge, LAJ and court-office communications in Concurso 36/2012; and
- `archive/judicial-intelligence/ANALYSIS_PROTOCOL.md`, which remains controlling for judicial-document analysis.

The purpose is to prevent one proceeding from being treated as the whole case, while also preventing materially connected evidence from being stranded because different courts, islands, authorities or professional bodies hold different fragments.

The output is not a macro-case allegation and not a conspiracy map. It is a **source-controlled proceeding map** capable of showing:

1. what each organ was asked to decide or verify;
2. which people, entities, assets and Community powers were actually within its scope;
3. what evidence was before it;
4. what it decided and did not decide;
5. how a premise, testimony, document or outcome moved into another proceeding;
6. what later consequence followed; and
7. which finite primary record can confirm, reject or narrow the asserted connection.

## 2. Scope of “proceeding”

Maintain separate classes. Do not call all of them court cases.

### A. Judicial proceedings

- insolvency and its sections/incidents;
- civil preliminary disclosure;
- civil, cambiario and enforcement proceedings;
- criminal preliminary proceedings;
- mortgage enforcement;
- appeals and extraordinary/high-court routes;
- signed judicial orders, judgments, decrees and LAJ acts within them.

### B. Fiscalía / pre-judicial criminal files

- diligencias de investigación, diligencias preprocesales, expedientes gubernativos and territorial referrals;
- identify the Fiscalía office, reference, destination court if any and whether the act is receipt, investigation, archive, referral or merits decision.

### C. Judicial-governance and court-office files

- CGPJ DI/recurso/inspection files;
- TSJ or other competence treatment concerning a judge;
- LAJ supervisory/coordination files;
- distinguish judicial discipline, court functioning, LAJ supervision, appeal and criminal investigation.

### D. Administrative, tourism, urbanism and transparency files

- Cabildo de Lanzarote tourism/order files;
- Ayuntamiento de Yaiza works, licence, activity, tax and information-access files;
- tourism-register and inspection acts;
- Comisionado de Transparencia proceedings.

### E. Professional-regulatory files

- ICALPA, COA Lanzarote, COAGC and any appellate professional body;
- a technical reference, visado number or college identifier is not automatically a disciplinary proceeding.

### F. Police registrations without a located assigned proceeding

- preserve registrations, receipt/rejection status and requested evidence preservation;
- do not describe them as police investigations until a diligencias/atestado/reference or substantive action is located.

### G. Contextual or comparator proceedings

Include only in a clearly separated lane where they concern a downstream operator, recurring court, professional or evidential method but **do not decide Sun Park/Comunidad merits**. Never import an allegation or finding from a comparator into the Sun Park record.

## 3. Geographic allocation

Record at least four geographic fields:

1. **originating facts / asset location**;
2. **first-instance or administrative organ location**;
3. **appeal/supervisory organ location**; and
4. **present custodian location**.

Examples:

- a Lanzarote-origin dispute may be decided on appeal by the Audiencia Provincial in Gran Canaria;
- a Gran Canaria concurso may produce testimony used in an Arrecife criminal or notarial/registry process;
- a Tenerife enforcement or criminal proceeding may concern Matkator rights physically linked to Sun Park;
- a national regulator may hold a cross-island file without being assigned to one island.

Do not allocate a proceeding solely by where a later appeal or recipient institution sits.

## 7. Cross-proceeding transmission matrix

For each material document or factual premise, create a chain:

`origin proceeding → document/act → transmission mechanism → receiving proceeding → treatment → downstream use`.

Priority transmission families:

1. Concurso 36/2012 → DP 1132/2018 / Arrecife;
2. DP 1132/2018 / Auto 804 → later Fiscalía, court and public descriptions of possession/operator/authority;
3. Community minutes/certificates → Concurso 36/2012, civil claims and 2022 project/title representations;
4. Mercantil testimonios 2018/2021/2022 → notary, Registry, Arrecife, Fiscalía and DP 1901;
5. Diligencias Preliminares 1041/2017 → credit-assignment knowledge in Concurso 36/2012 and later complaints;
6. Arrecife enforcement/dación → 2022 deed, 2023 termination and current title/remedy analysis;
7. Tenerife cambiario/ETJ → Matkator title/remate and DP 748;
8. DIP 2/2026 → DP 1901/2026;
9. CGPJ/TSJ/LAJ supervisory files → only the institutional questions actually submitted, not automatic proof of underlying facts;
10. Yaiza/Cabildo/tourism records → title, promoter, operator, Community and unit-perimeter representations.

A document mentioned in one proceeding is not proven to have been before another without a filing, service record, testimony, remisión, order or other transmission evidence.

## 8. Fragmentation and identity tests

Before saying “same facts”, compare:

- date/period;
- person/entity;
- legal capacity;
- asset/finca/right;
- Community versus CEXP;
- LPB estate versus Matkator/third-party property;
- conduct alleged;
- offence/cause of action;
- victim/affected interest;
- evidence available at the time;
- remedy requested;
- issue actually decided;
- finality and appeal status.

Classify the relationship as:

- SAME_EVENT_SAME_ISSUE;
- SAME_EVENT_DIFFERENT_LEGAL_ISSUE;
- RELATED_EVENT_NEW_EVIDENCE;
- DOWNSTREAM_CONSEQUENCE;
- COMMON_DOCUMENT_DIFFERENT_SCOPE;
- COMPARATOR_ONLY;
- NO MATERIAL CONNECTION;
- UNRESOLVED.

## 9. Mandatory safeguards

Never convert:

- registration into investigation;
- a Fiscalía referral into a merits decision;
- a tourism/municipal file into validation of civil title;
- a Community act into authority over all private units or the hotel business;
- a provisional criminal archive into civil/tourism title;
- an appellate act into de novo investigation;
- an AP decision seated in Gran Canaria into a Gran Canaria-origin dispute;
- a technical/visado identifier into a professional-disciplinary file;
- one proceeding’s evidence into another proceeding’s knowledge without transmission proof;
- a later consequence into causation;
- recurrence of actors/courts into coordination;
- a comparator case into proof about Sun Park;
- absence from the reviewed repository into non-existence.

Preserve adverse and exculpatory material with equal prominence, including decisions that protected the estate, refused CAM-related acts, rejected a party’s possession theory, answered an access request for a lawful custody reason or otherwise narrowed the Project Sun Rock thesis.
