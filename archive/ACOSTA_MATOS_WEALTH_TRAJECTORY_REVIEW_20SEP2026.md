## 1. Why the trajectory matters

The relevant economic roles are property owner, developer, builder, hotel manager, investor, financier and participant in recurring-income structures. They can produce distinct benefits without identical ownership. The later whole-platform correction means that a missing direct hotel-income transfer does not close a possible opportunity, mandate, commitment, security, represented-authority or financing-benefit enquiry. Every such connection still needs proof and an applicable remedy.

## 3. Turnover series: do not sum incomparable figures

The thread recorded a 2023 EUR28m **forecast**, later reported 2024 EUR44m turnover, approximately EUR50m reported for 2025, and a EUR60m **2026 forecast**. These relate to the hotel business as reported, not the family's personal income, assets or net profit. ## 4. The direct Sun Park / HNT connection

The [2022 BORME announcement](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BORME-C-2022-7271) expressly identifies transfer of the Sun Park economic unit from Construcciones Acosta Matos to Hotel New Trend for transformation and operation as MYND Yaiza. The [June 2024 mercantile publication](https://www.boe.es/diario_borme/txt.php?id=BORME-A-2024-122-35&lang=es) records Grupo Patrimonial Acosta Matos as HNT's sole shareholder.

These are concrete corporate links. They do not independently establish that all disputed third-party property or operating rights were validly included. Obtain the asset/liability schedules, conveyances, consideration, original authority and property-by-property history.

The source-controlled financing chronology records Series F/G MYND-related financing totalling **EUR6,573,703.10**, split **EUR1,598,849.32** and **EUR4,974,853.78**. Use the exact source and instrument status rather than infer application to each invoice, wage, property or recipient. Commitment, subscription, disbursement, loan balance, expenditure and criminal benefit are different quantities.

The [GC/836/P06 BOE award](https://www.boe.es/boe/dias/2023/05/26/pdfs/BOE-A-2023-12305.pdf) identifies HNT, Yaiza, EUR11,469,714 investment, EUR3,440,914.20 subsidy and 60 jobs. Award is not payment or fulfilment. RIC tax relief, RICPE capital, national regional incentive, State-aid permission and ERDF contribution require separate identification and reconciliation; do not count a co-financing share again as an additional subsidy.
