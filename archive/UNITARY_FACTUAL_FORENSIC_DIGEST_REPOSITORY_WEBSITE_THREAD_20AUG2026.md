## 4. Correct source-status rule for the factual digest

For all future digests, witness declarations must be integrated as a separate evidence class:

`PRIMARY DOCUMENT` ≠ `SOURCE-DERIVED DECLARATION` ≠ `PARTY ALLEGATION` ≠ `LEGAL CONCLUSION`.

A source-derived declaration can prove or support:

- that the witness made or held a particular observation, recollection, understanding or question;
- notice or chronology, if independently attributable and date-controlled;
- a retrieval lead;
- context for why a document request was made.

It cannot, merely by repeating a documentary proposition, upgrade that proposition into independently corroborated fact.
