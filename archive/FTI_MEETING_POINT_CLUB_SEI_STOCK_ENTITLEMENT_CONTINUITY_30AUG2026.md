# FTI / Meeting Point / Club SEI — Stock Entitlement and Insolvency Continuity Note

**Date:** 30 August 2026  
**Status:** Investigative continuity proposition; not a concluded factual or legal finding.

## Continuity rule

> **Small percentage does not mean immaterial. Test entitlement first, scale second.**

The explanatory “offside” analogy is used only to preserve the logic: if the relevant legal, commercial, accounting or insolvency boundary was crossed, a small quantitative share does not by itself cure the underlying issue. The applicable legal, accounting and insolvency tests must be established independently from the analogy.

## Proposition to test

From at least the 2017–2018 period, contemporaneous material relating to **Sun Park / Lava Verde / Club SEI** should be tested for whether **Meeting Point or the wider FTI perimeter** was presenting, marketing, allocating or economically relying upon hotel or room stock as available before the legal basis for title, control, contractual entitlement or authority had been secured.

Contemporaneous brochures, booking interfaces and live marketing are evidence of presentation and marketing. They do **not**, by themselves, prove loaded inventory, concluded bookings, recognised revenue, balance-sheet recognition, legal title or contractual entitlement. Those stronger propositions require independent records.

The specific balance-sheet proposition is therefore an evidential question to be tested against accounting records, management accounts, inventory/capacity systems, contracts, audit material, financing documents and insolvency records; it is not inferred merely from retail marketing.

## Spain / Germany financial relevance

Where the group’s financial condition was being assessed in Spain or Germany, the review should test whether the Sun Park / Club SEI position entered, directly or indirectly, any assessment or representation concerning:

- solvency and liquidity / cash flow;
- ability to continue trading;
- security, collateral or reassurance offered to counterparties;
- expected returns or profitability;
- forward hotel capacity or room stock;
- supplier and creditor confidence;
- presentations to financiers, auditors, courts, insolvency professionals, SEPI or other public bodies.

Even if quantitatively small compared with total group stock, the issue remains potentially material to the compliance analysis **if** legal entitlement or control was absent, premature, unsupported or misstated. Scale is a separate question from entitlement.

## Why the scale point must not defeat the inquiry

Sun Park / Club SEI should not be dismissed as immaterial merely because it represented, or may have represented, only a small fraction of Meeting Point / FTI’s overall hotel capacity. The threshold inquiry is whether the relevant stock, economic benefit, room capacity, contractual availability or return was treated as available at all, and on what lawful basis.

Only after that threshold has been established should the review quantify the financial significance, creditor effect, accounting consequence, insolvency consequence or public-money consequence.

The “offside” analogy is therefore a continuity aid, not a legal conclusion: the question is first whether the line was crossed, not how far beyond the line the relevant conduct extended.

## Insolvency and pre-insolvency continuity

The later German insolvency and the Spanish insolvency / pre-insolvency record should be reviewed together to determine:

1. who knew what and when about Sun Park / Lava Verde / Club SEI;
2. which contemporaneous documents existed in Spain and Germany;
3. whether office-holders and professionals reconstructed how the stock entered commercial, operational, accounting or financial presentation;
4. whether the position was disclosed to courts, auditors, insolvency practitioners, creditors, financiers, SEPI or other public bodies;
5. whether any unsupported or incomplete representation was corrected, explained, left unresolved or obscured;
6. whether any effect on creditor treatment, public funding, security, recoveries, solvency analysis or cash-flow analysis can be evidenced;
7. whether Spanish and German records tell the same story or expose a material discrepancy that requires explanation.

## Allegation / proof firewall

There is a basis to investigate the user’s allegation that later professional or private-actor conduct may have involved coordination, concealment, suppression, sanitisation or other conduct capable of obscuring the earlier Sun Park / Club SEI position.

**That is an allegation requiring proof and is not stated here as a concluded fact.** No individual, professional, office-holder, institution or public body is to be attributed misconduct without source-specific evidence establishing the relevant act, knowledge, authority or duty, chronology and causal significance.

The forensic task is therefore to establish, document by document: **who knew what; when; in which capacity; under what authority; what was represented; what was omitted; what was later disclosed or corrected; and what consequence, if any, followed for creditors, public money or insolvency outcomes.**

## Evidence standard

This note does not convert a live booking or retail interface, brochure, marketing page or room display into proof of loaded inventory, bookings, revenue, ownership, balance-sheet recognition, lawful control or contractual entitlement.

Those propositions remain separately evidential and must be proved from the appropriate primary records. Equally, absence of a large quantitative exposure must not be used as a substitute for answering the entitlement question.
