## 1. Evidential vocabulary

- **PRIMARY_SOURCE_FACT** — direct BBC correspondence, BBC programme material, contemporaneous correspondence, preserved group capture, court/registry record or other first-order record.
- **INSTITUTIONAL_STATUS** — what the BBC or another institution expressly said it had done or decided.
- **ADVERSE_SOURCE_ASSERTION** — a customer, contributor, opposing party or publisher's assertion; proves the assertion was made, not its truth.
- **COUNSEL_POSITION** — position advanced by Sun Park/Aweswell counsel.
- **PERSONAL_RECOLLECTION / GROUP_POSITION** — statement made by the affected group; requires independent corroboration for contested facts.
- **REASONABLE_INFERENCE** — inference tied to documented facts but not independently proved.
- **WORKING_HYPOTHESIS** — a proposition to be tested by disclosure or witness evidence.
- **OPEN_EVIDENCE_GAP** — material proposition for which the necessary primary record has not been located.

## 2. What can presently be reconstructed about the BBC item

### 2.1 Official programme record

A contemporaneously preserved BBC programme page identifies the programme as *Rip Off Britain: Holidays*, Series 7, Episode 12 of 15, scheduled/broadcast on BBC One on 22 January 2019 at 09:15 for 45 minutes. The BBC synopsis included the proposition that the programme would examine a sunshine resort that had started turning away guests who had booked and paid for stays. **PRIMARY_SOURCE_FACT.**

The preserved programme record identifies presenters Angela Rippon, Gloria Hunniford and Julia Somerville and lists production credits including executive producer Rob Unsworth and series producer Gaby Koppel. **PRIMARY_SOURCE_FACT.**

### 2.2 Present limit on reconstructing the transmission

The connected source set does **not yet contain a certified master recording or complete verbatim transcript of the Sun Park segment**. The current reconstruction therefore uses:

1. the BBC's contemporaneous programme synopsis;
2. BBC pre-broadcast correspondence;
3. Sun Park's pre-broadcast replies and legal notices;
4. the BBC's later 2025 statement quoting part of the Sun Park position that it says was carried in the programme; and
5. later archive/availability correspondence.

Until the transmitted audiovisual copy, script/running order and legal/editorial file are obtained, no page should imply that every sentence, image, caption, contributor statement or juxtaposition in the broadcast has been independently reconstructed. **OPEN_EVIDENCE_GAP.**

### 2.3 Sun Park's position was not wholly omitted

In February 2025, BBC series producer Lizz Martin stated that the 2019 programme had reflected Sun Park's position and specifically said that the broadcast included the explanation that activities for members and guests had ceased because of alleged illegal activities against the hotel by third parties. **INSTITUTIONAL_STATUS / PRIMARY_SOURCE_FACT as to what the BBC says it broadcast.**

This materially narrows the current complaint. The defensible question is not whether the BBC gave Sun Park *no* position at all. It is whether the programme's weighting, verification, provenance, editing and context fairly conveyed the underlying distinctions and whether later evidence warranted correction or qualification.

## 3. Provenance reconstruction

### 3.1 The BBC's first located formal approach — 30 November 2018

The first formal BBC approach located in Gmail is a 30 November 2018 email from Philip Jeffery of *Rip Off Britain*. It said that the BBC had been contacted about Sun Park by four named customers, including a married couple and two other customers, and put allegations concerning bookings, payments, closure and refunds to Sun Park for response. **PRIMARY_SOURCE_FACT as to contact and allegations; ADVERSE_SOURCE_ASSERTION as to the truth of customer allegations.**

For public publication, the four customers should normally be described as **four named customers/contributors** unless individual identity is necessary to explain a provenance link already in the public domain. Their contact details must not be published.

### 3.2 BBC editorial reason for continuing

In the January 2019 production correspondence, the BBC said that it intended to proceed because several customers had described confusion and disappointment. It invited a further broadcast response and said that, if no further response were supplied, it would reflect Sun Park's position using the material already received. **PRIMARY_SOURCE_FACT / INSTITUTIONAL_STATUS.**

This establishes the BBC's stated editorial rationale. It does not establish that the customer accounts were correct, independently verified or complete.

### 3.3 Sun Park placed ownership / operation / possession / legal-dispute issues before the BBC

The pre-broadcast correspondence and the 3 January 2019 letter before action put the BBC on notice that Sun Park disputed the simplified narrative, distinguished the operation from underlying ownership and litigation, alleged a material change of control/possession by third parties, and disputed customer characterisations. **COUNSEL_POSITION / PRIMARY_SOURCE_FACT as notice.**

The letter before action also raised source-provenance concerns and alleged links between adverse customer/community activity and the developing BBC story. Those allegations prove that the BBC had notice of the provenance challenge before broadcast; they do not prove the underlying coordination alleged by the group. **COUNSEL_POSITION.**

### 3.4 October 2018 New Horizons provenance lead

A preserved New Horizons group capture dated 8 October 2018 records an **Ian Griffiths** seeking access to the group. A member described him as a journalist investigating Sun Park. **Phil Lee** stated that he had been in contact with him and recommended that he be invited/admitted. **PRIMARY_SOURCE_FACT as to what the capture records.**

Separate later public programme-credit material identifies an Ian Griffiths as a producer/edit producer on *Rip Off Britain*. That later professional identification does **not** independently prove that the October 2018 group account was the same person or that he was acting for the BBC at the time. The identity/capacity bridge remains an **OPEN_EVIDENCE_GAP**.

This is presently the strongest located lead for reconstructing how a journalist may have entered the former-customer network before the BBC's formal 30 November approach. It must not be upgraded into a statement that New Horizons, Phil Lee or any private actor commissioned or caused the BBC programme.

### 3.5 No primary CAM commissioning link located

The repository, Gmail and Drive scan has not located a primary email, instruction, payment, production note, contributor agreement, message, call record or witness statement showing that CAM, JDAM, another Acosta Matos-related person, Hotel New Trend, Canarian Hospitality or MYND:

- contacted the BBC to initiate the story;
- instructed a customer or group administrator to contact the BBC;
- paid for or supplied the BBC item;
- supplied images or scripts to the BBC;
- directed the BBC's editorial framing; or
- coordinated with the BBC production team.

**Status: NOT ESTABLISHED / OPEN EVIDENCE GAP.**

## 10. Publication rule

A bilingual public page may be published because a sufficient **primary BBC source set has been located to support a limited provenance/notice chronology**. It must prominently state that the broadcast itself is only partially reconstructed until the archive master/transcript is obtained.

The public page must not:

- state that CAM caused or coordinated the programme;
- state that the BBC proved the customer allegations;
- state that the material-control event was finally adjudicated unlawful;
- state that CAM used the programme in court unless the primary court-use record is later located;
- state that Mike/Julie/New Horizons caused the BBC programme;
- state a quantified booking or reputational loss without supporting accounting/expert evidence;
- attribute malice, dishonesty or recklessness to a BBC employee without evidence.

## 11. Current forensic conclusion

The source set supports a **BBC provenance problem worth investigating**, not a proved private-actor/BBC coordination theory.

The BBC had a customer-source path, received Sun Park's rebuttal and provenance challenge before broadcast, proceeded for its stated consumer-interest reasons, and says it included at least a concise Sun Park counter-position. An October 2018 New Horizons capture now supplies a significant pre-formal-approach provenance lead involving a journalist named Ian Griffiths and Phil Lee; the critical identity/employer/assignment bridge remains open. Later publication and commercial records show a continuing adverse predecessor narrative and favourable successor narrative, but the BBC's causal contribution to any later CAM/HNT/MYND benefit has not been established.

The next evidential objective is therefore finite: **obtain the BBC production/source file and any actual downstream adverse-party use records, then measure rather than assume the claimed benefit and detriment.**
