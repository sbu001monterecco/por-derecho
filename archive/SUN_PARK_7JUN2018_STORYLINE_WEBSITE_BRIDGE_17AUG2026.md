# SUN PARK — 7 JUNE 2018 STORYLINE / WEBSITE BRIDGE

**Date:** 17 August 2026  
**Status:** INTERNAL STORYLINE ADDENDUM / WEBSITE IMPLEMENTATION GUIDE  
**Read with:** `SUN_PARK_7JUN2018_RUNUP_SOURCE_INGEST_17AUG2026.md`, P19, master storyline, correction register and missing-evidence register.

## 1. Editorial objective

The public website should stop treating **7 June 2018** as either:

- a one-line “takeover” assertion; or
- an isolated property incident.

The stronger evidence-led story is a **sequence of changing control, access, customer contact and project preparation**, while preserving the difference between:

- a historic minority-unit possession dispute;
- CAM's creditor/defined-unit-owner position;
- Community authority;
- practical hotel-wide control;
- formal 2022 title;
- later redevelopment/operation.

The visitor should understand the chain without being asked to accept criminal conclusions.

## 2. Reader-facing storyline in eight beats

### Beat 1 — Sun Park was one operating hotel but legally fragmented

The site's first mental model remains:

> **One hotel business. Several legal perimeters.**

LPB, the Comunidad de Propietarios, the Comunidad de Explotación/operator, Monterecco/Pink, Matkator, CAM and other owners must not be collapsed.

### Beat 2 — the possession/governance conflict predates the concurso

The newly controlled AP Sentencia 89/2014 shows that minority owners had already litigated possession of **18 defined bungalows**. The court ultimately ordered the Comunidad de Explotación and Monterecco to vacate those units.

This is important adverse evidence.

Public meaning:

> There were genuine unit-level possession conflicts before and during the early insolvency period.

Not:

> The minority perimeter had authority over the whole hotel.

### Beat 3 — the Community cost/debt/access conflict was still being worked through before CAM arrived

The 2016 meeting transcript shows representatives on opposing sides discussing:

- who had actually borne maintenance and utility costs;
- the distinction between LPB and the operating entity;
- the logic and historical origin of Community charges;
- voting/access disputes;
- possible regularisation/offset of costs and debt; and
- tourism-use constraints and majority/minority interests.

This is a **pre-CAM institutional/economic context**, not proof that any debt was fabricated.

### Beat 4 — by late 2017 / early 2018 the project alleges a new phase: owner-like entry, inspection, measurement and client/staff messaging

The website should present the pre-7-Jun warning record as a chronology, not as a verdict:

**31 Oct 2017** → alleged owner-like visit/security/client departure messages  
**8 Jan 2018** → alleged contested access/inspection/measurement  
**26 Feb 2018** → contemporaneous email memorialising alleged force/security/ownership statements and planned measuring  
**1 Mar 2018** → alleged hotel tour/project presentation/measurements  
**11 Apr 2018** → criminal complaint records these allegations **before** the later 7-Jun event

The phrase **“before the takeover”** is justified chronologically. The phrase **“proof of conspiracy”** is not.

### Beat 5 — 18 May to 7 June is the security-to-control hinge

The Community/security decision immediately before 7 June must be shown as a distinct question:

> What exact security mandate existed, who voted, who paid, which doors/areas did it cover, and did it authorise exclusion/change of possession beyond ordinary security?

This is a better public question than asserting that a Community vote automatically authorised or invalidated everything that followed.

### Beat 6 — 7 June is the material-control threshold, not the formal-title date

Controlled website formulation:

> **The available record supports a material change in access, security and practical operation on 7 June 2018: locks/cylinders and chains/padlocks were changed, private security was installed or present, and the onsite operating side was excluded from normal access. Gil Marer characterises the cumulative effect as a seizure/full practical possession of the hotel. Whole-hotel legal title or possession was not transferred by a reviewed judicial act on that date, and the exact authority and finca-by-finca reach remain disputed.**

Mandatory caveat:

> **The principal witness used in Control 21 says JDAM was not physically present on 7 June.**

Do not depict him visually or textually as the onsite actor that day unless a stronger source emerges.

### Beat 7 — after control shifted, the struggle also became one over customers, identity and narrative

The website should connect, but not conflate:

- direct contact with guests/residents;
- instructions or representations about who now controlled/owned the complex;
- customers preparing/leaving/collecting belongings;
- later use of `Sun Park` identity/customer-contact routes;
- Lava Verde public presentation;
- private/social-media discussion; and
- later media amplification.

The 6-Feb-2019 DI 248 submission proves that material concerning the Lava Verde web, a “new owner” meeting and Facebook communications was formally presented to Fiscalía.

It does **not** by itself prove who authored each post or that all clients were deliberately mobilised.

### Beat 8 — later public/project life must be kept separate from the legality of the 2018 event

The sequence can be shown:

`Sun Park operation → pre-title access/project preparation → 7-Jun material-control shift → Lava Verde → Club Sei / Meeting Point ecosystem → RICPE → 2022 title → MYND Yaiza`

The audience must be told:

> Chronological and physical continuity is not automatic legal or criminal responsibility.

### Section D — Who did what?

No group accusations. Use actor cards:

**LPAM** — onsite acts/instructions attributed by witness; exact powers/title to verify.  
**ACR** — Community/security role and onsite presence attributed; exact mandate to verify.  
**FMMM** — predecessor administration/documentary role; presence/acts must be independently sourced by episode.  
**JDAM** — pre-7-Jun access/project/ownership representations alleged and source-traceable; **principal witness says not physically present on 7-Jun**.  
**AC** — exact security request/authority/perimeter remains a central source question.  
**CAM** — creditor/defined-unit owner/de facto beneficiary roles must be separated from whole-hotel title.

### Section E — What courts and Fiscalía actually did

Show adverse and favourable material together.

- AP 89/2014: minority claimants won defined-unit possession case.
- DI 248: complainants later put CAM/AC/security/Lava Verde/new-owner material before Fiscalía.
- 7-May-2019: Fiscalía archived DI 248 and treated the disputes as non-criminal/sub judice on the material before it.

This section is essential to credibility.

## 12. Storyline lock

The website should ultimately allow a sceptical reader to understand this proposition:

> **The significance of 7 June 2018 is not that it magically transferred title. It is that, against a background of earlier unit-level possession disputes and a pre-existing Community conflict, the available record supports a sudden change in practical access and operational control after months of alleged owner-like access, measuring/project preparation and client/staff messaging. The legal authority and responsibility for each act remain matters to prove. What happened afterwards — customer displacement, project rebranding, redevelopment and later formal title — must be traced from that control threshold without turning chronology into automatic culpability.**
