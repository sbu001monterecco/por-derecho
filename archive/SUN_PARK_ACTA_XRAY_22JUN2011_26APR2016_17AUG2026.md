**Date:** 17 August 2026  
**Status:** SOURCE-CONTROLLED DOCUMENT REVIEW; legal validity remains separate.

## Controlling question

Both minutes distinguish ownership/participation represented at the meeting from owners treated as entitled to vote because they were considered current with Community obligations. The source-supported question is:

> How did a meeting with a dominant LPB coefficient represented come to have resolutions approved by a much smaller coefficient classified by the organisers as entitled to vote, and what documentary/legal basis supported that classification?

This is a governance/debt/voting verification question, not by itself proof of criminal falsification or unlawful capture.

## 22 June 2011

**Source:** SPACTA-001, 16-page scanned original, SHA-256 controlled. The minute is headed `ACTA DE LA JUNTA GENERAL EXTRAORDINARIA DE LA COMUNIDAD DE PROPIETARIOS SUN PARK` and states 13:30 commencement in Playa Blanca.

### Meeting and agenda

The minute records a dispute before formal constitution involving LPB representatives and a move of the meeting to apartment 509. This is verified only as the minute-maker's recorded account; the underlying confrontation requires corroboration.

The agenda joins in one event: prior-minute approval; Community accounts; approval/recovery of alleged owner debts and authority for the president to act; termination/account-rendering concerning Comunidad de Explotación Sun Park and a replacement maintenance contract; new administrator; and questions.

### Ownership represented versus voting pool

The attendee schedules record LPB representation across **166 bungalows (63.910%)**, commercial premises and two solarium/pool elements. Adding the individually printed LPB coefficients gives approximately **75.671% represented coefficient**, subject to title/coefficient verification and transcription error.

The same minute separately lists owners treated as `CON DERECHO A VOTO` after checking Community-payment status. The visible listed coefficients total approximately **6.930%**.

**Control:** the disparity is source-supported by the minute. Whether the coefficients, debt/current-payment classifications and vote exclusions were legally correct is OPEN.

### 2008 exploitation/maintenance arrangement expressly acknowledged

The minute itself discusses the **25-Jul-2008** pact. It records the president explaining that owners integrated into the Comunidad de Explotación were exempted from ordinary Community monthly charges in exchange for CEXP assuming the relevant maintenance/budget expenditure. It further records that, if CEXP had performed and could prove performance, units in the exploitation would be current as at 30-Jun-2011; the dispute then turns to proof of those payments.

This means the 2011 minute does not describe the historic liability model as a simple ordinary fee regime. Actual performance/payment still requires bank, accounting, invoices and contracts.

The minute also records lawyer Esteban López Noriega discussing the 25-Jul-2008 Community/CEXP pact, de facto continuation after the stated period, and a conflict-of-interest concern arising from overlapping representation. This proves what the minute records, not a final legal determination.

### Authority effects

The minute records the voting-eligible pool approving debt/recovery positions, ratification of CEXP maintenance-contract termination, a Pamanil maintenance proposal and Pamanil administration. It is signed under `Vº Bº La Presidenta` by Asunción Aizpurúa Sánchez.

**2011 mechanism to test:** `dominant LPB represented coefficient → debt/current-payment classification → small voting pool → debt/maintenance/administrator resolutions → downstream governance apparatus`.

## 26 April 2016

**Source:** SPACTA-019, preserved 77-page PDF with readable text layer and annexes.

### Pre-meeting package

The preserved April photographs now identify:

- SPACTA-009: signed explanatory letter dated **6-Apr-2016** about the 26-Apr meeting;

- SPACTA-012–018: supporting schedules/debt-list material still requiring complete classification.

The explanatory/convocation material tells owners considered not current with Community fees that they could attend/participate although they were said not to have voting rights. Legal effect remains open.

### Recording and professional/security presence

The minute states all present consented to recording the meeting. It also records a Community-contracted security person, lawyer Juan Carlos Prieto Puente and fiscal/accounting adviser Leopoldo Soler Cullen.

**New evidence target:** locate and preserve the actual recording.

### Express participation and voting figures

The minute records:

- **19 owners present/represented**;
- **89.727%** total coefficient present/represented;
- LPB at **72.976%** in the attendee schedule;
- a separate `CON DERECHO A VOTO` list totalling **11.039%**.

It repeatedly records LPB and several other owners as present/represented but without voting rights, disagreeing and asking that their objections be entered in the minute.

### November-2015 challenge disputed inside the ACTA

At point 3, the administrator is recorded as saying nobody had challenged the November-2015 ACTA. The same paragraph records Patricia Domínguez for LPB saying LPB **had** challenged it, followed by the statement that the organisers had no record of that challenge.

**Next proof:** filing receipt, docket and service record.

### 2011 authority explicitly relied upon

The 2016 minute expressly roots the president's claimed authority in the 2-Feb-2011 and 22-Jun-2011 chain and recounts related litigation. The ACTA's summary must be checked against the signed judgments/orders before stating their exact legal effect.

### Historic accounts expressly not approved

At point 5 the administrator states that only limited income/expense information was available, mainly from bank extracts obtained shortly before the meeting, and that the full historical documentation was not available. The meeting records **non-approval of the 2010–2015 accounts**.

This materially qualifies any shorthand that the 2016 meeting approved a fully documented historic accounting position.

### Retroactive fee reset and debt/enforcement powers

At point 8 the minute records a proposed per-bungalow Community charge reduction from **€171.89 to €22.26**, to be applied **retroactively from January 2010 through December 2015** because of the service/fee dispute. It records approval by those entitled to vote, with LPB/other non-voters objecting, and says recalculated debt schedules would be attached.

Points 10–13 then record debt recalculation/negotiation, authority for judicial collection and litigation representation, rejection of a proposed 15% annual surcharge while retaining collection thresholds, and bank/signatory decisions.

At point 7 it records continuation of the administrator while noting his company had changed from **Pamanil S.L. to Pamalexsha S.L.**

## Controlling conclusion

The preserved ACTAS support a document-led mechanism that can be shown without overclaiming:

> A dominant ownership coefficient was present/represented while a much smaller coefficient was treated as entitled to vote because of asserted Community-payment status; that voting pool adopted resolutions affecting governance, historic charges, debt enforcement, maintenance relationships and office-holders. The decisive question is whether the underlying debt and authority chain were valid at each step.

The Project may allege deliberate manufacture, falsification, abuse or criminal instrumentalisation, but those characterisations require actor-specific proof of document falsity/invalidity, knowledge, intent, use, benefit and causation. The ACTAS make the theory more precise and testable; they do not automatically prove it.
