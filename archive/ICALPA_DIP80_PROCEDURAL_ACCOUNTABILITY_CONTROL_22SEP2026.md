## Respondent-awareness asymmetry

The September 2026 opposition gives a new institutional-governance fact. The Administrator expressly refers to ICALPA as one of the institutions to which Gil Marer has complained, and uses that regulatory activity as part of his litigation narrative.

This proves **respondent awareness of the ICALPA route by September 2026**. It does **not** prove how he learned of DIP 80, whether ICALPA served him, what representations he has made, or whether ICALPA has carried out any particular internal act.

The resulting externally visible asymmetry is:

- the respondent can rely on the existence of the professional-regulatory route in court;
- the reporting side has an opening notice, rapporteur identity and many registration receipts;
- the located record still does not identify whether preliminary information ended in archive, formal disciplinary proceedings, suspension, transfer or another reasoned procedural state.

This should be described as **an accountability / traceability issue**, not as proof of favouritism, protection or bad faith by ICALPA.

## Governing procedural framework

The disciplinary regulation hosted by ICALPA provides, in summary:

- preliminary information exists to determine whether a disciplinary file should be opened;
- the rapporteur may carry out necessary initial enquiries;
- the resolution of preliminary information is archive or opening of a disciplinary file;
- archive is notified to the complainant;
- formal opening identifies the respondent, facts, possible classification, instructor, deciding organ and rights, and is communicated to the complainant;
- if a criminal process concerns the same facts, or inseparable facts, the disciplinary procedure is to be initiated and suspended pending the criminal outcome;
- a formal disciplinary file is ordinarily to be resolved within six months, subject to lawful suspension/extension.

This control does not assume that the published CGAE disciplinary regulation is ICALPA's only applicable procedural source in every respect; it uses the regulation as the official published framework requiring ICALPA/CCCA/CGAE clarification where necessary.

## Regional / national routing

The regional request is not to substitute for ICALPA's first-instance merits role. It asks for:

- competence/routing;
- preservation;
- conflict/abstention safeguards;
- clarification of how a preliminary file avoids indefinite procedural limbo;
- identification of any appropriate autonomous-council function where handling by a constituent Bar itself becomes part of the governance question.
