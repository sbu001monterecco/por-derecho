# Project Sun Rock / Por Derecho — Multi-journalist outlet coverage rule

**Control date:** 23 August 2026  
**Status:** mandatory media-campaign coverage control  
**Send control:** `EMAIL_SEND_FINAL_AUTHORIZATION_RULE.md`

**Maximum-distribution overlay:** `archive/MAXIMUM_MEDIA_DISTRIBUTION_MULTI_RECIPIENT_RULE_23AUG2026.md`

## Core rule

A newspaper, digital newspaper, news magazine, broadcaster or comparable editorial organisation is **not treated as fully covered merely because one journalist has been contacted**.

For every material media outlet, identify and pursue at least **two distinct named editorial professionals**, where two suitable current professionals can be verified.

A generic newsroom, `redaccion@`, `info@`, contact form, switchboard, press mailbox or general editorial address does **not** count as the second named journalist. One named journalist plus a generic newsroom Cc therefore remains:

`OUTLET COVERAGE = PARTIAL`.

An outlet-published general or regional newsroom address may nevertheless be used as an honest professional routing route for a named package when no direct address is verified and the package says `A la atención de [name]`. It is never described as that person's direct address. Under the maximum-distribution overlay, all suitable named lanes may be prepared concurrently and no same-outlet response window, spacing rule or two-person ceiling applies.

## Role differentiation

The two or more people must be selected for distinct, documented editorial reasons, not merely to increase recipient count. Prefer differentiated roles such as:

- economics / business / investment;
- investigations / courts / public integrity;
- tourism / hotels / travel;
- local / municipal / island affairs;
- public administration / politics;
- finance / tax / RIC / public funds;
- section editor / commissioning authority; or
- another beat directly tied to a source-controlled module.

For every named professional record:

`NAME → CURRENT ROLE / BEAT → WHY THIS PERSON → STORY MODULE → PUBLIC CONTACT BASIS → PRIOR GMAIL HISTORY → CURRENT COVERAGE STATUS`.

## Coverage states

- `NOT STARTED` — no suitable named journalist sent.
- `PARTIAL — ONE NAMED JOURNALIST` — one named editorial professional sent.
- `PARTIAL — SECOND LANE PREPARED` — first journalist sent and second named lane prepared but unsent.
- `MULTI-JOURNALIST COVERAGE — TWO OR MORE SENT + VERIFIED` — at least two distinct named editorial professionals have received verified, recipient-appropriate packages.
- `DOCUMENTED EXCEPTION` — fewer than two only because a second suitable current editorial professional cannot reasonably be verified, the outlet is genuinely single-person, or another documented editorial reason makes a second approach inappropriate.

A documented exception is not presumed. Record the reason.

## Separate-personalisation rule

Do **not** solve this rule by blindly copying several journalists on the same generic message.

Default:

`OUTLET THESIS → PERSON-SPECIFIC WHY-YOU → ROLE-SPECIFIC SOURCE ANCHORS → ROLE-SPECIFIC FINITE ASK`.

Where two journalists genuinely work the same story together, a shared package may be appropriate, but the package must still explain why both are relevant. Do not mass-CC multiple staff merely to manufacture multi-journalist coverage.

This personalisation rule does not require sequential outreach. Separate tailored packages for several people at one outlet may all be active concurrently.

## Send-control interaction

This rule authorises **research, verification, coverage planning and draft preparation only**.

Every actual transmission to every additional journalist remains a separate outbound act governed by `EMAIL_SEND_FINAL_AUTHORIZATION_RULE.md`. No first-journalist approval extends automatically to a second journalist.

## Retrospective application

This rule applies to media outlets already contacted in the current campaign.

Run a retrospective coverage audit of all active outlets. Where only one named editorial professional has been sent, identify at least one additional suitable current editorial professional and record their role/beat and reason for fit.

Do not describe an outlet as campaign-complete merely because a single journalist or director was contacted.

## Outlet-coverage dashboard requirement

The media continuation state should maintain or link to an outlet-level dashboard showing:

`OUTLET | JOURNALIST 1 + ROLE | STATUS | JOURNALIST 2 + ROLE | STATUS | OPTIONAL JOURNALIST 3 | COVERAGE STATE | NEXT ACTION`.

This is separate from the neutral public-safe journalist/media register and must not expose private contact details on the public website.

## Final principle

The campaign objective is not `one journalist per newspaper`.

It is **multiple credible editorial entry points inside each important outlet**, each selected for a real role-based reason, each receiving a source-led package appropriate to that role, without spam, blind duplication or accidental send.
