## 2. Controlled meaning that must survive deletion

The communication directly supports that the insolvency administrator:

1. addressed the Community presidency in that institutional capacity;
2. asked for a General Meeting to be convened;
3. proposed that the meeting consider adopting an agreement to hire security;
4. stated the purpose as preventing access by “unauthorised persons” and preventing deterioration or improper use of common and privately owned areas;
5. stated that LPB held the majority of participation quotas in the Community.

The document does **not**, by itself, establish:

- who qualified as an “unauthorised person”;
- whole-complex authority;
- title or authority over Matkator or third-party units;
- authority to break locks, change cylinders, place chains, seize keys or perform locksmith acts;
- authority to exclude workers, guests, owners or other rights-holders;
- transfer of title, possession or hotel operation;
- the validity, attendance, voting or scope of a later Community resolution;
- the identity, instructions, payer or conduct of a security contractor;
- that the acts of 7 June 2018 were ordered by the insolvency administrator;
- criminal common purpose or final criminal legal characterisation.

Mandatory publication language: **requested**, **proposed**, **stated**, **later implemented only if separately proved**.

Never paraphrase this email as an order to take possession or as blanket authority for every act performed on 7 June 2018.
