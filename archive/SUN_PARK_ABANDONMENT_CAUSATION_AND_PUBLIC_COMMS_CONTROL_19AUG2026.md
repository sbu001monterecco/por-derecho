## 1. Core causation thesis

The current working thesis is not merely that later descriptions of Sun Park as “abandoned” were incomplete.

The sharper proposition to investigate is:

> **Sun Park was a functioning hospitality/community business before the 7-Jun-2018 rupture. The allegation is that the material/operational displacement contributed to the interruption of that business and that the resulting condition was later re-presented as evidence that the hotel had simply been “abandoned”.**

The causal sequence is therefore the evidential issue:

`operating hotel → access/control rupture → operational interruption / exclusion → later deteriorated or closed condition → “abandoned hotel” narrative → redevelopment/acquisition story`

## 3. Public wording control

Avoid unsupported absolutes such as:

- “the takeover alone caused every later closure/deterioration”;
- “the hotel was full” unless occupancy data for the relevant date is produced;
- “there were no prior operational problems”;
- “every later reference to abandonment was deliberately false”.

Use one of these controlled formulations:

> **The evidence requires a causation test: was Sun Park found abandoned, or did the later condition follow an earlier displacement of the operating business and control structure?**

or, when clearly marked as the claimant's allegation:

> **Our allegation is that they did not encounter an abandoned hotel; the operating business was displaced, and the condition produced after that rupture was later used as part of the abandonment narrative.**

Guests onsite plus forward reservations are relevant to live trading/continuity; detailed occupancy percentages and quantified loss belong in the damages/accounting record.

## 4. Responsibility hierarchy

The public and legal analysis must preserve the distinction between **primary private-actor conduct** and **institutional enablement / failure**.

### Primary alleged conduct

The principal alleged responsibility lies with the private actors who allegedly:

- planned access/control;
- executed or participated in the material shift;
- obtained or exercised practical control;
- advanced redevelopment/ownership/project representations;
- derived commercial/economic benefit.

### Secondary institutional question

The separate question is:

> **Which institutions or office-holders knew what, had what duty/capacity, and by act, omission, silence, delay or non-restoration may have enabled the private operation to succeed, persist or become economically irreversible?**

Do not invert that hierarchy. The durable formulation is:

> **This is not a private operation created by institutional failure. It is an alleged private operation whose success, persistence and later commercial consequences may have been enabled or insufficiently corrected by institutional acts or omissions.**

Institutional actors must remain actor-specific and source-specific. Existing complaints and supervisory proceedings are not findings of guilt.
