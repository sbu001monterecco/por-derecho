## 4. Authority and status rules

Every repository and website analysis of this record must retain these controls:

1. LPB alone is the debtor; the mixed-ownership hotel is not automatically the insolvency estate.
2. A party filing proves that a representation was made, not that its allegations are true.
3. Receipt is not substantive examination; joinder is not acceptance; referral is not a decision.
4. Judicial or LAJ authority must be separated from physical, registry and accounting implementation.
5. A standing or procedural dismissal does not adjudicate merits the court did not examine.
6. A document missing from the reviewed corpus may exist elsewhere; non-location is not evidence of non-existence or deletion.

The operative analytical chain is:

`source → exact proposition → authority/condition → implementation → response/challenge → finality → registry/accounting consequence → remaining proof`
