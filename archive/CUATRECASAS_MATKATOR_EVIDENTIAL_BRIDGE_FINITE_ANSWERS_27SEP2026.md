## Controlling proposition

This is not presented as a conventional unpaid-fees dispute with Matkator. Gil Marer states categorically that Matkator never retained Cuatrecasas and owed it no underlying professional-fee debt. The located July-2019 schedule identifies 20 invoices to AWESWELL and two to LPB, none to Matkator. Matkator nevertheless appears as issuer of four pagarés totalling EUR 182,517.72 and later as judicial debtor in Cambiario 1048/2019 and ETJ 163/2020.

The investigation therefore focuses on the **legal and evidential bridge** between those states: the purpose and conditions of delivery; any agreement, authority, consideration, assumption or guarantee relied upon; liability asserted independently of professional retainer; information used in title formation and service; reconciliation of invoices/WIP/instruments/recoveries; and any later transfer or third-party economic benefit.

The absence of a Matkator retainer or invoice does not itself determine enforceability of a pagaré. No current source establishes a CAM/HNT-connected assignee or beneficiary.

## Three propositions that must not be collapsed

1. Who retained Cuatrecasas.
2. Who owed any underlying professional fee.
3. What liability, if any, arose independently from signing the pagarés.
