# Concurso 36/2012 — classification of `Annex IV - Operaciones de Liquidacion 1SEP2022.pdf`

- **Control date:** 29 August 2026
- **Purpose:** prevent a connected-source candidate from being mistaken for the missing primary Insolvency Administration quarterly report.
- **Status:** `DERIVATIVE_ANALYTICAL_MATERIAL__NOT_PRIMARY_AC_FILING`
- **Evidence effect:** none on the P0 family denominator.

## Source-level finding

A connected-mail attachment named `Annex IV - Operaciones de Liquidacion 1SEP2022.pdf` was inspected after it surfaced during the targeted recovery sweep for the July/September 2022 AC-report gap.

The document is a 26-page analytical workbook/export. Its visible content contains recalculations, commentary on AC fees and accounting, legal/factual annotations, internal positions and cross-references to other acts and reports. The wording and structure are not those of a standalone authenticated Insolvency Administration quarterly filing. The inspected copy does not establish a court filing registration, LAJ receipt, AC signature, report ordinal, or identity with filing 5367/2022 or the 15th quarterly liquidation report.

Accordingly:

- it may be retained as a **locator/cross-check** for dates, concepts and possible source references;
- statements in it are **not promoted as primary evidence merely because they appear in this document**;
- it does **not** close the missing underlying document filed as `5367/2022`;
- it does **not** establish that `5367/2022` was the 15th quarterly liquidation report;
- it does **not** close `AC-REPORT-SERIES` or `FINAL-STAGE`;
- any useful proposition found in it must be traced to the referenced primary court/LAJ/AC/bank/accounting source before promotion.

## Controlled consequence

The top recovery target remains:

1. recover the actual quarterly report filed as `5367/2022`, including any later service/supply copy;
2. determine its date and ordinal from the primary source itself;
3. independently locate the 15th quarterly liquidation report if it is not the same authenticated source;
4. only then reconcile the final accounts/conclusion chain.

This classification is deliberately source-protective: a plausible derivative bridge is useful for discovery, but it cannot be used to manufacture primary-source closure.
