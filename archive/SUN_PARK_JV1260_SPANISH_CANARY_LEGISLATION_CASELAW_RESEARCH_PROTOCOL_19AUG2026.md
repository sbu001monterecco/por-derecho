## 1. Purpose

This protocol preserves the substantive research architecture developed in the 19-Aug-2026 ChatGPT thread concerning a possible mismatch between:

- the physical or registered description of a Sun Park unit as an `apartamento`;
- its juridical identity as a finca within a horizontally divided property;
- its possible status as a tourist accommodation unit within an integrated hotel establishment;
- the rights and burdens arising from CEXP, Community, contractual, planning and tourism arrangements; and
- the bundle of rights actually necessary to obtain possession from a hotel operator.

The controlling question is not whether the word `apartamento` appeared in a deed or pleading. It is:

> **What complete juridical asset did the Arrecife court have to decide, what version of that asset was put before it, and did any material omission or re-characterisation causally affect the decision?**

A useful shorthand is `legally truncated asset`: an asset described through title or physical form while material possession, exploitation, tourist-use, contractual, Community or regulatory relationships are removed from the decision model. This is a research concept, not a finding that truncation or deception occurred in JV 1260/2011.

## 7. Spanish criminal-law module

### 7.1 Estafa procesal

Research the historical and current versions of the Código Penal provisions governing fraud, especially CP article 250.1.7, and build an element matrix for:

1. a judicial proceeding;
2. manipulation of evidence or analogous procedural fraud;
3. intentional deception rather than error, negligence or arguable legal interpretation;
4. judicial error or materially distorted judicial understanding;
5. a resolution caused by that error;
6. economic prejudice to a party or third person;
7. causal connection;
8. attempt versus consummation;
9. actor-specific participation and benefit;
10. prescription and competent jurisdiction.

Test separately whether an omission can qualify, when a technically true but cumulatively misleading presentation can qualify, and when a disputed legal characterisation is insufficient.

For JV1260/AP89, the present candidate questions include:

- whether material 2008 participation/cession context was omitted or selectively framed;
- whether the maintenance-key event was accurately described when introduced on appeal;
- whether any actor knew the asserted description was materially misleading;
- whether the AP's possession inference depended upon it;
- whether the same outcome would have followed on the complete record.

No matrix may mark an element `PROVED` merely because the outcome was adverse or because a different procedure might have been preferable.

### 7.2 Documentary and testimonial offences

Where the source record justifies it, test separately:

- authenticity and alteration of documents;
- false public, official, mercantile or private documents;
- knowing use of a false document in court;
- false Community certificates or minutes;
- false witness or expert evidence;
- the different procedural status of party allegation, witness testimony, expert evidence and counsel submission.

Do not call a disputed pleading `false testimony` or a contested certificate `falsified` without satisfying the offence-specific elements.

### 7.3 Actor-specific discipline

For each claimant, company, Community office-holder, lawyer, procurator, witness or later institutional user, build:

`POWER / ROLE → KNOWLEDGE → EXACT ACT OR OMISSION → DOCUMENT USED → JUDICIAL OR INSTITUTIONAL RELIANCE → CONSEQUENCE → BENEFIT / HARM → CAUSATION → INNOCENT EXPLANATION`.

Do not transfer knowledge or intent automatically between client, counsel, company, Community and later acquirer.

## 13. Causation and falsification tests

For every candidate deception answer:

> **What would JPI nº 4 or the AP probably have done on the complete, accurate record?**

Test whether the court might have:

- reached the same result;
- required a different action;
- rejected precario because another title existed;
- limited relief to specific units or keys;
- required another party;
- distinguished CEXP from Monterecco;
- treated the 28-May event differently;
- granted possession despite the hotel/tourism context.

Actively seek the strongest claimant-side explanation:

- genuine registered ownership and possessory grievance;
- non-participation in CEXP;
- lack or expiry of operator title;
- actual disclosure of the hotel/tourism structure;
- full opportunity for defence;
- accurate maintenance-key description;
- a lawful appellate evidential inference;
- good-faith reliance on legal advice;
- immateriality of any omission;
- limitation of current remedies.

The Project hypothesis survives only if it remains stronger after these alternatives are tested.
