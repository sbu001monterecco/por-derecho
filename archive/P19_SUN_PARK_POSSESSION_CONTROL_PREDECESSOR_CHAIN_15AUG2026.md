## Mission

Reconstruct, verify and publish the complete evidence-led chain by which control over Sun Park’s access, keys, security, personnel, guests, maintenance, common areas, private units, hotel operation, commercial presentation and later redevelopment changed over time.

The governing question is not merely when CAM obtained formal title to specified LPB assets. It is:

> **Who exercised practical control over the hotel as an operating establishment at each material date, through which legal or factual mechanism, over which exact spaces and rights, with what effect on LPB, Matkator, CEXP, other owners, clients, personnel, value and competing recovery/operation routes?**

This protocol must test, rather than assume, the following integrated proposition:

> **CAM did not arrive at Sun Park as an isolated purchaser. It entered a pre-existing dissident/Community architecture that had developed since at least 2011 through offices, debt and voting machinery, document custody, litigation, maintenance/access relationships, certifications, information and practical control advantages. From at least October 2017, contemporaneous party records describe physical incursions, owner-like assertions and interference with the hotel operation. On 7 June 2018, the evidence supports a decisive change in de facto access and operational control. The exact legal possession, finca-by-finca perimeter, actor instructions and authority for every act remain matters for primary-source verification.**

The output must be capable of confirming, narrowing or disproving this proposition. It must not convert a long chronology into collective intent or treat later title as retrospective authority.

3. **PARTY ALLEGATION — FULL PRACTICAL POSSESSION**  
   Gil Marer alleges that the cumulative result amounted to CAM and allied Community actors taking full practical possession/control of Sun Park as a hotel, notwithstanding fragmented title and absent whole-hotel judicial delivery.

4. **OPEN SCOPE QUESTION**  
   The exact spaces, fincas, rooms, common elements, utilities, records, personnel and functions controlled by each actor—and for what duration—must be mapped from primary evidence.

Preferred public formulation:

> **From 7 June 2018, the evidence supports de facto material control over Sun Park’s principal access and practical operation by CAM-linked and Community actors. Gil Marer alleges that this amounted to full practical possession of the hotel. It was not a judicial transfer of whole-hotel title or possession, and the exact finca-by-finca perimeter and authority for each act remain under verification.**

Do not say that CAM’s predecessors possessed the whole hotel continuously from 2011 unless proved.

> **From at least 2011, the pre-existing Montelanza/Molina-associated Community perimeter developed and used governance, debt, vote, document, maintenance, access and litigation mechanisms capable of affecting the hotel operation and LPB’s practical control. Actor-by-actor continuity, instruction, knowledge and benefit must be proved; family, professional or corporate proximity alone is not shared intent.**
