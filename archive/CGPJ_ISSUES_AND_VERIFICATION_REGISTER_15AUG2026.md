# CGPJ ISSUES & FINITE-VERIFICATION REGISTER — 15 AUG 2026

This is a public-safe analytical register. It records what has been alleged or submitted, not findings of misconduct.

## 1. DI 169/2026 / Alzada 286/2026
- Status: DOCUMENTED PROCEDURAL TRACK.
- Located records include the CGPJ communication providing the final DI agreement, the registration/processing of Recurso de alzada 286/2026, and later July 2026 evidential submissions.
- Open question: what evidence was substantively examined at DI stage and what evidence will be examined on appeal?
- Finite verification: identify the administrative record/index, reports requested, materials incorporated, legal grounds applied, and final appellate decision when issued.

## 2. Appearance-of-impartiality / third-party access assertions
- Status: DOCUMENTED ALLEGATIONS + WITNESS MATERIAL; underlying relationship/contact is NOT treated as established.
- The July 2026 appeal package identifies contemporaneous communications and witness material concerning alleged statements by a private actor about access/contact with the judge, plus a later observed courthouse interaction.
- Required separation: alleged statement; witness observation; contemporaneous recording in email/document; technical evidence actually obtained; technical evidence not obtained; any contrary explanation; what the CGPJ checked.
- Finite verification: official hearing/attendance records, any preserved recordings where lawful/available, documentary metadata, and the CGPJ administrative record showing whether those materials were requested or assessed.

## 3. Judicial conduct vs legal merits
- Status: ANALYTICAL BOUNDARY.
- Many complaints concern decisions affecting assets, liquidation, title, creditor standing, AC supervision or procedural treatment. The CGPJ is not an appellate court.
- Required test: separate disagreement with legal reasoning from allegations concerning impartiality, conduct, delay, case functioning or disciplinary standards.
- Finite verification: classify each complained-of event by competence and identify the lawful remedy/body.

## 4. Court-functioning / inspection dimension
- Status: OPEN INSTITUTIONAL QUESTION.
- Relevant themes may include delay, repeated non-resolution, management of a long-running insolvency proceeding, and whether the cumulative functioning of the court warrants inspection attention independently of the merits of individual rulings.
- Finite verification: determine whether the Servicio de Inspección or Comisión Permanente received, requested or produced any report concerning the functioning of the relevant judicial organ, and identify the outcome.

## 5. Insolvency-administrator supervision
- Status: DOCUMENTED JUDICIAL-ACCOUNTABILITY QUESTION; misconduct not presumed.
- The public site separately records questions concerning the insolvency administrator, accounting bridge, asset/title treatment and judicial supervision.
- Finite verification: for each material AC act, identify what was placed before the court, what judicial control was requested or exercised, and which resulting order explains the next patrimonial step.

## 6. LAJ / court-office boundary
- Status: DOCUMENTED SEPARATE TRACK.
- Testimonios, notifications, certification and procedural-office actions must not be attributed automatically to the judge or to CGPJ disciplinary supervision.
- Finite verification: allocate each act to judge, LAJ, office or other body; identify review/supervision route; cross-link the LAJ testimony and comparative-access ledgers.

## 7. Property-right significance
- Status: PUBLIC-INTEREST CONTEXT.
- This matter illustrates why judicial-governance safeguards matter where judicial acts affect ownership, possession, insolvency estate, enforcement, liquidation and access to remedies.
- Do not present the CGPJ as having power to reverse judgments outside the remedies established by law.
