- Status: **ANALYTICAL BOUNDARY / EXPRESS CGPJ CONTRARY POSITION RECORDED.**
- Many complaints concern decisions affecting assets, liquidation, title, creditor standing, AC supervision or procedural treatment. The CGPJ is not an appellate court for those judicial decisions.
- The 14 May archive expressly treated the complaint as raising matters within the jurisdictional sphere rather than facts classifiable as disciplinary misconduct under arts. 417–419 LOPJ. That reasoning is part of the record and must remain visible.
- The original 10 March communication, however, expressly said it was not asking the CGPJ to review the legal content of resolutions or treating disciplinary irregularity as established; it framed the request as institutional verification of judicial supervision. That narrower framing is also part of the record.
- Required test: separate disagreement with legal reasoning from allegations concerning impartiality, conduct, delay, case functioning or disciplinary standards.
- Finite verification: classify each complained-of event by competence and identify the lawful remedy/body.
