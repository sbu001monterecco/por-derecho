## 2024 regulation control
The current national disciplinary regulation entered into force on 2-Sep-2024 and replaced the 2009 regulation. The regulation:
- retains preliminary information to identify facts, potential responsibility and whether to open a disciplinary proceeding;
- states that preliminary information should not last more than three months save exceptional circumstances;
- strengthens the complainant's participation rights and right of appeal.

Use the current 2024 regulation, not the superseded 2009 framework, for present procedural-accountability analysis.

## Ten finite DIP 80 questions
1. Does DIP 80 remain preliminary information?
2. Has archive or disciplinary opening been ordered?
3. If preliminary information continues, what exceptional circumstances justify exceeding three months?
4. Date/type of latest material procedural act?
5. Has the professional been asked for information/representations?
6. Have third parties been asked for information?
7. Have related judicial proceedings triggered or been assessed for suspension?
8. Has a conflict/recusal check been performed?
9. What participation/appeal rights apply to the complainant at this stage?
10. What is the next expected procedural act?

These questions are designed to avoid requesting confidential deliberations or prejudicing defence rights.
