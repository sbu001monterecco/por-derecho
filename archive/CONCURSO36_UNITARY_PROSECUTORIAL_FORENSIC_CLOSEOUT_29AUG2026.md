## 7 June 2018 control point

The unitary layer preserves the strongest current formulation:

- contemporaneous and later evidence supports a material access/security/locks/control episode;
- no controlled judicial order has been identified granting CAM universal possession of the mixed-title hotel, eviction authority or whole-hotel operating control;
- the Insolvency Administrator described a narrower security/access/maintenance route and denied authorising the principal takeover as alleged;
- every physical act therefore requires actor, finca/space, authority, instruction, execution, adoption/ratification, knowledge, purpose and effect.

The question is not whether the insolvency process had authority over LPB property. The question is whether any actor converted a limited authority, creditor right, Community role or unit title into wider practical control without the required independent legal bridge.
