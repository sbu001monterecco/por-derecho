## Transaction-identity test

Do not reduce the issue to one arithmetic dispute. Apply this chain:

`PLAN / OFFER → JUDICIAL AUTHORISATION → PUBLICITY / BETTER-BID CONDITIONS → FINAL ORDER → TESTIMONIO → DEED → REGISTRY → ACCOUNTING / SURPLUS`.

For each transition ask:

- Was the legal object the same?
- Was the asset perimeter the same?
- Was the economic amount the same?
- Was the debt being extinguished the same debt and classification?
- Was the €400,000 treated separately or absorbed?
- Was any condonation preserved, modified or extinguished?
- Was any change expressly judicially authorised?
- What did the notary/Registry rely on?

A mismatch is an **investigative/legal question**, not automatically proof of nullity, fraud, misappropriation or misconduct.

## Evidence discipline
Absence of a located bridge is not proof that no bridge exists. Court approval cannot substitute for identifying what figures and records were actually supplied to the court. Each figure must be attributed to its source rather than to the judge, AC, CAM or notary by assumption. Likewise, a numerical identity between an Edicto threshold and a deed figure does not by itself prove that both amounts had the same legal function.
