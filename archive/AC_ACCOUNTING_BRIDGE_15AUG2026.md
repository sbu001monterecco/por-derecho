## Strongest finite question
Can the Administrador Concursal produce one certified, arithmetically reproducible bridge from the recognised CAM credit and every later interest/adjustment through the EUR 400,000/locales component, 2021 bidding, 2022 adjudication/deed, cancellations, payments/compensations, estate costs and any surplus — with a source document and accounting entry for every number?

## Evidence discipline
Absence of a located bridge is not proof that no bridge exists. Court approval cannot substitute for identifying what figures and records were actually supplied to the court. Each figure must be attributed to its source rather than to the judge, AC, CAM or notary by assumption.
