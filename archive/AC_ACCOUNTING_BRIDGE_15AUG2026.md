`source document → legal character → amount → date → accounting entry → asset affected → court act → notarial/registry implementation → cash/compensation → cancellation → residual/surplus`.

The 19-Aug-2026 source refresh adds an important distinction that must now control all later work:

> **€13,168,082.02 and €13,568,082.02 are not interchangeable figures.** The 29-Jan-2021 court-office Edicto breaks the better-posture threshold into €11,941,921.02 for the first-rank loan, €1,226,161.00 for the second-rank loan, and a separate €400,000 for non-mortgaged locales/pools. Therefore:
>
> - mortgage-loan components: **€11,941,921.02 + €1,226,161.00 = €13,168,082.02**;
> - mortgage-loan components plus the separate €400,000 non-mortgaged-asset component: **€13,568,082.02**;
> - adding the Edicto's separate €1,145,798.29 community-debt component gives **€14,713,880.31**, before unknown taxes/costs.

This directly supersedes any thread-derived statement that the 21-Feb-2022 deed itself necessarily used **€13,568,082.02** as its global `valor de contraprestación`. Connected party-prepared 2026 reconstruction documents instead state that the deed used **€13,168,082.02**; the deed binary/certified copy must still be rebound before that proposition is promoted to primary-verified status.

A further 19-Aug primary-source promotion closes the earlier **€102,895.34** arithmetic lead. The signed 25-Jan-2021 Auto records that the 23-Jul-2019 Audiencia Provincial calculation used **€3,079,104.66** in first-rank default interest plus continuing daily default interest up to the mortgage-guarantee cap of **€3,182,000**. By Jan-2021, after CAM supplied its calculation, the court held that the better bidder had to offer the full **€3,182,000**. The mathematical movement is therefore **€102,895.34**, and its stated legal/economic explanation is additional daily default interest reaching the cap. What remains open is the underlying day-by-day calculation and its relationship to the definitively recognised concursal credit—not whether the delta exists.

## Transaction-identity test

Do not reduce the issue to one arithmetic dispute. Apply this chain:

`PLAN / OFFER → JUDICIAL AUTHORISATION → PUBLICITY / BETTER-BID CONDITIONS → THIRD-PARTY OFFER / LICITATION → FINAL ORDER → TESTIMONIO → DEED → REGISTRY → ACCOUNTING / SURPLUS`.

For each transition ask:

- Was the legal object the same?
- Was the asset perimeter the same?
- Was the economic amount the same?
- Was the debt being extinguished the same debt and classification?
- Was the €400,000 treated separately or absorbed?
- Was any condonation preserved, modified or extinguished?
- Was any change expressly judicially authorised?
- What did the notary/Registry rely on?

A mismatch is an **investigative/legal question**, not automatically proof of nullity, fraud, misappropriation or misconduct.

## Evidence discipline
Absence of a located bridge is not proof that no bridge exists. Court approval cannot substitute for identifying what figures and records were actually supplied to the court. Each figure must be attributed to its source rather than to the judge, AC, CAM or notary by assumption. Likewise, a numerical identity between an Edicto threshold and a deed figure does not by itself prove that both amounts had the same legal function. The 25-Jan-2021 Auto explains why the first-rank default-interest threshold reached €3.182m; it does **not by itself** establish that the same amount was a finally recognised concursal credit or a lawfully compensable deed amount.
