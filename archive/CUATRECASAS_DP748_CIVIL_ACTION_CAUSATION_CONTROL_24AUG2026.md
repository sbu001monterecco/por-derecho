## 1. Controlling position

The Cuatrecasas analysis does not replace, diminish or exonerate the alleged principal conduct and benefit attributed to the Acosta Matos perimeter, its alleged predecessors or representatives, the Insolvency Administration, or disputed judicial conduct. It asks a separate and potentially cumulative question: whether Cuatrecasas acts, omissions, silence or inaction—measured against the mandate, work and billing—enabled or aggravated adverse outcomes, and whether later La Laguna enforcement choices created direct additional exposure.

No current public formulation may state that Cuatrecasas joined the Acosta Matos perimeter, shared a plan, committed an offence, caused every loss, or bears adjudicated civil/professional responsibility.

## 2. Five-link causation test

1. accepted mandate, work, billing and payment by entity/workstream;
2. material risk and knowledge at the relevant time;
3. exact act, omission, silence, warning, implementation or handover event;
4. actor-specific adverse act, decision, benefit and outcome;
5. but-for/material-contribution counterfactual, recoverable loss and reversal cost.

Chronology, third-party benefit and a documentary gap are not substitutes for this chain.

The controlling counterfactual is a **real and substantial opportunity**, not guaranteed rescue: competent, timely and integrated performance may have prevented or materially contained identifiable increments of the later chain. Each increment must still be tested against legal availability, probability, intervening causes and entity-specific loss.
