## 5. Correction lock — “the hotel was in concurso”

The unsafe proposition is:

> `Sun Park/the hotel was in concurso, so the AC/court/LPB creditor could exercise insolvency-derived authority over the whole hotel.`

The corrected proposition is:

> **Concurso 36/2012 was LPB's insolvency proceeding. LPB's estate did not automatically absorb the whole mixed-ownership hotel, Matkator/third-party property or every separate operating/common-area right.**

Under the historical Ley 22/2003 framework:

- art. 76 defined the estate by reference to property and rights integrated in the **debtor's** patrimony;
- art. 80 preserved a specific separation/delivery mechanism for third-party property in the debtor's possession in the statutory circumstances;
- art. 43 required conservation of the estate and permitted judicial assistance for that purpose.

Those provisions reinforce the need to identify the actual legal source for any action outside LPB's estate; they do not themselves determine the title/possession status of every Sun Park area.
