## 2. Thread incidents preserved as rules, not private recipient records

The thread exposed two operational failure modes relevant to future agents:

1. a duplicate-send incident followed by excessive corrective messaging; and
2. a later duplicate-send incident caused by invoking the send action a second time instead of switching to read-only verification.

The public repository preserves the resulting controls but intentionally does not publish the affected private recipient identities, exact subjects, native message IDs or private email bodies.

Required future behavior:

- after one positive send result, no second send invocation for that package;
- verification uses native Sent read/search only;
- after any duplicate incident, stop outbound activity for that recipient/thread;
- do not send a correction automatically;
- if correction is genuinely useful, prepare a new exact package and obtain fresh authorization.
