# MULTI-CLAIMANT, CROSS-BORDER RECOVERY AND AUTHORITY PROTOCOL

**Control date:** 18 August 2026

## 1. Controlling conclusion

Project Sun Rock / Por Derecho must be presented and managed as a coordinated portfolio of legally distinct claims, complaints, appeals, investigations and recovery routes. A common factual and economic history does **not** create one universal claimant, one universal loss, one applicable law or one competent court.

The programme may coordinate the common evidence, causal reconstruction, forensic work, counsel, funding and recovery strategy. It must not merge legal personality, claim ownership, settlement authority, limitation, privilege, costs or recovery accounts.

## 2. Source status and present limit

The controlled source corpus supports a wider perimeter than LPB's insolvency alone. It records Aweswell's UK parent, creditor, funder, foreign-investor and recovery roles; LPB and Matkator; historical operating and commercialisation structures; a UK-facing distribution and growth platform; and legal, regulatory, supervisory, administrative and accountability routes in Spain and outside Spain.

Some of that material is party-authored statutory, board, accounting or claim-planning material. It supports the existence and intended architecture of a cross-border recovery programme. It does **not**, without claimant-specific primary evidence, prove:

- that every associated entity or counterparty suffered a legally recoverable loss;
- that every potentially affected party wishes to claim;
- that Aweswell owns or controls every claim;
- that a general mandate, assignment, subrogation or representative authority exists;
- that every proposed foreign forum has jurisdiction;
- that every pleaded or provisional amount is independently quantified, recoverable or non-duplicative.

The initial control scan did not locate one blanket authority covering every independent third party. That is a **current corpus result**, not proof that no authority exists elsewhere.

## 3. Meaning of “Sun Rock Group”

`Sun Rock Group` may be used as a historical, economic and strategic umbrella for the ownership, operating, funding, commercialisation and recovery network developed around Hotel Sun Park.

It must not be used as though it were one legal person. Each company, community, operating structure, contractual counterparty and individual rights-holder retains its own:

- legal identity and status;
- assets and contracts;
- duties and liabilities;
- cause of action;
- applicable law and forum;
- limitation period;
- privilege and settlement authority;
- recovery and accounting treatment.

## 4. Claimant classes

### A. Core verified entities

- **Aweswell Limited:** its own direct parent-company, creditor, funder, contractual, investment, recovery-cost and cross-border rights, subject to proof and applicable law.
- **Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. (LPB):** estate, property, productive-unit, income, creditor, contractual and insolvency causes of action, subject to the standing and control rules of the concurso.
- **Matkator, S.L.:** its own extraconcursal property, title, possession, use, works, income and damages position.

No loss is moved between these entities merely because Aweswell is the parent or because the hotel operated as one economic platform.

### B. Historical operating or associated structures

The Explotadora / CEXP / OpCo and any other historical operating, commercialisation or associated entity require separate verification of legal existence, capacity, representation, ownership, current controller, direct injury, insolvency or dissolution status and authority to instruct or litigate.

### C. Independent third parties

Owners, creditors, commercial counterparties, distributors, suppliers, workers, contractors, customers, investors, insurers or other persons may have their own direct rights. Their claims remain theirs unless a recognised legal basis transfers or authorises control.

Commercial proximity, common evidence, common defendants, participation in the wider platform or apparent inactivity does not make their claims Aweswell's property.

### D. Public and regulatory interests

A regulator, prosecutor, tax authority, anti-fraud body or public administration may pursue public-law objectives, sanctions, correction, confiscation, recovery of public money or institutional accountability. Those functions are not private damages claims belonging to Aweswell or another private claimant.

## 5. Authority gate

Aweswell or the coordinated recovery programme may state that it pursues an independent third party's claim **on that party's behalf** only where the file contains a legally sufficient basis such as:

- a written mandate or engagement;
- a valid assignment;
- subrogation;
- agency with the necessary litigation and settlement authority;
- joinder or another recognised procedural representation mechanism;
- authority held by an insolvency office-holder;
- court permission or court-recognised standing;
- another valid basis under the applicable law.

Until that gate is passed, the programme may:

- preserve and organise common evidence;
- invite the affected party to participate;
- fund or support an independent forensic assessment;
- coordinate instructed counsel where conflicts permit;
- pursue Aweswell's own direct loss arising from the same events;
- preserve a potential recovery route without asserting ownership of the third party's claim.

It may not settle, release, waive, appropriate, assign, quantify as its own or publicly misdescribe the third party's claim.

## 6. Dormant is not abandoned

Use `DORMANT`, `NOT PRESENTLY PURSUED`, `PARTICIPATION UNVERIFIED` or `AUTHORITY PENDING` unless primary evidence establishes a release, settlement, waiver, discontinuance, final adverse disposition or another legally operative abandonment.

For every apparently inactive claimant determine:

1. whether the person or entity still exists and who controls it;
2. what direct injury it suffered;
3. whether a claim was made, settled, released, waived, discontinued or assigned;
4. whether an insurer, liquidator, administrator or other office-holder now controls the claim;
5. whether limitation has expired, been suspended or interrupted;
6. whether the claimant wishes to participate;
7. whether its interests conflict with Aweswell, LPB, Matkator or another claimant.

Silence, delay, change of ownership, lack of funding or withdrawal from commercial contact is not by itself legal abandonment.

## 7. One factual matrix, multiple legal routes

Maintain separate but coordinated lanes for:

1. **civil and contractual:** damages, debt, restitution, declarations, injunctions and contractual remedies;
2. **mercantile and corporate:** company, shareholder, director, governance, transfer and structural-modification issues;
3. **concursal:** estate assets, creditor recognition, liquidation, adjudication, surplus, accounts, office-holder duties and insolvency remedies;
4. **criminal / penal:** reports, evidence, victim participation, confiscation and associated civil recovery where competent law permits;
5. **regulatory and supervisory:** market integrity, investor information, professional or regulated conduct and corrective action;
6. **administrative and public-authority liability:** public decisions, records, licences, tax, public funds, tourism, transparency and compensable public-service harm where the legal elements are met;
7. **professional and disciplinary:** separate standards and remedies before professional bodies;
8. **asset tracing and enforcement:** preservation, recognition, execution and recovery where defendants or assets are legally reachable.

A criminal, regulatory or disciplinary route may produce investigation, correction, sanction, confiscation, referral or evidence. It does not automatically produce private compensation.

## 8. Jurisdiction and applicable-law control

Spain and the Canary Islands remain central to the hotel property, LPB concurso, local administration, local public funding and many alleged acts and consequences. They are not presumed to be the exclusive forums for every direct loss or defendant.

A UK or other foreign route may be evaluated only where claimant-specific facts establish a proper connection through, for example:

- claimant or defendant domicile;
- governing-law or jurisdiction clause;
- place of performance or harmful event;
- professional duty assumed in that jurisdiction;
- direct loss suffered there;
- payment, account, communication, business operation or evidence located there;
- assets available for enforcement there.

For every proposed route record jurisdiction, governing law, service, limitation, costs, evidence, enforceability and interaction with existing Spanish proceedings. Use `applicable foreign law` or `private international law analysis`; do not use “international law” as a generic substitute.

## 9. Claimant-specific forensic intake

No claimant or amount enters the consolidated recovery dashboard until the following gates are completed or expressly marked open:

1. **Identity and status:** exact legal name, identifier, jurisdiction, current existence and controller.
2. **Claim ownership and authority:** direct owner, office-holder, assignee, subrogated party, mandated representative or disputed.
3. **Direct injury:** asset, contract or interest affected; date; immediate and consequential loss; person who actually suffered it.
4. **Defendant and causation:** duty/relationship → act or omission → knowledge → immediate effect → propagated effect → claimant-specific loss.
5. **Applicable law and forum:** jurisdictional gateway, property situs, insolvency exclusivity, contract clauses and enforcement location.
6. **Limitation and procedural history:** accrual, knowledge, expiry, interruption/suspension, prior proceedings, settlement, release, discontinuance and preclusion risks.
7. **Evidence and quantum:** primary documents, accounting, expert methodology, counterfactual, mitigation and confidence level.
8. **Non-duplication:** identify overlap with property value, fruits, operating income, rent, distribution margin, shareholder loss, insurer recovery, criminal restitution or public-authority compensation.
9. **Conflicts, privilege and data protection:** separate engagements, information barriers, settlement authority, disclosure and lawful data handling.
10. **Recovery ledger:** allocate every payment, judgment, settlement or restitution to the claimant and loss it actually satisfies.

## 10. Public-language controls

### Safe formulations

- “We coordinate a multi-claimant recovery programme.”
- “Aweswell pursues its own rights and properly authorised claims.”
- “Potentially affected parties are being identified and their rights preserved.”
- “Participation, standing, applicable law, jurisdiction and quantum remain claimant-specific.”
- “Some potential claims are dormant or not yet fully particularised.”
- “The common evidence is coordinated; ownership of each claim remains separate.”

### Qualification required

- “We act for affected parties.”
- “We pursue their claims.”
- “The third party abandoned its claim.”
- “The claim belongs to the Group.”

### Do not publish without proof

- “Aweswell owns all third-party claims.”
- “Every affected counterparty has assigned its rights.”
- “All foreign losses will be recovered in the Spanish concurso.”
- “The criminal proceedings belong to Aweswell.”
- an aggregate third-party damages figure not supported by claimant-specific, non-duplicative expert work.
