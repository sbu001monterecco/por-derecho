## 3. Evidence-status rule for this pitch

The outreach preserves the distinction between:

- **VERIFIED FACT** — source-supported dates, entities, public funding/investment records, public commercial material, court/registry/primary documents;
- **PARTY ALLEGATION** — including allegations of hotel mobbing/acoso hotelero, fraudulent RIC materialisation, misuse of funds, unlawful takeover/control or other wrongdoing unless independently established;
- **EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE** — convergence questions supported by multiple documentary anchors but not yet adjudicated;
- **UNRESOLVED QUESTION** — exact asset/work/invoice/employment overlap, knowledge, actor-specific intent, certification/control and source-and-use questions;
- **CORRECTED/SUPERSEDED** — later source corrections control over earlier shorthand.

Do not state `fraudulent RIC`, `FEDER fraud`, `criminal takeover`, collusion, conspiracy or equivalent as an established fact absent a competent source establishing that proposition.
