**Date:** 21 August 2026

**Scope:** DP 1901/2026; DP 1956/2026; the separate judicial criminal complaint presented on 18 June 2026 and its later ampliación; DI 169/2026 / Recurso de alzada 286/2026; Concurso 36/2012; CAM / RICPE / San Telmo evidence interfaces.

## 1. Terminology correction — daily reference no. 24

Do **not** refer to the separate judicial criminal route as `Control 24`, `DP 24`, `procedure 24`, `case 24` or any equivalent that could imply an official proceeding number.

The controlled formulation is:

> **the separate criminal complaint presented on 18 June 2026 concerning specified judicial resolutions and conduct, and its later ampliación, both registered or linked by the Decanato under the daily reference no. 24 corresponding to that presentation.**

That daily reference is an intake/registration locator only. It is **not** a NIG, Diligencias Previas number, formal TSJC proceeding number or proof that a criminal proceeding has been opened. Formal destination, reparto and procedural status remain matters for certified verification unless a later signed institutional record establishes them.

This addendum supersedes repository shorthand that uses `Control 24` as though it were the controlling name of that route.

## 2. Strict division of procedural labour

### DP 1901/2026 — private-actor culpability route

DP 1901/2026 is the criminal route in which Por Derecho has placed the **alleged culpability of JDAM, LPAM, CAM / Acosta Matos entities and the other individually identified private actors**, subject always to actor-specific proof.

Its evidential subjects include, where supported by primary material:

- Community authority, debt, voting and documentary machinery;
- access, security, possession and works;
- private instructions, planning, adoption, ratification and benefit;
- title and ownership representations;
- RICPE / investor-facing project representations;
- financing, project preparation and later exploitation;
- use of documents before public authorities, investors, notaries, registries or courts.

Do not transfer those imputations wholesale into DP 1956/2026.

### DP 1956/2026 — actor-specific AC route

DP 1956/2026 concerns the **Administrador Concursal's own possible culpability**, not the culpability of the private actors by association.

For every relevant private act or institutional result, apply the AC attribution chain:

> **information available → specific concursal duty/power → act or omission of the AC → information supplied to or withheld from the court → institutional result → AC implementation/maintenance/non-correction → accounting/rendition → incremental patrimonial consequence.**

The proper questions are what the AC knew, verified, communicated, recommended, authorised, implemented, restored or failed to restore, preserved or failed to preserve, accounted for, and ultimately rendered.

Judicial approval does not automatically absorb the AC's authorship of factual premises, figures, recommendations, implementation acts, custody of funds, restoration duties or accounting. Conversely, conduct of the AC does not itself establish criminal or disciplinary responsibility of the judge.

### Separate judicial criminal route

The separate complaint presented on 18 June 2026 and its later ampliación are the autonomous route for the possible criminal significance of **specified judicial resolutions and conduct**, subject to the much higher actor-specific judicial threshold.

DP 1956/2026 should not become an indirect prosecution of the Mercantile judge. Its proper role is to preserve the **judicial interface**:

- what the AC knew;
- what he supplied or omitted;
- what actually reached the court;
- what judicial act followed;
- what the AC then implemented, maintained or failed to correct;
- what patrimonial consequence followed.

That same evidence may later have a different legal use in the separate judicial criminal route.

### DI 169/2026 / Recurso de alzada 286/2026

This is the separate **institutional / disciplinary / supervisory** route. DI 169/2026 was archived on 14 May 2026; the challenge under article 608 LOPJ gave rise to **Recurso de alzada 286/2026**, whose processing was confirmed by the CGPJ. The 15 July traceability submission was joined to that appeal file. No merits ruling on the appeal is treated as established unless a later signed decision is located.

The CGPJ route must not be described as a criminal proceeding or as proof of judicial wrongdoing. Its finite questions concern supervision, traceability, institutional handling and preservation without asking the CGPJ to decide civil title, credit quantum or criminal guilt.

## 3. Cross-proceeding rule

Use this formula throughout the repository and website:

> **procedures separate; objects and legal standards distinct; connected knowledge; limited common evidence; coordinated preservation; individual attribution; no automatic joinder; no collective culpability; no procedural renunciation.**

The separation of proceedings must not allow:

- a judicial decision to absorb the AC's own factual inputs, implementation or accounting;
- the AC's conduct to predetermine judicial culpability;
- DP 1901 private-actor allegations to be imported into DP 1956 without actor-specific proof;
- the CGPJ appeal to be treated as proof of criminal wrongdoing;
- or procedural separation to fragment the factual chain so completely that common evidence cannot be traced.

## 4. Liquidation-accounting bridge — mandatory reconciliation

The accounting issue is not merely that different figures exist. The question is whether the AC maintained and can produce a **contemporaneous, legally segmented and arithmetically reproducible bridge** through the liquidation.

Minimum milestone chain:

> **textos definitivos → CAM offer / liquidation route approved 16 April 2018 → third-party improvement threshold → Auto AP 78/2019 of 23 July 2019 → 2019 Supreme Court interest doctrine → 2021 competitive process → amount actually extinguished → separate EUR 400,000 component → 21 February 2022 deed → registry / charge treatment → any surplus → final estate accounts / rendition.**

Do not flatten legally different concepts. At minimum distinguish:

- recognised privileged credit;
- outstanding principal;
- pre-insolvency interest;
- post-insolvency remuneratory interest;
- moratory interest;
- mortgage-security ceiling;
- amount a third party had to exceed;
- payoff figure;
- credit actually extinguished;
- sale price / value of consideration;
- compensation / set-off;
- condonation;
- costs and estate expenses;
- the separate EUR 400,000 component;
- any surplus due to LPB's estate.

The recurring figures around EUR 9.052m, EUR 3.079m, EUR 12.768m, EUR 400k and EUR 13.168m must be assigned to their exact legal and accounting functions before any conclusion is drawn.

**Source-control rule:** absence of the complete bridge from the current corpus is not proof that it never existed. If it exists and reconciles the operation, it may materially support a lawful explanation. If it does not exist, was created only later, or depends on migration of a competitive threshold into extinguished debt without a lawful bridge, that becomes a central AC-specific issue.

## 5. CAM 2018 works / unity of exploitation / RICPE — current controlled frame

### CAM offer and unity of exploitation

CAM's January 2018 offer treated Sun Park as an economically integrated tourist productive unit and expressly relied on the Canary Islands tourism framework and the principle of **unity of exploitation**.

Do not overstate that principle. It requires unitary tourist operation through one operating undertaking; it does **not** by itself require one registered owner or prove one commercial brand.

### 2018 works

CAM's February 2021 court filing stated, in substance, that it owned 54 apartments and had begun **interior works in several apartments of its own** in 2018 under an asserted municipal licence, because it expected the complex to enter into operation.

Do **not** state that this document literally admits an "integral refurbishment of the hotel" in 2018. The broader project-wide character is an evidence-based hypothesis to test against the offer, works file, common-installation activity, security/access record, later RICPE project and final MYND transformation.

The legally important question is what project-level expectation of control, acquisition, reopening or return justified expenditure on a minority-unit perimeter inside an establishment whose tourist use depended on unitary exploitation.

### RICPE 2020 representation

The 4 June 2020 RICPE investor material identified Sun Park and described CAM as the relevant `sociedad titular`, while the same project material also contained an `en estudio` caveat.

Treat this as a **representation/title contradiction to be explained**, not automatic proof of fraud. Determine the intended meaning of `sociedad titular`, the source documents supplied, due-diligence status, investor wording and whether later corrections were made.

### CNMV / AEAT / Government correction

Do not say that CNMV registration substantively verified CAM's title. CNMV filing/registration does not by itself adjudicate civil ownership or guarantee the truth of each underlying project representation.

The currently controlled binding AEAT report is dated **17 November 2022**, and Decreto 224/2022 is dated **1 December 2022**, both after the 2022 adjudication. Therefore do **not** state that the Tax Office had named CAM owner years before the final adjudication unless an earlier primary report is located.

The correct open question is whether an earlier 2020–2021 AEAT / Government idoneity or authorisation file existed and, if so, what title, possession, litigation, licence and project documents were supplied and verified.
