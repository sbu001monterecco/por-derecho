These should not become cluttered with a litigation inventory unless a proceeding is directly necessary to explain the proposition on that page.

## Public wording pattern

For each disclosed proceeding use:

**Reference → procedural status → exact issue within scope → what it does not decide → source/primary-document link where safe.**

Avoid:

- “X is under investigation” where only a complaint/registration exists;
- merging a professional complaint with a civil or criminal proceeding;
- treating a referral as admission on the merits;
- treating an appeal as a new underlying complaint;
- publishing privileged lawyer correspondence or internal strategy.
