## 1. Trigger

When a material **Audiencia Provincial de Las Palmas** decision is received, recovered or materially clarified in the relevant Concurso / Calificación / AC-accountability perimeter, re-open and review the **September–October 2018 CAM / Administrador Concursal mortgage-credit correspondence chain** before deciding whether any further submission is justified.

Do not treat receipt of an AP decision as an automatic reason to file. The AP decision is a **review trigger**, not a predetermined merits conclusion.

## 2. Decision rule

After reading the AP decision together with the controlled primary record, decide expressly between:

1. an **ampliación** of an existing criminal complaint concerning the Administrador Concursal;
2. a narrower **evidential / procedural submission** placing the correspondence on the record without enlarging the complaint theory;
3. referral to counsel / preservation pending further source closure; or
4. **no further filing** if the AP reasoning, contrary evidence, finality, competence, duplication or evidential weakness makes further action unjustified.

Record the reason for the selected route.

## 3. What the review must test

The September–October 2018 correspondence must be tested for actor-specific evidence of:

- what CAM requested, represented or asserted about the mortgage credit;
- what the AC knew, received, stated, recommended, implemented or omitted;
- dates, recipients and exact sequence;
- consistency with the 16-Apr-2018 Plan-approval and separate interest/mortgage-responsibility acts;
- consistency with the June 2018 bidder/suspension acts;
- consistency with the 17-Oct-2018 AC testimony request, 22-Oct-2018 LAJ delivery and 24-Oct-2018 CAM execution pressure/request;
- relationship, if any, to the 28-Nov-2018 deed implementation;
- whether later 2019, 2021 or 2022 acts cure, replace, contradict or leave the earlier conduct unresolved;
- knowledge/intent evidence, causation and benefit/consequence where any criminal theory is considered;
- contrary, exculpatory or innocent explanations.

Do not collapse CAM, the AC, the Judge, LAJ, Registry or notary into a single actor.

## 4. AP decision control

For any AP decision used in the review, identify:

- exact chamber/section, date, proceeding and resolution;
- whether final or subject to further remedy;
- exact issue decided;
- whether the September–October 2018 correspondence was before the AP;
- whether the AP made a factual finding, legal conclusion, procedural ruling or merely recorded a party position;
- whether the reasoning strengthens, weakens or is neutral to the proposed use of the correspondence;
- whether the ruling affects standing, competence, res judicata/preclusion, duplication, limitation or procedural vehicle.

Do not attribute to the AP findings it did not make.

## 5. Source hierarchy and filing boundary

Primary/native sources control. Pleadings, complaints, lawyer summaries and prior AI analysis do not outrank signed judicial acts or native correspondence.

Before any filing, verify the relevant email/message chain from connected primary-source custody (Gmail/Drive/File Library where available), preserve source identifiers and attachments, and distinguish:

- verified primary fact;
- party/actor position;
- inference;
- legal argument;
- unresolved evidence gap.

No allegation of criminality follows merely from disagreement over mortgage-credit treatment, Registry difficulty, refusal of convalidation, valuation disagreement or an allegedly erroneous AC/judicial position.
