## 1. Why this is part of Calificación rather than a separate subsidy story

Sentencia 163/2023 and the adverse Calificación narrative attribute serious causal responsibility to Gil Marer in relation to LPB’s insolvency and its aggravation. A complete causation audit cannot stop at the selected pre- or early-concurso allegations while treating the same hotel’s later works, financing, public support, employment, operation and value as unrelated external events.

Project Sun Rock alleges that the same Hotel Sun Park/MYND Yaiza and materially overlapping assets, works, costs, value and employment outputs acquired multiple financial lives while:

- LPB’s predominant identified property count remained within Concurso 36/2012 until the later title sequence;
- Matkator and other integrated property remained outside the estate;
- the hotel was under a disputed de facto control/access regime;
- RICPE’s July-2021 internal certification recorded fragmented title, conditional ownership and incomplete due diligence;
- CAM placed that certification into the concurso record the next day;
- later RIC finance, regional incentive, FEDER-linked support and current exploitation arose around the same hotel.

Those facts and allegations are relevant to:

- causal attribution;
- intervening or concurrent causes;
- alternatives to liquidation;
- value preservation and destruction;
- estate opportunity and loss;
- actor knowledge and corrective capacity;
- benefit and patrimonial consequence;
- credibility and completeness of the AC/Fiscal/Judge narrative.

They do not automatically prove Gil’s innocence, culpability of another actor, double funding, unlawful aid or judicial misconduct. They are mandatory evidence that must be integrated rather than fragmented away.
