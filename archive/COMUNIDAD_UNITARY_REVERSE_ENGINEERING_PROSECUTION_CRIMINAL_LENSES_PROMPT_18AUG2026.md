## 1. Mission

Reverse-engineer the Sun Park Community/CEXP history from the later operational and financial outcome back to the first authority, debt, voting, custody and access propositions that made that outcome possible.

Do **not** begin by selecting a criminal offence or by treating “the Community” as one actor with one intention.

Start with this finite question:

> **Did a pre-existing Community/CEXP administrative and documentary apparatus become, deliberately or otherwise, the mechanism through which fragmented ownership was converted into apparent authority, voting/debt control, physical access and project availability; and were any materially inaccurate propositions then knowingly used to obtain judicial, insolvency, administrative, investment, financing or economic consequences that could not have been obtained on the true underlying facts?**

A lawful or valid answer may be that one or more conversions were properly authorised, independently verified or immaterial. The prompt must actively search for that evidence.

## 2. Reverse-engineer from the end state

Start from the visible later position and move backwards:

`MYND operation / HNT / financing / public support`

← what asset and rights were treated as available?

← what title, project perimeter, works and authority were presented?

← what RICPE, public bodies and advisers independently verified?

← what practical control, keys, security, maintenance or access existed?

← what Community decisions, debt and voting consequences supported that control?

← who held books, accounts, bank mandates, minutes and institutional representation?

← what authority chain arose in 2011 and before?

← who actually owned, participated, consented and could decide?

For every arrow classify:

- `VERIFIED`;
- `VERIFIED WITH LIMIT`;
- `DEPENDENCY ONLY`;
- `EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE`;
- `CONTRADICTED`;
- `BROKEN`;
- `UNRESOLVED`.

Do not fill a broken arrow with narrative.

## 3. Central conceptual test

Investigate the Community principally as a possible **authority-and-control infrastructure**, not automatically as the ultimate beneficiary.

Test whether limited Community powers concerning common elements and expenses were allowed to mutate into broader propositions concerning:

- hotel-wide representation;
- ownership or availability of private units;
- voting power;
- debt certification;
- access and security;
- maintenance and works;
- operation or exploitation;
- authority before the Insolvency Administrator, Court, public bodies or investors;
- or the practical ability to deliver Sun Park as an investable project.

Community presidency or representation must not be equated with title to private units or universal authority over the hotel business.

## 6. Criminal/investigative lenses

Run these lenses without assuming that their legal elements are satisfied:

### A. Authority and documentary chain

- Were minutes, appointments, certificates, debt figures, attendance, proxies or representations materially inaccurate?
- Who created, approved, signed, stored or circulated them?
- Who knew the underlying title, attendance, debt or vote records?
- Where were they later used?

### B. Procedural deception / use in proceedings

- Was a materially inaccurate authority, debt, title or possession proposition knowingly introduced into a judicial or administrative process?
- Was it capable of inducing, and did it actually induce, a material decision?
- What prejudice followed?

Do not call an inaccurate submission `estafa procesal` without proving deception, reliance, decision and prejudice under the law applicable at the event date.

### C. Administration of another’s assets / disloyal management

- What property, funds, books or powers were entrusted to the actor?
- What precise power or duty existed?
- Was the act within or outside that authority?
- What patrimonial harm or third-party advantage resulted?

### D. Insolvency-estate interference

- Did Community debt, access, works, liabilities, certificates or practical control affect the LPB estate or third-party assets?
- What did the Insolvency Administrator know?
- What duty to reconcile, preserve, disclose or challenge existed?
- What later act is inconsistent with that knowledge or duty?

### E. Physical control and exclusion

- Who ordered locks, keys, chains, security, access codes, forced entry, exclusion, maintenance or works?
- Over which exact unit, premises or common element?
- Under what authority?
- What lawful-protection explanation exists?

Physical access is not ownership. Physical exclusion is not automatically judicial eviction.

### F. Financing, public support and cost reuse

- Did Community-derived authority, works, costs or employment propositions migrate into RICPE, RIC, Regional Incentives or FEDER files?
- Were the same assets, works, invoices, employees, periods or value used more than once?
- What source-and-use and cumulation analysis exists?

Multiple sources do not prove duplicate funding; legal separation does not prove absence of overlap.

### G. Professional duties

For administrators, accountants, lawyers, auditors and advisers identify:

- engagement;
- client;
- duty;
- remuneration;
- information available;
- warning given or omitted;
- conflict;
- reliance and harm.

### H. Coordinated plan or participation

Do not infer a common plan from timing, relationships or later benefit alone.

Require communications, instructions, shared drafts, meetings, division of functions, concealment, coordinated timing or another specific evidential bridge.

## 8. Falsification requirements

The theory must be narrowed or rejected where primary evidence establishes, for example:

- a valid and complete 2011 authority chain;
- accurate debt calculations and lawful voting consequences;
- proper custody and disclosure;
- finca-specific authority for 2018 access, security or works;
- independent verification by RICPE or public bodies;
- contemporaneous warnings limiting authority;
- distinct costs, invoices, assets and employee populations;
- no material reliance or prejudice;
- no actor-specific knowledge;
- or no evidential bridge between actors.

Do not graduate the theory into a conspiracy where shared purpose remains unproved.

## 11. Stop rules

Stop and downgrade before publication or filing if:

- a proposition depends only on later benefit or association;
- institutional possession is being converted into personal knowledge;
- material control is being converted into ownership;
- an invalid act is being converted into a crime without elements;
- a later title is being projected backwards;
- a funding overlap is being asserted without invoice/cost evidence;
- an actor’s strongest lawful explanation has not been tested;
- the law applicable at the event date has not been fixed;
- or a critical conversion remains unsupported.
