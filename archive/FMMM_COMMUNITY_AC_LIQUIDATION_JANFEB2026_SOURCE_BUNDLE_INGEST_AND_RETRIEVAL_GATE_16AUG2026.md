## 3. What the 14-Jul-2026 forensic report materially adds

The report is most useful not for its internal confidence percentages, but for its **method** and its disciplined separation between documented relationship and criminal conclusion.

### 3.1 Controlling methodological contribution — non-circular provenance

The strongest reusable rule is:

> **An act, certificate, debt statement or Community document does not prove the authority of the person or body that created it merely because that same document says the authority existed.**

For every Community proposition trace independently:

`TITLE / OWNER → POWER / REPRESENTATION → APPOINTMENT → NOTICE → ATTENDANCE → VOTE → QUOTA → ACT → ORIGINAL CUSTODY → CERTIFICATE → DOWNSTREAM USE`.

This is the correct method for testing Community debt, voting exclusion, security, access, 2018 and 2022 acts and any later reliance by CAM, the AC, the court or third parties.

### 3.2 FMMM as a pre-concurso continuity node

The report records the preserved DP 1132/2018 declaration as placing FMMM in Community administration from **February 2011**, before the June-2012 declaration of LPB's concurso. It also identifies the need to reconcile a temporal inconsistency in that testimony concerning whether LPB was already in concurso when he began.

**Controlled use:**
- FMMM's own declaration is strong evidence of his claimed functional start date and role;
- validity of the appointment, contract, invoices, powers and exact periods remains open;
- do not turn the claimed role into a finding that he acted unlawfully from 2011 without the underlying originals.

### 3.3 2016 knowledge: accounts, debt, contracts and maintenance

The report uses the 11-Jun-2016 PwC/Simó/Prieto/FMMM meeting to show that the Community-debt dispute was not a simple `non-payment` proposition. The meeting concerned who bore real expenditure, operation/maintenance obligations, historical accounts, invoices, electricity and retroactive charges.

**Safe proposition:** FMMM was a central and informed interlocutor on Community accounts/debt by 2016.

**Unsafe upgrade:** `the meeting proves FMMM fabricated the debt`. It does not.

### 3.4 Pamalexsha / FMMM / Shaila / Antonio — office, custody and economic continuity

The report maps later corporate material as:

- FMMM — 33% Pamalexsha; reported Community administrator; later AGM board member;
- Shaila Cogolludo — 34% Pamalexsha; reported Community administration/custody functions;
- Antonio Cogolludo — 33% Pamalexsha; reported Community president/security role;
- Pamalexsha — 18.77% AGM.

This supports a real, documentable bridge between a pre-existing Community-administration nucleus and the later AGM perimeter. Family relationships can help identify channels, custody and economic interest, but **do not prove common intent or joint criminal responsibility**.

### 3.5 2018 security/access chain

The report reinforces the current A03/DP1132 architecture:

- 18-May-2018 Community/security decision requires original notice, attendance, ownership, proxies, voting rights, arrears/vote exclusions, exact resolution and security contract;
- the 31-Jul-2018 AC declaration records a request associated with Laura Acosta Matos and a limited authorisation to force a specific LPB door for an alleged fault;

This is fully consistent with CR-018 and the existing Allegation-03 controls.

### 3.6 4-Feb-2022 Community act as a high-value convergence document

The report identifies a 4-Feb-2022 Community act in which the available material describes:

- Laura Acosta representing CAM;
- JDAM being designated Community president;
- FMMM acting as secretary-administrator;
- Community debt/certifications;
- a CAM project/reform/licensing context;

**What this supports now:** the document is a priority convergence point for Community authority, CAM project preparation, debt/certification and title chronology.

**What remains open:** original act, notice, attendee list, ownership at the date, proxies, quotas, voting exclusions, acceptance of office, project annexes, administrative use and exact legal effect.

Do not publish `clandestine act` or `false act` as a factual conclusion merely because the January-2026 complaint uses those labels.

### 3.7 AGM / Orion — correct corporate formulation

The report materially corrects loose complaint wording that can sound as if FMMM or the Cogolludos held a direct office in Orion.

**Controlled formulation:**

## 5. The better integrated frame

The bundle should **not** be framed publicly as `ten documents proving one conspiracy`.

The stronger and more credible frame is:

> **Continuity of infrastructure, authority, documents and benefit — tested actor by actor.**

A pre-existing Community apparatus concerning administration, debt, voting, document custody and access existed before CAM's later creditor/unit-owner/project roles. The record then shows a 2018 interface between Community/security mechanisms, limited AC authority and private-actor access. A later 2022 Community document and the subsequent AGM/Orion structure create further continuity of people, entities and economic interests. The finite question is whether each later actor lawfully used an independent Community structure, opportunistically capitalised on it, or knowingly adopted/instrumentalised it. The answer must be proved act by act.

`PRE-EXISTING COMMUNITY AUTHORITY / DEBT / VOTING / CUSTODY`

→ `CAM CREDIT / UNIT / PROJECT ENTRY`

This chronology is a **map for proof**. It is not itself proof of unlawful coordination.

## 10. Website wording model

### Spanish — strongest safe framing

> **No proponemos que una relación societaria posterior pruebe un pacto anterior, ni que un acta pruebe por sí sola la autoridad de quien la firma. La pregunta es más concreta y verificable.** Antes de la llegada de CAM ya existía una estructura comunitaria que administraba cuentas, deuda, voto, documentación y acceso. En 2018 esa estructura se cruzó con el nuevo acreedor/comunero, la seguridad privada y autorizaciones limitadas de la Administración Concursal. En 2022 vuelve a aparecer como soporte de deuda, cargos y proyecto; y años después varios de sus protagonistas aparecen conectados económicamente con JDAM a través de Pamalexsha y AGM, gestor de Orion. Eso justifica reconstruir autoridad, comunicaciones, custodia, uso y beneficio acto por acto. **No convierte la cronología en una conspiración probada.**
>
> En la Calificación, esta reconstrucción tiene una función muy precisa: comprobar si se atribuyó a Gil/Pink una consecuencia económica —rentas no cobradas y agravación de insolvencia— sin reconstruir con la misma intensidad el sistema upstream que condicionaba inventario explotable, ocupación, costes, deuda comunitaria, capacidad de voto, acceso, control y recuperación. La sentencia de primera instancia sigue siendo materialmente adversa en esa rama y está recurrida. El objetivo documental es saber qué parte de esa causalidad resiste cuando se integra todo el sistema.

### English — strongest safe framing

> **A later corporate relationship does not prove an earlier agreement, and a Community minute does not prove the authority of its own author. The question is narrower and testable.** Before CAM's later entry, a Community structure already handled accounts, debt, voting, records and access. In 2018 that structure intersected with the new creditor/unit-owner perimeter, private security and limited insolvency-administrator authorisations. In 2022 it appears again around debt, offices and the project; later several of the same people are economically reconnected with JDAM through Pamalexsha and AGM, Orion's manager. That requires an act-by-act reconstruction of authority, communication, custody, use and benefit. **Chronology is not proof of conspiracy.**
>
> For the Classification case, this reconstruction serves a specific purpose: to test whether a downstream economic consequence — uncollected rent and alleged aggravation of insolvency — was attributed to Gil/Pink without equally reconstructing the upstream system affecting exploitable inventory, occupancy, costs, Community debt, voting capacity, access, control and recovery. The first-instance judgment remains materially adverse on that branch and is appealed. The documentary task is to determine what remains of that causation theory once the whole system is tested.
