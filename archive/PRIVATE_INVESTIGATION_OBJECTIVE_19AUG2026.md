# OBJECTIVE — WHY THIS EVIDENCE IS PUBLIC

The public purpose of the anonymised 8 May 2018 investigation evidence is to support a narrow Project Sun Rock objective:

> **Show, with dated records, that pre-title access and hotel-project preparation must be investigated as a chronology preceding the later Lava Verde / Club Sei / Meeting Point commercial pathway, rather than beginning the story with the 2022 deed.**

The evidence is not published to accuse the investigator or the anonymised executive. It is published to identify finite records that can establish who attended Sun Park, under what authority, in what capacity, what information was gathered, and whether that information later entered the Lava Verde / Club Sei project.

This supports the broader open-kimono methodology: state what is known, what is alleged, what is missing, what would change the analysis, and what competent authorities can obtain directly.
