## Primary-act closure

The earlier AC-interference audit correctly warned against inferring causation merely because a lawyer later ceased acting. That evidential caution no longer governs this narrow matter in the same way because the relevant contemporaneous authority-change evidence has now been rebound.

1. Cristo Suárez Pimentel acted for LPB in DP 1041/2017.
2. No documented voluntary resignation by Cristo has been located.
3. On **25 January 2018**, the Administrador Concursal communicated to Cristo the termination of the professional relationship he maintained with LPB, requested `venia` in DP 1041/2017 and instructed him to abstain from future LPB action. The procurador was separately told not to file pleadings signed by Cristo or another lawyer without the AC's authorisation, with a carve-out for necessary acts in DP 1041 at that point.
4. Cristo continued broader Sun Park legal work after January 2018. A total-resignation narrative is therefore positively unsafe.
5. The **5 March 2018** Decree in DP 1041/2017 records that **LPB requested withdrawal**, CAM did not oppose, and the court terminated the proceeding. Its intervention table lists **Francisco Borja Rodríguez Batllori Laffitte** as LPB's lawyer and **Alejandro Válido Farray** as procurador.
6. The **13 May 2024** judicial-office order independently confirms that DP 1041/2017 was finalised and archived because of the claimant's withdrawal under the 5 March 2018 Decree and states that no proceedings derived from it.

## Current classification

`C5 — DIRECT CONTEMPORANEOUS EVIDENCE THAT AC INTERVENTION CAUSED THE END OF CRISTO'S SPECIFIC FORMAL LPB MANDATE`

This is a matter-specific causation finding about representation. It does **not** establish that the AC personally authored, signed or instructed the later withdrawal pleading, and it does not establish improper motive or unlawfulness.
