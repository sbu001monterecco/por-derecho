# 6. Knowledge: why a knowing-overstatement allegation is unusually strong here

A knowing-falsehood allegation requires more than showing that a proposition was later rejected.

The distinctive feature of Allegation 01 is **source possession and authorship**:

- the contrary cooperation response is reproduced inside the AC's own adverse report;
- the accounting material already received is acknowledged inside that report;
- the rescue / operator / financing information is recorded in that report;
- the AC's earlier 2013 report itself confirms attendance at every requested meeting and demonstrates substantial knowledge of the debtor's legal/economic/accounting state.

Accordingly, Por Derecho may state as an **evidence-based first-person allegation**:

> Gil Marer alleges that the AC knowingly overstated and materially distorted the collaboration record. The allegation is grounded not merely in later disagreement but in the fact that the AC himself possessed and reproduced evidence inconsistent with the global picture of "absence of collaboration" used to support a severe culpability case.

### Boundary

Do **not** publish as an adjudicated fact that the AC committed a criminal falsehood. The current controlled status is:

- `BROAD PROPOSITION CONTRADICTED / NARROWED`;
- `KNOWING OVERSTATEMENT — STRONGLY EVIDENCE-BASED ALLEGATION`;
- `DELIBERATE LIE / CRIMINAL FALSEHOOD — USER ALLEGATION, NOT ADJUDICATED`.

# 7. The alleged self-serving effect — what the frame displaced

The collaboration frame did more than create an adverse description of Gil. It changed the subject of inquiry.

The material in the AC's own record directed attention toward questions including:

1. the Comunidad / Comunidad de Explotación governance and economic structure;
2. the real allocation of hotel operating income, costs, maintenance obligations and Community charges;
3. the collectability and legal basis of amounts owed by Community / third-party actors;
4. the economic viability of continued hotel operation;
5. secured-credit negotiations and restructuring routes;
6. recapitalisation / convenio / exit possibilities;
7. third-party operator interest and customer demand;
8. later disputes over control, value, liquidation and disposition of assets;
9. by January 2019, DI 248/2018 allegations in which Gil/Aweswell were asking Fiscalía to investigate the AC/CAM perimeter while asserting that a financed route to conclude the insolvency was being pursued.

### Controlled framing

It is defensible to say that the **effect** of the broad non-cooperation narrative was to redirect scrutiny:

`What happened to the business, value, control and recovery routes?`

became

`Why was Gil not cooperating with the AC?`

That effect is documentable as an evidential/narrative displacement.

### Motive boundary

The stronger proposition — that the AC **intentionally designed** the allegation to distract from scrutiny of his own administration, the Community/CAM perimeter, liquidation decisions or unrealised recovery routes — remains an `EVIDENCE-BASED MOTIVE HYPOTHESIS` unless direct or sufficiently convergent actor-specific evidence establishes purpose.

The presently supportable non-exclusive motive hypothesis is:

> defence of the AC's own administration and of the liquidation/disposition trajectory already being challenged by Gil/Aweswell.

Do not convert that hypothesis into an established subjective fact.

# 9. Public wording — strongest defensible version

### Spanish

> **Alegación 01 — “falta de colaboración”.** La Administración Concursal sostuvo que la falta de cooperación le había impedido conocer adecuadamente las causas económicas de la insolvencia. Su propio expediente demuestra una realidad más compleja: en 2013 reconocía que Gil había asistido a todas las reuniones a las que fue convocado; en 2019 reprodujo respuestas expresas de cooperación, reconoció contabilidad ya recibida y dejó constancia de información sobre explotación, financiación, convenio, operadores y demanda comercial. La Sentencia 163/2023 tampoco validó una ausencia global de colaboración: reconoció la entrega de la documentación contable poseída por LPB y mantuvo sólo una cuestión más estrecha sobre soportes de determinados créditos. **Gil Marer alega que la presentación global fue conscientemente exagerada y autoservicial porque la propia AC poseía y reproducía los hechos que la contradecían.** No se presenta aquí como una falsedad penal ya declarada, sino como una acusación de falsedad consciente que se somete a prueba documental.

### English

> **Allegation 01 — “failure to collaborate”.** The insolvency administrator claimed that lack of cooperation had prevented him from properly understanding the economic causes of insolvency. His own record shows a materially more complex reality: in 2013 he acknowledged that Gil had attended every meeting to which he was summoned; in 2019 he reproduced express cooperation responses, acknowledged accounting already received, and recorded information about hotel operation, financing, the proposed arrangement, operators and customer demand. Judgment 163/2023 likewise did not validate a global absence of collaboration: it recorded delivery of the accounting documentation LPB possessed and preserved only a narrower issue concerning supporting material for specified credits. **Gil Marer alleges that the global portrayal was knowingly exaggerated and self-serving because the AC himself possessed and reproduced the facts that contradicted it.** This is not presented as an already adjudicated criminal falsehood, but as a knowing-falsehood allegation tested against the documentary record.
