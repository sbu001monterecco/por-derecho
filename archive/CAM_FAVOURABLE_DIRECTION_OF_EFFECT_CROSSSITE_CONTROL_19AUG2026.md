## 1. Why this cross-site layer exists

The repository now contains enough independently dated episodes that the question can no longer be framed only as “was this individual decision right or wrong?” The more useful institutional test is whether different legal, judicial, administrative and commercial transitions repeatedly moved in the same practical direction.

The controlled proposition is:

> **Across several distinct Sun Park/LPB workstreams, the Acosta Matos perimeter repeatedly appears on the beneficiary side of material legal or patrimonial transitions while LPB loses options, leverage, control, competitive position or evidentiary routes. The repetition creates strong optics of directional alignment and justifies targeted investigation of duty, influence, coordination and causation. It does not itself prove conspiracy, corruption or improper motive.**

The purpose of this control is to make that proposition visible across the website without turning a pattern of effects into a criminal verdict.

## 2. The recurring sequence to test

The site should repeatedly ask the same questions:

`LPB protection / option → AC act or omission → judicial response / non-response → CAM-perimeter gain → LPB loss → estate benefit? → alternative innocent explanation? → missing proof?`

This is deliberately more demanding than a narrative accusation. It requires every episode to identify both the beneficiary and the supposed benefit to the insolvency estate.

## 4. Strongest current framing of the Administrador Concursal

### `FUNCTIONAL_DELIVERY_CAM_FAVOURABLE_EFFECT`

The phrase is intentionally about outcome, not secret intention.

Where the AC:

- supported CAM recognition;
- enabled or accepted a transition favourable to CAM;
- failed to reverse a prior effect;
- controlled information, authority, standing or litigation choices; or
- remained the continuous institutional actor while LPB-side counsel and strategies changed,

the repository should ask:

> **What contemporaneous analysis shows that the result was in LPB's insolvency-estate interest, and what measurable benefit did LPB receive?**

A decision can be lawful and still require an estate-interest explanation. A creditor can benefit from lawful insolvency administration. The potential breach issue arises only if specific duties, authority, conduct, patrimonial harm and causation are established.

### Role-boundary test

The retracto reconstruction creates a particularly important boundary question: did any step move the AC from neutral estate administration/supervision into procedural control or advocacy affecting an LPB cause of action, and if so what authority, conflict analysis and estate-interest rationale governed that move?

The exact Cristo authority-change instrument and DP 1041 termination act remain P0. Until recovered, this is a role-boundary question rather than a concluded breach.

## 5. Strongest current framing of the Mercantile Court / judge

### `ACTIVE_AND_PASSIVE_CAM_FAVOURABLE_EFFECT_PATTERN`

The site should distinguish:

- **active judicial effect** — rulings that recognise, implement or preserve a CAM position; and
- **passive/deferential effect** — decisions not to require further verification, not to adopt a neutral control, or to rely on AC verification at a point where LPB sought protection.

The 15 February 2018 order is a clean example because it both:

- actively recognised CAM and rejected LPB's Article 1535 theory; and
- expressly relied on the AC's favourable verification rather than demanding all title material itself.

The same order corrected some amounts in LPB's favour. That counter-record prevents the pattern from being reduced to “every decision was pro-CAM.”

The proper public question is therefore:

> **Across the chronology, when CAM needed recognition, continuation or implementation, what level of judicial intervention was used; when LPB needed disclosure, preservation, neutral verification, restitution or competitive protection, what equivalent intervention was used?**

That is a test of intervention asymmetry and deference. Improper bias or prevaricación require additional proof, including the applicable legal threshold and state of mind.

## 6. Optics of coordination: what can be said now

The pattern creates **strong optics of convergence or coordination** because:

1. materially different episodes repeatedly move in the same beneficiary direction;
2. the same institutional actors recur at key control points;
3. the CAM perimeter later remains present across title, project, finance and operation layers; and
4. LPB-side protections or alternatives repeatedly fail to produce an equivalent enduring position.

But “optics of coordination” must not be converted into “coordination proved.”

The repository should explicitly invite the evidence that would move the proposition:

- communications between actors around decision points;
- instructions or requests linking one actor's conduct to another's expected act;
- unexplained timing or document exchange that cannot be accounted for by ordinary procedure;
- conflict/recusal records;
- private benefit or quid pro quo evidence;
- common planning documents; or
- testimony independently corroborating agreement or influence.

Without such evidence, the site should use **convergence**, **directional alignment**, **recurring beneficiary**, **intervention asymmetry**, **role-boundary concern** and **indicia requiring investigation**.

## 7. Duty/breach/criminal ladder

The site should visually separate five levels.

### Level 1 — repeated effect

**Strongly supported with limitations.** CAM repeatedly appears as beneficiary in distinct material transitions.

### Level 2 — possible professional / insolvency-duty breach

**Investigative question.** Did a specific AC act/omission depart from estate interest, exceed proper role boundaries or fail the applicable duty of care/loyalty, causing measurable loss?

### Level 3 — possible administración desleal

**Criminal hypothesis, not finding.** Requires actor-specific powers, legally relevant abuse/excess/disloyal exercise, patrimonial damage and causation.

### Level 4 — possible improper influence / judicial wrongdoing

**Criminal hypothesis, not finding.** Requires evidence of influence or an unjust judicial act at the criminal threshold plus the legally required knowledge/intent. Adverse or asymmetric outcomes are insufficient.

### Level 5 — concerted action / conspiracy

**Highest-threshold hypothesis.** Requires evidence of communication, agreement or a common criminal plan beyond parallel or aligned outcomes, tied to a legally cognisable offence and the required intent.

The value of the public website is to show why Levels 2–5 deserve finite investigative questions without pretending that Level 5 has already been proved.
