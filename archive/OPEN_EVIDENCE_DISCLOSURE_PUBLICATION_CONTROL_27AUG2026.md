# Open Evidence & Disclosure publication control — 27 August 2026

**Purpose:** publish evidential gaps and assistance requests that can safely be made public without disclosing privileged material or tactical litigation strategy.

## Public principle

Project Sun Rock / Por Derecho is a transparency-led record. Where a missing document, custody trail, authority record, accounting denominator or procedural act is capable of confirming, narrowing or disproving a published proposition, the public website should identify that gap and invite the relevant court, authority, regulator, professional adviser, corporate custodian or other record-holder to help close it through the lawful route.

The public invitation is symmetrical: supportive and contradictory/exculpatory evidence are equally welcome. If reliable primary evidence shows that the public record is wrong, the project should correct the public record visibly.

## What is public

- the five documentary bridges;
- the two quantitative denominators;
- public/source-controlled institutional and corporate custody lanes;
- the class of record requested;
- why the record matters to a public proposition;
- whether a question remains unresolved;
- invitations to preserve, identify custody, produce through lawful process, clarify status, or provide contrary material;
- public monitoring subjects and publication-quality commitments.

## What is deliberately not published

Transparency does not require disclosure of:

- privileged legal advice or counsel work product;
- witness preparation, examination strategy or protected communications;
- internal legal theories not yet put into issue;
- tactical sequencing, filing thresholds, deadline calculations or settlement positions;
- draft pleadings, draft complaints or exact unfiled submissions;
- private contact data, private recipient routes or confidential provider locators;
- native private email bodies or third-party personal data;
- security-sensitive evidence-preservation methods;
- any material whose publication would prejudice lawful litigation, investigation, privacy, privilege or evidence integrity.

## Assistance protocol

A relevant custodian can help by doing one or more of the following:

1. confirm whether the identified record exists;
2. identify the correct custodian or lawful route;
3. preserve the record and relevant metadata;
4. provide access or facilitate production where lawful;
5. provide a certified index if full production is restricted;
6. provide contradictory, exculpatory or contextual material;
7. correct an identity, date, capacity, procedural status or other factual point;
8. explain the lawful basis for a transition that the current record cannot yet bridge.

## Legal/publication boundary

The public register is not a subpoena, discovery request, court filing, complaint, appeal, waiver, admission or merits finding. It does not create a duty where none exists. It does not establish wrongdoing merely because a record has not yet been produced. It does not waive privilege, confidentiality, litigation rights or procedural objections.

Every formal external act continues to require the applicable lawful route and any separate authorization required by repository governance.
