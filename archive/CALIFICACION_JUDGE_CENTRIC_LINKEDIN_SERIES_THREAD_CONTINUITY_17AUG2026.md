## 2. Maximum-pressure rule

The user wants maximum effective pressure, but the strongest pressure is **forensic specificity**, not unsupported adjectives.

Preferred challenge:

> **QUIERO EL PUENTE DOCUMENTAL / PRODUZCAN EL PUENTE DOCUMENTAL.**

Meaning:

- exact allegation;
- exact contrary source;
- date it reached the court;
- filing/service/receipt bridge;
- who actually had it before them;
- what the court/actor did with it;
- what was omitted or rejected;
- why the later adverse finding survived;
- what consequence followed.

Do not increase rhetorical pressure by adding unproved corruption, payment, collusion, clandestine agreement or motive. That can reduce effective pressure by giving the target an easy overstatement objection.

## 3. Judge-centric use of institutional passivity / neutrality

## 7. LPB is not the hotel — controlling actor/property/exploitation map

This distinction is central to the new publication series.

### LPB

LPB was:

- the debtor in Concurso 36/2012;
- functionally the **principal property-owning company** / principal propco within a mixed-ownership hotel complex;
- owner of a majority, but not all, of the real-estate perimeter;
- holder of its own contractual rights, receivables, leases and economic interests.

`Propco` is economic shorthand, not a Spanish statutory classification.

Do not write:

`LPB = the entire Hotel Sun Park = all registered property = the hotel operator = all hotel exploitation`.

### Mixed ownership

A later 21-Jul-2021 certified schedule records 262 properties:

- 190 LPB;
- 54 CAM;
- 18 third parties.

This is structural corroboration that LPB held the predominant property count but not the whole perimeter. Do not back-project the exact 190/262 ratio to June 2018 without a June-2018 title schedule. Preserve `property count ≠ built area ≠ value ≠ voting power`.

### Hotel exploitation

Hotel exploitation was a legally distinct layer. The 6-Jun-2018 signed industry-lease document places the operator-side party with Clubotel La Dorada / ONA Hotels and the lessor-side with the Comunidad de Propietarios, while reciting a separate historical CEXP exploitation structure.

## 8. Matkator — extraconcursal property and injury track

Matkator is a separate, non-concursada company. Its property did **not** become part of LPB's insolvency estate merely because it was physically and operationally integrated into Hotel Sun Park.

Mandatory rule:

> **LPB estate ≠ Matkator property.**

For public drafting, the strongest current proposition is that Matkator presents a separate **extraconcursal injury / ownership / access / works / use / exploitation / fruits / value** track.

The 2025 Matkator complaint and related sources contain allegations of unauthorised transformation, exclusion and exploitation. Those allegations are not automatically proven by the complaint. Finca-by-finca title, works, access, revenue, authority and damages require primary reconciliation.

Judge-centric significance:

> **If acts around Concurso 36/2012 affected property outside LPB's estate, the judicial/AC causation analysis cannot be confined to an internal debtor-estate story. It must ask whether court/AC acts or omissions enabled or failed to address harm to extraconcursal rights and whether later Calificación attribution ignored the actual distribution of capacity and control.**

Do not imply that the Mercantile Court automatically had insolvency jurisdiction over every Matkator right. The question is actor-specific notice, power, effect and procedural route.

## 9. Private-actor / Comunidad instrumentalisation hypothesis

The thread developed a stronger, more disciplined formulation than simply alleging that `the Comunidad was captured`.

### Current project allegation

Gil/Project Sun Rock alleges that a private-actor perimeter used or captured functions of the Comunidad/governance machinery to obtain or reinforce practical influence over:

- debt certification;
- voting / exclusion;
- representation;
- access;
- security;
- keys/locks;
- maintenance/common-area authority;
- project/reform decisions;
- later operational/control consequences.

This is a **party allegation / evidence-based investigative hypothesis**, not an adjudicated finding of criminal capture.

### Acta / debt falsification

Do not publish `the acta/debt was falsified` as an established fact merely because a complaint uses that characterisation.

Use the non-circular authority test:

`TITLE / OWNER → POWER / REPRESENTATION → APPOINTMENT → NOTICE → ATTENDANCE → VOTE → QUOTA → ACT → ORIGINAL CUSTODY → CERTIFICATE → DOWNSTREAM USE`.

A Community act/certificate does not prove the authority that created it merely because the same document recites that authority.

Where falsification is alleged, attribute it expressly and identify the exact act/debt certificate/source that must be tested.

### `Shadow administrator` / `administrador de hecho`

Do not state that private actors **were** shadow/de facto administrators unless the legal/factual test is satisfied.

The stronger current formulation is:

> **Gil alleges that certain private actors came to exercise functions resembling de facto or shadow administration over important parts of LPB's productive assets and the wider hotel platform, through combinations of creditor position, Community governance, access/security, project/works and later acquisition/operation. Whether that reaches the legal status of `administrador de hecho` must be proved function by function and period by period.**

The repository already supports a strong **multi-capacity conflict chronology** for CAM/perimeter actors. That is distinct from the stricter legal classification of de facto administrator.

## 10. Control/capacity is the causal question

For every post-7-Jun-2018 allegation of:

- deterioration;
- non-operation;
- lost income;
- inability to maintain;
- missing documentation;
- non-recovery;
- failure to rescue/exit;
- alleged aggravation of insolvency;

ask:

> **Who actually had legal power, material access, practical possession, security/keys, records, banking/PMS access, staff/operator control and the practical ability to perform the alleged act at that date?**

Keep separate:

- title;
- legal possession;
- material/de facto control;
- procedural authority;
- actual access/capacity.

A later title deed does not retrospectively authorise earlier material control. Conversely, a material-control event does not itself prove whole-hotel legal title or criminal intent.
