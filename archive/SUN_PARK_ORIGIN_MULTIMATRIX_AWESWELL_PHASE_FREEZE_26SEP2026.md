## Phase 2 — 2008 whole-hotel transaction object versus actual completion

This is the key frozen transition.

### Layer A — what the transaction was presented as

Alimarket, dated **16 July 2008**, reported that Monte Lanza had reached a June agreement with the Israeli investor Multimatrix to transfer **the property and operation of Aparthotel Sun Park (3E – 220 apartments)**. It described the property as acquired through Luchy and operation as passing through the exploitation structure.

For Truth Machine purposes this is material evidence of the **transaction object as publicly/market commercially represented: the hotel**, not a small isolated parcel sale.

It is contemporaneous trade-press evidence. It is not a deed and cannot prove finca-by-finca completion.

### Layer B — what the buyer-side record says was actually acquired

A Multimatrix-commissioned impairment study dated 24 March 2010 states that on **18 June 2008**, through foreign subsidiaries, Multimatrix purchased the Sun Park hotel rights/activities comprising:

- **171 accommodation units**;
- **29 facility areas**;
- hotel management rights;
- for **EUR 11.8 million**.

A controlled 15-Jul-2008 CEXP-period record separately places LPB with **171 apartments + 29 commercial properties** and Monte Lanza with **11 commercial properties**.

The graph therefore must preserve both truths simultaneously:

> **WHOLE HOTEL = transaction object / intention / contemporaneous market representation.**

The difference is not noise. It is a first-class state transition and a P0 investigative object.
