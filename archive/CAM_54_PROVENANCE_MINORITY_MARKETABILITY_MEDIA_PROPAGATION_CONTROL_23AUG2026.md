# Project Sun Rock — 54 CAM properties: provenance, minority marketability and media-propagation control

**Control date:** 23 August 2026  
**Status:** source-controlled reverse-engineering / mandatory outreach context  
**Purpose:** prevent `54 CAM` from being used as a clean ownership starting point or as shorthand for acquisition/control of Sun Park as a whole.

## 1. Controlling conclusion

The July-2021 RIC Private Equity certificate's `54 CAM / 190 LPB / 18 terceros` split is an important internal control because it contradicts the earlier integrated whole-project narrative. But **54 CAM is not itself a complete provenance answer**.

Whenever the 54 figure is used, reconstruct:

`finca → prior owner/entity → operating/CEXP status → Community debt/vote/litigation context → seller authority → deed/judicial instrument → price → payment/set-off → Registry date → possession/access → CAM capacity at acquisition → purchaser knowledge → later use in RICPE/HNT/MYND narrative`.

No acquisition of one or several private units automatically conferred title to the rest of the hotel, CEXP rights, authority over the Owners' Community, or consent of other owners.

## 2. Why minority units were structurally difficult to sell or value

The repository and public site show a structurally unusual asset:

- Sun Park functioned economically as a hotel while title was divided unit by unit.
- The 2008 transaction left LPB as majority PropCo while minority/private units remained outside LPB and hotel exploitation sat in a separate owners-derived structure.
- Owner participation in CEXP was not universal; some owners opposed/reserved or later disputed participation.
- AP Judgment 89/2014 upheld the individual proprietary/possessory position of a seven-claimant, 18-unit perimeter against Monterecco/Pink's possession/operation. This is adverse evidence against any theory that hotel operation itself erased individual title.
- At the same time, a private unit inside a physically integrated hotel could be economically dependent on common access, utilities, hotel operation, licensing and the behaviour of the Community/operator. Individual title was real; standalone economic autonomy could still be difficult.
- The 2015 and 2016 Owners' Community records show extreme separation between attendance/representation and voting power, attributed debt exceeding €3.6m, incomplete accounting records, retroactive fee reduction from €171.89 to €22.26 and recalculated balances/certificates. These facts justify reconstruction of whether disputed Community debt, voting exclusion, litigation, access or service uncertainty burdened marketability or bargaining position. They do **not** by themselves prove that any owner was coerced to sell.

The correct question is therefore not simply `could a minority sell?`, but:

> What exactly could that owner convey, with what operating/use position, subject to what Community/CEXP dispute or debt, at what price, and what did the buyer know about those constraints?

## 3. Montelanza / Molina / JSP provenance requires heightened review

The controlled Montelanza/Molina register separates people, entities and units and prohibits collapsing them into one undifferentiated faction.

Relevant public/repository controls include:

- Montelanza's historical Sun Park ownership/operation role;
- JSP/Sun Group development links and Montelanza governance/capital connections;
- JSP properties 8503–8507, which the public Acosta Matos page already identifies as five adjacent commercial properties originating in the founding/developer perimeter, later appearing in the Matkator/Aweswell acquisition pipeline and ultimately in the CAM perimeter;
- Montelanza properties 8499–8500, whose bridge remains unresolved: a 2016 conditional allocation route, April-2017 Montelanza Registry position and an April-2018 schedule naming CAM require reconciliation of conditions, onward instrument, authority, price/payment and Registry entry.

Accordingly, every CAM acquisition originating in the Montelanza/Molina/JSP perimeter requires property-by-property heightened review. Heightened review means **prove the chain**; it does not mean invalidity, fraud, coercion or liability is presumed.

## 4. Purchaser-knowledge asymmetry

CAM must not be analysed as if it were always an arm's-length outsider encountering Sun Park for the first time.

At different dates the CAM/JDAM perimeter appears in distinct capacities including:

- creditor/credit purchaser;
- owner of specific properties;
- Community participant;
- later project/promoter perimeter;
- material-control beneficiary/alleged participant;
- RICPE governance/project connection; and
- later contributor into HNT/MYND structures.

Each capacity must remain legally distinct, but the sequence raises a **purchaser-knowledge and due-diligence question**: what did CAM know when acquiring each of the 54 properties about minority litigation, CEXP/operation, Community debt, seller authority, estate boundaries, access, title disputes and other owners' rights?

## 5. The critical 2020 → 2021 contradiction

The public record preserves categorical November-2020 RICPE-webinar statements attributed to José Acosta that the three complexes had been acquired with own funds, belonged to the company and were unencumbered, together with fixed Sun Park project particulars.

The July-2021 RICPE certificate then recorded:

- only 54 properties in CAM;
- 190 LPB properties;
- 18 third-party properties;
- future/conditional CAM title to the LPB block;
- no signed LOI; and
- comprehensive due diligence not begun/completed at that date.

The bounded public conclusion is therefore:

> the integrated 2020 ownership/control presentation was not a complete and accurate account of the property/title position later documented internally by RICPE.

That contradiction does not by itself establish dishonest intent, criminal liability or the validity/invalidity of any particular CAM acquisition.

## 6. Media propagation: upstream narrative ≠ knowing media falsehood

Future outreach and website text must separate four propositions:

1. **UPSTREAM REPRESENTATION:** CAM/RICPE/Canarian Hospitality/Government or another identifiable source made or supplied a project/title/finance representation.
2. **MEDIA ATTRIBUTION/REPUBLICATION:** an outlet reported, quoted, summarised or reproduced that representation.
3. **INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION:** the outlet independently checked title, property perimeter or the underlying primary document.
4. **KNOWINGLY FALSE PUBLICATION:** the outlet knew a proposition was false and nevertheless published it.

Evidence for (1) or (2) does not prove (3) or (4).

For La Voz/EKN specifically:

- 9-Dec-2022 reporting expressly relies on Government approval, RICPE financing architecture and quotations from Enrique Guerra;
- 23-Mar-2023 reporting expressly says the refurbishment was presented by Canarian Hospitality and later uses `según sus promotores` language.

This supports asking whether the newspaper **propagated an upstream project narrative** and what it independently verified. It does not support accusing La Voz of knowingly publishing a falsehood.

Do not state that CAM or RICPE `caused the newspaper to publish` unless the actual press release, email, briefing, dossier, instruction or other transmission bridge is recovered. Where the article itself identifies a presentation or quoted source, say exactly that.

## 7. Mandatory media question set when `54 CAM` appears

A journalist-facing draft using the 54 figure must ask:

1. Which 54 properties?
2. Who sold/transferred each one and in what capacity?
3. Which came from Montelanza/Molina/JSP or other minority perimeters?
4. What did AP 89/2014 or other property-specific litigation mean for the right being sold?
5. What Community debt, set-off, voting or access issue affected the seller/unit, if any?
6. What deed, judgment or instrument transferred title?
7. What price was agreed and what payment or set-off occurred?
8. When was each transfer registered?
9. What did CAM know as purchaser, creditor and/or Community participant?
10. What rights beyond bare unit title were claimed or represented after acquisition?
11. How did a 54-property position become translated into a public/investor picture of an acquired/integrated Sun Park project?
12. What exact source did each media outlet receive and what, if anything, did it independently verify?

## 8. Publication and outreach discipline

Preferred formulation:

> `54 CAM` is not proof that CAM owned Sun Park. It is a checkpoint showing a limited, fragmented property position that itself requires seller-by-seller provenance reconstruction. The later public project narrative must be tested against that provenance and against the 190 LPB and 18 third-party properties recorded in the same internal certificate.

Avoid:

- `the 54 were stolen`;
- `the minorities were forced to sell`;
- `CAM bought the hotel`;
- `RICPE knew the whole transaction was fraudulent`;
- `the press knowingly lied`; or
- `CAM/RICPE caused publication` without a transmission source.
