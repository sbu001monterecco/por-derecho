## C/D correction and proof boundary

`C1` and `C2` are the alleged adverse documentary phases. `D-MIXED` and `D-OPEN` are evidence statuses, not people, entities or criminal perimeters. D cannot be described as criminal. A D record may enter an actor-specific hypothesis only through proof of the individual act, capacity, source, knowledge and connection.

The criminal hypothesis is framed as possible knowing creation, adoption or use of materially false or ineffective authority, debt, voting or ACTAS to produce exclusion, control, cost, finance or benefit. Each proposed bridge still requires actor, capacity, document, alleged falsity/inefficacy, knowledge, purpose, act of use, recipient, patrimonial effect, causation, beneficiary, contrary evidence, independent title and reasoned exclusion of error or lawful alternative.
