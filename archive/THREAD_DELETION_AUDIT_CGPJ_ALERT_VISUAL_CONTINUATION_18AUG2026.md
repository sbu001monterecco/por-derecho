## 4. Visual design decisions that must survive deletion

The implemented design is a **causal-supervision map, not a conspiracy diagram**.

### Central spine

`pre-existing risk → RICPE/title alert → CAM/AC opposition → judicial availability/knowledge at controlled level → contradictory October 2021 descriptions → 26-Jan-2022 decisional fork → implementation tempo → deed/Registry → increasing irreversibility`

### Three zones

1. what was known / reported;
2. what the insolvency system did;
3. what happened later.

Later consequence is expressly separated from historical judicial knowledge.

### Arrow semantics

- solid = documented procedural/transactional progression;
- dashed = causal/reliance relationship requiring verification;
- dotted/open = unresolved documentary bridge.

### 2022 tempo

The public visual shows:

- 26 Jan — contradiction acknowledged/reframed; July material confirmed admitted but irrelevant;
- 8 Feb — CAM requests testimonies;
- 14 Feb — LAJ issues testimonies;
- 16 Feb — challenge;
- 21 Feb — deed;
- 20 Apr — Registry;
- 26 Apr — later challenge decision.

This is labelled **tempo of implementation**, not proof by itself of unlawful haste.

### Plan/orders versus implementation

The visual does not pre-label the final transaction “non-compliant”. It compares prior liquidation/order architecture with implementation and uses statuses such as:

- documented treatment/contradiction;
- actor-by-actor separation;
- needs reconciliation;
- open bridge / production required.

Controlling question: if implementation differed from prior plan/order architecture, what act authorised the difference and on what information?

## 8. Mobile QA defect identified in the continuation

User screenshots of both ES and EN mobile rendering exposed a genuine contrast/accessibility defect:

- `.hero-map` set `color:#fff`;
- the pale `.rule.warning` callout changed the background but did not reset text color;
- result: near-white text on a near-white warning panel, materially reducing legibility on mobile.

The screenshots themselves are not copied into the repository; they served as UI/QA evidence. The durable project intelligence is the defect mechanism, fix and verification state.
