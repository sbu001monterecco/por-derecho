## Purpose

Record the lawyers and firms who acted for Gil Marer, LPB, Aweswell or the related Project-side perimeter, and test whether conduct by the Insolvency Administrator affected their ability to defend those interests.

This register does not assume that every substitution, withdrawal, unpaid fee or disagreement was caused by the Insolvency Administrator. Each entry must separate:

`MANDATE / WORK → AC ACT OR OMISSION → IMMEDIATE EFFECT → LATER WITHDRAWAL OR SUBSTITUTION → CAUSATION GRADE`
