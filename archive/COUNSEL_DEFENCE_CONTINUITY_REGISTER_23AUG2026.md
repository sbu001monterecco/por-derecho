## Purpose

Record the lawyers and firms who acted for Gil Marer, LPB, Aweswell or the related Project-side perimeter, and test whether conduct by the Insolvency Administrator affected the defence.

Every entry preserves one integrated mandate account:

`ACCEPTED / BILLED SCOPE → AC ACT OR OMISSION → PROFESSIONAL RESPONSE OR LIMIT → RESULT → DETERIORATION / TRANSITION → CLIENT POSITION + CONTRARY EVIDENCE`

A formal withdrawal reason is relevant evidence, but it must not be used as a complete characterisation of the earlier mandate, its practical effectiveness or the effect of AC conduct. Internal source-status analysis remains necessary; the public account must not reduce a nuanced mandate to a binary causation badge.

## Publication locks

1. The 19 February 2020 *providencia* granted the prior procedural licence required by Article 215.2; it is not a merits ruling or proof that a complaint was filed, admitted, upheld or resulted in liability. Its procedural character does not erase its documented practical effect on the defence.
2. “Abusive” is Gil/Patricia’s characterisation, not an adjudicated finding.
3. A temporal sequence alone is not a causal finding, but an integrated mandate history must include practical and cumulative effects shown by the record.
4. A professional’s stated withdrawal reasons and strongest contrary evidence remain visible without being treated as an exhaustive explanation of mandate performance or AC effect.
5. Parrilla’s substitution remains a client decision.
6. Cuatrecasas’s formal withdrawal reason remains attributed to unpaid fees. Public wording must also preserve the firm’s broader billed work, favourable results, mandate boundaries, conditioned responses and the client’s attributed protection-failure allegation.
7. Garrigues’s work predates the relevant AC conduct; no causal claim is made.
8. Current Kepler–Karst identities are professional-continuity notes, not corporate-successor liability.
9. Remaining professionals are B1, B2, B3… pending verified client, mandate, dates and publication decision.
10. Privileged advice, strategy and weaknesses remain private.
