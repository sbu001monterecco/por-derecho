**Control date:** 21 August 2026  
**Status:** ACTIVE SOURCE-STATUS CONTROL / PUBLIC-SAFE PROPOSITION MAP  
**Purpose:** preserve the contradiction identified by Gil Marer without conflating the Owners’ Community with the Comunidad de Explotación (CEXP), or converting an allegation of appropriation into an adjudicated fact.

## 1. The contradiction to test

Gil Marer alleges that the disputed Owners’ Community governance line which emerged in 2011 rejected or displaced the CEXP exploitation structure, yet a later Acosta Matos-era Owners’ Community purported to exercise powers reaching directly into hotel operation and was followed by the segregation of the hotel economic unit into Hotel New Trend, S.L.U. and later MYND operation.

The strongest source-controlled formulation is not currently that José Daniel Acosta Matos became President of CEXP. **No reviewed primary source located in this pass establishes that proposition.** The located 4 February 2022 instrument identifies him as president of the **Owners’ Community**, a legally distinct body.

The material contradiction is instead:

`2011 COMMUNITY COMPETENCE LIMIT → CEXP MAINTENANCE DISPLACED → SEPARATE CEXP GOVERNANCE STILL DOCUMENTED → 2022 OWNERS’ COMMUNITY PURPORTS TO AUTHORISE UNIFIED HOTEL OPERATION → HNT HOTEL-ECONOMIC-UNIT SEGREGATION`

The missing bridge is the primary legal/governance instrument, if any, by which CEXP was validly removed, dissolved, replaced, retired, or transferred its owners-derived exploitation mandate, rights or obligations to HNT or the later operator.

## 3. The “denial → appropriation → replacement” allegation, correctly bounded

Gil Marer’s allegation can be preserved as follows:

> The minority-led/disputed Owners’ Community line first rejected or displaced the exploitation-side CEXP structure; after the 2018 material-control change, the later Acosta Matos-era Community purported to exercise hotel-operation powers that the Community itself had treated as outside its competence in 2011; the subsequent HNT/MYND structure then operated the hotel. Gil alleges that this amounted to appropriation and retirement/replacement of CEXP without valid CEXP authority.

This is a **PARTY ALLEGATION / INVESTIGATIVE THESIS**, not a finding that JDAM self-appointed as CEXP president, that HNT unlawfully succeeded CEXP, or that any named person committed an offence.

## 4. Produce the succession bridge

The public and internal investigation should require five finite records:

1. **CEXP office/governance bridge.** Produce the CEXP resolution(s), notices, member list, proxies, quorum and votes appointing, removing or replacing its president/office-holders after the last controlled CEXP governance record.
2. **CEXP extinction/transfer bridge.** Produce the instrument dissolving, retiring, replacing or otherwise terminating CEXP, or transferring its collective exploitation mandate, rights and obligations.
3. **Community competence bridge.** Identify the legal and documentary basis on which the Owners’ Community purported in 2022 to approve licences and unified hotel operation despite the 2011 record’s own competence boundary.
4. **HNT/operator rights bridge.** Produce the instrument by which HNT and/or the later hotel operator acquired exploitation rights over the relevant units and common operating structure, separately from corporate segregation or later title to particular assets.
5. **Downstream reliance bridge.** Identify exactly what CEXP/Community/HNT authority documents were supplied to Yaiza, Cabildo, professional bodies, investors, financiers, tourism authorities and the current operator, and what each recipient verified.

## 5. What would narrow or defeat the allegation

The repository must preserve contrary material capable of narrowing the case, including:

- a valid CEXP meeting and succession/dissolution instrument;
- a statute or contract authorising the later transition;
- valid owner-by-owner exploitation mandates to HNT/current operator;
- a judicial or administrative determination squarely deciding CEXP succession/termination on the merits;
- proof that the 2022 Community language was limited to common-property/licensing matters and did not purport to transfer private-unit exploitation rights; or
- evidence that HNT/current operation arose independently from valid later title/consents rather than from the disputed Community authority chain.

## 7. Non-conflation rule

Always keep separate:

`OWNERS’ COMMUNITY ≠ CEXP ≠ LPB ≠ MONTERECCO/PINK ≠ CAM/CAMSA ≠ HNT ≠ CURRENT HOTEL OPERATOR`

A natural person’s office in one body does not establish office in another. Corporate segregation of an economic unit does not, by itself, establish transfer of every owners-derived collective exploitation right. Material control does not, by itself, establish governance succession.
