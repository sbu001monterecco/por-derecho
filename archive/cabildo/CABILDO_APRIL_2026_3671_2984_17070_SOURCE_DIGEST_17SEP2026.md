## 2. Executive finding

The three files form a coherent administrative sequence, but they do **not** prove civil ownership of every Sun Park finca.

1. **3671/2022** began as a Community of Owners request to restore the tourism exploitation from 159 units / 477 places to the original 220 units / 660 places.
2. Cabildo expressly identified a representation/title deficiency in March 2022: the first Community certificate authorised works on **common areas**, not the all-unit tourism modification, and Cabildo required deeds for units owned by the applicant-side owners plus an enabling title from the owners of the remaining units.
3. The response package later contained a materially broader certificate, a 220-unit schedule, property/title material, a 206-row purchase matrix, individual authorisations, plans, municipal material and later reform documents.
4. By 8 August 2022 the response no longer asked to reincorporate 220 units. It expressly requested reincorporation of **206 units / 618 places** and asserted that enabling titles had been supplied for those 206 units.
5. **2984/2023** then presented Hotel New Trend, S.L. (“HNT”) as owner of the inmueble called Sun Park by reference to a 16 December 2022 segregation deed and asked to change property/exploitation titularity and the name to MYND Yaiza. Cabildo required further title/classification material; no final merits act appears in the package reviewed.
6. **17070/2023** re-presented the HNT change in October 2023 on a narrower administrative footing: change the holder/exploiter and commercial name, expressly **without changing capacity or category**. On 18 October 2023 the RGT registered HNT as holder and MYND YAIZA as the commercial name, with one-star classification; Cabildo then notified HNT and Yaiza.

The safe evidential conclusion is therefore a **progressive administrative transition of asserted authority and tourism-register holder**, not proof that HNT or CAM acquired every privately owned finca or every right in the complex.

## 3. 3671/2022 — all-unit reincorporation file

### 3.1 Opening request

The Cabildo executive summary records:

- opening: 22 February 2022;
- entry: 2022-E-RE-3061 of 21 February 2022;
- interested party: Comunidad de Propietarios Sun Park;
- historic tourism basis asserted: 220 units / 660 places in 1991;
- 2012 reduction asserted: 61 units / 183 places removed, leaving 159 units / 477 places;
- request: reincorporate the totality of the establishment — 220 units / 660 places — invoking the unity-of-exploitation principle.

The 220-unit schedule in document 16 is internally arithmetically consistent:

- **220 rows total**;
- **159 marked “EN EXPLOTACIÓN”**;
- **61 marked “NO EXPLOTACIÓN”**;
- total fixed places: 440;
- total convertible places: 220.

That schedule is an applicant-side administrative representation. It is not itself a Land Registry title schedule.

### 3.2 Cabildo’s 14 March 2022 requirement

The document titled `Requerimiento de documentación para modificación unidades` is a central primary source. Cabildo records the then-current RGT position as one-star, 159 units / 477 places, with the Community as exploitation holder.

Cabildo also states that the certificate supplied with the initial filing only authorised the representative to seek permits/licences for reform, conservation and maintenance of the **common areas**, and expressly says that this was **not the object of 3671/2022**.

Cabildo therefore required, among other things:

1. accreditation/authority for this specific procedure;
2. documentation of adaptation to Decreto 142/2010;
3. an updated plan distinguishing existing and proposed units;
4. a unit/place schedule;
5. **deeds for the units owned by the relevant owners and an enabling title issued by the owners of the remaining units**; and
6. maintenance contracts.

This is important because the authority/title issue was not invented retrospectively: Cabildo itself identified it as a condition for processing the all-unit request.

### 3.3 Two materially different 18 February 2022 certificates

The April corpus contains two certificates both dated 18 February 2022 and both purporting to certify point 2 of the 4 February 2022 extraordinary Community meeting.

- **Document 12** states that the meeting approved the reform/conservation/maintenance project for the **common areas** and authorised the representative to seek the permits/licences required for immediate works on those common areas.
- **Document 13, page 2** contains materially expanded wording. In addition to the common-area language, it says the agenda/debate and authorisation included requesting from Cabildo the modification of establishment data for incorporation of **all accommodation units and places for which the complex was authorised**, under the unity-of-exploitation principle.

Cabildo’s 14 March requirement tracks the narrower common-area certificate and says it was insufficient for the 3671 object. The later response package contains the broader certificate.

**Controlled conclusion:** there is an objective documentary discrepancy between two certificates carrying the same date and referring to the same meeting item. The corpus does **not**, by itself, establish why the wording differs, whether one superseded/corrected the other, or whether either faithfully reproduces the original minute. The original signed acta, native certificate files, issuance metadata and Community minute book are therefore priority verification evidence.

### 3.4 August title/authority response: 206, not 220

The 8 August 2022 response (`3671-35`) says it is supplying the enabling titles requested by Cabildo and states:

- enabling titles are accredited for **206 accommodation units / 618 places**;
- deeds, a unit summary, location plan and owner authorisations are supplied;
- the final request is expressly the reincorporation of **206 units / 618 places**.

This is a material change from the original 220/660 request.

The accompanying purchase matrix (`3671-36`) contains **206 rows**. Table extraction shows:

- 205 rows labelled `CAMSA`;
- one row labelled `JUAN MEDIR MORTAL`;
- only **205 unique apartment labels**, because apartment label `159` appears twice with different registry-finca numbers;
- comparison against the 220-unit schedule leaves **15 distinct apartment labels absent** from the 206-row matrix: `501, 505, 507, 511, 551, 559, 561, 603, 701, 708, 808, 901, 909, 910, 914`.

Because one apartment label is duplicated in the matrix, the numerical 14-row difference between 220 and 206 corresponds to 15 distinct labels missing from the cross-list. This needs source-level reconciliation; it should not be “corrected” by inference.

### 3.5 Authorisations

The 1 August 2022 CAMSA authorisation (`3671-38`) authorises the Community, described as the exploitation company, to seek reincorporation of **the units of which CAMSA is holder**, and also authorises a future data modification for a change to another exploitation company if necessary.

A separate redacted personal authorisation (`3671-40`) states that its signer, “en su propio nombre y derecho”, authorises reincorporation of **the unit of which the signer says he is holder** and a future change of exploitation company. The redacted source text itself does not identify the unit number. Any unit identification carried in later filenames/annotations must therefore be cross-checked against the deed/Registry record rather than treated as text of the authorisation.

### 3.6 Unit/title conflicts requiring a certified crosswalk

The 3671 purchase matrix is an applicant-submitted title representation, not a Registry certification. It contains positions that conflict with later Matkator-controlled records and therefore require certified reconciliation. Examples:

- the matrix associates apartment **758** with CAMSA and finca **8675**;
- later Matkator-controlled evidence describes finca **8584** as apartment **758**, with cadastral reference 5137001FS1953N0088JY;
- the matrix associates apartment **510** with CAMSA and finca **8585**;
- later Matkator-controlled work records associate finca **8588** with apartment **510**, subject to current Registry certification and a full historical crosswalk.

This is not a basis to choose one side by assertion. It is a direct documentary conflict requiring a certified `finca → CRU → cadastral reference → historic apartment → current physical room/configuration → Registry holder by date` reconciliation.

The standing recovery rule remains: **8584/8588 are examples, not the recovery denominator.** Every affected finca/unit must be proved separately.

### 3.7 No final merits act established from the reviewed executive summary

The executive summary’s finalisation fields are blank in the reviewed copy. The April corpus includes later notifications and municipal/Cabildo communications, but the current review has not established a final merits act that conclusively determines the civil-title questions raised by the unit lists.

The next request should therefore seek a certified statement of current/final procedural status, not assume there was no final act.

## 4. 2984/2023 — HNT request for titularity/exploitation/name change

### 4.1 Opening position

The executive summary records:

- opening: 17 February 2023;
- entry: 2023-E-RE-3086 of 16 February 2023;
- applicant: Hotel New Trend, S.L.;
- applicant-side statement: HNT says it is owner of the inmueble called Sun Park by virtue of a 16 December 2022 segregation deed;
- request: change **property titularity and exploitation titularity** to HNT and change the commercial name to MYND YAIZA.

This is an applicant representation within an administrative file. It does not by itself establish ownership of every finca.

### 4.2 Segregation deed boundary

The 16 December 2022 deed states that the Sun Park assets transferred in the partial demerger/segregation are the **registered fincas owned by CAMSA in the Sun Park complex**, identified in Annex 2 of the segregation project, and that HNT acquires that segregated patrimony by universal succession.

The deed/project also gives accounting effect from **1 January 2022**.

Safe use:

- the deed supports transfer of the CAMSA-owned finca perimeter identified in the segregation project;
- it is not proof that third-party Sun Park fincas were transferred;
- an accounting-effect date is not automatically the date of civil acquisition of every property right;
- the full Annex 2 inventory and subsequent Registry effects are necessary for an exact property crosswalk.

### 4.3 Cabildo requirement

Cabildo’s requirement records the then-current RGT position as one-star, 159 units / 477 places, with the Community as exploitation holder. It also records that HNT was seeking a modification associated with four-star classification and 220 units / 660 places.

Cabildo therefore required, among other things:

- enabling title/ownership documentation from Acosta Matos concerning the Hotel Sun Park complex; and
- if applicable, the provisional-classification application for the four-star / 220-unit / 660-place proposal.

The reviewed package contains requirements/receipts and source documents, but no final merits act has been established in this control.

## 7. Connected context: Matkator, Cabildo knowledge and later disputes

### 7.1 Pre-2022 Cabildo baseline

The separately produced 498/2020, 547/2020 and 21219/2021 files establish that Matkator’s ownership/affected-unit position had already been put before Cabildo, Cabildo said its tourism file did not then show Matkator among recorded owners, and Cabildo requested ownership proof. The 2021 produced intake presently does not itself show the promised nota simple.

The April 3671 corpus therefore sits directly on top of a pre-existing title/identity question. The March 2022 requirement is consistent with Cabildo independently recognising that a tourism-exploitation request covering additional units needed unit-specific authority/title material.

### 7.2 Matkator examples are not the denominator

Later primary/controlled material identifies Matkator interests including fincas 8584 and 8588, and other fincas remain in the wider asserted recovery perimeter. The correct control remains:

> **8584/8588 are examples — not the recovery denominator.**

The April administrative matrices should be tested against every relevant Registry finca, not used to collapse the perimeter to two examples.

### 7.3 Relationship to Yaiza 2022 files

The same period contains Yaiza files concerning works, legalisation, first occupation and legality protection. The appropriate next matrix is:

`finca/unit → owner/title by date → Community authority relied upon → Yaiza works/legalisation/occupation act → Cabildo tourism inclusion/holder act → HNT/MYND/operator treatment → source document → verification → downstream effect`.

No inference should be made that a municipal works/occupation act decided civil title, or that an RGT entry validated every title representation used elsewhere.

## 8. Procedural use after 17 September 2026

### 8.1 Decreto 2026-6999

The 17 September 2026 production under Decreto 6999 contains 498/2020, 547/2020 and 21219/2021 but not 3671/2022. The April corpus proves that 3671/2022 **had already been supplied** in April 2026 as `3671_2022.zip`.

Therefore the correct issue is **traceability and completeness**, not “Cabildo never gave us 3671”. Ask Cabildo to:

- identify the April 3671 delivery as the prior satisfaction/production relied upon, if that is its position;
- confirm whether the April native ZIP is complete and still corresponds to the file as currently held;
- identify any additions, later documents or final act after the April production;
- reconcile why the finite August request named four files but the 17 September folder contained three without expressly identifying the prior 3671 production.

### 8.2 Decreto 2026-7000 / Comisionado

The three files materially strengthen the severance argument. They show that Cabildo holds identifiable, existing administrative records: applications, certificates, title matrices, plans, deeds, requirements, reports, RGT changes and notification transmissions. A transparency authority need not perform a criminal investigation or create a forensic reconstruction to order access to such pre-existing records.

The Comisionado filing should therefore stay document-access focused and should not ask the Comisionado to decide ownership, criminality, causation or damages.

### 8.3 Continuing patrimonial harm / Ley 40/2015

For institutional communications, the evidence can support the proposition that alleged continuing patrimonial harm warrants preservation, traceability and mitigation while questions remain open. It does **not** establish liability under articles 32–37 of Ley 40/2015; that remains expressly reserved and would require the statutory elements, causation and quantification.

## 11. Raw-binary withholding note

The three raw ZIPs are intentionally **not** included in the public GitHub repository. Reasons:

- the files contain personal identifiers and source evidence requiring a public/private review;
- the repository governance forbids committing raw private evidence or privileged/personal material merely to preserve it;
- `3671_2022.zip` is approximately 356.6 MB and is above ordinary GitHub file-size limits;
- the GitHub continuity mirror does not replace the controlled native custody source.

The repository record should therefore point to this digest/manifest and the controlled evidence custodian, not to a public raw ZIP.
