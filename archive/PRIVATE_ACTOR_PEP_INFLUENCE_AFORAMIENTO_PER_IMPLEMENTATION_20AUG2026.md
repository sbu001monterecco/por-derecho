## Current legal/source control

1. **PEP/PRP:** Ley 10/2010 art. 14 controls. PEP officeholder status is separate from family/allegado enhanced-diligence status.
2. **Influence:** CP arts. 428–430 are not satisfied by access, acquaintance, favourable outcomes or a claim of influence alone. A graded 0–8 evidence ladder was created.
3. **Aforamiento:** office/status/date/law/jurisdiction-specific; no inference from business prominence or access. Historic Canary rules must not be confused with the post-2018 Statute.
4. **PER:** debtor/date/statute-specific. LPB / Concurso 36/2012 begins with historical Ley 22/2003 art. 93 and transitional law, not retrospective use of current TRLC shorthand.
5. **PV-LSC:** Article 231 LSC is separate. Orion’s issue-document family contains express Article 231 related-person disclosures around JDAM/LPAM and FMMM/Pamalexsha/AGM.
