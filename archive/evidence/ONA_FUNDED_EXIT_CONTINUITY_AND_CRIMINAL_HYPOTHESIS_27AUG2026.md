# ONA funded-exit continuity and criminal-hypothesis evidence spine

**Cut-off:** 27 August 2026  
**Status:** public-safe evidential synthesis. This record distinguishes documented fact, contemporaneous attributed statement, party allegation, evidential inference and unresolved criminal hypothesis. It does not convert allegations into adjudicated findings.

## 1. Controlling proposition

The reviewed contemporaneous record supports a materially stronger proposition than treating ONA / Clubotel La Dorada, S.L. as an unrelated rival bidder. Read as one continuous sequence, ONA's proposed acquisition and operating role was embedded in a wider coordinated funded-exit strategy involving the LPB/Aweswell side, Carlos Sanz / Pledge Investments, Daniel Irigoyen, Cuatrecasas and other advisers. The stated objective of that wider strategy was to determine the amount properly payable in Concurso 36/2012 and obtain sufficient financing to exit the concurso through payment or consignation to creditors.

The personación pleading itself should not be overstated: Clubotel's formal interest was framed as acquisition of Sun Park and access to the procedural information required to participate. The creditor-payment / funded-exit purpose is established by reading that pleading together with the contemporaneous transaction, debt-quantification and financing correspondence.

## 2. Before 7 June 2018

- ONA had already produced an April 2018 letter of intent and business plan.
- On 26 April 2018 Carlos Sanz approached the Administrador Concursal directly concerning a proposed offer and requested the applicable terms, the amount of the offer to be improved and the relevant deadline.
- On 27 April 2018 Francisco de Borja Rodríguez-Batllori Laffitte declined to provide the requested substantive commercial information and directed Carlos toward formal personación and a request through the concurso court.
- ONA's operating/acquisition work continued and the project record contains industrial-lease documentation dated 6 June 2018.
- Therefore ONA, Carlos Sanz, Daniel Irigoyen and the surrounding financed-exit architecture were already materially in place before the 7 June 2018 material-control event alleged by the project side.

## 3. After 7 June 2018

- The financing, debt-reconciliation and funded-exit work continued after 7 June; the evidence does not support the simplistic proposition that the event automatically killed all financing.
- In September 2018, Carlos Sanz, Daniel Irigoyen, Cuatrecasas, Juan Tomás Parrilla and the LPB/Aweswell side worked around a submission seeking the amount required to exit the concurso.
- Daniel Irigoyen reported that he had told the judge that financing was approved to exit the concurso by payment or consignation to all creditors and sought a coordinated process with the court and the Administrador Concursal.
- Cuatrecasas correspondence discussed removing a subtle reference to ONA from one proposed filing. The correct inference is not that ONA was absent, but that ONA's role was deliberately not named in that particular submission.
- In 2019 Clubotel La Dorada, S.L. sought formal personación in Concurso 36/2012 through Daniel Irigoyen and challenged the refusal of that personación.
- Later ONA novation, proposed guarantee and concurso-conclusion workstreams are consistent with continuity of the same exit architecture, subject to document-specific verification of each legal person's rights and obligations.

## 5. Criminal-investigation lens

The evidential question is not whether chronology alone proves a criminal scheme. The proper actor-specific test is:

**act or omission -> knowledge -> legal/procedural duty or transaction dependency -> causal effect -> intent indicators if alleged -> benefit or downstream effect -> contrary evidence -> unresolved proof gap.**

For the private adverse perimeter, the inquiry is whether any specific actor knew of the funded exit or the operating/access conditions and intentionally acted to obtain material control, frustrate access, divert income, impair due diligence or alter the transaction's feasibility for an unlawful purpose.

For the Administrador Concursal, the inquiry is whether specific acts or omissions concerning information access, masa-active protection, debt quantification, procedural access, preservation of the operating asset, or treatment of competing exit routes were merely disputed insolvency administration or whether contemporaneous evidence supports knowledge and intentional facilitation of an unlawful private advantage.

For the Magistrate-Judge, the inquiry must be resolution-by-resolution and omission-by-omission. The project may allege severe procedural and protective failures, but any criminal hypothesis requires proof beyond adverse outcomes: jurisdiction, duty, knowledge, intentional misuse of judicial power or other legally relevant mens rea, causal effect and exclusion of innocent procedural explanations.

## 6. 7 June 2018 causation rule

The strongest defensible proposition is:

> By 6 June 2018, the ONA operating/acquisition and wider financing architecture materially existed. The 7 June 2018 material-control event therefore occurred against an already-developed exit structure. Work continued after 7 June, so causation must be proved component by component: which rights, access, possession, income, security, valuation, due-diligence assumptions or closing conditions were impaired; by whose act or omission; with what knowledge; and with what effect on financing, exit timing and later control of the hotel.

This formulation is stronger evidentially than saying, without proof, that 7 June alone killed the transaction.
