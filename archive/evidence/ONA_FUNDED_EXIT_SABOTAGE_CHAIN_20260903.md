## 1. Why this supplement exists

A preservation audit on 3 September 2026 identified a material gap between the controlled Gmail/court corpus and the public ONA timeline. The earlier public ledger established the funded-exit architecture through 2019, but it did not preserve the strongest causal sequence recovered from contemporaneous records:

> **26 September 2018 debt-quantum admissions → 28 November 2018 separate locales/pools transaction → 24 December 2018 contemporaneous warning that the separation damaged both the debt-payment exit and future competitive bidding → February 2019 ONA contractual continuation → 24 October 2019 judicial finding that the separate sale contradicted the unitary liquidation structure and should not have proceeded separately.**

This supplement closes that gap so future searches and successor threads can start from the repository rather than re-mine Gmail.

## 2. Controlling evidential conclusion

The controlled record supports a stronger proposition than merely saying that the Insolvency Administrator did not help a financing attempt.

1. A real, developed, multi-party exit existed: ONA/Clubotel as hotel operator; Carlos Sanz/Pledge as transaction/finance coordinator; Stoneweg/Varia and other capital routes; Cuatrecasas and Daniel Irigoyen on transaction/insolvency implementation; a route to fund, pay or consign recognised concursal liabilities, conclude Concurso 36/2012 and refinance an intact operating hotel.
2. The integrity of the productive unit, a reliable debt figure, access/valuation/due diligence, and the ability to preserve the operating/security package were transaction dependencies.
3. Contemporaneous professional records show the Insolvency Administrator was informed of the exit and later warned that separating the hotel's unencumbered operating elements would damage both the payment/conclusion route and a future auction.
4. The 24 October 2019 court order independently held that the separate sale of the locales/pools contradicted the approved liquidation plan and the Provincial Court's interpretation of a **unitary productive-unit disposal**, and that allowing the unencumbered facilities to be sold separately would undermine third-party improved bids.

**Evidence-supported allegation:** Gil Marer alleges that this sequence forms part of direct, active sabotage of the funded exit and deliberate enablement of the adverse private-actor/CAM perimeter through acts and omissions of the Insolvency Administrator.

**Publication boundary:** the objective interference/impairment propositions below are document- and judgment-supported. Deliberate criminal intent, concert with CAM/private actors, and counterfactual certainty that a particular financing route would have closed remain allegations/proof questions unless established by a competent final decision.

## 5. Judicial self-corroboration layer

The strongest public-safe point is that the later court ruling independently validated the structural proposition at the centre of the contemporaneous warning.

### December 2018 — warning
Daniel Irigoyen contemporaneously recorded that he told the AC that separation of the business/common elements damaged both the proposed debt-payment exit and future auction competition.

### October 2019 — judicial finding
The Commercial Court later held that:

- the plan and appellate interpretation contemplated an acquisition of the **productive unit as a whole**;
- the AC's separate sale of the locales/pools contradicted that structure;
- the court's own June 2018 partial suspension had been erroneous because the whole unitary disposal should have been stayed;
- the separate operation had been carried out without prior judicial authorization and notified after it was consummated; and
- separating the unencumbered hotel installations impaired the practical prospect of third-party improved offers.

This is **self-supporting corroboration of the structural interference point**. It does **not**, by itself, adjudicate why the AC acted, whether he coordinated with CAM, or whether a crime occurred.

The public record independently establishes several underlying acts/effects, including the later judicial finding that the separate locales/pools sale contradicted the unitary liquidation structure. Deliberate intent, concert and criminal liability remain allegations requiring actor-specific proof and competent adjudication.**
