The ONA/Clubotel record is a counterfactual evidence spine for the Sun Park / Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. insolvency history. Read as one sequence, it supports that Clubotel La Dorada, S.L./ONA was not merely an unrelated bidder against LPB. The 2019 Clubotel personation pleading must still be described accurately: its formal interest was acquisition of Sun Park and access to the insolvency information needed to pursue that interest. The creditor-payment exit purpose comes from the combined contemporaneous record, not from rewriting that pleading.

## Seven actor-specific lanes

The criminal hypothesis is not collective. For each lane the required test is:

**act or omission → capacity/duty → actual knowledge → transaction dependency → causal effect → intent where alleged → benefit/result → contrary evidence → official outcome.**

### Francisco Mario Matos Matas

Gil Marer alleges a pre-existing minority/community governance and debt-control architecture that later supported adverse control. The ONA record is relevant only if evidence proves his personal knowledge of the funded exit and a personal act/omission affecting owner cooperation, access, operation or governance. Association, chronology or later alignment is insufficient.

### Antonio Cogolludo Rojas

His documented community/presidency capacities must be connected to specific instructions, security/access decisions or other conduct and to actual knowledge of the ONA alternative before any criminal inference. A community role by itself is not criminal participation.

### Shaila María Cogolludo Ramos

Her personal functions, votes, administrative/banking role if any, knowledge and acts must be proved separately. Family, employment or corporate links cannot transfer another person's knowledge or intent.

### José Daniel Acosta Matos

The record permits investigation of alleged creditor/control, later project and beneficiary continuity, but the 7 June eyewitness material elsewhere expressly records that he was not present that day. Any allegation of instruction, adoption, retention, later use or benefit therefore requires independent communications, authority and financial tracing. Later benefit is circumstantial context, not proof of a prior plan.

### Laura Patricia Acosta Matos

The 7 June dossier attributes operational instructions to her through a contemporaneous eyewitness account. That is stronger actor-specific evidence than relationship-based inference, but still requires authority, communications, exact acts, purpose, scope, corroboration and the preserved contrary/official 2018 outcome. Her conduct cannot be transferred to José Daniel or any other person.

### Francisco de Borja Rodríguez-Batllori Laffitte — Insolvency Administrator

The ONA chain adds a specific April gatekeeping event to the already disputed 2018–2019 record. The criminal hypothesis attributed by Gil is strongest when framed as a sequence to test: information requested and not substantively supplied; later knowledge of a creditor-payment exit; actions/omissions concerning control, preservation, debt quantification, access, cooperation and Clubotel personation; and the eventual asset/result. Proof must show personal duty, legally available alternative, knowledge, causal effect and the mental element of any alleged offence. Lawful insistence on formal standing, reliance on court rulings or a defensible insolvency judgment remains contrary evidence.

### Alberto López Villarrubia — Magistrate-Judge

The ONA record provides a proposed knowledge-and-counterfactual ledger. If the certified docket corroborates Daniel Irigoyen's account that the court knew of financing intended to pay/consign all creditors, later decisions and omissions can be tested against the measures legally available to preserve asset value, quantify liabilities, supervise the AC, protect access and maintain material competition. Adverse rulings, delay, error or judicial discretion do not establish prevaricación. Any criminal theory requires the exact applicable legal elements, a specific act/ruling or duty, the required degree of objective injustice/arbitrariness, knowledge/intent and causation.

## Causation rule around 7 June 2018

Do **not** publish “7 June killed the deal” as a proven fact. The evidence shows work continued on 8, 12 and 13 June and later. The stronger test is condition-specific:

1. what access/possession/operation/income/title/security condition existed;
2. who controlled it;
3. what changed on or after 7 June;
4. who knew of the ONA/funding route;
5. whether a lender/operator/buyer delayed, repriced, conditioned or abandoned because of that change;
6. which judicial/AC remedy was requested and available;
7. what later benefit/result accrued and to whom; and
8. what alternative lawful explanation exists.
