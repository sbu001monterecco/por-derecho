## 11. 7 June 2018 — causation controls

The existing takeover page should remain the controlling boundary for the material-control event.

Current reconstruction requires simultaneous display of:

- ONA package materially developed by 6 June;
- 7 June material rupture/control event as alleged and documented to the extent stated on the takeover page;
- financing work continuing after 7 June;
- Stoneweg/Varia conditioned offer on 12 June;
- judicial-exit workstream on 13 June;
- later ONA / financing activity;
- official outcome of the 2018 criminal complaint: provisional dismissal confirmed on appeal, if that remains the official record.

Therefore the correct question is not “did 7 June automatically kill the transaction?” but:

> Which specific ONA/lender/buyer/security/judicial condition was affected by which specific act or omission, for how long, with what measurable transaction consequence, and what did the relevant actor know at that time?
