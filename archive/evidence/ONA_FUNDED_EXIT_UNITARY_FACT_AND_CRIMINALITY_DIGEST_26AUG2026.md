## Controlled conclusion

The current repository, live website and source-controlled mailbox record support a continuous ONA/Clubotel transaction-and-procedure sequence before, during and after 7 June 2018. Clubotel La Dorada, S.L./ONA was not merely an unrelated bidder adverse to Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. (LPB). The combined record supports its role as intended hotel operator and acquisition participant within a coordinated funded-exit architecture involving the LPB/Aweswell side, Carlos Sanz/Pledge Investments, Daniel Irigoyen, Cuatrecasas, Juan Tomás Parrilla and external finance/acquisition routes.

The documented wider objective was to establish the legally payable insolvency amount, obtain sufficient finance, pay or consign the recognised creditors, conclude Insolvency Proceeding 36/2012, restore ordinary corporate capacity and then implement the security/operating structure contemplated for the post-exit stage.

The Clubotel personation pleading must be described precisely: in isolation it formalised an acquisition and information-access interest. It did not itself state that Clubotel would pay the final list of claims. Its wider funded-exit significance arises only when it is read with the earlier and later transaction, debt, finance and court/AC correspondence.

## Why ONA materially strengthens the criminal analysis

The ONA record supplies a documented counterfactual against which causation can be measured. Before the 7 June material-control event there was already an operator/acquisition package. Immediately after it, finance and court/AC exit work continued. In September the team was still seeking a usable debt figure and all-creditor payment/consignation route. In 2019 Clubotel sought formal participation.

Accordingly, the principal investigation question is not merely whether there was 'an offer'. It is whether identified actors knew of a developed creditor-paying alternative, controlled dependencies needed to preserve or convert it, and through their own acts or omissions materially impaired access, possession, operation, income, information, valuation, collateral, debt certification, personation, consignation or court implementation.

Later control, adjudication, finance, operation and economic benefit within the Acosta Matos-connected perimeter are relevant outcome evidence to trace actor by actor. They do not by themselves prove an earlier criminal agreement or intent.
