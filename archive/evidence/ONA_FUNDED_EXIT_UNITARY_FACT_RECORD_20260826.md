## 1. Controlling conclusion

The documentary record is strongest when read as one continuous sequence rather than as isolated offers or filings:

> **Clubotel La Dorada, S.L. / ONA was not merely an unrelated third-party bidder appearing independently against LPB. Its proposed operating and acquisition role formed part of a coordinated funded-exit architecture involving the LPB/Aweswell stakeholder side, Carlos Sanz / Pledge Investments, Daniel Irigoyen, Cuatrecasas and several financing or acquisition routes. The stated wider objective was to determine the amount properly payable in Concurso 36/2012, obtain sufficient funds, pay or consign the recognised creditors, conclude the insolvency proceeding and then restore professional, unitary hotel operation.**

Clubotel's formal 2019 personación papers described its immediate legitimate interest in acquisition of Sun Park and access to the insolvency record. They did not, standing alone, state that Clubotel itself would pay the texto definitivo. The wider all-creditor funded-exit purpose is established only by reading that filing with the April 2018 gatekeeping correspondence, the ONA operating documents, the June court-exit work and the September 2018 debt-and-finance correspondence.

## 6. Criminal-hypothesis test

Gil Marer alleges that the ONA package and its security architecture were deliberately frustrated through an adverse continuum involving five private actors, the AC and the judge. That allegation must be tested through the same actor-specific bridge for each person:

> **act or omission → legal/factual capacity and duty → actual knowledge of the ONA package → specific transaction or security dependency affected → contemporaneous reaction → measurable impairment → intent, if alleged → benefit or destination → contrary explanation → remaining proof.**

### Private actors — separate lanes

The public record must keep **Francisco Mario Matos Matas, Antonio Cogolludo Rojas, Shaila María Cogolludo Ramos, José Daniel Acosta Matos and Laura Patricia Acosta Matos** separate. It may test alleged participation in parallel governance, control, access, possession, exploitation, community decisions, creditor leverage and later benefit. It must not transfer knowledge, intent or responsibility between them because of family, company or professional association.

### Insolvency Administrator — separate institutional lane

The alleged criminal relevance is not merely that the AC rejected a proposal. It is whether, after being approached and later informed of a developed funded exit, he failed or refused without lawful justification to provide or procure the debt and procedural outputs necessary for payment, failed to preserve the hotel and security package, or opposed Clubotel's access in a manner that knowingly assisted diversion or frustration. The current record raises those questions; it does not by itself prove criminal intent.

### Magistrate-Judge — separate judicial lane

The allegation is that the judge had notice of the exit architecture and later of approved all-creditor financing, yet allegedly failed to protect the asset, preserve the operative/security perimeter, require a reproducible debt figure or give an effective procedural route, thereby enabling adverse control and defeating creditor payment. Proof requires the certified docket, filings, orders, hearing records, knowledge chronology and a lawful-alternative analysis. Judicial error or omission is not automatically a crime.

## 7. Counter-record and limitations

- The ONA lease and finance routes contained conditions; no public-safe record presently proves final drawdown or satisfaction of every condition.
- Continued work after 7 June does not prove there was no impairment; equally, the 7 June chronology alone does not prove that it caused every later failure.
- Clubotel's personación filing formally relied on an acquisition interest. The wider funded-exit purpose comes from the combined record, not from rewriting the pleading.
- The AC did respond to Carlos Sanz. The supported criticism is the substance and effect of the response, not literal silence.
- No criminal conviction or final finding is asserted against any named person.
- Exact knowledge, intent, causal contribution and benefit remain actor-specific proof questions.
