## 3. Strongest safe finding

The meeting establishes an **actor-specific knowledge checkpoint** for FMMM on 11 June 2016.

FMMM personally received and discussed a detailed Sun Park presentation concerning:

- accounting and expenditure from 2008–2015;
- the asserted allocation of maintenance and supply costs between operator/exploitation structures and the Community of Owners;
- the distinction between Luchy Playa Blanca and the exploitation entities;
- owner access, voting and governance conflict;
- Concurso 36/2012 and the role/authorisation of the insolvency administrator;
- the existence and possible transfer of mortgage credit;
- consequences of a future creditor acquiring and enforcing that credit;
- the need for durable governance and exploitation arrangements.

This proves neither the truth of every proposition put to FMMM nor later wrongdoing. It does establish that his later knowledge cannot be analysed as if Sun Park were an unknown historical matter he would first have had to discover from outside sources.

## 5. Implication for later FMMM / Pamalexsha / AGM / Orion analysis

The proper later question is:

> **What knowledge travelled with the person?**

The 2016 meeting allows a later disclosure/conflict analysis to start from demonstrated direct exposure rather than a hypothetical possibility of discovery.

However, the meeting does not itself prove:

- a pact or coordination between FMMM and later RICPE/AGM/Orion actors;
- criminal intent;
- that Sun Park was material to Orion Rental SOCIMI in 2024;
- that the Orion Documento de Emisión was misleading;
- that a specific Orion, AGM or Abbaco disclosure duty was triggered.

Those propositions require actor-specific evidence of later role, knowledge retained/declared, applicable duty, materiality, disclosure process and, where relevant, intent.

High-value later documents therefore include FMMM director questionnaires, conflict/related-party declarations, management representations, due-diligence questionnaires, Abbaco Markets verification material and board minutes.
