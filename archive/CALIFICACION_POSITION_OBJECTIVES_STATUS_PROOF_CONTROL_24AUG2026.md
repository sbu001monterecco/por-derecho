**Proceeding:** Concurso 36/2012 · Sentencia 163/2023 · AP Las Palmas, Sección 4ª · RPL 2523/2025

**Public state:** publication authorised; implementation subject to repository, CI and live verification

**Purpose:** preserve one non-fragmented formulation across the repository and public website while separating the appeal from criminal, civil, insolvency, regulatory, administrative, professional and disciplinary routes.

## 1. Canonical position

> Our position is not that every adverse decision is criminal. The documentary sequence requires an independent investigation into whether the Calificación process was used to invert responsibility and divert scrutiny: broad Administrator allegations, an exceptionally severe two-page Fiscal opinion with very limited visible individual reasoning and a facial actor-description defect, institutional reuse of the adverse position in DI 248, selected judicial adoption, and a possible corresponding benefit within the Acosta Matos perimeter. No institution is asked to accept that conclusion in advance. Each actor and authority is asked to produce the documents capable of proving, narrowing or disproving it.

The controlling analytical formula is:

> **TRANSFER OF VALUE + INVERSION OF RESPONSIBILITY + DIVERSION OF SCRUTINY**

This is a serious, document-based investigative thesis. It is not an adjudicated criminal fact and does not prove common design, personal coordination, knowing falsity, criminal intent, purposeful benefit, capture or retaliation.

## 2. Canonical objectives

1. Obtain the complete certified record.
2. Secure an independent and reasoned determination of RPL 2523/2025.
3. Investigate actor-specific criminal hypotheses where the evidence crosses the applicable threshold.
4. Restore the correct claimant's property, rights, income and economic position where legally possible.
5. Recover non-duplicative compensation for proven harm that cannot be reversed.
6. Correct insolvency, civil, corporate, administrative, regulatory, Registry and public-funding records where the evidence requires it.
7. Pursue civil, insolvency, professional, disciplinary, regulatory, administrative or criminal responsibility only to the extent supported by primary evidence and within each authority's competence.

The objective is correction, recovery and proportionate accountability, not retaliation. No claimant's standing, authority, remedy or quantum is merged with another's. Restitution and compensation remain subject to no-double-recovery control.

## 4. Appeal firewall

RPL 2523/2025 must be decided on its procedurally usable record, pleaded grounds, adversarial process and applicable law. The public criminal-misuse thesis is not extra-record evidence and seeks no predetermined outcome.

The legitimate expectation is narrow and exacting: the Sala should independently confront the pleaded defence, evidence, reasoning, causation and proportionality issues, identify which propositions survive scrutiny and explain why. Institutional independence includes the capacity to correct first-instance error; it is not deference to it.

An adverse decision would not prove offence, retaliation or capture. A successful appeal would not prove the criminal thesis. All other civil, criminal, regulatory, administrative, professional and disciplinary rights remain reserved in their proper forums.

## 7. Source-state and language controls

- `documented` means the identified primary or procedural record is located within its stated scope;
- `documented and contested` means a record is located but materially challenged, appealed or subject to contrary evidence;
- `open hypothesis` means finite investigation is possible but the conclusion is not proved;
- `not located` records corpus state only and supports no inference of nonexistence;
- the Fiscal defect is a facial actor-description defect that may be a drafting error; it is not described as a confession, fabrication or proof of knowledge;
- actor identity, acts, knowledge, competence, causation, damage, benefit and strongest innocent explanation remain separate fields;
- LPB, Aweswell, Gil, Pink, Patricia, Matkator, CEXP, the Administrator, Fiscalía, judicial actors and Acosta Matos perimeter persons/entities remain legally and evidentially separate.

## 8. Publication exclusions

Do not publish private emails, privileged legal advice, protected-witness material, confidential recordings, unnecessary personal/tax data, credentials or non-public storage links. Public pages may describe source status and finite production demands without exposing protected content.
