### Extraconcursal perimeter

- LPB alone was the debtor in Concurso 36/2012.
- Matkator was not in that insolvency.
- The Comunidad de Explotación del Complejo Sun Park was a separate operating structure.
- Those facts mean that the whole hotel, minority-owned units, customer relationships, distribution systems and non-LPB rights did not automatically become part of LPB's insolvency estate.
- A specific concursal authorisation, judicial act, omission or failure to correct may nevertheless have produced practical effects outside LPB if notice, duty/power, act/omission, affected right and causation are proved.
- This event-by-event method replaces global phrases such as “active/passive enabling” when used as if they were already established findings.
