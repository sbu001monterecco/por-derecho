## Exact-document extraction rule

For every document expressly mentioned/shown in the Vista, later add:

- exact name/date;
- author/recipient;
- component + timestamp;
- who introduced it;
- proposition for which relied upon;
- repository/Drive locator;
- whether the original/native source is held;
- whether the oral description matches the document;
- whether missing.

A document existing elsewhere in the case is not automatically proof that the Judge personally reviewed it. The Vista can prove oral presentation only after the spoken record is verified.
