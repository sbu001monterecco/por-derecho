## Definition of 100%

Evidential accounting can be 100% even when a source is unavailable, but only if the unknown is explicit and includes:

- what is missing;
- likely holder/source;
- route to obtain or certify it;
- effect if unavailable;
- what would change the assessment;
- the competent human gate.

Actual institutional adoption cannot be manufactured by Por Derecho.
