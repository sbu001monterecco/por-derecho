# Mandatory supplement to PD-JSP-RESEARCH-20260905-V2

Date: 5 September 2026. WORKER research only. Read together with `JSP_PERIMETER_FORENSIC_MASTER_PROMPT_V2_05SEP2026.md`. These events are documentary pressure points, not findings of asset stripping, insolvency crime, concealed assets or causation.

## F01 — 2018 refinancing process

BORME-C-2018-1969, dated 16 April 2018, records JSP's sole administrator cancelling the extraordinary meeting scheduled for 24 April because the company was immersed in a refinancing process and expected contractual documents for its closing. Recover the refinancing term sheet, lenders/creditors, security, waivers, valuations, conditions, board/shareholder information, advisers, closing documents, proceeds/application of funds and later amendments/defaults. Reconcile this with the 2017 shareholder questions about Sun Park/Explobeach disposal and with the 2018 Monte Lanza/CAM title events. Do not infer that refinancing was illicit or caused the later concurso.

Source: https://boe.es/diario_borme/txt.php?id=BORME-C-2018-1969

## F02 — January 2021 capital restructuring before the July concurso

BORME-A-2021-22-35 entries 52301 and 52302, registered 26 January 2021, record elimination of JSP share series so all shares had the same nominal value and a capital reduction of **EUR 3,029,199.20**, leaving subscribed capital of **EUR 245,169.00**.

Obtain the general-meeting resolution, notarial deed, purpose and legal modality of the reduction, balance used, creditor safeguards, any repayment/return to shareholders, cancellation of losses, reserve treatment, share-exchange schedule, cash movements and auditor/expert material. Determine whether the reduction merely repaired the balance sheet, restructured nominal capital or transferred value. Do not call it asset stripping or creditor prejudice without the underlying documents and economic effect.

Source: https://www.boe.es/borme/dias/2021/02/03/pdfs/BORME-A-2021-22-35.pdf

## F03 — 2023 liquidation sales prove an inventory and sale trail exists

International Auction Group's published March 2023 terms identify autos **440/2021**, the historical Mercantile Court no. 2, **Praxis** as insolvency administrator and the JSP/Celgán concurso. They state that auctioned assets were detailed in the **inventory of the active estate** and identify, among other lots, industrial fincas and inventory values. This is third-party/appointed-agent sale material, not the complete court inventory or proof that all estate assets were included.

Recover the court-approved mandate/rules, the complete inventory version underlying each auction, valuations, marketing, bid logs, adjudications, bank receipts, fees and distribution treatment. Reconcile every auction with quarterly liquidation reports and final account. Search other 2023 sale processes, including trademarks and other intangible assets, without assuming a published auction platform contains every liquidation transaction.

Source: https://iagauction.com/storage/sale/9169/Bases-y-Condiciones-Subasta---JSP-OK.pdf
