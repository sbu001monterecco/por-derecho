## 3. Four scope corrections

1. **Sentencia, not auto.** The operative decision is Sentencia 163/2023, 28 September 2023. No inference of deliberate misdescription is permitted without proof of who named the file or how Romera received/described it.
2. **LPB, not Sun Park as a whole.** The debtor was Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U.; the judgment did not classify a mixed-ownership hotel, every owner, every finca or every operating entity as culpable.
3. **First instance and appealed.** Appeals had already been filed before January 2025. The open provenance question includes what the transmitting source said, if anything, about appeal/finality.
4. **Object mismatch.** The judgment did not adjudicate every Sun Park title, Matkator rights, whole-hotel 2018 control, Meeting Point/Club Sei authority, the precise commercialisation chain, RIC/RICPE governance, incentives/FEDER controls or every later CAM/HNT/CH/MYND act.
