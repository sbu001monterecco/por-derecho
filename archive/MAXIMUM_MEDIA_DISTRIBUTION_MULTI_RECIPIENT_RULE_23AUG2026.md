# Project Sun Rock / Por Derecho — Maximum media distribution and multi-recipient rule

**Control date:** 23 August 2026
**Status:** CONTROLLING MEDIA-CAMPAIGN DISTRIBUTION RULE
**Scope:** every newspaper, digital outlet, broadcaster, programme, journalist, reporter, editor and verified newsroom route
**Send control:** `EMAIL_SEND_FINAL_AUTHORIZATION_RULE.md`

## 1. Maximum-distribution instruction

For every material media outlet, research and prepare all suitable current named editorial professionals whose verified role or beat provides a genuine entry point for a source-controlled Project Sun Rock / Por Derecho module.

Multiple journalists, reporters and editors at the same outlet may be active at the same time. A prior or pending approach to one person at an outlet does **not** place another suitable current person at that outlet on hold and does **not** create an outlet-spacing requirement.

The campaign state is:

`MAXIMUM DISTRIBUTION — ALL SUITABLE VERIFIED NAMED LANES ACTIVE; NO SAME-OUTLET HOLD OR SPACING`.

## 2. What maximum distribution permits

- more than two suitable named professionals at the same outlet;
- separate person-specific packages to several reporters, editors or commissioning professionals at the same outlet;
- an official general newsroom or regional newsroom address when no verified direct professional address is public;
- honest routing through such an address using `A la atención de [name]`;
- an additional general-desk package where it has a distinct editorial-routing purpose; and
- preparation of all suitable lanes without waiting for a response window from another journalist.

An official general address is a verified **professional routing route**. It is not represented as the named person's direct or personal address. A named package routed through a general inbox still counts as a named-journalist lane only when the intended person, role, reason for fit and exact package are explicit.

## 3. What maximum distribution does not permit

- guessing or pattern-generating private or direct addresses;
- mislabelling a newsroom address as a person's own email;
- blind mass-CC or one undifferentiated allegation-heavy blast;
- duplicate bodies that omit a real person-specific reason, source emphasis and finite ask;
- concealing prior person, colleague or outlet history;
- describing a follow-up or second outlet lane as an untouched cold approach; or
- sending, drafting in Gmail, scheduling or queuing without the separately applicable authority.

Every named lane must record:

`NAME → CURRENT ROLE / BEAT → WHY THIS PERSON → STORY MODULE → VERIFIED DIRECT OR OFFICIAL ROUTING BASIS → DUAL GMAIL HISTORY CLASSIFICATION → EXACT PACKAGE STATE`.

## 4. Gmail-history interaction

The fresh, pagination-complete person-and-organisation Gmail gate remains mandatory before readiness and again before any later authorised send when the result may have changed.

Prior contact is a **classification fact**, not by itself a hold. It determines whether the message is accurately presented as a first person-specific approach, a new lane at a previously contacted outlet, a follow-up, a correction, a reply or another class. Material collisions must be read and reflected in the package; they do not reinstate automatic outlet spacing.

Unresolved identity or unverified routing remains a fail-closed accuracy condition. Record it as `IDENTITY RESEARCH ACTIVE — DO NOT GUESS` or `ROUTE RESEARCH ACTIVE — DO NOT GUESS`, not as a same-outlet distribution hold.

## 5. Personalisation and source controls

Each person receives a separate package with:

- a recipient-specific subject and salutation;
- a truthful explanation of why their current role or beat is relevant;
- source anchors appropriate to that role;
- bounded questions that the recipient can verify; and
- the complete mandatory media core package.

All packages must preserve the distinctions among:

1. private financing / RIC Private Equity and the later corporate structure;
2. the Reserva para Inversiones en Canarias as a tax incentive, not by itself a regional subsidy or direct Treasury payment;
3. the separate regional-incentive file `GC/836/P06`; and
4. any separate European/FEDER operation, its eligible expenditure, certification, payment, verification and accumulation controls.

Coexistence of those layers does not by itself establish overlap, double financing, fraud, illegality or responsibility.

## 6. Authorization remains per transmission

This rule authorises research, route verification, list maintenance and package preparation. It does not authorise transmission.

Before every actual email, `EMAIL_SEND_FINAL_AUTHORIZATION_RULE.md` still requires fresh exact approval of:

- To / Cc / Bcc;
- subject;
- complete body;
- both attachment filenames;
- every link; and
- the exact transmission act.

Approval of one journalist never authorises another journalist, even at the same outlet. An approval covering an explicitly enumerated set of exact final packages may cover only that exact set.

## 7. Supersession

This rule supersedes earlier media-campaign language requiring:

- one named lane at a time;
- a response window before a second person at the same outlet;
- outlet-level spacing;
- a same-outlet hold merely because a colleague was contacted or named as fallback; or
- stopping after two named professionals.

Historical contact, response, bounce and delivery evidence remains intact and must be classified accurately. Non-media legal, evidential, identity-verification and safety holds are not changed by this rule.
