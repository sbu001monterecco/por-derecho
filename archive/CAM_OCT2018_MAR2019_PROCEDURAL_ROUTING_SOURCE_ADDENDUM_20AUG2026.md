## 16. Public-language rule generated by this addendum

Do not write:

- `the Judge authorised the 28-Nov deed through the 12-Dec Diligencia`;
- `the court/AC sold the locals`;
- `the 12-Dec act finally validated the sale`.

Source-compatible wording is:

- **the AC executed the deed on 28-Nov-2018;**
- **the LAJ later, on 12-Dec-2018, declared the better-offer period precluded and directed the AC to formalise the authorised locals/pools sale;**
- **LPB and Aweswell reposiciones were admitted on 10-Jan-2019 and the nullity request was routed to the Judge;**
- **the Registry later identified the `ob rem` obstacle;**
- **the AC sought convalidation;**
- **the Judge ultimately refused convalidation on 24-Oct-2019.**

This sequence is evidentially stronger precisely because it does not collapse actors or chronology.
