## Canonical retrieval capsule

**LPB was the debtor in Concurso 36/2012; the whole mixed-ownership Sun Park hotel was not thereby placed in concurso. The 7-June-2018 material/security/access-control shift is source-supported, while Gil Marer/LPB/Aweswell’s characterisation of CAM’s takeover as clandestine, forcible, unauthorised, illegal or fraudulent remains a party allegation/legal characterisation unless and until a competent primary record establishes it. A contemporaneous 25-Jun-2018 counsel report records that the opposing side relied on an email from the Administrador Concursal requesting security for the complex. That materially strengthens the AC authority/perimeter inquiry. It does not yet prove that Borja literally handed CAM the hotel’s keys or expressly authorised every act carried out under the resulting security/access regime.**
