## 1. Purpose

This protocol asks a set of legally different questions that must never be collapsed into one accusation:

1. Is a private actor actually a **PEP / persona con responsabilidad pública (PRP)** under the applicable anti-money-laundering framework?
2. If not, is the actor a **family member or known close associate / allegado** of a verified PEP/PRP, so that enhanced AML diligence may nevertheless be relevant?
3. Is there objective evidence of **access to**, **contact with**, **a claim of access/influence over**, **an intervention directed to**, or **actual influence over** a public officeholder?
4. Does any actor hold or historically have held a status that creates **aforamiento / special criminal jurisdiction**, and for which dates and acts?
5. Is any actor a **persona especialmente relacionada (PER)** with a particular insolvency debtor under the insolvency law applicable at the legally relevant date?
6. Is any actor a **persona vinculada** to an administrator under **Article 231 of the Ley de Sociedades de Capital (PV-LSC)** or another transaction-specific related-party regime?

The purpose is enhanced due diligence, source reconciliation and finite verification. It is **not** to reverse-engineer a conclusion that a private actor is politically connected, protected, corrupt, influential, aforado or specially related merely because the actor is wealthy, prominent, professionally networked, appears with officeholders, obtains a favourable outcome or participates in a regulated/publicly supported project.

## 2. Controlling legal taxonomy

### 2.1 PEP / PRP — Ley 10/2010, Article 14

Spanish AML law uses **persona con responsabilidad pública**. PEP is useful international shorthand; public pages should explain the Spanish statutory term.

The current Article 14 includes persons who perform or have performed important public functions, including specified senior national, autonomous, local, judicial, prosecutorial, public-enterprise, international-organisation, political-party, union and business-organisation roles. Lower/intermediate public employment is not automatically within the category.

**Critical distinction:** Article 14 separately extends enhanced measures to **family members and allegados**. A family member or allegado of a PEP/PRP is not thereby the officeholder and must not simply be labelled “a PEP” without explaining the legal basis.

For `allegado`, test the statutory facts: public/notorious joint ownership or control of a legal person/instrument with the PEP/PRP; another close business relationship; or ownership/control of a structure publicly known to have been created for the PEP/PRP’s benefit.

**Official source:** BOE, Ley 10/2010, art. 14, current consolidated text.

### 2.2 Access, influence and tráfico de influencias — separate evidential ladder

A relationship or contact is not criminal influence. Código Penal arts. 428–430 require materially more than access or acquaintance. In particular, art. 429 addresses a private person who actually influences a public official/authority, exploiting a personal relationship, to obtain a resolution capable of generating an economic benefit.

Use this ladder. Do not skip levels:

| Level | Evidential proposition | Public label |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | No material evidence located | NO MATERIAL EVIDENCE LOCATED — bounded search only |
| 1 | Ordinary/public contact, event attendance, professional interaction | CONTACT / PUBLIC ACCESS ONLY |
| 2 | Repeated institutional/business access demonstrated by sources | REPEATED ACCESS — no influence inference |
| 3 | Actor self-claim or witness-attributed claim of personal access/influence | CLAIM OF ACCESS/INFLUENCE — attributed, unverified |
| 4 | Independently corroborated private communication channel/contact | VERIFIED CONTACT CHANNEL — content/effect separate |
| 5 | Documented request, message, intermediation or intervention directed to an officeholder about a decision | DOCUMENTED INTERVENTION — effect separate |
| 6 | Relevant decision/outcome follows the intervention with traceable chronology | SEQUENCE VERIFIED — causation not yet proved |
| 7 | Evidence supports causal influence plus decision/benefit nexus | EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE — legal elements still tested |
| 8 | Competent finding or legally sufficient proof | FORMAL / COMPETENT FINDING |

**Never write:** `access = influence`, `claim of influence = actual influence`, `favourable result = influence`, or `influence = tráfico de influencias`.

### 2.3 Aforamiento

Aforamiento is a **jurisdictional status tied to a qualifying office/status and the law in force**, not a social or economic characteristic. It is not created by wealth, connections, public-contract exposure, political acquaintances or ability to contact an officeholder.

For every candidate:

`person → exact office/status → start/end dates → act/date under review → legal provision then in force → competent court → whether status continued at procedural date`.

Use current LOPJ Article 57 and, where relevant, Article 73 and the applicable autonomous statute. For Canary offices, do not reuse obsolete pre-2018 special-forum wording without a historical-law check. The current Canary Statute provisions on parliamentary inviolability/arrest and Government-member arrest protection are not to be paraphrased as a universal aforamiento rule.

### 2.4 PER — persona especialmente relacionada with an insolvency debtor

**PER is Project shorthand; the statute uses “persona especialmente relacionada”.** It is a debtor-specific insolvency category, not a generic “connected person” or corruption label.

For LPB / Concurso 36/2012, begin with the **Ley 22/2003 Article 93 text in force at the legally relevant date**, not current TRLC Article 283 as if it applied retroactively. The historical provision for a corporate debtor included, among other categories, qualifying shareholders, de iure/de facto administrators, liquidators and general attorneys, certain group companies/common shareholders, and a rebuttable presumption for specified assignees/adjudicatees of credits acquired from specially related persons within the statutory pre-declaration period.

Current TRLC Articles 282–284 are useful for present-law comparison but do not replace the historical analysis of a 2012 declaration.

Mandatory PER matrix:

`insolvency debtor → candidate → role/ownership/control → credit/relationship → date right arose → acquisition/assignment date → declaration date → statutory version/transitional rule → category relied on → consequence → contrary evidence`.

**Control:** a post-declaration credit assignment or later prominence in the debtor’s asset perimeter does not become PER merely because a later relationship looks close. Identify the precise statutory route.

### 2.5 PV-LSC — persona vinculada under Article 231 LSC

This is a separate corporate conflict/related-person concept. Article 231 includes specified family relationships and legal persons/entities in which an administrator has significant influence or specified board/high-management connections, among other categories.

The Orion Rental SOCIMI issue-document family contains an express Article 231 related-person disclosure around **JDAM** and **FMMM**, including **LPAM** within the JDAM family/corporate related-person disclosure and FMMM’s Pamalexsha/AGM/family-related perimeter. That is a **documented PV-LSC disclosure for that corporate context**. It is not proof of PEP/PRP status, PER status, political influence, criminality or improper conduct.
