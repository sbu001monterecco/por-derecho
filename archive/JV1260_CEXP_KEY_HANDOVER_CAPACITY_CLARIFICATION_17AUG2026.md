## 4. The capacity point is material, not semantic

A recurring risk in the Sun Park record is **entity compression**: the same natural person appears in communications involving different Community, exploitation and corporate bodies and later readers infer that the bodies are interchangeable.

They are not.

For the physical key handover, the direct witness gives the capacity as:

> **Gil Marer — President of CEXP.**

The repository must therefore distinguish at least:

- Gil Marer personally;
- Gil Marer acting as President of CEXP;
- CEXP itself;
- MRSP / Monterecco Sun Park, S.L.;
- Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. (LPB);
- Pink Canary Services;
- Monterecco or other operating/holding entities;
- the Comunidad de Propietarios;
- the individual finca/unit owners.

Presence of the same individual across parts of the wider structure does not prove that a particular physical act was carried out for every entity, nor that every entity possessed the same units, keys or rights.

That formulation is preserved as **Gil Marer's present assessment**, not as a judicial finding.

Repository analyses should therefore keep these propositions separate:

1. **Contemporaneous conduct:** compliance with the court-imposed position and key handover.
2. **Present legal/evidential challenge:** dispute as to the factual/legal premises and downstream use of the event.
3. **No automatic ratification:** compliance with an imposed judicial situation is not, without more, evidence that every underlying allegation was accepted as true, that every downstream inference was correct, or that distinct entities were thereby merged.

Whether the event legally constituted waiver, acquiescence, ratification or any other legal effect must be assessed from the actual pleadings, orders, notarial record and applicable law rather than asserted from the physical act alone.
