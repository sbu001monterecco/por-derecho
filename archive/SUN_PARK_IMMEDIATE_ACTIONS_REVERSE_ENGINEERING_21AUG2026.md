## Major legal correction: Article 93 is not the complete 2017 control

The published lender chronology places CAM's entry as credit assignee in October 2017, after the 2012 insolvency declaration.

Accordingly:

1. References to Art. 93.2.2 and 93.3 must be identified as references to historic Law 22/2003, not current TRLC Article 93.
2. Historic Art. 93.3 is not by itself the natural rule for a 2017 post-declaration transfer because its presumption is tied to acquisition within the two years before the insolvency declaration and to credit belonging to a specially related person.
3. Historic Art. 97.4.4, introduced by Law 38/2011 and now reflected in TRLC Art. 310.2.4, is the sharper post-declaration substitution/classification control: when the later creditor is a specially related person under Article 93, classification is to take the outcome less burdensome to the insolvency / lower ranking between the initial and later creditor.
4. Supreme Court Civil Chamber Judgment 22/2026, 14 January 2026, appeal 68/2023, ECLI:ES:TS:2026:2 expressly distinguishes pre-declaration and post-declaration assignments and identifies the Article 97.4.4 rule for post-declaration assignment to a specially related person.
5. None of this proves that CAM was a specially related person. That statutory status is the predicate to test, not assume.
