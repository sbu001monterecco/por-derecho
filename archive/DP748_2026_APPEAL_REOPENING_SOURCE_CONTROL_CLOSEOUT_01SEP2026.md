The sustainable appellate/reopening question is not that “Cuatrecasas executed nonexistent properties”. It is:

> Whether a named participant, in a named ETJ writing concerning finca 8584, made or adopted a concrete factual representation about the property's identity/subsistence after documented notice of the contrary account; whether that factual representation was false, material and known to be false; whether it was used to seek adjudication for EUR 77,840 with reservation of assignment; whether it was sufficient to cause judicial error; and what resolution and patrimonial effect followed.

- finca 8588 as an auction lot;

- falsedad documental without an identified false document/modal conduct; and

On current evidence, the procedural-continuity ground is strong; the merits grounds are arguable; reopening on the new ETJ material is stronger than asserting that the criminal judge ignored a document not shown to have been filed in DP 748.
