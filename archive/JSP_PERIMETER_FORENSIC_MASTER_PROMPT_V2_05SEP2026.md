### C. Frustration of execution / asset shielding

### D. Punishable insolvency after the 2015 reform

Relevant hypotheses can include concealment/destruction of estate assets, economically unjustified disproportionate transfers, unjustified below-cost sales, fictitious credits and serious accounting/document irregularities. ### E. False corporate accounts/documents
