## 7 June 2018 control hinge

The controlled record contains contemporaneous material reporting a forced/material change of control involving access, locks/security and exclusion at Sun Park on 7 June 2018. The current controlled court corpus does not identify a specific order authorising that physical-control episode.

Safe public formulation:

- the material-control episode is documented/reported;
- authority for that episode is not located in the controlled record;
- this does not prove no authority existed;
- the required closure is filing/notice -> receipt -> traslado -> AC position -> judicial/LAJ response -> protective/inspection/access/accounting measure -> implementation.

The 24 February 2021 order must not be described simply as the court having ignored the access/works/estate complaints: located material shows that those allegations and the insolvency administrator's response entered the judicial treatment chain. The sharper question is what protection, inspection, restoration, accounting or corrective follow-up occurred and with what consequence.

## Multiple simultaneous asset lives

The same mixed-title physical complex must be reconciled through at least six legal/economic lives:

1. LPB estate property and rights within Concurso 36/2012.
2. Secured-credit collateral supporting CAM's privileged-credit position.
3. Assets subject to liquidation/adjudication/direct-sale mechanics and competing-offer litigation.
4. Physical/operational components affected by practical control after 7 June 2018, with authority to be reconstructed.
5. A contemplated whole-project RIC/RICPE hotel-rehabilitation investment life before final acquisition of the LPB perimeter.
6. Later adjudication, redevelopment and hotel-operating life, requiring reconciliation with final title, possession, works, revenue and insolvency accounting.

This framework is deliberately asset-specific. LPB, CAM, Matkator and other third-party rights must not be collapsed into one hotel-wide title.

## RIC/RICPE checkpoints

### 11 November 2020 webinar

The recovered RIC Private Equity webinar presents the old Sun Park aparthotel as a project of 220 tourist apartments to be transformed into a four-star hotel, with a stated investment of EUR 12 million and works forecast for the first quarter of 2021. The same presentation includes broader statements concerning CAM's three tourism-renovation projects, acquisition with own funds and absence of mortgages. Whether those broader statements were intended to apply to Sun Park in its then-current title/security perimeter is a specific reconciliation question, not a finding of falsity.

### 19 June 2021 El Día article

The contemporaneous article states that RIC Private Equity expected to finance the Sun Park renovation once the judicial adjudication order concerning various units became final. This is important because the public investment narrative itself remained conditional on judicial finality.

### 20 July 2021 RICPE certification

The certification signed by Enrique Guerra Suárez gives a precise 262-finca perimeter:

- CAM: 54 units;
- LPB insolvency estate: 159 apartments, 28 commercial units, 1 reception and 2 pools/solaria;
- third parties: 18 fincas.

It states that the Sun Park project remained at a preliminary stage; no letter of intent/full due diligence had been completed for it; and progress depended on CAM acquiring at least the LPB assets.

### 21 July 2021 CAM Section 5 filing

CAM itself states to the insolvency court that it had never claimed to be sole owner of Sun Park; that it owned only 54 units; and that the insolvency rights it then held derived from its privileged-creditor position over LPB units. CAM filed the RICPE certification in that court submission.

## Multitrack judicial-response tests

### Track A — filings / procedural notice

For every material filing: receipt, service/transfer, order/DIOR/providencia, appeal, finality and implementation must be bound together. `Ignored` is reserved for a certified demonstrable non-response; otherwise use adverse act, partial treatment, response not located or chain not closed.

### Track B — masa activa

Build a common-ID ledger for LPB assets, fruits/income, receivables, occupation/use, deterioration/works, recovery claims, funded exits, sale/adjudication proceeds and restitution/accounting. Any judicial-failure allegation requires actor-specific knowledge, available protective power, response and causal loss.

### Track C — unidad productiva

Keep hotel/business continuity separate from bare real estate: reservations/data, contracts, revenue, licences, services, goodwill, staff/organisation and going-concern value. Identify the owner/controller of each component before and after 7 June 2018.

### Track D — extraconcursal harm

Maintain a separate Matkator/third-party ledger. The RICPE certification independently confirms that 18 fincas belonged to third parties. Insolvency 36/2012 cannot itself substitute for title, consent or other authority over non-LPB rights.

### Track E — RICPE / investor-facing life

Compare the 2020 webinar, 19 June 2021 article, 20 July certification and 21 July court filing against Registry title, security/mortgage status, adjudication finality and possession on each date. Divergence is a verification point. Do not infer investor deception, fraud, reliance, funding or damage without the missing commitment, due-diligence, approval and money-flow records.
