## Evidential rules
Maintain, proposition by proposition:
- VERIFIED FACT;
- PARTY ALLEGATION;
- EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE;
- UNRESOLVED QUESTION;
- CONTRADICTED/CORRECTED;
- SUPERSEDED.

Never collapse:
`prepared ≠ proposed ≠ admitted ≠ formally scheduled ≠ citation ordered ≠ served ≠ available ≠ attended ≠ called ≠ testified ≠ heard ≠ accepted ≠ relied upon ≠ reflected in judgment`.

Never convert:
- institutional availability into personal judicial knowledge;
- an inaccessible-file report into proof the Judge did not read the evidence;
- a two-day scheduling order into proof a 26-Jul hearing occurred;
- a recording interval into proof of deletion/manipulation;
- a registered CGPJ supplement into proof of examination or acceptance;
- an adverse judgment into proof of criminal conduct;
- a procedural disagreement into a disciplinary offence.
