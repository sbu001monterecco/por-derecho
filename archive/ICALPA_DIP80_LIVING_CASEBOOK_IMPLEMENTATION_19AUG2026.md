## Evidential boundaries

- Registration proves registration, not review, verification or use.
- Opening of preliminary information is not a finding of professional breach.
- Absence of the professional response from the reviewed public corpus is not absence from the official file and is never admission.
- DIP 79 and DIP 80 remain separate. RE-007499 is expressly marked for document-allocation control because it contains a DIP 79-linked copy.
- Continuing consequences are not automatically continuing misconduct; act-specific temporal analysis is mandatory.
- ICALPA professional-conduct competence is kept separate from insolvency-court and criminal competences.
- Urgent protection is analysed separately from culpability.
