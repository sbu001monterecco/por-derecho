## 2. Whole-site institutional-read conclusion to preserve

A serious CGPJ reader should not be asked to adopt the whole Project Sun Rock causal narrative as proof of judicial misconduct.

The public website tells a much wider chain involving fragmented title, Community/CEXP governance, debt/voting, creditor position, material access/control, the insolvency estate, the Insolvency Administrator, RICPE/investment narratives, public authorities, adjudication, office instruments, Registry effects, later hotel operation, professional accountability and recovery.

For CGPJ purposes that wider record must be filtered through the narrower test:

`verifiable risk → direct judicial knowledge OR AC knowledge/reporting duty → available supervision / information / protection power → identifiable response → next patrimonial step → degree of reversibility → later corrective opportunity`

The strongest institutional formulation is therefore **not** "many bad/adverse decisions by one judge". It is a longitudinal reconstruction of whether supervision of the Insolvency Administrator and protection of the insolvency estate can be traced before successive difficult-to-reverse patrimonial steps.

## 3. Reader-routing conclusion

### Homepage / whole-site read

The global site is powerful for causation and cross-institutional context but is **not the optimal first landing page for CGPJ** because a first-time Council reader encounters private-actor allegations, Community governance, RICPE, public money, professional firms, criminal routes and recovery before the Council's own narrow competence is fully delimited.

### Dedicated CGPJ route

The dedicated Commission reader room is the appropriate institutional front door because it:

- starts with the adverse CGPJ record;
- preserves the jurisdictional-merits boundary;
- uses three knowledge levels;
- organises seven supervisory nuclei;
- separates Judge / AC / LAJ-office / Inspection / disciplinary / criminal routes;
- treats every open proposition as a finite documentary verification question.

Whole-site material should enter the CGPJ route only where it supports **notice, consequence, continuity or a finite verification proposition**, not as automatic proof of historical judicial knowledge.

## 4. Simulated institutional-team reads — preservation summary

Likely first question is procedural rather than factual:

### B. Promotor de la Acción Disciplinaria

Strongest likely resistance:

- debt, evidence, bidding, adjudication, title and many refusals are jurisdictional decisions subject to ordinary remedies;
- adverse or arguably erroneous rulings do not themselves establish disciplinary misconduct.

The residual question the site should force is narrower:

**After excluding merits review, did the complaint still contain autonomous supervision/functioning issues that required separate classification rather than global subsumption as jurisdictional disagreement?**

No thread conclusion states that disciplinary liability is proved.

### C. Servicio de Inspección

The Inspection-facing formulation is likely the most institutionally intelligible for the supervision spine:

- AC reporting completeness/timing;
- estate protection and condition verification;
- treatment of access/works/preservation concerns;
- court information architecture;
- long-running insolvency management and traceability;
- treatment of repeated requests;
- 2025 AC-removal applications as later supervisory checkpoints;
- office functioning and documentary implementation.

The key Inspection question is not whether the debt or adjudication was legally correct, but how judicial-service functioning and supervision can be reconstructed.

### D. Comisión Permanente — judicial members

Expected safeguards:

- judicial independence and prohibition on disciplinary second-appeal review;
- protection against hindsight;
- distinction between institutional availability of information and personal judicial knowledge;
- legitimacy of reasonable reliance on the AC;
- consideration of favourable/neutral judicial acts as counterevidence.

The website's strongest answer is to ask **when, if ever, accumulated risk and notice made further supervisory verification necessary**, rather than claiming the judge could never rely on the AC.

### E. Comisión Permanente — jurist members / legal support

## 5. Seven nuclei — reader-simulation refinements

The seven nuclei in the canonical evidence map remain controlling. Preserve these specific reader reactions:

1. **Funded exit / progressive knowledge:** Daniel Irigoyen is a contemporaneous professional source, not a judicial minute. Verify what actually entered the court record and what treatment followed.
2. **Credit / threshold / amount absorbed:** numerical differences are not themselves misconduct; the institutional question is documentary reconciliation and the legal function of each figure.
3. **28-Nov-2018 / EUR 400k / 24-Oct-2019 non-validation:** this is strengthened by a judicial act favourable to the complainant's position. The question is what later implementation/restitution/reconciliation gave effect to that act before the assets re-entered the 2021–22 chain.
4. **Masa activa / physical condition:** strongest finite question remains: if pericial/judicial inspection was refused, what neutral equivalent gave the court reliable information about asset condition before liquidation continued?
5. **AC supervision:** likely strongest functioning/Inspection module. Reconstruct AC knowledge → verification/reporting → court requirement → alternative control.
6. **ONA/Ottawa / material competition:** much is jurisdictional-merits territory; retain only separable functioning issues such as access, information, timing, perimeter and procedural treatment unless the full bidding record supports more.
7. **Adjudication → LAJ → deed → Registry → accounts:** actor separation is mandatory. Do not attribute testimony, finality, service or Registry-facing acts automatically to the judge.

## 6. Cross-cutting modules

### Insolvency estate / extra-insolvency perimeter

LPB was the insolvent debtor; Sun Park as a whole was not. The dual supervision test is:

- was the property/right actually inside LPB sufficiently protected;
- did insolvency-derived authority create material effects outside LPB over Matkator, third parties, exploitation or other independent rights without a corresponding legal bridge?

### Community information-quality

CGPJ should not be asked to decide the validity of the Community structure. The relevant supervision question is what verification occurred when contested Community debts, certificates, votes or authority propositions entered the AC/court information chain.

### External RICPE / regulatory / public-funds context

Use only for notice, consequence, continuity or finite verification unless incorporation into the insolvency file is proved. Do not impute external/later knowledge to the judge retroactively.

### LPAM / appearance of impartiality

Keep discrete. The current record supports existence of a contemporaneously recorded allegation and later witness material, not proof of friendship, call history, improper private contact, influence, bias or corruption.

### Calificación

Calificación adds a different analytical category: positive judicial adoption of selected propositions rather than only omission/supervision. It must remain tied to exact propositions, contrary material demonstrably known at the time, and the pending appellate posture. The judgment also rejected/narrowed some AC/Fiscal propositions, which is mandatory counterevidence.

### EUR 3,032,010.34 / alleged personal-gain theory

Keep heavily segregated from the CGPJ supervision spine. The amount, source and judicial adoption can be audited; a corrupt agreement, personal gain or clandestine benefit route is not established by the current controlled record.
