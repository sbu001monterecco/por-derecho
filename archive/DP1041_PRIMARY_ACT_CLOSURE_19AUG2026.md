# DP 1041/2017 — PRIMARY-ACT CLOSURE

**Control date:** 19 August 2026  
**Scope:** Article 1535 / PH122 → CAM / DP 1041/2017 / Cristo Suárez Pimentel authority change / claimant withdrawal  
**Publication status:** PUBLIC-READY-WITH-QUALIFICATION

## 1. What is now closed by primary evidence

### 25 January 2018 — Cristo's specific formal LPB mandate

Contemporaneous AC-to-Cristo correspondence records that the Administrador Concursal:

- stated that Cristo had acted for LPB without the AC's required authorisation;
- communicated termination of the professional relationship Cristo maintained with LPB;
- requested `venia` in DP 1041/2017;
- instructed Cristo to abstain from future LPB action; and
- instructed the procurador not to file future LPB pleadings signed by Cristo or another lawyer without AC authorisation, while initially preserving necessary acts in DP 1041.

**Controlled finding:** direct documentary causation exists for the end of Cristo's specific formal LPB mandate in this matter. Matter-specific classification: **C5**.

No voluntary resignation by Cristo has been located. His later broader Sun Park activity must remain separately visible.

### 5 March 2018 — claimant-side withdrawal

Decreto 139/2018, JPI nº 2, DP 1041/2017 states:

- LPB requested withdrawal from the proceeding;
- CAM did not oppose and had expressed conformity;
- LPB was treated as withdrawn;
- the proceeding was terminated;
- the hearing scheduled for 25 April 2018 was suspended; and
- the intervention table lists **Francisco Borja Rodríguez Batllori Laffitte** as LPB's lawyer and **Alejandro Válido Farray** as procurador.

**Controlled finding:** the procedural endpoint is no longer open. DP 1041 ended because LPB withdrew.

### 13 May 2024 — archive independently reaffirmed

A Diligencia de Ordenación issued after an Aweswell filing states that DP 1041/2017:

- was finalised and archived because of the claimant's withdrawal;
- was terminated by the 5 March 2018 decree; and
- had no proceedings derived from it.

The intervention table again records Borja as LPB's lawyer and records Javier Sixto Seijas for Aweswell.

## 2. What remains open

The judicial acts do **not** identify:

1. the exact underlying withdrawal pleading;
2. who internally decided that LPB should withdraw;
3. who instructed the lawyer/procurador;
4. who drafted the pleading;
5. who signed it;
6. the filing/transmission channel and any cover communication;
7. the complete Cristo-to-successor handover/venia/power sequence;
8. any contemporaneous cost-benefit or estate-interest analysis supporting withdrawal; or
9. whether an independent substantive Article 1535 action was filed elsewhere.

## 3. Strongest safe public formulation

> The record now proves a sequence of formal-control change and procedural termination. On 25 January 2018 the insolvency administrator directly terminated Cristo Suárez Pimentel's specific formal LPB mandate and imposed an AC authorisation gate over future LPB procedural action. By 5 March 2018 the court record lists Borja as LPB's lawyer and records that LPB had requested withdrawal from DP 1041; CAM did not oppose and the proceeding was terminated. In 2024 the court independently reaffirmed that the file was archived because of that claimant-side withdrawal. What remains open is the internal decision and filing chain behind the withdrawal. This sequence does not by itself establish improper motive, collusion or that Article 1535 would have succeeded.

## 4. Prohibited overstatements

Do not publish as established fact:

- “Cristo resigned.”
- “The AC personally drafted or signed the desistimiento.”
- “The AC personally instructed the desistimiento” unless the instruction evidence is recovered.
- “The AC destroyed a winning Article 1535 claim.”
- “The judge blocked a valid retracto claim.”
- “The 19 December 2017 JPI admission decided the credit was litigioso.”

## 5. Consequence for repository controls

This note supersedes any repository wording that still describes:

- the DP 1041 termination act/date as unknown;
- the 25 January authority-change instrument as unrecovered; or
- Cristo's matter-specific departure as merely client testimony.

The remaining P0 question is no longer **whether** the route ended or **whether** Cristo's formal LPB mandate was terminated by the AC. It is **who decided and instrumented LPB's withdrawal, on what estate-interest analysis, and with what procedural authority chain**.
