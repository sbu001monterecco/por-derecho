## CR-CAL-RET-001 — The retaliation position is an identified allegation, not an adjudicated fact

Use:

> Gil Marer alleges that the 25 July 2023 hearing and later punitive treatment should be examined as possible retaliation under Articles 35, 36 and 38 of Law 2/2023.

Do not write that the hearing, judgment, Judge, Insolvency Administrator or Public Prosecutor has already been judicially or regulatorily determined to have committed retaliation. Chronology supports investigation; it does not by itself prove motive or causation.

## CR-CAL-RET-002 — Article 36.2 is the definition; Article 36.3 contains examples

Article 36.2 defines retaliation as unlawful or directly/indirectly unfavourable treatment creating a particular disadvantage in the professional context solely because of reporting or public disclosure. The non-exhaustive examples are in Article 36.3. Do not describe a court hearing or judgment as an expressly listed Article 36.2 example.

## CR-CAL-RET-003 — Article 38.4 presumption is conditional

Law 2/2023 Article 38.4 creates a rebuttable presumption only after the reporting person reasonably demonstrates:

1. a communication or public disclosure made in accordance with the Law; and
2. detriment.

The person taking the detrimental measure may prove duly justified grounds unrelated to the reporting. Never invoke the presumption without testing Articles 2, 3 and 35, the channel and recipient, professional context, reasonable grounds, temporal scope, detriment and causation.

## CR-CAL-RET-004 — The current RICPE temporal disclaimer was incomplete

Superseded wording limited the retaliation analysis to post-entry-into-force underlying acts or omissions and treated 2021 only as context. That was too categorical because the Sixth Additional Provision extends protective measures to communications concerning Article 2 acts or omissions occurring since Directive (EU) 2019/1937 entered into force.

The correction does **not** retroactively turn 2021 conduct into an infringement. It requires four dates to remain separate:

1. date of the underlying act or omission;
2. date and channel of the communication;
3. date of the alleged adverse measure; and
4. date the applicable protection regime entered into force.
