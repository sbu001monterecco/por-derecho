# Project Sun Rock — Institutional Message Discovery Protocol

**Purpose:** guide future ChatGPT research/maintenance threads to scan the live website, Depository, Gmail, Drive/Library and court/institutional records and identify other people, offices or entities that may justify an evidence-first hub, open message, accountability page or dedicated visualisation similar in function to the Ministerio Fiscal treatment.

Updated: 15 August 2026

## Core principle

Do not begin by deciding that a person or institution “deserves an open letter”. Begin by testing whether the record contains a sufficiently important, documentable and audience-relevant institutional question that benefits from a dedicated public explanation.

The model to reuse is not “accusation page”. It is:

**neutral identifier / mark → evidence hub → documented chronology → what was known → what was done → what remained unresolved → what happened next → public-safe analytical question → source access → correction route.**

The Ministerio Fiscal implementation is the reference pattern, but each actor must be independently assessed against its own competence, role, evidence and procedural limits.

## First scan: identify candidate actors/offices

Scan the live site and build a candidate register. Include any person, office, court, professional actor, public authority or private institutional counterparty that satisfies several of the following:

- appears repeatedly in the core chronology;
- exercised a formal power capable of materially affecting rights, evidence, assets, procedure or remedy;
- received documented notice before a consequential event;
- issued or signed decisions central to the case theory;
- occupies a procedural position readers routinely misunderstand;
- is currently represented only by scattered references rather than a coherent evidence page;
- has an unresolved contradiction, duty/response question, or “what happened after notice?” issue;
- is important to rule-of-law / tutela judicial efectiva / insolvency-governance / public-accountability audiences;
- already has a mark/logo/card on the site whose click-path should lead somewhere more useful;
- has enough primary evidence for an evidence-led page without relying mainly on allegation.

## Candidate scoring model

Score each candidate 0–5 on:

1. **Documentary depth** — primary sources, signed decisions, emails, filings, orders.
2. **Institutional importance** — power over rights/remedy/assets/evidence.
3. **Chronological centrality** — appears across multiple critical phases.
4. **Notice/knowledge evidence** — demonstrable receipt or participation before later events.
5. **Response-accountability gap** — a concrete unanswered “what did you do after X?” question.
6. **Audience value** — helps ordinary readers, journalists, lawyers or institutions understand the case.
7. **Legal/publication safety** — can be framed accurately without relying on defamatory or speculative allegations.
8. **Visual/navigation benefit** — current site is fragmented or mark/card has no useful destination.

Total /40.

Suggested thresholds:

- **32–40:** strong candidate for dedicated evidence-first hub/open message.
- **25–31:** build or strengthen an accountability page first; consider open message after more source work.
- **18–24:** keep within existing institutional/actor pages and improve cross-links.
- **<18:** no dedicated message; maintain only as a node/tag/source relationship.

The threshold does not override legal/publication concerns. A high score with weak primary evidence must remain internal until verified.

## Required audience simulation before publication

For every proposed dedicated message, simulate at least:

- ordinary Spanish reader;
- investigative journalist;
- subject-matter lawyer;
- judge/prosecutor/public official where relevant;
- hostile/sceptical reader;
- investor/business reader if patrimonial issues matter.

Ask:

1. What do they think happened after 30 seconds?
2. What do they think the actor's role was after 2 minutes?
3. Can they distinguish documented fact from allegation?
4. What is the strongest reasonable rebuttal by the actor?
5. Does the page answer that rebuttal or merely repeat the thesis?
6. What one missing document would most change the assessment?

## Dedicated-page architecture

Prefer this structure:

1. **Hero question** — one document-answerable institutional question, not a guilty verdict.
2. **60-second chronology**.
3. **Role / legal competence** — what this actor could and could not do.
4. **Knowledge timeline** — what was demonstrably before them and when.
5. **Response timeline** — affirmative decisions, referrals, omissions/non-located responses.
6. **What happened next** — clearly separated from causation.
7. **Competing explanations / strongest defence**.
8. **What this establishes / does not establish**.
9. **5–10 answerable questions**.
10. **Primary evidence links**.
11. **Correction / right-of-reply invitation**.
12. **Formal open message**, only if justified, beneath the evidence-first layer.

## Mark / logo navigation rule

For public institutions and public-law professional bodies:

**official mark → neutral evidence/institution hub → clearly labelled Project Sun Rock analysis/open message.**

Do not use an official-looking mark as a direct visual endorsement of an accusatory message. Preserve provenance/disclaimer rules in `INSTITUTIONAL_MARK_SOURCES.md`.

For private entities, use the entity name/brand only within applicable rights and provenance rules, and avoid designs implying endorsement.

## Evidence discipline

For each candidate, maintain:

- actor/office ID;
- exact role and period;
- competence/powers;
- primary documents;
- knowledge dates;
- decisions/actions;
- requested actions;
- non-located responses;
- later events;
- causation status;
- strongest defence;
- contradiction register;
- missing-document register;
- personal attribution limits;
- publication-safe thesis;
- statements explicitly **not yet publishable**.

Never convert:

- receipt → substantive review;
- silence in corpus → proof no action existed;
- adverse decision → bias;
- procedural error → prevaricación;
- later consequence → causation;
- institutional knowledge → personal knowledge by every office-holder;
- association/sequence → collusion.

## End-of-cycle deliverable

Every discovery cycle must produce:

- candidate register with scores;
- recommended page/message type;
- source sufficiency %;
- audience clarity %;
- legal/publication-risk note;
- exact missing primary documents;
- proposed navigation path from existing mark/card/page;
- ES/EN parity requirement;
- whether to publish now, build evidence first, or decline a dedicated page.

The objective is not to multiply accusatory pages. The objective is to make the website's **institutional accountability architecture proportionate to the evidence**, so every major actor with a material, documentable role can be understood, tested and corrected by readers without collapsing distinct legal responsibilities into one narrative.
