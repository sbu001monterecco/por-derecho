## Permanent arithmetic control

Primary Jan-2021 sources:

- AP-linked first-rank default interest: `€3,079,104.66 + daily accrual → €3,182,000 cap`;
- delta: `€3,182,000 − €3,079,104.66 = €102,895.34`;
- `€7,792,421.02 + €967,500 + €3,182,000 = €11,941,921.02`;
- `€839,411 + €72,250 + €314,500 = €1,226,161.00`;
- `€11,941,921.02 + €1,226,161.00 = €13,168,082.02`;
- `€13,168,082.02 + €400,000 = €13,568,082.02`;
- `€13,568,082.02 + €1,145,798.29 = €14,713,880.31`.

Taxes/costs were separately stated but unquantified.

## Evidence rule

A mathematically derivable subtotal is **not automatically a legal price, a recognised credit, a compensable amount, a deed consideration or a surplus base**. The legal function must come from the source document that uses the number. Likewise, a better-posture interest threshold reaching the mortgage cap does not by itself prove that the same amount was definitively recognised as concursal credit.
