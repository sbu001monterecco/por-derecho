## Completion rule

For this project, `100%` means: every distinct judicial / LAJ / court-office / appellate procedural act presently identifiable from the recoverable corpus is either (a) primary/certified/official-copy digitised and fully digested, or (b) explicitly recorded as a missing original with all known secondary references and documented retrieval attempts.

It does **not** mean 100% of every document that may ever have existed. The gold-standard denominator remains reconciliation against an official/certified court index.
