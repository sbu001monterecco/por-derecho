## 3. Legal/completeness audit — controlling position

The strongest defensible position is **not** that Intervención General acted unlawfully across the board.

### 3.1 Points on which Intervención has a substantial legal basis

Intervención General has a strong position that a private complainant cannot, merely by filing communications:

- compel it to open an omnibus financial-control/audit investigation;
- require prospective, all-encompassing verification of every private, cadastral, insolvency, tax, financing and public-funds issue connected to the asset; or
- obtain indiscriminate access to confidential third-party financial-control/audit material.

The response's distinction between **RIC suitability** and an autonomous-community subsidy is also substantially defensible as framed: RIC suitability is not, simply by involving the Canary Government, identical to an autonomous subsidy under Intervención's ordinary control perimeter.

These points should be conceded rather than attacked.

### 3.2 Where the August answer is materially incomplete or over-broad

The user's later submissions did not consist only of demands for an omnibus audit. They also asked narrower questions about acts already acknowledged by the Administration itself, including:

- the basic metadata and documentary scope of Intervención's own transfer to Hacienda/UE;
- the administrative consequence of Justice's declaration of incompetence;
- the treatment of later registrations;
- the continuity of the Commission/SIINF route; and
- whether later enlargements were linked to the earlier institutional action.

Confidentiality of a beneficiary's audit file does not itself answer those questions.

Therefore the best characterisation is:

> Intervención General was substantially correct in rejecting a right to compel an omnibus audit or obtain confidential third-party control files, but SALIDA 699645/2026 is materially incomplete as to narrower, severable questions concerning the Administration's own remissions, routing, procedural continuity and SIINF treatment.

### 3.3 Standing / interested-party point

Intervención's reliance on the absence of recognised interested-party status is too categorical if applied globally.

The correct approach is proceeding-specific:

- a third party may have no right to enter a confidential control proceeding concerning another beneficiary;
- but questions about the treatment, routing and decision on the user's own submission are analytically different;
- and any rights in RIC, regional-incentive or other administrative files must be assessed separately rather than collapsed into one universal standing conclusion.

### 3.4 SIINF / Decreto 91/2024 issue

The first Intervención response expressly records Commission consideration under the institutional framework created by **Decreto 91/2024**.

Accordingly, the August statement that the internal channel is available does not by itself answer the historical procedural question:

> Was GM-INS-2025-01 formally processed under SIINF@GOBCAN? If yes, what was the code/status in the legally communicable perimeter? If no, on what procedural basis did the Commission analyse it and what happened to later enlargements?

The final filing therefore relies on the procedural framework of Decreto 91/2024, including the provisions governing forwarding of information received outside the formal channel, admission/inadmission and procedural closure, without alleging a proven violation where the internal procedural facts remain unknown.

### 3.5 Other legal anchors preserved for counsel validation

Potentially relevant legal anchors identified in the audit include:

- **Article 14, Ley 40/2015** — inter-organ competence/remission mechanics;
- **Article 4, Ley 39/2015** — interested-party analysis must be proceeding-specific;
- **Article 62.3, Ley 39/2015** — potentially relevant where a complaint specifically alleges damage to public assets; this requires issue-specific counsel validation and should not be presented as automatically applicable to every RIC/public-funds allegation;
- **Decreto 91/2024** — SIINF routing, admission/inadmission and closure mechanics.

Do not overstate any of these as a judicially established breach.
