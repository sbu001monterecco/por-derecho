## Public evidential boundary

The project may allege and investigate acts, omissions, silence, deficient handover, causal enablement and later fee-enforcement choices. It must not convert those propositions into findings of negligence, collusion, procedural fraud, indirect insolvency fraud or intentional instrumentalisation without the evidence required for the exact proposition.

“Enablement” remains a causal proposition. It requires a defined duty, knowledge, available protection, act or omission, intervening decision, counterfactual and loss. Temporal sequence or later benefit is insufficient.

A procedural-fraud hypothesis requires an exact deception or legally material omission, knowledge, fraudulent intent, causal judicial error, patrimonial disposition, loss and improper benefit. Disputed billing or professional criticism does not itself satisfy those elements.

## La Laguna → Las Palmas control

The same hotel and related companies can appear in multiple proceedings without proving an intentional procedural bridge.

Before alleging that La Laguna was instrumentalised to affect Concurso 36/2012, the record must identify:

- the exact originating act;
- author, signatory, instructor and approver;
- prior professional knowledge;
- immediate La Laguna effect;
- the exact transmission mechanism;
- the Las Palmas recipient;
- actual reliance or procedural effect;
- patrimonial consequence;
- beneficiary and affected legal person;
- foreseeability;
- separate proof of purpose if intent is alleged;
- counterfactual and ordinary-enforcement explanation.

Until transmission and reliance are proved, the controlled status is:

> **OPEN DIRECTIONAL BRIDGE — EFFECT NOT YET PROVED.**

A 2026 defence-side use of material from one proceeding to seek protection in another establishes that current counsel made a defensive procedural connection. It does not establish that the original claimant created the first proceeding in order to manipulate a separate court.

## Media architecture

Maximum sustainable pressure is defined as maximum documentary visibility, answerability and correction discipline—not maximum rhetorical escalation.

Five controlled public release tracks are now recognised:

1. **Professional lifecycle** — mandate, billing, knowledge, advice, acts, omissions, communication and handover.
2. **Matkator inversion** — the transition from adviser within the rescue perimeter to claimant and executant against a company and property inside the same hotel reality.
3. **Fee and instrument chain** — client, instructor, beneficiary, invoice, payer, pagaré, debtor, title, asset, adjudication, possible cession and final beneficiary.
4. **La Laguna–Las Palmas map** — documented edges, missing transmission and reliance, counterfactual and strongest non-fraud explanation.
5. **Institutional answer tracker** — finite questions, documents produced, right of reply, corrections, professional-body status and court outcomes.

Every material release must state:

- its primary-source basis;
- evidential status;
- strongest foreseeable defence;
- missing proof;
- finite questions;
- response status;
- correction and version history.

Non-response is not an admission.
