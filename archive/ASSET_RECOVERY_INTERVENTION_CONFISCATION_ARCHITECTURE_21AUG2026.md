## 1. Strategic shift

The project must no longer ask only whether historic acts were lawful or unlawful. It must also ask, **asset by asset and authority by authority**, whether the present system is preserving the possibility of an effective remedy if those historic acts are ultimately found actionable.

> **PRESERVE FIRST. TRACE THE VALUE. DECIDE THE MERITS AFTER.**
>
> The chain is now sufficiently visible to pose a concrete institutional question: which assets, income streams, rights, accounts, records and metadata are being preserved today; by which competent authority; under which statutory power; and from what date?

This is not a request for one authority to take control of every company or person connected with the matter. The legal architecture is stronger and more precise: **one common value-tracing matrix, with each competent authority exercising the preservation, intervention, supervision, seizure, administration or recovery power Parliament has assigned to it.**

**Administrative fragmentation does not break economic traceability.**

## 2. Economic tracing chain

Every future recovery analysis must test:

`ORIGINAL ASSET / RIGHT`
→ `POSSESSION / CONTROL`
→ `TRANSFER OR TRANSFORMATION`
→ `CORPORATE LAYER`
→ `FINANCING`
→ `WORKS / CAPEX`
→ `HOTEL OPERATION`
→ `REVENUE / FRUITS`
→ `REFINANCING`
→ `DISTRIBUTION / SUBSTITUTE VALUE`
→ `CURRENT HOLDER / BENEFICIARY`

A later corporate reorganisation, authorisation, financing act, registration, refurbishment, operator or brand does not by itself prove illegality. **Nor does it, by itself, extinguish traceability or rewrite the legal quality of earlier acts.**

## 3. Spanish intervention and preservation stack

### 3.1 Criminal judicial intervention — Código Penal article 33.7(g)

Current Spanish criminal law expressly provides judicial intervention of a legal person to safeguard workers or creditors. The intervention may cover the whole organisation or be limited to installations, sections or business units. The judicial decision defines its content, appoints the intervener and sets reporting obligations; the intervener has access to premises and information. The investigating judge may also order judicial intervention provisionally during the investigation.

Primary authority: Código Penal, art. 33.7(g):  
https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1995-25444

**Case-use rule:** test entity by entity against procedural posture, alleged predicate offence, legal-person status, proportionality and statutory purpose.

### 3.2 Entities/groups without legal personality — Código Penal article 129

Where qualifying offences are committed within, with the collaboration of, through or by enterprises, organisations, groups or entities lacking legal personality, article 129 permits consequences with the content of article 33.7(c)–(g), subject to its requirements.

Primary authority: Código Penal, art. 129:  
https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1995-25444

**Case-use rule:** Community, CEXP, communities of property, owners' groups and LPB must not be collapsed into each other. First establish the legal nature of the specific structure and the offence/attribution basis.

### 3.3 Seizure, attachment and confiscation preservation — Código Penal article 127 octies

Article 127 octies provides that, to guarantee the effectiveness of confiscation, property, means, instruments and proceeds may be seized or attached and placed in deposit by judicial authority **from the first investigative steps**. Article 127 septies provides equivalent-value confiscation where execution against identified property/proceeds cannot be carried out in whole or part, subject to statutory conditions.

Primary authority: Código Penal, arts. 127–127 octies:  
https://www.boe.es/eli/es/lo/1995/11/23/10/con

**Case-use rule:** never label a later asset “criminal proceeds” merely because it sits downstream in the chronology. Build the tracing bridge and identify the alleged predicate act, transformation, holder, consideration, knowledge test and applicable confiscation provision.

### 3.4 ORGA — recovery and management, not merely final sale

Real Decreto 948/2015 configures the Oficina de Recuperación y Gestión de Activos as an auxiliary body of the Administration of Justice with functions of locating, recovering, conserving, administering and realising effects, property, instruments and proceeds from criminal activity within its legal remit.

Primary authority: Real Decreto 948/2015:  
https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2015-11427

**Case-use rule:** separate tracing, judicial restraint, management and final confiscation. ORGA does not itself decide guilt or create the underlying restraint.

### 3.5 CNMV intervention / substitution — Ley 22/2014 art. 102 + Ley 35/2003 arts. 72–75

This is a priority route.

Article 102 of Ley 22/2014 expressly applies the intervention and substitution regime of Ley 35/2003 to **SCR, SICC, SGEIC and depositaries**.

Under Ley 35/2003:

- art. 72 permits CNMV intervention or provisional substitution in situations of exceptional gravity that seriously endanger patrimonial balance, client assets, financial stability or the general interest, and where grounded indications exist but the true patrimonial position cannot be deduced from accounts and records;
- art. 73 provides for interveners/provisional administrators, immediate enforceability and, when necessary, direct taking of possession of offices, books and documents for execution/examination;
- art. 74 provides that, after intervention, acts and decisions of persons or bodies with decision-making, executive, representative or control powers require express approval of the appointed interveners, subject to the statutory terms;
- art. 75 governs effects of substitution.

Primary authorities:  
Ley 22/2014 art. 102: https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-11714  
Ley 35/2003 arts. 72–75: https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2003-20331

**Case-use rule:** establish the exact regulated classification of the RICPE vehicle, manager and depositary before making an entity-specific demand. HNT, Canarian Hospitality, MYND and downstream companies are not automatically within article 102 merely because they are economically connected to an SCR investment.

### 3.6 Tax / public-funds / EU-funds preservation

Keep AEAT, Canary tax/finance bodies, Intervención General, granting authorities, IGAE/SNCA and EU bodies distinct. The common evidential task is transversal reconciliation:

`RIC`
→ `RICPE / HNT financing`
→ `works / eligible costs`
→ `regional incentives`
→ `FEDER / EU dimension`
→ `payments / invoices / certifications`

The existence of several successive financial or administrative acts must not substitute for independent verification of the facts each act assumes.

## 5. Strong public propositions now supportable

1. **The issue is no longer only historic accountability; it is present-day preservation of an effective remedy.**
2. **Administrative fragmentation is not a defence to economic traceability.** Different authorities may have different powers over different slices of one value chain.
3. **Later layering does not, by itself, cleanse or destroy provenance.** Nor does it prove illegality. The legal answer depends on tracing evidence and the statutory test.
4. **Spanish law already contains intervention, seizure, management and confiscation-preservation mechanisms outside insolvency.** They are not abstract policy ideas.
5. **CNMV has a specific statutory intervention/substitution architecture for the regulated categories named in Ley 22/2014 art. 102.** The immediate question is whether the exact RICPE vehicle/manager/depositary classification and evidence engage it.
6. **Criminal courts can preserve confiscation effectiveness from the first investigative steps under CP 127 octies.** Waiting for final judgment is not the only preservation model contemplated by law.
7. **A productive asset can be preserved economically, not merely frozen physically.** ORGA exists in part to conserve/administer value within its legal remit.
8. **The Canary public-funds route now has a precise cross-file question:** who reconciled RIC, RICPE/HNT finance, eligible works, regional incentives and the FEDER/EU dimension end-to-end?
9. **Recent institutional status must be stated exactly:** Anticorrupción receipt/admission-pending; AEAT processing commenced; CGPJ general-information transmission bounced; Canary reconciliation/preservation request sent.
10. **The burden has shifted from visibility to institutional action:** identify what is preserved, who holds it, under what power and from what date.
