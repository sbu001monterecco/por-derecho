## Cross-institutional convergence test

For each document or proposition appearing in more than one institutional track, record:
1. source/originator;
2. date;
3. recipient;
4. legal/administrative purpose;
5. exact asset/entity perimeter;
6. whether title, operation, funding, tax, works or tourism status was being asserted;
7. whether the receiving institution independently verified it or merely received it;
8. later use by another institution;
9. any contradiction/correction;
10. current unresolved primary-source need.

## Publication discipline

- Received ≠ examined.
- Referred ≠ resolved.
- Tourism operating unity ≠ ownership unity.
- Municipal treatment ≠ registered title.
- Financial-control remit limitation ≠ deliberate inaction.
- Alert ≠ proof of fraud.
- Generic statement that checks occurred ≠ proof of what was checked.
- Absence from the current corpus ≠ non-existence in the authority’s file.
