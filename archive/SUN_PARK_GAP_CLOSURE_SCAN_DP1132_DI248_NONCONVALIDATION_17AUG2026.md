## 0. Evidential discipline

Use throughout:

- **Class 1:** primary official / signed / certified source.
- **Class 2:** primary contemporaneous private/business record.
- **Class 3:** party pleading describing underlying evidence.
- **Class 4:** later recollection / allegation / analysis.
- **Class 5:** inference requiring further evidence.

A located document closes only the proposition authenticated by that document. `Not recovered` never means `does not exist`.

# 11. JV 1260/2011 — adverse appellate merits source recovered

The AP Las Palmas, Sección Quinta, **Sentencia 89/2014 of 13-Jan-2014** is now located as a primary source. It resolves **Rollo 793/2012** arising from JPI nº4 Arrecife JV 1260/2011. The same Drive binary and SHA-256 were previously misidentified in this file as Sentencia 16/2014 / appeal 570/2012; CR-066 preserves that correction.

This is important **adverse evidence**.

The judgment records that the plaintiff owners had placed apartments into the CEXP operation and that the lower court had ordered eviction against CEXP but absolved Monterecco for lack of passive standing. The Audiencia partially reversed and extended the eviction result to **Monterecco Sun Park, S.L.**.

The appellate reasoning found, for that historical period and dispute, sufficient evidence of practical possession and an intertwined relationship among CEXP, Monterecco and LPB to justify the result, including evidence concerning hotel promotion/administration, tourist operation, income/expenses/accounting and shared/overlapping operational relationships. It applied a veil-piercing analysis and ordered Monterecco also to vacate the plaintiffs' apartments, with first-instance costs; no appellate costs were imposed.

### Consequence

A broad proposition that Monterecco had **no practical possession/control at all** in the 2011–2012 period is contradicted by this judgment and must be retired.

### Limit

The judgment does **not** establish that Pink had identical legal or practical capacity continuously in 2014, 2017 or June 2018. Periodisation remains compulsory.

The remaining JV gap is mainly finality/execution/cassation consequences and any later possession enforcement, not the appellate merits outcome.
