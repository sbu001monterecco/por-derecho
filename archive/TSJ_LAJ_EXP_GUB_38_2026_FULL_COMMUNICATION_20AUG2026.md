## 5. Decision — controlled digest

### 5.1 Preliminary information

The decision treats preliminary information under Article 168 ROCSJ as discretionary and says the supplied material reveals no disciplinary matter. It adds:

> “Da la impresión de que cerrada la vía del Magistrado, se acude como alternativa dirigirse o poner el foco en el Letrado de la Administración de Justicia.”

This is an institutional inference about motive, not an evidential finding. The 15 August source expressly separated the LAJ / Office-of-the-Court object from review of judicial merits and said partial factual overlap did not mean identity of object.

### 5.2 Inspection / supervision

The decision distinguishes inspection of services for which LAJs are responsible from scrutiny of an individual LAJ in a concrete proceeding and says no grounds exist for an office inspection.

The alzada must concede the prohibition on using inspection as a substitute for procedural appeals while asking the superior authority to determine whether office-level creation, custody, certification, notification, delivery and traceability are nonetheless service questions capable of limited inspection or certification.

### 5.3 ATLANTE

The decision states that the interested party misunderstands ATLANTE because the system preserves electronic information, incorporated material cannot be deleted or altered, and procedural activity is traceable.

This proposition is preserved as an **institutional statement**, not as an independently verified technical conclusion about every draft, external copy or downstream use. Its finite consequence is:

> If incorporated information is preserved and traceable, the Administration should be able to certify the relevant index, identifiers, creation/incorporation dates, signatories, notification events, testimony-generation records, recipients, delivery events, corrections and replacements within the lawful system perimeter.

### 5.4 Requests 4–8

The decision says these can be pursued within Concurso 36/2012 by a party or person with a legitimate and direct interest under Article 234 LOPJ. It does not determine that the requested records do not exist or that the chains are regular. It creates a written institutional representation that a production route exists.

### 5.5 External use

The decision says examination of notarial, Registry, corporate, financing/investment and public-incentive use lies outside the Government File and even outside the concurso's own scope. The controlled response is to sever that work institution by institution while preserving the shared factual dependency.

### 5.6 CGPJ DI 169/2026

The decision characterises the Promotor's communication to the TSJ as being for information only, but states that the Government Secretariat had no recorded receipt as of the date of resolution.

Controlled formulation:

`CGPJ communication ordered → TSJ Government Secretariat reports no recorded receipt → sending and receiving metadata require reconciliation.`
