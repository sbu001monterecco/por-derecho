## Legal-source control

The Law 2/2023 section was checked against current official sources on 16 August 2026. Public drafting preserves these limits:
- internal reporting is preferred only where it can be handled effectively and the informant considers there is no retaliation risk;
- an informant can go directly to an external channel;
- anonymous reporting is permitted in the statutory channels described;
- retaliation, threats and attempted retaliation are prohibited when the statutory regime applies;
- the law does not provide blanket criminal immunity and does not legalise criminal acquisition/access to information;
- exact external-channel competence may depend on territorial scope;
- where facts may constitute crime, reporting to police/prosecutorial/judicial authorities remains available.

## Publication posture

This is a public invitation to lawful reporting and evidence preservation, not an allegation that employees are collectively responsible. It invites exculpatory or corrective information as well as corroboration. The page expressly prohibits unlawful system access, theft of devices/documents, unlawful database copying, fabrication, story-coordination, evidence destruction or metadata alteration.
