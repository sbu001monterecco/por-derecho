**Date:** 16 August 2026

## Purpose

Publish a separate worker/informant letter addressed to current and former staff of Construcciones Acosta Matos and companies described collectively, for convenience only, as Grupo/Acosta Matos Group. The page encourages persons with direct work-context knowledge to preserve lawfully held evidence and use lawful internal, external, police, prosecutorial or judicial reporting routes.

## Evidence anchor used publicly

The page uses the 6 June 2019 digital/customer incident as a controlled example:

1. A former Sun Park customer received an email from `SUN PARK <sunpark264@gmail.com>` asking him to remove belongings because the complex would shortly be reformed.
2. The customer forwarded the message to the official Sun Park channel and reported that email addresses had been written on luggage after an instruction to do so.
3. A preserved Google account-recovery screen for `sunpark264@gmail.com` shows `rosa@acostamatos.com` as the recovery email.

Primary/document-route references include `Customer email ref SUN PARK email from CAM - 06JUN2019.pdf`, `Sign in – Google accounts - 6JUN2019.pdf`, and the Control 21 consolidated filing/annex route.

## Mandatory attribution boundary

The recovery screen does **not** prove:
- who created `sunpark264@gmail.com`;
- who controlled it at every relevant time;
- who sent the customer email;
- that the human behind `rosa@acostamatos.com` committed any offence.

Accordingly the public letter identifies the addressee only as **Rosa / the person identified through the corporate address `rosa@acostamatos.com`**, and expressly says that authorship, account creation/control and personal liability are not attributed on the basis of the screen. No full civil name is asserted because the controlled record has not independently verified one.

## Legal-source control

The Law 2/2023 section was checked against current official sources on 16 August 2026:
- BOE, Ley 2/2023, especially arts. 3, 4, 7, 10, 16–24, 35–38.
- Autoridad Independiente de Protección del Informante (AIPI), current external-channel guidance.
- Gobierno de Canarias information on internal/external reporting routes.

Public drafting preserves these limits:
- internal reporting is preferred only where it can be handled effectively and the informant considers there is no retaliation risk;
- an informant can go directly to an external channel;
- anonymous reporting is permitted in the statutory channels described;
- retaliation, threats and attempted retaliation are prohibited when the statutory regime applies;
- the law does not provide blanket criminal immunity and does not legalise criminal acquisition/access to information;
- exact external-channel competence may depend on territorial scope;
- where facts may constitute crime, reporting to police/prosecutorial/judicial authorities remains available, and Law 2/2023 itself provides for referral to the Ministerio Fiscal where appropriate.

## Corporate-channel statement

No specific current public Acosta Matos internal-reporting URL was verified for this publication. The page therefore does not invent or endorse one. It states the general art. 10 rule (including the 50+ worker threshold) and asks any legally obliged Acosta Matos entity to identify its Internal Information System and responsible person.

## Publication posture

This is a public invitation to lawful reporting and evidence preservation, not an allegation that employees are collectively responsible. It also invites exculpatory or corrective information. The page expressly prohibits unlawful system access, theft of devices/documents, unlawful database copying, fabrication, story-coordination, evidence destruction or metadata alteration.
