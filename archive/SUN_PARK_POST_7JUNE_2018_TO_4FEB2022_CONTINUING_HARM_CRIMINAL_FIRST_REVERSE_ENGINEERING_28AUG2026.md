### 5.3 Why not simply place every cost on CAM?

**Strongest lawful explanation:** common elements and legally necessary baseline works ordinarily burden owners under valid Community rules and coefficients; the project sponsor may lawfully volunteer only for upgrades beyond that baseline. A valid majority owner may also receive incidental value from common works without that making the allocation criminal.

**Strongest adverse hypothesis:** actors already developing a four-star project classified the enabling base works as Community expense, used inherited debt/vote machinery to approve the burden through a 20.993% attendance perimeter, imposed most of it on LPB and a share on Matkator while those owners allegedly lacked practical use/income, and then used the resulting project/licence/operation resolutions immediately before title consolidation. If done with known lack of authority, false debt, materially false cost classification or intent to shift project cost and appropriate value, the meeting could be a new act rather than a passive consequence of 2018.

**What decides between them:** constitutive title/statutes; notice and agenda; complete owners/title/coefficient list; debt ledger; vote qualification; proxies and abstentions; independent quantity survey; separation of necessary common work from private-unit/four-star work; tendering; contractor margin and related-party disclosure; payment agreements; invoices/bank trail; title and use of every affected unit; and later HNT/MYND accounting.

### 5.7 What can, what does and what could establish deliberate exclusion

| Evidentiary question | Controlled answer |
|---|---|
| **What can prove the act of exclusion?** | The native convocatoria and drafts; the source owner/address database; recipient/distribution list; certified-mail, burofax, email-server or platform logs; returned items; noticeboard photograph and signed diligence; messages or instructions changing/omitting recipients; full attendee/proxy/vote package; and ACTA distribution/receipt records. A complete custody search must precede any inference from missing records. |
| **What does the present record already support?** | A serious inference requiring production: low attendance; no LPB/Matkator representative among the three visible company rows; a sole CAM project; approval of debt, a large owner levy, licence action and all-unit unitary operation; attendee unanimity; Gil's direct account of no pre-meeting knowledge and indirect Thompson forwarding afterward; the seventeen-day proximity to deed 457; and an earlier record of contested Community debt/vote/access mechanisms. Each fact is individually equivocal, but their convergence makes intent a legitimate investigative question. |
| **What could establish deliberate purpose rather than error?** | The strongest package would combine: a known correct LPB/Matkator recipient/address; selective service to aligned owners; no service or a knowingly false service recital for the affected owner; contemporaneous instruction to keep that owner out or avoid dissent/challenge; selective or absent direct ACTA communication; authenticated Thompson forwarding and upstream distribution; rapid use of the resolutions; and actor-specific admissions, metadata or communications. A comparable pattern across earlier meetings can corroborate purpose if the same custodians, addresses and methods are proved. |
| **What establishes causation and patrimonial significance?** | Proof that valid notice would have enabled an entitled owner/representative to attend, deliberate, vote or object; that ACTA communication would have enabled timely dissent/challenge; that the resolutions were then used for debt collection, licences, unitary operation, title, finance, works or benefit; and that a defined loss or lost remedy followed. |
| **What does not suffice alone?** | The 20.993% figure, surname/family relation, board office, attendance, the image, chronology, a missing file before a complete search, non-receipt by a person who was not the legal recipient, or later benefit. These can orient production but do not independently prove intent or guilt. |

“Will prove” cannot honestly mean a guaranteed outcome before production and adversarial testing. It means a **convergent evidentiary package capable of supporting the inference beyond an innocent notice failure**, while preserving contrary evidence: an alternative valid recipient, correct service, an authorised proxy, lawful second call, later ACTA communication, independent advice, mistake, record-system failure or lack of actor knowledge.

If deliberate non-notice and concealment until after the meeting are proved, the significance is not simply procedural. ## 7. Additional criminal significance — the correct taxonomy

### A. Continuing effects of an earlier act

Loss of access, income, evidence or rescue capacity may continue after the initial event. That is relevant to harm, causation, restitution and limitation analysis, but it does not automatically make the original offence continuing or permanent.

### B. New substantive acts

Post-event lock changes, exclusion, works, use of units, new debt certifications, false documents, procedural submissions, voting resolutions, dispositions, project/finance representations or revenue retention may each be a new offence if their own elements are proved.

### C. Participation by later adoption

A later actor does not inherit guilt. Participation may arise only where evidence proves that the actor knew the material facts and intentionally induced, cooperated in, ratified or independently used the unlawful mechanism in a legally sufficient way under the historical text.

### D. Omission

For the insolvency administrator or another gatekeeper, omission requires a concrete legal duty, actual knowledge, practical capacity, causal equivalence and the offence's mental element. For the magistrate, the judicial-offence framework requires the exact resolution or required act and its historically applicable elements; an adverse ruling is not enough.

### E. Corporate benefit and succession

CP 31 bis requires an eligible predicate offence, a qualifying natural person/channel, corporate benefit and the relevant control/supervision failure. CAM→HNT continuity is relevant to asset/benefit/liability tracing and CP 130.2 questions, but does not transfer natural-person guilt or create retroactive liability.

### 8.2 The prosecution-grade multiple-financial-lives test

Blended finance is not inherently unlawful. A project may lawfully combine owner contributions, private capital, RIC investment, regional incentives, FEDER and operating revenue. The criminal or recovery significance begins only where the evidence proves one or more of the following, with historical law and thresholds applied:

1. **False availability/title/authority:** a person knowingly represented LPB, Matkator or third-party units, common areas, exploitation rights or project control as available when they were not.
2. **Same cost claimed twice:** the same invoice, quantity, work, contractor payment or depreciation base was charged to owners and also declared as financed/eligible without permitted disclosure and adjustment.
3. **Same employment output reused:** the same jobs/FTE/period were counted under incompatible commitments or were materially misstated.
4. **Other aid/finance concealed:** a required declaration omitted the Community levy, RICPE/RIC finance, another subsidy, related-party support or revenue.
5. **Insolvency/extraconcursal perimeter concealed:** gatekeepers were not told that 190 LPB and 18 third-party properties remained outside CAM's then title or that project implementation affected LPB assets formally subject to insolvency-administration and judicial-supervision safeguards, and separate extraconcursal rights. The existence of formal safeguards is not a guarantee that they worked in practice.
6. **Related-party or conflict controls bypassed:** an actor with knowledge and stop/approval power progressed the project without a lawful waiver, disclosure or abstention.
7. **False certification or use:** a specific application, certificate, invoice, progress report, job record, title schedule or judicial/Community document was objectively false, knowingly authored or used, and caused the relevant patrimonial/public-funds decision.
8. **Retention and benefit:** the receiving entity or person obtained a quantifiable benefit traceable to the proved act, with repayments, legitimate own investment, third-party protections and no-double-recovery deductions applied.

Possible public/EU subsidy fraud, ordinary or procedural fraud, disposition without authority, disloyal administration, insolvency/frustration, documentary falsity/use and corporate-liability routes remain **candidate legal characterisations**. None can be assigned from the coexistence of funding layers, the EUR 2,989.54 discrepancy, board office, family relationship or arithmetic aggregation.
