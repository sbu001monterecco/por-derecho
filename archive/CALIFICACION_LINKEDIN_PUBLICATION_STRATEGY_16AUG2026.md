## 2. Different jobs for LinkedIn and the website

### LinkedIn / public article

**ACCUSATION FIRST.**

The article should normally lead with the accusation because its communicative purpose is to state clearly what Gil alleges and why he is asking for institutional/documentary examination.

Recommended structure:

`ACCUSATION → DEFINE IT PRECISELY → 2–3 PROOF WINDOWS → KNOWLEDGE/CONTRADICTION TEST → STRONGEST DEFENCE → WHAT REMAINS → PRODUCE THE DOCUMENTARY BRIDGE → CORRECTION/RIGHT OF REPLY`

The article should not attempt to reproduce the entire Calificación dossier. It should use only the strongest examples necessary to make the accusation intelligible and document-led.

### Website Calificación page

**EVIDENCE / STATUS FIRST; ACCUSATION AS THE CONCLUSION OF THE CHAIN.**

The website is the verification destination. A sceptical judge, Fiscal, insolvency lawyer, journalist or institutional reader should be able to test the accusation without first having to accept it.

Preferred order:

1. materially adverse Sentencia 163/2023 + appeal/current status;
2. what the judgment retained / rejected / narrowed;
3. documentary counter-record / positive agency;
4. serial allegation audit;
5. evidence-before-actor / knowledge matrix;
6. strongest defence / what remains after accepting it;
7. Gil Marer's accusation, including prevaricación allegation;
8. correction / right-of-reply / what evidence would change the site's view.

This does **not** weaken the accusation. It increases rebuttal resistance by making the reader encounter the proof architecture first.

## 3. The decisive evidential grammar

For every high-risk judicial accusation, future threads should work proposition by proposition:

`JUDICIAL FINDING → CONTRARY PRIMARY EVIDENCE → DATE → TRANSMISSION TO COURT → ACTOR-SPECIFIC KNOWLEDGE GRADE → TREATMENT / OMISSION → CONSEQUENCE → STRONGEST REASONABLE EXPLANATION → WHAT REMAINS AFTER ACCEPTING THAT EXPLANATION`

Then, only where supported:

`INFERENCE → GIL MARER'S ALLEGATION`

Do not convert institutional file presence into personal reading or knowledge.

## 4. The strongest pressure mechanism

The preferred pressure is **maximum documentary pressure**, not more adjectives.

Recurring challenge:

> **Produce the documentary bridge.**

That means asking for, where material:

- the filing;
- the receipt/service record;
- the debt calculation/certificate;
- the order/providencia;
- the hearing record;
- what evidence was before the actor;
- what the judgment did with it;
- what failed and why;
- the current appeal/remedy status.

The aim is to force the debate away from reputation, deference or competing narratives and onto finite verifiable propositions.

## 6. Why `rescue package` is strategically wrong

The term can imply that LPB/Sun Park was intrinsically commercially failed and simply needed saving.

Gil's position is different: the extraordinary commercial structure became necessary in the context of private-actor interference, Community/governance dysfunction, concursal administration/protection choices and judicial handling that allegedly impaired ordinary operation/refinancing/exit.

That causation remains a party allegation / evidence-based causation hypothesis requiring actor-specific proof. It must not be converted into an adjudicated fact.

## 7. Multi-lender / staged-security publication rule

For LinkedIn and the public website:

- it is normally unnecessary to identify one or more lenders unless their identity is materially relevant;

## 9. Counterevidence must remain visible

Do not produce an `everything was always against us` narrative.

Mandatory counterevidence includes, where relevant:

- the Judge's reported 13-Jun-2018 openness to exploring the conclusion route;
- the 26-Jun-2018 suspension/protective decision;
- Sentencia 163/2023 rejected or narrowed significant AC/Fiscal allegations;
- June financing had material conditions;
- not every court-file document is proved personally read by the Judge;
- adverse decision or error alone does not establish prevaricación.

Using counterevidence increases credibility and narrows the accusation to the propositions that actually survive scrutiny.

## 10. What not to claim without separate proof

- shared criminal intent among Judge/AC/Fiscal/private actors;
- personal judicial knowledge merely because a document existed institutionally.

Those propositions require their own evidence chains.

## 12. Publication pressure test

For a proposed LinkedIn article, score effective documentary pressure rather than rhetorical aggression.

High pressure comes from:

- naming the responsible public actor in relation to sourced acts;
- stating the accusation plainly as an allegation;
- identifying the exact judgment/proceeding;
- showing actor-specific contrary knowledge;
- selecting 2–3 difficult-to-reconcile examples;
- asking finite questions;
- inviting documentary correction;
- linking to a source-rich dossier.

Pressure usually falls—not rises—when unsupported motive, insult, speculation or unproved collusion is added.
