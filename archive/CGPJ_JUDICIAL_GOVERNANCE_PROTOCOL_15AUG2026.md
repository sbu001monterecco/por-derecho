## Core public question

- appeals and judicial remedies;
- disciplinary review of judges/magistrates;
- inspection of court functioning;
- court-office / LAJ functions;
- criminal investigation.

Explain the roles, based on current official sources, of the Promotor de la Acción Disciplinaria, Comisión Permanente, Servicio de Inspección, Comisión Disciplinaria and Pleno. Before naming current office-holders or commission members, verify them from current official CGPJ/BOE sources.

## Finite-verification standard
For each disputed issue ask: which official record could confirm, reject or materially narrow it? Prefer requests for identifiable records, logs, recordings, attendance data, procedural entries, notifications, judicial acts and institutional reports over requests for conclusions about motive.

## Competence allocation
Classify every issue as: judicial discipline; court-functioning/inspection; appeal/legal merits; LAJ/office; criminal; or outside CGPJ remit. Where outside remit, identify the correct body.

## Property-right explanation
Explain why judicial governance matters in practical terms: judicial decisions can affect ownership, possession, liquidation, enforcement, creditor priority, company survival and access to remedies. Judicial independence and lawful accountability are complementary safeguards.
