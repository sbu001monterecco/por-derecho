## 1. Controlling attributed position

The current Por Derecho position is:

> **Gil Marer and Aweswell allege one continuing economic-criminal enterprise,
> advanced through successive adoption and divided functions across changing
> actors, capacities, instruments and institutional stages.**

In Spanish:

> **Gil Marer y Aweswell alegan una sola empresa continuada de criminalidad
> económica, desarrollada mediante adopción sucesiva y división de funciones
> entre actores, capacidades, instrumentos y fases institucionales cambiantes.**

This allegation requires one forward and backward reconstruction of the alleged
authority–asset–control–value chain. It rejects analysis that treats potentially
dependent Community, contractual, concursal, creditor, possession, accounting,
title, finance, funding and operation events as unrelated merely because they
arose in different legal or procedural files.

The allegation does **not** merge legal persons, proceedings, properties,
professional roles or mental states. Every person's alleged adoption,
participation, act or omission, knowledge, intent, causal contribution, benefit
and legal responsibility must be established separately.

## 2. Legal and evidential boundary

`ALG-ENT-018` is a **PARTY ALLEGATION / PROSECUTORIAL-INVESTIGATIVE MODEL**. It is
not a judgment that any offence, criminal enterprise, common pact, criminal
organisation, criminal group or individual guilt has been established.

The factual/prosecutorial expression `one continuing economic-criminal
enterprise` is distinct from:

- CP 570 bis `organización criminal`;
- CP 570 ter `grupo criminal`;
- CP 74 `delito continuado`;
- a continuing or permanent offence;
- an original agreement among all later actors;
- participation under CP 27–29;
- corporate liability under CP 31 bis;
- commission by omission under CP 11;
- consummation of a particular offence; and
- the participation or limitation period for a particular person or act.

None of those follows from the label. Each must be tested under the law in force
when the alleged act occurred and with the exact offence elements.

`ALG-ORG-011`, the historic autonomous allegation that the perimeter itself
constituted a statutory criminal organisation, remains retired as an autonomous
theory. This addendum does not reactivate it. CP 570 bis and 570 ter remain
alternative element-specific questions if later evidence warrants them.

## 8. Burden and conclusion standard

For this Project's editorial record, **Por Derecho bears the evidential burden**
of substantiating every asserted link and offence element. In a criminal
proceeding, the accusing party or parties retain the burden of proving guilt. No
person bears a burden to disprove `ALG-ENT-018`, prove innocence, explain an
apparent gap or produce an exculpatory record.

Internal labels such as `SUPPORTED`, `STRONG` or `CORROBORATED` do not establish
criminal liability. Every report must distinguish the threshold for preservation,
production, investigation, accusation/charging, opening of trial and conviction.
The presumption of innocence, `in dubio pro reo` and proof beyond reasonable
doubt remain controlling at the guilt stage.

The unitary analysis may show one connected factual and economic sequence while
still producing different legal results for different actors, dates and alleged
offences. Non-fragmentation is a method for testing dependencies; it is not a
shortcut around individual proof.
