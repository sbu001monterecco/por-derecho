# INSTITUTIONAL-READER UNITARY PROCEEDINGS RULE

**Date:** 30 August 2026  
**Status:** controlling additive public-narrative / analytical / continuity rule  
**Applies with:** Proceedings Master Register, Proceedings Interconnectivity Map, anti-fragmentation/convergence rule, criminal-evidence map, chronology, evidence, asset and counsel/procurador controls

## 1. Purpose

Por Derecho must explain its proceedings corpus in a form that a conscientious institutional reader can test without first accepting the project's conclusions. The intended reader may be a judge, magistrate, prosecutor, judicial inspector, CGPJ member, LAJ, regulator, investigator or experienced lawyer. Public prose must speak to that reader's professional responsibility and evidential method without artificial salutations, flattery, threats or pressure for a predetermined outcome.

The website is not a substitute for a filing, appeal, complaint, personación or lawful procedural communication. It is a public evidential/navigation record. Formal relevance to a live proceeding must be advanced through the lawful procedural channel for that proceeding.

The governing invitation is narrower and stronger than an accusation:

> **Can a decision in one legally separate file be complete if a materially relevant part of the same asset, credit, insolvency administration, evidence, control history or alleged-harm chain sits in another procedural silo?**

## 2. Institutional completeness test

For every material proceeding, decision, referral or response, test all of the following:

1. What exact material was before this court, chamber, office or authority?
2. What materially connected proceeding, event, asset, credit, evidence family or institutional response existed elsewhere at the relevant time?
3. Was that connection formally communicated, procedurally cognisable, referred, preserved or otherwise available to be considered?
4. If the file is read alone, what material part of the wider asset, causation, control, evidence or alleged-harm story disappears?
5. If another file appears to proceed from a materially inconsistent factual premise, has that contradiction been identified and reconciled?
6. What is documented fact, what is an authority statement, what is party allegation, what is analytical inference, what is contrary evidence and what remains unresolved?
7. Is there a documented unitary institutional acknowledgement of the relevant cumulative phenomenon, or only separate responses? If none is located, state only **NOT LOCATED IN THE CONTROLLED CORPUS**, never **DID NOT EXIST**.

A legally correct view of one procedural file can still be materially incomplete if evidence necessary to understand the file is located in another procedural silo. Conversely, contextual overlap never proves legal joinder, coordination, intent, wrongdoing or liability.

## 3. The current Audiencia Provincial convergence — exact references

The public record must identify the current Audiencia Provincial de Las Palmas convergence accurately. It is not three arbitrary cases and it is not one legal appeal. It consists of three materially interdependent appellate **objects** arising from Concurso 36/2012:

1. **Calificación:** `RPL 2523/2025`.
2. **Administrador Concursal separation/removal:** `RPL 3304/2025`, with `RPL 3319/2025` recorded as accumulated/linked within that separation-removal appellate track.
3. **Administrador Concursal fees/remuneration:** `RPL 421/2026`.

Do not describe `RPL 3319/2025` as the fees appeal. That would collapse two distinct appellate objects and recreate the very fragmentation/identity problem the graph is designed to prevent.

### 3.1 Mandatory three-object question

The website must make the following point clearly but without telling the Audiencia Provincial what conclusion to reach:

> These appellate objects are procedurally distinct, but each arises from the same insolvency administration and the same Concurso 36/2012 estate. They overlap in chronology, evidence, the Administrador Concursal's role, the treatment of the productive unit/hotel and alleged patrimonial consequences. The institutional question is therefore not whether they share one roll number—they do not—but whether any one can be understood completely without recognising the material record sitting in the other two.

For the calificación appeal ask, source-first: can the narrative of culpability be evaluated without understanding the disputed administration whose removal was sought and whose remuneration is separately challenged?

For the removal/separation track ask: can the conduct said to justify separation be assessed without the same chronology, evidence and patrimonial consequences that bear upon calificación and remuneration?

For the fees/remuneration track ask: can remuneration be treated as wholly detached from the administration, work, consequences and disputed acts for which removal was sought?

These are cross-reading questions. They are not findings that one appeal controls another, not a request for procedural joinder and not proof of misconduct.

## 6. Criminal/prosecutorial-first, evidence-first

The public narrative may identify criminal/prosecutorial questions where the controlled evidence justifies doing so, including alleged fraude procesal, fraude concursal, prevaricación, administración de hecho, knowing facilitation, obstruction/suppression, asset diversion or other relevant offences. Criminal vocabulary is not a rhetorical substitute for proof.

Every material criminal proposition must retain one of the repository's evidential states, including where applicable:

- documented fact;
- court/authority statement or procedural fact;
- project allegation / party allegation;
- analytical inference or investigative hypothesis;
- contrary evidence; and
- unresolved question / source gap.

A repeated allegation across several pages does not become a fact through repetition or interlinking.

## 7. How to write to institutional readers

Prefer language that invites professional verification:

- **The question is not whether these proceedings share a docket number. They do not. The question is whether their factual and patrimonial intersections were sufficiently material that an informed decision requires recognition of the other records.**
- **Jurisdictional boundaries do not eliminate the need for institutional memory.**
- **The burden is not to pretend that separate files are one case; it is to explain their intersections accurately.**
- **The strongest safeguard against an erroneous narrative is the ability to move proposition → source → proceeding → response → consequence → related proceeding, and then backwards again.**
- **No conclusion is requested here. The request is methodological: do not decide the part without first testing whether the whole materially changes the part.**

Avoid:

- insults or institutional taunts;
- guilt by association;
- threats;
- unsupported accusations of corruption;
- treating silence/non-location as proof of suppression;
- treating an adverse decision as proof of criminal intent; and
- suggesting the public website itself is an extra-record submission to a live court.

The intended reaction is:

> **I may not accept every allegation, but I cannot responsibly understand this file without testing the materially connected record.**

## 8. Public “case prism” requirement

The Proceedings Interconnectivity Map should operate as a **case prism**:

**ONE PROCEEDING IN → WHOLE MATERIAL CONTEXT OUT.**

For a material proceeding, the public reader should be able to discover:

- why it matters;
- what came before;
- what followed;
- direct procedural relationships;
- unitary convergence relationships;
- shared assets / credit / events / evidence;
- what the authority decided or recorded;
- what remains unanswered;
- what becomes invisible if the file is read alone; and
- the source/evidence exit needed to verify the proposition.

The map must remain a navigation layer, not a merits adjudication.

## 9. Continuity / future-thread rule

Future work materially changing any of the current Audiencia appeals, Valencia ORD 1859/2023-9, Arrecife mortgage enforcement, Meeting Point/FTI, Cuatrecasas/Matkator, Ministerio Fiscal response chronology or historical possession/exploitation chain must assess:

1. standalone procedural identity;
2. canonical/master-register registration status;
3. direct procedural links;
4. unitary convergence links;
5. evidence/provenance status;
6. public institutional-reader explanation;
7. reverse discoverability; and
8. whether a known contradiction or gap remains visible.

A future thread is incomplete if it updates the isolated file while leaving a known material convergence bridge stale, one-way or missing.
