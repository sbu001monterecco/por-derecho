## Storyline proposition

The 18 June 2026 Control 22 complaint should no longer be read as a complaint beginning with the 7 June 2018 control event. The evidence now supports a longer question:

> **Did a court-supervised insolvency administration, already operating against a background of banking/conflict warnings, repeatedly exercise or fail to exercise entrusted powers in ways that preserved or strengthened the Acosta Matos beneficiary position while LPB lost information, protection, control, competition or recovery options — and what did the estate receive in return?**

The judge-specific Control 24 route asks the corresponding but legally distinct question:

> **At each point where the court knew or was formally placed in a position to supervise, what active intervention, independent verification or neutralisation occurred, and why did the resulting sequence repeatedly remain favourable in effect to the same CAM-centred perimeter?**

Neither proposition proves a crime. Both are now material master-storyline questions.
