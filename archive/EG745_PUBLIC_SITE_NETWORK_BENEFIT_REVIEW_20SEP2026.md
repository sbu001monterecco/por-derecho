## Eight findings that change the substantive response

1. **The Orion connection is broader than FMMM alone and contains a disclosed commercial transaction.** P301, physical pages 28–29, identifies Acosta Matos, Guerra and FMMM-related management interests and 2025 CAM construction billing. The older G301 single-person framing is stale. The response can request contracts, invoices, payment tracing, approval and conflict records around a named transaction. It should not state that Sun Park funds financed Orion without the connecting ledger.

2. **PwC has a dated cross-asset connection that can be investigated without imputing knowledge across a firm.** P312 identifies fiscal due diligence on CAMSA's Merlín project beginning in March 2021 and Uría Menéndez's preparation of an investment-contract model. P313 separately records Carlos Saavedra's non-director secretary role alongside Grupo Acosta Matos in a 2026 company. Neither proves Carlos personally handled Merlín, used 2016 confidential material, or received a reward. The concrete question is what mandate and conflict review existed in each legal entity.

3. **Grant Thornton's earlier client-side intake makes the later relationship inquiry specific.** G323 reports a 23 November 2020 named conflict list, 27 November clearance, 30 November NDA with Grant Thornton Advisory SLP, and 29 December proposal. A proposal is not proof work began. Its later reported distinction between employment/partnership and a commercial relationship needs entity-specific reconciliation. P305 independently confirms a current public professional association; P306 separately confirms GT's audit role for Portfolio. Shared branding does not prove shared knowledge.

4. **The procedural chain has identifiable decision points, rather than simply an allegation of institutional silence.** G316/G319 describe the 25 January 2018 termination of Cristo's LPB mandate, the 5 March withdrawal entered for LPB, and a 13 May 2024 judicial confirmation of closure. The admitted disclosure proceeding did not itself establish a right of retracto. Native instructions, authority, actual withdrawal and contemporaneous estate-interest analysis remain necessary before attributing unlawful purpose. The 15 February 2018 decision also corrected sums in LPB's favour and rejected the disputed-credit thesis.

5. **The valuation theory now has a sharper knowledge and use test, with meaningful corrections.** G320/G322 identify two allegedly hash-matched GESVALT annexes to an Aweswell LexNET receipt on 9 March 2018, preceding the 16 April plan decision. Original files are retained privately; this scan reads the public register, not those bytes. Filing is not personal judicial receipt or reading. The earlier theory that ACTÚA photos were all or predominantly negative images of blocks 4–5 is expressly weakened/refuted. The surviving inquiry concerns supplied unit data, representative sampling, calculation and dated use.

6. **Portfolio has given a substantive response that must be acknowledged.** G303 reports its 11 September 2026 confirmation that four 7 September documents were incorporated into the December 2024 file, reviewed internally and their content sent to CNMV. Date, exact transmitted package, CNMV receipt, board consideration and reasoned conclusions remain distinct. G308 is a public proposed letter, not proof it was sent. A blanket allegation of no review/no contact would overstate the record.

7. **The economic perimeter can extend beyond LPB without claiming ownership of everyone's property.** G321 distinguishes 262 hotel-related titles and a connected operating platform from the 18 units in the early possession case. G306 distinguishes the 159-unit and 31-property transaction branches. G310 makes clear that financing, grants, asset value, operating revenue and profit are different measurements. Economic interdependence supports investigation of common services, access, operation and downstream cash flows; each damaged right, owner, debtor and legally recoverable loss still requires its own identification.

8. **There is new independent contrary material and several stale formulations.** P308 contains EY's unmodified 2025 audit opinion for Orion; it must not be conflated with GT's audit of Portfolio. Earlier judicial protection, the adverse 2014 appeal, genuine but conditional rescue proposals, RICPE's archived complaint and GT's previously closed channel must remain visible. The current GT 2024_04 page was unavailable in this round, so a new reopening or current status cannot be inferred.

## Proposed Spanish module for the substantive response

Se solicita examinar la alegación de una actuación coordinada que habría convertido posiciones privadas controvertidas en control, título y rendimiento económico mediante decisiones sucesivas de representación, información, valoración, conservación, financiación y supervisión. La hipótesis se formula por actos y personas, con expresa identificación de las resoluciones y explicaciones contrarias. No se sostiene que la mera pertenencia a una firma, un consejo o una familia demuestre concierto delictivo.

El perímetro de investigación debe comprender los derechos y la explotación interdependiente del hotel, además de los activos de LPB, y los beneficios posteriores únicamente en la medida en que pueda acreditarse su conexión causal. Deben separarse ingreso bruto, beneficio neto, financiación, subvención, valor patrimonial y perjuicio indemnizable, identificando titular y evitando duplicidades.

Para comprobar la hipótesis se interesa conservar y contrastar: el expediente original de representación y desistimiento de PP 1041; la cadena de encargo, presentación y uso de las valoraciones; las decisiones sobre prueba testifical y alternativas de continuidad; los contratos y pagos vinculados específicamente identificados; los registros de conflictos y acceso a información; y el tratamiento posterior de las comunicaciones por los órganos de control. Respecto de E.G. 745/2026, se pide motivar qué hechos y documentos se examinaron, qué exclusiones de alcance se acordaron, quién es competente para cada comprobación pendiente y qué traslado trazable procede, sin presentar esa petición como una reapertura o estimación ya acordada.
