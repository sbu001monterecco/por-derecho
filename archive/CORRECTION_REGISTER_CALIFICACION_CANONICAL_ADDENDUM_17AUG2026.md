# CORRECTION REGISTER ADDENDUM — CALIFICACIÓN CANONICAL OUTCOME CONTROLS

Date: 17 August 2026  
Baseline: `main` at `2abc0ab85c8408850e8974d535013445a6c7928b`  
Status: **mandatory wording controls for every repository and public reference to Sentencia 163/2023 and appeal RPL 2523/2025**

## CR-CAL-001 — Late filing was not attributed to Gil Marer

**Incorrect or vulnerable wording:** collective language stating or implying that Gil, LPB and PINK were all punished for filing the concurso late.

**Controlling correction:** Sentencia 163/2023 held that the late-filing cause was attributable exclusively to former administrator Uri Omid. It expressly states that Gil assumed office in January 2012, made the former Art. 5 bis communication on 4 April 2012 and filed on 4 June 2012, and therefore complied with the timetable applied by the court.

**Mandatory formulation:**

> The judgment treated late filing as a culpability cause concerning LPB and attributed it personally to Uri Omid, not Gil Marer.

## CR-CAL-002 — PINK was declared accomplice only in the rent branch

**Incorrect or vulnerable wording:** saying PINK was punished for accounting, late filing, collaboration or alzamiento, or presenting PINK as accomplice to the entire AC package.

**Controlling correction:** the judgment identifies PINK as accomplice only in the general-clause finding concerning non-payment/non-collection of rent under the 6-Feb-2012 agreement.

**Mandatory formulation:**

> PINK was declared an accomplice only in the rent non-payment/non-collection branch and lost any creditor rights; the judgment did not uphold either alzamiento branch against it and did not duplicate the rent damages award in the calificación judgment.

## CR-CAL-003 — “Connivencia” has branch-specific treatment

**Incorrect or vulnerable wording A:** “The judgment proved connivencia.”  
**Incorrect or vulnerable wording B:** “The judgment rejected connivencia completely.”

**Controlling correction:**

- in the alzamiento branch, the judgment described asserted connivencia as a mere allegation without evidentiary corroboration and rejected the branch;
- in the rent/complicity branch, it inferred that PINK operated in connivencia with LPB while rent remained unpaid.

**Mandatory formulation:**

> The same term received different treatment in different branches; every reference must identify the relevant branch and must not convert one branch’s reasoning into a universal finding.

## CR-CAL-004 — Fiscal opinion contains a literal role contradiction

**Incorrect or vulnerable wording:** silently replacing the signed text with a cleaned-up accusation against the social administrator, or asserting that the wording proves deliberate misconduct.

**Controlling correction:** the first bullet literally states `situación de insolvencia agravada por dolo o culapa del administrador concursal`, while the opinion then identifies Uri Omid and Gil Marer as responsible social administrators. It may be a drafting error.

**Mandatory formulation:**

> The signed opinion contains a facial role contradiction. The verified fact is the text; its cause and intent remain unproved.

## CR-CAL-005 — “Not located” is not “does not exist”

**Incorrect or vulnerable wording:** “There is no appellate judgment,” “the appeal is definitely undecided,” or “the AP has not ruled.”

**Controlling correction:** a formal providencia fixed 4-Jun-2026 for deliberation and judgment. Finite Gmail and Drive searches through 17-Aug-2026 did not locate an appellate judgment or terminating resolution.

**Mandatory formulation:**

> No appellate judgment or terminating resolution was located in the controlled searches through 17 August 2026; this does not establish that none exists.

## CR-CAL-006 — Signing the lease was not itself the surviving culpable conduct

**Incorrect or vulnerable wording:** “The judge punished Gil for signing a low-rent lease.”

**Controlling correction:** the judgment agreed that the lease was not culpable merely because the rent did not cover the mortgage instalment. The retained conduct was failure to claim payment or terminate during the period identified by the court.

## CR-CAL-007 — Two accounting branches had different outcomes

**Incorrect or vulnerable wording A:** “The accounting case was rejected.”  
**Incorrect or vulnerable wording B:** “The court proved no accounting existed.”

**Controlling correction:**

- substantial breach of mandatory accounting-book duties was upheld;
- the separate presumption concerning formulation/audit/deposit of annual accounts was rejected;
- the record includes accounting material supplied, so the dispute was not binary accounting/no accounting.

## CR-CAL-008 — Broad receivables culpability was rejected; narrow support issue survived elsewhere

**Incorrect or vulnerable wording:** either claiming total validation of the €1.358m package or total irrelevance of every receivable document.

**Controlling correction:** the court rejected the broad general-clause theory because recovery and execution were uncertain and corrected the €737,338.85 self-debt construction. It separately used missing support for €518,908.69 and €737,338.85 under the narrowed collaboration branch.

## CR-CAL-009 — Deficit liability was requested and rejected

**Controlling correction:** the AC and Fiscalía requested deficit coverage. The judgment rejected it because the necessary specific causal/quantification argument was not supplied and the court would not construct it without violating defence rights.

## CR-CAL-010 — Calificación is not a criminal conviction

**Mandatory formulation:**

> Sentencia 163/2023 is a materially adverse civil/concursal first-instance judgment under appeal. A culpable classification and its patrimonial/disqualification effects are not, by themselves, a criminal conviction.
