## 1. Controlling proposition

This note preserves the current evidential position that the decision to use **Monterecco Sun Park, S.L. (later Pink Canary Services, S.L.)** must not be analysed as a free-standing corporate preference divorced from the conditions inherited at Sun Park.

Gil's present attributed explanation is that the originating private layer was incomplete/frustrated performance of the 2008 restructuring by a seller/minority perimeter associated, in specific acts, with Montelanza, S.L. and Molina-linked owners. Separately, divided titles, voluntary CEXP participation and unresolved seller/unit completion created structural vulnerability; the disputed 22-Jun-2011 debt/vote/authority matrix amplified the conflict; and Bankia enforcement/scheduled auction was the immediate documented 2012 trigger. The operating solution emerged within those inherited conditions, operator litigation, service/public-authority pressure and the practical need to continue operating an existing hotel.

The Project further alleges that those pressures later developed into a coordinated pattern of **mobbing / instrumentalisation** across Community governance, litigation, tourism/licence, electricity, water, public authorities, concursal administration and ultimately control of the asset.

The Project also alleges that the Administrador Concursal aided, enabled, adopted or failed to restrain important parts of that pressure architecture.

These are **PARTY ALLEGATIONS / INVESTIGATIVE HYPOTHESES** unless and until the specific coordination, act, duty, knowledge, adoption/use, benefit/consequence and causation are established actor-by-actor. This note does not convert temporal convergence into a criminal finding.

## 14. How to prove or disprove coordination

For every alleged mobbing act, complete:

`ACTOR`

→ `LEGAL / PROFESSIONAL CAPACITY`

→ `SOURCE OF POWER OR DUTY`

→ `WHAT THEY KNEW AND WHEN`

→ `COMMUNICATION / INSTRUCTION / DOCUMENT RECEIVED`

→ `ACT OR OMISSION`

→ `WHO ELSE KNEW / ADOPTED / IMPLEMENTED IT`

→ `IMMEDIATE EFFECT ON OPERATOR / OWNER / HOTEL`

→ `BENEFIT OR STRATEGIC CONSEQUENCE`

→ `DEPENDENCY ON EARLIER COMMUNITY / OWNER AUTHORITY`

→ `COUNTER-EVIDENCE / INNOCENT EXPLANATION`

→ `STATUS`.

Evidence that could materially strengthen coordination includes:

- shared representatives giving instructions across Community, litigation and utilities;
- repeated forwarding of the same disputed ACTA / certificate to different institutions;
- communications between dissident-owner representatives and AC/PwC/private actors before consequential acts;
- evidence of coordinated timing;
- instructions to utilities / authorities using a disputed capacity;
- adoption after express notice of contrary title / operator / authority documents;
- evidence that one actor funded, instructed or benefited from another track.

Evidence that could materially weaken the theory includes:

- independent regulatory action on valid grounds;
- independent utility/customer-account issues;
- valid owner termination rights;
- lawful independent Community authority;
- independent AC investigation reaching the same conclusion from primary sources;
- lack of communication / knowledge between alleged participants;
- the same operational consequence being inevitable without the disputed act.
