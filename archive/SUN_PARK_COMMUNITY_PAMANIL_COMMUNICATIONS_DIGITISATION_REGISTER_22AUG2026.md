## Criminality / legality control

Gil alleges that specified Pamanil/Pamalexsha acts may have been used as vehicles in conduct he characterizes as fraudulent or economically criminal and that the Community debt/vote machinery was unlawful. The inventory records that allegation but does not adjudicate it. Each act requires proof of actor and principal, legal duty/authority, false statement or document, knowledge, intent, reliance, causal use, benefit/harm and the strongest lawful explanation. A defective resolution, missing account or failure of proof does not automatically establish a crime.
