## 1. The revised thesis

Track A and Track B are not simply parallel eviction cases, and their connection does not depend on finding an express citation of AP 89/2014 in a later AC pleading. They form parts of a wider, time-ordered network connecting the same hotel operating system through different owners, contracts, Community organs, possession narratives, financial burdens, court records and later beneficiaries.

The revised thesis is **multipath connection with episodic reciprocal use**. A private demand, Community certificate, access instruction, AC report, fiscal position or judicial result can become an input in another proceeding. The resulting position can subsequently return as a defence, a credibility argument, an access justification, or a commercial/reputational advantage. The actors and legal capacities remain distinct; neither shared interests nor a dense graph proves shared criminal intent.

**Gil Marer's allegation** is that actors within the historical Montelanza/Molina and later Acosta Matos perimeters, together with identified acts or omissions of the AC and judicial actors, knowingly instrumentalised those connections to displace the operating side and consolidate or defend control and value. This report tests that allegation rather than treating it as an established criminal plan.

Three conclusions must be reported independently:

1. **Connection:** what actually links the two events, actors, assets or records?
2. **Effect:** what legal, operational, economic or reputational consequence did the link cause?
3. **Criminal significance:** what offence-specific conduct, unlawfulness, knowledge, mental element and causation are proved?

A link can be well documented while its criminal significance remains unresolved. Conversely, an unresolved citation does not erase separately documented governance, title, access or result-reuse links.

## 4. Twelve distinct connection mechanisms

### 4.1 Same operating asset, different rights

Both routes affect the same economically interdependent hotel, but not necessarily the same units, ownership interests, mandate, remedy or period. Shared operating dependence explains why exclusion from a bounded subset of units can matter to the wider business without establishing whole-hotel legal identity. [S1-S2]

### 4.2 Historical representation and Community governance

The Track A reconstruction places Alvaro Campanario in representation of much of the future claimant perimeter and in the 2011 meeting/procedural-strategy history. GitLab separately maps later Prieto, Lopez Noriega, Cogolludo and administration-company roles. These are dated relationship links. ### 4.3 Debt, voting and organ control

A Community debt figure can serve several functions: accounting entry, demand, voting exclusion, certification, concursal claim or bid burden. Those are different legal functions and must be tracked separately. The factual questions are who supplied each figure, its bank/invoice basis, challenges or consignation, the applicable voting denominator, and its actual later use. The feedback hypothesis is debt classification influencing organ decisions that then generate further debts, certificates or access measures. It must be proved step by step. [S1-S2]

### 4.4 Operating-cost and rent causation

The LPB/Pink contract is not just a EUR24,575 monthly rent: the reviewed control also records EUR33,915.29 of LPB Community charges assumed by the operator, before additional burdens. The 2013 accountant memorandum proposed intercompany offsets using supplied information; it is not proof every amount was paid or legally set off. Removing operating inventory while shared costs persist is a plausible cash-flow mechanism, not a completed damages calculation. A monthly model must test occupancy, available units, pricing, management, market conditions, expenses and payments against lawful alternatives. [S2]

### 4.5 Keys, possession and the scope of authority

## 5. Five return paths, with explicit limits

### Loop I — governance and operating pressure

Historical owners/representatives -> Community debt and voting treatment -> Community decisions -> operating/access/cost effects -> later rent or insolvency arguments.

Parts of this chain are documented in the controls; the full economic causal path remains to be quantified. The return aspect is that decisions made through an organ can later reinforce the organ's asserted authority or debt claims. No universal false-debt conclusion is made. [S1-S2]

### Loop II — access authority and later evidential support

Private access request -> AC authorisation -> Community/security action -> dispute and AC testimony -> appellate assessment of the complained-of conduct.

The chain explains how an AC act can become useful both operationally and evidentially to other parties. It does not establish that later judicial reliance was fraudulent or that the maintenance explanation was false. [S1-S2]

### Loop III — calificacion returns against the counter-complaint

AC calificacion case -> Ministerio Fiscal adverse position -> DI248 archive of the complaint against AC/CAM.

This is an especially important **documented institutional return path**. The recovered decree's second substantive page expressly states that the Ministry had already supported culpable classification and responsibility of the former administrator, now complainant. It separately invokes Pink/Patricia's adverse status when considering the commission allegation. [S5, physical page3]

The archive also gives other reasons: generic allegations, available concursal/civil remedies, court supervision and weaknesses in the commission email. It is therefore inaccurate to say calificacion was its sole basis. Neither the subsequent partial rejection of calificacion allegations nor the decree's facial date error proves knowing misconduct. The October2019 corrective order cannot be backdated into a prosecutor's May2019 knowledge. [S3, S5]

### Loop IV — judicial outcome returns as the AC's defence

Pink dispute/enforcement -> selected calificacion findings and monetary award -> subsequent AC removal defence.

The September control shows why tracing only repeated vocabulary misses a connection: a party can use the judgment result without naming every underlying source dispute. This is a result-dependency link, not proof that the result was created for that later purpose. [S4]

### Loop V — judicial outcome affects external scrutiny

Judicial material -> third-party transmission -> newsroom receipt -> refusal to publish.

The last two steps are directly supported by the native newsroom message. The identity, instructions and knowledge of the transmitting party remain a separate open edge. The Project's wider CAM/Laura attribution must not replace a missing delivery record. A publisher may lawfully reassess a story after receiving an adverse judgment. [S6]

## 6. The judge appears in two different models

A **judicial-product model** asks how an order or judgment was used by others. It can be proved without showing the judge knew of any later use.

A **judicial-conduct model** asks whether a particular judge knowingly issued an unjust resolution, or committed another specifically defined offence. It requires different evidence. Under CP250.1.7 the deception route includes error induced in a court; under CP446 the focus is a knowingly unjust judicial decision. These theories must not be carelessly merged concerning the same proposition and decision.

The concursal judge also appears through countervailing decisions. The 24 October 2019 non-convalidation order and the rejection/narrowing of important calificacion allegations must remain visible. They defeat a simplistic claim of uniform ratification, while not resolving every allegation about other acts. [S3, S9]

## 7. New discriminating insights

**Do not let the citation question monopolise the investigation.** AP89-to-AC express reliance is one open link. Meeting overlap, specific title transfers, receipt of notices, access authorisations, fiscal reliance and result reuse are separate links that can survive even if that citation is never found.

**Track what changes while the source travels.** Compare origin and later wording for lost qualifications: 18 units becoming the whole hotel; possession becoming ownership; an allegation becoming a finding; an appealed partial judgment becoming universal vindication; no new bookings becoming no maintenance or no activity. A change can be lawful shorthand, error or deliberate distortion; only the exact comparison and knowledge evidence discriminate.

**Track corrections as actively as allegations.** Who received the 2019 non-convalidation or the 2023 rejection of particular accusations? Did a later statement accurately preserve those limits? A documented later use after receipt of a decisive correction is more informative than merely counting repeated adverse decisions.

**Do not multiply evidence by copying it.** One root claim repeated in an AC report, fiscal opinion, judgment summary and website is not four independent confirmations. Record root source, transformation, adopter and new corroboration separately.

**Power can be exercised through control of practical access before final title.** Test the period of actual admission/exclusion, supplies, bookings, maintenance and works separately from deed/registration dates. Conversely, an owner's lawful safety access does not itself establish operation of the hotel.

**Timing is a lead, not an answer.** AP89 on13January2014 and the recorded AC claim on17January are close. Retrieve drafting history, service, instructions and annexes before inferring that the judgment caused the filing. A claim drafted earlier would weaken that causal inference.

**Intermittence is a feature of the model, not missing proof to fill with guesswork.** A person may participate in one episode, disagree in another and later use an existing result. The graph must allow episodic coalitions, independent decisions and contrary acts; it must not draw continuous cooperation across silent years.

**Successive adoption is not retroactive authorship.** A later actor can knowingly use an earlier wrong without having planned the original act; proving later knowledge is still necessary. This is analytically distinct from alleging one original pact involving everyone.

## 8. Legal corrections and limits

The [Penal Code](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1995-25444) must be applied in the version appropriate to each act, with favourable-law rules where applicable. Its current consolidation is not permission to project post2015 patrimonial offences backward.

- CP250.1.7 and446 separate induced judicial error from knowing judicial injustice. Neither judicial reuse nor an adverse result alone proves an offence. Personal enrichment is not a necessary element of every offence being examined.
- CP11,28 and29 require individualised analysis of duties, contributions and participation; a duty to act alone does not prove criminal omission.
- CP423 expressly extends the relevant bribery provisions to insolvency administrators. That warrants retaining a properly evidenced benefit/inducement inquiry if a concrete source emerges; it is not evidence that any bribe occurred, nor a blanket classification of every AC act as a public official's act.
- CP259.3 includes an imprudent modality. Correct the earlier blanket 'intent' shorthand: the mental element must match the particular offence, statutory version and accused person's legally relevant role.
- CP570bis/ter require their own structure and criminal-purpose elements. A prior conviction for a predicate crime is not an automatic prerequisite to investigation. Nor does an ordinary corporate/professional network satisfy these offences.

Under [LPH15.2](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1960-10906), voting exclusion has conditions and excludes both the affected person and coefficient from majority computation. Consequently, a tiny eligible percentage is an important reconstruction target, not self-proving illegality. Test lawful debt, maturity, challenge/consignation, notices, actual ownership/proxies and the item-specific majority. Security decisions do not themselves create private title or tourist operating authority.

[LEC222, historical text relevant to Track A](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2000-323&p=20130629&tn=1) requires careful treatment of finality, object, parties and legally extended effects. A civil judgment's bounded effect is not a universal criminal exoneration; equally, a criminal hypothesis does not erase an adverse civil holding. LEC460 permits specified later evidence on appeal, so the mere May2012 timing is not procedural wrongdoing.

[LECrim17](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1882-6036&p=20250103&tn=1) supplies a procedural framework for connected offences, including facilitating or impunity-related relationships, subject to its conditions. One analytical evidence model does not automatically require one court, one joined proceeding or displacement of special jurisdiction. Preservation, competence, limitation and the precise effect of previous archives require counsel's event-specific review; later reuse does not automatically restart an old limitation period.

## 9. Visual specification: show events and transfers, not a guilt network

The preferred visual is a **time-ordered relationship explorer**, with a compact transfer matrix and separate episode cards. Avoid a permanent double-arrow triangle joining private actors, AC and judge: it falsely suggests continuous, symmetrical cooperation and shared knowledge.

Use separate lanes for historical private owners/representatives, Community administration, later CAM/Acosta Matos actors, AC, each court/judge, each fiscal act, and external recipients. Keep affected entities and assets separately selectable. Every visible connection names what travelled: document, authority, possession assertion, money calculation, title, or judicial result.

Use three visible evidence states: primary re-read; source-controlled prior analysis; open inference. Show countervailing decisions with a separate counter-edge marker, not as low-strength positive links. Line thickness must never encode guilt. Source counts must not grow when the same original is mirrored.

Select an episode to reveal: origin/date; receiving actor/date; legal capacity; exact proposition or action; source locator; observed effect; unresolved causal steps; offence-specific questions; lawful alternative; contrary evidence; next discriminating document.

The principal episode views are: (a)2011-2014 owner/Community/possessory route; (b)2016-2018 notice/access/authority; (c)2019 calificacion-to-counter-complaint return; (d)2019 and2023 corrections; (e)2023-2026 monetary, defence and external reuse; (f)later title/corporate-economic destination.

A returned link always goes to a **later event**. The time-expanded graph remains chronological; no future event causes an earlier one. Filters must allow the reader to hide hypotheses and see only supported connections, then reintroduce the open questions without losing their status.
