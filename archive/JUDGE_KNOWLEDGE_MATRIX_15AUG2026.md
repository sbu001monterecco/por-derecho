## Control rule
Silence in an order is not proof the court lacked knowledge. Presence of a document in the docket is not proof the judge personally adopted every proposition in it. A procedural, utility or timing ruling is not automatically a merits determination of the underlying allegation. The certified electronic index and complete orders prevail over party summaries.
