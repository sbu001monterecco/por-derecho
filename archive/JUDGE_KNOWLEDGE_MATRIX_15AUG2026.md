## Control rule

Silence in an order is not proof the court lacked knowledge. Presence of a document in the docket is not proof the judge personally adopted every proposition in it. A same-day professional report is strong contemporaneous evidence but not a judicial transcript. A draft is not a filing. Informal notice is not formal incorporation. The certified electronic index, complete orders, hearing record and service logs prevail over party summaries.
