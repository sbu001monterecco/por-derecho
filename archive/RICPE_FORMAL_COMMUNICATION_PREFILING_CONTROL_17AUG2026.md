## 3. Status vocabulary — do not collapse stages

Use the following stages separately:

`PREPARED → DIGITALLY SIGNED → FILED/SUBMITTED → RECEIVED/ACKNOWLEDGED → ADMITTED OR REJECTED → EXAMINED/INVESTIGATED → DECIDED → REMEDY/FOLLOW-UP`

Controlled rules:

- `prepared` does not mean signed;
- `signed` does not mean filed;
- `filed` requires a receipt, channel certificate, native sent email or equivalent transmission record;
- `received` does not mean admitted, examined or accepted on the merits;
- `admitted` does not mean the facts were verified;
- `shared with CNMV or another authority` does not mean RICPE referred it, that the authority accepted competence, opened an investigation or adopted the allegations;
- an institutional reference proves the procedural event stated by that reference, not every underlying proposition in the communication.
