## Initial parallel criminal / forensic result

### Civil / mercantile / concursal / administrative result

The project already contains sufficient controlled material to justify a unitary dependency architecture: ownership, Community authority, debt, Concurso, later title, funding and public-authority treatment are analytically connected factual questions even though the legal consequences remain proceeding-specific.

### Criminal / investigative / forensic result

The architecture is justified as an investigative method, but this implementation pass does **not** establish a criminal offence or collective scheme. It identifies the correct offence-neutral sequence to test:

`objective fact → actor → contemporaneous knowledge → representation/document use → recipient/reliance → benefit/harm → causation → contrary/exculpatory evidence`.

The highest-value criminal/forensic evidence remains the same keystone primary material that matters to the civil/authority analysis, especially the 2008 title/completion map, complete 2011 authority/vote/debt package, later ACTAs, money/funding/DD records and institutional-receipt/verification evidence.

### Knowledge / intent status

Not promoted globally. Institutional receipt must remain separate from individual knowledge; later repetition must remain separate from independent verification; institutional/judicial action must remain separate from proof of knowing unlawful participation.

### Contrary / exculpatory evidence

Future passes are expressly required to search for valid proxies, completed escrituras, independent vote recalculations, lawful funding waivers, independent title verification, corrective notices and other evidence capable of defeating the Project's working hypotheses.

## Forensic-audit concept

The new authority gateway is designed so that a competent court/authority could, if procedurally appropriate, use a neutral mandate rather than a party theory:

> Independently reconcile ownership, authority, debts, assets, money flows, valuations, funding and institutional representations and identify unexplained inconsistencies without assuming Por Derecho's allegations are correct.

The site does **not** state that a court-appointed forensic auditor currently exists or that a new judge/recusal/reassignment is legally available. Those remain proceeding- and law-specific questions.
