## 3. Controlling proposition preserved

> There is a unitary forensic question whether powers entrusted to the insolvency administration for LPB ceased, at some point, to serve the debtor/estate independently and began functioning as a mechanism implementing objectives of a pre-existing adverse private perimeter and its possible economic successor. This is stronger than a mere adverse-outcome narrative but narrower than an allegation of formal agency or collusion.

## 5. Evidence scale preserved

- `L0` — neutral/loyal explanation adequately documented.
- `L1` — isolated adverse effect.
- `L2` — repeated same-beneficiary/adverse-perimeter effect.
- `L3` — conflict / independence warnings requiring explanation or safeguards.
- `L4` — strong functional alignment difficult to reconcile with estate interest.
- `L5` — functional/de facto adverse-agency hypothesis: evidence of knowing implementation of adverse objectives using administrator powers, without yet proving formal mandate or criminal agreement.
- `L6` — direct concerted-action evidence: instructions, representation, agreement, consideration, common planning or knowingly coordinated joint action.

The L5/L6 separation is mandatory. Do not silently convert functional alignment into collusion.

## 6. Power-source test preserved

For each material AC act or omission ask:

> **Was a power entrusted to the AC for LPB/the estate used to produce an outcome an adverse private actor could not itself lawfully produce?**

Then identify:

- source and purpose of the AC power;
- whose problem the act solved;
- whose legal theory/information was adopted;
- what independent verification occurred;
- immediate and downstream beneficiary;
- measurable benefit to LPB/the estate;
- neutral alternative;
- conflict disclosure / abstention / enhanced court supervision;
- act-specific validity, liability and remedial consequence if a conflict is ultimately proved.

The pre-CAM Monte Lanza/Molina/Community architecture is a documented dispute architecture. Whether the AC merely administered it or progressively validated/operationalised parts of it remains an act-specific evidential question.

## 10. Recovery / downstream boundary preserved

No global automatic nullity follows from a loyalty/conflict/independence breakpoint.

Each downstream act or right must be tested separately for:

- authority;
- invalidity / ineffectiveness where legally available;
- rescission / correction / reversal;
- material indefensión;
- accounting;
- restitution / unjust enrichment;
- patrimonial damages;
- independent title, registration, good faith, consideration, third-party protection or other legal basis.

HNT/MYND/RICPE/RIC/public-support positions are therefore tested for dependency, not invalidated by association.
