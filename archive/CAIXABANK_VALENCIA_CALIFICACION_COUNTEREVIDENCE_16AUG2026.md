# CAIXABANK VALENCIA / CALIFICACIÓN — COUNTER-EVIDENCE CONTROL

**Date:** 16 August 2026  
**Status:** `CONTROLLING SUPPLEMENT — EVIDENCE / RECOVERY / PUBLICATION-SAFETY`

## Purpose

This control connects the live Valencia banking litigation with the Calificación culpable record in Concurso 36/2012. It is designed to prevent three opposite errors:

1. omitting a live and potentially substantial third-party recovery route when discussing loss, causation or patrimonial prejudice;
2. treating the pending CaixaBank claim as already won or its pleaded quantum as adjudicated; and
3. using a lawsuit filed after Sentencia 163/2023 to claim, without separate proof, that the 2019 Fiscal or the first-instance Judge knew of that later lawsuit when they acted.

The correct evidential use is as a **live recovery/counter-evidence track requiring reconciliation with the Calificación causation and loss narrative**, while keeping actor knowledge and chronology source-specific.

That proposition is presently **source-attributed to CaixaBank's expert report**; the underlying native AC authorisation must still be independently matched before it is treated as a freestanding verified AC document.

### Controlled recovery formulation

Use:

> a live, material and potentially substantial recovery claim pleaded at €1,124,445.84 plus the relief, interest and costs sought, but contested and not yet adjudicated.

Do **not** convert “potentially substantial” into “certain”, “won”, “recoverable as of right” or an adjudicated €1.124m asset.

### Publication boundary on “witness in favour of the bank”

The citations establish that CaixaBank itself proposed/requested Borja. They do **not**, by themselves, prove the substantive direction of his evidence or that his testimony is in fact favourable to CaixaBank.

Public-safe language is therefore:

> “The Administrador Concursal, Francisco de Borja Rodríguez-Batllori Laffitte, is a documented witness whom CaixaBank itself requested/proposed in the pending Valencia proceeding; the court record also says he was proposed by Aweswell.”

Do not state as established fact that Borja has testified falsely, colluded with CaixaBank, has an unlawful conflict, or is substantively “the bank's witness” unless the actual testimony or a later source supports that proposition.

## 5. Evidential use against the Calificación narrative

### A. Administrador Concursal — Borja

**Verified facts:**

- Borja was the Administrador Concursal whose Calificación report advanced materially adverse propositions against Gil/Aweswell/LPB.
- CaixaBank's expert report describes part of the present banking claim (€573,591.53) as within AC authorisation.
- CaixaBank itself proposed/requested Borja as a witness in the related Valencia banking litigation; Aweswell also proposed him.

**Evidence-based inference / required reconciliation:**

The combination is relevant to consistency, neutrality and cross-proceeding reconciliation. A Calificación narrative that attributes patrimonial deterioration or culpable causation to debtor-side conduct must be tested against a live third-party banking recovery route connected with the same economic history, including what the AC knew, authorised, valued, preserved or pursued.

Borja's witness role for evidence requested by CaixaBank increases the need to compare his Valencia evidence with his concursal reports, credit-recognition work, Calificación allegations and any authorisation of the banking claim. It is **not by itself proof** of bias, collusion or conflict of interest.

### B. Fiscal Ricardo de Mosteyrín Sampalo

Ricardo de Mosteyrín Sampalo's Calificación opinion is dated **12 March 2019**.

The current CaixaBank action was filed later; CaixaBank's expert report describes the demand as filed on **13 October 2023**. That filing date is presently source-attributed to the bank's expert report unless/until the native filing stamp is separately controlled.

Therefore the later lawsuit cannot be used as proof that the Fiscal knew of this specific 2023 proceeding in 2019.

It **can** be used now as counter-evidence when testing whether the adverse Calificación account, if still relied upon, treated bank-side causation, financial-product loss and recoverable third-party claims as closed, absent or exclusively attributable to debtor management. Earlier underlying bank-dispute knowledge must be proved from pre-2019 sources actor by actor.

### C. Judge Alberto López Villarrubia

Sentencia 163/2023 was signed **28 September 2023**. The current CaixaBank action is described in CaixaBank's expert report as filed **13 October 2023**, after that judgment.

Accordingly, do **not** say that Judge Alberto López Villarrubia “ignored ORD 1859/2023-9” when signing Sentencia 163/2023 unless a different pre-judgment filing/source proves otherwise.

The live claim is nevertheless material **subsequent counter-evidence** to any continuing or appellate treatment of loss, causation or irreversibility as fixed and exhausted. If potentially recoverable loss from the banking relationship remains live, that recovery route must be reconciled before the wider patrimonial history is described as closed, solely debtor-caused or finally irrecoverable.
