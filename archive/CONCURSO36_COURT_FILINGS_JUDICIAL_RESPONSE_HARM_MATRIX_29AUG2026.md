## 4. Harm / destruction thesis: what is evidence and what is causation

### Masa activa

The evidence supports a sequence requiring reconciliation across:

- bid/privileged-credit amounts and competitive-offer architecture;
- access/control and preservation of LPB assets;
- attempted funded exit / better offer;
- asset disposition and later non-convalidation/renewed competition rulings;
- AC liquidation reports and accounting;
- the later €2,098 treasury endpoint and still-open final-account/conclusion family.

This supports investigation of **estate-value erosion, lost recovery routes, accounting and causation**. It does not, by itself, prove that one actor or one judicial decision caused the whole economic loss.

### Unidad productiva / operational continuity

The contemporaneous record describes an occupied hotel with activity, maintenance/service work, partial renovation and a live funded operating/exit route around June 2018. The 7 June event therefore must be tested for its effect on operational continuity, customers, access, staff/security, renovation, investor diligence and the better-offer route.

Public wording may say **alleged destruction/damage to productive-unit continuity** or **operational-harm thesis**. It must not state that the productive unit was destroyed by named persons as an adjudicated fact.

### Physical condition after 7 June

Photographs dated 9 July 2018 document visibly green/discoloured pool water, residue/staining, accumulated leaves/debris and neglected-looking common areas/corridors. These are condition evidence. The title/annotations of the source attribute responsibility to named actors; that attribution is party-authored and is not established by the photographs themselves.

### Extraconcursal harm

The correct question is not whether a single physical hotel was affected, but **which legal person's asset/right was affected by which act and under what authority**.

| Perimeter | Inside LPB estate by default? | Potential spillover/overreach issue | Required proof |
|---|---:|---|---|
| LPB fincas/rights | Yes, if inventoried/owned | estate preservation, disposition, income, accounting | title/inventory/order/accounting |
| Aweswell shareholder rights | No | standing, information, funded recovery route | corporate standing + procedural acts |
| Matkator fincas | No | access, possession, works, value, operational effects | certified title + act-by-act authority |
| CEXP rights/receivables | Not automatically | exploitation mandate, receivable, control/income | governing instruments + accounting |
| Pink/operator contracts | Not automatically | operational appearance, utilities/contracts, tax consequences | contracts, transfer, actual operation |
| Other owners' fincas | No | access, possession, common-area/operational effects | finca-by-finca title/consent |
| Community/common elements | Separate competence | security/common-area authority used as claimed basis for wider control | resolutions, statutes, legal authority |

This is the **overreach-of-the-concurso thesis**: identify where a power valid only within LPB's insolvency perimeter allegedly produced effects on non-LPB property, rights, operation or legal persons. It is a source-testing framework, not a conclusion that every external effect was unlawful.
