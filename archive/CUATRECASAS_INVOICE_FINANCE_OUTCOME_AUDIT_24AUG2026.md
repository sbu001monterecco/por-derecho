## Controlling conclusion

The evidence supports a developed, closure-capable 2018 transaction package combining operator, valuation, due diligence, bridge finance, security mechanics, concurso payment and a reciprocal standby-acquisition route. It does not support reducing the Stoneweg route to an abstract indication, treating replacement security as a logical catch-22, or splitting the Lagune/Elaia materials into unrelated commercial routes.

The public-safe sequence is:

1. ONA sourced the Stoneweg route. A 17-May Stoneweg communication forwarded by ONA on 18 May described the revised term sheet as approved subject chiefly to an ECO valuation sustaining 65% LTV.
2. On 8 June the package sent to Stoneweg expressly combined up to EUR 15 million of bridge finance, a proposed security package, the signed ONA operating documents, Cuatrecasas due diligence, the ECO valuation and the EUR 26 million purchase offer described contemporaneously as an Elaia SOCIMI offer. The sender explained that two preferential-acquisition lines had been omitted from the circulated copy because they referred to the bridge-financing discussions.
3. The original 30-May Lagune offer, signed for Lagune and initialled throughout the located copy, offered EUR 26 million from own funds. Its clause 5 provided a preferential-acquisition right if the seller accepted and received financing from named bridge lenders. The V2 copy removed the lender names while retaining the financing trigger. Together with the Aweswell-signed Elaia-addressed right, these are linked instruments within one reciprocal standby-acquisition package, not separate commercial routes.
4. On 12 June Stoneweg itself emailed that, following the agreed call, it attached an `Oferta Vinculante`. The attached instrument names **Varia Structured Opportunities S.A. (VSO)**, sets the lesser of EUR 15.5 million or 65% ECO and allocates approximately EUR 13.84 million to discharge the concurso debt. It is titled `Oferta Vinculante Condicionada`. The located PDF's execution boxes are blank, so the email transmission and instrument status must not be converted into a claim that this particular copy was countersigned.
5. The VSO instrument contemplated loan and available security in the same transaction, with LPB security after definitive judicial authorisation. The intended mechanics were incoming funds, court/creditor payment or consignation, release/cancellation of the existing debt/security and attachment of replacement security.
6. On 13 June Carlos Sanz recorded that Daniel Irigoyen had used the term sheet to give the judge stronger arguments; the meeting was described as positive, with the judge willing to solve the concurso through debt cancellation and lift it before summer. The contemporaneous note says simultaneous mortgage had not been obtained and required continued work. This is a closing-mechanics issue, not proof that no viable funding route existed.
7. The August term sheet was executed but remained a term sheet with conditions, expiry and definitive-document requirements; no definitive facility or drawdown is thereby proved.

The earlier formulation that stressed only a “real and substantial route” was too compressed because it imported caution from a later Fiduciam route into the May–June Stoneweg/VSO record. The corrected formulation is:

> A concrete signed-and-transmitted transaction package existed: signed ONA operating documents, a signed EUR 26 million Lagune/Elaia standby-acquisition package, the Stoneweg-transmitted VSO `Oferta Vinculante Condicionada`, an ECO valuation, Cuatrecasas due diligence and a defined court presentation. Ordinary conditions and simultaneous replacement security were implementation mechanics, not a logical deadlock. Gil Marer's position is that the 7-June forceful and unlawful takeover prevented conversion and that, absent that event and with competent integrated implementation, the package would have closed through at least one available financing route. That causation proposition is preserved as his attributed case and must be tested through condition ownership, satisfaction, waiver, prevention, witness evidence and expert analysis.

The caution that remains is document-specific: the reviewed VSO PDF has blank execution boxes; its committee, definitive facility and drawdown are not located; and the 13-June record says the simultaneous-mortgage mechanics still required resolution. Those gaps do not negate the package or create a catch-22. They identify what Cuatrecasas must reconstruct to answer the claimant's prevention and causation case.

## Matkator: three distinct economic questions

Do not merge:

1. **Duplicate cause** — whether promissory notes secured fees already included in another claim or separate consideration.
2. **Over-security/value mismatch** — whether collateral and reserved recourse exposed value materially beyond the reconciled debt.
3. **Actual double receipt** — whether the same principal euro was received twice.

Gil Marer's attributed position is that recourse reserved against `other assets`, combined with the physical/economic integration of Matkator's hotel units and absence of a current independent valuation, exposes the Matkator hotel-unit perimeter to whole-hotel-equivalent economic value. He further maintains that the promissory-note claim likely duplicates fees already claimed, creating concrete economic double-dipping, over-security and overpayment risk. His probability assessment is that an unconnected remate assignee or ultimate beneficiary is close to zero, because the asset, physical control, hotel operation, financing and value-capture vectors converge on the Acosta Matos-connected perimeter. Whether the same principal has actually been received twice, and the legal identity of an assignee, remain separate documentary questions.

The current proof boundary is equally important: the Cambiario record shows embargo over fincas 8584 and 8588, but only finca 8584 entered the located 2024 auction record and no complete later target-asset schedule is controlled; EUR 77,840 was recorded as a credit; autonomous promissory-note liability remains possible; registered title remains relevant; and no current record proves actual double receipt. The full target-asset schedule, instrument cause, present independent valuation, assignment/beneficiary chain and one cross-credit ledger are decisive.

## Remate and third-party-transfer control

The procedural possibility is concrete. Cuatrecasas's filing dated 10 February 2026 states that its 17 September and 18 October 2024 applications sought adjudication with reservation of cession, and it again requested adjudication at 70% while expressly reserving cession of the remate to a third party. The latest located ETJ act, signed 28 July 2026, admitted Matkator's reposición against the 19 June order. It does not decide the merits of that appeal or identify an assignee. No located record proves completed adjudication, cession, registration, possession or later transfer.

The accounting and professional-control audit must separate: assignment of the underlying claim; cession of the remate/adjudication position; adjudication followed by later asset transfer; and any recovery-sharing, nominee or side arrangement. Each route requires the legal object, instrument, recipient relationship, consideration, court/Registry effect, debtor credit, conflict/former-client/confidentiality review and—where legally applicable—KYC, beneficial-owner and source-of-funds control.

Current LEC article 647.3 expressly addresses identification of cedent and cessionary, proof of priced cession, debtor credit for the full remate/adjudication amount and treatment of overprice. The applicable version and transitional rule for each act in this 2024-origin auction remain for current counsel to validate. The audit therefore requests the temporal-law basis rather than assuming current wording controls every earlier step.

No raw KYC entitlement is assumed. If disclosure is restricted by privilege, professional secrecy, third-party rights or Law 10/2010, the appropriate production target is a non-privileged certification, dates, control owners, result, document index and lawful category/reason for withholding.
