## Statutory correction

The controlled statutory name is **Disposición Transitoria Tercera de la Ley 25/2015**. The official consolidated BOE text sets the first-six-month, seventh-month and post-thirteenth-month rules in that provision: <https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2015-8469#dttercera>. Source text using “Disposición Adicional Tercera” remains visible as a source literal but is not the repository's legal label.

## Completion test

No amount may be described as actually paid, improperly paid, legally due or recoverable until it is reconciled against:

1. every remuneration request and reasoned order;
2. every extension after month twelve and proof of hearing/notification;
3. all invoices, tax records and interest calculations;
4. complete quarterly reports and estate accounts;
5. unbroken estate-bank statements; and
6. a competent merits determination, if one exists.
