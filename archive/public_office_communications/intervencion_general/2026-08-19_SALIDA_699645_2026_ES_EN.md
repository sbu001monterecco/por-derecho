## Controlled substantive digest of SALIDA 699645/2026

### 1. Nature of the requests

The Intervención General characterises the requests as seeking broad certificates, third-party document checks, identification of possible funding sources and promotion of general control or investigative measures across multiple facts, persons, administrations and possible proceedings.

### 2. Its asserted competence

The Office describes its remit as internal economic-financial control of the Canary autonomous public sector through the procedures, plans and concrete control files legally assigned to it. It says it has no general, open-ended power to investigate or certify every possible aid, fund, incentive, payment, financing source or civil, urban, insolvency, corporate and tax issue connected with the asset.

### 3. RIC lane

It restates that the suitability of RIC financial instruments is handled through the competent Finance body, a prior binding AEAT report and a Canary Government decision. It says that RIC suitability is not, by itself, an autonomous subsidy or direct regional-treasury payment and that the Intervención General does not participate in fiscalising that declaration.

### 4. Critical clarification of the June response

The Office expressly states that SALIDA 497011/2026 was **not** a general certificate that no other aid, subsidy, regional incentive, European funding, payment, certification or separate administrative file existed.

That is the controlling correction. It prevents the June reply being used as a universal “no public funds” or “no other files” certificate.

### 5. Other routes remain separate

The letter says that regional incentives, public aid, European/ERDF funds, tax obligations, RIC-linked instruments and local-authority actions must, where applicable, be pursued through their specific procedures and competent bodies.

### 6. No merits determination

The Office says the response does not prejudge:

- the truth of the reported facts;
- the existence or nonexistence of irregularities;
- civil, administrative or criminal responsibility;
- rights, remedies or procedures available before other competent bodies.

### 7. Access and confidentiality position

It argues that complainant or third-party status does not by itself confer interested-party status or general access to a possible financial-control file concerning another entity. It invokes statutory reservation and confidentiality rules. This public record treats that as the Office’s legal position, not as a universal judicial determination of every possible access right.

### 8. SIINF

The letter notes the availability of the Canary internal information system for objective indications involving organs or authorities of the Canary autonomous administration. It does not, by that statement alone, confirm a new specific referral or disclose the outcome of any confidential channel handling.
