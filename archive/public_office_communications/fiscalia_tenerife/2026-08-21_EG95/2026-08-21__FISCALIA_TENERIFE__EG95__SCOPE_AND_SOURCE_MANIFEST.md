## 1. Controlling proposition

The Decree signed on 21 August 2026 by the Chief Prosecutor of the Provincial Public Prosecutor's Office of Santa Cruz de Tenerife opened and archived **Expediente Gubernativo 95/2026**. It treated the new submission principally as a request to review the Public Prosecution Service's position in its report of 8 June 2026 and stated that no parallel prosecutorial criminal-investigation proceedings could be opened because the matter was judicialised in DP 748/2026, even though that judicial file was archived.

The sent email did include a request to revisit the Fiscalía position in DP 748/2026, but its recorded scope was wider. It also requested:

1. preservation and possible association with the sender's January 2026 communications and earlier Fiscalía records;
2. separation from economic/fiscal files transmitted to Las Palmas;
3. finite examination of ETJ 163/2020 and Cambiario 1048/2019;
4. a bounded local Tenerife evidence step;
5. individual documentation of any decision not to pursue each requested line; and
6. preservation against irreversible asset effects pending clarification.

This is a documented **scope/characterisation difference**. It supports a respectful objection that the Decree's summary did not exhaust the sent communication. Standing alone, it does not prove bad faith, an offence, or that the routing conclusion was legally invalid.

- EG 95/2026 is an administrative/governance file opened to process the communication.
