# I. Controlled Spanish transcription

**FISCALÍA PROVINCIAL DE SANTA CRUZ DE TENERIFE**  
**FISCAL JEFE**

## DECRETO DEL ILMO. SR. FISCAL JEFE DE LA FISCALÍA PROVINCIAL DE SANTA CRUZ DE TENERIFE DON JOSÉ LUÍS SÁNCHEZ-JÁUREGUI Y ALCAIDE

El día 21 de Agosto de 2026 se recibe en esta Fiscalía correo electrónico remitido por Don Gil Marer, en que aporta documentos referentes a actuaciones judiciales seguidas ante la Plaza n.º 4 del Tribunal de Instancia de La Laguna, Diligencias Previas 748/2026, incoadas por una denuncia del mismo, en las que se dictó auto de sobreseimiento provisional y archivo con fecha 24 de Marzo, ratificado con fecha 16 de Julio de 2026, previo informe en tal sentido del Ministerio Fiscal de fecha 8 de Junio, solicitando el Sr. Gil Marer con la nueva aportación de documentos, la revisión de la postura del Ministerio Fiscal expuesta en su citado informe, por lo que

Procede incoar **EXPEDIENTE GUBERNATIVO**, y teniendo en cuenta que no pueden incoarse nuevas DIP, como las incoadas a los ordinales 7, 12 y 20/2026 por denuncias similares del Sr. Gil Marer, ni por el presente, al estar judicializado el procedimiento en las diligencias previas mencionadas de la plaza n.º 4 de Instrucción del Tribunal de Instancia de La Laguna ni poder, por tanto, acordarse las diligencias que el reclamante interesa del Ministerio Fiscal, fuera del procedimiento judicial, aunque se encuentre archivado, el **ARCHIVO** del mismo, notificando el presente Decreto al reclamante en el correo electrónico de remisión.

En Santa Cruz de Tenerife, a 21 de Agosto de 2026

**EL FISCAL JEFE**

Fdo.: José Luis Sánchez-Jáuregui y Alcaide

**NOTA.** Seguidamente se cumple lo ordenado, quedando registrado como Expediente Gubernativo núm. 95/2026.

# II. Full English translation

**PROVINCIAL PUBLIC PROSECUTOR’S OFFICE OF SANTA CRUZ DE TENERIFE**  
**CHIEF PROSECUTOR**

## DECREE OF THE HONOURABLE CHIEF PROSECUTOR OF THE PROVINCIAL PUBLIC PROSECUTOR’S OFFICE OF SANTA CRUZ DE TENERIFE, MR JOSÉ LUIS SÁNCHEZ-JÁUREGUI Y ALCAIDE

On 21 August 2026, this Public Prosecutor’s Office received an email sent by Mr Gil Marer, in which he provided documents concerning judicial proceedings conducted before Seat No. 4 of the Court of Instance of La Laguna, Preliminary Proceedings 748/2026, which had been opened following a complaint made by him. In those proceedings, an order for provisional dismissal and closure was issued on 24 March and was confirmed on 16 July 2026, following a report of the Public Prosecution Service dated 8 June supporting that outcome. By means of the new documentary submission, Mr Gil Marer requests review of the position of the Public Prosecution Service set out in that report. Accordingly,

It is appropriate to open an **ADMINISTRATIVE/GOVERNANCE FILE** (*expediente gubernativo*). However, taking into account that no new DIP proceedings may be opened—such as those opened under numbers 7, 12 and 20/2026 following similar complaints by Mr Gil Marer—nor may such proceedings be opened in respect of the present submission, because the matter has been brought before the courts in the above-mentioned Preliminary Proceedings before Investigation Seat No. 4 of the Court of Instance of La Laguna, and because, consequently, the investigative steps sought by the complainant from the Public Prosecution Service cannot be ordered outside those judicial proceedings, even though they have been closed, the present file is hereby **CLOSED**, and this Decree shall be notified to the complainant at the email address from which the submission was sent.

Santa Cruz de Tenerife, 21 August 2026

**THE CHIEF PROSECUTOR**

Signed: José Luis Sánchez-Jáuregui y Alcaide

**NOTE.** The foregoing order is then carried out, and the matter is registered as Administrative/Governance File No. 95/2026.

# III. Terminology and evidential limits

- **Expediente gubernativo 95/2026:** an administrative/governance file opened by the Provincial Public Prosecutor’s Office to process the communication. The Decree does not describe it as a new criminal-investigation file.
- **DIP:** *Diligencias de Investigación Penal*, prosecutorial criminal-investigation proceedings conducted by the Public Prosecution Service.
- **DP 748/2026:** *Diligencias Previas 748/2026*, judicial preliminary proceedings before Investigation Seat No. 4 of the Court of Instance of La Laguna.
- **Procedural effect stated by the Decree:** Fiscalía considers that the requested investigative steps cannot be undertaken through a parallel prosecutorial investigation because the matter is already judicialised in DP 748/2026, even though that judicial file is archived.
- **What the Decree does not decide:** it does not state that the newly supplied documents are false, irrelevant or exculpatory; it does not determine the underlying debt, asset-selection, prior-knowledge, recovery-alternative, assignment or beneficiary questions; and it does not make a finding on criminal responsibility.
- **Outstanding primary-source gap:** the separate decision said by this Decree to have confirmed the provisional dismissal on 16 July 2026 was not located in the Gmail/Drive scan used for this digest. For that date and procedural event, this Decree is presently the located source.
