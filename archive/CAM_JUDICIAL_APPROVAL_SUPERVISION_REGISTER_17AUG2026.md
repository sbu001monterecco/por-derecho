### What was before the Judge

The Auto expressly records LPB/Aweswell observations to the AC Plan, including:

1. challenge to the reduced real-estate valuation based on a valuation supplied by CAM, with LPB relying on a higher valuation;
2. challenge to CAM's dación proposal because of both the amount of CAM's recognised credit and the lower valuation attributed to the real estate;
3. challenge to the low price for the non-mortgaged real estate because it was below CAM's own valuation;
4. request that, before accepting CAM's dación, a period be opened for better offers and, if a better offer appeared, licitación.

These are party objections recorded by the court, not judicial findings that the valuation/price was improper.

### What the Judge rejected

On the CAM-credit objection, the Auto says a **separate same-date clarification Auto** governs interest under art. 59.1 LC and mortgage-responsibility limits. It therefore does not accept that objection in the Plan-approval Auto.

Separate stable ID for that act: `C36-JUD-2018-04-16-002`.

### What the Judge accepted / modified

The Auto expressly says that **the remaining observations should be accepted**, and applies the last paragraph of art. 155.4 LC. It therefore imposes a competition safeguard:

- judicial authorisation and conditions must receive the same publicity as the relevant auction;
- if a better bidder appears within **10 days** of the last announcement, the Judge will open licitación among all offerors and set the bond.

This is an affirmative judicial safeguard and must be preserved in any fair Judge-accountability analysis.

### What the Judge expressly approved

The dispositive part:

1. approves the AC Plan as definitive, **with the precisions in the second legal ground**;
2. approves the CAM disposal proposal contained in the AC Plan;
3. for the enumerated mortgaged apartments: **dación en pago** of the privileged credits encumbering them;
4. for fincas 8.508–8.536, 8.653 and 8.654 (locales + pools/solarium): **direct sale for €400,000**;
5. CAM must bear transaction expenses/taxes, including plusvalía, and Community quotas recognised in the definitive creditor lists as contingent claims;
6. the CAM authorisation remains subject to the publication / 10-day better-bid / Judge-run licitación safeguard above.

### Fallback liquidation rules also approved

If the proposed dación did not occur, the Auto modified the Plan with detailed fallback rules. Among them:

- direct sale of estate assets generally could be carried out by the AC without further judicial authorisation under the approved Plan, save the special-credit exceptions stated in the Auto;
- purchase offers would be directed to the AC, who would communicate the selected offer's price/payment/conditions to the court and identify an email address for improvements;
- in absence of improvements the AC could formalise the selected offer; if improvements existed, the AC would choose the option it considered most beneficial and formalise it;
- judicial auction rules required title notes, occupancy/state/location, auction values and updated privileged-credit amounts;
- quarterly AC reports had to detail realised operations, amounts obtained, pending assets and mass-credit status.

**Control:** these fallback rules must not be confused with the specific CAM approval route, which was expressly conditioned by the art. 155.4 publication/better-bid safeguard.

### Affirmative Judge actions

The Auto fixes the then-required economic amounts for a competing bid using CAM's 17-May balance/liquidation materials:

- first-rank credit total stated: **€11,887,314.33**;
- second-rank credit total stated: **€1,278,518.03**;
- plus the €400,000 non-mortgaged-assets limb and other conditions of the approved transaction.

The Auto also:

- states that a better proposal is to be filed within the applicable 10-day period;
- rejects Aweswell's request that a possible bidder be entitled to condition its proposal on a prior review/due-diligence process over the assets, reasoning that the law did not provide that mechanism and interested parties could consult the inventory and valuation in the proceedings;
- provides that the Judge will determine whether an offer constitutes an improvement;
- states that once an improved offer exists, the Judge opens licitación and fixes bond;
- provides for successive bidding before the Judge until the highest offer remains.

### Supervision question created — not a finding

Because the Judge expressly rejected mandatory pre-bid asset review and directed interested parties to the file's inventory/valuation, the audit must test what information, inspection, condition and title material was actually available equally to prospective bidders. This is a source-verification issue; the Auto alone does not prove unfair competition.

## 26-Jun-2018 suspension — countervailing judicial act

The separately controlled 26-Jun-2018 Auto suspends realisation of the enumerated mortgaged fincas while leaving other pronouncements unchanged. This is affirmative judicial intervention adverse to immediate completion of the apartment dación and must be retained as counterevidence against any simplistic theory of uninterrupted judicial facilitation.
