# No-inference rule — unresolved La Laguna judicial identities

- A later judge in the same proceeding does not become the signer of an earlier act without source proof.
- A judgment naming a judge does not identify the LAJ for earlier decrees/diligencias.
- A filed subsidiary appeal does not establish admission, transmission, an appellate roll, panel or LAJ.
- Cooperation office-holders do not become office-holders of the requesting court.

These are permanent source-attribution rules for the La Laguna `^` perimeter.
