# GROUP RECOVERY, RESTITUTION AND ACCOUNTABILITY OBJECTIVES

Evidence/strategy cut-off: 15 August 2026.

## Controlling principle

**Recovery first. Accountability in service of recovery. Punishment only where independently justified.**

Project Sun Rock / Por Derecho seeks restoration of the lawful economic and patrimonial position of Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. (LPB), Matkator, S.L., Aweswell Limited and other affected Group interests. Accountability of public office-holders, insolvency office-holders and private actors is pursued only insofar as evidenced acts or omissions are relevant to:

1. restoring businesses, assets, title, possession, access, control and other rights;
2. recovering fruits, revenues, value and causes of action;
3. removing continuing judicial, documentary, registry, administrative or economic obstacles;
4. proving causation actor by actor; and
5. obtaining compensation for proven harm that cannot lawfully be reversed.

Disciplinary or criminal consequences are separate secondary consequences. They are not presumed from an adverse outcome, chronology, professional proximity, procedural error or economic loss.

## Recovery hierarchy

### 1. Preserve the Group and the claims
Protect LPB from avoidable extinction or loss of economically valuable causes of action. Preserve Matkator and third-party rights outside the insolvency estate where the evidence requires that separation. Preserve Aweswell and other cross-border investor/shareholder claims without conflating them with LPB's estate.

### 2. Restore identifiable property and rights
Where a legally available route exists, seek restoration of the correct Group entity's property, title, possession, access, corporate rights, contractual rights, income rights and other patrimonial interests.

### 3. Reverse continuing consequences
Historical originating acts may have present consequences. Audit and, where legally possible, correct continuing effects in court records, testimonios/certifications, notarial instruments, Registry entries, corporate succession, financing, exploitation, administrative files and public-support representations.

### 4. Account for fruits and economic benefit
Trace hotel exploitation, rents, revenues, fruits, savings, appreciation and other benefits only where a documentary and legal attribution can be made to a specific asset or right.

### 5. Recover damages for what cannot be restored
Build damages from evidence and causation, not from a global headline value. Separate asset loss, lost income/fruits, diminution of investment value, financing effects, professional/recovery costs and other legally recoverable heads of damage.

### 6. Allocate responsibility actor by actor
For each material event use:

`duty/power → verified knowledge → act/omission → implementation → present consequence → causation → remedy → quantum`

Do not infer shared intent or collective responsibility from common timing or participation in the same institutional sequence.

### 7. Accountability follows proof
Maintain separate routes for:

- jurisdictional correction / appeal / nullity / insolvency remedy;
- civil or insolvency-estate liability;
- professional or disciplinary responsibility;
- court-functioning / inspection / LAJ supervision;
- State liability where legally applicable;
- criminal investigation where the elements of a specific offence are independently evidenced.

## The three accountability nodes within the recovery programme

### Administrador Concursal — follow the money, assets and estate administration

**Recovery function:** produce the complete economic and accounting reconstruction necessary to determine whether LPB's estate or directly affected parties suffered remediable or compensable depletion.

Finite question:

> Can the Administrador Concursal produce one certified, arithmetically reproducible bridge from the recognised CAM credit and later interest/adjustments through the EUR 400,000/locales component, bidding, adjudication/dación, cancellations, payments/compensations, estate costs, remuneration and any surplus, with a source document and accounting entry for every number?

Desired recovery outputs: accounting, restitution/reintegration analysis, asset/value recovery, damages/insurance analysis and only then any separate professional/disciplinary/criminal consequence justified by proof.

### Mercantil nº 1 / Judge — follow knowledge, decisions and continuing legal effects

**Recovery function:** identify which judicial acts or omissions presently stand between the Group and restoration, what evidence was actually before the court, what was decided, and which lawful corrective route remains available.

Finite question:

> For each patrimonially material decision, what primary material was actually before the court, what issue was within scope, what was decided, what remained undecided, what consequence followed and what remedy can still correct a continuing effect?

Desired recovery outputs: proper jurisdictional correction where available, removal of continuing legal obstacles, complete appellate record and separate governance/disciplinary analysis only where conduct outside protected judicial merits is evidenced.

### LAJ / Oficina Judicial — follow the documents, finality, service and portability

**Recovery function:** reconstruct the official documentary machinery by which judicial acts became certifiable, deliverable and usable outside the court, and determine whether any defect is relevant to present title, Registry or patrimonial consequences.

Finite question:

> For every material testimony, DIOR or certification, can the Justice Administration produce the complete chain: request → decision → certified content → expression of finality → service → delivery → external use → current effect?

Desired recovery outputs: certified reconstruction, correction of any defective documentary chain where legally possible, title/Registry remedy analysis and separate supervisory/disciplinary/criminal consequences only where the evidence supports them.

## Three planes that must remain separate but connected

1. **Concursal — LPB:** estate, value, income, realisation, debts, payments, causes of action and exit/restoration.
2. **Extraconcursal — Matkator / third parties:** property, possession, access, works, exploitation, fruits and rights not automatically absorbed into LPB's estate.
3. **Cross-border / transnational — Aweswell and foreign interests:** investment value, capital, financing, recovery, costs and remedies crossing Spain's border.

Do not convert operating unity, hotel branding or later economic integration into proof of ownership unity.

## Public formulation

> **Por Derecho seeks restoration, not retaliation: recovery of the Group's businesses, assets, rights, income and lawful economic position wherever possible; compensation for proven harm that cannot be reversed; and proportionate responsibility for each public or private actor only to the extent that primary evidence establishes their individual contribution to the loss or its continuing effects.**

## Evidential safeguards

- Adverse outcome ≠ misconduct.
- Judicial error ≠ prevaricación.
- Testimonio issued ≠ improper use.
- Court approval ≠ independent verification of every underlying number.
- Missing document in our corpus ≠ proof that it does not exist.
- Chronology ≠ causation.
- Group/perimeter label ≠ common legal identity, knowledge or intent.
- Registration/receipt ≠ investigation or merits acceptance.
