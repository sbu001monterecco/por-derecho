# GROUP RECOVERY, RESTITUTION AND ACCOUNTABILITY OBJECTIVES

Evidence/strategy cut-off: 18 August 2026.

## Controlling principle

**Recovery first. Accountability in service of recovery. Punishment only where independently justified.**

Project Sun Rock / Por Derecho seeks restoration of the lawful economic and patrimonial position of Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. (LPB), Matkator, S.L., Aweswell Limited and other affected Group interests. Accountability of public office-holders, insolvency office-holders and private actors is pursued only insofar as evidenced acts or omissions are relevant to:

1. restoring businesses, assets, title, possession, access, control and other rights;
2. recovering fruits, revenues, value and causes of action;
3. removing continuing judicial, documentary, registry, administrative or economic obstacles;
4. proving causation actor by actor; and
5. obtaining compensation for proven harm that cannot lawfully be reversed.

Disciplinary or criminal consequences are separate secondary consequences. They are not presumed from an adverse outcome, chronology, professional proximity, procedural error or economic loss.

## Recovery hierarchy

### 1. Preserve the Group and the claims
Protect LPB from avoidable extinction or loss of economically valuable causes of action. Preserve Matkator and third-party rights outside the insolvency estate where the evidence requires that separation. Preserve Aweswell and other cross-border investor/shareholder claims without conflating them with LPB's estate.

### 2. Restore identifiable property and rights
Where a legally available route exists, seek restoration of the correct Group entity's property, title, possession, access, corporate rights, contractual rights, income rights and other patrimonial interests.

### 3. Reverse continuing consequences
Historical originating acts may have present consequences. Audit and, where legally possible, correct continuing effects in court records, testimonios/certifications, notarial instruments, Registry entries, corporate succession, financing, exploitation, administrative files and public-support representations.

### 4. Account for fruits and economic benefit
Trace hotel exploitation, rents, revenues, fruits, savings, appreciation and other benefits only where a documentary and legal attribution can be made to a specific asset or right.

### 5. Recover damages for what cannot be restored
Build damages from evidence and causation, not from a global headline value. Separate asset loss, lost income/fruits, diminution of investment value, financing effects, professional/recovery costs and other legally recoverable heads of damage.

### 6. Allocate responsibility actor by actor
For each material event use:

`duty/power → verified knowledge → act/omission → implementation → present consequence → causation → remedy → quantum`

Do not infer shared intent or collective responsibility from common timing or participation in the same institutional sequence.

### 7. Accountability follows proof
Maintain separate routes for:

- jurisdictional correction / appeal / nullity / insolvency remedy;
- civil or insolvency-estate liability;
- professional or disciplinary responsibility;
- court-functioning / inspection / LAJ supervision;
- State liability where legally applicable;
- criminal investigation where the elements of a specific offence are independently evidenced.

The programme coordinates evidence and strategy while ring-fencing legal identity, standing, applicable law, limitation, privilege, costs, settlement authority and recovery accounting.

### Meaning of “Sun Rock Group”

`Sun Rock Group` may be used as a historical, economic and strategic umbrella for the ownership, operating, funding, commercialisation and recovery network developed around Hotel Sun Park. It is not one juridical person.

Every legal person, community, operating structure, commercial counterparty and individual rights-holder retains its own assets, contracts, liabilities, causes of action, forum, limitation and recoveries.

### Claimant classes

1. **Aweswell Limited:** its own direct parent-company, creditor, funder, contractual, investment, recovery-cost and cross-border rights.
2. **LPB:** estate, property, productive-unit, income, creditor, contractual and insolvency causes of action, subject to the standing and control rules of the concurso.
3. **Matkator:** its own extraconcursal property, title, possession, use, works, income and damages position.
4. **Historical operating or associated structures:** Explotadora / CEXP / OpCo and any other structure only after legal existence, capacity, representation, current control and claim ownership are verified.
5. **Independent third parties:** owners, creditors, counterparties, distributors, suppliers, workers, customers, investors, insurers or other persons whose claims remain theirs unless a recognised legal basis transfers or authorises control.
6. **Public and regulatory interests:** prosecution, supervision, sanctions, correction, confiscation, public-money recovery and institutional accountability remain functions of the competent public body rather than private claims belonging to Aweswell.

### Authority gate

Aweswell or the coordinated programme may say it pursues an independent third party's claim `on that party's behalf` only where the file contains a legally sufficient mandate, assignment, subrogation, agency, joinder/representative mechanism, insolvency-office-holder authority, court-recognised standing or another valid basis under the applicable law.

Until then, the programme may preserve evidence, invite participation, support independent forensic work, coordinate properly instructed counsel and pursue Aweswell's own direct loss arising from the same events. It may not appropriate, settle, waive, assign, quantify as its own or publicly misdescribe the third party's claim.

The present controlled public record does not show one blanket authority covering every independent third party. That is a present corpus result, not proof that no authority exists elsewhere.

### Dormant does not mean abandoned

Use `DORMANT`, `NOT PRESENTLY PURSUED`, `PARTICIPATION UNVERIFIED` or `AUTHORITY PENDING` unless primary evidence establishes a release, settlement, waiver, discontinuance, final adverse disposition or another legally operative abandonment.

Before reactivation or coordinated pursuit, verify legal existence/control, direct injury, prior proceedings, settlement/release, insurer or office-holder control, limitation, willingness to participate and conflicts.

### Separate procedural routes

Maintain distinct but coordinated lanes for:

- civil and contractual recovery;
- mercantile and corporate remedies;
- concursal / insolvency-estate remedies;
- criminal or penal reporting, investigation and associated recovery where the competent law permits;
- regulatory and supervisory action;
- administrative, tax, public-funds and public-authority-liability routes;
- professional and disciplinary accountability;
- asset tracing, recognition and enforcement.

A criminal, regulatory or disciplinary route may produce investigation, correction, sanction, confiscation, referral or evidence. It does not automatically produce private compensation.

### Spain is central, not automatically exclusive

Spanish and Canary Islands courts and authorities remain central to the hotel property, LPB concurso, local administration, public funding and many alleged acts and consequences. No UK legal action is being pursued. Any non-UK foreign route would require claimant-specific facts establishing a proper jurisdictional and applicable-law nexus through claimant or defendant domicile, contract, duty, place of performance or harm, direct loss, payment, business operation, evidence or enforceable assets.

For each proposed route record jurisdiction, governing law, service, limitation, costs, evidence, enforceability and interaction with existing Spanish proceedings. Do not use “international law” as a generic substitute for a concrete foreign-law or private-international-law analysis.

## Claimant-specific forensic intake and liability management

No claimant or amount enters the consolidated recovery dashboard until the following gates are completed or expressly marked open:

1. **identity and status** — exact legal name, identifier, jurisdiction, current existence and controller;
2. **claim ownership and authority** — direct owner, office-holder, assignee, subrogated party, mandated representative or disputed;
3. **direct injury** — asset, contract or interest affected, date, immediate and consequential loss, and person who actually suffered it;
4. **defendant and causation** — duty/relationship → act or omission → knowledge → immediate effect → propagated effect → claimant-specific loss;
5. **applicable law and forum** — jurisdictional gateway, property situs, insolvency exclusivity, contract clauses and enforcement location;
6. **limitation and procedural history** — accrual, knowledge, expiry, interruption/suspension, prior proceedings, settlement, release, discontinuance and preclusion risks;
7. **evidence and quantum** — primary records, accounting, expert methodology, counterfactual, mitigation and confidence level;
8. **non-duplication** — reconcile property value, fruits, operating income, rent, distribution margin, shareholder loss, insurer recovery, criminal restitution and public-authority compensation;
9. **conflicts, privilege and data protection** — separate engagements, information barriers, settlement authority, disclosure and lawful data handling;
10. **recovery ledger** — allocate every payment, judgment, settlement or restitution to the claimant and loss it actually satisfies.

A further broad, undirected scan is not a precondition to publishing this controlled architecture. The next evidence pass must be targeted claimant by claimant and search equally for documents that prove, narrow, transfer, extinguish, settle, insure or time-bar a proposed claim.

## The three accountability nodes within the recovery programme

### Administrador Concursal — follow the money, assets and estate administration

**Recovery function:** produce the complete economic and accounting reconstruction necessary to determine whether LPB's estate or directly affected parties suffered remediable or compensable depletion.

Finite question:

> Can the Administrador Concursal produce one certified, arithmetically reproducible bridge from the recognised CAM credit and later interest/adjustments through the EUR 400,000/locales component, bidding, adjudication/dación, cancellations, payments/compensations, estate costs, remuneration and any surplus, with a source document and accounting entry for every number?

Desired recovery outputs: accounting, restitution/reintegration analysis, asset/value recovery, damages/insurance analysis and only then any separate professional/disciplinary/criminal consequence justified by proof.

### Mercantil nº 1 / Judge — follow knowledge, decisions and continuing legal effects

**Recovery function:** identify which judicial acts or omissions presently stand between the Group and restoration, what evidence was actually before the court, what was decided, and which lawful corrective route remains available.

Finite question:

> For each patrimonially material decision, what primary material was actually before the court, what issue was within scope, what was decided, what remained undecided, what consequence followed and what remedy can still correct a continuing effect?

Desired recovery outputs: proper jurisdictional correction where available, removal of continuing legal obstacles, complete appellate record and separate governance/disciplinary analysis only where conduct outside protected judicial merits is evidenced.

### LAJ / Oficina Judicial — follow the documents, finality, service and portability

**Recovery function:** reconstruct the official documentary machinery by which judicial acts became certifiable, deliverable and usable outside the court, and determine whether any defect is relevant to present title, Registry or patrimonial consequences.

Finite question:

> For every material testimony, DIOR or certification, can the Justice Administration produce the complete chain: request → decision → certified content → expression of finality → service → delivery → external use → current effect?

Desired recovery outputs: certified reconstruction, correction of any defective documentary chain where legally possible, title/Registry remedy analysis and separate supervisory/disciplinary/criminal consequences only where the evidence supports them.

## Three planes that must remain separate but connected

1. **Concursal — LPB:** estate, value, income, realisation, debts, payments, causes of action and exit/restoration.
2. **Extraconcursal — Matkator / third parties:** property, possession, access, works, exploitation, fruits and rights not automatically absorbed into LPB's estate.
3. **Cross-border / transnational — Aweswell and foreign interests:** investment value, capital, financing, recovery, costs and remedies crossing Spain's border.

Do not convert operating unity, hotel branding or later economic integration into proof of ownership unity.
