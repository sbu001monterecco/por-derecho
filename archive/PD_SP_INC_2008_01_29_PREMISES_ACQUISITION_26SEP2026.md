# PD-SP-INC-2008-01 — 17 June 2008 LPB 29-premises acquisition accounting incident

**Parent register:** `PD-SP-INCIDENT-REGISTRY-001`  
**Incident ID:** `PD-SP-INC-2008-01`  
**Control date:** 26 September 2026  
**Status:** SOURCE-CONTROLLED TRANSACTION / ACCOUNTING INCIDENT — NOT A FINDING OF WRONGDOING  
**Public-safe rule:** source propositions are separated from attributed allegations, inference, contrary/cross-source evidence and open proof gaps. Raw private provider identifiers and personal data are not published here.

## 1. Source facts

### LPB buyer-side accounting

The recovered 2008 accounting for LUCHY PLAYA BLANCA, S.L. records on **17 June 2008**, entry **000013**, `COMPRA 29 LOCALES SUN PARK`, at **EUR 1,368,048.00**.

The recovered mayores split the same entry into:

- `2100000001 TERRENOS 29 LOCALES SUN PARK`: **EUR 410,414.40**;
- `2110000001 CONSTRUCCIONES 29 LOCALES SUN PARK`: **EUR 957,633.60**.

Those two purchase components total **EUR 1,368,048.00**.

The bank-account ledger also records on 17 June 2008 entry 000013 `COMPRA 29 LOCALES SUN PARK` as **EUR -1,368,048.00**.

The same accounting package separately records:

- **171 apartments** acquired on 17 June 2008 for **EUR 9,234,000.00**;
- a funding/provision entry on 18 June 2008 labelled `CARGO PROVISION DE FONDOS 171+29` for **EUR 844,000.00**;
- 7 July 2008 ITP entries for the 171 apartments and the 29 premises, including **EUR 88,920.00** for `COMPRA 29 LOCALES SUN PARK (ITP)`.

These entries establish what LPB's accounting records say. They do not by themselves establish the complete deed terms, registered title chain, seller identity for each finca, or criminal liability.

### Transmission / custody checkpoint

A recovered 28 June 2012 correspondence package concerning requested LPB accounting identifies `DIARIO 2008 LUCHY.pdf` among the journals supplied into the insolvency-administration / adviser communication channel.

This supports transmission of the accounting package. It does **not** by itself prove that the Administrador Concursal personally read, verified or adopted the 17 June 2008 premises entry.

## 2. Cross-source control — EUR 1,368,048 vs EUR 1,368,000

Monte Lanza's separately controlled 2008 annual-account record states a **17 June 2008 sale for EUR 1,368,000**, parallel to apartment sales by certain owners, and separately records an option concerning **11 premises not sold**.

Accordingly:

- **EUR 1,368,048.00** = source-supported LPB buyer-ledger purchase amount for the 29 premises;
- **EUR 1,368,000.00** = source-supported Monte Lanza corporate annual-account sale figure.

The **EUR 48 difference is preserved as an unresolved reconciliation point**. No inference of fraud, falsity, diversion, rounding method or wrongdoing is made from the difference alone.

## 3. Criminal-first significance

Gil Marer's attributed hypothesis is that the 2008 commercial object was acquisition of an integrated Sun Park hotel perimeter and that incomplete implementation left a residual owner/seller perimeter that later became relevant to Community authority, litigation, Concurso 36/2012, the 7 June 2018 material-control event and downstream economic benefit.

This incident supplies a hard accounting marker for one **completed buyer-side acquisition limb**: 29 premises recorded by LPB on 17 June 2008.

It may be used to test, but does not itself prove:

- which of the 262 registered elements were contractually committed;
- which sellers completed or failed to complete;
- the composition of the alleged circa-23% residual perimeter;
- whether later Community actors derived authority or leverage from non-completed titles;
- knowing adoption, assistance, agreement or criminal purpose by any later actor;
- causation of later loss.

## 4. Evidential classification

**SOURCE FACT**
- LPB accounting entry 000013 records 29 Sun Park premises at EUR 1,368,048.00 on 17 June 2008.
- The asset split is EUR 410,414.40 land + EUR 957,633.60 construction.
- The LPB bank ledger records the same EUR 1,368,048.00 purchase debit.
- Separate LPB accounting records 171 apartments at EUR 9,234,000.00.
- Monte Lanza's annual accounts separately state EUR 1,368,000 for a 17 June 2008 sale.

**ATTRIBUTED ALLEGATION**
- The intended object was the complete integrated 262-element Sun Park acquisition and a residual seller/owner perimeter frustrated or failed to complete part of it.

**INVESTIGATIVE INFERENCE**
- The 29-premises entry is relevant to reconstructing the completed portion of the intended acquisition and measuring the residual perimeter. It is not proof of the legal obligation or breach of any non-completing owner.

**CONTRARY / CROSS-SOURCE EVIDENCE**
- Monte Lanza states EUR 1,368,000 rather than LPB's EUR 1,368,048.
- Monte Lanza's source also refers to 11 premises not sold / subject to a separate option, requiring exact title and finca reconciliation.

**OPEN PROOF GAPS**
1. Original deed(s) and invoice(s) for entry 000013.
2. Exact finca schedule for the 29 premises.
3. Seller-to-finca-to-price allocation.
4. Bank statement / settlement proof matching the ledger debit.
5. Tax-base reconciliation for the EUR 88,920 ITP entry.
6. Explanation of the EUR 48 buyer/seller accounting difference.
7. Relationship between the 29 acquired premises, Monte Lanza's 40-premises baseline and the 11-premises option.
8. Registry history and completion status for every affected finca.
9. Contractual option/mandate obligations for the alleged residual seller perimeter.
10. Actor-specific knowledge, assistance, causation and benefit in later Community / concurso / control events.

**ADJUDICATED FINDING**
- No adjudicated finding is identified here establishing the exact 29-premises consideration, the legal explanation for the EUR 48 difference, a seller breach, criminal agreement or criminal liability.

## 5. Unitary routing

Read this incident with the existing anti-regression sequence:

`2008 intended whole-hotel transaction → completed / residual ownership split → originating owner / representative / legal perimeter → 2011 Community debt / vote / authority machinery → operator / possession litigation → Concurso 36/2012 and alleged AC adoption / enabling → 2017–2018 CAM credit / title / Community convergence → security / access + 7-Jun-2018 practical-control pivot → later title / project / operating / finance benefit`.

Every arrow remains a proposition to prove. Family relationship, representation, attendance, professional association, parallel interest, chronology and later benefit do not themselves prove criminal agreement.

## 6. Source-control consequence

Future references must not collapse the two figures:

- cite **EUR 1,368,048.00** as the LPB buyer-accounting figure for the 29-premises entry;
- cite **EUR 1,368,000.00** as Monte Lanza's annual-account sale figure;
- preserve the EUR 48 difference until the deed, invoices, settlement and tax records reconcile it.
