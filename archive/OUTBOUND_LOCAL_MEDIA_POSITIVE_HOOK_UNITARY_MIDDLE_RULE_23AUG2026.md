# Project Sun Rock / Por Derecho — Local Media Positive-Hook / Unitary-Middle Rule

**Control date:** 23 August 2026  
**Status:** mandatory local-media narrative rule  
**Scope:** Lanzarote, Yaiza, Playa Blanca and Canary Islands media where the recipient's beat includes local government, tourism, courts, planning, business, public finance or institutional accountability  
**Send gate:** `EMAIL_SEND_FINAL_AUTHORIZATION_RULE.md`

## 1. Purpose

Local-media outreach must not reduce Project Sun Rock to either:

- a historical accusation/litigation bundle; or
- a soft institutional invitation stripped of the underlying documentary story.

Where the evidence supports both, use the following architecture:

`POSITIVE PRESENT-TENSE HOOK → FULL UNITARY DOCUMENTARY MIDDLE → CONSTRUCTIVE LOCAL ENDING / FINITE ASK`

This architecture preserves the entire relevant story while giving a local journalist a current, independently reportable reason to engage.

## 2. Positive present-tense hook

Where current and accurate, open with the documented invitation to the Ayuntamiento de Yaiza and Cabildo de Lanzarote to enter a direct, constructive and competence-respecting working conversation.

The hook may include:

- the separate invitations to Yaiza and the Cabildo;
- the joint invitation;
- the public `Carta abierta a Lanzarote`;
- a proposed preparatory videoconference and later working meeting;
- tourism quality, responsible investment, employment, infrastructure, innovation, talent and sustainable activity; and
- the proposition that institutional clarity may create space for a positive future route.

Do **not** say that either institution has accepted, rejected, ignored, mediated or begun negotiations unless current primary evidence proves that exact state.

The invitation does not ask either administration to decide private title, insolvency, criminal liability or matters outside its competence and does not waive pending rights/proceedings.

## 3. Full unitary middle — do not omit the story

The middle of the local-media email may be long where needed. It should explain, with evidence-status discipline, the relevant unitary chain:

`fragmented Sun Park ownership / exploitation`  
→ `alleged long-term economic, community, access and operational pressure (hotel mobbing / acoso hotelero)`  
→ `creditor / enforcement / insolvency sequence`  
→ `material access/control transition in June 2018`  
→ `later commercialisation / refurbishment / integrated hotel operation`  
→ `RIC Private Equity / investment representations`  
→ `Hotel New Trend / MYND Yaiza`  
→ `RIC indirect/collective materialisation`  
→ `regional incentive GC/836/P06`  
→ `FEDER identification`  
→ `current documentary reconciliation and recovery questions`.

Where economic-crime or fraudulent-materialisation theories are relevant, state them as allegations/reported concerns, never as adjudicated fact unless a competent decision establishes them. Preferred forms include:

- `hemos denunciado hechos que consideramos susceptibles de constituir delitos económicos`;
- `sostenemos que debe investigarse una posible materialización fraudulenta del incentivo fiscal RIC`;
- `esa caracterización permanece pendiente de comprobación institucional`.

Do not write that RIC, regional incentives, FEDER, public-private cooperation, hotel renovation or later commercial success itself proves fraud, double financing or wrongdoing.

## 4. Mandatory local documentary anchors

Where the local story uses the institutional-resolution module, consider the following anchors, subject to current source control:

1. LPB—not the entire horizontally divided hotel—was the debtor in Concurso 36/2012.
2. The 2021 RICPE certificate identified `54 CAM / 190 LPB / 18 terceros` within 262 properties and recorded then-existing conditionality / incomplete diligence states.
3. Yaiza's produced files include the 2022 works/legalisation/occupation sequence and a contemporaneous technical observation that certain works then underway did not correspond to the technical documentation examined and were not then enabled by the examined prior communication; later legalisation/occupation must be preserved as contrary/contextual evidence.
4. The Cabildo identified expediente `3671/2022` as a request to include the `totalidad de las unidades alojativas del establecimiento` in tourist exploitation; the document-led question is who applied, in what capacity, for what units, with what authority/title/consent and what the tourism administration checked.
5. RICPE → HNT/MYND → GC/836/P06 → FEDER is a source-controlled finance/support sequence whose exact cost, source-and-use, title/availability and accumulation reconciliation remains a documentary question.

Every use must distinguish what is verified from what is alleged, inferred, adverse or still missing.

## 5. Adverse-evidence requirement

A strong local-media email must include material contrary evidence where it is necessary to prevent a misleading impression.

For the 2018 control/access track, preserve the current status that the criminal route ended in provisional dismissal confirmed on appeal. Do not present that outcome as a final adjudication of every title, property, administrative or financial issue outside the scope of that proceeding.

Where later legalisation or authorisation exists, do not use an earlier municipal incidence to imply that the final project necessarily remained unregularised.

## 6. Constructive ending

After the full middle, return to the current positive route.

A suitable closing proposition is:

> The immediate local question is whether Yaiza and the Cabildo will identify the competent interlocutors, clarify their own records and consider a coordinated working conversation—each within its own powers—while the documentary and legal questions continue through their proper channels.

The journalist's finite ask may be:

- seek comment from Yaiza and Cabildo;
- confirm receipt/routing of the invitations;
- ask who is competent to respond;
- examine a short primary-source index;
- test one or more public-record contradictions; or
- consider whether the outlet's earlier reporting should be updated or clarified.

## 7. Outlet-own-publication rule

If the recipient/outlet previously published material propositions about Sun Park, MYND Yaiza, RICPE, Canarian Hospitality, Acosta Matos, investment, renovation, jobs or public support, personalise the email around those publications.

Ask separately:

- what was attributed to a company/institution/press release;
- what was independently verified;
- whether the article intended to describe physical/operational transformation rather than unitary registral ownership;
- whether later primary evidence makes a clarification/update useful; and
- whether the outlet wants the short primary-source index.

Publication ≠ independent verification, and a reasonable attributed description of a tourism project is not automatically a legal conclusion about title.

## 8. Newsroom-routing rule

Where no current direct named-journalist work address is verified:

1. use a current official editorial/newsroom address as `To`;
2. where justified, use one current official general or direction address as a limited `Cc` fallback;
3. identify the named journalist/director clearly in the first line;
4. explain that the fallback address is for internal routing, not to imply personal receipt;
5. exclude advertising, subscriptions and unrelated administrative addresses;
6. avoid multiple duplicative newsroom mailboxes unless there is a documented routing reason; and
7. preserve the public source used to verify each route.

A current government/media directory may supplement an outlet's own contact page where the outlet obfuscates the exact address, but prefer the most recent/current source.
