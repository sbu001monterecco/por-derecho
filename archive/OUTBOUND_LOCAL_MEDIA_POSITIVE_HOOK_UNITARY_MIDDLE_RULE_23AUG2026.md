# Project Sun Rock / Por Derecho — Local Media Positive-Hook / Unitary-Middle Rule

**Control date:** 23 August 2026  
**Status:** mandatory local-media narrative rule  
**Scope:** Lanzarote, Yaiza, Playa Blanca and Canary Islands media where the recipient's beat includes local government, tourism, courts, planning, business, public finance or institutional accountability  
**Send gate:** `EMAIL_SEND_FINAL_AUTHORIZATION_RULE.md`

## 1. Purpose

Local-media outreach must not reduce Project Sun Rock to either a historical accusation/litigation bundle or a soft institutional invitation stripped of the underlying documentary story.

Where the evidence supports both, use:

`POSITIVE PRESENT-TENSE HOOK → FULL UNITARY DOCUMENTARY MIDDLE → CONSTRUCTIVE LOCAL ENDING / FINITE ASK`.

## 2. Positive present-tense hook

Where current and accurate, open with the documented invitation to the Ayuntamiento de Yaiza and Cabildo de Lanzarote to enter a direct, constructive and competence-respecting working conversation.

The hook may include the separate and joint invitations, the public `Carta abierta a Lanzarote`, a proposed preparatory videoconference/later working meeting, and tourism quality, responsible investment, employment, infrastructure, innovation and sustainable activity.

Do **not** say either institution has accepted, rejected, ignored, mediated or begun negotiations unless current primary evidence proves that exact state. The invitation does not ask either administration to decide private title, insolvency or criminal liability outside its competence and does not waive pending rights/proceedings.

## 3. Full unitary middle — do not omit the story

The middle may be long where needed. Explain, with evidence-status discipline:

`fragmented Sun Park ownership / exploitation`
→ `alleged long-term economic, community, access and operational pressure (hotel mobbing / acoso hotelero)`
→ `creditor / enforcement / insolvency sequence`
→ `material access/control transition in June 2018`
→ `later commercialisation / refurbishment / integrated hotel operation`
→ `RIC Private Equity / investment representations`
→ `Hotel New Trend / MYND Yaiza`
→ `RIC indirect/collective materialisation`
→ `regional incentive GC/836/P06`
→ `FEDER identification`
→ `current documentary reconciliation and recovery questions`.

Where economic-crime or fraudulent-materialisation theories are relevant, state them as allegations/reported concerns, never as adjudicated fact unless a competent decision establishes them. Suitable forms include:

- `hemos denunciado hechos que consideramos susceptibles de constituir delitos económicos`;
- `sostenemos que debe investigarse una posible materialización fraudulenta del incentivo fiscal RIC`;
- `esa caracterización permanece pendiente de comprobación institucional`.

Do not write that RIC, regional incentives, FEDER, public-private cooperation, hotel renovation or later commercial success itself proves fraud, double financing or wrongdoing.

## 4. Mandatory `54 CAM` provenance gate

The 2021 RICPE certificate's `54 CAM / 190 LPB / 18 terceros` split must **never** be presented as a clean ownership starting point or shorthand for CAM acquiring Sun Park.

Before using `54 CAM`, apply:

`WHICH FINCA → PRIOR OWNER → MINORITY / MONTELANZA / MOLINA / JSP PERIMETER → OPERATING/CEXP POSITION → COMMUNITY DEBT/VOTE/LITIGATION → SELLER AUTHORITY → TRANSFER INSTRUMENT → PRICE/PAYMENT/SET-OFF → REGISTRY → POSSESSION → CAM CAPACITY → PURCHASER KNOWLEDGE → LATER PROJECT REPRESENTATION`.

The narrative must preserve these controls:

- Sun Park operated economically as one hotel while private title remained unit-by-unit.
- AP Judgment 89/2014 recognised individual proprietary/possessory rights in an 18-unit claimant perimeter against Monterecco/Pink's operation. That supports **unit-specific title**, not whole-hotel authority for those owners or a later purchaser.
- The 2015/2016 Community records show disputed debt/voting and accounting conditions capable of affecting bargaining position or marketability: very high represented attendance but very limited voting entitlement, alleged debt above €3.6m, incomplete records, retroactive fee reduction and recalculated balances. This is a reason to investigate sale conditions, **not proof that minorities were forced to sell**.
- JSP 8503–8507 and Montelanza 8499–8500 already carry heightened provenance controls in the repository/public site. The 8499–8500 bridge in particular requires the conditional 2016 route, 2017 Registry status, later CAM schedule, authority, price/payment and registration to be reconciled.
- CAM must not automatically be treated as a purchaser with no prior knowledge. Its changing creditor, specific-owner, Community-participant, project/promoter and later RICPE/HNT-related capacities create a purchaser-knowledge question that must be tested act by act.

The mandatory journalist question is:

> How did a limited 54-property position—whose own seller-by-seller provenance still requires reconstruction—become translated into a public/investor account of Sun Park as an acquired, available and integrated hotel project while the same internal certificate recorded 190 LPB and 18 third-party properties?

Read `archive/CAM_54_PROVENANCE_MINORITY_MARKETABILITY_MEDIA_PROPAGATION_CONTROL_23AUG2026.md` whenever `54 CAM` appears in media outreach.

## 6. Media propagation and causation boundary

Where a media outlet repeated the later integrated project narrative, separate:

`UPSTREAM REPRESENTATION → MEDIA ATTRIBUTION/REPUBLICATION → INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION → KNOWINGLY FALSE PUBLICATION`.

Evidence of an upstream representation or an attributed article does **not** prove the outlet independently verified it or knowingly published a falsehood.

Do not write that CAM/RICPE `caused` an outlet to publish unless the actual press release, email, dossier, briefing or other transmission bridge is recovered. Where an article itself identifies the source—e.g. a Canarian Hospitality presentation or direct RICPE quotations—state only that documented attribution.

For La Voz/EKN, ask what Government/RICPE/Canarian Hospitality/promoter source it received, what was independently checked, whether the article described physical/financial transformation rather than unitary title, and whether the later 54/190/18 evidence warrants clarification/update.

## 7. Adverse-evidence requirement

For the 2018 control/access track, preserve that the criminal route ended in provisional dismissal confirmed on appeal. Do not present that outcome as adjudicating every title, property, administrative or financial issue outside its scope.

Where later legalisation/authorisation exists, do not use an earlier municipal incidence to imply the final project necessarily remained unregularised.

## 8. Constructive ending

After the full middle, return to the current positive route. A suitable closing proposition is:

> The immediate local question is whether Yaiza and the Cabildo will identify competent interlocutors, clarify their own records and consider a coordinated working conversation—each within its powers—while the documentary and legal questions continue through their proper channels.

Finite asks may include: seek institutional comment; confirm routing; identify competent officials; examine a short primary-source index; test a public-record contradiction; or consider whether earlier reporting needs clarification/update.
