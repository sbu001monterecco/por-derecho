## 1. Controlling conclusion

The two procedural families must be analysed together, but they must **not** be collapsed into one proceeding, one actor, one legal capacity or one proven criminal agreement.

The source-controlled working hypothesis is:

> **functional repetition / operator displacement through successive legal mechanisms, with a further hypothesis of reciprocal procedural instrumentalisation and successive adoption.**

### Track A — private-owner route

**JPI no. 4 Arrecife · JV 1260/2011 → Rollo 793/2012 → Audiencia Provincial de Las Palmas · Sentencia 89/2014.**

A private-owner perimeter litigated over 18 identified units. The claim was later extended against Monterecco Sun Park, S.L. after an asserted change in exploitation. First instance found insufficient proof of Monterecco possession/operation of the claimant units. AP 89/2014 reversed that result, treated Monterecco as possessing/operating the 18 units, accepted the extension against it and ordered CEXP/Monterecco to surrender those units to the claimants.

That is a materially adverse judicial result. Its scope remains bounded: it does not adjudicate universal whole-hotel title, extinguish every CEXP right, decide every non-claimant unit, establish a valid universal succession from CEXP to Monterecco, determine the whole later Community authority structure, or decide criminal responsibility.

### Track B — insolvency-administration / institutional route

The later route includes:

- the 6-Feb-2012 LPB–Monterecco/Pink operating agreement;
- the AC's 17-Jan-2014 contract-resolution/rent claim;
- the 24-Nov-2015 Pink judgment and later execution;
- the distinct 22-May-2017 possession/key narrative;
- Auto 804/2018 and the AC authority/testimony relied upon there;
- the AC's 11-Feb-2019 calificación report;
- Ministerio Fiscal participation in the calificación section;
- Sentencia 163/2023 and its proposition-specific adoption/rejection of AC theories;
- later appeals and connected AC-removal proceedings.

### The criminal-law bridge

The strongest legally disciplined bridge is **not**:

> “all actors were members of one original conspiracy”.

It is:

> **an earlier private or institutional act may become a later actor's criminally relevant instrument if the later actor knowingly adopts, supplies, authenticates, deploys, conceals or materially relies upon a deceptive or unlawful proposition/instrument and thereby makes an actor-specific causal contribution to a later offence or unlawful patrimonial effect.**

That is a **successive-adoption** model. It permits investigation of later knowing use without inventing an original pact. It equally permits the evidence to show independent lawful conduct, mere convergence, legal error, negligent investigation, good-faith reliance or no criminality.

Permitted edge labels:

- **DIRECT DOWNSTREAM USE**

- **SHARED FACTUAL DEPENDENCY**
- **TEMPORAL PREDECESSOR ONLY**

- **CONTRADICTED**

## 3. Six legal planes that must never be collapsed

The same factual event can matter in several legal domains without one domain proving another.

1. **Civil title / possession / precario** — who owns or is entitled to possess the particular unit.
2. **Tourist-operation authority** — who may lawfully exploit the establishment as a tourist undertaking under Canary law.
3. **Horizontal-property governance** — Community debt, voting, office, access to common elements and owner duties.
4. **Insolvency administration** — LPB estate, AC duties, preservation, liquidation, claims and calificación.
5. **Judicial/prosecutorial process** — what each court/Fiscal actually received, decided, adopted or rejected.
6. **Criminal responsibility** — offence-specific act, authorship/participation, mens rea, causation, result and temporal law.

**Rule:** a conclusion valid on plane 1 does not silently answer plane 2; a Community resolution on plane 3 does not create title on plane 1; AC/Fiscal adoption on planes 4–5 does not prove criminal intent on plane 6.

## 4. Private title and tourist unity can both be true

A central source of analytical error is to force the Sun Park facts into only one of two propositions:

1. individual registered owners had real property/possessory rights; or
2. Sun Park was economically and administratively structured as a tourist establishment requiring unity of exploitation.

Both can be legally relevant at the same time.

### 4.1 Civil possession / precario

Article 250 LEC supplies the verbal-proceeding route for recovery of possession by a person claiming entitlement to possess property ceded in precario. JV 1260/2011 therefore had a legally recognisable private-possession vehicle.

The fact that the complex functioned economically as a hotel does not erase each claimant's registered title or every possible possessory remedy.

### 4.2 Canary tourism law

Ley 7/1995, de Ordenación del Turismo de Canarias, contains the principle of **unidad de explotación** for tourist accommodation establishments. The historical/current statutory family separates:

- ownership of units;
- the title empowering the tourist operator;
- unitary commercial/administrative operation;
- units excluded from lawful tourist offer where statutory conditions are not met.

Official 2012 Canary materials applying the then regulatory scheme also treated the identity of the operating company and the percentage of units under one operator as legally material.

Therefore AP 89/2014 can be authoritative within its decided possession dispute as to the 18 units without becoming a universal holding that the entire hotel ceased to be an integrated tourist establishment or that any owner could independently operate a hotel unit touristically outside the applicable tourist-law structure.

### Analysis rule T-1 — title is not operating authority

**Registered title to a unit ≠ universal authority over hotel exploitation.**

### Analysis rule T-2 — unity of exploitation is not confiscation of title

**Tourist unity ≠ extinction of private title or all possessory rights.**

### Analysis rule T-3 — the criminal question is the representation made to the court

For Track A, the relevant question is whether the claimant side accurately disclosed the legally material operating structure, CEXP instruments, accession status, keys, actual possession, tourism regime and the change to Monterecco; or whether any actor knowingly manipulated a material evidential proposition in a way capable of causing judicial error.

An incomplete legal theory is not automatically a crime.

## 5. Community law: debt → voting exclusion can be lawful or can be an instrument, depending on proof

Ley de Propiedad Horizontal art. 15.2 allows an owner who is not current in **matured Community debts** to participate in deliberation but not vote, unless the debt has been judicially challenged or judicially/notarially consigned in the statutory circumstances. The acta must identify owners deprived of voting rights and their person/quota is not counted for majorities.

Article 16 links meeting notices to delinquency/voting information; article 18 provides mechanisms to challenge resolutions, including for illegality, serious prejudice or abuse.

### Consequence

The chain:

**debt → voting exclusion → minority voting pool → appointment / certification / litigation**

is not criminal merely because it exists. It becomes criminally material only if evidence shows, actor by actor, one or more of the following:

- the debt did not exist, was not matured, belonged to a different legal person, duplicated operating charges or lacked Community authority;
- a certificate or ledger was materially falsified;
- voting exclusions were knowingly applied despite payment, challenge, consignment or lack of legal entitlement;
- coefficients/proxies/attendance were manipulated;
- the resulting resolution was then knowingly used to obtain a judicial or patrimonial result through deception.

### Analysis rule C-1 — authority before arithmetic

Before accepting any “Community debt” number, prove:

**creditor identity → legal basis → debtor identity → period → maturity → invoices/ledger → Community resolution/authority → payment/credit/set-off → challenge/consignment → certificate author/capacity → use in voting/litigation/concurso.**

### Analysis rule C-2 — preserve the 2012 incoming-side error

The incoming Monterecco/Pink side's admitted early use of an incorrect Community/maintenance-type charging mechanism must remain visible. It cannot be suppressed. But that mistake also cannot be transformed into legal validation of later Community debt certificates, office-holder authority or voting exclusions.

## 6. Insolvency law: the AC had affirmative duties, but breach is not automatically criminal

The applicable historical framework is primarily Ley 22/2003 Concursal, version by version.

### 6.1 Diligence and loyalty

Article 35 required the insolvency administration to perform with the diligence of an **ordenado administrador y representante leal** and placed its functions under judicial supervision.

Article 36 imposed civil liability for damage to the debtor/estate caused by acts or omissions contrary to law or lacking due diligence.

### 6.2 Conservation and business continuity

Articles 43 and 44 made conservation/administration of the estate and continuation of business relevant insolvency objectives, subject to the procedural regime then in force.

After liquidation, article 145 altered the debtor's administration/disposition powers and the AC's role.

### 6.3 Liquidation architecture

Articles 148 and 149 required a liquidation plan and, where feasible, gave legal significance to sale of establishments/exploitation/production units as a whole rather than assuming atomised disposal was always preferable.

This does not mean every whole-hotel rescue proposal had to be accepted. It does mean that fragmentation, unitary-operation value, competitive alternatives and value preservation were legally material insolvency questions.

### 6.4 Calificación

Historical article 169 required a **reasoned and documented** AC report concerning the relevant facts and, where culpability was alleged, identification/justification of affected persons/complices and damages. Ministerio Fiscal then participated through its statutory opinion.

Articles 170–172 governed the resulting contradictory process and judgment.

### Critical firewall

The Ley Concursal maintained separation between insolvency **calificación** and criminal responsibility. A culpable-insolvency judgment does not itself establish a crime; conversely a criminal investigation is not legally dependent on a prior civil calificación finding.

### Analysis rule I-1 — AC knowledge must be date-specific

For every alleged omission or adoption by the AC, prove what was actually in his file and when:

**received source → date → exact proposition → contrary source → legal duty → practical power → act/omission → explanation → later effect.**

### Analysis rule I-2 — AC reliance is not neutral simply because it came from another actor

The AC may lawfully rely on information supplied by owners, the Community, creditors or professionals. But if a materially disputed proposition was decisive, the forensic question is whether he tested it to the degree required by his duty and whether there is proof of knowing adoption of falsity rather than mere error, negligence or contested legal judgment.

### Analysis rule I-3 — no “institutional laundering” presumption

A private proposition does not become true because the AC repeats it. An AC proposition does not become true because Fiscalía repeats it. A proposition does not become fact beyond its adjudicated scope because a judgment later uses it.

Each institutional adoption is a new evidential node.

## 9. Documentary falsity — arts. 390–396 CP

Documentary falsity must be analysed by:

**document class → author status → mode of falsification → authenticity/identity/content → use → knowledge → harm.**

### Public authority / official-document falsity

Article 390 contains several falsification modes for authority/public officials, including alteration, simulation, attribution of non-existent intervention/statements and false narration.

### Private actor and public/official/mercantile documents

Article 392 applies to a private person who commits the **first three** falsification modes of article 390.1. Mere mendacious narrative by a private person, without alteration/simulation/false attribution of authorship or intervention, is not automatically documentary falsity under article 392.

Supreme Court doctrine has repeatedly treated purely ideological false narration by a private person as outside article 392 when authenticity has not been falsified.

### Private documents

Articles 395–396 require their own elements and harmful purpose/knowing use.

### Consequence for this case

Do **not** write “falsedad documental” merely because a pleading, letter or certificate contains a disputed proposition.

First identify:

- was the document physically/legally altered?
- was a document simulated?
- was a person falsely shown as intervening?
- were declarations falsely attributed?
- was the author a public official acting in office?
- was the document later knowingly used?

### Rule D-1 — separate four things

**false fact allegation ≠ false testimony ≠ false document ≠ procedural fraud.**

They may overlap, but they have different elements.

## 10. False testimony / expert falsehood — arts. 458–460 CP

Where a material proposition came from a witness, expert or interpreter in judicial proceedings, distinguish:

- article 458: witness knowingly false;
- article 459: maliciously false expert/interpreter opinion;
- article 460: reticence, inaccuracies or concealment of relevant facts in the legally defined circumstances.

This is potentially relevant to:

- sworn testimony about possession, keys, authority or access;
- expert/accounting evidence concerning debt, valuation or rent;
- any judicial testimony by an actor who had first-hand knowledge.

It is **not** automatically applicable to lawyer advocacy or an ordinary party pleading.

Article 252 concerns a person with powers to administer another's patrimony arising from law, authority or legal transaction who exceeds those powers and thereby causes patrimonial damage.

The AC undeniably had statutory functions over LPB's estate. But that does not automatically satisfy article 252.

Required proof:

**specific administered patrimony → exact power/duty → act beyond that authority or disloyal exercise → causation → quantified damage → mens rea → defence/justification.**

- access/control authorisations;
- asset preservation;
- disposals;

- estate versus third-party assets;

## 13. Judicial prevaricación — arts. 446–449 CP

The judge must be analysed separately from the private actors and AC.

Article 446 requires a judge/magistrate to issue an **unjust** judgment/resolution **knowing** its injustice. Article 447 addresses manifestly unjust resolution through gross negligence/inexcusable ignorance. Articles 448–449 concern refusal to decide or malicious delay in their respective terms.

Supreme Court doctrine requires far more than:

- an adverse result;
- a legal error;
- a decision later reversed;
- judicial acceptance of one party's evidence.

### Required judicial test

For each challenged resolution:

1. exact judicial proposition;
2. competent legal rule and factual record;
3. material contrary evidence actually before the judge;
4. degree of legal/factual departure;
5. whether there is a rationally arguable interpretation;
6. evidence of knowledge of injustice;
7. causal/patrimonial effect;
8. appellate treatment;
9. exculpatory evidence.

### Important contrary evidence

Sentencia 163/2023 did **not** simply adopt everything requested by the AC. The controlled record shows at least one AC economic-rationality branch was rejected and the separate deficit request was not accepted for lack of sufficient justification, while other Pink/non-enforcement propositions were adopted.

That cuts against any undifferentiated assertion that “the judge merely rubber-stamped the AC”. It does not immunise any particular proposition. It makes the correct analysis **proposition-specific**.

### Rule J-1

**Judicial adoption ≠ prior coordination.**

### Rule J-2

**Repeated adverse decisions ≠ knowing injustice unless the objective and subjective prevaricación elements are proved for the particular resolution.**

## 14. Commission by omission — art. 11 CP

The user's theory includes institutional omissions. Criminal omission cannot be built from “they should have done more”.

Article 11 requires a special duty to act and legal equivalence between non-prevention and causation in the circumstances defined by law, including a specific legal/contractual duty or creation of the risk.

For each omission hypothesis:

**actor → legal position → exact duty → knowledge → practical capacity to act → required act → omission → equivalence to causing result → mens rea/negligence permitted by offence → result → lawful explanation.**

### AC

Potential guarantor duties derive from the insolvency statutory role, but equivalence and offence-specific mens rea remain separate.

### Judge

Judicial duties are controlled by jurisdiction/process. Failure to adopt a complainant's preferred investigation or legal view is not by itself commission by omission.

### Ministerio Fiscal / other authority

Any omission theory requires the exact statutory duty in the exact capacity and proceeding.

## 15. Participation — arts. 27–29 CP

Spanish criminal responsibility is individual.

Relevant modes include:

- direct/co-authorship;
- inducement;
- necessary cooperation;
- complicity.

### Critical temporal rule

A later person who merely receives a benefit from an earlier offence is not thereby an author/accomplice of that earlier offence.

The later person may be independently liable only if evidence establishes:

- prior or simultaneous inducement/cooperation;
- knowing later use constituting a new offence;
- concealment/handling/laundering or another applicable offence;
- participation in a continuing offence where legal requirements are met.

### Rule PAR-1 — benefit is not participation

**benefit + relationship + chronology ≠ authorship or complicity.**

### Rule PAR-2 — successive adoption can create a new offence

A later actor may still become independently criminally relevant if, with the required knowledge and intent, they deploy the earlier false instrument in a new judicial, patrimonial or concealment act.

This is the principal legal mechanism for testing “reciprocal instrumentalisation” without inventing an original pact.

An adverse judicial or AC act that benefits a private actor is **not evidence of bribery by itself**.

## 18. Money laundering — art. 301 CP

Later acquisition, operation, financing or refinancing of assets connected to Sun Park does not become money laundering merely because upstream proceedings are challenged.

The laundering hypothesis requires:

- identified property/proceeds;
- criminal origin/predicate;
- the actor's knowledge in the legally required form;
- acquisition/possession/use/conversion/transfer/concealment conduct within article 301;
- traceable value flow.

### Rule AML-1

**First prove predicate criminal proceeds; then trace the asset/value; then prove knowledge.**

Do not reason backwards from later wealth, financing or ownership to predicate criminal guilt.

## 19. Limitation and temporal law

No single limitation period governs this system.

For every offence candidate build:

**offence → date/continuing period → maximum statutory penalty under applicable version → article 131 period → article 132 starting/interruption events → any procedural direction against a determined person → later more-favourable law analysis → status.**

A civil limitation/review deadline is not the same as criminal prescription.

For AP 89/2014, the extraordinary civil review route under LEC arts. 510–512 has strict grounds and time limits. The ordinary five-year outer limit for review of a final civil judgment is therefore a major obstacle to treating revision as an open present-day remedy for a 2014 judgment absent an exceptional legally available route. That does not itself prevent evidential examination of the historical proceeding in a later criminal or other competent investigation.

---

## 20. LEC review / maquinación fraudulenta as comparator

Article 510 LEC permits review of a final judgment on defined grounds including decisive documents, false documentary/testimonial/expert evidence and a judgment unjustly obtained through bribery, violence or fraudulent machination.

Article 512 imposes stringent time limits.

The civil concept of **maquinación fraudulenta** can help identify what kind of deceptive procedural conduct matters, but:

- it has its own civil-review requirements;
- it is not identical to estafa procesal;
- expiry of civil review does not retrospectively prove the judgment correct;
- criminal liability still requires the separate Penal Code elements and limitation analysis.

## 23. Strongest prosecution hypothesis

Subject to proof, the strongest integrated hypothesis is:

> A historically disputed owner/Community structure produced possession, authority, debt and operator propositions. Some of those propositions obtained judicial or institutional force in one proceeding. Later actors may then have reused, broadened or operationalised them in a different proceeding. The criminal issue is whether any actor knowingly transformed a bounded or false proposition into a deceptive instrument that caused a new judicial/patrimonial result, and whether later actors knowingly adopted it rather than independently and lawfully reaching the same conclusion.

This avoids two weaknesses:

1. requiring proof of a single secret agreement beginning in 2011; and
2. treating mere repeated adverse outcomes as proof of crime.

## 25. New mandatory rules of analysis

### CRIM-XPROC-01 — one economic system, separate legal subjects
Analyse the hotel as one economic platform while preserving unit, owner, CEXP, Comunidad, LPB, Pink, Matkator, creditor, AC and court identities.

### CRIM-XPROC-02 — successive adoption
A later actor need not have joined an original plan to become relevant. Test whether the actor knowingly adopted or redeployed a deceptive/unlawful instrument in a later act.

### CRIM-XPROC-03 — no transferred mens rea
Knowledge, intent and purpose never transfer through family, corporate, professional, creditor or procedural relationships.

### CRIM-XPROC-04 — proposition provenance
Every important adverse proposition must carry an origin, first source, later users and exact changes in wording/scope.

### CRIM-XPROC-05 — bounded judgments
A judgment is authoritative for what it decided. Never inflate AP89's 18-unit outcome into whole-hotel authority or Auto804 into title/tourism adjudication.

### CRIM-XPROC-06 — institutional adoption is a new node
AC, Fiscalía and judge are separate receiving actors. Each adoption/rejection must be mapped independently.

### CRIM-XPROC-07 — legal error firewall
Do not call disputed interpretation, negligence or adverse adjudication “crime” without satisfying the offence elements.

### CRIM-XPROC-08 — document taxonomy
Classify disputed material as pleading, private document, official document, certificate, testimony, expert report, accounting record, acta or judgment before selecting a falsity offence.

### CRIM-XPROC-09 — omission requires guarantor analysis
No criminal omission theory without exact duty, knowledge, practical power, equivalence and causation.

### CRIM-XPROC-10 — benefit follows offence, not vice versa
Trace benefit after identifying a potentially unlawful act. Do not infer the predicate offence from later benefit.

### CRIM-XPROC-11 — temporal criminal law
Apply the Penal Code version in force at the act date and compare later law only under article 2.2.

### CRIM-XPROC-12 — contrary-evidence parity
Every inculpatory retrieval queue must carry an equally explicit contrary/exculpatory retrieval queue.

### CRIM-XPROC-13 — no double counting
The same key event, debt figure, unit transfer or judgment cannot be counted as multiple independent corroborations merely because repeated in later documents.

### CRIM-XPROC-14 — criminal/civil remedy separation
Civil review, nullity, appeal, damages, AC liability, disciplinary routes and criminal liability each have independent elements and limitation periods.

### CRIM-XPROC-15 — proof-grade language
Use: DOCUMENTED / JUDICIAL HOLDING / PARTY ALLEGATION / EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE / OPEN / CONTRADICTED / NOT ESTABLISHED.

### CRIM-XPROC-16 — successor-benefit firewall
A later successor, purchaser, creditor or operator is not retrospectively responsible for an earlier proceeding merely because it benefits from the result. Prove prior/simultaneous participation or a later independent knowing-use offence.

### CRIM-XPROC-17 — proposition expansion test
Whenever a later source relies on an earlier judgment, compare the exact proposition actually decided with the broader proposition later asserted. Treat any scope expansion as a separate evidential event.

### Key doctrinal controls

- **Estafa procesal:** manipulation/deception must cause judicial error and a prejudicial resolution; adverse advocacy alone is insufficient.
- **Falsedad privada in official/mercantile documents:** article 392 does not automatically criminalise a private person's merely mendacious narrative; identify alteration/simulation/false attribution.
- **Prevaricación judicial:** objective injustice plus the requisite subjective knowledge; reversal/error is insufficient.
- **Temporal law:** article 2 CP prohibits retroactive aggravation and requires comparison with later more-favourable law.
- **Prescription:** articles 131–132 require offence-specific penalty/date/interruption analysis; no unitary clock.

## 28. Bottom line

The records support treating the two procedural families as one **forensic dependency system** because they concern the same hotel platform, overlapping authority/possession/operator propositions and later institutional reuse. They do **not** support erasing the legal boundaries between the cases or declaring a collective criminal enterprise as established.

The most productive criminal-first theory is:

**bounded private litigation → proposition/evidence production → later knowledge and institutional adoption → broader operational/patrimonial effect → later private benefit/control**, tested one arrow and one actor at a time.

The most important open discriminator remains **knowledge**: who knew that a proposition was false, materially truncated, bounded, superseded, duplicated or legally inapplicable at the moment they used it, and what did that use cause?

Until that discriminator is proved, the correct classifications are investigative hypotheses, not findings of guilt.
