# CORRECTION REGISTER ADDENDUM — MATKATOR 8584 HOTEL TITLE / REMATE / RESTITUTION

**Addendum ID:** `PD-CR-ADD-MAT8584-20260903`  
**Date:** 3 September 2026  
**Status:** controlling addendum; read with `archive/CORRECTION_REGISTER.md`

## CR-MAT8584-001 — analytical object

**Old/incomplete:** finca 8584 described analytically as an apartment or isolated real-estate unit.  
**Corrected:** its historic registry description may remain, but the current analytical object is a registered title component inside a regulated, functionally unified hotel establishment.

## CR-MAT8584-002 — rights bundle

**Old/incomplete:** title treated as bare ownership of a physical room.  
**Corrected:** title is the legal anchor for property/common-element, possession/access, governance, hotel-exploitation authority, accounting/revenue, evidential standing and restitution/restoration rights, subject to title/contract/statutory proof.

## CR-MAT8584-003 — timing of prejudice

**Old/incomplete:** prejudice depends upon a later cession of the remate.  
**Corrected:** adjudication itself may impair title-based standing, fragment past/current/future injuries, introduce a claimed third-party registered position and make restoration or restitution harder.

## CR-MAT8584-004 — beneficiary identity

**Old/incomplete:** beneficiary described simply as unknown.  
**Corrected:** the formal adjudicatario/cesionario remains unproved; the specific evidence-based terminal-beneficiary hypothesis is the CAM/HNT/Canarian Hospitality/Acosta Matos-controlled MYND Yaiza perimeter because it is alleged to control and exploit the integrated hotel space. This is an inference, not proof of cession, nominee arrangement or concert.

## CR-MAT8584-005 — restitution

**Old/incomplete:** absence of recovery to date used as an indicator that restitution is impossible or commercially irrelevant.  
**Corrected:** Matkator alleges that the absence of effective inspection, accounting, possession and restoration is itself the result of challenged procedural, concursal and institutional conduct. That allegation requires proof and cannot silently become a presumption legitimising the status quo.

## CR-MAT8584-006 — insolvency boundary

**Old/incomplete:** because 8584 is extraconcursal, it has no relationship to Concurso 36/2012.  
**Corrected:** the title remains outside LPB’s estate, but its forced transfer may have productive-unit, valuation, claim, recovery, evidence, funded-exit and accountability consequences that must be mapped without importing the title into masa activa.

## CR-MAT8584-007 — Acosta Matos multitrack

**Old/incomplete:** Acosta/HNT treated only as remote background to ETJ/DP748.  
**Corrected:** the perimeter is the specific alleged current controller/operator and the dominant real-beneficiary hypothesis for legal consolidation, while criminal agreement, formal recipient and individual intent remain unproved.

## CR-MAT8584-008 — Cuatrecasas attribution

**Old/incomplete:** criminal significance framed solely as the lawful request for adjudication/cession.  
**Corrected:** lawful procedural mechanisms are not criminal by themselves. The finite question is whether later machinery was used with actor-specific knowledge and materially complete disclosure of the hotel-title, possession, exploitation, beneficiary and restitution consequences.
