## Completeness and non-inference rules / Reglas de exhaustividad y no inferencia

- An email address, copied recipient, billing contact, proposal, invoice or meeting entry does not by itself establish a retained substantive mandate.
- A firm name does not establish the identity of the individual author or decision-maker.
- A professional's role, client, proceeding and period must be recorded separately.
- Proposal-only and review-candidate entries must never be described as retained former counsel until primary evidence supports that conclusion.
- Omission does not prove absence. Archive backfill remains open and corrections update the same professional record rather than creating silent duplicates.
- Public profile and display-mode decisions remain controlled separately under the person-perimeter policy.
