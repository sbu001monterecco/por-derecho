4.2 Gil Marer and CEXP offices

- The located notarised/apostilled instrument dated 1 December 2011 appoints
  Gil Marer as “Director and Sole Administrator” for an indefinite period. It
  does not use the office “President”. The grantor's authority and compatibility
  with CEXP statutes and resolutions remain to be verified.
- The first located express CEXP presidential appointment is 28 August 2014.
- The located CEXP minutes record re-election on 7 April 2017 for a stated
  three-year period ending in April 2020.
- Article 14 of the located statutes states one-year terms for President,
  Secretary and Treasurer. The conflict with the three-year language in the
  2014 and 2017 minutes remains unresolved.
- No duly convened, notified, constituted and voted CEXP removal or replacement
  has been located. That absence is important evidence but does not, by itself,
  prove continuing or current representative authority.
- Gil Marer should not be described as “President since 2012”. The accurate
  formulation is: management appointment in December 2011; express presidency
  from August 2014; documented re-election in April 2017; present authority not
  claimed and term/removal questions reserved.

4.3 Matkator and standing

- Matkator is documented as a separate CEXP participant and as a separate,
  non-insolvent property holder.

  not be collapsed into LPB, the Owners' Community, CEXP, Pink Canary Services,
