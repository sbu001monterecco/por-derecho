# ALBERTO LÓPEZ VILLARRUBIA — UNITARY ALLEGATION LEDGER

**Concurso Ordinario 36/2012 · 16 August 2026**  
**Status:** `INTERNAL UNITARY ALLEGATION MAP / SOURCE-CLASSIFIED / NOT A FINDING OF CRIMINAL LIABILITY`

## Purpose

This is the controlling non-fragmented map of allegations made by Gil Marer / Aweswell / LPB concerning Magistrado-Juez **Alberto López Villarrubia** in and around Concurso Ordinario 36/2012.

The allegations must be read as **one evidence system**, not as disconnected complaints about isolated orders. Remedies remain jurisdictionally separated: appellate merits to the competent appellate court; discipline/functioning to CGPJ; criminal allegations to the competent Fiscalía/TSJ route; LAJ/office acts require separate attribution; administrative/regulatory issues remain with their competent authorities.

Evidence labels: **VERIFIED/DOCUMENTED FACT**, **PARTY ALLEGATION**, **EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE**, **UNRESOLVED QUESTION**, **COUNTEREVIDENCE/LIMIT**.

## 1. Controlling unitary allegation

`PARTY ALLEGATION`

Gil Marer alleges that Alberto López Villarrubia's conduct cannot fairly be assessed as a collection of unrelated adverse rulings. The allegation is of a **continuous asymmetric judicial mechanism** in which, from 2018 onward, the court was repeatedly placed on direct or legally relevant notice of matters concerning: a funded/financable route to pay or consign liabilities and conclude/narrow the insolvency; disputed CAM/Acosta Matos credit quantum and interest; CAM/related material control, access and works before final title; the 2018 OB REM/locales transaction later not convalidated; preservation and valuation of the estate; the insolvency administrator's accounts, fees, conflicts and litigation choices; later calificación accusations and procedural fairness; and the downstream private-actor consequences of the judicial narrative.

Gil alleges that protective, supervisory, contradictory and evidential mechanisms were repeatedly refused, deferred, fragmented or closed without a sufficiently traceable decisional bridge, while decisions carrying adverse consequences for Gil/LPB/Pink and favorable practical consequences for CAM/Acosta Matos became progressively difficult to reverse. He further alleges that selected AC/Fiscal propositions were eventually **positively adopted as judicial findings** in Sentencia 163/2023 and used to impose severe professional and patrimonial consequences.

Gil alleges that this pattern was **knowingly unjust / prevaricatory** and functionally operated in favor of private actors, particularly the Acosta Matos perimeter. In the specific €3,032,010.34 calificación award, Gil alleges that the AC and Judge knowingly fabricated/falsified the amount for direct or indirect personal gain kept clandestine.

**Boundary:** the record documents many underlying acts, notices, rulings, allegations and effects. It does **not yet establish** the required criminal mens rea, a corrupt agreement, an intimate friendship, a payment/benefit to the Judge, or an actor-specific procurement chain by which a private actor caused a knowingly unjust judicial result.

## 2. Connected modules of the one alleged mechanism

### 2.1 Knowledge / notice

`DOCUMENTED + SOURCE-COMPLETION REQUIRED`

The controlled criminal complaint correctly separates: (a) direct judicial knowledge from filed writings, hearings, incorporated reports and rulings; (b) matters known to the AC that should have been checked/reported, where judicial receipt must be proved; and (c) external context that does not establish historical judicial knowledge unless incorporated.

The June–September 2018 Irigoyen/Parrilla email chain is relied on as contemporaneous evidence of meetings/calls about a financed conclusion route, debt quantum and the effect of the ~€13.1m figure on third-party bids. These emails are **not judicial minutes** and require authentication and cross-checking against docket entries, filings and witness evidence.

### 2.2 Funded/financable exit allegedly frustrated

`PARTY ALLEGATION / EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE`

Gil alleges that operator, valuation, due diligence and financing work showed a materially structured rescue/payment route in 2018, not merely an embryonic idea. The allegation is not that the Judge had to approve a commercial proposal. It is that the official record should identify the judicial treatment: filing/proveído, transfer to AC, corrected liability figure, deadline, protection, decision or reasoned rejection. The alleged absence of a temporally useful route is context for later decisions, not a stand-alone crime by omission.

### 2.3 CAM credit, interest and alleged circular closure

`DOCUMENTED DISPUTE / PARTY ALLEGATION OF KNOWING ARBITRARINESS`

The theory distinguishes recognized credit, contractual debt, remuneratory/default interest, mortgage-liability maximum, privilege/classification, third-party improvement threshold, price/counter-performance, waiver and any remnant.

Gil alleges: April-2018 rulings contemplated later interest liquidation; CAM's May-2018 private/notarial balance acts were used to build a figure near €13.1m for third-party competition; the AP 2019 ruling concerned the improvement threshold rather than final liquidation/classification; the 12-May-2020 order combined closure/res judicata reasoning with future definitive fixing; and later rulings closed the issue without a traceable contradictory liquidation/classification bridge.

The Parodi/Talleres Jinámar comparison is a **knowledge comparator**, not automatic proof: Gil alleges the same Judge elsewhere applied the 2019 Supreme Court interest doctrine and rejected an overbroad res judicata treatment of unliquidated interest. Factual/legal comparability must be tested.

### 2.4 2018 OB REM/locales transaction and failure to neutralize effects

`DOCUMENTED CORE / IMPLEMENTATION GAP OPEN`

The 28-Nov-2018 transaction concerning 29 premises and two solariums was later addressed by the court. The 24-Oct-2019 order is relied upon as recognizing that the separate transaction did not conform to the unitary liquidation structure and could not be convalidated as presented.

The unitary allegation is that, once the court identified the non-convalidation problem, there should be a traceable implementation chain: restitution, registry correction/cancellation, OB REM restoration, possession/inventory correction, €400,000 accounting treatment, and a clear definition of what later entered the 2021 competition. A valid restitution/replacement-title chain would narrow this allegation.

### 2.5 Asset protection, material control, works and evidence preservation

`PARTY ALLEGATION WITH DOCUMENTED APPLICATIONS`

Gil/Aweswell allege CAM/related actors acquired material access/control and undertook works/alterations before final title while LPB assets remained under concursal protection. In 2021 Aweswell sought independent inspection/pericial/protective measures; the Judge rejected them. Rejection is not criminal by itself; the issue is the reasonableness of that response when viewed with alleged loss of access, physical alteration, the 2019 non-convalidation issue and approaching adjudication.

### 2.6 2021 competition, third-party bidder/ONA and alleged material predetermination

`PARTY ALLEGATION / SOURCE-COMPLETE LICITATION RECORD REQUIRED`

Gil alleges formal bidding did not equal materially equal competition if CAM combined creditor position, possession/access, information, registral/functional pieces and a heterogeneous credit/waiver offer while outsiders needed fresh capital and faced uncertainty. The third-party bidder's reported €14.8m-plus interest and ONA access/personation are evidential leads. Exact edict, timestamps, LexNET service, data-room/inspection access, deposits, full offers and comparative methodology must be reconstructed before calling the competition rigged as fact.

### 2.7 2022 adjudication, title and transaction-identity bridge

`DOCUMENTARY RECONCILIATION GAP`

Differences among ~€9.052m, ~€12.251m, ~€13.166m, ~€13.168m, €7.065m, €5.186m and €400k do not themselves prove fraud because the figures may represent different concepts. The unresolved question is whether a continuous court-authorized chain links recognized credit, interest liquidation/classification, CAM offer, waiver, third-party threshold, exact property perimeter, adjudication, completion/clarification, testimonios, deed, extinguished credit, accounts and remnant.

Gil alleges the Judge knowingly permitted a materially different transaction to become title without the required prior decisional reconciliation. A complete certified chain could rebut that allegation.

### 2.8 AC supervision as an enduring judicial responsibility

`DOCUMENTED 2025 SUPERVISION DISPUTE / BROADER MERITS ALLEGED`

The AC-removal/civil-liability corpus alleges: years without annual accounts after suspension of debtor powers; opaque/inadequate reporting; disputed liquidation fees and interest; failure/obstruction concerning the Bankia/CaixaBank financial-risk action; obstruction of the real assignment-price inquiry; harmful asset realization; failure to preserve unitary hotel operation; professional-conflict issues; and attempts to delegitimize Aweswell scrutiny.

A 20-Mar-2025 court-office Diligencia is relied upon for the proposition that no order exonerating annual accounts and no annual-account documentation was located in the reviewed proceedings.

The 12-Sep-2025 and 11-Nov-2025 rulings are central. The 13-Jan-2026 Fiscalía complaint says the Judge acknowledged ex-officio supervisory powers yet declined to examine each challenged AC act because he did not perceive indicia activating those powers. Gil characterizes this as: **no examination because no indicia; no indicia because the detailed allegations are not examined.** Whether this is legal error, disciplinary deficiency or criminal injustice must remain separated.

### 2.9 Calificación as positive judicial adoption

`DOCUMENTED JUDICIAL FINDINGS + PARTY ALLEGATION OF KNOWING FALSEHOOD`

Sentencia 163/2023 is a separate accountability event because the Judge did not merely leave AC allegations unanswered. In Fundamento Cuarto he adopted selected propositions as judicial findings: Pink/LPB `connivencia`; Pink paying no rent; Gil at least grossly negligent and potentially intentional in the non-claim; and a causal nexus with aggravation of LPB insolvency.

For those propositions, the adjudicative finding belongs to the judgment. The Judge did **not** adopt all AC allegations; that counterevidence must remain visible. The allegation is that he selected and adopted the surviving propositions that produced the operative punishment.

### 2.10 €3,032,010.34 fabrication/personal-gain allegation

`DOCUMENTED AWARD / EXPRESS PARTY ALLEGATION OF CRIME`

The judgment says the AC requested €3,032,010.34 as the principal for which execution of the 24-Nov-2015 rent judgment had been dispatched under a 6-Sep-2016 order. It reasons that because execution against Pink found no attachable property, the damage was correctly quantified as that principal and `nunca se cobrará`, and orders Gil to pay €3,032,010.34 plus legal interest increased by two points.

Gil expressly alleges that **Francisco de Borja Rodríguez-Batllori and Alberto López Villarrubia knowingly fabricated and/or falsified that amount for direct or indirect personal gain kept clandestine**. Preserve that as Gil's allegation, not as an adjudicated fact.

Proof targets: reconstruct the rent judgment/execution principal euro-by-euro; identify rent/payments/credits/set-offs/maintenance and legal basis; identify what the AC knew and what was before the Judge; explain the bridge from €24,575/month/relevant period to execution amount to calificación damages; trace any alleged benefit route actor-by-actor. The clandestine-benefit route is **not verified**.

### 2.13 Impartiality/contact allegation — Laura Patricia Acosta Matos

`CONTEMPORANEOUS WITNESS-STATEMENT EVIDENCE / RELATIONSHIP UNPROVED`

### 2.15 Criminal threshold / proposition-by-proposition knowledge test

`CONTROL RULE`

For every alleged unjust judicial proposition ask: (1) what exact fact/legal proposition did the Judge adopt/refuse; (2) what primary evidence/law contradicted it then; (3) what proves that contradictory material was before/known to the Judge; (4) what reasonable explanation exists; (5) what material effect followed and was it predictable; (6) does repetition support knowledge/purpose or are events independently explained; and (7) what evidence supports alleged private benefit, procurement, communication or personal gain.

A wrong, adverse, appealed, poorly reasoned or reversed ruling is not by itself proof of prevaricación. Conversely, favorable passages elsewhere do not immunize a distinct proposition proved knowingly false or knowingly unjust.

It refused to extend complicity to Patricia where the AC report lacked argument/evidence and expressly invoked defence rights; it rejected/narrowed other branches and refused requested deficit liability for inadequate justification. - Irigoyen/Parrilla emails are contemporaneous, not judicial minutes.
- The 2020 Laura statement and later staying-behind observation justify verification but do not prove intimate friendship, improper contact, bias, cohecho or a duty to abstain.
- LAJ/testimonio/DIOR/service acts cannot automatically be attributed personally to the Judge.
- Fiscalía did open and archive DIP2/2026; criticize scope/verification/fragmentation, not total silence.
- CGPJ registration/archive/appeal processing proves procedure, not guilt or innocence; CGPJ is not an appellate criminal court.
- Private economic advantage to CAM/Acosta Matos does not itself prove procurement/agreement/corruption/personal benefit.

## 5. One record, different legal routes

- **Audiencia Provincial / appeal:** correctness, nullity, evidential error and legal consequences.
- **CGPJ:** disciplinarily relevant functioning/conduct/supervision/delay/impartiality appearance within its competence.
- **Criminal Fiscalía/TSJ:** whether a specific judicial resolution was knowingly unjust (or met another criminal threshold) with contemporaneous knowledge/mens rea.
- **LAJ/judicial office:** service, testimony, notification, custody and implementation with actor-specific attribution.
- **Civil/concursal recovery:** reversibility, restitution and damages independently of criminal result.

**Non-fragmentation means one evidence graph, not one institution doing everyone else's job.**
