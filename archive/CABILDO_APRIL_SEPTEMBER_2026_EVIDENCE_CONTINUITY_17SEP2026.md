- Cabildo stated that the Community certificate then produced authorised common-area reform/conservation/maintenance applications, not the tourism-unit modification itself.
- Cabildo required applicant authority, adaptation material, updated unit plan/list, deeds for own units and owner-issued enabling title for other units, plus maintenance material.
- An August 2022 applicant submission asserted authorisations for **206 units / 618 places** and supplied a unit/finca matrix, titles and owner authorisations. These remain applicant-supplied propositions/documents unless a later official act states what was independently verified.
- Yaiza Exp. 5944/2022 issued a definitive adverse compatibility report: the proposed **one-star, 220-unit / 660-place** configuration did not comply with the then-current Plan General; AB8 required hotel use of at least three stars and listed 318 places.

A 14 March notification to the Community was later recorded as rejected/non-accessed on 25 March; no motive or actual knowledge is inferred from that status alone. Cabildo sent the same adverse report/hearing notice to **Hotel New Trend, S.L. on 27 March 2023**, and the electronic receipt records access by HNT that same day at 11:35.
