### 3671/2022 — what the presently recovered primary material establishes

- The February 2022 request was made in the name of the Sun Park Owners' Community and sought restoration of the tourism record to the full historic 220 units / 660 places.
- Cabildo's 14 March 2022 requirement recorded the then-RGT position as **one star, 159 units / 477 places**, with the Community as exploitation holder.
- Cabildo expressly observed that the Community certificate then produced authorised common-area reform/conservation/maintenance applications, **not the tourism-unit modification that was the object of 3671**.
- Cabildo required applicant authority, adaptation material, an updated unit plan/list, deeds for own units and owner-issued enabling title for other units, plus maintenance material.
- An August 2022 applicant submission asserted authorisations for **206 units / 618 places** and supplied a unit/finca matrix, titles and owner authorisations. These are recorded as applicant-supplied claims/documents unless and until a Cabildo act shows what was independently verified.
- Cabildo requested Yaiza's prior municipal compatibility report. Yaiza's definitive Exp. 5944/2022 report concluded that the proposed **one-star, 220-unit / 660-place** configuration did **not** conform to the then-current Plan General: AB8 required hotel use of at least three stars and listed 318 places.
- The presently accessible extracted April set does not establish the final Cabildo disposition after that adverse municipal input. The final act/current effect therefore remains an open documentary question, not an assumed approval or refusal.

### 2984/2023 — February 2023 change request

- HNT's February 2023 material sought MYND Yaiza as a **four-star, 220-unit / 660-place** hotel and requested holder/name changes.
- The contemporaneous Cabildo requirement stated that the registered position remained **Sun Park, one star, 159 units / 477 places, Community as exploitation holder**.
- Cabildo required qualifying property title and, if the four-star / 220 / 660 proposal was pursued, a provisional four-star classification request.
- No later compliance filing or final act appears in the disclosed 2984 native corpus. This does **not** justify inventing a formal withdrawal or final decision.
- The December 2022 segregation deed transferred a defined CAMSA economic unit to HNT and stated a segregated patrimonial value of **€10,213,382.82**. Its annex is a defined CAMSA asset/property schedule; it is not treated as a statement that HNT thereby owned every independent Sun Park finca.

### 17070/2023 — October 2023 holder/name modification

- In October 2023 HNT again sought a holder/name modification, but the tourism communication preserved **one star, 159 units / 477 places**.
