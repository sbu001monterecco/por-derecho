# How far the trolley analogy fits — EN / ES

**Control:** PD-RESEARCH-TROLLEY-MEDIA-20260920. Analytical supplement; not a finding of purpose, guilt or institutional capture. [Index](README.md) · [Legal scope](TROLLEY_ORIGINS_AND_LEGAL_SCOPE_ES_EN.md).

## English

### The position and the question are distinct

Gil Marer's attributed position remains that the Sun Park Community, property, insolvency, financing and related conduct formed an organised, coordinated and continuing criminal mechanism benefiting the Acosta Matos perimeter. This research does not recast that allegation as merely civil. It also does not convert the allegation into adjudicated guilt. See the existing [whole-platform consolidation](../../PRESIDENCIA_PLATFORM_RECOVERY_THREAD_20SEP2026.md) and [wealth review](../../ACOSTA_MATOS_WEALTH_TRAJECTORY_REVIEW_20SEP2026.md).

The trolley analogy asks a narrower question: could the increasing consequences of investigating and correcting an alleged original wrong become a reason for leaving the original rights-holders to bear the loss? It is most useful at the later correction stage, not as an account of how every initial transaction occurred.

### The mechanism to test

Alleged initial displacement → subsequent representations or transactions relying on the disputed position → more commercial and institutional dependencies → increased apparent cost of reopening the original question.

Each arrow needs a dated source and a causal explanation. A later approval may rely on an earlier representation without independently adjudicating it; that possibility must be checked in the actual decision file. The existing platform review identifies property, management, construction, finance, brand, mandate and reputational relationships separately. Media coverage supplies leads to those transactions, not proof of the purpose of judicial decisions.

The important question is: **did a decision-maker examine evidence challenging the arrangement under the duties of that office, or did the perceived consequences of accepting it become a reason to avoid examination?** This remains an evidential question, not a conclusion about a named official.

### Three explanations must remain available

1. A lawful adverse outcome: the relevant allegation was adequately considered but failed the applicable legal or evidential requirements.
2. Fragmented examination: different bodies answered narrower questions, leaving material connections insufficiently examined without sharing an unlawful plan.
3. Knowing maintenance: particular decision-makers avoided required examination to preserve benefits, previous decisions or the existing arrangement.

The third most closely resembles the proposed sacrifice-one-to-protect-many hypothesis. Neither the first nor the second is excluded merely because several results were adverse. Conversely, failure to establish a common plan would not dispose of separately proved misconduct by an individual.

### Why the literal trolley is incomplete

The supposed single victim represents different people and legal persons. Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. (LPB), Matkator, S.L.U., the parent and other owners do not share one interchangeable estate, entitlement or loss. The [group review](../../AWESWELL_2025_ACCOUNTS_WIDER_GROUP_REVIEW_20SEP2026.md) remains the relevant rights-holder map.

The other track also contains different interests: employees and innocent investors; continuity of a business; the positions of creditors; and, in the allegation, avoidance of accountability by those responsible. Protecting innocent third parties is not the same objective as shielding wrongdoing.

No evidence reviewed establishes an unavoidable binary choice between denying the claim and destroying the business. Investigation, safeguarding evidence, continuing operations, preserving recoverable value, establishing responsibility and selecting final remedies are separate questions. Their practical compatibility needs evidence and the correct legal route; this is not a promise that every protective measure is available.

There is no single operator with one lever. Each actor's role, information, duty, opportunity to act and contribution must be established. Inaction is not automatically neutral, but omission liability is not established without the relevant duty and other requirements. The legal study prevents the metaphor from replacing those tests.

### What the actual records contribute

The [Fiscal production matrix](../../../evidence/fiscalia/2026/MF_SHORTCOMINGS_DP1901_EG745_PRODUCTION_MATRIX_18SEP2026.md) identifies a dated29July Fiscal archive request recorded in the14September DP1901 order, with the report, author/assignment and examined corpus still to be produced. The E.G.745 inspection archive addresses specificity of an alleged Fiscal irregularity; it does not adjudicate the full property history. DI22's15September judicialisation reason requires identification of the actual court proceeding and factual overlap. See the [public scan derivative](CROSS_SOURCE_SCAN_PUBLIC_ES_EN.md).

These are specific questions about whether and where material was examined. They do not demonstrate a common decision to sacrifice rights for investment or employment. The contemporaneous2018 alternative-transaction record also contains conditions and initial receptiveness; it cannot be reduced to unconditional funds deliberately refused.

### Extent of the analogy

The structural resemblance is substantial as a hypothesis about concentrated injury and expanding reliance. An unavoidable emergency is not established. A shared institutional intention to sacrifice the original rights-holders is not proved in the reviewed record. No numerical probability of guilt or institutional rating is assigned.

The test should connect: **right-holder → right → act → source → recipient's demonstrated knowledge → duty → decision and reasons → practical consequence → available remedy → contrary explanation.** The cost of correction is a fact to investigate, not an answer to whether the starting position was lawful.

## Español

### Posición y cuestión analítica

Se conserva la posición atribuida a Gil Marer: un mecanismo presuntamente organizado, coordinado y continuado de carácter penal, relacionado con Comunidad, activos, concurso, financiación y beneficios del perímetro Acosta Matos. No se rebaja a una controversia meramente civil ni se presenta como culpabilidad declarada.

La analogía pregunta algo más concreto: ¿puede el coste creciente de investigar y corregir un supuesto daño inicial convertirse en razón para que los titulares originales sigan soportándolo? Resulta especialmente pertinente al llegar a la fase de corrección, no como explicación automática de toda la historia.

### Secuencia que debe probarse

Desplazamiento alegado → representaciones u operaciones posteriores apoyadas en la posición controvertida → mayor número de dependencias → mayor coste aparente de revisar el punto de partida. Cada conexión exige fuente, fecha y causalidad. Una autorización posterior no demuestra por sí misma que se haya resuelto independientemente la premisa de la que partió.

Deben mantenerse tres explicaciones: una resolución adversa lícita tras examen suficiente; una revisión fragmentada que deje conexiones sin examinar, sin plan común; o el mantenimiento consciente por personas concretas para proteger beneficios, decisiones previas o el estado existente. Sólo la tercera corresponde de forma estrecha a la hipótesis de sacrificio. La acumulación de resultados adversos no decide entre ellas.

### Límites del ejemplo

La presunta víctima única comprende distintos titulares y personas jurídicas. LPB, Matkator, matriz y terceros no forman una sola masa ni comparten automáticamente legitimación o daño. Del otro lado tampoco hay un interés único: conservar empleos e inversiones de buena fe no equivale a proteger a posibles responsables frente a rendición de cuentas.

No se ha demostrado que sólo quepan negar los derechos o destruir el negocio. Investigación, preservación documental, continuidad operativa, conservación de valor, determinación de responsabilidad y remedios finales deben analizarse por separado. Su compatibilidad y disponibilidad no se presumen.

Tampoco existe una sola persona con una sola palanca. Capacidad, conocimiento, deber, oportunidad y contribución requieren prueba individual. La omisión no se vuelve irrelevante por ser omisión, pero tampoco constituye delito sin sus requisitos. La metáfora no sustituye el derecho.

### Aportación de los expedientes y conclusión

DP1901 aporta una actuación fiscal fechada, pero exige producir informe, autor, asignación y corpus. E.G.745 resuelve sobre suficiencia de la imputación inspectora, no toda la propiedad. DI22 exige concretar qué mismos hechos estaban judicializados y dónde. Las fuentes de2018 conservan una alternativa seria, sus condiciones y la receptividad inicial. Las matrices existentes enlazadas arriba mantienen tanto la alegación como sus límites y prueba contraria.

La semejanza estructural es relevante; la inevitabilidad del sacrificio no está acreditada; la intención institucional común no está probada. No se asignan porcentajes de culpabilidad ni valoraciones de autoridades. La pregunta controlable es si se examinó lo que exigía el deber aplicable o si las consecuencias de reconocerlo evitaron ese examen. La respuesta requiere enlazar titular, derecho, acto, fuente, conocimiento acreditado, deber, decisión, efecto, remedio y explicación contraria.
