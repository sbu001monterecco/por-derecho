# Acosta Matos: Canary media and corroborating-source register, 2010–2026

Research cut-off: 20 September 2026. Public-source research; no repository, email or institutional submission was changed.

## Scope

This is a selected, auditable register of 36 sources and leads, not an exhaustive newspaper archive. Contemporaneous Canary media coverage for 2010–2017 was sparse in the accessible search results. Later retrospective reporting and reproduced judgments are explicitly labelled and are not represented as newspaper reports from those earlier years. The denser retrieved reporting concerns 2018–2026.

“Perimeter” means individually evidenced ownership, governance, financing, construction, management, franchise, professional or association links. It does not mean every named entity is owned by Acosta Matos, shares its liabilities, or participated in alleged wrongdoing. Publicity, forecasts and company statements are not independent audits. Repeated releases and translations do not multiply independent confirmation.

Access descriptions distinguish opened material, indexed passages and unresolved archival leads. This register contains source locators and short research notes, not full copyrighted articles. No conclusion is drawn that subsequent business wealth originated in Sun Park or that authorities decided to suppress an owner's rights.
