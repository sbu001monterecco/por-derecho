## English

### Origins: several kinds of antecedent, not a demonstrated chain of borrowing

Michael J. Sandel teaches the dilemma in Harvard's *Justice* course; he is a Professor of Government, not its originator. His opening lecture compares sacrificing one person with saving five. [Harvard course](https://sandel.scholars.harvard.edu/justice); [Harvard profile](https://sandel.scholars.harvard.edu/).

The Plank of Carneades is a much older necessity dilemma, not a railway example. Aquinas's discussion of self-defence is a background source for the intended/foreseen-harm distinction, not a complete modern trolley theory. These were intellectual antecedents raised in the thread, not a claim that one text demonstrably transmitted the example to another. [Aquinas, II-II, q.64, a.7](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/3064.htm#article7).

Frank Peter Schuster's 2023 criminal-law study identifies Kohler's automobile case (1915, pp.431ff.), Engisch's related treatment (1930, p.288), and Welzel's switchman case (1951, p.51). This update checked that scholarly reconstruction, not the three original German volumes. It does not establish an absolute first occurrence or Foot's knowledge of those texts. [Schuster, *Criminal Law Forum*](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10609-023-09452-0).

Philippa Foot's *The Problem of Abortion and the Doctrine of the Double Effect*, *Oxford Review* 5 (1967), pp.5–15, supplies the canonical modern Anglophone driver/tram example. Its bibliographic record was recovered; the original issue was not newly reproduced here. [Bibliography](https://philpapers.org/versions/FOOTPO-2).

Judith Jarvis Thomson's *Killing, Letting Die, and The Trolley Problem* appeared in *The Monist* 59(2), April 1976, pp.204–217. The publisher record confirms that the phrase was already in the 1976 title. Her *The Trolley Problem*, *Yale Law Journal* 94 (1985), pp.1395–1415, is a later major development, not the first naming in 1985. The attempted Yale PDF retrieval in this update failed; that does not erase the bibliographic lead. [1976 publisher record](https://academic.oup.com/monist/article-abstract/59/2/204/1360123); [1985 DOI](https://doi.org/10.2307/796133).

**Correction to the first conversation answer:** do not say that Foot demonstrably borrowed from German law, or that her independent invention has been proved. The transmission is not established. The ancient, medieval, German legal and modern philosophical examples should remain distinguishable.

### Comparative law: useful questions, not imported outcomes

The directly rechecked Strasbourg example is *Korporativna Targovska Banka AD v. Bulgaria* (2022). The operative holdings identify Article 6 violations concerning practical judicial review and the bank's representation, plus an Article 1 Protocol 1 violation concerning safeguards around licence withdrawal leading to insolvency. The Court dismissed the just-satisfaction claim. This is a cross-procedure safeguard comparison, not proof of the Por Derecho allegations or a guaranteed damages outcome. [ECtHR judgment, operative points4–8](https://hudoc.echr.coe.int/app/conversion/docx/html/body?id=001-218922&library=ECHR).

*Ivcher Bronstein v. Peru* (2001) was raised for the interaction of administrative acts, shareholder rights and judicial guarantees. *Brumărescu v. Romania* (1999) was raised for property and final-judgment certainty. They remain comparative research leads for precise paragraph-by-paragraph application; this worker does not certify fresh full-text retrieval of both. The Inter-American judgment is not a directly binding Spanish adjudication. [Ivcher official case entry](https://www.corteidh.or.cr/ver_ficha_tecnica.cfm?lang=es&nId_Ficha=200); [ECtHR case database](https://hudoc.echr.coe.int/).

The earlier *R v Dudley and Stephens* (1884) comparison concerns necessity and intentional killing. It is not a rule for civil property transfers. UN prosecutor standards, Council of Europe prosecutor standards, IBA judicial-integrity work and World Justice Project indicators were contextual research leads, not evidence of misconduct by a particular official in this case. [UN Guidelines on prosecutors](https://www.ohchr.org/en/instruments-mechanisms/instruments/guidelines-role-prosecutors); [IBA judicial integrity](https://www.ibanet.org/Legal_Projects_Team/judicialintegrityinitiative); [WJP factors](https://worldjusticeproject.org/our-work/research-and-data/factors-rule-law).

The EU Charter protects property and effective judicial remedies, but Article51 limits its application to Member States acting within EU-law scope. The mere presence of an EU-related instrument somewhere in a business network does not establish Charter jurisdiction over every dispute. [Charter Articles17,47,51](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/treaty/char_2012/oj/eng).

### Spain: the insolvency correction is indispensable

**The earlier broad criminal-prejudiciality explanation must not be used to claim an automatic insolvency stay.** Current TRLC Article519 expressly keeps the insolvency and its sections running despite related criminal proceedings. Article520 supplies a particular route for patrimonial measures affecting the estate, through the insolvency judge at the criminal court's request. The applicable historical text and transition rules must be checked for past acts. [BOE, Articles519–520](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2020-4859).

LECrim Article773.2 provides for the Fiscal's pretrial diligences to cease on learning of a judicial proceeding over the same facts. Thus the DI22 question is the identity of the facts/proceeding, the actual material transmitted and its handling—not a rule that invoking judicialisation is inherently unlawful. [LECrim](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1882-6036).

Criminal Code Article20.5 makes necessity conditional, including proportionality of harms and absence of intentional creation of the necessity situation. It is not an all-purpose permission to protect commercial convenience. Nothing in the inspected decisions expressly invokes a trolley rationale or this defence. [Criminal Code](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1995-25444).

Constitutional property, judicial-protection, judicial-independence and prosecutorial-function provisions identify different safeguards. They do not make every adverse decision unlawful, merge corporate and shareholder standing, or turn an administrative notice into a judicial remedy. [Constitution Articles24,33,117,124](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1978-31229).

Directive(EU)2024/1260 was identified for asset-recovery safeguards and its 23November2026 transposition deadline. It must not be represented as an unconditional present domestic power to freeze an entire business network. Its temporal/application conditions and implementing law require examination. [Directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2024/1260/oj/eng).

### Application boundary

The useful inquiry is one connected factual record examined under separate competences: ownership, contracts, estate administration, alleged crime, administrative acts and procedural integrity. Actual recusal, reassignment, inspection, appeal, restraint, restitution or compensation needs its own legal requirements. No automatic migration to a new judge, guaranteed criminal prosecution, collective liability or successful supranational remedy is certified.

## Español

### Origen: antecedentes distintos, no transmisión demostrada

Sandel utiliza el dilema en *Justice* y es profesor de Gobierno en Harvard; no lo originó. La tabla de Carneades pertenece a los antecedentes de necesidad, y la reflexión de Tomás de Aquino sobre legítima defensa al contexto de intención y previsión. No son versiones idénticas del tranvía ni prueban una cadena de transmisión intelectual. Las fuentes de Harvard y de Aquino figuran arriba.

El estudio de Schuster de2023 identifica el automóvil de Kohler(1915), el tratamiento de Engisch(1930) y el guardagujas de Welzel(1951). Se comprobó ese estudio, no los tres originales alemanes. No acredita el primer ejemplo absoluto ni qué había leído Foot. Debe conservarse esta diferencia entre reconstrucción académica y fuente histórica original.

Foot publicó su ejemplo del conductor en1967. El título de Thomson de1976 ya contiene *The Trolley Problem*, según el editor de *The Monist*. El artículo de1985 en *Yale Law Journal* desarrolla el problema; no es su primera denominación. La recuperación directa del PDF de Yale falló en esta actualización. No se presenta como probado ni el préstamo de Foot ni su invención independiente.

### Derecho comparado y límites

La sentencia de Estrasburgo sobre *Korporativna Targovska Banka* declara vulneraciones relativas al acceso efectivo al control judicial, representación del banco y garantías frente a la arbitrariedad en la retirada de licencia que desembocó en insolvencia; desestima la pretensión de satisfacción equitativa. Sirve para comparar garantías entre procedimientos, no para demostrar los hechos de Sun Park ni asegurar una indemnización.

*Ivcher Bronstein* y *Brumărescu* se conservan como líneas comparativas sobre derechos patrimoniales y garantías, pendientes de aplicación precisa por párrafos; este paquete no certifica nueva lectura íntegra de ambas. La sentencia interamericana no es una resolución vinculante del caso español. *Dudley and Stephens* es un antecedente sobre necesidad y homicidio, no una regla de transmisión de inmuebles. Los estándares de ONU/Consejo de Europa y trabajos IBA/WJP son contexto normativo o metodológico, no prueba individual de conducta ilícita.

Los artículos17 y47 de la Carta de la UE deben leerse con su artículo51: es necesario establecer el ámbito de aplicación del Derecho de la Unión. No basta una conexión genérica con fondos o instrumentos europeos.

### Corrección española

**Prejudicialidad penal no equivale a suspensión automática del concurso.** El artículo519 TRLC mantiene su tramitación; el520 regula medidas patrimoniales sobre la masa por el juez concursal a solicitud del órgano penal. Para actuaciones pasadas debe examinarse la versión legal aplicable. La revisión conforme al artículo773.2 LECrim exige identificar los mismos hechos judicializados y sus remisiones, no presumir que invocar judicialización sea ilícito.

El estado de necesidad del artículo20.5 CP está sujeto a requisitos; no autoriza de forma general sacrificar derechos por conveniencia empresarial. Ninguna decisión examinada invoca expresamente ese razonamiento. Las garantías de los artículos24,33,117 y124CE no fusionan legitimaciones ni sustituyen los recursos judiciales. La Directiva2024/1260 tampoco se convierte en una potestad doméstica incondicionada de embargo de todo un perímetro por citar su plazo de transposición.

La aplicación útil mantiene un solo relato factual y competencias, legitimaciones, estándares y remedios separados. No se certifican recusación automática, traslado forzoso, derecho a condena, responsabilidad colectiva ni éxito internacional.
