## 3. Source upgrade 1 — AP Las Palmas Sentencia 89/2014 is now a controlling adverse primary source

### Verified holding

Sentencia 89/2014, AP Las Palmas, Sección 4ª, dated **13-Jan-2014**, arose from JV 1260/2011 concerning **18 identified bungalows** owned by the minority claimants.

The AP held, within that defined dispute, that:

- Monterecco Sun Park, S.L. possessed the apartments in question;
- the copies of the keys were at reception;
- Monterecco carried out tourist exploitation and had the copies of the keys;
- the relationship among Monterecco Sun Park, S.L., Comunidad de Explotación and LPB was sufficiently close in that case to justify veil-piercing; and
- the appeal was upheld so that Monterecco, as well as the Comunidad de Explotación, was ordered to vacate the claimants' apartments.

### Publication lock

This judgment is **adverse evidence and must be shown**, not minimised.

It does **not** establish:

- that Monterecco/LPB possessed every unit in the mixed-ownership hotel;
- that the 18 claimants had authority over LPB/Matkator/common/operating rights;
- that later Community actors could take hotel-wide control;
- that the 2018 CAM/security takeover was authorised; or
- that the legal identities of LPB, CEXP and every later operator may generally be ignored.

**Website value:** it proves that possession/exploitation disputes over minority units were real, litigated and pre-dated the concurso. It also helps explain why later pages must distinguish `unit-level possession judgments` from `whole-hotel operating/control authority`.

## 4. Source upgrade 2 — 2016 meeting records a pre-CAM attempt to reconcile Community costs/debt and expressly separates LPB from the operator

The 11-Jun-2016 transcript records a professional meeting involving representatives of LPB/PwC, Jonathan Simó, Juan Carlos Prieto Puente and, later, Francisco Mario Matos Matas.

High-value propositions in the transcript include:

1. Simó describes a review of exploitation-company accounting and states that operating/maintenance expenditure was recorded in detailed books with invoice/date/provider detail.
2. The discussion expressly separates **LPB** from **the exploitation entity** when allocating hotel/community expenditure.
3. Prieto Puente is recorded accepting, subject to checking the underlying documents, that before the 2011 rupture there was a period in which owners were not treated as owing separate Community charges because the exploitation structure bore the costs.
4. The meeting repeatedly returns to the need to determine actual expenditure and offset/regularise owner/community positions rather than simply preserve a large accrued-debt number.
5. The transcript records the majority side's hotel-business role and the need to find a solution compatible with tourism use while minority owners sought access/use solutions.

### Evidence status

The PDF is a **transcript/derivative**, with inserted comments and editorial highlighting. It is valuable as a retrieval map and contemporaneous-context source, but the **native audio and a clean speaker/timecode transcript remain the controlling authentication target**.

### Storyline significance

This materially improves the predecessor chapter:

`2008 exploitation architecture → 2011 rupture → disputed Community debt/voting/access → 2016 attempted economic reconciliation → CAM arrival/defined-unit acquisitions and credit position → 2017-2018 access/project preparation`.

It weakens any simplistic narrative that the Community-debt/control dispute appeared only after CAM arrived.

## 8. Source upgrade 6 — DI 248/2018 archive is adverse evidence and must travel with the allegation

The Fiscalía Provincial decree dated **7-May-2019** archived DI 248/2018.

Its reasoning, in substance, included:

- the complained-of facts were considered too generic/inconcrete and not criminal **per se**;
- the insolvency administrator operated under direct supervision of the Mercantile judge and his decisions were judicially reviewable;
- the core disagreements were considered **sub judice** in insolvency/civil proceedings;
- the liquidation plan was described as judicially approved and its appeal pending;
- CAM was treated as recognised privileged creditor and recipient of assets in dación; and
- Fiscalía concluded that the record before it did not disclose a criminal offence.

The decree also stated that the complainant could reiterate the matter before the competent instruction court under the applicable procedural route.

### Publication lock

Do not say:

- “Fiscalía validated the takeover as lawful”; or
- “Fiscalía proved CAM owned the whole hotel.”

The archive is a real adverse institutional outcome. Its precise scope and reasoning must be shown. It did not adjudicate whole-hotel title, each pre-title access act, client data, Matkator rights, later project finance or every later-developed source.

## 12. Strongest defensible synthesis after this ingest

The ten-source tranche materially strengthens the following **evidence-led** storyline:

> **Sun Park's 2018 rupture did not arise in a vacuum. A documented possession/governance/exploitation conflict over minority units existed before LPB entered concurso. A 2016 professional meeting shows an unresolved but actively discussed Community-cost/debt/access architecture before CAM's arrival. By late 2017 and early 2018, contemporaneous party records describe CAM/JDAM-linked access demands, owner-like representations, measuring/project preparation, private-security planning and communications affecting staff and clients. Those allegations were formally made before the 7-Jun-2018 material-control event. The available source-controlled record then supports a 7-Jun shift in practical access/security/operational control, while the exact legal authority and finca-by-finca reach remain disputed. Later Lava Verde/public-owner representations and the DI 248 record show that the conflict moved from access and operation into project identity and public narrative before formal 2022 title.**

This synthesis **does not establish a criminal conspiracy, fraudulent takeover, unlawful eviction or institutional collusion**. Those require actor-specific proof.
