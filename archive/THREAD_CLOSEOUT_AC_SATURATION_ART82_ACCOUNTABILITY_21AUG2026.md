## Article 82 TRLC — no broad merits filing by default

Article 82 is a judicial supervision/information mechanism. It can be useful for a narrowly defined production, preservation, accounting or ongoing estate-protection request. It should not be used by default as a substitute forum for determining historic AC wrongdoing.

The Article 83 consequence remains controlling: a question resolved concerning exercise of the AC office may produce an unappealable order and may not later be raised through a concursal incident. Therefore any Article 82 request must be mapped in advance against the exact object of the pending removal/AP route, DP 1956, calificación and any civil-responsibility action.

**Presumption:** if the same historical issue is already being litigated, preserved or better determined in a dedicated responsibility/removal/criminal route, do not invite a duplicative Article 82 merits determination.

## No redemption by late cure

Later production, correction, restitution, improved accounting or a new explanation does not erase the historical record. It must be recorded chronologically as a later event and assessed separately for mitigation, continuing harm and evidential consequence.

For each material AC issue preserve:

`original act/omission → knowledge at the time → contrary notice → patrimonial effect/risk → later correction or non-correction → mitigation consequence → responsibility consequence in each competent proceeding`

A late cure is neither retroactive exoneration nor automatic proof of prior guilt.

## Judicial firewall

Every event must be classified as one of:

1. direct AC act/omission;
2. judicial result materially conditioned by AC factual input, calculation or professional recommendation;
3. AC implementation/non-correction after judicial authorisation;
4. court-only decision.

Judicial approval does not by itself convert AC factual premises, calculations, recommendations, implementation, signatures, custody of funds, restitution duties or later accounting into purely judicial conduct. Conversely, an adverse judicial ruling cannot be attributed to the AC as criminal conduct without an actor-specific evidential bridge.
