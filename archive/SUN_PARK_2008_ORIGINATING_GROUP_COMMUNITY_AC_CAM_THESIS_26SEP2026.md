1. CONTROLLING THESIS


The alleged causal origin of the Sun Park matter is not the later Acosta Matos phase. Gil’s position is that it begins with the incomplete implementation of the 2008 whole-hotel transaction.


On that case theory, the commercial object was one integrated hotel: 100% of Sun Park, all 262 registered fincas/elements, property plus exploitation, for the Israeli Multimatrix buyer perimeter. The controlled source base independently supports that the transaction was publicly presented as a transfer of hotel property and operation, while the recovered buyer-side completion evidence presently supports a narrower completed perimeter. Intended object and proved completion must therefore remain separate facts.


Gil alleges that a residual owner/seller perimeter representing roughly 23% failed to complete or otherwise frustrated the intended transaction and later supplied or controlled important parts of the personnel, representation, legal and administrative structure through which the Owners’ Community acted against LPB and the hotel operation.


“Originating perimeter” is an investigative classification. It does not mean that every person in the perimeter breached the same contract, shared one purpose, acted jointly or committed an offence.

The alleged mechanism to investigate is continuity of personnel and machinery: a residual seller/owner conflict becomes a representation and governance structure; that structure generates or controls minutes, books, accounts, certificates, debt positions, voting qualification, resolutions, litigation positions and access/possession propositions; those propositions then travel into later proceedings and control decisions. The stronger allegation to test is whether, after acquiring that knowledge, the AC later adopted, relied upon, implemented through, or failed independently to reconstruct contested propositions generated by the pre-existing Community apparatus, and whether later assistance became knowingly coordinated with outcomes sought or benefited from by the Community/CAM perimeter.


The later convergence lane must be proved through objective title, corporate, Community and control bridges. Priority examples include the AMENEM/TENGOLF chain and the Inversiones Salinetas/Cogolludo chain into CAM, the continued Community-administration roles, the 2018 AC/Community security-access interface, and the later Acosta Matos Community/project configuration. 5. ALLEGED COMMUNITY INSTRUMENTALISATION


Gil’s allegation is that the Community organ came to be used not merely for neutral horizontal-property administration but as a mechanism producing authority with effects against LPB and the integrated hotel operation.


Mechanism to test:


contested officeholding / representation
→ administration, books, accounts and certificates
→ debt attribution
→ voting qualification or exclusion
→ resolutions and litigation
→ operator/possession pressure
→ use of Community-generated material in the concurso and other proceedings
→ security/access authority

The 2011 material is central because it records a large LPB represented coefficient while a much smaller subset was treated as vote-qualified. That is not itself proof that the debt or voting treatment was unlawful. The questions are whether the debt was correctly calculated, consistently applied, properly certified and lawfully used to determine authority.


The 2016 professional-meeting material is relevant because it places private-interest representation and Community officeholding in the same source-described roles. That overlap is a serious governance/conflict question, not an automatic finding of illegality.

The alleged sequence is:

8. STRONGEST PRESENT FORMULATION


A seller/residual-owner dispute arising from the incomplete 2008 whole-hotel transaction was followed by a Community-side representation, administration and litigation apparatus. That apparatus produced and reused propositions concerning authority, debt, voting, operation and possession against LPB and the hotel. During Concurso 36/2012, the Administrador Concursal allegedly did not fully neutralise or independently reconstruct that apparatus and later used or delegated through it in material respects. CAM then acquired credit and title positions and entered the same Community/control environment. Existing people, documents and mechanisms progressively converged with the Acosta Matos perimeter, which later obtained security/access, practical control, redevelopment and ultimately title/project/operating benefit.


The investigative question is whether contested premises were knowingly adopted or reused, whether assistance was coordinated, and whether those acts caused patrimonial or procedural harm.
