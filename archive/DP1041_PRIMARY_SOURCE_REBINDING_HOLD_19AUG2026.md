## 4. Why this hold matters to deletion safety

The latest canonical public layer intentionally uses a stricter current-session evidential standard. That is correct for publication, but it creates a continuity risk: deleting the research thread without preserving the earlier source inspection could make future investigators believe those documents had never been seen.

This hold therefore preserves three layers separately:

1. **historic prior source inspection** — what was previously reported after direct document review;
2. **current canonical public status** — what is presently rebound and safe to publish;
3. **future verification task** — retrieve originals and reconcile the two.

No raw private email addresses, Gmail message IDs, privileged correspondence or unredacted personal data are preserved here.
