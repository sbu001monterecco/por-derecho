**Purpose:** define the custodian-side material required to reconcile the Por Derecho recovered docket against the official record and support a defensible 100%-of-recoverable completeness statement.

2. inventory of Sections 1–6 and every pieza separada / incidente;
3. resolutions index identifying Autos, Providencias and Sentencias by date and section;
4. LAJ act index identifying Diligencias de Ordenación and Decretos;
5. notification/service index or equivalent traceability for material resolutions;
6. list/index of testimonios, mandamientos and external-office communications;
7. appellate-remittal index identifying material transmitted to the Audiencia Provincial;
8. certified index of the Calificación record;
9. certified 25-Jul-2023 Vista minute / attendance record;
10. complete official audiovisual component index and preservation status;
11. citation/service returns for the formal 25/26-Jul-2023 witness/expert programme;
12. Arrecife exhort and videoconference traceability;
13. record, if any, identifying the disposition of the formally scheduled 26-Jul evidence;
14. record of the 24-Jul-2023 evidence-access problem and any remediation, if such a court-office record exists;
15. confirmation whether any court-media or documentary component is archived outside the ordinary electronic docket.

## Reconciliation method once received

Create:

`archive/CONCURSO_36_2012_OFFICIAL_INDEX_RECONCILIATION.md`

For every official index entry mark:

- `MATCHED — PRIMARY COMPLETE`
- `MATCHED — DUPLICATE / SAME ACT`
- `MATCHED — SECONDARY ONLY / PRIMARY TO RECOVER`
- `MISSING FROM POR DERECHO`
- `OUTSIDE DEFINED SCOPE`
- `OFFICIAL ENTRY REQUIRES CLARIFICATION`

Only after that reconciliation should the project consider saying that the **court-file denominator itself** has been closed.

## Framing

This is a traceability, preservation and access request. It does not allege that a missing Por Derecho copy is missing from the court, destroyed, concealed or improperly withheld.
