## 2. Corrected controlled proposition — Aweswell and Matkator

The controlled correspondence supports all of the following distinctions:

1. Cuatrecasas sent invoices, proformas and payment-related communications to Aweswell/Gil and engaged in extrajudicial discussions or negotiations concerning professional fees.
2. That correspondence must not be described as a judicial fee-recovery action against Aweswell.
3. Within the controlled corpus reviewed as at 3 September 2026, **no court claim by Cuatrecasas against Aweswell for those fees has been identified in Spain or the United Kingdom**.
4. This is a controlled-corpus non-finding, not universal proof that no such proceeding could exist outside the material reviewed.
5. The judicial recovery route presently identified in the source record was directed against Matkator through Cambiario 1048/2019 and then ETJ 163/2020.
6. The absence of an identified Aweswell action does not by itself prove that Matkator lacked an autonomous or guaranteed obligation, that the promissory notes were invalid, that all invoices were due, or that any procedural impropriety occurred.

The correct formulation is therefore:

> **The record documents invoicing and extrajudicial payment discussions with Aweswell, but no judicial fee-recovery action by Cuatrecasas against Aweswell has been identified in the controlled Spain/UK corpus. The judicial route identified was pursued against Matkator through Cambiario 1048/2019 and ETJ 163/2020. The legal, documentary and authority basis for that choice of debtor must be reconstructed from the promissory notes, engagement and billing records, corporate authority and the certified court files.**

Do not revert to either inaccurate extreme:

- `Cuatrecasas never invoiced or asked Aweswell to pay`; or
- `Cuatrecasas judicially pursued Aweswell for the fees`.
