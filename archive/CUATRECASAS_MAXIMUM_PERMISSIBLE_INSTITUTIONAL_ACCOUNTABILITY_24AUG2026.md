## 1. Controlling position

The pressure objective is not repetition, personal targeting or rhetorical escalation. It is to make each material proposition answerable through a defined record, preserve the evidence needed for independent adjudication and ensure that each competent institution sees the part that belongs to it.

The principal alleged responsibility of the Acosta Matos perimeter, the Monte Lanza/Molina dissident perimeter and their representatives, the Insolvency Administration and any separately examinable judicial conduct remains distinct. Potential Cuatrecasas responsibility is separate and potentially cumulative. It does not replace, reduce or exonerate those actors.

The strongest permissible causation proposition is not that competent professional work would certainly have prevented every later event. It is that the accepted and billed rescue work, pre-event knowledge and later implementation opportunities support a serious hypothesis that timely, integrated and entity-correct performance could have preserved a real and substantial opportunity to prevent or materially contain identifiable parts of the adverse chain. Each increment still requires proof of duty, available alternative, probability, intervening causes and loss.

## 5. Counterfactual proof standard

For every alleged omission, the private analysis must show:

`accepted duty → contemporaneous risk → competent available action → probability of success → protection lost → intervening actor → incremental loss`

The public formulation may state a real and substantial opportunity only where the record supports more than abstract possibility. It may not state certain prevention unless the alternative outcome is demonstrated by primary evidence and, where necessary, independent expert analysis.
