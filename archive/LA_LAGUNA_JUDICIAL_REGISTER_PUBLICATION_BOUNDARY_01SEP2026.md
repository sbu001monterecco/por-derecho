# La Laguna judicial `^` register — publication boundary

Date: 1 September 2026

The public release may expose canonical names/IDs of judicial office-holders, court organs, proceeding references, source-controlled act dates/capacities, public-safe procedural chronology, explicit gap states and closure triggers.

The public release must not expose raw private emails, Gmail/provider message IDs, signed-download URLs, authentication tokens, unnecessary personal identifiers, privileged legal advice, or private-only source locators. Public machine registers use stable source-control IDs where a private source filename/path is unnecessary.

Identity registration is evidentiary plumbing. It does not establish criminality, civil liability, intent, collusion, agreement with a party, merits treatment or responsibility for an act not source-attributed to that individual.
