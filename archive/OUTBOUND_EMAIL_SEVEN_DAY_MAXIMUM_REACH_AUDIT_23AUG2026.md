## 1. Purpose

This audit reverse-engineers the recent external sent/received communication pattern and the current public website to improve future exposure, engagement and action without turning reach into indiscriminate mass-mailing.

It is not a complete public reproduction of the mailbox. Private addresses, private bodies, Gmail message IDs and restricted attachments are intentionally omitted. Future work must re-query Gmail for the native communication record.

## 2. Core conclusion

**Maximum exposure should mean maximum relevant reach, not maximum CC count.**

The last-week pattern shows that the strongest outcomes came from:

- a recipient with a defined function;
- a finite ask;
- a stable thread/reference;
- a proportionate attachment/link package; and
- clear separation between awareness email and formal procedural filing.

The highest operational risks came from:

- heterogeneous recipient groups in one distribution;
- corrections to recipient sets or controlling versions;
- test/placeholder sends from the real Gmail account;
- dead addresses / bounces;
- ambiguous attachment filenames; and
- assuming that email receipt equals procedural validity or merits examination.

## 3. High-signal findings from recent sent/received mail

### 3.1 Specialist thread + reference numbers improve traceability

Portfolio Stock Exchange generated numbered requests **81** and **82** and its acknowledgment expressly invited additional comments by reply. Future material for that matter should normally remain in the existing request/thread structure rather than opening parallel chains.

**Lesson:** when a ticket/reference exists, treat it as an asset. Preserve it and use it.

### 3.2 Specialist office channels can produce stronger evidence of actual handling

CGPJ Recursos confirmed that earlier material was received and joined to Alzada 286/2026 and that two further files plus the audiovisual-source link were being incorporated while the appeal remained in progress.

**Lesson:** measure exposure by evidenced handling—incorporation, routing, ticketing, request for further material—not merely by delivery.

### 3.3 Acknowledgment is useful but is not a merits decision

Recent Fiscalía General / Secretaría Técnica responses confirmed receipt and indicated further management. This is evidence of receipt/routing, not proof of agreement, investigation or merits acceptance.

**Lesson:** exposure metrics must preserve `RECEIVED ≠ EXAMINED ≠ VERIFIED ≠ DECIDED`.

### 3.4 Email is not always a legally operative filing

Fiscalía Anticorrupción acknowledged an emailed communication but required a signed submission through an accepted formal mechanism before the complaint could proceed.

**Lesson:** every authority-facing package needs a channel-preflight question: `Is email enough for the act requested?`

### 3.5 Broad mixed-audience distributions increase correction risk

The recent consolidated German/Spanish FTI / Meeting Point / Club Sei / Sun Park / SEPI distribution combined public authorities, professional/advisory recipients, investors and media and then required a recipient correction.

The message itself attempted to distinguish existing recipients from newly informed media, but the distribution still illustrates why recipient function and ask should be separated by audience lane.

**Lesson:** use controlled exposure waves and specialist clusters rather than one broad CC field.

### 3.6 OOO/redirects are routing intelligence, not send authority

Recent German-media and financial/regulatory OOO messages identified return dates or alternative contacts.

**Lesson:** record the return date and candidate route. Never automatically resend merely because an auto-reply supplies another address.

### 3.7 Bounces must stop the route

Recent delivery-failure notices confirm the need to stop and re-verify after a bounce rather than repeatedly retrying an address.

**Lesson:** address reliability is part of recipient scoring.

### 3.8 Gmail must never be used as a test environment

The recent mailbox contains accidental/test-style transmissions, including placeholder/test messages and an unintended empty `DO NOT SEND` message during the Lourdes preparation sequence.

**Controlling correction:** the real Gmail account must never be used to test connector behaviour, addresses, attachment handling, threading, deletion or send functionality. Testing is draft-only or non-mail.

### 3.9 Compact first-contact media packages are cleaner

The Lourdes Santana first-contact package used a recipient-specific Spanish narrative, the two Spanish source-map PNGs, the controlled San Telmo webinar link and the public website link.

**Lesson:** a Level-1 journalist package is preferable to a document dump when the objective is to obtain a first `yes, send me the dossier` response.

### 3.10 Repeated source assets need canonical filename control

Recent institutional sends repeatedly used the PwC and San Telmo/RICPE visuals, but some mailbox copies carried duplicate-style suffixes such as `(2)`.

**Lesson:** the outgoing manifest should resolve every recurring visual to one canonical filename per language/version before final approval.
