## 1. Start with the intended transaction, not the eventual fragmentation

Gil Marer's expressly adopted account is that the intended transaction was for **one hotel, 100%, all 262 fincas**, with Multimatrix as the Israeli investor/buyer. He alleges that owners representing approximately 23% failed to attend the required notarisation/completion, breached their sale commitments, and then obstructed completion and trading through acts and omissions. He distinguishes a 2008–2011 phase from an aggravated Community-governance phase after 2011 and later adoption by Acosta Matos-related actors.

This is not the proposition that Multimatrix deliberately bought a majority and accepted an indefinitely disruptive minority as the agreed endpoint. However, the exact denominator, sellers, powers, commitments, conditions, tender, completion notices, nonappearance and available remedies must be proved from the sale/completion package. A statement of intention for the whole enterprise and completed conveyance of every title are different facts.

Primary predecessor control: `archive/declarations/008_GIL_TEXT_ATTRIBUTION_GARRIGUES_WITNESS_MONTELANZA_MOLINA_ROOT_CAUSE_20260822.md`. The 2023 complaint's historical section, reviewed privately in the thread, contains the whole-transfer and 77/23 allegations and identifies supporting exhibits. A pleading proves what was alleged and identifies source targets; it is not the judgment establishing those allegations.

### CEXP statutes and the April/May 2008 instruments

The six-page CEXP statutes copy describes participation by subscribers, annual duration/renewal and express withdrawal language, an 80% early-dissolution provision subject to existing commercial commitments, operating/delegation powers, common-purpose restrictions, accounting and contribution duties. The copy alone does not authenticate every signature, amendment or participating unit.

The source-controlled **29-April-2008 ACTA** records an option with **Victory Properties**, linked to an 18-February meeting; CEXP formation before execution of that option; the intended transfer of operating risk; employee protections; and express optional participation. It records approximately 84.988% attendance and a 74.90% approval figure, with a separately reported opposing/reserved figure. These are source literals, not a fresh arithmetic certification.

**Do not equate attendance with approval or with sale obligations.** Optional accession to CEXP does not by itself answer whether a seller was separately bound to convey property. Conversely, majority approval of an operating structure does not automatically prove authority to convey each absent owner's finca.

The existing 15-page compilation identifies a **26-May-2008 Montelanza–CEXP operating transfer**, with stated effect from 27 May, in CEXP's own name and with operational/administrative responsibilities. It must be read with its annexes and the individual entitlement record. See `evidence/community/actas/2008-04-29/transcript-es.md` and the public governance-instruments edition.

### Individual exploitation contracts, 12-page compilation

The recovered compilation includes parts of 2009 and 2010 apartment-specific arrangements. It contains exclusive-use/term provisions, allocation of expenses and returns, recognition of operating rules, and clauses requiring notice of a sale and stating that a new owner is subrogated into the contractual position.

These are **concrete successor-wording leads**. They do not prove that every Molina/Montelanza seller signed the same contract, that every excerpt is complete, that all successor clauses are opposable to every purchaser, or that old personal damages automatically pass. Verify the full executed contract, signatory, finca, duration, assignment/acceptance, registration/notice and later transfer deed. The public derivative does not expose apartment owners' names, identity numbers, addresses or signatures.

A contemporaneous early-March-2012 outgoing adviser communication recovered in the thread also describes the earlier sale/exploitation conflict, asserted guarantees, costs and Community disputes. It is a party account and a private-custody target, not professional corroboration. It requires reconciliation with the older repository statement that the earliest verified adviser activity was later in March. It does not establish advice before the February 2012 instruments. No privileged advice or raw correspondence is published here.

## 5. Legal routes that must be tested, not conflated

### Specific performance and the original buyer

Investigate the binding promise, exact seller/property, representation, price, conditions, buyer tender, notices, default, later variation/rescission and present rights-holder. Starting references include Civil Code arts.1091,1124,1257,1259,1278–1280,1450 and1451 and LEC art.708. The April Victory Properties reference, Israeli Multimatrix, any UK entity, LPB and the later Aweswell acquisition require a documented rights chain.

No claim is certified live or extinguished here. Limitation, interruption, litigation scope, later agreements and remedy-specific time rules need counsel review. A general ongoing-harm allegation does not decide them.

### Operating unity and remedies

Analyse the tourism law applicable at the relevant date, registered title/statutes, full operating instruments and later decisions. Operating unity is not automatically ownership unity. A contractual/regulatory breach may support particular performance, cessation, damages or enforcement; a compelled sale needs its own legal basis and competent process. No private physical compulsion is authorised or advocated.

### What a later buyer may acquire or face

Distinguish: opposable property burdens; defined common-expense exposure; express assumption/subrogation; prior contractual/proprietary rights and priority; fraudulent-transfer remedies; corporate/universal succession; and the buyer's own later knowing participation or wrongful conduct. Acquisition of a finca does not automatically transfer every seller's personal debt. It equally does not automatically cleanse every prior right or confer whole-hotel operating authority.

Civil Code arts.1257,1205 and1473 and Mortgage Law art.34 are starting points, not an adjudication of CAM's protection. Notice of a dispute and assumption of debt are different. A purchaser's own later adoption/continuation can be relevant even where a particular predecessor liability did not run with title.

Primary legal sources: [Civil Code](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1889-4763), [LEC](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2000-323), [Mortgage Law](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1946-2453). Retrieve the applicable historical version and case law before asserting a particular remedy.
