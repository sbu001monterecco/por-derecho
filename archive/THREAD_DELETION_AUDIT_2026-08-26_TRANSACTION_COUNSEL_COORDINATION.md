## 2. Durable strategic decisions

### Institutional “house and rooms” model

The wider institutional group should be treated as a house containing separate legal and commercial rooms. Hospitality investment, private banking / wealth management, corporate finance / structured finance, co-investment and wider international relationships may be strategically coordinated, but each retains its own legal entity, mandate, conflicts process, confidentiality perimeter and decision-makers.

Interest in one room must not be represented as commitment by another. Receipt of an NDA, willingness to meet or preliminary mandate-fit discussion is not investment approval, banking mandate, credit approval or capital commitment.

The group is one possible institutional route within a controlled parallel process; it is not the sole or exclusive counterparty unless exclusivity is later expressly documented.

### Sponsor positioning

The project-side holding company should be presented as sponsor / originator of a controlled hospitality pipeline rather than merely as the presenter of one asset. Asset identity, authority, ownership / operating structure, transaction status, routes to control or vacant possession, conditions precedent and delivery limitations must remain transaction-specific and disclosed only through the authorised confidentiality perimeter.

### Complementary counsel structure

The intended architecture is complementary:

- the established group-side coordinator maintains internal continuity across the holding company, group entities, governance, proceedings, recoveries, capital priorities and specialist coordination;
- a prospective external relationship partner and law-firm platform may add institutional, real-estate, hospitality, financing, fund, banking, NDA, transaction-documentation and cross-border capability;
- scope, conflicts, privilege, disclosure, staffing, economics and decision authority must be agreed before substantive reliance.

A personal relationship, former role, professional familiarity or informal willingness to explore acting is not a completed conflict clearance, engagement or authority to approach a third party.

### Final routing decision from this thread

The live confidentiality document and full strategic briefing were to be sent to the group-side coordinator first. The next intended sequence is:

1. sponsor and group-side coordinator catch up;
2. sponsor accurately briefs the coordinator on the preliminary investment-manager call;
3. coordinator speaks with the prospective external relationship partner;
4. the three participants agree the complementary legal architecture; and
5. only then is any external legal contact or no-names institutional approach considered.

No uncoordinated direct approach to the legal contact within the institutional group is authorised by this thread.

## 3. Confidentiality-document analysis preserved

- make the recipient responsible for permitted affiliates and representatives;
- require prior written consent before disclosure to external clients, investors, co-investors, unrelated vehicles or other third parties;
- review the confidentiality duration; and
- review the entire-agreement language against any earlier confidentiality history.

The current private document is not recorded as signed, accepted or superseding any earlier arrangement.
