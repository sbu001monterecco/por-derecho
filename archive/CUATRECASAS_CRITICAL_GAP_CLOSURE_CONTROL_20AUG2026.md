## Control rule

This file is a **gap register, not an allegation register**. A missing, unlocated or unreconciled document does not establish misconduct. “Not located” does **not** mean “does not exist”. An unresolved beneficiary does **not** imply a particular beneficiary. A procedural or professional concern does **not** establish criminal intent, conspiracy or a completed criminal offence.

The public interface may identify the documentary question and the evidence required to close it. It must **not** publish privileged advice, current-counsel strategy, private working notes or material whose publication could prejudice live proceedings.

## Critical interface invariants

Any reader-facing rendering of these reminders must preserve all of the following:

1. **Not located ≠ nonexistent.**
2. **Allegation ≠ finding.**
3. **Knowledge/context ≠ intent.**
4. **Physical/economic integration ≠ automatic loss of registry title or automatic fraud.**
5. **Reservation of cession ≠ proof that a cession occurred.**
6. **No beneficiary may be named without source proof.**
7. **Civil/deontological/criminal routes remain distinct even where they use overlapping facts.**
8. **Current-counsel advice, private work product and strategy stay behind a privilege firewall.**

## INPARSA / Hoteles Beatriz comparison — correction lock

The INPARSA / Hoteles Beatriz comparison is a **comparative hypothesis raised by the client side**, not a proved modus operandi and not a broad legal conclusion adopted by counsel merely because counsel considered contact/comparison potentially useful. Future repository and site language must attribute the comparison accurately and avoid converting a client-generated analogy into counsel endorsement or independent corroboration.
