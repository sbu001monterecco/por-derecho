## 1. Evidential classification

Every proposition from this source set must remain one of:

- **VERIFIED PROCEDURAL FACT** — shown by a signed court order, filed pleading or identified procedural reference;
- **VERIFIED DOCUMENTARY FACT** — shown by the document itself, such as the existence/content of a complaint, email or dossier;
- **PARTY ALLEGATION** — asserted by Matkator, Aweswell, Gil Marer, CAM, the AC or another party, but not thereby proved;
- **EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE** — reasoned connection supported by identified material, expressly labelled;
- **UNRESOLVED** — requires registry, expert, accounting, contract, revenue, procedural or custody evidence;
- **CONTRADICTED / CORRECTED** — unsafe proposition narrowed by stronger/later material.

Mandatory controls:

1. **Complaint ≠ finding.** The 2025 complaint/expansion establish what Matkator alleged/requested, not criminal responsibility.
2. **Photograph ≠ ownership attribution.** A photograph may show works/condition, but without a reliable unit map does not prove whose finca is depicted, who performed the work or under what authority.
3. **Later title/operation ≠ retrospective authority.** A 2022 corporate/registry/operational event does not automatically validate earlier entry, access, works, exclusion or commercialisation.
4. **LPB estate ≠ Matkator property.** Matkator is a separate non-concursada company; do not merge its extraconcursal property with the LPB estate.

## 4. Finca / unit map

### 4.1 Fincas 8584 and 8588

The complaint/expansion states Matkator is registered owner of:

- **Finca 8584 — CRU 35019000361734**;
- **Finca 8588 — CRU 35019000361772**.

The party visual dossier associates them with original Sun Park apartments **511** and **507**. It alleges their former space now corresponds to MYND rooms **1503** and **1507**, with parts incorporated into **1515** and **1517**.

**Classification:** ownership assertion is supported by identified 30-Jan-2025 simple notes cited in the pleadings, but a certified complete historical/current Registry chain is still needed. The room mapping is a party-produced visual reconstruction and expert-retrieval lead, not a final cadastral/registry boundary conclusion.

### 4.2 Fincas 8497 and 8498

The expansion asserts Matkator acquired/owns:

- **Finca 8497 — CRU 35019000360867**;
- **Finca 8498 — CRU 35019000360874**.

It also says 2025 registry notes showed HNT as registered owner from December 2022 while Matkator says it has no knowledge of a valid transfer. The pleading itself presents alternatives: registry error or possible documentary falsity.

**Classification:** **UNRESOLVED TITLE/REGISTRY CONFLICT.** Do not state falsification, unlawful transfer or continuing Matkator title as an adjudicated fact until deeds, registry entries, presentation history, authority, signatures, notices and any rectification/cancellation proceedings are obtained.

### 4.3 Whole-complex transformation proposition

The initial visual dossier alleges an original structure of **220 apartments + 40 commercial premises** was transformed into **324 hotel rooms + 6 commercial premises**, with changes in physical boundaries and participation coefficients.

**Classification:** PARTY ALLEGATION / EXPERT-TESTING SIGNAL. BORME material, plans, aerial imagery and public room material support a retrieval path, but the exact legal/physical transformation requires constitutive title/amendments, approved municipal plans/licences, Registry/Cadastre geometry, unit-by-unit expert inspection, Community consents, deeds/schedules and current hotel inventory.

Unity of hotel exploitation does not erase unit-level legal title.

## 5. Controlled chronology

### 31 Oct 2017, 8 Jan 2018 and 1 Mar 2018

The signed 2-May-2018 Arrecife order records complaint allegations that José Daniel Acosta Matos entered or sought access to Sun Park on those dates, made owner-like assertions and acted with others. At that initial stage the court held that a coercion theory could not be excluded and opened investigation on that limited episode.

**Do not expand:** no whole-hotel ownership/possession/guilt finding; no authorship finding for every interference event.

### 6 and 19 Mar 2018

The same order provisionally archived two reported damage episodes because the complaint did not provide indicia identifying the author. Preserve this adverse procedural fact.

**Do not expand:** unknown authorship of those incidents does not decide other entry/control allegations or later works.

### 2 May 2018 — DP 1132/2018 opening/severance

The court:

- opened preliminary proceedings about the identified site-presence episodes;
- ordered statements from complainant, witnesses and JDAM as investigated person;
- requested information from JM nº1 Las Palmas concerning Concurso 36/2012;
- provisionally archived the two damage episodes for unknown author; and
- inhibited the alleged “maquinación engañosa” aimed at the Mercantile Court to the Las Palmas deanery.

This is an express **Arrecife ↔ Las Palmas transmission/jurisdiction bridge** requiring the receiving reference and actual transmitted material.

### 12 May 2020 — LPB reposición order

The court dismissed LPB’s reposición. For this source set, the key correction is that demolition/common-area/access allegations were not adjudicated on their merits: they were treated as new facts and resulting requests that were extemporaneous within that appeal.

**Correct formulation:** the court declined to address those new requests in that procedural vehicle.

### 18 Dec 2020 — Aweswell estate-preservation application

Aweswell alleged ongoing demolition/alteration, deterioration and restricted expert access and asked for privately funded expert access, prohibition/notice against further works, judicial inspection, testimony to Fiscalía and an AC report on active estate/works.

This is contemporaneous notice-to-court/AC but allegations remain party assertions unless independently verified.

### 16 Feb 2021 — CAM opposition

CAM asserted it was a creditor and did not own LPB apartments merely through the credit; separately owned 54 Sun Park apartments; 2018 reforms concerned only its own units and were licensed; and Community resolutions supported security/access measures.

This is material counterevidence. Test exact finca schedule, deeds, licence, plans, dates and access instructions.

### 24 Feb 2021 — reasoned Auto

The signed Auto partially upheld Aweswell only because original motivation was too brief and maintained denial of inspection/expert valuation. It recorded:

- works inside apartments where ownership could not be determined from the photographs;
- debris in common areas, characterised by the court as no more than aesthetic effect once removed;
- works in common areas involved in the non-convalidated isolated sale, initially under a public deed, appearing from photos/party positions to have been paralysed;
- estate preservation as an AC function;
- possible utility of a differently framed responsibility action against CAM or AC, while considering the requested measure dilatory in the liquidation posture then before it.

**DECIDED:** denial of requested expert/judicial-inspection relief in that procedural posture, with fuller reasons.

**NOT DECIDED:** no global finding that no damage existed; no finca-by-finca ownership ruling; no Matkator title ruling; no validation of every work/access act; no final rejection of separately framed responsibility action.

### 23 Feb / 8 Mar 2021 — expert-access correspondence

Gil Marer asked the AC for written authority to enter with experts/notary, quantify alleged harm and prevent further interference. The AC’s earlier reply proposed a videoconference.

**Source status:** direct party correspondence. Useful for notice/request chronology; responsibility/possession/instruction/coordination assertions require independent proof.

### Nov 2022 — structural transaction/publication

The 2025 complaint relies on BORME-C-2022-7271 concerning structural transfer/segregation of the Sun Park economic unit from CAM to HNT for transformation/operation as MYND Yaiza. Future work must inspect the complete deed/project, schedules, title perimeter and registry consequences rather than rely solely on BORME summary or party paraphrase.

### 20 Feb 2025 — initial Matkator complaint

The complaint names HNT and Canarian Hospitality and alleges unauthorised structural reform/division/fusion, digital-lock exclusion, commercial exploitation without accounts/payment, and patrimonial loss. Its visuals link Registry/Cadastre, plans, aerials, project material and public hotel-room information. These graphics are valuable source-discovery tools but remain party-produced reconstructions.

The dossier’s €13,432 annual / €94,024 seven-year lost-profit model is an assumption-based party damages model, **not adjudicated damages**.

## 9. Adverse / alternative explanations that must be tested

1. **CAM defined-unit ownership:** CAM said it owned 54 apartments and worked only inside those units.
2. **Municipal licence:** CAM said 2018 works were licensed; verify applicant, title, plans, unit list, dates, conditions and actual work perimeter.
3. **Community safety authority:** CAM said security/access measures followed Community resolutions/technical safety concerns; verify notices, attendance, votes/proxies, minutes, contracts/instructions/invoices and legal scope.
4. **AC non-delivery position:** AC stated it had not delivered LPB assets to CAM and had sought information about works; verify requests/responses/inspections/follow-up.
5. **Procedural posture:** 2020/2021 denials may reflect timing, liquidation posture, evidential uncertainty and allocation of preservation functions rather than merits acceptance of CAM account.
6. **Registry/corporate mechanics:** 2022 HNT entries may result from a structural transaction whose schedules must be read in full; surprising registry result is not by itself falsification.
7. **Hotel-operating geometry:** hotel room renumbering/internal allocation does not automatically amend legal boundaries; unchanged registry description does not prove physical unit remained intact.
