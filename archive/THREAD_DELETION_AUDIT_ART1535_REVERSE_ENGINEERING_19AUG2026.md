# THREAD DELETION AUDIT — ARTICLE 1535 / DAY ZERO / REVERSE ENGINEERING 360

**Audit date:** 19 August 2026  
**Scope:** conversation sequence covering limitation/preservation architecture, Article 1535 nine-day trigger, LO 1/2025 Article 7, Acosta Matos/CAM perimeter, Concurso 36/2012, Insolvency Administrator, judicial-boundary treatment, dedicated Day Zero page, and 360° reverse-engineering implementation.

## Material propositions preserved

1. Limitation/preservation must be analysed claim by claim, not as one global clock.
2. Article 1535 must be treated as a distinct credit-assignment track and not conflated with unit-level preferential-acquisition rights.
3. Public control rule: **no Day Nine without proving Day Zero**.
4. A claimed Article 1535 trigger must identify the relevant credit, qualifying transfer, assignee, debtor, demand for payment and receipt; transaction economics remain a critical evidence layer.
5. LO 1/2025 Article 7 creates a statutory preservation layer capable in qualifying circumstances of interrupting prescription or suspending caducity, but its specific application to a live nine-day Article 1535 period remains **COUNSEL VALIDATION REQUIRED**.
6. LO 1/2025 must not be represented as automatically reviving a right a court determines had already expired.
7. Criminal overlap does not automatically freeze civil caducity; Article 40 LEC relevance must be mapped fact by fact and by actual judicial procedural posture.
8. Insolvency Administrator liability/limitation requires a separate actor-specific ledger of act, control, knowledge, damage, knowledge date, cessation and interruption/preservation events.
9. Any claim-specific “latest safe date” remains **COUNSEL VALIDATION REQUIRED**.
10. Judicial acts in Concurso 36/2012 are part of the institutional causal record; inclusion does not itself establish personal misconduct, collusion or a relationship with Acosta Matos.
11. Dedicated bilingual Day Zero pages are the canonical detailed treatment; related pages should summarise and link rather than duplicate the full doctrine.
12. 360° macro-rule: **the present does not prove the past**.
13. Reverse-engineering method: start from the current Sun Park/MYND end-state, work backwards through each legal/economic bridge, then run a counterfactual removal test on each contested node.
14. Public shorthand: **PROVE THE BRIDGE · PROVE THE DEPENDENCY · PROVE THE CONSEQUENCE.**
15. The strongest opposing case must be carried inside the system: non-litigiosity, historical trigger/expiry, Article 1536/structural exclusion, finality/preclusion, independent downstream title, third-party protection, AC time bar, and absence of criminal prejudiciality.
16. The objective is not to presume that all downstream acts fail; it is to force each downstream position to identify its independent bridge and what survives if an upstream node is removed.
17. Cross-site propagation is required across Article 1535/DP1041, CAM, Concurso/AC, lender/title, restitution/recovery, Matkator/extraconcursal, Community/CEXP, RICPE/funding and same-asset routes.
