# Aweswell Limited — full recovery perimeter visual and correspondence rule

**Control date:** 27 August 2026  
**Entity:** Aweswell Limited, UK company no. 07716847  
**Status:** repository, website and future-correspondence control

## Canonical distinction

Whenever Aweswell Limited's 31 July 2025 accounts or balance sheet are visualised, quoted, summarised or relied upon, present two separate layers:

1. **Filed accounting layer:** the statement of financial position shows an underlying group asset interest of £1, current assets £0, net assets £1 and shareholders' funds £1. This is the directors' provisional carrying presentation under constrained reporting conditions and is not an economic valuation.
2. **Directors' asserted recovery/economic layer:** the directors' stated position is that the full economic value of the group business and recovery perimeter remains subject to establishment, recovery, measurement and recognition. That asserted perimeter includes the claimed full unencumbered ownership/value of Sun Park / MYND Yaiza, the hotel business/platform, attributable hotel income, creditor and restitution rights, compensation and all legally recoverable damages.

## Mandatory words

Use substantially this formulation:

> Aweswell Limited and its directors do not attribute a £1 or zero economic value to Sun Park / MYND Yaiza, the hotel business, the group, its income or its recovery rights. The £1 in the filed 2025 statement of financial position is a provisional carrying presentation under constrained reporting conditions. The directors' stated position is that the full economic value of the business and recovery perimeter remains to be established, recovered, measured and recognised incrementally as title, control, income, quantum and recovery certainty are proved in and out of court.

## Mandatory visual convention

Any balance-sheet visual must display, side-by-side or immediately adjacent:

- **Recognised/filed carrying presentation:** £1 underlying group asset interest; £0 current assets; £1 net assets; £1 shareholders' funds.
- **Asserted full recovery perimeter:** no single accounting number may be invented before reliable measurement and recognition. Show components instead: hotel ownership/property value; business/platform value; hotel income; creditor/restitution rights; compensation; damages and enforcement recoveries.

Use the label **"Directors' asserted recovery/economic perimeter — subject to proof, successful recovery, measurement and accounting recognition"**.

## Accounting boundary

Do not convert the asserted full recovery perimeter into a statement that the 2025 recognised net assets already equal the claimed economic value. Property, earned income, receivables, restitution, compensation and damages may have different recognition thresholds and periods. Maintain an auditable bridge from £1 to every later recognised amount.

## Third-party misuse

Correct any third-party statement that converts the £1 carrying presentation into a substantive assertion that:

- the hotel, group or subsidiaries were worth £1 or zero;
- the directors waived or abandoned ownership, income or recovery rights;
- the accounts admit no loss or damages;
- recovery is capped at £1;
- Companies House or any court valued the hotel at £1.

Preserve the exact statement, source and response. Do not attribute bad faith without evidence.
