## Actor-separation rule

No public page may merge the Administrator, the Fiscal signatory, other Fiscalía personnel, the first-instance Judge or members of the Acosta Matos perimeter into one actor. Each allegation must preserve:

`act/omission → date → source → receipt/knowledge → authority/duty → contrary material → response → decision/effect → alleged harm → possible benefit → strongest counter-explanation`.

Aligned advantage, institutional reuse, relationship, proximity, motive or outcome does not alone prove agreement, shared intent or criminal responsibility.

## Appeal firewall

RPL 2523/2025 is the culpability appeal. The Provincial Court should decide it solely on the procedurally usable record, pleaded grounds, adversarial process and applicable law. The public criminal-misuse thesis:

- is not addressed to the Court;
- is not extra-record evidence;
- asks no predetermined appellate outcome;
- does not ask the Court to investigate the wider Sun Park record de novo;
- does not make appeal success proof of crime or appeal failure proof of capture;
- preserves criminal, civil, regulatory and disciplinary routes as distinct proceedings with distinct standards.
