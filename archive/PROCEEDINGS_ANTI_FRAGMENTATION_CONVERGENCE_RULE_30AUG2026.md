# PROCEEDINGS ANTI-FRAGMENTATION / CONVERGENCE RULE

**Date:** 30 August 2026  
**Status:** controlling additive analytical / continuity rule  
**Applies with:** Proceedings Master Register, Proceedings Interconnectivity Map, chronology, evidence, asset and counsel/procurador controls

## 1. Core rule

Por Derecho must never confuse **procedural separateness** with **factual, evidential, patrimonial or causation independence**.

A proceeding may be legally separate because it sits before another court, chamber, jurisdiction, territory, procedural class or authority. That separateness does not answer whether it concerns:

- the same hotel / productive unit / underlying assets;
- the same Concurso 36/2012 estate or patrimonial consequences;
- the same credit, mortgage, enforcement, possession or exploitation history;
- the same actors, successors, alleged beneficiaries or enabling platform;
- the same event chain or alleged intra- and extraconcursal harm;
- the same evidence, omissions, professional conduct or institutional knowledge; or
- the same alleged causation pathway.

Accordingly, all future analysis must distinguish:

1. **LEGAL / PROCEDURAL IDENTITY** — whether two files are formally one proceeding, parent/child, appeal, accumulated, referred or otherwise directly linked in law; and
2. **UNITARY FACTUAL / EVIDENTIAL / PATRIMONIAL CONVERGENCE** — whether separate files must nonetheless be analysed together because they concern materially overlapping assets, events, consequences, evidence or alleged harm.

The first cannot be inferred from the second. The second must not be ignored merely because the first is absent.

## 2. Anti-atomisation principle

Fragmentation is an analytical risk. A reader, court, prosecutor, professional or authority who sees only one procedural silo may miss the cumulative significance of conduct, omissions, patrimonial consequences, repeated actors, shared evidence or sequential effects that become intelligible only when the files are reconstructed together.

The repository must therefore preserve both views simultaneously:

**FILE-BY-FILE LEGAL ACCURACY**

and

**WHOLE-SYSTEM CONVERGENCE ANALYSIS**.

No proceeding is to be described as independent merely because it has its own number or organ.

## 3. Three Audiencia Provincial appeals — mandatory convergence lens

The following three current Audiencia Provincial de Las Palmas appellate tracks must always be presented as legally distinct but analytically interdependent:

- the appeal arising from the **calificación** of Concurso 36/2012;
- the appeal arising from the **claim/demand for removal or separation of the Administrador Concursal**; and
- the appeal arising from the **challenge/claim concerning the Administrador Concursal's fees**.

The controlling analytical proposition is:

> These are separate appellate objects, but each concerns the same insolvency administration, the same Concurso 36/2012 estate and chronology, overlapping acts/omissions, the same productive unit / hotel consequences and the legitimacy, conduct, incentives, decisions and patrimonial effects of the concursal administration. They must therefore be capable of being read together and cross-referenced in both directions.

The project may contend that atomising those appeals risks concealing cumulative causation or benefitting actors said by the project to have gained from fragmentation. That is an **attributed project allegation / analytical thesis**, not an adjudicated fact.

For each of the three appeals, the map must expose:

- the underlying first-instance filing / demand / decision;
- the Administrador Concursal conduct or issue relied upon;
- overlap with the calificación record;
- overlap with the alleged patrimonial destruction / loss of the productive unit / hotel-control chronology;
- common evidence and common omissions;
- common actors and professional lineage where independently sourced;
- the other two appeals as **unitary-convergence bridges**; and
- the explanation: **"legally separate appeal; materially overlapping insolvency-administration and patrimonial record"**.

## 4. CaixaBank / Valencia — underlying credit and asset bridge

The CaixaBank proceedings in Valencia must not be treated as commercially or evidentially remote from Concurso 36/2012 merely because they sit outside Las Palmas.

Where supported by the record, maintain explicit bridges through:

- the original financing / mortgage / swap / financial-product package;
- the debt and security structure affecting LPB and the hotel assets;
- the chronology leading into insolvency and enforcement;
- alleged defects, costs or liabilities affecting the estate; and
- downstream patrimonial consequences for the hotel / productive unit / group perimeter.

The correct public-safe explanation is ordinarily:

> **Separate Valencia proceeding; directly relevant to the financing and security package underpinning the insolvency and hotel assets.**

Do not imply the Valencia court has accepted the project's wider insolvency thesis unless a source says so.

## 5. Meeting Point / FTI pre-concurso — operator, stock, control and insolvency bridge

Any Meeting Point / FTI pre-concurso or insolvency-related file before the Mercantile Court in Las Palmas must be tested for direct contextual convergence with Sun Park / Club SEI / Lava Verde / the hotel where the repository contains evidence of commercial use, operator involvement, inventory/stock treatment, security/return representations, control, exploitation or public-money exposure.

The project's position that such a connection **was, or should have been, recognised** is an attributed contention requiring source-by-source support. Any proposition that actors deliberately hid or suppressed that connection remains an investigative allegation requiring actor-specific evidence of knowledge, conduct and intent.

The map must nevertheless prevent the connection from disappearing merely because the legal files are separate.

## 6. Arrecife mortgage enforcement — concurso / asset / title bridge

Mortgage enforcement concerning the hotel, units, mortgage credit, dación, cancellation, title or satisfaction must be hard-linked analytically to Concurso 36/2012 through the underlying asset and secured-credit chain.

If the enforcement was pursued or resolved outside the concurso, that procedural separation must be visible **together with** the question of what effect the enforcement had or should have had on:

- the active estate;
- the secured-credit position;
- title / possession / control;
- liquidation or adjudication;
- recoveries and accounting; and
- the productive unit.

The repository must expressly test whether compartmentalisation produced inconsistent, duplicated, omitted or unaccounted-for patrimonial consequences. Do not presume such a defect; evidence-grade it.

## 7. Cuatrecasas / cambiario / La Laguna / Matkator bridge

The Cuatrecasas cambiario and later La Laguna execution against Matkator must be mapped not only as professional-fee or enforcement files but also, where supported, through their relationship to:

- the timing of litigation and enforcement relative to the concurso; and

## 8. Ministerio Fiscal — compartmentalisation audit

All Ministerio Fiscal files, referrals, decrees, responses and appearances touching the matter must be capable of being read as one **institutional-response chronology** while retaining each office/file's distinct legal identity.

For every Fiscal response ask:

- What corpus was actually before that office?
- What earlier/later Fiscalía files were known, referred, cited or omitted?
- Was the intra-concursal and extraconcursal harm presented together or split?
- Was the hotel / productive unit / asset destruction presented as a cumulative phenomenon or only as isolated events?
- Did a territorial or subject-matter referral preserve the whole evidential package or narrow it?
- Is there one documented unitary acknowledgement of the alleged cumulative harm, or only compartmentalised responses?

The project's proposition that Ministerio Fiscal repeatedly compartmentalised the matter is an **analytical / attributed allegation** until supported office-by-office. Absence of a located unitary acknowledgement is not proof that none exists.

## 9. Historical Montelanza / Molina possession-exploitation chain

Historical proceedings brought by Montelanza / Molina-side minorities concerning possession, exploitation, removal, eviction/desahucio or the operating Community/CEXP must be treated as part of the long-run control history of the hotel and therefore hard-linked analytically to Concurso 36/2012.

Where the record shows a change or substitution of the demanded party from the exploitation/community perimeter to Monterecco / later Pink Canary Services, preserve and explain:

- the exact proceeding and appeal lineage;
- the exact party substitution or recharacterisation;
- the authority and procedural basis for that change;
- its consequences for possession, exploitation, liabilities or enforcement;
- how that history was or was not reflected in the concurso; and
- how later actors relied upon, inherited, cited, ignored or repurposed it.

The project's allegations concerning later use by Acosta Matos-side actors, the Administrador Concursal, the concurso judge or other enablers must remain actor-specific, source-graded and separated from the documented procedural minimum.

## 10. Mandatory connection lenses

In addition to direct procedural edges, the interconnectivity system must support these non-merits analytical lenses where source-backed:

- `SAME_CONCURSO_ESTATE`
- `SAME_PRODUCTIVE_UNIT_OR_HOTEL`
- `SAME_UNDERLYING_ASSET_OR_FINANCING`
- `SAME_SECURED_CREDIT_OR_MORTGAGE_CHAIN`
- `SAME_POSSESSION_EXPLOITATION_CONTROL_HISTORY`
- `SAME_PATRIMONIAL_EFFECT`
- `SAME_ALLEGED_HARM_CHAIN`
- `SAME_EVIDENCE_OR_EVENT_FAMILY`
- `INSTITUTIONAL_RESPONSE_CHAIN`
- `PROFESSIONAL_CONDUCT_LINEAGE`
- `SUCCESSOR_OR_BENEFICIARY_ALLEGATION`

These are contextual/analytical lenses, not joinder findings. Each must carry provenance and status.

## 11. Mandatory question for every proceeding

Every proceedings audit must answer:

> **If this file is read alone, what material part of the wider asset, causation, control, evidence or alleged-harm story disappears?**

Then ask the reverse:

> **If this file is connected to the wider graph, what exactly is documented, what is inference, what is allegation, and what remains unproven?**

This two-question test is the project's anti-fragmentation control.

## 12. Publication language boundary

Public pages must never state or visually imply that all connected proceedings are legally one case. Use wording such as:

- **legally distinct, materially connected**;
- **separate file, shared asset / evidence / chronology**;
- **procedurally separate, patrimonially convergent**;
- **unitary analytical lens, not procedural joinder**; and
- **project allegation / investigative hypothesis** where intent, concealment, beneficiary coordination or deliberate atomisation is asserted.

## 13. Continuity rule

Future work on any proceeding named above must test and preserve both:

1. its exact standalone legal/procedural identity; and
2. its material links into the wider Concurso 36/2012 / Sun Park asset, control, financing, exploitation, professional, institutional-response and alleged-harm graph.

A future thread is not complete if it updates one proceeding while leaving a known convergence bridge stale, missing or one-way.
