## Mission and central question

Conduct a continuous, criminal-prosecutorial-first, document-led reconstruction of the JSP corporate and hotel perimeter from its creation and Playa Blanca expansion through the 2008 Sun Park transactions, retained interests, Montelanza liquidation, transfers involving the Comunidad de Propietarios and Construcciones Acosta Matos, and JSP's separate insolvency, liquidation and any eventual conclusion or extinction. Follow assets, rights, authority, consideration, accounting, custody and knowledge in both chronological directions. Use civil, corporate, property, administrative and insolvency analysis to establish the predicates of any criminal hypothesis, not to assume away possible autonomous criminal conduct.

The central question is: **For each asset or right, who owned it, who had contracted to transfer it, what actually completed, who was legally authorised to dispose of it afterwards, what consideration and benefit each person received, which estate or creditor bore any loss, what entered the relevant accounts and insolvency inventory, and what each participant demonstrably knew?**

Gil alleges a continuing appropriation/control scheme and possible illicit dealings affecting both the Sun Park/LPB/Aweswell/Matkator interests and JSP's creditors. Test the strongest legally tenable version and the best competing lawful explanations. Do not treat the existence of crime, majority ownership, hidden consideration, captured governance or clandestine communications as an established premise. A civil irregularity does not automatically establish a crime; a civil label does not exclude a distinct crime supported by its own elements.
