# LOCKED PUBLIC WORDING — AUTHORITY AVAILABILITY

Use this formulation consistently:

### ES

> **La identidad y acreditación profesional del investigador, la identidad de la persona anonimizada y el original íntegro no anonimizado se conservan y están disponibles para su entrega a jueces, Fiscalía, policía, reguladores u otros órganos competentes cuando sea jurídicamente procedente.**

### EN

> **The investigator's identity and professional accreditation, the identity of the anonymised person and the complete unredacted original are retained and available for provision to courts, prosecutors, police, regulators or other competent authorities where legally appropriate.**

Do not imply that an authority has requested or received the material unless a receipt/transmission record exists.
