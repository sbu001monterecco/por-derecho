## Controlling proposition

**The present does not prove the past.** The existence today of an ownership, creditor, operating, funding or control position does not itself prove that every upstream bridge by which that position was reached was legally sufficient or economically independent.

The controlling methodology is therefore:

1. start at the current Sun Park / MYND end-state;
2. work backwards through each indispensable legal and economic bridge;
3. identify the evidence, legal basis, actor, date and decision supporting each arrow;
4. hypothetically remove each contested node;
5. identify which downstream rights survive independently and which depended on that node;
6. run the same proof discipline on the strongest Por Derecho case and the strongest opposing case.

Public shorthand: **PROVE THE BRIDGE · PROVE THE DEPENDENCY · PROVE THE CONSEQUENCE.**
