# Institutionalisation / instrumentalisation chain — RIC, regional incentives, EU co-financing and platform continuity

Updated: 15 August 2026

## Purpose

Canonical public-safe control record for the proposition that documentary positions originating in or around Concurso 36/2012 may have acquired downstream operative life across notarial/Registry acts, corporate restructuring, private RIC investment, regional incentives, EU co-financing representations and current hotel exploitation, while the same RICPE platform later generated a separate Orion Rental SOCIMI branch.

This is an **evidence-led traceability hypothesis**, not a finding of a coordinated scheme.

## Controlling distinctions

For this project:

- **functional instrumentalisation** means that a document, certification, procedural position, title representation or institutional act may have been used as an instrument enabling or supporting later reliance;
- **documentary institutionalisation** means that a premise about title, availability, perimeter or authority may have been repeated, accepted or embedded in later investment, corporate, administrative or operating processes;
- **serial reliance** means that a later institution, adviser or counterparty may rely on a documentary product accepted by an earlier actor without independently returning to the underlying title, finca, authority or factual source;
- **institutional enablement** means the cumulative effect of legally distinct gates — judicial/notarial/Registry, private investment governance, RIC tax eligibility, CNMV supervision, regional-incentive control and EU/public-finance control — each of which can increase the project's documentary credibility or financeability without validating every upstream proposition;
- **platform continuity** means reuse or evolution of the same investment vehicle, governance network, investor infrastructure, advisers or operating know-how across different projects. It does not establish a transfer of money or value between those projects.

None of these expressions, without additional evidence, establishes agreement, corrupt purpose, shared knowledge, falsity, fraud, collusion, criminal responsibility or improper public financing.

The controlling question is not whether every later actor participated in an earlier wrong. It is whether each later actor independently verified the legal and factual premise on which it relied after that premise had already been qualified, disputed or narrowed.

## Evidence-controlled architecture

### 1. Concurso / judicial-office origin

The LAJ testimony ledger identifies finite 2018 and 2021–2022 chains requiring production of the actual testimonios/certifications, service logs and downstream-use evidence.

Public-safe question:

`underlying judicial decision → request → testimony/certification/finality → notification/delivery → external use`

Issuance of a testimony does not prove improper use, and downstream use does not prove LAJ knowledge or intent.

### 2. Notarial / Registry / corporate bridge

The audit must identify which judicial-office instrument or combination of instruments was relied upon for the 2022 deed and later Registry/corporate effects, including the CAM→HNT perimeter.

Later title does not retroactively answer authority, possession, access, project planning or representation questions from 2018–2021, and a conveyance of LPB assets does not automatically convey Matkator or other third-party property.

### 3. 2019 — RICPE governance before the Sun Park investor presentation

The official CNMV register identifies RIC Private Equity Investment Partners, S.C.R., S.A. as venture-capital company no. 295, registered on 25 October 2019, and records **José Acosta Matos as director from 4 November 2019**. Enrique Guerra Suárez appears as secretary from 25 October 2019 and later as Director General from 9 July 2020.

Controlled significance:

- Sun Park was not simply an external proposal arriving in 2020 from a wholly unrelated developer to an institutionally separate investment platform;
- the governance overlap creates a finite related-party/conflict/information-flow question concerning proposal, review, abstention, approval, due diligence and investor communication;
- it does **not** establish that every RICPE director knew or approved each CAM/JDAM representation, that conflict procedures failed, or that the relationship was improper.

Required records include board and committee minutes, conflict declarations, abstentions, proposal papers, Control Unit material, adviser mandates and circulation/approval records for Sun Park investor communications.

### 4. 2020–2021 — investor presentation and RICPE's later narrowing of the title picture

The controlled RICPE evidence package identifies:

- 4 June 2020 investor material presenting Sun Park in the RICPE project perimeter associated with CAM and identifying CAM as the project company/title-holder in the table reproduced in the corpus;
- the 11 November 2020 webinar and related project representations, including José Acosta speaking as CAM Director General and RICPE director;
- RICPE's investor-facing presentation of due-diligence, risk and professional-control architecture;
- a 20 July 2021 certification attributed to Enrique Guerra Suárez recording a 262-finca perimeter: **54 CAM, 190 LPB and 18 third-party**, together with future-acquisition dependency, absence of LOI/full due diligence and earlier project withdrawal;
- CAM's filing of that certification in Concurso 36/2012 on 21 July 2021.

Public-safe significance: the 2021 material creates a finite reconciliation question between the earlier investor-facing simplification and RICPE's later documented knowledge of fragmented title and conditional project maturity. It does not by itself establish deception.

The core comparison is:

`2020 representation / portfolio communication` versus `20 July 2021 RICPE internal documentary perimeter`.

The required answer is: what changed, who knew it, what was corrected, what was communicated to investors/CNMV/advisers, and what later document justified reactivation?

### 5. CNMV — supervision as a separate gate, not project approval

The official CNMV register confirms RICPE's regulated status. It also states that responsibility for the content and accuracy of the prospectus/DFI lies with the manager or self-managed vehicle and that the CNMV does not verify that content.

The controlled CNMV response of 22 January 2026 separately confirms that supervisory actions arose from the January 2021 filing and that the located/obtained supervisory documentation did **not** include the Enrique Guerra certification of July 2021 or CAM's 21 July 2021 court filing.

Controlled significance:

- `regulated/supervised entity ≠ CNMV approval of Sun Park's title or investor representations`;
- `supervisory action confirmed ≠ public knowledge of its conclusions`;
- `two July-2021 documents absent from located supervisory corpus ≠ negligence`;
- the finite question is whether the materially narrower 2021 title/conditionality record was ever later incorporated and, if so, whether it caused reassessment or correction.

### 6. Late 2021–2022 — reactivation and documentary bridge

The corpus identifies a 29 December 2021 RICPE capital/investment decision linked to Sun Park and later review material in early 2022. That route must be reconciled against the simultaneous concurso/documentary sequence:

`18 May 2021 definitive approval → two separate 15 October 2021 confirmation/challenge Autos → two 26 January 2022 clarification Autos (no new award) → 14 February 2022 testimony/finality episode → 21 February 2022 deed → Registry → CAM→HNT corporate restructuring`.

The audit must identify the precise document or combination of documents that moved the project from **future ownership / incomplete due diligence** to a status regarded as sufficient for investment, financing, transformation and downstream administrative reliance.

Chronological proximity does not prove causation. Judicial-office issuance does not prove knowledge of later investment/public-funding use.

## RIC financing — figure-control rule

The controlled RICPE dossier attributes to the audited 2023 accounts total Series F/G financing for HNT/MYND Yaiza of **€6,573,703.10**.

However, different reproduced project/prospectus materials in the evidential corpus show a different component split from the split used in the controlled audited-account reconstruction. Therefore:

- the **€6,573,703.10 total** may be used only with attribution to the controlled audited-account record;
- do **not** publish one exact Series F / Series G split as finally reconciled until the native audited-account pages, project/prospectus versions, loan agreements, approvals, drawdowns and payment records are compared;
- the discrepancy is an evidential reconciliation issue, not proof of irregular accounting or misstatement.

The audited 2023 material also records that the participating loans were entered into after the statutory RIC procedure, with Canary Government idoneity decrees preceded by binding AEAT reports. That is a fiscal/investment-control layer, not a blanket civil-title warranty.

### 7. Regional incentives — GC/836/P06

The official/public record identifies:

- beneficiary: **HOTEL NEW TREND, S.L.**;
- location: Yaiza;
- eligible/project investment: **€11,469,714.00**;
- published subsidy: **€3,440,914.20**;

The published award establishes the concession figures. It does not by itself establish payment, final compliance, final eligibility of every cost, absence/presence of overlapping finance, or civil title to every unit in the operating perimeter.

The Dirección General de Fondos Europeos transparency record, expediente **502395**, identifies HNT as the holder of GC/836/P06 and records incorporation of communications into that administrative transparency file. Access was partial and subject to stated limits. That is institutional traceability, not proof of a substantive audit outcome.

### 8. Current EU co-financing representation

As verified on 15 August 2026, the official MYND Hotels website publicly states that the project to modernise the Sun Park apartments and transform them into the four-star MYND Yaiza hotel was **co-financed by the European Union through the Regional Economic Incentives programme/line**.

Controlled significance:

- there is a current public EU co-financing representation directly tied to the Sun Park→MYND Yaiza transformation;
- do not silently relabel that representation as FEDER/ERDF or identify a specific EU fund/programme share unless the underlying award/control file proves it;
- this does not itself establish misuse, double funding, false certification or loss to the EU budget;
- RIC/private capital, the regional-incentive subsidy and EU co-financing are legally and financially distinct layers even when they converge on the same economic project.

### 9. Current exploitation — why the issue is not historical

The current official MYND Yaiza website markets rooms, hotel services, restaurants and other guest products. The transformed asset is therefore presented and operated as a current commercial hotel, not merely as a closed historic transaction.

That current exploitation is **not automatically claimant damage**. It is relevant because any unresolved title, availability, third-party-right or financing-perimeter question can have continuing consequences through present possession/use, revenue, asset value, financing capacity, public-support compliance or restriction of another person's rights.

Correct temporal model:

`originating act → documentary implementation → serial/external reliance → investment/public-support layer → current exploitation/value → ongoing or remediable consequence`

## 10. Orion Rental SOCIMI — separate platform-continuity branch

The controlled RICPE 2023 audited material records that on **16 June 2023 RIC Private Equity constituted Orion Rental SOCIMI, S.A. with €60,000 capital**, initially as sole shareholder, and that after a later 2023 capital increase RICPE's holding fell below 1%. The same management report describes Orion as born with the same philosophy and institutional DNA as its RICPE founder.

RICPE's September 2023 project material simultaneously shows MYND Yaiza as an existing portfolio investment and Orion as a project then "en estudio".

Controlled significance:

- Orion is **not institutionally unrelated to RICPE**: RICPE itself founded it;
- Orion should be analysed as a distinct branch of **platform reuse/evolution**, not as the next step in the Sun Park public-funding chain;
- the relevant question is what capital relationships, investor base, governance, professional gatekeepers, RIC architecture, management infrastructure, track record and institutional credibility moved from RICPE into Orion.

### Orion no-money-flow rule

At present the controlled record does **not** establish that:

- Sun Park/MYND Yaiza hotel revenue funded Orion;
- the €3,440,914.20 regional-incentive/public-support amount funded Orion;
- EU-cofinanced expenditure was transferred to Orion;
- RICPE's Series F/G MYND financing was recycled into Orion;
- disputed LPB/Matkator property or value was used as Orion capital/security.

Those are separate money-flow hypotheses and require bank, accounting, loan, security and source-of-funds evidence.

The public-safe architecture is therefore a fork, not a line:

## Three simultaneous harm planes

Audit estate value, realisation, income, credit/payment accounting, alternatives to liquidation, restitution/reintegration/damages claims and the present ability to conclude or reverse consequences of Concurso 36/2012.

### Extraconcursal — Matkator and third parties

Audit title, Registry position, possession/access, works, exploitation, income/fruits, community treatment, financing/development value and current use of rights that were not part of LPB's estate merely because they were physically or operationally connected with Sun Park.

### Cross-border / transnational — Aweswell and foreign capital/rights

Audit foreign-shareholder/investor value, inbound capital or funding, impaired recovery, financing/refinancing opportunity, professional/accounting/tax/enforcement costs, foreign counterparties and any remedy or consequence crossing Spain's border.

The cross-border plane must remain separate from the distinct EU-financial-interest plane. An affected foreign investor is not the same legal proposition as EU co-financing.

## Institutional enablement — competence-specific map

| Node | What its involvement can establish | What it does not automatically establish |
|---|---|---|
| LAJ / court office | existence/content/service/delivery of court-office instruments | legality of every downstream use; knowledge of later financing/public-support use |
| Notary / Registry | documentary formalisation / registration of specified acts | historic truth of every earlier representation; ownership of non-conveyed third-party rights |
| RICPE board / committees / advisers | investment proposal, governance, due diligence, approval and financing record | that every promoter statement was independently true |
| CNMV | regulated status and supervisory activity within its legal remit | approval of Sun Park title or verification of prospectus/project factual truth |
| AEAT / Canary Government RIC gate | fiscal/RIC eligibility and statutory idoneity procedure | civil-title warranty over every project asset/right |
| Regional Incentives / DGF | grant eligibility, beneficiary/project/cost control within the incentive file | proof that every underlying civil/property dispute was resolved |
| SNCA / IGAE / EU-control functions | checks concerning EU/public financial interests within their remit | automatic fraud finding from an alert or co-financing statement |
| MYND / operator perimeter | present commercial use/operation under its contracts/title chain | that every physical/operationally integrated right belongs to HNT/MYND |

## Maximum-pressure public proposition

> The issue is not whether all these actors acted together. It is whether a premise about title, availability and project perimeter moved through successive documentary and institutional gates without each later actor independently returning to the underlying assets and rights after that premise had already been materially qualified. If every gate performed the necessary verification, the records can demonstrate it. If not, the same chain can identify where a divergence arose, broadened or persisted.

And separately:

> Orion is relevant because RICPE itself founded the later vehicle. That proves platform continuity, not a Sun Park-to-Orion money flow. The correct question is what governance, capital, investor, management and professional infrastructure was reused, and whether any actual financial bridge exists.

## What must not be inferred

This chain does **not** establish:

- a coordinated conspiracy among the court, LAJ, AC, CAM, HNT, RICPE, CNMV, public-funds authorities, MYND, Orion or AGM;
- that RIC capital is itself EU/public money;
- that the regional incentive was paid or improperly paid;
- that EU funds were misused;
- double funding;
- that CNMV approved Sun Park or verified investor-document factual truth;
- that AEAT/Government RIC idoneity certified civil ownership of every asset/right;
- that every unit or right used by the current hotel belongs to Matkator/LPB/another claimant;
- that every euro of current hotel revenue is recoverable damage;
- that money, subsidies, EU support or disputed asset value flowed from MYND/Sun Park into Orion;
- LAJ or official knowledge of any downstream misuse merely because an instrument was issued or an administrative file existed.

## Publication rule

Use **ongoing alleged harm**, **ongoing concursal and extraconcursal consequences under audit**, **cross-border consequences under audit**, **functional instrumentalisation / documentary institutionalisation / serial reliance to be verified**, and **RICPE→Orion platform continuity without presumed money flow**.

Do not reduce the matter to a historic insolvency dispute merely because some originating acts occurred in 2018 or 2022. Equally, do not convert current operation, financing, subsidy, EU co-financing, common governance or later platform reuse into damage or wrongdoing without the asset/right/cause or actual money-flow bridge.
