# CGPJ Alert → Irreversibility Visual Control

**Date:** 18 August 2026  
**Status:** public-visualisation control / evidential boundary / bilingual publication support

## Purpose

This record controls the ES/EN visual pages:

- `es/cgpj-alerta-irreversibilidad/index.html`
- `en/cgpj-alert-to-irreversibility/index.html`

The visual is a causal-supervision map, not a finding of judicial misconduct and not a conspiracy diagram.

## Controlling proposition

The strongest public formulation is not that the judge never saw the RICPE material. The controlled public record states that the material entered the proceeding, CAM and the Insolvency Administration opposed it, the LAJ placed joined material before the judge on 7 September 2021, and the 26 January 2022 decision confirmed the July material was admitted but treated it as irrelevant.

The institutional question is therefore:

`documented alert → opposition → judicial availability/knowledge at the controlled level → identifiable supervisory treatment → implementation → later consequence → increasing irreversibility`

The map asks what independent control reconciled the alert, the property perimeter, the Insolvency Administrator position, earlier judicial architecture and the transaction ultimately implemented before later title, Registry, works, capital and operation made reversal materially harder.

## Three visual zones

1. **What was known / reported** — includes external context only at its controlled knowledge level.
2. **What the insolvency system did** — court/IA/LAJ acts kept actor-specific.
3. **What happened later** — consequence and reliance, expressly not automatic historical judicial knowledge.

## Arrow semantics

- solid: documented procedural/transactional progression;
- dashed: causal/reliance relationship requiring verification;
- dotted/open: unresolved documentary bridge.

The page must not visually imply that every downstream event was caused intentionally by an earlier actor.

## Matkator / extra-insolvency lane

LPB was the insolvent debtor; the whole Sun Park complex was not the insolvency estate. Matkator and third-party property/rights remain outside LPB unless an independent legal bridge is proved for the relevant effect.

The visual asks what legal bridge authorised each alleged/material effect outside LPB — access, works, use, revenue or operational integration — and who verified it. It does not state that the judge personally took or transferred Matkator property.

## Prior architecture vs final implementation

The comparison table deliberately uses these statuses:

- documented treatment/contradiction;
- actor-by-actor separation;
- needs reconciliation;
- open bridge / production required.

It does not pre-label the final adjudication as legally non-compliant. The controlling question is: if implementation differed from prior plan/order architecture, what act authorised the difference and on what information?

## Normalising / legitimising effect boundary

The public visual may state that later judicial/registral title, works, financing and operation can have an objectively normalising or legitimising effect on an earlier commercial narrative. It must not state that the purpose of the judicial sequence was to “whitewash” CAM/RICPE unless later competent evidence establishes actor-specific intent.

The visual must end with documents that could confirm, narrow or defeat Por Derecho's interpretation, including:

- Matkator/third-party legal bridge;

Primary evidence and later official decisions control over this visual and this analytical record.
