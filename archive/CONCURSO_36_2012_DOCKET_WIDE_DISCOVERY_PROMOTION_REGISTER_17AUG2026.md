## 1. Purpose

This register activates the full docket-completion workflow for Concurso Ordinario 36/2012. It is the bridge between the historical discovery workbook `Guión - ProcConcursal - íntegro y cronológico.xlsx` and the canonical primary-source registers.

The workbook has now been read end-to-end. It contains more than six hundred indexed rows covering party filings, judicial decisions, LAJ acts, notifications, AP acts, evidential documents and later annex material. It is a discovery spine only; no row becomes VERIFIED solely because it appears there.

## 2. Governing promotion rule

For each candidate court/LAJ/office act:

`WORKBOOK ROW → LOCATE UNDERLYING SOURCE → VERIFY DATE/TYPE/SIGNATORY → EXTRACT OPERATIVE PART → RECONSTRUCT NOTIFICATION/APPEAL → PROMOTE TO MASTER REGISTER`

Where the primary binary is not yet recovered, classify as `DISCOVERY WORKBOOK`, `REFERENCED BY PRIMARY ACT`, or `OFFICIAL LATER REFERENCE` rather than guessing.
