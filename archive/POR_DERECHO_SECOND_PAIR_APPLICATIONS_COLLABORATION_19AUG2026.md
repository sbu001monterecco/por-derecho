## Application registry

1. **ICALPA DIP 80/2026 — reference implementation.** First real-case living casebook. The methodology must expose uncertainty, contradiction, competence and human gates; it must never convert opening or concern into guilt.
2. **ICALPA DIP 79/2026 — next live application.** Focal, three-act application. It must not inherit DIP 80 evidence or longitudinal assumptions.
3. **ICAM — synthetic institutional pilot.** No live ICAM file, adoption, endorsement or participation is claimed.
4. **CCACM — Council-layer synthetic pilot.** No adoption or engagement is claimed. The proposed test concerns review/appeal traceability and competence separation.

## Collaboration ladder

- observe;
- correct;
- red-team;
- synthetic pilot;
- live-case method application;
- institutional pilot;
- institutional adoption only after express institutional confirmation.

## Evidential governance

The following remain non-negotiable:

- registered ≠ reviewed;
- reviewed ≠ verified;
- verified ≠ sufficient;
- sufficient ≠ culpability;
- silence is not admission;
- continuing consequence is not automatically continuing misconduct;
- evidence does not cross from one case to another without a human relevance decision;
- every material concern must expose its strongest reasonable contrary explanation;
- every application must state what would change the view;
- every consequential branch ends in a competent human gate.

## Institutional wording rule

Never use “partner”, “adopted”, “approved”, “endorsed”, “collaboration with”, or equivalent wording for ICALPA, ICAM or CCACM unless there is an express source establishing that institutional relationship.

The permitted pre-adoption language is: **proposed pilot**, **pilot-ready**, **public methodology application**, **independent demonstrator**, or **invitation to correct/red-team/test**.
