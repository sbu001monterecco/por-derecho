## Mission

Conduct a source-complete, contradiction-aware reconstruction of **who exercised practical control over Sun Park, through which authority and instruments, over which spaces, people, information and commercial functions, and from what date**.

This protocol is designed to correct three recurring forms of fragmentation:

1. treating 7 June 2018 as an isolated access incident rather than a possible transfer of effective hotel-wide control;
2. treating CAM as an innocent outsider that appeared only after acquiring a credit or formal title, without testing its attributed physical presence, access activity and project preparation from Q3/Q4 2017 onward; and
3. treating the pre-existing Monte Lanza/Molina-associated dissident, Community and administration machinery as unrelated history, rather than testing whether CAM knowingly adopted, used, escalated or benefited from mechanisms already operating since 2011 or earlier.

The protocol must also follow the control pathway into **Lava Verde, Club Sei, Meeting Point/FTI, RICPE, HNT, MYND Yaiza and Meeting Point Spain proceeding 357/2024**, while preserving legal-person, title, competence and evidence boundaries.

This is not a direction to state a conspiracy or automatic succession of liability. It is a direction to test **continuity of mechanism, knowledge, adoption, use, escalation, benefit and non-restitution**.

## 2. Locked distinction: title, possession and control

Maintain four separate concepts throughout.

### A. Formal title

Who held registered or contractual title to each finca, credit, business right, Community power or operating mandate on the relevant date.

### B. Legal possession

Who claimed or was judicially recognised as possessing a defined property or right, within the exact parties, units, period and legal issue decided.

### C. Material or de facto possession

Who in practice occupied, held keys, controlled gates, admitted or excluded people, used private or common spaces, directed guards or locksmiths, conducted works, inspected units, dealt with clients or staff, and presented the asset to operators or investors.

### D. Effective hotel-wide control

Who had the practical capacity to determine access to and use of the hotel as an operating whole, including reception, circulation, guests, staff, maintenance, information, room inventory, project preparation, marketing and commercial counterparties.

Never treat these concepts as interchangeable.

A later deed or adjudication does not retrospectively create authority for earlier control. Conversely, evidence of material control does not itself prove legal title or criminal intent.

## 3. Central working propositions to prove, narrow or reject

### Proposition 1 — 7 June 2018 was a control threshold

Test the proposition that:

> **From 7 June 2018, CAM and actors aligned through the Community/security structure exercised effective, externally observable control over Sun Park as a hotel, not merely access to a limited number of CAM-owned apartments. The principal access regime was physically replaced; access was placed under guard control; the onsite responsible person was excluded; locks, chains or cylinders were changed; and the practical ability to admit, exclude, inspect, work on, prepare and present the complex shifted to that perimeter. Gil Marer characterises this as full material possession of the hotel.**

The investigation must determine:

- which gates, reception areas, circulation routes, common areas, private units, LPB units and Matkator units were controlled;
- who held every key/code/device before and after the event;
- who instructed, contracted and paid guards and locksmiths;
- who was excluded, admitted or required to leave;
- whether guests, staff, suppliers, technicians, notaries, operators, valuers or owners could enter without approval from that perimeter;
- whether the control was temporary, limited, continuous or later expanded;
- whether any court order or valid private title authorised hotel-wide possession, eviction, operator replacement or control;
- what the AC authorised, ratified, knew, reported, restored or failed to restore;
- what the judge knew and what protective measures were sought or refused;
- how the control affected operation, ONA diligence, goodwill, data, revenues, works and later project preparation.

### Proposition 2 — CAM’s material entry began before 7 June 2018

Test the proposition that CAM’s role did not begin with the June event or the 2022 title, but included attributed activity from Q3/Q4 2017 onward:

- an attributed 2 October 2017 incursion or attempted access through a rear entrance, with damage visible to a chain/closure and a separate reported locksmith attempt;
- 31 October 2017 security/client interference allegations and owner-like presence later examined in the Arrecife criminal record;
- the timing of those events before or around the 20 October 2017 credit-assignment route;
- January–March 2018 visits, measurements, access demands, asserted ownership, discussions of security, refurbishment, opening and possession;
- visits with advisers or prospective project participants;
- the March 2018 certified-call material concerning access, force, Community support, security, works and intended possession;
- the February/March 2018 onsite witness account of CAM-linked visitors saying the hotel was theirs, measuring hotel spaces and showing it to others.

Do not publish the personal identity of an actor from photographs alone. Use visual evidence to establish scene, damage, timing and the need for production; use testimony, metadata, access records and native communications for attribution.

### Proposition 3 — CAM entered through a pre-existing apparatus

Test the proposition that:

> **CAM did not enter Sun Park independently of the pre-existing Monte Lanza/Molina-associated dissident and Community structure. It entered through or benefited from legal positions, unit ownership, Community offices, debt and voting machinery, litigation outcomes, documentary custody, access mechanisms, relationships, information and security arrangements that had been created or consolidated before CAM’s arrival.**

The relevant historical sequence begins no later than:

- the 2008 exploitation-transfer architecture and immediate dissident challenge;
- the 2011 governance, administration, possession, maintenance and debt rupture;
- the 22 June 2011 meeting, including LPB’s exclusion from voting for alleged arrears and operational decisions taken by a small voting set;
- the Pamanil/FMMM administration and documentary-custody route;
- efforts by minority owners to recover keys or independent possession;
- Proc. 1260/2011 and the later AP 89/2014 possession judgment concerning defined units;
- Community accounts, certificates, voting exclusion, litigation and bank/control developments through 2014–2017;
- the June 2017 offices, powers, debt and administration structure;
- seller-by-seller and finca-by-finca transfers, options or acquisition channels later used by CAM.

The investigation must distinguish:

- **continuity of mechanism** from automatic succession of liability;
- lawful exercise of an owner’s rights from interference with a unitary hotel operation;
- a final possession judgment concerning 18 units from hotel-wide authority;
- Community authority over common matters from authority over LPB, Matkator, CEXP, guests, staff or the hotel business;
- knowledge and adoption by CAM from conduct merely inherited without knowledge.

The liability question is CAM’s own **knowledge, due diligence, adoption, use, escalation and benefit**, not an assumption that CAM inherited every predecessor’s personal liability.

`material access and control → measurements / pilots / works → Lava Verde project → Club Sei / Meeting Point sales-distribution platform → RICPE investor presentation and finance → 2022 formal title → CAM–HNT–MYND exploitation`.

### Publicly acceptable core wording

The following formulation may be used once the cited sources are attached to the page-level evidence bridge:

> **The direct record supports that on 7 June 2018 Sun Park’s principal access regime was physically displaced: the coded entrance was broken, chains or locks were installed, uniformed security controlled entry and the onsite responsible person was excluded. From that date, the Community/security/CAM-aligned perimeter exercised effective practical control over access to the hotel. Gil Marer alleges that this amounted to full material possession of Sun Park as an operating whole. No judicial order granting CAM possession of the whole hotel, or title over every LPB, Matkator or third-party unit, has been located.**

### Q3/Q4 2017 wording

> **The record contains attributed evidence of CAM-linked incursions or attempted access from October 2017, before or around the credit assignment, including damage to an entrance closure, a reported locksmith attempt and later judicial examination of owner-like presence and security/client interference. Attribution, authority and the precise scope of each episode remain subject to the native images, metadata, witnesses, security records and certified DP 1132 record.**

### Predecessor continuity wording

> **The alleged mechanism did not begin with CAM. The documentary history shows a dispute from 2008 and a structural rupture by 2011 involving Community governance, debt, vote, maintenance, keys, possession and litigation. The question is not automatic succession of liability: it is whether CAM knew of, adopted, used, escalated or benefited from that pre-existing machinery when it acquired units, the credit, access and the later project position.**

### Prohibited shortcuts

Do not state without additional proof:

- that CAM held lawful possession or ownership of the entire hotel from 7 June 2018;
- that every hotel unit was physically entered;
- that every guard, Community officer or predecessor acted under CAM’s instructions;
- that all guests or staff were expelled;
- that every predecessor act was criminal;
- that CAM inherited every predecessor’s liability;
- that a later title cured every earlier act;
- that Meeting Point 357/2024 concealed the relationship;
- that either judge in 357/2024 personally knew of the Sun Park material;
- that recurring judicial assignment proves coordination.
