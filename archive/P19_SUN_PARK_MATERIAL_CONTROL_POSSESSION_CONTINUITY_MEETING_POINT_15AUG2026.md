# P19 — SUN PARK MATERIAL CONTROL, POSSESSION CONTINUITY AND PRE-TITLE COMMERCIALISATION

**Predecessor apparatus → CAM entry → 7 June 2018 effective control → Lava Verde / Club Sei / Meeting Point → 2022 title and later exploitation**  
Reusable agentic protocol · **Version 2** · 15 August 2026

## Mission

Conduct a source-complete, contradiction-aware reconstruction of **who exercised practical control over Sun Park, through which authority and instruments, over which spaces, people, information and commercial functions, and from what date**.

The governing question is not merely when CAM obtained formal title to specified LPB assets. It is:

> **Who exercised practical control over Sun Park as an operating hotel at each material date, through what legal or factual mechanism, over which exact spaces and rights, and with what effect on LPB, Matkator, CEXP, other owners, guests, personnel, value, operation, recovery routes and later commercialisation?**

This protocol corrects four recurring forms of fragmentation:

1. treating 7 June 2018 as an isolated access incident instead of a possible transfer of effective hotel-wide control;
2. treating CAM as an outsider that appeared only after acquiring the credit or formal title, without testing its attributed physical presence, access activity and project preparation before 7 June;
3. treating the pre-existing Monte Lanza/Molina-associated dissident, Community and administration machinery as unrelated history, instead of testing whether CAM knowingly adopted, used, escalated or benefited from mechanisms already operating since 2011 or earlier; and
4. separating that control history from Lava Verde, Club Sei, Meeting Point/FTI, RICPE, HNT, MYND Yaiza and Meeting Point Spain proceeding 357/2024.

This is not a direction to state a conspiracy or automatic succession of liability. It is a direction to test **continuity of mechanism, knowledge, adoption, use, escalation, benefit, disclosure, preservation and non-restitution**.

### 1.2 “Full possession” must be divided into four propositions

Never use **“CAM had full legal possession of the hotel from 7 June 2018”** as an undifferentiated verified fact.

Maintain:

1. **FORMAL TITLE**  
   Who held registered or contractual title to each finca, credit, business right, Community power or operating mandate.

2. **LEGAL POSSESSION**  
   Who claimed or was judicially recognised as possessing a defined property or right, within the exact parties, units, period and issue decided.

3. **MATERIAL OR DE FACTO POSSESSION/CONTROL**  
   Who in practice occupied, held keys, controlled gates, admitted or excluded people, used private or common spaces, directed guards or locksmiths, conducted works, inspected units, dealt with guests or staff and presented the asset to operators or investors.

4. **EFFECTIVE HOTEL-WIDE CONTROL / FULL PRACTICAL POSSESSION ALLEGED**  
   Who had the practical capacity to determine access to and use of Sun Park as an operating whole, including reception, circulation, guests, staff, maintenance, information, room inventory, project preparation, marketing and commercial counterparties.

Preferred public formulation:

> **From 7 June 2018, the available evidence supports de facto material control over Sun Park’s principal access and practical operation by CAM-linked and Community actors. Gil Marer alleges that the cumulative effect amounted to full practical possession of the hotel as an operating whole. It was not a reviewed judicial transfer of whole-hotel title or possession, and the exact finca-by-finca perimeter, duration and authority for each act remain under verification.**

### 1.3 The predecessor apparatus is not automatic CAM agency or co-authorship

The strongest controlled proposition is:

> **CAM did not enter Sun Park independently of the pre-existing Monte Lanza/Molina-associated dissident and Community structure. It entered through or benefited from legal positions, unit ownership, Community offices, debt and voting machinery, litigation outcomes, documentary custody, access mechanisms, relationships, information and security arrangements created or consolidated before CAM’s arrival.**

Do not say that every predecessor acted for CAM or that CAM inherited every predecessor’s personal liability. The test is CAM’s own **knowledge, due diligence, adoption, use, escalation and benefit**.

## 3. Central working propositions to prove, narrow or reject

### Proposition 1 — 7 June 2018 was the material-control threshold

Test:

> **From 7 June 2018, CAM and actors aligned through the Community/security structure exercised effective, externally observable control over Sun Park as a hotel, not merely access to a limited number of CAM-owned apartments. The principal access regime was physically replaced; entry was placed under guard control; the onsite responsible person was excluded; locks, chains, padlocks or cylinders were changed; and the practical ability to admit, exclude, inspect, work on, prepare and present the complex shifted to that perimeter. Gil Marer characterises this as full material or practical possession of the hotel.**

Determine:

- which gates, reception areas, circulation routes, common areas, private units, LPB units and Matkator units were controlled;
- who held every key, code and access device before and after;
- who instructed, contracted and paid guards and locksmiths;
- who was excluded, admitted or required to leave;
- whether guests, staff, suppliers, technicians, notaries, operators, valuers and owners could enter without approval from that perimeter;
- whether the control was temporary, limited, continuous or later expanded;
- whether any court order or valid private title authorised whole-hotel possession, eviction, operator replacement or control;
- what the AC authorised, ratified, knew, reported, restored or failed to restore;
- what the judge knew and what protective measures were sought, granted, denied or left outside the procedural vehicle;
- how the control affected operation, ONA diligence, goodwill, data, revenues, works and later project preparation.

### Proposition 2 — CAM’s attributed material entry began before 7 June

Test CAM’s attributed activity from **Q4 2017 onward**, while searching Q3 independently:

- the attributed **2 October 2017** incursion or attempted access through a rear entrance, with damage to security closures/padlocks and private-security presence;
- native photographs, customer complaints and later court/police use;

- the 8 January 2018 reported visit with security/escort, locksmiths and hotel plans;
- January–March 2018 access demands, measurements, asserted ownership, security, refurbishment, opening and possession planning;
- visits with advisers or prospective project participants;

- Community authority over common matters from authority over LPB, Matkator, CEXP, guests, staff or the hotel business;
