## 1. Governing position

The Project's strongest presently defensible proposition is not that every named actor has already been proved to belong to one uninterrupted criminal conspiracy. It is that multiple independently documented personal, representational, corporate, title, voting, creditor, security/access, project and operating bridges support a substantial functional-convergence investigation from the 2009–2011 Community/litigation perimeter into the later CAM/Acosta Matos structure.

Maximum pressure must therefore be produced by **specificity and traceability**, not by collapsing evidence grades:

`actor ID → capacity → act/omission → date → source → notice/knowledge → consequence → benefit → contrary explanation → missing proof → requested action`.

Every public or institutional use of this protocol must preserve these boundaries:

- allegation is not adjudication;
- relationship is not automatic knowledge or intent;
- notice is not knowledge of falsity;
- benefit is not concert;
- later convergence may corroborate adoption, ratification, knowledge and benefit but cannot by itself prove a common plan in 2011;
- the Administrador Concursal remains on a separate officeholder enablement/adoption track unless new evidence establishes private-perimeter membership;
- adverse and exculpatory evidence must be preserved and published with the allegation it affects.

## 2. Pressure sequence

### PRESERVE

Demand immediate preservation of native documents, metadata, communications, devices, accounts, ledgers, minutes, access records, images, recordings, security/locksmith records, court audit trails, title and banking records, professional files, due-diligence reports and board papers.

### DISCLOSE

Seek certified or native records with provenance. A summary, reconstructed spreadsheet or recollection is not an adequate substitute where the primary record exists.

### EXPLAIN

Require each actor to identify:

- legal or contractual authority;
- client, principal or beneficiary;
- information received;
- verification undertaken;
- instruction given or relied upon;
- decision or omission;
- immediate and later consequence;
- financial, professional, voting, possession or project benefit;
- strongest innocent explanation.

### INVESTIGATE

Test offence families and professional duties element by element. Do not use the umbrella phrase `estafa concursal` as a substitute for proving fraud, procedural reliance, insolvency conduct, disloyal administration, documentary falsity/use, coercion, damage or qualifying public-office conduct.
