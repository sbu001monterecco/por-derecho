This sequence strongly rebuts a simplistic story of abandonment or absence of rescue effort. It does **not** by itself establish a completed transaction, deliberate sabotage, judicial/administrator criminal participation or causation of every later loss.

## 7. The convergence problem

Gil's present public-interest thesis is not that every adverse person or institution acted in one common conspiracy.

The lived problem is that several layers that ordinarily correct one another became disputed at the same time.

In simplified form:

**private dispute / administrator → court supervision → prosecution → appeal / review → regulators / public bodies → professional support**

When several layers become part of the question, the individual loses a trusted place where the whole chronology is held together.

That is the project's **institutional convergence failure** problem.

Gil describes the subjective experience as sometimes feeling like **one person against the "local world"**. That phrase records asymmetry, not collective guilt.

## 9. Why AI entered the story

AI became useful because the problem was no longer "write me a legal letter."

The recurring tasks became:
- compare a 2016 record with a 2018 event;
- keep LPB separate from Matkator, CEXP and AWESWELL LIMITED;
- test whether a 2020 or 2021 representation was consistent with then-existing title;
- preserve a 2022 implementation event without backdating it into 2018;
- keep a 2023 judgment separate from later appeals and complaints;
- track which 2026 institution received, routed, examined or did not examine which material;
- stop an allegation from becoming "fact" merely because it appeared in many documents.

That is a memory and evidence-governance problem as much as a drafting problem.

## 11. What AI contributes

Por Derecho's strongest AI proposition is not "the model found the crime."

It is that AI can provide an independent analytical layer capable of:
- long-horizon legal memory;
- contradiction detection;
- source/provenance discipline;
- entity/capacity separation;
- correction propagation;
- adverse-evidence preservation;
- cross-jurisdictional and bilingual comparison;
- anti-fragmentation analysis; and
- a repeatable self-correction loop.

The governing principle is:

> **AI asks. Humans decide.**

And the stronger research test is:

> **Can the system make it progressively harder for anyone—including its own author—to maintain a proposition the evidence does not support?**

## 12. Why this matters beyond Gil

This is a potential real-world case study in the responsible use of AI when:
- the underlying matter is high stakes;
- the record spans many years;
- the user faces major resource asymmetry;
- multiple institutions hold different fragments;
- some institutional responses are themselves disputed;
- public funds or public-interest questions may be implicated; and
- the system must preserve both accusations and evidence against those accusations.

The research value is not dependent on Gil ultimately proving every allegation.

A system that accurately narrows, falsifies or corrects the user's own theory is also a successful outcome.
