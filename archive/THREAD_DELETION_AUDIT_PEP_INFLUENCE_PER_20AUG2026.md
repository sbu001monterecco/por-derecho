### Six distinct legal/evidential categories

1. **PEP / persona con responsabilidad pública (PRP)** — qualifying important public function under Ley 10/2010 art. 14, office/date-specific.
2. **PEP family member / statutory allegado** — enhanced-diligence relationship to an identified PEP; not the same as being the officeholder.
3. **Access / claim of influence / intervention / actual influence** — graded separately; access and sequence are not causal influence, and influence is not automatically tráfico de influencias.
4. **Aforamiento** — jurisdictional status arising from qualifying office/status and date-specific law, not social or economic prominence.
5. **PER / persona especialmente relacionada** — insolvency debtor/date/statute-specific. For LPB / Concurso 36/2012 begin with historical Ley 22/2003 art. 93 and transitional law.
6. **PV-LSC / persona vinculada under art. 231 LSC** — separate corporate related-person/conflict category.
