## Source-status rule

Use these levels throughout:

- **VERIFIED FACT:** proved by an original institutional receipt, court filing/notice, signed act or other primary source.
- **PARTY ALLEGATION / TESTIMONY:** a proposition actually made by a party or witness, but not thereby independently proved.
- **EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE:** a conclusion reasonably drawn from verified events, expressly identified as an inference.
- **OPEN QUESTION:** requires further evidence.

No part of this control establishes corruption, collusion, friendship, improper influence, prevaricación or any criminal offence as adjudicated fact.

## The 4 February 2021 court filing — why it matters

The 4-Feb reposición is a critical knowledge bridge because it expressly alleged, inside Concurso 36/2012, that:

- CAM was acting publicly as though it were owner of LPB / Sun Park assets while it remained a creditor/acquisition candidate in the concurso;
- CAM was presenting Sun Park publicly as its project;
- investor interest was being sought through RIC Private Equity;
- RICPE webinar/web material and material remitted to CNMV were annexed or invoked; and
- the same hotel was therefore being represented externally in an investment/project life while the LPB asset perimeter remained subject to the insolvency process.

These propositions remain **Aweswell's allegations in a verified formal filing**. The filing and its court registration are facts; the underlying allegations require their own proof.

## Calificación relevance — truth inversion / diversion of scrutiny

The surviving Pink/rent causal frame can be summarised, without erasing its adverse legal status, as:

`PINK RENT NOT COLLECTED → GIL DID NOT SUFFICIENTLY RECOVER IT → LPB LOSS / AGGRAVATION → €3,032,010.34 CONSEQUENCE`

The unitary evidential challenge is that the prior record requires an earlier stage:

`KNOWN PARALLEL LIVES → ACTUAL CONTROL / ACCESS / CAPACITY → LEGAL DUTY → REAL ABILITY TO COLLECT / OPERATE → COMPETING CAUSATION → ATTRIBUTED CAUSATION → CULPABILITY`

The user's **truth-inversion thesis** is that a narrower real or disputed fact — unpaid contractual rent — was converted into culpability without adequately reconstructing actual power, control, operating capacity and competing causal actors.

The user's **diversion-of-scrutiny thesis** is that an outward-facing question — who was controlling, presenting, financing, acquiring and extracting prospective value from the same hotel while LPB remained in concurso — receded while punitive causal scrutiny concentrated inward on Gil/Pink.

These are party allegations / evidence-led hypotheses. They do not, by themselves, establish knowing falsification, collusion or prevaricación.
