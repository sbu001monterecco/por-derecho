# Open message to the Ministerio Fiscal — updated thesis, objective and recipient map

**Project Sun Rock / Por Derecho · 15 August 2026**

## Updated thesis

The message is no longer aimed at one territorial Fiscalía or at obtaining another isolated answer to another filing. It is directed to the **Ministerio Fiscal as a single institution**, and within it to every office and person that has received, decided, referred, supervised, reported on, or been asked to review a material part of the Sun Park / LPB / Aweswell / Matkator matter.

The thesis to be tested is **not** “Fiscalía de Las Palmas is the problem.” It is broader and more institutional:

> A prosecution system can become functionally instrumentalised when each isolated decision may appear defensible, yet no level assumes responsibility for reconciling the whole history, correcting inherited factual premises, distinguishing later facts from earlier ones, preserving legally distinct asset/entity perimeters, and testing whether repeated referral back into the same decisional perimeter has produced genuine cumulative reconsideration.

This is a question of **institutional path-dependence, fragmentation, asymmetric scrutiny and functional effect**, not a pre-judged allegation of corruption or personal collusion.

## Updated objective

The objective is to obtain a **cross-institutional, auditable response** in which each relevant level can identify:

1. what it demonstrably received;
2. what it actually decided or did;
3. what it referred to another office;
4. what it did not decide or left unresolved;
5. what later evidence altered or contradicted earlier premises;
6. what happened after its intervention;
7. whether the overall sequence produced sufficiently cumulative, objective and evidence-responsive reconsideration.

The message asks the Ministerio Fiscal to use its unity of action, hierarchy and specialist/territorial architecture as a mechanism for **correction and coordination**, not merely for routing.

## Public audience

The open message is also deliberately addressed to judges, prosecutors, lawyers, journalists, academics, investors and citizens concerned with **rule of law, tutela judicial efectiva, EOMF legality/impartiality, and institutional accountability**.

They are not asked to adopt Project Sun Rock's conclusion. They are asked to examine whether the documentary sequence supports the institutional questions posed.

## Controlling discipline

Naming a person means attributing a documented public act or identifying the current holder of a relevant office. It does **not** attribute the entire history, institutional knowledge, bias, affinity, collusion, capture or improper motive to that individual.

The public thesis remains:

> The strongest answer to a concern about capture or functional instrumentalisation is not a declaration that it does not exist. It is a traceable institutional process capable of demonstrating that materially new evidence, reversed procedural roles, corrected factual premises and distinct legal perimeters received genuinely independent and cumulative consideration.
