## 1. Result

The repository previously had a strong 2017–2022 transaction spine and selected full-text orders, but not a single source-controlled reconstruction of the court file from the definitive-text/convenio threshold. The 24-August sweep located and classified a **65-item 2016–2021 docket working denominator**, plus one connected external complaint, five backward-foundation controls and a separate calificación lane. Judicial/LAJ acts, party or insolvency-administration filings, annexes and external complaints are not equivalent evidence.

This closes the narrative gap between the 2016 definitive texts, the unsuccessful 2017 convenio route, liquidation, the 2018 plan/appeal family, the 2019 non-convalidation control and the 2021 licitation/adjudication sequence. It does not close the certified-docket denominator, annex completeness, service/finality, implementation or accounting.

## 2. Reverse-engineered procedural route

### A. Common phase to convenio

- **28-Apr-2016:** the insolvency administrator filed definitive texts. LPB opposed on 16-Jun-2016. These are party/AC positions; the complete resolution chain must be read with them.
- **20-Jan-2017:** a court order modified the definitive texts to include a contingent ordinary Community credit for pre-insolvency fees without its own amount. Post-insolvency mass claims were not thereby converted into a finally quantified debt.
- **22-Mar-2017:** the court ended the common phase, opened convenio and called a 28-Jun-2017 creditors' meeting.
- **19/27-Apr-2017:** LPB's proposal and viability plan advanced preservation of activity over liquidation. Ordinary claims were offered 100% with no haircut after a one-year wait; subordinated claims followed after ordinary performance; Aweswell guaranteed payment. Privileged creditors were excluded absent adhesion, and the proposal referred to refinancing negotiations with the specially privileged creditor.
- **15-May-2017:** Promontoria challenged the route. The filing records its position, not the outcome by itself.
- **19-Dec-2017:** because no convenio was accepted, the court opened liquidation, suspended LPB's powers over its own patrimony, dissolved LPB, required a plan within fifteen days and quarterly reports.

### B. Liquidation plan and appeal family

- **15-Feb-2018:** the current three-page primary-source control recognized CAM as transferee of specified Promontoria specially privileged claims. A located Drive copy carries 8/9/14-February layers, but those are superseded pending direct reinspection and are not published as act dates. The AC filed the liquidation plan on 9-Feb-2018.
- **15-Feb to 16-Mar-2018:** AC clarification and LPB observations defined the dispute over interest, value, better offers and realization structure.
- **16-Apr-2018:** two different orders exist. One clarified that specially privileged interest could increase only up to the mortgage limits. The other approved the liquidation plan with conditions: dation of listed mortgaged apartments; a separate direct sale of listed premises/pools for EUR 400,000; publicity; a ten-day better-offer mechanism; and quarterly reports. It did **not** adopt Actúa's valuation figures as findings.
- **4/15/26-Jun-2018:** the court clarified that any later licitation would be a flexible appearance with successive bids; the LAJ ordered publicity; the court then suspended realization of the listed mortgaged apartments pending appeal while liquidation continued for other estate assets.
- **23-Jul-2019:** the Audiencia Provincial partially allowed LPB's appeal only on the interest ceilings used to calculate what a third-party improver would have to offer. It maintained the dation/direct-sale structure and did not make the definitive interest calculation or classification. The source filename says 29 July; the order itself is dated 23 July.

### C. Non-convalidation and amount-setting

- **12-Dec-2018:** the LAJ declared the better-offer period closed and directed formalization of the premises/pools sale.
- **24-Oct-2019:** the court set that Diligencia aside and refused to validate the 28-Nov-2018 deed because the transaction was consummated without prior judicial authorization and did not comply with the approved plan. It ordered renewed publicity and required CAM and the AC to certify the then-current default-interest and Community figures. This is a material favourable control, but it is not a finding of criminal conduct.
- **12-May-2020:** the court dismissed LPB's reconsideration. It promised party submissions before finally setting interest/Community amounts and treated newly raised demolition/access/inspection requests as extemporaneous in that procedural vehicle. That is a procedural rejection, not a merits finding that the alleged events did or did not occur.
- **2020 certification sequence:** LPB, CAM, the Community and the AC advanced conflicting calculations. Each remains a source statement until bridged to the final judicial amount and underlying loan/Community ledgers.

### D. Publicity, licitation and approval

- **25/29-Jan-2021:** the court and LAJ published a better-offer route including EUR 13,168,082.02 across the two mortgage loans, the separate EUR 400,000, taxes/costs and EUR 1,145,798.29 Community charges. Those figures describe a third-party threshold; they are not automatically CAM's cash price or one recognized insolvency credit.
- **4-Feb-2021:** Aweswell and LPB challenged the route. Their filings preserve objections; they are not adjudicated facts.
- **6-May-2021:** one order rejected LPB's nullity application; another dismissed Aweswell/LPB reconsideration and confirmed the 25-Jan publicity order.
- **12-May-2021:** the court opened the 18-May licitation and fixed a EUR 735,694.02 bond for the improving bidder. The private bank account in the source is excluded.
- **18-May-2021:** Order 164/2021 definitively approved CAM's proposal after recording that the third-party bidder did not appear and did not lodge the bond. It approved dation for the listed apartments and EUR 400,000 direct sale for the listed premises/pools, with CAM bearing taxes/costs and pending Community charges.
- **15-Oct-2021:** two distinct orders dismissed challenges respectively to the 18-May order and 12-May Providencia. Later 26-Jan-2022 clarifications remain separate and do not create a new 2022 adjudication.

## 3. Adverse record that must remain prominent

1. The 24-Nov-2015 Pink/Monterecco judgment is materially adverse: it resolved the lease for non-payment, ordered Monterecco to vacate 159 apartments, 29 premises and four pools, and awarded EUR 2,733,923.64 plus interest, later rent and costs. It belongs in the foundation even where later criticism is advanced.
2. The 23-Jul-2019 AP order rejected LPB's principal attack on the dation/valuation framework and accepted only the interest-ceiling branch.
3. The 12-May-2020 order rejected LPB's reconsideration and did not entertain demolition/access requests in that motion.
4. The 6-May-2021 orders rejected nullity and reconsideration; the 18-May order approved CAM; the 15-Oct orders rejected later challenges.
5. A source-led case can criticize reasoning, evidence handling, asymmetry or implementation, but an adverse result is not by itself proof of abuse, knowing judicial participation or criminality.

## 4. Strongest source-supported criticism

The strongest current criticism is structural and testable:

- the file repeatedly risks collapsing different money functions—recognized principal, accruing interest within mortgage limits, third-party improvement threshold, EUR 400,000 cash component, Community charges, deed consideration and final accounting;
- authorization, public notice, licitation, deed, Registry and estate accounts are separate acts, yet the complete implementation bridge remains unavailable;
- the 24-Oct-2019 refusal to validate an already consummated deed demonstrates why post hoc notice cannot substitute for prior authorization and plan compliance;
- the located record does not yet show a complete Article 152/424 report series, full hearing/appearance record, third-party offer authority/funds, service/finality chain or final accounting bridge; and
- estate perimeter must remain finca-specific: LPB's property cannot be expanded to Matkator or other third-party property by narrative shorthand.

Gil Marer's broader allegation is that adverse actors instrumentally used civil, insolvency and criminal proceedings inside and outside 36/2012. The repository records this as an attributed allegation and a retrieval hypothesis. To strengthen it responsibly, each proposed criticism must identify act, actor, contemporaneous source, knowledge evidence, materiality, decision effect, benefit/harm, causal bridge, contrary evidence and innocent alternative.

## 5. Website decision

The website should present the procedural spine, decisive adverse/favourable holdings, money-function control and finite missing-source requests. It should not publish raw party PDFs, signatures, IDs, addresses, bank data, confidential expert material or email bodies. Public full text is appropriate for redacted official acts after page-faithful review; party filings receive balanced source cards paired with the counter-position and outcome.
