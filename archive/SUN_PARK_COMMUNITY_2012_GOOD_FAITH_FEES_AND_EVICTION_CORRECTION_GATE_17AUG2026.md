## 1. Why this correction exists

Future Project Sun Rock / Por Derecho work must **own and explain**, rather than conceal or rationalise away, an early-2012 operational mistake made by the incoming hotel-management side.

The user’s present correction is that when the incoming management team, including the reserved declarant from Gil Marer's personal/business perimeter, arrived in the Sun Park situation in early 2012 they were **new to the pre-existing ownership / Community / exploitation conflict, materially misinformed and, in the user’s account, misadvised**. During the first period of operating the hotel they applied and invoiced charges described as Community / maintenance-type fees to owners who had withdrawn their units from the common hotel exploitation while continuing to occupy, rent, lend or otherwise use those units and consume hotel/common infrastructure such as electricity and water.

The user now states expressly that **this charging model was wrong / incorrect as a matter of the legal and governance structure then applicable**, notwithstanding the practical problem of non-participating owners consuming services without contributing to the operating cost base. The Project must continue to acknowledge that error wherever the invoices or early-2012 Community-cost practice are material.

This is a **correction and admission**, not a deletion instruction. Do not suppress the invoices, correspondence or accounting if located. Preserve them, classify them accurately, and explain the context and the later correction.

## 2. Mandatory distinction — the 2012 mistake is not a precedent validating later Community-organ authority

Future threads must not reason:

> `Gil / incoming operator invoiced Community-type fees in 2012, therefore the later disputed Community organ, administrator, debt certificates, vote exclusions or authority claims were valid.`

That inference is prohibited unless a competent source independently establishes the relevant legal authority.

The Project position is instead:

1. the incoming management side temporarily used an incorrect fee/invoicing mechanism;
2. the reason given by the user was an operational cost anomaly: owners outside the common hotel exploitation were nevertheless consuming water, electricity, occupation/access and other infrastructure without contributing on the same basis as participating units;
3. the user accepts responsibility for the incorrect charging approach and does not seek to convert it into a lawful Community-governance precedent;
4. the existence of those invoices may show what the incoming operators believed or attempted at the time, but **does not by itself establish valid Community authority, valid debt, valid vote exclusion, valid office-holding, or a lawful later capture/continuity of the Community organ**;
5. equally, the Project must not use the later correction to pretend the invoices never existed or that every operational concern behind them was imaginary. The legal mechanism and the underlying cost/non-payment problem are separate propositions.

## 7. Core anti-instrumentalisation rule for future searches

Whenever an adverse pleading, Community minute, AC report, Fiscal position, judgment, owner statement or later actor relies on early invoices issued by the incoming operator, future threads must ask all of the following:

1. What exact invoice / ledger / communication is being relied on?
2. Who issued it, in what capacity and on what stated legal basis?
3. Was the issuer purporting to act as hotel operator, CEXP, Comunidad, owner, administrator or something else?
4. What services / costs were actually being recovered?
5. Which unit / owner was charged and what was the participation status at that date?
6. Did the invoice purport to create Community voting debt, or was it an operating-cost recovery mechanism?
7. Was the invoice challenged, paid, credited, reversed or litigated?
8. What did the incoming operators later learn that caused them to abandon/correct the approach?
9. Is a later actor using the invoice merely as evidence of historical practice, or improperly treating it as legal validation of a later authority structure?
10. What independent statute, contract, acta, title, court decision or other legal source establishes the later actor’s authority regardless of the 2012 invoice?

The burden of proving a later Community organ’s legitimacy does **not** disappear because an earlier incoming operator made the same or a superficially similar charging error.
