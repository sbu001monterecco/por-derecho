## Controlling rule

Use the person's full, date-correct and capacity-correct title on the first
narrative reference. Do not use bare `Alberto`, `Juez Alberto`, `Judge Alberto`
or `Magistrado Alberto` as a narrative designation. A later reference may use
`el magistrado López Villarrubia` / `Magistrate López Villarrubia`.

## Reorganisation verified from official sources

Organic Law 1/2025 provides that existing courts were transformed into sections
of the new Courts of Instance; judges and magistrates moved to the corresponding
section with the same cardinal number and retained their pending matters. The
remaining courts—including the mercantile courts—were transformed on **31
December 2025**. Judicial positions within each section are identified by
cardinal number.

The current CGPJ directory identifies:

- **Sección de lo Mercantil del Tribunal de Instancia de Las Palmas de Gran
  Canaria. Plaza n.º 1** — magistrado D. Alberto López Villarrubia; and
- **Sección de lo Mercantil del Tribunal de Instancia de Las Palmas de Gran
  Canaria. Plaza n.º 3** — Guillermo Fernández García.

## Meeting Point 357/2024 boundary

The 24-Oct-2024 event predates the reorganisation. Its organ, if established,
must therefore be named as the **then Juzgado de lo Mercantil n.º 3**, not
retroactively as `plaza n.º 3`. The three Public Insolvency Register notices are
evidence of attribution; they are not by themselves proof that the magistrate
personally signed the underlying act or acted in a particular substitution,
support or allocation capacity.

Until the signed original and allocation trail are produced, use the
register-attribution formulation above. Do not write that the magistrate
`substituted`, `joined`, `signed`, `concealed` or `failed to disclose` as an
established fact. Gil Marer's conflict, concealment, injury and crossed-line
theory remains a deliberate attributed allegation and is not weakened by this
capacity discipline.

## Preservation exceptions

Do not alter:

- exact quotations or historical captions;
- signed-document transcriptions;
- official source literals;
- hash-controlled historical catalogue literals (correct the reader-facing
  rendering or add an adjacent terminology note; do not invalidate custody
  hashes merely to modernise an honorific);
- filenames, slugs, URLs or search aliases; or
- the immutable CAEPR personal name.

Place the controlled title outside a quotation or source literal. Historical
court names remain historically correct; the current name does not operate
retroactively.
