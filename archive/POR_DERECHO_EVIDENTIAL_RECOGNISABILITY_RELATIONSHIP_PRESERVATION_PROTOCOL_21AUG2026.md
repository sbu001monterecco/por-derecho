# POR DERECHO — EVIDENTIAL RECOGNISABILITY AND RELATIONSHIP-PRESERVATION PROTOCOL

**Control date:** 21 August 2026  
**Status:** `CANONICAL CROSS-CUTTING ADDENDUM — UNITARY RECONSTRUCTION / SECOND PAIR / INSTITUTIONAL HANDOFF`  
**Applies to:** Project Sun Rock / Por Derecho substantive legal, regulatory, administrative, professional, criminal, concursal, tax, funding and public-authority work.

> **This protocol does not establish that any actor, court, authority, professional or institution fragmented a matter deliberately or unlawfully. It defines how Por Derecho must preserve the relationships between evidence when lawful procedural separation risks making a connected factual reality unrecognisable.**

## 1. Governing principle

**Separate responsibility. Integrate evidence.**

Different actors, proceedings, legal persons, jurisdictions, remedies, dates and competences must remain legally distinct. At the same time, where two or more facts derive meaning from their relationship, the analytical system must preserve that relationship rather than treating each fact as a self-contained universe.

The aim is not to create one super-proceeding or one super-authority. It is to prevent a common factual dependency from disappearing merely because different decision-makers lawfully see different parts of it.

## 2. Evidential recognisability

**Evidential recognisability** is the ability of a competent independent reader to understand the material meaning of evidence after it has been divided among files, institutions, legal categories, actors or time periods.

Evidence can remain physically intact while its evidential meaning is weakened or lost if the relationships that make it significant are not preserved.

Example structure:

`Document A` may be accurate in isolation.  
`Document B` may also be accurate in isolation.  
The material evidential proposition may nevertheless be the relationship `A → B`, including timing, dependency, adoption, contradiction, use, omission, consequence or change of position.

Accordingly:

> **Sometimes the evidential object is not Document A or Document B. It is the relationship A → B.**

## 3. The breadth paradox

Por Derecho must test for the following procedural failure mode:

> **The whole can be too broad to investigate. The pieces can be too small to reveal the whole.**

A broad submission may properly be rejected, narrowed, transferred or divided because a body lacks competence to determine the whole matter. But if every resulting fragment is then assessed without the minimum context needed to understand its relationship to the others, the overall evidential reality can become procedurally invisible.

This is a **recognisability problem**, not automatically a fault or misconduct finding.

## 4. Relationship types that must be preserved

Where materially relevant, record explicit edges between facts or propositions, including:

- source → later repetition;
- authority → act;
- title → claimed control;
- possession → exploitation;
- debt → vote exclusion;
- Community authority → certificate;
- certificate → concursal or judicial reliance;
- control → commercial presentation;
- commercial presentation → investor/funding reliance;
- adjudication → later title claim;
- prior act → later retrospective description;
- notice → later decision;
- contradiction → correction or non-correction;
- challenged act → remedy sought;
- administrative file → judicial record;
- private input → institutional receipt → verification → act;
- asset → funding representation → works → operator → income;
- document → recipient → use → benefit/harm;
- one institution's unresolved fact → another institution's competence.

A relationship edge must state its source status. Sequence alone does not prove causation, adoption, knowledge or coordination.

## 5. Mandatory recognisability test

For every material multi-file or multi-forum issue ask:

1. **Relationship** — what relationship between otherwise separate facts is necessary to understand the issue?
2. **Fragmentation point** — where is that relationship lost or obscured when the matter is divided by actor, legal person, proceeding, jurisdiction, time, asset, remedy, professional function or evidence bundle?
3. **Reason for separation** — is the separation legally required, procedurally ordinary, administratively convenient, caused by pleading or evidence choices, accidental, strategically used by a private actor, or presently unexplained?
4. **Minimum context** — what is the smallest lawful context the receiving decision-maker needs to assess its own issue fairly without exceeding competence?
5. **Relationship custodian** — which canonical record, evidence graph or human reviewer preserves the connection if no single authority may decide the whole?
6. **Finite restoration step** — what document, certification, referral, cross-reference, annex, chronology, graph edge or institutional handoff would restore recognisability?
7. **Counterfactual** — could the fragment reasonably produce a different interpretation when seen with the missing relationship?
8. **Contrary explanation** — what lawful or innocent explanation supports the separation or weakens the claimed relationship?
9. **Status** — is the relationship verified, strongly supported, contested, open, contradicted or sequential only?
10. **Human gate** — who is competent to decide what consequence, if any, follows?

## 6. Fragmentation classification

Use the least accusatory classification supported by evidence:

### F0 — no material fragmentation
The relevant relationship remains visible and the division creates no material analytical loss.

### F1 — lawful / ordinary compartmentalisation
Separate treatment follows competence, procedure, privacy, legal-person boundaries or ordinary case management and the material relationship remains recoverable.

### F2 — consequential procedural atomisation
The separation is lawful or ordinary, but the resulting fragments no longer expose a material relationship needed for fair understanding.

### F3 — transmission / notification / record fragmentation
Material context is lost because documents, notifications, attachments, metadata, transfers, indexing, registration or record provenance do not travel with the issue.

### F4 — strategic private fragmentation hypothesis
There is evidence that a private actor may have used different entities, time periods, transaction descriptions, files or audiences in a way that requires reconciliation. This is an investigative classification, not a finding of deception.

### F5 — possible coordinated fragmentation requiring investigation
Use only where actor-specific evidence supports communications, instructions, shared plans, coordinated timing, adoption or another concrete coordination mechanism. Similar outcomes, institutional sequence or common interest are insufficient.

Do not skip directly from F1/F2 to F5.

## 7. Relationship evidence is not guilt evidence

A relationship can be material without being unlawful.

The following are prohibited shortcuts:

- fragmentation → conspiracy;
- inconsistent description → deliberate falsehood;
- institutional non-verification → knowing participation;
- transfer between bodies → evasion of responsibility;
- lack of competence → merits rejection;
- adverse outcome → bias or misconduct;
- repetition → independent corroboration;
- sequence → causation;
- economic benefit → participation;
- broad similarity → coordination.

Where criminal or disciplinary relevance is considered, return to actor-specific knowledge, intent, act, reliance, benefit/harm, causation and contrary explanation.

## 8. Relationship-preservation data model

For each material relationship capture:

`REL-ID | proposition A | proposition B | relationship type | date range | source(s) | source status | forum(s) holding A | forum(s) holding B | forum with both | relationship visible? | reason for separation | consequence of loss | contrary explanation | restoration step | competent human gate`

Suggested IDs:

`REL-YYYY-NNN`

A relationship ID does not replace the canonical proposition IDs or evidence IDs. It connects them.

## 10. Integration with The Second Pair of Eyes

Do **not** add a seventh core check.

The locked sequence remains:

`Source → Authority → Perimeter → Contradiction → Consequence → Reversibility`

Recognisability is a cross-cutting overlay, especially across **Perimeter**, **Contradiction** and **Consequence**:

- **Perimeter:** what was excluded, and did exclusion remove a relationship essential to understanding the included issue?
- **Contradiction:** do two fragments become contradictory, compatible or explanatory only when viewed together?
- **Consequence:** did loss of the relationship materially affect the decision, remedy, routing, burden, delay or ability to verify?

Every live application should state `RECOGNISABILITY: preserved / degraded / unresolved / not material` where the issue spans more than one file, institution, legal person or time perimeter.

## 11. Institutional handoff rule

A body need not decide matters outside its competence. Where a material relationship crosses competence boundaries, Por Derecho should formulate the smallest lawful shared-fact package:

1. the common fact or relationship;
2. why it matters within the recipient's own competence;
3. what the recipient need not decide;
4. the two or three primary sources needed first;
5. what remains unresolved;
6. the competent holder or next recipient for the unresolved branch;
7. whether referral, certification, preservation or information exchange is legally available or merely proposed.

This is designed to preserve context without asking an institution to become a super-authority.

## 12. Public/private source boundary

Recognisability does not justify publishing confidential, privileged, tax, personal, sealed, restricted or redistribution-sensitive evidence.

The public layer may show:

- proposition IDs;
- relationship IDs;
- dates;
- source type and lawful public references;
- status;
- open question;
- institutional relevance;
- synthetic illustrations.

The private evidence layer retains native documents, protected correspondence, metadata and restricted annexes. Future reviewers must re-query lawful source systems rather than reconstruct private evidence from public summaries.

## 14. Synthetic red-team requirement

The independent review battery must include a **fragmented-truth / breadth-paradox** case in which:

- no individual document is false;
- each institution acts within a plausible competence boundary;
- the material issue becomes visible only through two or more cross-file relationships;
- one variant contains benign lawful compartmentalisation;
- one variant contains harmful but non-deliberate atomisation;
- one variant contains evidence of strategic private audience/perimeter switching;
- the method must distinguish the variants without guilt scoring.

Passing the test requires the method to restore the necessary relationship while preserving competence, uncertainty, privacy and a competent human decision gate.

## 15. Public explanatory formulation

Permitted concise formulation:

> **Separate responsibility. Integrate evidence.** Complex matters often have to be divided among different courts, authorities, companies and legal questions. That separation can be entirely lawful. The risk arises when the relationship between the fragments disappears. Por Derecho's method therefore asks not only whether each document is accurate in isolation, but whether the connections needed to understand it remain visible.

Permitted supporting formulation:

> **The whole can be too broad to investigate; the pieces can be too small to reveal the whole.** The answer is not one super-authority. It is traceable relationships, finite handoffs and human decisions within competence.

## 16. What would change the view?

For every fragmentation hypothesis actively search for evidence that:

- the receiving authority did have the connected material;
- the relationship was expressly considered;
- the legal separation necessarily excluded the other material;
- the allegedly contradictory descriptions concerned genuinely different perimeters;
- the later description was corrected or independently verified;
- no decision materially depended on the missing relationship;
- an innocent workflow, notification, indexing or representation explanation accounts for the gap.

A method that cannot clear a fragmentation concern when these facts are established has failed.

## 18. Controlling caution

The purpose of this protocol is to make the record **more falsifiable and more independently reviewable**, not more accusatory.

The central discipline is therefore:

> **Preserve the relationship. Attribute responsibility only when separately proved.**
