## Control rules

- `not located` does not mean `did not exist`;
- a later external use does not establish that the issuing LAJ knew of it;
- the ATLANTE statement is not proof that every external copy is preserved;
- `general file-inclusion representation ≠ item-level certified incorporation`;
- `receipt / taking knowledge ≠ agreement ≠ admission ≠ merits finding`;
- the CGPJ/TSJ gap is not proof of non-dispatch or loss;
- appeal filing is not proof of forwarding, admission, examination or outcome;
- document-chain irregularity is not automatically disciplinary or criminal wrongdoing.
