## Non-negotiable continuity rules

- Do not convert allegation, receipt, registration, opening or urgency into culpability.
- Do not treat silence or a response not located publicly as admission.
- Keep DIP 79 and DIP 80 evidence separate unless a human relevance decision explains cross-use.
- Do not compress the entire 2012–2026 history into one continuing professional act.
- Preserve strongest contrary explanation and “what would change my view?” for every material module.
- Every consequential branch ends at a named human gate.
- Radical transparency applies to method, criteria, alternatives, provenance and reasons—not to confidential case files or unnecessary personal data.
- The correct current-state question is not “is the professional guilty?” but “what reasoned human procedural decision is required now, on what verified record?”
