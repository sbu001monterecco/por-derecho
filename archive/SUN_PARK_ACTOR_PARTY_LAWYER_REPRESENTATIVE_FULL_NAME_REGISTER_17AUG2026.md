## F. Public naming rules

1. First relevant reference: full verified name.
2. Acronym/initials only after full-name introduction.
3. Preserve accents and compound surnames where the primary source supports them.
4. If two sources differ, record the exact source variants and do not silently choose one.
5. `Named in Project conflict screen` ≠ `proved wrongdoer`.
6. `Lawyer/representative of X` ≠ `co-author of X’s alleged conduct` without separate proof.
7. `Witness` ≠ `adverse counsel`.
8. `Same firm` ≠ `same knowledge` without evidence of document access/communication.
9. Protected private witnesses remain role-described rather than publicly named.
