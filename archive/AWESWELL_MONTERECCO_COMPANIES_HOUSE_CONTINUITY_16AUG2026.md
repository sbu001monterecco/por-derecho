## 2. Legal effect

**Classification: VERIFIED FACT — PUBLIC OFFICIAL SOURCE.**

Under section 81 of the Companies Act 2006, a registered change of name takes effect from the date stated in the certificate. It does not affect the company's rights or obligations and does not render legal proceedings defective. Proceedings may continue under the company's new name.

The 2014 event was therefore:

- a change of corporate name;
- not a transfer of LPB shares from Monterecco to Aweswell;
- not a novation;
- not the introduction of a new investor;
- not the incorporation of a successor holding company; and
- not an interruption of the UK company's legal identity or standing.

Documents dated before 3 June 2014 may properly identify the shareholder or contracting party as Monterecco Sun Park Limited. Documents dated after the registered change should ordinarily identify the same company as Aweswell Limited, and may use wording such as:

> Aweswell Limited (company no. 07716847), formerly Monterecco Sun Park Limited.

For Spanish evidential use:

> Aweswell Limited, sociedad británica nº 07716847, anteriormente denominada Monterecco Sun Park Limited.

## 3. Relationship to the LPB acquisition chronology

The existing Spanish and Israeli evidence records the relevant company under its then-current name, Monterecco Sun Park Limited, when it acquired the LPB shares from Multimatrix Limited in December 2011 and was publicised as LPB's sole shareholder in Spain in January/February 2012.

The later appearance of the name Aweswell Limited does not evidence a second acquisition or a transfer between two UK companies. It is the later registered name of the same shareholder.

The remaining source-completion questions concern:

- later group funding and recovery expenditure; and

They do **not** concern a supposed corporate succession from Monterecco to Aweswell.

## 4. Commercial reason for the name “Aweswell”

**Classification: UNRESOLVED QUESTION / EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE.**

Companies House records the legal mechanism and dates but does not state why the members selected the name Aweswell Limited.

The former name was expressly project-specific: “Monterecco Sun Park Limited”. The later use of Aweswell as a wider holding, financing and recovery identity is consistent with a move to a broader, less asset-specific corporate name. That is a reasonable commercial interpretation, but it is not an official Companies House fact.

Do not publish a definitive motive unless supported by a contemporaneous source such as:

- the 2-June-2014 special resolution or accompanying explanatory papers;
- board minutes;
- shareholder correspondence;
- instructions to the company-formation agent or accountant;
- branding or group-restructuring documents; or
- correspondence notifying Spanish advisers, tax authorities, banks or registries of the reason for the change.

## 5. Publication rule

Use the following public formulation:

> Monterecco Sun Park Limited was renamed Aweswell Limited in June 2014. Companies House confirms that both names refer to the same UK company, number 07716847. The change did not transfer the LPB shareholding or create a successor company; it changed the registered name of the existing shareholder.

Do not use:

- “Aweswell acquired Monterecco”;
- “Monterecco transferred LPB to Aweswell”;
- “Aweswell succeeded Monterecco”; or
- “the Aweswell–Monterecco corporate chain”

unless the phrase is expressly referring to documents or economic arrangements other than the legal identity of company no. 07716847.
