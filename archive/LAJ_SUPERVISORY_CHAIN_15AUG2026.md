## Coordination significance

The three submissions were deliberately framed as one institutional chain, not as three independent attempts to obtain parallel findings. Each later registration identifies the earlier recipient(s). The third filing expressly states that the three recipients now know of the same corpus and can coordinate competence, custody, preservation and response without fragmentation or a control gap.

Public-safe proposition:

> Three relevant LAJ supervisory/coordination levels were formally placed on notice on 15 August 2026 through separate, cross-referenced REGAGE registrations using the same evidential corpus.

Do not convert that proposition into:

- “three investigations are open”;
- “the Ministry/TSJ has accepted the allegations”;
- “the LAJ acted unlawfully”;
- “the judicial-office acts caused every downstream event”.

Those matters remain subject to evidence and institutional determination.
