# Homepage mission-critical control — 4 September 2026

**Control ID:** `PD-HOME-MISSION-CRITICAL-20260904-01`

## Controlling objective

The homepage is the command surface of the public record. It must let a first-time reader understand the asset, the legal perimeters, the three core accountability routes, the shared chronology, institutional handling, evidence discipline, recovery path and future/public-interest layers without deleting the detailed record below.

## Non-removal rule

This release is additive. It does not delete, hide or replace any existing homepage section. Existing critical sections remain part of the page and retain their substantive content and anchors.

## Visual hierarchy

The homepage enhancement adds:

1. a stronger mission-critical treatment of the existing hero without replacing its headline, lead, photograph or existing calls to action;
2. a sticky reader rail for the most important entry points;
3. a case map immediately after the hero;
4. three separate procedural lanes for DP 1901/2026, DP 1956/2026 and Control 24;
5. a six-stage factual timeline shared by those lanes;
6. an action-to-action network linking the core routes to the Administrator, Judge, Control 22, separation/fees, Fiscalía, the public-authority reconstruction, the full-digitisation control and updates;
7. visible evidential/procedural boundary language.

## Canonical reader model

`ACTOR → CAPACITY → EVENT → SOURCE → DECISION/OMISSION → ASSET/RIGHT → CONSEQUENCE → CONTRARY / ALTERNATIVE → OPEN PROOF`

## Firewalls

- interlinked does not mean merged;
- shared evidence does not mean shared liability;
- filing does not mean admission;
- later title, operation, financing, investment, subsidy or registration does not validate predecessor authority;
- the homepage is a navigation/presentation surface, not evidence by repetition.
