## 2. Material findings

### BO-001 — acquisition from Multimatrix

**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT — PRIMARY DOCUMENT.

The Spanish deed titled *Escritura de publicidad del cambio de socio único* is dated **3 January 2012**, protocol 18. It records that **Monterecco Sun Park Limited** had acquired the whole of LPB's share capital from **Multimatrix Limited** under a Tel Aviv notarial deed dated **1 December 2011**, duly apostilled.

The same instrument records Gil Marer's appointment as incoming sole director pursuant to a sole-shareholder decision dated **2 January 2012**.

The deed was presented to the Lanzarote Mercantile Registry on **11 January 2012**. The official BORME entry identifies Monterecco Sun Park Limited as LPB's sole shareholder and gives the registry date as **14 February 2012**.

**Correction:** do not describe 3 January as the BORME or registration date. Distinguish:

1. purchase deed — 1 December 2011;
2. management decision — 2 January 2012;
3. Spanish publicity deed — 3 January 2012;
4. registry presentation — 11 January 2012;
5. registry/BORME data date — 14 February 2012.

**Open:** the complete Aweswell–Monterecco economic, financing and control chain, consideration, valuation, capitalisation and handover file.

### BO-002 — February 2012 viability and refinancing plan

**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT — PRIMARY DOCUMENT.

A plan dated **28 February 2012** sought a five-year principal grace period, debt reduction and an operational restructure. It describes three commercial lanes:

1. **Summers Villages** — earlier customers, residents and family/children segments;
2. **Sun Park Living** — medium and longer stays;
3. a proposed tour-operator platform.

The plan records current principal of approximately **€8.632 million**, proposes a lower refinanced total and identifies asset sales, pending operations and payment-on-account components.

**Inference:** before the concurso, LPB and the incoming investor/management perimeter were attempting both financial restructuring and a less conventional distribution model. This materially strengthens the existence and date of the direct-market platform.

**Limit:** the plan does not prove the lender was obliged to accept the proposal, that every projection was achievable or that every later loss resulted from minority interference.

### BO-003 — 5 March 2012 bank figures

**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT — PRIMARY DOCUMENT.

The lender's correspondence of **5 March 2012** used different measures:

- **€940,737.53** — global outstanding position across the two facilities plus estimated costs;
- **€54,653.80** — amount separately said to be required to bring the second facility up to date.

The same thread contained cure figures for the first facility and stated that the amounts were calculated through 2 March and varied daily.

**Correction:** the €940,737.53 and €54,653.80 figures are not competing statements of the same quantity.

**Open:** whether the account treatment, grace period, pledged funds, cure rights, acceleration, costs and amount certified for execution were contractually and legally correct.

### BO-004 — primary auction schedule

**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT — PRIMARY DOCUMENT.

The **15 May 2012** diligence from **Juzgado de Primera Instancia nº 1 de Arrecife**, Mortgage Execution **90/2012**, identifies Bankia, S.A.U. as claimant and Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L. as defendant. It refers to the execution order of **1 February 2012** and fixes the auction for **22 June 2012 at 10:00**.

Its numbered schedule runs from **1 to 158**. Each listed property carries an auction type of **€94,900.67**, producing an aggregate scheduled type value of **€14,994,305.86**.

**Correction:** the primary 15-May auction count is **158**, not 166.

**Reconciliation:** a later insolvency-administrator account refers to **159 apartments after prior sales**; a contemporaneous advice/summary layer referred to **166**. These are different documentary measurements. The 158-property court schedule controls the public description of the auction unless a property-by-property reconciliation proves otherwise.

### BO-005 — defensive filing sequence and insolvency test

**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT — PRIMARY DOCUMENT / VERIFIED LEGAL FRAME.

The controlled filing sequence is:

- filing cover — 1 June 2012;
- court entry — 4 June 2012;
- declaration — 6 June 2012;
- scheduled auction — 22 June 2012.

The declaration recorded assets of **€21,603,513.82** and liabilities of **€10,186,394.72**, an arithmetical difference of approximately **€11.42 million**.

The historical statutory insolvency test concerned whether the debtor could regularly meet due obligations. A stated asset surplus therefore does not by itself disprove actual or imminent insolvency; equally, a liquidity crisis does not validate every lender calculation or make a concurso the only reasonable protective route.

### BO-006 — advice and decision boundary

**Classification:** PRIVILEGED SOURCE — PUBLIC-SAFE CONCLUSION ONLY.

Contemporaneous advice identified more than one route: refinancing, voluntary suspension by the lender and a timely insolvency declaration before the auction. The record supports treating the filing as a **time-critical defensive measure**.

**Correction:** the available evidence does not establish that advisers simply or recklessly directed LPB into an obviously unnecessary concurso. Any negligence or causation proposition requires the complete privilege-reviewed instructions, information set, alternatives, decision records and counterfactual analysis.

### BO-007 — LPB-only perimeter

**Classification:** VERIFIED LEGAL-PERIMETER FACT.

LPB alone was the debtor in Concurso 36/2012. Matkator was not. The Comunidad de Explotación del Complejo Sun Park was a separate operating structure. The community of owners, individual proprietors, customer records, bookings, distribution systems, goodwill and other third-party rights were not automatically absorbed into LPB's estate.

Any responsibility of an insolvency administrator, judge, LAJ or other actor for alleged extraconcursal harm must be established through a specific duty, notice, legal capacity, act or omission, causal consequence and available corrective measure.

### BO-008 — direct-market platform

**Classification:** VERIFIED EXISTENCE / CAUSATION PARTLY OPEN.

The February 2012 viability plan and later contemporaneous business records support:

- direct reservations;
- resident and repeat-customer segments;
- medium/long-stay sales;
- call handling and conversion processes;
- booking and payment records;
- sales reporting and reactivation campaigns;
- negotiations with external operators.

The record supports the existence and economic substance of a direct-market platform. It does not yet prove that every tour operator declined solely because of the Monte Lanza/Molina dispute or quantify actor-specific loss.

### BO-009 — Valencia and concursal accounting remain separate

**Classification:** VERIFIED PROCEDURAL STATUS / RECONCILIATION OPEN.

Ordinary Proceeding **1859/2023-9**, Court of First Instance 27 of Valencia, remains pending, with trial presently recorded for January 2027. It concerns the banking and financial-product lane.

A controlled expert/administrator comparison identifies a **€517,750.04** difference between one expert principal figure and the amount recognised by the insolvency administrator. This may matter to Concurso 36/2012 but does not automatically determine the separate Valencia claim.
