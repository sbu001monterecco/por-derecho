## Mandatory formulation

Use this distinction:

> Intervención General states that the RIC suitability declaration is not itself an autonomous-community subsidy and falls outside its financial-control role; it **does not** certify that the Sun Park/MYND Yaiza asset/project received no other public aid, regional incentives, EU/FEDER financing, payments, certifications or administrative treatment. Those are separate documentary questions for the competent bodies.

## Why this matters

This correction is both favourable and limiting:

- **Favourable:** the June competence response can no longer safely be used as a blanket “no public money / no other file” answer.
- **Limiting:** the August letter is not affirmative proof that a particular additional payment or irregularity existed. It keeps those questions open and routes them to the competent authority.
- **Procedural:** Intervención also asserts restrictive third-party access rules for financial-control files. Treat that as the institution’s access position, not as a universal adjudication of all transparency, whistleblower, criminal-procedure or other lawful access routes.
