## 1. Purpose

This control consolidates the Pink Canary Services / Sun Park reconstruction into one non-fragmented case model. It is designed for repeated machine and human review, including future GitLab restoration, without losing the distinction between:

1. documented fact;
2. attributed allegation or assertion;
3. reasonable inference;

6. offence-specific element still requiring proof.

The criminal/prosecutorial-first lens is a routing discipline. It does **not** convert a civil, mercantile, insolvency, tax, Community or professional dispute into a crime by label. Every possible criminal characterisation must still establish conduct, unlawfulness/falsity, knowledge, intent where required, reliance, causation, benefit/harm, the offence-specific element and the strongest lawful alternative explanation.
