# elEconomista / Javier Romera — Evidential Boundaries and Open Investigation Task

**Date fixed:** 21 August 2026  
**Status:** Canonical control note / open investigation  
**Scope:** January 2025 elEconomista / Javier Romera incident; Meeting Point commercialisation contradiction; adverse LPB insolvency material; publication stoppage.

## Controlling evidential boundaries

These boundaries must be preserved in all future repository work, website drafting, institutional communications, media outreach, legal analysis and evidential summaries unless stronger primary evidence justifies a documented status change.

1. **Established — Romera editorial sequence and 20 January non-publication message.**  
   The contemporaneous record establishes that Javier Romera / elEconomista was actively verifying the proposed story and that, on 20 January 2025, after receipt of adverse judicial material, Romera communicated that the story could not be published on that basis.

2. **Established — adverse insolvency proceeding/document concerns LPB / Concurso 36/2012.**  
   The relevant insolvency perimeter is Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L. and Concurso 36/2012. References describing it loosely as the insolvency of “Sun Park” must not be treated as a legally precise identification of the debtor or proceeding.

3. **Strong contemporaneous attribution, but authentication still desirable — contradictory oral positions attributed to Meeting Point and Laura/CAM concerning Meeting Point commercialisation.**  
   Contemporaneous written reconstructions preserve materially contradictory positions attributed to Meeting Point and to Laura/CAM concerning whether Meeting Point had commercialised the hotel / Sun Park-related hotel product. These attributions are evidentially significant but must not be silently upgraded into authenticated admissions without recovery and verification of the native audio, message, call note or direct source record.

4. **OPEN — identify exactly who sourced, sent and framed the adverse judgment/document for elEconomista.**  
   The current preserved evidence establishes that Romera received adverse judicial material and relied on it when communicating the non-publication decision. It does **not yet conclusively establish** who located the document, who selected it for transmission, who sent it to Romera, from which account or intermediary, what accompanying explanation was supplied, or who framed it as disposing of the wider Sun Park / Meeting Point story.

5. **Inference / allegation, not adjudicated fact — deliberate use of the adverse material to stop the exposé.**  
   Project Sun Rock’s position is that the Acosta Matos response channel deliberately deployed or procured the adverse material in order to stop the elEconomista exposé. That proposition remains an inference/allegation to be proved actor-by-actor. It is not an adjudicated fact and must not be represented as one.

## Open investigation job

**Who sourced, selected, transmitted and framed the adverse LPB / Concurso 36/2012 judgment or related material that reached Javier Romera on or before 20 January 2025, and what does the native evidence show about the purpose, wording and chain of transmission?**

- Recover all contemporaneous Sixto Abogados communications referring to Romera, elEconomista, Meeting Point, Laura Acosta Matos / Laura-CAM, the adverse judgment, publication timing and the Meeting Point contradiction.

  - the call with Romera;

  - Romera’s verification contact with the Meeting Point representative in Las Palmas;
  - any subsequent inbound contact attributed to Laura Acosta Matos;
  - the contradictory Meeting Point / Laura-CAM statements.

- Determine whether the document was sent directly by Laura Acosta Matos, by another CAM/Acosta Matos representative, by counsel, by an insolvency actor, by Meeting Point, or by another source. Do not infer identity from sequence alone.

- Determine whether any person involved in 357/2024, Meeting Point restructuring, CAM/Acosta Matos, the insolvency administration or related legal representation had reason to know of the press verification exercise or the document transmission.
- Red-team all hypotheses, including innocent explanations: ordinary press verification; unsolicited public-record provision; misunderstanding of procedural effect; editorial risk aversion; independent sourcing of the judgment; or inaccurate recollection of oral statements.
