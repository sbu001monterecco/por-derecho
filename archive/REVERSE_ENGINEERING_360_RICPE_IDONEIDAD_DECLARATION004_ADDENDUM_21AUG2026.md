## 2. New evidence-status rule

This rule applies equally to evidence favourable and adverse to Por Derecho.

## 7. Strongest lawful reconciliation scenarios

The 360° reconstruction must preserve at least these scenarios until the primary file resolves them:

1. **Single-file scenario:** one competent file/final act lawfully covered both phases or instruments.
2. **Separate-authorisation scenario:** Series F and Series G were covered by separate applications, reports, resolutions or other competent acts.
3. **Amendment/extension scenario:** the original process was later modified, enlarged, corrected or supplemented.
4. **Prior-cost/reimbursement scenario:** Series F lawfully reimbursed eligible expenditure already incurred or discharged supplier/bridge obligations.
5. **Alternative-documentary-basis scenario:** another lawful documentary basis explains the sequence.

The fact that a scenario is possible does not prove it. The fact that the public file is incomplete does not disprove it.

## 8. Criminal-forensic threshold

The 360° record may investigate whether later formalisation or financing relied on incomplete or inaccurate premises. It may not jump from timing tension to criminality.

For any criminal theory, prove separately:

`exact proposition → exact actor → knowledge/intent → act/use → recipient → dependency/error → benefit → prejudice → causation → contrary evidence`.

A witness's concern, recollection or question is not a substitute for any of those elements.
