## Lock meanings

### `LOCKED_CANONICAL`
Identity, relationship or role proposition supported strongly enough to control future copy.

### `LOCKED_MINIMUM_DATE`
The actor is proved involved **by** the stated date. Older evidence may move the minimum earlier. Later summaries may not move it later.

### `PROVISIONAL`
Supported but incomplete; further source work required.

### `OPEN_IDENTITY`
The source identifies a person/entity incompletely or conflictingly. Do not guess.

### `SUPERSEDED`
An older repository/public proposition has been replaced by a stronger source-led proposition. Preserve the old proposition in the supersession log.

## Four-date rule

Every chronology entry must distinguish:

1. **earliest surviving evidence date**;
2. **proved involved by**;
3. **claimed/reported earlier involvement**;
4. **latest surviving evidence date**.

`First document found` must never be rewritten as `entry into the hotel` unless the source expressly says it was the entry.

## Machine rule

No homepage card, visual, dossier or AI-generated chronology may:

- use a start date later than a locked minimum;
- convert a minimum date into a first-entry date;
- expand an alias from memory;
- merge similarly named Molina actors without explicit identity proof;
- change an entity form without a corporate source;
- replace a locked fact without a supersession record.
