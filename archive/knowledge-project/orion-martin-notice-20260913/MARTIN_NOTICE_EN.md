## UK nexus: positive leads and remaining identity proof

The Companies House exact-full-name officer record for Martin / Martin Paul Graham reports British nationality, UK residence declarations and historical FTSE International and EDX London directorships, together with Blue Jack Consultancy and Blue Jack Associates roles spanning December 2024. These are concrete UK professional leads. Exact name and market-sector career are strong identity indicators, but the public sources reviewed do not contain a common personal identifier linking the Spanish BORME person and UK officer record. The cross-register identity bridge therefore remains open. No home address or birth detail is published. A residence declaration does not establish where the messages were sent or Portfolio decisions taken.

This supports targeted production about relevant business locations, delegated responsibilities, communications, decisions and handover during the historic directorship. It does not establish current UK management, UK control of Portfolio, a UK investor base, automatic English jurisdiction or English governing law. The former-director role is established independently; UK-record attribution retains its separate identity qualification.

For England and Wales, proper service, an arguable actor-specific cause of action and the appropriate forum require separate analysis under CPR Part 6 and PD6B. Rome II Article 4 ordinarily concerns direct damage within its scope; the company-law exclusions in Article 1(2)(d) prevent treating it as a universal rule for directors' corporate duties. Spanish corporate-governance duties remain separately analysed. FSMA section 21 requires an actual investment invitation or inducement, communicator, business context, UK effect and authorisation/exemption analysis. A private compliance acknowledgement is not itself an investment promotion.
