# CARLOS SAAVEDRA — CALIFICACIÓN MISSING-WITNESS CONTROL

**Date:** 19 August 2026  
**Status:** MANDATORY INTERNAL EVIDENCE / PROCEDURAL RECONSTRUCTION CONTROL

## Controlling proposition

Carlos Saavedra/PwC is a documented contemporaneous knowledge-holder concerning the 2016 Sun Park ownership, Community, alleged-debt, voting, insolvency, access, governance and legal-response architecture. The surviving record also documents direct client notice of allegations regarded as potentially criminal, an express instruction to pursue a penal route, and PwC contact with the Administrador Concursal.

If Carlos was proposed, admitted, summoned, expected or otherwise positioned to testify at the vista de calificación but his evidence was ultimately not heard, the repository must reconstruct **who caused or requested that non-practice, on what procedural basis, what reasons were recorded, and what evidential consequences followed**.

This control does **not** state that the judge, Fiscalía, Administración Concursal or any party deliberately suppressed evidence. Motive must not be inferred without proof.

## Mandatory institutional questions

1. **Administración Concursal:** did the AC know, when preparing or maintaining the calificación case, that Carlos/PwC held this contemporaneous body of evidence? What assessment was made of its relevance to causation, alternative responsibility and third-party conduct?
2. **Fiscalía:** was Fiscalía informed of the June–September 2016 PwC chain before Carlos's testimony was not heard? If not, why not?
3. **Juzgado:** what judicial or procedural decision resulted in Carlos not testifying, who requested or supported that outcome, and what reasons were recorded?
4. **All three:** where is the documentary record identifying who requested, agreed to, acquiesced in or otherwise caused the non-practice?
5. **Calificación completeness:** what consideration was given to whether Carlos could provide material evidence concerning conduct by actors other than the debtor's directors, the AC's own knowledge, disputed Community debt, governance, access/control and alternative causation of loss?
6. **Later convergence:** did the court, AC or Fiscalía know of or consider later PwC/Carlos professional convergence with the RICPE / Acosta Matos perimeter, and if so what relevance was attributed to it?
