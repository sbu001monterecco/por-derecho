## 4. Evolution of the theory

### 2023 frame

The AN querella presented a broad, integrated theory: dissident-owner conduct + Community governance/debt + PH122/CAM credit acquisition + AC conduct + material takeover + liquidation/adjudication + RIC/investor project were framed as parts of a single coordinated scheme. The pleading invoked many criminal offences and an aggregate damages claim approaching EUR 90m.

**Classification:** HISTORICAL PARTY THEORY.

### 2026 evidence method and current attributed frame

The 2026 work deliberately decomposes the evidence into actor-, duty- and document-specific questions:

- **DP1901:** private actors, exact documents, title/control, Community authority/debt/voting, credit chain, valuation and project representations;
- **DP1956:** AC entrusted powers, actual knowledge, concrete acts/omissions, patrimonial harm and third-party advantage, with Article 252 CP as the principal hypothesis;
- **Control 24:** exact judicial acts, record knowledge, supervision and traceability; no inference of prevaricación merely from adverse or difficult procedural outcomes.

That decomposition is an evidential method; it is not a fragmentation or retirement of Gil Marer and Aweswell's current case theory. As of 26 August 2026, their controlling attributed position is `ALG-ENT-018`: **one continuing economic-criminal enterprise, advanced through successive adoption and divided functions**. In that factual/prosecutorial theory, different actors may be alleged to have entered at different times, performed different functions, adopted earlier results or preserved later benefits. It does not allege that every actor made the original plan, performed every act or remained involved for the whole period.

**Current instruction:** test the whole alleged enterprise forwards and backwards while proving each actor, episode and proposed offence separately. Evidence acquisition and reconciliation remain mandatory. Do not convert the unitary allegation into a conviction, import one offence's elements into another, infer an original pact, or infer knowledge, intention or participation from relationship, office, chronology or later benefit alone.

## 5. Thematic crosswalk

### 5.1 Title, exploitation and ownership

**2023 allegation:** CAM allegedly represented itself as sole owner and commercially/project-wise behaved as owner before consolidated title, while LPB remained majority owner and the concurso continued.

**2026 treatment:** separate:
1. registered/legal title;
2. legal possession;
3. de facto/material access and control;
4. operational/commercial control;
5. later corporate transfer/continuity.

**Current status:** MIXED. Some title dates and corporate changes are verifiable; broad claims of unlawful ownership appropriation remain PARTY ALLEGATION unless tied to a specific act and source.

**Evidence gap:** finca-by-finca registry chain, precise 2017-2022 title dates, judicial authorisations and actor capacity at each event.

### 5.2 Community governance, voting and debt

**2023 allegation:** the dissident perimeter took control of Community governance, prevented LPB majority control/voting and fabricated/escalated debts used against LPB and in the concurso.

**DP1901 treatment:** investigate the exact legal authority of Community office-holders, debt certificates, accounts, minutes, voting rights, links to CAM and whether Community machinery was knowingly used or adopted by later actors.

**Current status:** PARTY ALLEGATION + DOCUMENTARY INVESTIGATION. A predecessor Community/disident apparatus is **not automatically CAM's agent**.

**Evidence gap:** complete minutes, attendance/proxies, ownership bases for votes, accounts, debt resolutions, judicial treatment, CAM knowledge/adoption/use/benefit.

### 5.3 Credit assignment and privileged-credit quantum

**2023 allegation:** PH122/CAM/AC allegedly manipulated or inflated the mortgage credit and deprived LPB of a retracto route.

**2026 treatment:** establish exact creditor legal person, economic purchaser, assignment deed, price, registered holder, recognised concurso amount, enforcement amount, security cap, substitutions and any actual litigiosity at assignment date.

**Current status:** MIXED. The existence of distinct figures is documentary; fraudulent inflation or unlawful suppression of retracto remains an allegation/legal hypothesis.

**Do not flatten:** EUR 9.052m recognised credit ≠ asserted EUR 12.251m debt certificate ≠ offer value ≠ adjudication value ≠ cash paid.

### 5.4 Valuation

**2023 allegation:** ACTUA valuation around EUR 9.776m was a deliberately false/custom valuation to facilitate acquisition below value; Gesvalt ECO was around EUR 25.647m.

**2026 treatment:** valuation divergence is a testing signal only.

**Current status:** VERIFIED that different valuations/methods/figures existed once primary reports are anchored; intent/fraud is PARTY ALLEGATION.

**Evidence gap:** scope, date, purpose, ECO applicability, inspected units, condition assumptions, DCF inputs, title/occupation assumptions, flight/development rights.

### 5.5 2017-2018 inspection, access and material control

**2023 allegation:** CAM/JDAM/LPAM/Community/FMMM used security/locks and physical entry to take the hotel before title, exclude the existing operation and begin destructive works.

**DP1901 treatment:** establish the actor-by-actor transition from inspection/planning to material control, without calling it whole-hotel legal possession.

**Current status:** EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE that **7 June 2018 is a material/de facto access-control transition** subject to exact perimeter. Repository correction CR-017 also identifies an earlier specifically dated CAM-linked physical incursion on 2 October 2017.

**Evidence gap:** exact fincas/areas, authority relied upon, security instructions, witnesses, police/court evidence, photographs, works sequence and actual exclusion of each owner/operator.

### 5.6 Financed exit from concurso

**2023 allegation:** AC/CAM blocked financing and alternatives that could have paid creditors and allowed LPB to leave concurso.

**DP1956 treatment:** test actual proposals and AC responses rather than assume a viable exit: 6 Jun 2018 operator contract, 13 Jun communication, 10 Sep 2018 request to quantify all recognised debt, later financing offers and whether an operable global figure was produced.

**Current status:** INVESTIGATIVE HYPOTHESIS. Financing communications may be VERIFIED; ability to complete exit, conditions, funds and causation require proof.

**Evidence gap:** committed funds, conditions precedent, full liability schedule, creditor responses, AC numerical reconciliation and reasoned decisions.

### 5.7 OB REM / EUR 400,000

**2023 allegation:** the 28 Nov 2018 sale/payment was carried out despite suspension, later not convalidated, but title/funds were not fully unwound and the AC used money for fees/gastos.

**DP1956 treatment:** make this a forensic reconciliation track, not an automatic criminal conclusion.

**Current status:** CORE VERIFIED/UNRESOLVED SEQUENCE: deed/payment and later non-convalidation can be established from primary records; the fate/accounting/legal effect must be reconciled.

**Evidence gap:** deed, estate bank entry, fee payments, registry entries/cancellations, 2019 no-convalidation order, subsequent transaction treatment, final accounts.

### ALG-ENT-018
**Proposition:** Gil Marer and Aweswell allege **one continuing economic-criminal enterprise, advanced through successive adoption and divided functions**, connecting the Community/debt/voting machinery, creditor and control transitions, the LPB insolvency perimeter, the 2017–2019 access/realisation sequence, the later adjudication/operation/value chain, and the alleged institutional preservation of the resulting position.
**Class:** ACTIVE CURRENT PARTY ALLEGATION / FACTUAL AND PROSECUTORIAL CASE THEORY. It is not a judicial finding.
**Source and independence rule:** the allegation is Gil Marer and Aweswell's expressly adopted position. It is not inferred from, and receives no evidential support merely from, the reserved declarant mailbox/Drive manifest. That manifest changes discovery priority only until native originals are lawfully acquired, authenticated and tested against contrary material.
**Participation rule:** successive adoption and divided functions do not require an allegation that every actor joined an original pact or performed every step. They also do not retroactively create authorship, participation or complicity under Articles 28–29 CP. For each person and period establish the relevant capacity, actual knowledge, legally relevant intent, concrete contribution or omission, duty where omission is alleged, causal role, benefit/effect, withdrawal or termination, and the strongest innocent explanation. Treat distinct post-offence conduct under its own possible legal route and elements.
**Offence rule:** the factual expression “continuing enterprise” does not classify any conduct as a `delito continuado`, permanent offence, criminal organisation or criminal group, and does not change consummation, the participation window or prescription for any offence. Apply the offence text in force at the material time, lex mitior where applicable, and offence-specific lead, charging, trial and conviction thresholds.
**Statutory-structure boundary:** `ALG-ENT-018` is analytically separate from Articles 570 bis and 570 ter CP. A statutory organisation requires its own structural and temporal elements; a criminal group is a residual alternative with its own elements. Do not plead both classifications for the same grouping and period except expressly in the alternative, and do not revive `ALG-ORG-011` merely by using the factual enterprise formulation.
**Public use:** YES, but only with direct Gil Marer/Aweswell attribution, `allegation ≠ finding`, individual/offence-specific proof, material contrary record, presumption of innocence and the temporal disclaimer above in the same containing block.
