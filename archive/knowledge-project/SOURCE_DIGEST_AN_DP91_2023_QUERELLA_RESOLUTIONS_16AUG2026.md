## 4. 2023 narrative architecture

### 4.1 2008 purchase, exploitation structure and minority-owner conflict

The pleading says LPB acquired the majority of Sun Park after a contemplated whole-complex transaction was not completed by a group of owners, and that the hotel was subject to a unitary-tourist-exploitation architecture. It alleges that the non-selling/non-adhering owners interfered with the unitary exploitation, resulting in reduced effective inventory and burdens falling disproportionately on the operating structure.

**Classification:** MIXED. Corporate/title documents, statutes, contracts and historical proceedings may verify individual elements; the asserted causation and illegality remain PARTY ALLEGATION unless separately established.

### 4.2 Community governance and alleged debt manufacture

The pleading alleges that from 2011 a dissident-owner perimeter took control of the Owners' Community, excluded or constrained LPB's voting/control, terminated or interfered with the exploitation arrangements, and generated changing Community debt figures that were later used against LPB and inside the concurso.

Figures described in the pleading change over time, including approximately EUR 605k, EUR 718,663.24, EUR 809,902.19, EUR 1.145m, EUR 3.8m and approximately EUR 4.5m depending on period and asserted basis.

**Classification:** PARTY ALLEGATION as to fabrication/manipulation/intent. The existence of particular minutes, certificates, claims and court treatment can be separately VERIFIED when anchored to originals.

### 4.3 Insolvency causation and failed convenio

The pleading argues that the breakdown of the hotel's unitary exploitation and related conflicts were a substantial cause of LPB's financial distress and voluntary concurso. It further alleges that the proposed 2017 convenio, said to provide full creditor payment, was frustrated through the conduct/position of Promontoria Holding 122 B.V. (`PH122`) and the AC while CAM was negotiating to acquire the privileged mortgage credit.

**Classification:** MIXED. Concurso dates, convenio documents and credit-transfer events are capable of primary verification. The alleged coordination, motive and causal responsibility are PARTY ALLEGATION.

### 4.4 Mortgage-credit assignment and credit quantum

The pleading describes the chain Caja/Bankia -> SAREB -> PH122/Cerberus -> CAM and contrasts a privileged credit recognised in the concurso at approximately EUR 9,052,251.69 with later figures attributed to CAM/PH122 of approximately EUR 12,251,446.21 plus interest.

It alleges that the increase was improper and that the assignment should have triggered or preserved a retracto/litigious-credit route.

**Classification:**
- assignment chain / recognised-credit figures: VERIFY FROM PRIMARY CONCURSO, NOTARIAL AND ASSIGNMENT RECORDS;
- claim that the difference was unlawful or fraudulent: PARTY ALLEGATION;
- current retracto analysis must use the dedicated 2026 credit-ownership/retracto work, not this 2023 formulation alone.

### 4.5 Alleged EUR 350,000 payment to the AC

At p.25 the pleading alleges that CAM had been negotiating with Cerberus for around a year with the AC's assistance and that a payment of EUR 350,000 was made for that assistance/intermediation. The pleading says an email reflected the amount and also referred to a recording that was not then in the querellant's possession.

**Classification:** HIGH-RISK PARTY ALLEGATION — NOT PUBLICATION-READY. Do not describe this as a bribe, commission paid to the AC, cohecho or proven payment without authenticated source material proving payer, payee, legal basis, date, account, purpose and receipt. This theory is not carried forward as an autonomous 2026 hypothesis absent new evidence.

### 4.6 Asset valuation and liquidation positioning

The pleading contrasts an ACTUA valuation around EUR 9.776m with a Gesvalt ECO valuation around EUR 25.647m and alleges that the lower valuation was engineered to benefit CAM in liquidation. It also discusses ACTUA's own income/DCF/land figures and the offer architecture for apartments versus locals/pools.

**2026 correction:** the valuation spread is a **testing signal**, not self-proving fraud. Any public or legal use requires method-by-method comparison, effective dates, asset perimeter, assumptions, title/occupation condition and valuation purpose.

### 4.7 2018 material access/control and works

The pleading alleges that CAM and people aligned with the Community used locks/security/access measures to take de facto control, exclude the existing hotel operation and begin demolition/reform before CAM acquired consolidated legal title. It attributes statements to CAM/JDAM about entry, ownership and future refurbishment.

**Controlling repository correction:** 7 June 2018 may support a transition in **de facto/material/access/operational control**. It does **not** by itself establish legal possession of every finca, whole-hotel ownership, or 2022 title retroactively authorising earlier acts. Exact finca/actor/perimeter proof remains necessary.

### 4.8 28 November 2018 local/pool transaction and later no-convalidation

The pleading describes a EUR 400,000 transaction concerning locals/pool-solarium assets while the integrated liquidation was disputed/suspended, followed by later judicial non-convalidation. It alleges that the registration/funds/accounting were not properly unwound and that AC fees/gastos were paid from those funds.

**Classification:** CORE DOCUMENTARY TRACK. The transaction, payment, registration, later no-convalidation and accounting treatment must be reconciled from deed, bank/estate account, registry, court orders and AC accounts. Do not infer disappearance, double use or misappropriation merely from an unreconciled sequence.

### 4.9 Adjudication, mortgage satisfaction and accounting bridge

The pleading alleges inconsistencies between:

- recognised privileged credit around EUR 9.052m;
- CAM's offer/credit figures;
- the AP Las Palmas treatment of offer value;
- a later 2022 adjudication deed around EUR 13.168m; and
- whether mortgage obligations, condonations, assumed charges, cash consideration and the EUR 400k were correctly reflected in the estate.

**Classification:** UNRESOLVED ACCOUNTING/LEGAL BRIDGE. Do not collapse recognised debt, offer value, adjudication value, assumed liabilities, condonation and cash movement into one number.

### 4.10 RIC / RPE / Sun Park / Lava Verde / MYND Yaiza investor narrative

The pleading alleges that CAM/RIC Private Equity promoted the Sun Park project as if CAM owned the relevant hotel asset, free of charges, with refurbishment still to be financed, while CAM lacked consolidated title and works had already begun. It describes a project amount changing from about EUR 12m to EUR 16m and later naming/vehicle changes including Lava Verde and MYND Yaiza / Hotel New Trend.

**Classification:** PARTY ALLEGATION pending exact source-by-source authentication. The 2026 work should compare each dated prospectus/webinar/certificate against the title, concurso, works and corporate records existing on that same date. Do not paraphrase promotional statements more strongly than the original source.

### 4.11 EU/public-funds element

The 2023 pleading speculates that European funds may have been used or implicated through related energy/investment structures.

**Classification:** UNRESOLVED / SPECULATIVE IN 2023 SOURCE. Do not state that EU funds financed Sun Park/MYND unless the exact programme, beneficiary, application, declaration, eligibility basis, grant/loan decision, drawdown and expenditure trail are proved.

### 4.12 Claimed loss

The querella states an approximate aggregate loss of **EUR 89,646,211.25**, composed of several categories including asset damage, flight/development rights, lost hotel profits, rent/maintenance claims, tourism-business loss, alleged AC responsibility, adjudication/retracto and property-administration items.

A later appellate filing in the same bundle refers to approximately **EUR 87,691,648.81**.

**Classification:** PARTY CLAIM / UNRESOLVED CALCULATION. Do not silently harmonise the two totals. The 2026 Control 21/22 methodology expressly avoids a global damages number until an independent forensic schedule removes overlap, tests causation and distinguishes LPB, Matkator, Aweswell, operators and other owners.

## 6. Procedural disposition of the Audiencia Nacional route

### AN-DP91-2023-01 — initiation

**VERIFIED PROCEDURAL FACT:** Juzgado Central de Instrucción nº 3 opened Diligencias Previas 91/2023 by Auto of 2 November 2023 and sent the matter to the Ministerio Fiscal to report on admission, competence, possible diligences and procedure.

### AN-DP91-2023-02 — inadmission/archive

**VERIFIED PROCEDURAL FACT:** Auto of 25 January 2024 declined admission of the querella, referring to lack of accredited criminal relevance and lack of competence of JCI nº 3, and archived the matter.

The appellant also complained that the Ministerio Fiscal report referenced by the court had not been provided to the party.

### AN-AP120-2024 — appeal

**VERIFIED PROCEDURAL FACT:** Audiencia Nacional, Sala de lo Penal, Sección 3ª, Rollo de Apelación 120/2024, Auto 119/2024 of 8 March 2024 dismissed the appeal.

The later cassation filing records the AN's reasoning as recognising that the narrative could present some appearance of criminality but considering that the pleading did not precisely establish the grounds for Audiencia Nacional competence, including because parts were expressed as hypothetical/future/suspicion-based and the affected investor population/loss was not concretely identified.

**Interpretation limit:** this is an admissibility/competence outcome, not a trial finding resolving the factual truth of each pleaded allegation.

### TS-CAS1867-2024 — attempted cassation

**VERIFIED PROCEDURAL FACT:** Tribunal Supremo, Sala Segunda, Recurso de Casación 1867/2024, declined admission of the cassation challenge against the 8 March 2024 AN order. The Supreme Court reasoned that the statutory cassation route for this type of order required a prior judicial resolution amounting to a founded imputation, which was absent. Costs were imposed and loss of the deposit was ordered if one had been constituted.

**Interpretation limit:** the Supreme Court did not conduct a merits trial of the 2023 allegations.

## 7. What the 2023 route does and does not prove

It proves procedurally that a broad querella was filed, DP 91/2023 was opened for threshold processing, admission was refused, the AN appeal failed and the Supreme Court did not admit cassation.

It does **not** establish:

- that all allegations in the querella were true;
- that all allegations were false;
- that the named persons committed the pleaded offences;
- that the EUR 350k allegation was proved;
- that there was a criminal organisation;
- that EU funds were used in Sun Park;
- that the aggregate damage figure was judicially accepted; or
- that the underlying documentary issues were subjected to a full evidential criminal investigation/trial.

## 8. Relationship to 2026 investigative architecture

The 2023 querella is valuable as a **historical allegation map and source locator**, but it is no longer the controlling analytical frame.

The 2026 approach deliberately decomposes the former global theory into narrower, evidence-led tracks:

- DP1901/2026: private-actor documents, title/control, Community/debt/voting, credit, valuation and project-representation testing;
- DP1956/2026: AC entrusted powers, knowledge, acts/omissions, estate conservation, financed exit, OB REM/EUR400k, credit/adjudication/accounting and possible Article 252 CP analysis;
- Control 24 / TSJ route: exact judicial acts, supervision and traceability, without treating procedural tension as proof of prevaricación.

**Current methodological instruction:** evidence acquisition and reconciliation first; do not expand offence allegations unless new primary evidence establishes the missing elements.
