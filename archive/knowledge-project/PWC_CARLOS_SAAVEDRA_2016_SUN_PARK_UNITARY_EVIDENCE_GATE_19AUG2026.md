## Mandatory analytical discipline

**Do not reverse-engineer guilt. Reverse-engineer the evidential chain required to prove or disprove knowledge, conflict, participation, assistance, benefit, causation and intent.**

Use the project taxonomy throughout:

- **VERIFIED FACT** — supported directly by a primary/contemporaneous source or an exact institutional record.
- **INSTITUTIONAL / PARTY STATEMENT** — what an identified participant, court filing, authority, company or adviser stated; not automatically true on the merits.
- **ALLEGATION** — pleaded or asserted wrongdoing that remains to be proved.
- **INFERENCE** — reasoned proposition supported by facts but not itself directly observed/proved.
- **OPEN QUESTION** — finite question capable of documentary resolution.
- **EVIDENCE GAP** — missing source needed to move an issue to a higher evidential status.

Chronology is not causation. Proximity is not participation. A PwC-network relationship is not automatically knowledge by every PwC legal entity or professional. Conversely, separate PwC legal entities must not be used to erase a proven common professional history where actual people, referrals, records or conflict systems connect the matters.

# II. What the 11 June meeting establishes about PwC / Carlos knowledge

The transcript repeatedly identifies **Carlos Saavedra Rodríguez de Palacio and Miguel Hernández Lorenzo of PwC Tenerife as acting in representation of Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.** This is a direct professional-side designation in the preserved transcript, not an inference based only on billing.

## 1. Documentary/economic reconstruction mandate — VERIFIED

At approximately **00:03:26**, Carlos describes the intended exercise in substance as reviewing the documents to determine **what had been paid, who paid it, and on whose account**, while distancing the exercise from a merely partisan report.

This establishes direct Carlos-level knowledge of a documentary reconciliation exercise extending into historic accounting, payments and allocation of economic responsibility.

## 2. LPB / exploitation vehicle / owners' Community were understood as distinct — VERIFIED

The meeting spends substantial time distinguishing:

- Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.;
- the Comunidad de Explotación / historical exploiting vehicle(s);
- the Comunidad de Propietarios;
- private unit owners;
- hotel/tourist operating costs versus complex/property maintenance costs.

At approximately **00:15:20**, Jonathan Simó expressly says in the historic exploitation-cost analysis that **Luchy was not the entity being treated as the exploiting vehicle**. Carlos then directly questions why owner charges were imposed during a period in which, on the analysis being discussed, those costs did not correspond to owners.

**Control:** this proves the distinctions were being analysed; it does not by itself adjudicate the legal correctness of every contract, invoice, quota or debt.

## 3. Detailed 2008–2015 accounting / maintenance analysis — VERIFIED as material discussed

Jonathan Simó explains an accounting reconstruction based on balances, ledgers, contracts, invoices and supporting material and describes approximately **EUR 1.918 million** of selected maintenance-type expenditure over 2008–2015. He distinguishes hotel-operation personnel/costs from costs said to relate to maintenance of the complex.

This establishes that the PwC-side meeting was not a superficial legal consultation. It addressed how Sun Park functioned economically and who bore costs.

## 4. Community debt / morosidad / voting consequences — VERIFIED as issues understood

The transcript records sustained discussion of:

- whether historic Community liabilities had actually arisen;
- whether expenses had already been met through the exploitation architecture;
- owner quotas;
- alleged debt accumulation;
- the effect of alleged morosidad on voting rights;
- possible reconciliation/offset of expenditure made for Community benefit.

At approximately **00:21:49–00:23:18**, Miguel expressly connects the alleged debt problem to other owners, voting exclusion and the need for the Administrador Concursal to understand the objective data, and says the matter would be discussed with **Paco Matos**.

At approximately **00:23:47**, Juan Carlos Prieto proposes taking owner expenditure to a future Junta and reconciling/condoning debt against qualifying expenditure.

**Control:** words such as “ficticia” spoken in the discussion are evidence of the participant's position/description, not proof that a competent court has found every disputed Community debt fictitious.

## 5. Majority/minority and hotel-operation architecture — VERIFIED as contemporaneous discussion

At approximately **00:17:25**, Juan Carlos Prieto tells the LPB/PwC side in substance that **they had the majority and determined the hotel business**, while discussing how minority/private owners could use or dispose of their units without frustrating the tourist use.

This is a contemporaneous statement by the opposing-side lawyer and is relevant to the economic/governance architecture being negotiated. Exact title percentages remain a registry/document question for each date.

## 6. Individual owners, deeds and access — VERIFIED as issues understood

The meeting discusses private owners unable to access apartments, owners inside/outside the exploitation arrangement, deeds and future treatment of private units. Around **00:18:40**, Miguel asks whether Thompson is the person who signed the deed being discussed.

Read together with the 26 April act, this establishes PwC knowledge reaching below the generic “minority owners exist” level into identifiable private-owner/unit/deed issues.

## 7. Direct Carlos ↔ Francisco Mario Matos Matas interaction — VERIFIED

The 11 June transcript places Carlos and **Francisco Mario Matos Matas (FMMM / Paco Matos)** in direct, substantive discussion of Sun Park.

The source family also records Carlos referring to what had previously been committed/agreed at the earlier Junta (“si recuerdas…” / the work they had committed to at the Junta). The meeting repeatedly discusses what the Administrador Concursal knew or should understand, what information should be shown to him, and how the accounting/Community issues interacted with the concurso.
