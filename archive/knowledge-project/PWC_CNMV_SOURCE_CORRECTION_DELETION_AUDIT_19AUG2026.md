## 2. Mandatory correction

### VERIFIED / INSTITUTIONAL STATEMENT

The 22 January 2026 CNMV response establishes that:

1. the January 2021 communication was transferred internally by the Investor Department;
2. **supervisory actions concerning RICPE were carried out** as a result;
3. the later 20 July 2021 Enrique Guerra certification and the 21 July 2021 CAM judicial filing were **not in the documentation obtained through those supervisory actions**.

### NOT ESTABLISHED BY THIS SOURCE

The CNMV response does **not** say or establish that:

- CNMV contacted PwC;
- CNMV contacted RICPE's auditor;
- CNMV requested information or assurance from PwC;
- PwC responded to CNMV;
- PwC supplied a title, ownership, availability or due-diligence assurance concerning Sun Park to CNMV;
- CNMV relied on PwC for a Sun Park ownership/title conclusion;
- PwC or any auditor "cleared" Sun Park, CAM or the RICPE project for CNMV.

Unless a separate primary CNMV/RICPE/PwC supervisory request, response, production inventory or equivalent institutional record is located, every such proposition remains **OPEN QUESTION / NOT ESTABLISHED**.
