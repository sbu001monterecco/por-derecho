## Molina faction discipline

Use the following structure in all future work:

`person → entity/units → representative → lawyer → meeting/proceeding → date → sourced position`

Never use:

- `the Molina family` as a substitute for named parties;
- `Molina dissidents` without listing who, when and against which resolution/party;
- a family relationship as proof of shared legal strategy;
- one person's representation as proof of authority for another.
