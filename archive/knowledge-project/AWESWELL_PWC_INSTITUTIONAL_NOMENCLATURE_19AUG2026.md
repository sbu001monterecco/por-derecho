# Aweswell Limited — PwC institutional nomenclature control

**Date:** 19 August 2026  
**Status:** canonical wording control for the PwC / Sun Park workstream, public page and Madrid/London escalation.  
**Purpose:** distinguish Aweswell Limited itself from its Spanish subsidiaries, investments and asset perimeter, and prevent loose use of “Aweswell perimeter” from obscuring corporate identity, client status or foreign-investor standing.

## 1. Short institutional description

Use:

> **Aweswell Limited, a UK holding company and foreign investor in Spain, directly and indirectly affected by the Sun Park matter, including through its Spanish subsidiaries and investments.**

## 2. Expanded institutional description

Where greater precision is useful, use:

> **Aweswell Limited is the UK holding company at the head of the relevant investment perimeter. It was directly affected in its own capacity and indirectly through its Spanish subsidiaries, including companies holding and operating interests connected with Sun Park. It should therefore also be understood in this correspondence as a foreign investor in Spain whose Spanish investment perimeter was affected by the matters under examination.**

## 3. Drafting rule

Do not use “Aweswell perimeter” as a substitute for the corporate identity of Aweswell Limited.

Distinguish:

1. **Aweswell Limited** — UK HoldCo; foreign investor in Spain; a distinct legal person and former PwC Legal UK client on the documented April 2016 engagement.
2. **Spanish subsidiary and investment perimeter** — Spanish subsidiaries, investments, assets and operating/property interests connected with Sun Park, including LPB and Matkator where the evidence supports the particular relationship being described.
3. **Sun Rock / Sun Park investment perimeter** — a broader descriptive project/investment expression; not itself a legal entity and not a substitute for identifying the relevant company, owner, client or asset.
4. **Later Acosta Matos / RICPE / HNT / MYND perimeter** — a separate later counterparty/beneficiary/professional-work perimeter whose precise entity and mandate relationships must be proved rather than assumed.

## 4. Former-client formulation

Use in the PwC page and Madrid/London escalation:

> **The surviving records demonstrate that PwC's professional relationship was not confined to an isolated Spanish operating company or to the entity appearing on a particular invoice. Aweswell Limited, the UK HoldCo and foreign investor at the head of the relevant Spanish investment perimeter, was itself a formal PwC Legal UK client, jointly with Sun Rock Limited in the identified April 2016 engagement. In parallel, Landwell/PwC Canarias was advising within the Spanish Sun Park/Luchy/Matkator perimeter. Contemporaneous KYC correspondence further connects those workstreams through corporate-structure information attributed to Carlos Saavedra/PwC Tenerife and supplied to PwC Legal UK.**

This does not establish that every PwC legal entity shared every document or every professional's knowledge. It establishes why the historical relationship cannot accurately be reduced to “Landwell issued an invoice to Matkator”.

## 5. Institutional chain to test

Use this as the high-level institutional map:

`Aweswell Limited — UK HoldCo / foreign investor in Spain`

`→ Spanish subsidiaries, investments and Sun Park asset/operating interests`

`→ PwC Legal UK formal representation + Landwell/PwC Spain/Canarias professional work`

`→ subsequent impairment/displacement of the Spanish investment under examination`

`→ later PwC professional relationships within the Acosta Matos / RICPE / related perimeter`

Every arrow remains evidence-controlled. The map is not a finding that a later PwC mandate caused the impairment or that any PwC professional committed wrongdoing.

## 6. Professional-conduct / deontological sentence

Use:

> **The former-client and professional-conduct analysis must therefore take account not only of individual Spanish entities but of Aweswell Limited's position as the UK HoldCo and foreign investor whose Spanish subsidiary and asset perimeter formed the subject of the earlier PwC professional work.**

## 9. Permanent control

The institutional issue should not be reduced to the identity on a single invoice. Equally, group-level language must not be used to erase legal-entity separateness. The analysis should remain dual:

- identify the exact PwC legal entity, team, client, mandate and file; and
- preserve the cross-border fact that Aweswell Limited sat at the head of the Spanish investment structure affected by the Sun Park matter and was itself a documented PwC Legal UK client.
