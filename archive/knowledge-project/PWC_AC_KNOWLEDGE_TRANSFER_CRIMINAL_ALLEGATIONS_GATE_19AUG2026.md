## Core evidential rule
Do **not** state as established fact that PwC transmitted every criminal allegation to the AC or that PwC thereby “imputed criminal knowledge” to him. The safe strong formulation is:

> PwC possessed documented knowledge of allegations regarded by the client as potentially criminal before its confirmed direct contact with the Administrador Concursal. The unresolved question is how much of that knowledge was communicated, what the AC already knew independently, and what legal or procedural consequences followed from that knowledge.
