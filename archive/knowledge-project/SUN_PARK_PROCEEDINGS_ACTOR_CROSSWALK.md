## Anti-fragmentation rule

An actor's role in a Community meeting does not automatically prove litigation authority. A lawyer's advice does not automatically prove filing. A proceeding summary does not replace the pleading, power, order, recording or judgment.

For every future claim, retrieve:

`caption → parties → power/representation → filing → order → hearing/recording → outcome → appeal → current status`
