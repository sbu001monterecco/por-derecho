## 2. Correct current status

Use this status:

> **DP 1956/2026 — provisional dismissal / sobreseimiento provisional recorded on 21 July 2026. No filed reform/subsidiary appeal is currently established in the controlled corpus.**

Do not shorten that to:

- `finally closed`;
- `dismissed on the merits`;
- `acquittal`;
- `free dismissal / sobreseimiento libre`;
- `appeal pending`;
- `reopening pending`;

The existence of the proceeding and its provisional dismissal are procedural facts. They do not establish the truth or falsity of the underlying allegations.

## 3. Public legal frame

The public website may explain, without reproducing legal advice, that:

- Article 641 LECrim governs provisional dismissal;
- provisional dismissal is legally distinct from free dismissal;
- Fiscalía General del Estado Circular 1/2021 explains that a provisionally dismissed proceeding may in appropriate circumstances be reopened if materially new evidence or indications are later obtained, subject to the applicable legal requirements and a judicial decision;
- this possibility is **not** a prediction that DP 1956/2026 will reopen.

Public pages must cite the official BOE/LECrim/FGE source rather than attribute this legal proposition to private legal advice.

## 4. Privilege / strategy firewall

It is sufficient publicly to state the verified procedural status and the general legal rule governing provisional dismissal.

## 5. What may become materially relevant later

Future repository scans should test for **new evidence**, not merely more allegations. Potentially material categories include, without implying that any one will be sufficient:

1. a later judicial decision that establishes or materially clarifies a concrete irregular act, duty, loss or causal relationship;
2. newly recovered primary documents concerning a specific, individualised act already within the DP 1956 evidential perimeter;
3. a competent professional/deontological, regulatory or public-control decision establishing a concrete factual proposition relevant to that perimeter;
4. primary records produced by a professional firm or other custodian showing knowledge, instructions, accounting treatment, authority, relationship or document provenance;
5. evidence arising in DP 1901/2026 or another legally distinct proceeding that is genuinely new to the DP 1956 record and relevant to the criminal elements under examination.

The correct test is always source-led and element-specific. Institutional receipt, referral, an open file or a complaint by itself is not a reopening trigger.

## 7. Relationship to other tracks — keep separate

- **DP 1901/2026:** separate criminal/documentary route; current status must be independently sourced.
- **AP Las Palmas / AC-separation appeals:** civil/insolvency appellate route; any later ruling may be evidence but is not automatically a criminal finding.
- **ICALPA DIP 80/2026:** professional/deontological route; any decision must be read within its own competence.
- **RSM NNR4-1025C2F66 / Grant Thornton 2024_04:** private professional/compliance files; their value is preservation, perimeter identification, production and cooperation, not adjudication of criminal liability.
- **CNMV / SNCA / public or EU-funds controls:** distinct regulatory/control functions; a finding must be tied to the exact proposition it establishes.

Do not merge these tracks into one proceeding or describe their coexistence as institutional endorsement.
