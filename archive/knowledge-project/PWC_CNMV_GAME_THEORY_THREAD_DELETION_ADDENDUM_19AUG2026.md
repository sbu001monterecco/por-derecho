## 4. Lawful game-theory / prisoner's-dilemma design for PwC Madrid escalation

The objective is **not** reputational coercion, threat or a quid pro quo. The objective is to make truthful early cooperation the rational low-risk equilibrium.

### Cooperation package to offer

PwC can obtain a documented first-mover integrity position by:

- confirming a legal hold/preservation step across relevant PwC entities and former personnel;
- appointing one senior Madrid-level institutional owner independent of the historical Canary Islands team;
- giving finite yes/no/date/reference answers on client perimeter, conflicts, KYC, independence, information barriers and CNMV contact;
- identifying which PwC legal entity/team held each mandate;
- separating privileged legal advice from non-privileged factual metadata and records;
- correcting any factual error publicly with equivalent prominence;
- stating what independent review, if any, has been opened.

### Reciprocal Por Derecho position

If PwC cooperates, Por Derecho should accurately record the cooperation, narrow or close questions resolved by evidence, preserve privilege/confidentiality, and give factual corrections/replies equivalent prominence.

### Non-cooperation consequence — factual only

If PwC does not answer finite questions, Por Derecho may accurately record that the questions were put and remain unanswered and may put the same documentary questions to competent independent institutions. Silence is not to be represented as an admission or proof of wrongdoing.

### Payoff logic

- **PwC cooperates early:** PwC supplies contemporaneous context before third-party records define the chronology; uncertainty contracts.
- **PwC stays silent while third-party records emerge:** PwC loses informational control and later explanations face a harder reconciliation burden.
- **PwC confirms no relevant later mandate/contact after checking:** that materially narrows the theory and should be published fairly.
- **PwC confirms a relevant later mandate/contact:** the inquiry becomes finite and document-specific rather than speculative.

The escalation email should therefore ask for preservation + independent review + finite metadata answers, with a clear response period, rather than accuse or threaten.
