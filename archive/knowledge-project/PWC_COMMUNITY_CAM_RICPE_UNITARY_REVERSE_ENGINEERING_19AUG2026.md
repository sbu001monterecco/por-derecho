## VERIFIED / source-supported perimeter

The controlled corporate and title record places Aweswell Limited as the UK parent and sole shareholder of LPB and Matkator. The group’s filed accounts describe the historic Sun Park asset as a productive-unit structure involving property, exploitation and operating value, while expressly identifying the later allegations as the directors’ good-faith position rather than adjudicated findings.

The 26-Apr-2016 Community act and the 11-Jun-2016 meeting record show a live dispute over:

- majority/minority interests;
- the owners’ Community;
- the exploitation vehicle / hotel operator;
- accounts and historic costs;
- alleged Community debt and morosidad;
- voting rights;
- access to individual apartments;
- deeds/private-title interests;
- Concurso 36/2012 and the insolvency administrator;
- the future governance and operating solution for the hotel.

## ALLEGATION to test

Aweswell/the Project alleges that a minority/dissident perimeter progressively captured or instrumentalised the Community organ and used Community debt, voting, documentation, administration, security or access mechanisms in a way that impaired the majority/productive-unit position.

## What would prove “capture” rather than ordinary contested governance

For each decisive Junta and office-holder:

1. ownership percentage and title on that date;
2. voting eligibility and any debt-based exclusion;
3. proxy/representation authority;
4. notice, quorum, agenda and resolution;
5. presidency/secretary/administrator appointment;
6. bank/security/key/CCTV/contract authority;
7. later challenge, annulment or confirmation;
8. communication of the resulting apparent authority to courts, AC, CAM or third parties;
9. value/control consequence.

Without this matrix, “capture” remains a party characterisation.

# 10. RIC / regional incentives / FEDER: the “house of cards” dependency test

The controlled corpus establishes multiple later finance/public-support layers around MYND Yaiza. The RICPE Council communication records regional-incentive figures of approximately **EUR 11.469m eligible investment, EUR 3.4409m aid and 60 jobs**, and records that the permanent project plaque expressly identifies **FEDER**. Those figures/source details should be re-opened from the primary administrative file before external use.

## The user’s house-of-cards hypothesis

> If CAM/HNT lacked lawful title, availability or authority over assets essential to the Sun Park/MYND project, then later RIC investment, investor finance, regional incentives or FEDER support resting materially on contrary ownership/availability representations may require reassessment.

## Why the cascade is not automatic

A foundational title defect does **not** automatically void every later financing or public benefit. For each programme or investor decision prove:

1. who applied / contracted / benefited;
2. what asset/title/availability representation was required;
3. exact eligibility condition;
4. what was actually represented;
5. what the decision-maker knew;
6. whether the disputed unit/assets were material to eligibility/value;
7. what costs were eligible and actually paid;
8. funding source and use;
9. compatibility/double-funding rules;
10. audit/certification and any correction/recovery mechanism;
11. causal consequence of any false/incomplete representation.

The strongest public-funds theory is therefore **dependency and materiality**, not rhetorical collapse.
