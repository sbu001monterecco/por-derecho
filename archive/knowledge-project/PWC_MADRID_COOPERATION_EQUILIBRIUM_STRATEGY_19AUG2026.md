# PwC Madrid Cooperation-Equilibrium Strategy — Sun Park / RICPE / CNMV

**Date:** 19 August 2026  
**Status:** internal institutional-strategy control; not a finding of wrongdoing and not a legal conclusion that any reporting duty existed.  
**Purpose:** make preservation, independent review, factual clarification and bounded cooperation the rational low-risk response for PwC while preserving Por Derecho’s evidence-before-conclusion discipline.

## 1. Strategic objective

The objective is **not** to threaten, embarrass, blackmail, coerce or publicly punish PwC. The objective is to create a lawful cooperation equilibrium in which a sophisticated repeat institutional player has more to gain from early preservation and factual clarification than from avoidable opacity.

PwC’s legitimate long-term interests include:

- professional reputation;
- regulator credibility;
- independence and conflict systems;
- former-client confidentiality;
- audit/legal quality;
- litigation defensibility;
- accurate institutional records;
- avoiding factual narratives being reconstructed exclusively from third-party materials.

Por Derecho’s reciprocal interest is an accurate, falsifiable record that distinguishes PwC legal entities, teams, mandates, dates and duties rather than treating “PwC” as one undifferentiated actor.

## 3. CNMV transparency question — finite and non-assumptive

Do **not** ask “Why didn’t PwC report this to CNMV?” unless a specific reporting duty is first proved.

The strategic question is:

> What did PwC know; when did it know it; what later PwC mandates overlapped with the same asset/perimeter; what conflict, independence, former-client and investor-protection assessment followed; and was there any communication with CNMV—whether initiated by CNMV or PwC? This forces an answer without assuming the answer.

## 6. Repeated-game / cooperation-equilibrium model

PwC is a repeat institutional player. In a repeated game, truthful early clarification can reduce uncertainty and future reconstruction risk.

### Outcome 1 — PwC cooperates / institutions cooperate

- maximum early factual certainty;
- quickest narrowing of false or overstated propositions;
- PwC helps define the accurate entity/team/mandate chronology;
- preservation and conflicts/independence review become verifiable institutional facts.

### Outcome 2 — PwC cooperates / others remain silent

- PwC obtains a first-mover integrity advantage;
- it can separate its own conduct from other actors;
- it can correct the record before third-party materials dominate the narrative.

### Outcome 3 — PwC remains silent / CNMV or others later produce records

- highest informational risk for PwC;
- the chronology may be reconstructed from CNMV, RICPE, client, court and third-party records without PwC’s context;
- silence remains **not evidence of guilt**, but it reduces PwC’s ability to shape the accuracy of the factual record.

### Outcome 4 — everyone remains silent

- uncertainty remains open;
- evidence-preservation and documentary-transparency requests continue;
- no adverse inference of guilt should be published merely from silence.

## 12. Response matrix

### Full cooperation

Record:

- preservation confirmed;
- reviewer/contact appointed;
- finite questions answered;
- entity/mandate map supplied;
- any corrections incorporated;
- remaining privilege/confidentiality boundaries identified specifically.

Next step: narrow/close resolved questions and ask only genuinely outstanding ones.

### Partial cooperation

Record precisely what was answered and what remains open. Do not characterise partial production as concealment without evidence.

Next step: convert unresolved issues into smaller binary/date/reference questions.

### Privilege/confidentiality-based response

Accept that some substantive material may be protected. Ask for non-privileged metadata where possible:

- existence of file;
- date/range;
- legal entity/function;
- preservation status;
- whether a review occurred;
- broad category of restriction.

Do not demand waiver.

### Silence / no substantive answer

Record only:

> “This question was put to PwC on [date]. No substantive answer has yet been received.”

Then ask competent third parties the same finite documentary questions. Do not say silence constitutes admission.

### Factual denial

Identify exactly what proposition is denied, preserve the denial with equivalent prominence, and request the minimum source material or metadata capable of resolving the contradiction.
