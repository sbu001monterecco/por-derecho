## Statement-quality issue

The public record does **not** establish that the 21 December 2024 statement was knowingly false. It does establish that the categorical wording requires a more precise explanation.

A sophisticated professional-services organisation should distinguish at least:
- legal entity;
- employment/partnership status;
- contractor/commercial-service status;
- title and public professional presentation;
- email/system/office access;
- professional supervision and risk management;
- conflict/independence controls;
- work performed inside and outside the firm's perimeter;
- professional-responsibility and insurance perimeter.

Accordingly, the correct public formulation is:

> The 21 December 2024 categorical statement may have been intended in a narrow legal-entity sense, but without an entity-by-entity explanation it is potentially materially incomplete and capable of misleading a reader about the broader acknowledged professional/commercial relationship and Grant Thornton-branded professional presentation.

Do **not** publish “GTIL lied”, “GT concealed”, “fraudulent statement” or equivalent absent proof of knowledge and intent.

## Professional-responsibility / potential co-liability analysis

### Spanish statutory route to verify — not a conclusion
Ley 2/2007, de sociedades profesionales, Article 11.2 provides that for social debts arising from professional acts, the professional company and the professionals, members or not, who acted are jointly and severally liable, subject to the applicable contractual or extra-contractual rules. Article 11.3 requires professional companies to maintain liability insurance. Additional Provision 2 extends the Article 11 regime to collective professional activity even where not constituted as a professional company, with a statutory presumption where professional activity is carried on publicly under a common/collective denomination or documents/invoices/fees are issued under it.

**This does not establish that Cuyás Abogados, Grant Thornton Canarias, Grant Thornton Spain or GTIL is liable for any act in Concurso 36/2012.** Applicability depends on facts still to be established, including whether any relevant act was professionally performed through, for, under the authority of, or within the collective professional perimeter of Cuyás/GT Canarias/another entity.
