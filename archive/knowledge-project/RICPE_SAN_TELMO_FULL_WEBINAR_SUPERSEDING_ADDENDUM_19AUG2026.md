## Full-scan finding 1 — Eduardo / San Telmo was not merely interviewing RICPE
Around 07:57–08:48, Eduardo Sánchez is visibly the speaking participant. He asks about the investment alternative and then states, in the auto-generated transcript, in substance:

> `nosotros en el despacho ... metimos unos cuantos clientes`

He places this in the context of the **first investment** Enrique had been discussing and explains that they regarded it as an interesting alternative for clients/investors.

### Safe proposition
By 30 November 2021, Eduardo Sánchez publicly described **the firm / “el despacho” as having introduced or placed several clients into a RICPE investment**. This is a contemporaneous source statement made directly to RICPE Director General Enrique Guerra in a San Telmo-branded event.

### Consequence
This is materially stronger than mere media proximity or a one-off interview. It establishes a contemporaneous, transaction-adjacent **San Telmo ↔ RICPE client/investor-introduction connection**, as stated by Eduardo himself.

### Boundary
The source does **not** yet establish whether San Telmo acted as investment adviser, distributor, marketer, introducer, referral source, legal adviser, tax adviser, placement agent, or some other role. Do not assign a regulated or contractual role without the underlying records.
