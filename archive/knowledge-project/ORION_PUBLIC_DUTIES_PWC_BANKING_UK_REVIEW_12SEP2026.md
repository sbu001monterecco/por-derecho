# Orion / RICPE / Sun Park: professional knowledge, financing and public accountability

Control: 12 September 2026. Public evidence and investigation brief. GitLab is the canonical repository; the corresponding GitHub copy preserves cross-repository continuity. This is not a judicial finding, a submission, an investment offer or a fictional prospectus.

## Position and scope

Gil Marer alleges a continuing mechanism in which private actors captured or misused the Sun Park Community's decision-making organs, asserted artificial debt and voting restrictions, and generated or used false or materially misleading minutes, certificates and submissions. The alleged downstream uses include the insolvency proceeding, Ayuntamiento de Yaiza, Cabildo de Lanzarote, investment documentation and public-funding processes. He asks that the later Acosta Matos, RICPE and Orion relationships be investigated as possible continuation, adoption or benefit from that mechanism, including reciprocal failures involving Cajasiete. These are express allegations requiring investigation; they are not adjudicated collective guilt.

The inquiry is both forward and backward: original authority/debt/document → recipient/knowledge → verification or adoption → decision → title/control/finance → benefit or injury; then trace each current benefit back to its legal and factual foundations. Each arrow needs its own source. The legal persons, assets and proceedings remain distinct. A later lawful title or independent decision can narrow a particular causal chain; a later investment structure does not automatically cure an earlier defect.

The precise issuer is **ORION RENTAL SOCIMI, S.A.**, not a separately verified rental-receivables issuer. Portfolio Stock Exchange is operated by **European Digital Securities Exchange, S.V., S.A.**, Spanish NIF A88474713, CNMV investment-firm registration 314. The user's reference to “Portugal Stock Exchange” is treated here as Portfolio; no Portuguese exchange is accused or addressed. [1]

## PwC's prior work and the criminal-notice distinction

The controlled record establishes substantial Landwell/PwC Canarias work in 2016 concerning the Sun Park project, LPB, Matkator, Community governance, private-owner interests and operating arrangements. Carlos Saavedra's team included Miguel Hernández Lorenzo, Elena Santos Ramos and Alejandro Perera González in documented roles. Billing to Matkator does not delimit every matter known to the team. Conversely, work affecting the wider Aweswell perimeter does not establish that every entity or owner was a contractual client: engagement letters, acceptance/KYC records and powers must identify each client and privilege holder.

The primary correspondence rescan establishes three separate propositions:

1. Carlos sought project and operating documents in March 2016, including material for a proposed arrangement with the insolvency administrator. This supports operational knowledge extending beyond a single invoice.
2. June and July correspondence records the client's instruction to pursue a criminal route. The 20 July acknowledgement was authored by Miguel; Carlos was copied. A received client instruction is not a PwC-authored legal conclusion or a filed criminal complaint.
3. PwC's 12 September 2016 reply confirms a general telephone conversation with the insolvency administrator concerning proposed operating arrangements. It says there was no detailed report or exchange of emails about that conversation. It does not establish the alleged statement that the entire matter was criminal or identify the targets of such a statement.

Gil's account that PwC told the administrator criminal action was required must therefore remain an attributed allegation and a finite production question: identify speaker, date, participants, contemporaneous note/audio, exact proposition, identified persons and the administrator's response. The wider FMMM/Cogolludo/Pamalexsha perimeter cannot be inserted into a 2016 quotation whose source does not name it.

The **10 June 2016 working meeting** is a different event. The controlling 9 September correction establishes that date and the client's authenticated direct-delivery provenance. Historical “11JUN2016” labels are preserved aliases. Two located AMR recordings total approximately 126m36s; the later derivative states 144 minutes. The public 176-entry extract is a subset of a much larger derivative. Complete audio/transcript alignment, technical authentication and proposition-level speaker attribution remain open. The 11 September knowledge package's contrary date/provenance shorthand is expressly superseded on these points; its substantive notice analysis remains available subject to those controls. [2]

The derivative contains detailed PwC/Simó explanations to FMMM and Prieto of allegedly fictitious debt, voting consequences and use of papers in the insolvency/court setting. This is material notice and potential knowledge evidence. It is not a clean confession by FMMM of inventing debt. The recording does not establish that the insolvency administrator or either Cogolludo was present or personally received its contents.

Match each works cost to payer, contractor, asset, period, invoice, payment, RIC contribution, bank loan, grant/incentive and any EU cofinancing. ## Public duties and possible cross-border routes

Portfolio's public-information responsibilities are not confined to private contracts. Law 6/2023 articles 68–69 address orderly operation and publicly available instrument information; article 71 separately provides a holders' damages route against the responsible issuer/directors. EU market-abuse duties distinguish issuer disclosure from operator surveillance/reporting. The absence of a final criminal conviction does not prevent checking a numerical error, material risk or misleading omission. [9–10]

For affected non-investor businesses, Spanish Civil Code article 1902 and, where its market-conduct and standing requirements are met, the Unfair Competition Act offer distinct possible routes. Rectification, cessation and removal of effects can matter independently of investor compensation. A personal damages claim still requires a particular duty/wrong, actionable conduct, causal injury and the correct claimant. Being online or associated with a regulated venue is not a universal guarantee of compliance. [11–12]

**UK check:** HMRC's 31 July 2026 table names Portfolio as a recognised exchange for UK tax purposes but places its **entire market in the “Not listed” column**. This is neither FCA authorisation nor proof of UK operating presence. The current CNMV/Portfolio board and CNMV ≥5% shareholder list did not establish the asserted UK-resident director or UK controlling shareholder. The five disclosed ≥5% holdings total 73%; this is not a complete beneficial-ownership census. A similarly named UK company, European Digital Securities Ltd, has not been connected to the Spanish operator and must not be used as that connection. [1,13–14]

UK-facing promotions, actual UK management/decision-making, a relevant UK professional engagement or a defendant's own UK conduct could support a further inquiry. Financial-promotion rules can reach overseas communications capable of UK effect, subject to statutory conditions and exemptions. English tort claims and service abroad require their own basis; a UK shareholder, a website view or the claimant's UK incorporation is not enough by itself. General negligence, personal director responsibility and compensation cannot be inferred from the exchange label. [15–16]

Spain/CNMV remains the demonstrated market-supervision route. EU-funds concerns may separately justify a source-specific report to the competent managing authority, OLAF or EPPO where their mandates are engaged; no grant irregularity is proved merely by the network. A possible OECD National Contact Point process requires relevant enterprise/geographic links and supports mediation/recommendations, not compulsory damages. Human-rights proceedings concern attributable State responsibility and their admissibility rules, not a direct Strasbourg case against this private exchange. These are distinct routes, not one universal international jurisdiction. [17–19]

## Required compliance outcomes

Portfolio should give an indexed response to each material issue: evidence reviewed; exact duty; established or unestablished proposition; materiality assessment; affected document/version; responsible decision-maker; conflict safeguards; corrective action or reason for declining it; and next review date. Acknowledgement, internal circulation and referral do not equal acceptance of the allegations.

The 11 September 2026 reply confirms receipt of the four September documents, incorporation in the December 2024 file, relevant internal review and reported transmission of their content to CNMV. It does not disclose a complete merits decision or independent CNMV receipt of that exact package. It supersedes the claim that no Portfolio-to-CNMV transmission is confirmed, while leaving transmission particulars and outcome open.

Requested remedies include:

1. Preserve evidence, identify an independent review owner and resolve professional/bank/issuer conflicts.
2. Obtain complete engagement, related-party, bank-exposure, source/use, title, public-funding and document-to-decision schedules.
3. Reconcile the **original 7 November 2024 Orion memorandum**, **30 June 2026 update** and later communications; publish a dated correction log and consolidated replacement where warranted, preserving previous versions and communicating corrections to relevant recipients.
4. Treat the **4 June 2020 RICPE brochure** as the located historical anchor. Locate and authenticate the exact “2024 RICPE” edition referred to by Gil before making page-by-page claims about that version.
5. Decide expressly whether Portfolio should publish its **own notice**. Rule **5.2.7(b)** provides an exchange non-compliance notice within the stated decision process; other measures include corrective requests, suspension and exclusion. These require the relevant findings and procedure, not automatic adoption of the complainant's characterisation. [20]
6. Assess whether investor information, accounts, valuations, governance, professional controls or public-funding submissions need separate correction. Memorandum replacement is not automatically accounting restatement, refund or title restitution.
7. Address specific alleged continuing harm: who made what statement, to whom, after what notice, who relied on it, what decision changed, and which rights-holder lost what. Correct dissemination should reach the channels through which a misleading impression actually spread, including issuer/related-party communications where traceable.
8. Provide competent-authority referrals and lawful cooperation where required, with accurate receipt/decision status and reasoned treatment of contrary evidence.
