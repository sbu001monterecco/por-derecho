# SUN PARK CONTROL CONTINUITY — REPOSITORY AND WEBSITE UPDATE PLAN

**Cut-off: 15 August 2026**  
Read with `P19_SUN_PARK_MATERIAL_CONTROL_POSSESSION_CONTINUITY_MEETING_POINT_15AUG2026.md`.

## Objective

Make the public record state the control chronology more clearly without confusing material control with formal title:

> **The alleged displacement did not begin with the 2022 deed. A pre-existing dissident/Community apparatus had affected governance, debt, vote, maintenance, keys, possession and hotel operation since 2011 or earlier. CAM’s attributed physical entry and interference began by October 2017. On 7 June 2018 the principal access regime was physically replaced and effective practical control over the hotel shifted to the Community/security/CAM-aligned perimeter. Formal title to the LPB asset perimeter came later.**

The website should state this as a layered evidential proposition:

- direct facts concerning gates, locks, security and exclusion;
- evidence-based inference concerning effective hotel-wide control;
- Gil Marer’s allegation of full material possession;
- open proof concerning every unit, legal possession, authority, continuity and economic use.

## Editorial rule

Use **“effective material control,” “practical possession,” “de facto control”** and **“full material possession alleged”** precisely.

Do not substitute:

- “lawful possession”;
- “ownership of the hotel”;
- “judicial delivery of possession”;
- “entry into every unit”;
- or “criminal takeover proved.”

No court order granting CAM possession of the whole hotel has been located. The point is that practical control may have preceded and exceeded formal title.

### Threshold 1 — predecessor machinery

Title:

`2008–2017 · la interferencia no empezó con CAM`

Core text:

`Octubre 2017–marzo 2018 · presencia, acceso y preparación antes de la toma`
