## Objective

Make the public record state the control chronology clearly without confusing practical control with title:

> **The alleged displacement did not begin with the 2022 deed. A pre-existing dissident/Community apparatus had affected governance, debt, vote, maintenance, keys, possession and hotel operation since 2011 or earlier. The first specifically dated physical-incursion lead currently located is 2 October 2017—Q4, not Q3—followed by further reported access, locksmith/security activity, measurements, owner-like assertions and operational interference. On 7 June 2018 the principal access regime was physically replaced and effective practical control over the hotel shifted to the Community/security/CAM-aligned perimeter. Gil Marer alleges that this amounted to full practical possession of the hotel as an operating unit. Formal title to the LPB asset perimeter came later.**

The website must present this as layered evidence:

- direct and contemporaneous facts concerning gates, locks, security, exclusion and interruption;
- evidence-based inference concerning effective hotel-wide control;
- Gil Marer’s allegation of full material/practical possession;
- open proof concerning every unit, legal possession, actor instruction, duration, authority, continuity and economic use.

## Editorial rule

Use precisely:

- **effective material control**;
- **de facto control**;
- **practical possession**;
- **full practical/material possession alleged**.

Do not substitute:

- lawful whole-hotel possession;
- ownership of the hotel;
- judicial delivery of whole-hotel possession;
- entry into every unit;
- Q3 2017 as an established date;
- criminal takeover proved.

No reviewed court order granting CAM possession of the whole hotel has been located. The public point is that practical control may have preceded and exceeded formal title.
