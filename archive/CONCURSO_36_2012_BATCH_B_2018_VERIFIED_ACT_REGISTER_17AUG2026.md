## 16-Apr dual-act anti-conflation test

The seven-page liquidation-plan Auto itself expressly states that **an Auto de aclaración was issued "con esta misma fecha"** concerning the amount of CAM's recognised credit. The separate two-page 16-Apr copy independently exists and addresses that interest/privileged-credit question. The two documents therefore have distinct subject matter, page counts and source hashes while sharing the same act date, Judge and notification date.

**Regression rule:** never infer a single 16-Apr act from the shared date or shared 20-Apr notification. `C36-JUD-2018-04-16-001` and `C36-JUD-2018-04-16-002` remain separate stable acts.
