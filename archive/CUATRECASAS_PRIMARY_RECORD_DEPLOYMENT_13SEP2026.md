The controlled source spine now includes the 2017 convenio/viability route, Cuatrecasas's 27-Nov-2017 Article 176 precursor, its post-liquidation 19-Jan-2018 Article 176 update, the 30-Jan-2018 unity-of-exploitation/action note, the May-2018 Red Flag DD and early-June operator/industry-lease implementation work. This supports **professional and transactional readiness before 7 June 2018**. It does **not** by itself prove complete consignation, unconditional funds or a final Article 176 petition already filed before that date.

The date of the direct Daniel Irigoyen communication must remain exact: the presently controlled contemporaneous account places the meeting with the insolvency judge and the Administrador Concursal on **13 June 2018**, six days after the 7 June material-control event. The safe sequence is therefore: **readiness before 7 June → direct judge/AC communication on 13 June → continued later pursuit**. Do not retrodate the 13-Jun meeting.

The submission that an institutional actor should not benefit from its own acts is preserved as a **legal/causal argument**, not a substitute for proof. For filing, identify the exact act/omission, the condition it affected (access, payoff, security, DD, valuation, time or cash flow), the contemporaneous source proving the exit route was live, and the resulting causal effect.

### AC convenio evaluation report — recovery target

The report searched for in the 24-Sep-2026 truth review is the **Administrador Concursal's written evaluation of LPB's 2017 propuesta de convenio** under the then-applicable Ley Concursal. It evaluated the proposal in relation to the payment plan and, where applicable, the viability plan. It was **not** the creditor vote and **not** a judicial finding that the convenio had failed.

What is presently located:

- The court order of **26 June 2017** expressly says the AC's escrito de evaluación de la propuesta de convenio was to be made available in the court office.
- The same order refused to adjourn the creditors' meeting set for 28 June 2017.
- Juan Tomás Parrilla wrote contemporaneously on **26 June 2017** that the AC's evaluation was negative and summarised the reason as insufficient proof, in the AC's view, that the viability plan would succeed.
- Parrilla also stated that the convenio was between LPB and its creditors and that it was not the AC's role to negotiate it for LPB.

**Missing:** the actual text of the AC evaluation report itself. Do not reconstruct Borja's precise reasoning from Parrilla's summary.

**Next-thread retrieval target:** official Concurso 36/2012 docket/LexNET and counsel files for 'evaluación de la propuesta de convenio', '26/06/2017', and registry '3671/2017'. The 26-Jun order lists 3671/2017, 4421/2017 and 4436/2017 as filings respectively associated with the AC, Ayuntamiento de Yaiza and the debtor; **treat the association of 3671/2017 with the evaluation as a search lead, not a confirmed identity, until the source is recovered**.
