## 1. Controlling principle: anti-conflation is not anti-connectivity

The repository must simultaneously preserve two truths:

1. **Different persons, companies, courts, proceedings, titles and patrimonies cannot be legally collapsed into one another without source support.** Matkator is not LPB; Aweswell is not Matkator; ETJ 163/2020 is not Concurso 36/2012; DP 748/2026 is not an appellate instance of ETJ.
2. **Formal separation does not prove substantive disconnection.** The same hotel, physical works, operational unit, professional mandate, knowledge history, transaction sequence, property transformation or economic result can create relevant factual, evidential, contractual and causative bridges across legally distinct proceedings.

Therefore the rule is:

> **Do not conflate; do connect.** Every connection must be labelled by type — physical, economic, contractual, professional, evidential, procedural, registry, insolvency, causative or alleged criminal — and only the specific bridge proved by the source may be asserted.

## 3. Three different meanings of “connected”

### 3.1 Legally/procedurally connected

The source-locked procedural chain is:

`TF-CIV-001 Cambiario 1048/2019 → TF-CIV-002 ETJ 163/2020`

DP 748/2026 is a distinct criminal proceeding:

`TF-CIV-002 ETJ 163/2020 ⇄ TF-CRI-003 DP 748/2026`

The reciprocal arrow means that DP 748 examines alleged autonomous conduct associated with ETJ, while ETJ filings now expressly invoke and characterise DP 748. It does not mean the criminal court supervises ETJ merits.

### 3.2 Physically/economically connected

The client-side case concerns assets said to have been physically and functionally integrated into the same Sun Park / later MYND Yaiza hotel operation. Physical hotel integration may be highly relevant to possession, value, use, notice, valuation, disclosure and economic effect.

But physical/economic unity does **not by itself** establish that a Matkator finca became legally part of LPB, the insolvency estate, or another owner’s title. That requires registry/title/estate evidence.

### 3.3 Professionally/causatively connected

If the same law firm previously advised on protection, recovery, restructuring or monetisation of the integrated perimeter, later affirmative enforcement against a separate group company or asset can be professionally connected even though the court proceedings are formally separate. The questions include former-client duties, confidentiality, knowledge carried forward, conflict governance, authority, causation, accounting and whether later steps undermine or contradict earlier mandate objectives.

Those connections must be established actor-by-actor and mandate-by-mandate, not inferred solely from the firm name.
