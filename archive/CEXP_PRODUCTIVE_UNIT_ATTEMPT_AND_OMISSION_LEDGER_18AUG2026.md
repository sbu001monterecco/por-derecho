# CEXP PRODUCTIVE-UNIT ATTEMPT AND OMISSION LEDGER

**Control date:** 18 August 2026  
**Status:** `PUBLIC-SAFE FORENSIC LEDGER — PRIMARY/EXTERNAL RESPONSE + CONTEMPORANEOUS PARTY POSITION + EXPERT WORK PRODUCT`  
**Purpose:** establish what was raised, prepared, transmitted, answered or pursued concerning CEXP and the Sun Park productive unit during the life of Concurso 36/2012, without converting intended presentation into proved admission or privileged advice into public evidence.

## Controlling conclusion

> **The CEXP/productive-unit case was repeatedly advanced during the concurso and was not invented after the calificación.**

The located record documents a sustained 2015–2018 chain concerning Community/CEXP governance, exploitation costs, LPB income rights, CEXP-related receivables, access/control, productive-unit economics and judicial/AC follow-up. Some entries have direct AC responses; others have stamped-document metadata, expert work product or contemporaneous preparation expressly aimed at the AC or the court.

The strongest present conclusion is:

> **Repeated advancement + item-specific evidence of role limitation, operative restriction, alleged non-action or perceived need for judicial intervention.**

It is **not** yet a global finding that every item was formally admitted, rejected or deliberately suppressed.

## Evidential classes

- **VERIFIED EXTERNAL RESPONSE** — direct communication to/from an external decision-maker or office-holder.
- **STAMPED-DOCUMENT METADATA** — contemporary record proving existence of a sealed/stamped item and intended transmission, without treating the unstated content as proved.
- **AC KNOWLEDGE / ACCOUNTING RECORD** — contemporaneous evidence that the AC addressed the relevant CEXP accounting/economic issue.
- **EXPERT WORK PRODUCT** — economic or forensic report prepared for LPB/Aweswell/court use; methodology may be relevant but confidentiality/privilege/publication limits apply.
- **CONTEMPORANEOUS PARTY POSITION** — a position stated at the time, not an independent finding.
- **JUDICIAL/AC PRESENTATION ATTEMPT** — record of an attempt, instruction or preparation to place material before the Judge/AC; admission/readership must be verified separately.
- **FORMAL INFORMATION REQUEST** — counsel-prepared or externally directed request concerning operation/accounts/information.

## 11 June 2018 expert note — public-safe findings

The located PDF is a 15-page economic note prepared by Four Mirror Advisors / eXW Forensic & Compliance for Aweswell and LPB, expressly for contemplated proceedings and competent-court use. It is marked **“Borrador / Sujeto a Cambios”** and **private/confidential**.

Public-safe propositions from the document are limited to the following:

1. It identifies in the AC’s January 2013 report an LPB short-term financial investment described as **“Dividendo a Cobrar de C.E. Sun Park” — €737,338.85**.
2. It disputes the proposition that LPB simply “owed itself” a proportion because LPB participated in CEXP.
3. Its economic theory is that non-participating/minority owners allegedly increased CEXP costs and reduced exploitable inventory/income, thereby reducing returns to CEXP participants, particularly LPB.
4. It calculates a wider **preliminary** economic burden/claim materially above the €737,338.85 recorded amount. That wider amount is expert work product and must not be represented as a recognised debt, judgment or accounting asset without independent reconciliation.
5. It frames CEXP as the operating vehicle for the hotel and distinguishes the Owners’ Community from the exploitation economy.
6. It is contemporaneous evidence that an expert productive-unit/economic-rights analysis existed in June 2018.

The repository should **not publish the confidential report itself or privileged surrounding advice** merely because its existence supports the chronology.

## What this ledger now supports publicly

The website may safely say:

> **The productive-unit value was not a late reconstruction. The record documents a repeated 2015–2018 attempt chain in which LPB/Aweswell and their advisers raised CEXP governance, costs, receivables, income rights, exploitation structure and judicial/AC follow-up. Direct AC correspondence proves knowledge of a material CEXP accounting layer; expert work product and presentation efforts show contemporaneous attempts to quantify and defend it.**

It may also say:

> **In identified instances the record shows an AC role limitation, operative-control restrictions, contemporaneous allegations of non-action, and efforts to seek judicial intervention.**

It must **not yet** say globally:

- every CEXP document was formally filed and admitted;
- the Judge personally read every item;
- the AC deliberately suppressed the entire productive-unit case;
- every CEXP asset or receivable belonged to LPB’s estate;
- the expert’s wider preliminary quantification was judicially recognised.
