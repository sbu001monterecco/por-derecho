## Controlled finding

The communication records a request by the insolvency administrator of Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. to the President of the Sun Park Owners’ Community to convene a General Meeting for the purpose of adopting a resolution to hire security. The stated purposes were to prevent access by unauthorised persons and prevent deterioration and improper use of common and privately owned areas.

This is direct evidence of the proposed institutional route. It is not, without additional sources, evidence of:

- authority over Matkator or third-party units;
- authority to break locks, change cylinders, place chains or seize keys;
- authority to exclude workers, guests, owners or other rights-holders;
- the identity of “unauthorised persons”;
- the validity or content of a later Community vote;
- instructions given to any security or locksmith contractor;
- criminal common purpose or final criminal characterisation.
