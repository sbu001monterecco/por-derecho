## 1. Material new intelligence created by the thread

This thread identified and formalised a methodological refinement not previously captured as a standalone doctrine:

1. **Evidential recognisability** — evidence may remain intact while its material meaning is lost if relationships between fragments are not preserved.
2. **Relationship-as-evidence** — sometimes the material evidential object is not Document A or Document B but the source-backed relationship `A → B`.
3. **Breadth paradox** — **the whole can be too broad to investigate; the pieces can be too small to reveal the whole.**
4. **Controlling principle** — **Separate responsibility. Integrate evidence.**
5. **Caution** — fragmentation is not itself evidence of conspiracy, judicial/official misconduct, bias, deception or coordination.
6. **Operational answer** — preserve minimum lawful context and finite institutional handoffs without asking any authority to become a super-authority.
7. **Second Pair architecture** — recognisability is an overlay across the existing six checks, not a seventh check.
8. **Foundation/research boundary** — the initiative may steward and test the methodology, but founder-related live matters do not independently validate it and must remain subject to quarantine, recusal, contrary evidence and external red-team controls.

## 4. Source-status and allegation safeguards

Preserve these distinctions in all later reuse:

- lawful institutional/procedural compartmentalisation may be necessary and benign;
- consequential procedural atomisation may exist without deliberate wrongdoing;
- missing transfer/notification/indexing context should be investigated as provenance before fault attribution;
- materially different private audience/perimeter descriptions justify reconciliation, not automatic deception findings;
- coordinated fragmentation requires actor-specific evidence such as communications, instructions, shared plans or a comparable concrete mechanism;
- adverse decisions, common economic interest, professional proximity, institutional sequence and similar outcomes are insufficient to prove coordination;
- relationship evidence does not itself prove causation, knowledge, intent, benefit, liability or guilt.
