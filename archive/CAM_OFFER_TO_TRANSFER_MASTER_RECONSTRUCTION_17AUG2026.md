## Non-negotiable actor-separation rule

Every stage must be analysed as four separate layers:

> **CAM REQUEST / POSITION → AC RECOMMENDATION OR IMPLEMENTATION → JUDGE DECISION OR PROVED OMISSION → CONSEQUENCE**

Never collapse those layers into “the court/AC”. A CAM proposal is not an AC conclusion. An AC recommendation is not a judicial finding. An AC deed or implementation step is not automatically attributable to the Judge. A Judge-signed order is attributable to its actual signatory, but its silence is not an omission unless the issue was before the Judge, the Judge had power/duty to act, the record shows no responsive act, and a material consequence is evidenced.

## Evidence classifications

1. **VERIFIED PRIMARY FACT** — direct source proves the stated proposition.
2. **PARTY / ACTOR POSITION** — CAM, AC, LPB/Aweswell or another actor states the proposition.
3. **EXPERT OPINION** — valuation/economic/professional conclusion.
4. **EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE** — inference stated expressly as such.
5. **UNRESOLVED / GAP** — source still required.

### Structure of the offer

The offer contains two linked acquisition limbs.

#### Limb A — 159 mortgaged apartments

CAM states that it acquired the secured credits from Promontoria Holding 122 B.V. by deed dated 20-Oct-2017.

CAM states balances at 20-Oct-2017 of:

- first-rank mortgage: **€11,061,851.43**;
- second-rank mortgage: **€1,189,594.78**;
- aggregate stated balance: **€12,251,446.21**, before later interest.

CAM offers to acquire the 159 mortgaged LPB apartments by **dación / cesión en pago**. The offer states an apartment value / price of **€7,065,384.42** and proposes full cancellation/condonation of the difference necessary to extinguish the two mortgage debts, which CAM estimates at approximately **€5,186,061.79**, in addition to later accrued interest treatment stated in the offer.

CAM also proposes to assume transaction expenses/taxes and the Community amounts attached to the acquired fincas, and says that if the acquisition succeeds it would seek closure of Arrecife mortgage execution 90/2012.

**Control:** the €12.251m stated credit cancellation is not cash paid into the concurso. The €7.065m figure is the offer's value/price for the apartment limb. The economic audit must separately track debt extinguishment, cash, assumed liabilities and actual estate benefit.

#### Limb B — 29 locales + 2 pools/solarium

The offer separately identifies fincas 8.508–8.536, 8.653 and 8.654 and proposes **cash purchase for €400,000**.

CAM's own offer relies on an ACTÚA valuation of the reception/pools/solarium/locales perimeter of **€489,361.96** and argues that those elements are functionally tied to the tourism exploitation unit. It expressly links the €400,000 proposal economically to the debt cancellation/condonation offered in Limb A.

### Important source proposition

The native offer is therefore not accurately summarised as “CAM offered €400,000 for Sun Park.” It is a **two-limb combined acquisition proposal**: secured-apartment dación plus direct cash purchase of the locales/pools perimeter, with CAM presenting the combined economics as beneficial because of debt cancellation and assumed costs/liabilities.

The AC concluded that, because the €400,000 could pay estate claims and part of concursal claims, **the sale on those conditions was beneficial to the concurso**.

This is a central AC-specific endorsement proposition and must not be attributed to the Judge.

### Alternative route in the Plan

The Plan separately provides an alternative judicial-auction route if the CAM dación/direct-sale proposal was not admitted. It also authorises the AC to sign public/private documents necessary to execute the Plan provided the terms do not contradict it.
