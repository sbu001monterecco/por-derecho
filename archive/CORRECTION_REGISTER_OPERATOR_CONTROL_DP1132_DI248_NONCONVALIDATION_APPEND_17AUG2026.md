## OC-CORR-01 — DI 248/2018 cannot be presented as an unresolved/prosecution-supported criminal track

**Unsafe formulation:** DI 248/2018 substantiated or remained open on the AC/CAM criminal allegations.

**Corrected formulation:** Fiscalía Provincial archived DI 248/2018 by decree dated 7-May-2019, concluding that the material then presented did not disclose a criminal offence and advising that the complainant could reiterate before a competent investigating judge under article 773 LECrim.

**Boundary:** archive is an adverse official assessment, but not an acquittal, judicial merits judgment or proof that every underlying factual allegation was false.

## OC-CORR-02 — The 24-Jan-2018 `intermediación/comisiones 3%` email is not proof of a commission paid to the AC

**Unsafe formulation:** a 3% commission to the AC is established by the email.

**Corrected formulation:** the Jan-2019 complaint raised a commission suspicion. The May-2019 Fiscalía decree recorded that the relied-on email contained an `intermediación/comisiones 3%` item in a credit-assignment calculation, while noting that the email did not mention the AC and that no recipient reply was supplied.

**Required proof before escalation:** native thread + closing/settlement statement + invoice/payment/accounting/beneficiary chain.

## OC-CORR-03 — Pink/Monterecco role must be periodized

**Unsafe formulation:** Pink Canary / Monterecco continuously had the same full possession, operator and maintenance role throughout 2012–2018.

**Corrected formulation:** Feb-2012 adverse-party litigation identifies Monterecco as a new exploitation company; Mar-2014 party material identifies Pink as the renamed Monterecco; a Sep-2018 complainant pleading states that after an AC-driven contract/litigation transition Pink delivered possession to LPB, CEXP was reactivated, and Pink thereafter remained a booking manager.

**Required method:** distinguish ownership, tourism title, contractual operator, bookings, revenues, staffing/maintenance and physical access for each period.
