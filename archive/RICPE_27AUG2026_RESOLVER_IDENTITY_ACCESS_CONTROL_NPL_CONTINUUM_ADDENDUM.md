## 2. Legal/governance significance

Under Spanish Law 2/2023, Article 8, the governing body appoints the physical person responsible for the internal information system, or a collegiate body that must delegate management powers to one member. Appointment and cessation are reportable to the competent independent informant-protection authority. Article 9 places diligent handling responsibility on the System Responsible Officer.

RICPE’s public ethics-channel page states that the channel is for reporting regulatory breaches and that its management is governed by confidentiality, data protection, information security, non-retaliation and presumption of innocence. RICPE also publicly describes a collective Compliance Unit. These public materials do not, on the evidence presently located, identify the formally appointed System Responsible Officer for this communication.

Therefore the immediate production question is not “who do we suspect?” but **“who was formally authorised, who actually touched this file, under what role, and what did each user do?”**
