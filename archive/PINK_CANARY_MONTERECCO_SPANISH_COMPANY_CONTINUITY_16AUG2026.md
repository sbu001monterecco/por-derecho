# PINK CANARY SERVICES / MONTERECCO SUN PARK — SPANISH COMPANY CONTINUITY
## Controlled identity correction — 16 August 2026

## 1. Controlling conclusion

**Monterecco Sun Park, S.L. and Pink Canary Services, S.L. are successive registered names of the same Spanish legal person, NIF B76564517.**

They are not two Spanish companies in a transfer or succession chain.

They must also be kept completely separate from the UK company:

| Jurisdiction | Earlier name | Later name | Stable identifier |
|---|---|---|---|
| United Kingdom | Monterecco Sun Park Limited | Aweswell Limited | company no. 07716847 |
| Spain | Monterecco Sun Park, S.L. / S.R.L. | Pink Canary Services, S.L. / S.L.U. | NIF B76564517; Registry sheet H TF 49739 |

The similarity of the former names does not merge the two legal persons.

## 2. Primary company-book evidence

**Classification: VERIFIED FACT — PRIMARY DOCUMENT, with date-status qualification.**

The digitally signed *Libro Registro de Socios PINK CANARY SERVICES SL 2012-2021* records:

- for the 2012 exercise: **MONTERECCO SUN PARK SL**, CIF **B76564517**;
- for the 2013 exercise: **PINK CANARY SERVICES SL**, CIF **B76564517**;
- the same sole shareholder and the same 100 participations; and
- an entry reading **“Cambio de denominación social” — 30/09/2013**.

This is strong primary evidence of corporate continuity and of the internal company-book date attributed to the name change.

The document was digitally signed on 1 October 2021. It is therefore a later formalised corporate book recording the earlier event; it is not, by itself, the original 2013 notarial deed or the Mercantile Registry inscription certificate.

## 3. Judicial confirmation

**Classification: VERIFIED FACT — PRIMARY JUDICIAL DOCUMENT.**

Sentencia 163/2023, in the classification section of Concurso 36/2012, states that LPB's 6-February-2012 operating lease was granted to:

> MONTERECCO SUN PARK, S.L. (actualmente denominada Pink Canary Services, S.L.)

The judgment therefore independently confirms that the later Pink Canary name belongs to the same Spanish company that contracted under the Monterecco Sun Park, S.L. name.

This continuity statement must be kept separate from the judgment's other contested or appealed findings.

## 6. Legal and evidential consequences

The name change did not, by itself:

- transfer the 6-February-2012 operating contract;
- create a new hotel operator;
- transfer customer, booking or operating rights to a successor company;
- change the NIF;
- create a new debtor, creditor or litigant;
- separate pre-change from post-change rights and obligations; or
- connect the Spanish company legally to the UK name change beyond the group relationships that must be proved separately.

Documents before the Spanish name change may properly use **Monterecco Sun Park, S.L.** Later documents may use **Pink Canary Services, S.L.** Both names must be normalised to NIF **B76564517** when building entity, contract, banking, tax, litigation and damages chronologies.

Do not state:

- “Pink Canary acquired Monterecco Sun Park, S.L.”;
- “Pink Canary succeeded Monterecco as hotel operator” merely because the name changed;
- “the operating contract was assigned from Monterecco to Pink Canary” without a separate assignment instrument;
- “Aweswell was renamed Pink Canary”;
- “Monterecco Sun Park Limited was renamed Pink Canary”; or
- that UK company no. 07716847 and Spanish company NIF B76564517 are the same company.
