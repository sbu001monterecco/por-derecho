## 2. The Meeting Point contradiction is materially self-damaging

A contemporaneous 17 January 2025 record sent to Romera attributes two incompatible positions:

- **Meeting Point side:** Meeting Point had **never commercialised the hotel**;
- **Laura/CAM side:** Meeting Point **had commercialised it** at some stage.

The attribution still requires authentication against each interlocutor's native message/audio and must not be published as an authenticated written admission.

However, the independent 2020 commercial sources materially change the significance of the contradiction:

- a preserved Meeting Point Hotels corporate brochure presents Meeting Point Hotels as founded by FTI Group, includes **Club Sei** among its brands and describes **Club Sei Lanzarote** as a 315-room hotel in Playa Blanca;
- a 27 January 2020 sonnenklar.TV capture offers **Club Sei Lanzarote at Calle Janubio 3**;
- a preserved historical Sun Park website capture identifies **Sun Park at Calle Janubio 3**.

**Controlled evidential effect:** Club Sei Lanzarote was publicly presented/distributed in 2020 at the same address as Sun Park within the FTI/Meeting Point commercial ecosystem. This materially corroborates the substance attributed to Laura/CAM in the limited sense of **public presentation/distribution**.

It does **not** yet establish:

- the exact Spanish contracting entity;
- legal authority over each unit;
- the precise inventory;
- consummated reservations or guest stays;
- commissions, invoices, revenues or payment flows;
- or the legality of the commercialisation.
