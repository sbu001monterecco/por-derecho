## 6. “The report against itself” — meaning and publication control

This phrase is **editorial shorthand only**. It is not a legal claim that a document can incriminate itself, nor a claim of self-incrimination by the insolvency administrator.

It means:

> the adverse 47-page insolvency-administrator report itself reproduces facts that qualify, weaken or contradict parts of its own accusatory framing.

The four principal same-source examples are:

1. **Cooperation:** the report reproduces Gil's 30-Jan-2018 response saying he was seeking the best way to cooperate, denying any intention to obstruct and answering requests item by item.
2. **Rescue / restructuring:** the report records Haya engagement, contemplated parent recapitalisation, convenio/viability work, a banking letter of intent, operator offers, waiting customers and agent/tour-operator contacts.
3. **Accounting already received:** it records PDF journals and trial balances for 2008–2011 and part of 2012. The dispute was not literally `accounting/no accounting` but legalisation, completeness and statutory sufficiency.
4. **No-allegation branches:** on grave false documents, fraudulent asset exits and simulated/fictitious patrimony, the AC itself records that nothing was known/alleged.

Radical transparency requires these same-source qualifications to sit beside the adverse allegations. It equally requires publication of the allegations that harmed Gil and the findings Sentencia 163/2023 retained.
