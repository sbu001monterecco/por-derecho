For every event this audit keeps separate:

1. whether a meeting occurred or is only later recited;
2. who called or purported to call it;
3. legal authority to convene;
4. notice/service and actual knowledge;
5. attendance, representation, quorum and voting entitlement;
6. proposal wording, actual vote and recorded resolution;
7. validity;
8. implementation or non-implementation;
9. circulation, withholding and later reliance;
10. knowledge;
11. intent;
12. causation;
13. benefit; and
14. any civil, criminal, administrative or tax characterization.

Occurrence or attendance does not prove authority, quorum, validity, intent or control. A recorded resolution does not prove implementation. Implementation does not by itself prove causation, benefit or legal characterization. Later receipt or reliance does not prove pre-meeting knowledge.
