# ONA / FINANCE CONDITIONS + STAGED SECURITY — SOURCE/POSITION CONTROL · 16 AUG 2026

**Status:** `PARTY POSITION + VERIFIED TRANSACTION-STRUCTURE EVIDENCE + OPEN PERFORMANCE LEDGER`

## Gil Marer / Aweswell position

`PARTY POSITION`

Gil Marer states that the 2018 commercial-exit financing conditions were capable of being met and that Aweswell/related transaction parties were able and prepared to deliver the required security package **in stages as the financing process advanced**, including interim security during the concursal/pre-exit phase and LPB property security after exit made that step available.

Gil further states that staged delivery of collateral/security in this type of financing process was normal transaction practice and should not be mischaracterised as evidence that the financing did not exist or could not close.

This position may be used publicly only with the source-status distinction below.

## What the recovered documents independently verify

`VERIFIED`

1. Cuatrecasas contemporaneous correspondence expressly described the pre-exit package as **`garantías provisionales`** while the mortgage was not yet available and said the package covered available value broadly.
2. The later executed **Binding Term Sheet** expressly separates:
   - `Security Package on closing`; and
   - `Security Package once the Company exits liquidation`.
3. That term sheet expressly includes an Aweswell undertaking to cause the LPB mortgage/security to be created after LPB exits liquidation/insolvency and includes other security at closing.
4. The later term sheet ties the financing architecture to due diligence, ECO valuation, the ONA operating agreement, business plan, evidence of creditor amounts, judicial/documentary evidence of the amount required to exit and formalisation of the relevant security.
5. The September correspondence records continued work on financing/security documentation after the executed term sheet was transmitted.

These facts independently establish that **staged security was part of the actual contemplated transaction architecture**.

## Public-safe formulation

> **The financing was structured to be secured in stages, not to remain unsecured until LPB left the insolvency process. Contemporary legal correspondence described provisional security over available value, and a later executed binding term sheet expressly separated security at closing from the LPB mortgage/security to be granted after exit. Gil Marer/Aweswell's position is that the conditions were capable of being met and the security package was to be delivered progressively as the transaction advanced. A route-by-route condition ledger is still being completed before claiming that every condition of every facility had formally been satisfied.**

## Why this matters

This distinction prevents two opposite errors:

- **understatement:** `no LPB mortgage yet = no finance / no security`; and
- **overstatement:** `executed term sheet / staged security = every condition satisfied and money unconditionally drawable`.

The controlled position is the commercially and evidentially stronger middle: a developed multi-route finance process with documented staged-security mechanics, plus a party position that the conditions were meetable and being worked through, subject to completion of the performance ledger.
