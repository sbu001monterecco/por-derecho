## Site-wide missed opportunity #1 — one common accountability grammar
Every institutional page should expose the distinction:

`RECIBIDO → EXAMINADO → VERIFICADO → DECIDIDO → ACTUACIÓN/CORRECCIÓN`

These are different evidential states. A document proving one stage must not be used silently to prove a later stage.

Public shorthand:
- VERIFIED / DOCUMENTED: primary-source supported proposition.
- ALLEGED: claim made in an identified source.
- INFERENCE: evidence-based proposition not compelled by the record.
- OPEN: an identifiable official record could still answer it.
- CORRECTED: prior proposition superseded by stronger evidence.

## Site-wide missed opportunity #2 — institutional competence before criticism
Every page should answer in its first screen or first scroll:
1. What is this body?
2. What can it do?
3. What can it not do?
4. Why did it matter here?
5. What single official record would materially answer the current question?

- AP4: `APPEAL → QUESTION → RECORD BEFORE CHAMBER → CORRECTIVE CAPACITY → CURRENT STATUS`.
- Cabildo: `OPERATING TOTALITY ≠ OWNERSHIP TOTALITY`.
- Yaiza: `ACTOR RECOGNISED → SOURCE OF AUTHORITY → UNIT PERIMETER → MATKATOR TREATMENT`.
- Intervención: `REMIT → REFERRAL → RECEIPT → CONTROL/NO CONTROL → OUTCOME`.
- SNCA: `ALERT → SOURCE CHECKED → RESULT → REFERRAL/ACTION`.

## CGPJ deep audit

### What already works
- Hero correctly distinguishes governance from appeal.
- DI 169/2026 → Alzada 286/2026 is visible.
- Strongest defence is explicit.
- LAJ boundary is correctly preserved.
- Five principal questions are finite and record-answerable.

### What was underused
1. The page named Promotor, Inspection, Permanent Commission, Disciplinary Commission and Plenary but did not visually assign question types to them.
2. The reader could not instantly see the difference between appeal/legal merits, judicial conduct, court functioning, LAJ/office matters and criminal investigation.
3. Appearance-of-impartiality material was described in prose but lacked the safer and more persuasive `concern → official record capable of testing it` visual.
4. It did not link official CGPJ institutional sources explaining Inspection / Permanent Commission practice.
5. The strongest public proposition was not visually isolated: `we do not ask the public to believe the allegation; we ask what was checked`.

### Five-question quality
Current five questions remain strong. Question 4 (Inspection/Permanent Commission) becomes materially stronger when the reader first understands that Inspection actually reports on dysfunction and court functioning and that the Permanent Commission handles Inspection-originated matters in ordinary CGPJ practice.

## Navigation audit
The flat "Who should answer what?" card wall is informative but does not communicate the system. It should evolve toward four families:
- JUSTICE / GOVERNANCE
- INVESTMENT / SUPERVISION
- PUBLIC MONEY / INTEGRITY
- LOCAL / TOURISM / PROPERTY
with private-actor/perimeter tracks presented separately from public institutions.

The central rule remains: `role → knowledge → response → unresolved question → primary evidence / current route`.

## Publication discipline
More restraint can increase pressure. Prefer questions that force one of three useful outcomes:
- YES + identify the record;
- NO + state why;
- OUTSIDE COMPETENCE + identify the competent body.

Do not use visual design to imply a finding stronger than the underlying source classification.
