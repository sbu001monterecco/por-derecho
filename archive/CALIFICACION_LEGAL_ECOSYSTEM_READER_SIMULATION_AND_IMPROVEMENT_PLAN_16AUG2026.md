# CALIFICACIÓN — LEGAL / JUDICIAL ECOSYSTEM READER SIMULATION + IMPROVEMENT PLAN

**Date:** 16 August 2026  
**Status:** `CANONICAL UX / CREDIBILITY / INSTITUTIONAL-READER IMPROVEMENT PLAN`

## Purpose

This memo incorporates the simulated read of the current Calificación page by likely professional audiences in **Las Palmas, the wider Canary Islands and Madrid**: Mercantile judges, Audiencia Provincial magistrates, Fiscal prosecutors, insolvency specialists, lawyers acting for adverse parties, Fiscalía Superior / specialist Fiscal readers, judicial-governance/CGPJ readers, Bar/professional-regulation lawyers, academics and nationally recognised insolvency/appellate practitioners.

This is an internal design and litigation-communication tool. It does **not** claim that any real person or institution has read the site or holds the simulated views below.

## Core assessment

The page has moved beyond a grievance narrative. Its present institutional strength is that it:

- openly discloses the materially adverse Sentencia 163/2023 and the live appeal boundary;
- distinguishes the AC, Fiscal and Judge as separate actors with separate acts and responsibilities;
- shows that parts of the AC/Fiscal package were rejected or narrowed rather than treating the accusation as a single validated block;
- is now supported by a source-complete 47-page AC crosswalk and serial allegation audits;
- places the adverse case beside a contemporaneous rescue / finance / operation / asset-protection / recovery counter-record;
- preserves adverse facts inside Allegation 01 and Allegation 02 rather than pretending every accusation was fabricated.

The principal remaining weakness is **reading order**: the most explosive mens-rea/criminal conclusions (`falsedad consciente`, `prevaricación`) appear before a sceptical institutional reader has personally worked through the strongest documentary architecture. That creates an avoidable risk that a judge, Fiscal or specialist lawyer mentally classifies the page as hostile advocacy before reaching the material that is hardest to dismiss.

## Simulated reader conclusions

### 1. Las Palmas — Mercantile judge
Likely first professional question: **what exact contrary material was actually in the court file and before Judge Alberto López Villarrubia when each disputed factual proposition was signed?**

Likely resistance: judicial-collegial defensiveness and the proposition that error / disagreement / rejection of some AC allegations does not itself prove knowing falsehood.

**Design implication:** evidence-before-Judge should become more visually important than the criminal label.

### 2. Las Palmas — Audiencia Provincial magistrate
Likely reflex: **the appeal is decided from the appellate record, not from a website.**

Likely concern: avoid any appearance that public communication is intended to pressure a pending appellate panel.

**Design implication:** place a prominent `APPEAL / NON-INTERFERENCE BOUNDARY` explaining that the page is a public documentary traceability record, not a substitute for or request to influence RPL 2523/2025.

### 3. Las Palmas — Fiscal prosecutor
The 12-Mar-2019 wording referring literally to insolvency aggravated by intent/fault of the `administrador concursal` will likely be treated first as a possible drafting error.

**Design implication:** frame this as a **finite textual-verification question**, not as self-proving criminal evidence: if error, what subject was intended; if not error, how is the wording reconciled with the persons later identified?

The DI248→calificación→DI248 archive sequence is institutionally stronger as a **circularity / anchoring / independent-review question** than as proof of personal coordination.

### 4. Las Palmas — insolvency specialist
Likely strongest reaction: the serial audit is becoming difficult to dismiss globally because it preserves adverse material and distinguishes narrow surviving points from rejected amplification.

**Design implication:** make the serial allegation methodology a first-class navigation device and publish the same template for every allegation:
`exact proposition → strongest adverse fact → same-source contradiction → court treatment → appeal status → actor knowledge → open evidence`.

### 5. Adverse-side lawyer
Likely attack surface:
- `knowing falsehood`;
- `prevaricación`;
- any personal-gain proposition;
- any statement that jumps from chronology/benefit to motive or procurement.

**Design implication:** separate **documentary proposition**, **knowledge proof**, **inference**, and **Gil Marer's allegation** visually and semantically so none can be mistaken for another.

### 6. Fiscalía Superior / senior Fiscal reader
Likely institutional question: whether different Fiscal acts inherited an adverse frame without sufficient independent re-testing after the procedural roles reversed.

**Design implication:** keep Mosteyrín / Herrera / Sánchez Ulled separated and add a concise `WHO DECIDED WHAT?` strip.

### 7. TSJC / judicial-governance / CGPJ reader
Likely question: which matters are appellate merits, which are court-functioning/disciplinary, and which are criminal allegations?

**Design implication:** add a four-lane jurisdiction box:
`APPEAL MERITS | JUDICIAL GOVERNANCE / INSPECTION | CRIMINAL ALLEGATION | RECOVERY / DAMAGES`.

### 8. Bar / professional-regulation lawyer
Likely credibility view: contradiction matrix is strong; subjective-knowledge / criminal-characterisation layer is the legal-risk point.

**Design implication:** force the progression:
`FACT → CONTRARY SOURCE → TRANSMISSION / KNOWLEDGE → LATER ACT → INFERENCE → ALLEGATION`.

### 9. Madrid — specialist Fiscal / CGPJ / national appellate reader
Likely preference: finite dossier over narrative volume.

**Design implication:** add a 90-second professional summary containing **five verified propositions, five finite questions, three current evidence gaps**.

### 10. National insolvency academic / specialist
Likely positive reaction: the claim-to-source / serial-audit method is recognisable as serious litigation analysis.

Likely criticism: too many legal questions compete on one page (concursal merits, Fiscal conduct, judicial knowledge, media use, recovery).

**Design implication:** turn the main page into a gateway with compact modules leading to tightly bounded sub-dossiers.

# Specific page improvements

## Priority 1 — Reorder the first screen for sceptical institutional readers

### Current risk
The accusation of knowing falsehood / prevaricación appears before the sceptical reader has traversed the documentary proof architecture.

### Proposed order
1. **Case status:** materially adverse first-instance Sentencia 163/2023; appealed; current verified AP status.
2. **What the judgment actually retained / rejected / narrowed.**
3. **Documentary counter-record:** rescue, finance, operation, preservation, financed-exit work.
4. **Serial allegation audit:** Allegations 01, 02, 03…
5. **Evidence-before-actor test.**
6. **Then: Gil Marer's accusation and requested accountability.**

### Why stronger
This does not weaken the accusation. It makes the accusation the **conclusion of a demonstrated chain** rather than the reader's first encounter with the case.

## Priority 2 — Add a `90-SECOND PROFESSIONAL READ`

A compact top-of-page institutional panel:

### Five verified propositions
- Sentencia 163/2023 is materially adverse and appealed.
- the 47-page AC report has been read in full and cross-walked;
- the judgment rejected or narrowed important AC/Fiscal propositions while retaining others;
- DI248 and the calificación are documentarily linked through Ministerio Fiscal acts;
- contemporaneous rescue / viability / finance / protection activity exists and must be tested against the accusation record.

### Five finite questions
- what contrary material was before the AC when each allegation was made?
- what contrary material was before Fiscal Mosteyrín?
- what contrary material was before Judge López Villarrubia when Sentencia 163/2023 was signed?
- what did the court expressly reject/narrow and why did other adverse findings survive?
- what is the current outcome/status in RPL 2523/2025?

### Three open evidence gaps
- complete evidence-before-actor transmission matrix;
- certified 25-Jul-2023 hearing/service material;
- complete/current appellate record and terminating resolution if any.

## Priority 3 — Build the `EVIDENCE BEFORE ACTOR` matrix

This is the highest-value credibility improvement.

Minimum columns:

| Disputed proposition | Contrary primary document | Date | Was it before AC? | Was it before Fiscal? | Was it before Judge? | Proof of transmission/receipt | Later act/statement | Knowledge grade | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use only graded statuses:
- `PROVED BEFORE ACTOR`;
- `STRONG TRANSMISSION INFERENCE`;
- `INSTITUTIONALLY AVAILABLE ONLY`;
- `NOT YET PROVED`.

Never upgrade institutional possession to personal knowledge.

## Priority 4 — Add `STRONGEST DEFENCE / WHAT REMAINS AFTER THAT DEFENCE`

For every actor and every high-risk allegation, show the strongest reasonable defence first.

Examples:

### Fiscal wording
**Strongest defence:** `administrador concursal` may be a drafting error.

**What remains after accepting that defence:** identify the intended subject and explain why the operative accusation/individualisation should still be regarded as reliable despite the facial textual defect.

### Judge
**Strongest defence:** disagreement, evidential assessment or legal error does not establish prevaricación.

**What remains after accepting that defence:** identify only propositions where contrary material was demonstrably before the Judge and the signed finding is alleged to be incompatible with that record.

### AC
**Strongest defence:** some delays / missing documentation / collection failures were real.

**What remains after accepting that defence:** whether a narrower adverse fact was expanded into a materially broader non-collaboration/causation narrative despite contrary facts within the AC's own administration and report.

This format materially raises rebuttal resistance.

## Priority 5 — Separate concursal merits from criminal characterisation

Create a visual box:

### `YOU DO NOT NEED TO ACCEPT THE CRIMINAL ALLEGATION TO TEST THE DOCUMENTARY PROBLEM`

Then separate:
1. **Concursal / appellate issue** — whether the factual/causal finding is sustainable.
2. **Institutional accountability issue** — whether contradictory evidence was properly examined.
3. **Knowledge issue** — whether the individual actor demonstrably knew the contrary fact.
4. **Criminal allegation** — Gil Marer's separate allegation that, where the knowledge element is proved, the act crossed into knowing injustice/falsehood.

This keeps the site forceful while reducing the easy rebuttal `he calls every adverse ruling corruption`.

## Priority 6 — Add an appellate non-interference boundary

Suggested concept:

> **RPL 2523/2025 is for the Audiencia Provincial to decide from the judicial record. This public page does not ask any magistrate to decide from the website or outside the record. Its purpose is documentary traceability, correction, public accountability and preservation of the questions that remain open while the first-instance judgment continues to have effects.**

This is particularly important for a Las Palmas / Canary judicial reader.

## Priority 7 — Reduce media material on the main Calificación route

Keep elEconomista as evidence of **downstream portability/effect** of Sentencia 163/2023, but move detailed procurement/instrumentalisation allegations into the dedicated media dossier.

On the Calificación page retain only:
- judgment travelled outside the insolvency proceeding;
- January-2025 documented consequence;
- link to separate source-controlled elEconomista dossier.

Reason: professional readers should not have the central concursal/judicial audit diluted by a separate media-intent dispute.

## Priority 8 — Add a `WHAT WOULD CHANGE OUR VIEW?` correction box

Invite specific rebuttal evidence:
- proof a disputed document was never delivered to a named actor;
- proof a cited rescue instrument was not executed or was ineffective;
- certified source correcting chronology;
- filed/service copy resolving Pink/Patricia notification conflict;
- appellate judgment changing status;
- evidence explaining textual or causal anomalies.

This materially improves fairness and makes public correction part of the site architecture rather than a disclaimer.

## Priority 9 — Introduce legal-status chips on every high-risk proposition

Use consistent labels:
- `DOCUMENTED FACT`;
- `FIRST-INSTANCE FINDING`;
- `APPEALED`;
- `REJECTED / NARROWED BY JUDGMENT`;
- `PARTY ALLEGATION`;
- `EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE`;
- `OPEN EVIDENCE`;
- `SUPERSEDED / CORRECTED`.

No high-risk sentence should require the reader to infer which category it belongs to.

## Priority 10 — Create professional verification paths instead of audience-targeted persuasion

Do not create tabs labelled `For Judges` / `For Prosecutors` in a way that could look like lobbying.

Use neutral verification paths:
- `WHAT THE AC ALLEGED`;
- `WHAT FISCAL SAID`;
- `WHAT THE JUDGE DECIDED`;
- `WHAT THE RECORD CONTAINED`;
- `WHAT THE APPEAL CHALLENGES`;
- `WHAT REMAINS OPEN`.

This serves each professional audience without appearing to target decision-makers personally.

## Priority 11 — Publish source anchors beside propositions

Where public-safe, each major proposition should have a compact source anchor:
`source type · date · author/organ · procedural location · status`.

Example:
`AC report · 11-Feb-2019 · Francisco de Borja Rodríguez-Batllori · 47 pp · complete report reviewed`.

The objective is to let a professional reader move from statement to source provenance in one step.

## Priority 12 — Keep the serial audit visually dominant

Allegation 01 and Allegation 02 are currently a major credibility asset because they preserve the strongest adverse fact while testing overstatement.

The page should include a compact serial-audit index showing:
- completed;
- current;
- next;
- result status.

Do not permit the more rhetorical accountability modules to visually overshadow the allegation-by-allegation work.

# Website-wide improvements triggered by this simulation

## A. Common actor-accountability grammar
All judge / AC / Fiscal / CGPJ pages should converge on:

`POWER / DUTY → VERIFIED KNOWLEDGE → ACT / OMISSION → IMPLEMENTATION → CONSEQUENCE → CAUSATION → REMEDY`

and for disputed propositions:

`STATEMENT → CONTRARY SOURCE → TRANSMISSION → KNOWLEDGE GRADE → LATER ACT → INFERENCE → ALLEGATION`.

## B. Common `strongest defence` component
Every accountability page should contain the strongest reasonable defence and what remains unresolved after accepting it.

## C. Common pending-proceeding boundary
Where an appeal/instruction/administrative review remains pending, make clear that the website is not a substitute for the competent record or decision-maker.

## D. Recovery-first cross-linking
Every accountability page should link the alleged act to the concrete recovery consequence:
- asset/right affected;
- income/fruits affected;
- legal/documentary effect continuing;
- remedy sought;
- quantum status.

## E. Mobile / first-three-minutes audit
Professional readers may never reach the lower half of a long page. The first mobile screens should communicate:
1. adverse judgment disclosed;
2. appeal status;
3. what is actually disputed;
4. why documentary re-examination is justified;
5. where the sources/audit are.

## F. Search / retrieval language
Retain high-recall names and proceeding numbers in metadata and internal search records, but public headings should prefer legal propositions over accusatory adjectives.

## G. Right-of-reply / correction architecture
Every high-risk actor page should expose a clear route for documentary correction and make corrections visibly versioned.
