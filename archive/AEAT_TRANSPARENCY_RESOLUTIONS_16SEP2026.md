## Current transparency-law position

The principal current authority remains **R CTBG 0072/2026 (26 January 2026)**. Its controlled relevance is that article 95 LGT is a partial/specific confidentiality regime rather than an automatic absolute bar; article 114 LGT does not by itself dispose of every LTAIBG access question; non-party status is not automatically decisive; and access/partial access must be analysed according to whether derived proceedings are pending, archived or completed and whether third-party tax data can be segregated.

The requests sought routing metadata, classifications, units, dates, remissions, protocols and administrative records and expressly accepted redaction/partial access. They did not request protected third-party returns or the merits of a pending inspection.
