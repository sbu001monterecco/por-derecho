## What the 16 January 2025 press release asked the newsroom to investigate

The press release framed a broad alleged systemic-fraud story. Its core journalistic propositions were:

- alleged unauthorised takeover / commercialisation of a Lanzarote hotel by CAM while the asset was under judicial/insolvency protection;
- alleged commercialisation links involving Meeting Point and FTI Touristik;
- an alleged layered-financing thesis of approximately €14.5m around the same hotel-reform project, described in the press note as c. €3.5m European funds, c. €6.5m RIC-related investment and c. €4.5m reform costs attributed through judicial proceedings;
- questions about whether FTI's Bavarian COVID support and Meeting Point's SEPI support had any relationship to the CAM hotel-project perimeter;
- allegations and political/institutional questions concerning the promotion and supervision of RIC investment structures during the COVID period;
- an assertion that the matter should be investigated as a public-funds / whistleblower / rule-of-law issue rather than reduced to a private dispute.

Publication rule: these were propositions put to journalists for investigation in January 2025. Do not reproduce the original press release's categorical verbs as established fact without independent source support. The press release is valuable to explain **what story elEconomista was considering**, not to prove that story.

## March 2025 Madrid querella — storyline use only unless independently corroborated

A querella dated 14 March 2025, prepared for filing/reparto in Madrid against Laura Patricia Acosta Matos, records the user's side's account of the January media episode. It alleges that:

- the planned elEconomista publication concerned alleged irregularities involving CAM;
- Romera received a call attributed to Laura Acosta Matos objecting to the proposed publication and asking that the outlet refrain from publishing;
- an email was then allegedly sent containing an unredacted/non-final insolvency-court order relating to Gil Marer / LPB;
- after receipt of that order, Romera decided not to proceed with the planned publication.

The querella itself is a party pleading, not a judicial finding. Its principal evidential value at this stage is chronological and source-completion oriented. It says that a copy of the relevant email and its attachments were filed as documentary exhibits and requests Javier Romera as a witness. Therefore the complete filed querella package — especially the referenced documentary exhibits — is a priority source target.

- That elEconomista had a legal or ethical obligation to publish.
- That the March 2025 querella establishes the underlying allegations; it is a pleading unless and until a judicial decision establishes otherwise.
