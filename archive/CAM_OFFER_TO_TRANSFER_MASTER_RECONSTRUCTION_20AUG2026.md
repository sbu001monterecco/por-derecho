## 1. Non-negotiable actor-separation rule

Every material event must be analysed through four separate layers:

> **CAM REQUEST / POSITION → AC RECOMMENDATION OR IMPLEMENTATION → JUDGE DECISION OR PROVED OMISSION → CONSEQUENCE**

Never collapse those layers into “the court/AC”. Specifically:

- a CAM proposal is not an AC conclusion;
- an AC recommendation is not a judicial finding;
- an AC deed/implementation step is not automatically attributable to the Judge;
- a Judge-signed order is attributable to the actual judicial act, but judicial silence is not a proved omission unless the issue was before the Judge, the Judge had a relevant power/duty, the controlled docket shows no responsive act, and a material consequence is evidenced;
- Registry/notarial acts are independent control nodes and must not be collapsed into CAM, AC or Judge conduct;
- later approval/cure does not retroactively authorise an earlier act already refused convalidation.

This rule is additive to the repository-wide unitary-case, source-status, contradiction/exculpatory and actor-specific criminal/forensic controls.

## 2. Evidential status legend

1. **VERIFIED PRIMARY FACT** — directly established by a controlled primary source.
2. **PARTY / ACTOR POSITION** — proposition stated by CAM, AC, LPB/Aweswell or another actor.
3. **EXPERT OPINION** — valuation/economic/professional conclusion.
4. **EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE** — inference expressly labelled and capable of being strengthened or defeated.
5. **UNRESOLVED / GAP** — material source or reconciliation still required.

No civil/registry/concursal defect is automatically a criminal offence. Criminal relevance must be tested actor-by-actor for source, knowledge, representation/omission, intent where required, causation, benefit/consequence and contrary explanation.

## 16. Criminal / forensic parallel — investigation hypotheses only

The current source chain justifies forensic testing of possible false-document/false-representation, procedural-fraud, disloyal-administration, auction/bidding and property-control theories **only as hypotheses**. No criminal conclusion follows merely from:

- an adverse Registry qualification;
- a later refusal of convalidation;
- a valuation dispute;
- a later cure route;
- an AC recommendation a party considers wrong;
- a judicial decision a party considers legally erroneous.

For any criminal theory identify the exact representation/omission, author, document, audience, knowledge/intent evidence, reliance, causal consequence, benefit and exculpatory explanation. Preserve lawful explanations and contrary acts, including the 16-Apr better-bid safeguard, 26-Jun suspension, 24-Oct-2019 non-convalidation and 2021 re-tender.
