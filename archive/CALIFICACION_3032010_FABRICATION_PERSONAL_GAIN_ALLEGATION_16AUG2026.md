# CALIFICACIÓN — €3,032,010.34 FABRICATION / PERSONAL-GAIN ALLEGATION

**Concurso Ordinario 36/2012 · Sentencia 163/2023 · 16 August 2026**  
**Status:** `PUBLIC FIRST-PERSON ALLEGATION / DOCUMENTED AMOUNT-PROVENANCE CHALLENGE / BENEFIT ROUTE OPEN`

## Purpose

Record Gil Marer's express instruction that the Por Derecho calificación dossier must state, in his own name, his allegation that **Francisco de Borja Rodríguez-Batllori, as insolvency administrator, and Judge Alberto López Villarrubia knowingly fabricated and/or falsified the €3,032,010.34 damages amount ultimately imposed on Gil, and did so for direct or indirect personal gain maintained clandestinely**.

This is a stronger allegation than the existing proposition that the AC constructed a materially false culpability narrative or that the Judge knowingly adopted materially false factual/causal findings. It must therefore remain grammatically attached to Gil Marer and must not be presented as an adjudicated criminal fact.

## 1. What is documented

### A. The AC sought the exact €3,032,010.34 amount

The complete 11-Feb-2019 AC calificación report and Gil Marer's 6-Jun-2019 opposition establish that the insolvency administrator sought **€3,032,010.34** in damages against Gil. Gil's 2019 opposition already attacked that amount as unjustified, disproportionate and lacking an explained causal basis.

### B. The contractual monthly rent stated in the record is €24,575

Sentencia 163/2023 records the agreed rent as **€24,575 per month** under the 6-Feb-2012 arrangement with Monterecco Sun Park / Pink Canary Services.

### C. A Pink/Patricia appeal expressly challenges a different AC rent figure

The 2023 Pink/Patricia appeal states that the AC was claiming **€2,733,923.64** and says that, for the 46 months from February 2012 to November 2015, €24,575 per month produces **€1,130,450**, before taking account of rents the appeal says were in fact paid.

This appeal arithmetic is a party argument, but it creates a concrete source-provenance problem: the controlled record currently contains at least three materially different figures that must be reconciled rather than silently treated as equivalent:

- contractual rent × the 46-month period identified on appeal: **€1,130,450**;
- AC amount challenged in the Pink appeal: **€2,733,923.64**;
- AC damages request and judgment award: **€3,032,010.34**.

### D. The Judge did not independently derive €3,032,010.34 in the damages paragraph

Sentencia 163/2023 reasons that, because execution against Pink found no attachable property, the damage is `correctamente cuantificado` by the **principal for which execution had been ordered and which the judgment says will never be collected**. It then orders Gil to pay **€3,032,010.34 plus legal interest increased by two points from the date of the judgment**.

The judgment therefore adopts the execution principal as the damages figure. The currently controlled judgment text does not, in that damages section, reconstruct month-by-month rent, payments, credits, offsets, maintenance obligations, interest, costs or the bridge from €24,575 per month to €3,032,010.34.

### E. Adjacent contrast: deficit liability was rejected for lack of justification

Immediately afterwards, the judgment rejects the separate deficit-liability request because the AC and Ministerio Fiscal merely sought it without the necessary justification. This does not by itself invalidate the damages award, but it makes the provenance and justification of the exact damages figure a particularly important audit question.
