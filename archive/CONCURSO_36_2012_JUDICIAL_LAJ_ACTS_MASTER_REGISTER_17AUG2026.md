## 1. Purpose

This register is the control layer for **all recoverable judicial and procedural acts** in Concurso 36/2012. It exists to prevent selective chronology and actor-blurring.

It must distinguish:

- acts personally signed by a Magistrado;
- acts signed by the LAJ;
- court-office / administrative notifications;
- party filings merely received by the court;
- acts of other courts or the Audiencia Provincial;
- later summaries/reconstructions.

The named-Judge accountability page must not be treated as complete until this register is materially populated and the selected public acts are checked against the wider recovered record.

## 2. Source hierarchy

1. Signed/certified judicial or LAJ source.
2. Court notification / LexNET / official copy.
3. Native attachment from counsel/procurador email.
4. Contemporaneous procedural index.
5. Historical discovery workbook.
6. Later party pleading or analytical reconstruction.

### Discovery spine

A historical workbook located in Google Drive, **`Guión - ProcConcursal - íntegro y cronológico.xlsx`**, contains a long row-by-row chronology with tomo/page references and explicit warnings where material is incomplete. It is useful as a retrieval map but is **not primary evidence**. Every row promoted to VERIFIED must be checked against the underlying act.

## 3. Stable ID rule

Stable IDs use:

- `C36-JUD-YYYY-MM-DD-NNN` — judicial act;
- `C36-LAJ-YYYY-MM-DD-NNN` — LAJ act;
- `C36-OFC-YYYY-MM-DD-NNN` — court-office / notification act;
- `C36-EXT-YYYY-MM-DD-NNN` — external court/AP act relevant to the chain.

Do not renumber an existing ID merely because interpretation changes.

## 6. Public-selection rule

Before an act is selected for the future Alberto López Villarrubia page, the register must record:

- exact binary/source;
- signatory;
- operative effect;
- what facts the act itself recites;
- what it does not decide;
- appeal/review status;
- later consequence;
- any materially favourable/neutral counterpart act.

## 7. Actor separation

`JUDICIAL DECISION → LAJ PROCEDURAL IMPLEMENTATION → NOTIFICATION/SERVICE → RESULT`

These stages must not be collapsed.

A LAJ act does not become an Alberto act merely because it occurred in Mercantil nº1. A signed judicial act does not cease to be attributable to its Magistrado merely because the LAJ later implemented it.
