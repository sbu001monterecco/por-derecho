Control date: 16 August 2026  
Status: **VERIFIED PROCEDURAL ROUTING / PRIVATE-SOURCE LOCATORS**

## Purpose
This control closes a narrower gap left by the earlier CGPJ chronology: several AGE/RedSARA presentations have separate official `notificaciones.060@correo.gob.es` state-transition emails recording that the originating registry sent the submission to the **Registro General del Consejo General del Poder Judicial (CGPJ)** for processing.

These notices establish a technical/registry routing event. They do **not** prove incorporation into Alzada 286/2026, substantive examination, acceptance, merits or truth of any allegation.

## Distinct procedural layers
Do not collapse the following concepts:

1. **AGE/RedSARA presentation / registration by the recurrent** — proves formal presentation at the electronic registry.
2. **060 state `ENVIADA A DESTINO`** — proves that the originating registry later recorded transmission to the named destination registry.
3. **CGPJ Recursos `entrada en el CGPJ`** — an independently worded CGPJ statement about entry into the Council.
4. **Joinder to Alzada 286/2026** — requires a CGPJ record tying the filing to the appellate expediente; this is positively proved for the 15 July filing.
5. **Substantive examination / reasoned treatment** — requires the administrative record, report or decision; receipt/routing alone does not prove it.
