## Controlling position

Project Sun Rock alleges, on a substantiated documentary basis, that the same Hotel Sun Park/MYND Yaiza economic project and materially overlapping assets, works, costs, value and employment outputs acquired multiple financial and institutional lives while the predominant identified property count remained within LPB’s Concurso 36/2012 and other integrated property remained extraconcursal.

Do not reduce that position to “several distinct layers converged.” The instruments may be legally distinct while the qualifying base overlaps. The mandatory question is whether the same property, work, invoice, cost, job, value, collateral or project representation was used more than once and whether every gate received a complete account of the other layers and the ownership/concurso position.

Do not convert the allegation into a competent finding of fraud, unlawful aid, double payment or criminal intent unless a primary decision does so.

## Mandatory unitary reconstruction

Every substantive pass must maintain or advance all seven ledgers:

1. **Asset/right ledger** — finca/CRU/cadastral reference, owner, concurso status, control, works, valuation, finance, support, current use/revenue.
2. **Funding ledger** — instrument, amount, beneficiary, purpose, property perimeter, costs, security, jobs, approvals, conditions, drawdowns/payments.
3. **Invoice/cost ledger** — supplier, work, property, amount, payer, funding claim, eligibility and duplication result.
4. **Employment ledger** — employee/FTE, employer, period, workplace, commitment(s), payroll and duplication result.
5. **Valuation ledger** — version, perimeter, assumptions, current/post-works value, capex, income, recipient and financing use.
6. **Disclosure ledger** — institution, documents received, title/concurso, other finance, grants, conflicts, project withdrawal and response.
7. **Control/waiver ledger** — prerequisite, checker, deficiency, escalation, authorised exception or control failure, decision and consequence.

A search that retrieves only one amount or one company is incomplete if it does not test how the result affects the other six ledgers.
