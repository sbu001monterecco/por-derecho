## Controlling position

Project Sun Rock alleges, on a substantiated documentary basis, that the same Hotel Sun Park/MYND Yaiza economic project and materially overlapping assets, works, costs, value and employment outputs acquired multiple financial and institutional lives while the predominant identified property count remained within LPB’s Concurso 36/2012 and other integrated property remained extraconcursal.

The 17-Aug-2026 CNMV/RICPE/Catastro ingest strengthens the verified anchors without converting the allegation into a finding. The record now directly controls: the 13-Jan-2021 CNMV warning; the CNMV 22-Jan-2026 statement that the 20-Jul-2021 RIC certification and 21-Jul-2021 CAM filing were not among the documents obtained through the described 2021 supervisory work; the 2022 BORME CAM→HNT Sun Park→MYND continuity; RPE's 2023 two-component MYND finance; the separate HNT/Yaiza regional-incentive figures; and the 4-Mar-2026 Catastro type-902/Matkator facts.

Do not reduce that position to “several distinct layers converged.” The instruments may be legally distinct while the qualifying base overlaps. The mandatory question is whether the same property, work, invoice, cost, job, value, collateral or project representation was used more than once and whether every gate received a complete account of the other layers and the ownership/concurso position.

Do not convert the allegation into a competent finding of fraud, unlawful aid, double payment or criminal intent unless a primary decision does so.

### CNMV supervisory-corpus boundary

Use the signed CNMV 22-Jan-2026 answer precisely. It establishes that the 20-Jul-2021 RIC/Enrique Guerra certification and CAM's 21-Jul-2021 judicial filing did **not appear among the documentation obtained through the supervisory actions described by CNMV** following the 13-Jan-2021 complaint.

It does not by itself prove CNMV inaction, concealment, negligence, intent, causal responsibility or what confidential supervisory conclusions were reached. Preserve the Article 233 secrecy boundary and retrieve actor/document-specific transmission evidence before moving from corpus gap to responsibility.

### 7-Jan-2025 OLAF/Justice alert boundary

The 7-Jan-2025 alert is **historically probative notice of the allegation**, not transaction-level proof of the FTI/Meeting Point rescue-money bridge. It alleged that FTI Bavarian COVID support, Meeting Point/SEPI rescue finance and RICPE/CAM finance formed a `triple dipping` structure around the same Sun Park asset/income.

Current repository control is stricter:

- do not state that FTI's public support financed Sun Park without a traced source-and-use chain;
- do not state that Meeting Point/SEPI rescue money financed Sun Park without a traced source-and-use chain;

This control does **not** dilute the separately substantiated same-asset allegation based on the current verified Sun Park-specific anchors: fragmented title/internal RICPE narrowing, later RICPE/HNT finance, regional incentive, FEDER identification and the same current operating hotel.

## Mandatory unitary reconstruction

Every substantive pass must maintain or advance all seven ledgers:

1. **Asset/right ledger** — finca/CRU/cadastral reference, owner, concurso status, control, works, valuation, finance, support, current use/revenue.
2. **Funding ledger** — instrument, amount, beneficiary, purpose, property perimeter, costs, security, jobs, approvals, conditions, drawdowns/payments.
3. **Invoice/cost ledger** — supplier, work, property, amount, payer, funding claim, eligibility and duplication result.
4. **Employment ledger** — employee/FTE, employer, period, workplace, commitment(s), payroll and duplication result.
5. **Valuation ledger** — version, perimeter, assumptions, current/post-works value, capex, income, recipient and financing use.
6. **Disclosure ledger** — institution, documents received, title/concurso, other finance, grants, conflicts, project withdrawal and response.
7. **Control/waiver ledger** — prerequisite, checker, deficiency, escalation, authorised exception or control failure, decision and consequence.

A search that retrieves only one amount or one company is incomplete if it does not test how the result affects the other six ledgers.
