## 1. Source-status rules

Use these categories:

- **VERIFIED CONNECTED-SOURCE METADATA/CONTENT** — the connector exposed the message/document and its substantive content.
- **PROVED TRANSMISSION** — metadata/source proves transmission to the identified address or formal route; it does not automatically prove reading or substantive consideration.
- **CONTEMPORANEOUS PROFESSIONAL ACCOUNT** — a professional recorded a conversation or event close in time; this is not the same as a direct statement by the other actor.
- **PARTY-REPORTED LEAD** — a party records what a third person reportedly said; it requires independent source completion before substantive reliance.
- **NO-RESULT SEARCH** — no responsive item was located in the search performed; this is not proof that no item exists.

Do not upgrade transmission into reading, institutional availability into personal knowledge, or a party-reported lead into a verified expert report.
