# MASTER EXECUTION PROMPT — CGPJ COMISIÓN PERMANENTE VISIT READINESS

**Date:** 18 August 2026  
**Project:** Por Derecho / Project Sun Rock  
**Purpose:** Prepare the public website and internal repository for rigorous review by the Comisión Permanente of the Consejo General del Poder Judicial, its support staff, the Servicio de Inspección, the Promotor de la Acción Disciplinaria, or any institutional reader examining DI 169/2026 / Recurso de Alzada 286/2026 and the judicial-governance perimeter of Concurso 36/2012.

## Governing principle

Prepare the site so that a reader may disagree with every allegation or inference and still find the site useful, fair, auditable and institutionally actionable.

Do **not** optimize for accusation. Optimize for:

1. provenance;
2. procedural chronology;
3. institutional competence;
4. finite verification;
5. adverse material shown prominently;
6. alternative explanations;
7. the exact primary document that would confirm, reject or materially narrow each proposition;
8. correction and right of reply.

Never imply that a CGPJ visit, review, meeting, endorsement or cooperation has occurred unless a primary source proves it. A page prepared for a possible institutional visit must say that preparation does not imply a scheduled or confirmed visit.

## Reader journey to implement

### 1. Start with the strongest institutional answer against our position

Before any allegation or theory, show:

- DI 169/2026 was archived on 14 May 2026;
- the Promotor maintained that archive on 10 July 2026;
- the Promotor's reasoning, as currently reconstructed from the primary record, treated the matters raised as outside disciplinary misconduct / within the jurisdictional sphere;
- the later appeal and supplements do not, merely by being filed or routed, prove that the archive was wrong;
- presentation, registry routing, incorporation into a file, examination, acceptance and truth are different states.

### 2. Separate institutional bodies and powers

Visually distinguish at minimum:

- Comisión Permanente;
- Servicio de Inspección;
- Promotor de la Acción Disciplinaria;
- Comisión Disciplinaria;
- Pleno;
- court / judge;
- LAJ / judicial office;
- Provincial Court / appellate route;
- Ministerio Fiscal and criminal-investigation routes where relevant.

Never treat “the CGPJ” as a single black box.

### 3. Build the two missing-lawful-bridge investigations

#### Bridge A — quantitative / creditor bridge

Reconstruct the party-corpus sequence commonly expressed as roughly **€9.052m → ~€13.166m → adjudication/finality**.

Do not publish the figures as judicially fixed facts unless the primary resolution proves them. Instead ask:

- What exact judicial act fixed principal?
- What exact act fixed interest, period and rate?
- What exact act fixed classification / enforceable amount?
- What was provisional, calculated by a party or administrator, or carried forward without a separate fixing act?
- Which figures were used for tender, set-off, adjudication or deed mechanics?
- What later act, if any, made each component final?

The deliverable is a document-to-number bridge, not an accusation.

#### Bridge B — 2019 non-validation to 2022 title

Reconstruct:

**24 October 2019 non-validation / lack of prior authorization → 2021 tender / sale process → 18 May 2021 definitive approval → two separate 15 October 2021 confirmation/challenge Autos → two 26 January 2022 clarification Autos / testimony-finality bridge (no new award) → 21 February 2022 deed → registration / external effects.**

For every step identify:

- application;
- evidence before the court;
- judicial order;
- LAJ / office act;
- administrator act;
- deed/testimony;
- registry effect;
- unresolved contradiction;
- exact document needed to reconcile it.

### 4. Apply a public source-status grammar

Every material proposition must be visibly classifiable as one of:

- **A — primary institutional record**;
- **B — registered party filing / primary party document**;
- **C — contemporaneous correspondence / witness material**;
- **D — inference / allegation / open verification question**.

Do not convert C or D material into A-level findings by repetition or typography.

### 5. Appearance-of-impartiality material

Keep any LPAM / access / contact / abstention or recusal-related material as a separate verification module.

Public wording must preserve that the material does **not**, without more, establish:

- intimate friendship;
- influence;
- partiality;
- corruption;
- disciplinary responsibility;
- criminal responsibility.

Ask instead whether finite official records exist that can confirm, reject or materially narrow the allegations and whether the competent body considered them.

### 6. Build the “Comisión Permanente reader room”

Create a bilingual ES/EN direct-access page, suitable for a time-constrained institutional reader, containing:

- a 60-second orientation;
- the adverse CGPJ decisions first;
- a competence map;
- the two missing-lawful-bridge investigations;
- a “known / disputed / missing / document that resolves it” matrix;
- a short list of finite verification requests;
- strongest reasonable alternative explanations;
- right of reply / correction protocol;
- direct links into the existing CGPJ, judge/court, LAJ, institutional accountability, calificación, 7 June 2018 and public-authority reconstruction pages.

Do not place this page as a sensational homepage feature. It should be a high-quality direct institutional entry point and may be linked from existing CGPJ/judicial-governance pages.

### 7. Current institutional context

Before publishing names or composition of the Comisión Permanente, verify against the current official CGPJ source. Record the date of the official composition and its effective date. Do not infer individual members' views, responsibility or prior knowledge from membership.

### 8. Publication safety

Before merge / publication:

- run the repository's publication-integrity and relevant reader-journey checks where available;
- verify ES/EN parity;
- verify canonical / hreflang links;
- verify no statement turns an allegation into a finding;
- verify all adverse decisions remain visible;
- verify no unredacted personal data or confidential material has been introduced;
- verify institutional logos are identification only and do not imply endorsement;
- verify the page expressly states that preparation for a possible institutional review does not imply a scheduled or confirmed visit.

## Success test

A member of the Comisión Permanente should be able to answer, within minutes:

1. What exactly is being asked of the CGPJ rather than the appellate court?
2. What has the CGPJ already rejected or archived?
3. What later material was filed and what is its verified procedural status?
4. Which points concern discipline, inspection/functioning, LAJ/office acts, appeal merits or another authority?
5. What are the two missing documentary bridges?
6. What finite official records would resolve the remaining questions?
7. What is the strongest innocent / lawful alternative explanation?
8. How can the CGPJ correct the public record if Por Derecho has something wrong?

The objective is **radical transparency of method with strict evidential restraint**.
