# Extraconcursal and ongoing harm audit — Concurso 36/2012

Updated: 15 August 2026

## Controlling proposition

The consequences under examination are not confined to historic effects inside Concurso 36/2012. The audit must distinguish the insolvency estate and rights of Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. (LPB) from rights, assets, corporate interests, economic positions and causes of action belonging to Matkator S.L., Aweswell Limited and other third parties.

The existence, legal basis, causation and quantum of each item of extraconcursal harm remain matters for evidence-led verification. This register does not assume that every alleged loss was caused by a judicial-office act, the insolvency administrator, CAM, RICPE or any other actor.

## Why the harm is potentially ongoing

The relevant consequences did not necessarily end with the 2018 transaction, the 2019 non-convalidation, the 2022 adjudication/dación, the end of the Arrecife enforcement in 2023 or any later corporate restructuring. The audit must test whether present-day title, possession, access, exploitation, income, financing, public incentives, works, community governance, restrictions, registry positions or representations continue to affect rights that did not belong to LPB's insolvency estate.

Accordingly, the correct temporal frame is:

`originating act → documentary/judicial implementation → external use → downstream transaction or representation → present legal/economic position → continuing or remediable harm`.

## Persons and interests requiring separate treatment

### Matkator S.L.

Audit independently from LPB:

- ownership and registry position of Matkator fincas and any physical or functional incorporation into the hotel project;
- access, possession, use, works, demolition, alteration or exclusion affecting Matkator property or rights;
- community charges, voting, banking, operating arrangements or other obligations attributed to Matkator;
- any representation to notaries, registries, investors, lenders, RIC/REF bodies, regional-incentive authorities, tourism/municipal bodies or operators that treated Matkator property as available to the project;
- loss of use, rental/exploitation value, financing opportunity, development value, restitution rights, costs and other continuing patrimonial consequences.

### Aweswell Limited

Audit independently as shareholder/investor and holder of its own rights:

- impairment of shareholding value and control in LPB and Matkator;
- loss of opportunity to recapitalise, refinance, conclude or exit the insolvency process through alternative financed solutions;
- interference with shareholder information, governance, litigation or recovery rights;
- costs of protecting subsidiaries and assets, including professional, financing and enforcement costs where legally recoverable;
- continuing diminution or diversion of economic value traceable to present exploitation or use of assets/right positions;
- any direct causes of action belonging to Aweswell rather than LPB.

### Other third-party interests

Create a third-party schedule for every owner, creditor, community participant, contractual counterparty, investor or other person whose rights may have been treated as part of the Sun Park project without belonging to LPB's estate.

For each third party record:

`identity | right/asset | source of title | whether inside LPB estate | act affecting it | external representation/use | current status | economic effect | remedy/authority | evidence status`.

## LAJ / judicial-office relevance

The audit must determine whether any testimonio, DIOR, decree, certification, mandamiento, expression of finality or other judicial-office instrument was used outside Concurso 36/2012 in a manner that:

1. represented a broader title, authority, availability or asset perimeter than the underlying judicial act actually established;
2. was relied upon in notarial or registry acts;
3. supported later corporate, investment, financing, public-incentive, tax, tourism or operating representations;
4. affected rights of Matkator, Aweswell or other third parties; or
5. remained uncorrected or unneutralised after a later judicial decision altered or rejected the underlying premise.

No personal knowledge or intent is attributed to any LAJ merely from downstream use. Knowledge, purpose, foreseeability, later notice and corrective response must be proved separately act by act.

## Public-funds and incentive interface

The audit should cross-check any title/availability representation against the full public-finance perimeter, including where applicable:

- RIC/REF tax-incentive treatment and AEAT/Government of the Canary Islands files;
- Spanish regional incentives and the GC/836/P06 project file already identified in the evidential corpus;
- any EU co-financing or other Union financial-interest component;
- grants, eligible investment, employment commitments, guarantees, loans and other public support;
- the exact beneficiary, funded asset perimeter, title/availability representations, eligible expenditure and source-of-funds reconciliation.

The existence of public aid does not prove misuse. The purpose is to establish whether the asset/title perimeter represented to the granting or control authority accurately excluded property and rights outside LPB/CAM/HNT as appropriate.

## Ongoing-harm matrix

For every alleged current harm create one row:

`affected person | right/asset | originating event | documentary bridge | current actor/beneficiary | current use or restriction | recurring income/value effect | public/private financing linked | present remedy | limitation/prescription issue | primary evidence | status`.

Minimum categories to test:

- continued possession or use;
- inability to access or exploit property;
- title/registry impairment;
- physical works or irreversible alterations;
- income/fruits generated from disputed perimeter;
- hotel operating revenues attributable to disputed assets or rights;
- financing or capital value supported by disputed perimeter;
- community charges, votes or governance consequences;
- public incentives or eligible-investment value attributable to disputed perimeter;
- lost financing, refinancing, sale or development opportunities;
- professional and enforcement costs;
- restitution, reintegration, rectification, damages and accounting claims;
- reputational/investor consequences only where legally and evidentially supportable.

## Investigative objective

The objective is not to relitigate every issue inside Concurso 36/2012. It is to identify the boundary of the insolvency estate and then audit every material consequence that crossed that boundary.

For each consequence answer:

1. Whose right or asset was affected?
2. Was it legally inside LPB's insolvency estate?
3. What exact act purported to affect it?
4. What judicial-office or other documentary instrument enabled external reliance?
5. Who relied on it and for what purpose?
6. What economic or legal result followed?
7. Does that result continue today?
8. Who currently receives the corresponding use, control, income, financing benefit or legal advantage?
9. What corrective, restitutionary, accounting, registry, administrative or damages remedy remains available?
10. What primary evidence is still missing?

## Publication discipline

Use "ongoing alleged extraconcursal harm" or "ongoing consequences under audit" until a primary document establishes the particular right, causation and present effect. Do not describe all current hotel value, revenue, financing or public aid as damage to Matkator/Aweswell merely because the wider project is disputed.

Equally, do not describe the issue as purely historical merely because the originating judicial or notarial act occurred in 2018 or 2022. Present possession, use, financing, exploitation, public support, registry effects and deprivation of third-party rights must be tested as current facts.
