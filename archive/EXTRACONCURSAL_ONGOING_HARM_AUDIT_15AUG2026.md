# Concursal, extraconcursal and cross-border ongoing harm audit — Concurso 36/2012

Updated: 15 August 2026

## Controlling proposition

The consequences under examination must be analysed on three simultaneous and potentially overlapping planes:

1. **Concursal harm** — harm to Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. (LPB), its insolvency estate, creditors, liquidation/restructuring options, income, causes of action and ability to exit or conclude Concurso 36/2012.
2. **Extraconcursal harm** — harm to rights, assets, corporate interests, economic positions and causes of action that belong to Matkator S.L., Aweswell Limited and other third parties and were never part of LPB's insolvency estate merely because they were connected physically, economically or operationally with Sun Park.
3. **Cross-border / transnational harm** — harm whose affected owner, investor, capital source, financing relationship, payment flow, professional cost, enforcement route, tax consequence, regulatory interface or remedy crosses the Spanish border, including the direct position of Aweswell Limited as a foreign investor/shareholder and any other foreign investor, lender or rights-holder identified by the evidence.

These planes must be investigated alongside one another. A concursal act may produce both legitimate concursal consequences and separate extraconcursal or cross-border consequences. Conversely, a cross-border consequence is not automatically unlawful merely because a foreign investor is affected.

The existence, legal basis, causation and quantum of every alleged harm remain matters for evidence-led verification. This register does not assume that every alleged loss was caused by a judicial-office act, the insolvency administrator, CAM, RICPE or any other actor.

## Why the harm is potentially ongoing

The relevant consequences did not necessarily end with the 2018 transaction, the 2019 non-convalidation, the 2022 adjudication/dación, the end of the Arrecife enforcement in 2023 or any later corporate restructuring. The audit must test whether present-day title, possession, access, exploitation, income, financing, public incentives, works, community governance, restrictions, registry positions or representations continue to affect LPB itself and/or rights that did not belong to LPB's insolvency estate.

Accordingly, the correct temporal frame is:

`originating act → documentary/judicial implementation → external use → downstream transaction or representation → present legal/economic position → continuing or remediable harm`.

## Three-plane harm map

For every material event create three parallel columns:

`concursal effect | extraconcursal effect | cross-border effect`.

Do not assume that one column excludes another.

Examples to test include:

- loss or diversion of LPB estate value alongside separate impairment of Matkator assets;
- impairment of LPB restructuring/refinancing options alongside direct loss to Aweswell as foreign shareholder/investor;
- current exploitation or income generated from an asset perimeter that may include LPB, Matkator and third-party rights;
- Spanish judicial/notarial/registry acts later relied upon in foreign investment, financing, accounting, tax, compliance or recovery decisions;
- professional, funding and enforcement costs incurred outside Spain as a direct consequence of Spanish proceedings or asset-control events;
- present inability of a foreign investor or rights-holder to exercise, monetise, finance, recover or protect rights linked to the Spanish assets.

## Persons and interests requiring separate treatment

### LPB — concursal plane

Audit independently:

- loss of estate value, income or realisation value;
- impairment of alternative restructuring, refinancing, settlement or exit options;
- transfers, payments, credit calculations, adjudication consideration and liquidation-accounting consequences;
- estate causes of action, restitution/reintegration rights and damages claims;
- current benefit derived by third parties from assets or value originating in the LPB estate;
- whether historic acts continue to constrain the present ability to conclude or reverse the effects of the concurso.

### Matkator S.L. — extraconcursal plane

Audit independently from LPB:

- ownership and registry position of Matkator fincas and any physical or functional incorporation into the hotel project;
- access, possession, use, works, demolition, alteration or exclusion affecting Matkator property or rights;
- community charges, voting, banking, operating arrangements or other obligations attributed to Matkator;
- any representation to notaries, registries, investors, lenders, RIC/REF bodies, regional-incentive authorities, tourism/municipal bodies or operators that treated Matkator property as available to the project;
- loss of use, rental/exploitation value, financing opportunity, development value, restitution rights, costs and other continuing patrimonial consequences.

### Aweswell Limited — extraconcursal and cross-border plane

Audit independently as foreign shareholder/investor and holder of its own rights:

- impairment of shareholding value and control in LPB and Matkator;
- loss of opportunity to recapitalise, refinance, conclude or exit the insolvency process through alternative financed solutions;
- interference with shareholder information, governance, litigation or recovery rights;
- costs of protecting subsidiaries and assets, including professional, financing and enforcement costs where legally recoverable;
- continuing diminution or diversion of economic value traceable to present exploitation or use of assets/right positions;
- any direct causes of action belonging to Aweswell rather than LPB;
- capital injected from outside Spain, foreign-currency or cross-border funding consequences, and any impaired recovery of such capital;
- foreign professional, accounting, tax, financing or enforcement consequences generated by the Spanish events;
- any cross-border recognition, enforcement, insolvency, investment-protection or jurisdictional question that becomes relevant on the evidence.

### Other third-party interests

Create a third-party schedule for every owner, creditor, community participant, contractual counterparty, investor, lender or other person whose rights may have been treated as part of the Sun Park project without belonging to LPB's estate.

For each third party record:

`identity | jurisdiction/country | right/asset | source of title | whether inside LPB estate | act affecting it | external representation/use | current status | economic effect | remedy/authority | evidence status`.

## Cross-border harm track

The audit must not treat the matter as territorially exhausted merely because the principal assets and proceedings are in Spain.

For every material event, test whether consequences crossed borders through:

- foreign ownership or shareholder rights;
- inbound investment or shareholder funding;
- outbound loss, stranded capital or impaired recovery;
- foreign lenders, advisers, insurers, accountants, counterparties or investors;
- cross-border payments, financing, security, guarantees or tax treatment;
- foreign litigation, enforcement, recognition or insolvency consequences;
- regulatory disclosures or representations to non-Spanish recipients;
- reputational/investor consequences only where specific, documented and legally supportable;
- EU-law or EU-financial-interest questions where an actual Union nexus exists, without conflating those with the separate UK/foreign-investor dimension.

For each cross-border consequence record:

`affected person/entity | home jurisdiction | Spanish originating act | documentary bridge | cross-border transmission/reliance | foreign consequence | current status | monetary/non-monetary effect | available forum/remedy | evidence`.

## LAJ / judicial-office relevance

The audit must determine whether any testimonio, DIOR, decree, certification, mandamiento, expression of finality or other judicial-office instrument was used inside or outside Concurso 36/2012 in a manner that:

1. produced or contributed to a material concursal consequence for LPB;
2. represented a broader title, authority, availability or asset perimeter than the underlying judicial act actually established;
3. was relied upon in notarial or registry acts;
4. supported later corporate, investment, financing, public-incentive, tax, tourism or operating representations;
5. affected rights of Matkator, Aweswell or other third parties;
6. travelled into or influenced a cross-border investment, financing, accounting, regulatory or recovery context; or
7. remained uncorrected or unneutralised after a later judicial decision altered or rejected the underlying premise.

No personal knowledge or intent is attributed to any LAJ merely from downstream use. Knowledge, purpose, foreseeability, later notice and corrective response must be proved separately act by act.

## Public-funds and incentive interface

The audit should cross-check any title/availability representation against the full public-finance perimeter, including where applicable:

- RIC/REF tax-incentive treatment and AEAT/Government of the Canary Islands files;
- Spanish regional incentives and the GC/836/P06 project file already identified in the evidential corpus;
- any EU co-financing or other Union financial-interest component;
- grants, eligible investment, employment commitments, guarantees, loans and other public support;
- the exact beneficiary, funded asset perimeter, title/availability representations, eligible expenditure and source-of-funds reconciliation.

The existence of public aid does not prove misuse. The purpose is to establish whether the asset/title perimeter represented to the granting or control authority accurately excluded property and rights outside LPB/CAM/HNT as appropriate.

## Ongoing-harm matrix

For every alleged current harm create one row:

`harm plane (concursal/extraconcursal/cross-border) | affected person | jurisdiction | right/asset | originating event | documentary bridge | current actor/beneficiary | current use or restriction | recurring income/value effect | public/private financing linked | present remedy | limitation/prescription issue | primary evidence | status`.

Minimum categories to test:

- continued possession or use;
- inability to access or exploit property;
- title/registry impairment;
- physical works or irreversible alterations;
- income/fruits generated from disputed perimeter;
- hotel operating revenues attributable to disputed assets or rights;
- financing or capital value supported by disputed perimeter;
- community charges, votes or governance consequences;
- public incentives or eligible-investment value attributable to disputed perimeter;
- lost financing, refinancing, sale or development opportunities;
- stranded or impaired foreign capital and shareholder funding;
- foreign professional, accounting, tax and enforcement costs;
- professional and enforcement costs in Spain where legally recoverable;
- restitution, reintegration, rectification, damages and accounting claims;
- reputational/investor consequences only where legally and evidentially supportable.

## Investigative objective

The objective is not to relitigate every issue inside Concurso 36/2012. It is to establish the complete consequence map of the concurso and then identify every material consequence that either remained within the insolvency estate, crossed the estate boundary, crossed a national border, or did more than one of those things simultaneously.

For each consequence answer:

1. Whose right or asset was affected?
2. Was it legally inside LPB's insolvency estate?
3. Did the consequence operate inside the concurso, outside it, cross-border, or on several planes at once?
4. What exact act purported to affect it?
5. What judicial-office or other documentary instrument enabled reliance?
6. Who relied on it and for what purpose?
7. What economic or legal result followed?
8. Does that result continue today?
9. Who currently receives the corresponding use, control, income, financing benefit or legal advantage?
10. What corrective, restitutionary, accounting, registry, administrative, cross-border or damages remedy remains available?
11. What primary evidence is still missing?

## Publication discipline

Use "ongoing alleged harm", "ongoing concursal and extraconcursal consequences under audit" and "cross-border consequences under audit" until primary evidence establishes the particular right, causation and present effect. Do not describe all current hotel value, revenue, financing or public aid as damage to LPB, Matkator, Aweswell or third parties merely because the wider project is disputed.

Equally, do not describe the issue as purely historical or purely concursal merely because the originating judicial or notarial act occurred in 2018 or 2022. Present possession, use, financing, exploitation, public support, registry effects, foreign-investor consequences and deprivation of third-party rights must be tested as current facts.
