## 1. Mission

Plan, draft, validate and prepare a **CNMV-specific** communication concerning RICPE / Sun Park / MYND Yaiza.

Do not merely resend or summarise the 17 August 2026 RICPE letter.

Treat that RICPE communication as:

- a document already presented to the supervised entity;
- evidence of the questions and preservation requests put to RICPE;
- a potential annex;
- not a substitute for a concise supervisory communication addressed to CNMV.

The CNMV communication should answer:

- why CNMV is receiving this now;
- what CNMV already received or later stated;
- what is genuinely new;
- what RICPE documented internally on 20 July 2021;
- what happened on 21 July 2021;
- what changed before later re-entry and financing;
- what CNMV can reasonably verify within its competence;
- what belongs to other authorities;
- and what finite action or reconciliation is requested.

## 4. Central supervisory question

Use one controlling question:

> **What changed between the position documented within RICPE on 20 July 2021 — fragmented ownership, future/conditional CAM acquisition, unsigned LOI and full due diligence not started — and the later re-entry, financing and implementation of the Sun Park / MYND Yaiza project?**

Break it into finite questions:

1. Who introduced and sponsored the project?
2. What related-party and conflict record existed?
3. What was represented in 2019–2020?
4. What was independently verified before those representations?
5. Who produced, approved, received and used the 20 July certification?
6. Why was the project withdrawn or conditioned?
7. What new fact, document, condition or waiver allowed re-entry?
8. What title and practical availability were verified at each date?
9. What valuation and downside supported each decision?

13. What works, assets and employment supported each component?
14. What information or correction to investors was considered when the project premise changed?
15. What records have been preserved and who controls them?

## 5. Competence map

### Within a reasonable CNMV / RICPE supervisory perimeter

- project and investor information;
- governance and internal controls;
- related parties and conflicts;
- abstentions and information access;
- due diligence;
- valuation;
- conditions and waivers;
- project withdrawal and re-entry;
- investment/financing approval;
- monitoring;
- preservation;
- correction or disclosure;
- reconciliation with earlier supervisory work.

### Civil / registry / Community / insolvency context

Use only to test the premises entering the regulated project.

Do not ask CNMV to decide title, invalidate minutes or substitute for the insolvency court.

### Tax / RIC / Regional Incentives / FEDER

Use only where necessary to reconcile project description, assets, costs, financing, employment or disclosure.

Do not ask CNMV to decide fiscal materialisation, final grant eligibility or EU-funds recovery.

### Criminal matters

Do not turn the CNMV communication into a general criminal complaint.

If other criminal proceedings are mentioned, identify only their status and relevance. Do not ask CNMV to determine guilt or intent.

## 10. Confidentiality and informant protection

Assess whether and how to request:

- confidential treatment;
- protection of personal data;
- preservation;
- non-retaliation;
- treatment as an informant under the applicable legal framework.

Keep protection status separate from the merits of the supervisory information.

Do not publish or attach without necessity:

- RICPE Channel access codes or credentials;
- private verification codes;
- certificate identifiers;
- signature images;
- privileged legal advice;
- personal information unrelated to the supervisory issue.

## 11. Adversarial test

Construct the strongest RICPE defence:

- 2020 material was preliminary;
- July 2021 shows controls worked;
- the project was withdrawn appropriately;
- later title resolved the condition;
- financing occurred only after sufficient evidence;
- HNT was a legitimate vehicle;
- financing and public-support costs were distinct;
- valuation and conflicts were properly handled;
- CNMV already examined the relevant issues.

For each defence produce:

`DEFENCE → DOCUMENT THAT WOULD CONFIRM IT → DOCUMENT THAT WOULD WEAKEN IT → CURRENT STATUS → SHOULD CNMV/RICPE BE ASKED?`

The communication must remain useful even if several defences are correct.

## 12. Language control

Use:

- `requires reconciliation`;
- `apparently incompatible`;
- `no documentary explanation has yet been located`;
- `question for verification`;
- `possible material discrepancy`;
- `must be determined`;
- `subject to documentary alternative explanation`.

Avoid unnecessary conclusions such as fraud, conspiracy, concealment, money laundering, corruption or duplicate funding unless clearly attributed as an allegation and indispensable to identify another proceeding.

Maximum documentary pressure; minimum rhetorical inflation.

## 15. Stop rules

Stop before filing if:

- the CNMV draft contradicts the RICPE letter;
- a material amount or date lacks a source;
- a draft is being represented as previously filed;
- the exact attachment version is uncertain and not disclosed;
- private Channel credentials appear;
- privileged material is included without need;
- an inference is presented as fact;
- a criminal allegation is presented as a finding;
- the asks materially exceed CNMV competence;
- the current official filing route has not been verified;
- or the website states that CNMV received the 2026 communication before a receipt exists.

## Final principle

> **Do not send CNMV the whole case as an undifferentiated complaint. Give CNMV the prior supervisory history, the July-2021 documentary hinge, the finite “WHAT CHANGED?” question, the new RICPE communication and a minimum sufficient bundle.**
