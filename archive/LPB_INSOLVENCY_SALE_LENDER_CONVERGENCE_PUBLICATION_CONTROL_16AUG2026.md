## Controlling propositions

### Debtor / estate boundary

**VERIFIED / CONTROLLED:** Concurso 36/2012 concerned **Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. (LPB)** and its estate. It did not automatically place the whole Sun Park complex, CEXP, Monterecco/Pink, Matkator, Aweswell or other owners into the insolvency proceeding.

### Auction correction

**CONTRADICTED / CORRECTED:** the primary 15-May-2012 Arrecife diligence schedules **158 properties**, not 166. Preserve 159 and 166 only as separate measurements/layers pending a property-by-property reconciliation.

This is controlled by CR-024 and ME-052.

### 2012 insolvency test

The June 2012 declaration recorded approximately **€21.603m assets** and **€10.186m liabilities**. That asset surplus does not, by itself, establish that LPB could not legally be insolvent. Under the historical Ley Concursal framework, actual insolvency concerned regular ability to meet due obligations.

The public page therefore does **not** claim the declaration order was legally wrong merely because assets exceeded liabilities.

### Filing chronology / causation boundary

The controlled record supports:

- 28-Feb-2012 viability/restructuring plan, including Summers Villages, Sun Park Living and a proposed tour-operator lane;
- 5-Mar-2012 separate global-position and cure figures, which must not be collapsed;
- 15-May-2012 auction diligence with 158 properties and 22-Jun-2012 auction date;
- early-June filing sequence, with the declaration before the auction.

**EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE / BOUNDED CONCLUSION:** the enforcement/auction was the immediate timing trigger for the defensive insolvency filing.

Do not convert that into proof that the bank caused every element of the crisis, that the filing was unnecessary, or that professional advice was negligent. Those remain source-dependent and privilege-sensitive.

### Operating / direct-market platform

The 28-Feb-2012 viability plan establishes that direct-market and tour-operator architecture existed before the insolvency proceeding. It therefore supersedes any narrative that direct distribution arose only after the filing.

Operator-specific performance, loss and causation remain subject to ME-056.

### Creditor chain vs product-liability lane

Keep separate:

1. the mortgage-credit ownership / insolvency-accounting / assignment / enforcement lane; and
2. the disputed banking-product / swap / pledge / second-mortgage / Valencia liability lane.

A discrepancy between the two accounting systems is not automatically proof of overstatement or wrongdoing. The Valencia claim is pending/contested and must not be presented as decided.

### Later title / earlier control

Later adjudication, dación, Registry title or satisfaction does not retrospectively authorise every earlier act of access, control, commercialisation or exploitation. Conversely, earlier disputes do not by themselves invalidate later title, finance or operation.

## Convergence page function

The sale-lender convergence page is a map of **temporal and evidential intersection**, not a finding of conspiracy or joint liability. It keeps property, credit, operation and insolvency as distinct legal/economic perimeters and then asks where decisions concerning the same hotel platform interacted.

Its downstream RICPE/RIC/regional-incentive/FEDER references use the repository's serial-reliance rule: later gatekeepers should identify what they independently checked, while later support is not treated as validation of every upstream premise and an upstream dispute is not treated as proof of later funding impropriety.
