`EXACT AC PROPOSITION → WHAT AC KNEW → SAME-SOURCE CONTRADICTIONS → WHOLE ECONOMIC/OPERATIONAL SYSTEM WHERE RELEVANT → ACTOR-SPECIFIC DUTY/POWER → JUDICIAL RESULT → APPEAL STATUS → WHAT THE ALLEGATION DISPLACED → KNOWLEDGE GRADE → MOTIVE GRADE → PUBLIC WORDING → OPEN EVIDENCE`.
