## SERIAL LIST — SUBSTANTIVE ALLEGATIONS ONLY

This is the clean serial order for future work. Statutory headings under which the AC effectively said he had no relevant information are listed separately and must not be inflated into accusations.

1. **Lack of collaboration / obstruction of the AC.**  
   Global allegation that LPB/Gil failed to cooperate, preventing proper identification of the economic causes of insolvency.

2. **Failure to pursue and recover third-party receivables.**  
   Approximately €1.358m relied upon, including Sun Energy Spaces, customer balances and Community/CEXP-related receivables. The broad Art. 164.1 culpability/causation construction was rejected; the narrower supporting-document issue survives and is appealed.

3. **Pink/Monterecco operating agreement and unpaid rent.**  
   3A: contract-entry/inadequate-rent theory — rejected.  
   3B: non-payment/non-collection/late enforcement aggravation theory — materially adverse at first instance and appealed.

4. **Substantial accounting breach — Art. 164.2.1 LC.**  
   The AC alleged failure to keep/provide required accounting books. The AC himself recorded continuing accounting and receipt of journals/trial balances. Sentencia 163/2023 nevertheless adopted an adverse finding centred on non-supply of the statutory Libro Diario during the concurso. That factual/legal finding is appealed; contemporaneous Jonathan Simó/CLS/BDO material points materially the other way and requires year-by-year verification.

5. **Alzamiento / concealment theory involving Pink, overseas receipts and alleged `connivencia`.**  
   The AC inferred an asset-concealment/alzamiento arrangement from Pink's lack of attachable Spanish assets and possible hotel-related receipts reaching England/UK-connected accounts/entities. Sentencia 163/2023 rejected this branch as insufficiently corroborated. **CURRENT AUDIT.**

6. **Alzamiento concerning the €19,140.25 deposit/account balance.**  
   Separate branch. Sentencia 163/2023 rejected it, accepting the swap/pledge explanation and lack of LPB disposal power as alleged.

7. **Late filing for insolvency.**  
   Sentencia 163/2023 accepted late filing but attributed it to the earlier administrator, Uri Omid, not Gil.

8. **Failure to collaborate by not supplying particular documents.**  
   Separate from the global rhetoric. The judgment accepted that LPB delivered accounting documentation it possessed but retained a narrower adverse supporting-document finding concerning specified receivables; appealed.

9. **Failure to formulate/approve/deposit annual accounts during the concurso.**  
   Separate statutory presumption rejected by Sentencia 163/2023.

10. **Pink Canary Services as accomplice.**  
    Adverse first-instance complicity finding on the surviving rent/non-collection branch; appealed.

11. **Patricia Domínguez as accomplice.**  
    Rejected by Sentencia 163/2023 for insufficient individualised reasoning/support.

12. **Personal sanctions against Gil.**  
    AC sought 15-year disqualification, €3,032,010.34 damages and deficit liability. First instance imposed 10-year disqualification and €3,032,010.34 on the rent branch, rejected deficit liability; materially adverse findings appealed.

13. **Deficit liability.**  
    Rejected because the AC/Fiscal did not provide the required specific causal and quantitative justification and the court could not construct the missing case for them without defence-rights consequences.

### Starting judicial status

Sentencia 163/2023 rejected the specific Pink/England/`connivencia` alzamiento branch for insufficient corroboration. The audit must now determine exactly:

1. what documents/facts the AC relied upon;
2. which bank accounts/entities were actually identified;
3. whether any receipt was legally Pink's, LPB's, Community/CEXP money, customer money or another person's money;
4. whether LPB ever had title/control/disposal power over the funds alleged to have been concealed;
5. whether a transfer or concealment act was identified, rather than inferred from geography or collectability;
6. what the AC/Fiscal/Judge knew about the legal separation of Spanish Pink from UK Monterecco/Aweswell;
7. what corroboration was missing when the court rejected the theory;
8. whether the theory diverted scrutiny from the actual operating-economics, Community, debtor, banking or recovery questions.

**Do not pre-decide the intent question.** Rejection of the allegation is powerful accountability material; it is not automatic proof that the person advancing it knew it was false.
