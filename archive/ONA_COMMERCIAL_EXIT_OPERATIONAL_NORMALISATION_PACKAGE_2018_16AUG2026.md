## 1. Controlling frame

Do **not** call the 2018 structure a generic `rescue package`.

The source-complete formulation is:

> **ONA-centred commercial exit and operational-normalisation package** — a coordinated structure intended to preserve/re-establish hotel operation, finance payment of recognised insolvency liabilities, conclude or exit Concurso 36/2012, restore LPB's ordinary corporate capacity, and address the fragmented ownership/operation problem through a combination of operator, business plan, professional due diligence, valuation, staged security, external finance and, where feasible, acquisition of minority-held assets.

ONA/Clubotel was the **operating anchor**, not necessarily the legal or financial leader of every component. The package also involved Aweswell/LPB/Matkator, transaction advisers, lawyers and multiple external finance routes. Preserve legal-person separation.

The existence and development of this structure is documentary. Gil Marer/Aweswell's allegation that the extraordinary structure became necessary because of private-actor interference, Community/governance dysfunction, disputed insolvency-administration conduct and judicial handling remains **PARTY ALLEGATION / EVIDENCE-BASED CAUSATION THEORY**, to be tested actor by actor.

## 2. Executive finding

The earlier project shorthand understated the commercial depth of the 2018 exit attempt.

The controlled source set now supports all of the following:

1. **A signed hotel-operation instrument existed by 6 June 2018**, with a suspensive link between hotel-operation effectiveness and sufficient money being placed into Mercantil nº1 to permit LPB to leave the insolvency process.
2. **A transaction stack existed by 8 June 2018**: hotel-operation documents, operator business plan, Cuatrecasas due diligence, ECO valuation presented at approximately EUR 25.6m, bridge-finance structuring, an interim security package and a separate acquisition/exit route.
3. **External finance was not dependent on one named lender.** Distinct financing routes were pursued before and after 13 June 2018. Public writing does not need to identify lenders to establish that proposition.
4. The June financing route shows **advanced but conditional finance**. One contemporaneous instrument required stringent conditions, including a day-one mortgage structure; counsel later warned that route was not yet a legal `safe harbor`.
5. The source set separately verifies **a later executed binding term sheet**, signed by Aweswell/Matkator and a different finance route, which expressly structured security in stages and contemplated funding sufficient to pay recognised insolvency debt/interest and take LPB out of the insolvency/liquidation process.
6. That later binding term sheet contains an explicit `Security Package on closing` and a separate `Security Package once the Company exits liquidation`. It therefore proves that the commercial architecture itself contemplated **interim security first and LPB mortgage security after exit**.
7. The later term sheet also required a duly executed ONA lease agreement, a satisfactory business plan, ECO valuation, due diligence, evidence of the amount required to exit, and contemplated potential acquisition of minority-held assets as part of restoring the unitary operating perimeter.
8. On 13 June 2018 Daniel Irigoyen contemporaneously reported that he had put to the Mercantile Judge a route coordinating fund + shareholder/company + operator to pay the concursal debt and conclude the insolvency, and then discussed the route with the insolvency administrator. This is a professional contemporaneous account, **not a judicial minute**.

The correct evidential conclusion is not `the money was unconditionally available on 13 June`. It is stronger and more accurate:

> **The 2018 record documents a developed, multi-route commercial financing and operating architecture, with real operator documentation, professional DD/valuation, conditional finance offers, interim collateral designed for the insolvency stage, post-exit mortgage security, and continuing alternative finance work. The ONA-centred package is material to the Calificación counter-record because it tests categorical portrayals of abandonment, obstruction, non-collaboration or indifference to LPB's survival/value.
