## 1. The one question this control owns

The inquiry is not whether a credit can lawfully be transferred, serviced, recognised in an insolvency proceeding or enforced. Each can occur lawfully. The controlling question is:

> **Did the same originating economic obligations retain a reproducible, lawful and single-satisfaction identity as they moved through instruments, holders, balances, proceedings and property consequences—or were any components duplicated, materially altered, falsely certified, procedurally instrumentalised or recovered beyond their lawful economic and legal function?**

Answer that question through one end-to-end ledger, without merging legally distinct actors, instruments or proceedings.

### 8.1 Non-retroactivity and catalogue lock

The general Spanish legal-person criminal-liability regime entered into force on **23 December 2010**. It cannot be used to impose legal-person criminal responsibility for the 17-Jun-2008 origination or 27-May-2010 transaction. Later acts must be dated separately and tested under the version then in force, including the express offence catalogue, the legal person's eligibility and any statutory exclusion. CP 130.2 preserves or transfers an otherwise applicable legal-person responsibility in the corporate events it lists; it does not cure a missing temporal or offence-catalogue predicate.

## 9. Notarial falsity protocol

Never allege `falsified notarised documents` as one undifferentiated event. Create one row per instrument:

`document/protocol/page/clause → exact literal → declarant/author → source of asserted fact → contemporaneous comparator → nature of claimed falsity → knowledge at execution → later use → decision/disposition → benefit/harm → defence → proof status`.

Keep these possibilities separate:

1. physical or digital alteration of an authentic instrument;
2. simulation or creation of an inauthentic instrument;
3. false identity/signature/intervention attribution;
4. an authentic deed faithfully recording a materially false party recital;
5. an arithmetic, snapshot-date, classification or typographical error;
6. a legally disputed recital that is not objectively false; and
7. a correct recital later used outside its original function.

No current source proves notarial participation, knowledge or intent. The notary is an independent control node and must not inherit a party's alleged state of mind.

For a private party, a knowingly inaccurate narrative or debt recital is not, merely for that reason, one of the three falsity modes incorporated by CP 392 from CP 390.1.1°–3°. Test whether the evidence instead proves an essential alteration, a simulated document capable of misleading as to authenticity, or false intervention/attribution. If the theory is later knowing use of a document already proved false, test CP 393 separately: knowing presentation in court, or other knowing use with the purpose of harming another. A notary's possible CP 390.1.4° route cannot be inferred from faithfully recording what an appearing party actually declared.

## 10. Strongest countermodels that must travel with the allegation

- lawfully accrued interest, mortgage-responsibility caps and different snapshot dates can explain some balance movements;
- claim balance, recognised insolvency credit, secured amount, mortgage responsibility, competitive threshold, deed consideration and cash recovery can be different legal concepts;
- an NPL can lawfully be transferred for less than face value, and later lawful recovery above transfer price is not by itself fraud or unjust enrichment;
- the Bankia→SAREB transfer occurred under a special statutory framework;
- assignment and procedural substitution can preserve a pre-existing credit rather than create a new one;
- the €30 movement may be a payment, correction or transcription issue; its source must be obtained before assigning intent;
- the €102,895.34 Protocol 457 step has a source-supported cap explanation;
- an authentic notarial deed proves the act and recitals made, not the independent truth of every party-supplied balance;
- separate collateral, ranks, asset pools and proceedings may have legitimate parallel functions if the single-satisfaction ledger closes; and
- accounting error, legal disagreement, negligence or incomplete records are alternatives to deliberate falsification or fraud.
