## 6. Interference taxonomy

Every episode must receive one principal classification:

- `D1 — DIRECT DOCUMENTED MANDATE ACT`: termination, revocation, venia control, instruction or authority decision directly affecting a specific mandate.
- `D2 — DIRECT DOCUMENTED PROFESSIONAL PRESSURE`: a proved threat, complaint, querella or deontological step, with filing, recipient, date, outcome and exact language distinguished.
- `S1 — STRUCTURAL CONSTRAINT`: standing, debtor authority, access, information, power, funding or procedural dependency that required workaround or created measurable risk.
- `I1 — INDIRECT PRIVATE-ACTOR EFFECT`: conduct by an identified Acosta Matos actor that changed the legal or evidential environment without a proved instruction to the AC.
- `J1 — DOCUMENTED CONVERGENCE`: separately proved AC and private-actor acts connected by a communication, common transaction, coordinated step or other documentary bridge.
- `M1 — MIXED CAUSATION`: AC/perimeter effect exists but fee, strategy, confidence, conflict or client instruction is also material.
- `O1 — ORDINARY PROFESSIONAL EVENT`: ordinary adverse advocacy, lawful statutory control, fee dispute, strategic disagreement, voluntary handover or unsupported chronology.
- `OPEN`: source, actor, effect or causation is not yet sufficiently established.

Do not use `J1` merely because two acts benefited the same party or occurred close in time.

## 7. Causation and harm controls

Preserve the repository's C0–C5 scale and score two questions separately:

1. **workstream impact**; and
2. **exit, reduction or non-personation causation**.

For each episode test:

`POWER / CAPACITY → VERIFIED KNOWLEDGE → ACT / OMISSION → PRACTICAL EFFECT → LAWYER RESPONSE → HANDOVER / EXIT EFFECT → COUNTERFACTUAL → STRONGEST ALTERNATIVE → BENEFICIARY`.

Professional harm, reputational harm and personal consequences must be separate fields. Personal or medical causation is not publishable merely because timing is close or because an affected person or client sincerely attributes it to the episode.

## 8. AC and Acosta Matos must remain separate actors

The page should use three lanes:

| Lane | What belongs there | Publication rule |
|---|---|---|
| Administrador Concursal | institutional powers, instructions, authority gates, positions, communications and filings attributable to the AC | identify source, legal capacity, strongest statutory defence and measured effect |
| Acosta Matos perimeter | acts of exact private legal persons or individuals, including adverse litigation, possession/control, complaints or communications | identify the exact actor; do not use the perimeter as a substitute for legal identity |
| Proved convergence | communication, transaction, shared instruction or coordinated procedural/economic step linking the two lanes | publish only the proved bridge; beneficiary or parallel effect alone is insufficient |

Do not state that the AC was part of the private perimeter as established fact. Treat that as a working allegation requiring actor-specific proof.
