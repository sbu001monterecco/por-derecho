## 4. Visual differentiation: fact, allegation and inference

The visual must not present every arrow as equally proved.

Use separate evidence-state treatment:

- **VERIFIED FACT:** existence/date/content of the relevant proceeding or source-supported procedural act;
- **PARTY ALLEGATION:** that the proceeding was brought or used through a captured/falsified/usurped Community authority structure;
- **EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE:** a dependency link supported by contemporaneous documents but not yet conclusively proved;
- **OPEN EVIDENCE:** missing notice, minute, mandate, debt, proxy, title/coefficient, standing or custody document needed to close the chain.

This distinction strengthens rather than weakens the criminal dimension because it forces actor-specific proof instead of relying on rhetoric.

→ `benefit / consequence`
