# JSP / Monte Lanza: alleged frustration of Multimatrix's options

Continuation of PD-JSP-RESEARCH-20260905 and PD-JSP-EXECUTED-20260905-01. Source review and user clarification: 5 September 2026. This is a publication candidate, not a certificate of deployment or adjudicated liability.

## Controlling attributed position

Gil Marer states his belief that JSP and Monte Lanza frustrated Multimatrix's ability to exercise the options through which it intended to acquire 100% of the Sun Park hotel, comprising 262 fincas, alongside acts and omissions of the separately identified non-JSP dissident interests associated with the Monte Lanza / Molina perimeter. Investigate the cumulative alleged mechanism, criminal/prosecutorial first, without replacing individual actors, obligations, causation or proof with an undifferentiated collective allegation.

A stated expiry date is not evidence that exercise was unobstructed, voluntarily abandoned or lawfully defeated. Conversely, this allegation does not establish that every owner granted an option, that every retained unit was unlawfully withheld, or that a particular option must now be treated as exercised. The original instruments, full schedules, exercise requirements, notices, ability to complete and relevant conduct must decide those questions.

## Finite proof obligations

**P1 — original rights:** every option, amendment, beneficiary/assignment and seller-specific schedule; distinguish intended262-finca consolidation from an executed obligation over each unit.

**P2 — before/after ownership:** map direct JSP property, Monte Lanza's assets, individual shareholders' apartments, completed sales, retained units and later purchasers. Equity is not title; later non-LPB owners are not automatically original defaulting sellers.

**P3 — actual prevention:** for each alleged act/omission identify actor, dated capacity, duty/right, document or action withheld, notice, exercise/closing step prevented, evidence and causal effect. Test buyer readiness, funding and ability to comply without the alleged impediment, including lawful explanations and buyer-side causes.

**P4 — 2010 disposition:** match the delivered units and recipients to the eleven-unit schedule; obtain the debt, valuation, approvals, deed, existing encumbrances, releases and any consent. Do not infer recipient identity from JSP's shareholding or later meeting representation.

**P5 — functional and community effects:** test loss of access, use, operating facilities, income, votes or authority only against particular documents and dates. Note7 of E07 describes intended facilities and leases, not proof of their actual use or later obstruction. Its overlapping references to unit11 require reconciliation.

**P6 — non-JSP dissidents:** identify each owner/entity, representative, relevant resolution/contract and act. CEXP membership was recorded as voluntary; opposition or non-adherence alone is not breach. Preserve the adverse AP89/2014 title/possessory result and the separate Bankia/financing, operational and market explanations in the canonical historical control.

**P7 — conditions and onward title:** prove acceptance, corporate approval, completion and registration of E08, then any separate onward Community/CAM disposition, price and authority. The recovered deed is not the complete Community-to-CAM chain. Keep8498's different presentation separate.

**P8 — economic destination and recovery:** reconcile receipts, liquidation quota, retained rights, JSP shareholder explanations and JSP's later insolvency inventory. Monte Lanza's ordinary liquidation and JSP's later insolvency are different. Establish claimant succession, standing, loss and action-specific limitation/interruption before stating a live remedy.

## Legal research, not a merits finding

The Civil Code provisions on good faith (7,1258), obligations not left to one party's discretion (1256), breach (1101) and prevention of a condition (1119) are questions for instrument-specific analysis. Article1119 is not an automatic rule reviving or exercising an expired purchase option. Criminal characterisation requires its own historical elements, knowledge, intent, participation and harm; contractual non-completion alone is insufficient. This record does not decide standing, prescription or guilt.
