## Non-deletion rule

Do not delete older inconsistent text merely to make the record appear cleaner. Where necessary, preserve the prior source or allegation and add a clear correction/supersession marker so later readers can see how the evidential position evolved.

## Public-language rule

Strong public formulations remain possible, but the strongest safe version is the one that states the adverse source and then identifies the unresolved evidential question. For example:

> The 28-November deed, cheque receipt and the AC's initial use of the €400,000 are now documented. The unresolved question is what concrete contractual, Registry, accounting and possessory reconciliation followed the later judicial refusal to convalidate that transaction, particularly before the 2021–22 disposal route.

That is stronger and more precise than an unsupported claim that no payment occurred or that every effect automatically disappeared.
