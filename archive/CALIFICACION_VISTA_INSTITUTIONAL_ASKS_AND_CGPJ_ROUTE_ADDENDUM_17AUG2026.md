## 6. CGPJ route analysis
### Current route recommendation
**Primary:** supplement the existing **Alzada 286/2026 / DI 169/2026** record with the newly recovered Vista-functioning material, expressly framed as newly reconstructed evidence relevant to court administration, evidence accessibility, witness-programme traceability, record completeness and effective-defence safeguards.

**Parallel, if needed:** request **Servicio de Inspección / UAC** examination of the court-functioning dimension, without asking the CGPJ to review the merits of Sentencia 163/2023.

**Do not default to a wholly new disciplinary complaint yet.** A new complaint becomes stronger only if: (a) a genuinely new actor-specific disciplinary fact is established; (b) the pending Alzada is terminated without addressing these newly presented non-merits facts; or (c) the CGPJ expressly indicates that the new functioning facts require a separate expediente.

### Why this route is stronger
- The CGPJ itself states that it cannot correct or annul judicial decisions or entertain a complaint merely disagreeing with a judgment; those issues belong to procedural appeals.
- The CGPJ can receive complaints/denuncias concerning functioning and possible disciplinary breaches, and its UAC forms part of the Promotor service.
- Current official 2026 inspection guidance contemplates measures concerning court functioning and possible professional-duty failures without entering the jurisdictional merits.
- DI 169/2026 is already under Alzada 286/2026 on the current controlled record; no later substantive decision or verified Inspection treatment of the 28-Jul supplement was located through 17-Aug-2026.
