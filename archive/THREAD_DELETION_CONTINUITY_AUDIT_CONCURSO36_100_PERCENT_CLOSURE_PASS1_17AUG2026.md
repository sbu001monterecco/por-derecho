## 2. Definition of 100% that must survive deletion

`100%` means **100% of the presently identifiable/recoverable docket accounted for**, not every document that may ever have existed.

An act is accounted for only if it is either:

- primary/certified/official-copy digitised and fully digested; or
- explicitly logged as missing original with its entire known secondary chain and retrieval attempts.

The strongest 100% claim requires reconciliation against the official/certified court index.
