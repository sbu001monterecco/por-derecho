## Locked evidential rules

- allegation ≠ fact;
- registration ≠ review;
- review ≠ verification;
- verification ≠ sufficiency;
- sufficiency ≠ culpability;
- silence ≠ admission;
- continuing consequence ≠ automatically continuing misconduct;
- urgent protection ≠ provisional guilt;
- cross-file/context material ≠ case evidence without a human relevance decision;
- a later fact may not be projected backwards to justify or condemn an earlier act;
- every material issue must expose the strongest reasonable contrary explanation and what would change the assessment;
- red-card escalation is a methodological file-state candidate, not a finding about a person;
- every consequential route remains a competent human decision.

## 100% methodology definition preserved from this thread

- **Live-case integration 100%** means the method is actually applied to every defined module/act and public/repository implementation is complete.
- **Evidential completeness 100%** means complete evidential accounting, not omniscience: every material unknown is identified with likely holder, acquisition/certification route, effect if unavailable, what would change the view and human gate.
- **Institutional-state accounting 100%** may include an authoritative refusal/inability to certify; it must not be replaced by inference.
- **Adoption readiness 100%** is controllable by Por Derecho; actual institutional adoption is external and may be recorded only after express institutional confirmation.
- **Ponente reader-experience 100%** means the implemented public demonstrator supplies the defined present-state, protected-interest, escalation, contrary-case, finite-action, accessibility and print controls. It does not mean ICALPA adoption or official-file completeness.
