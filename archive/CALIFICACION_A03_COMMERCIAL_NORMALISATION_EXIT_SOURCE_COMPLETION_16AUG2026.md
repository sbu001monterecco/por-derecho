# CALIFICACIÓN A03 — COMMERCIAL NORMALISATION / EXIT SOURCE COMPLETION

**Date:** 16 August 2026  
**Proceeding:** Concurso ordinario 36/2012 — Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. (`LPB`)  
**Status:** `CONTROLLED SOURCE-COMPLETION SUBLEDGER — PART OF ALLEGATION 03 UNITARY SYSTEM — NOT A STANDALONE CAUSATION FINDING`  
**Controlling parent:** `archive/CALIFICACION_ALLEGATION_03_UNITARY_COMMUNITY_PRIVATE_ACTORS_AC_CAUSATION_16AUG2026.md`

## Purpose

This source-completion record prevents the June-2018 operator / financing / valuation / due-diligence / exit work from being described either as:

1. a vague or hypothetical `rescue package`; or
2. proof that unconditional finance had already closed and the court/AC simply refused to release LPB.

The correct unitary frame is a **commercial normalisation, refinancing, operating and insolvency-exit architecture** assembled against an already disputed hotel-wide operating, Community, access/control, creditor and insolvency environment.

The reason an extraordinary structure was needed is itself part of the causation dispute. Gil Marer alleges that private-actor interference, Community/governance dysfunction, the AC's administration/protection choices and later judicial handling created or maintained barriers between a commercially valuable hotel platform and LPB's ability to operate, refinance and leave Concurso 36/2012 normally. That proposition remains a **PARTY ALLEGATION / EVIDENCE-BASED CAUSATION HYPOTHESIS** to be tested actor by actor. It is not an adjudicated fact.

## 1. The June 2018 architecture was commercially developed, not merely aspirational

### 1.1 Hotel operating instrument — 6 June 2018

`VERIFIED PRIMARY DOCUMENT`

The recovered signed 16-page **Contrato de Arrendamiento de Industria Hotelera**, dated **6 June 2018**, identifies its legal parties as:

- **Comunidad de Propietarios del complejo Hotel Sun Park**, represented in the instrument by Gil Marer/Summers; and
- **Clubotel La Dorada, S.L.**, represented by Carlos Barrau Ribera.

The instrument does **not** facially name LPB or `ONA Hotels` as the legal contracting tenant. Contemporaneous transaction emails nevertheless describe the agreement as the contract with **ONA as hotel operator**, and the contract's rent/accounting clause refers to the hotel-management system **OnaSystem**.

### Correction boundary

Do not write `LPB signed a hotel-management contract directly with ONA Hotels` or treat Clubotel La Dorada and ONA as the same legal person unless the corporate/contractual relationship is separately sourced.

Public-safe formulation:

> **Contemporaneous transaction correspondence described ONA as the intended hotel operator; the recovered signed industry-lease instrument itself names the Sun Park Owners' Community and Clubotel La Dorada, S.L. as the legal parties.**

### Commercial terms visible in the signed instrument

The contract records, among other things:

- a **15-year term**;
- a guaranteed fixed rent of **€5,000 per year per apartment available for management**, subject to the contractual mechanics;
- a variable-rent mechanism tied to **70% of GOP (Gross Operating Profit)**;
- hotel operation as an integrated establishment rather than an apartment-by-apartment passive lease; and
- a contractual description of Sun Park as **262 registered units: 220 apartments, 40 commercial premises and 2 pool/installation units**.

These are contractual representations and commercial terms. They do not by themselves prove the Owners' Community had valid legal power to bind every private finca or every owner.

## 2. The operating contract was expressly interlocked with exit from Concurso 36/2012

### 2.1 Signed suspensive-conditions addendum — 6 June 2018

`VERIFIED PRIMARY DOCUMENT`

The recovered two-page signed addendum, also dated **6 June 2018**, says the hotel letting was subject to two express suspensive conditions:

1. **payment into the account of Juzgado de lo Mercantil nº 1 de Las Palmas of sufficient funds to permit LPB to leave its insolvency proceeding**; and
2. a **favourable General Meeting of owners resolution** authorising the President to subscribe the contract on behalf of the hotel owners.

The addendum set a deadline of **31 May 2019**, subject to successive one-month extensions at the tenant's request where non-compliance was not attributable to it.

If the conditions failed for a cause directly attributable to the landlord side, the document contemplated the contract becoming ineffective and a damages claim by the tenant.

### Significance

This is a direct documentary bridge between:

`HOTEL OPERATOR → OWNER/COMMUNITY AUTHORITY → MONEY INTO MERCANTIL Nº1 → LPB EXIT FROM CONCURSO → POSSESSION / HOTEL OPERATION`.

The operating arrangement and insolvency-exit route were therefore not separate stories.

## 4. Stoneweg / Varia: material finance, but conditional — not unconditional cash

### 4.1 Contemporaneous email description

`VERIFIED CONTEMPORANEOUS CORRESPONDENCE`

On **12 June 2018**, Gonzalo Barrios of Stoneweg sent the financing document after the call and described the attachment in the email as an **`Oferta Vinculante`**, adding that they awaited news after the next day's meeting.

### 4.2 The attached instrument itself

`VERIFIED PRIMARY DOCUMENT`

The attached document is more precise. Its title is **`Oferta Vinculante Condicionada`** and it is issued by **Varia Structured Opportunities S.A. (`VSO`)** to **Aweswell Limited**.

It contemplated financing of up to **€15.5m**, subject also to a 65% LTV ceiling based on the lower acceptable ECO valuation.

The use-of-funds schedule included:

- **€13.84m** for payment of concursal and estate (`contra la masa`) liabilities;
- **€660,000** for Matkator debt refinancing; and
- **€1m** for transaction/financing costs and fees.

### Material conditions

The recovered instrument was conditional on, among other matters:

- first-ranking mortgages and the agreed LTV;
- **authorisation from Juzgado de lo Mercantil nº 1 de Las Palmas to grant the mortgages in favour of VSO**;
- a letter/certificate from the AC/liquidator confirming total concursal and estate liabilities did not exceed **€13.84m**;
- an acceptable first-class ECO valuation of at least the stated threshold;
- legal/registry due diligence concerning Matkator assets;
- approval by the VSO management/credit committee; and
- other corporate/control conditions.

The recovered copy does not establish final closing or drawdown; the Aweswell acceptance/signature block appears unexecuted on the copy visually reviewed.

### Counterevidence that must remain visible

A later **2 July 2018** Cuatrecasas email expressly advised obtaining an executed/signed offer and said, in substance, that the instrument was subject to several conditions and was useful but **not yet a safe harbour**.

Therefore:

> **The financing architecture was serious and advanced, but it was conditional. It must not be rewritten as unconditional committed cash already available to the court.**

Equally, its conditions matter to causation because successful closing depended on outputs from the **court and AC** — notably mortgage authority and a reconciled debt certificate — as well as lender approvals and other conditions.

## 5. 13 June 2018 — reported direct judicial and AC notice

### 5.1 Daniel Irigoyen contemporaneous report

`CONTEMPORANEOUS PROFESSIONAL / WITNESS ACCOUNT — NOT JUDICIAL MINUTES`

Daniel Irigoyen's email sent on **13 June 2018** after his meetings states that he had attended the Commercial Court and then met the AC.

He recorded that he presented a route coordinating:

- the fund;
- the shareholder/company; and
- the hotel chain/operator,

with the objective of **concluding the insolvency by paying the concursal debt**.

The reasons he recorded included restoration of LPB's autonomy, hotel exploitation, the ability to defend the company outside concurso, and avoiding the uncertainty/irreversibility of the CAM liquidation path.

According to Irigoyen's report, the Judge:

- allowed the conclusion route to be explored;
- asked for an immediate conclusion application;
- contemplated requiring the AC to update the debt and a deposit/guarantee;
- said the CAM-improvement route should also be preserved in the meantime; and
- indicated that he would speak with the AC about the possible route.

Irigoyen separately recorded that he told the AC about the proposed conclusion and asked for collaboration, including a correct update of the privileged debt.

### 5.2 Carlos Sanz same-day corroborating communication

`CONTEMPORANEOUS CORROBORATING COMMUNICATION`

Carlos Sanz separately reported the same day that Irigoyen had attended the court to defend the position of cancelling/concluding the insolvency, that the meeting appeared positive, and that the next step was an Aweswell conclusion application while funding work continued.

### Evidential limit

These communications prove what the professionals recorded contemporaneously. They do **not** replace:

- a court minute;
- a filed LexNET/asiento application;
- a judicial order;
- an AC debt certificate;
- proof that every commercial attachment was personally reviewed by the Judge; or
- proof that every condition to financing was capable of satisfaction.

CR-005 remains controlling: the historical Article-176 conclusion materials are **draft unless filing proof is located**.

## 6. 2019 continuity: the operator structure survived, but the hotel condition had changed

### 6.1 Signed 12 February 2019 addendum

`VERIFIED PRIMARY DOCUMENT`

A later signed addendum dated **12 February 2019** identifies the same 6-June-2018 industry-lease relationship between the Sun Park Owners' Community and Clubotel La Dorada, S.L.

The addendum records that, in the intervening months, the tenant side had observed deterioration and states that the complex was then abandoned / without an operating hotel activity. It does **not** allocate legal responsibility for that condition.

Clubotel La Dorada states willingness to invest a **minimum of €1m** in Sun Park installations before possession, with project scope subject to technical inspection and agreement. The addendum modifies the fixed-rent timing so that it would not accrue until **12 months after completion of the minimum €1m works**.

### Significance and boundary

This is evidence that the operator/commercial structure did not simply disappear immediately after June 2018. It also documents a materially changed physical/operating condition by February 2019.

It does **not** prove why the hotel reached that condition. Causation must be tested against the separate control/access, maintenance, security, AC, Community, operator, market and judicial records.

## 7. The unitary causal issue — why `rescue package` is the wrong shorthand

`CONTROLLED FRAMING`

Calling the June-2018 stack a `rescue package` risks silently conceding the premise that Sun Park/LPB had simply failed commercially and needed an external saviour.

The source-controlled description is:

> **commercial normalisation, refinancing, hotel-operation and insolvency-exit architecture**.

The complete causal test is:

`PRE-EXISTING HOTEL VALUE / OPERATING PLATFORM`

→ `MINORITY / COMMUNITY / BANKING / INSOLVENCY CONSTRAINTS`

→ `PRIVATE-ACTOR ACCESS / CONTROL / SECURITY DISPUTE`

→ `WHAT LPB / AWESWELL / COMMUNITY / OPERATOR COULD ACTUALLY CONTROL`

→ `SIGNED OPERATING STRUCTURE + BUSINESS PLAN + DD + ECO VALUATION + FINANCE + ALTERNATIVE SALE`

→ `FINANCE CONDITIONS DEPENDING PARTLY ON AC / COURT OUTPUTS`

→ `13-JUN REPORTED JUDICIAL + AC NOTICE`

→ `WHAT WAS ACTUALLY FILED / PROVED / DECIDED`

→ `26-JUN SUSPENSION AND LATER PROCEDURAL / CONTROL EVENTS`

→ `FEB-2019 OPERATOR ADDENDUM RECORDING DETERIORATED / NON-OPERATING CONDITION`

→ `11-FEB-2019 AC CALIFICACIÓN NARRATIVE`

→ `2023 JUDICIAL FINDINGS AND CURRENT APPEAL`.

The allegation to investigate is not `the Judge rejected a fully funded rescue`. The stronger and more accurate question is:

> **What happened to a commercially developed exit/normalisation structure whose operation depended in part on an AC debt reconciliation and judicial authority, while the same hotel was simultaneously subject to disputed Community/private-actor access and control dynamics — and how was that history later reconciled with the calificación findings about Gil/Pink's conduct and causation?**

That is an **EVIDENCE-BASED ACCOUNTABILITY QUESTION**, not an adjudicated criminal conclusion.

## 8. Actor-specific allocation — no collective-liability shortcut

### Private actors / Community

Gil alleges that private interference and governance displacement materially impaired ordinary operation and increased the need for an extraordinary normalisation structure. The record supports a serious investigation of debt/voting, access/security, unit availability, control and benefit; it does not yet prove a single coordinated unlawful scheme or sole causation.

### AC

The AC had a distinct duty/power position over LPB's estate and was asked in the reported 13-June discussion to update/reconcile debt and support a lawful conclusion route. Separate records also place him inside the Community/security/access system. The audit must establish what he actually received, certified, calculated, authorised, opposed or failed to do. The mere failure of the transaction is not proof of AC obstruction.

### Judge / court

The Irigoyen record is unusually probative of reported direct notice of the proposed conclusion architecture, but it is not a court minute. The Judge is also reported as permitting exploration of the route, which is mandatory counterevidence to a simplistic `continuous obstruction` thesis. The key issue is the later **decisional bridge**: what was filed, what debt/guarantee requirement was set, what was decided, and how that record relates to later adverse findings.

### Gil / Aweswell / LPB / Community-side proposer

The transaction record is strong evidence of active commercial work, but it does not immunise the user-side actors from scrutiny. The audit must test Community authority, exact property perimeter, execution of financing conditions, signatures, actual cash, and whether the structure was legally/operationally capable of closing.

## 9. Corrections / publication controls created by this pass

1. **Do not call the June stack simply a `rescue package`.** Use `commercial normalisation / refinancing / operation / insolvency-exit architecture` and state the disputed causal reason it became necessary separately.
2. **Do not say ONA was the facial legal tenant in the recovered signed contract.** The legal tenant is Clubotel La Dorada, S.L.; ONA is how contemporaneous transaction correspondence described the intended operator context, and OnaSystem appears in the contract. Exact corporate/operational relationship remains to be sourced.
3. **Do not call the Stoneweg/VSO instrument unconditional committed funding.** The email called the attachment `Oferta Vinculante`; the instrument is titled `Oferta Vinculante Condicionada` and contains material judicial, AC, appraisal, committee and other conditions.
4. **Do not say the Judge personally reviewed every element of the transaction package.** The Irigoyen account supports reported direct discussion of the architecture; certified filing/incorporation is a separate proof question.
5. **Do not call the Article-176 conclusion application filed without filing proof.** CR-005 controls.
6. **Do not infer the cause of the February-2019 deterioration from the operator addendum.** It records condition; causation remains separate.

## 11. Public-safe core formulation

### Spanish

> **No era un “rescate” de un hotel comercialmente muerto. Era una arquitectura de normalización, refinanciación, explotación y salida del concurso que debe entenderse dentro del conflicto que ya afectaba a Sun Park.** En junio de 2018 existían un contrato hotelero firmado, un plan operativo, due diligence de Cuatrecasas, una tasación ECO presentada en la operación, financiación externa avanzada pero condicionada y una alternativa de venta. El propio contrato hotelero hacía depender su efectividad de ingresar en Mercantil nº1 fondos suficientes para sacar a LPB del concurso y de obtener la autorización comunitaria correspondiente. La oferta financiera, a su vez, dependía entre otras cosas de autorización judicial para las hipotecas y de una certificación de deuda de la AC. Por eso la pregunta no es si había una idea genérica de “salvar” LPB. La pregunta es qué ocurrió con una estructura comercial cuya materialización dependía parcialmente de decisiones y certificaciones institucionales, mientras el hotel atravesaba simultáneamente un conflicto de acceso, control y gobernanza que debe atribuirse actor por actor.

### English

> **This was not a “rescue” of a commercially dead hotel. It was a commercial normalisation, refinancing, operating and insolvency-exit architecture that has to be understood inside the conflict already affecting Sun Park.** By June 2018 there was a signed hotel operating instrument, an operating plan, Cuatrecasas due diligence, an ECO valuation used in the transaction process, advanced but conditional external finance and an alternative sale route. The hotel contract itself made effectiveness conditional on sufficient money being paid into Mercantil nº1 to take LPB out of insolvency and on the relevant Community approval. The financing instrument in turn depended, among other matters, on judicial mortgage authority and an AC debt certificate. The question is therefore not whether there was a generic idea to “save” LPB. It is what happened to a commercial structure whose implementation depended partly on institutional decisions and certifications while the hotel was simultaneously experiencing an access, control and governance conflict that must be attributed actor by actor.
