## 3. 1 June 2012 insolvency memorandum — early viability purpose

### Source

LPB insolvency memorandum dated 1-Jun-2012 (exact private filename withheld).

### VERIFIED SOURCE CONTENT

The memorandum is presented as Gil Marer's Article 6.2.2 Ley Concursal memorandum as sole administrator of LPB. It records the company/history, hotel structure and causes of the financial position. Its concluding viability proposition states that:

> `La viabilidad de la sociedad pasa por un convenio de quita y espera con sus acreedores...`

### Classification

`VERIFIED FACT — CONTEMPORANEOUS PARTY FILING / BUSINESS POSITION`

This establishes that, at the opening of the insolvency, the recorded position was not merely abandonment or liquidation: the memorandum itself identified a creditor arrangement as the proposed viability route.

It does **not** prove that the proposed convenio would necessarily have succeeded, that every stated historical cause was correct, or that all later rescue steps were already fixed in June 2012.

## 4. 2017 convenio / viability plan — concrete preservation and restructuring programme

### Source

Google Doc `Plan Viabilidad Concurso 2017`, dated in the text as:

- propuesta de convenio: **29 March 2017**;
- plan de viabilidad: **17 April 2017**.

### VERIFIED SOURCE CONTENT

The document expressly states two objectives:

1. permit LPB to overcome its patrimonial/financial crisis while **conserving business activity**; and
2. satisfy creditors.

It presents the proposed convenio as more favourable than liquidation because isolated liquidation would remove future resources from business activity and, in the plan's analysis, reduce value.

The same document records or states, as part of the proposed programme:

- resources for the convenio were intended to derive from business activity;
- negotiation with the privileged-credit holder for refinancing;
- **Aweswell Limited as guarantor** of LPB obligations under the convenio;
- stated negotiations concerning Santander restructuring;
- a stated Aweswell commitment to recapitalise LPB with own funds and guarantee remaining concurso payments;
- numerous negotiations since the concurso with Promontoria representatives concerning the debt;
- a strategy to restructure the debt over the main tourist-unit asset perimeter;
- proposed immediate leasing of 29 commercial units and two solariums described in the plan as operationally important and unencumbered;
- a stated negotiated **five-year lease with a British tour operator and Spanish local operator**, with a notarised/translated copy referenced in the annexes;
- projected business receipts intended to support creditor payments.

### Classification and publication boundary

`VERIFIED FACT — CONTEMPORANEOUS PARTY/BUSINESS PLAN EXISTS AND CONTAINS THESE STATED ACTIONS/COMMITMENTS.`

The plan is strong evidence of concrete rescue/restructuring work and commitments before the 2019 calificación accusation. It is **not** proof that the projected revenue, EBITDA, repayment schedule, refinancing or lease outcomes were ultimately realised.

Projected amounts in the plan must be labelled as projections if used publicly.

## 10. Controlling public narrative

The Calificación page and the wider website should use two interlocking grammars.

### A. Recovery / positive-agency grammar

`ADVERSE EVENT → DOCUMENTED RESPONSE → WHAT WE SOUGHT TO PRESERVE/RECOVER → WHAT HAPPENED NEXT → CURRENT REMEDY`

### B. Accountability grammar

`POWER/DUTY → VERIFIED KNOWLEDGE → ACT/OMISSION → IMPLEMENTATION → CONSEQUENCE → CAUSATION → REMEDY/QUANTUM`

Together they answer both questions:

1. **What happened to the business/assets/rights?**
2. **What did we actually do about it, and what remains recoverable now?**

## 12. Temporal discipline — do not overclaim success

Until the current primary procedural record proves formal conclusion, do **not** say that LPB's concurso has already been successfully concluded.

Preferred wording:

> **We continue to pursue the lawful conclusion of Concurso 36/2012 and the recovery/restoration of the business, hotel interests, rights, income and damages affected by the wider chain.**

Separate clearly:

- already achieved;
- preserved/protected;
- formally challenged;
- pending;
- currently sought.
