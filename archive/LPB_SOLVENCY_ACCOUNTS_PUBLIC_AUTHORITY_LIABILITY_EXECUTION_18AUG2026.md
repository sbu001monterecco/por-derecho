## 1. Mission

Run a complete forensic, accounting, insolvency, appellate, causal and public-authority-liability reconstruction directed to the following proposition:

> The strongest defensible case is that the Hotel Sun Park productive unit and the wider Aweswell / LPB / Matkator / Explotadora perimeter repeatedly demonstrated substantial patrimonial coverage, continuing commercial value and identifiable value-preserving exit capacity. The existence of Concurso 36/2012 must therefore not be converted into a presumption that the hotel was economically extinct, that LPB was structurally insolvent at every relevant date, that liquidation was inevitable, or that the adverse accounting and solvency allegations in the calificación were true as one undifferentiated package.

The investigation must be forceful but exact. It must not rely on the legally unsafe shortcut that assets exceeding liabilities automatically disproves every form of insolvency. It must distinguish:

1. balance-sheet / patrimonial coverage;
2. regular ability to meet enforceable obligations as they fell due;
3. temporary liquidity restriction;
4. disputed or incorrectly quantified debt;
5. going-concern and productive-unit value;
6. availability of restructuring or rescue finance;
7. necessity, proportionality and creditor benefit of liquidation;
8. value lost, displaced or captured after liquidation and the June 2018 rupture.

The offensive question is:

> Where the record repeatedly showed substantial asset coverage, a 100% convenio was filed, rescue financing was pursued, an ECO valuation exceeded €25.6 million, and the same productive unit was later transformed and commercialised by others, who decided that liquidation was the only lawful or creditor-protective outcome, on what complete information, after rejecting or neutralising which alternatives, and with what resulting allocation of value, income and benefit?

## 10. Articles 32–37 of Ley 40/2015

Preserve and activate public-authority-liability rights through the correct route.

### 10.1 General administrative route

For each public administration establish:

- specific public service;
- competence;
- normal or abnormal functioning;
- act or omission;
- notice;
- effective, economically assessable and individualised injury;
- direct causal link;
- absence of a legal duty to bear the harm;
- date of manifestation / stabilisation;
- quantum;
- limitation date.

### 10.2 Concurrent administrations — Article 33

Determine whether responsibility is:

- joint / solidary in an integrated public action;
- allocable by competence, protected public interest and degree of intervention;
- independent for separate acts.

### 10.3 Private-law entities and concurrent private actors — Article 35

Identify any public entity acting through a private-law body and any concurrence with private subjects. Do not use Article 35 to erase the private actor’s separate liability.

### 10.4 Article 36

The injured party claims against the Administration. Any recourse by the Administration against an authority or employee is a separate internal step. Do not frame the ordinary Article 36 route as a direct personal damages claim against an individual official.

### 10.5 Article 37

Preserve separate criminal and civil-delict routes and analyse when factual dependency affects the administrative procedure.

### 10.6 Administration of Justice

Article 32.7 directs judicial-administration liability to the Organic Judiciary Act regime. Build a separate file for:

- judicial error;
- abnormal functioning of the Administration of Justice;
- the required prior declaration where applicable;
- Ministry of Justice claim route;
- causation and quantum;
- time limits.

Do not treat appeal, CGPJ complaint and patrimonial-liability claim as interchangeable.

### 10.7 Prescription

Article 67 of Ley 39/2015 generally provides a one-year period. Create an urgent limitation calendar.

A reservation in the Aweswell accounts, a website statement, SAIP, complaint or institutional notification must **not** be assumed to interrupt prescription unless the applicable law and procedural act support that conclusion.

### Controlled reservation wording

> Without admission as to limitation, accrual, causation, competence or quantum, Aweswell Limited, LPB, Matkator and the relevant rights-holders expressly preserve the right to formulate, particularise, expand or activate claims for patrimonial liability under Articles 32 to 37 of Ley 40/2015 and, for the Administration of Justice, under the applicable Organic Judiciary Act regime, in respect of effective, economically assessable and individualised harm directly or indirectly attributed to identified public-service acts or omissions. This reservation records the position but does not replace any required administrative claim, establish liability or interrupt prescription.

## 14. First controlled execution findings

1. The present record strongly supports repeated positive patrimonial coverage in the concurso figures reproduced by the board annex.
2. The 2017 convenio was a real filed proposal for 100% treatment of recognised ordinary and subordinated credits, but it was not approved or implemented.
3. The June 2018 financing record supports active rescue efforts and a potential amount sufficient to address the recognised concurso perimeter, but the offer was materially conditional.
4. The 7 June 2018 Gesvalt ECO valuation supports material productive-unit value and contradicts a simple zero-value narrative.
5. The strongest legal position is therefore against **structural insolvency, economic extinction and inevitable liquidation**, not yet an unqualified conclusion that no cash-flow insolvency existed at any date.
6. The Aweswell filing is best used as a prudential parent-level continuity and rights-preservation record. It is not a substitute for LPB’s accounts or a Companies House adjudication.
7. The calificación defence should use the filing as an index to the contemporaneous conduct record and as evidence of non-abandonment, prudence and continued recovery work.
8. Public-authority liability must be divided between ordinary administrative services and the special Administration of Justice route.
9. A generic Articles 32–37 reservation is useful for notice and consistency but should not be treated as interrupting prescription.
10. The next urgent work product is an administration-by-administration limitation and causation register.
