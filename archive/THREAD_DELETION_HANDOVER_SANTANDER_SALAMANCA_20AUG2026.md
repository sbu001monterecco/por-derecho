## Gating due-diligence list

Before paying above the base residential range or relying on Santander finance, obtain and reconcile:

1. Full title/finca schedule distinguishing residential, commercial, storage, parking and common elements.
2. Current notas simples and, ideally, certified registry extracts for all properties included/excluded.
3. Horizontal-division deed, community statutes, quotas, voting/control and service-charge allocations.
4. Unit-by-unit current residential rent roll: rent, lease start, term, deposit, arrears, occupants, utilities, litigation, side letters, indexation and termination rights.
5. Actual collections for at least 12 months, by unit and by tenant.
6. Legal-use pack: licences, cédulas, LFO / first occupation or equivalent, change-of-use/subdivision licences, municipal certificates.
7. Technical/capex report: structure, MEP, fire, ventilation, accessibility, roof, façade, common areas, lifts, electrical, water, sanitation, asbestos and deferred-maintenance estimate.
8. Commercial separation: what is included in residential package, what remains with seller, what rights/servitudes affect access, roof, plant, signage, extraction, delivery, terraces, noise, parking and service areas.
9. Existing Santander debt/security/account relationship at target-company level.
10. Tax/debt/community certificates and any pending sanctions, urban-discipline or habitability/health/fire files.
11. Seller authority / UBO / shareholding / corporate-approval evidence for any share deal.
12. Bankable structure memo addressing acquisition finance, lawful borrower level, guarantees, third-party injection, sponsor support and financial-assistance risk.
