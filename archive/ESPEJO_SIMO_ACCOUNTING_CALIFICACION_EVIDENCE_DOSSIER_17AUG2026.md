## 1. Purpose and evidential conclusion

This dossier reconstructs the professional accounting, economic and expert work performed by **Jonathan Simó Morales / CLS** and **David Espejo Navarro / Expert Witness–Four Mirror Advisors** for LPB/Aweswell/PINK/Sun Park, and traces how that work fed the 2019 Calificación oppositions, later evidence preparation and the appeal against Sentencia 163/2023.

The present source set supports a stronger and more precise proposition than either extreme narrative:

> **There was a substantial and continuous professional accounting/economic infrastructure around LPB, PINK and the Sun Park operating perimeter. Accounting and tax professionals were actively producing records, responding to AC/document requests, preparing annual-accounting material, reconstructing balances, supporting litigation, testing receivables/debt, reviewing valuation/liquidation assumptions and working with lawyers/finance advisers on preservation and exit solutions. This materially contradicts any simplistic formulation that there was “no accounting”, “no professional oversight”, “no economic reconstruction” or “no attempt to resolve the concurso”. It does not, by itself, prove that every formal accounting/legal obligation was satisfied, that every book was legally legalised/filed on time, or that every receivable had complete supporting documentation when later requested.**

That distinction is controlling. The evidence is strongest when used to challenge overbroad propositions and to reconstruct what actually existed, what was supplied, what was professionally analysed and what remained specifically incomplete.

## 2. Evidential categories

- **VERIFIED PRIMARY/CONTEMPORANEOUS SOURCE:** native/direct email, signed report, filed/assembled litigation document or contemporaneous professional work product.
- **PARTY PLEADING / APPEAL POSITION:** proposition asserted in the 2019 opposition or 2023 appeal; it may accurately describe underlying evidence but is not independently elevated merely because it appears in a pleading.
- **EXPERT OPINION:** Espejo’s professional conclusion based on defined material; not a judicial finding.
- **UNRESOLVED / SOURCE GAP:** exact filed version, statutory sufficiency, receipt/read by a named actor, underlying source schedule or Vista testimony still requiring proof.

## 14. Appeal significance

The professional record is potentially strongest on four appellate themes:

### A. Accounting versus formal-accounting deficiency

The record permits a disciplined distinction between `accounting existed / was professionally maintained and supplied` and `particular formal books/legalisation/deposit requirements were not satisfied`. The appeal should not depend on collapsing those propositions.

### B. Cooperation

Direct communications, document bundles and opposition reconstruction materially weaken a categorical obstruction narrative. The remaining question becomes narrower and testable: **which exact requested supporting documents were missing, why, when, who controlled them and what causal significance did the omission have?**

### C. Receivables / causation

Jonathan/CLS and Espejo material shows that the Community/CEXP balances were the subject of professional analysis well before judgment. This strengthens the case for examining whether the broad non-recovery/aggravation theory and the narrower missing-support theory were treated consistently.

### D. Recovery-through-adversity / active solution

The 2018 professional network and work products are strong contemporaneous evidence that the owners’ side was trying to quantify liabilities, challenge value destruction and build a route to a solution. That evidence is relevant to any narrative of passivity, abandonment or deliberate aggravation, subject to the legal effect of the proposed solution and the court/AC’s actual powers.
