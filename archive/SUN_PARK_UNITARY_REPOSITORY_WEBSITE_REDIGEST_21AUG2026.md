## Unitary thesis

Sun Park was one economically integrated hotel platform but never one legal
person, one ownership title, one operator, one debt, one insolvency estate or one
claimant. The central forensic task is therefore not to atomise every event or to
collapse every layer into LPB. It is to reconstruct how propositions, authority,
money, control and value travelled between legally distinct layers:

`property -> operation -> governance -> finance -> enforcement -> LPB concurso ->
credit assignment -> material control -> works/valuation -> adjudication/title ->
investment/public support -> hotel income -> proceedings and recovery`.

A limited LPB concurso could be, and the group alleges was, repeatedly used or
allowed to function as a premise affecting a wider hotel platform. That proposition
must be tested actor by actor and date by date. Economic convergence is not legal
identity. Repeated aligned advantage warrants investigation; it does not prove a
common plan, criminal agreement or shared intent.

The recovery objective remains the maximum lawful restoration and recovery of the
entire economic platform and every separately held right: hotel property, hotel
income, operating rights, estate value and surplus, extraconcursal property,
shareholder/direct rights, personal and professional damage, interest, costs and
equivalent value where restoration in specie is unavailable. Entity separation is
a recovery discipline, not a surrender of the whole-platform objective.

## End-to-end reconstruction

### 1. 2008 structural origin

The located record describes transfers of individually divided assets rather than
a simple sale of Montelanza, S.L. as one company. LPB became a majority PropCo,
while CEXP/operation, common governance and third-party units remained distinct.
Divided titles, voluntary CEXP participation and unresolved seller/unit completion
created a documented structural vulnerability. Gil's present, narrower root-cause
position is that incomplete or frustrated performance of the 2008 restructuring
by a seller/minority perimeter associated, in specific acts, with Montelanza, S.L.
and Molina-linked owners began the later causal chain. That is an attributed party
position, not a finding of breach, shared intent, dishonesty, sole causation or
liability. It requires a seller–finca–obligation–performance–defence map.

### 2. 2008-2010 banking package

The original mortgage loan, interest-rate product/swap, second financing,

2011; Spanish corporate publicity and management changes followed in January and
February 2012. Separately, Community/CEXP authority, owner participation, charges,
