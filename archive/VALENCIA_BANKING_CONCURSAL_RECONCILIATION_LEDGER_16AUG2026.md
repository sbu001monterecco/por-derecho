### What it cannot retrospectively prove

- Fiscal Ricardo de Mosteyrín Sampalo's Calificación opinion is dated **12-Mar-2019**.
- Sentencia 163/2023, signed by Judge Alberto López Villarrubia, is dated **28-Sep-2023**.
- CaixaBank's expert report describes the present Aweswell claim as filed **13-Oct-2023**.

Accordingly, ORD 1859/2023-9 itself cannot prove that the Fiscal knew of the later lawsuit in 2019 or that Judge Alberto ignored that later proceeding when signing the 28-Sep-2023 judgment. Earlier knowledge of the underlying bank dispute, financial-product issues or recovery potential must be proven separately from sources available to each actor at the relevant time.

The live claim is nevertheless **subsequent counter-evidence** against any continuing treatment of the relevant patrimonial loss as necessarily fixed, solely debtor-caused or irrecoverable without reconciliation of the bank-side recovery route.
