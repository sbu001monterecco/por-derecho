## 2. Mandatory dual-capacity separation

Every authority-facing, evidence-custodian-facing and media-facing communication in this workstream must keep two capacities separate.

### Capacity A — directly/indirectly interested and injured-party position

Where factually and legally supportable for the specific subject, Gil Marer may state that he writes because he and/or the relevant Aweswell/Oswell/LPB/Matkator perimeter is directly and/or indirectly affected, interested and/or allegedly injured by the matters under review. The communication must identify the concrete nexus rather than use a generic status label. Where standing, procedural recognition or the exact injured legal person is disputed or unverified, state that limitation.

### Capacity B — reporting-person / alertador / whistleblowing position

Separately, Gil Marer states that he has acted and continues to act as a reporting person / alertador / whistleblower, supplying information and allegations he considers grounded and in the public interest. Any reference to Directive (EU) 2019/1937, Spain's Law 2/2023, Germany's Hinweisgeberschutzgesetz, or Great Britain/Northern Ireland protected-disclosure regimes must retain the applicability qualifications in `AUTHOR_REPORTING_PERSON_ALERTADOR_FOOTER_CONTROL_22AUG2026.md`.

### Non-merger rule

Never imply that:

- personal or corporate injury automatically confers protected-whistleblower status;
- asserted whistleblower status proves standing, injury or the underlying allegations;
- every Spanish, German, EU or UK regime applies to every communication;
- a public-interest report substitutes for a creditor claim, procedural appearance, criminal complaint or evidence request; or
- one legal person's injury or standing automatically transfers to another group company, shareholder, director or beneficial owner.

The preferred formulation is: **“I write in two separate capacities: first, as a directly and/or indirectly affected, interested and allegedly injured party where the specific facts and legal relationship support that position; and, separately, as a reporting person/alertador communicating matters I consider to engage the public interest. The legal basis and scope of each capacity must be assessed independently.”**

## 3. Authority before arithmetic; legal person before group label

For every debt, repayment, loan, guarantee, asset, share, intercompany receivable, restructuring claim or transaction:

1. identify the exact legal person;
2. identify creditor and debtor;
3. identify date, amount, instrument, rank/security and governing proceeding;
4. identify the primary source;
5. identify actual cash/value movement where known; and
6. separate fact from inference.

Never treat `FTI`, `Meeting Point`, `BLUESEA`, `Portobello`, `SEPI`, `the German estate` or `the group` as a single legal person when the source permits a more exact identification.

## 4. Current controlled proposition

The workstream must presently distinguish the following propositions unless later primary evidence changes them:

- the Spanish FASEE support was a **EUR 31 million** operation for the identified Meeting Point Spanish recipients, not a direct FASEE loan to FTI Touristik GmbH;
- reported early repayments require transaction-by-transaction source tracing rather than an assumption that they came from the later German insolvency estate;
- the reported approximately **EUR 300 million intra-group layer** is a principal unresolved object because shareholder value and intercompany-creditor value are legally and economically different;
- the consideration, liability assumption and value allocation in the BLUESEA/Portobello restructuring/control transfer are principal unresolved objects;
- German federal and Länder public exposure must be separated by instrument, guarantor and debtor;
- EU state-aid approval is not proof that national Spanish or German rescue money was EU-origin funding; and
- preservation or repayment of Spanish subsidiary creditors can reduce residual shareholder value without, by itself, proving an unlawful preference, diversion or transfer from the German insolvency estate.

## 5. Finite hypothesis test

The question “were Spanish public creditors effectively protected or repaid at the expense of German creditors or taxpayers?” must be tested through separate mechanisms:

| Mechanism | Required proof | Allowed state |
|---|---|---|
| Direct transfer | traced German-estate/group cash into a Spanish recipient and onward use | `CONFIRMED`, `DISPROVED`, `SUPPORTED_UNPROVEN`, `NO_EVIDENCE` |
| Intercompany-credit impairment | exact FTI/Meeting Point creditor, receivable, rank, restructuring treatment and recovery | same |
| Shareholder residual-value effect | valuation/shareholding + subsidiary creditor waterfall | same |
| Transaction-value diversion | SPA/restructuring consideration and recipient/value allocation | same |
| Challengeable preference/asset transfer | transaction facts + competent-law legal test | same |

Economic incidence must never be promoted automatically to unlawfulness or criminality.

## 6. Evidence-closure loop

Each open FTI / Meeting Point / SEPI question must have one canonical gap object. Do not duplicate the same missing evidence across multiple registers.

Each gap must contain:

- question to be answered;
- exact legal persons;
- proposition affected;
- documents/data required;
- likely custodian;
- lawful retrieval route;
- request history and response history;
- contrary/limiting evidence;
- current status; and
- closure condition.

Allowed statuses are:

- `OPEN_UNREQUESTED`;
- `REQUEST_PREPARED_HOLD`;
- `REQUEST_SENT_RESPONSE_PENDING`;
- `PARTIAL_PRODUCTION`;
- `PRODUCED_NEEDS_RECONCILIATION`;
- `CLOSED_CONFIRMED`;
- `CLOSED_DISPROVED`;
- `CLOSED_INCONCLUSIVE_AFTER_REASONABLE_ROUTES`;
- `BLOCKED_COMPETENCE_OR_STANDING`.

A gap is not closed merely because a recipient does not answer. After reasonable routes have been exhausted, close it as **inconclusive**, record the non-production fact accurately, and state what conclusion cannot safely be reached.

## 8. Evidence-request escalation ladder

Use the least duplicative competent route first:

1. exact corporate/insolvency/public-credit custodian;
2. follow-up identifying the unanswered finite documents/questions;
3. formal transparency/administrative access route where legally available;
4. competent supervisory/reporting-person route where scope is verified;
5. judicial/insolvency production route where standing and procedure permit;
6. public-interest media inquiry using only public-safe propositions and giving material subjects a fair opportunity to respond.

Never use a broad CC list as a substitute for a debtor-specific, custodian-specific or proceeding-specific request. Completed sends are historical facts, not continuing authority for a resend.

## 9. Media escalation rule

The media objective is evidence production and accountable scrutiny, not conviction by publicity.

A media package must:

- lead with a small number of independently checkable questions;
- separate documented fact, allegation, inference and open proof;
- state the exact legal persons and dates;
- identify the strongest primary documents;
- disclose material contrary facts, including alternative causes of FTI's wider insolvency;
- avoid claiming EU funding where only national funding/state-aid approval is established;
- invite the journalist to obtain the missing documents independently;
- preserve right of reply for the materially affected entities/persons; and
- avoid publishing private mailbox material, personal data, privileged advice or unsent strategy.

Preferred investigative hook for the next media round:

> **A cross-border value-tracing question:** Spain's Meeting Point perimeter received EUR 31m of FASEE support and later entered a restructuring/control transfer, while German FTI creditors and public guarantors face substantial losses. Which entity paid SEPI, what happened to the approximately EUR 300m intra-group claims, what consideration was paid in the BLUESEA/Portobello transaction, and what value did the German insolvency estates actually recover from the Spanish subsidiaries and receivables?

This is a question to investigate, not a statement that Spanish creditors were unlawfully paid with German creditor money.

## 10. Authority escalation rule

Any authority package must ask for one or more concrete acts: preservation, linkage to a named file, production/status confirmation, competence referral, or examination of a precisely described transaction/evidence issue. Do not request a generic endorsement of the project's thesis.

Where allegations of criminality or public-fund misuse are raised, classify each proposition as:

- documented fact;
- attributed allegation;
- evidence-based inference;
- unresolved hypothesis;
- potential legal characterisation requiring competent investigation; or
- adjudicated finding.

Do not present a potential offence as established guilt.

## 11. CI / monitor continuity safeguard

The repository contains dedicated FTI / Meeting Point continuity and asset-transaction workflows. A failed run is itself an unresolved continuity event.

Therefore:

- never report `scan complete`, `monitor current`, `live verified` or equivalent if the relevant specialist workflow is failing;
- record the failing run/SHA and identify whether failure is source availability, validator logic, dependency/runtime, route/render, data-contract or substantive inconsistency;
- distinguish a technical monitor failure from evidence of an asset transaction;
- do not allow repeated failed scheduled runs to disappear into email noise; and
- after repair, require a fresh successful run and preserve its bounded scope. A successful bounded monitor does not prove the absence of unmonitored transactions.

## 12. Conclusion discipline

Every substantial update to this workstream must end with five headings:

1. **What is proved**;
2. **What is supported but not proved**;
3. **What is contradicted or disproved**;
4. **What remains genuinely unresolved**; and
5. **Which single next document or response would most change the conclusion**.

The objective is to reach narrower, stronger conclusions — including a negative conclusion where the evidence disproves a hypothesis — rather than preserve every allegation indefinitely.
