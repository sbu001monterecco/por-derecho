# Concurso 36/2012 — 7 June continuity governance closeout

**Cut-off:** 29 August 2026  
**Branch:** `codex/concurso36-7june-continuity-governance-20260829`

## Purpose

This layer closes a continuity defect in the public repository: the pre/post-7-June-2018 notice question must not be reduced to whether LPB/Aweswell counsel filed one particular pleading. The controlled audit now treats notice and protection as a multi-channel institutional chain and separately audits the Insolvency Administrator, debtor/shareholder counsel, the Mercantile Court and the Arrecife criminal proceedings.

## Evidence-control rules

1. Allegation is not adjudicated fact.
2. Draft is not filed pleading.
3. An order directing judicial assistance is not, by itself, proof of receipt or response.
4. `No response located` does not mean `no response existed`.
5. Actual notice, constructive notice, formal filing and testimonial knowledge are different evidentiary classes.
6. The LPB insolvency perimeter does not automatically absorb the whole mixed-title Sun Park complex.
7. Counsel and Insolvency Administrator responsibilities are audited independently.

This was professional analysis, not a judicial finding.

The safe public conclusion is therefore stronger than “constructive notice” but narrower than claiming verbatim proof of everything said to the judge: **multiple independent documentary and testimonial channels support actual institutional notice by 13 June 2018; the exact formal and oral content must still be closed source-by-source.**

## Insolvency Administrator — independent accountability lane

The audit no longer treats the IA as a passive recipient whose role ended because debtor counsel could also file. The 2018 Ley Concursal baseline includes diligence (art. 35), responsibility for insufficiently diligent acts/omissions damaging the estate (art. 36), conservation/administration of the estate and judicial assistance (art. 43), and liquidation reporting to the judge (art. 152).

This does **not** by itself establish a statutory same-day reporting breach. It creates the required questions: what did the IA verify after notice; what did he report to the judge; what directions/protective measures did he seek; how did he record access, possession, security, operation, income, works, deterioration and funded exits; and how did he separate LPB property from Community, Matkator and third-party property?

## Definitive texts — correction

The former categorical proposition that no definitive-text modification procedure existed is rejected by primary orders:

- 20 January 2017: modification concerning the Community of Owners' contingent ordinary pre-insolvency dues, without own amount.
- 8 February 2018: Promontoria→CAM creditor succession. The order expressly states that assignment changes creditor identity only, **not amount**, and fixes special privilege at **€857,373.81 + €8,194,877.88 = €9,052,251.69**, plus contingent enforcement costs without own amount.

No later order has yet been located in the audited corpus formally modifying that fixed amount to **€13,168,082.02**, the figure later used as set-off in the adjudication/deed route. The proper public issue is a reconciliation gap — principal, interest, costs, orders and set-off basis — not a pre-judged finding of unlawful increase.
