## 5. What this source directly establishes

1. Before the 26-Apr meeting, Gil asked the AC for signed permission both for his own attendance and for attendance by the PwC lawyers.
2. Borja responded that Gil did not need AC permission merely to attend, while asserting that unpaid Community fees prevented him voting and therefore prevented patrimonial decisions requiring AC intervention.
3. Borja expressly refused to authorise **contracting** PwC on the information then supplied.
4. The reasons stated by Borja were lack of LPB/Luchy treasury, avoidance of additional credits against the estate, lack of demonstrated necessity and lack of proposed-fee information.
5. Borja directly notified the three copied PwC professionals and asked them not to provide services to Luchy without his express consent, warning of legal measures otherwise.
6. Borja simultaneously asserted that his own court-approved fees were earlier credits against the estate.
7. The debt, voting, accounting-source and PwC-attendance issues were therefore joined in direct pre-meeting correspondence, not reconstructed only after the event.
