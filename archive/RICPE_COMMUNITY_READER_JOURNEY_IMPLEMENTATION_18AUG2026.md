## 4. Publication-safety rules preserved

The reader journey deliberately does not:

- create `/comunidad-penal/` or `/ricpe-criminal/`;
- present disputed Community authority as adjudicated falsity;
- convert physical/material control into judicial possession or title;
- convert later title into retroactive validation of earlier acts;
- convert funding coexistence into established double/triple funding;
- convert institutional receipt into validation;
- convert later benefit into earlier participation;
- attribute knowledge or intent by association.

The separate criminal/investigative lens shown publicly states that act, knowledge, contrary notice, representation/omission, use, reliance, benefit, harm, causation and lawful alternative explanation must be assessed actor by actor.
