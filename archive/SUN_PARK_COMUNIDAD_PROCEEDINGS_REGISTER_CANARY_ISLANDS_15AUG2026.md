## Classification

- `DIRECT` — directly concerns Sun Park, the Comunidad/CEXP, LPB, Matkator, the credit, access/control, title, operation or later asset transfer.
- `CONCURSO_BRIDGE` — connects another file to Concurso 36/2012 through evidence, testimony, credit, title or consequence.
- `ADMINISTRATIVE_PERIMETER` — tourism, urbanism, works, operator, unit, transparency or public-record file.
- `SUPERVISION` — judicial governance, LAJ supervision or professional regulation.
- `REGISTERED_ONLY` — submission/receipt exists; an assigned investigation/file or merits act is not located.
- `CONTEXTUAL` — relevant downstream operator/court/professional context but not a Sun Park merits determination.
- `TECHNICAL_REFERENCE_NOT_PROCEEDING` — identifier located, but no separate proceeding is established.

Source status:

- `VERIFIED_PRIMARY`
- `VERIFIED_PROCEDURAL`
- `CORPUS_REPORTED_PRIMARY_PENDING`
- `OPEN_REFERENCE`
- `CONTEXT_ONLY`

`^` confirms only reconciliation to an immutable CAEPR identity; it does not confirm any role, authority, allegation, act, liability, procedural status, outcome or finality.

# VI. CROSS-PROCEEDING QUESTIONS

For each direct proceeding answer:

1. What exact event, asset, unit, right, entity and legal capacity was within scope?
2. What evidence was actually before the organ?
3. What was decided?
4. What was not decided?
5. Was the decision final, provisional, appealed, accumulated or superseded?
6. Which later actor relied on it?
7. What filing, testimonio, remisión, deed, Registry entry or administrative communication proves that reliance?
8. Did a later use broaden the original holding?
9. Did the act affect LPB, Matkator, Aweswell, a Community/CEXP interest or a third party differently?
10. What finite primary record can close the remaining issue?

## Cross-island control rule

The same names, hotel or document appearing in several files does not make them one proceeding. Conversely, separate proceedings must not be treated as evidentially isolated where a primary transmission bridge proves that one supplied a premise or document to another.
