## Public-interest framing

The letter now states clearly that the matter is not framed merely as a private dispute over a hotel. Sun Park is treated as the originating asset node for a wider documentary question concerning what was represented about ownership, availability, operation, financing and commercial continuity; what each later gatekeeper independently checked; and what tax, regulatory, financing and public-support decisions subsequently relied on those premises.

The public chain is stated as:

`SUN PARK / LPB / MATKATOR → CAM / HNT → RICPE → RIC → REGIONAL INCENTIVES → FEDER/ERDF → MYND → later management, financing and market structures`

This is a traceability architecture, not a finding that every link is unlawful.

## Allegation / evidence boundary

Gil Marer's working allegation is that originating acts may have enabled later tax-incentive, financing or public-support materialisation on false or incomplete premises. The page does **not** present as established:
- RIC fraud;
- subsidy fraud;
- EU-budget loss;
- false pretence by every later actor;
- CNMV approval of upstream title/property facts;
- collective knowledge or common intent;
- automatic invalidity of later finance, grants or operation because an upstream issue is alleged.

The page instead asks the serial-reliance question: what did each gatekeeper independently verify against original sources, and what premise did it inherit?

The page expressly says that a person need not know the whole Sun Park history. A later-stage item can still be evidentially important if it helps establish who knew what, when, from which document, concerning which asset and with what authority.
