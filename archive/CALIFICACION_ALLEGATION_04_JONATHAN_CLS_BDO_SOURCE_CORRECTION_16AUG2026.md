## Knowledge / accountability consequence

The court's adverse finding cannot safely be paraphrased by Por Derecho as `the Libro Diario was missing` or `LPB did not keep the Diario`.

The source-complete formulation is:

- **Court finding:** Diario not supplied — adverse, appealed.
- **Contemporaneous contrary evidence:** substantial and specific.
- **Objective historical question:** unresolved pending the complete year-by-year transmission/receipt file.

If the complete CLS/BDO/AC transmission trail proves that the relevant Diario was in fact delivered, the first-instance finding would be factually contradicted by primary evidence. That conclusion should be made only after matching the exact files, dates, recipients and acknowledgements.
