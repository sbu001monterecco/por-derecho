## 1. Controlling distinction

For this evidence family, always preserve:

> **prepared / proposed ≠ admitted ≠ formally scheduled ≠ citation ordered ≠ served ≠ available ≠ attended ≠ called ≠ testified.**

A defence preparation document is not proof of judicial admission. A person named in a court heading is not automatically proof that the person testified. Lack of a visible remote label in the supplied audiovisual is not proof of absence from the courtroom. A judgment stating that evidence was practised does not, without the minute/transcript, identify every witness actually heard.

## 2. Primary procedural anchors now verified

A signed/official court scheduling order dated **5 April 2023**, following suspension of an earlier 9-March hearing because of the lawyers' strike, reset the Vista for **25 and 26 July 2023 at 09:45**.

The order:

- ordered that witnesses/experts be cited;

- assigned in-person or videoconference mode;

This is stronger than the internal defence document `TESTIGOS Y PREGUNTAS CALIFICACION`: it is a formal court scheduling/citation order.

### 2.3 Sentencia 163/2023 — official account of the completed hearing date

Antecedente de Hecho Décimo of **Sentencia 163/2023** states:

> `La vista se celebró el día 25 de julio de 2023... Una vez practicada la prueba y tras informe de las partes, quedaron los autos vistos para sentencia.`

This is a material procedural fact. It means that although the 5-April order had reserved **25 and 26 July**, the judgment records the Vista as having been completed on **25 July** and the matter then left for judgment.

What the judgment does **not** explain in that passage is how the 26-July programme was disposed of person by person: whether particular evidence was advanced to 25 July, waived, excluded, unnecessary, unavailable, not served, or otherwise not practised.

### 2.4 24 July 2023 Espejo → court communication — access to filed CD evidence

A contemporaneous email/PDF chain records that on **24 July 2023**, David Espejo Navarro wrote directly to court personnel after receiving a telephone call from the court. He recorded that the caller had said they were unable to open certain documents attached to a CD previously filed in the procedure, including expert evidence produced by his side.

Espejo wrote that, with such short notice, it was impossible to resupply the documentation and that he did not know how many of the documents on the CD were inaccessible.

**Evidence class:** VERIFIED CONTEMPORANEOUS PROFESSIONAL COMMUNICATION recording what Espejo says court personnel told him. It is not itself a judicial finding about the complete state of the file.

**Publication control:** do not adopt Patricia Domínguez's separate inference that the Judge personally had not read the case material. The proved proposition is the Espejo communication about a reported document-access problem immediately before the Vista.

## 5. Important corrections to earlier shorthand

### Correction A — “the other scheduled witnesses did not attend” is too strong

The current record does not justify a blanket statement that all persons not visible by remote label failed to attend.

The court formally scheduled a larger two-day programme. The judgment then records completion on 25 July. The certified hearing minute, full audiovisual/index, service returns and evidential rulings are still required to establish why each 26-July item was not separately practised on that date.

### Correction B — 26 July is not presently a proven “missing hearing day”

The 5-Apr order reserved 26 July, but Sentencia 163/2023 says the Vista was held/completed on 25 July and left for judgment. Therefore do not describe 26 July as a missing video or erased hearing without evidence that a hearing actually took place that day.

The correct open question is:

> **How was the formally scheduled 26-July witness/interrogation programme disposed of when the court treated the Vista as completed on 25 July?**

### Correction C — court-access problem is not proof of judicial non-reading

Espejo's 24-Jul email proves a contemporaneous report that court personnel could not open some documents on the filed CD. It does not prove which documents the Judge personally reviewed, when, or what effect the access issue had on the decision.

2. the retained lawyer's 21-Jul-2023 renunciation four days before the hearing;

6. supplied audiovisual components that visually establish some key participants but remain without a verified spoken transcript;

**Permissible conclusion now:** these facts create a concrete evidential basis for investigating whether the defence had an effective opportunity to deploy the admitted/scheduled evidence and whether the record used for adjudication was procedurally complete.

**Not yet permissible as verified fact:** that the Judge intentionally prevented witnesses, intentionally refused to consider evidence, knowingly adjudicated without reading the evidence, or manipulated the recording.
