## 1. Controlling decision

The Master Proceedings Register is no longer to be treated as a register that must remain absent from the public site. The user expressly authorises and instructs publication of a dedicated, bilingual, interlinked public proceedings register in support of the multitrack storyline/timeline, the project's stated position and its truth-seeking objectives.

This governance supersedes earlier wording that prohibited a public aggregate page, automatic navigation link or sitemap/public-page promotion merely because the register was previously designed as an internal knowledge-management index.

## 2. One canonical master; two treatment layers

The canonical operational source remains:

`archive/PROCEEDINGS_MASTER_REGISTER.csv`

The public page is a **controlled projection**, not a second competing master. It may read the canonical CSV and render only the fields suitable for public procedural navigation.

The public projection must preserve, at minimum where populated:

- Master ID / legacy identity;
- whether the record is `TRUE`, `FALSE` or `UNVERIFIED` as a proceeding/file;
- record type and proceeding class;
- stream/track and geography;
- origin organ and current custodian;
- reference / secondary reference / NIG where suitable;
- date or period;
- connection and object/purpose;
- current recorded status and latest known event;
- appeal/review and parent/child or linked-proceeding relationships;
- source status; and
- open reference/evidence gaps.

The public projection must **not** automatically render internal notes, private strategy fields, private source anchors, privileged legal advice, personal contact details or rows explicitly classified for internal-only treatment.

## 3. Meaning of publication

Publication is a transparency and navigation function. It does not convert the register into a merits finding.

A public row does **not** mean:

- wrongdoing is established;
- an authority has accepted the factual or legal merits;
- a complaint has become a criminal prosecution;
- a registration receipt is itself a separate proceeding;
- a referral is an admission;
- an appeal has succeeded;
- an unresolved candidate reference has been verified; or
- legally distinct tracks become one case merely because they concern the same hotel, actors or events.

The public page must visibly explain `TRUE`, `FALSE` and `UNVERIFIED` and retain source-status / open-gap language.

## 4. Public-treatment compatibility rule

The historic value:

`INTERNAL_KNOWLEDGE_REGISTER_NOT_AUTO_PUBLISHED`

is a **legacy non-automatic-publication label** and no longer prevents inclusion in the user-authorised controlled public aggregate projection.

Rows carrying explicit treatments such as:

`INTERNAL_ONLY_NOT_SITE_AGGREGATED`

or another unambiguous private/internal-only classification remain excluded from the public projection unless separately reclassified with source- and privacy-aware review.

## 5. Multitrack storyline / timeline integration

The public proceedings register is a procedural spine for the wider Project Sun Rock chronology. It must be interlinked with the recovery/story timeline and relevant proceeding-specific pages so readers can move both ways:

**timeline / storyline → procedural tracks → individual proceeding/file → source/evidence page**

and

**proceeding/file → parent/child / appeal / referral relationships → timeline / storyline context**.

The objective is not to collapse multiple legal tracks into a single theory. The objective is to expose their documented intersections, sequence, institutional routing, contradictions, gaps and different legal effects.

## 6. Truth-seeking and evidential discipline

The public register should assist truth-seeking by making uncertainty visible. Open source gaps are to be published where suitable rather than cosmetically removed.

Future work must continue to distinguish:

- verified primary or procedural facts;
- source-reported material still awaiting primary completion;
- project allegations or positions;
- legal/investigative inference;
- contrary evidence; and
- unresolved proof.

No publication convenience permits upgrading a source state merely to make the public register appear complete.

## 7. Counsel / procurador lineage remains mandatory

Publication of the proceedings register does not displace the controlling counsel/procurador governance. Future proceedings maintenance must continue to apply:

`archive/COUNSEL_PROCURADOR_FILING_LINEAGE_GOVERNANCE_30AUG2026.md`

and the linked professional, filing, procurador and gap registers.

The public master may expose procedural relationships without automatically publishing private counsel communications or privileged strategy. Where representation is publicly shown, it must remain source-led, period-specific and individually attributable.

## 8. Continuity requirements for future threads

Any future thread that materially changes a proceeding/file, reference, status, custodian, parent/child link, appeal, source status or open gap must assess the public register impact.

A proceedings-maintenance pass is not complete until it asks:

1. Does the canonical CSV need updating?
2. Does the public projection change automatically from that update?
3. Is the row public-eligible or explicitly internal-only?
4. Are parent/child, appeal, referral and linked-proceeding relationships still accurate?
5. Does the timeline/storyline need a contextual link or correction?
6. Does counsel/procurador filing lineage need propagation?
7. Are source-status and open-gap language still honest and current?
8. Has the public publication audit passed?
