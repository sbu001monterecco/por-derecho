# Project Sun Rock / Por Derecho — Recipient-Fit Dossier Playbook

**Control date:** 23 August 2026  
**Status:** canonical internal outreach-personalisation layer  
**Purpose:** convert the unitary case record into audience-specific, evidence-led outreach without factual drift or mechanical templating  
**Send gate:** `EMAIL_SEND_FINAL_AUTHORIZATION_RULE.md`

## Why this file exists

The repository already controls evidence status, corrections, email safety and maximum-reach strategy. What was missing was a reusable bridge between the **unitary case** and the **recipient’s actual interests**.

Future outreach should therefore use:

`UNITARY CASE → RECIPIENT ROLE/BEAT → RELEVANT STORY MODULE → 3–5 CHECKABLE ANCHORS → SMALL SOURCE KIT → FINITE ASK`.

The goal is not to change the facts for different audiences. The goal is to select the part of the same source-controlled record that the recipient can realistically understand, test, report, regulate, preserve or act on.

## Mandatory recipient-fit questions

Before drafting, answer:

1. Who is this person or organisation now?
2. What do they actually write about, regulate, invest in, advise on or investigate?
3. What current role gives them a reason to care?
4. Is there a second role — think tank, university, professional body, board, foundation, investor platform — that creates a distinct but legitimate angle?
5. What is the smallest verifiable story module that fits that role?
6. What is the strongest contrary/adverse material they should know?
7. What exactly are we asking them to do?
8. What source package lets them decide whether to engage without receiving a document dump?

## Audience modules

### 1. Journalist / editor — general public-interest

**Core angle:**
`Sun Park → fragmented ownership/operation → alleged long-term pressure or interference → material control transition → later investment/transformation → MYND Yaiza → public-interest question`.

**Best first ask:**
“Would you examine the short documentary chain and tell us whether you want the primary-source dossier?”

**Do not:** lead with every proceeding or every allegation.

### 2. Economic / markets / investment journalist

**Core angle:**
`asset provenance → credit/title/control → RIC/RICPE → Hotel New Trend / MYND → private finance → incentives → due diligence / disclosure / source-and-use → legal certainty / recovery`.

**Questions that fit this audience:**
- What did each investor/adviser know at each stage?
- What was represented about title, control, project maturity, availability and litigation?
- Were successive investment/funding representations mutually consistent?
- How were downside risks, adverse proceedings and third-party rights assessed?
- What does a long gap between asserted rights and effective recovery mean for investment certainty?

**Mandatory limitation:** public finance, RIC investment, restructuring or later commercial success do not by themselves prove wrongdoing.

### 3. Legal-certainty / regulation / think-tank audience

**Core angle:**
`formal legal rights and fragmented title → institutional handling → creditor/control transition → investment and regulatory approvals → effective protection/recovery`.

Use where the recipient writes or works on:
- legal certainty;
- property rights;
- regulatory quality;
- investment climate;
- institutional effectiveness;
- rule-of-law economics;
- transaction certainty;
- enforcement / effective recovery.

**Reusable comparative discipline:** a domestic hotel/asset dispute is not automatically equivalent to international investment arbitration or another sector. State the analogy as a shared question about legal certainty, enforcement and investment expectations, not as legal identity.

### 4. Tourism / hospitality journalist

**Core angle:**
`Sun Park operating history → tour-operator/commercialisation evidence → control/brand transition → Club Sei / Meeting Point / FTI where sourced → MYND Yaiza → current hotel economics and tourism narrative`.

Focus on what was actually marketed, operated and represented to the market at each stage.

### 5. Investigative / cross-border journalist

**Core angle:**
`same asset + multiple legal/financial lives + cross-border actors + public/private finance + professional knowledge + institutional notice`.

Use stronger provenance tools:
- exact dates;
- entity identities;
- public registers;
- court/institutional documents;
- transaction/investment materials;
- source maps as orientation only.

### 6. Regulator / prosecutor / public authority

**Core angle:** function-specific only.

The email must answer:
`WHAT IS WITHIN YOUR COMPETENCE → WHAT EXACT RECORD SHOULD BE ASSOCIATED/PRESERVED/REVIEWED → WHAT FORMAL CHANNEL CONTROLS`.

Ordinary email awareness must never be confused with a legally operative filing.

### 7. Professional body / auditor / law firm / compliance

**Core angle:**
`knowledge → instructions → conflicts → due diligence → professional role → preservation → correction/right of reply`.

Do not ask the recipient to decide the entire case. Ask finite questions about their own role, records or professional responsibility.

### 8. Investor / financial-market / transaction participant

**Core angle:**
`asset provenance → title/control → litigation/insolvency perimeter → project/investment representations → incentives → source-and-use → current value/recovery exposure`.

Separate notice from accusation. Emphasise verifiable diligence questions.

### 9. Witness / historical participant

**Core angle:** their own recollection and records.

Avoid leading packages. First ask what they remember, what records they hold and whether they will preserve them.

## Dual-role recipient rule

A recipient may legitimately have more than one relevant role — for example journalist + think-tank director, academic + media commentator, investor + industry association office-holder.

When a dual role exists:

1. verify both roles currently;
2. identify the overlap rather than combining unrelated themes;
3. state why the matter may be relevant to each role;
4. do not imply the second organisation endorses the first role or the Project;
5. keep the ask finite — e.g. journalistic examination, analytical case study, referral to the correct colleague.

### Proven example: Diego Sánchez de la Cruz

The 23-Aug-2026 routing email used a dual-role fit:

- journalist/economic analyst writing on legal certainty, investment, property, regulation and institutional failure; and
- co-founder/director of Foro Regulación Inteligente, whose public remit includes regulatory quality and investment/business conditions, including Canary Islands regulatory-climate work.

The email did **not** claim Sun Park was legally equivalent to renewable-energy arbitration disputes. It used the narrower shared question: what happens to investment certainty when asserted or recognised rights remain disconnected for years from effective protection or recovery?

This is a reusable model for future dual-role recipients.

## Historical-address routing rule

Where the only documented address is historical:

- label it internally as historical;
- do not say it is the person’s current direct address;
- use it only where the organisation/address is legitimately connected to the person’s former role;
- make the message a routing/permission request: may you forward this, or who is the appropriate contact?;
- include a concise explanation sufficient for the organisation to assess relevance;
- do not automatically resend to a new address supplied by an auto-reply without fresh user approval.

A historical address can be a **routing channel**, not a fabricated current contact.

## Dossier paragraph pattern

For a recipient who needs more context than a short first-screen, use one dense but controlled paragraph:

`ASSET / PERIMETER → WHAT CHANGED → LATER INVESTMENT/CONTROL/FINANCE → WHY THIS RECIPIENT’S BEAT MATTERS → WHAT IS VERIFIED/ALLEGED/OPEN → ONE OR TWO SOURCES → FINITE ASK`.

The paragraph may be long if necessary, but it must remain readable and must not collapse evidence classes.

## Public website lesson from the 23-Aug scan

The current live `/en/collaborate/` route is already strong for outreach because it explicitly offers:

- `Journalistic verification and responsible coverage`;
- public-sources-first working boundaries;
- no inherited conclusions;
- private-by-default collaboration;
- correction/right-of-reply routes; and
- a contact-first model.

This route should be considered for journalists who first need confidence in **how** the Project handles evidence rather than a deep specialist dossier.

The still-open strategic gap is a neutral bilingual media briefing route that gives a 60–90 second case orientation plus the five strongest anchors.

## Success criterion

A good outreach email should allow the recipient to answer one question quickly:

> “Is there enough here, within my actual role, to justify looking at the source record?”

If the email requires them to understand the entire repository before deciding whether to engage, it has failed as outreach.
