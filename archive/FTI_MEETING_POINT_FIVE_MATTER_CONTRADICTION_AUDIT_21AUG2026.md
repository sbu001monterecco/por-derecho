# FTI / Meeting Point: five-matter contradiction audit

**Status:** source-controlled working audit, 21 August 2026
**Scope:** (1) commercialisation, (2) FTI / Meeting Point notice and custody, (3) entity separation, (4) Meeting Point Spain 357/2024, and (5) the January 2025 *elEconomista* chain.
**Publication rule:** this record describes source status and finite production requests. It does not publish raw correspondence, private attachments, access tokens, personal contact data, or privileged material.

## Reading rule

Every proposition below is classified as one of:

- **Verified source fact:** a primary court record, controlled contemporaneous record, or independently preserved public source supports it.
- **Attributed position:** a party, correspondent, listing, or publication made the statement; the statement is not thereby proved true.
- **Evidence-based inference:** the source permits a limited inference, stated with its limits.
- **Open production question:** a finite original record is required before a conclusion can be drawn.

No category may be used to imply a judicial conflict, personal knowledge, unlawful exploitation, non-compliance with an insolvency proceeding, or criminal responsibility unless the necessary primary evidence is obtained and tested fairly.

## Executive result

The strongest controlled proposition is narrower and more useful than a blended accusation:

1. Multiple contemporaneous public materials place **Club Sei Lanzarote / Meeting Point Hotels** at the Calle Janubio 3 / Playa Blanca perimeter and present it for sale or booking. This is materially inconsistent with any unqualified proposition that the brand was never commercialised there.
2. Those materials do **not** establish the exact contracting entity, title, operational authority, actual stays, revenue, possession, legality, or the scope of any relationship with Sun Park. The 279-room project, 315-room product and 54-apartment court account require a title-and-inventory crosswalk.
3. The FTI / Meeting Point correspondence establishes notice, preservation requests and a route to the Meeting Point administrator. A direct FTI Consulting response also states that the historical conduct pre-dated FTI Consulting's involvement. That counterevidence must remain attached to every custody proposition.
4. The signed 18 February 2025 order in 357/2024 verifies the Auren appointment and the identity of its electronic signer. It removes any basis for saying that the two judicial names alone establish an unexplained irregularity. The October signed decision and allocation record are still required.
5. The *elEconomista* material supports a limited document-provenance and scope question: a material called an `auto` in the email chain was in fact a September 2023 judgment in LPB Concurso 36/2012, with appellate context. It does not prove who physically transmitted it, newsroom collusion, or an obligation to publish.

The current record supports disciplined disclosure, preservation and source-production requests. It does not presently support treating any person or entity as proved to have committed a crime or breach.

## 1. Commercialisation: Club Sei / Meeting Point at the perimeter

### Verified source facts

| Source | What it supports | What it does not support |
|---|---|---|
| Meeting Point Hotels / FTI Club Sei brochure, January 2020 | A Meeting Point Hotels portfolio included Club Sei Lanzarote, described with 315 rooms in Playa Blanca. | A contract for a specified building, actual sales, revenue, operator status, or the exact legal entity on the other side. |
| Contemporary SonnenklarTV capture, January 2020 | Club Sei Lanzarote was presented at Calle Janubio 3, Playa Blanca, with 315 units. | Title, authority, actual availability, or the provenance of the stored copy beyond the controlled capture. |

| CAM public project material and 2021 court account | CAM publicly described Lava Verde as a 279-room project; its 2021 court account referred to 54 apartments within a mixed LPB / third-party complex. | That either figure maps automatically to the Club Sei listing, or that either account is false. |

### Contradictions and controlled inferences

| Tension | Best controlled restatement | Required source to resolve it |
|---|---|---|
| Public 2020 product and booking materials versus a blanket denial of commercialisation | Any denial must identify the relevant brand, property, date, sales channel and legal entity. The public record is enough to test an unqualified denial, not enough to establish trading. | Executed contracts, allotments, PMS/channel-manager logs, OTA extranet history, reservations, invoices, commissions and bank records. |
| 279 rooms, 315 rooms and 54 apartments | The figures concern potentially different commercial, physical and title perimeters. They cannot be treated as interchangeable. | `room/unit -> finca -> owner at date -> access authority -> project inclusion -> operating/distribution authority -> later title` crosswalk. |
| January 2020 market presentation versus October 2020 postponement reporting | The record may reflect pre-launch distribution, a stale listing, test availability, partial operation, or another explanation. ### Finite requests

1. Identify each Meeting Point, FTI, Youtravel, OTA, hotel-management and counterparty legal entity by legal name, registration number and capacity.
2. Produce the contract chain, property schedule, allotment/inventory schedule, access authority and any termination or postponement notice.
3. Preserve and produce native OTA/channel-manager/PMS history, reservations, cancellations, guest arrivals, invoices, commissions and bank settlement records for the relevant dates.
4. Produce a finca/title and unit/room reconciliation that explains the 279/315/54 descriptions without asserting that one proves the other.

## 2. FTI / Meeting Point notice and custody

### Verified source facts

- Controlled 2024 correspondence records a reference to `2024-94`, requests for material and a route for communications. It proves the correspondence and requests, not the asserted facts or a response on the merits.
- A January 2025 preservation notice was sent to a Meeting Point recipient. Its assertions are the sender's positions; its requests and transmission are the verified elements.
- A direct FTI Consulting response states that the historical conduct referred to pre-dated FTI Consulting's involvement and says the message was passed to the Meeting Point administrator. A later FTI Consulting response declines further disclosure by reference to confidentiality and notes the comments received.

### Contradictions and limits

| Proposition to test | Controlled result |
|---|---|
| FTI Consulting participated in the historical commercial events | The direct response is counterevidence: it distinguishes FTI Consulting's timing. Do not collapse FTI Consulting into FTI Touristik or Meeting Point. |
| A preservation notice proves the recipient held or destroyed records | Delivery and the request are proved; custody, review, destruction, transfer and substantive response are not. |
| No located reply proves no reply was made | Absence from the controlled corpus is not proof that no reply was made by another route. |
| A sender's description of an individual's corporate role proves that role | It is an attributed statement until confirmed by a contemporaneous registry or corporate record. |

### Finite requests

1. Preserve mailbox, ticketing, legal-hold, document-management and forwarding logs for the 2024-25 notices.
2. Identify the recipient entity, capacity, administrator/insolvency role and legal basis for any confidentiality position.
3. Produce the acknowledgement, preservation decision, custodian list, search protocol, transfer record and result index, or confirm that no such record exists.
4. Keep FTI Consulting's timing response alongside all future requests to prevent entity conflation.

## 3. Entity separation

### Verified source facts

- The signed 18 February 2025 order names Meeting Point Hotelmanagement (Canaries), S.L.U., Meeting Point Spain, S.L.U. and Meeting Point Investment, S.L.U. as the entities making the Article 583 communication.
- Public commercial sources use brand and group terms including Club Sei, Meeting Point Hotels, MP Hotels, Youtravel and FTI.
- FTI Consulting describes itself separately from the historic matters in its direct response.

### Control rule

Do not equate a brand, marketing name, parent/group reference, director description or recipient address with a contracting or operating legal person. Each proposed link must show:

`actor -> legal entity -> capacity -> document -> property/inventory -> date -> authority -> transaction/record`

### Finite requests

1. Current and historic company extracts, director appointments and group structure at each relevant date.
2. Signatory authority and exact contracting capacity on every contract, listing feed and payment instruction.
3. Intercompany, assignment, servicing and data-custody agreements capable of locating records after restructuring.

## 4. Meeting Point Spain 357/2024

### Verified source facts

- A signed order dated 18 February 2025 in Commercial Court 3, Las Palmas, proceeding 357/2024, appoints Auren Reestructuraciones, S.L.P. as restructuring expert. It records the three entities' joint Article 583 communication of 23 July 2024, treated as filed on 31 July 2024, and their 23 January 2025 Auren request. The order is electronically signed by Guillermo Fernandez Garcia.
- An archived Public Insolvency Register notice for the same debtor, court and proceeding attributes the 24 October 2024 extension decision to Alberto Lopez Villarrubia and refers to an 18 September 2024 decree.
- The register itself limits its assurance as to the integrity of data received from its source.

### Corrected restatement

The signed February order confirms the later appointment and signer. The different judicial names on the February order and October register notice do **not** by themselves establish substitution, reassignment, data error, conflict, personal knowledge, coordination, misconduct or a duty connected with Sun Park.

The remaining targeted question is procedural traceability: obtain the signed 18 September decree, signed 24 October decision, allocation/reassignment/correction record, Article 583 annexes and Auren working-file index. If Meeting Point Spain was a counterparty or record custodian, that is a separate evidential question, not an assumed fact.

### Finite requests

1. Signed 18 September 2024 decree and initial allocation record.
2. Signed 24 October 2024 extension decision.
3. Any substitution, reinforcement, secondment, reassignment or register-correction decision.
4. Article 583 communication, schedules and annexes, restricted as required by law but indexed sufficiently to test whether Sun Park / Club Sei records exist.
5. Auren acceptance, mandate, information requests, records-received index, report and workpaper index.

## 5. January 2025 *elEconomista* chain

### Verified source facts

- In the controlled 17 January exchange, Javier Romera said that he had not been censored, that the subject was being checked, and asked to await judicial material expected on Monday.
- In the controlled 20 January exchange, Romera said a document had been sent and described it as an `auto` declaring the `concurso de Sun Park` culpable; the material held with that exchange is Sentencia 163/2023 of 28 September, concerning the qualification of LPB Concurso 36/2012 and with an appeal route/context already in existence by January 2025.
- The controlled record does not identify the physical sender of the document. The best current inference is a response channel associated with CAM / Acosta Matos, not a person-specific transmission.
- A later Madrid threshold order does not establish that a named individual personally transmitted the document; it refers to the material as a `Sentencia`, not an `Auto`.

### Contradictions and controlled inference

| Tension | Best controlled restatement |
|---|---|
| `auto` / `Sun Park concurso` versus the attachment | The description did not match the controlled attachment's form, date, proceeding scope or appellate context. That is a material scope and labelling issue. |
| Statement that material was checked or tied down versus a decision not to publish | The chain supports a request for the native inbound transmission, verification steps and decision record. It does not establish a duty to publish or a coordinated suppression. |
| Assertions about a CAM / Acosta Matos intervention | The response-channel inference may justify a source request. It does not identify a physical sender, an instruction, bad faith or newsroom participation. |

### Finite requests

1. Native inbound message, headers, attachment hashes and transmission metadata for the January 2025 document.
2. The newsroom verification and decision record, subject to journalistic-source protections and applicable law.
3. Certified status, scope and appellate history of Sentencia 163/2023 as at 20 January 2025.
4. Authenticated transcript and provenance for preserved audio files before any reliance on their contents.

## Cross-matter production sequence
