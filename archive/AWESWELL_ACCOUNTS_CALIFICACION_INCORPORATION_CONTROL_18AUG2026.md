## 1. Controlling request

Por Derecho calls upon the competent court and, where procedurally applicable, the Provincial Court hearing the pending calificación appeal to:

1. **formally acknowledge** the official Companies House filing by Aweswell Limited of the document titled *Consolidated Provisional Group Statutory Accounts for the year ended 31 July 2025*;
2. receive the officially filed English version, the Board-authorised Spanish translation, the Companies House filing record and the supporting board/accounting materials;
3. **admit, incorporate or otherwise receive** that material through the legally available procedural route;
4. assess it together with the underlying Spanish primary record and independent forensic-accounting evidence;
5. issue an **express and reasoned determination** on its procedural status, relevance, scope and probative weight; and
6. if the proposed route is unavailable, identify the correct route through which the accounts, subsidiary records and expert reconstruction may be considered.

The request is deliberately more precise than asking the court simply to “accept the accounts”. It does not ask any court to treat every directors’ statement as established fact. It asks that an official, dated and bilingual filing, prepared by the parent and sole shareholder of the company subject to Concurso 36/2012, be received, tested and expressly addressed rather than excluded by silence.

### 2.2 Board and PropCo1 accounting materials

Source: Aweswell Board / PropCo1 accounting annex — 27 pages (exact private filename withheld).

The Board material dated 20 April 2026 records an intended accounting and procedural bridge:

- pursuit of the legal authorisation and filing of the Spanish subsidiaries’ annual accounts;
- proposed submission of those accounts and/or an expert report to the Las Palmas court in the LPB/PropCo1 context;
- use of valuations, balance-sheet, profit-and-loss and related material to substantiate the claimed value of the hotel and entitlement to trading income; and
- a wider evidential and damages roadmap.

The Board annex is a **party-prepared evidential and claim roadmap**. It is not independent proof of every allegation, number, legal conclusion or damages head that it contains. Its proper significance is to evidence corporate authorisation, intended use, scope of the reconstruction and the need for supporting primary and expert material.

## 3. Why the material is relevant to the calificación

The filing is potentially relevant to propositions concerning:

- accounting conduct and the practical availability of records;
- the allocation of accounting power, responsibility and access during the concurso;
- Aweswell’s continuing parent, shareholder, creditor and funding role;
- cooperation, good faith, attempted remediation and non-abandonment;
- patrimonial coverage versus date-specific cash-flow capacity;
- rescue, convenio, refinancing and creditor-payment alternatives;
- productive-unit, hotel-operation and wider platform value;
- treatment of contingent recoveries and prudence;
- causation and the alleged generation or aggravation of insolvency; and
- whether the first-instance narrative had the benefit of the complete accounting, patrimonial and recovery record.

The accounts are not self-proving. Their relevance lies in the propositions they identify, the formal public record they create and the primary evidence they organise for scrutiny.

## 4. Requested reasoned determinations

The competent court should be asked to decide expressly:

1. whether the filed accounts and supporting materials are admitted, incorporated or otherwise received;
2. the procedural title and legal basis under which they are considered;
3. the propositions for which they may properly be used;
4. the evidence that remains necessary to authenticate, reconcile or test those propositions;
5. their relevance to the accounting, cooperation, solvency, rescue-alternative and causation issues in the calificación;
6. whether the existing factual narrative should be confirmed, qualified, reconsidered or corrected; and
7. if the proposed route is refused, the legally appropriate alternative route.

## 5. Strict non-overclaim controls

The public and procedural position must state prominently that the filing does **not**, by itself:

- constitute an audit;
- mean Companies House verified the directors’ factual assertions;
- replace LPB’s complete Spanish annual accounts;
- cure every historic Spanish filing or accounting obligation;
- prove registered title to every hotel unit or element;
- establish that LPB could pay every enforceable debt on every relevant date;
- reverse Sentencia 163/2023 or determine the pending appeal;
- establish civil, professional, criminal or public-authority liability;
- interrupt every limitation or prescription period;
- quantify or recognise the asserted recovery perimeter as a current accounting asset;
- confer authority to pursue independent third-party claims; or
- create an executable right to restitution, payment or enforcement.

## 6. Controlled terminology

The filed title may be quoted exactly.

For explanatory text, the preferred formulation is:

> **Aweswell’s officially filed provisional parent-level group accounts and continuity record.**

This avoids implying a conventional, fully reconciled consolidation of complete subsidiary accounts unless and until that technical accounting conclusion is independently confirmed.

## 7. Two material reconciliation issues

### 7.1 Court versus insolvency-administration responsibility

The English and Spanish material contain materially different formulations concerning responsibility for historic non-filing or blocked filing.

Until the primary procedural record is complete, use:

> **The ordinary Spanish subsidiary-reporting route was materially impaired. The precise allocation of power, duty, access, action and omission among the directors, the insolvency administration, the court and other relevant actors remains under primary-document reconstruction.**

### 7.2 Physical/productive-unit perimeter versus registered-title perimeter

Broad references to 262 registered elements must be reconciled with the Board annex’s account of an approximately 77.593% completed acquisition and approximately 22.407% uncompleted or disputed perimeter.

Every use of “whole hotel” must specify whether it means:

- physical complex;
- productive unit;
- operating/exploitation perimeter;
- commercial inventory;
- intended transaction perimeter; or
- registered-title perimeter.
