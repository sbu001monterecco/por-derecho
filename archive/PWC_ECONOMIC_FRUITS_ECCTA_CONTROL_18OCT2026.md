## Purpose

Lock the source-bounded formulation developed for the next PwC UK / Global governance communication.

The analytical chain is:

**PwC knowledge / notice → prevention, escalation, conflict, independence and information-governance decisions to be produced → subsequent asset / finance / operating-value accumulation → post-1 September 2025 ECCTA section 199 applicability review.**

The chain defines an investigation. It does not pre-judge causation.

## Mandatory boundaries

1. Do not call the economic markers below “fraud proceeds” unless and until the underlying fraud, causation and tracing are proved.
2. Do not attribute the entire Acosta Matos / RICPE / Canarian Hospitality platform to PwC.
3. Do not transfer knowledge among Landwell/PwC Tax & Legal, PricewaterhouseCoopers Auditores, PwC UK, PwC Spain or any other PwC entity without an evidence bridge.
4. Do not apply ECCTA section 199 retrospectively. The offence came into effect on 1 September 2025.
5. For section 199, separately prove: qualifying organisation; associated person; specified fraud offence; intended benefit to the organisation or qualifying client; UK nexus; and absence/inadequacy of reasonable fraud-prevention procedures.
6. Company-wide Canarian Hospitality sales / revenue are not MYND Yaiza asset-specific revenue.
7. Loan funding, grant awards, sales, operating revenue, profit, asset value, distributions and recoverable loss are different measures and must not be added together.
8. Preserve contrary evidence, independent pre-existing Acosta Matos business, third-party funding, brands, operators and legitimate commercial explanations.

## Economic-fruits tracing rule

Trace separately:

**title/control → investor capital → refinancing → grants/public support → hotel conversion → rooms/bookings → operating income → fees/profits → distributions → reinvestment → further capital-raising capacity.**

For every increment classify:
- independent/pre-existing value;
- third-party capital;
- legitimate operational value creation;
- public-support contribution;
- disputed Sun Park-derived value;
- causal status: proved / supported / alleged / open / disproved.
