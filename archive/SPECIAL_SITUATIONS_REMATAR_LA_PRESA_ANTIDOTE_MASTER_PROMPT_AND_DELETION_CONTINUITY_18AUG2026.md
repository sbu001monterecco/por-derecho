## 1. Controlling decision preserved from the thread

The principal/right book for the **“rematar la presa” / “finish off the prey”** concept is **Special Situations** (the existing Por Derecho book page uses the canonical English title **Special Situations** and the subtitle **When Default Becomes an Investment**).

This book should be treated as the dominant home of the concept, while the Por Derecho website may later use it more sparingly as a late-stage interpretative question after the evidential record has been presented.

The book should not use the phrase merely as a striking anecdote or accusation. It should test whether the metaphor expresses a deeper structural problem and then develop the **antidote**: the author’s subsequent reconstruction of agency, memory, evidence, visibility, institutional traceability and accountability, culminating in Por Derecho and related methods.

Working narrative arc:

`VULNERABILITY → FRAGMENTATION / LOSS OF AGENCY → “FINISH OFF THE PREY” → RECONSTRUCTION → AGENCY → ANTIDOTE → SYSTEM DESIGN`

The phrase should be **earned by the narrative**. It should not appear too early. The reader should first understand the chronology, incentives, decisions, gaps, competing explanations and consequences; only later should the remembered phrase arrive as a possible name for the pattern.

## 2. Source-status controls for the remembered conversation

The originating thread records the author’s present recollection of a conversation with a professional identified inconsistently in the chat as **Ignacio Palacín** and later **Ignacio Paredeste**. The identity must therefore be independently verified before public attribution.

The remembered conversation contained two distinct propositions:

### A. Auction / execution proposition

After hearing the explanation of the auction and execution history, the professional allegedly considered that there may have been **negligence in the execution of the relevant Audiencia Provincial resolution**, specifically because the **threshold for a better bid / mejora** may have been confused with **CAM’s debt**. The remembered advice was that a filing should be made specifically addressing negligence or error in execution of the appellate resolution.

**Status:** personal recollection / evidential lead only. Must be tested against the primary judicial resolutions, auction terms, execution acts and arithmetic before any negligence statement is treated as established.

### B. “Rematar la presa” proposition

The author recalls the professional saying, in substance, that insolvency administrators only exist, or ultimately only serve, to **“rematar la presa.”**

Best contextual English translation preserved from the thread:

> **“to finish off the prey.”**

The expression conveys administering the final blow to something already caught, wounded or vulnerable. Alternative explanatory rendering: **“to deliver the coup de grâce to the prey.”** The book should generally preserve the Spanish phrase and gloss it once in English rather than replacing it with a neutral insolvency phrase such as “liquidate the asset,” which would lose the metaphor.

The author recalls responding that this was the first time in many years someone had summarised so profoundly how he experienced the role of the insolvency administrator in the Sun Park story: a person who, from the author’s perspective, had **“dedicated himself to finishing off the prey.”**

**Status:** personal recollection and subjective interpretation. Not a verified verbatim quotation; not proof of motive, intent, professional misconduct or criminality.

## 3. Central intellectual move for Special Situations

Treat **Special Situations** as the presumptive principal home of the “rematar la presa / finish off the prey” thesis and rigorously test that choice.

The strongest conceptual inversion is already latent in the book’s existing positioning:

> **A “special situation” can be opportunity to the outsider and extreme vulnerability to the insider.**

In distressed investing, a borrower’s default, litigation, insolvency, asset fragmentation or corporate event may create an opportunity for investors, claim purchasers, servicers, creditors or restructuring capital.

The book should ask what that exact same “opportunity” looks like from inside the company, asset or person that has become distressed.

Core questions:

- When does legitimate value discovery become value extraction?
- When does restructuring become dismantling?
- When does enforcement cease to preserve economic value and instead make loss irreversible?
- When does a distressed borrower, business or asset cease to be treated as a participant and become an object around which other actors’ claims, rights, fees, strategies and opportunities are organised?
- What protects the vulnerable subject from becoming institutional “prey”? 
- Can a predatory outcome emerge even if no single actor regards themselves as a predator?

The book must distinguish lawful distressed investing, hard bargaining, ordinary insolvency consequences, professional error, negligence, structural dysfunction, opportunism, abuse and proven illegality.

## 4. Structural model: the prey metaphor

Test the metaphor as a sequence, not as a conclusion assumed in advance.

### 4.1 THE WOUND

What initially makes the subject vulnerable?

Possible factors include leverage, liquidity stress, litigation, lender action, disputed debts, governance breakdown, default, insolvency and information asymmetry.

### 4.2 THE ISOLATION

What removes or reduces the subject’s ability to defend itself?

Examples: loss of management control; insolvency restrictions; dependence on professional intermediaries; costs; delay; procedural fragmentation; evidential disadvantage; inability to see the whole record.

### 4.3 THE ENCIRCLEMENT

Map the simultaneous legitimate processes surrounding the weakened subject:

- insolvency;
- enforcement;
- property/title;
- corporate transactions;
- tax;
- planning/tourism;
- professional discipline;
- regulatory supervision;
- financing and investment;
- judicial and administrative proceedings.

### 4.4 THE FINAL BLOW

Identify the event or chain of events that converts vulnerability into effectively irreversible loss. Do not assume it is one decision.

### 4.5 THE LEGITIMATION

Ask how a proposition or outcome can gain progressively greater institutional legitimacy because later actors rely on earlier acts, files, certifications, orders, professional statements or apparent authority.

This should connect directly to Por Derecho’s proposition-propagation, institutional-reliance and evidential-traceability methodology.

## 5. Competing explanations must remain visible

Neither the website nor the book should force the reader into one interpretation. Test at least these explanations:

1. **Ordinary insolvency outcome** — difficult but conventional consequences of insolvency, enforcement and creditor competition.
2. **Cumulative mistake / negligence** — serious errors, poor execution, passivity or professional failure without improper purpose.
3. **Institutional fragmentation** — each actor dealt with only one compartment and nobody preserved responsibility for the whole.
4. **Structural liquidation bias** — once insolvency began, the system became progressively oriented toward disposal/enforcement rather than preservation of the operating business and collective value.
5. **“Rematar la presa”** — the documented sequence may leave a reader asking whether an already weakened business was treated less as something to preserve than as something whose destruction, transfer or loss was progressively completed.
6. **More serious coordinated explanation** — only if evidence supports raising the question; temporal association, professional relationships and adverse outcomes are not proof of coordination or unlawful intent.
7. **Other explanation** — identify stronger alternatives emerging from the evidence.

The site and book should state what evidence supports each interpretation, what evidence contradicts it, what remains unknown and what evidence would change the assessment.

## 6. The antidote

The book must not stop at victimhood or counter-accusation. The second major movement should examine whether the author’s subsequent actions can be understood as an **antidote to the “finish off the prey” mechanism**.

The antidote is not revenge. It is reconstruction of agency and accountability.

### PATHOLOGY → ANTIDOTE

**Fragmentation → Reconstruction**  
Build one chronology crossing proceedings, companies, assets, institutions and years.

**Evidential asymmetry → Evidential recovery**  
Recover files through archives, registries, access requests, Companies House, BORME, property records, court material, public filings and correspondence.

**Institutional memory loss → Persistent memory**  
Create permanent evidence registers, proposition IDs, source links, contradiction registers and cross-proceeding traceability.

**Silence / non-response → Traceable escalation**  
Request → refusal / silence → appeal → resubmission → supervisory route → preservation and publication of the unresolved question.

**Authority inflation → Source regression**  
Ask of every material proposition: **What is the original evidence?**

**Narrative capture → Open evidence**  
Expose primary sources and keep competing explanations visible.

**Dependence on gatekeepers → Distributed scrutiny**  
Allow lawyers, journalists, regulators, academics, investors and citizens to inspect the record independently.

**The prey cannot speak → Agency**  
Turn the object of proceedings back into an investigator, archivist, petitioner, whistleblower, publisher and system designer.

**Opaque institutional process → Open-kimono methodology**  
Show what is known, unknown, disputed, inferred, contradicted, missing and capable of changing the conclusion.

## 7. Por Derecho as practical counter-model

Test whether Por Derecho’s wider architecture can be explained as a practical response to the same pathology.

Possible formulation to test:

> **The opposite of “finishing off the prey” is not merely saving the prey. It is designing a system in which vulnerability increases institutional duties, traceability and scrutiny rather than decreasing the vulnerable party’s effective rights.**

Por Derecho’s response may include:

- recover the asset / business where legally possible;
- reconstruct the record;
- preserve institutional memory;
- make propositions traceable to sources;
- expose contradictions and uncertainty;
- require reasoned answers;
- give the other side a right of reply;
- allow conclusions to be corrected when evidence changes;
- distribute scrutiny;
- build better structures for future projects and capital;
- turn case experience into reusable public-interest infrastructure.

A particularly important proposition to test:

> **Por Derecho is simultaneously an antidote and evidence of the disease: a functioning rule-of-law system should not require one individual to reconstruct dozens of disconnected files over many years merely to understand how an outcome was produced.**

How a distressed enterprise can lose agency and become an object of processes.

Only here, after the reader understands the record, introduce the remembered phrase and test whether it fits.

### PART VI — WHAT IF EVERYONE WAS SIMPLY DOING THEIR JOB?
Present the strongest benign account from the insolvency administrator, creditor, court, CAM, regulator, investor and other relevant actors. This section is mandatory.

### PART VII — THE ANTIDOTE
The years of reconstruction: SAIPs/access requests, refusals, appeals, resubmissions, evidence recovery, cross-checking, whistleblowing, regulatory complaints and institutional escalation.

### PART VIII — OPEN KIMONO
Turn the experience into a method: facts, sources, uncertainties, competing hypotheses, missing documents, right of reply and correction triggers.

### PART IX — FROM PREY TO PARTICIPANT
The subject of the process becomes an active investigator and public auditor of the process.

### PART X — NOBODY SHOULD NEED TO DO THIS
System-design and reform: what institutions should do so vulnerability triggers protection and scrutiny rather than easier extraction.

## 16. Central editorial principle

The project should aim to make the reader capable of reaching the difficult interpretation without being instructed to adopt it.

The strongest formulation preserved from the thread is:

> **We have shown you the record. There is more than one possible explanation. Which explanation best fits the evidence?**

Only then may one possible answer be introduced:

> **“rematar la presa” — “to finish off the prey.”**

The corresponding constructive proposition is:

> **The antidote to fragmented power is traceable responsibility; the antidote to institutional opacity is radical evidential transparency; and the antidote to becoming an object of the process is restored agency.**
