## Evidential cut-off and limits

- complaint registration, opening and outgoing communication are represented as procedural facts;
- allegations are not findings;
- respondent material is described as not located in the reviewed public corpus, not as absent internally;
- RE-009765/2026 and RE-009768/2026 are receipts whose exact assignment must be certified;
- RE-007476 remains DIP 79 material unless a human relevance decision lawfully imports a defined proposition;
- the 27-Aug-2026 date is an ordinary control date, not an asserted automatic expiry;
- criminal, civil, insolvency-merits and removal-from-office decisions remain with their competent bodies.
