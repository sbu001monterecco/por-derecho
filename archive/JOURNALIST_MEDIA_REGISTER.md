## Controlling publication rule

This register records **identity / professional-media context only** unless a separate field is expressly added for another purpose.

**A person's presence in this register does not mean that Project Sun Rock has contacted them, emailed them, received a reply from them, or published material about them.**

The fact that an email was prepared, sent, received, acknowledged, unanswered, redirected or followed up may be preserved in a separate source-controlled communication record where necessary for continuity, but it must **not automatically be published on the public website**.

Website publication of any outreach/contact fact requires a separate, explicit editorial decision and source/publication-safety review. Repository presence, Gmail state, or inclusion in this register is not website-publication authority.

Do not publish private email addresses, personal telephone numbers, Gmail message identifiers or other non-public contact details in this register.

## Maintenance instructions

1. Verify current professional identity and media organisation from reliable current/public sources where practical.
2. Keep entries neutral and professional.
3. Do not infer outlet endorsement.
4. Do not record speculative affiliations.
5. Preserve outreach status/private contact details outside this neutral register.
6. Never use inclusion here as authority to publish that the person was contacted.
7. Update role/media changes while preserving material historical context where relevant.
8. During active outreach use the maximum-reach, recipient-fit and exact-send controls.
9. Classify silence, bounces, acknowledgements and out-of-office replies narrowly.
