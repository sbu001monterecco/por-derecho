# SUN PARK — ACTA AUTHORITY, LEGITIMACY AND VISUAL CONTROL

**Date:** 17 August 2026  
**Status:** CANONICAL VISUAL / RETRIEVAL CONTROL FOR COMMUNITY-MINUTES AND AUTHORITY-LINEAGE WORK  
**Scope:** 2008 baseline → 2011 disputed governance rupture → Concurso 36/2012 → 2016/2018/2019 downstream acts → 2022 formal-title threshold → current consequences.

## 1. Controlling proposition

The reader-facing story must not be presented merely as a sequence of competing `ACTAS`. It must be shown as an **authority-lineage problem**:

1. what ownership and participation structure existed at each date;
2. who had authority to convene and decide;
3. who attended or was represented;
4. what voting denominator was used;
5. whether any owner was excluded through disputed debt or other mechanisms;
6. what power the resulting minute claimed;
7. what later actors treated that minute as authorising; and
8. whether later title, judicial, concursal or administrative events actually validated that earlier authority.

The Project's allegation is that a **2011 minority/non-participating perimeter displaced or captured Community governance through a disputed first authority act/minute and later debt/voting and administrative mechanisms, with downstream effects continuing through later minutes and decisions**. That allegation may support civil, concursal, administrative and criminal investigation. It is **not to be published as an adjudicated criminal fact unless a competent final source establishes the relevant offence and actor-specific responsibility**.

## 2. Critical terminology correction — do not say `full ownership` of Sun Park without proof

Sun Park was legally fragmented by units. Current repository control supports a large LPB/participating-majority position after the 2008 transactions and a unitary hotel-operation model, not an undifferentiated statement that one actor owned `100% of the hotel`.

Preferred wording:

- **participating/compliant majority ownership**;
- **majority ownership participating in the unitary hotel operation**;
- **LPB-led majority perimeter**;
- **owners participating in the common hotel exploitation model**.

Use `full ownership` only in a narrowly defined sense, such as `full legal ownership of the specific units held by that owner`, where the title source supports it. Do not use `full ownership of Sun Park` as shorthand for majority position or operational unity.

This distinction is essential because the later dispute concerns exactly the difference between **ownership of particular units, Community voting/governance, hotel-operation authority and practical control**.

### Lane C — separate legal thresholds that do not automatically cure Rail B

Run a third lane underneath both rails:

`6 JUN 2012 — LPB CONCURSO OPENS`

`7 JUN 2018 — MATERIAL / ACCESS / OPERATIONAL CONTROL THRESHOLD`

`JAN–FEB 2022 — LPB-PERIMETER ADJUDICATION / DACIÓN / FORMAL TITLE THRESHOLD`

`LATER 2022 — CAM→HNT CORPORATE / PROJECT / OPERATIONAL FORMALISATION`

The core reader instruction is:

> **A later legal event must not be drawn backwards as if it automatically validated every earlier Community act, voting exclusion, access decision, security instruction or exercise of authority.**

Formal title in 2022 is therefore visually important because it is **late**. It may change rights prospectively or within its exact legal perimeter, but it does not by itself prove that a disputed 2011 minute, a later Community office, or a pre-title act over LPB/Matkator/third-party property was valid when performed.

## 5. The `ACTA X-RAY` card — mandatory fields for every important minute

Every important minute shown publicly or used analytically should have the same compact visual card:

| Field | Reader question |
|---|---|
| **Date / document** | What exact acta or certificate is this? |

| **Attendance / proxies** | Who attended and with what proxy? |

| **Denominator** | What total was used to calculate majority/quorum? |
| **Decision** | What exactly was approved? |
| **Power claimed** | What authority did the minute claim to create or exercise? |
| **Implementation** | Who acted on it afterwards? |

The value of the repeated card is cumulative: by 2018/2019/2022 the reader can see whether a later acta has an independently proved authority chain or simply inherits a disputed parent.

## 6. `Legitimacy is not a colour` — visual safety rule

Do **not** use green for `good/legitimate people` and red for `criminal/bad people`.

Use colour only for evidential state:

- solid / neutral = primary-document-supported;
- amber = disputed / contested;
- patterned or dashed = evidence-based inference;
- red outline = material contradiction or unresolved authority defect;
- dark legal bar = later formal title / judicial / statutory threshold.

This prevents the design from visually asserting guilt beyond the evidence.

## 7. The reader-facing comparison should be `authority-compliant vs authority-contested`, not `majority good vs minority bad`

The strongest defensible contrast is:

**Participating-majority / authority-compliant model**

versus

**minority/non-participating / authority-contested model alleged to have displaced the first**.

Majority status alone does not make every act valid; minority status alone does not make an act invalid. The evidential question is whether each body, vote and office holder acted under valid authority and within its legal perimeter.

This framing is stronger because it makes the alleged capture **provable or disprovable through documents**.

## 13. Criminal-characterisation control

The Project expressly alleges that parts of the Community-governance displacement involved falsification, usurpation or instrumentalisation and may form part of criminal conduct rather than a merely civil disagreement. Preserve that allegation where relevant and do not dilute it into `just a civil dispute`.

At the same time:

- identify the exact allegedly false document or representation;
- identify the actor who created, signed, used, instructed, adopted or benefited from it;
- prove knowledge and intent separately;
- distinguish document falsity from legal invalidity, irregularity or disputed interpretation;
- distinguish later reliance from participation in original creation;
- preserve adverse rulings and lawful alternative explanations;
- do not infer criminal liability merely from minority status, later benefit, chronology or association.

The visual should therefore make the criminal theory **more testable and more precise**, not merely more emphatic.
