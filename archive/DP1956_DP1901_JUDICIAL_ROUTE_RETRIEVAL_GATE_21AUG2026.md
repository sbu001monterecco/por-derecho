- CAM stated that it owned 54 apartments;
- in 2018 it began interior works in several of its own apartments under an asserted municipal licence;
- it linked the expenditure to an expectation that the **complex would enter into operation**;
- it maintained that LPB units were not affected.

CAM's January 2018 acquisition offer separately used the Canary Islands tourism framework and the economic logic of **unity of exploitation** in support of a whole-complex acquisition strategy.

### Investigative inference

The limited-unit explanation is not impossible. It must, however, be reconciled with:

- an integrated hotel whose useful tourism operation depended on unitary exploitation;
- the economics of renovating a minority of units before secure project-wide control;
- Community/security/common-installation activity;
- later whole-project investor presentation;
- and the eventual integrated hotel conversion.

**Do not state predetermined adjudication as fact.** Ask what expectation of acquisition/control/reopening justified the 2018 expenditure and what communications, approvals, budgets and internal investment decisions supported it.
