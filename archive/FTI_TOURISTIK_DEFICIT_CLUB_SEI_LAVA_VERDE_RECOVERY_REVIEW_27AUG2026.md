## 1. Controlling result

The independently verified record supports two propositions which must be read
together without being collapsed:

1. **FTI Touristik GmbH remains in an open insolvency process with a strong
   evidence-based ordinary-creditor deficiency.** On 27 May 2025 the
   administrator reported almost EUR 980 million of filed claims, more than
   73,000 creditors, several further years of claims review and realisation, a
   distribution only at the end, and an expected quota described as very low.
   The same release said that `Masseunzulänglichkeit` no longer existed because
   the estate covered estate costs and liabilities. That is estate-cost
   sufficiency, not proof that ordinary unsecured creditors will be paid in full.

2. **Club Sei Lanzarote had an announced wholesale route and contemporaneously
   live public-facing retail offer/booking interfaces.** Travel Weekly reported
   on 1 February 2019 that UK agents would be able to sell the planned Club Sei
   resorts through Youtravel. Controlled captures then show Club Sei Lanzarote
   on 1-2-FLY and Ab-in-den-Urlaub on 25 November 2019, Loveholidays on 21 January
   2020, and a Meeting Point Hotels brochure panel. This does not establish
   loaded or available inventory, an accepted booking, payment, revenue, a guest
   stay, opening, commission, title, whole-site authority or a valid management
   agreement.

The contrary evidence is central: Touristik Aktuell reported on 27 October 2020
that FTI postponed Club Sei implementation because of COVID-19 and supplied no
new implementation date. That report prevents the retail interfaces from being
treated as proof of operation and creates finite questions about pre-postponement
inventory, reservations, deposits, supplier commitments, costs, customer data,
cancellations and impairments.
