## 2. Scope: what counts as a communication

Do **not** restrict the scan to judgments or formal pleadings. Include, separately classified:

1. **Party → court/judge communications**
   - escritos, recursos, annexes, solicitudes, aclaraciones, objections, complaints and documentary submissions;
   - proof of filing, incorporation, service and procedural disposition.

2. **Court/judge → parties communications**
   - autos, providencias, judicial remarks preserved in contemporaneous professional correspondence, hearing statements, directions and requests;
   - distinguish signed judicial acts from reported oral comments.

3. **Party/counsel → LAJ or court-office communications**
   - testimony/certification requests, information requests, service questions, expediente-access requests, reminders, complaints and follow-ups.

4. **LAJ/court office → parties or third bodies**
   - DIORs, decretos, testimonios, certificates, finality statements, notifications, transfer/delivery acts, remisiones, oficios, mandamientos and ATLANTE/LexNET entries.

5. **Professional internal communications reporting judicial or office contact**
   - full email threads, meeting reports, call notes, WhatsApp messages, voice notes and instructions prepared before or after a meeting.

6. **Informal or non-public contact assertions**
   - alleged telephone, personal, courthouse, daily-contact or privileged-access claims;
   - preserve who said it, who heard it, when it was recorded, whether another witness corroborates it and what objective record could test it.

7. **Institutional supervision and complaint communications**
   - CGPJ DI 169/2026, Recurso de alzada 286/2026, inspection/court-functioning routes;
   - LAJ supervisory chain before the Secretaría de Gobierno del TSJ de Canarias, Secretaría de Coordinación Provincial de Las Palmas and Secretaría General para la Innovación y Calidad del Servicio Público de Justicia;
   - criminal, appellate or professional routes only to the extent they preserve or test the judge/LAJ communication record.

8. **Downstream reliance on court-office material**
   - documented use of testimonios, finality certificates or judicial acts before notaries, the Property Registry, companies, financiers/investors, regulators or public bodies.

A forwarded copy is not a new independent communication unless it created a new recipient/knowledge event. A later summary is not independent corroboration merely because it repeats an earlier allegation.

## 7. Evidence and attribution model

Use both evidence level and proposition status.

### Evidence level

- **P1 PRIMARY AUTHENTIC** — signed court/LAJ/registry/institutional original or authentic copy.
- **P2 PRIMARY COMMUNICATION** — native email, message, filing or receipt with demonstrated sender/recipient.
- **P3 CONTEMPORANEOUS PROFESSIONAL RECORD** — lawyer/procurador/business report created at the time.
- **S1 SECONDARY RECONSTRUCTION** — later dossier accurately citing primary records.
- **A1 ALLEGATION / WITNESS ACCOUNT** — assertion requiring corroboration.
- **H1 HYPOTHESIS** — proposition to test.

### Proposition status

- VERIFIED FACT
- DOCUMENTED PARTY ALLEGATION
- WITNESS ACCOUNT
- EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE
- DISPUTED
- OPEN EVIDENTIAL GAP
- CONTRADICTED
- CORRECTED / SUPERSEDED
- INTERNAL ONLY

### Attribution confidence

- **DIRECT DOCUMENTED** — source itself is authored/signed by the actor.
- **DIRECT RECEIPT DOCUMENTED** — service/receipt to the actor or office is proved.
- **CONTEMPORANEOUS REPORTED** — a professional or witness recorded a conversation at the time.
- **LATER RECONSTRUCTION** — later summary of an earlier event.
- **UNVERIFIED / HEARSAY** — no adequate corroboration yet.

Never upgrade an A1/H1 proposition through repetition.
