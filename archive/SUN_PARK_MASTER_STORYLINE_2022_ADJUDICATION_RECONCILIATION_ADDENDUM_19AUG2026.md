## Interest transition — now explained, not merely observed

The signed 25-Jan-2021 Auto records:

- 2019 AP first-rank default interest: **€3,079,104.66**;
- continuing daily default interest until mortgage cap: **€3,182,000**;
- Jan-2021 better-posture requirement: full **€3,182,000**.

Thus:

`€3,182,000 − €3,079,104.66 = €102,895.34`.

Use this as:

> **a source-supported interest-accrual transition inside the better-posture calculation**, not as an unexplained inflation, fabricated interest or automatically recognised concursal credit.

The underlying CAM calculation, exact days/rate and credit-classification effect remain open.
