**Public status:** attributed Por Derecho allegation and active forensic investigation — not a finding of guilt

## Non-negotiable objective

Re-scan the entire Por Derecho repository, public website and connected primary-source architecture as one system, then make the following allegation immediately visible wherever it materially changes the interpretation of the case:

> **Por Derecho alleges a serious de facto-management and knowing-facilitation architecture:** private actors performed recurring management functions over Community authority, debt, voting, access, security, maintenance and practical asset control, while the Insolvency Administration requested, authorised, adopted, relied upon or failed adequately to delimit important parts of that architecture. The investigation asks whether those documented functions crossed the threshold from lawful protection, ordinary creditor conduct or professional error into de facto administration, knowing facilitation, essential cooperation or coordinated participation.

This must be stated as an **attributed allegation under active investigation**, not as a criminal judgment or settled legal characterisation.

## Immediate visibility priorities

The website must show, above the fold or immediately after the hero, four separate propositions:

1. **DOCUMENTED ANCHORS**
   - recurring private functions over Community debt, voting, access, security and maintenance;
   - AC request for security, attendance at Community meetings, identified access/maintenance authorisations and use of creditor-generated debt material;
   - later Community/debt figures used in liquidation or better-bid architecture;
   - no currently located post-liquidation incident expressly re-quantifying the Community claim, enlarging the recognised privileged total or converting every later interest component into definitive concursal credit.

2. **POR DERECHO ALLEGES**
   - the private actors operated as unrecognised functional managers or shadow administrators over critical LPB/estate and wider hotel functions;
   - the AC knowingly enabled, adopted, relied on or allowed that management architecture to operate without adequate appointment, delimitation, verification, accounting or accountability;
   - the architecture may have converted disputed Community authority and debt into control, liquidation advantage, title, later operation and downstream finance.

3. **NOT PROVED**
   - a criminal agreement;
   - full administrator-in-fact status for every actor or function;
   - knowing falsity of a specific document;
   - procedural fraud, blanket double charging, judicial prevarication or collective liability;
   - automatic knowledge or complicity of later financiers, investors, advisers, officials or operators.

4. **DECISIVE EVIDENCE**
   - certified complete court docket and post-2016 modification index;
   - native Community authority, voting, minutes, accounts, invoices and bank records;
   - vendor/payer/recharge duplicate-cost crosswalk;
   - native AC–Community–CAM communications;
   - security, locksmith, key/code and access logs;
   - offer, appraisal and interest workbooks;
   - final accounts, EUR 400,000 trail, deed notice and property-by-property Registry chain.

## Route-specific wording rules

### Community / authority routes

Emphasise disputed appointment, voting, debt certification, record custody and implementation. Do not say that every Community defect is criminal.

### AC / professional routes

Emphasise request, authorisation, adoption, reliance, verification, independent judgment, delegation and accounting. Do not attribute knowledge or intent to a firm merely from the conduct of an individual without a sourced professional relationship.

### CAM / material-control routes

Emphasise actor-specific function, instructions, funding, practical control, pre-title benefit and later adjudication. Preserve legal-person separation and the lawful-creditor/owner defence.

### Definitive-text / liquidation / adjudication routes

Emphasise the difference between recognised credit, contingent or estate claims, mortgage responsibility, better-bid thresholds, non-cash consideration and final accounting. State “not yet located”, not “no incident existed”.

### Judge / LAJ / appellate routes

Emphasise notice, evidence, adversarial process, exact decision, unresolved matters and implementation. Do not infer prevarication or criminal knowledge from repeated CAM-favourable effects alone.

### Notary / Registry routes

Emphasise the title, authority, calculations and testimonios presented, what was checked, what was registered/cancelled and which later rights depend on an independent title.

### RICPE / finance / public-support routes

State that the upstream allegation makes title/control due diligence, conflicts, conditions, waivers, drawdowns and use of funds material. Do not infer that investors, advisers, regulators or funding bodies knew or participated merely because the project reached them.
