## 5. Statutory connection of the office

The current LOPJ provides that:

1. the LAJ corps is hierarchically organised under the Ministry of Justice through the **Secretary-General of the Administration of Justice**, **Government Secretaries** and **Provincial Coordinating Secretaries** (Article 463);
2. there is a Government Secretary at each High Court, selected from the LAJ corps, who also serves as Secretary of its Government Chamber (Article 464);
3. the Government Secretary directs LAJs in the relevant territory and has inspection, disciplinary-initiation, organisation, service-instruction and incident-management functions (Articles 464–465); and
4. the same statute prevents a Government Secretary from giving case-specific instructions where an LAJ acts as judicial certifier or exercises process-ordering and process-direction functions (Article 465.8).

Therefore Rubio Faure's office is correctly described as the senior LAJ governance office for the TSJ Canarias territory. It has a real institutional connection to LAJ and Office-of-the-Court services, but it is separate from judicial adjudication by a judge or chamber.
