## 1. Controlling thesis

The Sun Park Community record must never be analysed only as a civil, mercantile, insolvency or Horizontal Property Law dispute.

> **Por Derecho expressly alleges that the Community organ and its documentary outputs were criminally instrumentalised: disputed debt and voting exclusions were used to manufacture apparent authority; ACTAS, appointments, certificates, bank powers and purported approvals then converted that appearance into operational, professional, administrative, financial, investor-facing and litigation effects.**

This is an absolute attribution of the **criminal case thesis to Por Derecho/Gil Marer**, not a declaration that a named person has been finally convicted or that every element of any offence is already proved. The repository must preserve both propositions at the same time:

1. this is **not merely a legitimacy or LPH dispute**; and
2. individual criminal responsibility still requires actor-specific proof and a competent determination.

The absence of proof that all actors joined a single orchestrated plan beginning in 2011 does not neutralise the criminal inquiry. A person who did not originate an apparatus may still incur separate responsibility if evidence proves knowing adoption, use, escalation, certification, signature, benefit, concealment or downstream deployment.

## 2. Correct periodisation

The prior label `successor phase` is superseded for this track.

| Period | Controlled description | Criminal/investigative question |
|---|---|---|
| 2011 | **Alleged origin/authority rupture** | Were debt, vote and office outputs knowingly manufactured or used to displace the majority/operating structure? |
| 2011–2017 | **Propagation and compounding** | Which ACTAS, certificates, claims, custody acts and representations repeated or enlarged the disputed authority? |
| 2017/2018 | **Later entry/adoption threshold** | What did each Acosta Matos-perimeter actor know; what authority was shown; what was adopted, requested, signed, used or benefited from? |
| 2018–2022 | **Adoption/culmination and documentary conversion phase** | Did disputed Community authority become project authority, bank authority, permission/licensing authority, cost allocation, unified-operation authority or third-party reliance? |
| After 2022 | **Downstream use and benefit audit** | Where were the ACTAS/project/visado/certificates used; who knew the authority dispute; what disposition, decision, finance, operation or litigation effect followed? |

A later ownership, insolvency or control event is a separate title threshold. It does not retrospectively validate earlier authority and does not immunise later knowing use of allegedly false or unauthorised outputs.

## 3. No document self-proves authority

Apply this complete chain to every meeting and every downstream instrument:

`TITLE / OWNER → POWER / REPRESENTATION → APPOINTMENT → NOTICE → ATTENDANCE → VOTE → QUOTA → ACT → CUSTODY → CERTIFICATE → DOWNSTREAM USE`

The following are never self-validating:

- a meeting notice or ACTA;
- an office label such as president, secretary or administrator;
- a claim that an entity was the majority owner;
- a statement that a resolution was unanimous;
- a Community seal, certificate, debt schedule or bank mandate;
- a project cover, promoter field or signature block;
- a professional signature or visado;
- a licence, tourism, registry, finance or judicial file that reproduces the earlier statement; or
- a later deed, award, operation or investment.

`Existence of document ≠ authority.`
`Purported approval ≠ valid ratification.`
`Later reliance ≠ cure of the originating defect.`
`Civil enforceability or procedural effect ≠ absence of criminal relevance.`

## 7. Potential Código Penal routes — issue map, not conviction

The evidence must be mapped to the law in force at the date of each act. The provisions below are investigative candidates, not a conclusion that all apply:

| Potential route | Element that must be proved | Repository control |
|---|---|---|
| CP arts. 390–396 — documentary falsehood/use | falsity in an essential element; document classification; authorship or knowing use; intent/prejudice where required | an inaccurate, invalid or disputed ACTA is not automatically criminally false; prove what was simulated/altered/attributed and who knew |
| CP arts. 248 and 250.1.7 — fraud/procedural fraud | sufficient deception, error, disposition/decision, economic prejudice and profit intent; for procedural fraud, manipulation/fraud causing judicial error and a harmful decision | filing a disputed document is not enough without transmission, reliance, causal decision and actor-specific knowledge |
| CP art. 251.1 | false attribution of power to dispose of property followed by sale, charge or lease causing prejudice | Community authority over common administration must not be conflated with power over private units; identify the actual disposition |
| CP art. 252 — disloyal administration | actual powers to administer another's property, excess/breach and patrimonial damage | where the thesis is zero authority, counsel must reconcile that premise with the provision's requirement that powers existed |
| CP arts. 290–293, especially 292 | statutory corporate scope plus false/abusive records or harmful agreement through fictitious majority and resulting prejudice | applicability to an Owners' Community is a legal question; do not publish automatic application from similarity of mechanism |
| CP arts. 257–261 — frustration/insolvency offences | the date-specific debtor/actor, insolvency status, prohibited act, intent and patrimonial/creditor effect | an insolvency context does not make every Community or project act an insolvency offence |
| CP art. 570 bis | stable/indefinite group of more than two coordinated to commit crimes | family, company perimeter, repeated benefit or role convergence alone is insufficient |

### Article 262 correction

- **Article 262 Criminal Code** concerns alteration of prices in public contests/auctions. It is not a generic offence of “manipulating insolvency proceedings.” If invoked, the specific public contest/auction conduct and date must be identified.
- **Article 262 LECrim** is the professional/public-function reporting provision referenced in the College communications. Do not conflate the two statutes.
