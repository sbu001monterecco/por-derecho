## 1. Governing objective

Convert a fragmented multi-proceeding record into a finite, source-controlled set of common factual propositions capable of independent verification.

The system must allow a competent outsider to answer, with primary-source traceability:

1. Who owned what, unit/finca by unit/finca, at each material date?
2. Who could validly vote?
3. Who therefore had authority, and through which ACTA/appointment chain?
4. What debts, rights and certifications were created or asserted through that authority?
5. Which later private, AC, judicial, regulatory, administrative, funding and transaction acts actually depended on those propositions?
6. Which later actors independently revalidated the underlying facts and which merely repeated earlier propositions?
7. What criminal/investigative/forensic questions arise independently of the civil, mercantile, concursal or administrative analysis?

The system must be capable of producing conclusions that strengthen, weaken, qualify or reject Project Sun Rock / Por Derecho working hypotheses.

## 3. Three simultaneous output layers

Every major finding must be separated into:

### A. Factual truth layer
What the documents, dates, title, money, votes, signatures, notices and institutional receipts establish.

### B. Legal consequence layer
What those facts may mean under each distinct legal/procedural regime. Do not transplant a conclusion from one proceeding into another without testing party/issue/standard/preclusion differences.

### C. Institutional action layer
What a competent authority could realistically verify, preserve, request, reconcile, correct, refer, investigate, commission, adjudicate or decline to pursue.

## 7. Dependency graph

For every significant downstream act construct machine-readable and human-readable dependencies.

Example structure:

`JUD-2022-004 → AC-2021-007 → DEBT-2011-001 → AUTH-2011-001 → ACTA-2011-001 → VOTE-2011-001 → UNIT-*`

Classify each foundation:

- `INDEPENDENT` — later actor independently verified root facts;
- `DEPENDENT` — later actor relied on an earlier proposition;
- `CIRCULAR` — documents ultimately validate one another in a loop;
- `BROKEN` — required link not presently proved;
- `CONTRADICTED` — upstream primary evidence positively conflicts.

Repeated propositions are not independent corroboration unless their evidential origin is independent.

## 8. Root-of-authority test

For every purported Comunidad/CEXP representative recursively ask:

Who appointed X? → which ACTA? → who voted? → which units/fincas supplied those voting rights? → did those persons actually own those units at that date? → had purchases completed? → were proxies/attendance valid? → were any voting restrictions/exclusions applicable? → did the resolution still pass if disputed votes are removed?

Never stop at a later document stating that someone “was president” or “represented the Comunidad.” Locate the independently verifiable root or mark the chain incomplete.

## 10. Unit/finca reconstruction and majority engine

Create a controlling `UNIT-NNN` register mapping, where evidence permits:

finca registral → CRU → cadastral reference → apartment/room/unit identity → 2008 seller/intended buyer → completion/payment/escritura/registration → owner by date → exploitation/participation → voting entitlement → later transfer/adjudication → present identity.

Calculate both:

- **Nominal majority** — what an ACTA/document claims;
- **Reconstructed majority** — what current unit/title/completion evidence supports.

For material meetings run four scenarios:

1. claimed vote;
2. verified-evidence vote;
3. conservative vote;
4. maximum adverse-case vote.

State whether disputed votes are outcome-determinative. Never hide uncertain units inside an aggregate percentage.

## 11. Same-asset / different-life control

When the same unit/asset appears contemporaneously in more than one legal/economic perimeter, classify the relationship:

1. compatible;
2. potentially compatible but requires explanation;
3. materially inconsistent;
4. apparently mutually exclusive;
5. unresolved.

Do not label multiple legal/funding lives fraudulent merely because they coexist. Reconcile title, asset perimeter, costs, invoices, employment, funding source, dates and economic purpose first.

## 12. Temporal consistency and first-source rules

For every proposition distinguish:

- document creation date;
- event/effective date claimed;
- signature/execution date;
- registration date;
- notification date;
- first reliance date;
- later retrospective description.

A later document does not by itself establish an earlier ownership/authority fact.

Apply **SHOW ME THE FIRST SOURCE**: follow repeated claims backwards until primary evidence or an evidential dead end is reached.

- independent verification;
- accurate disclosure;
- legitimate funding/waiver rationale;
- correction notice;
- lack-of-knowledge evidence;
- lawful business/procedural explanation.

Maintain first-class registers of both contradiction and potentially exculpatory material. Do not bury evidence favourable to CAM/HNT, AC, Judge, Fiscalía, RICPE, Yaiza, Cabildo or another criticised actor.

## 15. Institutional-reliance and decision-vs-evidence ledgers

For every institution record:

Institution → date → source/submission received → proposition relied upon → verification actually undertaken → action/decision → consequence.

For major decisions compare:

Evidence available beforehand → evidence acknowledged → material evidence not addressed → finding → consequence.

This must permit the system to distinguish inaccurate private input, institutional non-verification, genuine independent verification, different institutional conclusions and insufficient evidence.

## 16. Claim-evolution / narrative-propagation control

Track how a proposition changes language over time, e.g.:

“X is authorised” → “X acts as president” → “the Comunidad represented by X” → “the recognised Comunidad.”

Classify whether later wording merely repeats, strengthens without new evidence, introduces a new basis, omits a qualification, independently revalidates, or converts a disputed proposition into apparently settled language.

Use terms such as “narrative laundering through repetition” only where documentary progression actually proves that mechanism.

## 17. Mandatory parallel criminal / investigative / forensic command

**No material act of analysis may be performed solely through a civil, mercantile, concursal, property or administrative lens.**

Every material event/document receives a separate criminal/investigative/forensic pass asking:

- what objectively happened;
- who acted;
- what was represented/certified/omitted;
- what contemporaneous primary evidence shows;
- what the actor demonstrably knew at that exact time;
- what contrary notice already existed;
- what document was used and who received it;
- whether reliance is demonstrable;
- who obtained economic/procedural benefit;
- what harm/risk arose;
- whether the act was repeated;
- whether concealment/withholding is evidenced or merely suspected;
- whether there is actor-specific coordination evidence;
- the strongest non-criminal explanation;
- what evidence would distinguish mistake, negligence, conscious disregard, knowing conduct and intentional participation.

### Fundamental separation

**Civil invalidity does not automatically establish criminality.**  
**Formal civil/judicial/administrative validity does not automatically exclude separate criminal relevance.**

A formally valid act may have been procured using inaccurate private input; a civil defect may simply be a mistake. Analyse separately.

## 18. Criminal-event triage schema

For every material event capture:

Event ID | date | actor | conduct | representation/omission | objective record | knowledge evidence | contrary notice | document used | recipient | reliance | benefit | harm/risk | repetition | concealment evidence | coordination evidence | strongest alternative explanation | criminal relevance | missing evidence.

Criminal relevance classifications are investigative triage, never guilt scores.

## 19. Actor-specific criminal discipline

Never attribute collective knowledge or intent merely from affiliation, family/professional ties, institutional sequence or common economic interest.

For every potentially adverse actor separate:

1. objective act;
2. objective inconsistency/falsehood if any;
3. knowledge;
4. purpose/intent evidence;
5. use/reliance;
6. benefit;
7. harm;
8. causation.

Do not jump from inconsistency directly to intent.

Where public officials/judicial/prosecutorial actors are involved, an adverse or arguably erroneous decision is not itself evidence of criminal conduct. Require actor-specific evidence of function, notice, decision, objective departure alleged, available lawful explanation, repeated conduct where relevant, motive/benefit if evidenced, and causation.

## 20. Mistake-to-intent ladder

Classify only to the level supported:

1. lawful/innocent act;
2. unresolved;
3. mistake;
4. negligent failure;
5. serious/repeated failure requiring explanation;
6. conscious-disregard/recklessness hypothesis;
7. knowing conduct strongly supported;
8. intentional participation supported by evidence.

No skipping levels.

## 21. Candidate criminal-law categories — offence-neutral first

Where facts warrant examination, test applicable Spanish criminal-law elements without forcing the evidence into an offence label. Potential categories may include documentary falsity/false certification, deception/property offences, misappropriation/disloyal administration-type conduct, punishable insolvency/asset-concealment issues, public-subsidy/funding offences, public-office offences, corruption/improper influence, private-sector conflict/corruption issues, concealment/destruction of evidence, and participation/facilitation.

For each candidate theory record facts potentially relevant, legal element, present evidence, missing element, contrary explanation and current status.

Before offence labels, produce an **offence-neutral fact package**:

Actor X on date A represented Y to recipient Z; contemporaneous document Q did/ did not contradict Y; recipient took action R; benefit/harm T followed or is unproved.

## 22. Follow-the-money + follow-the-asset dual command

For material financial events reconstruct:

source → payer → account → recipient → legal/contractual justification → accounting treatment → ultimate use → beneficiary.

Separately reconstruct:

asset → owner → possession/control → exploitation → voting/authority use → transaction → financing representation → works → current benefit.

Then reconcile both. Benefit is an investigative lead, not proof of participation or illegality.

## 23. Document-use and certification chains

For important documents/certificates reconstruct:

creation → source information → author/approver/signatory → transmission → recipient → later reproduction → institutional reliance → legal/economic consequence.

For certifications ask who had authority, what underlying records were available, whether contrary records existed, who requested it, purpose, recipient and consequence.

## 24. Metadata / provenance forensics

Where lawfully available preserve original filename, timestamps, author/PDF metadata, signatures, email headers/message IDs, attachment hashes and source-system/version history. Metadata differences are not manipulation by default; classify expected, unexplained or potentially material differences.

## 25. Pattern, coordination and instrumentalisation controls

Test repeated mechanisms but do not infer conspiracy from parallel conduct.

Coordination requires evidence such as communications, instructions, shared drafts, contemporaneous plans, meetings, division of functions, coordinated timing or transaction structure.

For alleged institutional instrumentalisation separate:

private input → institutional receipt → independent verification → institutional act → downstream benefit/consequence.

Do not progress from inaccurate private input to knowing institutional participation without separate actor-specific proof.

## 26. Causation and counterfactual discipline

For claimed consequences test:

1. temporal order;
2. factual dependency;
3. legal dependency;
4. actor knowledge;
5. alternative cause;
6. intervening independent decision;
7. counterfactual result.

Classify causation as demonstrated, likely contributory, possible, sequential only, unsupported, or unresolved.

For every contested vote/document/representation ask what happens if it is removed. Outcome sensitivity matters to materiality.

## 27. Criminal / forensic heat map and gap register

Rank investigative priority using materiality, knowledge evidence, reliance, economic consequence, repetition, independent corroboration, alternative explanation and recoverability.

Maintain a separate criminal missing-evidence register focused especially on knowledge, intent, document creation, money flows, benefit and coordination. Identify likely lawful custodians and production/preservation routes; never suggest unauthorised access.

## 28. Forensic testimony discipline

For significant witness propositions cross-test testimony against contemporaneous documents, independent witnesses, accounts/objective events and contradictions. Classify independently corroborated, partially corroborated, unsupported but plausible, contradicted or unresolved.

## 33. Clean-room review

Maintain a neutral review mode for a new judge, appellate court, prosecutor, regulator, auditor or expert. It must contain only entities, proceedings, dates, propositions, evidence, contradictions and unresolved questions—no rhetorical characterisation.

Purpose: permit independent reconstruction without inheriting either Por Derecho's framing or any predecessor's assumptions.

## 34. “No need to solve the whole case” rule

Every authority route must explicitly state that the institution need only determine propositions within its lawful competence. CNMV need not adjudicate Comunidad law to verify investor disclosures; Yaiza need not resolve the entire Concurso to identify applicant/title/authority records; Fiscalía need not adopt a global theory to investigate a discrete document-use or money-flow chain.

## 36. Institutional handoff engine

## 37. Forensic-audit readiness

Prepare the repository/site so that, if lawfully ordered or commissioned, it can support a court-appointed expert, independent insolvency auditor, forensic accountant, prosecutorial financial expert or public-sector auditor.

## 45. Unexplained-transition detector

Where narratives change—creditor→owner, prospective→current title, disputed→accepted authority, incomplete DD→funding, contested possession→assumed control—identify the new evidence/legal event that bridges the change. If none is located classify `UNEXPLAINED_TRANSITION`, not wrongdoing.

## 46. Diffusion-of-responsibility test

Test whether several institutions possessed different material fragments while no one reconstructed the whole. Record the fragment each held, date, and evidence of formal exchange. Do not call this institutional failure without separately proving the relevant duty and facts.

## 47. “What would change our mind?” command

For each major adverse proposition state the specific evidence that would require revision or withdrawal—valid proxy, completed escritura, independent vote recalculation, funding waiver, missing judicial ruling, independent title verification, corrective notice, etc.—and actively search for it.

## 48. Public right of reply / correction

Criticised persons/institutions should have a structured way to provide missing documents, corrections or alternative explanations. Substantive documentary responses must be ingested as evidence, assigned IDs, tested against the graph and capable of changing status.

## 56. Breakpoint analysis

## 57. Case-theory neutrality

Maintain three competing models:

1. Por Derecho's propagation working hypothesis;
2. lawful independent-process explanation;
3. mixed system in which some chains propagated, some were independently revalidated, some were erroneous and some were legally immaterial.

Every new source must be tested against all three.

## 61. Overriding principle

> **Every material act receives a civil/legal analysis, a criminal/investigative analysis, a forensic economic analysis, an actor-specific knowledge analysis, an adversarial alternative explanation, a causation/materiality test and a documented statement of what the evidence does—and does not—presently establish.**

The purpose is neither to criminalise the civil case nor to civilise conduct that may warrant criminal investigation.

The public-authority end state is:

> **One canonical factual architecture, many legally correct institutional views, no evidential shortcuts, and no requirement that any authority accept Por Derecho's global theory before it can independently verify the facts within its own competence.**
