## 2. Source-complete findings

### 6 June 2018 — hotel-operation / insolvency-exit structure

`VERIFIED PRIMARY DOCUMENTS`

A signed hotel-industry lease dated 6 June 2018 exists. The facial legal parties are:

- the **Sun Park Owners' Community**; and
- **Clubotel La Dorada, S.L.**

Contemporaneous transaction correspondence described the package as including ONA as the intended hotel operator, and the contract itself refers to `OnaSystem`. That commercial/operator context must not be used to collapse ONA and Clubotel into one legal person without a separate source bridge.

A signed suspensive-conditions addendum dated the same day directly linked the hotel-operation structure to the insolvency exit. Its effectiveness depended on:

1. **sufficient funds being paid into the account of Mercantil nº1 to permit LPB to leave Concurso 36/2012**; and
2. a favourable Owners' General Meeting resolution authorising the contract.

**Classification:** `VERIFIED COMMERCIAL / CONTRACTUAL INTERLOCK`.

**Limits:** the instruments do not by themselves prove that the Community possessed valid authority over every finca or every owner, that ONA and Clubotel were legally identical, that the conditions were satisfied, or that the documents entered the court record.

### 8 June 2018 — developed transaction stack

`VERIFIED CONTEMPORANEOUS CORRESPONDENCE`

The transaction email circulated to prospective financiers records a wider package containing:

- hotel operator/industry-lease documents and operating business plan;
- Cuatrecasas due diligence;
- an ECO valuation presented in the transaction at approximately **€25.6 million**;
- a bridge-finance structure being assembled;

**Classification:** `CONTEMPORANEOUS COMMERCIAL PACKAGE / DEVELOPED TRANSACTION PROCESS`.

**Limit:** the email does not prove every figure, legal proposition or asset percentage was correct, that every attachment reached the Judge, or that the transaction closed.

### 12 June 2018 — conditional financing architecture

The reviewed primary financing instrument is titled **`Oferta Vinculante Condicionada`**, issued by **Varia Structured Opportunities S.A. (`VSO`)** to Aweswell Limited. Stoneweg's same-day email described the attachment more briefly as an `Oferta Vinculante`.

The instrument contemplated a facility of up to **€15.5 million**, including:

- **€13.84 million** allocated to concursal and estate/post-petition liabilities;
- **€660,000** for Matkator refinancing; and
- **€1 million** for transaction/finance costs.

Material conditions included:

- first-ranking mortgage security and LTV requirements;
- **judicial authorisation from Mercantil nº1 to grant the mortgages**;
- an AC/liquidator letter/certificate confirming total concursal and estate liabilities did not exceed €13.84m;
- acceptable ECO valuation / legal DD; and
- VSO management/credit approval and other conditions.

**Classification:** `VERIFIED DOCUMENT / CONDITIONAL COMMERCIAL STRUCTURE`.

**Limit:** the reviewed copy does not prove final Aweswell acceptance, committee approval, closing, drawdown or immediately drawable cash. A later 2 July Cuatrecasas communication expressly advised obtaining the offer duly executed and described it as useful but not yet a `safe-harbour` because of its conditions.

### 13 June 2018 — Daniel Irigoyen's same-day professional report

Daniel Irigoyen Fujiwara sent a detailed professional email on the evening of the meeting. It records that he told the judge that he had been engaged to coordinate:

- a fund;
- the shareholder/company; and
- a hotel chain/operator.

The account records a proposed conclusion route based on payment of concursal debt, restoration of LPB's autonomy and control, continued hotel exploitation and the ability to defend or bring claims against the minority/private perimeter outside the concurso.

It also records a substantive reported judicial response: explore the route, present the conclusion request promptly, obtain an updated debt figure through the AC, preserve the improved-offer route in the meantime and potentially require a deposit or guarantee.

**Classification:** `CONTEMPORANEOUS PROFESSIONAL ACCOUNT / REPORTED PERSONAL JUDICIAL NOTICE`.

**Limit:** this is not a judicial minute, audio recording or verbatim transcript. It proves what counsel contemporaneously reported, not independently every word said by the judge or that the Judge personally reviewed each transaction annex.

### 13 June 2018 — Carlos Sanz's same-day corroborating account

A separate email sent by Carlos Sanz after speaking with Irigoyen reports that Irigoyen had been at the court to defend conclusion of the concurso, that there appeared to be an opportunity to pursue that route, that urgent action was required and that the next step was an Aweswell-side application seeking exit from the concurso. ## 5. Canonical proposition

The strongest source-controlled proposition is:

> **By June 2018 there was a commercially developed normalisation/exit architecture linking a hotel operator structure, business planning, professional due diligence, valuation, external finance and an alternative sale route. The signed operator addendum made hotel-operation effectiveness depend directly on sufficient funds being paid into Mercantil nº1 to permit LPB to leave insolvency, while the VSO financing itself depended in part on judicial mortgage authority and an AC debt certificate. By 13 June, a contemporaneous professional account records that the Judge had been personally briefed on the fund + shareholder/company + operator conclusion route and gave a substantive response permitting it to be explored. A separate same-day account corroborates the occurrence and core strategy. The formal filing, debt certification, deposit/guarantee, financing closing and judicial-disposition chain remains unproved.**

This evidence materially contradicts any broad portrayal that Gil/LPB/Aweswell never attempted to preserve, operate, refinance or exit the concurso. It does not automatically disprove a specific accounting, rent, documentation or collaboration ground.

Gil Marer further alleges that the extraordinary architecture became necessary because private-actor interference, Community/governance dysfunction, AC administration/protection choices and judicial handling had prevented or impaired ordinary operation, refinancing and exit. **That causal attribution is not established merely by the existence of the architecture and must be proved actor by actor.**
