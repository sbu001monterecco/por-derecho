## 2. Source-complete finding

### 12 June 2018 — conditional financing architecture

The reviewed financing material described a facility of up to **€15.5 million**, including **€13.84 million** allocated to cancellation of concursal and post-petition debt, together with other transaction and CAPEX uses.

**Classification:** `VERIFIED DOCUMENT / CONDITIONAL COMMERCIAL STRUCTURE`.

**Limit:** the reviewed copy contained blank signature boxes and material conditions, including judicial authorization/consent, an updated AC-certified debt amount and further transaction approvals. It supports a concrete and financeable structure. It does not prove an executed, unconditional or immediately drawable loan.

### 13 June 2018 — Daniel Irigoyen's same-day professional report

Daniel Irigoyen Fujiwara sent a detailed professional email on the evening of the meeting. It records that he told the judge that he had been engaged to coordinate:

- a fund;
- the shareholder/company; and
- a hotel chain/operator.

The account records a proposed conclusion route based on payment of concursal debt, restoration of LPB's autonomy and control, continued hotel exploitation and the ability to defend or bring claims against the minority/private perimeter outside the concurso.

It also records a substantive reported judicial response: explore the route, present the conclusion request promptly, obtain an updated debt figure through the AC, preserve the improved-offer route in the meantime and potentially require a deposit or guarantee.

**Classification:** `CONTEMPORANEOUS PROFESSIONAL ACCOUNT / REPORTED PERSONAL JUDICIAL NOTICE`.

**Limit:** this is not a judicial minute, audio recording or verbatim transcript. It proves what counsel contemporaneously reported, not independently every word said by the judge.

### 13 June 2018 — Carlos Sanz's same-day corroborating account

A separate email sent by Carlos Sanz after speaking with Irigoyen reports that Irigoyen had been at the court to defend conclusion of the concurso, that there appeared to be an opportunity to pursue that route, that urgent action was required and that the next step was an Aweswell-side application seeking exit from the concurso. It also reports that Irigoyen had met the AC and expected collaboration to the extent legally required.

**Classification:** `INDEPENDENT SAME-DAY CONTEMPORANEOUS CORROBORATION OF THE REPORTED MEETING AND CORE STRATEGY`.

**Limit:** Sanz was summarising his telephone conversation with Irigoyen. The email corroborates contemporaneity, occurrence and core substance; it is not a second eyewitness account of the judge's exact words.

### 13 June 2018 — the attached one-page scheme

The attachment `Esquema Escrito para proceder a la conclusión del concurso.docx` contains blank fields and a proposed structure. It envisages:

- a comfort letter;
- a financing declaration tied to the amount certified for conclusion;
- fund/operator contracts;
- any deposit or guarantee required by the court;
- an exact AC update of the passive;
- an AC report under former art. 176 LC; and
- a request that the court require the AC to certify the debt so consignation and conclusion could be pursued.

**Classification:** `DRAFT / PROCEDURAL BLUEPRINT`.

**Limit:** the reviewed attachment is not signed, stamped or accompanied by a LexNET acknowledgement. It does not prove filing or incorporation into Concurso 36/2012.

### 13–14 June 2018 — protection information and timing

After an Arrecife order concerning the alleged material takeover was obtained, Patricia asked whether it should also be presented to the Mercantile Court. Rosa Gual responded that it should be communicated but that timing should be weighed because of the positive impression arising from the ONA adviser’s court visit. Juan Tomás Parrilla proposed discussing and assessing the issue.

**Classification:** `CONTEMPORANEOUS PROFESSIONAL STRATEGY RECORD`.

**What it proves:** the team was simultaneously considering rescue/conclusion and protection/takeover information.

**What it does not prove:** that the Arrecife material was withheld permanently, that it was formally filed on that date, or that the judge personally reviewed it.

### 14 June 2018 — consignation route continuing

Juan Tomás Parrilla reported that the interest challenge and possible suspensive appellate measure would proceed in parallel and that the **consignation of the passive remained in progress**.

**Classification:** `CONTEMPORANEOUS PROFESSIONAL ACCOUNT OF STRATEGY CONTINUATION`.

**Limit:** this does not prove money was deposited, that all conditions were satisfied or that a formal conclusion application had already entered the docket.

### 15 June 2018 — informal communication boundary

Juan Tomás Parrilla reported that:

- the court already knew there were preliminary criminal proceedings in Arrecife;
- in his view a further asset-protection filing would not be useful until a concrete precautionary measure existed there; and
- in parallel, he had been informed that the possibility of concluding the concurso by paying was being transmitted to the court **informally**.

This is the clearest contemporary boundary located in the refreshed scan.

**Classification:** `CONTEMPORANEOUS PROFESSIONAL ACCOUNT / INFORMAL-NOTICE EVIDENCE`.

**Consequence:** it strengthens the proposition that the conclusion/payment route was active and known institutionally at an informal level. It simultaneously prevents the repository from treating the June route as a proved formal filing without the missing LexNET/asiento/proveído chain.

### 14 June 2018 derivative account to proposed experts

A party-authored email to proposed expert witnesses states that the judge requested an expert chronology and allowed up to 25 days to complete the conclusion route.

**Classification:** `PARTY / DERIVATIVE ACCOUNT — NOT INDEPENDENTLY CORROBORATED`.

Those details do not appear in Irigoyen’s detailed same-day report as judicial requirements and no signed judicial act confirming them was located in the refreshed scan. They must not be published as established judicial fact unless an independent source is obtained.

### 26 June 2018 — protective counterevidence

The court suspended realization of specified fincas because the pending appeal could materially affect the conditions of the proposed transaction.

**Classification:** `VERIFIED JUDICIAL COUNTEREVIDENCE`.

**Consequence:** this judicial act must remain visible. It does not resolve every access, control, title, protection or later-calificación issue, but it contradicts any formulation that every judicial act was uniformly adverse or that the court never applied a protective brake.
