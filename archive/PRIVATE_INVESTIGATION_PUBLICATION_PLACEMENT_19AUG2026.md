# PRIVATE INVESTIGATION 8 MAY 2018 — WEBSITE PLACEMENT AND OBJECTIVE

## Controlling public proposition

> By 8 May 2018, before the 7 June change in material control, a private first-phase investigation had already identified and examined a hotel-industry professional connection with a Meeting Point/Labranda background relevant to the Sun Park pre-title project pathway. The investigator and executive are anonymised publicly; identifying information and the unredacted source are available to competent authorities. The report is investigative material, not an adjudicated finding.

## Why publish it

The value of the report is **chronology and preservation**, not accusation. It supports Project Sun Rock's objective of demonstrating that the later Lava Verde / Club Sei / Meeting Point pathway did not emerge from nowhere after the 2022 deed. A potentially relevant hotel-industry connection was already being investigated before 7 June 2018.

## Publication controls
