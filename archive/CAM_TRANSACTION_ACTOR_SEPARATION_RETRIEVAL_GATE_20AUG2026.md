# CAM TRANSACTION ACTOR-SEPARATION RETRIEVAL GATE

**Control date:** 20 August 2026  
**Status:** `MANDATORY / CONTROLLING FOR CAM-AC-JUDGE-LAJ-REGISTRY-NOTARY TRANSACTION WORK`  
**Scope:** Concurso ordinario 36/2012 — Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U.; CAM offer, Plan de Liquidación, 2018 direct-sale/dación architecture, `ob rem`, non-convalidation, 2021 licitación/approval, 2022 closing and downstream title/accounting.

## 1. Non-negotiable actor-separation rule

Every material transaction event must be reconstructed through distinct actors and acts:

> **CAM REQUEST / POSITION → AC RECOMMENDATION OR IMPLEMENTATION → JUDGE DECISION OR PROVED OMISSION → CONSEQUENCE**

Where the sequence contains a **LAJ / oficina judicial**, **Registry** or **notarial** act, insert that act as its own independent node rather than attributing it to CAM, the AC or the Judge.

Examples:

- CAM offer ≠ AC endorsement.
- AC endorsement ≠ judicial finding.
- AC request for testimony ≠ judicial order itself.
- LAJ issuance/delivery of a testimony ≠ Judge approval of a later implementation act.
- AC execution of a deed ≠ a deed executed by the Judge.
- Registry qualification/suspension ≠ a court ruling.
- AC request for convalidation ≠ judicial convalidation.
- Later judicial approval or a later deed ≠ retroactive authorisation of an earlier act already refused convalidation unless a source expressly establishes the cure and its scope.

Never use shorthand such as `the court/AC allowed CAM...` where the underlying record permits the actual actor and act to be identified.

## 2. Judicial-omission threshold

Do not call silence a judicial omission unless all four predicates are established:

1. the exact issue/document was before the Judge;
2. the Judge had the relevant procedural power or duty to act;
3. the controlled docket shows no responsive judicial act after an adequate search; and
4. a material consequence attributable to the alleged omission is evidenced.

Preserve affirmative judicial acts that cut against an adverse theory, including suspension, competition safeguards, refusal of convalidation, re-tender/licitation or other corrective measures.

## 3. Later-cure rule

For every later approval, adjudication, deed, Registry entry or cancellation ask separately:

1. what earlier defect/problem existed;
2. what later act was intended to cure or replace it;
3. whether the later act expressly addresses that earlier problem;
4. the exact asset/finca perimeter covered;
5. whether title, possession, Registry, money, accounting and third-party consequences were each reconciled;
6. whether the cure is prospective, substitutive or expressly retroactive.

Do **not** infer retroactive cure merely because a later lawful transfer route exists.

## 4. Economic-separation rule

Track separately:

- cash paid into the estate;
- debt extinguished/set off;
- mortgage-responsibility ceilings;
- liabilities/taxes/Community/IBI obligations assumed;
- property/value transferred;
- any `sobrante`;
- refunds, re-credits or accounting adjustments.

Do not equate face debt with cash consideration, and do not collapse the €400,000 locales/pools limb into the apartment dación.

## 5. Source-status rule

Use the repository categories without upgrading them:

- **VERIFIED PRIMARY FACT**;
- **PARTY / ACTOR POSITION**;
- **EXPERT OPINION**;
- **EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE**;
- **UNRESOLVED / GAP**;
- **SUPERSEDED / CORRECTED** where applicable.

A pleading, complaint, later lawyer summary or ChatGPT reconstruction never silently outranks the signed/native source.

## 6. Criminal/forensic boundary

Civil invalidity, Registry suspension, refusal of convalidation, valuation disagreement, an AC recommendation alleged to be wrong or a judicial ruling alleged to be erroneous do not by themselves establish crime.

For any criminal theory require actor-specific proof of:

- exact representation, concealment, act or omission;
- actor and legal-person identity;
- audience/recipient;
- knowledge/intent evidence;
- reliance/use;
- causation;
- benefit or consequence;
- contrary/exculpatory explanation.
