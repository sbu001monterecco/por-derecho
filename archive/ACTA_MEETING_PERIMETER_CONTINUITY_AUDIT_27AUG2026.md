## Attribution rule

`documented convener / body` and `attributed perimeter` are different fields.

- A Community officer, representative, attendee or beneficiary does not automatically prove who legally called the meeting.
- Attendance or objection by LPB/Gil does not convert an adverse-lane meeting into an LPB/Gil meeting.
- A project-side lineage label does not attribute acts to Gil before his documented entry.
- “Adverse” is Gil Marer's attributed position and the project's documentary classification. It is not a judicial finding of coordinated conduct, fraud, criminal purpose or guilt.
- Montelanza, Molina-linked people/entities, Pamanil, CAM and Acosta Matos actors remain separate persons and legal entities. The visual sequence does not merge them.

## Continuity/deletion conclusion

The conversation becomes deletion-safe for **continuity** only after this package is merged, the live GitHub Pages routes are read back, the deployment log is updated and the final merge SHA is recorded. The private custody archive is already independently preserved and hash-controlled.

Even after that closeout, “deletion-safe” will not mean:

- every historical meeting or ACTA exists;
- every notice, proxy, annex, audio or official minute book has been found;
- OCR is certified or manually verified line by line;
- a received copy is authentic, valid or legally effective; or
- the perimeter classification proves conspiracy, fraud, criminality or guilt.

Until merge and live readback: **NOT deletion-safe yet.**
