## 1. Controlling proposition

> **ONE REGULATED HOTEL PRODUCTIVE AND OPERATIONAL SYSTEM; MANY LEGALLY DISTINCT TITLE COMPARTMENTS; ONE TITLE CANNOT BE USED TO ERASE ANOTHER.**

A Sun Park/MYND Yaiza finca is not correctly classified merely by repeating an historic registry label such as “apartment.” It must be analysed simultaneously as:

1. a registered private-property compartment;
2. a bundle of inseparable common-element and quota rights;
3. a node in a regulated unitary hotel operation;
4. a possible owner-consent and operator-authority node;
5. a possession, access, room-inventory, revenue and data node;
6. an evidential and remedial platform for accounting, restoration and restitution;
7. a procedurally disposable asset whose transfer may affect rights and injuries far beyond the bare register entry.

The hotel’s operational unity does not merge ownership. Separate ownership does not make each title an autonomous hotel business.

## 2. What this asset **is**

Subject to the exact title, statutes, contracts and current law, each hotel finca may comprise or anchor:

- exclusive title to a delimited private space;
- inseparable participation in common elements, installations and services;
- a Community quota and associated governance rights and obligations;
- owner-side authority relevant to the lawful inclusion of the space in a unitary hotel operation;
- rights to identification, possession, access, inspection and protection against unauthorised alteration;
- claims to accounts, fruits, rents, compensation or exploitation income where legally supported;
- standing to obtain plans, licences, PMS/room mapping, operator and revenue evidence;
- restoration, separation, restitution, damages and other remedies;
- value within the wider hotel productive system without thereby becoming property of another legal person.

## 3. What this asset **is not**

A hotel finca is not automatically:

- an autonomous apartment capable of independent hotel exploitation;
- identical to a current hotel room number or present physical footprint;
- extinguished because walls, doors, numbering, access or use were changed;
- a share in HNT, Canarian Hospitality, CAM, LPB or the current operator;
- part of LPB’s insolvency estate merely because it lies within the same hotel;
- transferred by a share sale, productive-unit transaction, corporate contribution, hotel-management agreement or CAM→HNT operation unless the title or a legally sufficient right was actually included;
- subject to an owner’s consent merely because a majority or operator controls reception, common services or the hotel business;
- lawfully exploitable merely because the establishment is operated under unity of exploitation;
- an ETJ 163 asset unless the certified execution file and title identify it as such;
- evidence of crime merely because it was acquired, adjudicated, operated or integrated.

The classification prevents two opposite errors: **economic merger of separate patrimonies** and **atomisation of one hotel-wide economic and remedial event**.

## 6. “Finca-on-finca legal violence” — controlled analytical meaning

The user’s expression **“finca on finca legal violence/crime”** is preserved as an analytical prompt, not treated as a statutory offence or as literal interpersonal violence.

The controlled public term is:

> **title-on-title injury / lesión jurídica título-sobre-título**

It describes the alleged use of control derived from one cluster of titles, common elements, Community power, reception/access, operator machinery, debt, litigation, Registry or regulation to strip another legally distinct title of practical identity, possession, utility, revenue, voice or remedy.

The mechanism may occur through cumulative layers:

1. **physical absorption** — alteration, division, fusion, renumbering or incorporation of the corresponding space;
2. **access displacement** — control of keys, reception, security, entrances or common services;
3. **governance displacement** — exclusion, defective notice, voting or acts said to manufacture authority or debt;
4. **operational absorption** — room inventory, booking, marketing, guest allocation, cleaning and service integration;
5. **economic extraction** — collection and retention of revenue or value without the accounting or authority claimed by the title holder;
6. **documentary re-description** — treating a fragmented-title hotel as though the operator/controller owned or validly controlled the whole usable establishment;
7. **procedural deployment** — using monetary, enforcement, concursal or other proceedings to transfer, neutralise or weaken the remaining title and its remedies;
8. **Registry consolidation** — creating a new formal title position after the physical/economic result already exists;
9. **regulatory/finance normalisation** — relying on whole-hotel asset, authority, room-count, cost or operating representations in tourism, tax, finance, RIC/RICPE, incentives or public-aid files.

No single layer proves an offence. The criminal and civil significance depends on actor, authority, knowledge, deception or omission, consent, causal effect, benefit and the strongest lawful explanation.

## 9. Penal-law translation: no generic “enablement” shortcut

The legal analysis must separate the available concepts:

### 9.1 Estafa procesal

The pleaded theory must be tested against the exact alleged manipulation of evidence or analogous procedural fraud, error induced in the judicial decision-maker, resulting resolution and economic prejudice. The claimant’s position may be that these elements were already completed; the procedural status remains unadjudicated.

### 9.2 Conspiracy or proposition

Concerted interests, sequential benefit or professional proximity are not enough. The alleged agreement, participants, object offence and resolution to execute it must be particularised. For estafa, conspiracy/proposition is expressly punishable under the Penal Code.

### 9.3 Authorship, necessary cooperation and complicity

“Enablement” must be translated actor by actor into alleged co-authorship, use of another as instrument, inducement, necessary cooperation or complicity, with the act, timing, necessity/contribution, knowledge and intent stated.

### 9.4 Encubrimiento / concealment

Encubrimiento is a specific post-offence category for a person who did not intervene as author or accomplice. Where the same actor is alleged to have participated in the underlying offence, the analysis should ordinarily test continuing authorship/participation, evidential concealment or a different autonomous offence rather than use “encubrimiento” as an undifferentiated label.

### 9.5 “Fraude concursal”

There is no permission to use this as a fact-free omnibus label. The record must identify the candidate offence or civil/procedural wrong: estafa procesal in a concursal act, insolvency offences, false-document use, obstruction, misappropriation, disloyal administration, prevaricación, or another specifically supported route. Each requires its own actor, act, elements, causal chain and source classification.
