## Intelligence / corroboration rule

Every transcript proposition must remain classifiable separately as:

- `CAPTURED_STATEMENT_ONLY`
- `CONTEMPORANEOUS_SUPPORT_FOUND`
- `LATER_SUPPORT_FOUND`
- `CONTRADICTED_OR_DISPUTED`
- `PROCEDURAL_OR_NONFACTUAL`
- `OPEN_CORROBORATION`

No transcript statement is upgraded to established fact merely because it appears in a recording/transcript. Contemporaneous emails, minutes, court acts, later testimony and other records must be linked proposition-by-proposition, with contrary material retained.
