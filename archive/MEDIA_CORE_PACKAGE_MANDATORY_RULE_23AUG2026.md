# Project Sun Rock / Por Derecho — Mandatory Media Core Package Rule

**Control date:** 23 August 2026  
**Status:** CONTROLLING MEDIA-OUTREACH HARD STOP  
**Applies to:** every outbound email to a journalist, editor, newsroom, media organisation, media-related foundation/think tank contact, journalistic routing address or other recipient being approached in a media capacity.  
**Send gate:** `EMAIL_SEND_FINAL_AUTHORIZATION_RULE.md`

## 1. Mandatory package

Every media-facing outbound email — including a first approach, reply, resend, correction, supplement, follow-up, routing enquiry or request for comment — must contain the complete **Media Core Package**:

1. the appropriate-language **PwC 2016 knowledge-point source map**;
2. the appropriate-language **San Telmo / RICPE / Sun Park source map**;
3. the direct controlled San Telmo webinar link, timestamped at approximately 08:08;
4. at least one current public Por Derecho / Project Sun Rock website link; and
5. a short evidential-limits statement explaining that the maps are explanatory source indexes and that the webinar does not by itself prove wrongdoing, coordination, defective advice, unlawful finance, causation or responsibility.

Recipient personalisation may change the subject, opening, story module, evidential emphasis, supporting links and finite ask. It may **not** remove the Media Core Package.

The fact that the recipient previously received one or more elements does not permit silent omission. Attach and include the complete package again unless the user expressly approves an exact omission for that exact message.

## 5. No discretionary downgrade

The following do **not** create an exception:

- describing the message as Level 0 or link-led;
- preferring brevity;
- concern about document overload;
- deciding that one map is less directly relevant to the chosen story module;
- the recipient already having received the files;
- a prior thread containing the webinar;
- a one-word instruction such as “send”; or
- the drafter’s editorial judgment that fewer materials may improve engagement.

The words “normally”, “consider”, “where relevant” or “smallest package” in older protocols are subordinate to this hard rule for media-facing email.

## 6. Exception rule

An omission is valid only where the user expressly approves the precise exception for the exact recipient and exact transmission, for example:

> Send this exact email to [recipient] without [named component].

The readiness record must identify:

- the omitted component;
- the reason;
- the user’s exact approval of the omission; and
- the remaining package.

General approval to send is not approval of an unmentioned exception. Silence is not an exception.

Do not present the package as ready and do not invoke a send action.

## 8. Final authorisation

The final approval must cover:

- exact To/Cc/Bcc;
- exact subject and full body;
- both exact source-map filenames/versions;
- the exact webinar URL;
- every Por Derecho and supporting link; and
- any exact exception.

A later change resets approval.

## 9. Post-send verification

Immediately after an authorised send, read the actual sent message and verify:

- `SENT` status;
- exact recipients and subject;
- both exact source-map attachments;
- direct webinar URL in the body;
- at least one Por Derecho link in the body;
- evidential-limits text; and
- the expected message/thread relationship.

Only then may the message be marked:

**SEND STATUS: SENT + VERIFIED — COMPLETE MEDIA CORE PACKAGE PRESENT.**

If the sent copy is incomplete, do not automatically correct it. Report the discrepancy, prepare a complete corrective package and obtain fresh exact authorisation.

## 10. Triggering control failure

A 23 August 2026 media email was initially prepared and sent without the two standard source maps and controlled webinar because the drafter treated the media package as discretionary and classified the message as link-led. A separately authorised same-thread correction was then sent with the full package.

The control lesson is permanent:

> Recipient tailoring selects the story; it does not silently remove mandatory media-core components.

No private address, Gmail identifier or full private message body is reproduced in this public-safe rule.
