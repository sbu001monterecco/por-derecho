## 7. Criminal-first legal controls

1. **Judicial conduct:** test CP Articles 446–449 act by act. A knowingly unjust resolution, refusal to judge or malicious delay requires its own statutory facts and mental element; an appeal, alleged contradiction or reversal is not enough.
2. **AC/private-actor patrimonial conduct:** use offence-neutral fact packages before testing deception/procedural-fraud, disloyal-administration, insolvency, document-use or participation theories. Require entrusted power, representation, knowledge, reliance/disposition, harm, benefit and causation as applicable.
3. **Procedural retaliation/obstruction:** do not treat Law 2/2023 or the newsroom event as CP Article 464 by analogy. Article 464 has its own violence/intimidation or retaliatory-act elements and procedural-person perimeter.
4. **Law 2/2023:** Articles 35, 36 and 38 require communication-specific qualification, detriment and causation. The Article 38.4 presumption is conditional and rebuttable. The Law does not itself convert retaliation into an offence.
5. **DP 1956/2026:** provisional dismissal remains the controlling status. A favourable RPL 2523/2025 result could be genuinely new evidence only if it proves a new element; it does not automatically reopen the file.
6. **Civil protection remains parallel:** criminal-first reader order must not allow limitation, interim preservation, restitution, appeal or asset-accounting work to lapse.
