## 4. Red-card meaning locked

The visible state is:

`RED-CARD CANDIDATE — IMMEDIATE HUMAN CUMULATIVE-RISK REVIEW`

It is not:

- a finding of professional breach;
- culpability or credibility scoring;
- a declaration of lack of suitability;
- an automatic suspension or sanction recommendation;
- an ICALPA decision;
- an assertion of ICALPA participation or endorsement.

It means that Por Derecho's methodology identifies a combination of current exposure, third-party rights, institutional reliance, public/private financial interests, evidential dispersion and practical irreversibility that warrants an express human decision on whether escalation is necessary.

A competent human remains free to confirm, downgrade or reject the candidate, but reasons, proportionality, alternatives, review date and clearance conditions must remain visible.

## 5. Locked safeguards

- allegation ≠ fact;
- registration ≠ review;
- review ≠ verification;
- verification ≠ sufficiency;
- sufficiency ≠ culpability;
- silence ≠ admission;
- continuing consequence ≠ automatically continuing misconduct;
- urgent protection ≠ provisional guilt;
- red attaches to file state, never name or portrait;
- colour is never the sole status signal;
- DIP 79 evidence does not enter DIP 80 without an explicit human relevance/provenance decision;
- context does not replace act, duty, date, knowledge, capacity, causation or the professional's explanation;
- the strongest reasonable contrary case and what would change the view must be shown;
- every consequential branch ends in a competent human gate;
- concern resolution receives equal visual legitimacy to escalation.
