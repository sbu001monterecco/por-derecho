## Portfolio rule

These are separate books only insofar as each continues to earn a distinct reader promise, audience, source base and narrative architecture. Working titles and sequencing may change. Public status does not imply an announced retail date or preorder.

**Role:** flagship human/institutional narrative.  
**Core line:** One Asset. One History.  
**Promise:** Sun Park, Playa Blanca, SunRockers, rupture, MYND Yaiza and the attempt to reconstruct one continuous history while the outcome remains live.  
**Canonical files:** `REASON_TO_BELIEVE_LIVE_MANUSCRIPT.md`, `BOOK_INTELLIGENCE_LIVE.md`.

## Book 2 — Law-mower Man [working title]

**Role:** AI / legal-memory / information-asymmetry book.  
**Promise:** What happens when AI can interrogate the whole case rather than one procedural fragment at a time?  
**Canonical file:** `LAW_MOWER_MAN_CONCEPT_ANALYSIS.md`.

## Book 3 — The SunRockers [working title]

**Role:** illustrated/oral/community history.  
**Promise:** preserve the lived Sun Park / Sun Park Living experience: longer stays, friendship, participation, later-life reinvention, Playa Blanca and human memory.  
**Dependencies:** archive review, image rights, oral-history consent, adverse/contrary memories as well as positive ones.  
**Core intelligence:** `../intelligence/SUNROCKERS_HUMAN_STORY_AND_ETHOS.md`.

## Book 4 — Justice in Pieces / Justicia en fragmentos [working title]

**Role:** broader institutional/systemic investigation.  
**Promise:** examine how one continuous history can become fragmented across courts, prosecution, insolvency, administrative systems and appeals; test whether Canary Islands/Las Palmas has distinctive features or merely provides an intense case study of wider Spanish problems.  
**Boundary:** comparative/systemic claims must be researched and proved, not inferred from Sun Park alone.

## Book 5 — Special Situations / Situaciones Especiales [working title]

**Role:** distressed-credit / claims-trading / NPL professional-investigative book.  
**Core proposition:** default is simultaneously a crisis for a borrower/debtor and a potential asset/opportunity for another market participant. The book investigates the ecosystem that converts payment difficulty into tradable claims, servicing mandates, enforcement strategies, restructurings, asset control and investment returns.

**Core subjects:**

- non-performing loans (NPLs);
- distressed and defaulted claims;
- claims/debt trading;
- loan portfolio sales;
- servicing and special servicing;
- securitisation / fund structures where relevant;
- enforcement and collateral;
- insolvency and restructuring;
- loan-to-own / credit-to-control strategies where documented;
- special-situations and opportunistic capital;
- valuation discounts and recovery economics;
- information asymmetry between originator, servicer, purchaser, debtor and court;
- legitimate distressed investing versus disputed, abusive or legally defective conduct.

**Sun Park laboratory:** Bankia → SAREB → Project Meridian → Promontoria Holding 122 / Cerberus perimeter → servicing → later creditor/acquirer route. This chain must be sourced precisely and must not substitute for broader market research.

**Reader promise:** explain the often-invisible market behind a default: who buys the claim, why, at what economics, who services it, how enforcement changes incentives, and when a distressed credit can become a route to underlying asset/control value.

**Target audiences:** investors, lawyers, restructuring/insolvency professionals, borrowers/owners, journalists, business-school/law students, regulators and general readers of financial-investigative nonfiction.

**Research requirement before full manuscript:** broaden beyond Sun Park with Spanish/EU/US comparative cases, primary transaction documents, servicing models, NPL market data, legal doctrine and interviews from multiple sides of the market.

**Publication boundary:** do not describe legitimate NPL/special-situations activity itself as an “underworld” in a criminal sense. `Underworld` is a narrative metaphor for a market largely invisible to ordinary borrowers/readers. Distinguish lawful investment, hard bargaining, disputed conduct and proven illegality.

## Provisional Book 6 — 4 Green Houses, One Red Hotel [working title]

**Role:** focused professional-accountability / adviser-to-creditor investigation centred on Cuatrecasas.  
**Core question:** what happens when a law firm that knew the rescue, ownership, financing and vulnerability of an asset later becomes a creditor executing against an entity inside that same perimeter?  
**Narrative hinge:** Matkator as both strategic property-holder in the Sun Park perimeter and later enforcement debtor.  
**Canonical file:** `FOUR_GREEN_HOUSES_ONE_RED_HOTEL_LIVE_MANUSCRIPT.md`.  
**Publication boundary:** the title is provocative, but the manuscript must not state collusion, intentional procedural fraud, misuse of confidential information, professional misconduct or an Acosta Matos-linked remate beneficiary as fact without proof. Cuatrecasas's strongest foreseeable defence must remain inside the book.

**Portfolio status:** provisional standalone book. It should not be added to the public five-book shelf until the author decides whether it earns a separate commercial reader promise or should remain a major strand/derived work within `Reason to Believe` or `Justice in Pieces`.

## Portfolio separation

- `Reason to Believe` = what happened and what it meant to live through it.
- `Law-mower Man` = what AI/legal memory revealed about complexity and information asymmetry.
- `The SunRockers` = what the place/community was and what people remember.
- `Justice in Pieces` = what the case may reveal about institutions and fragmentation.
- `Special Situations` = the financial market that turns defaulted obligations and distressed claims into investment opportunities and sometimes routes to control/value.
- `4 Green Houses, One Red Hotel` = the focused question of professional continuity, mandate protection, withdrawal/handover and later adverse enforcement by a former adviser.
