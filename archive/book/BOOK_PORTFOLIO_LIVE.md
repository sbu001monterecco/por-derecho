## Portfolio rule

These are six separate books because each currently earns a distinct reader promise, audience, source base and narrative architecture. Working titles and sequencing may evolve. Public status does not imply an announced retail date or preorder.

Do not force all new intelligence into `Reason to Believe`. Every material discovery should first be assigned to the book whose editorial territory it actually changes.

## Book 1 — Reason to Believe / Razón para creer

**Role:** flagship Spanish/Canary Islands justice and single-case-memory book.  
**Canonical line:** `ONE ASSET · ONE HISTORY · ONE MEMORY` / `UN ACTIVO · UNA HISTORIA · UNA MEMORIA`.  
**Core thesis:** a citizen can experience one continuous economic/factual controversy while the Spanish legal/institutional system divides it among proceedings, courts, prosecution, insolvency, enforcement, registries and public administrations. The book tests whether the rule of law — experienced principally through the Canary Islands — can retain the memory of the complete history, protect effective rights and correct legally relevant failure.  
**Case study:** Sun Park / MYND Yaiza.  
**Boundary:** this is not the SunRockers lifestyle/community book. The detailed 50+ community, friendship, participation and longer-stay history belongs primarily to Book 3 and appears here only as limited context for what the asset/place was and why it mattered. AI appears only as a later assisted-memory/research tool, not as the main subject.  
**Canonical files:** `REASON_TO_BELIEVE_LIVE_MANUSCRIPT.md`, `BOOK_INTELLIGENCE_LIVE.md`.  
**Public proposition:** `Many proceedings should not mean many realities.` The rule-of-law sequence is `remember → connect → hear → protect → review → correct`.

## Book 2 — Law-mower Man [working title]

**Role:** AI / legal-memory / information-asymmetry book.  
**Promise:** what changes when an ordinary person can finally interrogate the whole documentary record rather than one procedural fragment at a time? AI can change the economics of search, comparison, chronology, entity resolution and persistence; it does not supply truth or replace primary evidence, lawyers or judges.  
**Canonical file:** `LAW_MOWER_MAN_CONCEPT_ANALYSIS.md`.  
**Boundary:** working title requires professional trademark/confusion clearance; no official relationship with *The Lawnmower Man* film/story is implied. Avoid superhero/revenge framing.

## Book 3 — The SunRockers

**Role:** illustrated/oral/community human history.  
**Core line:** `50+ · GROWN-UP · ACTIVE · TOGETHER` / `50+ · ADULTOS · ACTIVOS · JUNTOS`.  
**Promise:** preserve the lived Sun Park / Sun Park Living experience: grown-up adults mainly 50+, longer stays, friendship, participation, contribution, activity, later-life reinvention, Playa Blanca and belonging.  
**Boundary:** not a young-family resort book and not a care-home/elderly-dependence proposition. Historic project-owned material may be used where rights permit; identifiable people and third-party photographs remain subject to consent/privacy/copyright checks.  
**Core intelligence:** `../intelligence/SUNROCKERS_HUMAN_STORY_AND_ETHOS.md`.

## Book 4 — Justice in Pieces / Justicia en fragmentos

**Role:** broader institutional/systemic investigation.  
**Core proposition:** a system can possess every file and still lose the complete history.  
**Promise:** examine how legitimate jurisdictional/procedural compartments can fragment chronology, prior knowledge, contradictions, economic effects and later developments; ask who sees the whole when each institution sees only its lawful part.  
**Boundary:** `Reason to Believe` is the lived documented stress test; `Justice in Pieces` is the systemic/comparative proposition. Canary Islands/Las Palmas is a case-study geography, not a pre-written systemic verdict. Comparative/systemic claims must be researched and proved beyond Sun Park.

## Book 5 — Special Situations / Situaciones Especiales

**Role:** distressed-credit / claims-trading / NPL professional-investigative book.  
**Core proposition:** default is simultaneously a crisis/liability for one party and a potential asset/investment opportunity for another.  
**Promise:** explain the often-invisible market behind a default: who buys the claim, why, at what economics, who services it, how restructuring/enforcement changes incentives, and when a credit position can become a route to collateral, control or underlying asset value.

**Core subjects:** NPLs; distressed/defaulted claims; debt/claims trading; loan portfolio sales; servicing/special servicing; securitisation/fund structures where relevant; enforcement/collateral; insolvency/restructuring; loan-to-own or credit-to-control where documented; special-situations capital; valuation/recovery economics; information asymmetry.

**Sun Park laboratory:** Bankia → SAREB → Project Meridian → Promontoria Holding 122 / Cerberus perimeter → servicing → later creditor/acquirer route. The chain must remain precisely sourced and cannot substitute for wider Spanish/EU/international research.

**Publication boundary:** do not present legitimate distressed investing as criminal. `Underworld` may only operate as a metaphor for a market largely invisible to ordinary readers. Distinguish lawful investment, hard bargaining, disputed conduct and proven illegality.

## Book 6 — 4 Green Houses, One Red Hotel

**Role:** focused professional-advice/accountability book.  
**Core question:** what can professional advice ultimately cost when measured not only by invoices but by what happens around the client's asset?  
**Evidence sequence:** `mandate → knowledge → advice → action/inaction → withdrawal/handover → later events → causation → loss`.  
**Cuatrecasas layer:** `Four Houses` may operate as a delayed literary echo of the proper name Cuatrecasas, but Cuatrecasas is not to be presented as though “Four Houses” were its official English name. `Green` remains layered literary wordplay, not an established finding of incompetence. `Red Hotel` means the hotel/situation was already in a red-alert condition.  
**Discipline:** bad outcome ≠ professional negligence. The strongest reasonable professional defence belongs in the book. Do not state collusion, intentional procedural fraud, misuse of confidential information, professional misconduct or an Acosta Matos-linked remate beneficiary as fact without proof.  
**Canonical file:** `FOUR_GREEN_HOUSES_ONE_RED_HOTEL_LIVE_MANUSCRIPT.md` where present/private controls permit.  
**Portfolio status:** standalone Book 6 with dedicated public EN/ES pages and a place on the six-book public shelf.

## Portfolio separation

- `Reason to Believe` = Spanish/Canary Islands rule of law, one continuous case/history/memory, effective protection and capacity for correction.
- `Law-mower Man` = AI-assisted documentary capability, legal memory and information asymmetry.
- `The SunRockers` = 50+ people/community/place/belonging.
- `Justice in Pieces` = systemic institutional fragmentation and comparative design.
- `Special Situations` = the financial market that transforms distressed/defaulted claims into investment opportunities and sometimes routes to control/value.
- `4 Green Houses, One Red Hotel` = professional mandate/knowledge/advice/handover/causation/accountability.

If two books begin making substantially the same reader promise, their boundaries must be redesigned before publication.
