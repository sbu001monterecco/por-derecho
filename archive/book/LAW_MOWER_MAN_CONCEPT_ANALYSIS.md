# LAW-MOWER MAN — transformative concept analysis

**Status:** working book-intelligence candidate · 15 August 2026

## Executive conclusion

**Standalone-book viability: 88/100, subject to title/trademark clearance and disciplined separation from the 1992 film's protected expression.**

The concept is strongest when it is not an adaptation, sequel or character-copy of *The Lawnmower Man*. It should be an original nonfiction work about a real person whose information-processing capability changed through AI while confronting a vast, fragmented legal-economic corpus.

The safest and strongest formulation is:

> **Gil is the identifiable human Law-mower Man. The opposition is represented primarily through original structural archetypes — gatekeepers, fragmenters, repeat players, beneficiaries, institutional silos and information asymmetry — while named real people or entities enter only through documented acts, roles and consequences.**

Do not map real adversaries one-to-one onto copyrighted fictional villains.

## 1. Source-work distinction

Stephen King's original short story and the 1992 film are materially different works. The King story concerns a bizarre lawn-service encounter and does not contain the film's central virtual-reality/intelligence-augmentation plot. The 1992 film instead follows Jobe Smith, whose cognitive capabilities are radically increased through experimental technology/virtual reality, with institutional/scientific actors losing control of the consequences.

The distinction matters both creatively and legally. The film itself became the subject of litigation by King over marketing that associated his name with a film he argued bore little meaningful resemblance to his story. That history is a useful warning: cultural reference should be explicit and honest, not used to suggest an official connection that does not exist.

## 2. Original nonfiction proposition

The core real-world proposition is not that AI made Gil superhuman.

It is:

> **AI changed the economics of reading, comparison and persistence.**

For years, a single continuous history could be distributed across courts, creditors, companies, email, registries, public authorities and thousands of documents. Modern AI does not decide the truth, but it can make previously impractical cross-corpus questions practical to ask.

Candidate central lines:

- **I wasn't becoming more intelligent. I was becoming less blind.**
- **AI didn't give me the truth. It gave me the ability to stop losing the thread.**
- **For years the system had more files than I could read. Then the economics of reading changed.**
- **The machine did not know the answer. It let me keep asking the whole question.**

Preferred current line:

> **The machine did not know the answer. It let me keep asking the whole question.**

## 3. Gil's transformation arc

### Stage 1 — Buried

One individual faces accumulated case files, companies, creditors, emails, decisions, proceedings and institutional language. The information exists, but its volume and fragmentation make continuous reconstruction extraordinarily difficult.

### Stage 2 — Searching

ChatGPT and connected-source tools begin to support search, translation, chronology, comparison, classification and iterative questioning.

### Stage 3 — Mapping

The material becomes structured into:

- one asset history;
- title rail;
- credit rail;
- money rail;
- control rail;
- decision rail;
- knowledge rail;
- consequence rail.

### Stage 4 — Seeing

The result is not omniscience. It is visibility: contradictions, stale assumptions, missing documents, recurring entities and causal chains become easier to identify.

### Stage 5 — Testing

The system must attack its own conclusions through adverse evidence, competing explanations, source provenance, legal distinctions and publication red-teaming.

### Stage 6 — Public memory

The personal reconstruction expands into the Knowledge Project, Fundación Por Derecho, *Reason to Believe*, the live manuscript/intelligence system and the broader question of whether AI can help institutions and citizens preserve legal memory.

## 5. Adversarial archetypes

### The Gatekeepers

Control access to information, procedure, property, decisions or institutional channels.

Visual language: doors, checkpoints, segmented files, inaccessible corridors.

### The Fragmenters

Represent the effect of dividing one continuous history into many procedural or institutional containers.

Visual language: one asset split into many windows/case numbers.

### The Repeat Players

Actors with professional continuity, specialised knowledge, institutional familiarity, capital or procedural endurance unavailable to most individuals.

Visual language: established networks, repeated pathways, dense institutional nodes.

### The Beneficiaries

Represent persons/entities receiving economic, strategic or institutional benefit from an outcome.

Visual language: value flows and consequence maps. Never use criminal iconography unless a criminal finding exists.

### The System

The most important antagonist can be non-human:

- information asymmetry;
- fragmentation;
- institutional amnesia;
- procedural inertia;
- complexity;
- routing;
- responsibility diffusion.

This is stronger than reducing the book to 'Gil versus villains'.

## 6. Rules for named adversaries

Do not write or illustrate:

`Real person X = film villain Y`.

Instead use:

`film function → structural real-world analogue → documented acts → critical difference`.

Examples:

- a film corporation controlling access to experimental systems may inspire discussion of **institutional or commercial information asymmetry**, but does not establish that a real company shared the film corporation's motives;
- a film gatekeeper may inspire a visual metaphor for **documented access restrictions**, but a named real person should be described only through evidence-backed acts;
- a fictional antagonist's hidden agenda must never be used to imply hidden intent by a real judge, prosecutor, insolvency professional, creditor or executive without evidence.

## 7. One Asset. One History as the 'algorithm'

The most original bridge from the project to this book is:

> **Follow the thing, not only the file.**

Maintain simultaneously:

1. Follow the title.
2. Follow the credit.
3. Follow the money.
4. Follow the control.
5. Follow the decisions.
6. Follow the knowledge.
7. Follow the consequences.

AI's role is to make the simultaneous maintenance of these rails cheaper and more persistent, while human/legal review preserves procedural distinctions.

## 8. Institutional amnesia

Candidate core intellectual proposition:

> **A legal system can possess records without possessing memory.**

Storage means documents exist. Memory requires the ability to recognise:

- earlier facts;
- later contradictions;
- changed assumptions;
- who knew what and when;
- what was never decided;
- what consequences followed.

Candidate proposition:

> **AI's most important contribution to justice may be less artificial intelligence than artificial institutional memory.**

This should be strongly red-teamed before publication.

## 9. The Law-mower metaphor system

- **Lawn / ground:** underlying factual reality.
- **Overgrowth:** accumulated procedural/documentary complexity.
- **Weeds:** duplication, stale assumptions, inconsistent names, routing, irrelevant documents, contradictions and missing connections.
- **Mower:** AI-assisted search/reconstruction.
- **Operator/gardener:** human judgment.
- **Map:** chronology/knowledge graph.
- **Cuttings:** duplicate, unsupported or irrelevant material removed from the active view.
- **Danger of mowing too aggressively:** loss of nuance, context and legally important boundaries.

Central warning:

> **A legal AI system without provenance is not a mower. It is a chainsaw.**

## 10. Sun Park as the opening laboratory

The opening laboratory should be Sun Park / Playa Blanca rather than a generic AI essay.

Narrative progression:

Sun Park → SunRockers → ownership/economic history → LPB/Matkator/Aweswell → Comunidad → Bankia → SAREB → Meridian → PH122/Cerberus → servicing → CAM/HNT → insolvency/enforcement → judicial/prosecutorial/administrative tracks → MYND Yaiza → AI reconstruction.

The governing question is:

> **What happens when AI is asked to preserve legal distinctions but stop treating every institutional container as a separate history of the same underlying asset?**

## 11. Counter-case / red team

The book must give serious space to these objections:

1. AI may amplify Gil's confirmation bias.
2. Cross-file pattern recognition can create false connections.
3. Legal fragmentation often exists for legitimate jurisdictional reasons.
4. 'Whole case' thinking can erase legal boundaries.
5. More information can create more confidence without more correctness.
6. Institutional actors cannot reasonably know every document in every related file.
7. A litigant may use AI as an accusation generator rather than an evidence tool.

Required response architecture:

- source provenance;
- verified/inference/disputed/gap labels;
- adversarial evidence;
- competing explanations;
- separate legal/evidential/narrative layers;
- human publication approval;
- correction history.

## 12. Visual identity — Gil as Law-mower Man

Gil can be recognisably portrayed, provided final artwork uses an authorised/current likeness.

Preferred visual:

- recognisable contemporary Gil, three-quarter or rear-profile;
- ordinary clothes, not copied film costume;
- Playa Blanca / Atlantic / volcanic terrain;
- Sun Park/MYND physical place as anchor;
- a landscape overgrown with files, procedural branches, timelines and corporate/credit nodes;
- one continuous luminous evidence line through the terrain;
- where the line passes, the underlying geography/history becomes legible;
- institutional/commercial pressure represented as abstract architecture/networks/silhouettes, not caricatured named villains.

Avoid:

- film poster composition;
- copied VR visuals;
- Jobe Smith likeness/costume;
- glowing-superhuman imagery;
- named judges/prosecutors/executives rendered as sinister villains;
- criminal symbols implying guilt.

Core visual message:

> **One man. Millions of words. One history that should not disappear inside the files.**

## 14. Title/IP preliminary assessment

Under US Copyright Office guidance, titles, names, short phrases, ideas, systems and methods are not protected by copyright as such, while original expression is. That does **not** resolve trademark, unfair-competition, passing-off or consumer-confusion questions.

The working title **LAW-MOWER MAN** is deliberately close in sound/concept to a famous film title. Before ISBN, Amazon metadata, advertising spend or merchandise, perform professional clearance across at least US, UK and EU trademark databases and publishing/entertainment uses.

Until then, label it **working title / internal-public concept**, not cleared final brand.

Safer alternative titles to retain:

- **THE WHOLE CASE** — *How AI Could Give Justice Its Memory Back*
- **JUSTICE REMEMBERS**
- **ARTIFICIAL MEMORY**
- **THE LAW MOWER**

## 17. Publication stance

This book should never claim that AI proved criminality, corruption, collusion or judicial capture.

The strongest thesis is structural:

> **Complexity can create information asymmetry and institutional amnesia even without conspiracy. AI may reduce the cost of reconstructing continuity, but only if provenance, adverse evidence and human judgment remain central.**

That formulation is original, scalable beyond Sun Park and much more durable than a villain-versus-hero narrative.

## 18. Current recommendation

**Proceed as a serious Book 2 candidate.**

Use `LAW-MOWER MAN` as the working title while clearance is pending. Develop the nonfiction concept independently. Make Gil visually and narratively concrete. Keep the adversarial world structurally metaphorical until documentary evidence justifies naming specific actors and acts.

The best marketing proposition currently is:

> **LAW-MOWER MAN**  
> **What Happens When AI Reads the Whole Case?**

Secondary proposition:

> **Can AI Help Justice Remember?**

The book earns separate existence if it becomes a general theory of **AI-assisted legal memory and the changing economics of complexity**, rather than merely retelling the Sun Park dispute with a science-fiction metaphor.
