## 2. Title collision / differentiation scan

### Reason to Believe — AMBER

The exact title is heavily occupied across religion, biography and romance, including books by R.C. Sproul, Harry Freedman, Dovid Gottlieb, Gina Gordon and Rebecca Yarros. The title remains usable as a working title but needs strong subtitle/author/visual differentiation and a final professional metadata/trademark check.

**Current differentiation recommendation:**

`REASON TO BELIEVE`  
`ONE ASSET. ONE HISTORY.`  
`GIL MARER`

Consider whether the final metadata title should include a longer subtitle even if the front cover remains minimal.

### Law-mower Man — AMBER / HIGH-CLEARANCE NEED

The hyphenated phrase is distinctive but intentionally echoes a famous film/story title. Retain only as a working title until US/UK/EU trademark, passing-off and retailer-confusion clearance. The concept itself remains strong under fallback titles such as `THE WHOLE CASE` or `JUSTICE REMEMBERS`.

### The SunRockers — AMBER

The term `Sunrockers` is used publicly by the Japanese basketball team Sunrockers Shibuya and may create search noise. The historic SunRocker/SunRockers project corpus makes the term authentic to this story, but title differentiation may benefit from a subtitle strongly anchoring `Sun Park / Playa Blanca`.

### Justice in Pieces — AMBER

`Justice in Pieces` is already the name of a Canadian legal education series/platform. A final book title should therefore either be differentiated strongly with subtitle/author or reconsidered before ISBN/advertising investment.

### Special Situations — AMBER

The term is generic and deeply established in investment literature. There are historic books such as Maurece Schiller's works on special-situations investing and many modern finance texts/chapters. Strong subtitle differentiation is essential. A more ownable title may eventually be desirable.

**Recommended working form:**

`SPECIAL SITUATIONS`  
`WHEN DEFAULT BECOMES AN INVESTMENT`

## 6. Rights / consent blockers

- copyright ownership/licence for `sun park.jpg`;

- rights in brochure/website photography and graphic design;
- consent/publication basis for identifiable guests/staff in photographs;
- rights in third-party review/platform screenshots;
- third-party logos/brand marks;
- MYND or other current operator imagery/branding if reproduced.

### Can deploy now without those clearances

- newly generated original cover artwork that does not reproduce protected third-party visual expression;
- text descriptions and source-disciplined hooks;
- abstract/pre-AI documentary textures created for the books;
- share buttons and book-specific URLs;
- archival material described in prose without reproducing the asset;
- invitation for former guests/staff to contact the project, subject to a suitable privacy/consent workflow.
