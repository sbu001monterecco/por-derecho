## 5. Controlling conclusions preserved

### 5.1 Strict statutory PER status is not established

The public record does not declare that CAM or an identified natural person **was** a statutory *persona especialmente relacionada* or a de facto administrator of LPB. The historical insolvency law applicable at the legally relevant date must be applied before closing that classification.

### 5.2 Possible de facto administration remains a live inquiry

The legally relevant question is whether CAM itself, or conduct legally attributable to identified people, amounted to sufficiently stable, autonomous and general management of LPB, including real decision-making power and substitution for formal corporate direction.

This is an open inquiry, not a published finding.

### 5.3 Communero and creditor status are insufficient by themselves

Being a creditor, debt assignee, secured creditor, owner, communero, Community participant, bidder, proposed purchaser or awardee does not automatically establish statutory related-party or de facto-administrator status.

### 5.4 Practical control is evidence, not the conclusion

Keys, security, access, maintenance, works, data, systems, possession, operation or income may be relevant evidence. They do not automatically prove general corporate management of LPB. Each act must be attributed by identifying who requested, authorised, ordered, executed and paid for it.

### 5.5 Accumulated roles create an independent material conflict

Even if strict PER status fails, the accumulation of roles remains independently material. The capacity ledger includes, where supported by date-specific evidence:

- debt assignee and secured creditor;
- owner or communero of identified properties;
- Community participant or beneficiary;
- security, access, maintenance or works actor;
- negotiator, bidder and proposed purchaser;
- awardee;
- participant in the later HNT/economic-benefit perimeter.

The resulting questions concern enhanced disclosure, segregation, information symmetry, independent valuation and independent verification.

### 5.6 Legal bodies and asset perimeters must remain separate

The following are not interchangeable:

- the owners’ Community;
- CEXP or the exploitation community;
- any community of property;
- any informal owners’ group;
- LPB as insolvent owner;
- Matkator;
- third-party owners and assets.

Every minute, vote, debt certificate, access decision, work, payment and asset must be attributed to the correct body and property perimeter.
