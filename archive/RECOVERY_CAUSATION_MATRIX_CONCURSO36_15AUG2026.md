## Actor lenses

### AC
Follow the money, estate assets, accounting, reports, bids, deeds, corrective action and preservation of causes of action. For post-7-Jun-2018 issues, also identify what the AC could itself authorise, prevent, restore, require or report and what practical control had shifted outside LPB.

### Court / Judge
Follow evidence actually before the court, issue within jurisdiction, decision, reasoning, protective alternatives, appellate correction and continuing effect. A later adjudication/title act must not be used as retroactive proof that a materially earlier control act was authorised.

### LAJ / Office
Follow request, DIOR, testimony/certification, finality, service, delivery, purpose, external use and current documentary effect.

### Private actors / notary / Registry / operators
Follow legal identity, authority, title, consideration, implementation, succession, possession, exploitation, fruits and current benefit without collapsing distinct legal persons into one perimeter. Creditor status, defined-unit title, material control, whole-hotel legal possession and later adjudication are separate propositions.

## Recovery outputs

For each row, close the chain with one or more of:

- preservation;
- production/certification;
- accounting;
- jurisdictional correction;
- restitution/reintegration;
- deed or Registry correction where legally available;
- return of possession/access/control;
- accounting for fruits/revenues;
- damages/insurance/State-liability analysis;
- disciplinary/professional consequence where independently proved;
- criminal referral only where a specific offence is independently evidenced.

## Status discipline

Use: `VERIFIED FACT` · `PARTY ALLEGATION` · `EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE` · `UNRESOLVED QUESTION` · `CORRECTED/SUPERSEDED`.

Do not use this matrix as proof of a conspiracy. Its function is the opposite: to force every asserted link to stand or fall on its own evidence.
