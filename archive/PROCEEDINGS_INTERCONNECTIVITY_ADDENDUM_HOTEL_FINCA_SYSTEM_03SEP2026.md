## 5. Prohibited graph shortcuts

Do not create any edge that implies:

- 8584 is the only relevant Matkator title;
- every hotel title is owned by CAM/HNT;
- physical integration transfers legal title;
- operational unity merges estates;
- all Acosta acquisitions were non-consensual;
- a valid deed eliminates prior injury or owner-accounting questions;
- DP 748, ETJ 163 and Concurso 36/2012 are one proceeding;
- chronology or common benefit proves conspiracy;
- “enablement” is a standalone offence;
- “fraude concursal” identifies an offence without an actor and act.
