# Concurso 36/2012 — judicial response chain closeout (2021–2022)

Date: 29 August 2026

## Purpose

This continuity record closes a material gap in the public multi-track treatment of Concurso 36/2012. It replaces generic statements that the judge “ignored” the 2021 RICPE/title/estate warning with an act-by-act chain showing what entered the proceeding, what reached the decision-maker, how it was treated, and how the resulting orders connected to later certified copies, deed and registration.

This document does **not** declare judicial misconduct, prevaricación, collusion, fraud or criminal causation. Those require separate competent investigation and proof.

## Primary contradiction verified

Two signed Autos dated **15 October 2021** have been re-opened and compared.

### Auto resolving challenges to the 12 May 2021 Providencia

The order states that the challenges had become moot because, by 18 May 2021, the disposal proposal in CAM's favour had been definitively approved. It states that **“habiéndose celebrado la subasta y adjudicado las fincas al mejor oferente”** the challenges no longer had useful effect.

Source locator: `Adjunto1(55).pdf` — signed 15 October 2021.

### Auto resolving challenges to the 18 May 2021 Auto

The order states that the relevant event was not a judicial auction but a flexible appearance/offer-improvement mechanism. It further states that **“la licitación ni siquiera se celebró”** because the improving bidder did not appear and did not lodge the required amount.

Source locator: `Adjunto1(56).pdf` — signed 15 October 2021.

### Safe conclusion

The contradiction is textual and primary-source verified. No inference is needed to say that the two orders describe the decisive May event incompatibly. The legal consequences of that contradiction for finality, transparency, defence rights, adjudication and later title acts remain matters for legal analysis; the contradiction alone is not proof of intent or judicial wrongdoing.

## Five judicial failure/omission tests

The public page now separates five audit questions instead of making one generic accusation:

- **Decisional coherence:** what legal effect follows from the two 15 October descriptions being incompatible?
- **Treatment of new documentary evidence:** what integrated decision reconciled the 54 CAM / 190 LPB / 18 third-party perimeter, investor promotion, estate risk and bidding competition before maintaining adjudication?
- **Protection of masa activa and unidad productiva:** what inspection, preservation, inventory, fruits/revenue accounting, works control or going-concern safeguard was actually ordered and implemented?
- **Extraconcursal perimeter:** what title, consent or order justified control, works, use or operation over each non-LPB finca?
- **From warning to title and capital:** what complete title/litigation/third-party/works file was given to each later decision-maker and what corrections were made to the 2020 investment presentation?

## Publication rule

Use “ignored” only where a certified record demonstrates a material request received and left without response. For this 2021–2022 chain the stronger and more accurate formulation is: **formally notified; documentary material served; placed before the judge; later confirmed admitted; two contradictory orders issued; contradiction challenged; material ultimately declared irrelevant; CAM-favourable result maintained; title-enabling acts followed.**
