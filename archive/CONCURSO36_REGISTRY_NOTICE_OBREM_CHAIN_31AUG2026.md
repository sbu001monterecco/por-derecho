## Controlling result

The current primary-source chain supports a narrower conclusion than either “the 26 June order stopped everything” or “the later sale was judicially authorised.” The 26-Jun-2018 Auto was an operative partial suspension: it stopped realisation of the expressly listed mortgaged fincas while stating that liquidation of other estate assets should continue. LPB had asked for broader suspension. The later signed 24-Oct-2019 Auto treated that partial approach as erroneous when applied to a liquidation architecture the court described as unitary; held that the 28-Nov-2018 unlinking/sale had been completed without prior judicial authorisation; set aside the 12-Dec-2018 LAJ direction; and refused convalidation.

This does not make the 26-Jun Auto void or criminal. It supplies a literal implementation defence for the AC, but not blanket authority to depart from the Plan, defeat `ob rem` links or treat a post-deed LAJ direction as prior judicial authorisation. The 24-Oct corrective ruling is material contrary/exculpatory evidence against a theory that the judge knowingly perpetuated the transaction. Its practical implementation remains open.

## Actor-specific and multitrack assessment

### CAM / Acosta Matos and the Insolvency Administrator

The finite allegation is not that the 26-Jun order literally suspended every asset. It is that the actors may have used its residual wording to consummate a separate transaction inconsistent with the approved unitary architecture and without prior judicial authorisation. The AC's strongest defence is the express continuation clause. The response is the 24-Oct court finding, the transaction/authorisation chronology, the `ob rem` qualification and the still-open money/title/possession/accounting bridge. Knowledge, purpose, benefit and causal contribution remain actor-specific and unresolved.

### The judge

The correct judicial test is decision-specific. The 26-Jun partial scope may have been legally available under former Article 197.6 LC even though the same court later called the partial approach erroneous in the Plan's unitary context. The 24-Oct correction is not proof of earlier knowing injustice; it is contrary evidence that must remain prominent. Any prevaricación hypothesis requires much more than error, inconsistency or an adverse outcome.

### Tías Property Registry

Institutional receipt is proved. The Registrar's response gives a reasoned Article 6/Article 18 Mortgage Act position and the qualification itself identified an `ob rem` obstacle. The present record therefore does not support “the Registry ignored the warning” or a collaboration conclusion. The remaining test is documentary: presentation journal, qualifying title, full qualification, notices to persons with standing, entries and later cancellations/rectifications.

### Cuatrecasas / later La Laguna fee-enforcement lane

The Registry intervention is a protective act and must be credited. It defeats a total-inactivity or total-abandonment proposition. It does not resolve whether the entire mandate was competently scoped, urgently pursued, sustained after the Registrar's response, reconciled after non-convalidation, handed over, or correctly billed/enforced. Professional negligence and later fee enforcement are separate causal tracks. DP 748 is a finite procedural-fraud hypothesis only if an exact deceptive representation or title, judicial error/reliance, prejudicial decision and patrimonial consequence are proved. This source chain does not prove Cuatrecasas joined a CAM/AC/judicial plan.

## Criminal / investigative boundary

“Estafa concursal” is recorded only as party shorthand, not as an autonomous finding or a substitute for elements of a specific offence. For procedural fraud, the chronology is decisive: the 28-Nov deed predates the 12-Dec LAJ direction by fourteen days, so that direction cannot have induced execution of the already completed deed. A viable investigative chain would have to identify a separate deceptive representation/use, recipient, judicial error, economically prejudicial resolution and causal benefit/harm—for example in a later convalidation or implementation step—while testing the legitimate partial-suspension interpretation.

Error plus correction is insufficient for judicial prevaricación. The proof question is a specified judicial decision, objective injustice at the required legal threshold, actor-specific knowledge or gross inexcusable ignorance, and causal consequence. No controlled source in this pass establishes those elements.
