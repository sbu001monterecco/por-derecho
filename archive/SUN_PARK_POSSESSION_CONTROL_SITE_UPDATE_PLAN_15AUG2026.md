## 1. Controlled editorial position

The public site should become clearer and stronger on three propositions, while preserving source limits.

### A. 7 June 2018

**Public fact/inference combination:** the evidence supports a decisive de facto change in control of Sun Park’s principal access and practical operation through security, locks/cylinders, chains/padlocks, key/access restrictions, exclusion and interruption of the prior operation.

**Attributed allegation:** Gil Marer says the cumulative effect was full practical possession of the hotel by CAM and allied Community actors.

**Boundary:** this was not a reviewed judicial transfer of whole-hotel possession or title; the exact finca-by-finca reach, duration and actor instructions remain to be certified.

### B. Before 7 June 2018

The event must no longer appear as a sudden isolated incident.

The first specifically dated physical-incursion lead currently located is **2 October 2017 — Q4 2017, not Q3**. Contemporaneous party communications then describe further access, security/locksmith activity, plans, measurements, owner-like assertions and hotel-operation interference through January–March 2018.

### C. Predecessor architecture

CAM did not create the control machinery in 2017–2018. The repository should show the pre-existing architecture developed from at least 2011 through Community offices, debt/vote mechanisms, document custody, litigation, maintenance/access relationships and later security implementation. This is functional continuity to prove actor by actor—not automatic legal succession or collective criminal liability.
