## 2. Approved plain-English accusation

> **I accuse Judge Alberto López Villarrubia of knowingly turning a distorted and materially incomplete account of what happened in Luchy Playa Blanca’s insolvency into judicial findings against me.**
>
> I say this was not simply a judge reaching a conclusion with which I disagree. My accusation is that, by the time he signed Judgment 163/2023, the court had already been confronted over years with evidence showing that I was trying to preserve LPB, keep the hotel business alive, obtain financing, pay or clarify the debts, find a lawful way out of the insolvency, challenge what I considered excessive debt and interest calculations, and protect company and third-party assets from private actors.
>
> In June 2018, there was even a contemporaneously documented attempt to put before the court a structured and financeable route to pay the insolvency liabilities, restore LPB’s independence and continue operating the hotel. I say that history is fundamentally incompatible with the later picture of me as someone who simply obstructed, failed to cooperate, abandoned the company or deliberately aggravated its insolvency.
>
> My accusation is that Judge López Villarrubia did not merely fail to correct the insolvency administrator’s account. In Judgment 163/2023 he **adopted selected parts of that account as his own judicial findings**, even though other important accusations made by the insolvency administrator and the prosecutor were rejected or substantially narrowed by the judgment itself.
>
> I particularly challenge the findings concerning Pink Canary, unpaid rent, alleged non-cooperation, causation of insolvency damage and the €3,032,010.34 award. I say those findings must now be placed beside the complete documentary record and reconstructed line by line.
>
> I also accuse him, more broadly, of having presided over a process in which repeated warnings about debt calculation, asset ownership, physical control of the hotel, works, liquidation, third-party property, the conduct of the insolvency administrator and attempts to rescue the company were not given the effective judicial protection or scrutiny they required, while decisions with increasingly irreversible consequences were allowed to proceed.
>
> **My criminal allegation is judicial prevarication:** that he knowingly issued an unjust judgment. In ordinary English, I am accusing him of knowing that material parts of the factual and causal case he was adopting could not fairly be reconciled with the record before the court, yet signing the judgment and imposing serious personal and financial consequences anyway.
>
> I am **not** saying that every adverse decision proves a crime, that every document in the court file was necessarily personally read by him, or that I have proved corruption, payment, collusion or a secret agreement with the private parties. Those are separate propositions requiring separate evidence.
>
> What I am saying is narrower and more serious: **I believe the documentary record can show, proposition by proposition, that he knew enough about the contrary reality for the surviving adverse findings in Judgment 163/2023 not to be explained simply as an innocent misunderstanding.**
>
> That accusation has not been judicially established. It is my accusation, and I am asking for it to be tested against the complete certified court record, the hearing record, the documents received by the court and the chronology of what the court knew and when.

### Short approved version

> **I accuse Judge Alberto López Villarrubia of knowingly converting a materially false and selective insolvency narrative into punitive judicial findings against me, despite a documentary record showing years of attempts to rescue LPB, finance its exit from insolvency, preserve the hotel and defend its assets. I allege that this crossed the line from judicial error into knowingly issuing an unjust judgment. I am asking for that accusation to be tested against the complete court record, not accepted on my word.**

## 3. Approved Spanish public rendering

> **Acuso al magistrado Alberto López Villarrubia de convertir conscientemente un relato distorsionado y materialmente incompleto de lo ocurrido en la insolvencia de Luchy Playa Blanca en conclusiones judiciales contra mí.**
>
> No sostengo simplemente que un juez llegó a una conclusión con la que discrepo. Mi acusación es que, cuando firmó la Sentencia 163/2023, el órgano judicial llevaba años siendo confrontado con documentación que mostraba que yo intentaba preservar LPB, mantener vivo el negocio hotelero, obtener financiación, pagar o aclarar las deudas, encontrar una salida legal del concurso, impugnar lo que consideraba cálculos excesivos de deuda e intereses y proteger activos de la sociedad y de terceros frente a actores privados.
>
> En junio de 2018 existió además un intento documentado contemporáneamente de llevar al Juzgado una vía estructurada y financiable para pagar el pasivo concursal, devolver autonomía a LPB y continuar la explotación hotelera. Sostengo que ese historial es fundamentalmente incompatible con la imagen posterior de que yo simplemente obstruí, no colaboré, abandoné la empresa o agravé deliberadamente su insolvencia.
>
> Mi acusación es que el magistrado López Villarrubia no se limitó a no corregir el relato del Administrador Concursal. En la Sentencia 163/2023 **adoptó selectivamente partes de ese relato como conclusiones judiciales propias**, aunque la misma sentencia rechazó o redujo sustancialmente otras acusaciones importantes formuladas por la Administración Concursal y el Ministerio Fiscal.
>
> Impugno especialmente las conclusiones relativas a Pink Canary, las rentas no cobradas, la supuesta falta de colaboración, la causalidad del perjuicio concursal y la condena de €3.032.010,34. Sostengo que esas conclusiones deben colocarse ahora junto al expediente documental completo y reconstruirse línea por línea.
>
> Le acuso también, en un plano más amplio, de haber presidido un proceso en el que advertencias reiteradas sobre el cálculo de deuda, la titularidad de activos, el control material del hotel, las obras, la liquidación, bienes de terceros, la conducta del Administrador Concursal y los intentos de rescate de la empresa no recibieron, en mi opinión, la protección o el escrutinio judicial efectivos que exigían, mientras se permitía avanzar a decisiones con consecuencias cada vez más difíciles de revertir.
>
> **Mi acusación penal es de prevaricación judicial:** sostengo que dictó a sabiendas una sentencia injusta. En lenguaje corriente, le acuso de saber que partes materiales del relato fáctico y causal que estaba adoptando no podían conciliarse justamente con el expediente que tenía ante sí y, aun así, firmar la sentencia e imponer consecuencias personales y patrimoniales graves.
>
> **No** sostengo que toda decisión adversa demuestre un delito, que todo documento incorporado al expediente fuera necesariamente leído personalmente por él, ni que haya probado corrupción, pago, connivencia o un acuerdo secreto con los actores privados. Esas son proposiciones distintas que exigen prueba propia.
>
> Lo que sostengo es algo más preciso y más grave: **creo que el expediente puede demostrar, proposición por proposición, que conocía suficiente realidad contraria como para que las conclusiones adversas que sobrevivieron en la Sentencia 163/2023 no puedan explicarse simplemente como un malentendido inocente.**
>
> Esta acusación no ha sido establecida judicialmente. Es mi acusación y pido que sea contrastada con el expediente judicial certificado completo, la grabación y acta de la vista, los documentos efectivamente recibidos por el órgano judicial y la cronología de qué sabía el Juzgado y cuándo.
