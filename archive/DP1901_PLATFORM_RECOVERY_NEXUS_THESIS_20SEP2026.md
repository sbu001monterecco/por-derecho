Ref.21 concerns the five named private persons and their individually attributed acts. The source keeps the insolvency administrator's separate complaint and the judge complaint distinct. Its claimed wrong is not merely a disagreement with the judge's 2025 decisions.

The contemporary chronology supports Ref.21's link to DP1901: Gil's firsthand account, the 25-June communication to the procuradora, and the 9-July operational mapping/tender. ### B. The judge complaint's wider subject versus the 2025-only reasoning

Ref.24 itself was not just a repetition of the original DIP2 dispute. Its 31-page public principal package contains four front pages and 27 principal pages. The complaint identifies five broader pillars: the financed exit and knowledge; credit/debt treatment and later res judicata reliance; the OB REM operation and non-validation; bidding/adjudication; and the creditor/bidder/acquirer identity chain. The September order's first ground expressly acknowledges a broader pre-2025 nucleus outside DIP2's delimited object. Its subsequent reasoning nevertheless centres on prevaricación standards, disagreement with the two 2025 resolutions, asserted absence of objective support, and appeal wording. That is a concrete **scope-comparison question**, not proof that the court read no annex or that any allegation was established.

The court's phrase about no recorded appeal must be compared with the corporate parties and appeal objects in the source record. Do not treat LPB/Aweswell appeals as necessarily proving that Gil personally appealed the same resolutions in the same capacity.

## 5. Why the subject collision matters economically

The complaint sought access to records not generally in the complainant's possession: corporate messages, Community books/audio/powers, security instructions, bank and project ledgers, CAD/BIM, customer/digital records, investor material and public administrative files. Those records may determine who controlled the platform, what they represented, what decisions followed, what was financed and who obtained value.

The recovery connection therefore runs through a specific sequence:

**private allegation and identified proof request → official receipt/association → competent consideration → decision on production/preservation → evidence capable of tracing loss/gain/assets → any justified recovery or protective measure.**

Impairment at an early stage can matter to evidence availability, identification of respondents/beneficiaries, valuation, tracing and effective relief. But a refusal to investigate does not itself establish the alleged underlying loss, every defendant's liability, that a measure would have been granted, or a particular additional damage amount.

For each alleged incremental injury record the source request, competent power, actual omission/decision, realistic alternative, time-sensitive record/asset, later consequence and supporting evidence. Separate new losses from lasting effects of older injury. No presumptive sum is added to the accounts' recovery profile.

The **15-September DI22 decree** joins the August20/26/27 submissions, maintains archive and relies on judicialisation. It does not identify the relevant judicial proceeding on its face. Its content is not proof that it meant DP1901 or that the same facts were all included there. Under LECrim art.773.2 the same-facts issue is legally material; judicialisation is not inherently improper.

The risk to test is a loop in which one route declines action because another is said to be examining the facts while the other route has a different subject or has not demonstrably received the relevant corpus. **An institutional acknowledgement or archive is not a universal merits clearance for all later corporate, tourism, investor or funding decisions.** Evidence of an actual later reliance on the archive should be recorded separately from a forecast that someone might rely on it.

E.G.745 inspection, judicial remedies, the Fiscal Superior communication, CGPJ/TSJC functioning records, and compensation/recovery routes remain separate. A weak or missing personal attribution requires an identified source task; the publication does not assert that a specific inspection proceeding was legally mandatory.
