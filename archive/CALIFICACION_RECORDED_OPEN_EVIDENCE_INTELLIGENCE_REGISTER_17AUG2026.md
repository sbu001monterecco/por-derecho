## 1. Purpose

This register captures the **intelligence behind the phrase “open evidence.”** It is not merely a list of missing documents.

For each open item it records:

- what is already verified;
- the precise proposition still unproved;
- why the item matters;
- which public or institutional claim it could strengthen, narrow or defeat;
- the possibility that retrieval may produce adverse or neutral evidence;
- the actor-specific transmission or knowledge issue;
- the best retrieval route;
- custody, privilege and publication controls;
- and the wording that must remain qualified until the source is obtained.

It supplements, and does not replace:

- `archive/MISSING_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md`;
- `archive/MISSING_EVIDENCE_REGISTER_CALIFICACION_REPEAT_SCAN_APPEND_17AUG2026.md`;
- `archive/CALIFICACION_GMAIL_DRIVE_ACTOR_KNOWLEDGE_SOURCE_ADDENDUM_17AUG2026.md`;

- `archive/CALIFICACION_PRIMARY_SOURCE_REPEAT_SCAN_PROMPT_17AUG2026.md`;
- and the current correction, storyline and maintenance controls.

A missing item is not proof that it exists, that it is favourable, that it was withheld, or that wrongdoing occurred. Equally, an unresolved item must not disappear into a generic “more evidence needed” label where the present record already identifies a concrete source, actor, period and proposition.

## 2. Open-evidence status categories

- **LOCATED — CUSTODY INCOMPLETE:** source content is located, but native export, headers, certified copy, hash or chain-of-custody remains incomplete.
- **PARTIAL PROCEDURAL RECORD:** a procedural act is verified, but the complete docket, service record, recording, annex set or outcome is missing.
- **ACTOR-KNOWLEDGE GAP:** the underlying document exists, but the route by which it reached a named actor is not proved.
- **SOURCE LEAD — NOT YET VERIFIED:** a report, statement or transaction is reported by another source but its primary record is not recovered.
- **ECONOMIC RECONCILIATION GAP:** some figures or costs exist, but a complete revenue/cost/cash/control reconstruction is absent.
- **CONTROL/CAPACITY GAP:** title, procedural power, material control, access and practical ability have not yet been allocated year by year.

## Control conclusion

**“Open evidence” is now a structured intelligence programme, not a disclaimer.** Each item has a current evidential floor, a precise unanswered proposition, a competing-outcome analysis, an actor-knowledge consequence, a retrieval path and a publication threshold. Until a source is recovered, the underlying allegation must remain at the strongest level that the present verified record supports—neither diluted nor upgraded beyond proof.
