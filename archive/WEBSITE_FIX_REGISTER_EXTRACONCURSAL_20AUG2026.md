## Controlling clarification to surface

The website should make the following distinction visible and repeated in the main reader journey:

> LPB is the concursal lane. Matkator and Aweswell create separate extraconcursal property, tax, income, financing, platform and recovery-rights lanes that cannot be absorbed or answered merely by invoking LPB's concurso.

The site already contains important fragments of this position, but the 31 July 2026 communication makes it more direct, current and preservation-oriented.

## Text to fix or avoid

Avoid formulations that imply:

- the LPB concurso automatically disposes of Matkator or third-party units;
- the whole Sun Park hotel was the bankruptcy estate;
- the Owners' Community was a neutral, validly functioning organ unless the text says `purported`, `disputed`, `instrumentalised` or equivalent;
- public funds or EU funds were definitively irregular;
- institutional silence equals guilt;
- all downstream actors necessarily knew every upstream defect.

Preferred formulations:

- `presunta o alegada cadena de desplazamiento patrimonial extraconcursal`;
- `mecanismo privado extraconcursal de ocupación, transformación, explotación y monetización`;
- `investigación material, finita y patrimonialmente diferenciada`;
- `finca-habitación-ingreso-beneficiario`;
- `título, autoridad, acceso, obra, operación, ingresos y beneficiario`;
- `confirmar o descartar mediante fuentes primarias`.
