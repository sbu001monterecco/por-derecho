## Qualification rule

A statement such as `no recuerdo`, `creo`, `posiblemente`, `según me dijeron` or an expert opinion must be preserved with its qualifier. A qualified recollection is not equivalent to a categorical factual assertion.

## Judicial/AC discipline

An evidential inconsistency may justify a question or criticism. It does not by itself establish knowing falsehood, concealment, bias, prevaricación, collusion or intentional omission. Those require the separate actor-specific knowledge/duty/act/causation tests in the canonical repository controls.
