## Phase 3 — judge actor-specific culpability matrix

For every material judicial act or omission record:

- date;
- information actually before the judge;
- proof of knowledge;
- procedural/legal duty;
- decision or omission;
- reasons given;
- evidence accepted/rejected;
- hearing/contradiction afforded;
- third-party or extraconcursal rights implicated;
- immediate and downstream economic consequence;
- consistency with other rulings;
- strongest lawful explanation;
- evidence against that explanation.

Use this graduated classification only:

0 ordinary judicial disagreement; 1 arguable error; 2 significant procedural irregularity; 3 repeated objectively unexplained safeguard departure; 4 known material contradiction followed by unexplained disregard; 5 evidence potentially supporting arbitrary/knowingly unjust decision-making; 6 evidence potentially relevant to criminal culpability.

Do not move levels without identifying the evidence of knowledge/intent still required.
