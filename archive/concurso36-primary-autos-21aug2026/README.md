## Purpose and controlling rule

This directory preserves the analytical outputs of the 21 August 2026 critical-autos scan and its later reverse-engineering redigest. It makes the conclusions, corrections, evidential vocabulary, source map and closure tasks durable in the public repository.

These files are **analysis and indexes, not the primary evidence itself**. A report, spreadsheet row, Gmail/Drive locator, quotation or document description does not replace the signed judicial act, native notarial deed, certified Registry record, bank record, official court docket or other controlled source binary.

The authority order is:

1. the complete, authentic primary document within its proper legal and factual scope;
2. a verified derivative or official copy;
3. the reverse-engineering redigest in this directory;
4. the first-scan report and evidence index;
5. supporting workpapers, repository summaries and website prose.

Where the redigest expressly corrects or qualifies the first scan, the redigest controls this analytical package. A later-recovered primary original may supersede both.

## Evidential use

Retain the proposition labels used by the reports: `PROVEN BY PRIMARY DOCUMENT`, `STRONGLY SUPPORTED`, `INFERENCE`, `UNRESOLVED`, `CONTRADICTED` and `DOCUMENT NOT YET LOCATED`.

An absence means only that the item was not identified in the reviewed corpus. It does not prove non-existence in the certified court, notarial, Registry, bank or administrative file. In particular, use **“No primary evidence of payment has yet been identified”** unless a primary record proves payment or non-payment.

Names identify documentary, professional, corporate or procedural roles. Gaps, errors, adverse rulings, role overlaps and later benefits do not by themselves prove intent, collusion, criminality, professional misconduct, procedural illegality or invalidity.
