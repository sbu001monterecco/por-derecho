# A. Executive forensic finding

## 1. The judicial perimeter was LPB's estate, not “Sun Park” as an undivided hotel

**PROVEN BY PRIMARY DOCUMENT.** The 6 June 2012 declaration concerns LPB. The 19 December 2017 liquidation auto suspended LPB over **“su patrimonio”**. The 16 April 2018 approval stated: **“Se excluyen de la liquidación los bienes de propiedad ajena.”** Its 159-finca list omits Matkator samples 8584 and 8588, whose 2018 Registry notes show Matkator as 100% owner. No examined auto supplies an extraconcursal transfer mechanism. **NO PRIMARY JUDICIAL AUTHORISATION YET IDENTIFIED.** [S01–S03, S18–S19]

## 2. The 26 January 2022 “adjudication” description is wrong on the evidence currently held

**PROVEN BY PRIMARY DOCUMENT.** Definitive approval to CAM is the auto of **18 May 2021**. The two 26 January 2022 autos only clarify separate 15 October rulings; one says the event was a **“comparecencia de licitación por presentación de mejor oferta y no una subasta.”** Neither January fallo adjudicates assets, fixes consideration or decides surplus. The accurate sequence is: approval 18 May; challenges dismissed 15 October; clarification 26 January; notarial implementation 21 February. [S12–S17]

## 3. The 2021 approval was asset-specific and transaction-specific; it was not an order delivering the whole hotel, the business, or possession

**PROVEN BY PRIMARY DOCUMENT.** The 18 May auto approved (i) dación of the enumerated 159 mortgaged LPB apartment fincas, (ii) direct sale of fincas 8508–8536, 8653 and 8654 for €400,000, and (iii) specified costs/taxes/Community conditions. It does not expressly transfer third-party units, CEXP/business rights, licences, goodwill, furniture, keys, pre-title income or whole-complex possession. Annex II required future deeds, excluded business/labour succession and granted no present exploitation. It also duplicates finca 8579 and omits 8519; later judicial/notarial lists use the corrected range. [S03, S12, S42]

## 4. No examined order authorises the June 2018 practical-control transition or whole-hotel exploitation before title

The 26 June 2018 auto only stayed realisation of listed apartments. On 24 February 2021 the AC said it had not delivered LPB possession; CAM said it owned 54 units and relied on Community decisions. The court recorded fragmented title and factual uncertainty, then refused relief without granting CAM authority. Protocol 457 states traditio occurred on 21 February 2022. For the alleged 7 June 2018 control change and pre-title works, operation or income: **NO PRIMARY JUDICIAL AUTHORISATION YET IDENTIFIED.** Private/community/administrative authority remains **UNRESOLVED**. [S05, S09, S17]

## 5. The 2018 locales deed was not judicially convalidated; its later implementation remains incompletely reconciled

Protocol 2150 was executed on 28 November 2018; the LAJ direction to formalise came fourteen days later. The AC's 20 December report says the €400,000 cheque became effective and identifies initial fee-payment steps. That proves the AC's account, not an independent bank ledger. On 24 October 2019 the judge found the deed occurred **“sin autorización de este Juzgado”** and ordered **“No haber lugar a convalidar.”** The 18 May 2021 auto later approved the €400,000 branch, but Protocol 457 omits those 31 fincas. Reversal, legal cure/fresh conveyance, final cash application and Registry outcome remain **UNRESOLVED**. [S07, S12, S32–S35]

## 6. Debt and value figures have different legal functions

The record uses non-interchangeable figures: €9,052,251.69 recognised secured credit; €12,251,446.21 reported burden; €13,168,082.02 better-offer mortgage threshold/deed consideration; €9,776,003.13 whole-complex appraisal including foreign fincas; €7,096,913.83 LPB real estate; €6,607,551.87 plan/AP 159-unit appraisal; and Annex II's inconsistent €7,065,384.42. The Community €1,145,798.29 was a bidder condition, expressly not a judicially allowed claim. The AP also recites €9,052,877.88—€626.19 above the 8 February operative components. [S06, S10, S26, S29, S42]

The 8 February auto changed creditor identity at the then-recognised amount. A separate 16 April auto permitted interest growth within mortgage maxima. Later rulings explain the route to the €3,182,000 first-loan cap; that exact cap step makes the €13,168,082.02 arithmetic work. What remains **UNRESOLVED** is whether every component had accrued, was correctly classified and was compensable at closing. Assignment economics, accrual certificate, payoff and AC closing ledger are missing. [S08, S17, S26–S30]

## 7. A surplus cannot yet be calculated reliably, and payment cannot be presumed

Plan rules reserve any excess to the estate/creditors, but no asset-matched closing value, final allowed debt, transfer statement and estate ledger are held. **No primary evidence of payment has yet been identified.** This does not prove non-payment. Missing are a sobrante calculation/deposit, final accounts and conclusion order. Finca 8751's 6 May 2022 note shows CAM title but still displays the mortgage under `CARGAS`; later cancellation certification is absent. [S03, S23]

## 8. Creditor succession is evidenced; its economics and final payoff are not

The 8 February auto recognises CAM instead of PH122 for €9,052,251.69 plus unquantified contingent costs and says assignment changes holder, not the definitive-text quantum. Finca 8751's Registry chain supports Bankia→SAREB→PH122→CAM for that sample. But Protocol 2248 schedules, assignment price/payment, AC verification report and final payoff are missing. Entity-suffix variations are presently clerical/nomenclature issues, not evidence of intent. [S23, S26]

## 9. Subsequent corporate shorthand does not repair a missing property-by-property bridge

**PROVEN BY PRIMARY DOCUMENT.** The November 2022 BORME segregation notice says CAM transferred, in block and by universal succession, a unit described as the Sun Park complex/economic unit to HNT for transformation and hotel exploitation. That proves the announced corporate restructuring. It does not prove that CAM had acquired every third-party finca or every private right described by that shorthand. The HNT chain must still be tested against each CAM title and each non-CAM asset. [S21]

## 10. Overall answer to the central question

**No — not yet.** A strong chain can be constructed for: declaration of LPB's insolvency; suspension of LPB's powers; approval of a specific 159-finca/31-finca proposal; the better-offer process; definitive approval to CAM; and notarial implementation of the mortgaged-finca branch. The chain breaks at least at:

1. the legal authority for the June 2018 practical-control transition;
2. the authority and accounts for pre-title exploitation, works and income;
3. any bridge from Matkator/other third-party title into the insolvency transaction;
4. Annex II's duplicated/omitted finca and inconsistent 159-unit valuation → corrected plan/order scope;
5. the 2018 locales deed → 2019 non-convalidation → 2021 approval → final payment/accounting/registry sequence;
6. the final judicially allowed/compensable debt at closing;
7. any asset-matched sobrante calculation and payment;
8. terminal disposition of the 2022 review chain, proof of the deed's five-day court communication, subsequent charge-cancellation order/mandamiento and registry implementation;
9. the executed/registered CAM→HNT asset/passive schedule and property-level continuity; and
10. the final accounts/conclusion of the insolvency.

# Method and limitations

The scan covered the Por Derecho repository at clean commit `a50e034b13ae9942ac18f277bdb8f2274e3b60dd`, its evidence indexes, connected Google Drive, connected Gmail, uploaded/recovered documents, document digests and official public corporate material. The repository audit was read-only. Primary judicial or registry text controls over later summaries. A document's presence in a party filing proves that it was filed only where a receipt/diligencia establishes that; it does not prove the truth of the pleading. A later deed, registry entry or professional assertion is evidence of that later act or representation, not automatic proof that an earlier judicial condition was satisfied.

Completeness constraints remain material. The full court expediente was not available as a certified docket export. Some items are present only as party copies, quotations, filenames or incomplete scans. Negative findings are therefore phrased as “not identified in the evidence examined”, never as proof that no such document exists in the court file.

## Record 1 — declaration of concurso, 6 June 2012

**A. Identification.** Auto, Juzgado de lo Mercantil nº 1 de Las Palmas de Gran Canaria, Concurso Ordinario 36/2012, NIG 3501647120120000351; Judge Alberto López Villarrubia; 9 pages; complete scanned copy, S01. No schedules are appended to the copy. The act was dated 6 June, electronically/physically notified in the following days.

**B. Parties and application.** LPB, through procurador Tomás Ramírez Hernández, applied on 4 June for voluntary insolvency. The order records no opposing party because it was the debtor's voluntary application. The application asserted LPB's centre of main interests at its Yaiza registered office and present insolvency. Creditors and third-party Sun Park owners were not adjudicated against by this opening act.

**C. Facts accepted.** The court accepted territorial competence, plurality of creditors and present insolvency from the Article 6 LC materials. It recorded the application figures: assets €21,603,513.82 and liabilities €10,186,394.72. Those opening estimates were not a later asset-by-asset valuation or a finding that every physical Sun Park unit belonged to LPB.

**D. Legal reasoning.** Articles 3, 6, 10.1, 14, 22, 40.1, 55–56, 85 and related provisions of the then Ley Concursal. Because the concurso was voluntary, the default rule was intervention rather than suspension of LPB's powers. The court saw no then-present reason to depart from that rule.

**E. Operative part.** Spanish:

> “Se declara en concurso, que tiene carácter de voluntario, al deudor […] LUCHY PLAYA BLANCA S.L.U.”

> “El deudor […] conservará las facultades de administración y disposición sobre su patrimonio, quedando sometido el ejercicio de éstas a la intervención de los administradores concursales, mediante su autorización o conformidad.”

> “Se nombra administrador del concurso […] a […] FRANCISCO DE BORJA RODRÍGUEZ-BATLLORI LAFFITTE, abogado.”

English:

> “The debtor LPB is declared in voluntary insolvency.”

> “LPB retains its powers of administration and disposition over its assets, but their exercise is subject to the insolvency administration's authorisation or agreement.”

> “Francisco de Borja Rodríguez-Batllori Laffitte, lawyer, is appointed insolvency administrator.”

The order also required an updated creditor list and inventory as at the declaration date, authorised the AC to access LPB's establishment/books, and required the AC to state whether assets in Mortgage Enforcement 90/2012 were affected to business activity. It directed registry/publicity steps and listed certain fincas for notice. That list is not framed as a final or exhaustive ownership adjudication.

**Conditions, limits and reserved matters.** The declaration took immediate effect, but registry/publicity, acceptance of the AC appointment, credit communications and the updated inventory remained to occur. Ownership of each hotel finca, CEXP rights and third-party property were not decided.

**WHAT HAD TO HAPPEN BEFORE THIS ORDER COULD LAWFULLY PRODUCE THE EFFECT LATER ATTRIBUTED TO IT?** Nothing further was needed for LPB to be in concurso. But the order could not lawfully become authority over a non-LPB asset without later proof that the asset was in LPB's estate or a separate legally sufficient third-party transfer/consent/order.

**Procedural-rights note.** This act affected LPB directly. It did not purport to deprive Matkator or another owner of title, so absence of those parties here cannot be converted into approval of later interference with their assets.

**AC-information note.** At opening, the court received LPB's Article 6 materials, not yet the later definitive inventory. Any reliance on the €21.603m opening asset figure as a 2018/2022 judicial sale value is **CONTRADICTED** by the document's function.

## Record 3A — CAM creditor-holder recognition, 8 February 2018

**A. Identification.** Auto, Juzgado de lo Mercantil nº 1 de Las Palmas, Concurso ordinario 36/2012, Section 1; Judge Alberto López Villarrubia; 3 pages; complete authentic copy, S26. It does not itself prove the assignment price, payment to PH122, every schedule, the later amount of interest actually accrued, the final 2022 payoff or title to the mortgaged property. The separate 16 April clarification [S30] supplied an express cap rule for later interest; it did not retroactively turn every cap amount into an accrued sum. The reliance on AC verification is a stated judicial reasoning premise, not independent production here of the complete deed.

**WHAT HAD TO HAPPEN?** The auto had to become final or survive appeal; the assignment had to remain valid; later interest/costs had to accrue, remain within the mortgage maxima, be classified and calculated lawfully; and any dación could extinguish only the final compensable secured claim under the later plan/orders. The complete Protocol 2248 and appeal/finality record remain P0/P1.

# Overall chain finding

The evidence establishes a substantial but discontinuous legal chain:

> LPB declaration → AC-controlled liquidation of LPB's patrimony → CAM offer/AC plan for enumerated LPB assets → appeal-limited debt thresholds → judicial refusal to convalidate the prematurely consummated locales branch → republicised better-offer process → definitive CAM approval on 18 May 2021 → confirmation/clarification and court-office finality treatment → Protocol 457 dación of scheduled mortgaged LPB fincas → later CAM/HNT segregation notice.

It does **not** yet establish an unbroken chain for the physical complex as a whole. The principal breaks are ownership of third-party fincas; the authority and economics of 2018–2021 possession/exploitation; Annex II's finca/value defects; locales performance after non-convalidation; the final compensable debt and its accounting treatment, including €626.19 and €30 source inconsistencies; the sobrante; terminal 2022 review/finality; post-deed court/Registry implementation; and execution/perimeter of the CAM→HNT segregation.

Those breaks are capable of proof or disproof. The required repairs are concrete documents: the certified court event log and originals, inventory/plan schedules, creditor assignment/payoff file, closing and estate bank ledgers, court-deposit evidence, Registry histories, operator/Community contracts, licence files, and the HNT segregation project. Until obtained, the evidentially correct conclusion is **UNRESOLVED**, not accusation.
