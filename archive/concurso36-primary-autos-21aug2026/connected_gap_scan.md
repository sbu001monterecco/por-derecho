## Evidential classification used

- **PRIMARY — JUDICIAL:** signed/sealed court, LAJ or appellate document.
- **PRIMARY — AC FILING:** document authored/filed by the administrador concursal; it proves the AC's report, not necessarily every fact asserted in it.
- **PRIMARY — NOTARIAL / REGISTRY:** authentic instrument or registry certification; its proof is limited to its contents and statutory effect.
- **PARTY FILING / ASSERTION:** application, certificate or representation by a party; not independent verification.
- **SECONDARY / ANALYTICAL:** compilation, spreadsheet, correspondence or later analysis.
- **DOCUMENT NOT YET LOCATED:** exact connected searches did not return the primary document. This is documentary absence in the searched sources, not proof that the document never existed.

## 8. Bottom-line connected-source conclusion

The connected search materially improves the documentary spine: it supplies the original and AC inventories, ACTÚA valuation materials, the 2018 balance acts, the 2020 CAM certificate, the post-deed April 2022 AC report, and a detailed finality/review chain through January/March 2023. It also sharpens the principal break:

> The located inventory documents identify LPB's assets; ACTÚA later values a wider 262-finca functional complex; the 2022 AC report asserts transfer under Protocol 457. The connected corpus still contains no primary instrument that bridges third-party title into the estate, no primary proof that the €400,000 or any sobrante was paid/consigned, and no complete post-transfer Registry/accounting pack.

Accordingly, the legal/evidential chain cannot yet be described as unbroken **asset by asset, condition by condition and euro by euro**. The first documents capable of repairing it are the certified definitive court inventory, complete Protocols 2248/2150/457 with annexes and receipts, court-account/AC bank evidence, and finca-specific Registry files.
