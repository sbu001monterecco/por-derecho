### Conditions taxonomy correction

The first scan sometimes grouped approval, finality, reporting, registration and cancellation as if all were identical conditions precedent to title. That is too broad. The defensible classification is:

- **express judicial/offer terms:** asset schedule, consideration mechanism, specified costs/taxes/Community obligations and future formalisation;
- **procedural/finality gates:** approval and then-current enforceability/testimony, now substantially evidenced through later dismissals;
- **performance/reporting duties:** execution, delivery of copies and any five-day court reporting commitment;
- **Registry/cancellation steps:** implementation, priority/opposability and charge status; their precise legal consequence requires property-law analysis and must not automatically be labelled a condition precedent to civil title;
- **accounting/surplus duties:** triggered only if the operative legal formula and matched closing data establish an amount due.
