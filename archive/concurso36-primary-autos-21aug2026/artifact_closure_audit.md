## 1. Executive result

The prior report makes several important corrections that survive this artifact-level challenge:

1. **PROVEN BY PRIMARY DOCUMENT:** the order declaring the concurso concerns LPB; the liquidation order reaches LPB's patrimony; and the plan-approval order expressly excludes property owned by others.
2. **PROVEN BY PRIMARY DOCUMENT:** the definitive judicial approval identified in the controlled record is dated 18 May 2021. The two 26 January 2022 autos are clarification/completion acts, not the original approval or a new whole-hotel adjudication.
3. **PROVEN BY PRIMARY DOCUMENT:** the 26 June 2018 auto suspended realisation of specified mortgaged fincas. It was not an operative grant of whole-complex possession, works authority or hotel-operation authority.
4. **PROVEN BY PRIMARY DOCUMENT:** the judge refused to convalidate Protocol 2150 on 24 October 2019, and the 12 May 2020 ruling confirmed that refusal. This does not by itself establish civil nullity, restitution or the ultimate Registry position.
5. **PROVEN BY PRIMARY DOCUMENT:** the 21 February 2022 deed identifies a 159-finca dación and states consideration of €13,168,082.02. It does not on its own establish title to omitted third-party fincas, the final insolvency-allowed payoff, charge cancellation, a surplus payment, or the separate locales branch.
6. **UNRESOLVED:** no located primary judicial order has yet been identified as authority for a 7 June 2018 whole-complex control transfer or pre-deed whole-hotel exploitation. This is a qualified search result, not proof that every CAM presence or act lacked a private, Community, co-owner or administrative basis.
7. **UNRESOLVED:** no primary payment record for an asserted sobrante appears in the audited artifacts. That does not prove either that a surplus legally arose or that it was not paid.

The report nevertheless does **not yet accomplish the central reconstruction asset by asset and euro by euro**. It is strongest as a judicial-document audit. It does not yet prove the factual control sequence, full property universe, complete debt/accrual bridge, economic benefit, finality, registration, CAM→HNT execution, current operator position or final insolvency accounting.

The most important new audit finding is methodological: the report often correctly distinguishes what a document says from whether the underlying fact is true, but the evidence index does not preserve that distinction in machine-readable form. A document, a recital within it, the truth of the recital, filing of the document, and the legal effect of the document need separate fields. Until that is corrected, a row labelled `PROVEN BY PRIMARY DOCUMENT` can be misread as proving more than the report intended.

## 2. Artifact integrity and reproducibility

### 2.1 What is preserved correctly

- The report and CSV hashes in the manifest match the current files.
- The CSV contains 72 sequential records and parses to 17 fields per record.
- The bundle contains the report, CSV and manifest.
- The report identifies a stable repository commit.
- The report warns that absence from the examined corpus is not absence from the certified court file.

### 2.2 Preservation defects that remain

1. The bundle contains **only the three output files**, not the three workpapers or controlled source binaries. It preserves conclusions, not a self-contained evidential corpus.
2. Of the source-key entries, only S01 displays a complete SHA-256. Several entries show only hash prefixes and most show no hash. S30 expressly records unresolved historic hash conflict.
3. Composite source IDs prevent one-document/one-hash control. S16 combines two autos, two applications and a LAJ act; S24 combines an AC report and a LAJ act; S35 is a 282-page mixed package; S36 combines several separate acts.
4. Page/folio anchors are generally absent. Citations such as `[S17]` point to a 76-page deed and `[S35]` to a 282-page mixed bundle without stable page coordinates.
5. The native third-party bidder Google document is mutable and has no frozen export, revision identifier, export timestamp or hash.
6. Gmail/Drive IDs preserve retrievability only while account access and remote objects remain available. They are not substitutes for evidence exports with hashes and metadata.
7. `complete_copy=yes` in the CSV means different things. Thirty such rows also say that an annex, support pack, service record or later implementation record is absent. A complete core document and a complete filed transaction package are different states.
8. Sixteen CSV rows use compound evidence labels. Some combine `PROVEN BY PRIMARY DOCUMENT` with `UNRESOLVED` or `DOCUMENT NOT YET LOCATED`. The document may be proven while another proposition is unresolved, but the current schema does not identify which proposition receives which label.
9. The `retrieval_priority` field mixes custody status and priority in free text (`controlled; ... P0`, `P1 report`, and similar values). It cannot be sorted reliably as a P0/P1/P2 register.
10. Apparent electronic signatures and CSV codes are described as “authentic”, but no signature-validation or official-CSV verification log accompanies the artifacts.

**Required control:** generate one immutable evidence-manifest row per binary or native revision with source ID, document ID, exact filename, source system, retrieval time, byte size, full SHA-256, MIME type, page count, signature/CSV validation result, source relationship, filed-copy status, and page-level quotation anchors.
