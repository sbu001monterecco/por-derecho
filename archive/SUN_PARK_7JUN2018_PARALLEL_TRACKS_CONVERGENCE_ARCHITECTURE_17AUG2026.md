## 2. Why 7 June deserves hub status

The significance of the date comes from the intersection of at least four different temporal thresholds:

1. **material-control threshold** — the source-supported change in access/security/practical operation on 7 June 2018;
2. **continued court-protection threshold** — LPB and its estate remained within Concurso 36/2012 while the whole mixed-ownership hotel did not become one insolvency estate;
3. **project / commercialisation-before-title threshold** — later hotel/project identities and investor propositions appear before the 2022 formal-title threshold for the LPB perimeter;
4. **formal title / finance / operation threshold** — adjudication, deed, later corporate/project identity, finance/support and MYND operation occur later and must not retrospectively determine the legality or authority of earlier control.

This creates the core reader-facing sequence:

`operating hotel + fragmented rights`  
→ `pre-7-Jun access/project/security preparation`  
→ **`7-Jun material-control rupture`**  
→ `customers / staff / operation / access consequences`  
→ `Lava Verde / Club Sei / Meeting Point commercial life`  
→ `RICPE Sun Park investor life`  
→ `2022 title formalisation`  
→ `HNT / MYND / RIC / regional incentive / FEDER-linked life`  
→ `current operation, value and recovery claims`

Chronology and physical continuity justify investigation and reconciliation. They do **not** by themselves prove common intent, unlawful funding, criminal responsibility or the legal invalidity of every later act.

## 4. The “crushing tracks” idea — translate it into disciplined public language

The user's intuitive description — **parallel and interfacing / “crushing” tracks** — is valuable as an internal model, but the public site should express it with more forensic precision.

Preferred concept:

> **Multiple tracks converged on the same physical and economic hotel while remaining legally distinct. The recovery case asks whether actions in one track constrained, displaced or pre-empted rights and options in another.**

Examples to test, not assume:

- security/access decisions constraining hotel operation and rescue diligence;
- practical control affecting customers, records, staff, maintenance and income;
- external project preparation affecting estate value or recovery options;
- Community mechanisms affecting rights inside and outside the concurso;
- later investor/project representations assuming availability that must be reconciled with title and pending court protection;
- finance/support layers relying on overlapping property, works, value, costs or employment;
- adverse Calificación allegations being assessed without fully testing who retained post-7-Jun practical capacity.

For every claimed cross-track effect record:

`SOURCE EVENT → LEGAL/ECONOMIC BRIDGE → AFFECTED RIGHT/OPTION → CONSEQUENCE → EVIDENCE STATUS → OPEN COUNTERFACTUAL`

## 9. Recovery / damages significance

The convergence architecture also improves the damages story because it prevents “the hotel” from being treated as one undifferentiated loss.

After 7 June, separately trace alleged loss or displacement at:

1. **LPB PropCo / estate level** — property, value, rents/income, financing and recovery opportunity;
2. **hotel OpCo / operating level** — room revenue, contracts, staff, bookings, operating margin, business interruption;
3. **customer/community level** — repeat customer base, longer-stay proposition, direct relationships and goodwill;
4. **Matkator / third-party extraconcursal level** — possession/use/fruits/works/value/property-specific remedies;
5. **Aweswell / top-platform level** — group investment, cross-border capital, platform value, development pipeline and recovery costs;
6. **future-restoration level** — cost and value of reconstituting Sun Park/Project Sun Rock and resuming lawful operation if possession/control is recovered.

Do not automatically aggregate these heads. Avoid double recovery and prove claimant, right, causation, period and quantum separately.
