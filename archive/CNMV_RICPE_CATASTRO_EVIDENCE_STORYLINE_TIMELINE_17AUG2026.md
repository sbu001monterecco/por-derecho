This eight-document tranche materially sharpens three connected parts of the Sun Park/MYND record:

1. **CNMV notice and supervisory-corpus chronology.** The 13-Jan-2021 complaint proves an express warning to CNMV about title/ownership and investor-information concerns. 2. **Same-project finance/support continuity.** The 16-Nov-2022 BORME segregation, the 20-Sep-2023 RIC Private Equity prospectus and the 26-May-2023 BOE regional-incentive award provide a tighter primary-source chain from Sun Park/CAM to HNT/MYND and identify distinct private/RIC and public-incentive financial channels concerning the same operating hotel project.
3. **Catastro/Matkator control.** The signed 4-Mar-2026 Catastro response says no type-902 cadastral alteration file is recorded for parcel 5137001FS1953N or its constituent properties and identifies three Matkator cadastral references. This is a finite administrative fact; it is not proof that no cadastral, registry, planning or title alteration of any other kind occurred.

The combined effect is to strengthen the case for a recipient-by-recipient disclosure, title/perimeter, cost, invoice, employment and source-and-use reconciliation. It does **not** convert the Project Sun Rock double/triple-funding allegation into a finding of duplicate payment, investor fraud, unlawful aid, unlawful property alteration or intentional CNMV failure.

## 2. Evidential classification rule

Use only the following statuses when reusing this ingest:

- **VERIFIED FACT** — directly established by a signed/official/registered source or the face of the identified RPE document.
- **PROJECT/PARTY ALLEGATION** — asserted by Gil Marer / Project Sun Rock in a complaint, open letter or analytical submission, without treating the assertion as independently proved merely because it was filed.
- **EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE** — a conclusion reasonably suggested by two or more controlled facts but not itself stated by a competent primary source.
- **UNRESOLVED QUESTION** — requires additional transaction, title, audit, institutional or actor-specific evidence.
- **CORRECTED/SUPERSEDED** — prior shorthand that this tranche narrows or corrects.

A registered complaint proves notice and the content of the allegation, not the truth of the allegation. A regulator's receipt or supervisory response proves the institutional act stated, not acceptance of the complainant's theory.

## 5. Canonical Sun Park → HNT → MYND / finance storyline

### 5.1 Corporate/economic continuity is primary-source supported

The BORME notice published 16-Nov-2022 records that CAM and HNT approved on 30-Oct-2022 the segregation, in block and by universal succession, of the Sun Park 1-star tourist complex as an independent economic unit to HNT, for transformation into a 4-star hotel and later operation as **MYND Yaiza**.

**VERIFIED FACT:** this is the corporate transaction/narrative published in BORME.

It does **not** by itself establish that CAM held valid title to every finca/right physically or economically integrated into the hotel. The exact transferred perimeter remains a Registry/title question.

### 5.2 RPE's September-2023 prospectus expressly links MYND Yaiza back to Sun Park

The 20-Sep-2023 RPE prospectus identifies:

- `Mynd Yaiza`;
- `Hotel New Trend, S.L.` as the project company/titleholder column entry;
- **€1,598,849.78** for financing the refurbishment works;
- **€4,971,863.78** for financing employment creation;
- a footnote stating that MYND Yaiza was originally called Sun Park and that RPE financed **two different components of the project**.

The arithmetic sum of the two prospectus amounts is **€6,570,713.56**.

### 5.3 Amount-control correction

Earlier repository shorthand called **€6,573,703.10** a controlled RICPE/HNT total. This ingest proves that the **20-Sep-2023 prospectus-specific total** of the two stated MYND Yaiza components is **€6,570,713.56**.

Do not assume one number automatically falsifies the other. Until the audited-account source, Series F/G agreements, approvals, drawdowns and accounting treatment are reconciled, use:

- **€6,570,713.56 — VERIFIED prospectus-specific sum**;
- **€6,573,703.10 — earlier audited-account/repository reconstruction requiring source-to-source reconciliation**.

The difference is **€2,989.54** and must be explained rather than silently normalised.

### 5.4 Regional-incentive channel is separately verified

The BOE regional-incentives order published 26-May-2023 identifies for **Hotel New Trend, S.L., Yaiza**:

- investment: **€11,469,714.00**;
- subsidy: **€3,440,914.20**;
- employment: **60**.

This establishes a public-incentive channel concerning HNT/Yaiza. It does not establish that the subsidy was fully paid, which costs were finally eligible, which jobs were certified, whether expenditure overlapped RPE/RIC finance, or whether any cumulation rule was breached.

### 5.5 Correct multiple-financial-lives proposition after this ingest

The strongest evidence-safe formulation is:

> **VERIFIED:** the same Sun Park→MYND Yaiza project is traceably present in a CAM→HNT corporate segregation, RPE's two-component financing record and a separate regional-incentive record. **EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE:** those channels create a material need to reconcile project perimeter, works, eligible costs, employment and disclosure across instruments. **PROJECT ALLEGATION:** Project Sun Rock alleges double/triple funding and repeated financial lives of the same hotel. **UNRESOLVED:** invoice-level duplicate funding, unlawful cumulation, false certification, improper payment and criminal intent have not yet been established by the present source set.

This formulation is stronger than treating the channels as unrelated, and more accurate than calling duplicate funding proved.

## 6. Catastro / Matkator storyline control

### 6.1 What the 4-Mar-2026 Catastro response proves

The Gerencia Regional del Catastro de Canarias states:

1. no **type-902 cadastral alteration proceeding** is recorded for parcel `5137001FS1953N` or its constituent properties;
2. for Matkator, from its registration as cadastral holder, the identified properties are:
   - `5137001FS1953N0088JY`;
   - `5137001FS1953N0091JY`;
   - `5137001FS1953N0092KU`;
3. the communication is informative and not an appealable administrative act.

### 6.2 What it does not prove

Do not say that the Catastro response proves:

- no physical reform occurred;
- no cadastral change of any kind occurred;
- no Registry modification occurred;
- Matkator's Land Registry title is conclusively determined by Catastro;
- the transformation into MYND Yaiza was unlawful;
- every current room can already be mapped legally to an original finca.

Those propositions require separate planning, cadastral, Registry, deed, project, licence and expert evidence.

### 6.3 Why it matters to the same-asset audit

The Catastro response is nevertheless material because it preserves a concrete 2026 administrative snapshot in which three Matkator cadastral units remain identifiable while the hotel is operated as an integrated MYND Yaiza product. Combined with the BORME and RPE project narrative, this supports an **asset-perimeter reconciliation question**: which exact original finca/right maps to which current room/space, work package, valuation, financing/support inclusion and revenue stream?

CRI-07's 16-Mar-2026 communication asks Catastro to investigate that mismatch. The request itself proves notice/request, not the truth of the asserted mismatch.
