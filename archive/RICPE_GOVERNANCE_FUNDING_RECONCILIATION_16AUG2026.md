## New finite questions to preserve

### 1. Who introduced the 2020 project and how was related-party governance handled?

Recover the original proposal/introductory paper behind the investor description of a participating loan of up to €2m to an important Canary business group for refurbishment of Lanzarote apartments, within an €11m investment and then-estimated June-2020 completion/operation date. Determine what part referred to Hotel Sun Park and identify proposal origin, sponsor, related-party declaration, conflict analysis, circulation, abstention, approval and Control/Compliance treatment.

### 2. What valuation supported the investment proposition?

Recover every valuation, appraisal, investment-committee valuation or value assumption supplied to or used by RICPE for the Sun Park project. For each identify:

`asset/perimeter → owner assumed → valuer → date → method → value → encumbrances/title assumptions → purpose → approving body → financing/LTV use`.

Reconcile those values with the approximately **€9.77m January-2018 non-ECO valuation** already tracked by the project and with any value CAM supplied to Concurso 36/2012. The economic question is whether an approximately €9.77m asset value could support a circa €12m capital/funding proposition and, if so, through what capital, security, tranching, subordination, sponsor support or future-value assumptions. This is an **open reconciliation question**, not proof of impropriety. Do not impose generic bank LTV practice on RICPE as a legal rule; use financing ratios only as a benchmark requiring the actual RICPE underwriting assumptions, additional security, capital structure and valuation basis.

### 3. Build a source/use-of-funds map rather than summing finance layers

The project requires one reconciled ledger:

`date → project/works → legal beneficiary → asset/right → financer/public body → instrument → approved amount → paid/drawn amount → eligible cost → security → valuation → employment condition → approval/control → disclosure to Concurso/court → overlap/exclusion with other finance`.

At minimum test separately:

- approximately **€4.5m Community refurbishment financing/approval in November 2018** — source, contractual borrower/obligor, works, voting/authority, property perimeter, knowledge of LPB's concurso and whether/how the AC/court was informed;
- RICPE Sun Park/HNT/MYND financing, including the controlled €6,573,703.10 total and unreconciled tranche components;

The question is whether layers financed distinct eligible costs or whether any same cost, works, asset basis or employment commitment was counted twice. **Multiple finance layers do not themselves establish double funding, subsidy fraud or misuse.**

## Publication discipline

Use four statuses throughout:

1. **DOCUMENTED / VERIFIED** — supported by controlled primary/official material.
2. **EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE** — a connection justified by evidence but not itself an adjudicated fact.
3. **PARTY-SUPPLIED ALLEGATION/BENCHMARK** — supplied by the claimant and requiring source completion.
4. **OPEN QUESTION** — finite documentary answer requested from RICPE or another custodian.

Do not convert converging funding layers into “double/triple funding” as an established fact. The stronger and more defensible formulation is to demand a complete cost-by-cost, job-by-job, asset-by-asset and approval-by-approval reconciliation capable of proving or excluding duplication.
