### 2. What valuation supported the investment proposition?

Recover every valuation, appraisal, investment-committee valuation or value assumption supplied to or used by RICPE for the Sun Park project. For each identify:

`asset/perimeter → owner assumed → valuer → date → method → value → encumbrances/title assumptions → purpose → approving body → financing/LTV use`.

Reconcile those values with the approximately **€9.77m January-2018 non-ECO valuation** already tracked by the project and with any value CAM supplied to Concurso 36/2012. The user specifically raises the economic question whether an approximately €9.7m asset value could support a circa €12m capital/funding proposition. This is an **open reconciliation question**, not proof of impropriety. Do not impose generic bank 60–80% LTV practice on RICPE as a legal rule; use financing ratios only as a benchmark requiring the actual RICPE underwriting assumptions, additional security, capital structure and valuation basis.

## Publication discipline

Use four statuses throughout:

1. **DOCUMENTED / VERIFIED** — supported by controlled primary/official material.
2. **EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE** — a connection justified by evidence but not itself an adjudicated fact.
3. **PARTY-SUPPLIED ALLEGATION/BENCHMARK** — supplied by the claimant and requiring source completion.
4. **OPEN QUESTION** — finite documentary answer requested from RICPE or another custodian.

Do not convert converging funding layers into “double/triple funding” as an established fact. The stronger and more defensible formulation is to demand a complete cost-by-cost, job-by-job, asset-by-asset and approval-by-approval reconciliation capable of proving or excluding duplication.
