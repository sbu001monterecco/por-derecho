## Source-state rules

- A signed copy located in private custody is not automatically a certified court copy.
- A deposit receipt is not automatically the principal filing receipt.
- A reported filing supported by later cure/deposit steps is not the same as locating the filed instrument.
- A scheduling date is not a merits decision, service or finality.
- `Not located` is a bounded corpus result, not proof of nonexistence.
- A decision under appeal remains a decision; the appeal neither erases nor affirms it until decided.
