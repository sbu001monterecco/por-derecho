## English feed post

**SUN PARK / RICPE — WHAT WAS SAID, WHAT WAS LATER CERTIFIED, AND WHERE IS THE ENFORCEMENT TRAIL?**

The documentary sequence deserves a clear answer.

**4 June 2020 — RICPE investor brochure**  
Sun Park was listed with Construcciones Acosta Matos, S.A. as **“sociedad titular”**, while the project was also described as **“en estudio.”**

**11 November 2020 — RICPE investor webinar**  
José Daniel Acosta Matos presented Sun Park as a 220-apartment project, conversion to a four-star hotel and an investment of approximately **€12 million**.

The preserved webinar record attributes to him the statements:

> “Hemos comprado los tres complejos con fondos propios.”

and

> “Los tres inmuebles están libres de cargas.”

The same presentation architecture involved RIC Private Equity and its leadership. Enrique Guerra spoke about the investment platform, prospective fundraising and the control/filter process. I am **not** attributing the ownership statements to him personally; the question is what RICPE knew, approved, verified and communicated as an institution.

**20 July 2021 — RICPE’s own certification**

A very different documentary picture appears:

**54 properties — CAM**  
**190 properties — LPB**  
**18 properties — third parties**

The same record states that there was **no signed LOI** and that full legal, tax, financial, technical and insurance due diligence had not yet been completed.

That creates a finite question:

**Who approved the 2020 ownership and “free of charges” representations, what documentary title evidence supported them, and what correction was made once RICPE’s own internal record documented the materially different 2021 position?**

And then there is the regulatory question.

CNMV received the first warning in January 2021. CNMV has subsequently acknowledged that the reported facts were taken into account in supervisory activity.

But I have not located a **public sanction, intervention or corrective decision naming RICPE** arising from these representations.

That does **not** mean no confidential supervisory work occurred.

It means the public record still needs an answer:

**What was examined? What was corrected? What enforcement or investor-protection outcome followed?**

This is why we are asking for documents—not assumptions.

**Registration ≠ admission.  
Supervision acknowledged ≠ public enforcement outcome.  
Overlap ≠ automatically double financing.**

The objective is documentary reconciliation, accountability and the opportunity for every affected person and institution to respond with primary evidence.

**Documents. Dates. Decisions.**
