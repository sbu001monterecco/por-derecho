## 1. Non-negotiable provenance rule

Every material proposition must remain attached to its evidential class. Do not silently convert recollection, opinion, adverse pleading, counsel advice or a working theory into an established fact.

Use the following classes in future analysis and intake:

- `PRIMARY_SOURCE_FACT` — proposition directly supported by an identified primary record; record the exact source and date.
- `INSTITUTIONAL_STATUS` — what an authority has actually acknowledged, joined, transferred, admitted, rejected or decided; do not infer merits beyond the act.
- `PERSONAL_RECOLLECTION:<speaker>` — the named speaker's memory of an event; preserve the speaker and date of statement.
- `OPINION_INTERPRETATION:<speaker>` — the named speaker's inference, characterisation or theory.
- `ADVERSE_SOURCE_ASSERTION` — proposition appearing in an adverse-party, judicial, professional or third-party source that is not adopted merely by being recorded.
- `COUNSEL_POSITION` — advice/analysis actually attributable to counsel; do not broaden it into client fact or project-wide endorsement.
- `WORKING_HYPOTHESIS` — proposition to be tested against primary records.
- `OPEN_EVIDENCE_GAP` — a bounded missing record or unanswered bridge whose absence is itself not proof of wrongdoing.

Where a sentence combines more than one class, split it.
