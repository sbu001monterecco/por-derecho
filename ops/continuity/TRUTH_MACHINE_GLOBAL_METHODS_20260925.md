## Dynamic publication

## Scientific/economic-crime method stack — system-wide upgrade
- **CIA ACH:** competing hypotheses and disconfirming evidence; no probability is inferred from a document cue.
- **SUE:** statement–evidence consistency matrix; the document implementation does not simulate interview disclosure.
- **Verifiability Approach:** checkable detail classes and actual verification state; no truth probability from detail ratios.
- **Wigmore:** proposition/evidence graph with supporting, contrary and position-to-know branches.
- **FATF:** parallel financial rail (source → authority → debit → credit → destination/use).
- **FCA:** expected-control evidence (risk → control owner → expected artefact → monitoring/remediation).
- **PSFA:** evidence vs assumption, source quality, network/pattern/comparative analysis and red teaming.
- **PEACE:** preserve complete account before clarification/challenge; applied here as a document-review analogue only.
- **FBI linguistic cues:** areas for further probing only; never standalone evidence of deception.
- **NIST/ENFSI:** validation and competing-proposition firewall; no likelihood ratio or performance percentage without validated method/reference data.

### Falsehood firewall
`FALSE` requires matched proposition scope plus inspected reliable contrary evidence. `KNOWINGLY_FALSE` additionally requires separately sourced actor-specific knowledge at the relevant time. Deliberate misleading use and legal consequence remain further distinct questions. The system keeps falsehood, knowledge, intent, reliance and liability on separate rails.
