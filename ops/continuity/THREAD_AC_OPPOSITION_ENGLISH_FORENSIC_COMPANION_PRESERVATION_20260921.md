## Review legend

The English companion uses the following review states:

- **Yellow — verify / not accepted without verification:** a factual proposition requiring supporting records or further checking.
- **Orange — challenged/rejected reasoning:** a disputed inference, characterisation, incomplete account or contested legal position.
- **Red — identified textual inconsistency:** reserved for a specified contradiction or inconsistency; it does not itself establish deliberate falsehood.
- **Purple — evidential connection:** a passage relevant to a private actor, transaction, benefit, professional relationship or another proceeding; connection is not guilt by association.
- **Blue — express acknowledgement / position / notice:** wording relevant to what the author accepts, asserts, knew about or was told.
- **Green — corroboration / material qualification:** evidence or limitations supporting the author on a point or materially qualifying an adverse thesis.
