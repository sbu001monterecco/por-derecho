## Controlling rule
Analyse conduct, language, representations, sequencing, leverage and effects rather than character labels. Translate labels into falsifiable propositions supported by identified evidence.

Per episode:
ACTOR → WORDS/ACT → DATE → TARGET → SOURCE → CONTEXT → KNOWLEDGE → REPRESENTATION → CONTRADICTION → PRESSURE/LEVERAGE → OBJECTIVE/BENEFIT → EFFECT → LATER REPETITION → CONTRARY EXPLANATION.

Classify every proposition as documented fact, corroborated inference, pattern indicator, allegation requiring further proof, or adjudicated/officially determined fact.

## Comparative rule
Individual analysis comes first. Similarity may support a comparative operating-pattern hypothesis; it does not establish agreement, conspiracy, common intent or coordination. Coordination requires an independent evidential bridge.

Intent is not inferred from outcome alone. Where direct proof is absent, identify the inference and strongest reasonable contrary explanation.
