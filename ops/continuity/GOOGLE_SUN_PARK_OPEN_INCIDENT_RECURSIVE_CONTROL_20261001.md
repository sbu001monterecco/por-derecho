## Controlling closure rule

A Google reply, reinstatement, visibility on Maps/Search, acknowledgement, support-case closure, or absence of further verification demand **must never by itself close this incident**.

The incident may be marked `RESOLVED` only when the controlled record supports all applicable gates below:

1. **Correct live state:** the relevant Sun Park profile is visible with materially correct identity/status information.
2. **Authorised control:** the correct business owner or duly authorised representative has verified management access; primary ownership is known and controlled.
3. **Access integrity:** no unidentified or unauthorised owner/manager/access conflict remains unresolved.
4. **Recurring-state integrity:** any closed/permanently-closed, duplicate, ownership-request, verification, suspension or material-change anomaly has been separately accounted for.
5. **Native audit route:** Google has either provided the relevant business-data/audit/change-source material lawfully available to the rights-holder, or identified the authenticated formal route by which the rights-holder or competent authority can obtain it.
6. **Case preservation:** support case `5-5076000032482`, the stable profile/action references already controlled in the repository, and later case references are preserved with their attachments, routing and disposition metadata where lawfully obtainable.
7. **Stability verification:** after an apparent correction, perform internal rechecks at approximately 7 days and 30 days. These are Por Derecho operational controls, not Google policy requirements. Any recurrence reopens/escalates the incident automatically.
8. **No outstanding provider dependency:** no material Google question remains unanswered merely because the provider marked a ticket solved/closed or sent a template response.

If any gate remains open, status is `OPEN`, `PARTIAL` or `AWAITING_PROVIDER`, never `RESOLVED`.

## Recursive pursuit loop

After every Google response or material profile-state change:

1. **Read the whole response and attachments.** Preserve the exact case/reference, sender, timestamp and stated disposition.
2. **Compare against every closure gate.** Do not substitute “reinstated”, “live”, “verified”, “case closed” or “no further action required” for the broader requested outcome.
3. **Classify the provider response:** substantive / partial / procedural / template / misrouted / contradictory / no-response.
4. **Use the same case first** where a reply channel remains available, explicitly identifying what remains unanswered.
5. **Use the current official route matched to the problem:**
   - ownership/access conflict: request ownership; current owner normally has 3 days to respond; if denied, use the appeal option; if no response, use any claim/verify option Google makes available;
   - duplicate/ownership conflict: use Google's duplicate/ownership-resolution path;
   - suspension/disablement: use the Business Profile appeals tool and submit supporting evidence where available;
   - inaccurate/ineligible/misleading profile information: use Google Maps edit/redressal routes as applicable;
   - business data not otherwise available in Help Center/APIs and the EEA business-data route applies: use Google's **Business User Data Access Request** route referenced in its Business Profile policies.
6. **Ask finite questions.** For this incident, priority provider-native questions remain:
   - source/audit event for each closure, reopening, reinstatement and material profile change;
   - ownership/manager/request/verification history;
   - action-level timestamps and source channel;
   - authority/title material supplied for historical ownership-management requests, if lawfully disclosable;
   - audit/change-source record sufficient to distinguish user edit, owner/manager action, public suggestion, automated moderation, provider action or other source;
   - preservation and lawful disclosure route for provider-native records that cannot be released directly.
7. **Preserve contrary evidence and innocent explanations.** Do not attribute a Google state change to Lourdes Castillejo, MYND, Canarian Hospitality, HNT, CAM, Acosta Matos or any other named actor unless provider-native or independently authenticated evidence supports that attribution.
8. **Record the next action immediately.** Every incoming Google event must produce either a closure-gate completion or a dated next-action entry.
9. **Repeat until the closure rule is met.** Provider silence, ticket closure, a generic answer or temporary restoration is not completion.
