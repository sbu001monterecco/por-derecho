# AAFC / RICPE evidence recovery plan — 26 August 2026

Status: **PUBLIC-SAFE OPERATING PLAN**  
Scope: AAFC professional distribution, RIC Private Equity (RICPE), Construcciones Acosta Matos / Sun Park–MYND Yaiza, professional gatekeepers, later custody and public-funding traceability.

## Controlling objective

Secure, authenticate, preserve and reconcile the finite records capable of proving **or disproving** each organisation's precise role. Do not collapse professional distribution, investor introduction, due diligence, project operation, institutional visibility or later custody into one proposition.

### Controlled role codes

- **ROLE-01 — direct client/investor introduction.** Requires a direct statement, referral record, client-origin field, introduction message, fee record or investor-allocation record.
- **ROLE-02 — professional distribution/event organisation.** Requires event programme, invitation, registration process, recording, presentation distribution or equivalent event evidence.
- **ROLE-03 — legal/fiscal/valuation/risk/depositary gatekeeping.** Requires an engagement, opinion, due-diligence instruction, valuation/risk mandate, control record or signed certification.
- **ROLE-04 — hotel-project preparation/operation/commercialisation.** Requires project, construction, operating, management, marketing, booking, title/access or funding-use records.
- **ROLE-05 — institutional amplification/venue access.** Requires invitation, venue, speaker participation, approved wording or institutional communications. Presence is not endorsement.
- **ROLE-06 — later restructuring/compliance/transaction custody.** Requires integration, transfer, archive, ethics, compliance, restructuring or later-transaction records.

A source proving one role does not prove another.

## Current controlled findings

1. **AAFC:** documented ROLE-02 organiser/distribution channel for the 17 June 2021 RICPE session aimed at tax advisers, and associated October 2022 professional sessions. No present source establishes that AAFC recommended Sun Park/MYND Yaiza, acted as a paid introducer, received referral compensation or knew the allocation of investor funds.
2. **San Telmo:** Eduardo Sánchez publicly stated that “el despacho” introduced several clients into RICPE's “first investment”. This supports ROLE-01 at RICPE level.
3. **First-investment correction:** RICPE's own public video archive identifies Hotel AC Tenerife as the object of the company's first investment. The San Telmo quotation therefore must **not** be used as direct proof that the clients referred to by Sánchez funded Sun Park/HNT/MYND Yaiza. Project-allocation records are required.
4. **Sun Park marketing:** the preserved 11 November 2020 RICPE webinar contains investor-facing statements about small investors, project filters, professional advisers, guarantees, the Acosta Matos tourism-project portfolio, Sun Park as a 220-apartment / €12m project, participation by third-party RIC investors, and expected fundraising volumes.
5. **July 2021 checkpoint:** the signed 20 July 2021 RICPE certification records the 54/190/18 position, no signed LOI, comprehensive due diligence not yet initiated and a transaction that could fail. This is a mandatory reconciliation checkpoint against earlier marketing.
6. **Professional/prosecutorial transmission:** two source-controlled PNG graphics were later sent through PwC, RSM/San Telmo, Grant Thornton and Ministerio Fiscal / Fiscalía preservation routes. Transmission establishes delivery/custody only; it is not institutional acceptance or a merits finding.
