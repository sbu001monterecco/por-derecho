# Homepage Orientation Contract — 25 September 2026

The public homepage is an orientation and trust layer, not the master dossier.

Required first-level architecture:
- Recovery
- Future
- Por Derecho
- Evidence
- Updates
- More

Required homepage sequence:
1. concise hero and two primary actions;
2. current-priority strip;
3. three-route gateway;
4. 60-second orientation;
5. how-to-read-the-record status explainer;
6. current updates;
7. legal/editorial notice and footer.

Boundaries:
- detailed actor, proceeding, title, financing and institutional-accountability material belongs behind Recovery/Evidence routes;
- Future is a standalone page;
- institutional capital is one level deeper from Future and must not be linked directly from the homepage;
- Por Derecho remains legally/editorially distinct;
- EN homepage target <=120KB; ES target <=125KB;
- changes that violate these invariants fail CI.
