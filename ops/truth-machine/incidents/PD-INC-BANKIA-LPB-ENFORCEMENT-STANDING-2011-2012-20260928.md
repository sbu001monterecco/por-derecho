## 4. Grace period / acceleration control

The 20-Dec-2011 debt-liquidation notarial package records the 27-May-2010 €850,000 mortgage and an 18-month grace period. Bankia's 9-Dec-2011 liquidation recorded:

- remaining principal: **€839,441.00**
- ordinary interest: **€17,236.08**
- default interest: **€696.73**
- total: **€857,373.81**
- ledger field: **capital unpaid / capital in arrears: €0.00**

**Verified accounting fact:** the recorded pre-acceleration arrears were not missed principal instalments; the bank then accelerated the remaining principal.

**Open legal/contractual question:** whether the grace clause deferred principal only, while interest remained payable, and whether unpaid interest validly triggered total acceleration under the exact deed and registered acceleration clause then in force.

Do not use “the 18-month grace period proves the July-2011 default was impossible.” The exact deed wording controls.
