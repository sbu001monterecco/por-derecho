## Classification on receipt

For each recovered source classify separately:

- direct knowledge;
- affirmative act;
- pre-event operational instruction;
- post-event validation/justification;
- verified authority;
- disputed/absent authority;
- knowledge of authority defect;
- effect/benefit;
- corrective action;
- intent evidence;
- contrary explanation.

Do not infer intent from omission alone. Do not infer valid complex-wide authority from ownership of individual units alone. Do not convert “not located” into “did not exist.”
