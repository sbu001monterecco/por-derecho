## Source independence

The overview, officer, PSC and filing-history pages are separate views of the **same Companies House register family**. They must not be counted as four independent corroborating institutions.

Where a wider proposition matters, seek an actually independent source: statutory registers where applicable, native company records, share/register documents, executed trust/nominee instruments where any exist, board/shareholder records, accounts, contracts, bank/payment records, Spanish company/property records or another competent source.

## Decisive test
