## Success

An authorised successor, without this chat or the original mailbox connector, can locate, restore, verify and understand the preserved originals and current source-qualified memory, recover corrections and contrary evidence, identify missing material and resume the exact next unfinished task.

Evidence custody preserves originals; institutional memory preserves their relationships and limits. Neither substitutes for the other. The public website is an output, not the private vault.

## Separate acceptance gates

1. **SCOPE:** accessible mailbox locations and time partitions enumerated/reconciled, exact snapshot identified, exclusions explicit. A completed keyword search does not close the mailbox denominator.
2. **BASELINE_ACCOUNTING:** every historical record has stable identity, source, custody status and explained disposition. Preserve prior baselines when new records or corrections appear.
3. **NATIVE_RECOVERY:** complete original messages, attachments and applicable archive members actually acquired and verified. Explained missing material remains missing.
4. **CONTENT_IDENTITY:** compare actual bytes; distinguish versions; preserve every account/message/part and delivery occurrence even when attachment bytes deduplicate.
5. **USABLE_EVIDENCE:** complete readable content or explicit unreadable exception, source class, author/capacity/signatory, proceedings/events, filing/service status where proved, limitations, contrary material and corrections. Exceptions do not count as substantive review.
6. **PRIVATE_PROTECTION:** approved restricted custody, client-side encryption for private originals/indexes, separate recoverable keys, least privilege and independently controlled copies, including isolated/offline or appropriately immutable recovery storage. Test rather than assume protection.
7. **INDEPENDENT_RESTORE:** restore the declared snapshot without the original account/connector; verify every manifest member, indexes/configuration and critical formats. A sample is not a whole-vault restoration.
8. **MEMORY_REHYDRATION:** a fresh authorised reader follows a proposition back to its original, distinguishes fact/allegation/inference, recovers qualifications/corrections and resumes one exact task.
9. **ONGOING_INTAKE:** historical migrations and continuing business intake have separate idempotent checkpoints and exceptions-only re-entry. No shared watermark skips unprocessed partitions.

**OFFICIAL_DOCKET** is a separate gate: reconcile the official court index, sections/pieces, all-party filings and annexes, judicial/LAJ acts, service and appeals. Complete recovery of held digital sources does not certify the entire official court file.

## Counting and privacy

Unknown totals are null, not zero. Report record occurrences, native messages, attachment byte objects, containers/members, reviewed material, independent copies and restored objects separately. Filename/size groups are provisional, not a unique-file count. Union overlapping retrievals by stable IDs; never add overlapping batch totals. Hash equality establishes byte identity, not authorship, authenticity or truth.

Use a named versioned denominator for every percentage. Do not shrink scope to hide missing material. An intact small batch is not the whole corpus. Reopen status after a missing copy, hash mismatch, broken recovery key or newly unprocessed scope.

No private email, inventory row, source locator, filename, privileged strategy, key or credential belongs in this public edition, build logs or published output. Sensitive indexes and embeddings need the same controls as originals. Follow privately recorded approved-jurisdiction/provider restrictions. No new security configuration is claimed by this deployment.
