# DP 1901/2026 + E.G. 745/2026 — common institutional traceability, referral and preservation matrix

**Control date:** 20 September 2026  
**State:** ACTIVE CONTROL / DRAFT REQUEST ARCHITECTURE / NOT FILED  
**Purpose:** prevent fragmentation without collapsing distinct legal competences or distinct proceedings.

## Governing rule

**One factual spine; separate competences; no jurisdictional dead end.**

No recipient is asked to exercise powers belonging to another institution. Each recipient is asked to:

1. preserve and determine the component falling within its lawful competence;
2. identify and reason any component outside that competence;
3. transfer, coordinate or identify the competent destination where the applicable legal framework permits or requires it;
4. provide the sender, receiving authority, date, reference and object of any transfer;
5. preserve the source identity and procedural identity of Ref.21, Ref.22, Ref.24, DP 1901/2026, DP 1956/2026, DIP 2/2026 and E.G. 745/2026 instead of silently merging them.

A missing record remains **OPEN / AUTHORITY-PRODUCIBLE** until produced or a bounded, reasoned negative search is supplied.

## Evidential boundary

The 8–17 July 2026 ATLANTE performance incident in Las Palmas is a **competing technical explanation to test**, not evidence of manipulation. The Government of the Canary Islands publicly stated on 17 July that the incident was detected on 8 July, caused slowdown, and that ATLANTE remained operational. The relevant question is therefore whether the incident produced any documented delay, retry, reindexing, queue, association, transmission or other event affecting the exact DP 1901/NIG/IUP record. No such effect is asserted without system evidence.

## Competence map

### A. Court / reparto / judicial office
- **Decanato / Registro y Reparto:** source identity, daily references, scan/ingestion, initial routing, NIG/IUP creation.
- **LAJ / Oficina Judicial Plaza 6:** electronic docket/index, actual incorporation, transmissions, report receipt, notification record.
- **Presidencia / Sala de Gobierno TSJC:** applicable reparto rules and gubernative supervision of reparto.
- **Secretaría de Gobierno / Coordinación Provincial LAJ:** inspection/coordination of LAJ-responsible services and bounded service-functioning questions.
- **Canary DG Relations with Administration of Justice / ATLANTE governance:** technical custody, retention, incident scope and electronic-justice infrastructure. No merits review is requested.

### B. Judicial governance / discipline
- **CGPJ / Comisión Permanente / Recursos / Servicio de Inspección / Promotor as classified:** supervening event in Alzada 286/2026; LOPJ art.171 functioning inspection; disciplinary classification where actor-specific indications exist; criminal referral under art.409 only if its statutory threshold is considered met.
- Request express consideration of the **CGPJ–FGE coordinated-inspection agreement of 19 September 2019**, extended through 19 September 2027, for the common interface problem. This is not described as a private right to compel a joint inspection.

### C. Ministerio Fiscal
- **Fiscalía Provincial / file custodian:** bounded search for the 29-Jul report and allocation/transmission record.
- **Fiscal Superior de Canarias:** territorial headship/ordinary inspection and DIP2 competence history.
- **FGE / Inspección Fiscal — E.G.745:** use Reglamento MF arts.163–167. Art.164 permits diligencias informativas where indications may exist but author/elements are insufficiently determined; art.167 permits the indispensable checks and document requests to determine identity; art.165 permits reasoned archive, gubernative handling or referral according to nature/object; art.144 governs the criminal-indicia route for a Fiscal.
- The point is not that any Fiscal committed an offence; it is that **lack of a name is not necessarily the end of the inquiry when the institution holds the allocation records capable of identifying the author of a dated Fiscal act**.

### D. Other public administrations and evidence-producing routes
Keep these **connected but not merged** with DP1901/E.G.745:
- Canary DG Justice / ATLANTE;
- Gobierno de Canarias RIC, regional incentives, EU funds, Intervención and integrity/reporting routes;
- Cabildo de Lanzarote, Canary tourism register/inspection and Ayuntamiento de Yaiza;
- AEAT / CTBG / Canary transparency bodies;
- CNMV / RICPE / Portfolio / regulated-entity routes;
- SEPI/FASEE and other public-credit records;
- professional bodies and private custodians.

For ordinary public-administration interfaces, Ley 40/2015 arts.140–144 supports competence-respecting collaboration, information/document exchange and assistance. It does not override judicial or prosecutorial special regimes.

## Anti-fragmentation clause — controlled Spanish text

> **NO FRAGMENTACIÓN / REMISIÓN TRAZABLE.** No se solicita a este órgano que ejerza potestades ajenas a su competencia. Se interesa que preserve y determine íntegramente la materia comprendida en sus funciones; que identifique y motive cualquier extremo que considere ajeno; que, cuando el ordenamiento lo permita o imponga, lo remita o coordine con el órgano competente; y que haga constar órgano remitente, órgano receptor, fecha, referencia y objeto de la remisión. La existencia de competencias concurrentes o diferenciadas no debe producir una zona sin responsable institucional ni romper la trazabilidad del mismo hecho fuente.

## ATLANTE preservation clause — controlled Spanish text

> **INCIDENCIA ATLANTE COMO HIPÓTESIS TÉCNICA A COMPROBAR.** Consta públicamente que el 8 de julio de 2026 se detectó una incidencia de rendimiento de ATLANTE en la provincia de Las Palmas, dada por resuelta el 17 de julio, permaneciendo el sistema operativo según la información oficial. Esa incidencia coincide temporalmente con actuaciones críticas de DP 1901 los días 9 y 12 de julio. No se afirma que causara la colisión de identidad procesal ni que existiera manipulación. Se solicita preservar y, dentro de los límites legales de acceso, certificar si para el NIG/IUP y documentos definidos existieron eventos de retraso, reintento, reindexación, cola, asociación/reasociación, error, intervención manual, remisión o cualquier otra incidencia técnica relevante.

## E.G.745 attribution clause — controlled Spanish text

> **ATRIBUCIÓN INSTITUCIONALMENTE DETERMINABLE.** El Auto de 14 de septiembre identifica ya un acto Fiscal concreto: informe de 29 de julio de 2026 interesando el archivo. Permanecen abiertos autor, Fiscalía de adscripción, título de asignación, corpus, visado/instrucción y remisión. Se solicita que esas identidades y funciones se reconstruyan desde los registros institucionales antes de considerar definitivamente insuficiente la individualización, aplicando, cuando proceda, los mecanismos de los artículos 163 a 167 del Reglamento del Ministerio Fiscal.
