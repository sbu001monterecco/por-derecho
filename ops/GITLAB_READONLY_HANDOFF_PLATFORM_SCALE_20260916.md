2. Sun Park / MYND Yaiza remains a separate recovery track. Do not state that Sun Rock presently controls its P&L, title or operating cash flow.

4. **Unresolved damages and recovery claims are excluded** from the operating base case and gross hotel asset range.
5. Realised or sufficiently recognised recoveries may later strengthen cash, net assets, debt repayment and Sponsor capital; they are not ordinary project debt service today.
6. Future platform scale is **not present security**. Early private capital must stand on current issuer rights, covenants, controlled accounts, repayment rights and security actually granted.
7. No named bank, lender, investor, seller, intermediary or family-office list belongs on the public platform-scale page.
