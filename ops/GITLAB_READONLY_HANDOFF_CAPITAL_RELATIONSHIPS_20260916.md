## Capital audience

The public-safe gateway recognises potentially relevant conversations with:

- single-family offices and family principals;
- hotel-owning families and hospitality principals;
- institutional or professional private-capital decision-makers;
- selected strategic co-investors or professional lenders where appropriate; and
- trusted direct introducers or advisers who know a potentially relevant principal.

No person is treated as suitable merely because of wealth, experience or a self-selected label.

## Public/private firewall

Preserve all of the following when reconciling to GitLab:

1. no public coupon, target return, minimum subscription, allocation or application form;
2. no `invest now`, `subscribe`, `reserve notes` or retail investment funnel;
3. no public download of a private investment memorandum or term sheet;
4. no bank, adviser, lawyer, trustee, exchange or regulator presented as endorsing an investment by implication;
5. future platform scale is context only, not present collateral;
6. unresolved Sun Park recovery, damages and fruits are not current cash, recognised NAV or ordinary Montaña Roja debt-service support;
7. Montaña Roja remains independently underwritten and must stand on its own transaction, planning, technical, economic and financing merits;
8. any specific financing remains subject to legal, regulatory, tax, diligence, KYC/AML, approval and definitive-document requirements;
9. historical or superseded financing drafts are not current offers and must not be used as present terms;
10. restricted family balance-sheet information, recipient-specific terms and eligibility/classification decisions remain outside public Git.

## Instrument discipline

It must **not** state that any such instrument is final, approved, allocated, available for subscription or currently offered.
