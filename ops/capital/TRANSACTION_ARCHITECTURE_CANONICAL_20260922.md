# Montaña Roja — canonical transaction architecture

**Control date:** 22 September 2026

AWESWELL LIMITED is the UK Sponsor/HoldCo. It is not the acquisition target.

The transaction architecture separates:

1. Sponsor or independently sourced acquisition capital for shares, rights, contractual positions and control arrangements; and
2. separately underwritten direct real-estate/project financing for a ring-fenced Spanish ProjectCo.

No target company is to finance the acquisition of its own shares, and no financial-assistance structure is represented. Any ProjectCo facility concerns only approved property/project acquisition, development, refurbishment, construction, reserves, working capital or refinancing.

Each ProjectCo is intended to have separate equity, debt, assets, accounts, cash flows, security, governance and underwriting. Historic Sun Park recovery matters and disputed claims remain outside the Montaña Roja financing perimeter and are not represented as current collateral or debt-service support.

Current public status remains fail-closed:

- GREEN / PROCEED / ACTIVE EXECUTION
- acquisition not completed
- planning/licensing not represented as completed
- operator not appointed
- committed financing: NO
- definitive offer or term sheet: NO

Detailed counterparties, direct emails, replies, transaction economics, restricted diligence and private state transitions remain in the private Gmail, Google Drive and GitLab continuity layer.
