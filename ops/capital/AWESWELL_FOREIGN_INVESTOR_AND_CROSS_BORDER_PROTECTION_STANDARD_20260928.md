# AWESWELL foreign-investor and cross-border capital-protection standard — 28 September 2026

**Canonical control:** ^AW-SR-MR-FOREIGN-INVESTOR-PROTECTION-20260928  
**Applies to:** ^AW-SR-MR, ^AW-SR-MR-SRLN-2026, SRLN-2026, Montaña Roja acquisition/development, Spanish ProjectCos, and any future financing or restructuring that can materially affect AWESWELL LIMITED's foreign-investor position in Spain.  
**Status:** ACTIVE OWNER GOVERNANCE CONTROL — legal/tax/corporate implementation remains transaction-specific.

## 1. Controlling position

AWESWELL LIMITED is the UK HoldCo / Sponsor at the head of the relevant Spanish investment perimeter. The project must preserve the distinction between:

- AWESWELL LIMITED's own rights and capacities as UK parent, shareholder/investor, creditor or funder where evidenced;
- the rights, assets, liabilities and causes of action of each Spanish company, insolvency estate, ProjectCo or other right-holder; and
- the proposed UK NewCo PLC issuer/borrower and the rights of any Noteholder, lender, security agent or trustee.

For Montaña Roja, foreign-investor continuity is a **design requirement and evidence-control objective**, not a label added after closing. Subject to transaction-specific Spanish, UK and tax advice, the acquisition and funding architecture should preserve an evidenced non-resident UK Sponsor/investor relationship with the Spanish ProjectCo and should avoid unnecessary restructuring that would weaken the documented cross-border investment chain.

This control continues the project's existing treatment of AWESWELL as a UK HoldCo / foreign investor in Spain and the existing separation of concursal, extraconcursal and cross-border/transnational harm. Montaña Roja does not merge historic claims or move a loss from one legal person to another.

## 2. What this control is intended to protect

The purpose is to preserve every **lawful, actually available** protection and evidential advantage arising from a properly documented cross-border investment, including:

1. a contemporaneous record of investor nationality/residence, ownership, capital source, corporate authority and funds flow;
2. the Spanish administrative record of qualifying foreign investment;
3. the ability to evidence accepted and valid foreign-investment declarations;
4. the correct application of any foreign-investment screening or authorisation regime before an affected act;
5. corporate separateness, asset ownership, security priority and ring-fencing;
6. a reliable cross-border nexus for jurisdiction, regulatory, tax, professional, enforcement and dispute analysis;
7. preservation of any rights available under Spanish law, UK law, EU law, the EU–UK Trade and Cooperation Agreement or another applicable instrument, subject always to that instrument's actual scope and enforcement mechanism; and
8. a defensible record for future lenders, investors, courts, authorities, insurers, auditors and professional advisers.

## 3. Non-overstatement rule

Foreign-investment registration is **not**:

- immunity from Spanish civil, criminal, insolvency, tax, regulatory or administrative law;
- a bar to lawful attachment, enforcement, court orders, public-law measures or creditor remedies;
- a State guarantee, credit enhancement, security interest or assurance of repayment;
- automatic standing for AWESWELL to claim losses belonging to a Spanish subsidiary, insolvency estate or third party;
- proof of the merits, value, assignability or collectability of any historic claim;
- retroactive creation of treaty or international-law rights for a dispute whose relevant facts pre-date the protected investment or the legal instrument relied upon;
- an investor–State arbitration right merely because AWESWELL is a UK investor; or
- a representation that Noteholders, lenders or security agents acquire AWESWELL's public-law or international-law rights.

Every external document must preserve these boundaries.

## 4. Spanish foreign-investment record

Under Real Decreto 571/2023, non-residents are foreign investors for Registro de Inversiones purposes and, among other cases, an acquisition or holding reaching at least 10% of the capital or voting rights of a Spanish company is a declarable foreign investment.

For each Montaña Roja or later Spanish investment, the closing file must determine and evidence:

- the actual non-resident investor and ultimate-control chain;
- the Spanish investee/ProjectCo and exact percentage of capital and voting rights;
- the investment instrument, consideration and date of completion;
- the required D-1A or other declaration, responsible filer and statutory deadline;
- any D-1B divestment declaration;
- any D-4 annual memorandum obligation and calendar;
- any report from the Registro de Inversiones showing declarations recorded as accepted and valid;
- any change notification required by the applicable declaration rules; and
- the source documents supporting the filed data.

The ordinary D-1A post-investment declaration must not be described as prior investment authorisation.

## 5. Screening and pre-closing certainty

Foreign-investment screening is separate from investment reporting. Before any acquisition, control change, security enforcement or restructuring that may engage a suspended liberalisation regime, counsel must record a reasoned applicability conclusion.

Where material uncertainty remains, the project should consider the voluntary consultation under article 9 of Real Decreto 571/2023. The resolution is binding on the consulted administration in relation to the consultante. No transaction document may call a D-1A declaration a substitute for an authorisation or binding consultation.

The existing Canary Islands / defence-zone property control remains separate and must be analysed against the actual parcel, rights, ownership and transaction structure rather than inferred from a Spanish ProjectCo label.

## 6. Preservation covenant for transaction and financing design

Unless a later Board decision supported by Spanish/UK legal and tax review records a different lawful route, the following are standing design controls:

1. preserve AWESWELL LIMITED's identity as UK Sponsor/HoldCo and the evidence of its direct or indirect qualifying Spanish investment;
2. do not voluntarily change investor residence, nationality nexus, ownership chain or control in a way that could materially prejudice an available foreign-investor protection without a written before/after rights analysis;
3. do not transfer Montaña Roja merely to manufacture jurisdiction, treaty standing or a procedural advantage in an existing or foreseeable dispute;
4. maintain company books, UBO/control evidence, Companies House and Spanish corporate extracts, NIF/tax identifiers, board approvals, source-of-funds evidence, bank trails, subscription/acquisition documents and notarial records;
5. maintain separate ProjectCo accounts, contracts, debt, security, cashflows and governance;
6. prohibit automatic cross-collateralisation or commingling of Montaña Roja with Sun Park/MYND Yaiza or unrelated recovery assets unless the actual owner validly grants transaction-specific security after legal, priority, valuation and corporate-benefit review;
7. preserve all forum, jurisdiction, service, governing-law, privilege, limitation and remedy positions unless deliberately changed in executed transaction documents;
8. perfect Spanish security under Spanish law where Spanish assets are relied upon; a UK charge or programme label is not a Spanish mortgage or perfected Spanish proprietary right;
9. preserve appropriate insurance, escrow/blocked-account, payment-control, independent valuation, KYC/AML and beneficial-ownership protections; and
10. perform an event-driven review before any merger, migration, share transfer, refinancing, enforcement, material change of control, new guarantee or security grant.

## 7. SRLN-2026 / financing disclosure rule

Every Programme Admission Particulars, Pricing Supplement, private investment memorandum, lender pack and material financing summary that relies on the Spanish investment perimeter must use the same core treatment:

> AWESWELL LIMITED is the UK Sponsor/HoldCo. Where it makes or maintains a qualifying foreign investment in a Spanish ProjectCo, the group intends to comply with and preserve the applicable Spanish foreign-investment declaration, screening and evidence requirements. That framework supports corporate, regulatory and evidential continuity; it is not a State guarantee, immunity, security interest or assurance against litigation or enforcement. Investors acquire rights only through the definitive financing and security documents, and no historic claim becomes collateral merely because it forms part of the wider group history.

The disclosure package must include a separate risk statement that foreign-investment status, registration or cross-border law may not prevent adverse court, administrative, insolvency or enforcement outcomes and may not create a directly enforceable remedy for the Issuer, Sponsor or Noteholders.

## 8. Historic legal matters — continuity without retroactivity

Existing Sun Park / MYND Yaiza and other historic matters remain governed by their own contemporaneous facts, legal persons, claim ownership, funding history, procedural posture, jurisdiction and applicable law.

The Montaña Roja investment should:

- preserve the already documented institutional nomenclature of AWESWELL as UK HoldCo / foreign investor where factually supportable;
- preserve evidence of historic UK-to-Spain funding, ownership and affected capacities;
- preserve existing reservations of rights and cross-border characterisations where legally supportable; and
- avoid any drafting that implies Montaña Roja creates, validates, enlarges or transfers an historic cause of action.

Prospective protection is valuable precisely because it is established **before** future disputes arise, not because it is asserted after a dispute.

## 9. Change-control gate

A proposal is **AMBER / HOLD FOR RIGHTS ANALYSIS** if it would:

- reduce AWESWELL below a relevant qualifying participation threshold;
- interpose, remove or migrate an investor or HoldCo;
- change tax residence or central management/control;
- sell or pledge the Spanish ProjectCo;
- move title to a different group entity;
- grant security over Spanish assets or shares;
- cause a change of control;
- combine historic recovery assets with Montaña Roja collateral;
- waive jurisdiction, forum, service or public-law positions; or
- describe foreign-investor registration as investor protection beyond its actual legal effect.

Before implementation, record the business rationale, affected entities, before/after ownership chart, Spanish declaration/screening consequences, tax consequences, financing-consent requirements, and any material effect on existing or prospective rights/remedies.

## 11. Evidence and source boundary

This governance control is not a legal opinion. It records the owner's standing protection objective and the minimum evidence questions that counsel, tax advisers, transaction teams and capital providers must resolve. Where a legal proposition is material to closing, issuance, enforcement or public representation, the operative law and transaction-specific professional advice control.

Existing repository controls remain applicable, including the separation of legal persons, recovery rights, evidence states, public/private material and the rule that legal asset ≠ accounting asset ≠ recoverable value ≠ collateral.
