# AWESWELL / Montaña Roja — Investor Protection 2.0 Standard — 28 September 2026

**Control:** ^AW-SR-MR-IP2-20260928  
**Status:** PRIVATE / GOVERNANCE / COUNSEL CONTROL — not an external legal opinion, filing, insurance cover, permit or investor assurance.

## Purpose

This standard extends the existing foreign-investor and cross-border protection architecture from a rights-preservation model into a **pre-constituted defensibility system**. The objective is to create, before any adverse event, the evidence, standing, deadline control, transaction protection and procedural readiness needed to protect AWESWELL LIMITED and the relevant Spanish ProjectCo without overstating the legal effect of any registration, institutional contact or remedy.

The existing separation remains mandatory:

- AWESWELL LIMITED = UK Sponsor/HoldCo / foreign-investor anchor;
- Spanish ProjectCo = Spanish/EU project, property, licence and contract right-holder;
- NewCo = proposed financing/issuer vehicle only if validly incorporated/authorised;
- historic LPB / Matkator / other recovery rights remain claimant-specific and are not transferred by this standard.

## Preventive controls

### MR-JURIS-01 — interested-party and notification protocol
For every material planning, tourism, environmental, public-funding or other administrative file, identify the exact legal person, standing basis, accredited representative, service address, file reference, access request, notification request and docket-monitoring process. Institutional Aftercare correspondence never substitutes for personación, filing or appeal deadlines.

### MR-LGUM-01 — LGUM / SECUM / CNMC screen
Every material administrative barrier to establishment/access is screened for Ley 20/2013 mechanisms, including the Art. 26 operator route and Art. 27/28 CNMC/market-unity routes, while preserving ordinary administrative and judicial remedies.

### MR-INTERIM-01 — 48-hour adverse-action protocol
A material adverse administrative act, inactivity, inspection, enforcement step, licence issue, title threat or other irreversible-risk event triggers immediate collection of the act/service evidence, deadline calculation, standing, interim-relief analysis, LGUM/EU screen, Registry protection analysis and evidence preservation.

### MR-EU-01 — EU rights and comparator register
Classify ProjectCo establishment rights, AWESWELL capital-movement issues, Charter scope and preliminary-reference potential only where a genuine EU-law nexus exists. Maintain contemporaneous comparators for like hotel/planning/tourism/incentive treatment before alleging unequal or disproportionate treatment.

### MR-RELIANCE-01 — official reliance / legitimate-expectations record
Record material competent-authority statements, conditions, later project decisions and expenditure made in reliance before the investment is committed. Reliance must be reasonable and source-controlled; no informal comment is treated as automatically binding.

### MR-CLOCK-01 — procedure / silence register
Every administrative file has a maximum-period, suspension, extension, silence, service and appeal-clock record sourced to the specific procedure. General silence rules are not universal.

### MR-CONFLICT-01 — abstention / recusal protocol
Use formal abstention/recusal mechanisms only on objective statutory grounds and supporting evidence. Generalised allegations of bias are prohibited.

### MR-PERMIT-DEFENCE-01 — permit litigation-defensibility file
Each material planning/tourism/environmental permit file preserves competence, application version, title/standing, public information/consultation, technical reports, objections, responses, reasons, conditions, compliance and challenge/finality status.

### MR-REALRIGHTS-01 — Registry litigation protection
At the opening of title/registrable-right litigation, assess preventive Registry protection, including anotación preventiva de demanda where legally available, rather than waiting until third-party rights arise.

### MR-DISPUTE-01 — forum matrix
Contract-by-contract classification of mandatory Spanish forum matters, arbitrable private disputes, English-court clauses, interim venues, service, governing law, Hague recognition/enforcement and insolvency/public-law overrides.

### MR-TAX-RULING-01 / MR-TAX-MAP-01 — tax certainty
Screen material uncertain Spanish tax positions for advance DGT consultation where appropriate. For treaty-inconsistent UK–Spain taxation, preserve the Article 25 MAP route and treaty deadline alongside domestic tax remedies.

### MR-EVIDENCE-01 / MR-EVIDENCE-02 — evidence preservation
Use notarial acts, qualified eIDAS electronic timestamps and qualified registered-delivery evidence selectively for critical transient evidence, final Board packs, submissions, responses and controlled editions. Hash/time evidence proves integrity/date, not substantive truth.

### MR-TXN-RISK-01 / MR-PRI-01 — risk transfer
Test buyer-side W&I, title/specific-risk and commercially available private political/governmental risk cover against escrow, retention, indemnity and bank/surety alternatives. Insurance is not title or planning validation and does not create State backing.

### MR-CONTRACT-STEPIN-01 — project continuity
Critical project contracts should be reviewed for assignable licences, collateral warranties, direct notice, cure, step-in, novation, IP/design access and data/document continuity before appointment.

### MR-BONDS-01 — performance / advance-payment security
Price bank guarantees, performance/advance-payment bonds, seguro de caución and retention for material seller/contractor prepayment exposure; wording, trigger, expiry and issuer credit matter.

### MR-STATEAID-01 — state-aid defensibility
One evidence file feeds all public-support applications: beneficiary, project boundary, incentive effect/no-start, historic works/commitments, eligible costs, aid intensity, own financing, cumulation/double funding, procurement, DNSH, milestones, reporting and clawback. Preserve adverse eligibility evidence.

### MR-INSTITUTIONAL-01 — institutional network
Maintain scoped pre-dispute channels with ICEX, PROEXCA, DBT Spain, British Chamber/BISC and hotel-sector organisations such as ASOLAN/FTL/CEHAT. Institutional engagement must never be used to seek improper influence over independent courts or adjudicators.

### MR-SOLVIT-01 — conditional SOLVIT screen
Use only where the applicant, cross-border element, public authority and EU internal-market issue fall within SOLVIT's remit and court proceedings have not already displaced that route.

### MR-SERVICE-01 — service and notice infrastructure
Every relevant entity must have reconciled legal/electronic/contractual notice addresses, monitored mailboxes, named primary/backup monitors, substitution rules, escalation contacts and preserved service evidence.

## Pre-commitment protection certificate

Before material irreversible expenditure, record:

1. right/asset acquired and legal owner;
2. title/encumbrance evidence;
3. outstanding permissions/consents;
4. written official assurances relied upon;
5. comparator evidence;
6. appeal/interim-protection route;
7. insurance/escrow/guarantee support;
8. filing/registration needed for third-party protection;
9. amount committed;
10. Board authority and counsel exceptions.

## Status discipline

**PREPARED** = protocol/template exists.  
**APPROVED** = competent entity adopted it.  
**FILED / EXECUTED** = actual submission/agreement exists.  
**ACCEPTED / PERFECTED / REGISTERED** = competent authority/counterparty/registry evidence exists where required.  
**OPERATIONAL** = authoritative records and actual practice reconcile.

No AI output, spreadsheet colour, draft signature block, institutional contact, insurance enquiry or management statement independently creates GREEN status.
