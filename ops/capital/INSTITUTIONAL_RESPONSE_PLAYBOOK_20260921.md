# Institutional capital response playbook — 21 September 2026

**Control:** public-safe operating playbook.  
**Private source of truth:** connected Gmail + private Google Sheet **Sun Rock - Master Principal Capital Providers** + controlled Drive materials.  
**Do not write recipient-level campaign state into public GitHub.**

## Objective

Convert serious institutional responses into a controlled underwriting process while preserving one-send controls, confidentiality, legal/regulatory boundaries and counterparty separation.

Canonical progression:

`CONTACTED → ROUTED → INTEREST → CALL → NDA/CONFIDENTIALITY → PRIVATE_PACK → DD → INDICATIVE_TERMS → IC/CREDIT → TERM_SHEET/OFFER → CLOSING`

These are operational states, not public claims.

## Response classes

### “Please send more information”

1. Verify sender, organisation, role, jurisdiction and authority.
2. Identify the actual capital lane: Sponsor/HoldCo, ProjectCo equity, preferred capital, bank/private credit, development finance, strategic relationship or internal routing.
3. Confirm whether confidentiality/NDA is required before restricted material.
4. Send only the minimum tailored pack needed for the next decision.
5. Record version, recipient boundary and follow-up question in the private tracker.

### “Can we arrange a call?”

1. Identify intended attendees and decision roles.
2. Prepare a counterparty-specific briefing privately.
3. Define the call objective: mandate fit, capital type, milestones, internal owner and next diligence step.
4. After the call, record factual decisions/questions/action owners privately.
5. Do not publish the meeting, institution or interest unless separately authorised and supportable.

### “Send the model / data room / IM”

1. Confirm the recipient and internal mandate.
2. Check confidentiality and legal-distribution basis.
3. Use a controlled current version, not a historic/superseded financing draft.
4. Separate Sponsor/platform materials from project-specific materials.
5. Do not make unresolved recovery value ordinary ProjectCo collateral or debt-service support.
6. Preserve document/version/recipient records privately.

### “What financing are you seeking?”

Answer only the relevant lane.

**Sponsor/platform:** institutionalisation, execution capacity, diligence, controlled pipeline activation and longer-term growth; instrument follows mandate and diligence.

**Montaña Roja / ProjectCo:** acquisition/control capital first; later development/construction financing after transaction, planning, technical, cost, operator and Sponsor-equity de-risking.

Never imply committed financing, approved leverage, pricing or terms before they exist.

### “We may be interested”

1. Upgrade the private tracker to substantive interest.
2. Identify the actual decision owner and approval path.
3. Obtain a focused information request or bankability/underwriting roadmap.
4. Agree the next decision milestone.
5. Open a controlled transaction workstream.
6. Do not treat interest as allocation, approval, offer or commitment.

### “Please provide / discuss terms”

1. Confirm capital instrument and decision authority.
2. Prepare a counterparty-specific structure privately.
3. Separate economic terms from diligence assumptions and conditions precedent.
4. Keep competing institutions confidential.
5. Move to an offer-comparison matrix only when actual indicative terms are received.

## Public/private boundary

Public GitHub may explain:

- Aweswell Limited → Sun Rock → ring-fenced ProjectCo architecture;
- the distinction between Sponsor/platform and project capital;
- public project/readiness status;
- bottom-up underwriting methodology;
- the controlled institutional process;
- general capital and security principles.

Keep private:

- exact recipients and email addresses;
- whether a named institution has replied or is interested;
- correspondence bodies;
- private call notes;
- private pack/model contents;
- seller economics;
- lender/investor terms, pricing, leverage and proposed security values;
- family balance-sheet data;
- private legal advice;
- NDA/data-room state unless separately authorised.

## One-send control remains

One explicit user authorisation permits one successful external send. Read-only verification does not authorise a resend. A follow-up send requires fresh authority unless the prior send definitively failed or remained indeterminate before transmission.
