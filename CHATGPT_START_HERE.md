## Source hierarchy
Prefer, in order: signed primary documents; court/institutional originals; registered submissions and receipts; direct correspondence; registry/corporate records; contemporaneous third-party material; internal reconstructions; ChatGPT-generated analysis. ChatGPT prose never silently outranks primary evidence.

## Evidential categories
Every material proposition should be identifiable as one of: VERIFIED FACT; PARTY ALLEGATION; EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE; UNRESOLVED QUESTION; SUPERSEDED; CONTRADICTED/CORRECTED.

## Required workflow
`search → verify → classify → update canonical ledger → update public page if justified → cross-link → branch → diff/PR → merge → verify Pages → log deployment`

Do not edit `main` directly for substantive changes. Use a branch and PR. Before publishing, search for evidence that weakens or contradicts the working thesis as well as evidence that supports it.

## Publication controls
Never convert receipt into examination, referral into resolution, chronology into causation, institutional knowledge into personal knowledge, adverse decisions into bias, procedural error into prevaricación, or company-group labels into proof of shared intent/knowledge. Do not describe testimony as clandestine unless concealment is evidenced. Name people only in relation to sourced acts/roles.

For CGPJ work, always separate legal-merits/appeal issues from judicial discipline, court-functioning/inspection, LAJ/office functions, criminal allegations and matters outside CGPJ competence. An archive proves a procedural outcome, not automatically the falsity of every underlying factual allegation.

For cross-island proceeding work, separate originating facts/asset location, first-instance or administrative organ, appellate/supervisory organ and current custodian. A common hotel, actor or document does not fuse legally distinct proceedings; a document does not become evidence before another organ without a filing, testimony, referral, service record or other transmission bridge.

For possession/control work, separate formal title, legal possession, material or de facto possession and effective hotel-wide control. A later deed does not retrospectively authorise earlier control; evidence of material control does not by itself establish title or criminal intent. Continuity of mechanism from predecessor actors is not automatic succession of personal liability: prove CAM's own knowledge, adoption, use, escalation and benefit.

For media work, never convert a press pitch into proof, a journalist's decision not to publish into censorship, a third-party contact into improper influence, or an article's disappearance into proof of pressure. Preserve the exact pitch, verification exchange, document transmission, editorial outcome and later pleading as separate evidential layers.
