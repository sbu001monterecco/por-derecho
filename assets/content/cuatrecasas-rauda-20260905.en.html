From the professional mandate to recovery and the proposed remate assignment: one factual inquiry, distinct legal tests.

Attributed allegations, not a finding of guilt. The inquiry concerns whether particular professional and recovery decisions improperly affected former clients or connected rights and assets. It includes claims and enforcement before any possible assignment of the auction award. It does not assume that every affiliate was a client, every debt was invalid, or every connected actor shared an unlawful plan.

The criminal-first question, tested in both directions

Start with the requested or actual economic result—payment, adjudication, assignment, possession or consolidation—and work back to the judicial act, representation or omission, knowledge, instructions, competing interest and original professional duty. Then test the sequence forwards, source by source. A later benefit cannot supply missing evidence of an earlier agreement.

The proposed test is specific: who owed what duty; what information or authority they had; which act or materially misleading representation is alleged; what court decision or economic effect followed; and which person or entity benefited. Where procedural fraud is alleged, distinguish deception causing judicial error from a legal disagreement the court already knew about. No completed crime or shared intent is inferred solely from an enforcement request. Criminal Code, including Articles 250.1.7 and 467.

The counterposition remains visible. Cuatrecasas relies on a final money judgment, registered property identity, prior rejected challenges and provisional dismissal of the criminal case. It argues that physical transformation of property does not invalidate the judgment. These positions must be tested against the actual record rather than omitted. Legitimate fee recovery remains an alternative explanation. 3 September party pleading.

Not only the remate, and not only one property

The examination extends to relevant recovery mandates and claims affecting the identified former clients through connected companies and rights. Distinguish professional client, instructing party, invoiced entity, payer, promissory-note maker, judgment debtor, asset owner, creditor, acting professionals and any cessionary or funder. Finca 8584 must not be merged with 8588 or with the whole hotel; equally, one execution lot does not cap separately provable wider injury.

Finite proof still required

Present state: current ETJ and DP records, effective service, appeal processing and any actual award, assignment, registration or possession. Professional-to-recovery transition: accepted mandates, instructions, file access, handover, remuneration and conflict controls. Accounts: reconcile invoices, notes, judgments, interest, costs, payments and realizations; two claims or similar sums are not themselves proof of double recovery. Property and benefit: certified title, valuation and physical/registry crosswalk, assignment instruments, price, funding and economic beneficiary.

For professional liability, identify the protective or implementation step actually owed, whether it was available, and the resulting claimant-specific loss. Preserve the existing account's corrections about actual professional work and conditional financing. Neither the whole hotel value nor a financing offer's face amount is automatically recoverable damages.

No automatic suspension. A complaint alone does not stay enforcement under Article 569 LEC. Former-client conflict and secrecy rules, and any Article 1459 Civil Code acquisition issue, require their own legal tests; the latter must be examined together with its debt-payment exception.
