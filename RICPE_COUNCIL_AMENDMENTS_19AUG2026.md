## Missing valuation question elevated to a formal Council issue

The amended communication now requires production and reconciliation of the valuation actually provided to RICPE in support of the approximately 12 M€ investment/funding presentation.

The questions include:

1. What exact asset or asset perimeter was presented to RICPE?
2. What valuation was attributed to it?
3. Who commissioned and issued the valuation, on what date and methodology?
4. Was it an existing-condition value, post-refurbishment value, going-concern/business value, future value or another basis?
5. What charges, litigation, third-party ownership and title limitations were reflected?
6. How was that valuation reconciled with the approximately **9.77 M€** valuation attributed to CAM in Concurso 36/2012?
7. How was it reconciled with the **25,647,179.50 € Gesvalt ECO valuation dated 7 June 2018** referred to elsewhere in the controlled corpus?
8. How do the separate figures of a maximum **2 M€ participative loan**, approximately **11 M€ investment**, and approximately **12 M€ investor/funding presentation** relate to one another?

The document deliberately does **not** treat the difference as automatic evidence of misconduct. It is framed as a documentary and economic reconciliation question requiring the underlying valuation, model, sources-and-uses schedule, guarantees, approval materials and due-diligence record.

## Financing-cover benchmark — evidential safeguard

The thread considered the user's proposed comparison with conventional bank real-estate financing at roughly 60–80% loan-to-value. The amended communication preserves this only as an **indicative comparator requiring verification**, not as a universal legal or banking maximum and not as a direct test of RICPE's participative-equity/loan structure.

The controlling question is therefore: **what valuation criterion, security package, equity contribution, subordinated funding, future-value assumption or other credit/investment support did RICPE itself use when approving or presenting the transaction?**

## Court-protection / approval question added

The amended communication also elevates the institutional question of who approved or authorised the approximately 4.5 M€ Community refurbishment/funding route and what was disclosed to the Administrador Concursal and the insolvency court.

The question must be tested against the contemporaneous ownership and procedural perimeter, including the proposition that approximately **73% of the relevant Sun Park asset perimeter was held by LPB and subject to Concurso 36/2012 / judicial protection** at the relevant time.

The inquiry is deliberately framed as a verification question: identify the approving body and persons, the legal and factual authority relied upon, the voting/representation basis, the asset perimeter affected, what each decision-maker knew, and what was disclosed to or authorised by the insolvency administration and court.
