# Sun Park / MYND Yaiza — RICPE → HNT → GC/836/P06 financing chronology

**Date:** 21 August 2026  
**Status:** public-safe controlling chronology; evidence-led, non-presumptive

## Purpose

This record separates and connects four different layers that must not be conflated:

1. the statutory **RIC — Reserva para Inversiones en Canarias**;
2. **RIC Private Equity** as a separately named regulated investment vehicle;
3. the later corporate/project-company layer involving **Construcciones Acosta Matos, S.A. → Hotel New Trend, S.L./S.L.U. → MYND Yaiza**;
4. public-support and administrative files, including **GC/836/P06**, RIC/idoneidad, regional incentives and any EU/ERDF relationship that is established from primary records.

The chronology is not a finding of fraud, prohibited double financing, criminality or administrative illegality.

## Controlling chronology

### 2019–2021 — investor/materialisation phase

The repository records RIC Private Equity as the investment vehicle used in the investor-facing phase. Sun Park / the future MYND Yaiza project appears in investor materials before the later 2022 segregation into Hotel New Trend. The statutory RIC mechanism and RIC Private Equity are legally distinct and must remain so in all public text.

### 2020–2021 — project representation and works chronology

The evidence architecture contains investor-facing project material and physical-reform chronology for Sun Park. This establishes a timing question, not an illegality: what project, expenditure, rights and title were represented at each date, and what later financing or public-support files relied on those representations?

### 2022 — corporate segregation / project-company phase

The BORME segregation record reproduced in the source corpus identifies Construcciones Acosta Matos, S.A. as the segregating company and Hotel New Trend, S.L.U. as beneficiary of the hotel economic unit, for transformation and later operation as MYND Yaiza. Laura Patricia Acosta Matos is identified in formal capacities across the companies in that notice.

The legal effect of the segregation must not be projected backwards to prove title, authority or eligibility at earlier dates.

### 2022 onward — RIC/idoneidad and public-support phase

The repository separately controls:

- Decree 224/2022 and the RIC/idoneidad route;
- RICPE Series F — €1,598,849.32 — Hotel MYND Yaiza;
- RICPE Series G — €4,974,853.78 — MYND Yaiza employment creation;
- regional-incentives file **GC/836/P06**;

These are tracing questions. Their coexistence does not establish prohibited cumulation or double funding.
