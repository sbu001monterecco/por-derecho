# Por Derecho Case Laboratory — Case Prism implementation record

**Date:** 19 August 2026  
**Status:** bilingual synthetic Case Laboratory implemented on a dedicated branch before any application to the live legal matter.

## Governing boundary

The implementation follows the rule:

> **Test the method on a synthetic record before applying it to a live record.**

Case Prism is not Sun Park, does not anonymise Sun Park, and contains no real names, entities, documents, figures, procedures or decisions from the live matter.

## Synthetic design

Prism Park is a fictional logistics park with:

- twelve registered units;
- eight units owned by an insolvent operating company;
- four units owned by a non-insolvent third party;
- separate access, control-centre and energy rights;
- an operating agreement covering the whole park;
- a mortgage covering only the debtor-owned units;
- a valuation and offer covering the whole operating asset; and
- a proposed unified sale intended to preserve value and employment.

The legitimate commercial objective is made visible. The simulation does not treat urgency or economic unity as evidence of wrongdoing.

## Five-act experience

1. **The proposal** — commercial pressure, employment, cash runway and the legal-perimeter map.
2. **The record** — twelve synthetic documents revealed through three time-locked knowledge stages.
3. **The role** — insolvency practitioner, judicial decision-maker, prosecution where legally relevant, and institutional/professional review.
4. **The second review** — Source, Authority, Perimeter, Contradiction, Consequence and Reversibility.
5. **Outcomes and record** — concern reconciled, transaction restructured, or proceeding without reconciliation, plus an after-action report.

## Neutrality controls

- no scoring or predetermined correct answer;
- no automated conclusion of illegality, invalidity, crime, negligence or responsibility;
- a notarial mandate can reconcile the principal authority warning;
- a 1,700 m² numerical discrepancy is fully cleared as a terminology/measurement false positive;
- later evidence remains visibly later and is not projected back into the initial decision record;
- prosecution is not presented as an appellate court;
- professional oversight is not presented as a judicial remedy;
- all four human responses preserve competence and reasons.

## Visual and interaction features

- six toggleable perimeter layers: registered title, insolvency estate, mortgage, operation, commercial presentation and proposed sale;
- time-locked evidence room at T0, T1 and T2;
- four professional viewpoint panels;
- interactive human decision record;
- three outcome panels;
- print-ready after-action report;
- bilingual reciprocal routes;
- dedicated sitemap and robots registration.

## Future gate

The Case Laboratory does **not** authorise application to the live matter. Before any such use, the generic simulation should be reviewed for:

1. rapid comprehension by a neutral visitor;
2. genuine capacity to clear concerns;
3. visible human control;
4. accurate institutional competence;
5. accessibility and mobile behaviour; and
6. external criticism by an insolvency/property specialist and a responsible-AI reviewer.
