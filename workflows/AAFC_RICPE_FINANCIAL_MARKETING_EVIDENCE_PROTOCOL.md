# AAFC / RICPE financial-marketing evidence page — controlling prompt

## Objective
Create and maintain a dedicated bilingual AAFC page that secures and publishes a complete public-safe visual record of materially relevant RICPE / Acosta Matos investor-facing financial marketing, while preserving the distinction between professional distribution, direct investor introduction, gatekeeping, project operation and later evidence custody.

The page is an evidence-preservation and verification instrument. It must not convert association, event hosting, professional participation, later firm integration, receipt of correspondence or institutional silence into endorsement, knowledge, coordination, liability or admission.

## Controlled role codes
- `ROLE-01` — direct client or investor introduction.
- `ROLE-02` — professional distribution and event organisation.
- `ROLE-03` — legal, fiscal, valuation, risk, audit or depositary gatekeeping.
- `ROLE-04` — hotel-project preparation, operation and commercialisation.
- `ROLE-05` — institutional amplification or venue access.
- `ROLE-06` — later restructuring, compliance and transaction custody.

A source proving one role must never be used silently to prove another.

## Mandatory evidential boundaries
- AAFC is documented as an event organiser and professional-distribution channel (`ROLE-02`). Do not call it an introducer, investment intermediary, recommender, compensated promoter or endorser unless a separate source proves that role.
- San Telmo’s statement that “the firm” introduced clients into RICPE’s “first investment” is a separate `ROLE-01` edge. Do not identify that investment or those clients as Sun Park/HNT/MYND without a primary allocation record.
- Marketing claims about ownership, charges, due diligence, guarantees, supervision, public approval, repayment security or investor protection are representations. Displaying or repeating them does not verify their truth.
- The 20 July 2021 certificate is a documentary counterpoint. Its existence does not by itself establish fraud, intent or individual responsibility.
- Transmission to RSM, PwC, Grant Thornton, Fiscalía, CNMV or another body proves only the transmission, receipt or custody stated by the source. It does not prove institutional acceptance, investigation on the merits or a substantive finding.
- Silence is never admission. Preserve equivalent right-of-reply and correction visibility.
- Preserve the distinction between the Sun Park hotel complex, LPB’s insolvency estate, Matkator property and third-party property.

## Derivative, custody and integrity rule
For every public image derivative retain:
- source filename and source page range;
- source classification;
- native-source SHA-256 where available;
- derivative filename, dimensions, byte length and SHA-256;
- a caption stating what the image proves and does not prove;
- the date and method of generation.

The public page may use compressed web-resolution derivatives for reliable delivery. It must say so. The native PDF, original PNGs, recordings, full-resolution captures and private chain-of-custody records remain outside the public repository.

The browser loader must verify the derivative byte length and SHA-256 before display where Web Crypto is available. A missing or corrupted derivative must fail visibly rather than silently substituting another image.

No OCR-derived wording may be treated as a quote unless checked visually against the page image and, where available, against the native video/audio.
