Give readers one repeatable mental model across the connected Por Derecho pages:


The visual is a navigation and evidential-reconciliation aid. It is **not** a finding that the split was unlawful, that CaixaBank participated in the 2018 transaction, or that a Valencia outcome automatically invalidates downstream assignments, OB REM acts or adjudication.

## Controlled propositions

1. The located 2023 Valencia claim pleads the original financing relationship and asserts functional linkage among the swap, refinancing, second mortgage facility and pledge.
2. The located Valencia pleading does not itself plead the 28-Nov-2018 OB REM transaction as a cause of action.
3. The downstream mortgage-credit/security route is separately reconstructed as Bankia → SAREB → PH122 → CAM, subject to obtaining the native transfer schedules and allocations.
4. The 28-Nov-2018 transaction and later non-validation/re-approval/title chain remain separate legal acts requiring their own authority, accounting and title analysis.
5. A future Valencia judgment or settlement may require consequential reconciliation of balances, payments, rights, liabilities or defences. It does not produce automatic downstream invalidity.
6. Conversely, an OB REM/title conclusion does not by itself determine the Valencia banking claim.

## Visual questions

The module deliberately keeps four questions permanently visible:
- What exact balance left Bankia?
- Which liabilities remained upstream?
- Which securities, defences and payments travelled with the credit?
- What did CAM acquire, at what price and with what due diligence?

## Publication boundary

Do not convert the graphic into language such as:
- “CaixaBank caused the OB REM transaction”;
- “the 2018 deed is void because of Valencia”;
- “the credit assignment automatically carried every banking liability”;
- “the same actors formed a proven common plan.”

Those remain questions of primary evidence and applicable law.
