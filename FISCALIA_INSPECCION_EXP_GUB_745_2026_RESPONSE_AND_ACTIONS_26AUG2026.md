**Controlled position:** the broad characterisation of lack of clarity is difficult to reconcile with that structure without identifying which concrete fact, reason or request was allegedly unintelligible or insufficient. This is an appeal/subsanación argument, not a judicial finding.

### 4.2 “No irregularity attributable to a particular prosecutor in a particular matter”

The source expressly identifies DI 248/2018, DI 113/2022, DI 22/2026, DIP 2/2026, EG 49/2026 and DP 1901/2026.

Inspection request 5 asks the institution to identify, for each relevant act, who received, allocated, examined, reported, approved/visado, signed, incorporated, excluded, referred or archived; what material was available; what identity between facts/proceedings was adopted; and what investigative steps were carried out or rejected.

**Controlled position:** specific proceedings and categories of institutional acts are identified. Individual attribution was itself one object of the requested inspection because the relevant allocation and access records sit principally within institutional custody. The filing does not predetermine personal liability.

### 4.3 Article 68 subsanación issue

The source contains a section entitled `SIN ANEXOS: RECUPERACIÓN, CONEXIÓN Y SUBSANACIÓN`. It asks that if a previously registered piece cannot be found, the missing item and search be identified and a reasonable period be allowed to provide it before drawing an adverse consequence. Inspection request 12 repeats this logic.

Article 68.1 Law 39/2015 provides that where an initiation request does not satisfy Article 66 requirements, the interested party is to be required to cure the defect within ten days before being treated as having abandoned the request.

**Controlled position:** because the Decree itself relies on Article 66.1(c), the absence of an identified Article 68 cure step is a direct issue for reconsideration or judicial review. This record does not prejudge whether another specific statutory regime displaces or qualifies Article 68 in this Inspection context.
