# Concurso 36/2012 — canonical unitary criminal-first governance

**Status:** MANDATORY / repository-wide governance  
**Date:** 29 August 2026  
**Scope:** every future reconstruction, digest, evidence audit, publication, correction, handoff and continuity pass concerning Concurso 36/2012 and its connected Sun Park / Project Sun Rock tracks.

## Governing rule — unitary, non-fragmented, criminal-first

Every material Concurso 36/2012 task MUST be run as a **unitary, non-fragmented, criminal-prosecutorial, investigative and forensic analysis first**, **alongside** — never instead of and never isolated from — the concursal, civil, mercantile, administrative, regulatory, tax, property/registry, corporate, professional-liability and other legally relevant tracks.

This is an analytical-order and evidence-integration rule. It does **not** convert an allegation, disputed inference, adverse ruling, missing document or institutional act into a proved criminal offence.

The criminal/prosecutorial lens must be applied from the outset so that evidence relevant to possible deception, false authority, procedural effect, concealment, coordination, de facto control, patrimonial consequence, documentary integrity, knowledge, intent, beneficiary/harm and later implementation is not silently reduced to a civil, accounting or insolvency-only issue. At the same time, the complete non-criminal legal architecture must be analysed in parallel because legality, authority, duty, standing, title, procedural status and causation frequently determine whether a criminal hypothesis is supported, weakened or excluded.

## Mandatory integrated method

For every material incident, document family, person, entity, judicial act or implementation step:

1. **Reconstruct the event end-to-end.** Identify date, source, actors, entity/capacity, filing/receipt, court or LAJ response, service, challenge, finality, implementation and remaining gap.
2. **Run the criminal-prosecutorial-investigative-forensic analysis first.** Identify the alleged act, possible deception or concealment, claimed authority, duty, knowledge/intent evidence, causal effect, beneficiary/harm, documentary integrity, contrary evidence and missing proof.
3. **Run all parallel legal tracks alongside it.** At minimum test concursal, civil, mercantile/corporate, administrative/regulatory, tax, property/registry and professional-duty implications where relevant.
4. **Reconcile the tracks rather than fragment them.** A fact must not be parked on one page or in one legal category if it materially changes another track.
5. **Preserve actor-specific attribution.** Chronology, family, corporate association, office, creditor status, ownership, participation or proximity does not transfer knowledge, intent or liability from one actor to another.
6. **Preserve evidentiary status.** Distinguish primary evidence, corroboration, cross-reference, attributed allegation, inference, contrary evidence, unresolved question and source gap.
7. **Preserve counterweights.** Adverse, protective and corrective judicial or institutional acts must remain visible even where they cut against the working allegation.

## Authority before arithmetic

Any issue involving a purported `Comunidad`, community fees, quotas, debts, charges, voting, representation, possession, hotel control, works, exploitation or expenditure MUST begin with **identity, authority, capacity, standing, representation and procedural-status analysis before arithmetic**.

An unchanged amount does not establish lawful authority, lawful standing, valid representation, truthful provenance or lawful procedural effect. In particular, the **8 February 2018 “not change the amount”** point must never be treated as resolving the authority question merely because a figure did not change.

Every material `Comunidad` occurrence must identify, where the evidence permits:

- the exact natural person(s) acting;
- the exact legal person/body allegedly represented;
- the source of authority relied upon;
- whether formal personación or other recognised procedural status in Concurso 36/2012 is located;
- what act was performed;
- what the AC, judge or LAJ did with it;
- the procedural and patrimonial effect;
- the criminal/prosecutorial relevance alleged;
- contrary or innocent explanations; and
- the precise remaining evidence gap.

`NOT LOCATED` must never be rewritten as `DID NOT EXIST` without a complete certified denominator that supports that conclusion.

## Criminal allegations must remain criminal allegations

Where the repository records an allegation of fraude procesal, fraude concursal, false or unauthorised representation, document-related misconduct, de facto/shadow administration, diversion or control of assets/income, interference with the masa activa or unidad productiva, or other criminally relevant conduct, do not silently downgrade it into a generic civil dispute, fee dispute, governance disagreement or arithmetic question.

Equally, do not publish a criminal conclusion as established merely because the allegation is serious or repeated. For each potentially criminal theory, map:

`actor -> alleged act -> source -> duty/authority -> alleged deception/concealment -> knowledge/intent evidence -> procedural/patrimonial effect -> beneficiary/harm -> contrary evidence -> missing proof -> statutory-elements analysis -> evidentiary status`.

Use prosecution-grade wording such as `the evidence is relied upon in support of the allegation that...`, `this incident requires testing against...`, or `the present corpus supports / does not yet establish...` unless a competent final decision establishes more.

## Procedural-family rule

For every material court-record family use:

`family_id -> party filing -> verified receipt -> annexes -> court/LAJ act -> service -> challenge -> appeal/queja/revision -> finality -> implementation -> explicit remaining gap`.

A filing alone does not close a family. A decision alone does not close a family. An implementation step must be reconciled with the authority, title, notice, review and finality chain.
