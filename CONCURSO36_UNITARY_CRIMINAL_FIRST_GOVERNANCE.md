## Governing rule — unitary, non-fragmented, criminal-first

Every material Concurso 36/2012 task MUST be run as a **unitary, non-fragmented, reverse-engineered, criminal-prosecutorial, investigative and forensic analysis first**, **ahead of and alongside** — never instead of and never isolated from — the concursal, civil, mercantile, administrative, regulatory, tax, property/registry, corporate, professional-liability and other legally relevant tracks.

“Reverse-engineered” means reconstructing backwards and forwards from the observed procedural, physical and economic outcome to the source acts, authority, notice, knowledge, duties, decisions, omissions, implementation and causal links. It does **not** mean reasoning backwards from an adverse outcome to guilt.

This is an analytical-order and evidence-integration rule. It does **not** convert an allegation, disputed inference, adverse ruling, missing document or institutional act into a proved criminal offence.

The criminal/prosecutorial lens must be applied from the outset so that evidence relevant to possible deception, false authority, procedural effect, concealment, coordination, de facto control, patrimonial consequence, documentary integrity, knowledge, intent, beneficiary/harm and later implementation is not silently reduced to a civil, accounting or insolvency-only issue. At the same time, the complete non-criminal legal architecture must be analysed in parallel because legality, authority, duty, standing, title, procedural status and causation frequently determine whether a criminal hypothesis is supported, weakened or excluded.

## Mandatory integrated method

For every material incident, document family, person, entity, judicial act or implementation step:

1. **Reconstruct the event end-to-end.** Identify date, source, actors, entity/capacity, filing/receipt, court or LAJ response, service, challenge, finality, implementation and remaining gap.
2. **Run the criminal-prosecutorial-investigative-forensic analysis first.** Identify the alleged act, possible deception or concealment, claimed authority, duty, knowledge/intent evidence, causal effect, beneficiary/harm, documentary integrity, contrary evidence and missing proof.
3. **Run all parallel legal tracks alongside it.** At minimum test concursal, civil, mercantile/corporate, administrative/regulatory, tax, property/registry and professional-duty implications where relevant.
4. **Reconcile the tracks rather than fragment them.** A fact must not be parked on one page or in one legal category if it materially changes another track.
5. **Preserve actor-specific attribution.** Chronology, family, corporate association, office, creditor status, ownership, participation or proximity does not transfer knowledge, intent or liability from one actor to another.
6. **Preserve evidentiary status.** Distinguish primary evidence, corroboration, cross-reference, attributed allegation, inference, contrary evidence, unresolved question and source gap.
7. **Preserve counterweights.** Adverse, protective and corrective judicial or institutional acts must remain visible even where they cut against the working allegation.
