Riesgo alineado con el PAP

La preparación no elimina el riesgo de crédito, recuperación, proyecto o sistema jurídico.

La web pública resume la arquitectura de riesgos; el PAP controlador y los materiales específicos de cada emisión deben leerse antes de cualquier decisión de inversión a través de una vía privada lícita.

Riesgo material

Emisor / repago / concentración de recuperaciones

NewCo may initially depend on Sponsor support, project cashflow, refinancing or recoveries. Claims may fail or be delayed because of ownership, standing, limitation, merits, causation, quantum, procedure, appeal or collectability. Overlapping remedies cannot be counted twice.

Riesgo material

Escenario histórico institucional y de spillover

The Group has made formal, source-controlled allegations concerning historic Canary/Spain treatment. They remain unresolved allegations, not findings of corruption, collusion, prevaricación or institutional capture. Spillover, selective scrutiny, procedural friction or retaliation are risk scenarios to be mitigated, not predictions of unlawful conduct.

Riesgo material

Jurisdicción UK / España y límites del inversor extranjero

Foreign-investor records strengthen provenance and cross-border analysis; they do not create immunity, State backing, an assumed UK–Spain BIT/ISDS route or automatic foreign jurisdiction. English-law financing cannot displace mandatory Spanish property, security, insolvency, corporate or public-law rules.

Riesgo de pérdida total

El capital puede quedar impagado

Notes are not bank deposits. Private securities may be illiquid. Principal, cash return and PIK can remain unpaid; refinancing, sale, recovery proceeds or contingent participation may not materialise within the required time.

Mitigation architecture. Ring-fenced entities and cash, independent governance/counsel, deadline and service controls, rapid interim-relief preparedness, comparator and reliance evidence, permit-defensibility files, Registry protection where available, contract-specific forum/service/enforcement planning, evidence preservation, insurance/security testing, direct agreements/step-in rights, public-aid defensibility and institutional aftercare are designed to reduce risk and improve evidential/remedial position. None guarantees an outcome.
Azul · siguiente cierre

La ruta más corta desde la preparación A+ a una primera Serie ejecutable.

Son prioridades de secuencia, no promesas de plazo ni resultado.

1 · freeze issuer

Form and authorise NewCo

Freeze legal identity, ownership, directors, capital/commencement position and programme borrowing authority.

2 · review

Complete counsel / PMR / PAP process

Close factual and legal comments, disclosure cross-references and the formal ISM/SSP submission path.

3 · infrastructure

Appoint and onboard providers

Banking, paying/financial-rights institution, registrar/transfer, CSD/settlement and any trustee/security functions.

4 · evidence

Prove cash, financials, security and project facts

Replace model/draft states with actual bank, accounting, valuation, transaction, title, planning and perfection evidence.

5 · issue specific

Freeze the first Series

Pricing Supplement, uses, currency, tenor, return, investor route, security, settlement and conditions precedent.

6 · admission

Close and seek admission

Only after all relevant gates are evidenced should an eligible Series be described as issued, admitted, outstanding or available through the agreed professional distribution route.
