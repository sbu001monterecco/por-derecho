## Shared non-negotiables retained in all implemented versions

- Do not inherit the erroneous hotel roster or ownership implications from legacy concept 1.
- Do not inherit unverified portraits, names or literal “sales team” conduct from legacy concept 2.
- “Platform” means a network of different, sourced relationships; it does not mean every hotel is commonly owned or that every participant shares knowledge or responsibility.
- Preserve the distinction between hotel sales under management; Canarian Hospitality’s own reported turnover; ownership/equity; management/operation; proprietary or third-party brand; capital/RICPE/RIC; construction/rehabilitation; mandate/advisory/introduction; and media visibility.
- Do not add the `>€100m` portfolio-sales figure to the `€44m / ~€50m / €60m` company-turnover series.
- Keep the satire/non-advertisement disclosure readable at thumbnail size.
- Use questions and role labels rather than unsupported ownership, agency, criminal or intent assertions.
