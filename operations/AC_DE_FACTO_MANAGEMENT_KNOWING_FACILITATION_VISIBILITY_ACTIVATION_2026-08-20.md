## Public wording boundary

The public layer may say:

- private management functions and AC dependencies are documented in specific episodes;
- Por Derecho alleges unrecognised de facto management and knowing facilitation;
- the allegation affects interpretation of authority, debt, access, liquidation, title, operation and finance;
- criminal agreement, full administrator-in-fact status, knowing falsity, procedural fraud, prevarication and downstream knowledge are not proved.

- that criminal guilt has been established;
- that every private or later actor shared knowledge;
- that every Community charge was fabricated or duplicated;
- that no relevant incident exists merely because one has not yet been located;
- that later finance or public support was unlawful solely because of the upstream allegation.
