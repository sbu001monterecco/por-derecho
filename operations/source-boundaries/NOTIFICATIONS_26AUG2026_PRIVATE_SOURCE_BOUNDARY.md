# Private-source boundary — institutional notifications of 26 August 2026

The native notification messages and source documents remain outside the public repository except for previously approved publication-safe derivatives and hashes.

## Excluded private material

- private email addresses beyond public institutional identities needed for attribution;
- mailbox and provider message/thread identifiers;
- partial NIF/NIE values;
- direct DEHÚ/notification URLs and opaque notification identifiers;
- one-use security/authenticity codes;
- native unredacted signatures, recipient addresses and verification credentials;
- unsent email drafts and recipient lists;
- privileged advice or tactical filing analysis.

## Public-safe propositions retained

- institution and competent unit;
- file/expediente references already approved for publication;
- official subject and outcome;
- notification made-available and access timestamps;
- technical machine-readability result;
- public-safe source hashes already approved;
- action status and deadline-interaction controls;
- express evidential limits.

## Re-query rule

Future threads must re-query the authorised private mailbox or native evidence store when exact source wording or attachments are needed. They must not reconstruct private identifiers or credentials from public Git history, screenshots, chat memory or inferred URL patterns.
