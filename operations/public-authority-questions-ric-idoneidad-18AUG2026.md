## Public status rules

- **Documented**: supported by a primary or regulated-public source.
- **Requested**: a defined documentary request has been made.
- **Awaiting response**: receipt/competent-organ allocation is established and no substantive response has yet been recorded.
- **Partially answered**: a response addresses only part of the defined request.
- **Refused**: access has been expressly refused; the legal basis and remedy route must be recorded.
- **Under transparency appeal**: an appeal/reclamation has been filed; no inference on the merits follows from its pendency.
- **Answered**: the authority supplied an answer/document capable of resolving the defined question.
- **Contradiction unresolved**: primary sources remain materially inconsistent after reasonable reconciliation.
- **Corrected following new evidence**: earlier wording has been revised because new primary evidence changed the analysis.

Administrative silence, refusal, referral or partial disclosure is part of the traceability record but is not proof of wrongdoing.
