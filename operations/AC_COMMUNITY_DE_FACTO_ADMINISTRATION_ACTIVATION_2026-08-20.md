## Principal findings

1. Private actors performed stable functions over Community debt, voting, access, security and maintenance.
2. The AC requested security, attended Community meetings, authorised identified access/maintenance acts and used creditor-generated debt material in the liquidation record.
3. These facts support a serious functional de facto-management and knowing-facilitation investigation.
4. They do not yet establish criminal collusion, full `administrador de hecho` status, documentary falsity, procedural fraud or prevarication.
5. The Community pre-concurso claim was formally changed in Jan-2017 to ordinary contingent/no own amount.
6. The Feb-2018 order changed only the mortgage-credit holder, not recognised quantum.
7. No post-liquidation incident is currently located that finally re-quantifies the Community claim, enlarges the €9,052,251.69 privileged total or converts all later interest into definitive concursal credit.
8. Later Community figures were openly filed/contested. The current issue is possible functional category collapse and insufficient verification, not proven clandestinity.
9. Operator-side payment evidence makes duplicate charging a material hypothesis, but transaction-level matching remains necessary.
