# Sun Park alleged criminal-engineering investigation — activation record

**Activated:** 2026-08-19  
**State:** PR_OPEN — repository and public-site audit active  
**Scope:** Acosta Matos/CAM/HNT, RIC Private Equity perimeter, Insolvency Administration, judge, LAJ, notary, Land Registry, valuers, lawyers, operators, authorities and other potential enablers

## Controlling posture

This is a live, adversarial and potentially continuing forensic investigation. It is not a textbook exercise, and it does not presume criminal guilt.

Por Derecho investigates whether separate private, insolvency, judicial, procedural, notarial, registry, investment, financing and supervisory acts formed a multi-stage sequence through which disputed control or premises were converted into ownership, financing, operation and value that became progressively harder to reverse.

The investigation must:

- test the sequence as a whole without collapsing actors into collective guilt;
- allocate authority, knowledge, duty, action, omission, benefit and harm actor by actor;
- distinguish professional error, negligent gatekeeping, conscious disregard, knowing facilitation, essential cooperation, coordinated participation and concealment/retaliation;
- identify the strongest lawful or innocent explanation for every adverse hypothesis;
- preserve the presumption of innocence and the right of response;
- keep private drafts, witnesses, privileged communications and preservation strategy outside the public repository.

## Existing architecture retained

The existing 360-degree dependency rule remains controlling:

> The present does not prove the past. Prove the bridge, prove the dependency and prove the consequence.

The new layer does not replace the adjudication reconstruction or the Article 1535 work. It adds:

1. the eight-phase alleged engineering sequence;
2. actor-specific responsibility and judge/LAJ separation;
3. the E0–E7 enabler ladder;
4. false-premise propagation through formal acts;
5. criminal/non-compliance hypotheses tested element by element;

8. a unitary repository and website audit.

## Eight phases

1. Strategic entry: credit, fincas, Community position, information and litigation.
2. De facto control: keys, security, access, works, common areas and operation.
3. Economic/evidential construction: debt, interest, mortgage coverage, valuation and asset perimeter.
4. Judicial conversion: Administrator positions, plan, opposition, decisions, finality and adjudication.
5. Notarial/registry conversion: protocol 457, EUR 13,168,082.02, cancellation and registration.
6. Investment conversion: RICPE, conflicts, due diligence, reactivation, funding and public support.
7. Operational normalisation: CAM-to-HNT, MYND, revenue, improvements and third-party reliance.
8. Defence and irreversibility: finality, registration, corporate succession, forum fragmentation and delay.

## Criminal/non-compliance hypothesis families

These are tests, not findings:

- procedural or judicial deception;
- documentary or accounting falsity;
- punishable insolvency or frustration of recovery;
- disloyal administration or unauthorised disposition;
- coercion, obstruction or retaliation;
- conflict, corruption or improper influence where concrete evidence exists;
- knowingly unjust decisions only where the subjective threshold can be proved;
- concealment or assistance after the fact;
- conversion or laundering of allegedly unlawful benefit;
- investor, market or regulatory misrepresentation;
- subsidy, tax-incentive or EU-funds irregularity;
- direct, joint, induced, necessary-cooperation, complicity and corporate participation modes.
