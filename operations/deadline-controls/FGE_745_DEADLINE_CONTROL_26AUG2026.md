## Calendar rule

No repository page or future thread may manufacture a final calendar deadline merely by adding a nominal month or two months to 26 August. The exact route, notification effectiveness, month-end rule, non-working days, August rule and any special procedure must be checked against the current primary law and, where necessary, counsel before external filing.

## Escalation rule

The merits-election decision must be made early enough to permit final legal/factual review and filing within the verified period. Parallel C–F work must continue without being represented as an extension or waiver.
