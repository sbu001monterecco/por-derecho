# Deletion audit — RICPE / idoneidad / Series F–G — 18 August 2026

Purpose: identify content that should be removed, corrected, superseded or kept when publishing the new public-authority question track. This audit is about evidential hygiene, not deletion of adverse material.

## Governing deletion rule

Do not delete material merely because it is uncomfortable, contested or adverse. Delete or supersede only where content is demonstrably duplicate, stale, unsupported, privacy-sensitive, misleading because later primary evidence changed the factual position, or technically orphaned.

## Immediate findings

1. **Do not delete the 2020, 2021, 2022 or 2023 RICPE chronology.** It is required to understand the change in project description and financing.
2. **Do not delete the July 2021 correction/certification material.** It is a critical contemporaneous control point.
3. **Do not delete references to the December 2022 suitability declaration.** The correct treatment is to distinguish what is documented from what remains unproduced.
4. **Do not state or retain any categorical proposition that no Sun Park financing was allocated or disbursed after December 2022.** The audited-account evidence requires the more precise formulation that Series F financing/materialisation is documented and its eligible use/scope remains to be reconciled.
5. **Do not state or retain a categorical proposition that the December 2022 suitability decision was only for job creation unless the operative file proves that.** Current public material supports a two-phase question, not that conclusion.
6. **Do not state or retain a categorical proposition that a second suitability declaration existed.** No separate second declaration has yet been established in the reviewed material.
7. **Do not infer wrongdoing from administrative silence, refusal, referral or incomplete disclosure.** Preserve those procedural events and state their exact effect.
8. **Do not publish individual investors' tax returns or unnecessary personal tax data.** The public question can be answered from project-level and institutional records.
9. **Preserve right-of-reply and correction language.** Any verified primary-source correction should be incorporated with equivalent prominence.
10. **New transparency pages are additive, not replacements for the canonical RICPE dossier.** Their function is to isolate the Series F/Series G authorisation-and-use question and route it to competent authorities.

## Content to supersede if located

- “No allocation/disbursement to Sun Park” after the documented 2022 Series F materialisation.
- “The 1 December 2022 idoneidad was for employment only” unless supported by the operative decision/application/report.
- “There must have been another idoneidad” as a statement of fact.
- Any wording that treats the €1,598,849.32 as paid to CAM where the audited/public record identifies HNT as borrower/recipient.
- Any wording that equates public grant award with proof of full payment.
- Any wording that equates overlap between financing layers with unlawful double funding without expense-level reconciliation.

## Safe controlling formulations

- “No separate second authorisation has yet been located in the reviewed material.”
- “The competent authorities can establish whether one application/report/decree covered both phases or whether a separate/amended act existed.”
- “The documentary question is what eligible expenditure Series F financed and which administrative/AEAT instrument expressly covered that use.”
- “Administrative silence is not proof of irregularity; it is nevertheless part of the traceability record.”

## Deletion decision

No evidentially material RICPE chronology should be deleted as part of this update. The required action is **precision correction and supersession**, not erasure. Any actual file deletion should wait for a path-specific duplicate/privacy/technical finding and should be performed through a separate reviewable commit.
