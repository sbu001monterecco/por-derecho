# RICPE / MYND Yaiza — office responsibility chain

Date: 24 August 2026

Purpose: distinguish the public call to the competent public offices from the internal reminder/action list. The website must say who is responsible for holding, processing, deciding, supervising or producing each public record.

## RIC idoneidad / Decree 224/2022

Official procedure: Government of the Canary Islands, SIA 2843060 / SICAC 6143, “Supervisión de emisiones de instrumentos financieros aptos para la materialización de la RIC”.

- **Processing office:** Servicio de Política Tributaria, Viceconsejería de Hacienda y Relaciones con la Unión Europea.
- **Decision-maker:** Consejo de Gobierno de Canarias.
- **Required public answer:** complete expediente 02/2022 index; original RICPE application; project memoria; AEAT referral/report; proposal; Decree 224/2022 and annexes; any amendment/supplement; precise identification of whether Series F, Series G or both were covered.
- **Official procedure:** https://sede.gobiernodecanarias.org/sede/procedimientos_servicios_destacados/gestion_tributaria/tramites/6143

This is therefore a two-office regional chain: the Servicio de Política Tributaria processes and holds the administrative file; the Consejo de Gobierno adopted the idoneidad decision.

## AEAT binding report

- **Responsible public body:** Agencia Estatal de Administración Tributaria (AEAT), the body whose binding report is a prerequisite in procedure 6143.
- **Required public answer:** identify/produce the 17 November 2022 binding report, or a segregable version/metadata, and state whether its scope covered Series F, Series G or both and the treatment of already-incurred refurbishment expenditure, liabilities, reimbursement or bridge financing.
- **Official AEAT open-government entry:** https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/gobierno-abierto.html

## Regional Incentives GC/836/P06

- **Responsible State office:** Dirección General de Fondos Europeos, Ministerio de Hacienda.
- **Required public answer:** complete individual incentives file, application, project memorandum, eligible investment, other funding/incentives declared, compliance, liquidation/payment and employment evidence.
- **Official procedure:** https://sede.hacienda.gob.es/es-es/procedimientos/paginas/default?procAdminId=1430

## FEDER

For the 2021–2027 programme architecture:
- **Managing Authority:** Subdirección General de Gestión del FEDER, Ministerio de Hacienda.
- **Canary Islands Intermediate Body / coordinating centre:** Gobierno de Canarias, Dirección General de Planificación y Presupuestos.
- **Required public answer:** identify the exact operation, amount/percentage, eligible expenditure, payment/certification, verification/audit trail and cumulation/double-financing controls applicable to MYND Yaiza.
- **Official Canary management page:** https://www.gobiernodecanarias.org/fondoseuropeos/temas/fondos_estructurales_y_de_inversion/feder/periodo-2021-2027/gestion_seguimiento/index.html

Do not assign the FEDER question conclusively to the 2021–2027 architecture if the underlying MYND operation belongs to another programming period. First identify the operation/programming period; then name the exact competent managing/intermediate authority for that operation.

## Publication rule

The red ledger is dual-use:
1. an internal continuity reminder; and
2. a public call to the named competent offices.

The public-facing wording should not say the list exists “for us”. It should say: **the following offices hold the competence and/or the administrative record needed to answer these questions.**

Every office card should include: official name; role; precise requested deliverable; official link; status; and the next lawful escalation route where applicable.
