## Core issue preserved from working thread

The present record distinguishes two reported RICPE/MYND Yaiza financing phases:

- Series F / Proyecto Hotel MYND Yaiza: EUR 1,598,849.32, associated in the located corporate/regulatory materials with project/refurbishment/remodelling/transformation.
- Series G / MYND Yaiza Creación de empleo: EUR 4,974,853.78, associated with job creation.

The accounts/public record refer to a Canary Islands Government declaration of idoneidad dated 1 December 2022 and an AEAT binding report dated 17 November 2022. The operative scope of those documents has not yet been reconstructed from the complete primary administrative file.

The key new question is temporal and documentary: if refurbishment was already substantially executed by the time of the December 2022 Series F financing, what eligible works, invoices, liabilities, reimbursement, bridge financing or other permitted expenditure did the EUR 1,598,849.32 actually finance, and which authorization expressly covered that treatment?

Do not state that the financing was unlawful or that authorization was absent unless primary evidence or a competent finding supports it. State that the exact authorization scope and use-of-funds trail are unresolved and document-answerable.

## Four-way authorization test

Determine which proposition is correct:

1. Decree 224/2022 + the 17 November 2022 AEAT report covered both Series F and Series G.
2. Series F and Series G had separate applications/reports/idoneidad acts.
3. The original authorization was later amended, supplemented or extended.
4. No second act was required because the first act already covered both phases.
