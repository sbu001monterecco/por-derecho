## Purpose: make the underlying decision evidence accessible

The enquiry connects the rights and hotel business allegedly displaced from the AWESWELL LIMITED / LPB / Matkator platform with later representations, verification, approvals, financing and benefits. Gil Marer's 100% direct/indirect provenance position remains an attributed allegation defining the scope of enquiry, not an adjudicated tracing result, damages calculation or present entitlement to every later asset. Each causal link and independent lawful input must be tested.

The documentary sequence is: original right or asset → control and representation → actual recipient and dated receipt → required and recorded checks → individual proposal and decision → instrument and conditions → application of money or other benefit → later notice and subsequent conduct. A single chronology cannot supply a missing title, communication, payment or element of personal responsibility.

## Corrected institutional chain

Article 2 of the Order of 15 June 2017 identifies four distinct functions: the Viceconsejería instructs; AEAT issues its binding report; the Viceconsejería submits a proposal to the titular de Hacienda, who elevates it; the Government decides. The earlier two-office shorthand must not omit the ministerial elevation stage. The general procedure is not proof that a particular person signed a particular file. Obtain the original proposal, elevation, signature, delegation or substitution and decision. [L1]

## Evidence and correction rules carried forward

1. The July 2021 RICPE certification described a conditional project position and full due diligence not yet begun then. It does not establish that due diligence never occurred later. Obtain later reports, scope, qualifications and satisfaction of conditions.
2. Ownership of every finca and legally sufficient rights over an operating establishment are different questions. Examine the actual rights, duration, consent, representation and limitations relied upon.
3. The access decision Resolution 28/2026 is not the complete Decreto 224/2022 or its annexes. The binding report must be obtained in its own right. A report date repeated from a request is not independent certification of the original report.
4. The identifier 02/2022 remains reported pending its original source; the three-page access decision must not be cited as verifying it.
5. Preserve both source-specific totals: EUR 6,570,713.56 in the brochure and EUR 6,573,703.10 in the accounts. The EUR 2,989.54 difference remains a reconciliation question. The accounts amounts for F and G are EUR 1,598,849.32 and EUR 4,974,853.78. [R1]
6. EUR 6,366.38210 is a per-share subscription value in the reviewed project material, not an aggregate EUR 6.366 million. Keep the subscription unit and aggregate denominators explicit. [R1]
7. Do not count subscriptions and the resulting downstream loans as independent additional money. Separate issue proceeds, loans, expenditure, refinancing, tax treatment, grant award, actual payments and any verified EU contribution.
8. Preserve the issuer's response, declared conflict, CAM abstention and favourable Control Unit report. Request their underlying acts and scope; do not describe the conflict as never declared or the issuer as never having responded. [R1]
9. The reported absence of two July 2021 documents from a specified CNMV supervisory collection is not absence of all supervisory activity or proof that no other authority possessed them.
10. Historical decisions and later warnings need separate, connected timelines. Receipt in 2026 cannot alone prove personal knowledge in 2022; later conduct requires dated evidence of its own.
11. Grant publication does not prove payment, and conditional reference to European cofinancing does not establish a particular operation's EU contribution. GC/836/P06 must be followed through its individual conditions, certifications and payment record. [L3]
12. Registration, routing, extension, admission, decision, grant of access, delivery, intact-copy verification and publication are separate statuses. A technical download failure is not established refusal to disclose.
13. Original signed/filed records remain unchanged. Access to a record does not itself authorise indiscriminate republication of personal data, signatures, verification codes or confidential legal working material.

The corrections summarise the existing source-controlled reader and this thread's research register. They are not a fresh exhaustive audit of every underlying attachment. ## Transparency programme: continue rather than duplicate

The recorded August batch contains eight routes: five new requests and three supplements. Continue those records and existing appeals. Two proposed new requests address a different object: the general verification procedures held by Canary Hacienda and AEAT. The action register specifies source class and unresolved filing/custody checks; it must not be used as a substitute for original receipts. [R2–R4]

The central requests are for pre-existing applications and versions, rights documents, mandatory reports, clarification requests and replies, proposals and signatures, authorisation scope, individual grant conditions, eligible expenditure, employment verification, payment decisions and any identifiable European operation. Ask separately for existing general instructions, templates and allocation of responsibilities. Do not ask the transparency officer to create the applicant's forensic reconciliation, determine a crime, freeze assets or conduct a new investigation.

For each withheld item seek a decision identifying the applicable limit and considering partial access. Canary Law 12/2014 article 43.2 distinguishes mandatory reports from merely auxiliary information and ordinary computer processing from prohibited reworking; other lawful limits still apply. State Law 19/2013 governs existing information, partial access and processing; tax-data reserve and authorised institutional cooperation require separate consideration. [L4–L6]

## Six production groups supporting the wider case

- Rights and project versions: what could lawfully be operated, transformed and financed at each relevant point, compared with what was represented.
- Professional and administrative verification: reports, engagement scope, assumptions, qualifications, requests and replies.
- Individual public acts: the actual instruction, AEAT report, ministerial proposal/elevation, signatures and Government decision.
- Finance and application: coverage of F/G, subscriptions, loans, use of funds, costs, jobs, conditions, grant payments and any verified EU share.
- Cross-channel disclosure: the exact document version supplied to each court, regulator, administration or investor audience, with receipt evidence.
- Later notice and conduct: recipient-specific communications and subsequent decisions, corrections, payments, statements or omissions within that recipient's functions.

## Resumen en español

La transparencia debe permitir reconstruir qué documentación recibió cada órgano, qué comprobó, quién propuso y elevó la decisión y qué financiación se autorizó y pagó realmente. Se incorpora expresamente la fase de elevación por el titular de Hacienda; su atribución individual requiere el expediente, no solo el cargo o una aparición pública. Se mantienen separadas las series F/G, los préstamos privados, la RIC, la subvención y la eventual cofinanciación europea. Las alegaciones sobre el origen de los beneficios permanecen atribuidas a la parte y sujetas a prueba; se conservan la respuesta del emisor y los elementos contrarios.

Las nuevas SAIP deben pedir documentos generales preexistentes de control. Las peticiones individuales y reclamaciones ya registradas continúan sin duplicación. Una resolución de acceso no es el decreto solicitado; registro no es admisión; concesión de subvención no es pago; y una dificultad de descarga no demuestra ocultación. Este registro no presenta nuevos escritos ni acredita por sí mismo despliegue del sitio web.
