# RICPE / MYND Yaiza — idoneidad, AEAT, Series F–G

Opened: 18 August 2026
Status: open
Public pages: `/es/ric-idoneidad-serie-f-serie-g/` and `/en/ric-idoneidad-series-f-series-g/`

## Controlling rule

Seek existing documents and file indexes before opinions. Do not treat silence, refusal or an unresolved gap as proof of wrongdoing. Do not suppress a document-answerable inconsistency because the answer may be uncomfortable.

## Gobierno de Canarias / Consejo de Gobierno

- Obtain Decreto 224/2022 of 1 December 2022 in full, including annexes.
- Identify the financial instruments, amounts, project entity or entities and permitted uses declared suitable.
- Establish whether the decree covered Series F (€1,598,849.32), Series G (€4,974,853.78), both phases, or a wider project.
- Identify any later or parallel declaration, amendment, supplementary application or replacement act.
- If no second or amended act exists, request a signed statement of that defined absence after a search of the competent file.

## Viceconsejería de Hacienda / Servicio de Política Tributaria

- Obtain the complete file index first.
- Obtain the original RICPE application, project memorandum, instruments, amounts, implementation timetable, AEAT referral, AEAT report, internal reports, proposal, decree, annexes, amendments and monitoring records.
- Establish what description of MYND Yaiza was before the administration when suitability was proposed.
- Establish whether the record distinguished refurbishment/remodelling from the later job-creation component.
- Preserve the pending Canary Islands transparency appeal; use a supplemental filing rather than duplicating requests already within its scope.

## AEAT

- Follow up the direct SAIP registered on 7 July 2026 concerning the binding RIC report for Sun Park / MYND Yaiza.
- Obtain or identify the binding report dated 17 November 2022 and any supplement or amendment.
- Establish project-level scope, instrument, amount, intended use, timetable and conditions.
- Establish whether the €1,598,849.32 refurbishment financing and later €4,974,853.78 job-creation financing were analysed together or separately.
- Avoid requesting individual investors' protected tax returns where institutional/project-level material can answer the question.
- Before a silence appeal, confirm receipt by the competent AEAT organ and whether an extension or third-party procedure was notified.

## CNMV

- Reconcile Series F and G against audited accounts, capital increases, participatory loans, borrower identity, stated purpose, investor disclosures, conflicts, abstentions and Unit of Control review.
- Establish what information about the 1 December 2022 suitability declaration and 17 November 2022 AEAT report was available to investors and RICPE's governing/control bodies when Series F and G were issued.
- Reconcile later regulated disclosures with the 2020–2021 project descriptions and corrections.

## RIC Private Equity / Board / Unit of Control

- Request identification or production of the suitability application, AEAT report, Decree 224/2022, amendments and internal approval chain.
- Request the exact use of the €1,598,849.32 Series F financing: invoices, works certificates, payment dates, bridge funding/refinancing if any, bank value dates and drawdowns.
- Reconstruct rehabilitation completion, hotel opening/commissioning and Series F disbursement.
- Obtain Series G approval and employment-financing documentation separately.
- Give any complete documentary response equivalent public prominence and revise the chronology where primary evidence requires correction.

## Controlling public question

What eligible expenditure did the €1,598,849.32 December 2022 Series F financing fund if hotel rehabilitation had already been substantially carried out by then, and which application, AEAT report and Canary Islands suitability act expressly covered that use?

If one suitability package covered both Series F and Series G, the file should establish it. If a separate or amended authorisation existed, it should be identified. If neither existed, the competent authorities should explain the documentary basis on which each phase was treated as eligible.

- Original RICPE suitability application and project memorandum.

- Refurbishment invoices, works certificates, payment dates and prior sources of funds.
