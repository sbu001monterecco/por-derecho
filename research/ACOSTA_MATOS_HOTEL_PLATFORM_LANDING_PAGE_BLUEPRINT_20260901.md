# Acosta Matos hotel platform — future landing-page blueprint

**Control date:** 1 September 2026  
**Status:** BLUEPRINT ONLY — NO RENDERED PAGE CREATED.  
**Reserved candidate routes:** `/es/acosta-matos-plataforma-hotelera/` and `/en/acosta-matos-hotel-platform/` — `RESERVED_NOT_CREATED`.

## Reader objective

The future page should let a reader understand in roughly 90 seconds that a single **hotel platform** can comprise very different relationships — legal ownership, shareholding, co-investment, financing, advisory, mandates, construction, management, brands, media visibility and reputational association — without implying that one actor owns or controls all of them.

It should then allow deep inspection of every edge and source.

## A. Above the fold

### A1. Persistent satire disclosure

Hero label:

**CARICATURA / REPRESENTACIÓN SATÍRICA — NO ES UN ANUNCIO REAL**

Secondary disclosure:

**ANUNCIO SATÍRICO — PARODIA Y CARICATURA. NO ES PUBLICIDAD REAL NI UNA COMUNICACIÓN DE LAS ENTIDADES REPRESENTADAS.**

### A2. Working hero proposition

A single spoof-ad frame may visually place the hotel platform together. The accompanying copy must immediately say:

**Una plataforma de relaciones, no una afirmación de propiedad común.**

English equivalent:

**A platform of relationships, not a claim of common ownership.**

### A3. Hero controls

The first viewport should expose filters/toggles conceptually equivalent to:

`OWN` · `EQ` · `CO-INV` · `CAP` · `RICPE` · `ADV` · `MANDATE` · `MGT` · `BRAND` · `BUILD` · `MEDIA` · `OPEN`

A future implementation may use cards, chips, lines or a graph, but the role legend must never be hidden.

## B. Section order

### 1. The platform at a glance

A mosaic/network of all source-admitted hotel/project nodes. Each hotel card shows:

- hotel/project name;
- relevant date range;
- legal owner/SPV if source-identified;
- exact role badges;
- current/open verification state;
- direct source/evidence link;
- one-line distinction where a famous brand or manager is not the owner.

Default ordering should be chronological or evidential, not ranked by accusation.

### 2. One hotel, many roles

Use a compact role matrix:

| Hotel/project | Owner/title | Equity/co-invest | Capital/RICPE | Adviser/mandate | Manager | Brand | Builder/rehab | Media | Open |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Cells link to the exact underlying source/relationship record rather than only to an entity homepage.

### 3. Who owned? Who financed? Who managed? Who advised?

Four reader lanes:

- **Ownership / equity** — title, shareholder, co-investor and exact `%` state;
- **Capital** — equity, debt, RIC/RICPE, guarantees/collateral where sourced;
- **Mandates / advice / authority** — adviser, arranger, board, agent, representative, introducer;
- **Operations / brand / works** — manager, lessee, MYND/Sholeo, Radisson/other franchise, builder/rehabilitator.

The page must make it impossible to infer ownership merely by following a management/brand edge.

### 4. Credibility stack

Working heading:

**HOW CREDIBILITY ACCUMULATES** / **CÓMO SE ACUMULA LA CREDIBILIDAD**

Separate cards for:

- asset credibility;
- capital/investor credibility;
- professional/advisory credibility;
- brand credibility;
- operating credibility;
- mandate/governance credibility;
- institutional/public-authority credibility;
- media visibility/credibility.

Each card asks: **Was this later represented to obtain another role, mandate, investor, asset or authority?** The default status is a question until a sourced sequence exists.

### 5. Reputation whitewashing / reputational normalisation

Use a split factual/analytical presentation:

**Documented layer:** which reputable brand, adviser, investor, manager, professional, authority or media item was actually associated, when and in what capacity.

**Analytical layer:** whether the accumulation of these associations can create a reputational normalisation/whitewashing effect around disputed history.

**Intent boundary:** purposeful reputation laundering, concealment or deceptive recruitment is not a fact unless separately evidenced.

### 6. Predatory inclusion / credibility-transfer hypothesis

Working heading:

**WHO LENDS CREDIBILITY TO WHOM?** / **¿QUIÉN PRESTA CREDIBILIDAD A QUIÉN?**

Show only source-backed association edges. The explanatory panel must say that an included third party is **not thereby accused of knowing participation or complicity**.

Trace, where possible:

`third-party standing` → `association` → `public/investor use` → `new access/mandate/capital`.

### 7. Authority → access → mandate → capital/assets

A dated ladder/timeline should show only separately sourced steps:

`foothold` → `authority/role` → `access` → `mandate` → `capital/transaction role` → `asset/project opportunity` → `later benefit if traceable`.

This is the visual home for the **authority ratchet** thesis. Missing arrows display as `OPEN`, not as inferred links.

### 8. Media “coverage”

Working title:

**MEDIA “COVERAGE”: REPORTING, VISIBILITY — AND THE SATIRICAL QUESTION OF “COVER”**

Spanish:

**“COBERTURA” MEDIÁTICA: INFORMACIÓN, VISIBILIDAD — Y LA PREGUNTA SATÍRICA DEL “COBERTURA/AMPARO” REPUTACIONAL**

Use a dated media-source grid containing publication/outlet, date, title/theme, people/entities quoted, underlying hotel/project, and whether the item is reporting, interview, press-release recycling, award, sponsored/advertorial content if evidenced, or unknown format.

The satirical `cover` meaning must be labelled rhetorical. Do not imply knowing concealment by a journalist/outlet without actor-specific evidence.

### 9. RIC / RICPE / investor provenance

Interlink to the existing RICPE/Hacienda/Sun Park chronology and investment register. Preserve the controlling question:

**WHO WERE THE “INVESTORS” — INDEPENDENT CAPITAL, RIC TAXPAYERS/SUBSCRIBERS, INTRODUCED CLIENTS, RELATED PARTIES, OR INTERMEDIARIES?**

Do not collapse investor, subscriber, taxpayer, RIC materialiser, lender, introducer, arranger or beneficiary.

### 10. Chronology / platform expansion

Timeline from the pre-2018 Sun Park background through the current control date. Every expansion node shows role class, exact source date and open ownership/equity items.

### 11. Questions, not conclusions

Required closing question panel, including:

- What was owned, and by whom, at each date?
- What was merely managed, branded, financed, advised or built?
- Who supplied the economic risk capital?
- Who introduced investors or obtained mandates?
- What conflicts/related-party disclosures existed?
- Which reputable associations were used in subsequent narratives?
- What later access, capital, mandate or asset can actually be traced to an earlier role?
- Where is the causal chain only chronology or association?

### 12. Sources / methodology / corrections

Expose:

- source ledger;
- evidence-state legend;
- CAEPR identity/caret state;
- corrections register;
- open proof;
- contrary/lawful alternative explanations;
- right-of-reply mechanism already governed elsewhere in the repository.

## C. Interlink architecture

### C1. Parent/sibling hierarchy

Before publication, verify existing route inventory. Intended conceptual hierarchy:

- existing `/es/acosta-matos-perimetro/` and `/en/acosta-matos-perimeter/`: broader perimeter/context source pages;
- reserved hotel-platform page: specialist visual/relationship lens;
- hotel/entity/person/RICPE/proceeding/media pages: underlying evidence/detail nodes.

Do not replace the broader perimeter routes.

### C2. Bidirectional links

When the future page is created, every admitted relationship should have a reverse discovery path from the relevant underlying page where technically and editorially appropriate. Minimum targets:

- platform ↔ hotel/project;
- platform ↔ legal owner/SPV;
- platform ↔ named person/entity page;
- platform ↔ RIC/RICPE financing chronology;
- platform ↔ manager/operator/brand;
- platform ↔ adviser/mandate;
- platform ↔ media-source register;
- platform ↔ relevant proceeding/ACTA/public-authority event where the source actually connects them;
- platform ↔ open-proof/correction item.

### C3. Relationship IDs

Future rendered edges should use stable IDs from the machine foundation and investment registers, not free-text link labels. If an edge does not yet have a stable source/relationship ID, it remains `OPEN` and does not become a rendered factual arrow.
