# Acosta Matos perimeter — non-hotel investment/development census, 2017–1 September 2026

**Control date:** 1 September 2026  
**Scope:** public, source-graded non-hotel corporate-investment, property-development and patrimonial/marketing nodes relevant to the Acosta Matos perimeter.  
**Boundary:** a project appearing on the Acosta Matos website is evidence that the group publicly markets/presents that project or asset category. It is **not by itself proof of legal title, acquisition date, investment amount, beneficial ownership or source of funds**. Construction for third parties is excluded from the ownership/investment map unless a separate source proves an investment/equity role.

This file complements, and does not replace, `research/ACOSTA_MATOS_PERIMETER_INVESTMENT_MAP_2017_2026.md`.

## 1. Why this separate register is necessary

The hotel map cannot answer the user’s broader instruction to map “all things they invested in”. The group’s public perimeter also contains:

- a post-2023 real-estate investment/development company;
- residential promotions marketed for sale;
- planned residential promotions;
- a patrimonial rental/parking/local portfolio marketed by the group;
- construction/material/photovoltaic service activities that must **not** be mistaken for owned investment assets; and
- a 2026 multi-company investment/governance vehicle whose downstream portfolio has not yet been publicly identified in the finite scan.

The correct architecture therefore separates **hotel assets**, **real-estate development inventory**, **patrimonial rental inventory**, **investment vehicles** and **third-party construction/service work**.

The BORME extracts located in this finite scan identify board/governance appointments; they do **not** disclose who subscribed the €100,000 capital or the ownership percentages of Socios Inversores Canarios. Therefore:

## 8. Service/activity exclusions

The group’s site also markets:

- architectural/technical services;
- sale of construction materials / Tecnipinturas; and
- photovoltaic-project services.

These are **business activities, not automatically investment assets**. A third-party project page must not be included in the Acosta-owned asset register merely because Acosta designed or built it. Example: the Acosta page for Plaza de La Feria 6 expressly identifies **Inversiones y Explotaciones Patrimoniales, S.L.** as owner; that is a third-party construction/project relationship unless a separate equity source says otherwise.

## 9. Interconnection to the hotel/RIC/Sun Park tracing model

For each non-hotel node that later becomes material to the Sun Park/RICPE hypothesis, the tracing test is the same as for the hotel register:

`dated acquisition/land/title` → `equity/debt/intercompany source` → `works/development spend` → `sale/lease proceeds` → `distribution/refinancing/substitution` → `later asset/project`.

A common director, family name, website grouping or construction relationship is not a financial trace.

The most relevant cross-over questions are:

- did an Acosta owner-SPV or post-2023 beneficiary receive sale/refinancing proceeds from an earlier hotel/investment node;
- did Grupo Patrimonial, Promociones Acosta Matos, Acosta Matos Investments or Hotel Callao Adeje receive intercompany transfers/guarantees connected to Sun Park/HNT/RICPE;
- did Socios Inversores Canarios later acquire or fund any asset using Acosta-origin capital; and
- were any residential or patrimonial assets offered as collateral, investor presentation material, RIC materialisation targets or sources of repayment.

Each proposition remains `OPEN` until transaction evidence exists.
