# POR DERECHO — LEGAL AND NOTARIAL FORMATION INSTRUCTION PACK

**Status:** solicitor/notarial instruction draft — not legal advice, not an executed mandate, and not evidence that a foundation has been constituted or registered.  
**Date:** 20 August 2026  
**Proposed jurisdiction:** Canary Islands, subject to specialist confirmation of territorial competence and operating scope.  
**Public working name:** Por Derecho.  
**Potential registered name:** to be advised and cleared; Canary law requires the registered denomination to include the words **“fundación canaria”**.

## 1. Purpose of this instruction

This pack converts the public-interest concept and the stage-two governance working draft into a finite legal/notarial assignment. The requested adviser should not endorse the founders’ analysis of Sun Park or any other live matter. The assignment is to determine whether, and on what terms, an independent Canary foundation can lawfully and credibly be constituted.

The intended institution would develop research, education and human-supervised methods concerning legal memory, documentary provenance, procedural integrity, property and economic rights, institutional traceability and responsible technology. It would not act as a law firm, court, prosecutor, disciplinary body, complaints portal, litigation funder, claimant, property-acquisition vehicle or automated decision-maker.

## 2. Controlling status and non-reliance

At the date of this pack:

- Por Derecho is an **initiative in formation**, not a registered foundation.
- No Patronato, conflicts committee, scientific council or accredited programme has been constituted.
- No prospective patron, adviser, reviewer, university or professional body has accepted appointment or endorsed the initiative.
- Case Prism is under internal validation; no independent synthetic red-team review has yet been completed.
- DIP 79/2026 and DIP 80/2026 are experimental founder-related research applications, not independent validation or ICALPA adoption.
- The proposed acquisition of San Bernardo 27 is not complete and must remain legally and financially separate from formation of the institution.

## 3. Questions requiring a written legal view

### 3.1 Applicable law, territorial competence and seat

Please advise:

1. Whether the proposed activity would fall within the scope of Ley 2/1998 because the foundation would develop its functions essentially in Canarias, notwithstanding research, teaching or relationships outside the Archipelago.
2. Whether Las Palmas de Gran Canaria may be adopted as the initial registered seat before any acquisition of San Bernardo 27 is complete.
3. Whether national or international activity would require any additional registration, coordination or statutory wording.
4. Which current Protectorate, Registry and administrative unit has competence and which pre-filing consultation is advisable.

### 3.2 Founders and authority

Please identify the cleanest founder structure and the evidence required for each founder:

- identity, capacity and address;
- authority to make a gratuitous disposition;
- corporate resolutions where a legal person participates;
- beneficial ownership and anti-money-laundering material;
- representation at the notarial signing;
- whether any promoter-linked company should be excluded as founder to preserve independence.

The advice should expressly separate Gil Marer’s role as originator/promoter from the future independent powers of the Patronato.

### 3.3 Registered denomination

Please:

- confirm the naming requirements under Canary law;
- conduct or commission the appropriate name-availability check;
- advise on the relationship among **Por Derecho**, **Fundación Canaria Por Derecho**, **El Segundo Par de Ojos** and **Palacete Por Derecho**;
- preserve “El Segundo Par de Ojos” as a methodology/product identity rather than a competing legal entity;
- advise on trademark and domain checks as a separate workstream.

### 3.4 Public-benefit purposes and beneficiaries

Please convert the following into registration-ready purposes that benefit generic classes rather than named persons or founder-related claimants:

1. Preservation and study of legal and institutional memory.
2. Research into documentary provenance, evidential traceability, procedural integrity and effective remedies.
3. Research and education on property, commercial, insolvency, economic-crime-prevention and public-administration decision systems.
4. Responsible, explainable and human-supervised technology for professional education, research and procedural-quality assurance.
5. University, professional-school and continuing-education collaboration.
6. Synthetic case laboratories, comparative research and publication of methods, corrections and learning materials.
7. Cultural and archival preservation connected with legal history and civic institutions.
8. Promotion of public understanding of legal safeguards without giving individual legal advice.

The statutes must prohibit a purpose of determining the outcome of a live proceeding or advancing a founder’s private claim.

### 3.5 Dotation and financial viability

Please advise on two alternatives:

**Route A — presumed-sufficient dotation:** a dotation at or above the statutory €30,000 presumption in Article 12 of Ley 50/2002, subject to Canary application and confirmation.

**Route B — lower dotation:** only if counsel considers it prudent, supported by the first action programme and an economic-viability study demonstrating adequacy and sufficiency.

For either route, please confirm:

- the minimum initial payment and the permitted period for completing successive cash contributions;
- acceptable evidence before the notary;
- treatment of non-cash assets and independent valuation;
- whether intellectual property, licences or enforceable third-party commitments can form part of the dotation;
- why a mere intention to raise donations cannot be treated as dotation;
- restrictions on using potential litigation recoveries or uncompleted property transactions as dotation.

No Sun Park recovery, San Bernardo value or future public grant should be recorded as available capital unless title, authority, valuation and enforceability have been independently established.

### 3.6 First action programme and initial budget

Please prepare or specify the legal requirements for a first programme covering at least:

- legal constitution and governance activation;
- Case Prism independent review;
- research and publication controls;
- limited university/professional conversations;
- data-protection and security design;
- no intake of public cases or evidence;
- no deployed AI system;
- no public representation that a pilot, accreditation or partnership exists without written confirmation;
- San Bernardo preservation work only if lawful access and a separate mandate exist.

Please advise whether a one-year programme is sufficient for the deed and whether a three-year operating model should also be prepared for prudence, viability and fundraising governance.

### 3.7 Patronato and independent governance

Please propose registration-ready clauses for:

- number and categories of patrons;
- independent chair or lead patron;
- secretary, with or without voting rights;
- appointment, acceptance, duration, renewal, resignation, removal and vacancies;
- remote attendance, quorum and recorded voting;
- delegation limits and reserved matters;
- conflicts, related-party transactions and founder-related matters;
- abstention, recusal and information quarantine;
- no founder veto over correction, closure or an independent methodological conclusion;
- dissent recording;
- liability, insurance and indemnification within lawful limits;
- committees that are advisory unless legally delegated;
- remuneration or reimbursement boundaries.

The structure should be capable of protecting both the institution and conscientious professionals. It should not assume that any named person has accepted office.

### 3.8 Founder-related matter protocol

Please incorporate or advise on a binding protocol requiring:

1. written declaration that a matter concerns a founder, related company, adviser, property transaction, donor or live proceeding;
2. separation of records, funding and personnel;
3. exclusion of interested persons from receipt of restricted materials and decision-making;
4. an independent lead with power to narrow, correct, suspend or close the work;
5. publication of the strongest lawful alternative explanation;
6. identification of evidence that would change the view;
7. no transfer of evidence between matters without a recorded relevance, provenance, confidentiality and lawful-basis decision;
8. no use of the Foundation’s name to pressure a competent authority;
9. no ownership, funding or direction of the underlying private claim;
10. a correction and right-of-response route.

### 3.9 San Bernardo and property independence

Please state in the statutes or a binding policy that:

- the house is not the Foundation;
- constitution cannot depend on completing the acquisition;
- a seller, owner, donor, lender, funder, architect or professional adviser receives no governance influence by virtue of the property relationship;
- property acquisition, financing, valuation and diligence are conducted by the proper buyer and advisers, not by the Foundation unless and until a lawful later transfer is independently approved;
- no Foundation funds may be committed to the property before legal authority, conflicts review and Patronato approval;
- the Foundation may operate from another lawful seat.

### 3.10 Data, research, technology and publication controls

Please identify the minimum pre-operation package for:

- controller/processor allocation;
- records of processing;
- data-protection impact assessment where required;
- professional secrecy, privilege and protected-source handling;
- access control, encryption, retention and deletion;
- incident response and breach notification;
- model and dataset versioning;
- source citation and provenance;
- human review and override records;
- false-positive and false-negative evaluation;
- corrections, challenges and right of response;
- research ethics and publication approval;
- prohibition on guilt, credibility or person-risk scoring;
- prohibition on automatic consequential decisions.

The legal adviser should identify which controls belong in the statutes, which in Patronato policies and which in contracts or technical standards.

### 3.11 Tax, NIF, accounting and annual compliance

Please provide a sequenced checklist covering:

- provisional and definitive NIF;
- Modelo 036 and commencement declarations;
- whether and when to opt into Title II of Ley 49/2002;
- requirements that must continuously be satisfied for that regime;
- VAT/IGIC, withholding and employment implications where applicable;
- accounting framework and opening balance;
- annual accounts, inventory, activity report and budget/action plan;
- audit thresholds;
- annual filing with the Canary Registry/Protectorate;
- donor certificates and fundraising controls;
- public-grant and procurement controls if later relevant.

### 3.12 Notarial and registration route

Please produce a closing checklist for:

1. name clearance;
2. founder decisions and authorities;
3. dotation evidence and valuations;
4. final statutes;
5. first action programme and viability material;
6. identification and acceptance of first patrons;
