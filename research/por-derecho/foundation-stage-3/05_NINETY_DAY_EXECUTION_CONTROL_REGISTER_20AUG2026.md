# POR DERECHO + PALACETE POR DERECHO — 90-DAY EXECUTION CONTROL REGISTER

**Control date:** 20 August 2026  
**Purpose:** convert formation, governance, independent review and preservation into finite, evidence-gated actions.  
**Status rule:** planned, prepared, sent, accepted, commissioned, completed, merged and publicly live are different states.

## 1. Governing rules

1. No external role is claimed without written acceptance.
2. No registration is claimed before the public deed is registered.
3. No independent validation is claimed before a conflict-cleared reviewer completes the agreed work.
4. No institutional pilot or adoption is claimed without the institution’s express written confirmation.
5. No San Bernardo access, survey or intervention occurs without lawful written authority and buyer-side coordination.
6. No founder-related application is treated as evidence that the generic method is independently valid.
7. No public website update advances ahead of the underlying document.
8. Every material decision records owner, source, contrary case, dependency and next gate.

## 3. Status vocabulary

- **P0 — not started:** concept only.
- **P1 — prepared:** pack or draft exists but has not been sent.
- **P2 — sent/under consideration:** recipient has received it; no acceptance assumed.
- **P3 — accepted/commissioned:** written scope and responsibility accepted.
- **P4 — work in progress:** controlled execution underway.
- **P5 — completed internally:** deliverable received and checked.
- **P6 — independently validated/legally completed:** only where the relevant external/legal gate is met.
- **P7 — public status updated:** public wording read back and reconciled with evidence.

## 4. Day 0–15: activate the professional gates

### GOV-01 — legal/notarial formation instruction

- **Current:** P1 — instruction pack prepared.
- **Action:** send to the chosen legal coordinator.
- **Requested response:** accept lead, coordinate specialist, decline with referral, or recommend pause.
- **Evidence gate:** written response identifying scope and conflicts.
- **Do not claim:** instructed counsel until acceptance.
- **Target output:** preliminary route memorandum and fee/scope proposal.

### GOV-02 — scope and territorial competence

- **Current:** P1 — issue list prepared; no specialist opinion.
- **Action:** obtain written view on Canary competence, seat and national activity.
- **Evidence gate:** counsel memorandum citing current law/practice.
- **Stop condition:** material jurisdictional uncertainty or need to restructure purposes.

### PAL-02 — architect referral/shortlist

## 7. Day 61–90: go/no-go decisions

### GOV-09 — constitution decision

The founders decide one of:

- proceed to notarial deed and filing;
- proceed only after conditions;
- revise purposes/governance and repeat review;
- pause formation.

The decision record must include funding, Patronato readiness, conflicts, first programme and adviser recommendation.

### GOV-10 — deed, registration and activation

Only if GOV-09 is affirmative:

- execute deed;
- submit registration and fee;
- answer observations;
- obtain legal personality through registration;
- activate NIF/tax/accounting/bank/insurance;
- convene first Patronato.

Public status remains “constitution deed executed / registration pending” until actual registration evidence exists.

### BOARD-05 — first Patronato acceptance

- **Evidence gate:** signed acceptance and legally effective appointment.
- **Public naming:** separate express consent.
- **Do not claim:** independent majority before the appointment records establish it.

### RT-05 — red-team outcome

Record one of:

- continue testing;
- redesign and retest;
- do not deploy.

Public summary, if any, must state scope, synthetic nature, version, limitations and reviewer permission. An adverse outcome must not be hidden by launching a new unlabelled version.

### PAL-05 — survey go/no-go

Only after lawful authority and a proper professional appointment:

- approve a non-intrusive survey plan;
- agree photography/data handling;
- separate acquisition diligence from preservation work;
- identify intrusive tests requiring separate consent;
- keep the Foundation’s institutional plan subordinate to safety, fabric and legal constraints.

### WEB-01 — public status reconciliation

Update the public site only with documentary milestones:

- counsel instructed;
- independent reviewer commissioned;
- Patronato constituted;
- deed executed;
- registration completed;
- property acquisition completed;
- authorised survey completed.

Each update must identify the date, source class and what the milestone does **not** establish.
