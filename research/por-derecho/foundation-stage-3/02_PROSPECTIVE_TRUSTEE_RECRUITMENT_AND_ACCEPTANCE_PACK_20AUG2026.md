# POR DERECHO — PROSPECTIVE TRUSTEE RECRUITMENT AND ACCEPTANCE PACK

**Status:** prepared recruitment instrument. No person named or contacted through this pack is a patron, trustee, adviser or supporter unless and until that person gives informed written acceptance and the legally required appointment/registration steps are completed.  
**Date:** 20 August 2026

## 1. Purpose

Por Derecho needs an initial Patronato capable of converting a founder-originated public-interest idea into an institution that can disagree with, correct and—where necessary—stop the founders. Recruitment is therefore a governance exercise, not a search for prestige, endorsement or allies in the Sun Park matter.

The pack is designed for confidential, staged conversations. No candidate’s name, title, employer or interest may be published without explicit consent.

## 2. Current institutional truth

- Por Derecho is an initiative in formation and is not yet a registered Foundation.
- The Patronato described here is proposed, not constituted.
- No candidate has accepted office.
- No candidate is being asked to endorse the founders, the methodology, San Bernardo 27 or any legal allegation.
- Founder-related applications must be capable of being narrowed, corrected, suspended or closed by independent decision-makers.
- The house is not the Foundation; any property, donor or funding relationship must remain subordinate to the mission and conflicts architecture.

## 3. Proposed competence matrix

The first Patronato should collectively cover the following capabilities. One person may cover more than one area, but the governing body should not depend on a single individual.

### A. Independent chair / institutional governance

Experience in chairing boards, safeguarding independence, managing conflict, recording dissent and holding an executive/founder to account.

### B. Foundations, notarial and public-law compliance

Experience with Spanish foundations, Canary legal entities, Protectorate/Registry practice, statutes, public-benefit purposes and annual compliance.

### C. Judicial process, insolvency, property and economic rights

Capacity to test the methodology’s treatment of competence, title, authority, asset perimeter, time, remedies and reversibility without reviewing or lobbying a live adjudicator.

### D. Responsible AI, data governance and security

Experience in data protection, provenance, model evaluation, human oversight, information security, research ethics and prohibited automated outputs.

### E. University research and professional education

Experience designing serious research, clinical/synthetic case teaching, independent evaluation and professional learning without claiming accreditation prematurely.

### F. Finance, audit and risk

Experience with budgets, restricted funds, reserves, audit, grants, donor independence, procurement and related-party transactions.

### G. Canary civic, cultural or heritage perspective

Knowledge of Canary institutions, civic life, legal history, archives, heritage and the practical meaning of a permanent seat in Las Palmas—without making the acquisition of one building a condition of institutional existence.

## 4. Composition principles to be tested by counsel

The legal adviser should determine the final registration-ready structure. The governance objective is:

- meaningful independent presence, preferably an independent majority for founder-related decisions;
- an independent chair or independent lead for conflicts;
- no person whose present public or professional function makes participation incompatible or creates an appearance of influence over active matters;
- diversity of discipline, gender, generation and Canary/national perspective;
- no automatic seat for a donor, property counterparty, litigation adviser or technology supplier;
- staggered terms and a credible succession process;
- a secretary able to protect the quality of records, conflicts declarations and resolutions;
- a route to independent assurance and complaint handling outside the founder’s control.

## 5. Exclusion and caution criteria

A candidate should not be advanced, or should be subject to enhanced review, where that person:

1. exercises or recently exercised decision-making authority in a live founder-related judicial, prosecutorial, disciplinary, administrative, regulatory or property matter;
2. is counsel, expert, witness, funder, creditor, counterparty or paid adviser in such a matter;
3. has a material financial interest in Aweswell, LPB, Matkator, San Bernardo 27, a recovery, a future project or a supplier contract;
4. has publicly adopted a fixed view on the merits that would make independent correction implausible;
5. expects appointment to provide access to an authority or reputational endorsement;
6. cannot protect restricted information or accept recusal and information quarantine;
7. is unwilling to record dissent, publish corrections or accept a “do not deploy” outcome;
8. cannot commit the necessary time;
9. would create concentration of professional, family, donor or commercial influence.

Prior contact with Gil Marer does not automatically disqualify a person. It must be disclosed and assessed.

## 7. First-contact wording

The first contact should say, in substance:

> Por Derecho is an initiative in formation. We are not asking you to endorse its founders, to assess Sun Park, to join a Patronato now or to permit public use of your name. We are asking whether you would be willing to hear a short, critical account of the proposed public-interest mission and governance safeguards, and to tell us what would have to change before an independent person could responsibly consider any future role.

No attachment containing live-case evidence should accompany the first contact.

## 8. Structured first conversation

A 45–60 minute conversation should cover:

1. What public-interest problem does the candidate understand the initiative to address?
2. Does the current framing remain too dependent on the founders’ private experience?
3. Which purposes are legitimate and which should be narrowed or excluded?
4. What governance architecture would make independent disagreement real rather than cosmetic?
5. What founder powers should be unavailable or reserved?
6. How should founder-related matters be quarantined?
7. What would make the candidate refuse appointment?
8. What information, advice and insurance would the candidate require?
9. Could the methodology protect a conscientious professional as well as an affected citizen?
10. Who else should be consulted before any appointment?

The conversation is exploratory. It does not create fiduciary duties or an appointment.

## 9. Candidate declaration form

Before a candidate receives restricted material or a formal invitation, obtain a signed declaration covering:

### Identity and capacity

- full name, address and identification details required by counsel/notary;
- current offices, directorships, public functions and relevant professional memberships;
- confirmation of capacity and absence of a known legal bar to appointment.

### Relationships and interests

- relationship with Gil Marer, Patricia Domínguez, Aweswell, LPB, Matkator, Project Sun Rock and Por Derecho;
- relationship with any party, authority, court, prosecutor, lawyer, professional body, funder or property counterparty in founder-related matters;
- financial, employment, family, donor, supplier or property interests;
- prior statements or work that could reasonably be perceived as prejudgment.

### Independence commitments

The candidate should confirm willingness to:

- act for the Foundation’s purposes rather than a founder;
- disclose conflicts promptly;
- abstain and accept information quarantine;
- permit an independent lead to control founder-related work;
- preserve confidentiality and legal privilege;
- insist on the strongest contrary explanation and exculpatory evidence;
- record reasons and dissent;
- correct or retract a public statement where the evidence requires;
- support a pause or “do not deploy” outcome;
- reject improper influence from donors, property interests or suppliers;
- avoid informal approaches to decision-makers in live matters.

### Name and publicity

The default is **no permission** to publish the candidate’s name. Any later permission must specify:

- the exact title/role;
- the channels and duration;
- whether employer or professional body may be named;
- whether the candidate may withdraw consent before formal registration;
- that appointment is not endorsement of any live-case allegation.

## 10. Due-diligence and interview sequence

1. Candidate identified privately.
2. Initial conflict screen from public and disclosed information.
3. Exploratory conversation.
4. Candidate receives non-confidential concept and governance materials.
5. Written declaration.
6. Independent conflicts review.
7. Legal briefing on duties, responsibility and insurance.
8. Candidate interview with at least one independent participant.
9. Written role description and expected time.
10. Candidate decides freely whether to proceed.
11. Formal appointment/acceptance documents prepared by counsel.
12. Public naming only after the legal milestone and explicit consent.

## 11. Decision rubric

Each candidate is assessed on evidence, not prestige.

### Required

- integrity and willingness to challenge;
- relevant competence;
- time and reliability;
- conflict transparency;
- capacity to understand the difference between a research question and an allegation;
- acceptance of correction, recusal and human-decision controls.

### Strongly desirable

- experience with institutional formation or regulated governance;
- understanding of Canary context;
- capacity to communicate with universities/professional bodies;
- experience with data/AI or complex records;
- ability to help recruit capabilities the Patronato lacks.

### Disqualifying or presumptively disqualifying

- appointment sought to influence a live matter;
- hidden material relationship;
- demand for a predetermined conclusion;
- inability to preserve confidentiality;
- refusal to recuse;
- expectation of financial or property benefit not transparently approved;
- material misrepresentation during recruitment.

- whether Case Prism may proceed to independent commission;
- whether any live-case application should be paused;
- whether San Bernardo work may proceed and in what separate legal perimeter;
- first-year action plan, budget, insurance and risk register.
