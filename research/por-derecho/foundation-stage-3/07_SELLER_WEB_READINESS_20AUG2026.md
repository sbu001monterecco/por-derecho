# Por Derecho + San Bernardo — seller and adviser web-readiness control

**Date:** 20 August 2026  
**Status:** source package prepared for publication and independent public-edge verification.

## Purpose

Make the Por Derecho institutional pages and the direct San Bernardo page suitable for review by Carlos Llamas Sanz and the owner/seller perimeter without converting the website into a contract, a seller endorsement or a transaction diary.

## Controlling distinctions

1. **Public Foundation layer:** mission, methodology, governance, research, execution and the public preservation brief.
2. **Direct owner/adviser layer:** informed context, correction of family/building history, present documentary status and professional channels.
3. **Private transaction layer:** price, payment mechanics, ownership/powers, draft agreements, financing, diligence, remedies and legal advice.

## Direct-page corrections

The former seller-facing page was replaced because it still stated a 19 August verbal-understanding / written-confirmation status and used the Project Sun Rock visual shell.

The new page:

- remains `noindex,nofollow,noarchive` and outside the public sitemap;
- uses the Por Derecho institutional design system;
- states that the acquisition is not completed;
- records the 13 August 2026 seller-authorised IZADI communication only at a high level;
- does not reproduce price or payment terms;
- says that no all-owner signed sale, exclusivity or transfer commitment has yet been located;
- identifies Carlos Llamas Sanz as buyer-side legal coordinator;
- distinguishes contextual knowledge from endorsement;
- preserves access to Por Derecho and, behind an explicit context notice, Project Sun Rock;
- explains that the website can inform and be corrected, while only signed documents create obligations;
- keeps family photographs and concept images visibly qualified;
- provides a correction, restriction and withdrawal route.

## Public truth boundaries

The pages do **not** claim:

- a registered Foundation;
- a completed acquisition;
- an appointed Patronato;
- external validation or adoption of the method;
- support by every owner or seller;
- a binding seller resilience covenant;
- authorised access, survey, project or works;
- or that viewing the site is acceptance of any proposition.

## Verification

The dedicated workflow checks:

- Spanish and English Foundation routes;
- origin, governance, research, execution and public Palacete status wording;
- direct seller-page markers and exact `noindex` posture;
- absence of transaction terms from the public Palacete page;
- continued exclusion of the direct page from the public sitemap;
- internal route presence;
- CSS and JavaScript delivery;
- and the four San Bernardo historical/concept WebP assets.

Publication, source validity and public HTTP delivery remain distinct states. The page may be described as live and ready only after the post-merge public-edge job succeeds.
