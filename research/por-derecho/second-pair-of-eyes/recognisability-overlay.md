# Por Derecho — The Second Pair of Eyes
## Evidential recognisability / relationship-preservation overlay

**Control date:** 21 August 2026  
**Status:** mandatory cross-cutting overlay to `case-application-standard.md`; no change to the six canonical checks.

## 1. Locked method remains unchanged

The canonical sequence remains:

`Source → Authority → Perimeter → Contradiction → Consequence → Reversibility`

This overlay does not create a seventh check and does not create a new accusation category.

Its purpose is to detect whether a materially connected issue becomes misleading or unintelligible when facts are divided among separate files, institutions, legal persons, remedies or time periods.

## 2. Core rule

**Separate responsibility. Integrate evidence.**

A reviewer must preserve legal-person, competence, procedural and actor boundaries while also preserving any source-backed relationship necessary to understand the evidence fairly.

The method must be able to say both:

- `these matters must be decided separately`; and
- `these facts must still be read together for this limited purpose`.

## 3. Mandatory trigger

Run this overlay whenever a checkpoint materially spans at least two of:

- proceedings;
- institutions;
- legal persons;
- assets/fincas;
- professional functions;
- time periods;
- evidential bundles;
- remedy types;
- public/private presentations;
- ownership / possession / exploitation / funding perimeters.

## 4. Overlay fields

Every triggered checkpoint should add:

- `recognisability_status`: `preserved | degraded | unresolved | not_material`;
- `relationship_id`: canonical `REL-YYYY-NNN` where allocated;
- `relationship_to_preserve`;
- `fragmentation_point`;
- `reason_for_separation`;
- `minimum_context_required`;
- `forum_holding_fragment_a`;
- `forum_holding_fragment_b`;
- `forum_or_record_holding_both`;
- `material_effect_if_relationship_lost`;
- `strongest_benign_explanation`;
- `finite_restoration_step`;
- `competent_human_gate`;
- `what_would_change_the_view`.

## 5. How the overlay maps to the six checks

### Source
Identify not only the source for each proposition, but the source for the claimed relationship. Repetition is not an independent relationship source.

### Authority
Separate authority to create a document, authority to rely on it, authority to decide the resulting issue and authority to transfer or certify related material.

### Perimeter
Ask whether narrowing by person, asset, proceeding, date or remedy is legally correct but evidentially destructive. Record the smallest context needed to prevent that loss.

### Contradiction
Test whether apparent conflict disappears when perimeters are correctly separated, or appears only when two otherwise separate records are compared.

### Consequence
Do not infer prejudice. Identify the concrete decision, delay, burden, loss of remedy, verification failure or other consequence that may depend on the missing relationship.

### Reversibility
Prefer finite repair: a cross-reference, certified source, shared-fact schedule, annex, institutional handoff, record correction, chronology or relationship edge rather than a demand that one body decide the whole matter.

## 6. Classification

Use the canonical protocol classifications:

- `F0` — no material fragmentation;
- `F1` — lawful / ordinary compartmentalisation;
- `F2` — consequential procedural atomisation;
- `F3` — transmission / notification / record fragmentation;
- `F4` — strategic private fragmentation hypothesis;
- `F5` — possible coordinated fragmentation requiring investigation.

`F5` requires actor-specific coordination evidence. Similar outcomes, institutional proximity, adverse decisions, shared professional networks or economic interest are insufficient.

## 7. Required closure capability

The overlay must be capable of clearing the concern.

Close or downgrade a fragmentation alert where evidence establishes, for example, that:

- the relevant relationship was already before the decision-maker;
- the supposedly conflicting records concern genuinely different perimeters;
- separation was legally required and did not remove material context;
- the alleged downstream act did not rely on the missing fragment;
- a later independent verification cured the dependency;
- the claimed relationship itself is unsupported.

Residual insinuation after a concern has been reconciled is a method failure.

## 8. Breadth-paradox control

When a submission is criticised as too broad, cumulative, transversal or outside competence, do not respond automatically by preserving the same broad package.

Instead:

1. identify the recipient's lawful competence;
2. isolate the finite question within that competence;
3. preserve the one or two cross-file relationships necessary to understand it;
4. exclude material the recipient genuinely need not decide;
5. state what another institution must decide separately;
6. preserve a canonical relationship map outside the individual proceeding.

This is the operational answer to the breadth paradox:

> **The whole can be too broad to investigate; the pieces can be too small to reveal the whole.**

## 9. Public-output discipline

Public pages should normally display:

- the neutral relationship;
- source status;
- why it matters;
- the competence boundary;
- what remains unresolved;
- what would change the view.

Do not publish protected source material merely to prove that a relationship exists. Use public-safe proposition and relationship identifiers and re-query private sources where lawful access is required.

## 10. Human gate

No recognisability state decides illegality, misconduct, guilt, credibility, sanction or liability.

A human reviewer must determine whether the preserved relationship is relevant and what lawful procedural consequence follows.

The method succeeds when it makes a fragmented record **more understandable without making it more accusatory**.
