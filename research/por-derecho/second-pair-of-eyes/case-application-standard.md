# Por Derecho — El Segundo Par de Ojos
## Case Application & Institutional Collaboration Standard v1.0

**Control date:** 19 August 2026  
**Status:** public-interest methodology; human-controlled; no institutional adoption implied.

## Purpose

This standard governs how Por Derecho may apply *El Segundo Par de Ojos* to a live matter or offer it as a synthetic institutional pilot without turning the method into an accusation engine, a shadow appellate body or an automated decision-maker.

## Core sequence

Every material issue must pass through the same six checks:

1. **Source** — what document, version or primary material supports the proposition?
2. **Authority** — who had legal or professional power to act at the relevant date?
3. **Perimeter** — what person, asset, duty, file and time period are actually reached?
4. **Contradiction** — what reliable material or reasonable explanation points the other way?
5. **Consequence** — what legally or professionally relevant effect follows if the proposition is established?
6. **Reversibility** — what evidence, correction or later remedy could narrow, resolve or reverse the concern?

## Mandatory case-application fields

No live-case checkpoint is complete unless it records:

- neutral proposition;
- source and exact evidential state;
- actor and capacity;
- date / time perimeter;
- competent authority;
- strongest contrary explanation;
- respondent / affected-person position or explicit "not located in reviewed corpus" state;
- material unknowns;
- holder of each missing source where known;
- lawful route to obtain or certify the missing information;
- consequence if the information remains unavailable;
- what would change the assessment;
- next human decision gate;
- public/privacy classification.

## Evidence states

`referenced → received → registered → deduplicated → assigned → reviewed → transferred → contradicted → verified → considered → used / discarded with reason`

The following separations are mandatory:

- registered ≠ reviewed;
- reviewed ≠ verified;
- verified ≠ sufficient;
- sufficient ≠ culpability;
- continuing consequence ≠ continuing professional misconduct;
- context from another case ≠ evidence in this case without a human relevance decision.

## Application ladder

### 1. Reference implementation
A real public case is used to demonstrate the methodology. The method must display uncertainty, counter-evidence and institutional limits. No official adoption is claimed.

### 2. Next live-case application
A second real case is mapped only after the same rules are fixed in advance. This tests whether the method is reusable rather than engineered to one factual pattern.

### 3. Synthetic institutional pilot
An institution can test the workflow using a fictional or anonymised scenario. No confidential live file, personal data or production decision system is required.

### 4. Institutional adoption
This state may only be recorded after the institution itself has expressly adopted, commissioned, piloted or otherwise confirmed use. Readiness must never be described as adoption.

## Collaboration modes

- **Observe** — inspect the public method without participation.
- **Correct** — identify factual, procedural or competence errors for versioned correction.
- **Red-team** — challenge assumptions, false positives and missing contrary evidence.
- **Synthetic pilot** — use a fictional/anonymised problem to test the workflow.
- **Live-case method application** — Por Derecho applies the fixed rules publicly to a real case, subject to privacy and due-process safeguards.
- **Institutional pilot** — the institution tests the method under its own governance; adoption is not implied until expressly confirmed.

## Institutional response protocol

A professional body, respondent, complainant or observer should be able to improve the model by supplying one of four response types:

1. **Correction** — a proposition, date, attribution or source is wrong.
2. **Completion** — a missing procedural or evidential state can be authoritatively filled.
3. **Contradiction** — reliable evidence or reasoning points the other way.
4. **Competence objection** — the question belongs to another authority or lies outside the relevant professional standard.

Every accepted correction must preserve the prior version, correction date, reason and source.

## Non-negotiable safeguards

- AI supports; humans decide.
- No guilt, credibility or honesty scores.
- Silence is never admission.
- No publication of unnecessary personal data or protected material.
- No role substitution between preliminary review, instruction and resolution.
- No cross-case evidence reuse without an explicit human relevance decision.
- No automatic inference from association, appearance or professional proximity to conflict or misconduct.
- Every significant concern must have a path to resolution or reversal.
- Every consequential branch ends at a competent human gate.

## Success test

The method succeeds when a neutral reader can answer:

1. What is known?
2. What is alleged?
3. What remains unknown?
4. What contradicts the concern?
5. Who can decide?
6. What would change the view?
7. What human procedural act is needed next?

The method fails if it merely makes an accusation look more sophisticated.
