# Por Derecho — Foundation stage two

**Control date:** 20 August 2026  
**Repository status:** working instruments prepared for review  
**Public status:** Por Derecho remains an **initiative in formation**

## Purpose

This directory controls the second institutional stage of Por Derecho after the public methodology demonstrator:

1. a formation and governance working draft;
2. an independent red-team review protocol for the synthetic method; and
3. a San Bernardo 27 preservation and institutional-use brief kept strictly separate from acquisition diligence.

These instruments are designed to be criticised and replaced by valid professional instruments. They are not executed statutes, adopted policies, legal opinions, architectural surveys, review findings or institutional approvals.

## Mandatory boundaries

- **The founder’s experience may originate questions but may not control conclusions.**
- **The Foundation, if formed, may not be used to finance or conduct the founder’s personal litigation without an independently authorised, lawful and segregated public-interest basis.**
- **A related matter requires declaration, information quarantine, recusal, an independent lead and a reasoned record.**
- **A donor, owner, seller, funder or collaborator receives no influence over methods, appointments or findings.**
- **The house is not the Foundation.** Por Derecho must remain capable of operating without San Bernardo.
- **Preservation is not acquisition diligence.** No public brief establishes title, condition, price, funding, permissions or authority to inspect or intervene.
- **Synthetic testing comes before controlled real-case use.** Passing a synthetic review is not institutional adoption.
- **No guilt, credibility or person-risk scoring.** The method tests records, authority, perimeter, contradiction, consequence, reversibility and human decision controls.

## Review gates before legal formation

1. Spanish foundation-law advice on legal form, territorial scope, purposes and competent Protectorate.
2. Notarial and registry review of the proposed statutes and formation mechanics.
3. Tax, accounting, employment, insurance, data-protection and technology advice.
4. Independent trustee identification, diligence and written acceptance of real duties.
5. A documented first action plan, budget, risk register and external-review timetable.
6. Exact public status change only after the relevant legal acts are completed and evidenced.

## Review gates before any real or institutional method deployment

1. independent reviewer conflict declaration and mandate;
2. pre-registered synthetic cases, criteria, thresholds and stop conditions;
3. reproducible results including false positives and cleared concerns;
4. publication of limitations, dissent and Por Derecho’s response without rewriting the independent report;
5. redesign and retest of material weaknesses;
6. separate lawful-basis, privacy, competence, ownership and human-challenge assessment for any real application;
7. adoption language only after the institution itself confirms it.

## Review gates before any San Bernardo survey or intervention

1. legally sufficient authority and access permission;
2. acquisition completion or another documented mandate sufficient for the proposed activity;
3. architect/conservation, structural, services, fire, accessibility, planning and heritage scopes agreed by competent professionals;
4. privacy, archive-rights, occupant, neighbour and security controls;
5. no intrusive investigation without specific consent, method statement and insurance;
6. no project or works representation before required professional and public approvals.
