# Por Derecho — formation and governance working draft

**Date:** 20 August 2026  
**Status:** working draft for specialist legal and institutional review  
**Not an executed instrument:** this text is not a deed, statute, Board resolution, legal opinion, registration application or binding policy.

## 1. Intended public-benefit purpose

Por Derecho is intended to advance public-benefit work at the intersection of:

- preventive procedural integrity;
- documentary provenance and traceability;
- access to justice and effective protection;
- research and education concerning high-responsibility legal and administrative decisions;
- responsible use of artificial intelligence and other technology;
- institutional learning, correction and inter-authority traceability;
- preservation of legal and institutional memory.

The final purposes must be drafted and validated under the applicable Spanish foundation framework. The wording must not imply that Por Derecho is a court, prosecution service, disciplinary body, ombudsman, public authority, law firm, litigation funder or automated decision-maker.

## 2. Formation questions requiring counsel determination

Before an instrument is executed, specialist advice should determine at least:

1. the appropriate legal form;
2. the territorial scope and competent Protectorate;
3. the compatibility and precision of the proposed purposes;
4. the identity and capacity of founders;
5. the form, sufficiency, valuation and availability of the initial endowment;
6. the proposed registered office and any property-use arrangement;
7. the minimum and recommended composition of the Board of Trustees;
8. appointment, acceptance, term, removal, replacement and representation mechanics;
9. delegated authority and reserved matters;
10. annual action-plan, accounting, audit, filing, transparency and tax duties;
11. treatment of restricted funds, donations, grants, contracts and public money;
12. data-protection, information-security, employment, volunteer, insurance and health-and-safety duties;
13. modification, merger, extinction and destination-of-assets rules;
14. any additional Canary Islands, national, heritage, educational or technology-specific requirements.

Until these questions are professionally determined and the legal steps are evidenced, the public designation remains **“Por Derecho — initiative in formation.”**

## 3. Proposed governance architecture

### 3.1 Board of Trustees / Patronato

Proposed reserved responsibilities:

- protect the purposes and public-benefit character;
- approve strategy, annual action plan, budget and accounts;
- appoint and supervise executive leadership;
- approve risk appetite, internal-control framework and insurance;
- approve material contracts, borrowings, restricted funding and property decisions;
- approve the independence, conflicts, publication, data, security and method charters;
- receive direct reports on conflicts, retaliation, safeguarding and incidents;
- commission independent financial, legal, data, security and method review;
- approve or decline any controlled real-matter or institutional pilot;
- preserve dissent and reasons in minutes;
- ensure statutory and regulatory filings.

Proposed independence controls:

- a composition capable of deliberating without founder dependence;
- a skills matrix covering law, governance, finance, public administration, technology, data, education and civil society;
- written declarations of interests on appointment and at every relevant meeting;
- fixed or otherwise controlled terms and succession planning;
- a mechanism for meetings without the founder or executive present;
- minutes recording recusals, dissent and evidence relied upon;
- external advice available directly to non-conflicted trustees.

### 3.2 Conflicts and Integrity Committee

Proposed remit:

- maintain the interests register;
- classify founder-, trustee-, donor-, property- and collaborator-related matters;
- determine quarantine, access and recusal requirements;
- examine gifts, restricted funding and donor conditions;
- oversee protected reporting and anti-retaliation controls;
- approve exceptions to information barriers;
- review public status language for related matters;
- report directly to the Board.

This committee must not become an advocate for a connected person or a substitute disciplinary tribunal.

### 3.3 Scientific and Method Council

Proposed remit:

- approve research questions and synthetic benchmark design;
- scrutinise provenance, competence, timing, perimeter, contradiction and reversibility;
- commission red-team and false-positive testing;
- review AI, data, bias, security and accessibility controls;
- publish limitations and dissent;
- advise on training standards;
- recommend redesign, retest or no deployment.

It must not attribute legal guilt, determine credibility, decide litigation or issue generic certification beyond the scope actually tested.

### 3.4 Executive function

Proposed responsibilities:

- implement the approved plan and budget;
- manage people, contracts, procurement and operations;
- maintain role-based access and segregated records;
- operate the correction, incident and risk registers;
- ensure public statements match documented status;
- escalate material conflicts, incidents, regulatory issues and control failures;
- preserve auditable decision records.

Delegation should be written, limited, reviewable and subject to reserved matters.

### 3.5 External assurance

Potential assurance lines, selected by risk and not treated as interchangeable:

- annual accounts and financial controls;
- foundation-law and regulatory compliance;
- data protection and information security;
- research integrity and method performance;
- AI and human-factors review;
- safeguarding and protected reporting;
- property, heritage, fire, accessibility and health and safety;
- impact and beneficiary evaluation.

Every external review should state its exact scope, version, evidence, conflicts, remuneration, limitations and expiry.

## 4. Founder-related matter protocol

A matter is potentially related when a founder, trustee, officer, substantial donor, property counterparty, close family member or controlled entity has a personal, financial, professional, litigation or reputational interest that could reasonably be perceived to affect impartiality.

### 4.1 Required sequence

1. **Declare** the interest before the material decision.
2. **Classify** directness, materiality, duration, affected decisions and affected information.
3. **Quarantine** records, access, funding, advice, communications and personnel.
4. **Recuse** the connected person from framing, selecting, deciding and voting on the merits.
5. **Appoint** an independent lead under a written mandate and conflict check.
6. **Test** the strongest case supporting the concern and the strongest lawful contrary explanation.
7. **Record** evidence, unknowns, competence, proportionality, reversibility, dissent and what would change the view.
8. **Review** at a stated date or when a specified evidence event occurs.
9. **Publish only if authorised**, necessary and safe, with status and conflict visible.

### 4.2 Participation by the connected person

The connected person may:

- identify the issue;
- preserve and submit evidence;
- explain personal knowledge;
- identify potential harm;
- respond to adverse material;
- propose questions and corrections.

The connected person may not:

- select or dismiss the independent reviewer unilaterally;
- direct the conclusion;
- suppress a lawful contrary explanation;
- use a veto over correction or closure;
- access quarantined material without documented authority;
- describe a personal litigation expense as Foundation activity;
- use Foundation status to imply institutional endorsement of a personal position.

### 4.3 DIP 79/2026 and DIP 80/2026 status

These matters are experimental founder-related research applications. They may be used to test traceability, evidence states, timing, competence, contradiction and human gates under transparent conflict controls.

They are not:

- independent validation;
- ICALPA adoption or endorsement;
- an institutional pilot authorised by ICALPA;
- a disciplinary conclusion;
- a basis for transferring evidence from one matter to the other without a human relevance and provenance decision.

## 5. Independence and funding charter — proposed clauses

1. No donor, grantor, lender, seller, owner, sponsor or collaborator may purchase or condition a conclusion.
2. Restrictions on funds must be lawful, documented, compatible with the purposes and incapable of controlling persons examined, evidence considered or findings published.
3. Material donor identity, conditions and related interests should be disclosed to the competent governance body and, where appropriate and lawful, publicly.
4. Related-matter funding must be segregated and independently authorised.
5. Personal litigation, acquisition or commercial expenditure must not be presented as charitable or Foundation expenditure without a documented lawful public-benefit basis and independent approval.
6. Gifts, hospitality and benefits are subject to thresholds, declaration and refusal rules.
7. Restricted access, embargo and confidentiality must not be used to conceal a material conflict or control failure from the competent governance body.
8. Funding concentration risk must be monitored; exit plans should prevent a funder from obtaining de facto control.
9. Naming rights, appointments and property arrangements must preserve method and governance independence.
10. Any exception requires a written, time-limited, reviewable decision by non-conflicted people.

## 6. Method and publication charter — proposed clauses

Every consequential method output should identify, as applicable:

- the question being asked;
- the native source and version;
- the event date and knowledge date;
- the person or institution with competence;
- the legal and factual perimeter;
- the strongest contrary evidence or explanation;
- material unknowns;
- consequence and reversibility;
- the human decision-maker;
- the decision, reasons, date and review trigger.

Mandatory state distinctions:

- unknown;
- alleged;
- documented;
- independently verified;
- reconciled;
- unresolved;
- sufficient for a stated purpose;
- outside competence.

Prohibited or controlled outputs:

- guilt scores;
- credibility scores;
- person-risk scores;
- automatic referral, sanction, suspension, filing or adjudication;
- silence-as-admission;
- undisclosed cross-case evidence reuse;
- later evidence presented as earlier knowledge;
- institutional adoption claimed from preparation, receipt or access;
- `LIVE_VERIFIED` used as proof of the underlying merits rather than technical publication status.

Corrections should preserve:

- prior wording;
- replacement wording;
- date;
- reason;
- source;
- approving role;
- effect on dependent conclusions.

## 7. Data, privacy and security framework

Before processing personal or confidential material, Por Derecho should establish:

- purpose and lawful-basis mapping;
- data minimisation and collection controls;
- information classification;
- role-based and matter-based access;
- encryption in transit and at rest;
- key and credential management;
- vendor diligence and processing terms;
- retention and deletion schedules;
- protected-source and privilege handling;
- incident detection, response and notification;
- rights-request procedures;
- secure development and change control;
- backup, restoration and off-platform preservation;
- audit logs protected against silent alteration;
- impact assessment where required;
- safe withdrawal and human fallback.

No real-matter pilot should begin without a named controller/owner, lawful basis, minimised dataset, access model, incident route and human challenge mechanism.

## 8. Property and San Bernardo independence

Proposed controlling clause:

> Any property used by Por Derecho serves the purposes but does not constitute, control or define the institution. Ownership, occupation, funding, preservation, governance and method remain legally and operationally distinguishable.

Consequences:

- Por Derecho must be capable of operating without San Bernardo 27.
- Acquisition status must never be inflated.
- A seller, funder, owner or donor acquires no influence over appointments, methods, findings or proceedings.
- Public preservation material remains separate from private acquisition, financing and diligence files.
- No survey, access, investigation or intervention occurs without sufficient authority.
- Property costs, benefits, related-party interests and restrictions receive independent approval and accounting treatment.
- A conservation plan, operational plan and exit/continuity plan are required before reliance on the property becomes mission-critical.

## 9. Proposed first-year Board calendar after lawful formation

### First meeting

- verify formation and registrations;
- accept duties and declarations;
- approve reserved matters and delegations;
- adopt interim conflicts, finance, data, security, publication and safeguarding controls;
- approve bank, accounting, insurance and records arrangements;
- approve a limited first action plan.

### First 90 days

- complete risk and stakeholder registers;
- appoint independent advisers and committee members where appropriate;
- approve the synthetic red-team mandate;
- complete data and security baseline;
- approve correction and incident processes;
- determine property separation and authority controls;
- publish an exact governance and status statement.

### First year

- complete external method review;
- publish accepted and rejected recommendations;
- approve any controlled real-matter test only after independent gates;
- complete financial and compliance assurance;
- review funding concentration and conflicts;
- assess impact against the stated public-benefit theory;
- renew or stop activities that do not demonstrate lawful public benefit.
