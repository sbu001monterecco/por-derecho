## PP 1041 completion rule

Do not close GAP-P0-011 because the 5 March decree exists. The decree records a withdrawal request in LPB's name but does not supply the filing, author, authority, LexNET chain, Article 51.2 authorisation or authentic LPB intention.

Do not close GAP-P0-012 merely because the sequence objectively benefited CAM. De facto functional agency and individual involvement require communications, instructions, knowledge, conflict, ratification or benefit evidence actor by actor.

## General completion rule

A gap is not closed by locating a summary, later recital or email reference where the native deed, schedule, decision, filing, authorisation or ledger is required.
