## Completion rule

A gap is not closed by locating a summary, later recital or email reference where the native deed, schedule, decision or ledger is required.

An authentic notarial instrument may contain a disputed party recital without the instrument itself, a signature or the notary's conduct being false. “Split credit”, “multiple lives” and “NPL fraud” are not standalone offences. The evidence package must therefore prove or disprove the exact actor, document/data, use, knowledge, reliance, economic duplication and legally relevant intent rather than infer criminality from discounted acquisition, later recovery or the same credit appearing in more than one proceeding.
