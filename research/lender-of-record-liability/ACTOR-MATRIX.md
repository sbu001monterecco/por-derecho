## PP 1041 actor rule

The actor matrix must not collapse the procedural attribution “in LPB's name” into an authentic LPB decision. It must identify separately:

- nominal party;
- office-holder with insolvency-derived power;
- lawyer removed;
- procurator and filing channel;
- drafter, signer and filer;
- CAM objection and non-opposition;
- individual decision-makers or beneficiaries;
- authorising court and statutory basis.

Por Derecho's allegation is that the Administrator acted as a de facto functional agent for CAM/Acosta Matos and that JDAM and LPAM belong within the decision/benefit perimeter to investigate. That allegation is neither suppressed nor recorded as a final finding.

## Relationship controls

### Assignment is not universal succession

A credit assignment is analysed by the content of the assigned right, accessory rights, defences, warranties and each actor's own conduct. CAM→HNT is separately analysed as a corporate segregation stated to operate by universal succession.

### Servicing is not ownership

A servicer may communicate or act for a holder without owning the credit. Principal responsibility, direct servicer responsibility, acts outside authority and ratification are separate questions.

### Corporate group or family/perimeter association is not one legal person

Common brand, investment perimeter, family connection or commercial benefit does not remove separate personality or prove individual conduct. Control, instruction, knowledge, ratification, abuse, commingling or the elements of any exceptional veil-piercing or participation route must be proved.
