## Relationship controls

### Assignment is not universal succession

A credit assignment is analysed by the content of the assigned right, accessory rights, defences, warranties and each actor's own conduct. CAM→HNT is separately analysed as a corporate segregation stated to operate by universal succession.

### Servicing is not ownership

A servicer may communicate or act for a holder without owning the credit. Principal responsibility, direct servicer responsibility, acts outside authority and ratification are separate questions.

### Corporate group is not one legal person

Common brand, investment perimeter or commercial benefit does not remove separate personality. Control, instruction, abuse, commingling or the elements of any exceptional veil-piercing route must be proved.
