# PH122 / Cerberus / Haya / “Bankia external” perimeter

Updated: 20 August 2026.

## Naming control

The controlled primary and repository record supports **Promontoria Holding 122 B.V. / PH122**. It does not presently support “PH11” as the relevant holder. PH122 is therefore the canonical identifier unless a native transfer instrument proves another entity.

“**Bankia external**” is retained only as a forensic working label for the upstream Bankia/originator lane outside PH122's later asset ownership. It is not presented as a company, statutory category or finding of common legal personality.

## Strongest supported conclusion

> The PH122/Cerberus/Haya perimeter was not a passive servicing inbox. The primary correspondence shows a functioning debtor-facing decision channel: proposals were analysed against the debt holder's criteria, rejected by “the creditor,” and routed through named Cerberus and Bankia interlocutors. What remains concealed by the missing mandate and committee documents is the exact allocation of principal, authority, decision-maker, reasons, information and responsibility.

Separate legal personality remains the starting rule. Forensic perimeter treatment is justified because the same communications connect:

- PH122 as documented holder of the mortgage credits;
- Haya as the debtor-facing servicing, information and decision-communication channel;
- Cerberus as the investment/decision context expressly invoked in the correspondence;
- Bankia as a separately routed upstream/originator and residual-credit lane.

## Primary correspondence chronology

### 8 April 2015 — the proposal reached a decision forum

Aitor Cohrs of Haya stated that the proposal had been analysed “during the weekly conference.” He communicated that a one-year standstill did not fit the debt holder's criteria and requested greater detail on the remaining options, including amounts, timetables and proposed investors.

This proves more than receipt. It proves entry into a creditor-side evaluative channel. It does not by itself identify the meeting participants, principal, committee authority, materials considered or reasons.

### 18 May 2016 — creditor rejection communicated through Haya

After Cuatrecasas submitted a proposal described as intended for Haya or Cerberus, Haya initially required execution by the borrower and provided the Haya delivery address. The following day Haya communicated that the terms had been rejected “by the creditor” and that an executed version was no longer required.

This proves that a rejection decision existed and was communicated through Haya. It leaves open:

- the legal identity of “the creditor” for that decision;
- who made or approved the decision;
- the mandate under which Haya communicated it;
- the comparison with refinancing, recapitalisation or sale alternatives;
- whether a revised route was genuinely available.

### 11–19 May 2017 — one interface, two legally distinct lanes

LPB sent Haya the three-credit architecture:

- Bankia current-account/SWAP items;
- PH122 mortgage 2801, stated at €857,373.81;
- PH122 mortgage 30000, stated at €8,194,877.88;
- prior Bankia and PH122/Cerberus offers;
- the 2017 convenio and viability plan;
- a stated UK proposal involving €5 million immediate payment plus terms for the balance.

Haya replied that Aitor Cohrs was the appropriate Cerberus interlocutor, also named Montserrat Alvarez, and said it would separately provide the Bankia contact.

This correspondence is important because it both connects and separates the lanes. Haya was an operational interface through which the Cerberus/PH122 and Bankia issues were routed, but the credits and legal capacities were not identical.

## “Bankia external” lane

The upstream Bankia perimeter must retain its own ledger and causes of action:

- original mortgage and refinancing terms;
- SWAP or hedging product;
- pledged deposit or funds;
- current-account treatment;
- payments, settlements and charges;
- default and acceleration;
- enforcement instructions and conduct;
- transfer to SAREB;
- obligations and liabilities retained after transfer;
- CaixaBank/Bankia successor questions, including the Valencia proceeding.

Nothing in the PH122 assignment automatically moves every Bankia personal obligation downstream. Equally, the downstream credit is not a newly invented asset: its amount and defences depend on the originating record.

## Strongest attribution rule

The absence of the servicing agreement cannot be used to erase the documented conduct. It means attribution must proceed in stages:

1. Haya's own communications and acts are attributed to Haya.
2. PH122's holder status and benefit are attributed to PH122.
3. Cerberus-level control is attributed only when mandate, ownership, committee, instruction, ratification or benefit evidence proves it.
4. Bankia remains responsible for its own upstream conduct and any obligations not transferred.
5. CAM is tested for what it knew at acquisition and for its own subsequent conduct.

## Strongest public formulation

> By April 2015 the LPB workout was being analysed in a creditor-side weekly forum against the debt holder's criteria. In May 2016 Haya communicated that “the creditor” had rejected the proposal. In May 2017 Haya identified the Cerberus interlocutors and separately undertook to provide the Bankia contact. The evidential issue is no longer whether the proposals reached the creditor perimeter. They did. The issue is who decided, under what authority, on what information, for what reason and with what foreseeable effect on LPB's ability to refinance, exit insolvency or preserve estate value.
