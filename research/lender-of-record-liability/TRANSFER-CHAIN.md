# Transfer and succession chain

## Two connected lanes that must not be collapsed

| Lane | Controlled chain | Question |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate/product succession | Caja Insular de Ahorros de Canarias / La Caja de Canarias → BFA → Bankia → CaixaBank | Which origination, account, swap, pledged-funds, acceleration and personal obligations were retained or succeeded to? |
| Mortgage-asset ownership | Bankia → SAREB → Promontoria Holding 122 B.V. (PH122) → CAM | Who held or asserted the mortgage credit and security at each date, with what balance, schedule, economics and defences? |

CaixaBank's corporate succession from Bankia does not mean that CaixaBank held the mortgage asset after the SAREB transfer. Equally, the downstream mortgage asset was not a newly invented credit: its lawful amount and defences depend on the originating record. Haya is not a holder. Cerberus remains a sponsor/investment/decision context until a specific ownership, control, mandate, instruction or ratification link is proved.

## Required transfer record

Every transfer must eventually answer:

`transferor → transferee → date → legal form → instrument → assets/rights → accessories → balance → consideration → allocated price → payment → warranties → disclosures → servicing → debtor notice → proceedings → liabilities transferred/retained → later conduct`

## No “clean slate” assumption

An assignee does not receive a newly invented credit. The assigned asset must be reconstructed from the actual originating transaction, payments, defences, judgments and corrections.

## No “automatic inherited tort” assumption

The separate question is whether the assignee:

- assumed a liability contractually or by law;
- is affected by a defect/defence attached to the credit;
- incurred direct liability through its own conduct;
- is responsible for an authorised/dependent servicer;
- benefited from or ratified conduct;
- later transferred an economic unit by universal succession.
