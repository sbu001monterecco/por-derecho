# Transfer and succession chain

| Transfer | What is currently supported | What may travel | What does not automatically travel | P0 uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bankia/originator → SAREB (2013) | SAREB is identified as later holder | assigned credit and included accessories, subject to instrument/law | every personal contractual/tort obligation of the prior lender | complete instrument, schedule, linked products, retained obligations |
| SAREB → PH122 (2014 perimeter) | PH122 later held the loans; LPB sought LSAP and price | credit, security and defences affecting the credit as legally applicable | automatic parent/servicer liability | LSAP, schedules, allocated price, warranties, servicing |
| PH122 → CAM (20 Oct 2017) | assignment identified; later judicial substitution; prior/later dispute documented | acquired creditor position and accessories, subject to defences/rulings | all PH122/Haya/Cerberus historic personal liability | full protocol 2,248, price/payment, DD, warranties, loan balances |
| CAM → HNT (2022) | official notice states segregation by universal succession of the Sun Park economic unit | assets and liabilities legally included in the unit, subject to temporal law and deed | every CAM obligation regardless of scope, or automatic cure of antecedent defects | project, deed, balance, asset/passive/litigation schedules, retained liabilities |

## Required transfer record

Every transfer must eventually answer:

`transferor → transferee → date → legal form → instrument → assets/rights → accessories → balance → consideration → allocated price → payment → warranties → disclosures → servicing → debtor notice → proceedings → liabilities transferred/retained → later conduct`

## No “clean slate” assumption

An assignee does not receive a newly invented credit. The assigned asset must be reconstructed from the actual originating transaction, payments, defences, judgments and corrections.

## No “automatic inherited tort” assumption

The separate question is whether the assignee:

- assumed a liability contractually or by law;
- is affected by a defect/defence attached to the credit;
- incurred direct liability through its own conduct;
- is responsible for an authorised/dependent servicer;
- benefited from or ratified conduct;
- later transferred an economic unit by universal succession.
