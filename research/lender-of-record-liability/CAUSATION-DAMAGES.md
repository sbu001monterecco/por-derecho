# Causation, foreseeability and damages

## Two distinct controls

### Contractual lane

For a good-faith contractual debtor, the analysis must test whether the loss was foreseen or foreseeable when the obligation arose and a necessary consequence of the breach, together with actual loss and lost profit. Intentional misconduct may alter scope, but must be proved.

### Extra-contractual / office-holder lane

Do not translate English “remoteness” mechanically. Test:

`material causation → legal/objective attribution → scope of risk → intervening causes → victim/concurrent conduct → mitigation → incremental damage`

For the Insolvency Administrator, add:

`statutory power → duty to LPB/estate → information before act → authority/conflict → act or omission → estate/private benefit → immediate procedural consequence → downstream loss`

## PP 1041 causal sequence

The controlled sequence is:

1. the Court ordered CAM to disclose the PH122→CAM deed, price, payment date and costs;
2. CAM invoked the need for Insolvency Administration conformity;
3. the Administrator removed the lawyer who had obtained the order and controlled future procurator filings;
4. a withdrawal was filed in LPB's name;
5. CAM did not oppose;
6. the Court ended PP 1041;
7. the ordered information was not produced through that route.

This supports direct causation of the **loss of the existing information route**. It does not by itself establish that Article 1535 would have succeeded, that financing was certain or that every later Sun Park loss followed from the withdrawal.

Por Derecho alleges that the filing was false, unauthorised and deliberately harmful and formed part of de facto functional agency for CAM/Acosta Matos. Intent and common purpose remain to be proved.

## Three damage rings

| Ring | Heads of loss | Present treatment |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — proximal | loss of the PP 1041 disclosure order/opportunity; unnecessary procedural and corrective costs; asset/equity diminution; identifiable surplus; rents/fruits during a proven wrongful period; direct physical damage | strongest proximity; still requires authority, title, amount, period and counterfactual proof |
| 2 — foreseeable counterfactual | lost debt reduction/redemption; lost refinancing; lost convenio exit; identifiable hotel-operation period; operator agreement; financing costs | potentially foreseeable because the information sought concerned the credit's acquisition economics and active exit routes; requires legal availability, financeability, probability and causal proof |
| 3 — remote/high-proof | long-term platform, brand expansion, future developments, generic reputation/capital raising, wide multi-year projections | reserve; do not lead quantum with these claims |

## Actor-specific loss windows

| Actor | Earliest responsible window to test | Incremental question |
|---|---|---|
| originator/Bankia | origination/account/default/enforcement period | what loss followed from its own contract/account/enforcement conduct? |
| SAREB | period of its actual holding and decisions | what own conduct, if any, added harm beyond inherited position? |
| PH122/Haya | after documented 2015/2017 knowledge and relevant decisions | what financeable workout or estate value was lost because of an attributable decision? |
| CAM | from 20 Oct 2017 acquisition and later direct notice/conduct; specifically its January 2018 PP 1041 objection | what additional harm followed from acquisition, opposition to disclosure, proposal, valuation, enforcement, possession/title or income conduct? |
| Insolvency Administrator | at least from the 25 January 2018 representation intervention for PP 1041; earlier/later office acts separately | what procedural right, information, exit probability, estate value or surplus was lost because powers entrusted for LPB were used or omitted in a particular way? |
| JDAM / LPAM | only from any proved personal instruction, knowledge, ratification, benefit or own act | what personal causal contribution, if any, is established rather than inferred from perimeter association? |
| HNT | from legally effective succession and own conduct | what liability followed the unit and what new harm arose afterwards? |

## PP 1041 counterfactual ladder

Do not jump directly from the withdrawal filing to full hotel loss. Prove each bridge:

1. **Information bridge:** what the deed, price, payment and costs would have shown.
2. **Legal bridge:** which debt-reduction, redemption, accounting, negotiation or challenge route was legally available despite the later adverse Article 1535 order.
3. **Funding bridge:** what funds, lender approvals, conditions and timetable existed.
4. **Insolvency bridge:** how the route could have reduced/extinguished debt or supported convenio/liquidation alternatives.
5. **Asset bridge:** how preserving that route would probably have changed valuation, possession, dación, title or surplus.
6. **Allocation bridge:** what part of the loss is attributable to CAM, the Administrator, upstream actors, judicial decisions, claimant conduct or other causes.

## Counterfactual evidence

A Ring 2 claim needs, where relevant:

- complete PP 1041 disclosure order and missing production;
- withdrawal filing, authority and Article 51.2 record;
- signed or near-final term sheet;
- lender/credit committee approval;
- available funds;
- conditions precedent;
- valuation and security;
- operator/management agreement;
- evidence of reasons for failure/withdrawal;
- probability of completion;
- alternative routes;
- mitigation;
- period and discounting.

## Intervening-cause register

The defence is expected to rely on:

- pre-existing liquidity/default;
- the later adverse Article 1535 order;
- absence of firm redemption finance;
- owner and Community conflict;
- other insolvency-administration decisions;
- judicial orders/finality;
- title/ob-rem complications;
- independent financier committees/conditions;
- operating and market risk;
- claimant decisions and mitigation.

Each is to be tested, not ignored. The objective is not “one actor caused everything,” but a defensible allocation of incremental harm.

## Intentional-harm control

Por Derecho's position is that the PP 1041 act was deliberate and aimed at the detriment of LPB for the functional benefit of CAM/Acosta Matos. If intent is proved, it may affect legal attribution, scope of damage and available remedies. Until then, the repository must classify intent as a formal allegation supported by sequence and objective effect, not as an adjudicated fact.
