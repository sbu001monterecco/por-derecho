# Contradictions and strongest defence case

The architecture must be able to survive the opposing case.

| Our developing proposition | Strongest defence | Evidence that discriminates |
|---|---|---|
| defects/defences affecting the original credit remain relevant downstream | the credit and security were valid, quantified and lawfully assigned; personal originator claims are not opposable | native agreements, judgments, assignment clauses, balances and exact defence |
| originator remained responsible for own conduct | transferred legal position included relevant obligations or claimant lacks standing/limitation | transfer instrument, contract, duty, accrual and party identity |
| PH122/Haya knew a refinancing/workout was active | receipt and discussion did not create a duty, approval or financeable transaction | mandate, committee file, term sheet, approvals, conditions, reasons |
| rejection/stalling destroyed refinancing | financing remained conditional and would have failed for independent reasons | lender approval, funds, CPs, valuations, withdrawal evidence and counterfactual expert |
| CAM had notice and incurred own downstream liability | CAM lawfully bought and exercised a secured credit, relied on AC/court orders, and caused no independent harm | complete DD, communications, valuation instructions, decisions, appeal/finality, incremental-loss evidence |
| CAM's proposal was an unlawful appropriation architecture | it was a genuine post-default negotiation or lawful realisation with objective valuation/surplus protection | proposal/deed, timing, valuation independence, surplus mechanics, alternatives and consent |
| Haya conduct binds PH122/Cerberus | Haya was an independent servicer or acted outside authority; no ratification/control | servicing agreement, PoA, instructions, employer identity and ratification/benefit |
| group actors share liability | separate legal personality controls; group membership is insufficient | control/abuse/commingling facts or abandon group-wide theory |
| HNT inherited CAM liabilities | the disputed obligation was outside the segregated economic unit or retained by CAM | project, deed, balance, passive/litigation schedules and temporal law |
| loss includes full hotel/platform future | loss is remote, speculative and broken by insolvency, court, market and claimant causes | ringed quantum, actor windows, counterfactual proof, mitigation and sensitivity analysis |
| several actors are jointly liable | damage is divisible by actor/time and limitation is separate | causal allocation and indivisibility expert evidence |
| criminal proceedings can restore the asset | no independent offence is proved; civil/registry/finality and third-party protections control | offence elements, causal transfer, holder good faith, interim/final remedy authority |

## Adverse-decision rule

The 15 February 2018 order rejecting LPB's Article 1535 position and recognising CAM is not a footnote. It must appear in every relevant public and internal analysis, together with amount corrections and the appeal/finality gap.

## Exculpatory-material invitation

Each public page must invite complete deeds, schedules, decisions, explanations and corrective/exculpatory material. Corrections are entered in the repository correction register.
