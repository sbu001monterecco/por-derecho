# Credit, security and asset genealogy

The word “loan” is too imprecise for this matter. Each instrument must keep a separate ledger and legal history.

| Instrument | Origin/current issue | Holder chain presently carried | Required reconciliation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Principal mortgage facility | principal asset-backed exposure; acceleration and 2012 enforcement contested | original lender/Bankia → SAREB → PH122 → CAM | native terms, amendments, payments, interest, default, acceleration, each transfer balance, satisfaction |
| Approximately €850k mortgage/refinancing facility | linked to the wider refinancing/security package | original lender/Bankia → SAREB → PH122 → CAM | 2010 agreement, linked costs/products, payments/settlements, pledged funds, transferred balance |
| SWAP / cobertura | separate financial-product lane with possible links to refinancing and account treatment | originator/Bankia lane presently distinct from mortgage-holder chain | origination, disclosure, charges, settlement, whether transferred or retained, Valencia outcome |
| Pledged deposit/funds | possible accessory security or payment source | original lender/Bankia lane | amount, control, application, release and effect on debt |
| Concursal credit | claim recognised/substituted in Concurso 36/2012 | Bankia/SAREB/PH122/CAM sequence to reconcile | definitive texts, corrections, interest/costs, assignment figures, dación/adjudication, residual/sobrante |
| Sun Park economic unit | real estate, hotel business, rights, licences, income and liabilities are not one undifferentiated item | LPB/third-party perimeter → later CAM/HNT routes require property-by-property proof | active/passive schedules, third-party units, possession, operation, income, common elements, licences, liabilities |

## Mandatory ledger fields

For every credit date and transfer:

- original principal;
- ordinary interest;
- default interest;
- charges and linked-product entries;
- payments and settlements;
- pledged funds;
- cure amount;
- accelerated amount;
- recognised concursal amount;
- corrections;
- transfer balance;
- consideration/allocated price;
- adjudication or dación value;
- costs;
- cancellation/satisfaction;
- surplus or residual balance.

A title or satisfaction entry at the end of the chain does not answer whether each antecedent amount or act was correct.
