# Proceeding and remedy matrix

The same fact may have different relevance—and different admissibility or remedy—in different tracks.

| Proceeding/track | Question this architecture can answer | Potential remedy/use | Important limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Valencia 1859/2023-9 banking/product claim | origin, linked products, account treatment, default, acceleration, retained originator liability and causation | declarations, contractual/extracontractual relief as pleaded | does not decide all concursal, title, Community or downstream conduct issues |
| Mortgage Enforcement 90/2012 | amount, acceleration, security, auction schedule and defensive-filing causation | challenge/reconstruction depending on finality and procedure | later credit transfers do not reopen everything automatically |
| Concurso 36/2012 | recognised credit, substitution, estate interest, valuation, disposition, surplus and third-party property | concursal incidents, appeals, accounting/estate remedies as available | historical law, standing, finality and court orders control |
| DP 1041/2017 | deed, price, payment, costs and Article 1535 information route | documentary production and related civil route | 15 Feb 2018 adverse/mixed order must be carried; final outcome required |
| CAM/HNT corporate and registry route | assets/liabilities included in segregation; title and successor position | corporate/registry/civil consequences as legally available | BORME notice alone does not identify passive schedule |
| DP 1901/2026 / criminal route | whether independently criminal conduct caused asset disposition/harm | investigation, preservation, restitution/civil liability ex delicto if proved | no criminal conclusion; pacto comisorio is not itself treated as an offence |
| Article 1535/retracto work | technical litigious-credit status, timing, price and procedural route | narrowly defined civil consequence if requirements met | strict doctrine; adverse order; nine-day mechanics and knowledge trigger |
| AC separation/accountability | what the AC verified, reported and did for estate interest | separation/accounting/professional routes as available | distinguish adverse judgment from proof of misconduct |
| CNMV/RICPE/public-funds tracks | representations concerning ownership, guarantees, finance and works | supervisory/evidential use | regulatory relevance is not automatic lender liability |
| Public evidence record | transparent reconstruction and request for missing material | public accountability and correction | no trial by website; privilege, privacy and presumption controls |

## Cross-track proposition record

For every proposition, record:

- admissible source;
- party with standing;
- defendant/respondent;
- forum competence;
- procedural vehicle;
- limitation;
- interim relief;
- final remedy;
- whether asset return, estate restoration, registry correction or damages are realistically available.

## No universal proceeding assumption

The practical recovery architecture may require coordinated civil, concursal, registry, corporate and criminal-restoration routes. The repository must not promise one “magic button” capable of undoing the entire history.
