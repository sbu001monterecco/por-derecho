# Pacto comisorio / credit-to-title architecture

Updated: 20 August 2026.

## Executive conclusion

The strongest formulation presently supported by the controlled record is not that a classical commissory clause has already been proved. It is that there is a **serious, judicially determinable case of an indirect appropriation architecture**:

> A distressed secured credit was placed inside a sequence of creditor-side decision control, rejection or non-acceptance of alternative exits, transfer to a locally connected acquirer, a disputed valuation, material control before formal title, insolvency-administration gatekeeping, judicial dación and later universal succession—without a complete, reproducible debt-value-surplus account yet being available.

The legal question is therefore not confined to one clause. It is whether the **substance and combined operation** of the steps converted security into ownership while defeating the safeguards that distinguish lawful realisation from prohibited appropriation.

This is the strongest defensible case. It is not yet a final judicial finding of pacto comisorio, fraud, collusion or crime.

## Why this is more than a sequence of lawful-looking documents

The case gains force from the convergence of six proved or seriously evidenced features:

1. **The creditor perimeter received and decided upon workout proposals.** This was not a passive inbox.
2. **The borrower repeatedly proposed payment, refinancing, recapitalisation, bridge funding or sale routes.** The complete committee comparison of those routes has not been produced.
3. **The credit was then acquired by CAM and rapidly connected to a debt/valuation/dación proposal.**
4. **The valuation used in that route was contemporaneously attacked by another professional report on specific methodological and physical-condition grounds.**
5. **Material control preceded formal title.** Later title cannot retrospectively answer the authority for earlier control.
6. **The end-to-end debt, value and surplus ledger remains incomplete.** Without it, one cannot prove that the creditor received no more than lawful satisfaction.

The proposition is not that each step is automatically unlawful. It is that the steps must be examined together because each supplied an indispensable bridge to the same beneficiary outcome.

## Legal characterisation

Spanish law prohibits the creditor from appropriating mortgaged or pledged property merely by reason of non-payment. Spanish insolvency law may, however, permit judicially authorised dación or other realisation. The decisive distinction is therefore substantive:

- Was this a genuine post-default realisation with independent valuation, contradiction, market testing, lawful debt calculation and surplus protection?
- Or did the formal route operationalise a pre-shaped acquisition in which alternatives, information, value and reversibility were progressively removed?

The absence of a located classical ex ante appropriation clause is a real weakness. It does not end the inquiry if the evidence establishes an indirect, simulated, fiduciary or fraud-on-law architecture whose practical function was prohibited appropriation.

## Strongest pleading formulation

> The court should determine whether the acquisition of the secured credit, the documented creditor-side control over workout decisions, the restriction or loss of alternative exits, the disputed valuation, material control before formal title, insolvency-administration gatekeeping and the later dación formed an integrated realisation mechanism by which the creditor obtained the secured assets without a genuinely objective valuation and without preserving the debtor estate's excess value. If so, the legally dependent arrangement should be characterised, to the appropriate extent, as an indirect prohibited commissory appropriation and/or fraud on law, with accounting, restitution, surplus integration, partial ineffectiveness and other remedies determined actor by actor and asset by asset.

## Strongest opposing case

The defence will say:

- the original mortgage long pre-dated CAM;
- the dación was negotiated after default and therefore ex intervallo;
- the transaction extinguished debt rather than creating a future appropriation option;
- the liquidation route was judicially authorised;
- ACTÚA was a professional valuation and competing appraisals served different purposes;
- the body-dated 8 February 2018 order recognised CAM as assignee and rejected LPB's strict Article 1535 position; Judge signature 9 February, LAJ signature 14 February, `15FEB2018` filename/notification alias and stated 20-day appeal remain separately typed metadata;
- alternative transactions were conditional;
- later registry and third-party rights may survive independently.

This defence is serious. It is defeated only by primary evidence showing that the process lacked genuine independence, value protection or surplus accounting—not by rhetoric.

## Publication rule

Public wording may state that the controlled record supports a serious case requiring judicial determination of an indirect appropriation architecture. It must not state as an established fact that a court has already declared pacto comisorio, that every actor agreed to an unlawful plan, or that every downstream title automatically falls.
