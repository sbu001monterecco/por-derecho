## 3. Why this overlaps with Sun Rock

The overlap is not “Bankia appears in both matters”. It is the need to distinguish **asset succession** from **legal-person succession** and then apply corporate criminal-liability rules only after a predicate legal-person responsibility is established.

### Rail A — corporate / product / legal-person succession

`Caja Insular → BFA → Bankia → CaixaBank`

Questions:
- which rights and liabilities remained with or passed between legal persons;
- whether any historically applicable legal-person criminal responsibility existed at the relevant date;
- whether a later transformation, merger, absorption or split falls within CP 130.2;
- how civil liability arising from crime is treated separately from ordinary civil/product liability.

### Rail B — mortgage asset / NPL succession

`Bankia → SAREB → Promontoria Holding 122 B.V. (PH122) → Construcciones Acosta Matos, S.A. (CAM)`

Questions:
- exact asset, balance, security, defences, notices, servicing, consideration and procedural substitution;
- whether transferred data accurately described the credit and connected disputes;
- own conduct, knowledge, decision authority and benefit of each assignee.

**Boundary:** an asset assignment is not, without more, a CP 130.2 transformation/merger/absorption/split and does not automatically transfer criminal guilt, natural-person intent or ordinary civil/product liability.

### Rail C — later CAM corporate succession

`CAM S.A. → HNT economic-unit segregation / universal succession → later CAM total demerger beneficiaries`

Questions:
- exact legal form and effective date of each structural operation;
- asset, passive, litigation, contingency and liability schedules;
- whether any otherwise applicable legal-person responsibility is preserved or extended under CP 130.2;
- which successor received which legal relationship;
- which later acts are the successor's own conduct rather than inherited responsibility.

## 4. Lender-in-possession comparator

Project Sun Rock uses **“lender in possession”** only as a functional comparative label. It is **not asserted to be a standalone status created by Spanish insolvency law**.

The issue is whether CAM allegedly crossed from secured-credit / individual-property positions into de facto material possession, access, exclusion, security, operation, works or exploitation affecting LPB estate assets and separately other owners/CEXP rights.

The criminal-law analysis must therefore be actor- and act-specific:

1. exact right or authority held;
2. exact act performed or instructed;
3. knowledge and legally required intent;
4. qualifying natural-person offence, where required;
5. corporate attribution / benefit under CP 31 bis and offence-specific gates;
6. later structural succession under CP 130.2 only if an otherwise applicable legal-person responsibility exists;
7. causation, benefit, restitution and civil ex delicto kept separate from ordinary civil/product liability.

## 8. Non-negotiable boundaries

- Del Rosal's publication is a doctrinal source, **not an endorsement of Project Sun Rock**.
- This crosswalk does **not** claim he advised on the overlap in 2022.
- A transfer discount, later recovery, assignment, dación or corporate reorganisation is not itself proof of crime.
- CP 130.2 does not create retroactive corporate criminal liability.
- CP 130.2 does not transfer a natural person's guilt or intent.
- The continued-activity/substantial-identity language is the statutory rule for concealed/apparent dissolution; it is not a general substitute for proving a listed structural operation.
- Every allegation remains subject to contrary and exculpatory evidence.
