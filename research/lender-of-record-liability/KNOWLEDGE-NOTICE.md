## PP 1041 attribution rule

The current record supports a powerful sequence and an objective CAM-favourable outcome. It does not yet identify the withdrawal's drafter, signer, filer or the personal role of José Daniel Acosta Matos or Laura Patricia Acosta Matos.

Por Derecho formally alleges false attribution to LPB, deliberate estate harm and de facto functional agency for CAM/Acosta Matos. To attribute knowledge, intent or instruction personally or across entities, recover the communication, office, authority, distribution, conflict, ratification and benefit chain.

## Attribution tests

Before attributing knowledge from one entity/person to another, record:

1. legal employer/entity;
2. office and authority;
3. recipient and distribution list;
4. contractual mandate or power;
5. committee/approval chain;
6. whether the principal later ratified or benefited;
7. whether the fact was actually material to the later decision;
8. for PP 1041, the withdrawal filing metadata and Article 51.2 authority.

## Notice does not equal liability

Notice becomes legally relevant only when connected to:

- a duty or legal capacity;
- a later act or omission;
- causation;
- legally attributable harm;
- a viable forum and remedy.

The phrase “LPB withdrew” is not permitted as a knowledge or conduct shortcut. The verified statement is that a withdrawal was filed in LPB's name.
