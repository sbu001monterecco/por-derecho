# Legal-route matrix

“Lender liability” is an organising label, not a single Spanish cause of action.

| Route | Core proposition | Actor fit | Evidence gate | Main defence/control |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Assigned credit and accessories | the assignee receives the actual credit and legally included accessories | SAREB, PH122, CAM | each transfer instrument and schedule | scope depends on instrument and law |
| Defences/nullity affecting credit | a defect or defence connected to the source/amount of the credit may affect the assignee's asset | current holder/necessary party | native transaction, ruling, procedural posture | not every personal claim is opposable to assignee |
| Retained originator liability | sale of credit may leave upstream personal obligations/liability with originator | originator/Bankia successor lane | contract, duty, breach, transfer allocation | assignment may move some obligations; limitation/standing |
| Assignee's direct liability | assignee may answer for its own wrongful act/omission after acquisition/notice | PH122, CAM, HNT | duty, knowledge, act, fault, causation, damage | lawful exercise of creditor rights; judicial/Administrator decisions; intervening causes |
| Contractual liability | breach within a contractual relationship, with Arts. 1106–1107 loss limits | actor with relevant contract | contract, breach, foreseeability, necessary consequence | no contractual nexus; remote/speculative loss |
| Article 1902 direct fault | own negligent/intentional conduct causing legally attributable damage | servicer, assignee, other actor | act/omission, fault, damage, causal/legal attribution | no duty/fault; victim/third-party causes |
| Insolvency Administrator statutory responsibility | office-holder may answer for estate damage caused by unlawful or insufficiently diligent acts/omissions and must act as orderly administrator and loyal representative | Francisco de Borja Rodríguez-Batllori Laffitte / Insolvency Administration | historical Articles 35–36; office act; authority; duty; estate loss; causation; standing; insurance | acted lawfully, diligently, under supervision and for documented estate interest; standing/limitation/competence |

| Procedural authenticity / unauthorised representation | a filing attributed to LPB may be challenged if no authentic decision, authority or valid representation existed | filer, signatory, procurator, office-holder and any instructing/benefiting actor | original filing, signature, electronic certificate, mandate, instructions, court record and procedural remedy | authentic authorisation, ratification, harmlessness, finality or wrong procedural route |
| Loss-of-chance / estate-value responsibility | removal of an already-obtained disclosure route may generate recoverable incremental loss if legal/economic probability and causation are proved | Administrator, CAM and any proved participant | disclosure order, missing production, funding/legal counterfactual, probability, estate consequence | Article 1535 unavailable; no finance; speculative chance; later order/intervening causes |
| Mandate/representation | principal may be bound by authorised or ratified acts; agent may have own liability | PH122/Haya/Cerberus channel; CAM/perimeter where proved | agreement, PoA, authority, instruction, ratification | outside mandate; separate entity; no ratification |
| De facto functional-agency inference | where an actor uses exclusive power to solve another actor's problem and knowingly produce its benefit, the relationship may support attribution through the legally available doctrine, but the forensic label is not itself a cause of action | Administrator/CAM/Acosta Matos perimeter | knowledge, instruction, communications, conflict, common plan, ratification, benefit, applicable legal route | aligned effect is not agency; no instruction, control, agreement or intent; separate actors |
| Responsibility for others | responsibility may arise where statutory dependency/function elements are met | employer/principal undertaking | dependency, functions, act and damage | independent contractor; no control/function link |
| Concurrent causation / improper solidarity | several independent causes may answer for an indivisible injury in proper circumstances | multiple actors | causal contribution and indivisibility | damage divisible; separate periods; limitation not automatically shared |
| Unjust enrichment / restitution | benefit without legal basis may require restitution in a suitable route | beneficiary actor | enrichment, correlative impoverishment, absence of basis, subsidiarity | valid legal basis; alternative action |
| Universal corporate succession | liabilities included in a segregated economic unit may follow by law/instrument | CAM/HNT | temporal law, project, deed, balance, passive schedule | obligation outside unit; retained liability; statutory allocation |
| Exceptional veil piercing | corporate separation may be disregarded only on proved exceptional facts | group perimeter | abuse, fraud on law, commingling/control facts | group membership alone insufficient |
| Professional/deontological responsibility | conflict, lack of independence, procedural truthfulness and misuse of fiduciary office may be examined by competent professional bodies | Administrator and professional perimeter | code/rules applicable at date, office acts, conflicts, communications, harm and procedural jurisdiction | no breach, no competence, finality, acted under lawful authority |
| Criminal falsity / procedural fraud / disloyal administration analysis | if a non-existent LPB intention was knowingly attributed without authority to obtain a procedural or patrimonial result, each possible offence must be tested element by element | author, filer, instructor, cooperator and beneficiary only where individual elements are proved | original document, electronic attribution, intent, deception, court consequence, patrimonial purpose, harm and participation | authentic authority; no falsehood; no deception/intent; no causal result; wrong offence; no individual participation |

## PP 1041 controlling rule

The legal matrix does not start from “LPB withdrew.” It starts from the verified procedural event:

> **A withdrawal was filed in LPB's name and the Court ended PP 1041 on that filing.**

It then tests authenticity, authority, Article 51.2 compliance, instruction, conflict, estate interest, benefit, causation and the correct forum/remedy.

Por Derecho alleges false attribution, deliberate estate harm and de facto functional agency for CAM/Acosta Matos, including decision or benefit roles of José Daniel Acosta Matos and Laura Patricia Acosta Matos to be proved. The allegation must be preserved strongly while criminal and individual-liability conclusions remain pending.

## Pacto comisorio interface

The pacto-comisorio dossier asks whether a credit/security/appropriation architecture was prohibited or another remedy applies. The PP 1041 episode may support that architecture by showing the removal of information and alternatives before valuation and dación, but it does not convert pacto comisorio into a crime or automatically impose liability on every actor in the chain.

## Counsel gates

Before pleading, validate:

- historical text and applicability of Articles 35, 36, 51.2 and 145;
- competent route to challenge or obtain consequences from an unauthorised/false filing after closure/finality;
- standing and necessary parties;
- limitation/accrual and interruption actor by actor;
- res judicata/finality and the scope of the 15 February 2018 order;
- electronic-signature and LexNET proof rules;
- registry and third-party protection;
- concursal competence and estate standing;
- professional-disciplinary competence;
- criminal offence elements and participation;
- remedy actually available in the chosen forum.
