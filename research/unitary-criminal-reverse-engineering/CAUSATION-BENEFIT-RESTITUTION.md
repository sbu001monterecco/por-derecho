# CAUSATION, BENEFIT AND RESTITUTION

ACT → immediate effect → lost right/exit → intermediate decision → asset/business consequence → current benefit.

Damage rings: (1) property/equity/surplus/fruits/direct cost; (2) lost exit/refinance/operator period if probable; (3) remote platform/reputation/future-project loss.

Recovery: CP 109–111 restitution; CP 127+ tracing/cautelars; precise civil nullity/fraud-on-law/pacto-comisorio; insolvency debt/value/surplus account; Matkator/third-party recovery; dependent registry rectification; actor-specific damages.
