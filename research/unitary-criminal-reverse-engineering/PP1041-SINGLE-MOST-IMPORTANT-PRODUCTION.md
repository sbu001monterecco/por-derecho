# PP 1041/2017 — THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT PRODUCTION

**Control marker:** `pp1041-single-most-important-production-20260820`  
**Date:** 20 August 2026  
**Status:** controlling P0 evidence-demand memorandum

## Core conclusion

> **The certified PP 1041/2017 file together with the Insolvency Administrator's internal authority, instruction and estate-interest record is the single most important evidential production in the current Sun Park criminal-first reconstruction.**

It is the point at which the following questions converge:

- who caused the withdrawal filed in LPB's name;
- whether there was valid authority;
- whether the act was truthfully attributable to LPB;
- what the Insolvency Administrator knew about the PH122→CAM assignment, price, payment, debt, redemption and financing routes;
- whether continuation and withdrawal were compared in the estate's interest;
- whether a conflict with the immediate CAM/Acosta Matos benefit was identified and managed;
- whether loss of disclosure, debt satisfaction and a successful insolvency exit was foreseeable and intended;
- whether the act became an indispensable bridge within the alleged credit→control→title economic-offence architecture.

## Directly documented foundation

1. LPB obtained admission of a judicial route seeking the PH122→CAM deed, price, payment and costs.
2. On 25 January 2018 the Insolvency Administrator terminated the formal mandate of the lawyer pursuing that route and imposed an authorisation gate over future LPB action.
3. A withdrawal was subsequently filed in LPB's name.
4. CAM did not oppose.
5. The route ended and was later confirmed archived because of that withdrawal.

These procedural facts do not, by themselves, establish the identity of the true decision-maker, authority, purpose, conflict or criminality.

## Por Derecho's formal allegation

Por Derecho alleges that:

- LPB did not validly authorise the withdrawal;
- the filing was caused or enabled through the Insolvency Administrator-controlled representation;
- attributing the abandonment to LPB as its voluntary act was false in substance;
- the act deliberately destroyed a route capable of obtaining the assignment economics and supporting redemption, debt satisfaction, negotiation, appeal and a financed insolvency exit;
- the act was wholly contrary to LPB's and the estate's patrimonial interest;
- it was undertaken in conflict and materially benefited CAM and the Acosta Matos perimeter;
- it became an enabling institutional bridge in the alleged wider economic-offence architecture.

This remains a formal allegation pending certified primary production and competent determination.

## Why the production is decisive

### Authority and authorship

The original pleading, instruction, signature, power and filing metadata can identify who actually decided, drafted, authorised and filed the withdrawal and whether that person could bind LPB.

### Truth or false attribution

The native record can show whether LPB's name accurately reflected a competent LPB decision or was used to present another actor's decision as LPB's own.

### Duty, conflict and estate interest

The Administrator's internal file can show whether continuation and abandonment were compared, whether conflicts were analysed, and what measurable estate benefit justified ending a functioning disclosure route.

### Knowledge and purpose

The file can establish what the decision-maker knew about transfer price, payment, Article 1535, debt reconciliation, ONA, finance and the likely consequence of ending disclosure.

### Coordination and benefit

Communications with CAM, its lawyers and the wider creditor perimeter can prove or disprove request, coordination, ratification, notice or benefit.

### Causation and remedy

The production can connect one institutional act to loss of information, redemption, debt satisfaction, a developed insolvency exit, estate value and the later credit-to-title result.

## Mandatory production

1. Complete certified docket and index.

3. Native filing, signature and transmission metadata.
4. Procurator and lawyer powers and appointment records.
5. Instruction authorising withdrawal and identity of its author.
6. LPB corporate authority or competent insolvency authority.
7. Insolvency Administrator's written authority and legal basis.
8. Conflict analysis.
9. Contemporaneous estate-interest memorandum.
10. Communications with CAM, its lawyers and the creditor perimeter.
11. Internal AC communications, file notes and decision records.
12. CAM's non-opposition filing.
13. Article 1535, debt, redemption and settlement analysis.
14. ONA and financing comparison and counterfactual analysis.
15. Preservation, deletion, access and document-management logs.

## Criminal relevance

If the certified record establishes lack of authority, false attribution, knowing procedural use, concealed decision-making, conflict, deliberate estate harm and causal benefit, the evidence may support investigation of:

- procedural deception;
- documentary or procedural falsity;
- disloyal administration;
- participation, necessary cooperation or commission by omission;
- deliberate frustration of estate recovery or other insolvency-related offences;
- corporate benefit and responsibility where the statutory predicate and attribution requirements are met.

If the record instead establishes valid contemporaneous authority, independent advice, reasoned estate benefit and no coordination with the beneficiary perimeter, it may materially weaken or defeat those inferences.

## Civil, commercial and insolvency relevance

The same production may affect:

- validity and attribution of the withdrawal;
- professional and insolvency liability of the Administrator;
- causation of lost redemption, refinancing and insolvency exit;
- the alleged indirect pacto-comisorio / fraud-on-law architecture;
- damages and lost opportunity;
- restitution, accounting, surplus and dependency of later acts.

Use:
