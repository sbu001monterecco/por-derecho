## Purpose

This source map preserves the reconstruction of the funded-exit attempt that was already active before the possession change described in the contemporaneous management record on 7 June 2018. It is a proposition-level audit note. It does **not** republish private emails, personal contact details or native transaction files.

“Funded exit” means the coordinated architecture intended to produce and deploy sufficient funds to pay the recognised insolvency liabilities, activate professional hotel operation and complete a supervised exit from Insolvency Proceeding 36/2012. It does not mean that an unconditional facility had drawn by 6 June.

## Evidential vocabulary

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Signed / direct document** | Proposition visible in a dated instrument bearing the relevant signature(s). |
| **Contemporaneously reported** | Proposition stated in an email or professional account created at the time; not necessarily independently completed or judicially certified. |
| **Active negotiation / preparation** | Term-sheet, due-diligence, security or meeting activity showing a live route, without proof of closing. |
| **Post-cutoff continuity** | A later document that corroborates continuation of a route already visible before 7 June; it must not be backdated conceptually. |
| **Not established** | No presently controlled document proves the proposition. |

## Executive reconstruction

By the close of 6 June 2018, the record does not prove an unconditional funding drawdown or completed court exit. It does prove or contemporaneously record a coordinated package already in execution:

1. ONA/Clubotel hotel-industry lease and suspensive annex signed and dated 6 June.
2. Buyer-signed Lagune Hospitalidad indicative EUR 26 million own-funds proposal dated 30 May.
3. Live EG Real Estate bridge term-sheet/redline and Dentons due-diligence work.
4. Stoneweg meeting and staged-security preparation before the cutoff.
5. Cuatrecasas legal red-flag/due-diligence work and expert valuation/debt analyses.
6. Delivery of the insolvency and professional file to Daniel Irigoyen and a 6 June specialist meeting.
7. Additional institutional and lender outreach around the same package.

The Stoneweg/Varia conditional offer of 12 June and the August bridge term sheet are **post-cutoff continuity**, not routes invented after 7 June and not proof of a 6 June closing.

## H. Conditions and unresolved court mechanics

- definitive and reliable amount required to exit;

- title, access and asset-control facts needed for diligence and security;
- community approval required by the ONA annex;
- route-specific due diligence and investment/board approvals;
- definitive financing and acquisition documents;
- satisfaction, prevention, waiver or expiry of every condition.

The 4 June court order was being dealt with on 7–8 June and the debt calculation was challenged/clarification sought. The source map must therefore avoid presenting the court-exit amount or mechanism as settled by 6 June.

## I. The 7 June chronology and evidential restraint

The possession change described in the contemporaneous management record occurred immediately after the signed ONA package and while the other workstreams were active.

**Established:** temporal sequence and active workstreams.  
**Requires route-specific proof:** what was interrupted, what could still proceed, who controlled each condition, whether any condition was prevented, and the causal effect on each financing/acquisition route.  
**Not established by chronology alone:** illegality, civil liability, criminal conduct or a final causal conclusion.
