# Canary Resort House — Continuity Baseline (25 Sep 2026)

**Working codename:** ALISIO 28°N  
**Status:** Pre-clearance concept. **The ALISIO name is now RED-FLAGGED for external use pending professional clearance.**

This project is being developed as a Canary Islands resort-lifestyle house with a men-led launch. The central aesthetic is relaxed, material-led luxury associated with Tenerife and La Palma, with Lanzarote as a design/architecture chapter. The intended customer ranges from affluent resort/villa owners to aspirational premium-resort guests.

Core internal doctrine: **wealth without performance** — understated, tactile, comfortable and disciplined rather than logo-led or status-signalling.

Current product focus: resort shirts, polos, trousers/shorts, swim, lightweight outerwear, tailoring-adjacent pieces and accessories. A hero camp-collar linen shirt and an upper Atelier shirt tier are the first development priorities.

Reference-study framework (principles only, no copying):
- Tommy Bahama — silk/camp-shirt tactility and collectability
- Franck Namani — material luxury and relaxed affluence
- Corneliani — menswear discipline and construction
- Orlebar Brown / Frescobol Carioca — resort fit and category clarity
- Sunspel / Luca Faloni — premium simplicity and fabric execution

## Name-clearance control

A continuity audit found an existing **Alisio Wear** clothing brand in Santa Cruz de La Palma whose public positioning is Canary fashion inspired by volcanoes, sea and wind, with island-specific clothing. A separate Canary entity, **Grupo Alisio Canarias Inversiones, S.A.**, also uses the Alisio name. This is not a legal trademark conclusion, but it is enough to stop treating ALISIO as a presumptively available public brand.

Until formal EUIPO/OEPM and broader commercial clearance is complete, **ALISIO 28°N is an internal codename only**. The creative/product architecture must remain portable to a replacement name.
