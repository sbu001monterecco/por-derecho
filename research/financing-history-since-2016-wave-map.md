## Recurrent analytical dimensions

Every route should be coded against the same fields:

- target borrower / issuer / seller and exact legal entity;
- intended use of proceeds;
- debt, preferred equity, ordinary equity, seller finance, acquisition finance, operator capital or private-bank structure;
- ticket size, tenor, pricing and return requirement;
- available security before and after a court or title event;
- conditions precedent and third-party dependencies;
- NDA, term sheet, LOI, mandate, diligence, committee, definitive-documents and drawdown status;
- reason for pause or failure;
- whether the contact or institution remains commercially valuable;
- litigation, privilege, confidentiality and adverse-party exclusions.

## Controlled outcome labels

Use only:

1. outreach;
2. substantive discussion;
3. NDA / information exchange;
4. indicative proposal;
5. signed conditional term sheet or LOI;
6. diligence / documentation;
7. committee or credit approval evidenced;
8. definitive execution;
9. closing / cash movement evidenced;
10. declined, expired, superseded or unresolved.

Do not infer a completed account, mandate, credit approval, financing or transaction merely from meetings, onboarding, term sheets or adviser involvement.
