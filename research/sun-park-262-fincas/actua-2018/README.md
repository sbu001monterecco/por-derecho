# ACTÚA 2018 — spatial intelligence dossier

Updated: 19 August 2026.

## Purpose

This dossier tests how ACTÚA Valoraciones' 15 January 2018 description and valuation of Sun Park interacted with the CAM acquisition/dación offer and the insolvency administrator's proposed liquidation pathway. It is evidence-first and does **not** start from a finding that ACTÚA committed fraud.

> Was ACTÚA's physical, economic and valuation representation of Sun Park reliable, representative and fit for the transactional use to which it was subsequently put, and what did each actor know about the sample, condition, ownership and operating state?

## What the original ACTÚA valuation documents

ACTÚA identifies CAM as requester, describes the assignment as advice to determine `VALOR RAZONABLE`, and treats 262 independent registry properties as a `ÚNICA UNIDAD FUNCIONAL`.

It records:

- `Año de rehabilitación: No reformado`;
- categorical `mal estado de conservación y mantenimiento`;
- actual `SITUACIÓN Y OCUPACIÓN: Se desconoce`;
- Yaiza market occupancy input around 80.42%;
- approximately EUR 55/room/night;
- physical age 20 years;
- **57.14% physical depreciation**;
- replacement cost new EUR 12.190m;
- initial adaptation investment EUR 1.584m;
- update-method real-estate NPV about EUR 10.143m;
- selected value EUR 9.776m.

The 57.14% treatment is therefore direct ACTÚA evidence, not only GESVALT criticism.

### Falsification result

The visible photo record is **mixed**, not wholly negative. It contains deterioration/clutter but also intact/common areas, café/bar spaces, a common/reception area with people visibly present and an intact front office. Only three images are clear apartment interiors, all on page 21, and none is visibly identified by apartment, block, finca, owner or access provider.

Accordingly, the earlier formulation that ACTÚA's visible photo appendix was proved to be a negative Blocks 4–5 sample is **weakened by the primary source**.

## ACTÚA's 3 April counter-report adds the missing 61/access bridge

The recovered CAM LexNET package includes ACTÚA's 3 April 2018 counter-report replying to GESVALT. ACTÚA expressly states that:

- it could not access the interior of **100% of the complex**;
- because it lacked access to all interiors, it relied in part on **general exterior images** when concluding that the general state of the complex was `DEFICIENTE`;
- there were **`at least 61` unrefurbished units `according to information supplied to us`** (`conforme se nos informa`);
- it defended **57.14% physical depreciation for the total functional unit**, rejecting finca-specific depreciation because it treated the hotel/exploitation unit as depreciating as a whole;
- it checked historic GrafCan imagery and said the south-pool area had been inoperative from 2013.

This materially upgrades the 61 question. The number is no longer only a client-side allegation: ACTÚA itself recorded it. But ACTÚA attributes it to information supplied to the valuer, and the provider and exact 61-unit list remain unknown.

See `cam-lexnet-actua-filing-17apr2018.md`.

## GESVALT critique remains material

GESVALT's 1 March 2018 professional critique says, among other matters, that ACTÚA's valuation was not suitable for adequately calculating Sun Park market value, criticises uniform 57.14% depreciation, and says only approximately 44 units were poor while the rest were usable.

ACTÚA's April counter-report expressly contests that position and defends whole-unit depreciation. This is a documented professional dispute, not criminal adjudication.
