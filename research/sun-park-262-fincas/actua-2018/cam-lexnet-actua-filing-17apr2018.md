## Purpose

2. CAM's litigation position;
3. ACTÚA's own technical responses to GESVALT;

### A. Access limitation — direct ACTÚA statement

ACTÚA expressly says that it did **not** comply with the ECO reference framework in its entirety because it could not access the interior of **100% of the complex**.

Later in the same counter-report it states, in substance, that because it did not have access to the interior of the whole complex, it drew from **general exterior images** that the state of the complex as a whole was `DEFICIENTE`.

**Status:** PRIMARY ACTÚA statement.

This is highly material to the representativeness inquiry. It does not establish which individual units were or were not inspected.

### B. The 61-unit bridge — direct ACTÚA statement, source still second-hand

ACTÚA writes:

> `Existen al menos 61 que se encuentran sin reformar conforme se nos informa`

The important evidential distinctions are:

- the number **61** is now directly found in ACTÚA's own April 2018 counter-report;
- ACTÚA qualifies the proposition as information supplied to it (`conforme se nos informa`);
- ACTÚA does **not** in the located passage enumerate the 61 properties;
- ACTÚA does **not** identify them in that passage as architectural Blocks 4 and 5;
- the supplier/source of the 61-unit information is not identified in the passage.

**Status:** PRIMARY proof that ACTÚA relied/referred to a supplied 61-unrefurbished-unit proposition; the underlying unit count itself remains SOURCE-DEPENDENT / UNVERIFIED until its provider and schedule are located.

This materially upgrades the 61 inquiry from `party allegation only` to `ACTÚA-recorded supplied information`, while leaving the `two blocks` attribution and exact unit identity unresolved.

### C. Exterior/common-area reasoning

The counter-report says ACTÚA checked GrafCan historical imagery and concluded that exterior areas had not been renovated and that from 2013 a part was inoperative, specifically the south-pool area.

It then argues that the hotel is one functional unit and that common equipment, accessibility and inoperative components justify a whole-unit approach.

**Status:** PRIMARY ACTÚA reasoning. The accuracy and representativeness of the premises remain contestable.

### D. Uniform depreciation defence

ACTÚA expressly defends:

- original building age of about 30 years;
- theoretical 85.71% physical depreciation if nothing had been renovated;
- the existence of staggered maintenance/refurbishment;
- adoption of **57.14% physical depreciation for the total functional unit**;
- rejection of GESVALT's demand for a property-by-property depreciation percentage because ACTÚA treated the hotel/exploitation unit as depreciating as a whole.

This is more specific than the original valuation alone: ACTÚA did not merely mechanically apply 57.14%; in April it expressly defended **whole-unit rather than finca-specific depreciation as a methodological choice**.

**Status:** PRIMARY ACTÚA technical position.

### E. Market-rate defence

ACTÚA defends approximately EUR 55/room/night using a sample of 1–3-star holiday accommodation and argues that six-night stays are a better comparator than one-night checks. It also cites ISTAC 2015–2017 evolution data.

**Status:** PRIMARY ACTÚA technical position; correctness remains an expert question.

### F. Land/reversion defence

ACTÚA disputes GESVALT's reading of the land/reversion calculation and argues that ACTÚA land value was about EUR 4.552m, while the lower EUR 1.587m amount was an updated reversion component in the VAN calculation rather than its current land value.

**Status:** PRIMARY ACTÚA technical position.

## Evidential consequence for the 60 / 61 / approximately 44 problem

The evidence should now be stated as follows:

- **60** — 2017 acquisition/minority working reconstruction; native source still to recover.
- **61** — by 3 April 2018 ACTÚA itself records `at least 61` unrefurbished units **according to information supplied to ACTÚA**. The underlying provider and list remain unknown.
- **two blocks / 61** — the 17 April email to Cuatrecasas describes the 61 as two blocks; that `two blocks` characterisation has not yet been found in ACTÚA's own counter-report passage.
- **approximately 44 poor-condition units** — GESVALT professional critique.
- **Blocks 4+5 = 44 architectural apartments** — confirmed geometry, still not proof of identity with GESVALT's approximately 44.

## Stronger surviving forensic question

The source-supported question is now:

> ACTÚA expressly acknowledged incomplete interior access, relied in part on general exterior imagery, recorded at least 61 unrefurbished units on information supplied to it, and defended depreciating the 262-title functional unit as a whole at 57.14%. What was the source and unit-level evidential basis for the 61 figure, which interiors were actually inspected, and was that basis sufficiently representative to support the whole-unit physical-condition/depreciation conclusion?

This is materially stronger and more precise than alleging that the visible photo appendix simply consisted of selected Blocks 4–5 images.

## Safeguard

Do not convert:

- incomplete interior access into `no inspection`;
- `conforme se nos informa` into personal knowledge;
- `at least 61 unrefurbished` into `61 poor-condition units` without qualification;
- `61` into `Blocks 4+5`;
- CAM's pleading into an adjudicated finding;
- ACTÚA's methodology defence into proof that the methodology was correct;
- the evidential tension into established fraud or intent.
