# ACTÚA 2018 — primary-source digest

Updated: 19 August 2026.

This note records what the recovered ACTÚA sources themselves say and show. It supersedes earlier repository wording that treated the original ACTÚA report as missing, assumed its photo record was wholly negative, or treated 61 as supported only by the client-side April email. It does **not** determine criminal intent, registered title, access authority or legal validity.

## 13 photographs — correction to earlier theory
Pages 16–21 contain 13 substantive photographs, indexed in `actua-photo-geolocation.csv`. The record is mixed: deterioration/clutter is visible, but so are intact/usable-looking common areas, a reception area with people present, and an intact-looking front office. Only three clear apartment interiors appear, all on page 21, and none is visibly identified by apartment, finca, block, owner or access provider.

Accordingly, the earlier strong formulation that ACTÚA's visible appendix consisted largely/wholly of negative closed Blocks4–5 imagery is **weakened/falsified as stated**. The surviving question is representativeness and extrapolation.

## Source B — ACTÚA 3 April 2018 counter-report
Recovered as `18-4-16 DOC2_firmado(1).pdf`, ACTÚA ref `438_2018`, signed by Fayna Mª Navarro García.

ACTÚA says it could not access the interior of **100% of the complex** and that, because it lacked full interior access, general exterior images also led it to conclude the general state was `DEFICIENTE`. This proves an access limitation, not which interiors were entered or who supplied access.

ACTÚA also states: `Existen al menos 61 que se encuentran sin reformar conforme se nos informa`.

Thus ACTÚA itself recorded **at least 61 unrefurbished units**, but expressly attributes the proposition to information supplied to it. Provider/source and exact 61-unit schedule remain unresolved. The located passage does not identify the 61 as architectural Blocks4–5.

ACTÚA expressly defends **57.14% physical depreciation for the TOTAL functional unit**, rather than finca-specific depreciation.
