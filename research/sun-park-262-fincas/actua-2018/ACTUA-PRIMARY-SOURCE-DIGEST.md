# ACTÚA 2018 — primary-source digest

Updated: 19 August 2026.

## Evidential purpose

This note records what the recovered ACTÚA sources themselves say and show. It supersedes earlier repository wording that treated the original ACTÚA report as missing, assumed its photo record was wholly negative, or treated the number 61 as supported only by the client-side April email.

It does **not** determine criminal intent, registered title, access authority or the legal validity of the valuation.

## Uniform physical depreciation — primary evidence

ACTÚA's own calculation records normative life 35 years, physical age 20 years and **57.14% physical depreciation**. Replacement cost new is about EUR12.190m, net construction about EUR5.224m and land about EUR4.552m. Its update calculation produces a real-estate NPV of about EUR10.143m; ACTÚA selects the net-replacement result of **EUR9,776,003.13**. The difference alone does not establish wrongdoing.

## Photographic record — 13 first-class evidence objects

Pages 16–21 contain **13 substantive photographs**, indexed in `actua-photo-geolocation.csv` as `ACTUA-PHOTO-001` through `ACTUA-PHOTO-013`.

The record is **mixed**, not uniformly negative. It includes exterior neglect/restricted-looking areas, cluttered common/service interiors, landscaped/common areas with intact façades, café/bar/common areas appearing capable of use, a common/reception area with people visibly present, an intact-looking reception/front-office desk, and three clear apartment interiors on page 21 showing wear/stripping/deterioration.

The three apartment interiors are not visibly captioned by apartment, finca, block, owner or access provider. They may show one apartment or several; this remains unproved.

### Correction to earlier Project theory

The earlier strong formulation that ACTÚA's visible negative presentation consisted largely/wholly of closed Blocks 4–5/minority areas is **weakened/falsified as stated**. The surviving question is representativeness and extrapolation, not that the appendix was wholly negative.

## Source B — ACTÚA 3 April 2018 counter-report

Recovered in CAM's later court package as `18-4-16 DOC2_firmado(1).pdf`, ACTÚA ref `438_2018`, signed by Fayna Mª Navarro García and dated 3 April 2018.

ACTÚA expressly says it could not access the interior of **100% of the complex** and that, because it lacked full interior access, general exterior images also led it to conclude that the overall state was `DEFICIENTE`. This is PRIMARY evidence of an access limitation; it does not establish which interiors were entered or who supplied access.

ACTÚA also states: `Existen al menos 61 que se encuentran sin reformar conforme se nos informa`.

Therefore:

- ACTÚA itself recorded **at least 61 unrefurbished units**;
- ACTÚA expressly attributes that proposition to information supplied to it (`conforme se nos informa`);
- the provider/source and exact 61-unit schedule remain unknown;
- the located passage does not identify those 61 as architectural Blocks 4 and 5.

ACTÚA also expressly defends **57.14% physical depreciation for the TOTAL functional unit**, rather than finca-specific depreciation. Thus uniform whole-unit depreciation is an explicit ACTÚA methodological position, not merely an inference from spreadsheet mechanics.

## 60 / 61 / approximately 44 — corrected provenance

Keep distinct:

- **60** — 2017 acquisition/minority working reconstruction; native source still to recover.
- **at least 61 unrefurbished** — ACTÚA 3 April counter-report, expressly based on information supplied to ACTÚA; provider/list unresolved.
- **61 / two blocks / unused-poor** — 17 April client-side email to Cuatrecasas; the two-block geography has not been found in ACTÚA's located passage.
- **approximately 44 poor** — GESVALT professional opinion in its 1 March critique.
- **Blocks 4+5 = exactly 44 architectural apartments** — documented geometry; still not proof of identity with GESVALT's approximately 44.

Best current reconciliation, as inference only: 60/61 may concern a broader minority/closed/acquisition/refurbishment universe while approximately 44 may concern a physically poor-condition subset. The ACTÚA April source strengthens the existence of a 61-unit information input but does not identify its provider or geography.

## Strongest alternative explanation

A fair alternative explanation is that ACTÚA inspected more areas than shown in the 13-photo appendix, held non-photographic field observations and considered whole-unit depreciation justified by common-area, accessibility and exploitation-unit factors. Some deterioration is genuinely visible. Evidence strengthening that explanation would include unit-level field notes, a representative Blocks 1–9 interior sample, original omitted photos, and a reliable source schedule for the 61.
