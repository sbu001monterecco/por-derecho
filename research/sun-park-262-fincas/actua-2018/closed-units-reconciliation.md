# Closed-unit reconciliation — 60 / 61 / approximately 44

Updated: 18 August 2026.

## Why this file exists

The current evidence contains three close but non-identical descriptions of minority/closed/poor-condition units. They must not be merged for narrative convenience.

## A. 60 apartments — 2017 acquisition/status universe

A later evidential record reproduces/references the 2017 file `List of Fincas SPL - Acquisition - Status` as identifying 60 apartments plus 11 commercial/common-use elements (71 elements total). This is principally an acquisition/minority-status source. It is **not** itself a professional condition survey and does not prove that all 60 were closed, unusable or coordinated.

## B. 61 apartments in two blocks — 17 April 2018 contemporaneous party statement

Gmail `162d5ea4c51e8d7f`, sent to Cuatrecasas, states `2 bloques con 61 apartamentos sin uso y en mal estado`, and elsewhere in the same email says only two blocks belonging to minority owners were closed. The email attributes the closure to minority-owner conduct and alleges ACTÚA used those blocks to characterise the complex.

Classification: **CONTEMPORANEOUS PARTY ALLEGATION / INVESTIGATIVE LEAD.** It is not independent proof of the number, block identity or cause.

## C. Approximately 44 poor-condition units — GESVALT 1 March 2018 professional critique

GESVALT's professional counter-report states that ACTÚA depreciated all apartments at 57.14% as though they were in poor condition, whereas `tan solo existen aproximadamente 44 unidades que se encuentran en mal estado, estando el resto en condiciones de uso`.

Classification: **PROFESSIONAL OPINION / CONDITION EVIDENCE.** The located text does not name those 44 units or their blocks.

## D. Architectural capacity check

The confirmed GESVALT 2017 block layout gives:

- Block 4: 401–408 + 451–458 = 16 apartments.
- Block 5: 501–514 + 551–564 = 28 apartments.
- Blocks 4 + 5 = **44 apartments exactly**.

This exact match is evidentially important because the Project had independently identified Blocks 4–5 as a suspected concentration of closed/dissident stock. But an exact count match is not location proof.

## E. The contradiction that must remain visible

If the April email's phrase `two blocks / 61 apartments` meant the architectural Blocks 4 and 5, it cannot be literally correct because those blocks contain 44 apartments. At least one of the following must therefore be true:

1. `two blocks` was used loosely for a larger grouped area/perimeter;
2. the 61 count described a wider minority/closed universe rather than physical block capacity;
3. the two blocks were not Blocks 4 and 5;
4. the 61 figure was approximate or erroneous;
5. `approximately 44 poor units` is a physical-condition subset of a larger 60/61 closed/minority universe.

The fifth is presently a plausible working reconciliation, but remains unproven.
