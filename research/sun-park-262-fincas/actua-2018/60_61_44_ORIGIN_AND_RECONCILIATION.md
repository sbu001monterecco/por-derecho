# 60 / 61 / approximately 44 — origin and reconciliation

Updated: 19 August 2026.

## Rule
These quantities are not interchangeable. No located document equates all three.

## 60 apartments
Source class: working/historical acquisition-status evidence. A 2017 `List of Fincas SPL - Acquisition - Status` reconstruction preserved in later material identifies 60 apartments in a broader consolidation/acquisition universe. The exact native 2017 source remains to be recovered. Present meaning: wider minority/acquisition-status universe, not a physical-condition count.

## 61 units — directly found in ACTÚA's April counter-report
Source class: PRIMARY ACTÚA statement about information supplied to it; underlying source/list unresolved.

ACTÚA's 3 April 2018 counter-report states: `Existen al menos 61 que se encuentran sin reformar conforme se nos informa`.

Therefore ACTÚA itself recorded at least 61 unrefurbished units, but expressly attributed the proposition to information supplied to it. The supplier and exact unit schedule remain unknown, and the located passage does not identify architectural Blocks 4 and 5.

A 17 April 2018 email to Cuatrecasas separately describes `2 bloques con 61 apartamentos sin uso y en mal estado`. That email remains important for the two-block/unused/poor-condition characterisation, but the number 61 itself is now also found in ACTÚA's technical response.

## Approximately 44 poor-condition units
Source class: professional expert opinion. GESVALT's 1 March 2018 critical report states in substance that ACTÚA applied the same 57.14% depreciation across the apartment stock although only approximately 44 units were in poor condition and the remainder were usable. No ACTÚA 44-unit schedule is currently located.

## Blocks 4 + 5 = exactly 44 architectural apartments
- Block 4: 401–408 and 451–458 = 16.
- Block 5: 501–514 and 551–564 = 28.
- Combined = 44.

This numerical alignment is significant but remains a spatial hypothesis, not proof that GESVALT's approximately44 equals Blocks4+5. The contemporaneous `two blocks / 61` characterisation also means Blocks4+5 cannot literally account for 61 architectural apartments.

## Access limitation and photo correction
ACTÚA's April counter-report says it could not access the interior of 100% of the complex and relied in part on general exterior imagery in concluding the whole complex was deficient. The original report contains 13 substantive photographs; the record is mixed and only three are clear apartment interiors. Those three are not visibly identified by apartment, finca, block, owner or access provider. The primary photo record therefore does not itself prove a Blocks4–5 sample.

## Best current reconciliation
Working inference only: 60/61 may concern a broader minority/closed/acquisition/refurbishment universe while approximately44 may concern a physically poor-condition subset. Blocks4+5 remain one candidate for the approximately44 subset because their architectural capacity is exactly44. The ACTÚA April source strengthens the existence of a 61-unit information input but does not prove its source, identity or two-block geography.
