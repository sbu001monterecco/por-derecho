# 60 / 61 / approximately 44 — origin and reconciliation

Updated: 19 August 2026.

## Rule

These three quantities are not interchangeable. No document presently located equates all three.

## 60 apartments

**Source class:** working/historical acquisition-status evidence.

A 2017 `List of Fincas SPL - Acquisition - Status` reconstruction, preserved in later evidential material, identifies **60 apartments** within a broader 71-element consolidation/acquisition universe. The exact native 2017 source remains a target for recovery and verification.

A separate Drive source, `List of Fincas SPL - Junio 2014 - Apartamentos en Explotación 2014.pdf` (Drive id `1ReMjvQSg59geYk0kVAKU5Wysu6E4cOFY`), is relevant to earlier operating-status reconstruction but must not be substituted for the missing native 2017 acquisition-status source.

Present meaning: wider minority/acquisition-status universe, not a physical-condition count.

## 61 apartments / two blocks

**Source class:** contemporaneous party allegation.

Gmail message `162d5ea4c51e8d7f`, 17 April 2018, sent to Cuatrecasas, states that there were `2 bloques con 61 apartamentos sin uso y en mal estado` and alleges that ACTÚA's valuation relied on those closed minority areas.

The recovered complete ACTÚA report does **not** presently contain the same `61 apartamentos / dos bloques` statement.

Therefore the 61/two-block formulation must not be attributed directly to ACTÚA unless an earlier CAM/Community/ACTÚA source establishing its origin is found.

Present meaning: contemporaneous party description of a wider closed/minority stock or grouping, exact origin unresolved.

## Approximately 44 poor-condition units

**Source class:** professional expert opinion.

GESVALT's 1 March 2018 critical expert report states, in substance, that ACTÚA applied the same 57.14% depreciation across the apartment stock although only approximately **44 units** were in poor condition and the remainder were usable.

The complete ACTÚA report itself does not presently enumerate a 44-unit poor-condition schedule.

Present meaning: GESVALT's expert assessment/estimate of the poor-condition subset.

## Blocks 4 + 5 = exactly 44 architectural apartments

Confirmed physical capacity:

- Block 4: apartments 401–408 and 451–458 = 16;
- Block 5: apartments 501–514 and 551–564 = 28;
- combined = **44**.

This is a significant numerical alignment with GESVALT's `approximately 44` but remains a **spatial hypothesis**, not proof of identity.

The earlier contemporaneous `two blocks / 61` statement also means Blocks 4+5 cannot literally account for 61 architectural apartments.

## New primary ACTÚA photo result

The recovered ACTÚA report contains 13 substantive photographs on pages 16–21.

Primary-source review materially limits the earlier theory:

- the photographic record is mixed rather than wholly negative;
- only three clear apartment-interior photographs appear, all on page 21;
- those interiors have no visible apartment/finca/block captions;
- common-area images include maintained/intact spaces, a reception/front office and people visibly present;
- ACTÚA's text states actual `SITUACIÓN Y OCUPACIÓN: Se desconoce`;
- ACTÚA still applies a uniform 20-year / 57.14% physical depreciation in its primary calculation.

Therefore the current question is not whether ACTÚA proved 44 bad apartments photographically. It did not do so in the visible report. The question is what additional inspection evidence existed, if any, and whether it justified whole-stock uniform depreciation and categorical condition language.

## Best current reconciliation

**Working inference only:**

> 60/61 may concern the broader minority/closed/acquisition universe, while approximately 44 may concern the physically poor-condition subset identified by GESVALT. Blocks 4+5 remain one candidate for the approximately-44 subset because their architectural capacity is exactly 44.

This inference is weakened if the three ACTÚA apartment interiors geolocate outside Blocks 4–5 or if a unit-level GESVALT schedule identifies a different set.

It is strengthened if independent records identify the approximately 44 poor units as the complete apartment stock of Blocks 4 and 5.
