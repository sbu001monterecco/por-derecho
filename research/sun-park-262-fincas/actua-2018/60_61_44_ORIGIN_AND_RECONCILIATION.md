# 60 / 61 / approximately44 — origin and reconciliation

Updated: 19 August 2026.

These quantities are not interchangeable. No located document equates all three.

## 60
Working/historical acquisition-status evidence. A 2017 acquisition/minority reconstruction identifies 60 apartments. The exact native 2017 source remains to be recovered. It is not, by itself, a physical-condition count.

## at least61
PRIMARY ACTÚA statement about supplied information. ACTÚA's 3 April 2018 counter-report states: `Existen al menos 61 que se encuentran sin reformar conforme se nos informa`.

ACTÚA itself therefore recorded at least61 unrefurbished units, but expressly attributes the proposition to information supplied to it. The provider and exact unit schedule remain unknown; the located passage does not identify architectural Blocks4–5.

A 17 April 2018 email to Cuatrecasas separately characterises `2 bloques con 61 apartamentos sin uso y en mal estado`. That remains a party characterisation of geography/condition, not proof that ACTÚA's 61 were those two blocks.

## approximately44
GESVALT's 1 March professional critique says, in substance, that only approximately44 units were in poor condition and the remainder usable, while ACTÚA applied 57.14% depreciation across the apartment stock.

## Blocks4+5 = exactly44
Block4 contains 16 architectural apartments and Block5 contains28, combined44. This numerical alignment is significant but remains a spatial identity hypothesis. The contemporaneous `two blocks / 61` language cannot literally fit Blocks4+5 as architectural apartment counts.

## Access/photo implications
ACTÚA later acknowledged incomplete interior access and reliance in part on general exterior imagery. The original report's 13 substantive photographs are mixed; only three clear apartment interiors appear and none is visibly identified by apartment/finca/block/owner/access provider. The photo record therefore does not itself prove a Blocks4–5 sample.

## Best current reconciliation
Inference only: 60/61 may describe a broader minority/closed/acquisition/refurbishment universe while approximately44 may describe a physically poor-condition subset. Blocks4+5 remain a candidate for the approximately44 subset, not a conclusion.
