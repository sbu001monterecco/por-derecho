# ACTÚA / GESVALT commissioning and circulation timeline

Updated: 18 August 2026.

This chronology separates commissioning, client input, expert opinion, later mortgage valuation and subsequent circulation. The separation matters to evidential weight.

## 15 January 2018 — ACTÚA / CAM

ACTÚA's valuation is dated 15 January 2018 and is identified in the GESVALT counter-report as file `00405-18-00 Z-0`. GESVALT records that the ACTÚA architect stated that, at the request of ACOSTA MATOS S.L., they attended Sun Park to determine the stated reasonable value of the complex.

**Classification:** professional report's description of the ACTÚA instruction; verify against the ACTÚA engagement letter/invoice if located.

Gmail `161d1b8d919824af`, Juan José Herrera Bujalance (GESVALT) to Patricia Domínguez, cc Julio Gallego, Sergio Espadero and others: `Remito adjunta oferta para el informe pericial.`

## 1 March 2018 — client-side factual input to the expert workstream

Gmail `161e2fc0b6eb21f8`, Patricia Domínguez to the expert contact, cc GESVALT, provides information about 40 locals, operation of supermarket/night bar/kitchen/restaurant/reception, historical rental, separate local sales and attached plans/licence/Registry material.

**Classification:** client-provided information. Do not transform this email itself into an independent GESVALT finding.

## 1 March / signed 7 March 2018 — GESVALT critical report

GESVALT's `Análisis Crítico de la Valoración realizada por ACTUA` analyses ACTÚA file `00405-18-00 Z-0`. The report:

- addresses valuation purpose/methodology;
- identifies ACTÚA's commissioning by CAM;
- challenges hotel-rate inputs;
- challenges uniform 57.14% depreciation;
- states approximately 44 units were in poor condition, with the remainder usable;
- challenges the physical-condition/general-reform narrative;
- challenges land/reversion inputs;
- concludes the ACTÚA valuation is not adequate for calculating Sun Park market value.

The report contains the expert's Article 335.2 LEC objectivity statement and signature information.

**Classification:** professional opinion/expert evidence.
