## Geometry confirmed from GESVALT planimetry

Blocks 1–8 form the perimeter of an elongated rectangle and Block 9 runs transversely through the middle beside reception, dividing the central leisure space into two areas. The main entrance is on Janubio Street. With the viewer's back to Janubio Street and looking into the hotel, the plan reads:

- right of reception: Block 8;
- left of reception: Block 1, then Block 2;
- continuing around the left side: Block 3 (H10 White Suites side);
- opposing Blocks 2 and 1: Blocks 4 and 5;
- opposite Block 8: Block 6;
- right side returning toward the entrance: Block 7 (THB / former Sun Royal side);

This confirms the user's “digital 8” description. It is a schematic, not a cadastral survey.

## Valuation ladder

### TINSA — 28 October 2008 — EUR 23,029,792.44

Commissioned by Multimatrix Ltd. Property described as linked to an economic operation. TINSA used investment-cost and discounted-cash-flow approaches for IAS 40 fair value. It records an interior visit, approximate age 20 years, approximately two years since recent renovation and condition “Good.” The 2018 independent-expert reconstruction attributes EUR 20,437,551.32 to LPB's assets.

### Insolvency inventory / AC — 2012 — LPB approximately EUR 15,375,462.70

This is an LPB-only insolvency inventory figure, not a whole-hotel market appraisal. The 2018 independent expert described it as reflecting a crisis-era reduction of more than 24%.

### TINSA — 3 July 2015 — EUR 15,602,320.14

ECO mortgage valuation. Applicant LPB. Interior visit. TINSA states that the value corresponds to the group of properties functioning as one functional unit and cannot be broken down by registry property. The 2018 expert reconstruction places LPB's share at approximately EUR 11.2m.

### TINSA — 27 April 2017 — EUR 15,920,739.85

ECO mortgage valuation. Applicant LPB. Interior visit. Again values the properties as one functional unit. The report records the principal hotel condition as “Bueno,” with some elements “Regular/Medio.” The 2018 expert reconstruction attributes approximately EUR 11.5m to LPB, excluding locals and solaria.

### GESVALT RICS — 30 June 2017 — EUR 26,400,000

RICS Red Book market-value report, using a ten-year discounted cash flow. The report is the principal geometry source: nine blocks, 220 apartments and a unit-by-unit registry-value schedule. It says some rooms had been refurbished/renewed and assumes good structural repair/condition (without a structural survey). The 2018 independent-expert reconstruction attributes EUR 19,266,727 to LPB's assets.

A copy/reissue was used in the March 2018 valuation dispute. It should not be counted as a wholly independent new valuation unless the underlying valuation date is separately established.

### ACTÚA — 15 January 2018 — EUR 9,776,003.13

ACTÚA's report was supplied in support of the CAM acquisition/dación proposal and adopted by the AC's proposed liquidation plan. The plan records:

- whole complex: EUR 9,776,003.13;
- LPB locals + pools/solaria (31 titles): EUR 489,361.96;
- LPB 159 apartments: EUR 6,607,551.87;
- total LPB real-estate assets: EUR 7,096,913.83.

GESVALT's 1 March 2018 critical expert report is unusually important evidence. It states that ACTÚA applied the same 57.14% depreciation across the whole complex; that only approximately 44 units were in poor condition while the remainder were usable; and that ACTÚA's statement of poor conservation/maintenance did not reflect the physical reality shown by the photographic record. Its conclusions say the valuation was not suitable to calculate Sun Park market value, criticised the valuation principles/inputs, and said the report failed to make a property-by-property depreciation assessment.

Project Sun Rock alleges that the ACTÚA presentation was not merely inaccurate but part of a materially misleading valuation narrative. That allegation remains an allegation. The stronger presently proven proposition is narrower: a contemporaneous professional counter-report identified specific methodological and physical-condition defects, and the ACTÚA figure was then used in the CAM offer / AC liquidation pathway.

### GESVALT ECO — 7 June 2018 — EUR 25,647,179.50

Applicant Aweswell. Inspection 23 May 2018. Mortgage-purpose valuation under ECO/805/2003. The certificate describes the hotel as “Terminado” and its occupation as “Con ocupante.” It expressly identifies the 262 independent registry properties, 8497–8758, as a single exploitation unit and conditions the value to the set functioning as one exploitation.

This appraisal is a critical control because it uses the conservative ECO framework yet arrives at EUR 25.647m, only months after ACTÚA's EUR 9.776m.

## ACTÚA visual-sampling / Blocks 4–5 hypothesis

The project hypothesis is that ACTÚA's negative visual sample disproportionately represented closed/non-operating minority/Montelanza-Molina areas — especially Blocks 4 and 5 — and was then allowed to colour the description of the whole hotel.

This specific block attribution is **not yet treated as proven**. The public map therefore marks Blocks 4–5 as a **photo-geolocation priority / provisional attack-surface overlay**, not as a concluded fact.

The test is simple and falsifiable: geolocate every ACTÚA photograph to apartment number → block → owner/controller → operating status at the valuation date. If the negative images cluster in a limited subset while the rest of the hotel was usable/occupied, the photographs would undermine, rather than support, a whole-hotel abandonment narrative.

## Deletion-audit rule

Do not collapse different values into one “correct” number. Preserve purpose, date, scope, methodology and physical assumptions. In particular:

- whole-complex values and LPB-only values are not interchangeable;
- RICS market value and ECO mortgage value have different purposes;
- a company being inactive is not the same as the physical hotel being empty or unusable;
- the AC plan's “inactividad de la empresa” must not silently become “entire hotel abandoned”;
- ACTÚA's disputed condition narrative must remain separate from the later GESVALT certificate's “Con ocupante” finding;
- the Blocks 4–5 photographic-cluster allegation remains open until photo-by-photo geolocation is completed.
