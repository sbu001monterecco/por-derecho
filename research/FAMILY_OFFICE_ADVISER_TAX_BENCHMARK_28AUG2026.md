### Presentation lesson

The useful lesson is **entity-level presentation rather than a public list of every professional individual**. The Geneva family-office model also supports a central coordinating office that uses and orchestrates a network of specialist professionals as needs arise.

Bayard publicly describes a model of acting as a central superintendent/point of contact while drawing on a network of recognised specialist professionals. This supports Project Sun Rock's decision to separate:

- the Group / family-office-style coordinating perimeter;
- current advisers;
- approved advisers capable of renewed engagement;
- individual mandate details that need not be public.

## 2. Grosvenor — stronger family-owned tax-policy benchmark

Grosvenor publishes a dedicated tax policy and ownership/tax explanation. Its public structure separates:

1. tax governance and board responsibility;
2. compliance and filing obligations;
3. tax-risk management;
4. dealings with tax authorities;
5. adviser standards;
6. ownership and tax contribution.

The policy states that Grosvenor uses reputable, experienced advisers familiar with its principles and tax policy.

Relevant sources:
- https://www.grosvenor.com/about-us/how-we-work/tax-policy
- https://www.grosvenor.com/about-us/how-we-work/tax-policy/the-facts-about-our-ownership

### Project Sun Rock lesson

Use the **architecture**, not unsupported substantive claims. Project Sun Rock should state its governance position and current dispute status, but should not copy claims such as a particular tax-risk rating, aggregate tax contribution or universal tax residence unless independently verified for the Group.

Relevant sources:

### Project Sun Rock lesson

A useful public tax statement can explain that the Group seeks compliance and appropriate professional advice while preserving the right to use formal administrative and judicial processes where a tax position is disputed. **Compliance and dispute resolution are not opposites.**

## 4. Controlling Project Sun Rock formulation

The public statement should say, in substance:

> The Group's tax-governance position is to identify and meet the tax and filing obligations applicable to the relevant entity, transaction, asset and jurisdiction; to obtain appropriate professional advice; and to use lawful administrative and judicial review mechanisms where the Group or an affiliate disputes an authority's factual or legal position.

Do **not** state that:

- every Group entity shares one tax residence;
- every tax position has been audited or accepted;
- a contested assessment is an admitted liability;
- a pending dispute establishes wrongdoing by either side;
- the existence of an adviser relationship means a live mandate.

## 6. Adviser-classification boundary

Fieldfisher Spain and United Kingdom are to be displayed publicly only at firm level as **Approved Legal Adviser to the Group***.

The asterisk means that the Group, an affiliate and/or one or more principals have previously completed relevant client-acceptance, KYC and/or onboarding processes with the firm and there is presently a possibility of a new or renewed engagement. It does **not** mean a live current mandate, instruction on a specific matter, or active workstream.

Specific Fieldfisher lawyers may remain in repository chronology/evidence where needed, but are not to be listed on the public advisers page.
