## Cross-authority value chain

`asset/title`
→ `possession/control`
→ `corporate transfer/layer`
→ `RIC/private finance`
→ `works/capex`
→ `public incentives / EU dimension`
→ `hotel operation`
→ `revenue/fruits`
→ `refinancing/distribution`
→ `current holder`

Every institutional filing should identify **which link the addressee can lawfully control** and the next authority in the chain. Do not ask one authority to exercise another authority's power.

## Strong institutional wording

> **The issue is no longer whether enough material exists to justify asking preservation questions. The issue is which competent authority has preserved which asset, income stream, record or dataset, under which power and from what date. Where no preservation or transversal reconciliation exists, that absence should be stated expressly and attributed to the competent decision-maker.**

> **Successive corporate, financing or administrative layers cannot be used as a substitute for end-to-end reconciliation. The files may be separate; the economic chain is not.**
