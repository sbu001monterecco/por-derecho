2. Investor/project presentation in 2020–2021 must not be collapsed into the later 2022 Hotel New Trend segregation.
3. The 2022 segregation must not be projected backwards as proof of title, authority, availability or eligibility at an earlier date.
4. GC/836/P06 is treated as a first-class evidence node requiring the complete merits/payment/verification file.
5. EU/ERDF financing is an open traceability question unless and until a primary operation/file establishes it.
6. Resolution 90/2026 is a Treasury/custody/traceability checkpoint, not a finding of fraud or absence of controls.
7. Intervención's 19 August clarification does not certify either the existence or absence of other aid; it leaves those questions to the competent native files.
