## Editorial safeguards

Public content distinguishes documents, facts, allegations, inferences, open questions and official outcomes. Personal data, privileged material, legal strategy and unaudited capital or claim figures must remain outside this repository.

Public primary documents are stored under `assets/docs/` only after provenance, scope and redaction review; publication of a receipt or procedural record does not convert it into a merits finding.

### RICPE publication controls

Public-safe RICPE evidence analysis may be released when the Spanish and English versions are checked against the same source record and each proposition is clearly classified as documented fact, attributed allegation, inference or open question. Publication does not depend on prior delivery of a separate confidential communication. Any claim that RICPE received direct notice, acknowledged it, preserved records or failed to respond still requires a verifiable delivery/receipt record; the public response register must not infer guilt from silence.

Private witnesses, the raw audio, the full/working transcript, other privileged or confidential lawyer content, unnecessary personal data and live procedural strategy remain outside the repository. A short attributed phrase may be reproduced only when necessary to delimit the public allegation and accompanied by authentication and context limits. The public page may report controlled chronology and evidential metadata, but it must state the limits: no RICPE mandate to a third party is asserted without proof; pre-2023 conduct is not retroactively labelled under Law 2/2023; and no particular legal-advice or judicial outcome is attributed without contact, causation and counterfactual evidence. Any March 2021 allegation must distinguish the specific 26 May 2020 change of counsel for Aweswell's procedural representation in Concurso 36/2012 from any wider or residual professional relationship; it must not publish unnecessary fee history, describe that event as a firm-wide withdrawal or state that a later message completed a rupture. Any alleged later effect remains limited to a testable question concerning residual trust, confidentiality or assistance. Good-faith investors, prospective investors, advisers and public officials must not be described as complicit merely because they were exposed to a project or its marketing. Named persons and entities receive a finite, equally prominent right of reply and correction.

The named-person Camel Travel/Lanzarote Information side record was released on the publisher's instruction on 13 August 2026 as a tightly bounded subsidiary case. Public-page exhibits are reproduced only to the extent needed to show the documented contrast; connected 2013–2017 records remain private and are described with their evidential limits. The page carries a prominent right of reply and correction, and any substantive response or primary-evidence correction must be logged with equivalent prominence.

The 7 June dossier owns the full event, pre-event operating record, ONA alternative, aftermath, pilot evidence and insolvency-perimeter analysis. Its nested Camel Travel/Lanzarote Information side record owns only the documented dual-publication and potential travel-sales example; it is intentionally absent from the homepage, main navigation and updates feed. The Community dossier owns the authority/debt/voting record. Its nested minutes dossier owns the Owners’ Community/CEXP source-status chronology and a separately labelled non-minutes lane: a summons, later recital, working meeting, email, pleading, order or judgment must never be relabelled as Community minutes. The RIC dossier owns downstream investor, financing and HNT/MYND analysis; the homepage and updates pages provide gateways rather than competing event narratives.
