## Executive finding

The strongest non-fragmented presentation has three distinct accountability lanes:

1. **Direct private conduct.** Each private person and entity must be examined for the act personally or corporately performed, the capacity claimed, the authority relied upon, the knowledge shown by documents and the benefit sought or obtained. No collective guilt follows from family, professional, Community or corporate association.
2. **Primary institutional protection.** Francisco de Borja Rodríguez-Batllori Laffitte occupied the court-appointed role of Administrador Concursal in Concurso 36/2012. The central role-based question is what he knew, what powers and duties applied at each material date, and what concrete protective, investigative, accounting, recovery or restorative action he took for LPB and the estate.
3. **Subsidiary professional protection.** Cuatrecasas, Juan Tomás Parrilla Suárez and other lawyers did not occupy the same statutory office as the Administrador Concursal. Their conduct must be tested against the precise client, mandate, instructions, knowledge, available remedies, action taken and follow-through.

The governing proposition remains:

> **One physical and commercially integrated hotel did not mean one owner, one debtor, one insolvency estate, one operator, one Community, one client or one source of legal authority.**

The documents currently support a cumulative sequence of prior notice, contested Community authority and debt, material-control events, a disputed 2018–2019 realisation chain and later hotel continuity and value. They justify production, reconciliation and actor-specific explanation. They do not, without further primary and individualised proof, establish a concluded criminal scheme.

## Why the Cuatrecasas Registry intervention matters

The controlled corpus describes a **28 December 2018 Cuatrecasas communication to the Tías Property Registry** addressing a CAM-facing acquisition or transaction, the liquidation plan, suspension decisions and certain ob rem or linked-asset issues, and seeking negative qualification or cancellation.

That evidence is double-edged:

- it prevents an overbroad statement that Cuatrecasas took no protective action;
- it supports that external counsel regarded the transaction and Registry position as sufficiently serious to require formal intervention;
- it creates a benchmark for the response of the court-appointed insolvency administration;
- it does not establish that Cuatrecasas completed every protective step, that the Administrador Concursal breached a duty, or that any private actor committed an offence.

The original communication, annex set, Registry entry, outcome, client authority and privilege status must control any quotation or wider publication.
