This note records only the highest-leverage, smallest amendments identified after re-reading the current unitary 2018 protection workstream and scanning targeted 2018 emails. ## 1. Add a January 2018 perimeter-warning row

### Source sequence

- 3 January 2018: the Administrador Concursal requested delivery of the material possession of the apartments belonging to LPB within Sun Park, together with keys/access codes and information on third-party occupants.
- 18 January 2018: the AC requested additional LPB accounting/receivable material and then used broader wording: `con la finalidad de tomar posesión física del complejo`, requesting the electronic entrance-lock code and instructions to modify it.
- 19 January 2018: Patricia Domínguez forwarded both emails to Cuatrecasas, expressly identifying concern about the final sentence of the 18 January message and point 4 of the earlier request, and requested advice on how to respond. Cuatrecasas confirmed review and was preparing Aweswell's appearance and insolvency-conclusion work.

### Small amendment

Insert, before the May–June 2018 row:

> **3–19 January 2018 — perimeter warning.** The AC's 3 January request was expressly framed around possession of LPB-owned apartments. The 18 January wording broadened to `physical possession of the complex` and requested the main electronic access code and instructions to alter it. Cuatrecasas was asked on 19 January how best to respond. The wording does not by itself establish an intention or authority to take possession of non-LPB property. It does require production of the legal perimeter, inventory, non-LPB carve-outs, safeguards and implementation instructions governing any proposed control of the common access system.

### Open question

What exact judicial/legal authority, asset inventory, possession protocol and safeguards distinguished LPB property from Matkator, CEXP, Community/common-element and third-party rights before any main-access modification?
