# Unitary evidence digest — Sun Park / Luchy Playa Blanca / Concurso 36/2012

**Review date:** 24 August 2026  
**Status:** source-controlled evidential review; not a pleading and not a finding of criminal, civil or professional liability.  
**Scope:** repository, bilingual website, controlled evidence registers, previously extracted archive corpus and identified custody gaps.

## Executive finding

The matter is strongest when presented as a **cumulative documentary continuity case**, not as a theory dependent on one missing report or one alleged “smoking gun”. The principal supported sequence is:

1. Sun Park was operated and documented as an integrated hotel/tourist establishment while civil title remained fragmented among LPB, Matkator and other proprietors.
2. The Community of Proprietors, the Community of Exploitation and the hotel business were legally and economically distinct structures whose powers and assets could not safely be merged.
3. By June–July 2016, the dispute over exploitation expenditure, Community charges, voting, documentary custody and the debt attributed to LPB had been placed before professional advisers and was the subject of a formal notice to the insolvency administrator.
4. In 2018, Community/security/access measures and material control events occurred while title, authority, possession, exploitation and the liquidation route remained contested.
5. The 28 November 2018 transaction, its ob rem implications, the 20 March 2019 convalidation request and the 24 October 2019 decision form a discrete evidential chain that must not be retroactively collapsed into the later 2022 adjudication.
6. Later operation, refurbishment, corporate structuring, investor material, RIC references and public-incentive materials support continuity, use and value. They do **not**, without more, prove earlier criminal coordination or conscious falsity.
7. The central unresolved accountability question is whether the estate, hotel income, claims, assets, proceeds, Community debt and possible surplus were independently reconciled, protected and reported at each material stage.

The safest and strongest public formulation is therefore:

> The documents establish prior notice, contested authority, material-control events, a disputed realisation sequence and later economic continuity. They justify production, reconciliation and actor-specific explanation. They do not by themselves establish a concluded criminal scheme.

## Controlling evidence classifications

Every proposition should remain tagged internally as one of:

- **Primary-source confirmed** — original or authenticated contemporary instrument.
- **Official/judicial confirmed** — certified court, registry, notarial or administrative record.
- **Supported but incomplete** — several sources align, but a controlling original or complete file remains missing.
- **Reasonable inference** — a limited conclusion drawn from identified sources.
- **Party allegation** — a pleaded or stated position, not independently adjudicated.
- **Contradicted or qualified** — material adverse evidence exists.
- **Document pending** — exact source or certified record still required.

Public pages should use the shorter structure:

**FACT → DOCUMENT → INFERENCE → OPEN QUESTION → RIGHT OF RESPONSE**

## Legal and economic perimeters that must remain separate

For every date and act, distinguish:

1. **LPB insolvency estate:** assets, rights, claims, cash, liabilities and causes of action belonging to Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U.
2. **Civil title:** LPB units, Matkator units, third-party units and common elements.
3. **Community authority:** powers of the Community of Proprietors over validly governed common matters and legitimate expenditure.
4. **Exploitation rights:** the Community of Exploitation, operator mandates, contracts, staff, reservations, systems, goodwill and hotel income.
5. **Tourism/administrative title:** authorised operator, units, beds, classification, licence and later changes.
6. **Creditor rights:** the credit, security, units and procedural rights actually held by CAM or any predecessor at the relevant date.
7. **Possession and physical control:** access, keys, locks, security, works and exclusion, which are not automatically equivalent to title.

This separation is the single most important analytical control across the website.

## Strongest evidence in support of the matter

### 1. 1987 and 2008 hotel/exploitation instruments

**Support:** The statutes, the April 2008 collective decision and the May 2008 exploitation-transfer material support the proposition that Sun Park was conceived and operated as a hotel/tourist unit, while ownership remained finca-by-finca. They also identify a Community of Exploitation function distinct from the Community of Proprietors.

**Limit:** The precise quorum, participating proprietors, later accessions/exits, opposability, registration and any later termination or replacement must still be established from certified originals. Hotel unity does not erase separate civil title.

**Website use:** Present as the structural starting point, with a visual separating hotel unity from title fragmentation.

### 2. Jonathan Simó/CLS economic analysis and the 11 June 2016 meeting

**Support:** The controlled record identifies an economic analysis of 2008–2015 accounts and expenditure, separating hotel-operation costs from maintenance/common expenditure. The recorded meeting placed the Community debt, voting consequences, payments and supporting accounting before PwC personnel, Jonathan Simó, Francisco Mario Matos Matas and Juan Carlos Prieto Puente.

**Additional significance:** The meeting records a stated intention by the Community-side representative to conduct an exhaustive audit and prepare a further report. This is the strongest provenance lead for the still-unidentified historical report.

**Limit:** The original audio must remain preserved and compared with a clean expert transcript. Editorial annotations in later transcripts are not participant statements. The promised later audit does not prove that it was completed, outsourced to Laborý/CATRUDE or used by the insolvency administrator.

**Website use:** Elevate this to a clearly labelled **2016 knowledge checkpoint**.

### 3. Burofax of 12 July 2016 and proof of receipt

**Support:** The current controlled corpus records an integral burofax addressed to Francisco de Borja Rodríguez-Batllori Laffitte and proof of sending/receipt. Its evidential value is temporal: it places the Community-authority, debt, accounting, representation and conflict concerns before the insolvency-administration perimeter well before the May–June 2018 events.

**Limit:** Public reliance should be tied to a redacted original, proof of delivery and exact annex list. A later pleading describing the burofax is not a substitute for publishing or producing the primary source.

**Website use:** Make this the principal “notice” exhibit, with a compact box stating what it proves and what it does not prove.

### 4. Community records and access/security sequence in May–June 2018

**Support:** The 18 May 2018 Community record, witness material and the Arrecife record support a testable sequence involving an AC-related request, security/access measures, replaced locks, chains/candados, a guard and exclusion from parts of the complex. The live site correctly distinguishes a material-control event from a court-ordered delivery of possession.

**Limit:** The criminal investigation was provisionally dismissed and that outcome must remain visible. Testimony and party descriptions are not final findings. The complete certified DP 1132/2018 record, security contract, invoices, payer, instructions, logs and original communications remain necessary.

**Website use:** Preserve the formulation **“material control without a located CAM possession/eviction order”**, not “judicially proven unlawful takeover”.

### 5. Separation between LPB and non-estate property or rights

**Support:** The controlled analytical record consistently identifies LPB as the company in Concurso 36/2012 and keeps Aweswell, Matkator, third-party units, the Community of Proprietors, the Community of Exploitation and the hotel business distinct.

**Significance:** This prevents the later LPB adjudication from being used as a universal explanation for acts affecting Matkator, third-party units, exploitation rights, common elements or hotel income.

**Limit:** Each finca, contract, account, right and act still requires date-specific proof. The distinction does not itself prove misappropriation or criminal intent.

**Website use:** Add the perimeter graphic to every major page dealing with Community, AC, CAM, takeover, liquidation, RIC or MYND.

### 6. Suspension, the 28 November 2018 transaction and ob rem structure

**Support:** The filed material records a 26 June 2018 suspension affecting the realisation of specified mortgaged fincas, a 28 November 2018 sale of linked/free assets to CAM for EUR 400,000, and registered ob rem links based on destination and service. This supports a serious question about whether strategic elements were transferred while an interdependent realisation limb was suspended.

**Limit:** The complete suspension order, deed, registry entries, authority relied upon, payment trail and accounting treatment must be read together. It is unsafe to say the sale was finally declared criminal, void in every respect or never capable of later regularisation.

**Website use:** Create one transaction card that distinguishes: suspension; deed; registry objection; convalidation request; 2019 decision; later 2021–2022 steps.

### 7. Cuatrecasas communication to the Tías Property Registry, 28 December 2018

**Support:** The controlled corpus describes a formal position that the proposed/perfected CAM acquisition of 31 LPB assets conflicted with the liquidation plan, suspension decisions and certain ob rem links, with a request for negative qualification or cancellation. This is important contemporaneous corroboration that the transaction was treated by LPB/Aweswell counsel as legally serious and registry-facing.

**Limit:** It also shows that counsel took at least some action and therefore defeats any overbroad statement of complete inactivity. Privilege, confidentiality and the exact original must be reviewed before publication.

**Website use:** Describe the function and questions raised; do not publish privileged correspondence without deliberate review.

### 8. AC convalidation request of 20 March 2019 and Auto of 24 October 2019

**Support:** The 24 October 2019 decision is described as recording that the insolvency administrator sought convalidation of the 28 November 2018 deed. This is an actor-specific link between the AC and the transaction that requires a complete account of authority, purpose, disclosure, proceeds and restoration.

**Limit:** The exact operative part and legal effect of the 2019 decision must be quoted from the certified order. “Convalidation not granted” must not be expanded into a finding of criminality or automatic restoration.

**Website use:** Put the 2018 deed, 2019 application and 2019 order on one non-fragmented timeline.

### 9. 2022 adjudication and later hotel operation

**Support:** The 2022 adjudication and later MYND/Canarian Hospitality/RIC material establish a downstream corporate and economic reality and demonstrate that Sun Park retained substantial hotel/project value.

**Limit:** Later title or operation cannot retroactively legalise every earlier act. Equally, later value and financing do not prove earlier fraud, conscious falsity, coordination or misuse of public funds.

**Website use:** Repeat this limiting sentence wherever downstream investor, tax-incentive or public-funding material appears.

### 10. AC accounting, recovery, fees and removal record

**Support:** The live appeals and the documentary requests concerning estate accounts, claims, fees, recovery actions, income and final reconciliation keep the accountability questions current. They support independent audit and production requests.

**Limit:** The existence of appeals or complaints does not establish their merits. Do not imply that the appellate court has already accepted the underlying allegations.

**Website use:** Show live procedural status, the precise relief sought and the last verified date.

### 11. Laborý/CATRUDE historical-report trail

**Support:** March and May 2026 communications preserve a genuine recollection/search for a historic technical antecedent around 2018–2019. The 2016 promised audit supplies a plausible Community-side provenance route.

**Limit:** The report itself remains unidentified. There is no primary proof of title, author, commissioning lawyer, client, payer, recipient, AC receipt, filing in Concurso 36/2012 or use in Arrecife. `INFORME FAMARA.pdf` is excluded.

**Website use:** Keep it as a controlled custody and production question. It must not be used as affirmative proof of concealment or AC knowledge.

- Public RIC, investor and promotional materials: prove representations, project continuity and possible value; not earlier mens rea.
- Corporate relationships and later shared forums: identify possible custodians or conflicts to test; do not prove coordination.
- Press reporting: useful for chronology and public representations; not proof of title or judicial authority.
- The user’s March 2026 notes: prove recollection and the direction of the search, not the contents or provenance of the missing report.

2. The 2018 Arrecife investigation was provisionally dismissed and the appellate result must be accurately described.
3. The calificación produced an adverse first-instance outcome and remains subject to the exact live appellate position.
4. The Community, AC, CAM and other actors may rely on apparent authority, court supervision, later adjudication, operational necessity and registry or administrative acts; those explanations must be presented and tested.
5. Monterecco/Pink operated under real documents and administrative records for a defined perimeter; the chain from CEXP, its scope and any nullity remain contested.
6. Physical access, keys, operation, possession, civil title and legal authority are distinct; no one fact resolves all five.
7. No global damages figure should be stated until asset, income, claims, proceeds and counterfactuals are independently reconciled.
8. A failure to locate a document is not proof that it never existed or was concealed.
