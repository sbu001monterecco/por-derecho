## 3. Criminal-first, non-fragmented source synthesis

The nine verified filings are not nine isolated narratives. Read in their own terms, they ask the Ministerio Fiscal to test connected but actor-specific mechanisms:

1. **June-2018 financed exit / obstruction:** whether a developed and financed exit from Concurso 36/2012 was deliberately obstructed and, if so, by whom, with what knowledge and causal effect.
2. **OB REM / 2018 locales / funds:** whether non-convalidated acts, desvinculación and receipt/use of funds created an autonomous criminally relevant trace requiring account reconstruction.
3. **Liquidation-plan execution:** whether the later material/economic execution diverged from the approved framework and how the €13.168m and separate monetary lines were sourced, authorised and satisfied.
4. **Extraprocessual use of a non-final resolution:** whether a non-final concursal calificación/resolution was used outside its procedural setting in a way requiring autonomous investigation.
5. **AC funds management:** bank/account destination, disclosure and reporting of liquidation-phase funds, including the approximately €400,000 line.
6. **Hotel-unit / exploitation instrumentalisation:** whether fragmented ownership/community/exploitation structures were knowingly used as inputs into insolvency, control, valuation or exploitation consequences beyond their lawful perimeter.
7. **Community/accounting reciprocal-validation theory:** whether community governance, maintenance charges, accounts/certificates and concursal positions reinforced one another without the independent documentary foundation alleged to be required.
8. **AC acts and omissions:** whether individual acts/omissions affecting estate integrity, impartiality/transparency/diligence and administration of justice meet any criminal threshold after contrary material and available remedies are tested.
9. **Coordination indicia / investment-promotion material:** whether later public investment material and already-reported events justify functional connection and investigative impulse, without converting the filing into an appeal or merits revision.

These are **allegations and investigative requests**, not adjudicated facts. The same source graph can generate criminal, concursal, civil, mercantile, administrative, regulatory/public-funds, disciplinary and recovery outputs, but those outputs keep distinct legal tests and remedies.

## 6. Privacy and evidential boundary

The public derivative omits NIE/DNI, street address, email, telephone and free-text `Expone/Solicita`. It retains only registration/attachment provenance needed for verification. Registration and hash equality prove delivery/file identity only within the receipt chain; they do **not** prove the truth, legal classification, institutional adoption, examination, investigation, intent, causation or guilt.

The raw current filings should remain source evidence and need not be republished publicly merely to prove provenance. The combination `REGAGE + registered filename + SHA-512 + annex-page range` is sufficient to bind the public derivative to the registered source while minimising personal data.
