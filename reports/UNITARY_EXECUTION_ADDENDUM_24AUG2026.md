The central accountability question is not whether private actors pursued their own interests. Their conduct remains subject to actor-specific proof. The primary institutional question is:

> Given the pre-existing Community/dissident conflict, the 2016 accounting/authority warnings, the entry and later alignment of CAM/Acosta Matos interests, and the material-control and transaction events of 2018, what exact protective measures did the court-appointed Administrador Concursal take — before, during and after those events — to protect LPB and the insolvency estate?
