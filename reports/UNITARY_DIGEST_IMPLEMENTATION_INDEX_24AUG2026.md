## Central finding

The Cuatrecasas Registry intervention prevents an assertion of complete lawyer inactivity. It also creates a documentary benchmark:

> If external counsel considered the risk serious enough to approach the Property Registry, the Administrador Concursal should identify the contemporaneous protection, investigation, Court communication, restitution, Registry, proceeds and accounting measures taken from the court-appointed office.

This is a production and explanation request, not a finding of breach, collusion, corruption or criminal intent.

## Evidence and publication controls

- No private email body, attachment identifier/token, private address, personal telephone number or mixed unredacted archive is included.
- No privileged advice is quoted or published.
- No criminal, civil, insolvency or professional-liability conclusion is stated.
- The provisional dismissal and adverse procedural outcomes remain part of the controlling record.
- Later title, operation, financing or incentives do not retroactively determine the legality or knowledge attached to earlier acts.
- Commissioner, client, payer, intermediary, recipient and later user are separate roles.
- Physical access, possession, title, Community authority, exploitation rights and tourism/operator authority remain separate.
- “No responsive action yet located” always carries a production requirement and is not converted into “no action existed”.
