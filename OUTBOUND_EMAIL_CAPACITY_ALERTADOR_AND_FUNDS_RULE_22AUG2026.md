# Mandatory external-email capacity, reporting and public-funds rule — 22 August 2026

**Status:** canonical control for all substantive external Project Sun Rock / Por
Derecho emails. It implements a continuing instruction from Gil Marer. It is a
drafting and evidence-control rule, not legal advice, a power of attorney or a
substitute for the applicable statutory or procedural channel.

## 1. Scope and priority

Apply this rule to every substantive external message that requests preservation,
records, correction, routing, a meeting, a response, a public-authority action,
professional review, investor/commercial engagement or an alert concerning the
Sun Park / LPB / MYND Yaiza record. This includes messages to public bodies,
court or insolvency custodians, professional firms, companies, regulators,
funding bodies and journalists.

It does not authorise a send. A message still requires a recipient-specific
evidence, authority, language, duplication and delivery-history check. Do not
put private addresses, Gmail identifiers, privileged material or non-public
personal data into this public repository.

Every message within scope must identify Gil Marer, in terms appropriate to the
recipient and issue, as a **directly affected and interested party**. Where the
corporate context is material, use only the capacity actually supported by the
current record:

> e interesada. Actúo asimismo como director de AWESWELL LIMITED y, respecto de
> los intereses propios de MATKATOR, S.L., como su administrador.

For clarity, a message that touches LUCHY PLAYA BLANCA, S.L.U. (`LPB`) must add a
limitation equivalent to the following unless a current, specific authority says

> No pretendo ejercer por este correo facultades de administración, disposición
> o representación de LPB o de su masa, que corresponden al régimen concursal y,
> en su caso, a los órganos legalmente competentes. Tampoco comparezco como
> representante de una Comunidad de Propietarios o de una Comunidad de
> Explotación salvo acreditación expresa y vigente para el acto concreto.

Do not call Gil a sole director, sole administrator or LPB representative unless
the exact current corporate or procedural authority has been independently
verified for that communication.

## 3. Ley 2/2023 status — mandatory but conditional formulation

Every message within scope must also include the following, or an equally exact
variant:

> Actúo asimismo, para los efectos que procedan y en la medida aplicable, como
> persona informante o alertador a los efectos de la Ley 2/2023, de 20 de
> febrero. Solicito un tratamiento confidencial compatible con el cauce y la
> normativa aplicables. Esta referencia no presupone que este correo haya sido
> admitido en un sistema interno de información ni que se haya reconocido un
> estatuto protector específico.

Never state or imply that a recipient has accepted a formal protected report,
that the recipient must operate an internal information system, that the statutory
protection has automatically attached, or that confidentiality overrides a lawful
disclosure, procedural or data-protection duty.

## 4. RIC, regional-incentive and European-funds wording

Where the subject makes the broader funding record relevant, use the following
bounded formulation:

> La solicitud pide preservar y verificar, sin afirmar irregularidad, la
> coherencia de los registros relativos a la RIC, a RIC Private Equity como
> entidad diferenciada, a los incentivos regionales —incluido, cuando proceda,
> el expediente GC/836/P06— y a cualquier operación, cofinanciación o control
> europeo/FEDER que quede acreditado en fuentes primarias.

The message must immediately make clear that it is **not** asserting that a
particular recipient, Costa Papagayo, AFISCA, LPB, RIC Private Equity, Hotel New
Trend, MYND Yaiza or another person or entity received European funds, received a
payment, committed double funding, misused funds or acted unlawfully.

The following distinctions are non-negotiable:

1. **RIC** (`Reserva para Inversiones en Canarias`) is not **RIC Private Equity**.
2. `GC/836/P06` is a regional-incentives file; it is not automatically an
   EU/ERDF operation.
3. A reference to funding, an image, a public webinar, an institutional receipt,
   professional proximity or a recipient's silence is not proof of knowledge,
   coordination, liability, double funding or wrongdoing.
4. A Project Sun Rock allegation concerning potential overlap remains an
   allegation requiring asset-, cost-, job-, title- and source-and-use
   reconciliation. It must not be recast as an adjudicated finding.

Do not add amounts, payment claims, alleged duplication or beneficiary status
unless they are necessary to the recipient-specific request and traceable to a
primary source with the relevant qualification.

## 5. Content and evidence architecture

Every substantive message must separate, in this order where relevant:

1. **verified documentary fact**;
2. **stated allegation or dispute**;
3. **reasonable inference**;
4. **unresolved question**; and
5. **finite requested action**.

Use the smallest coherent request: preserve specified records, identify a
custodian, provide a procedural status, identify a lawful channel, correct the
record, or meet. Invite the recipient to provide correction, context, a contrary
source or an authoritative contact. Do not convert a request for preservation
into an accusation.

Before sending, confirm all of the following:

1. exact recipient and role, including whether a historical address is still an
   appropriate copy recipient;
2. no recent duplicate package was already sent to the same person or body;
3. sender capacity and LPB limitation are present and accurate;
4. the conditional Ley 2/2023 paragraph is present;
5. RIC, RIC Private Equity, regional incentives and possible EU/FEDER material
   are separated and cautiously framed;
6. each attachment is the approved version and is actually attached, not merely
   mentioned;
7. the webinar link is relevant and its evidential limits are stated;
8. no assertion exceeds the cited primary source or the stated allegation;
9. a correction/right-of-reply route is present; and
10. the user has expressly authorised the actual send.
