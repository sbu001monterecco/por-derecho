## Unit of analysis
Create an interaction envelope for **professional × client × matter × period**. Parallel mandates are allowed. Never infer one envelope from another.

Required fields: identity/firm ID; client; matter; inside/outside Concurso; mandate status; dates; client instructions; work actually performed; filings; AC interaction; adverse-party interaction where relevant; information available at the time; fees (source, payer, authority, amount, timing); professional reaction; effect on continuity/independence; formal transition reason; attributed Gil/Patricia allegation; strongest contrary evidence; strongest lawful alternative; causation status; knowledge/intent status; source completeness; open evidence; right-of-reply; publication classification.

## Truth-machine sequence
**observe → manifest → compare → classify → traverse dependencies → adversarial review → reconcile → exact-head verify → deploy if applicable → live readback → new envelope → recurse until closure.**

- **observe:** native contemporaneous records and custody.
- **manifest:** who said/did what, when and in what capacity.
- **compare:** instructions, filings, invoices, handovers, court records, later accounts and the professional's explanation.
- **classify:** fact, official act, attributed allegation, inference, counterevidence, gap.
- **traverse dependencies:** mandate, fees, authority, access, AC/adverse-party interaction, outcome and replacement counsel without transferring responsibility.
- **adversarial review:** strongest innocent/professional explanation and contrary chronology.
- **reconcile:** names, firms, clients, matters, dates and conflicting sources.
- **exact-head verify:** re-check current repository/register heads before mutation or publication.
- **deploy/readback:** only when authorised; publish smallest public-safe derivative and verify live.
- **recurse:** new contradiction, lawyer, matter, invoice, email, filing or transition reason creates a new envelope.

## Anti-inference rules
- Number of lawyer changes is **not evidence** of AC causation.
- Withdrawal is not proof the AC caused withdrawal.
- Payment is not proof of bribery or improper inducement.
- Contact is not proof of influence.
- A lawyer's later disagreement with the client is not proof earlier allegations were false.
- A lawyer's earlier agreement is not proof later misconduct.
- A procedural route/adverse ruling is not proof of criminal purpose.
- Similar mechanisms may be compared, but motive, knowledge, legality and causation remain event-specific.
- Garrigues remains in the genealogy although no AC causal link is presently alleged.
- Current counsel are included for continuity/audit, not retrospectively forced into a former-counsel attrition thesis.
