**Control ID:** PD-TM-COUNSEL-20260922-01

**Applies to:** every verified project-side lawyer, legal-team professional and procurador recorded in `assets/data/legal-professionals-register-v1.json`, across matters inside and outside Concurso 36/2012.

## Controlling purpose

This rule turns the professional-history question into a supervised evidence problem:

`person → firm → engagement → client → matter → event → source → claim-state → counterevidence → causal status`

It is not a blame list and it does not assign guilt scores. The machine asks what each professional actually did, what the Administrador Concursal or another actor actually did in relation to that mandate, what practical effect is evidenced, what lawful/innocent explanation exists, and what remains unproved.

The chronology starts with **Garrigues in 2012**, because the professional history must not begin artificially after entry into Concurso 36/2012. Later mandates overlap; they are not forced into a single succession.

Gil Marer alleges a recurring pattern of **institutional pressure, interference with independent professional relationships, economic and informational leverage, strategic isolation, and professional/commercial attrition**. The phrase is an attributed investigative hypothesis. It is not a judicial finding and is not a substitute for actor-specific proof.

## Truth Machine execution

For every lawyer, firm, procurador or legal-team professional:

1. Identify the exact person, firm, historical capacity, client, matter and date window.
2. Separate work **inside Concurso 36/2012** from criminal, Community, banking, tax, enforcement, transaction, regulatory and other outside-concurso work.
3. Retrieve the best available native/primary evidence before relying on summaries.
4. Distinguish individual evidence from firm/team-level context; do not transfer firm knowledge or conduct to every lawyer.
5. Classify every proposition as VERIFIED FACT, PARTY ALLEGATION, EVIDENCE-BASED INFERENCE, UNRESOLVED QUESTION, CONTRADICTED/CORRECTED or SUPERSEDED.
6. Test the mechanism actually evidenced: mandate displacement; professional/regulatory escalation; economic leverage; intermediation/access; information/control asymmetry; relationship disruption; alternative-pathway impairment; cumulative isolation/attrition.
7. Preserve the strongest contrary evidence, formal withdrawal reason, lawful alternative and innocent explanation beside the allegation.
8. Keep causation, knowledge, purpose, intent and criminal significance as separate fields.
9. Re-run the analysis recursively when a new native source, mandate record, payment record, filing/access record, venia/withdrawal record, contrary source or later decision changes a node.
10. Never use a numeric “truth score”. The machine reports evidential state and unresolved proof.

## Non-transfer firewall

- Lawyer ≠ firm; firm ≠ every partner or employee.
- Lawyer ≠ procurador.
- One mandate ≠ all mandates.
- One proceeding ≠ all proceedings.
- Presence in a CC chain ≠ retainer.
- Payment ≠ bribery.
- Contact ≠ improper influence.
- Withdrawal ≠ AC causation.
- A procedural licence ≠ wrongdoing.
- A later structural judicial finding ≠ retroactive proof of earlier intent.
- A professional relationship does not transfer client knowledge, intent, conduct or liability.

## Interaction-state rule

The machine-readable file `assets/data/counsel-professional-interaction-truth-machine-v1.json` assigns every register record an **interaction state**, **lane**, **attribution level** and **counterevidence/boundary**. An interaction state describes the present evidential problem; it is not an accusation or ranking.

Direct person-level states are reserved for sources that identify that professional. Team-level states require a further source bridge before person-specific publication. `NO_DIRECT_ACT_LOCATED`, `ROLE_REVIEW` and `MANDATE_SCOPE_OPEN` are affirmative uncertainty states and must not be silently upgraded.

## Digitisation and file rule

For relevant professional evidence, track: LOCATED → NATIVE_PRESERVED → TEXT_EXTRACTED → OCR_ONLY_IF_NEEDED → MANUALLY_VERIFIED → PUBLIC_SAFE_DERIVATIVE → SOURCE_LINKED → COUNTEREVIDENCE_LINKED.

Raw privileged advice, private email bodies, provider message IDs, unnecessary personal data, private tax/fee ledgers and live strategy stay outside public Git. Public surfaces use proposition-level summaries and approved derivatives. “Digitised” never means “proved”.
