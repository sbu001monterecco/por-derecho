## Automation and drift prevention

Any change to a controlled node, route, status, evidence state or relationship must update all affected derivatives in the same pull request or record an explicit blocking gap.

The focused validator must fail if:

- Control 24 disappears;
- Control 24 becomes a proceeding or receives a NIG/DP/reparto status without source-led governance;
- the Control 22 → DP 1956 edge is upgraded from `OPEN_REFERENCE` without a certified bridge;
- the three vertices are merged;
- Fiscalía becomes a fourth liability vertex;
- the 25 June supplement, CGPJ route, Alzada, DIP 2/2026, ICALPA 80/2026, AC separation/fees or RPL 2523/2025 becomes unreachable;
- the bilingual hub or global interlink surface disappears;
- the canonical search aliases drift; or
- an allegation/inference is rendered as an established fact.

Do not bypass a validator by renaming or deleting the controlled identifiers. Correct the graph and provenance instead.
