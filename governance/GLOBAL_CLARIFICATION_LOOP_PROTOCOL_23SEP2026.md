# Global Clarification Loop / Truth Machine protocol — 23 September 2026

**Control:** PD-CLAR-GLOBAL-20260923-01
**Purpose:** persistent human-clarification and evidence-reconciliation layer for Por Derecho / Project Sun Rock.

## Core rule
Clarification exists to resolve uncertainty, not to obtain adoption of the project position. A/B/C/D are equally legitimate outcomes. Always allow free text.

## Response choices
- **A — ADOPT:** proposition accurately reflects my recollection/position.
- **B — QUALIFY:** broadly correct but requires material correction/addition.
- **C — REJECT:** materially wrong; my account differs.
- **D — OPEN:** cannot presently confirm; preserve and seek evidence.

An answer is human evidence, not independent verification.

## Declarant separation
Gil Marer and Patricia Domínguez always receive separate answer objects. Never merge their recollections. Record declarant, date/time if available, firsthand/inference/legal-characterisation status, source conversation/email/document, and supersession links.

## Lifecycle
OPEN → HUMAN_CLARIFIED → SOURCE_CORROBORATED → ADVERSARIALLY_TESTED → RECONCILED → CANONICAL.

Additional states: CONFLICTED, SUPERSEDED, UNRESOLVED, DISPROVED.

CANONICAL is reopenable. New primary evidence can reopen any proposition while preserving history.

## Resolution envelope
Every material incident/proposition may maintain:
QUESTION → GIL → PATRICIA → PRIMARY SOURCES → ADVERSE SOURCES → CONTRADICTIONS → FOLLOW-UPS → RECONCILIATION → CURRENT CANONICAL STATE → OPEN PROOF → CONFIDENCE → PUBLIC-SAFE DERIVATIVE.

## Automatic reopening
Reopen/generate a clarification when:
- a primary source conflicts with a human answer;
- Gil and Patricia materially differ;
- dates/numbers conflict;
- a missing causal link becomes visible;
- professional/client capacity changes;
- inside/outside-concurso rights are collapsed;
- later approval is used to explain earlier authority;
- an adverse source supplies a plausible alternative;
- a criminal/deontological element remains unproved;
- a material proposition is being promoted beyond its source class.

Never delete the earlier answer. Mark conflict/supersession and preserve lineage.

## Anti-fragmentation
Digest connected events unitary across the AWESWELL/Sun Park platform, inside and outside Concurso 36/2012. Reject civil-only, concurso-only, LPB-only, payment-only and one-document atomisation where they obscure dependencies. Anti-fragmentation never means collective attribution: actor/entity/duty/knowledge/intent/causation/liability remain separate.

## Priority
Queue questions by materiality × uncertainty × contradiction × causal centrality × evidential opportunity. Highest-value questions surface first. Do not repeatedly ask settled low-value questions.

## ChatGPT interaction
Default compact gate:
**Which best reflects your recollection/position?**
A. Adopt.
B. Partly adopt — correction needed.
C. Reject.
D. Cannot confirm — keep open and seek evidence.

Allow free text instead of a letter. Ask only the minimum follow-up needed. Identify whether Gil or Patricia is answering.

## Email interaction
Email is optional asynchronous transport, not canonical storage. A clarification email must contain stable CLAR ID, proposition/question, A/B/C/D meanings and an invitation for free text. A reply becomes a source-attributed answer only after ingestion into the registry. It never automatically becomes VERIFIED/CANONICAL. No email sending is authorised by this protocol.

## System roles
- **ChatGPT:** interview, source synthesis, contradiction detection, question generation and reconciliation.
- **Google Drive/private files:** source-grade human statements, private evidence, full supporting dossiers.
- **GitHub:** canonical machine-readable clarification state/control plane.
- **GitLab:** independent parity/adversarial/provenance/publication verification plane.
- **Email:** optional question/answer transport.

## Truth safeguards
Preserve adverse evidence. Never silently harmonise conflicts. Chronology is not causation. Payment is not bribery by itself. Professional status is not mens rea. Judicial approval is material evidence but does not automatically answer every antecedent authority question. Silence/non-response is not admission.

## Cross-thread rule
All existing/future threads discovering a material unresolved proposition must search/reuse the stable CLAR ID if one exists, append evidence/answers, and avoid duplicate competing questions. Re-resolve repository heads before writes.

## Closure
A clarification is not closed merely because A/B/C/D was answered. Closure requires appropriate source testing for its materiality and an explicit current state with open-proof status.
