# Financial-Assistance Separation — Public Disclosure Control

**Date:** 24 September 2026  
**Status:** Sanitized governance receipt for the private-capital gateway; not legal advice and not a financing commitment.

## Approved public principle

Sponsor-level acquisition or control capital and property/project finance are separate funding requirements.

Any future project financing would be provided to the verified project/property borrower or ring-fenced ProjectCo, with controlled proceeds restricted to documented property, development, completion, operating, reserve, tax and fee uses. It would not be intended to fund the acquisition of shares in the operating or asset-owning company.

The sponsor may use its own resources and seller-accepted sponsor-level deferred consideration. The target company is not intended to provide funds, guarantees, security or other financial assistance for the acquisition of its own shares.

## Required public disclaimers

- The opportunity remains conditional and subject to diligence.
- No financing is committed or available on final terms.
- No definitive acquisition agreement has been completed unless separately stated.
- The financing structure, security, priority and permitted uses remain subject to legal, tax, regulatory, lender and definitive-document review.
- No public page constitutes an offer, invitation to invest, legal opinion or assurance that any transaction will complete.

## Prohibited public formulations

Do not say:

- “The lender finances the acquisition of the target.”
- “Project finance pays for the shares.”
- “The target’s assets secure the purchase of its own shares.”
- “The acquisition is financed by the target.”
- “The whole acquisition is asset-backed by the target.”

Use instead:

- “Sponsor-level share consideration.”
- “Separate property/project financing.”
- “Seller-accepted sponsor-level deferred consideration.”
- “Ring-fenced project proceeds.”
- “No target-company financial assistance is intended.”

## Firewall

This public GitHub receipt must not contain target names, seller identities, ownership assumptions, share price, deposits, note terms, lender names, legal advice, claims, bankable sources-and-uses or private correspondence. Those belong in the restricted GitLab/counsel record.
