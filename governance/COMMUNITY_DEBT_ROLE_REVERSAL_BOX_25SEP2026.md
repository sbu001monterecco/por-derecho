## 11. Comparative legal note

LPH article 15.2 makes payment status legally relevant to voting, while preserving exceptions where debts are judicially challenged or appropriately consigned. The case-specific question is therefore not whether arrears can ever affect voting; it is whether the **particular debt, due-status, challenge/consignation position, allocation and voting filter** used at Sun Park were legally and economically valid at each meeting.

Formal legal conclusions require the historical applicable text, complete litigation record and competent legal review.
