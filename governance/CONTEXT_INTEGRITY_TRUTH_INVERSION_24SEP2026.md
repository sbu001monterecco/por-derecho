# Context integrity and truth-inversion review

**Control:** PD-CONTEXT-INTEGRITY-20260924-01  
**Scope:** an additive extension to the Unitary Review Toolkit, R33/AC claim graph and professional-interaction Truth Machine. It creates no new claim family, offence, legal power, public allegation or automatic decision.  
**Research checked:** 24 September 2026. This is a project-designed operational taxonomy informed by the primary sources below, not a claim that an authority adopted these forty labels.

## 1. Controlling distinction

**Literal truth deployed to generate a false or materially misleading implication** is a hypothesis about the relationship between a source and its later use. It is not synonymous with fabricated words. An accurate quotation proves, at most, what the source records as said; it does not automatically prove that the statement's content is true, that the original speaker meant it literally, or that a later inference follows.

**ES:** Uso de una cita o dato literalmente fiel para inducir una inferencia falsa o materialmente engañosa mediante selección, descontextualización, supresión de contexto relevante o cambio de su función probatoria. La fidelidad literal, la verdad de lo afirmado, el significado contextual, la inferencia, el conocimiento, la intención y el efecto son preguntas separadas.

Keep seven separate objects: (1) source and exact words; (2) underlying asserted fact; (3) original context and speech act; (4) later use event and invited inference; (5) material context restored; (6) actor-specific knowledge/intent; (7) actual reliance, consequence and applicable legal elements. Each needs its own source state.

A short accurate summary can legitimately omit irrelevant detail. A protected/redacted source need not be published wholesale. The test is the **minimum material context necessary to avoid misleading the intended audience**. Context present in the full filing but visually buried, selectively emphasized, or absent from the summary is classified accurately: do not claim it was absent everywhere.

## 2. The farmer analogy: illustration, not evidence

The user supplied an accident-story illustration attributed to Ronald Reagan. Historical authorship/version attribution is not independently verified by this control. Use it as an explicitly invented teaching example, not as a proved event or a source about any real lawyer, officer or party.

Within the illustration, the farmer's reassuring words are accurately repeated. The missing explanation is fear after the officer's treatment of injured animals. **The fact of the utterance can be accurate while the proposed inference of no injury is unsupported.** The story does not establish the degree of injury, the later lawyer's knowledge of the context, or intention to deceive. No allegation of analogous threats in R33 follows from this metaphor.

## 3. Forty candidate mechanisms and their falsification tests

These are review prompts, not labels to attach automatically to a person. Test the same mechanism in our own pleadings, captions, graphics, translations and AI outputs. A cue can produce no finding, an accurate limited use, a narrowing, a scope mismatch or a supported misleading-inference assessment. Deliberateness remains separate.

| ID | Candidate mechanism | Required comparison / legitimate alternative |
|---|---|---|
| CI-001 | Selective quotation / quote mining | Compare selected words with their source passage and relevant linked records. Omitted irrelevant detail is permissible. |
| CI-002 | Negation, exception or qualifier deletion | Check ellipses and cropped lines for a deleted not, unless, only, subject to, or reservation. Typography alone is not proof. |
| CI-003 | Question-answer detachment | Preserve the actual question, preceding answer and follow-up; distinguish answering a narrow question from making a universal assertion. |
| CI-004 | Modal/conditional flattening | Compare may, could, planned, proposed and conditional terms with the later categorical claim. Conditions genuinely fulfilled need proof. |
| CI-005 | Speech-act / pressure erasure | Test whether thanks, apology, concession, politeness, irony or a reported fear is wrongly treated as factual admission, waiver or consent. Do not infer fear or coercion from a polite phrase. |
| CI-006 | Translation/transcription shift | Compare native language, OCR, punctuation, speaker attribution and versions. Preserve uncertainty; translation or recognition error may be innocent. |
| CI-007 | Misleading recontextualisation | Identify the original purpose and the later argumentative use; explain exactly what changed. New uses may be valid with proper qualifications. |
| CI-008 | True premise, false implication | Write premise and invited conclusion separately; identify the missing inferential step and evidence that would support or defeat it. |
| CI-009 | Allegation/opinion promoted to fact | Trace asserted, suspected, reported, expert opinion and official findings to their actual source status. Mere citation is not adoption. |
| CI-010 | Presupposition / loaded question | Isolate the premise embedded in the question and check whether it was ever established. Asking a genuine open question is not deception. |
| CI-011 | Equivocation / definition switch | Lock the meaning of terms such as owner, operator, debt, payment, approval and collaboration at each use. Legitimate different definitions must be stated. |
| CI-012 | Some-to-all generalisation | Test quantifiers and populations; one lawyer's apology cannot itself explain all departures. A genuinely representative basis needs evidence. |
| CI-013 | Chronology truncation | Restore relevant before/after steps, conditions, responses and supersession. Do not treat every remote event as material. |
| CI-014 | Retrospective knowledge | Separate event date, acquisition, receipt, reading and later adoption; a later exhibit cannot backdate personal knowledge. |
| CI-015 | Cause/effect reversal | Test order, mechanism and competing causes. A downstream reaction is not automatically the cause of an upstream event. |
| CI-016 | Correlation/benefit as causation | Identify the act, transmission and causal bridge; test lawful independent grounds and alternative causes. Benefit is not procurement or intent. |
| CI-017 | No search hit as nonexistence | Record corpus, query, dates, pagination, exclusions and expected recordkeeping. Absence becomes probative only after a reasoned completeness/expectation test. |
| CI-018 | Silence as admission | Record actual notice, opportunity, duties and protected reasons for nonresponse. Silence alone does not prove guilt or acceptance. |
| CI-019 | Character/motive attack instead of answer | Separate rhetoric about AI, obsession, length or motives from the factual proposition it purportedly answers. Tone alone does not establish falsity. |
| CI-020 | Judgment-scope migration | Compare parties, assets, dates, issues, posture, holding and remedy with the later use. Preserve genuine adverse holdings. |
| CI-021 | Procedural/finality inflation | Keep draft, filing, receipt, routing, admission, examination, decision, appeal and finality distinct. A dismissal is read by its actual grounds. |
| CI-022 | Approval as implementation | Separate permission/order from completed payment, conveyance, restoration, preservation or compliance. Trace execution independently. |
| CI-023 | Party recital as judicial finding | Distinguish a pleading quoted in a judgment from facts found, reasons adopted and operative relief. |
| CI-024 | Institutional as personal knowledge | Identify recipient, office, personal actor, date and actual transmission; institutional custody alone does not establish individual reading. |
| CI-025 | Entity/capacity substitution | Keep individual, company, estate, Community, CEXP, adviser, judge and AC distinct; link only the correct date-specific role. |
| CI-026 | Narrow denial as complete answer | State precisely what the denial covers and what it leaves unanswered. A legally correct limited denial is not necessarily evasive. |
| CI-027 | Strawman / false binary | Compare the position actually advanced with the one answered; allow multiple legally/factually plausible explanations. |
| CI-028 | Burden shift / impossible proof | Identify the actual legal burden, stage and accessible evidence; lack of opponent proof does not establish our hypothesis. |
| CI-029 | Fragmentation of cumulative evidence | Examine related items together and separately; document their joint effect without treating repetition as corroboration. |
| CI-030 | Circular / duplicate corroboration | Trace forwarding, quotations, derivative summaries and shared origins; two hosts, several models or many citations can repeat one source. |
| CI-031 | Association/motive replacing elements | Map each person's act, knowledge and relevant offence elements; relationship, office or incentive never supplies a missing element. |
| CI-032 | Financial category / payer conflation | Separate quote, offer, valuation, debt, recognised claim, reported payment, actual debit, recipient credit, ultimate source and beneficial use. |
| CI-033 | Denominator / sample selection | State universe, eligibility, exclusions, version and missingness; show how selection could change the result. |
| CI-034 | Unsupported numerical precision | Demand reproducible inputs and a named metric; prohibit invented credibility, lie, guilt or truth percentages and unsupported distributions. |
| CI-035 | Visual implication / crop | Audit image crop, arrows, colour, placement, captions and accessibility text against the same source rules; an illustration is not an exhibit of fact. |
| CI-036 | Witness variation / incentive omission | Preserve original notes/recordings, previous versions, exact questions, relevant benefits and contrary explanations. Variation alone is not lying. |
| CI-037 | Custody as substantive verification | A matching hash establishes byte identity, not authenticity, completeness, accuracy, legality, signature validity or merits. |
| CI-038 | Pipeline state as completion | Distinguish code written, locally tested, remote checked, merged, deployed, live-read and evidence reviewed. No completion by label alone. |
| CI-039 | Statement/composite double counting | Keep paragraph-level and statement-level records linked; paired annotations of one relation are not independent findings. |
| CI-040 | Own-output confirmation loop | Reopen our earlier conclusions against native evidence, not later AI summaries quoting those conclusions. A model-generated score is not corroboration. |

## 4. Investigation and prosecution-inspired operating sequence

**P01 — Scope and preservation.** Define matter, authority to access, source universe, dates and custodians. Preserve unchanged originals and complete message/attachment families lawfully; hash derivatives separately; log missing or inaccessible material. Prioritize genuinely perishable evidence. Maintain privilege, privacy, witness protection and legal-hold boundaries. Foreign investigative powers are not ours. [S1, S4, S5, S6, S7]

**P02 — Independent first reading.** Record literal content before reading the preferred thesis where practicable. Preserve both relevant favourable and unfavourable sources. Review a counter-hypothesis; do not manufacture symmetry where evidence is unequal. A fresh AI pass is not an independent witness or source. [S1, S2, S4, S5, S6]

**P03 — Decompose and reconnect.** Preserve immutable source -> block -> statement -> annotation IDs, then retain parent paragraph and material cross-document dependencies. Atomisation must not erase meaning; a whole-matter claim must not erase procedural distinctions. [Project implementation]

**P04 — Identify the use event.** Bind the exact later passage, speaker/author, capacity, date, audience, proceeding and proposition the statement is invoked to establish. Label an analyst-inferred implication as such. Preserve explicit caveats. An unresolved use event remains open. [S3; project implementation]

**P05 — Restore minimum material context.** Show side-by-side: original statement; later use; restored context; resulting difference. Test actor, capacity, time, object, proceeding, issue, quantifier and procedural posture. State whether restoration confirms, narrows, reverses, changes confidence in, or does not materially affect the inference. Not every disagreement is a contradiction. [S3; project implementation]

**P06 — Actively seek disconfirmation.** Pursue reasonable, relevant and proportionate enquiries both ways; record precise queries, custody limits and negative results. Seek the best lawful/professional explanation and identify the evidence that would defeat our hypothesis. Do not demand unrestricted private records or infer wrongdoing from privilege. [S1, S2, S4, S5, S6]

**P07 — Witness and communication integrity.** Preserve question-answer sequence, notes, recordings and substantive variations; distinguish personal adoption from third-party summary. Record relevant benefits, pressure allegations and supporting/contrary evidence individually. Use open, non-leading questions as a project interviewing safeguard; this tool does not conduct interviews or contact witnesses. No demeanour, voice, emotion or linguistic-pattern lie detector. [S4 for preservation/variation/benefits; other safeguards are project design]

**P08 — Trace money in parallel.** Reconcile origin -> account/balance -> authority/invoice -> debit -> recipient credit -> use/beneficiary, with dates and alternative inflows. A reported payment is not a completed bank trace; fungible funds require a justified tracing method, not a coloured arrow. Keep civil, insolvency, tax and criminal questions separate. [S7; project implementation]

**P09 — Test cumulative materiality.** Revisit combinations of independently relevant items, including adverse material. Do not discard an item merely because it is inconclusive alone. Do not multiply weight by the number of derivatives, annotations or repeated allegations. [S2, S4]

**P10 — Build actor/element and use/consequence matrices.** For each proposed offence or professional breach record applicable jurisdiction, law at the act date, procedural stage, actor/capacity, conduct/duty, required mental element, source, counterevidence, causation, effect, defence and open proof. A misleading implication is not automatically fraud, criminal falsity, perjury or obstruction. Separate possible misleading effect, actual receipt, actual reliance and subjective knowledge. Obtain competent legal review before a legal conclusion or filing. [S1-S6 support evidence discipline, not a universal offence test]

**P11 — Review, qualify and correct.** Require an attributable source review tied to exact inputs, with the strongest contrary record and lawful explanation visible. A supplied reviewer name/hash is accountability metadata, not identity authentication. Close a false positive explicitly. Restrict, qualify or withhold a public claim when material context cannot lawfully be shown. Preserve equal-visibility corrections and replies. [S2-S4; project implementation]

**P12 — Recurse without rewriting history.** New source/version/context/counterevidence -> dependency-review queue -> fresh source comparison -> recorded changed/no-change decision -> affected chronology/claim/actor/proceeding/source/visual/reader update -> existing release gates and live readback where applicable. Preserve superseded assessments. The queue itself is not an executed review, revised page or continuous background process. [Project implementation]

## 7. Implementation and integration

`legaltech/unitary_review/context_integrity.py` adds a separate context/inference envelope to the existing toolkit. It does not replace `engine.py`, the preserved legacy prototype, the pending Truth Machine v2, or the R33 continuity adjunct. A forty-ID taxonomy and six bounded lexical cue patterns are available; cues do not classify deception. Complete semantic classification remains a source-review task.

The `pd.forensic.v1` adapter preserves annotation IDs and text anchors and initializes every new context/use assessment as OPEN. It does not carry old assessments forward as if newly verified. The audit validates exact Unicode text offsets and derivative hashes; requires twelve explicit record checks; accepts a source-bound, attributed review or a separately labelled synthetic test expectation; and never authenticates the named reviewer. Returning a complete supplied record certifies its structure, not the correctness of its conclusions.

The dependency traversal returns stale-review tasks while retaining earlier assessments. Origin grouping is based on declared lineage and exact derivative duplicates, not a certification of source independence. The command writes a new output only and refuses overwrite. Outputs containing real source text stay private.

```sh
python -m unittest discover -s recovery_tests -p 'test_context_integrity.py' -v
python -m legaltech.unitary_review.context_integrity /private/case.json --from-forensic --out /private/new-context-envelopes.json
```

## 9. Protective-origin / causal-role-reversal context check — 25 Sep 2026

When an adverse insolvency or Calificación proposition is reviewed for context integrity, restore the **causal origin** as well as the quoted paragraph.

A later statement may be literally accurate yet materially incomplete if it begins after the lender-enforcement / protective-filing origin, excludes substantial net-asset context while implying economic worthlessness, omits contemporaneous viability/recapitalisation/funded-exit work, or presents a downstream liquidation/loss result as if it proves the incoming perimeter's original intent.

For these cases add the comparison:

`starting economic/legal condition → protective act → positive-agency/rescue evidence → intervening actor acts/omissions → later adverse proposition → invited causal inference → restored causal context → result`.

This is an extension of CI-013 chronology truncation, CI-015 cause/effect reversal, CI-016 benefit-as-causation risk and CI-029 fragmentation. It does not predetermine that the project-side causal account is correct.
