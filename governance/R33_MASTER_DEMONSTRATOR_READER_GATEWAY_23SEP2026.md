## Purpose

R33 — the 22-page opposition of the Insolvency Administrator in RPL 3304/2025, electronically signed 14 September 2026 and received from counsel on 21 September 2026 — is the current **master demonstrator** for the Por Derecho evidential method.

It is selected because it is a recent, bounded, formal and adversarial filing by a central actor and because proposition-level review exposes multiple connected planes at once. It is not selected because one pleading proves the wider case.

Preferred institutional descriptors are:
- **master demonstrator** — the primary worked example through which the public can see the method operate;
- **convergence document** — multiple documentary and procedural strands meet around it;
- **analytical hinge / linchpin** — individual propositions open into earlier and later records;
- **evidential junction box** — connections can be traced without assuming all wires form one circuit;
- **cross-section** — a September 2026 filing exposes historical strata deposited over earlier years;
- **stress-test specimen** — the methodology must preserve evidence favourable to the AC and material adverse to the publisher's hypotheses.

## Controlling evidential boundary

R33 proves the position taken by the Insolvency Administrator and the material incorporated in his filing. It does **not** independently prove every factual characterisation contained in the filing.

Disagreement, rhetorical tone, omission of context or an inconsistency does not by itself establish falsity, bad faith, criminal intent or liability.

Each material proposition follows:

`R33 proposition → exact page/passage → contemporaneous source → date/capacity → support → contrary/narrowing evidence → strongest lawful alternative → judicial/procedural treatment → open proof → current status`

The fourteen AC claim families remain attributed claim families, not fourteen findings.

## Ongoing reconstruction / dot-matrix rule

The public explanation must state clearly that the work is **not finished**. Documents remain under recovery, digitisation, authentication, reconciliation and analysis.

The publisher may state high conviction in important elements of the position advanced, but conviction must not be equated with proof or closure.

Use the recursive dot-matrix metaphor carefully:

- verified sources add evidential dots;
- new sources may sharpen an existing contour;
- contrary evidence may move or remove dots;
- a material correction can require dependent chronologies, actor maps, proceedings and public pages to be re-rendered;
- the process repeats until the highest defensible resolution permitted by the recoverable record is reached.

Canonical shorthand:

**RECOVER → OBSERVE → MANIFEST → COMPARE → CLASSIFY → CONNECT → ADVERSARIAL REVIEW → RECONCILE → EXACT-HEAD VERIFY → PUBLISH IF AUTHORISED → LIVE READBACK → REPRINT ↺**

The image is never allowed to be completed by guessing missing dots.

- read the filing itself;
- identify the status of a proposition;
- follow it to its primary source;
- inspect what contradicts or limits it;

The desired public effect is not “believe us”. It is: **inspect the chain and test the picture.**
