## Purpose

Strengthen the planned filing before presentation by preventing a historically accurate annex, older pleading, party statement, official summary, contractual term, damages figure, capacity description or allegation from being treated as a fresh admission, waiver, concession or silently re-adopted current position.

This control is protective but not evasive. It does not permit alteration of historical sources, concealment of adverse material or artificial re-characterisation. Historical documents remain intact. The current filing must state exactly what each source proves and what it does not prove.

## Governing rule

**Preserve the historical record exactly; never let preservation become silent adoption.**

For every proposition test:
1. Who said it?
2. When?
3. In what procedural or contractual capacity?
4. Is it fact, allegation, inference, legal argument, official outcome, adverse evidence or unresolved proof?
5. What is the present filing using it to prove?
6. Could the sentence be quoted elsewhere as an admission, waiver, ownership concession, capacity concession, damages election or inconsistent position?
7. If yes, insert an express current-scope qualification.
