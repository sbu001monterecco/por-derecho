# Public funding pursuit — governance receipt

**Control date:** 24 September 2026

AWESWELL LIMITED / Sun Rock is prepared to finance and assume the full project risk for a qualifying Canary Islands hotel development without relying on public support. Public programmes are therefore being assessed as supplementary sources.

The pursuit is organised into controlled workstreams:

- regional investment aid;
- FEDER/ERDF and PRTR;
- employment and training;
- energy and circularity;
- Canary tax regimes;
- public credit and guarantees;
- Cabildo and municipal programmes;
- eligibility, state-aid and double-funding review.

The public record does not treat any indicative percentage as a guaranteed award. Each source must be mapped to a defined eligible cost, beneficiary, timing rule, employment obligation, cumulative-aid limit and evidence file.

The private working record includes the detailed route matrix, current statuses, project-specific questions and application controls. It contains no seller identities, private financing terms, raw correspondence, confidential legal advice or disputed allegations.

Public funding will be pursued lawfully, independently, transparently and without double funding. This receipt is not a grant award, funding commitment, securities offer or legal/tax opinion.
