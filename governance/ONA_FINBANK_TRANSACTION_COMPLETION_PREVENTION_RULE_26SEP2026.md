# Finance / Banking Transaction-Completion and Prevention-of-Performance Rule — 26 Sep 2026

**Control ID:** PD-ONA-FINBANK-COMPLETION-PREVENTION-20260926-01
**Scope:** ONA / Cuatrecasas funded-exit close-out; Aweswell / LPB / Matkator / Sun Park; Article 176 / payment / consignation / refinancing / bridge / sale-backstop routes.

## 1. Required analytical role

For every funded-exit or debt-extinction analysis, add a finance-and-banking transaction-completion lens alongside the legal/prosecutorial lenses. Analyse the transaction as a competent lender, credit committee, restructuring banker, transaction counsel and workout adviser would: identify what capital was actually available; who was able, willing and ready; what objective conditions remained; who controlled each condition; what documents, funds, security, DD, operator arrangements, valuations and court/process steps were required; and whether a condition failed because financing was unavailable or because performance, receipt, recognition, implementation or debt extinction was prevented by another actor.

Do not fragment the analysis lender-by-lender and then infer aggregate unreadiness from one route's defect. Preserve exact maturity labels for every route, while also assessing the aggregate redundant financing ecosystem and contemporaneous alternatives.

## 2. Completion-state matrix

For each material date and route record separately:
1. Borrower/sponsor readiness.
2. Lender/investor readiness, including functional approval equivalents rather than only documents literally titled credit approval.
3. Funds/proof-of-funds/drawdown mechanics.
4. DD and valuation completion.
5. Security package and perfection requirements.
6. Operator contract and operating-case support.
7. Debt calculation / texto definitivo / amount-to-pay evidence.
8. Payment or consignation mechanics.
9. Court / insolvency-administrator / creditor cooperation or action required.
10. Objective third-party conditions.
11. Expiry, rejection, withdrawal or post-cutoff continuity.
12. Actor controlling each unsatisfied condition.
13. But-for test: whether the route could have completed absent the identified obstruction or independent failure.
14. Strongest contrary evidence and lawful alternative explanation.

## 3. Prevention-of-performance / debt-extinction hypothesis

Gil Marer / Aweswell-side case position alleges that the decisive problem was not absence of willing capital but conduct by the creditor-of-record / Acosta Matos perimeter and insolvency/judicial process actors that prevented, frustrated or refused the steps by which the relevant debt would be paid, consigned, recognised as extinguished and the concurso concluded.

Treat this as an attributed investigative/litigation hypothesis unless established by competent adjudication. Do not convert proximity, adverse conduct, procedural disagreement or non-cooperation by itself into collusion, criminal intent or criminal liability.

The controlling question is:
**Was a material closing condition genuinely unsatisfied because the financing side was unable/unwilling/unready, or was satisfaction/recognition of that condition prevented or frustrated by an actor who controlled or materially affected the payment, consignation, recognition, court or insolvency-process step?**

## 4. Criminal/prosecutorial test

Where prevention is alleged, test conduct actor-by-actor and act-by-act against the potentially relevant Spanish criminal-law elements and procedural/jurisdictional requirements. Preserve documentary facts, judicial findings, attributed allegations and analytical inferences as separate states. Search equally for innocent, procedural, commercial, credit, timing, documentation and legal explanations.

Criminal-first means element-first and causation-first; it does not mean treating the criminal conclusion as predetermined.

## 5. Internal explanatory analogy

For internal comprehension only, Gil Marer's analogy is: a debtor returns borrowed eggs to the shop; a person associated with the shop deliberately breaks the returned eggs and then says the debt remains unpaid. The institutional translation is **alleged prevention/frustration of performance and subsequent reliance on the non-performance allegedly produced or maintained by that conduct**.

Do not use “mafia” or “mob” as a factual/public characterization absent an adjudicated basis. Describe concrete conduct and evidential status instead.

## 6. Mandatory evidence rule

An “approval equivalent” includes functional evidence such as issuer-confirmed or signed offers, committee/risk clearance, approval/OK language, executable terms, funding-readiness statements, conditions-precedent progression, lender-counsel mobilisation, completed DD/security work, proof-of-funds, drawdown instructions or other evidence that a competent finance adviser would treat as demonstrating material funding readiness. Each item still receives the exact existing maturity label and must not be overstated.

Negative-proof rule: failure to locate a document literally called “credit approval” is not proof that lender readiness was absent.

## 7. Output requirement

Every future close-out must produce a unitary chronology showing:
capital route -> maturity -> remaining conditions -> condition controller -> attempted satisfaction -> response/conduct -> independent failure or alleged obstruction -> effect on payment/consignation/debt extinction -> post-event continuity -> contrary evidence -> open proof.

The purpose is to distinguish a financing failure from an implementation/extinction failure, and an implementation failure from an alleged deliberately induced failure, without collapsing those distinct propositions.
