## 4. Criminal/prosecutorial evidentiary standard

`source → capacity → knowledge → authority → representation/omission → act → use/adoption → economic/procedural effect → benefit/harm → communications/coordination → subjective element → contrary evidence/lawful alternative`.

Do not use “everyone colluded” as a substitute for proof. Relationship, office, adverse decision, later benefit or parallel conduct does not transfer knowledge or criminal intent.
