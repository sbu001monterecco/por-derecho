# Montaña Roja Hotel Business Plan - public-safe receipt

Control date: 24 September 2026  
Sponsor: AWESWELL LIMITED (UK)  
Platform: Sun Rock  
Project: Montaña Roja, Playa Blanca, Yaiza, Lanzarote  
Status: active execution planning; not acquired, licensed, financed or under construction

## Purpose

This public-safe record confirms the structure of the private business plan without publishing seller identities, private financing terms, legal advice, claim files, family balance-sheet information or restricted transaction documents.

The project is a proposed new high-quality hotel, currently modelled at approximately 300 accommodation units and a four-star or greater positioning, subject to transaction, planning, technical, funding, environmental and operating approvals.

## Core commercial proposition

The project is designed as a stand-alone hotel business. It does not rely on disputed recovery claims, historic hotel income, an existing adversarial brand, an unawarded grant or uncommitted financing.

The commercial strategy can use:

- an independent Sun Rock hotel brand;
- an experienced third-party operator;
- a franchise or soft-brand arrangement;
- direct booking, OTA and tour-operator channels;
- a legally independent successor commercialisation platform, only if separately cleared, contracted and commercially justified.

The existing hotel trading as MYND Yaiza is a separate operating and branding perimeter. This project does not claim affiliation with, ownership of, management of, or entitlement to the current MYND brand, goodwill, data, income or contracts.

## Funding and compliance architecture

AWESWELL is prepared to carry 100% of the project risk if necessary. External senior debt, whole loans, private credit, preferred capital, co-investment, seller finance, public support and tax routes are being pursued for liquidity and risk optimisation.

The following lanes remain separate:

1. Acquisition and corporate-control consideration.
2. Direct property and development finance.
3. Operating and pre-opening finance.
4. Employment and training support.
5. Public grants, guarantees and tax regimes.
6. Separate recovery or patrimonial claims.

No project facility is intended to finance a company purchasing its own shares. Acquisition consideration must be funded separately. ProjectCo development proceeds are restricted to approved project purposes, with separate accounts, sources-and-uses schedules, ledgers, invoices, draw conditions and reporting.

## Public-support strategy

The project will pursue every lawful and eligible route, including:

- Canary regional-investment incentives for qualifying hotel investment, jobs, civil works, equipment and environmental elements;
- FEDER/ERDF Canarias calls for eligible energy, circularity, climate, digital, accessibility and productive-investment sub-projects;
- FSE+/SCE training with hiring commitments;
- SCE hiring routes for eligible workers;
- successor PRTR and IDAE energy, circularity, renewables and storage calls;
- RIC, ZEC and DIC tax analysis through the correct Spanish/Canary entity;
- FOCIT, ICO, SODECAN and public-backed guarantee or loan products;
- Cabildo and Yaiza employment, skills, water, waste, energy, accessibility and local-development programmes.

A programme envelope is not an award. The 25% reference is not presented as a 25% government contribution. Any own-financing, incentive-effect, no-start, eligible-cost, state-aid, cumulation and reporting requirement will be checked against the current call before filing.

Where a programme is limited to existing operating establishments, that is treated as a stage limitation. It does not classify the new development as generally ineligible; the project will identify the correct construction-stage, development-stage or operating-stage route.

## Delivery gates

0. Secure conditional control and complete transaction perimeter.  
1. Complete title, corporate, planning, environmental, technical, tax, market and claims diligence.  
2. Approve acquisition vehicle, ProjectCo, operator, funds flow and financial-assistance analysis.  
3. Obtain planning, tourism, environmental, water, fire, access and utility approvals or valid applications.  
4. Close sponsor, seller, senior, preferred, equity and eligible public-support sources.  
5. Construct with independent quantity-surveyor, cost, safety, quality and change controls.  
6. Recruit, train, commission systems, obtain operating approvals and pre-sell responsibly.  
7. Open, measure guest experience and stabilise operations.  
8. Retain, refinance or exit only after independent valuation, audited operating data and covenant review.

## Hostile-reader standard

The project must be able to answer:

- Do we own or control the project today? Not yet represented; it remains active execution subject to gates.
- Is AWESWELL the target? No. It is the Sponsor/HoldCo.
- Are lenders being asked to finance shares? No. Acquisition and development are separate.
- Is the hotel licensed? Not represented as licensed until the relevant approvals are evidenced.
- Is public funding guaranteed? No. No award is counted before written confirmation.
- Does the project depend on MYND Yaiza? No. It must stand alone.
- Can the Sponsor fund the project? AWESWELL is prepared to assume the risk, but that is not a third-party funding commitment.
- What if costs rise or refinancing is unavailable? The project must be re-sized, delayed, retained or funded through verified sponsor/private capacity; no automatic rescue is assumed.

## Success definition

The project is successful only when it is legally controllable, technically buildable, independently financeable, publicly supportable where eligible, commercially differentiated, responsibly operated and capable of surviving downside conditions.
