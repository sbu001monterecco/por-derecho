# EU / Spain / Canary incentives — public governance receipt

**Control date:** 24 September 2026

AWESWELL LIMITED / Sun Rock will independently assess and pursue lawful European, Spanish and Canary Islands public-support routes for qualifying hospitality and other investment projects.

The record distinguishes:

- grants and public contributions;
- tax regimes and deductions;
- public guarantees;
- private debt and equity; and
- project-level operating finance.

The frequently mentioned “25%” is not recorded as a guaranteed government contribution. The current regional-investment framework is being assessed on the basis that the applicant may need to demonstrate at least 25% own financing, while any aid remains conditional on eligible costs, aid ceilings, state-aid rules, project viability, incentive effect, timing and individual approval.

Our application standard is:

- truthful and source-supported project and entity descriptions;
- no claim of ownership, licensing, approval, funding or eligibility before verified;
- no double funding;
- DNSH, anti-fraud, no-conflict and audit compliance;
- clear separation of sponsor, ProjectCo, asset, lender and investor roles;
- current legal/tax review before any filing or reliance.

This public receipt intentionally contains no seller identities, private counterparties, raw correspondence, disputed historical allegations, family balance-sheet information or confidential commercial terms. The detailed working record is maintained privately in GitLab and Google Drive.

This is a governance record, not a grant award, offer of securities, financing commitment or legal/tax opinion.
