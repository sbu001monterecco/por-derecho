## Controlling architecture

The public reading order is:

1. **Economic object / counterfactual:** was the ONA/Clubotel funded exit materially developed, and what conditions remained?
2. **7 June pivot:** what occurred, who acted, with what authority, and what changed in access, control, operation, information or security?
3. **Control of value and information:** what assets, accounts, payments and information were controlled by whom?
4. **Professional / economic leverage:** what did the fee, intermediation, "peace" and counsel-transition record actually show?
5. **Criminal proof:** actor → act/omission → knowledge → purpose → causal effect → benefit/harm → legal characterisation.
6. **Civil / mercantile / concursal predicates and remedies:** title, estate authority, claim classification, convalidation, standing, appeals and removal remain separate legal questions and must not displace the criminal reading order.

**Criminal first means element first, not offence-label first.**
