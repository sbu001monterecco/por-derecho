# Community-fee court-record completeness control — 25 Sep 2026

**Control ID:** PD-COMMUNITY-COURT-COMPLETENESS-20260925-01  
**Status:** ATTRIBUTED POSITION + PROCEEDING-BY-PROCEEDING COMPLETENESS TEST  
**Purpose:** prevent a ruling about Community governance, meeting validity, standing, interim relief or a particular charge from being silently treated as a complete adjudication of the entire Community-debt/accounting question.

## 1. Gil/AWESWELL position

Gil Marer states that, with one exception not yet source-identified in this control, proceedings touching Sun Park Community fees, morosity, voting or governance generally did not receive or adjudicate the complete underlying accounting/economic record.

He further states that private/community actors repeatedly supplied a partial or distorted factual frame and that later judicial treatment, including at first-instance and appellate level, was thereby capable of being used as institutional validation of propositions the relevant proceeding had not actually decided.

This is Gil/AWESWELL's categorical attributed position. It is not converted here into a finding that any judge knowingly accepted false evidence or committed an offence.

## 2. Source-controlled examples

### P.O. 562/2014
A contemporaneous 2020 case review states that this matter stopped at interim-measures stage. Do not cite it as a full merits adjudication of the Community debt.

### P.O. 213/2015 and appellate treatment
The same 2020 review states that the dispute concerned Community governance/convocation and meeting validity; it expressly distinguishes those issues from the underlying truth of the debt. Treat every later Audiencia ruling according to its exact object, parties, standing and holding.

### Judgment 91/2017
The controlled chronology records that the old monthly charge of about EUR171.81 was set aside after the Community submitted to the claim and that the 2016 EUR22.26 figure was noted. This materially alters the historical charge basis but does not itself decide every later debt, finca, accounting period or vote exclusion.

### Concurso treatment
The 20-Jan-2017 order made the pre-concurso Community claim ordinary contingent without its own amount. The AC's May-2020 request for the status of the Community-related proceedings shows that the continued basis/subsistence of that treatment still required checking. Do not describe the debt as finally established merely because it existed procedurally in the concurso.

## 3. Mandatory court-information completeness matrix

For every proceeding touching Community debt/fees/authority, record:

- exact proceeding/reference;
- exact judicial object;
- parties and standing;
- Community/body/officeholder identity;
- documents actually filed;
- accounting records actually filed;
- operator/CEXP cost material actually filed;
- owner/finca/debt denominator;
- challenge/consignation status;
- debt figure(s) before the court;
- retroactive quota history before the court;
- vote counterfactual before the court;
- expert evidence before the court;
- contrary material before the court;
- what the court expressly decided;
- what the court did not decide;
- later uses that expanded the decision beyond its actual holding.

## 4. Misdirection / procedural-fraud test

Where Gil/AWESWELL allege that a court was misled, test:

`representation/omission → author/transmitter → underlying source truth → knowledge of falsity/material incompleteness → contrary material available → recipient court → materiality → judicial reliance/adoption → decision effect → economic/procedural harm → benefit → lawful alternative`.

Incomplete evidence is not by itself proof of procedural fraud.

## 5. One-exception rule

Gil has stated that one proceeding dealt with the Community-fee issue on a materially fuller basis.

Current control state: **OPEN — EXACT PROCEEDING TO IDENTIFY FROM SOURCE**.

Do not guess the exception from memory or nominate Judgment 91/2017 merely because it changed the historical charge basis.

## 6. Judicial boundary

A judgment or order:
- proves the act and holding within its scope;
- may have binding procedural/substantive effect;
- may itself be adverse evidence;
- does not automatically prove every premise supplied by a party, officeholder, expert or AC;
- does not automatically validate later uses outside its holding.

Likewise, an adverse or legally mistaken decision is not automatically criminal. Any allegation of knowing judicial adoption of a false/invalid premise requires actor-specific proof of the record available, the reasoning, objective legal basis and required subjective element.
