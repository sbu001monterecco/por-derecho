# Dual-capacity / alertador communications rule — 26 September 2026

**Control ID:** PD-COMMS-DUALCAP-ALERTADOR-20260926-01
**Status:** standing communications control
**Scope:** external communications across Por Derecho / Project Sun Rock / AWESWELL-related legal, professional, regulatory, prosecutorial and institutional work, subject to recipient/context proportionality.

## 1. Mandatory dual-capacity framing

Where materially relevant, communications should identify Gil Marer as writing in two distinct but connected capacities:

1. **Former-client / business and economic-interest capacity:** as principal/shareholder-beneficiary and former client acting through **AWESWELL LIMITED**, the UK HoldCo/former client, in relation to its business, subsidiaries/operating perimeter and connected economic and patrimonial interests.
2. **Alertador / whistleblower capacity:** as a person who has made and continues to make reports/disclosures to relevant authorities in Spain, at EU level, in Germany and in the United Kingdom concerning the connected matters.

These capacities are cumulative, not mutually exclusive.

## 2. Legal-protection wording

Do not state categorically that every foreign whistleblower statute applies to every communication, recipient or fact pattern.

Preferred formulation:
- Gil has been acting and continues to act as an **alertador / whistleblower** before relevant authorities.
- He invokes, **where applicable**, the protections and safeguards arising under:
  - Directive (EU) 2019/1937;
  - Spain's Ley 2/2023, de 20 de febrero, including the protection conditions and anti-retaliation regime in arts. 35–38 and specifically art. 36.2;
  - Germany's Hinweisgeberschutzgesetz (HinSchG), where its personal/material scope is engaged;
  - the United Kingdom protected-disclosure framework under Part IVA of the Employment Rights Act 1996, as introduced by the Public Interest Disclosure Act 1998, where its statutory conditions are engaged;
  - and any other applicable national, EU or sector-specific protections.

No communication should imply that a recipient is conceding the applicability of any regime.

## 3. Matter-context formulation

The alertador capacity is connected to the wider matters Gil has reported concerning alleged economic wrongdoing/corruption and related professional/institutional conduct affecting or involving, among other things:
- the **Reserva para Inversiones en Canarias (RIC)** and connected tax-incentive/investment structures;
- European funding, including **FEDER / ERDF**;
- regional-investment incentives and other public-support mechanisms;
- connected assets, transactions and economic interests of the AWESWELL / Sun Park perimeter;
- and the wider adverse-party perimeter identified in the controlled evidential record.

These are **attributed allegations / reported matters requiring source-by-source proof**. Do not state that RIC funds were converted into FEDER funds, that public funds were misused, or that any person committed an economic crime unless the specific source record supports that proposition or a competent authority has determined it.

## 4. Retaliation / preservation rule

Where relevant to the recipient and purpose, communications may state that:
- the sender expects the communication, historic records and any response to be preserved and handled without retaliation or adverse treatment;
- Spain's Ley 2/2023 art. 36 expressly prohibits reprisals, threats and attempted reprisals for qualifying reports, and art. 36.2 defines retaliation broadly as prohibited acts/omissions or direct/indirect disadvantage suffered because of informer status/public disclosure;
- this notice is protective and preservational, not an accusation that retaliation has already occurred unless separately evidenced.

## 5. Former-professional overlay

For former lawyers/advisers, this rule sits **alongside**, not instead of:
- `PD-FCCOM-20260926-01`;
- the no-legal-advice/no-revival-of-mandate rule;
- the PD-CIR incident architecture;
- ICALPA DIP 80/2026 contextual boundaries;
- privilege/confidentiality and professional-secrecy controls.

Do not use whistleblower language to request privileged advice, revive a mandate, pressure a former professional to adopt Gil's allegations, or imply that the former professional is personally accused merely because they receive the communication.

## 8. Proportionality

The full clause is not mechanically required in every two-line operational email. For material communications, notices, preservation requests, former-professional reconciliation, institutional escalation and filings, include the full or compressed dual-capacity statement. For short follow-ups in an already-established chain, a short reference such as “I write in the capacities already notified” may suffice.
