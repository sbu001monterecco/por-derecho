# Global forensic document review · Truth Machine v2

**Control:** PD-TRUTH-METHOD-20260925-02  
**Status:** source-bound analytical architecture; not an institutional endorsement, forensic accreditation or automated lie detector.

## Mandatory intake trigger — adverse / non-project filings

The Truth Machine is a **default intake rule**, not an optional deep-dive. Every filing, pleading, opposition, submission, annex, evidential package or other material representation that does **not** originate from the Por Derecho / Gil Marer / AWESWELL LIMITED side and is made by a party or actor that is adverse or materially adverse in the relevant matter enters a Truth Machine review automatically.

External origin or adverse alignment creates a **review obligation, not a finding of falsity**. The standing hypothesis is **selection/context risk**: the document may prove fair and complete, or it may be narrowly selective, materially incomplete, context-distorting, contradictory, derivative, misattributed, temporally misleading, numerically distorted or procedurally overstated. The sources determine the state and degree.

Each incoming filing remains its **own track/envelope** while also being linked into the unitary evidence graph. Do not atomise individual propositions so far that the filing's overall theory, omissions, dependencies, requested inference or cumulative effect disappears.

Minimum per-filing pass:

1. preserve native source, version, hash and custody where available;
2. map source → block → proposition → requested inference;
3. test quotation fidelity, ellipses, qualifiers, negation, modality, quantifiers and denominators;
4. identify actor, entity, capacity, event time and knowledge/notice time;
5. compare each material representation with its underlying source and the complete contemporaneous record reasonably available;
6. identify omitted material context, selective extraction, contrary evidence and adverse evidence;
7. test chronology, attribution, financial scope, procedural status and authority/legal perimeter;
8. distinguish independent corroboration from derivative repetition, forwarding or later reuse;
9. trace recipient → adoption/use → decision/effect → beneficiary/harm → causation where the evidence permits;
10. record the strongest lawful/innocent alternative and the decisive evidence still needed; and
11. recurse whenever new upstream, contrary or downstream evidence changes a dependency.

Labels such as **false, lie, dishonest, knowingly false, bad faith, intentional deception or criminal purpose** are separate high-threshold conclusions. They require source-complete, actor-specific support, including separate evidence of relevant knowledge/intent where that element is asserted. Selectivity or omission alone does not establish intent.

A court, prosecutor or other institutional act is not automatically an adverse-party filing merely because its outcome is unfavourable. Material institutional outputs may be run through the same fidelity, context, provenance and record-versus-reality tests under a separate institutional-output lane without inferring adverse alignment from the result.

## Core sequence

PRESERVE → SOURCE/BLOCK/PROPOSITION → LINGUISTIC/SEMANTIC PASS → ACTOR/CAPACITY/TIME → SOURCE LINEAGE → SUPPORT/CONTRARY → RECORD-VERSUS-REALITY → FINANCIAL/PROCEDURAL SCOPE → DECISIVE TEST → DEPENDENCY GRAPH → SECOND PAIR/HUMAN SIGNOFF → RECURSE.

## Anti-fragmentation graph

ORIGIN → AUTHORITY → ACTOR → CAPACITY → ACT/OMISSION → DOCUMENT → PROCEEDING → TREATMENT → CONSEQUENCE → REUSE → BENEFICIARY → CONTRADICTION → OPEN QUESTION → SOURCE.

## Linguistic boundary

Language processing creates review candidates, not deception findings. Test quotation fidelity, negation, qualifiers, modality, quantifiers, attribution, speech act, chronology, knowledge timing, actor/entity/capacity, causation, context delta, procedural status, denominators, rhetoric and source reuse.

## Institutional lenses

The public data control `assets/data/truth-machine-methodology-v2.json` records bounded transferable methods from Companies House/Insolvency Service, CPS, DOJ, OLAF, FATF, UNODC, Europol EFECC, NIST and SWGDE. Their statutory powers and procedural rules are not imported into Spanish proceedings or Por Derecho.

## Dynamic implementation

The public interface polls methodology, priority-queue and R33 aggregate JSON every 60 seconds. Presentation can update from new deployed data; evidential state cannot. Source-change workflows reopen affected work, while substantive assessment remains source-review and human-signoff controlled.

## Metrics

Use explicit denominators only. Permitted process metrics include source identity, page coverage, anchor carry-forward, scan coverage, candidate review load and source-complete signoff rate. Do not output a document-wide truth score, lie probability, credibility score or guilt score.
