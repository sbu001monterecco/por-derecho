## Mandatory dual-public-link rule

Every material former-counsel / former-professional email must include both a GitHub Pages public link and a GitLab Pages public mirror link.

The two links are a resilience and preservation measure. They do not prove that either hosting provider has been compromised, and they do not substitute for primary evidence, court files, Gmail/Drive originals, or formal service.

> Por razones de continuidad, preservación y resiliencia —y ante incidentes y amenazas de ciberseguridad que venimos documentando en relación con este asunto— mantenemos redundancia pública en dos infraestructuras independientes. Estos espejos no sustituyen a las fuentes primarias ni a las comunicaciones formales.

Preferred English wording:

These mirrors do not replace the primary sources or formal communications.
