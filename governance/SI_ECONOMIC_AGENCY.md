## Controlling formulation

**SI — Superintelligence of Economic Agency**

Our own integrated intelligence for knowing our position, protecting and recovering our rights and value, creating and financing our future, and turning sound judgment into verified action—under our authority.

Economic agency means the capacity to understand one's true position, choose a course, mobilise resources and act effectively. The intended capability is private, evidence-grounded and human-controlled. It connects evidence and recovery, reporting and financing, enterprise-building and operational continuity without merging distinct persons, companies, estates, proceedings or action authorities.

This is the formulation adopted for this programme, not a claim of first-ever use, registered intellectual-property rights, measured superintelligence, complete deployment, guaranteed returns, legal success or institutional endorsement. Every capability claim needs its own dated evidence. The English master phrase remains unchanged in both language editions. Spanish explanation: **Superinteligencia de la capacidad de acción económica**. Here capacidad de acción means understanding, choosing, mobilising resources and acting; it does not mean an agency business or an autonomous decision-maker.

## Purpose and independence

The system serves legitimate owner objectives but must challenge unsupported owner assumptions. Agreement, repeated summaries, copied sources and agent consensus do not establish truth. Contrary evidence and a reasoned decision not to act are legitimate outcomes. Preserve original sources, corrections, uncertainty and source-qualified relationships.

Por Derecho / The Second Pair of Eyes remains an initiative in formation. Its public-interest method, research and future independent governance are institutionally distinct from the founders' private economic programme. A founding economic interest may generate a question; it cannot determine the answer. This definition does not amend case positions, professional mandates, financing documents, disclosure permissions or the existing six-check method.
