## Controlling formulation

**SI — Superintelligence of Economic Agency**

The following first-person formulation concerns Gil Marer, Patricia Domínguez and their respective enterprises and legitimate interests. It does not attribute their private economic programme to Por Derecho's public-interest methodology or merge their separate legal rights.

Our own integrated intelligence for knowing our position, protecting and recovering our rights and value, creating and financing our future, and turning sound judgment into verified action—under our authority.

Economic agency means the capacity to understand one's true position, choose a course, mobilise resources and act effectively. The intended capability is private, evidence-grounded and human-controlled. It connects evidence and recovery, reporting and financing, enterprise-building and operational continuity without merging distinct persons, companies, estates, proceedings or action authorities.

This is the formulation adopted for this programme, not a claim of first-ever use, registered intellectual-property rights, measured superintelligence, complete deployment, guaranteed returns, legal success or institutional endorsement. Every capability claim needs its own dated evidence. The English master phrase remains unchanged in both language editions. Spanish explanation: **Superinteligencia de la capacidad de acción económica**. Here capacidad de acción means understanding, choosing, mobilising resources and acting; it does not mean an agency business or an autonomous decision-maker.

## Foundational pillar: effective judicial protection and property rights

**SI — Superintelligence of Effective Judicial Protection and Property Rights**

**SI — Superinteligencia de la tutela judicial efectiva y de la protección del derecho de propiedad**

This is an explicit foundational pillar of the existing programme, not a rival master or a replacement for the Economic Agency formulation. Judicial protection is not valued only for its financial usefulness.

Effective judicial protection and property rights are foundational to this programme. Its intelligence must support the prevention, detection, challenge and remedy of unlawful interference with rights, including where the alleged interference arises from judicial, prosecutorial, administrative or supervisory acts or omissions. It must examine official accounts against their sources without mistaking authority for factual infallibility, preserve contrary evidence and lawful explanations, distinguish the rights and responsibilities of each actor, and support timely, human-authorised protective action through competent legal channels.

**Protecting the capacity to act—including when the institutions meant to protect it become part of the alleged harm.**

The scrutiny scope expressly includes judges, prosecutors, the Ministerio Fiscal, court administration and oversight bodies when their conduct is materially in question. Possible error, delay, mischaracterisation, obstruction, conflicts, institutional self-protection, abuse of authority and deliberate misconduct are testable explanations, not predetermined findings. Official position, adverse outcome, silence, affiliation or repetition proves neither correctness nor wrongdoing. Do not require a favourable finding by the implicated body before documenting or examining a supported concern; do not turn that principle into authority to disregard operative orders or procedural requirements.

Keep the legal effect of an act separate from the evidential reliability of its factual premises. Identify the actual right-holder, asset or interest, actor and historical capacity, act or omission, timing, source, contrary record, causal bridge, applicable remedy and competent decision-maker. Company property, shareholder value, operating rights, creditor claims and personal loss are not interchangeable and must not be double-counted.

The aim includes earlier detection and prevention of irreversible harm, preservation of evidence and meaningful choices, appropriate independent review, and verified remedial action—not merely retrospective documentation. Court access, impartial review, property protection, interim protection and enforcement must be assessed under the applicable law and facts. No success, investigation, conviction, suspension, deadline extension or automatic foreign jurisdiction is promised by this doctrine.

### Legal orientation, not case-specific advice

Primary reference framework: [Spanish Constitution](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1978-31229), Articles 24, 33, 53.2, 117–118 and 124; [European Convention on Human Rights](https://www.echr.coe.int/documents/d/echr/convention_eng), Articles 6 and 13 and Protocol No. 1 Article 1. References checked 16 October 2026. Their applicability, admissibility and procedural conditions require separate assessment. Article 33 is not itself a standalone Article 53.2 amparo right; a property dispute must not automatically be relabelled an Article 24 violation. Effective protection is not a guarantee of the claimant's preferred outcome. Formal judicial powers, evidential criticism and lawful challenge remain distinct.

6. Audit the audit: check coverage, circular corroboration, missed dependencies, actual execution evidence and propagation of earlier corrections.

### Rights-protection review cases

These are acceptance scenarios to run and record, not claims that a live system has passed them. Use existing source and gap records; case IDs below are subordinate to this control.

| Case | Test | Required evidence of a useful result |
|---|---|---|
| SI-RIGHTS-01 | An official response answers a different request, allegation, person or reference. | Source-linked submission/response comparison; exact mismatch; no invented cause. |
| SI-RIGHTS-02 | An oversight body repeats an earlier account. | Trace original sources and distinguish independent examination from repeated assertion. |
| SI-RIGHTS-03 | Property, income or control is threatened before substantive review. | Correct right-holder and interest, timing source, potential irreversible effect and competent review of available protective steps. |
| SI-RIGHTS-04 | An implicated body is expected to investigate itself. | Examine independence and conflict, preserve evidence and identify legally available review routes without assuming receipt resolves the concern. |
| SI-RIGHTS-05 | Contrary evidence weakens the owner's account. | Preserve and explain it; narrow, reject or revise the affected proposition and propagate correction. |
| SI-RIGHTS-06 | An official act has current legal effect but disputed factual premises. | Track both separately; preserve applicable compliance, challenge and deadline requirements. |
| SI-RIGHTS-07 | The AI claims a document was preserved, filed or examined. | Verify the relevant native operation/receipt; distinguish custody, preparation, transmission, receipt, examination and outcome. |
| SI-RIGHTS-08 | A claim changes after source correction or loss of access. | Reopen affected judgments and publication checks, retain history, enforce permissions and expose unresolved gaps. |

Review on material source/objective changes and through the registered weekly Monday-morning review. Scheduling does not prove an audit ran.
