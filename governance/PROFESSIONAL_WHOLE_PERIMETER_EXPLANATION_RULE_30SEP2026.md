# Professional whole-perimeter explanation rule

**Control ID:** PD-SP-PROF-WHOLE-PERIMETER-20260930-01  
**Status:** MANDATORY governance overlay  
**Parents:** CONCURSO36_UNITARY_CRIMINAL_FIRST_GOVERNANCE.md; governance/FORMER_PROFESSIONAL_EXPLANATORY_COMMUNICATIONS_RULE_26SEP2026.md

## Rule

Any material analysis or communication concerning lawyers, law firms, advisers, professional bodies or justice-system professionals in the Sun Park / LPB / Concurso 36/2012 perimeter must explain the **whole economic and institutional context before the professional conduct**.

Do not present a lawyer or firm as an isolated “professional problem”. First explain:

1. the underlying asset, business and value at stake;
2. the alleged displacement/control mechanism and the recovery/protection objective;
3. the asset, income, fruits, financing, capital, public-support or later-value position that may be affected;
4. the relevant litigation, insolvency, regulatory, tax or institutional setting;
5. the exact professional act or omission;
6. the function that act could have in implementing, preserving, defending, monetising, transferring or resisting recovery of the disputed economic position;
7. the resulting procedural and economic effect, if any;
8. the alleged beneficiary/harm, contrary explanation, missing proof and evidence status.

## Why-professionals-matter test

For every professional node ask, without presuming the answer:

`CLIENT / MANDATE -> CAPACITY -> KNOWLEDGE -> INSTRUCTION -> ACT OR OMISSION -> WORK PRODUCT -> PROCEDURAL EFFECT -> ECONOMIC EFFECT -> BENEFICIARY/HARM -> PAYMENT/SOURCE IF PROVED -> CONTRARY EXPLANATION -> MISSING PROOF -> EVIDENCE STATUS`.

Professional conduct may need to be tested for offensive/implementation functions, defensive/status-quo functions, recovery-resistance functions, or pressure/neutralisation functions. Legitimate advocacy, lawful fee recovery, ordinary adverse representation, good-faith professional judgment and ordinary institutional disagreement remain live alternative explanations.

## Economic-context requirement

Where relevant, distinguish and explain separately:
- possession/control;
- hotel operation and operating capacity;
- bookings, goodwill, licences and contracts;
- hotel income, rents and other fruits;
- debt, security, standing, valuation, title and enforcement;
- financing/investor/operator routes;
- tax-incentive and public-support structures;
- later investment, development, management, branding, asset accumulation and capital access;
- recovery, restitution and damages claims.

Never double-count or collapse ownership, operation, management, branding, financing, investment, tax treatment, public support, revenue and beneficial receipt.

## Serious-allegation context

Where the controlled corpus contains attributed criminal, corruption, public-law, institutional-shielding or professional-instrumentalisation hypotheses, do not silently reduce them to civil, fee or professional disagreements. State them as attributed allegations/hypotheses, explain their claimed economic function, and test them actor-by-actor and element-by-element.

No allegation becomes a fact because it is serious, repeated or economically coherent. No act, knowledge, intent or liability transfers by association.

## Mandatory reader sequence

For an external reader explain, in this order unless the task requires a shorter version:

1. What was the business/asset and how did it generate value?
2. What is the alleged displacement/control mechanism?
3. What assets, income, financing, public/tax support or later value are alleged to have been preserved, accumulated or placed beyond recovery?
4. What recovery/protection efforts were being made?
5. Why did professional services or institutions become economically and procedurally important?
6. What exact professional act/omission is being tested?
7. How could that act affect the disputed economic position or the recovery effort?
8. What is proved, alleged, contradicted or missing?
9. What concrete preservation, production, investigation, correction or decision is requested?

## Anti-fragmentation / anti-conflation

**Anti-conflation is not anti-connectivity.**

Keep every person, entity, proceeding, mandate, payment, act and institutional competence separate. At the same time, do not present them as economically unrelated where the evidence supports a causal or evidential connection.

## External-communication minimum

Every material external communication about professional conduct must contain a short **WHY THIS MATTERS ECONOMICALLY** paragraph before the professional allegation. It must identify:
- the asset/business/value at stake;
- the alleged disputed economic position;
- the recovery/protection objective;
- why the professional act could affect preservation, implementation, monetisation, defence or recovery;
- and the allegation/finding boundary.

## Evidence discipline

Use the existing repository evidence-status controls. Preferred language:
- “Gil Marer / AWESWELL allege…”
- “the evidence is relied upon in support of the allegation that…”
- “this act requires testing against the wider economic mechanism because…”
- “the present corpus establishes X but does not yet establish Y…”
- “an alternative lawful explanation is…”
- “the missing proof is…”

Do not infer criminal participation from representation, proximity, payment, professional affiliation or an adverse result alone.

## Boundary

This rule controls analysis, drafting, repository governance and explanation. It does not itself authorise an external communication, filing, publication, complaint, witness contact or other consequential act.
