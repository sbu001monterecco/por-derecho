## Why GitLab is strategically relevant
GitLab is part of the operating method rather than merely a code store. The project uses Git-native controls for source history, review, CI, deterministic validation, reproducible transformations, governed publication, rollback, machine-readable registers and continuity across people and conversations.

The GitLab relationship should be framed as a potential long-term fit around legaltech/evidence integrity, responsible AI and human decision controls, access to justice, public-interest research, secure institutional pilots, future commercial implementation, and Git-native governance.

No GitLab relationship implies endorsement of any live allegation or legal position.
