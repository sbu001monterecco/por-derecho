# Professional onboarding / conflicts reader — governance control

**Control date:** 28 August 2026  
**Purpose:** Make the public website and repository intelligible to a professional-firm compliance, conflicts, client-acceptance or pre-onboarding team without collapsing Group entities, treating allegations as findings, or mistaking public transaction-development material for a current mandate-opening list.

## Simulated professional-firm read

A compliance/conflicts reviewer should be able to answer five questions quickly:

1. **Who is the prospective client?** Project Sun Rock / Por Derecho is a working/public identity, not a legal person. Aweswell Limited is the public current UK holding-company perimeter. The exact client and instructing capacity for any new matter must be supplied in the confidential matter-opening request.
2. **Which related entities are not automatically clients?** Pink Canary Services, S.L., Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U., principals, owners and other connected entities retain separate capacities. Economic connection does not create a professional-client relationship.
3. **What does pre-approved adviser mean?** Prior relevant client acceptance/KYC/onboarding history plus possible renewed engagement; no live mandate is implied and fresh conflicts/client acceptance/KYC refresh/engagement processes may still be required.
4. **What existing names/proceedings are conflict-relevant?** Use the dedicated public reader and the source-specific public pages. Inclusion in the screening index is relationship-neutral and non-exhaustive; it is not a universal adverse-party classification.
5. **What is missing from the public record?** The current confidential new-matter counterparty/affiliate list and regulated onboarding documents. Those must come from the instructing channel.

## Conflict-screening boundary

The public reader may name current or historical entities that are materially relevant to the public dispute/recovery record. That does **not** mean:

- every named person/entity is adverse to every Group company or principal;
- every related entity is a client;
- a predecessor and successor share all liabilities or conflicts automatically;
- a professional firm appearing in an evidentiary record is adverse;
- a public name is sufficient to complete a conflicts search without aliases, affiliates and exact legal names supplied by the matter-opening team.

The professional firm's own conflict taxonomy and current internal records control its final clearance.

## Prospective transaction-development boundary

The public website is not the authoritative source for an unannounced financing, investment, acquisition, sale, restructuring or relationship-development mandate. Current prospective banks, lenders, investors, funds, sellers, co-investors, advisers and transaction vehicles must be supplied through the confidential matter-opening channel.

The repository may contain historical or public-safe transaction-development continuity derivatives. A name in such a derivative:

- is not proof of a current dialogue, mandate, approval, account or commitment;
- is not a complete current conflicts list;
- does not transfer knowledge of the legal/recovery record;
- does not authorise contact, disclosure or adverse action; and
- must be revalidated from the current private transaction record before external use.

This rule is consistent with `.github/governance/TRANSACTION_DEVELOPMENT_SEPARATION_AND_PUBLICATION_PROTOCOL.md`.

## KYC / AML material that must remain private

The public website and repository are **not** a KYC certificate, AML file, source-of-funds report or source-of-wealth report. Where requested by a regulated professional, the confidential onboarding pack should provide the current, certified or otherwise appropriate versions of matters such as:

- exact legal name, jurisdiction, registration number and registered office of the proposed client;
- current directors/officers and authority to instruct;
- ownership / UBO information and any required ownership chart;
- identity/address evidence for relevant natural persons;
- sanctions, PEP and other compliance information requested by the professional firm;
- source-of-wealth/source-of-funds evidence to the extent required for the actual mandate;
- exact matter description, counterparties, affiliates and related parties for conflicts; and
- any engagement-specific tax, regulatory or litigation disclosure required by the professional firm.

Do not publish those documents merely to make public onboarding easier.
