# Non-filing and payment-chain control — Juan Tomás Parrilla Suárez

**Purpose:** prevent a visual or narrative from converting an investigative question into an asserted fact.

## A. Fixed primary-source proposition

A recovered five-page copy dated 4 January 2019 bears the name of Juan Tomás Parrilla Suárez as LPB's letrado and contains a proposed court filing alleging disloyal conduct / possible procedural fraud by the Administrador Concursal, seeking protective relief, possible referral to the Ministerio Fiscal, and reserving AC responsibility/separation actions.

Status: `PRIMARY_SOURCE_COPY`.

Filing status: `UNRESOLVED`.

## B. User allegation to test

The project alleges that Parrilla refused or failed to execute legitimate protective instructions, including filing this writing, and that the circumstances of the end of his representation require examination together with conduct of the Administrador Concursal, the Concurso court/judge and the Acosta Matos perimeter.

Status: `ATTRIBUTED_ALLEGATION_REQUIRING_PROPOSITION_LEVEL_TESTING`.

Do not restate this as a finding.

## C. Economic-interaction proposition

Repository material records fee-recognition/payment discussions and later accounting material requiring reconciliation.

Status: `ECONOMIC_INTERACTION_DOCUMENTED__PAYMENT_CHARACTER_REQUIRES_RECONCILIATION`.

Prohibited shortcut: `payment -> bribe -> non-filing`.

Required chain:
`source record -> payer -> account/source of funds -> authority -> invoice/tax -> payee -> date/amount -> communication -> litigation act/omission -> alternative explanations -> causation evidence`.

Until every material link is supported, graphics must use labels such as **documented fee/payment issue**, **alleged influence**, **causation not established**, or a question mark; they must not show a definitive corrupt-payment arrow.

## D. Required falsification checks

- Locate any stamped/LexNET version proving filing.
- Locate contrary instructions in which client authorised non-filing or deferral.
- Test ordinary professional explanations: strategy, standing, procedural vehicle, duplication, timing, privilege, instruction changes.
- Reconcile whether any payment came from LPB/estate funds rather than personally from the AC.
- Reconcile invoice, tax and accounting treatment.
- Test whether representation ended for reasons unrelated to the questioned payment or protective instructions.
- Preserve evidence favourable to Parrilla, the AC, the judge or CAM with the same provenance standard as adverse evidence.

## E. Visual-publication rule

The planned composite may depict people/entities and the dated document, but disputed causal/payment relations must be visually encoded as allegations/questions unless source proof reaches the required state. The headline question may ask why the document was not filed **only after non-filing is independently verified**. Before that, use: **“Was this 4 January 2019 draft filed? If not, why not?”**
