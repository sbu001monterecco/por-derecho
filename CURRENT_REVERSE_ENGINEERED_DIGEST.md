## 2. Controlling unitary rule

> **Sun Park was one economically integrated hotel platform, but it was never one legal person, one title, one operator, one debt, one insolvency estate or one claimant.**

Every analysis must preserve both sides of that proposition:

1. **economic unity** explains why acts in property, governance, banking, insolvency, access, valuation, title, investment and operation may be causally connected; and
2. **legal separation** prevents the acts, authority, knowledge, intent, loss or remedy of one person or entity from being silently transferred to another.

The strongest case is therefore not “the whole theory must be accepted at once.” It is:

> **Produce each legal, factual and accounting bridge by which one state of affairs was converted into the next.**
