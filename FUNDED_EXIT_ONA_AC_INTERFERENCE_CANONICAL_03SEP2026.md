## 1. Controlling proposition

The 2018 ONA/Clubotel structure was a real, advanced, multi-party exit architecture, not a merely hypothetical rescue idea. Its documented sequence was:

**preserve Sun Park as an operating hotel unit → ONA/Clubotel operating package → external bridge/funding routes including Stoneweg/Varia → sufficient funds to pay or consign the concursal debt → conclude Concurso 36/2012 → restore LPB's ordinary corporate capacity → mortgage/refinance the intact asset → pursue optional minority-unit consolidation / take-out routes.**

The record does **not** establish that every financing condition had been satisfied or that closing/drawdown was guaranteed. It does establish a developed transaction with signed operating documentation, due diligence, valuation, multiple funding routes, staged security and a judicial/concursal implementation programme.

## 2. Publication/evidence classification rule

Every proposition in this record must be read according to one of these classes:

- **DOCUMENTED FACT** — directly shown by a native document/email/resolution.
- **JUDICIAL FINDING** — expressly stated by a court; distinguish appealed/non-final matters where applicable.
- **CONTEMPORANEOUS PROFESSIONAL STATEMENT** — statement recorded at the time by a lawyer/adviser about what occurred or was said.
- **PARTY ALLEGATION** — allegation made by Gil Marer/Aweswell/LPB or another party, not itself adjudicated.
- **ANALYTICAL INFERENCE** — inference produced by connecting separately sourced facts.
- **SOURCE_GAP** — proposition requiring further primary proof.

The label **“sabotage”** is used as Gil Marer’s allegation and as an analytical hypothesis supported by the causal chain below. No located judgment is said to have adjudicated criminal sabotage, collusion or criminal intent.

## 3. Stable evidence keys — do not require a future Gmail search

### PD-EV-EXIT-2018-001 — 6 June 2018 operating/finance package
**Class:** DOCUMENTED FACT  
Contemporaneous financing correspondence records the package expected for lenders: signed hotel-operator contract; a EUR 26m REIT acquisition/exit route; updated Gesvalt ECO valuation required for mortgage finance; full due diligence; and financing term-sheet work. The same financing dialogue identified insolvency-creditor repayment as the dominant use of funds and hotel common-area CAPEX (restaurant/kitchen, reception/café, gardens/pools/common areas and rooms).

**Meaning:** ONA was the operating anchor of an integrated exit/refinancing package. The hotel’s operating facilities were economically part of the transaction, not surplus assets irrelevant to the exit.

### PD-EV-EXIT-2018-002 — 7 June 2018 Daniel Irigoyen exit strategy
**Class:** CONTEMPORANEOUS PROFESSIONAL STATEMENT  
Daniel Irigoyen set out two parallel routes: (a) compete against CAM in the liquidation bidding process; and (b) conclude the concurso by proving sufficient funds, paying/consigning the total debt, showing guarantees and the ONA operating plan, obtaining the total debt figure and asking the Judge/AC to facilitate the process and suspend sale/adjudication while the solution was implemented.

**Meaning:** both the debt-payment exit and the defensive bidding route depended on an intact, financeable productive unit and an intelligible debt figure.

### PD-EV-EXIT-2018-003 — 8–13 June 2018 Stoneweg/Varia / Elaia package
**Class:** DOCUMENTED FACT + CONTEMPORANEOUS PROFESSIONAL STATEMENT  
The 8–13 June chain records an up-to-approximately-EUR15m bridge structure, principally to repay/consign concursal debt, together with staged security, an Elaia/Lagune EUR26m downside/acquisition route and the ONA package. On 12 June Stoneweg sent what it called an **“Oferta Vinculante”** subject to stated conditions. After the 13 June court meeting, Carlos Sanz reported that the Judge was receptive to a solution based on payment of the debts and conclusion of the concurso; the AC was expected to cooperate within his legal role. A simultaneous LPB mortgage while LPB remained in insolvency was a sequencing issue, addressed through staged security and post-exit mortgage mechanics.

**Limit:** offer/term-sheet stage is not the same as definitive facility, closing or drawdown.

### PD-EV-EXIT-2018-004 — 26 June 2018 partial suspension Auto
**Class:** JUDICIAL FINDING / PROCEDURAL ACT  
Auto of 26 June 2018, Concurso 36/2012, signed by Magistrado-Juez Alberto López Villarrubia (with LAJ Eduardo José Rebollo Sanz), suspended realization of the listed mortgaged apartments while stating that liquidation could continue as to other assets.

**Later correction:** the 24 October 2019 Auto expressly held that this partial suspension had been erroneous because the approved disposal was unitary and the entire liquidation process should have been suspended pending the appeal.

### PD-EV-EXIT-2018-005 — 26 September 2018 joint AC/Judge meetings
**Class:** CONTEMPORANEOUS PROFESSIONAL STATEMENT  
Daniel Irigoyen’s same-day notes record that:

1. the AC acknowledged the CAM EUR13.1m interest/debt calculation was wrong because it did not respect the mortgage-liability/security cap;
2. the Judge and AC agreed the EUR13.1m figure was erroneous;
3. the AC said he had not informed the Court when CAM filed the EUR13.1m notarial certificate and justified this on the basis that, with liquidation by dación in progress, the amount was effectively irrelevant to him; and
4. Daniel immediately objected that the amount was not irrelevant because third-party bids depended on it.

The notes also record continued discussion of paying/consigning and concluding the concurso, and possible discussions with CAM about the figure.

**Meaning:** the debt quantum was a transaction input for both third-party bidding and the amount the funded exit needed to pay/consign. A multi-million-euro overstatement was therefore capable of affecting financing and competition.

**Limit:** these are Daniel’s contemporaneous professional notes, not a verbatim court transcript.

### PD-EV-EXIT-2018-006 — 17 October 2018 AC call
**Class:** CONTEMPORANEOUS PROFESSIONAL STATEMENT  
Daniel reported that the AC wanted the debt and especially his fees paid and the concurso closed; Daniel understood the AC might accept a lower interest calculation using a two-year arrears-interest cap, producing an indicative figure around EUR12.2m subject to confirmation.

**Meaning:** the debt-quantification issue remained live and material to the exit.

### PD-EV-EXIT-2018-007 — 28 November 2018 separate locales/pools sale to CAM
**Class:** DOCUMENTED FACT + later JUDICIAL FINDING  
The later 24 October 2019 Auto identifies the 28 November 2018 public deed (protocol 2150, Notary Manuel Guzmán Ramos) by which LPB and Construcciones Acosta Matos, S.A. separately transacted the unencumbered fincas 8.508–8.536, 8.653 and 8.654 — the hotel locales/pools/operating facilities — for EUR400,000 within the CAM structure.

The approved liquidation structure had treated the mortgaged apartments plus these locales/pools as a **unitary productive-unit acquisition**, with apartments delivered by dación and the unencumbered facilities sold for EUR400,000.

### PD-EV-EXIT-2018-008 — 24 December 2018 Daniel warning to AC
**Class:** CONTEMPORANEOUS PROFESSIONAL STATEMENT + PROFESSIONAL INFERENCE  
Daniel recorded his conversation with the AC immediately after learning of the separate sale. He stated that he told the AC that separating the common/business elements **“fastidiaba toda la operación tanto de pago de la deuda como de una futura subasta.”** He also recorded that the AC was indifferent to the “unidad de negocio” point and maintained he was complying with the law.

Daniel’s own conclusion — that the AC/CAM movement was intended to prevent the solution progressing — is a **contemporaneous professional inference/allegation**, not a judicial finding.

A follow-up described the event as an “atropello” that broke the liquidation or concurso-conclusion process and proposed judicial challenge and supervening-facts submissions to the Audiencia Provincial.

**Meaning:** the causal harm to the funded exit and to competitive bidding was identified and communicated contemporaneously, not reconstructed years later.

### PD-EV-EXIT-2019-009 — ONA continued after the separate sale
**Class:** DOCUMENTED FACT  
In February 2019 ONA/Clubotel continued updating Sun Park operating documentation. On 21 February 2019 Susana Domenech, ONA Business Developer, sent a further addendum to the lease/operating agreement; updated proposal/business-plan material had also circulated earlier that month.

**Meaning:** the record does not support a simplistic claim that the ONA route had simply ceased to exist before the November 2018 locales sale.

### PD-EV-EXIT-2019-010 — 24 October 2019 judicial reversal / no convalidation
**Class:** JUDICIAL FINDING  
The Auto of 24 October 2019 (Concurso 36/2012; Magistrado-Juez Alberto López Villarrubia) held, among other things, that:

- the approved plan and the Audiencia Provincial interpretation contemplated a **unitary productive-unit** acquisition, including the mortgaged apartments and the unencumbered hotel operating facilities;
- the AC’s separate sale of fincas 8.508–8.536, 8.653 and 8.654 contradicted the 16 April 2018 first-instance Auto and the 23 July 2019 Audiencia Provincial Auto;
- the 26 June 2018 partial suspension had been erroneous;
- the whole liquidation should have been suspended while the appeal was resolved;
- separating the unencumbered facilities made the third-party improvement mechanism commercially ineffective because **“nadie va a presentar mejoras de ofertas si no se incluyen las instalaciones correspondientes a las fincas no gravadas”**;
- the separate transaction had been carried out without prior judicial authorization and was only reported to the Court once consummated; and
- the Court therefore refused to convalidate the deed and reopened the third-party improvement mechanism.

**Meaning:** the later court independently confirmed the central structural consequence Daniel had warned about in December 2018: separating the operating facilities from the mortgaged apartments impaired the integrity/competitiveness of the unitary disposal.

**Limit:** the Auto does not adjudicate criminal sabotage intent.

### PD-EV-EXIT-2019-011 — 30 October 2019 professional summary
**Class:** CONTEMPORANEOUS PROFESSIONAL STATEMENT  
Rosa Gual of Cuatrecasas summarized the 24 October Auto as accepting the arguments that the separate sale contradicted the unitary structure and that the 10-day third-party improvement window had to be reset. She wrote that the favorable outcomes might permit the complex to be saved.

## 5. Controlled sabotage analysis

### What is strongly evidenced

1. A developed ONA-led operating and financed-exit architecture existed.
2. The architecture required a coherent operating asset, a reliable debt figure and an implementable payment/consignation path.
3. The AC was directly engaged in the debt/exit discussions and was aware that third-party bidding depended on the debt figure.
4. The locales/pools were hotel operating facilities and part of the unitary productive-unit architecture.
5. The AC completed a separate locales/pools transaction with CAM.
6. Daniel contemporaneously warned the AC that this separation wrecked both the debt-payment exit and future auction route.
7. The court later independently held that the separate sale contradicted the unitary plan/AP interpretation and that third parties would not improve the offer without the unencumbered hotel facilities.
8. ONA continued working on the operating package after the sale.

### Analytical allegation now supported by that chain

Gil Marer alleges **direct, active sabotage of the funded exit** by the Administrador Concursal through a combination of commissions and omissions that enabled the private CAM/Acosta Matos perimeter and impaired the alternative ONA-led exit. The evidence above materially strengthens that allegation because several harmful consequences were identified contemporaneously and later corroborated by judicial findings about the unitary sale and third-party bidding.

### What remains unadjudicated / must not be collapsed into fact

- criminal intent;
- collusion/conspiracy between the AC and CAM/private actors;
- whether every financing condition would otherwise have closed;
- final but-for damages attributable to each actor;
- any alleged CAM→AC EUR350,000 payment or intermediation payment: **SOURCE_GAP — keep separate until independently proved**.
