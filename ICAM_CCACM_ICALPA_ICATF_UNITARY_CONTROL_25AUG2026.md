## 1. Locked identity dictionary

The following identities are not interchangeable:

- **ICAM** — Ilustre Colegio de la Abogacía de Madrid. Madrid first-instance professional-conduct body.
- **CCACM** — Consejo de Colegios de Abogados de la Comunidad de Madrid. Madrid appellate council for the administrative appeal described below.
- **ICALPA** — Ilustre Colegio de Abogados de Las Palmas. Separate Canary professional-control body. Current relevant references include DIP 79/2026 and DIP 80/2026.
- **ICATF / Tenerife Bar** — Colegio territorial de Santa Cruz de Tenerife. Separate from both CCACM and ICALPA.

**Hard rule for future threads:** `ICAM != CCACM != ICALPA != ICATF`.

Do not infer an institution from an acronym without confirming the documentary signature block, domain or formal heading.

## 4. Unreconciled ICAM chronology

A documentary chronology issue must be preserved neutrally:

- the **6 March 2026** ICAM 434/26 decision described the relevant professional relationship as **2018–2020**;
- the **1 July 2026** ICAM 1487/26 decision, when analysing the LinkedIn message, stated that the lawyer-client relationship had ended **more than seven years** earlier.

Taken literally at 1 July 2026, the latter formulation would place termination before July 2019. The two formulations therefore require reconciliation as to which engagement, relationship or professional perimeter each decision was describing.

**Publication rule:** label this an **unreconciled institutional chronology**, not evidence of bad faith, concealment or improper motive.
