## Appeal versus reopening

### Appeal

The appeal asks whether the court correctly assessed what was already filed by 16 July and whether concrete, useful, proportionate enquiries were left unanswered.

### Reopening

A post-finality reopening package should use genuinely new or distinct relevant indicia. It should not merely restate the same evidence. Priority sources include:

- certified complete ETJ file;
- signed submissions and proof of service/receipt;
- updated land-registry and cadastral certifications;
- expert architectural overlay mapping each finca to original/current physical units;
- proof of personal or institutional knowledge before a later act;
- adjudicatee/assignee/beneficial-recipient identity;
- exact judicial or patrimonial effect.
