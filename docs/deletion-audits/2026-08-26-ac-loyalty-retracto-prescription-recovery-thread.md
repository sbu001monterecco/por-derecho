## 3. AC loyalty-breakpoint doctrine preserved

The controlling question is:

> At what point, if any, did the Administrador Concursal cease acting as a loyal, independent insolvency office-holder for LPB / the estate and begin functionally advancing, protecting, adopting or implementing interests of parties adverse to LPB?

The analysis must start **before Acosta Matos**, not with CAM.

The predecessor sequence to investigate is:

`2008 sale / exploitation architecture → later fracture / dissident private perimeter → Monte Lanza / Molina / representatives / Community debt-vote-access machinery → AC knowledge and conduct → CAM arrival / creditor acquisition → June 2018 practical control → liquidation / adjudication → HNT / MYND / later finance`

The existence of this chronology is not proof of a common agreement.

### Four thresholds

1. **Loyalty breakpoint** — when, if ever, conduct ceased to be reconcilable with loyal administration of LPB/the estate.
2. **Conflict breakpoint** — when the AC knew or had to recognise material alignment with interests adverse to LPB.
3. **Independence breakpoint** — when disclosure, abstention, enhanced court supervision or separation may have become necessary.
4. **Functional / de facto adverse agency threshold** — whether AC powers derived from LPB were knowingly used to perform acts needed by the adverse perimeter to achieve objectives that those private actors could not themselves achieve through their own authority.

### Constructive / functional agency label

Internal canonical hypothesis:

`CONSTRUCTIVE_FUNCTIONAL_ADVERSE_AGENCY_HYPOTHESIS`

Public-safe Spanish formulation:

`hipótesis de agencia funcional / de facto a favor del perímetro adverso`

This is a forensic/evidential hypothesis, **not** a claim that “constructive agency” is already a freestanding Spanish legal doctrine or that the AC was formally retained as agent of Molina/CAM.

### L0–L6 evidence scale

- `L0` loyal/neutral administration adequately explained.
- `L1` isolated adverse effect.
- `L2` repeated same-perimeter benefit.
- `L3` conflict / independence warning signs.
- `L4` strong functional alignment difficult to reconcile with estate interest.
- `L5` evidence supporting functional/de facto adverse agency: knowing implementation of adverse objectives using administrator powers.
- `L6` direct concerted action: communications, instructions, representation, agreement, consideration, common planning or knowingly coordinated action.

Do not collapse L5 into L6. Functional agency is not automatically criminal collusion.

### Power-source test

For every material AC act ask:

> Was a power entrusted to the AC for LPB/the estate used to produce an outcome that the adverse private actor could not itself lawfully produce?

Then ask:

- whose problem did the act solve;
- whose legal position did it adopt;
- whose information did it rely on;
- who requested / anticipated it;
- what independent verification occurred;
- who obtained the immediate and downstream benefit;
- what measurable benefit LPB/the estate received;
- whether the act replicated a predecessor Monte Lanza/Molina/Community objective;
- whether later CAM leverage depended on it;
- whether a loyal/independent administrator had a rational estate-interest reason to act in the same way.

## 10. Prescription / limitation / caducity doctrine preserved

### There is no single clock

Never write:

- “the criminal case stops prescription”;
- “all limitation is suspended”;
- “everything is still alive”.

For every route record:

`CLAIMANT → DEFENDANT → ACT/OMISSION → LEGAL BASIS → CLOCK TYPE → DAY ZERO → INTERRUPTION/SUSPENSION → CRIMINAL LINK → CURRENT STATE → INTERIM RELIEF → FINAL RELIEF → DAMAGES/RESTITUTION`

### Criminal prescription — CP arts. 131–132

- Period depends on maximum statutory penalty.
- General start is commission, subject to statutory special rules for legally established continuing/permanent/habitual offences.
- A reasoned judicial resolution directing proceedings against a sufficiently identified suspect interrupts under art.132.
- A complaint/querella filed **before a judicial body** identifying the suspected person can suspend computation for up to six months pending the qualifying resolution; if it arrives within that window, interruption operates as the statute provides.
- Ordinary reporting to Fiscalía / police / administrative bodies is **not** described as automatic art.132 interruption.
- Continuing economic effects / operation / enrichment do not automatically make an offence continuing or permanent.

### Civil prescription

Key controls:

- CC1964.2 general personal-action period where no special period applies;
- CC1968.2 tort-type period;
- CC1969 actio nata;
- CC1973 interruption through judicial exercise, extrajudicial creditor demand or debtor acknowledgment.

Supreme Court same-facts criminal/civil doctrine:

- criminal proceedings can delay the civil clock where the same facts and arts.111/114 LECrim create a legal obstacle to the civil claim;
- a criminal complaint may amount to exercise of the civil action for art.1973 purposes in the recognised circumstances;
- for a personated party, the civil clock can depend on finality / correct notification of the criminal closure.

This is **not universal tolling**. Same facts, civil-action status, reservation/renunciation, legal obstacle, finality and notification must be proven.

### Caducity is separate

Examples:

- Art.1535: nine-day strict period tied to its legally relevant assignee demand / Day Zero analysis.
- Art.1524: nine days from Registry inscription or, failing inscription, actual knowledge.
- LPH18.3: three months / one year depending ground, with absent-owner communication rules.

Criminal proceedings do **not** automatically suspend caducity.

### LO 1/2025 / MASC

Art.7 LO1/2025 can interrupt prescription or suspend caducity under its statutory conditions.

For Sun Park short-period rights:

`COUNSEL VALIDATION REQUIRED`

It can protect a legally live right under the conditions of the statute; it must not be used publicly as an automatic revival theory for a right already expired.

## 11. AC liability clock preserved

Historical Ley Concursal art.36 / current TRLC arts.94–98 provide a special administrator-liability framework.

The estate-damage action has a four-year prescription structure tied to knowledge of harm and, in any event, cessation in office, with direct individual actions separately preserved.

No single global “AC limitation date” is accepted.

For each alleged breach:

`AC ACT/OMISSION → DUTY → ESTATE OR DIRECT HARM → ACTIONABLE/KNOWN DATE → AC CESSATION STATUS → INTERRUPTION → SAME-FACTS CRIMINAL LINK → DAMAGES → CURRENT COUNSEL VIEW`

Loyalty/conflict/functional-agency evidence may affect duty, knowledge, causation, validity and possible criminal characterisation; it does not by itself extend the statutory clock.

## 12. Judicial / State damages clock preserved

LOPJ292–293 routes are separate:

- judicial error;
- abnormal functioning of the Administration of Justice;
- any personal criminal hypothesis concerning a judge.

Controls:

- harm must be effective, economically assessable and individualised;
- reversal/annulment alone is not enough;
- judicial-error declaration ordinarily uses a strict three-month route;
- compensation uses a one-year period from when actionable;
- current appeals, finality and notification require counsel-led calculation.

No public exact deadline calculation without current counsel review.

## 13. Criminal route is also a recovery route

CP109–112 preserve:

1. restitution;
2. repair;
3. material and moral damages where legally available.

Additional controlled routes:

- CP111 same-asset restitution where legally possible, subject to third-party protection;
- CP121 potential subsidiary public-entity civil liability only under its conditions;
- CP122 participant-for-profit restitution/resarcimiento up to the value of participation.

Do not automatically classify later hotel value or every CAM/HNT/MYND benefit as criminal proceeds / unjust enrichment / recoverable damages.

## 14. Interim relief preserved

### Criminal

Potential statutory tools where predicates exist:

- LECrim589 security / embargo for potential pecuniary liabilities;
- LECrim764 patrimonial precautionary measures in abbreviated proceedings;
- CP127 octies seizure / embargo / deposit to secure confiscation.

### Civil

- LEC721 / 727 / 728 general precautionary framework;
- LPH18.4 interim suspension of a challenged Community resolution.

The correct question is:

> What asset, title, income stream, evidence, Registry position or economic value will be harder to restore if no proportionate legally available measure is taken before final judgment?

An allegation does not automatically freeze CAM/HNT/MYND assets.

“Interim damages” is not treated as automatic advance compensation. Interim strategy is preservation of final effective relief.

- patrimonial damages;
- lost profits where causation is proved;
