## 2. Controlling public architecture

### Track A — Sun Park / current MYND Yaiza

The open letter is a **resolution interface**, not a substitute for the detailed evidential record.

The public approach is to:

1. complete and reconcile the relevant institutional record;
2. identify the competent body and file for each question;
3. distinguish verified fact, party assertion, inference and open question;
4. resolve what documentary clarity and due process can resolve; and
5. preserve every applicable allegation, defence, appeal, administrative route, proceeding, recovery right and evidence-preservation request where disagreement remains.

The detailed evidence pages remain in place and are not diluted or withdrawn by the constructive open-letter formulation.

### Track B — Montaña Roja

The public description is deliberately limited to a **serious prospective investment under due diligence**.

Publicly safe proposition:

- prospective new high-quality hotel in Playa Blanca;
- complementary retail, commercial and public-facing space;
- measurable local employment, skills, enterprise and resource-efficiency ambitions;
- legal, corporate, planning, technical, environmental and financial verification first;
- ordinary institutional planning/tourism/technical dialogue only.

The public record **does not** announce a completed acquisition, agreed transaction structure, price, exclusivity, deposit, committed financing, planning permission, tourism authorisation, building licence, operator appointment, compensation entitlement, public subsidy, institutional support or construction start.

## 3. Anti-quid-pro-quo firewall

The following rule is controlling across both languages and future institutional communications:

> **No exchange of past for future.**

Future investment is not offered as consideration for the treatment of Sun Park/MYND Yaiza files. Resolution of those files is not conditional on institutional support for Montaña Roja. Neither route seeks preferential treatment, a shortcut, waiver or predetermined institutional outcome.

Each workstream must stand independently on its own documents, facts, law, procedures and merits.

## 4. Confidentiality firewall

Private Montaña Roja correspondence was reviewed to understand transaction seriousness, diligence status and current activity. A same-day private transaction update confirms that the opportunity remains actively under discussion.

That private material is **continuity evidence only**. It must not migrate automatically into the public repository, website or institutional outreach.

The following remain private unless separately authorised and independently verified for publication:

- private counterparties and personal circumstances;
- transaction price or historic pricing;
- payment, deposit, standstill or exclusivity mechanics;
- commissions and intermediary economics;
- beneficial-ownership material not independently public and necessary;
- private meeting arrangements and negotiation strategy;
- private legal advice;
- internal compensation valuations or theories; and
- unverified private-party assertions.
