## Core distinction

Proceedings remain legally distinct unless a competent source establishes joinder, accumulation, appeal, referral or another procedural relationship. Legal distinctness does **not** mean the files may be analytically isolated where evidence shows a shared hotel/asset, secured credit, exploitation/control, insolvency estate, productive unit, income, title, possession, enforcement, transaction, actor, event or alleged harm continuum.

The repository must therefore preserve two simultaneous propositions:

1. **do not collapse legally distinct files into one case**; and
2. **do not permit procedural compartmentalisation to erase or obscure documented cross-file relevance**.
