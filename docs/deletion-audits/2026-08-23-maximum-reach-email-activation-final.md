## 3. Seven-day communication findings preserved

The audit preserves these high-value lessons:

1. **Specialist threads/references outperform undifferentiated broadcasting.** Portfolio requests 81/82 created traceable channels and invited reply-based supplements.
2. **Actual incorporation is stronger than simple delivery.** CGPJ Recursos confirmed incorporation of new files and an audiovisual link while the appeal remained in progress.
3. **Acknowledgment is not merits review.** Fiscalía/FGE acknowledgment proves receipt/routing only.
4. **Email may not be legally operative.** Fiscalía Anticorrupción required a formally signed submission through an accepted route.
5. **Mixed-audience CC lists increase correction and comprehension risk.** The broad DE/ES FTI distribution required recipient correction and is now a control example, not a default model.
6. **OOO/redirect is routing intelligence only.** It never authorises automatic resend.
7. **Bounce stops the route until re-verification.**
8. **Real Gmail is not a test environment.** No placeholder/test/`DO NOT SEND`/invalid-address messages may be sent.
9. **Compact journalist first-look packages are preferable for initial engagement.**
10. **Recurring visual/source filenames require canonicalisation before final packages.**
