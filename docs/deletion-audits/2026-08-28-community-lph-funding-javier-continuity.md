- the Community's competence does not itself convey private title or universal hotel-operation authority;
- notice must be tested through the statutory recipient/address/delivery/fallback route in Article 9 and the call requirements in Article 16;
- necessary works under Article 10 and discretionary/improvement works require correct technical classification;
- Article 15.2 may suspend a debtor owner's vote in defined circumstances, but it does not erase notice or attendance rights;

- challenge and debt-enforcement consequences require the exact communication, liquidation, certificate and procedural record under Articles 18 and 21.
