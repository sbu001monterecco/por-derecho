10. “Split credit”, “multiple lives” and “NPL fraud” are analytical descriptions, not standalone offences. NPL discount or recovery above purchase price is not itself fraud. Unjust enrichment is principally a civil restitution concept and is not a substitute for offence elements.
11. An authentic notarial deed may contain disputed party recitals without proving that the deed was falsified or that the notary knew of falsity. Any falsity theory must identify the exact document, authentic comparator, author, essential alteration/simulation/false attribution, knowledge and use. A procedural-fraud theory must additionally prove deception, judicial error, an adverse resolution and economic harm.

The record supports a serious, bounded inquiry into balance provenance, legal function and double-recovery risk.
13. Assignment, group affiliation, servicing or economic benefit does not transfer natural-person intent or eliminate actor-specific proof. Penal Code article 130.2 may preserve or transfer an established legal person's criminal responsibility in a statutory transformation, merger, absorption or split, but it does not create the predicate offence or transfer an individual's mens rea.
