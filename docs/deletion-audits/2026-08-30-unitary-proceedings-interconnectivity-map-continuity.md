## What changed

The project now treats proceedings interconnectivity as a **map of maps** rather than an aggregate table alone.

The canonical CSV remains the single proceedings/file master. The map derives reader navigation from the public-eligible projection and keeps two relationship classes separate:

1. **direct procedural edges** — explicit parent/child or canonical-ID relationships in controlled relationship fields; and
2. **contextual / unitary-convergence bridges** — same recorded track, exact Connection value, geography, chronology or another independently source-controlled bridge through an asset, financing/security chain, estate, possession/exploitation history, patrimonial effect, evidence family or institutional-response chronology.

A context bridge is never silently converted into joinder, coordination, knowledge, wrongdoing, liability or identity of proceedings.

The additional controlling principle is:

> **Procedural separateness does not establish factual, evidential, patrimonial or causation independence.**

Future work must therefore preserve both the exact standalone legal identity of each file and the material whole-system links that would disappear if it were read in isolation.

## Internal digest contract

Future repository-wide proceedings work must traverse the corpus both:

**beginning → end**

and

**end → beginning**.

For each proceeding/file, resolve canonical identity, organ/reference/class, procedural status, parents/children/appeals/referrals/incidents/piezas, professional filing lineage, evidence/events/assets/transactions, chronology context, public routes, contrary evidence and open source gaps. Significant relations should be discoverable in both directions where appropriate.

Every file must also answer two anti-fragmentation questions:

1. **If this file is read alone, what material part of the wider asset, causation, control, evidence or alleged-harm story disappears?**
2. **If it is connected to the wider graph, what is documented, what is inference, what is attributed allegation, and what remains unproven?**

## Completion boundary

Do not say the proceedings corpus is `complete` or `fully interlinked` merely because all public CSV rows render.

Keep separate:

- node coverage;
- direct-edge coverage;
- unitary-convergence/context coverage;
- orphan/open-gap count; and
- deployment/live-route coverage.

A proceeding with no direct canonical edge may be genuinely independent or may expose an unresolved relationship gap. The renderer must not manufacture an edge to make the network look complete.
