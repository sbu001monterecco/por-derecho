## 3. What this package closes structurally

The release target is one controlled, bilingual finite-test object for every
public exact proceeding. Each object must expose:

- the exact Master ID and a finite question;
- the primary source needed and current source status;
- the recorded current custodian/organ candidate, while stating that the field
  is not itself a legal-competence finding;
- direct and contextual memberships derived only from controlled relationship
  and cluster records;
- an exact-file-specific decision dependency that is not shared boilerplate;
- the strongest contrary or innocent explanation;
- possible consequences if the source confirms or refutes the proposition;
- a canonical public-record route and an explicit proceeding-specific public
  primary-source route or primary-source route gap;
- Master Register, exact-trace and isolation routes; and
- an explicit institutional receipt/knowledge classification with independently
  sourced nine-axis provenance.

No confirmed/refuted consequence may treat the recorded candidate as legally
competent, empowered or obliged to act. It must remain conditional on exact
competence and a lawful procedural or institutional route.
The contrary explanation remains hypothetical and attributes no act to that
candidate without a primary source. Family assignment follows canonical record
type before mixed Stream substrings; administrative/professional perimeter,
judicial governance and civil professional liability remain distinct.

The finite question, decision dependency, strongest contrary explanation and
confirmed/refuted consequences must be unique to the exact file and bilingual.
A family template is taxonomy only. Repeated registry-maintenance,
source-retrieval or record-correction language cannot count merely because a
different reference has been inserted.

The nine institutional provenance axes are transmission, material
received/inventory, referral, registration, file incorporation, recipient
attribution, substantive examination, decision use and cross-file
acknowledgement. Every axis requires its own status, bilingual basis, bilingual
limitation and controlled source pointer or explicit source-not-located object.
Transmission and referral remain independent. Actor-specific receipt and
actor-specific knowledge remain separate and non-positive unless a
person-specific source proves the person, material/act, time and permissible
scope.
Every positive institutional grade must cite the exact episode field supporting
that axis; an episode-specific source-field override is required where the
global axis default would cite unrelated text.

## 4. Boundaries that remain open

This package deliberately does **not** close or relabel the following:

1. The finite shared-proposition matrix remains **43 / 97** exact proceedings
   represented and **54 / 97** without a non-`OUTSIDE` coordinate. Adding a
   finite exact-file test does not invent shared proposition membership.
2. Two direct relationship pairs remain source-reported/pending primary
   completion. They are not promoted to verified.
3. Dedicated narrative-dossier routes remain a partial positive subset. Every
   exact ID has Master/trace/isolation navigation; a missing dedicated dossier
   remains null or explicitly not established.
4. `CP-GAP-004` and `CP-GAP-005` preserve the open global
   counsel/procurador and filing denominators. Representation may not be
   inferred from another proceeding or professional relationship.
5. A Fiscalía episode profile records only the source-controlled axes available
   for that episode. The remaining office/files stay explicit. A raw matching
   reference is not receipt, file incorporation, examination, reliance or
   unitary cross-file acknowledgment.
6. Institutional receipt is not personal receipt; presentation is not
   incorporation; incorporation is not examination; examination is not reliance;
   and none establishes knowledge, intent, agreement, wrongdoing or liability.
7. `NOT_LOCATED` means not located in the controlled corpus, never nonexistent.
8. Transmission and referral are independently sourced grades. Neither may be
   copied from the other or used as proof of destination receipt, incorporation
   or examination.
9. Actor-specific receipt or knowledge remains `NOT_ESTABLISHED` unless an
   actor-specific source establishes the person, material/act, time and scope.
