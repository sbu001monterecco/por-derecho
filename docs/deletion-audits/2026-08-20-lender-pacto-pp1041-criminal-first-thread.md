## Controlling conclusions that must survive deletion

### 1. Lender-of-record liability is a framework, not one automatic inherited liability

The credit and its accessories may travel downstream. Defects or defences affecting the credit may bind or affect an assignee. Personal historic wrongdoing does not automatically transfer merely because the debt was sold. Each later holder, servicer, principal, successor or beneficiary must be assessed through its own capacity, knowledge, authority, conduct, benefit, causation and applicable succession rules.

### 2. Actor and capacity separation remains mandatory

Bankia/originator, SAREB, Promontoria Holding 122 B.V., Haya, Cerberus entities, CAM, HNT, the Administrador Concursal and later operating or financing actors remain legally distinct. Group or perimeter language is an attribution map, not proof of common legal personality, joint intent or collective liability.

### 3. PH122 is the source-supported holder; PH11 remains unresolved

The controlled record supports **Promontoria Holding 122 B.V. / PH122**. `PH11` must not be adopted as the relevant holder unless a native instrument establishes it.

### 4. “Bankia external / Bankia externo” is a forensic upstream lane

It covers origination, refinancing, SWAP, pledged funds, account treatment, default, acceleration, enforcement and retained originator obligations. It is not a company, statutory category or proof that separate persons share liability.

### 5. The strongest pacto-comisorio case is an integrated appropriation architecture

The controlling theory is not that a classical ex ante appropriation clause has already been found. The serious alternative case asks whether creditor-side decision control, restriction or loss of alternative exits, CAM's acquisition, disputed ACTÚA premises, material control before title, AC gatekeeping, the 2022 dación and an unreconciled debt–value–surplus account formed an indirect prohibited appropriation and/or fraud-on-law mechanism.

Judicially supervised post-default realisation, professional valuation, the adverse Article 1535 decision, conditional financing and independent later title remain substantial counterarguments.

### 6. PP 1041 attribution must remain exact

Do not write that LPB voluntarily withdrew as an autonomous fact.

Use:

> **A withdrawal was filed in LPB's name after the Administrador Concursal terminated the formal mandate of the lawyer pursuing the PH122→CAM deed, price, payment and costs. A voluntary and validly authorised LPB decision is not proved by the present controlled record.**

The mandate intervention, later filing in LPB's name, CAM's non-opposition and termination of the route are documented. Lack of authority, false attribution, deliberate detriment, conflict, coordination, beneficiary alignment and criminal purpose remain Por Derecho allegations pending certified primary evidence and competent determination.

### 7. The criminal route is actor-specific and element-specific

The preserved investigation tests, without treating guilt as adjudicated:

- estafa or estafa procesal;
- documentary, procedural or accounting falsity;
- administración desleal;
- insolvency-administrator-specific criminal provisions where temporally and factually applicable;
- punishable insolvency or frustration;
- disposition or use without authority;
- coercion, usurpation or damage relating to material control;
- participation, necessary cooperation or commission by omission;
- CAM corporate criminal liability;
- CAM→HNT continuity or successor exposure;
- public-funds or subsidy offences only where their independent elements are supported;
- criminal restitution and dependent civil, insolvency and registry recovery.

Civil invalidity is not itself a crime. A judicial, notarial, registry, corporate or financing act does not independently validate every upstream premise. A deceived decision-maker and a knowing participant are mutually distinct hypotheses and must not be collapsed.

### 8. The single most important production remains P0

> **The certified PP 1041/2017 file together with the Administrador Concursal's internal authority, instruction and estate-interest record.**

It is capable of proving or disproving the true decision-maker, authority, authorship, attribution, conflict, estate interest, knowledge, purpose, coordination, foreseeable harm, beneficiary and causal relationship between the withdrawal filed in LPB's name and the loss of disclosure, redemption, debt satisfaction and a financed exit from insolvency.
