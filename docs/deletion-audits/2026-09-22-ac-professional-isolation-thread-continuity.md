## Non-transfer / causation rules

Payment does not prove bribery. Contact does not prove improper influence. Withdrawal does not prove causation. A procedural licence does not prove wrongdoing. A later judicial finding of structural effect does not retroactively prove earlier intent. Relationship, sequence, association or benefit does not transfer responsibility.
