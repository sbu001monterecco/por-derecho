## Controls that must survive deletion

1. **DIP 79 and DIP 80 remain separate.** Shared documents justify coordinated preservation and indexing, not merged culpability or transferred evidence.
2. **Do not call all CAM material false as an established fact.** Identify the exact document, exact disputed proposition, author, professional use, knowledge, judicial effect, patrimonial consequence and causal link.
3. **Document authorship is not professional adoption.** The Community and RICPE certificates were authored by third parties; the lawyer's conduct must be assessed through verification, qualification, presentation, maintenance and correction.
4. **Whenever the San Telmo/RICPE statement is used, include both sources:**
   - timestamped: `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mHn9IJU0qI4&t=488s`
   - complete: `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mHn9IJU0qI4`
   - passage 08:08–08:12; reviewed context 07:57–08:27.
5. **Use the four established ICALPA addresses** for continuity unless an official direction changes the route.
6. **Use existing GitHub Pages links.** Do not introduce an Aweswell-domain link.
7. **Maintain the evidence ladder:** sent does not mean delivered, registered, incorporated, reviewed, contradicted or decided.
8. **Silence is not admission.** A reasoned archive remains a legitimate possible outcome.
