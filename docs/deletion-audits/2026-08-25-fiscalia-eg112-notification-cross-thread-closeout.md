## Notification and decision status

The official email received on 25 August 2026 attached a signed clarification Decree dated 23 August 2026 in Governmental File EG 112/2026. The official Decree:

1. records the 2 August communication and the opening and closure of EG 112/2026;
2. acknowledges that DIP 2/2026 was a criminal investigation concerning alleged judicial prevaricación and that it ended in provisional closure for want of rational indications of criminality;
3. states that the 2 and 20 August communications did not attribute a new or similar criminal irregularity to the same magistrate and therefore did not engage the Superior Fiscalía's objective competence through an aforado person;
4. declines to open a new criminal investigation or reopen DIP 2/2026 on that material;
5. characterises the “true object” as review of civil/mercantile proceedings concerning private interests;
6. states that the Superior Fiscalía lacks objective and territorial competence and identifies the Provincial Public Prosecutor's Office of Las Palmas de Gran Canaria as territorially relevant;
7. maintains the earlier EG 112/2026 decision in full; and
8. states that no appeal lies against the clarification.

This is an adverse official procedural position. It is not a judicial determination of the truth or falsity of every allegation, does not prove that every evidentiary module was examined, and does not itself establish transfer to or receipt by another competent office.

## Controlling institutional contradiction map

1. **Aforado premise:** the 19 August decision's categorical no-aforado position is narrowed by the 23 August acknowledgement that an earlier judge-related criminal-investigation module existed. The clarification's separate point—that the August communications did not renew an accusation against the magistrate—is compatible with the 20 August communication's own express boundary.
2. **Object:** the 2 August source expressly excluded a general review of the LPB insolvency proceeding and defined a private extraconcursal mechanism concerning Matkator, Aweswell and other non-insolvency assets. The Decrees instead characterise the “true object” as civil/mercantile review of private interests. This is the central textual incongruence.
3. **Criminal frame:** the 2 August source requested investigation, preservation and finite checks and identified alleged criminal hypotheses while acknowledging that no single document proved an offence. Fiscalía could conclude insufficiency or lack of criminal character; that is distinct from treating the communication as containing no criminal allegation.
4. **Source integrity:** the clarification does not correct Matkator/Malkator, loss of `no` from `patrimonios no concursales`, Marer/Mayer, 20/12 requested measures or the REGAGE-package/email-only provenance issue.
5. **Competence and referral:** the Decree identifies a territorial office but documents no reasoned transfer, preservation step or receiving reference. Because Fiscalía's stated premise is that no offence is disclosed, the absence is an open routing consequence, not a proved procedural breach.
6. **New sources:** the 20 August communication added later professional and judicial material with express limitations. The Decree answers only whether it strengthens a case against the same magistrate; it does not document classification, preservation or routing of the separate private extraconcursal module.
7. **Reliance on DIP 2/2026:** the clarification relies on the earlier provisional closure without addressing whether the formally registered 11 March correction to its appeal-status premise was incorporated, examined or treated as immaterial.
8. **Date and remedy citations:** the earlier Decree was signed on 19 August and notified on 20 August, while the clarification calls it a Decree dated 20 August. The no-appeal sentence cites Article 112 of Law 39/2015 and Article 124 of the Constitution; Fiscalía Circular 2/2022, section 15, is the more direct published authority for non-appealability of criminal investigation-proceeding decisions. This is a date error and reasons/fit question, not a repository finding of invalidity.
