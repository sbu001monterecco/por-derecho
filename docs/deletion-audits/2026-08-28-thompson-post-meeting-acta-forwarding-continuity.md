## 5. 2016-onward pattern control

The pattern cannot be inferred merely from the 2022 event. Each meeting or attempted meeting must be reconstructed through:

`date/body → convener → lawful recipient/address → list version → invitation/service/return/fallback → actual knowledge and source → attendance/proxy/vote → direct ACTA communication → indirect receipt → dissent/challenge → downstream use → actor → innocent explanation`.

The 26-April-2016 meeting was known and attended by the LPB/related side. It disproves a universal formulation that every meeting from 2016 was concealed. It does not disprove concealment of particular other meetings.
