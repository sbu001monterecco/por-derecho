No historical proposal, meeting or term sheet should be presented as a current facility or commitment without fresh evidence.

## Outbound-email incident and correction

The external-counsel mandate email was sent twice because two separate transmitting tool actions were invoked 26 seconds apart. Gmail did not retry the first message. The second call occurred during what should have been read-only verification.

The corrective control now requires:

- one exact authorisation for one outbound mutation;
- authorisation consumption and mutation lock before the first provider call;
- a closed lock after success, error, timeout or unknown outcome;
- read-only verification operations only;
- pre-send and post-send duplicate-window checks;
- exactly one matching native Sent message before successful verification; and
- fresh authority for any retry, correction, apology or follow-up.

The incident is therefore preserved as an operational failure and control improvement, without exposing the private communication.
