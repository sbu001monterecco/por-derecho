## 1. Boundary and proposition

> **Finca 8584 is not an LPB estate asset. Its forced transfer may nevertheless have material insolvency, productive-unit, recovery and accountability consequences.**

Matkator, LPB and Aweswell remain legally distinct. The economic and physical unity of Sun Park/MYND Yaiza cannot be used to import Matkator title into masa activa. The separate title cannot be used to erase the common hotel, control, value, income and remedial consequences.

## 2. Why the title can matter to Concurso 36/2012

The title may be relevant to the insolvency track because it forms part of the same hotel productive system in which LPB held the majority property/operating perimeter and through which the funded exit, valuation, operator solution and creditor recovery were structured.

The possible consequences include:

- integrity and deliverability of the hotel productive unit;
- valuation and competitive-bid assumptions;
- the ONA/Lagune/financing counterfactual;
- claims arising from access, works, possession and hotel-wide transformation;
- lost or redirected income and accounting;
- evidence of who controlled, altered and benefited from the hotel;
- restitution and recovery architecture;
- review of the AC’s preservation, investigation and claim-prosecution conduct;
- review of what the Commercial Court knew and how it responded;
- calificación, separation and appellate accountability.

Each consequence requires a specific LPB estate right, claim, value or duty. None follows automatically from physical proximity.

## 3. Productive-unit logic

The relevant structure is:

```text
separate Matkator title
        +
LPB majority hotel/operating perimeter
        +
common infrastructure, access, services and hotel operation
        =
one productive hotel system without unity of ownership
```

An act affecting a non-estate title may damage the hotel-level operating and recovery system without converting the title into estate property.

## 4. ONA/funded-exit direction

The existing repository records the funded-exit route as dependent upon an intact productive unit, reliable debt quantification, operator/investor deliverability and preservation of hotel value.

The 8584 issue should therefore be tested against:

- whether mixed-title hotel integrity was disclosed and protected;
- whether unauthorised control or works impaired deliverability;
- whether loss of Matkator title weakens a restitution/recovery package capable of restoring hotel value;
- whether the current ETJ disposition helps harden the result that the funded exit sought to avoid.

This does not prove that the ONA route would certainly have closed or that every later loss was caused by one omission.

Adverse decisions do not themselves establish prevaricación. The allegation must be tested through exact act, jurisdiction, knowledge, duty, omission, reasoning, consequence and lawful explanation.
