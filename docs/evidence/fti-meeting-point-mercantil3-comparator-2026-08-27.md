## Why this comparator is linked

A separate, facially unrelated insolvency dispute concerning the Beatriz Hoteles / Inparsa perimeter was publicly reported as involving a criminal complaint alleging prevaricacion in connection with proceedings before Juzgado de lo Mercantil n. 3 de Las Palmas. The Sun Park / Luchy Playa Blanca record must not treat that outside dispute as proof of wrongdoing in Concurso 36/2012 or in any Meeting Point proceeding. It is retained as a **comparator** because it raises independently testable institutional questions that overlap with our own complaints and preservation requests: judicial handling of distressed-company value; treatment of creditor/debtor proposals; procedural transparency; conflict/recusal and disclosure questions; protection of the insolvency estate and productive unit; and whether contemporaneous records permit later reconstruction of who knew what, when, and on what evidential basis.

The comparator is therefore relevant only to methodology and pattern-testing. Similarity is a reason to obtain records and test hypotheses, not to infer guilt from another case.

## Criminal-lens questions — framed for investigation, not conclusion

The strongest lawful formulation is a reliance-and-knowledge test:

- Was Sun Park / Club Sei represented anywhere as an owned, controlled, managed, contracted, pipeline or income-producing asset of Meeting Point/FTI or a related entity?
- Did any such representation enter SEPI, German/Bavarian/EU funding, bank, auditor, administrator, restructuring, valuation, insurance or investor materials?
- What legal basis was stated for marketing/selling Sun Park inventory at each relevant date, and who approved it?
- What revenues, receivables, commissions, deposits or customer liabilities arose from Sun Park/Club Sei, and where were they booked?
- Did FTI/Meeting Point compliance, executives, advisers or insolvency office-holders receive notice of the disputed provenance/rights, and what preservation or investigation followed?
- Did any person make a materially false statement, omit a material fact where disclosure was legally required, misappropriate proceeds, frustrate recovery, launder proceeds, or misuse public funds? These are **questions for evidence and competent authorities**, not findings.
- Could the answers affect the German insolvency estate, Spanish restructuring/insolvency proceedings, SEPI recovery, creditor recoveries, tax treatment, or asset-restitution claims?
