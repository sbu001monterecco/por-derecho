## 1. Perimeter-wide rule

## 2. Do not collapse three questions

For every finca, separate:

1. **lawful title acquisition** — who acquired what, from whom, under which instrument and with what consent/consideration;
2. **physical and operational control** — who possesses, accesses, alters, inventories, markets and exploits the corresponding hotel space;
3. **terminal benefit** — who receives hotel use, revenue, financing value, regulatory advantage or title consolidation.

A valid deed may prove acquisition of a particular title. It does not prove acquisition or consent concerning neighbouring or third-party titles, common rights, operator authority, past revenues or physical alterations.

## 3. Title-status taxonomy

Use the canonical T-A…T-G statuses:

- verified consensual acquisition;
- consent/validity/disclosure/authority/pressure disputed;
- alleged non-consensual possession or integration;
- judicial/administrative disposition;
- represented inclusion in corporate/productive-unit transfer;
- unresolved/source gap;
- common/service/operational asset.

No general statement that Acosta Matos “lawfully acquired the hotel” or “unlawfully took every finca” is permitted without the complete denominator and chain.

## 4. Title-on-title injury questions

For each title controlled by the Acosta perimeter, test whether that control was used to affect another title through:

- common-element or access control;
- Community representation, notice, voting or debt creation;
- physical works, fusion, division, renumbering or room reallocation;
- unified operator/PMS/booking/invoice control;
- withholding of accounts, fruits or owner information;
- procedural claims, enforcement, adjudication or Registry change;
- corporate, tourism, tax, finance or public-aid representations;
- obstruction or weakening of inspection, restoration and restitution.

The fact of hotel-wide control is relevant to capacity and benefit. It does not by itself establish criminal agreement, falsehood or lack of consent.

## 6. Tourism and operator authority

For every privately owned title included in the hotel operation, identify:

- owner at the relevant date;
- documentary enabling title supplied by that owner or a person with authority;
- exact unit/title schedule;
- approved room inventory and plans;

Unity of exploitation does not itself transfer property or manufacture owner consent.

## 7. Corporate and 2022 transaction modules

The CAM→HNT operation must be audited against:

- complete asset/right schedules;
- excluded titles and third-party rights;
- Registry implementation;
- Community/common rights;
- operator and management rights;
- construction/works assets;
- claims, liabilities and owner-accounting obligations;

A BORME or corporate description records what was represented. It does not by itself transfer a third party’s title.

## 8. RIC/RICPE, incentives, FEDER and finance

For each file, map:

`beneficiary entity → represented project/hotel → title schedule → room count → owner/operator authority → eligible asset/cost → works location → finance/security → revenue forecast → public decision → later use`.

The question is whether a representation included or depended on third-party title space or rights and, if so, on what authority. Convergence of several regimes does not itself prove double funding, fraud or misuse.
