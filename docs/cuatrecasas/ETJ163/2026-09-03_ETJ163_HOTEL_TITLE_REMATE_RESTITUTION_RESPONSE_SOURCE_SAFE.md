## 2. The execution object is a hotel title, not an autonomous apartment

Finca 8584 may remain a juridically identifiable registered property. That does not answer what the adjudicatee would receive in practice.

The material questions are:

- what present hotel space corresponds to the title;
- how it is accessed;
- who possesses and operates it;
- whether it is included in hotel room inventory;
- who receives revenue;
- what owner/operator authority is relied upon;
- what restoration or restitution rights depend upon continued title.

The title is the legal anchor for Matkator’s asserted property, possession, Community, hotel-operation, accounting, evidential and restitution rights.

## 3. Adjudication itself may be difficult to reverse

The pending risk is not confined to a later cession. A decree of adjudication and Registry presentation may:

- remove Matkator’s title-based standing;
- divide accrued injury from ongoing and future hotel rights;
- introduce a new claimed third-party position;
- weaken access, accounting and revenue claims;
- complicate restoration of the physical hotel component;
- permit the current disputed hotel configuration to be invoked as an irreversible status quo.

This does not predetermine the legal effect of any transfer or extinguish accrued criminal/civil claims. It identifies why preservation before perfection is materially different from a damages claim after transfer.

## 4. DP 711 and the current hotel-control perimeter

DP 711 is not invoked as proof that HNT, Canarian Hospitality or any person committed an offence. It is relevant because Matkator’s filed allegations identify:

- the alleged present physical controller and exploiter;
- unauthorised alteration/integration and exclusion;
- continuing hotel use and alleged absence of accounting;
- the specific restitution context affected by the ETJ transfer.

The Acosta Matos / HNT / Canarian Hospitality perimeter is therefore a specific real-beneficiary hypothesis, not a generic or remote third party. The formal adjudicatario/cesionario remains unproved.

## 5. Formal recipient, principal and post-transfer use

Any protective order or traceability request should separate:

1. formal adjudicatario;
2. formal cesionario;
3. person for whose account the recipient acts;
4. source/crediting of consideration where legally relevant and obtainable;
5. entity retaining physical possession and hotel use;
6. entity receiving hotel revenue after transfer.

A different formal name does not by itself establish an independent terminal beneficiary. Conversely, a lawful transparent transaction or settlement is not automatically criminal.
