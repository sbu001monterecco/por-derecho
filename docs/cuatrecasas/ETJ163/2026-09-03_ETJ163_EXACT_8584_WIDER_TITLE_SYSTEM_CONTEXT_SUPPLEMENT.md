## 1. Procedural precision

The current controlled ETJ remate/adjudication object is finca 8584. Relief in ETJ 163 must remain tied to the exact property and acts before that court unless the certified file establishes a wider object.

The wider title system is relevant to context, prejudice, proportionality, beneficiary, preservation and restitution. It does not make finca 8588 or every hotel title an object of this execution.

## 2. Correct contextual proposition

Suggested formulation:

> La finca 8.584 constituye el objeto registral concreto de esta ejecución. La referencia a la finca 8.588 y al sistema de títulos del hotel no pretende ampliar el objeto ejecutivo. Acredita que 8.584 no funciona como un apartamento turístico autónomo, sino como uno de varios títulos jurídicamente separados integrados en la explotación unitaria del establecimiento. Su adjudicación puede afectar derechos, prueba y restitución que exceden del valor aislado de la descripción registral, sin convertir otros títulos en bienes ejecutados.

## 3. Wider prejudice without estate collapse

The protective filing may explain that disposition of 8584 can affect:

- Matkator’s direct title, access, accounts, physical identification and restitution;
- the evidential and operational relationship with finca 8588;
- Aweswell’s subsidiary/client/investment and recovery position;
- LPB’s productive-unit, funded-exit, claim, evidence and recovery interests in Concurso 36/2012;
- the real-beneficiary inquiry concerning CAM/HNT/Canarian Hospitality;
- tourism, operator-authority, revenue and regulatory records.

It must also state that Matkator, Aweswell and LPB remain separate legal persons and that 8584/8588 remain outside LPB’s insolvency estate.

## 4. Preservation request

Where procedurally available, request preservation and traceability sufficient to answer:

1. exact material space corresponding to 8584;
2. current possessor/operator and owner enabling title;
3. adjudicatario, any cesionario, principal and terminal beneficiary;
4. intended post-transfer possession and hotel use;
5. whether the transfer assumes or affects rights concerning neighbouring or integrated spaces;
6. whether the current operator will continue receiving revenue;
7. what documents identify or exclude 8588 and other third-party titles;
8. what effect adjudication has on existing restitution, accounting and evidence claims.

## 5. Boundary

The wider context supports a tailored non-irreversibility and traceability request. It does not invite the ETJ court to decide criminal liability, title to every hotel finca, the merits of DP 711 or the validity of acts in Concurso 36/2012.
