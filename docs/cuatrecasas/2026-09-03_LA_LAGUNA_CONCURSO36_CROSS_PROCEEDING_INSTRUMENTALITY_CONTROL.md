## 1. Controlling claimant position

The claimant’s position is not limited to professional negligence and is not limited to a risk that an offence might occur in the future.

> **The claimant alleges a completed estafa procesal associated with the Cuatrecasas/Matkator La Laguna proceedings and alleges that those proceedings were or are being used as an instrument to participate in, enable, protect, conceal, regularise or make harder to reverse the wider alleged procedural and concursal fraud affecting Concurso 36/2012 and the Aweswell–LPB–Matkator hotel perimeter.**

This must be recorded as `PARTY_POSITION / ALLEGED_COMPLETED_OFFENCE`. It is not an adjudicated finding and must not be published as established guilt.

## 2. Why 8584 is evidence, not the perimeter limit

Finca 8584 is the current ETJ disposition object and therefore supplies a concrete act, title and requested consequence. The theory is wider:

- finca 8588 is also source-locked historically as a Matkator hotel title;
- every additional Matkator title must be included when proved;
- every other Sun Park/MYND title must be classified title-by-title where its acquisition, possession, integration, exploitation, transfer, financing or remedy bears on the common hotel result;
- the alleged criminal instrument is not simply “taking one apartment” but using a legal process against a title embedded in the hotel system in a way alleged to weaken the remaining rights, evidence and remedies of the wider perimeter.

## 3. Elements of the alleged La Laguna estafa procesal

The repository must identify, rather than merely label:

1. **actor** — individual lawyer, team, client, signatory, witness, expert or other participant;
2. **procedural device** — exact evidence manipulation, materially false premise, selective presentation, omission or analogous procedural fraud alleged;
3. **knowledge** — what the actor actually knew, source received and date;
4. **judicial error** — what proposition the court is alleged to have accepted;
5. **resolution** — exact judgment, order, decree, enforcement act or other decision produced;
6. **economic prejudice** — debt, title, costs, possession, access, revenue, standing, restitution and wider perimeter loss;
7. **benefit** — debt satisfaction, professional benefit, title consolidation, hotel control, continuing revenue or regularisation;
8. **causation** — why the alleged device, not merely an adverse legal ruling, produced the decision and prejudice;
9. **lawful explanation** — valid debt, lawful procedure, adequate disclosure, independent court reasoning and other adverse evidence.

The claimant may maintain that these elements are already satisfied by the filed record. The repository must nevertheless preserve the distinction between pleaded completion and judicial determination.

## 4. Concert and participation analysis

### Conspiracy / proposition

For each alleged participant, identify evidence of:

- agreement with at least one other person;
- the particular offence contemplated;
- resolution to execute it;
- invitation to participate where proposition is alleged;
- timing before completion;
- act or communication distinguishing concert from parallel self-interest.

### Authorship / cooperation / complicity

The word “enablement” is a routing prompt, not a sufficient legal conclusion. Record whether the alleged contribution is:

- direct or joint execution;
- use of another as an instrument;
- direct inducement;
- necessary cooperation;
- non-essential but knowing prior or simultaneous assistance;
- a separate later offence.

### Concealment / encubrimiento

Do not use encubrimiento as a catch-all. It ordinarily concerns qualifying post-offence conduct by someone who was not an author or accomplice. If the same actor is alleged to have joined the original plan, test continuing participation, later autonomous acts, document use, obstruction or regularisation instead.

## 6. The “title-on-title” criminal hypothesis

The alleged instrumentality is not that one finca physically attacks another. It is that control over some titles, common elements, operator machinery or procedural rights is used against another title so as to:

- erase or obscure its physical identity;
- exclude the owner from access and governance;
- exploit its corresponding space and revenue;
- deprive the owner of evidence and accounts;
- convert prior de facto control into later legal/Registry control;
- remove the title from the party seeking restitution;
- use the fragmented result against recovery in another proceeding.

This is described as **title-on-title injury**. It may support investigation of several offences or civil wrongs; it is not itself a statutory offence.

## 7. Concurso 36/2012 offence discipline

The repository must not state “fraude concursal” without identifying the route. Candidate routes must be separated:

- alleged estafa procesal through a concursal filing or decision;
- insolvency offences tied to acts by the debtor or persons acting for it;
- alleged false-document creation or use;
- alleged obstruction or concealment;
- alleged disloyal administration or misappropriation;
- alleged prevaricación or omission by a public official;
- civil/concursal invalidity, breach of duty or professional negligence that does not meet a penal threshold.

The La Laguna conduct may be relevant as an instrument, continuation, benefit-protection mechanism or evidence of knowledge. It does not automatically transform every act in Concurso 36/2012 into the same offence or create territorial/jurisdictional identity.

## 10. Counsel-facing drafting consequence

A DP 748 filing should preserve both levels:

- **specificity:** the exact act, court, title and resulting decision/prejudice required for the La Laguna criminal analysis;
- **context:** 8584 as the present worked example of a hotel-title class whose disposition allegedly affects 8588, any other Matkator rights, the hotel-wide control structure and the Aweswell/LPB/Concurso 36/2012 recovery perimeter.

The filing should not seek a general criminal retrial of Concurso 36/2012. It should explain why the La Laguna act has autonomous criminal significance and why its intended or actual hotel/concursal effects are relevant to motive, benefit, prejudice, restitution and finite evidence requests.
