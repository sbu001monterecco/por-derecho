## 2. Controlling correction

The appeal must not present finca 8584 as an autonomous apartment or argue that physical transformation automatically extinguished the registered title.

The exact object is:

> **a registered title component inside a regulated and functionally unified hotel establishment, whose corresponding physical space, possession, access, room identity, exploitation and revenue are alleged to be integrated into MYND Yaiza.**

The title is the legal anchor for Matkator’s asserted rights of ownership, possession, access, Community participation, hotel-exploitation authority, accounting, evidence, restoration and restitution.

## 4. Exact candidate act and current document

The candidate criminally relevant conduct is narrowed to the alleged knowing use or continuation of judicial adjudication machinery over the hotel-title component after notice of its disputed physical integration, possession, exploitation and restitutionary significance, in circumstances capable of producing an additional patrimonial disposition and impairing the injured party’s remedies.

The 3 September 2026 ETJ filing is not, by itself, proof of fraud. It is relevant because it individualises:

- **the exact property:** finca registral 8584;
- **the exact pending act:** adjudication;
- **the requested acceleration:** continuation “sin más dilación”;
- **the possible subsequent disposition:** cession of the remate to a third party;
- **the title position:** Matkator remains registered owner;
- **the material-transfer position:** the adjudicatee would take the property in its present condition.

## 5. Why adjudication itself is the patrimonial consequence

The prejudice does not arise only if a later cession to Acosta Matos or another party is proved. Adjudication itself may:

1. remove or weaken the title-based standing from which Matkator seeks access, inspection, accounts, restoration and restitution;
2. divide past injury, continuing hotel use and future rights between different holders;
3. introduce a new claimed Registry position capable of complicating restitution;
4. detach future hotel revenue and operator-authority questions from Matkator;
5. weaken the evidential bridge between historic title and current hotel space;
6. allow the existing disputed hotel configuration to be presented as irreversible or normalised.

A later transfer does not automatically extinguish accrued criminal responsibility or damages. The urgent issue is the possible impairment of specific restitutionary and continuing-right remedies.

## 7. Formal recipient and real beneficiary

The formal adjudicatario, any formal cesionario and the real or terminal beneficiary must be separated.

No completed cession or formal connected assignee is presently proved. The evidence-based beneficiary hypothesis is nevertheless specific: CAM / Hotel New Trend / Canarian Hospitality / the Acosta Matos-controlled MYND Yaiza perimeter, because that is the perimeter alleged to control and exploit the hotel into which the Matkator space is integrated.

An unrelated title holder would not thereby acquire an independently operating apartment or tourism business. Its practical value would appear to depend upon either:

- reversing the existing hotel integration through litigation and restoration; or
- reaching an arrangement with the perimeter controlling the hotel operation.

This supports investigation of the real beneficiary. It does not prove a nominee, clandestine payment, concert or criminal settlement.
