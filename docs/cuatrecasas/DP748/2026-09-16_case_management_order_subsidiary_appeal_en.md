## Source-safe English translation

The previous submissions lodged by Court Agent Ms Adriana Hernández Díaz, together with the accompanying document, are to be joined to the case file, with a copy to the Public Prosecutor.

As the appeal was lodged subsidiarily to the reconsideration application and the latter was in part unsuccessful, **the Public Prosecutor is to be given five days in which to make submissions**.

The order states that it may be challenged by reconsideration before the LAJ within three days.

---

## Verified procedural effect

From 16 September 2026 it is no longer accurate to state that Article 766.4 LECrim processing has not been located. The order verifies:

1. that the appeal was lodged subsidiarily with the reconsideration application;
2. that the reconsideration application was partly unsuccessful; and
3. that the submissions phase is being processed with a five-day transfer to the Public Prosecutor.

Still **not publicly verified** as of this control are:

- the Public Prosecutor's later submissions;
- any further party transfers;
- physical remittal to the Audiencia Provincial;
- an appellate roll number;
- the appellate Section, panel or judge; and
- the appellate outcome.
