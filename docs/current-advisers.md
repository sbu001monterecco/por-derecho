## Adviser-status classification rule

Every professional person or firm appearing in the repository should be classified, where possible, as one of:

- OUR CURRENT ADVISER
- OUR PRE-APPROVED ADVISER / POTENTIAL NEW ENGAGEMENT
- OUR HISTORICAL ADVISER
- PROSPECTIVE ADVISER / ONBOARDING IN PROGRESS
- THIRD-PARTY PROFESSIONAL
- ADVERSE-PARTY PROFESSIONAL
- ROLE UNDER VERIFICATION

No lawyer, adviser or professional may be classified as adverse merely because their name appears in an adverse proceeding, email chain, court filing or third-party document. Where status is uncertain, the default is **ROLE UNDER VERIFICATION**, not adverse.
