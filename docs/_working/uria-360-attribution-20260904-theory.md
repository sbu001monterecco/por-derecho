# Unitary theory to test

The publication and investigation should treat the CaixaBank matter as the **CaixaBank–Bankia / predecessor-lender / creditor-servicer-adviser perimeter**, not CaixaBank in isolation.

Central triangle:

1. Acosta Matos/private-actor perimeter;
2. Administrador Concursal;
3. concurso judge.

The allegation to test is two-directional: alleged capture/instrumentalisation of the CaixaBank claim by the AC and Acosta Matos perimeter with alleged judicial enablement; and whether persons within the CaixaBank/Bankia/adviser perimeter, after notice, knowingly continued, assisted, legitimised or benefited from that alleged instrumentalisation.

Actor-by-actor matrix must record: first notice/knowledge date; act after notice; contribution; coordination; benefit; counter-evidence; strongest sustainable legal attribution; and what evidence is still required.
