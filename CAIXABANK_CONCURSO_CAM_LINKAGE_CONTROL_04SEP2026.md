## IMPORTANT ATTRIBUTION CORRECTION — BORJA WITNESS

The public record must not characterise Francisco de Borja Rodríguez-Batllori Laffitte as a witness whom Aweswell independently selected, positively wanted, endorsed or affirmatively relied upon.

### Court-record fact

The later court citation formally records Borja as a witness proposed by both claimant and defendant. That procedural record is preserved and must not be rewritten.

### Claimant-side provenance and statement

Claimant-side correspondence records the initiative as coming from CaixaBank. A 14 January 2026 forwarding note describes the notification as: **“AC to declare as witness (proposed by Caixabank)”**.

**Gil Marer states, speaking for himself and the claimant legal team, that Aweswell did not want or independently choose Borja as its witness. The claimant side reluctantly agreed/adhered to CaixaBank's request because it was simultaneously managing pressure from several legal proceedings and did not wish to risk prejudicing the Valencia proceeding, antagonising the court or creating an avoidable procedural issue.**

Gil's informal description of the practical position is: **“We needed him like a hole in the head.”** That quotation is published as Gil Marer's first-person description of the claimant side's attitude, not as a judicial finding.

### Mandatory publication synthesis

**CaixaBank sought Borja's testimony. The court later recorded him procedurally as proposed by both sides after claimant adherence. Gil Marer states that Aweswell's adherence was reluctant and procedural, not affirmative witness sponsorship, endorsement or reliance.**

Do not reduce the formal court record to “solely proposed by CaixaBank”; equally, do not use the formal two-party designation to imply that Aweswell positively selected or wanted Borja.

Status: **COURT-RECORD PROCEDURAL FACT + CLAIMANT FIRST-PERSON CLARIFICATION.** The reason for claimant adherence is Gil Marer's statement and claimant-side provenance; it is not represented as a judicial finding.
