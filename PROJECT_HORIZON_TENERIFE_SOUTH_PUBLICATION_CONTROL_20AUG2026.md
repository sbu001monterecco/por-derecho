# Project Horizon · Tenerife South — publication control

## Purpose

Publish a bilingual, carefully qualified live redevelopment workstream in South-West Tenerife, municipality of Adeje, while preserving the exact private site and transaction perimeter.

## Public proposition

- Working public identity: **Project Horizon · Tenerife South** / **Project Horizon · Tenerife Sur**.
- Hospitality-led **active holiday community**, designed primarily for independent adults 50+.
- Flexible short, extended, seasonal and longer tourism-compliant stay concept.
- Hotel/resort/community/sport/wellness thesis; not a residential sell-down.
- Reuse, reposition and selective rebuild are all live design tests.
- Public geography stops at South-West Tenerife and the municipality of Adeje.

## Historical operating lineage

The public page may describe the historical Sun Park / Sun Rockers proposition only at the level supported by the archival record: community-based HolidayLiving, independent over-50s, singles and couples, repeat behaviour, social activity and historically longer stays. The historical record is not a forecast, and it does not establish continuity of contracts, rights, legal structures or products.

## Withheld transaction and location data

The public website must not disclose the exact private property, neighbourhood/site name, address, Registry/cadastral identifiers, precise land area, owner/seller identity, lender/security position, asking price, acquisition structure, financing discussions, adviser identities, private correspondence, historic exact redevelopment density or other information capable of unnecessarily reverse-engineering the site.

These items may be used internally for diligence and design testing but are not publication inputs.

## Legal / product boundaries

- Do not present Project Horizon as permanent residence, residential apartments, senior housing, retirement housing, assisted living, a care home, nursing accommodation, timeshare or fractional ownership.
- Do not represent an absolute age restriction without current specialist review. Public language is **designed primarily for independent adults 50+** / **concebida principalmente para adultos independientes de 50+**.
- Do not revive historic long-term contractual products or terminology without a fresh tourism, consumer, immigration/residency, financial-promotion and tax review.
- No medical, nursing, care, insurance or healthcare entitlement is promised.

## Transaction / authority boundaries

Public inclusion does not mean ownership, exclusivity, SPA, acquisition completion, site control, valuation, planning entitlement, tourism approval, operator appointment, lender/investor commitment, construction commitment or opening date.

No awareness, dialogue, support, consideration, endorsement or approval by the Ayuntamiento de Adeje, Cabildo de Tenerife, Gobierno de Canarias or another public authority is represented.
