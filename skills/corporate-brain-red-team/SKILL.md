# Corporate Brain Red Team

For each material proposition:

1. Quote or identify the exact proposition and current evidence status.
2. Identify the strongest supporting primary/official sources.
3. Identify contrary, limiting or later-correcting evidence.
4. Test source independence; duplicated copies of one origin are not independent corroboration.
5. Test identity, entity, capacity, asset, proceeding and date boundaries.
6. Test alternative explanations.
7. Separate chronology from knowledge, intent, causation and liability.
8. Identify missing sources that could materially change the conclusion.
9. Produce a traffic-light result:
   - GREEN: support complete for the stated scope and material contrary evidence addressed.
   - YELLOW: support exists but material qualification/gap remains.
   - ORANGE: source conflict, identity ambiguity or external verification dependency.
   - RED: unsupported or unsafe to state as fact.
10. Preserve the underlying position accurately; red-team review is not automatic dilution and not automatic escalation.

A red-team result never authorises an external act by itself.
