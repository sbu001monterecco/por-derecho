## Public assistance rule

Invite relevant custodians to:

- confirm existence;
- identify custody/lawful route;
- preserve records and metadata;
- facilitate lawful production;
- provide certified indices where full access is restricted;
- provide contradictory/exculpatory/contextual material;
- correct public factual errors;
- explain the lawful bridge between two states where the current record is incomplete.

## Litigation-strategy boundary

Do not infer the internal execution status of an action from this public layer. Do not publish privileged advice, witness preparation, tactical sequencing, deadline calculations, settlement positions, private contacts, draft pleadings, confidential provider locators, native private email bodies, third-party personal data or security-sensitive preservation methods.

Website publication does not itself authorise an email, filing, portal submission, complaint, appeal, preservation notice or meeting request. Formal external acts remain governed by the controlling action registers and separate authorization rules.

## Correction principle

The invitation is symmetric. Supportive, contradictory and exculpatory evidence are all relevant. Where reliable primary evidence changes a public proposition, correct the website visibly and preserve the supersession trail.
