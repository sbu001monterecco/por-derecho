La Laguna ⇄ Sun Park / Concurso 36/2012

A unitary, non-fragmented, source-first reconstruction of the relationship between Cambiario 1048/2019, ETJ 163/2020, DP 748/2026, finca 8584, Cuatrecasas's professional mandate and the possible patrimonial projection onto the Sun Park complex and the perimeter of Concurso 36/2012.

No judicial finding of crime, coordination or beneficiary

Reading rule: do not conflate; do connect. The separate legal personalities of Matkator, Aweswell and Luchy Playa Blanca, and the formal separation between Cambiario, ETJ, DP 748 and Concurso 36/2012, are preserved. That separateness does not make the documented professional, ownership, hotel, financing and patrimonial connections irrelevant.

2. Two new milestones: 1–3 September 2026

Proved

DP 748 · 16 July order communicated 1 September

The court accepts that the original dismissal order lacked concrete reasoning, but maintains provisional dismissal because the exact act, specific assets and minimum criminal indicium were not sufficiently particularised. It expressly records the possibility of reopening with other indicia.

Proved

ETJ · Cuatrecasas filing of 3 September

Cuatrecasas opposes Matkator's reconsideration appeal, expressly relies on DP 748, argues that physical transformation does not change the legal identity of finca 8584, and asks for enforcement to continue. It describes adjudication as the immediately pending procedural act and requests power to assign the auction award to a third party.

Boundary: no completed assignment or recipient is proved here.

3. Procedural-fraud allegation: what is already in the record and what remains to be verified

Party allegation · not a judicial finding

Matkator's 5 May 2025 nullity filing alleges, in substance, that the Cambiario moved toward service by edict while Cuatrecasas had direct channels to Matkator's administrator and continued debt communications. The allegation raises a possible relationship between known localisation information, alleged lack of effective defence and formation of the title later enforced.

Decisive primary-source check still required: original claim, localisation/service record, request for service by edict, order authorising it, and the contact information actually disclosed to the court.

known contact→edicts / allegedly incomplete localisation information→alleged lack of defence→Cambiario title→ETJ 163/2020
Public counter-evidence. The reconstruction also establishes genuine unpaid professional fees, promissory notes delivered on 22 November 2018 to cover invoices/costs, and direct collection/settlement efforts. This page therefore does not advance a “there was no debt” theory; the open question concerns the regularity of the instruments and procedural route leading to the title and enforcement.

4. Factual and professional bridge to Sun Park / Concurso 36/2012

Proved

Mandate and knowledge

Cuatrecasas acted in Concurso 36/2012, knew the Aweswell–Luchy–Matkator structure, produced hotel/financing due diligence and rescue materials, and received information about the physical and operational configuration of the complex.

Proved

Historic “Complex” objective

In October 2019 Cuatrecasas described its work in the Concurso as capable of contributing to “saving the Complex”. That contemporaneous record shows the mandate was not internally understood as a set of economically unrelated silos.

Proved

Finca 8584

The enforced property remains registered to Matkator according to Cuatrecasas's current position. Its physical/economic integration in the hotel is relevant context, but does not automatically make it an LPB insolvency-estate asset.
