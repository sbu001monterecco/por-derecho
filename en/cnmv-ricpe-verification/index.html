Why July 2021 is material

The preserved RICPE certification describes 262 properties: 54 CAM, 190 LPB and 18 third-party, places the principal acquisition as future/conditional, records absence of the necessary LOI and complete due diligence, and prior project withdrawal. CAM filed it in Insolvency 36/2012 the next day.

That does not prove an infringement or dictate any particular supervisory conclusion. It does create a finite question: what title/project-maturity picture did CNMV have when assessing investor-facing information, and when, if ever, did it receive the narrower July-2021 picture?

2019: RICPE governance is also part of the supervisory context

The official CNMV register identifies RICPE as venture-capital company no. 295 and records José Acosta Matos as director from 04/11/2019, before the 2020 Sun Park presentation. That relationship does not establish a poorly managed conflict. It makes it relevant to identify what disclosure, abstention, governance, Control Unit and related-party procedure applied to the project.

Supervised status has a precise limit

RICPE supervised by CNMV ≠ Sun Park approved by CNMV.

CNMV's own register information states that responsibility for the accuracy of the prospectus/DFI lies with the manager or self-managed entity and that CNMV does not verify that content. A regulatory framework can add institutional credibility; it does not make CNMV the civil guarantor of title, physical project perimeter or each promoter statement.
