The short route

Six checks, one control question.

The chronology identifies documents and verification responsibilities. It does not convert temporal sequence into knowledge, intent or breach.

1 · CNMV 2021

Sun Park alert

The controlled record identifies filing 2021002141. CNMV outgoing 2026008544 confirms supervisory actions arising from that filing.

2 · RICPE 2020

What was presented

Reconstruct representations about the project, acquisition, title, encumbrances, investment and timetable and who approved or circulated them.

3 · 20 Jul 2021

What RICPE knew

The internal certification described 54 CAM units, 190 LPB units and 18 third-party units, CAM acquisition as future/conditional, an unsigned LOI and full due diligence not started.

4 · 21 Jul 2021

External use

CAM filed that certification in Insolvency 36/2012. Purpose, authorisation, receipt and treatment should be reconstructable without assuming judicial use validated its content.

5 · afterwards

Re-entry and funding

Identify the document, satisfied condition, waiver, valuation and decision that allowed the project to move from the restrictive position to reactivation, HNT, financing and execution.

The formal communication to RICPE was submitted through its Ethical Channel and technically acknowledged by the platform. Admission, investigation, preservation, Board treatment and a merits decision are not yet established.

WHAT CHANGED?
AFTERWARDS · OBSERVABLE OUTCOME

Reactivation; later title within its exact perimeter; HNT; funding; regional incentive/ERDF; opening and operation as MYND Yaiza. Each step requires its own source, decision-maker and date.

Reading rule: neither present hotel operation nor later title retrospectively answers what RICPE knew, verified, conditioned or corrected in 2020–2021.

Separate competences

Relevant context does not mean CNMV must decide everything.

The value of a unitary reconstruction is to expose dependencies without erasing the limits of each legal regime.

Supervisory core to verify

RICPE and its internal chain

Project and investor information, governance, conflicts, controls, due diligence, valuation, conditions, funding, monitoring, preservation and any correction, to the extent falling within CNMV's competence.

Evidential context

Title, Community and insolvency

These help test the premises entering the project. Their civil, insolvency or registry validity belongs to their own regimes; CNMV is not asked to replace those bodies.

Separate branches

Tax, public aid and criminal law

RIC, regional incentive/ERDF and any criminal hypothesis retain their own elements, authorities and standards. Coexisting layers create a documentary reconciliation question, not an automatic conclusion.

Contradiction discipline: Project Sun Rock also publishes adverse material and accepts that valid authority, independent DD, properly managed conflicts, documented waivers, non-overlapping costs or a coherent contemporaneous explanation can weaken or modify parts of its analysis. Verified corrections should receive equivalent prominence.
