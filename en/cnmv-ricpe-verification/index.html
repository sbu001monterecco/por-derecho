SUPERVISION · alert → corpus → examination → action → reassessment

CNMV: the critical issue is no longer whether an alert was received, but what actually entered the supervisory corpus.

This page separates party allegations, facts confirmed by CNMV itself, and questions that remain open. It does not presume supervisory failure or treat an allegation as proof of fraud.

Confirmed documentary anchors

13 January 2021 · registered alert

The initial complaint told CNMV that RIC Private Equity was promoting Sun Park as a RIC investment project associated with CAM and alleged that the hotel's ownership did not match the representation presented to investors. That filing is a contemporaneous allegation, not a CNMV finding.

22 January 2026 · CNMV confirmation

CNMV's General Secretariat, outgoing reference 2026008544, confirmed that the documentation obtained in supervisory actions arising from filing 2021002141 did not contain either the July 2021 Enrique Guerra/RIC certification or CAM's judicial filing of 21 July 2021.

Supervisory secrecy

In the same response, CNMV stated that the remaining questions concerning supervisory actions were not public information and were subject to the secrecy duty under article 233 of Law 6/2023. That restriction does not establish what was or was not done substantively.

Why the missing documents matter

The two July 2021 documents that CNMV confirmed were not in the located corpus are material because, according to the records supplied by the reporting party, they directly addressed CAM's limited ownership, dependence on a future judicial acquisition, and the inability to proceed to full due diligence/investment until the necessary assets were acquired. The supervisory question is not whether those documents “prove fraud”, but whether their absence affected the sufficiency of the corpus used to assess investor-facing information.

Minimum verifiable chronology

  1. 13.01.2021 — initial filing 2021002141 concerning RICPE/Sun Park and investor risk.
  2. 2021–2024 — later submissions and reiterations identified in the documentary chain.
  3. 22.01.2026 — CNMV outgoing 2026008544 confirms the absence of the two July 2021 documents from the located corpus and limits other answers under supervisory secrecy.
  4. 25.02.2026 — formal whistleblower communication systematising discrepancies in ownership, legal consolidation, funding, brochure versions, valuation and operational continuity, and requesting supervisory action and preservation.
  5. 16.07.2026 — institutional communication requesting corpus remediation, preservation, functional identification of responsible units and cooperation with courts and prosecutors.

What the 25 February filing added

Ownership and consolidation

Comparison between investor-facing presentation of CAM as consolidated owner/portfolio asset and later documents placing the principal acquisition in 2022 and recognising third-party ownership.

Multiple brochure versions

Identification of 2020, 2022 and 2023 versions with changes in owner/promoter, project name, financing and status, raising the question of whether changes were traceable to investors.

Funding and valuation

Request to reconcile divergent valuations, RIC capital, insolvency-related financing and subsidies, without assuming improper duplication before the complete funding structure is reconstructed.

Finite questions for CNMV

  1. Which complete/native version of the 2020 brochure and which materials from the 11 November 2020 webinar were obtained and examined?
  2. What ownership, availability and consent documentation was obtained concerning CAM, LPB, Matkator and third parties?
  3. When, if ever, were CAM's 21 July 2021 filing and the RIC/Enrique Guerra certification incorporated?
  4. If absent from the initial corpus, was their materiality formally reassessed?
  5. Was RICPE or CAM required to correct, withdraw, qualify or update investor information?
  6. Which functional units handled receipt, classification, supervision, response, preservation and any reassessment?
  7. Was anything referred to prosecutors, courts, AEAT, SEPBLAC, public-funds authorities or another body?
  8. Without disclosing reserved information, can CNMV confirm whether the matter remains open, was closed, referred, or resulted in corrective action?

Publication rule: received ≠ examined; absence from the located corpus ≠ proof of negligence; supervisory secrecy ≠ no action; alert ≠ proof of infringement; supervised entity ≠ CNMV approval of the specific project.

Evidential status

Confirmed by CNMV source: supervisory actions arose from the January 2021 filing; the two July 2021 documents were absent from the documentation located/obtained in those actions; other supervisory detail was restricted by supervisory secrecy.

Alleged and documented by the reporting party: those documents were material to ownership, conditionality and due diligence, and later project versions and structures require comparison.

Not yet publicly established: which exact documents each unit examined, whether corrective requirements were issued, CNMV's substantive conclusions, whether a later reassessment occurred, and what referrals or coordination took place.
