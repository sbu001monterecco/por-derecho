COMPLETENESS CONTROL · NOT AN ALLEGATION LIST

Critical gaps that still have to be closed.

The Cuatrecasas/La Laguna reconstruction is advanced enough to work from, but it should not be represented as a complete corpus while these documentary questions remain open. Each reminder is closed by primary evidence, negative certification, or an explicit reasoned determination that the gap is immaterial.

Primary rule: “not located” does not mean “does not exist”. A documentary gap does not prove deception, conflict, beneficiary, concerted conduct or an offence. This interface makes the outstanding questions visible precisely so that inference does not replace the document.

Language locks

Do not turn an outstanding question into a conclusion.

Physical transformation is not automatically fraud. A reservation of cession does not prove a cession occurred. The Acosta Matos connection is published as a concrete leading hypothesis based on convergence of asset, operation and benefit—not as a proved identity. Knowledge or context is not intent. Silence or non-response is recorded only as unanswered; it is not proof of breach or wrongdoing. A cross-proceeding connection is not intentional instrumentalisation unless transmission, reliance and purpose are separately evidenced.

The INPARSA / Hoteles Beatriz comparison is preserved as a comparative hypothesis raised from the client side and must be attributed as such; it is not proof of a modus operandi and must not be presented as a general conclusion adopted by legal advisers.

Privilege and continuity

The names, private files, working notes and oral advice of current lawyers and procedural representatives do not form part of this public panel. Any continuity review of those materials should remain privileged. Their absence from the public corpus is not adverse evidence and must not be used to fill a gap against a third party.
