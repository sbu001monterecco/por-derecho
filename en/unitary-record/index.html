UNITARY RECORD · UPDATED 22 AUGUST 2026

One hotel platform. Several legal perimeters.

Sun Park was economically integrated, but it was never one legal person, one title, one operator, one debt or one insolvency estate. The task is to prove each bridge between them.

Control ruleThe present does not prove the past.

A later title, operating hotel, investment project or institutional response does not itself establish the validity of the earlier steps on which it may depend.

The integrated reconstruction

One causal map; no collapsed identities.

The record should be read forwards from structure and backwards from the present outcome. Each connection needs authority, source, disclosure, accounting, consequence and any contrary explanation.

01 · Property and operation

2008 structure

Individual property rights, common governance and hotel exploitation must remain distinct. Physical unity does not itself create universal title or mandate.

02 · Governance and finance

2011–12 pressure

Community authority, debt, voting, banking, enforcement and LPB’s defensive filing must be tested separately and from native records.

03 · Credit and control

2016–18 bridges

Notice, credit assignment, authority and material control require their own documentary chain; a creditor position was not automatically whole-hotel control.

04 · Title and benefit

2019–now

Validation, valuation, adjudication, deed, investment, works and current operation require property-by-property and account-by-account reconciliation.

Perimeter rule

What the record does—and does not—place in Concurso 36/2012.

Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. (LPB) was the debtor in the proceeding. That fact does not automatically place the whole complex, CEXP, the Owners’ Community, Matkator, Aweswell, third-party units, hotel income or operating rights inside the estate.

Four distinct planes

LPB: debtor and estate only. Owners’ Community: common-property governance. CEXP: operating/exploitation layer. Other owners and entities: independently evidenced rights and losses.

  • No later title decides every earlier authority question.
  • No judicial act automatically establishes private-party intent or liability.
  • No communication or institutional receipt proves the truth of its contents.
  • No allegation is a finding: every actor requires evidence of capacity, act, knowledge, causation and benefit.

Costa Papagayo

A defined documentary bridge—not a conclusion.

ETJ 370/2013 identifies a procedural route involving a Costa Papagayo community entity, a Sun Park Community perimeter and LPB. That does not itself determine debtor substitution, substantive liability, valid authority, disclosure or an agreement.

The verification sequence is finite: claimed capacity → authority source → contact or filing → document/information → decision or use → disclosure → in-concurso and extra-concurso effect → benefit/loss → contrary explanation. The full court file, accounting and communications record are still required.
