RECOVERED REPORTS

What Jonathan concluded — and what the reports cannot prove alone

Signed report · 22-Apr-2016

It analyses 2008–2011 using the records supplied and attributes most common-area maintenance and supplies to the Exploitation Community. Its table includes annual figures of €454,096.38 (2008), €678,265.40 (2009), €741,685.02 (2010) and €635,301.45 (2011).

V3 · 31-May / 6-Jun-2016

It expands the analysis to 2008–2015 and states a total of €1,918,532.40 classified as integral-maintenance expenditure borne on the exploitation side. Text totals include €414,697.10 maintenance, €860,036.14 water/electricity, €70,487.20 rubbish/taxes and €573,311.96 maintenance-linked personnel.

PINK economic report · 2012–Apr-2014

A separate Jonathan report attributes approximately €634,632.61 of analysed expenses to PINK, on invoices, Modelo 190 and trial balances. It also mentions assumed staff seniority liabilities said to exceed €900,000. It is a professional report based on company records, not an independent audit.

Evidential limit.

These documents establish what analysis Jonathan performed and what source material he said he used. They do not alone decide the legal validity of quotas, the absence of every owner obligation, the validity of a recognised insolvency claim or the commission of any offence. Those questions require the underlying records, contracts, ACTAs, IA treatment, judicial rulings and the classification hearing.

PWC AND THE MEETING

A report reviewed before the participants entered the room

The email sequence matters because it prevents the meeting being described as an improvised conversation. Jonathan sent the modified version and source material; PwC reviewed it, anticipated questions about evidential support and detail, and coordinated attendance. That establishes knowledge of this specific workstream by the recipients. It does not, by itself, establish a PwC institutional conclusion on fraud, criminality or legal debt validity.

Gil's account that Cristo activated the recorder and later delivered the recording to Gil/Patricia remains labelled as a direct first-hand witness account. The 2021 digital-custody chain separately proves later possession/transcription of two AMR files; it does not substitute for proof of the physical act of recording in 2016.
