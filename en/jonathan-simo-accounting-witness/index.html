WHY IT MATTERS

One documentary line runs across more than a decade

Jonathan Simó's importance here is evidential rather than biographical. The sources allow a sequence to be followed from LPB accounting work to the classification proceedings.

18 JUL 2012
Annual-account supervision by the IA

Patricia/Gil forward to Jonathan at jsimo@clsconsultoria.com a message from Rafael Franco: annual accounts to be formulated were to be supervised by the insolvency administration and the tax/accounting adviser could contact him directly.

6 JUN 2016
Jonathan sends the analysis and its sources

Jonathan sends a modified document “for your review” and part of the information used to prepare it. The attachment inventory includes an expense detail for 2008–2015, accounting ledgers for 2008–2011 and other supporting statements.

6 JUN 2016
PwC reviews the version

Miguel Hernández Lorenzo replies to Jonathan: “Nos parece bien. Tiremos con esta versión.” He adds that the Community meeting would probably raise questions about the support and detail for the expenses.

10 JUN 2016
Meeting arranged

Contemporaneous emails schedule the meeting for Friday the 10th at 12:00 and place it at Juan Carlos Prieto's office, Calle Emilio Castelar 4, fifth floor, Las Palmas.

12 JUN 2016
Immediate account of the meeting

An email to Carlos Saavedra and Miguel Hernández expressly refers to what “you, Jonathan and Cristo” had reported and proposes taking the debt issue and Jonathan's economic report to the insolvency administrator.

25 JUL 2023
Classification hearing: source still incomplete

The witness-preparation file identifies Jonathan as Witness 5. Until the full hearing is recovered, this page does not attribute the anticipated answers in that file as actual court testimony.

Date control. The Happy Scribe source filenames contain 10JUN2016, and contemporaneous emails schedule the meeting for Friday 10 June. Later transcript derivatives were labelled 11JUN2016. Por Derecho keeps that discrepancy open pending reconciliation with metadata and the original audio.

THE JUNE 2016 EVIDENCE

This was not simply a conversation: there was an analysis and supporting material

The analysis

The email chain identifies “Análisis Gastos de la Cdad Explotación”, including v3/v4 versions. Native full-text extraction of the DOCX is still outstanding in the current pass, so this page does not yet publish line-by-line conclusions from the report.

The supporting records

The inventory includes Detalle de gastos Cdad. Explotación 2008–2015, ledgers for 2008–2011 and P&G/CESP statements. A priority task is to reconcile which entries support each substantive conclusion.

PwC review

PwC is not included here by association. There is direct email evidence of review of the version and preparation for the meeting. That proves knowledge of this specific workstream; by itself it does not prove a PwC institutional conclusion on fraud, criminality or the legal validity of the alleged debt.
