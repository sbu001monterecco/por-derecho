From suspicion to a lawful protective decision

A criminal investigation asks whether particular acts constitute an offence and whether evidence or recoverable value must be secured. It is not merely a civil claim for payment. Criminal confiscation, criminal-case civil liability, insolvency estate recovery, tax recovery and grant recovery can intersect without becoming the same remedy.

Código Penal · arts. 31 bis, 33.7(g), 109–129, 408 · LECrim · arts. 105, 262, 282, 456, 544 quater, 588 octies, 589, 764, 803 ter

The operational question is: which defined record or asset, held by whom, can which authority preserve or investigate, under which threshold, with which safeguards, and what lawful decision or response follows? Powers are not all mandatory duties; a duty to assess or refer is not a guarantee that every requested measure will be granted.

Ley 50/1981 · arts. 3–5, 19 · Ley 22/2014 · arts. 85–86, 102 · Ley 39/2015 · arts. 4, 16.8, 21, 53, 62

Six requests, six legally distinct decisions

  1. Link, route and reconcile

    Associate the new material with the existing record; identify the competent functional team and a secure channel for source files. Keep supervisory reporting, investor complaints and SAIP distinct.

    Ley 22/2014 · arts. 85–86, 102

  2. Preserve and obtain defined records

    Preserve relevant CNMV-held versions and correspondence. Assess targeted requirements for investment approvals, title/conditions, source-and-use, conflicts, Series F/G and subsequent transfers under the applicable information/inspection powers.

    Ley 22/2014 · arts. 85–86, 102 · Ley 35/2003 · arts. 70–75

  3. Assess proportionate interim protection

    Compare available supervisory requirements, intervention or replacement under art.102/arts.72–75, and judicial protection requested under art.86.1(d). Identify the entity, evidence, risk, legal threshold and less disruptive effective alternatives.

    Ley 22/2014 · arts. 85–86, 102 · Ley 35/2003 · arts. 70–75

  4. Refer and cooperate where the legal trigger is met

    Place concrete criminal indications and a specified digital-preservation schedule before the competent prosecution/judicial-police authority. LECrim 588 octies is their power, not CNMV’s. EPPO requires a qualifying EU-financial-interest nexus.

    LECrim · arts. 105, 262, 282, 456, 544 quater, 588 octies, 589, 764, 803 ter · Ley 50/1981 · arts. 3–5, 19 · Ley 6/2023 · arts. 233, 243–244 · Regulation (EU) 2017/1939 · arts. 22–25, 31–32, 120

  5. Protect innocent stakeholders and operating value

    Design any justified measure around employees, guests, lawful suppliers, creditors and good-faith investors. Test liquidity, contracts, grant advances, costs and review rights; do not transfer business control to the complainant.

    Código Penal · arts. 31 bis, 33.7(g), 109–129, 408 · Real Decreto 948/2015 · Ley 35/2003 · arts. 70–75 · Ley 38/2003 · arts. 14, 30, 32, 34.4, 35, 37, 44–49

  6. Apply lawful publicity and provide the available response

    If intervention/replacement is adopted, apply BOE publication and Registro Mercantil registration under art.73.3. Assess protective disclosure within art.243’s actual scope, and communicate the procedural information legally available without exposing reserved investigations.

    Ley 35/2003 · arts. 70–75 · Ley 6/2023 · arts. 233, 243–244 · Ley 19/2013 · arts. 12, 14–16, 20, 24

This asks CNMV to assess action ex officio on supported information; it does not assert that Gil satisfies the specific shareholder/entity petition conditions of art.73.1. Any preferred rapid response interval is an operational request, not an invented statutory deadline.

The evidence chain is unitary; coercive powers and liabilities are not. Each authority must act within its own competence, thresholds and safeguards. A confidentiality limit is not proof of wrongdoing, but neither prevents every lawfully authorised inter-agency exchange.

The toolkit: who may do what, and on what conditions

01 · criminal

Targeted judicial intervention

The criminal court can intervene in a qualifying legal person or defined unit to protect workers or creditors: CP 31 bis/33.7(g), LECrim 544 quater.

Threshold / limit: Requires entity/offence attribution, a party application, hearing and proportionality. No automatic or generic ex parte hotel takeover.

Código Penal · arts. 31 bis, 33.7(g), 109–129, 408 · LECrim · arts. 105, 262, 282, 456, 544 quater, 588 octies, 589, 764, 803 ter

02 · criminal

Early restraint for confiscation

A competent court can seize or attach identified property/value from the initial investigative steps under CP 127 octies.

Threshold / limit: Show the recoverable-property hypothesis and preservation need; restraint is not guilt or final confiscation.

Código Penal · arts. 31 bis, 33.7(g), 109–129, 408 · LECrim · arts. 105, 262, 282, 456, 544 quater, 588 octies, 589, 764, 803 ter

03 · criminal

Security for financial liability

LECrim 589 and 764 provide security/attachment routes for properly identified pecuniary liabilities in the criminal case.

Threshold / limit: Civil liability within criminal proceedings is distinct from confiscation and from a separate civil action; avoid double recovery.

LECrim · arts. 105, 262, 282, 456, 544 quater, 588 octies, 589, 764, 803 ter · Código Penal · arts. 31 bis, 33.7(g), 109–129, 408

04 · criminal

ORGA tracing and management

ORGA can locate value and, when lawfully entrusted, conserve, administer and realise seized assets and income.

Threshold / limit: It assists justice; it does not independently decide guilt or permit a ministry to take over a hotel group.

Real Decreto 948/2015

05 · supervision

CNMV intervention or replacement

Ley 22/2014 art.102 imports Ley 35/2003 arts.72–75 for covered entities. Assess exceptional gravity or statutory accounting uncertainty.

Threshold / limit: Registration alone does not meet the threshold. Address hearing, the narrow urgency exception, BOE/registry publication and the broad approval effects of art.74.

Ley 22/2014 · arts. 85–86, 102 · Ley 35/2003 · arts. 70–75

06 · public-funds

Tax-enforcement business intervener

LGT 170.5 permits an official administrator/intervener in a qualifying business attachment where existing management threatens solvency.

Threshold / limit: Requires the particular tax-enforcement conditions and hearing; not a general monitor on allegation alone.

Ley 58/2003 · arts. 81, 93–95, 114, 170.5, 250 y ss.

07 · public-funds

Grant verification and recovery

The competent awarding/control body can verify expenditure, employment and other award conditions and pursue recovery on established legal grounds.

Threshold / limit: Award, payment, compliance, EU contribution and fraud are separate questions. Tax relief and public credit have distinct regimes.

Ley 38/2003 · arts. 14, 30, 32, 34.4, 35, 37, 44–49 · Regulation (EU) 2021/1060 · programme-specific scope

08 · public-funds

Retention of unpaid grants

LGS 35 provides reasoned retention of unpaid amounts within its recovery-procedure and preservation conditions.

Threshold / limit: Apply proportionality, duration and security alternatives. LGS 34.4 may affect advances after judicial intervention: test the liquidity impact.

Ley 38/2003 · arts. 14, 30, 32, 34.4, 35, 37, 44–49

09 · evidence

SEPBLAC financial intelligence

SEPBLAC analyses qualifying reports and shares intelligence through authorised channels; the FTF identifies account relationships.

Threshold / limit: Intelligence is not automatically trial evidence; FTF is not a complete bank-statement database and SAIP does not disclose confidential reports.

Ley 10/2010 · arts. 17–25, 43, 46–47

10 · evidence

Ownership and control reconciliation

Authorised access can reconcile beneficial ownership, shares, title, charges, accounts and management in fact.

Threshold / limit: Current registry data cannot be back-projected; branding, Catastro and civil title are not interchangeable.

Ministerio de Justicia · Registro Central de Titularidades Reales · LECrim · arts. 105, 262, 282, 456, 544 quater, 588 octies, 589, 764, 803 ter

11 · criminal

Ordinary proceeds confiscation

CP 127 governs legally qualifying effects, instruments and gains, including transformed proceeds.

Threshold / limit: Identify the offence/property connection. A later company name neither proves illegality nor ends the tracing enquiry.

Código Penal · arts. 31 bis, 33.7(g), 109–129, 408

12 · criminal

Equivalent-value confiscation

CP 127.3 and 127 septies may reach substitute assets up to the legally determined equivalent where statutory recovery conditions apply.

Threshold / limit: Identify the correct liable patrimony; no collective seizure of all associates or multiplication of recovery.

Código Penal · arts. 31 bis, 33.7(g), 109–129, 408

13 · criminal

Extended confiscation

CP 127 bis and 127 quinquies–sexies provide specific extended-confiscation regimes and evidential rules.

Threshold / limit: Check offence, factual indicators, presumptions and rebuttal. Not a general unexplained-wealth power over an entire family.

Código Penal · arts. 31 bis, 33.7(g), 109–129, 408

14 · criminal

Third-party confiscation

CP 127 quater addresses qualifying transfers to third parties with the relevant knowledge/suspicion conditions or statutory presumptions.

Threshold / limit: Protect hearing and good-faith rights; a lender, employee, supplier or investor is not automatically a knowing recipient.

Código Penal · arts. 31 bis, 33.7(g), 109–129, 408 · LECrim · arts. 105, 262, 282, 456, 544 quater, 588 octies, 589, 764, 803 ter

15 · criminal

Confiscation without conviction in limited cases

CP 127 ter and the LECrim 803 ter procedure apply only in their specified circumstances and with required judicial findings.

Threshold / limit: Not an automatic substitute for an archived complaint, insufficient evidence or inconvenient ordinary prosecution.

Código Penal · arts. 31 bis, 33.7(g), 109–129, 408 · LECrim · arts. 105, 262, 282, 456, 544 quater, 588 octies, 589, 764, 803 ter

16 · cross-border

Spain–Germany evidence order

A competent authority can issue/validate a necessary and proportionate European Investigation Order for specified evidence.

Threshold / limit: Not private discovery, not itself a joint investigation team and not the route for new UK requests.

Ley 23/2014 · título X

17 · cross-border

Cross-border freezing and confiscation

Regulation 2018/1805 recognises qualifying criminal orders between participating states, with the required certificate and safeguards.

Threshold / limit: Issuing, transmitting, recognising and executing are different states; do not describe cooperation as a completed freeze.

Regulation (EU) 2018/1805

18 · cross-border

EPPO investigation

EPPO investigates/prosecutes qualifying offences affecting EU financial interests within its material, temporal and territorial/personal scope.

Threshold / limit: EU State-aid approval, Spanish RIC tax relief and national SEPI/WSF credit are not automatically EU-budget expenditure.

Regulation (EU) 2017/1939 · arts. 22–25, 31–32, 120

19 · public-funds

OLAF and programme audit

OLAF and competent fund bodies can investigate administratively, reconcile expenditure and recommend recovery or criminal referral.

Threshold / limit: OLAF does not convict, confiscate or run hotels. Use the regulation applicable to the actual programme period.

OLAF · reporting and competence · Regulation (EU) 2021/1060 · programme-specific scope · Ley 38/2003 · arts. 14, 30, 32, 34.4, 35, 37, 44–49

20 · cross-border

UK management receivership comparator

In England and Wales a Crown Court management receiver under POCA ss.48–49 can preserve a restrained business's value.

Threshold / limit: This is the researched functional analogy, not a generic special-administration regime for hotels or an automatically available Spanish remedy.

CPS · Proceeds of Crime · POCA ss. 48–49

21 · cross-border

Spain–UK mutual legal assistance

Use the appropriate post-Brexit MLA/TCA route for identified UK evidence, property and qualifying orders.

Threshold / limit: Verify England/Wales, Northern Ireland or Scotland allocation. Aweswell's incorporation alone does not establish jurisdiction over every Spanish act.

UK Central Authority · mutual legal assistance

22 · cross-border

German investigation and asset arrest

Competent German prosecutors/courts investigate relevant facts and may secure value under the StPO/StGB conditions.

Threshold / limit: Separate each debtor/estate and actual German offence nexus; group insolvency is not a Sun Park causal finding.

StPO §160 · investigation of inculpatory and exculpatory facts · StPO §111e · Vermögensarrest · StGB §73 · Einziehung

23 · cross-border

Operational asset-tracing support

Europol Project A.S.S.E.T. and designated ARO/CARIN contacts support competent-authority tracing and cooperation.

Threshold / limit: These are not citizen-commanded seizure services and do not replace the domestic legal order.

Europol · Project A.S.S.E.T. · Real Decreto 948/2015

24 · criminal

Restitution and recovery accounting

Reconcile return of property, compensation, confiscation, tax/grant recovery and estate rights with their separate legal bases.

Threshold / limit: Confiscation does not automatically transfer assets to Gil or Aweswell. Identify rightful claimant, priority and amounts already recovered.

Código Penal · arts. 31 bis, 33.7(g), 109–129, 408 · Ley 4/2015 · arts. 2, 5, 7, 11, 12, 18 · Regulation (EU) 2018/1805

25 · rights

Lawful information and response

A correctly classified application can seek the receipt, referral information, decision or access available under its actual procedure.

Threshold / limit: No universal deadline, party status, right to secret material or right to a favourable outcome arises from merely reporting.

Ley 19/2013 · arts. 12, 14–16, 20, 24 · Ley 12/2014 de Canarias · arts. 46, 53 · LO 4/2001 · arts. 1–3, 6–12 · Ley 39/2015 · arts. 4, 16.8, 21, 53, 62 · Ley 2/2023 · arts. 2–3, 9, 17–20, 24, 35–36; DA 6

26 · rights

Review of alleged unlawful omission

Establish notice/knowledge, competent duty, required act, timing and the mental element under the chosen administrative, disciplinary or criminal route.

Threshold / limit: CP 408 requires intentional breach of the duty to promote prosecution. A lawful archive, secrecy or disagreement is not automatically criminal omission.

Código Penal · arts. 31 bis, 33.7(g), 109–129, 408 · Ley 50/1981 · arts. 3–5, 19 · Ley 39/2015 · arts. 4, 16.8, 21, 53, 62

27 · cross-border

Joint investigation team

Competent authorities can agree purpose, scope, participants, duration and evidence handling, with Eurojust support where appropriate.

Threshold / limit: A citizen's unitary theory does not create a team or merge every criminal docket and insolvency estate.

Eurojust · joint investigation teams · UK Central Authority · mutual legal assistance

28 · supervision

Inter-agency preservation protocol

Public bodies can define lawful custody, assistance, referrals and named responsibilities within their existing powers.

Threshold / limit: A Canary/State coordination group acquires no free-standing power to monitor a private company without a separate legal gateway.

Ley 40/2015 · arts. 140–144 · Ley 50/1981 · arts. 3–5, 19

29 · evidence

Urgent specified digital preservation

LECrim 588 octies empowers Fiscalía or Judicial Police to require protection of specified stored data pending judicial disclosure authorisation: up to 90 days, one extension within 180.

Threshold / limit: CNMV cannot issue this criminal order. Preservation is not disclosure, interception or indiscriminate access; a citizen email does not start the statutory period.

LECrim · arts. 105, 262, 282, 456, 544 quater, 588 octies, 589, 764, 803 ter

30 · evidence

Forensic accounting and valuation

LECrim 456 ff. supports expert examination of transactions, values, losses, mixed funds and alternative explanations.

Threshold / limit: Specify source data, method, conflicts, assumptions and challenge rights. An expert opinion is not a judicial finding.

LECrim · arts. 105, 262, 282, 456, 544 quater, 588 octies, 589, 764, 803 ter

31 · criminal

Structures without legal personality

CP 129 supplies limited ancillary consequences and interim intervention routes for qualifying non-personified entities and offences.

Threshold / limit: First establish legal nature and statutory offence coverage. Do not automatically apply to every owners' community or exploitation arrangement.

Código Penal · arts. 31 bis, 33.7(g), 109–129, 408

32 · supervision

CNMV records and inspection

Ley 22/2014 arts.85–86 and incorporated Ley 35/2003 arts.70–70 bis provide the supervisory information/inspection framework.

Threshold / limit: Identify the regulated subject, precise records, lawful purpose and limits. Preserve relevant CNMV-held records separately from requests to others.

Ley 22/2014 · arts. 85–86, 102 · Ley 35/2003 · arts. 70–75

33 · supervision

CNMV request for judicial asset protection

Ley 22/2014 art.86.1(d) expressly includes recourse to competent courts to request attachment or freezing of assets.

Threshold / limit: Assess the competent court, procedural gateway, property and threshold. This is not direct unlimited confiscation by CNMV.

Ley 22/2014 · arts. 85–86, 102

34 · supervision

Statutory publication of intervention

An intervention/replacement decision under the imported regime is immediately enforceable and must be published in BOE and registered in Registro Mercantil: art.73.3.

Threshold / limit: The decision has not been identified here as granted. Publication governs third-party effects; art.74 has broad approval consequences, not merely a soft monitor.

Ley 22/2014 · arts. 85–86, 102 · Ley 35/2003 · arts. 70–75 · Ley 6/2023 · arts. 233, 243–244

35 · supervision

Protective disclosure and supervisory secrecy

Assess legally permitted non-confidential notices and, where its issuer/market/materiality scope applies, disclosure under Ley 6/2023 art.243. Retain art.233 secrecy and lawful exchange conditions.

Threshold / limit: SCR registration alone does not establish a listed issuer or require publication of a confidential investigation. Distinguish public decisions from protected investigative methods.

Ley 6/2023 · arts. 233, 243–244
