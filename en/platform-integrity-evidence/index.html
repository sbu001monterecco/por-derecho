Preserve even if not disclosed

If third-party privacy prevents voluntary disclosure of a reporter's identity, the identifying layer should still be preserved so that a competent regulator, court, prosecutor or law-enforcement authority can seek it through lawful process.

Do not collapse systems

Security events, moderation decisions, user reports, profile edits and automated anti-abuse signals should remain separately attributable until native records show a relationship. A single public timeline is an investigative index, not proof of a single mechanism.

LinkedIn's own published routes

LinkedIn publishes an identity-verification process for restricted or inaccessible accounts and states that it uses Persona for certain recovery flows. LinkedIn's current compromised-account guidance also directs members who cannot access the account and noticed changes or suspect another person had access to submit its Report Unauthorized Account Access or Changes form as soon as possible. A separate current LinkedIn Ireland imprint identifies an alternative written contact route; that written escalation was sent on 23 September 2026 and verified in Sent mail. LinkedIn also says that a difference of opinion is not an appropriate reason to flag content and that it does not ordinarily share the identity of a person who flags an item.

EU / data-rights questions

Where applicable, Article 15 GDPR supports access to personal data about the account holder, including available information about the source where data were not collected from the data subject, subject to the rights of others. Under the EU Digital Services Act, relevant moderation restrictions may engage statement-of-reasons and internal-complaint rights, while Article 23 addresses repeated manifestly unfounded notices or complaints.
