What LinkedIn and other providers are asked to preserve.

  1. Profile-edit audit records for the 22 September 2026 photo change.

Reports and moderation

  1. Every report, complaint, notice or flag concerning the account or relevant posts, with timestamp, target, category and disposition.
  2. Stable pseudonymous source labels if reporter identity cannot lawfully be disclosed.
  3. Whether repeated reports came from the same or linked accounts and whether LinkedIn identified abuse or coordinated reporting.
  4. Human/automated review status, policy classification, appeals, reversals and statements of reasons.

Preserve even if not disclosed

If third-party privacy prevents voluntary disclosure of a reporter's identity, the identifying layer should still be preserved so that a competent regulator, court, prosecutor or law-enforcement authority can seek it through lawful process.

Do not collapse systems

Security events, moderation decisions, user reports, profile edits and automated anti-abuse signals should remain separately attributable until native records show a relationship. A single public timeline is an investigative index, not proof of a single mechanism.
