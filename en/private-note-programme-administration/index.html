SRLN-2026 · PRIVATE NOTE ADMINISTRATION

Administration infrastructure, not a public checkout.

AWESWELL LIMITED is designing the Sun Rock Private Note Programme around a controlled digital administration layer and independent professional agency roles. The objective is to make issuance, ownership, payments, security, notices, transfers and redemptions traceable from closing through maturity—without confusing software with the legal trustee, registrar, custodian or paying institution.

Role architecture

One digital control plane; legally distinct formal roles.

The platform is intended to make the transaction easier to administer, not to collapse legal capacities that need independence, permissions or fiduciary authority.

Issuer-side

Programme Administrator

The Sun Rock Note Administration Platform can run closing conditions, reference IDs, document/version control, shadow calculations, NAV tracking, security-status tracking, issuer accounting sub-ledger, notices repository and reconciled reporting.

Ownership

Registrar / Transfer Agent

The controlling holder register records issue, ownership, transfer, cancellation and redemption. The platform mirrors that record and flags discrepancies; it does not silently overwrite the legally controlling source.

Cash

Issuing & Paying / Settlement Agent

Subscription money, coupon payments and principal should move through formally appointed external settlement, escrow, account-bank and paying-agent infrastructure. The public website does not receive investor money.

Security

Note Trustee / Security Trustee

Where a trust/security structure is adopted, the independent trustee represents holders and/or holds security under the definitive documents. The platform cannot grant itself trustee discretion, enforcement powers or custody by description.

Calculations

Calculation Agent

The formal agent calculates contractual coupon, PIK, redemption and other amounts. The platform independently reproduces a shadow calculation for exception control and reconciliation.

Information

Information / Reporting Agent

Formal notices and holder communications sit with the party named in the documents. “Information Trustee” is not used as a vague catch-all: fiduciary representation belongs to the Note Trustee; communications and reporting belong to the Information Agent.

Lifecycle

Every Note should have a provable chain from consideration to redemption.

Programme capacity is not debt. A Note becomes outstanding only after the relevant allocation closes and the controlling register is updated.

1 · Eligibility & KYC

Permitted private recipient, authority, KYC/AML and sanctions controls.

2 · Definitive documents

Information Memorandum, applicable Series terms and subscription or creditor-settlement agreement.

3 · Consideration

Cleared cash at the external settlement layer, or a validly documented creditor settlement/novation.

4 · Closing certificate

Conditions precedent are certified; no certificate means no issue.

5 · Register entry

Holder, allocation and individual Note references are entered in the controlling register.

6 · Servicing

Calculations, payments, notices, covenant/NAV reporting and security status are reconciled through the lifecycle.

7 · Transfer

Only permitted transferees, with required eligibility/KYC and registration steps.

8 · Redemption

Amount calculated, funds paid, register cancelled, accounting derecognised and security released only under the governing documents.

Golden-source controls

The system is designed around reconciliation, not a single editable dashboard.

For every material data class there is a legally or operationally controlling source, with the platform serving as the synchronized administration layer.

Ownership

Golden source: Registrar / issuer register.
Platform: synchronized holder and Note mirror.

Cash

Golden source: Paying/Settlement Agent and bank records.
Platform: payment status and reconciliation.

Security

Golden source: Trustee/security documents and perfection evidence.
Platform: security-status register and alerts.

Accounting

Golden source: AWESWELL general ledger.
Platform: Note-by-Note accounting sub-ledger.

Recovery NAV

Golden source: approved valuation version.
Platform: coverage monitoring and supersession history.

Audit history

Issue, transfer, payment, security and redemption events are preserved by reference and timestamp; corrections supersede rather than erase history.

Three-way closing rule. A Note allocation should not be shown as issued/closed until the platform record, Registrar/Agent record and bank/accounting record reconcile, or a permitted exception is formally documented by the Closing Officer.

Issuance readiness

Architecture developed; first issue remains gated.

The programme remains in PRE-ISSUANCE. Before any Note can become outstanding, the definitive legal/corporate authority, independent recovery-value review, tax/accounting treatment, security/perfection, settlement-account and controlling-register conditions must be completed.

No outstanding Notes. Programme capacity is not borrowing and is not represented as issued debt. External trustee/agent roles are appointed only when signed mandates exist.
