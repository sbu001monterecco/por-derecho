Institutional knowledge before 7 June

23 Jan 2018 · AC. Rosa Gual recorded immediately after speaking with the insolvency administrator that she had explained the investor calls and that he said that, if all recognised claims were consigned, he would not oppose payment or conclusion. He declined to meet the investors because financing was AWESWELL’s sphere and he considered his own role to be LPB’s liquidation.
1 Feb 2018 · Court. AWESWELL’s filed appearance, paired to a LexNET receipt, expressly told the insolvency court that several investors wished to provide the funds required for LPB to overcome insolvency, seek conclusion under Article 176 and continue activity outside the concurso. This is formal court notice before 7 June.
13 Jun 2018 · direct communication. Daniel Irigoyen’s contemporaneous account adds personal presentation of the funded exit to the judge and AC after the 7 June material-control event. It is not retrodated.

Controlled conclusion: the evidence supports real readiness and institutional knowledge before 7 June, followed by direct personal communication on 13 June. It does not turn that evidence into a claim of complete consignation or a final pre-7-June Article 176 filing.

AC convenio evaluation report: still to be recovered

It is the insolvency administrator’s written evaluation of LPB’s convenio proposal under the then-applicable Insolvency Act, assessing the proposal against the payment plan and, where relevant, the viability plan. It was not the creditors’ vote and not a judicial ruling. The 26-Jun-2017 court order directed that the evaluation be made available in the court office. Juan Tomás Parrilla wrote that same day that the evaluation was negative and summarised it as the AC considering there was insufficient proof that the viability plan would succeed. The report text itself has not been recovered, so its precise reasoning is not reconstructed from that summary.

Recovery target: court/LexNET and counsel files around “evaluación de la propuesta de convenio”, 26-Jun-2017 and registry 3671/2017, without treating that registry number as confirmed for the report until the actual item is recovered. The official 28-Jun-2017 creditors-meeting minute must also be recovered to reconcile lack of quorum with the later “no proposal accepted” wording.
