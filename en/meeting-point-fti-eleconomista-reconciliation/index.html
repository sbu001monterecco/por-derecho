PUBLIC OFFERING · COMPLIANCE REPORT · EDITORIAL DECISION

Three records address the same question. None can replace the other two.

The public offering of Club Sei Lanzarote, FTI report 2024-94 and the January 2025 elEconomista exchange do not by themselves determine who contracted, authorised, marketed or received revenue in connection with Sun Park. Together, they fix a documentary route capable of confirming or excluding those questions.

Reading rule: an offering is not a contract; a compliance report is not responsibility; and a decision not to publish does not turn a judgment with a limited object into an answer to separate commercial questions.

Three sources, three limits

The comparison starts by separating what each record can establish.

Public source

Club Sei / Meeting Point

Corporate and commercial material places Club Sei Lanzarote in the Meeting Point/FTI ecosystem and shows a public offer associated with Playa Blanca.

It does not resolve:

The contracting entity, specific inventory, completed bookings, revenue, title or authority.

Private correspondence

FTI 2024-94 and Rödl

The located emails record FTI acknowledgement, a compliance reference, a planned interview and later requests for property and possession-recovery material.

It does not resolve:

The truth of allegations, an investigative outcome, an admission or liability of FTI, Meeting Point, Lars Creutzmann or the legal contacts.

Editorial correspondence

elEconomista / Javier Romera

The January 2025 exchange shows a request for documentary verification and that an LPB classification judgment was received as a reason not to publish.

It does not resolve:

The identity or intent of the material sender, bad faith by Javier Romera or elEconomista, or Meeting Point/FTI commercialisation or authority.

Controlled conclusion: the thematic overlap supports a finite request for records. It does not allow an allegation to migrate from one proceeding to another or personal knowledge to be attributed through professional or institutional proximity.

The strongest proposition the current record allows

The question is not a slogan about two insolvencies. It is a verifiable chain.

The chain to test is: announced product, authority to offer it, contracting entity, delivered inventory, processed bookings, economic flows, compliance notice and subsequent custody. Each relevant actor can confirm, qualify or exclude defined segments with primary records.

1

Entity and contract

Identify the Meeting Point/FTI entity, contract, dates, signatories and territorial scope.

2

Inventory and channels

Provide allotments, PMS, channel manager, OTAs and traceability of offered units.

3

Bookings and money

Separate a mere offer from bookings, collections, commissions, invoices, refunds and payments.

4

Authority and possession

Reconcile title and authorisations with the material requested in the property/possession review.

5

2024-94

Produce the index, scope, preservation, outcome and any handover into the Spanish proceeding.

6

357/2024

Produce the signed originals, allocation trail, annexes and expert file capable of excluding or confirming custody.
