What changes in the unitary reading

2022: contemplated, not proved executed

The authorisation request contemplated loans, bonds and a possible RIC debt issue, including term, nominal value, coupon and Article 27.4.D.4 use. This corpus does not establish issuance, subscription, settlement or allocation of a 2022 RIC issue.

Why the Government raises funds

The 2024 AFI contracting memorandum explains that the investor's financial-tax return can permit a lower coupon and ease the Canary Community's financing burden while financing eligible public investment.

Acosta Matos: no named bridge here

The OCR search found no Acosta, HNT, MYND or RICPE occurrence in this tranche. That does not prove absence from other records. The direct documented link remains RICPE→HNT, not a proved allocation of these bonds.
