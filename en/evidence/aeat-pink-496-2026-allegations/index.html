Alegaciones — Pink Canary Services / AEAT

Procedimiento Ordinario 496/2026 · Audiencia Nacional · Sala de lo Contencioso-Administrativo · Sección 7ª.

DRAFT STATUS

Source and status control

This page is a source-controlled public rendering of the legal-team document titled “002_Ampliacion_Resolucion_Expresa.docx”, received in the 28 August 2026 Sixto Abogados correspondence concerning “Separación AC - AEAT v Pink: Próximos pasos”. The draft itself is dated 1 September 2026.

As at 28 August 2026, this page does not represent that the 1 September draft has been filed. Filing status must be updated from the court or filing receipt. The underlying contentious-administrative proceeding is separately identified in the draft as PO 496/2026.

What the Alegaciones say

Proposed enlargement of the pending judicial appeal

  1. The draft states that Pink Canary Services, S.L.U. brought a contentious-administrative appeal on 1 September 2025 against the presumed rejection of its request for a declaration of nullity.
  2. It records that, once the dispute was already before the court, AEAT's Financial and Tax Inspection Department issued an express resolution on 29 September 2025 declining to admit the nullity request on the basis stated by the authority.
  3. The draft says Pink obtained the complete express resolution when it was served with the State Legal Service's opposition in the separate interim-measures piece.
  4. Pink's procedural position is that the later express resolution does not satisfy or change the claim already before the court, but formalises the same negative administrative outcome.
  5. The draft invokes article 36.4 of Law 29/1998 to ask that the pending appeal be enlarged so that the later express resolution is unequivocally incorporated into the judicial review.
  6. The relief requested is procedural enlargement of PO 496/2026 to encompass the AEAT resolution of 29 September 2025, together with procedural coordination of the enlarged challenge.

Classification

What this does — and does not — establish

Established from the working document: the legal team treats Pink/AEAT as an open contentious-administrative dispute and identifies PO 496/2026 before the Audiencia Nacional, Section 7.

Party/legal position: the proposition that the express AEAT resolution should be brought into the pending judicial challenge is Pink's legal position in the draft.

Not established by this page: that the draft has already been filed; that Pink will prevail; that AEAT's position is unlawful; or that any contested tax liability or administrative conclusion is final.
