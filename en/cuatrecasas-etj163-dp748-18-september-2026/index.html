This page publishes the result, the limits of what it decides, and the next protective steps that the client side has expressly authorised for public disclosure.

Boundary: no court has found Cuatrecasas, any judge, Acosta Matos-connected person or any prospective assignee guilty of an offence in these proceedings. The criminal allegations remain allegations. Public notice to any prospective remate recipient

No present source identifies an actual assignee. The client side nevertheless gives public notice of its legal position concerning any person considering receiving the remate or an economically equivalent position.

The proposition being preserved: a later judicially formalised transfer does not by itself rewrite the character of antecedent conduct if a competent criminal court later finds that conduct criminal. The third party's own legal position must be assessed separately by reference to title, consideration, knowledge, good or bad faith, Registry protection and restitution rules.
  • No third party is accused merely because it considers or receives an assignment.
  • The client side intends to identify and preserve the identity, instructions, consideration/payment, representation, beneficial-ownership and Registry/possession trail of any actual cession.
  • The client side will oppose any argument that the later appearance of a third party, by itself, retrospectively purges or validates antecedent conduct.
  • This web publication is not formal service and does not replace a burofax, court filing, Registry measure or other legally required notice.

Judicial accountability: what is being preserved

The client side disputes the 17 September reasoning. It is not publishing a present finding that the judge committed prevaricación merely by deciding adversely. It is instead creating a date-locked knowledge-and-decision record: what the judge had before her, the subsidiary relief requested, the 16 September criminal development, the 17 September ruling, any Article 215 response, and any later adjudication, cession, registration or possession act.

Accountability rule: if later facts meet the statutory thresholds for criminal, disciplinary or State-liability review, those questions belong to the competent institutions. The present purpose is preservation of knowledge, chronology, causation and personal authorship of later judicial acts — not a pre-judgment of guilt.
