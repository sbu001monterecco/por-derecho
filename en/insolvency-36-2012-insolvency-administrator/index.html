Insolvency Administrator: what was known, what could be done, and what was done?

This page does not allege corruption, collusion or unlawful intent. It isolates document-answerable acts and omissions so the Insolvency Administrator's role can be professionally reviewed at the moments of greatest patrimonial consequence in Insolvency 36/2012.

Three control points

26 June 2018

Mercantile Court No. 1 suspended the realization process for a defined set of properties because, if the appeal succeeded, the conditions of the proposed transfer in payment to Construcciones Acosta Matos S.A. would be substantially different. The remaining rulings stayed unchanged and liquidation could continue for other estate assets.

24 October 2019

The primary order already identified in the record documents an ob rem unlinking and sale carried out without prior judicial authorization and refuses later authorization or validation. The open question is what practical consequence that judicial control had for later acts.

2020–2022

The record should be reconstructed asset by asset: what information existed on title, LPB perimeter, Matkator/third-party assets, RIC, financing and exploitation before adjudication and transfer in payment, and what position the Administrator took in response.

Questions the record should answer

  1. What exactly was the Administrator's proposal referred to in the 26 June 2018 suspension order?
  2. What material or legal acts occurred during the suspension, and over which assets?
  3. After the 2019 non-validation order, what restitution, registry correction, estate-protection or court-notification measures were sought or adopted?
  4. What did the Administrator know about Matkator and other third-party rights before any representation of the complex as a single operating unit?
  5. What accounting bridge explains credit, interest, commercial premises, adjudication, cancellations, payments and any surplus?
  6. What documents were supplied to the Court and appellate bodies on each point?

Reasonable defence preserved

The Insolvency Administrator exercised statutory functions within a court-supervised process and could rely on the liquidation plan, judicial orders and information then available. Error, a disputed interpretation or even an act later found to lack prior authorization does not by itself prove corruption or concerted wrongdoing.

What this page does not establish

It does not establish crime, judicial wrongdoing, corruption, collusion, misappropriation or civil or criminal liability. Its narrower function is to fix a verifiable matrix of knowledge, power, act, judicial response and patrimonial consequence.
