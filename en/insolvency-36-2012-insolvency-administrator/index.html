Francisco Borja Rodríguez-Batllori Laffitte: the node between the insolvency estate and judicial supervision.

This page does not presume crime or convert every Administrator decision into judicial responsibility. It separates what the Insolvency Administration had to preserve/verify, what it reported to the Court, what it implemented, and what should be reconcilable in accounting and patrimonial terms.

Four questions before attributing responsibility

1 · What did he know?

Access/control, physical condition, community debts, debt quantification, 2018 operation, third parties, tender, title and operation.

2 · What had to be verified or preserved?

LPB property/rights, inventory, access, physical/economic integrity, documents and money under the applicable insolvency framework.

3 · What was reported to the Court?

For each risk, identify filing, quarterly report, annex, incorporation date, transfer to parties and judicial response.

4 · What was implemented or left open?

Operations, restitution after non-validation, tender, adjudication, deed, charge cancellation, later communication and final accounting.

CGPJ connection: if the Administrator reported, the judicial response should be identified; if he failed to report despite an applicable duty, the question is whether that omission was later detected and controlled when the risk reached the Court. That is the supervisory link, not automatic attribution.

Essential accounting bridge

Question: can one certified, arithmetically reproducible schedule connect recognised debt, interest/adjustments, €400,000/commercial units, tender, adjudication, cancellations, payments/set-offs, estate costs, fees and surplus or documented explanation of its absence?

  1. Principal, ordinary interest, default interest and any post-insolvency component separately.
  2. Commercial units / €400,000: origin, account, entry, application and treatment after non-validation.
  3. Value of assets transferred/adjudicated and debt extinguished.
  4. Administrator report of 25/01/2022 and calculations supporting the deed.
  5. Charges cancelled and enabling act.
  6. Final accounts: filing, transfer, approval/challenge and ruling.
  7. Surplus: calculation or documented explanation of non-existence.
  8. Matkator and third parties: keep out-of-estate property/rights separate from LPB.
Reasonable defence: the Administrator could rely on judicial rulings and disagree with LPB/Aweswell. A record missing from our corpus is not proof that it does not exist. The standard is production, date, incorporation and reconciliation.
