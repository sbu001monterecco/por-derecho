Supervision is not a substitute for historical responsibility

Article 82 TRLC is a supervision and information power, not a clean-slate procedure. It permits the insolvency judge to require specific information or a report from the Insolvency Administrator. Article 83 separately provides that orders resolving questions concerning exercise of the office are not appealable and that the matter resolved may not then be raised by concursal incident.

For that reason, Por Derecho does not treat a broad Article 82 request as an automatic next step. Any use should be narrowly lawyer-validated for production, preservation, accounting or continuing estate protection and checked against the removal/appellate, DP 1956, qualification and separate responsibility routes.

Non-erasure rule. Later production, correction, restitution or improved accounting may reduce continuing harm and may be relevant evidence. It does not retroactively determine whether an earlier act or omission complied with duty, and it does not by itself establish or extinguish criminal, civil or insolvency responsibility.
