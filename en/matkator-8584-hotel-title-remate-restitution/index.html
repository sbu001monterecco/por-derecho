The title is not the room: finca 8584, remate and restitution in a regulated hotel.

Sun Park / MYND Yaiza operates as a unified hotel establishment built across legally separate titles. Finca 8584 is not analysed as an autonomous apartment: it is the title from which Matkator asserts rights over space alleged to have been physically and economically integrated into the hotel.

Updated · 3 September 2026

Essential boundary. This page separates procedural and documentary facts, Matkator’s allegations, reasoned inferences and open questions. It does not state as fact that Cuatrecasas, Acosta Matos, Hotel New Trend, Canarian Hospitality, any professional or authority committed an offence; nor does it state that adjudication, cession, an intermediary or a common plan has already been proved.

CENTRAL DISTINCTION

One regulated hotel productive system; several legally distinct title compartments.

Not autonomous operation

The issue is not separate occupation of a dwelling. Access, reception, services, inventory, marketing, guests and revenue are organised as a hotel.

The title still matters

Physical transformation does not automatically extinguish the finca. Title remains the reference for space, authority, possession, revenue and restitution.

Separate estates

Matkator, LPB and Aweswell are distinct entities. Finca 8584 does not enter LPB’s estate merely because it forms part of the same hotel.

Do not atomise the result

Legal separation cannot erase that control, works, operation, finance, benefit and remedies concern one hotel system.

RIGHTS BUNDLE

What the title anchors.

Property and common elements

Private title and inseparable rights in relevant common elements, installations and services, subject to the title and applicable law.

Possession and access

Physical identification, access, protection against unauthorised alteration and possible recovery or delivery.

Governance

Information, participation, voting, challenge and consent rights where applicable within the property structure.

Hotel-operation authority

The owner-side legal basis relevant to inclusion of the space in a unified tourism operation and the operator’s enabling title.

Accounts, fruits and revenue

Accounts, compensation, rent, fruits or exploitation proceeds where supported by title, contracts and proved facts.

Evidence and standing

Plans, licences, inventory, PMS, inspection, operator, revenue and effective judicial protection.

Restoration and restitution

The legal reference for unwinding, repairing or compensating the alleged occupation, alteration, exclusion and exploitation.

Control. The existence and scope of each right must be proved through title, contract, legislation and facts; this page does not convert them automatically into recognised claims.

THREE PERIODS OF INJURY

1 · Accrued injury

Alleged occupation, alteration, loss of access, hotel use and earlier revenue.

2 · Continuing injury

Current possession, operation, inventory, bookings and revenue without the accounting or authority Matkator seeks.

3 · Adjudication injury

Loss of title, fragmented remedies, a new registered position and harder restitution before any later cession.

Critical correction: prejudice does not depend exclusively upon proving a cession to Acosta Matos. Adjudication itself may weaken the position from which Matkator seeks identification, access, accounts, restoration and restitution.

THREE RECIPIENT IDENTITIES

The formal name does not exhaust the beneficiary inquiry.

Formal adjudicatee

The person or entity receiving adjudication in the enforcement.

Formal assignee

Any person receiving the remate if cession is authorised and formalised.

Real or terminal beneficiary

The person retaining hotel possession, operation, revenue or the legal and economic advantage.

Specific hypothesis, not proved fact. Matkator maintains that the CAM / Hotel New Trend / Canarian Hospitality perimeter is the dominant economic beneficiary of consolidation because it controls the hotel operation into which the space is alleged to have been integrated. The formal assignee, any intermediation and any concert remain unproved.

An unrelated acquirer would not obtain an autonomous hotel business merely from the title. Its practical position would appear to depend on litigating to reverse the integration or reaching an arrangement with the hotel-control perimeter. A transparent agreement is not automatically criminal; criminal concern would arise if a nominal recipient concealed the real beneficiary, avoided accounts or restitution, or regularised the result through materially incomplete judicial disclosure.

CUATRECASAS

From protective mandate to enforcement: individual knowledge, not automatic attribution.

Cuatrecasas’s historical role in the Aweswell/LPB/Sun Park rescue makes it relevant to reconstruct what each team knew about mixed title, possession, access, valuation, finance, operator delivery and Acosta Matos. It does not automatically attribute all firm knowledge or criminal intent to each later professional.

The finite question is: what did each actor receive, when, what did they later state or omit when seeking adjudication, and what patrimonial effect could follow? Genuine fee claims and a potentially valid cambial obligation must remain in the record as adverse evidence; they do not by themselves answer the later use of enforcement.

CONCURSO 36/2012

Extraconcursal title, insolvency consequence.

Finca 8584 is not part of LPB’s estate. It nevertheless forms part of the same hotel productive system, and its transfer may affect integrity, valuation, claims, evidence, recovery and restitution linked to Concurso 36/2012, the ONA/funded-exit route, calificación and the appeals concerning the insolvency administrator.

Double rule: do not use economic unity to erase separate estates; do not use separate estates to erase the shared economic and remedial event.
