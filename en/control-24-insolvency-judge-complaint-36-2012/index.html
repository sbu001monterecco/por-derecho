The five documentary modules in Control 24

1 · Funded exit and judicial knowledge

The complaint identifies the ONA/Clubotel alternative, conditional financing, due diligence, valuation, operating agreement and June–September 2018 communications. The question is not whether the Court was bound to approve it, but what formally arrived, which payoff and security were required, which temporary protection followed and what useful decision responded.

2 · Credit, €13.1 million and threshold

The recognised credit must be separated from figures used for a third party to improve the proposal, interest liquidation, mortgage cap and credit eventually extinguished. Control 24 asks which act, if any, transformed an instrumental threshold into a figure with materially definitive effects.

3 · OB REM transaction and non-validation

The 28 November 2018 deed, the €400,000 price and the 24 October 2019 order lead to a finite question: what restitution, cancellation, regularisation, new title or accounting treatment implemented the non-validation before the assets reappeared in the later operation.

4 · Bidding, competition and adjudication

The requested inquiry was to reconstruct publicity, time, access, data room, deposit, Ottawa/ONA, economic comparability, asset condition, May 2021 decisions and 2022 title. The existence of an alternative interested party does not prove it was entitled to the award.

5 · Corporate identity

The complaint asks that CAM S.A., CAM S.L. and any other company or label be separated: who was creditor, enforcing party, bidder, adjudicatee, deed party and registered owner in each act. A typographical error or lawful succession may explain differences; deeds, powers, orders and registries must prove the answer.

Attribution method

Each module follows: existing document → incorporation and date → direct judicial knowledge → specific decision → effect → lawful alternative explanation → measure capable of confirming or excluding the hypothesis. An adverse ruling alone does not prove judicial misconduct.

What this page does not publish as proven fact

No finding of guilt

It does not state that the judge knowingly issued an unjust decision, acted because of an improper relationship, engaged in corruption or should be convicted.

No conversion of omission into intent

An omission requires a concrete duty, knowledge, ability to act, materiality, qualified injustice and causation. Legal disagreement, error or absence of a document from this corpus is insufficient.

No conflation of judge, court clerk and office

Judicial signature, procedural management, registration, notice, custody and remittal are attributed to the responsible person or unit in each act; they are not transferred automatically to the judge.

No import of another route's outcome

DIP 2/2026 and DI 169/2026 have their own results. They do not establish the destination or merits of Control 24, and Control 24 does not itself reopen those files.
