TSJC-directed notitia: nature and competence

The description faithful to the filing is written denuncia / notitia criminis / reporting of facts, expressly distinct from a formal querella and appearance as a prosecuting party. Describing it as a notitia does not mean it cannot also be a denuncia.

LOPJ article 73.3.b assigns the TSJ Civil and Criminal Chamber investigation and trial of alleged official-function crimes by judges within the autonomous community, unless reserved to the Supreme Court. LOPJ articles 406–409 provide initiation and transmission mechanisms; a formal querella is not the only statutory mechanism. This supports requesting the competence and transmission decision, without asserting TSJC admission or automatic invalidity of every preliminary step.

Same-day filing or earlier opening of the AC matter does not automatically make DP1956 the correct destination: connection and competence under LECrim articles 17–18 and the special judge-related competence require examination. Statutory check: 19 September 2026.
