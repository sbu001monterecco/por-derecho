Methodology and evidence levels

A connected reconstruction is not a collective conclusion. Every act, capacity, date and relationship retains its source and limits.

Evidence states

DOC — limited documentary support; HIP — attributed hypothesis; OPEN — unproved reliance; NOTICE — notice or receipt, not adoption; CONTRARY — contrary or lawful explanation; ADVERSE — preserved adverse outcome; GAP — outstanding source with a closure test.

What ^ means

On a label, ^ verifies canonical identity, not guilt, mandate, knowledge or outcome. As an audit instruction it additionally requires registration, provenance, relationships, chronology, publication, reciprocal links and continuity.

Verifiable closure

A located source, updated record, published page and established fact are different results. A gap is closed only at the level proved. Private originals are not published to simulate completeness.
