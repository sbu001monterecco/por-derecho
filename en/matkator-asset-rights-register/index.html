CONTROLLED REGISTER · MATKATOR · STEP 4 GATEWAY

Matkator asset & rights register: what is actually exposed?

This register turns the “Step 4” gateway from an abstract warning into a finite asset-by-asset map. It distinguishes the single current remate object from Matkator's wider debtor perimeter, and it refuses to convert LPB, Aweswell or group-level rights into Matkator property without proof.

Not a balance sheet. This page records only source-locked assets/right classes and explicit unknowns. It does not state that further enforcement will occur, that every Matkator right is attachable, or that Aweswell's separate assets are exposed.

WHY 8,588 MATTERS — AND WHY IT IS NOT “STEP 4” YET

The source record fixes a second Matkator hotel finca.

The 18 April 2017 note for finca 8,588 records apartment 510 and 100% Matkator ownership. The contemporaneous 25 May 2020 email described Matkator as a separate company outside the LPB concurso holding “a couple of properties” at Sun Park and sent the 8,584 and 8,588 notes together.

That is enough to register 8,588 as a historically confirmed Matkator asset. It is not enough to state current 2026 title, current mortgage status or present ETJ exposure.

“Free assets” cannot be back-filled into finca numbers.

Íñigo de Luisa's 11 June 2019 financing email referred to “free assets owned by Markator” as a possible mortgage route. The email did not identify finca numbers. Because the 2017 note for 8,588 still showed a mortgage, the register does not automatically equate that phrase with 8,584, 8,588 or both.

The correct next document is a fresh registry/title chain, not an inference.

EXCLUSIONS ARE PART OF THE REGISTER

A debtor perimeter is not a group perimeter.

NOT MATKATOR

LPB damages claim against minorities

The 11 June 2019 financing email described the damages claim as LPB's. It is excluded unless a later assignment or independent Matkator title is proved.

NOT MATKATOR BY DEFAULT

Aweswell shares / group lease / receivables

The rescue package discussed Aweswell/group pledges and receivables. Group-level financing language does not establish Matkator ownership. Aweswell's shares in Matkator are an Aweswell asset.

SEPARATE ESTATE

LPB Concurso 36/2012

LPB's insolvency estate remains LPB's. The register does not move LPB assets into Matkator merely because Aweswell owned both companies.
