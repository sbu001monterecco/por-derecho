Corporate transition and death: four anchors

5 Feb 2009

Gerardo Nicanor ceased as sole administrator when governance moved to a board, but remained president. José Daniel, Laura Patricia, Javier and Gerardo Zacarías entered the board.

7 Aug 2020

Gerardo Nicanor's substantive exit from the board/presidency. The four children continued: José Daniel president; Gerardo Zacarías vice-president; Laura Patricia secretary/delegated director; Javier director.

2 Feb 2024

Construcciones Acosta Matos, S.A. was extinguished through demerger. This should not be rewritten as four individual resignations.

Mar 2024

Gerardo Nicanor's death was publicly reported. The exact civil-registry date remains open.

Death does not erase the historical record. Death extinguishes the deceased person's personal criminal responsibility, but does not erase historical acts and does not by itself extinguish every civil/patrimonial, restitution, benefit, succession or evidential question. Nor does it transfer criminal culpability to heirs or relatives.

Attributed testimony concerning the origin of the intervention

Declaration 018 preserves, as a recollection attributed to Patricia Domínguez and still awaiting her direct adoption and independent corroboration, the account of her first meeting with José Daniel Acosta Matos at the Acosta Matos office in Las Palmas, accompanied by lawyer Cristo Suárez Pimentel.

According to that attribution, José Daniel explained in substance that dissident owners who were friends of his father asked the Acosta Matos perimeter to become involved because they wanted Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. removed from the hotel so the complex could be reunified/rebuilt. The account can be relevant to origin, knowledge and objective, but it is not a verbatim recording and does not by itself prove unlawful agreement, criminal intent, causation or any offence.
