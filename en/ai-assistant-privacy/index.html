AI · TRANSPARENCY · PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Ask the record without building a visitor profile.

The assistant is an artificial-intelligence system. It answers questions about public Project Sun Rock / Por Derecho pages by text or audio. It is not a substitute for professional advice, an authority, the official file or a protected reporting channel.

No persistent chat history

Project Sun Rock does not retain the question, answer or transcript in its analytics store.

Transient audio

Audio is used to transcribe the question and is not kept as a file by Project Sun Rock.

Separate analytics

Analytics are optional and work without storing conversation content or persistent visitor identifiers.

Intended processing

ControllerGil Marer for the editorial activity of this site unless a separate activity expressly identifies another controller. Contact and rights are set out in the full privacy notice.
TextProcessed transiently to retrieve relevant public pages and generate the answer. It is not kept as a Project Sun Rock conversation history.
AudioSent to the backend for transcription. The design does not perform speaker identification, identity recognition or voiceprinting. Project Sun Rock does not retain the original audio after the request.
AI providerThe backend is prepared to use the OpenAI API. Response requests use store:false. OpenAI states that API data is not used to train its models by default; technical/safety retention depends on the account configuration. European data residency and zero retention require an eligible configuration/project.
HostingThe backend is designed to run as a Netlify Function in Frankfurt, EU. Hosting providers may generate their own technical security logs under their terms.
Core legal basisThe design relies on the legitimate interest in responding to a request voluntarily initiated by a visitor through minimal, transient and expected processing. Before production deployment the balancing assessment should be documented and the basis reassessed if accounts, persistent history, identification or further purposes are added.
Optional analyticsActivated only by a separate checkbox. Daily aggregates cover language, text/audio, topic, page, answer status, source count, a voluntary broad interest category and approximate country. Questions, transcripts, answers, IP addresses and persistent IDs are not stored. The intended legal basis for this separate purpose is consent, and refusal does not disable the chatbot.
ProfilingNo individual profile is created, visitors are not tracked across sessions, and no legal or similarly significant decisions are made about them.
Sensitive dataNot requested. Do not submit confidential, privileged, special-category, children's or unnecessary personal information. If you need to report possible wrongdoing, use an official or protected route.
Provider boundary. Project Sun Rock not retaining content does not automatically mean no technical provider can apply security retention. Before activation, the processor agreement, AI account configuration, transfers and, where required, European residency/zero-retention option should be verified.

Useful insights without identifying a person

Aggregate analytics can show which topics attract questions, whether visitors prefer text or voice, which languages are used, which pages trigger questions, what proportion of answers lack sufficient sources and which broad visitor-interest categories people choose to state. The purpose is to improve content and navigation, not identify a particular visitor.
