UNITARY ANALYSIS · REVERSE ENGINEERING · 360°

The present does not prove the past.

Today's ownership, operation, financing or control position does not by itself prove that every upstream bridge by which that position was reached was legally sufficient, economically sound or free of earlier rights that remain material.

360° control rule: start from the end-state, work backwards through every indispensable bridge, identify the evidence and legal basis supporting each arrow, then hypothetically remove each contested node and ask what downstream rights survive independently. Prove the bridge. Prove the dependency. Prove the consequence.

This is a dependency map to be proved. It does not assert that every node is invalid or that every later act falls if an earlier node is disputed.

The removal test: “take this bridge away”

For every node: if this bridge is removed, extinguished, reduced, recalculated or found legally ineffective, what later position remains on an independent title and what part actually depended on that bridge?

Legal survival

Is there an independent registration, contract, adjudication, statute or third-party protection sustaining the later act?

Economic survival

Would the later value, funding or ability to operate exist to the same extent without the upstream asset, credit, security or control?

Procedural survival

What did a judgment or order actually decide, and which questions were never decided, remained conditional or depend on disputed facts?

Third-party survival

Which independent protections may a third party invoke, and which require good faith, consideration, registration, absence of knowledge or other predicates?

Micro-rule: Day Zero before Day Nine

For Civil Code Article 1535, the 360° method becomes a proof rule: no one should obtain an irreversible nine-day forfeiture merely by asserting that the clock started. The credit, transfer, assignee, debtor, relevant economics and specific demand for payment said to trigger the period must be identified.

Who controlled each LPB decision, what information existed, what action or omission occurred, what estate-interest analysis was recorded, and what effect followed.

Each finca needs its own title, authority to transfer, registration, possession, acquisition-right analysis and remedy.

2018 unit-rights workstream

This is separate from the credit route and must be tested transaction by transaction, without converting the existence of legal work into proof of an enforceable right.

Criminal track

Criminal proceedings do not automatically freeze civil rights. Relevance must be mapped fact by fact, claim by claim and by procedural stage.

CAM / HNT / funding / operation

Later layers must identify their independent title and which representations about ownership, control, funding or works depended on upstream facts.

The strongest opposing case belongs inside the analysis

CAM/Acosta Matos or another interested party may seek to prove that the credit was never litigious in the strict Article 1535 sense; that a valid historical trigger occurred and the period expired; that an exception or transaction structure excluded the right; that final orders closed particular issues; that later acts rest on independent titles; that third parties are protected; or that claims against the Insolvency Administrator are time-barred.

Por Derecho's answer is not to replace those defences with rhetoric. It is to require each defence—and our own case—to identify its fact, document, order, date, legal rule and dependency.

The judge: institutional reconstruction, not automatic personal allegation

Judicial acts within Insolvency 36/2012 belong in the causal map: what each act decided, on what material, what became final and what was never determined. Inclusion in the chain does not itself allege personal unlawful conduct or a relationship with Acosta Matos. Personal responsibility requires independent evidence and the competent legal route.

Question for anyone relying on the stability of the end-state

What is your independent bridge?
  1. Identify the act from which your right derives.
  2. Identify the asset, credit or power you received.
  3. Identify the order, deed, registry entry or contract supporting it.
  4. Explain whether the right survives if the prior contested node is removed.
  5. Identify which facts depend on Insolvency 36/2012, PH122→CAM, third-party title or matters now under investigation.
  6. Separate legal third-party protection from economic dependence on today's end-state.

Final control rule

THE PRESENT DOES NOT PROVE THE PAST.

For Article 1535: no Day Nine without proving Day Zero.
For the whole chain: no finality merely from today's appearance without proving each indispensable bridge and its independence.

The objective is not to presume invalidity. It is to prevent the end-state from substituting for determination of the steps that made it possible.
