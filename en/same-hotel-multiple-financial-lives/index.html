The same hotel, assets and works: multiple financial lives.

Project Sun Rock does not merely say that different funding sources coexisted. It alleges that the Sun Park/MYND Yaiza hotel and substantially overlapping economic bases — assets, refurbishment, costs, value and employment — may have been used repeatedly before different decision-makers while most of the identified property perimeter remained within Insolvency 36/2012 and other integrated property remained outside it.

SUBSTANTIATED ALLEGATION · NOT A JUDICIAL OR AUDIT FINDING

Project Sun Rock alleges possible double or triple funding and multiple parallel lives of the same hotel project. The record already establishes fragmented title, RICPE’s internal knowledge in July 2021, later RIC finance, the regional incentive and ERDF identification. What must now be produced and reconciled is whether the same assets, works, invoices, jobs, value or availability were used more than once, what each institution received and who allowed the project to progress despite unresolved prerequisites.

The structural contradiction

Controlled fact

Legally fragmented title

RICPE’s internal certification of 20 July 2021 described 262 properties: 54 CAM, 190 LPB and 18 third-party. It also recorded future/conditional CAM title, an unsigned LOI and full due diligence not yet begun.

Controlled fact

Economically unitary project

The same location was presented, refurbished, financed and later operated as an integrated hotel. Economic unity does not erase individual title; legal fragmentation does not deny that the same refurbishment and operating capacity may have supported several applications.

Audit rule: keep LPB, Matkator, third parties, CAM, HNT and each instrument legally separate; then reconcile them economically property by property, work by work, invoice by invoice, job by job and euro by euro.

The missing control chain

Sun Park requires reconstruction of RICPE’s normal project protocol and a stage-by-stage comparison with what actually happened:

OriginationWho introduced and sponsored the project.
AdmissibilityPromoter, title, capacity and minimum documents.
Due diligenceLegal, technical, financial, valuation and ownership.
GovernanceConflicts, recusals, committee, board and conditions.
MoneyWaivers, drawdown, use, monitoring and correction.

It is not enough to say CAM failed a prerequisite. The record must identify who had to check it, who knew of the deficiency, who could stop the project, whether a valid waiver existed and how the matter crossed into the next stage.

Each departure must be classified as: documented authorised waiver, control failure or unresolved evidential gap. A “blind eye” finding requires proof of knowledge, duty, ability to act and conscious inaction.

Valuation: €9.77m versus an €11–12m requirement

The allegation cannot be resolved by a generic banking rule. Every valuation must be produced and the economic bridge explained:

Perimeter

Which properties, common areas, rights, litigation, encumbrances and third-party assets each valuation included.

Current / post-works value

Existing-condition value, capex, completed value, income, occupancy and sensitivity.

Finance structure

Equity, participating loan, RIC, grant, ERDF, bank finance, guarantees and instrument conditions.

The correct question: what valuation, post-refurbishment value, equity contribution, security or additional support enabled an investment exceeding the alleged approximately €9.77m concurso-context value, and was that basis consistent before every recipient?

60 jobs, about 86 and an alleged operating need of about 50 FTE

Adding headlines without definitions would be wrong. The reconciliation must identify employees and periods:

60

The published GC/836/P06 commitment. Establish whether these were net, new, maintained, FTE, temporary or another metric.

≈86

The alleged June-2023 RICPE figure. Recover the native source and compare person by person and period by period.

≈50 FTE

Project Sun Rock’s alleged operating requirement. Test it through payroll, Social Security, rosters, departments and outsourcing.

Duplication allegation: if the same workers, contracts, FTEs or payroll costs supported more than one finance or support instrument, that must be identified and quantified. If the metrics were different, produce the definition that makes them compatible.

Inside and outside Insolvency 36/2012

Insolvency plane

What the IA and Court knew about refurbishment, investor presentation, finance, incentive and operation; what authority they exercised; what value and opportunity belonged to LPB.

Extraconcursal plane

Which Matkator and third-party rights were used, refurbished, valued, financed or operated; under what title, consent, exclusion or compensation.

One economically integrated hotel can contain property inside and outside an insolvency. Unity does not turn third-party property into estate property; fragmentation does not prevent investigation of whether the same unitary hotel repeatedly supported finance.

Orion: a separate branch; for Sun Park, one specific FMMM question

RICPE founded Orion and the issue document describes RICPE financing architecture. Those corporate facts remain on the Orion record. But Orion is not presented as Sun Park’s next financial life without a specific patrimonial bridge.

The Sun Park question is this: how did FMMM move from a paid property-administrator role in the Sun Park/Community environment — with duties of advice, diligence, warning, information and protection — into the later Pamalexsha/AGM/Orion perimeter, who facilitated that transition and what relevance does it have to his knowledge, independence, incentives or conflicts?

No responsibility is attributed to Orion, JDAM, Shaila, Antonio or anyone else by association. Each act, knowledge, duty and benefit must be proved individually.
