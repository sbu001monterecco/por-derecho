Current Spanish professional framework

Spanish lawyers are not literally “officers of the court” in the common-law sense. The relevant framework instead includes duties of procedural good faith, loyalty, honesty, independence, confidentiality and conflict control under Ley Orgánica 5/2024, the Estatuto General de la Abogacía Española and the LOPJ. Whether the facts here trigger a specific disclosure or cessation duty is a matter for the actual facts, applicable procedure and competent court/professional body.
