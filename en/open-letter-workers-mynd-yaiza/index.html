Open letter to the people who work there

To those who work now, or worked before, at MYND Yaiza, MYND Hotels, Canarian Hospitality and the hotels and businesses connected with the business environment we are investigating.

This letter is not against you.

We are not pursuing payrolls. We are pursuing responsibility. We do not confuse a worker with an owner, an employee with a director, or someone who arrived later with someone who made decisions earlier.

My name is Gil Marer. To me, the establishment now marketed as MYND Yaiza remains, historically and materially, Sun Park: the place where a decisive part of our business and personal history took place.

I want to speak to you directly because my position on the origin of what came afterwards is severe.

“Born in sin”

I am convinced, on the basis of the record we have assembled and the documentation we have taken into proceedings and to authorities, that the transformation of Sun Park and certain later business structures rest on a deeply flawed history of property, corporate acts, insolvency, finance and exploitation.

I express that with a deliberately strong metaphor: born in sin.

But that expression has a precise limit. It is my moral judgment and my position on the origin and construction of certain structures. It is not a collective criminal classification, it does not turn all related companies into one legal person, and it does not attribute crime, knowledge or intent to every person who works or has worked within them. Any responsibility, if it exists, must be proved individually.

My challenge extends to the establishment now called MYND Yaiza, Hotel New Trend, later developments of MYND Hotels, Canarian Hospitality and other business, financial and hotel-management structures appearing in the record. But connection does not mean legal identity, and employment does not mean participation.

The distinction I want on the record

A company may have owners, directors, advisers, financiers and people with decision-making power. It also has housekeepers, receptionists, cooks, waiters, gardeners, technicians, maintenance staff, administrators, entertainers, middle managers and many other people who simply do their jobs.

I do not confuse them.

I do not consider a person responsible because they wear a uniform, receive a salary, look after a guest, repair an installation or work inside an organisation whose origins I challenge.

A structure arising from events we denounce as unlawful can, years later, employ people who are entirely innocent. Innocence does not disappear because someone works inside a structure whose origins we question.

Many of you may have arrived years later. Some may never have heard the names Sun Park, Luchy Playa Blanca, Matkator, Construcciones Acosta Matos, RICPE or the proceedings discussed on this site. That matters. Chronology matters. Authority matters. Decision-making matters.

Responsibility is not transmitted through a payslip.

Our attention is directed at power, decision, knowledge, authority, disposition of assets and economic benefit where those can be proved through evidence. Presence is not participation. Partial knowledge is not guilt. Work is not complicity.

I invite you to examine the record

I do not ask you to accept my account because I say it. I invite you to explore this website, read the chronologies, open the public documents and form your own view.

You can start with the Sun Park control chronology, the documented business perimeter, the RICPE documentary trail, the RIC, incentives and funds chain, the status of DP 1901/2026 and our recovery and restitution objectives.

If you find something wrong, exaggerated or incomplete, I want to know that too. A truth that supports our position is welcome. A truth that corrects it is welcome as well.

If you know relevant facts: Spain's Law 2/2023

Spain has Law 2/2023 of 20 February on the protection of persons who report regulatory infringements and the fight against corruption.

Its personal scope includes, among others, employees and may extend to former employees where information was obtained in a work or professional context. The law provides external reporting channels and prohibits retaliation against people who report in accordance with its regime.

Not every disclosure or act is automatically protected. The law does not make lawful the acquisition of, or access to, information where that acquisition or access itself constitutes a criminal offence, and it does not remove other liability for acts unrelated or unnecessary to reporting an infringement.

If you personally witnessed facts that may be relevant, or if you already lawfully possess documents, messages, photographs, records or information capable of corroborating, qualifying or refuting facts in this history, I ask you to act with decency, care and respect for the rule of law.

Do not steal documents. Do not enter systems without authorisation. Do not unlawfully copy databases or personal data. Do not alter files, screenshots or metadata. Do not deliberately put yourself at risk.

If you have serious information, preserve the original intact, obtain independent legal advice if you need it, and consider communicating it through a lawful and competent channel: the Independent Authority for the Protection of Informants or the competent regional body where applicable, and, depending on the nature of the facts, the competent judicial, prosecutorial or police authorities.

I am not asking you to hand me company secrets. I am asking something different: understand that a legal framework exists for reporting and, if you possess legitimate evidence of relevant facts, seriously consider placing it in the hands of an authority with power to investigate it.

And if you know nothing, I ask nothing more of you

We do not assume every worker knows something. We do not expect anyone to invent, reconstruct or speculate. If you saw nothing, you saw nothing. If you do not know, you do not know.

But someone who does know something should be able to distinguish professional loyalty from silence concerning facts potentially relevant to the public interest. That decision is personal and should be made lawfully, safely and responsibly.

What I am not seeking

I do not want a worker to lose a job so that someone else can answer for what they did. I do not want to harm families, suppliers or people who simply live from their work. Our stated objective is to recover rights and property where the law allows, correct continuing consequences, obtain redress for proven harm, and require responsibility only from the person to whom it belongs and only to the extent it can be proved.

That does not reduce the seriousness of our position toward those who did hold power, information, decision-making capacity or economic benefit. It makes that position more precise.

This letter exists to make a distinction

Between those who work and those who decide.

Between those who inherited a reality and those who helped create it.

Between presence and responsibility.

Between knowledge and participation.

Between a company and the people who walk into it every morning to work.

I know which acts I am fighting. I want you to know, with the same clarity, who I am not fighting.

Gil Marer
