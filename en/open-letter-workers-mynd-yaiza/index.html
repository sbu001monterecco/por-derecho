OPEN LETTER · 16 AUGUST 2026

To current and former MYND, Canarian Hospitality and related tourism workers: one piece you know may matter

This invitation is addressed to people who work or worked at MYND Yaiza, MYND Hotels, Canarian Hospitality, Hotel New Trend and at hotels, companies, advisers, contractors, suppliers or tourism structures connected with the documented perimeter. I am not asking you to adopt our thesis. I am asking you to understand that, if you know facts or lawfully hold information capable of confirming, correcting or refuting the chain, it may have public-interest value even if it seems indirect or belongs to a later stage.

This is not merely a private dispute about a hotel.

The Sun Park property dispute is the point of origin of a wider documentary investigation into what was represented about the asset, its ownership, availability, operation, financing and commercial continuity; what each gatekeeper checked; and what tax, regulatory, financing and public-support decisions later relied on those premises.

The chain we are trying to verify

The public architecture of the record can be summarised as follows:

SUN PARK / LPB / MATKATOR → CAM / HNT → RICPE → RIC → REGIONAL INCENTIVES → ERDF → MYND → later management, financing and market structures

These are distinct layers and must remain separate. We do not say that an irregularity at the beginning automatically invalidates everything that followed. Nor do we say that every entity or person in the chain knew of, or shared, one common purpose. The question is narrower: were material representations about the asset, rights, availability, operation and investment accurate; who made them; who received them; what did each authority, adviser or financier independently check; and what happened afterwards?

The working hypothesis is serious, but it remains a hypothesis to be tested. Gil Marer alleges that certain originating acts may have enabled later tax-incentive, financing and public-support materialisation on false or incomplete premises. That allegation is not a judicial determination and does not justify describing RIC fraud, subsidy fraud or loss to EU funds as established without layer-specific evidence.

Why you may know something even if you know nothing about the “Sun Park case”

Many of you may have arrived years later. You may never have heard of Luchy Playa Blanca, Matkator, Insolvency 36/2012, Construcciones Acosta Matos or the proceedings discussed on this site. That does not make your experience irrelevant.

A person may know nothing about the origin yet have seen a later piece that helps reconstruct the chain: a marketing instruction, ownership document, due-diligence file, refurbishment budget, invoice, grant folder, bank communication, unit schedule, PMS access, customer migration, minute, RIC email, regulatory query, works certification, payment order or explanation of who was authorised to represent the hotel.

Asset and authority

Titles, deeds, unit lists, powers, authorisations, asset availability, right to operate, occupation, access, works and relations with owners.

Operation and customers

PMS, reservations, databases, reviews, digital profiles, telephone numbers, domains, photographs, room inventory, operator contracts, revenue and migration of historical information.

RICPE / RIC / financing

Due diligence, investment memoranda, committees, conflicts, Series F/G, loans, drawdowns, guarantees, budgets, invoices, source and use of funds, RIC materialisation and suitability documentation.

Incentives / ERDF / controls

GC/836/P06, beneficiary declarations, eligible expenditure, certifications, payments, verifications, audits, visits, ERDF publicity, corrections, recovery and communications with authorities.

CNMV and market information

Information supplied to investors, RICPE documentation, regulatory queries or responses, communications with CNMV or advisers, and any material showing what information was considered and when.

Evidence that contradicts our thesis

A valid authorisation, innocent explanation, due diligence that identified and corrected a problem, a genuine separation of assets, or any evidence showing that one of our inferences is wrong.

An indirect piece may be decisive.

You do not need to know the whole story. If your information helps answer who knew what, when, from which document, concerning which asset and with what authority, it may help an authority confirm, narrow or reject part of the chain.

The regulatory and public-funds dimension

The issue no longer ends with who lost or gained control of a hotel. The record is documentarily connected with RIC Private Equity Investment Partners (RICPE), the CNMV traceability track, the materialisation of RIC — the Canary Islands Investment Reserve, regional incentives and ERDF, and the later MYND operation.

That does not mean CNMV, AEAT, the Canary Islands Government, the regional-incentives authority or ERDF automatically “validated” all upstream facts. The serial reliance question is precisely what each gatekeeper checked against original sources and which premise it inherited from others.

MYND's permanent plaque expressly identifies ERDF. The exact percentage, certified and paid expenditure, eligibility, controls and any recovery remain to be completed documentarily. Likewise, RICPE, the RIC tax route, regional incentives and ERDF must not be collapsed into one pot or one accusation.

Who this invitation is for

Not only directors. It also includes current and former personnel in reception, reservations, revenue, sales, marketing, accounting, administration, finance, procurement, works, maintenance, IT, data protection, legal, compliance, HR, hotel management, development, asset management, project management, contracting, suppliers and external advisers who acquired relevant knowledge in a professional context.

It also reaches people at other hotels or tourism assets connected with the documented business perimeter if they saw processes, documents, instructions, financing structures or representation methods that may be relevant by comparison or continuity. That invitation does not presume that those hotels or companies bear any responsibility.

Employment is not responsibility

A company has owners, directors, advisers, financiers and people with decision-making power. It also has housekeepers, receptionists, cooks, waiters, technicians, gardeners, maintenance and administrative staff, and many others who simply work.

We do not confuse them. Responsibility does not pass through a payslip. Presence is not participation. Partial knowledge is not guilt. Working for a company does not make someone responsible for decisions taken by its owners or directors.

The phrase “born in sin”, used by Gil Marer as a moral description of the origin he challenges, is a metaphor and a personal position. It is not a collective criminal classification and does not assert that every related company or worker shares responsibility.

If you know something: preserve, separate and report lawfully

Spain's Law 2/2023 of 20 February establishes a protection regime for people who report specified infringements learned in a work or professional context. It can include current and former workers, among others, where statutory requirements are met.

Protection requires, among other conditions, reasonable grounds to believe the information was true when reported and within the law's scope. The law prohibits retaliation in protected cases and permits external reporting. It is not a blanket criminal immunity and does not make criminal system access or theft of information lawful.

The invitation is explicit

If you possess information directly or indirectly related to this chain, you do not need to agree with us. Preserve originals you already lawfully possess. Separate what you personally saw or heard from what others told you. Record dates, people, systems and where the underlying source can be found.

If the information may concern an infringement, consider reporting through a lawful and competent channel: the Independent Authority for the Protection of Informants, the competent regional authority where applicable, National Police, Guardia Civil, Public Prosecutor or the competent court, depending on the nature of the facts. If your own position could expose you to liability, obtain independent legal advice before making a statement.

We are not asking you to give us trade secrets, steal documents, unlawfully copy databases, enter systems without authorisation, alter metadata or coordinate stories.

If you know nothing, invent nothing

We do not assume every worker knows something. If you saw nothing, you saw nothing. If you do not know, you do not know. If a legitimate explanation contradicts our hypothesis, that explanation matters too.

What we ask is that anyone who does know one piece understands that they do not need to solve the entire case for their information to be useful.
