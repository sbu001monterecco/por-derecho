Consejo General de la Abogacía Española: a national and inter-Bar question, not a substitute first instance.

This page prepares a separate CGAE communication after activating the Canary regional route. It expressly respects RS-001022/2026: it does not ask the Consejo General to decide DIP 79, DIP 80 or other individual complaints at first instance.

National object: coordination among Bars and autonomous councils, competence conflicts, common deontological criteria, professional independence, preliminary-information traceability and preservation where one evidential matrix crosses several professional bodies.

1. What belongs at national level

Coordination

What national criterion should apply where Madrid, Las Palmas, Tenerife and the Canary Council touch different parts of one professional and documentary matrix.

Competence

How territorial conflicts and overlaps should be resolved or routed without the object fragmenting or disappearing among bodies.

Independence

What uniform safeguards protect lawyer freedom and independence where complaints, Bar threats or personal exposure connect with defence work.

Traceability

What minimum national standard should make it possible to know whether preliminary information remains open, was archived, became a formal file, was suspended or referred.

Not requested: a national sanction against a named lawyer, substitution for ICALPA/ICATF/ICAM, premature review of matters with no territorial decision, or any criminal/insolvency determination.

2. New systemic fact: DIP 80 and the visibility asymmetry

ICALPA confirmed territorial competence, received the complaint concerning the Insolvency Administrator and opened DIP 80/2026 as preliminary information on 27 May. Numerous later submissions were registered, but no later substantive communication has been located identifying archive, formal disciplinary opening, suspension, referral or a final decision of that stage.

September 2026: the Insolvency Administrator himself acknowledges before the Audiencia Provincial that Gil Marer approached ICALPA and uses that fact within his defence. No assertion is made about how he obtained that knowledge. The national question is whether a uniform traceability standard should prevent professional oversight from being visible to and usable by the concerned professional while the complainant cannot identify the basic procedural state.

4. Living draft — national CGAE communication

Subject: National and inter-Bar deontological consultation and coordination request — professional independence, competence, traceability and common criteria — Sun Park / Concurso 36/2012

To the Presidency, Secretariat and Professional Deontology Commission of the Consejo General de la Abogacía Española:

My name is Gil Marer. I write in my own name and in the legitimate interests of Aweswell Limited and Matkator, S.L.

This communication is not a first-instance disciplinary complaint against a named lawyer and does not ask CGAE to replace territorial Bars or the Canary Council. We know and respect response RS-001022/2026, which stated that ordinary complaints against lawyers do not fall to the Consejo General at first instance.

The purpose of this new communication is different and specifically national/inter-Bar.

Professional-regulatory matters currently exist or have existed before ICALPA, ICATF, ICAM and CCACM, in addition to a regional question we are activating before the Consejo Canario de Colegios de Abogados. The evidence and individual responsibilities remain separate, but recurring questions have arisen concerning competence, territorial referral, professional independence, custody/preservation, file traceability and interaction between disciplinary and criminal proceedings.

ICALPA DIP 80/2026 provides a concrete example. The route began with a formal competence enquiry in December 2025; ICALPA confirmed territorial competence in January 2026; the formal complaint concerning the Insolvency Administrator was registered in February; and preliminary information with a named rapporteur opened on 27 May. Multiple submissions have since been registered, but the currently located external record contains no substantive communication identifying whether preliminary information remains open, was archived, led to a formal disciplinary file, was suspended because of connected criminal proceedings or moved to another procedural state.

In September 2026 the concerned professional, in his opposition before the Audiencia Provincial in the appeal concerning his removal as Insolvency Administrator, expressly identified ICALPA among the institutions approached by us and used that activity within his defence narrative. We do not assert that ICALPA formally served DIP 80 on him and do not know the source of his awareness. The issue we raise is systemic: what minimum traceability standard should ensure that activated professional oversight produces an identifiable and verifiable procedural state, rather than only an external appearance of oversight.

We therefore respectfully ask CGAE, within its national competences:

1. to identify the general criteria governing coordination where one professional/evidential matrix affects several Bars and an autonomous council, while keeping individual responsibility separate;

2. to identify the route for consultations or competence conflicts between Bars and Councils where territorial referrals or connected acts span several jurisdictions;

3. to state whether a general deontological criterion exists or can be formulated on minimum preliminary-information traceability: whether it remains open, was archived, became a formal file, was suspended or referred, without requiring disclosure of confidential content;

4. to clarify the general criterion where criminal proceedings concern the same or inseparable facts and how the corresponding initiation/suspension should be documented;

5. to identify national criteria protecting lawyer independence and freedom of action where Bar-complaint threats, contemplated actions against lawyers/procurators or extra-procedural contacts may objectively affect defence continuity;

6. to identify the appropriate route through which the Canary Council may raise consultations, seek coordination or promote uniform criteria on these questions;

7. to preserve the distinction between an individual file and a systemic question, avoiding treatment of this communication as a new ordinary first-instance complaint against a named professional.

We will attach or link the communication sent to the Canary Council so that the national record accurately reflects that the regional route has been activated and that this national consultation does not seek to replace it.

I would be grateful for acknowledgement, a reference number and identification of the competent body for this consultation. If any part should use another CGAE route, I would appreciate written directions.

Yours faithfully,

Gil Marer
for himself and in the legitimate interests of Aweswell Limited and Matkator, S.L.
