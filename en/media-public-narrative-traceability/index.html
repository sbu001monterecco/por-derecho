Updates · Contact and corrections

How to cite without overstating the source

Source → Authority → Perimeter → Contradiction → Consequence → Reversibility. Cite the document and page, its date, the speaker, the proceeding and whether the proposition is a documented fact, allegation, inference, decision or open question. Explain what it proves and what it does not prove.

Document access: the routes assemble public copies, redacted versions, summaries and references; complete dockets and original records are not all public. This is not a confidential evidence repository, and linking a record does not certify access to every document it references.

The rule

Publication ≠ underlying truth · press release ≠ independent verification · programme repetition ≠ proof · physical transformation ≠ unitary ownership · hotel operation ≠ registered title.

We also preserve the reasonable editorial defence: an outlet may correctly have described a refurbishment, accurately reported customer experience or accurately attributed official/corporate information without purporting to determine disputed civil or registered ownership. If a documentary response corrects our reconstruction, we will incorporate it.

What we ask

Verify → explain → correct → update → restate, as appropriate. We do not request confidential journalistic sources beyond what is necessary and lawfully disclosable; we ask for the non-confidential basis and attribution of published propositions, relevant corrections, preservation of source/production records and an opportunity to complete the public record.
