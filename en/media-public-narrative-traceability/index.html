MEDIA · EDITORIAL TRACEABILITY

We do not ask the media to adopt our account. We ask what they verified.

When a broadcaster, specialist or local publication describes Sun Park, its later transformation into MYND Yaiza, RIC financing or the Acosta Matos / Canarian Hospitality perimeter, the public question is finite: what was attributed, what was independently checked, and what should now be clarified in light of later primary records?

How to read and cite this record

Source → Authority → Perimeter → Contradiction → Consequence → Reversibility. Identify the document and page; distinguish an actor’s statement from independent verification and keep adverse decisions and alternative explanations visible.

LPB, its insolvency estate, CEXP, Matkator, other rights-holders and the whole hotel are not the same perimeter. Registered title, possession, operation, funding and responsibility require different evidence. The Sun Park/MYND and Orion branches do not become one transfer chain through association.

Public copies, redacted versions, summaries and references are not complete court files. A graphic is an editorial guide; satire is not evidence. Dates of first documented presence do not themselves establish when a role actually began.

The rule

Publication ≠ underlying truth · press release ≠ independent verification · programme repetition ≠ proof · physical transformation ≠ unitary ownership · hotel operation ≠ registered title.

We also preserve the reasonable editorial defence: an outlet may correctly have described a refurbishment, accurately reported customer experience or accurately attributed official/corporate information without purporting to determine disputed civil or registered ownership. If a documentary response corrects our reconstruction, we will incorporate it.

What we ask

Verify → explain → correct → update → restate, as appropriate. We do not request confidential journalistic sources beyond what is necessary and lawfully disclosable; we ask for the non-confidential basis and attribution of published propositions, relevant corrections, preservation of source/production records and an opportunity to complete the public record.
