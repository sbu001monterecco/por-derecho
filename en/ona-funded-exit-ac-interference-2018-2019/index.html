6 JUN 2018 → 24 OCT 2019 · OPERATOR + FINANCE + DEBT + HOTEL FACILITIES + COMPETITION

The ONA funded exit and the evidence chain of alleged Insolvency Administrator interference

This dossier connects the ONA/Clubotel–Stoneweg/Varia architecture, the disputed CAM debt figure, the separate sale of hotel operating facilities, Daniel Irigoyen’s contemporaneous warning to the Insolvency Administrator, ONA’s continued work, and the later judicial ruling that the separation contradicted the unitary disposal structure.

Controlled conclusion. The record documents a real and advanced exit opportunity and acts/omissions objectively capable of impairing it. Gil Marer characterises that chain as direct and active sabotage by the Insolvency Administrator that enabled the CAM/Acosta Matos private perimeter. Purpose and criminal intent remain an allegation/evidential inference. The 24 October 2019 ruling confirms the structural defect and its effect on third-party improved bids; it does not adjudicate criminal sabotage or conspiracy.

Evidence chronology

6 JUN 2018

The financing package expected a signed hotel-operator contract, EUR26m take-out/acquisition route, ECO valuation, full due diligence and term-sheet work. CAPEX expressly included restaurant, reception, gardens, pools and common areas.

7–13 JUN

Daniel Irigoyen proposed proving sufficient funds, paying/consigning all insolvency liabilities, showing the ONA operating plan and concluding the proceeding, while preserving a parallel route to compete against CAM. Stoneweg issued an “Oferta Vinculante” subject to conditions on 12 June. After the 13 June meeting, Carlos Sanz reported judicial receptiveness to a debt-payment/conclusion route.

26 SEP

Daniel’s contemporaneous notes record that the AC and Judge accepted that CAM’s EUR13.1m calculation was wrong. Daniel records the AC as saying he had not informed the Court when CAM filed the certificate because the amount was immaterial to him in the dación/liquidation context; Daniel immediately objected that the amount mattered because third-party bids depended on it.

28 NOV

The unencumbered hotel facilities/locales and pools were separately sold within the CAM structure for EUR400,000. The later court ruling identifies those facilities as part of the unitary productive-unit structure together with the mortgaged apartments.

24 DEC

Daniel records telling the AC that separating the business/common elements “fastidiaba toda la operación tanto de pago de la deuda como de una futura subasta” — it wrecked both the debt-payment operation and a future auction. Daniel also records the AC as indifferent to the business-unit point and insisting that he was complying with the law. Daniel’s conclusion about an AC/CAM manoeuvre is his professional inference, not a judicial finding.

FEB 2019

ONA/Clubotel continued updating Sun Park operating documentation; a further lease/operating addendum was sent on 21 February 2019.

24 OCT 2019

The court refused to validate the separate sale, held that the disposal was unitary, said its own June 2018 partial suspension had been erroneous, stated the whole liquidation should have been suspended, and reasoned that no third party would improve the offer without the unencumbered operating facilities. The court also recorded that the separate transaction had occurred without prior judicial authorisation and had only been reported after completion.

Why the October 2019 ruling matters

The later ruling independently corroborates the central objective effect identified by Daniel in December 2018: stripping the unencumbered operating facilities out of the same commercial perimeter impaired the third-party improvement mechanism. That is powerful evidence of foreseeability and objective interference; it is not by itself proof of criminal motive.

Publication boundary

Documented: advanced ONA-led exit architecture; disputed debt quantum; separate facilities sale; contemporaneous warning; ONA continuation; judicial rejection of the separate sale and express third-party bidding rationale.

Still allegation/inference: deliberate sabotage, criminal collusion with CAM, guaranteed but-for closing, and final damages actor by actor.
