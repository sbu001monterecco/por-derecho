It is not established that LPB withdrew. It is established that a withdrawal was filed in its name.

This correction changes the centre of gravity of PP 1041/2017. The issue is not why “LPB chose to abandon” a useful route. The issue is who used LPB's name and procedural representation, with what authority, under what duty to the estate and for whose benefit.

POR DERECHO'S CENTRAL ALLEGATION

A false procedural attribution to LPB, deliberately contrary to the insolvent company and functionally favourable to CAM.

Por Derecho formally alleges that the withdrawal was not an authentic or authorised expression of the will of Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U.; that it was promoted or enabled by the Insolvency Administrator after removing the lawyer who had obtained the disclosure order; that no authorisation from the insolvency judge to withdraw has yet been located; and that the act destroyed a route capable of revealing the true acquisition price of the credit, reducing or extinguishing the exposure and opening a viability exit.

Por Derecho's case is that Francisco de Borja Rodríguez-Batllori Laffitte acted in this episode as a de facto functional agent for the CAM/Acosta Matos perimeter, for its objective benefit and that of decision-makers or beneficiaries to be determined, including José Daniel Acosta Matos and Laura Patricia Acosta Matos. This is a grave, formally articulated allegation to be proved; it is not presented as a final criminal conviction.

Three levels that must no longer be confused

Verified primary fact

On 5 March 2018 the Court recorded that “the party” LPB had requested withdrawal; CAM did not oppose; the proceeding ended; and the decree's heading identified Francisco de Borja Rodríguez-Batllori Laffitte himself as LPB's lawyer.

Central allegation

Por Derecho denies that LPB made an authentic decision and alleges that the use of its name was false, unauthorised, disloyal and deliberately damaging to the estate, within conduct functionally aligned with CAM/Acosta Matos.

Final proof outstanding

The complete pleading, signature, filer, LexNET user and metadata, instructions, specific authority, application to the insolvency judge, hearing of LPB and judicial authorisation order. The specific court must certify the file.

The documentary chain

12 JAN 2018

PP 1041 had already produced a concrete order against CAM

The Court fixed production of the PH122→CAM assignment deed and ordered disclosure of the specific price, the payment date and transaction costs. The route was not hypothetical or merely preparatory: it had a date, a defined object and a documentary obligation.

24–25 JAN 2018

CAM turned the Administrator's authority into its procedural defence

CAM argued that LPB's action lacked the required conformity of the Insolvency Administration and challenged standing. This does not itself prove prior agreement, but it identifies the Administrator's power as the obstacle CAM needed to activate to neutralise the order.

25 JAN 2018

The Administrator removed the lawyer and controlled the procurator

The Administrator notified Cristo Suárez Pimentel of termination of his professional relationship with LPB, requested transfer of the file, ordered him to abstain from all future LPB action and required the procurator not to file anything without his express consent. He also treated the power as revoked, save for necessary acts within PP 1041.

Direct causation: the office charged with protecting the estate took control of LPB's representation immediately after CAM invoked that same authority.

25 FEB 2018

CAM consented or did not oppose the withdrawal

The later decree records CAM's conformity or non-opposition. The direct beneficiary of the disappearance of the disclosure order did not have to produce in that route the deed, price, payment and costs that were the object of the diligence.

5 MAR 2018

The Court closed because a withdrawal was filed in LPB's name

The decree does not identify who instructed, drafted, signed or filed it. It does identify Francisco de Borja Rodríguez-Batllori Laffitte as LPB's lawyer. The controlled formulation is therefore not “LPB withdrew,” but: “a withdrawal was filed in LPB's name under representation controlled by the Insolvency Administrator.”

The legal barrier that makes judicial authorisation decisive

The then-applicable Insolvency Act required the Insolvency Administrator to act with the diligence of an orderly administrator and loyal representative (Article 35) and imposed liability for damage to the estate caused by unlawful acts or omissions or lack of due diligence (Article 36).

Where the debtor's powers were suspended, Article 51.2 provided that the Insolvency Administration substituted the debtor in pending proceedings, but required authorisation from the insolvency judge to withdraw, with the debtor to be heard. Opening liquidation placed LPB under suspension and applied those effects (Article 145).

Present position: no application for authorisation, hearing of LPB or Commercial Court order authorising withdrawal from PP 1041 has been located in the material available. Failure to locate it does not by itself prove non-existence; it makes the certified court file immediate P0 evidence.

De facto functional agency: the allegation to be proved actor by actor

Por Derecho need not prove a formal agency contract merely to articulate the functional hypothesis. The material test is:

Did the Administrator use a power entrusted for the protection of LPB in order, or with the conscious effect, of solving a problem CAM could not solve for itself—removing an information route adverse to CAM and producing a private benefit without documented benefit to the estate?

Exclusive power

CAM could not itself remove LPB's lawyer, control its procurator or validly file a withdrawal in the insolvent company's name. The Administrator controlled those gates by virtue of office.

Private necessity

CAM had identified lack of Administrator conformity as its defence to disclosure. The later intervention met precisely that procedural need.

Aligned result

The order disappeared; CAM consented; the information was not produced through that route; and the credit continued toward recognition, valuation and dación.

Individual attribution to José Daniel Acosta Matos and Laura Patricia Acosta Matos requires reconstruction of offices, instructions, knowledge, communications, ratification and benefit. Naming them identifies the decision and beneficiary perimeter requiring investigation; it does not replace that proof.

Criminal, civil, insolvency and professional relevance — without mixing thresholds

Criminal

If the certified file establishes that a non-existent LPB intention was attributed to it without authority and for the purpose of producing a ruling or patrimonial advantage, documentary/procedural falsity, procedural fraud, disloyal administration and civil liability arising from crime must be tested element by element. Criminal characterisation depends on authorship, intent, document, deception, ruling and harm; it is not presumed.

Insolvency and civil

Authority for the act, loyalty duty, liability for estate loss, loss of chance, restoration of value, challenge to dependent acts, accounting and connection to the indirect appropriation architecture.

Professional / ethical

Conflict, independence, procedural truthfulness, use of fiduciary powers, pressure on counsel, instruction traceability and the duty to explain estate benefit.

Evidential

The criminal complaint filed on 18 June 2026 proves that Por Derecho made the allegation and requested the full file. It does not by itself prove the truth of the alleged offences.

The strongest defence and the proof that decides it

The Administrator may say that opening liquidation vested him with representation; that PP 1041 was initiated without his approval; that Article 1535 was legally unavailable and was rejected on 15 February; that withdrawal avoided costs; that the procurator had sufficient authority; that judicial authorisation existed but has not yet been incorporated; that the decree preserved the possibility of another action; and that no firm funding existed for redemption.

None of those answers should be resolved rhetorically. The Article 51.2 authorisation, withdrawal pleading and metadata, estate-interest analysis, funding file and CAM communications decide the case. If they appear and demonstrate authority, contradiction, independent analysis and real benefit to LPB, the allegation weakens. If they do not exist, the allegation strengthens materially.

Unitary conclusion

The phrase “LPB withdrew” is removed from the controlled account.

The correct and strongest formulation is: after CAM invoked the Administrator's authority and the Administrator removed the lawyer who had obtained a disclosure order, a withdrawal was filed in LPB's name; the proceeding ended; the decree identified the Administrator as LPB's lawyer; and the specific judicial authorisation required for withdrawal has not yet been located.

Por Derecho alleges that the act was deliberate, falsely attributed to LPB, contrary to the estate and functionally executed for the benefit of CAM/Acosta Matos within the credit–control–title architecture. The certified file must now identify author, authority, intent, conflict, benefit and consequence.
