Cuatrecasas: DP 748/2026, La Laguna and the separate civil action.

This page states the position of Gil Marer, Aweswell and Matkator after testing Cuatrecasas’s work and billing against what it did, omitted, communicated, left unsaid, protected, implemented and handed over—and against the affirmative choices later made in Cambiario 1048/2019 and ETJ 163/2020.

Three simultaneous boundaries: this position neither exonerates nor displaces the principal actors in the Acosta Matos perimeter—including its alleged predecessors, the Monte Lanza/Molina dissidents and their representatives—or the Insolvency Administration and the disputed judicial conduct. It does not claim that Cuatrecasas joined that perimeter or shared a plan. It alleges, as a party position still to be adjudicated, that its acts, omissions, silence and inaction may have enabled or aggravated outcomes pursued or benefited from by those actors, while its later La Laguna conduct raises direct and separate issues.
Public position · 24 August 2026

The causal thesis, without substituting defendants

Improved position: Cuatrecasas was retained and billed within a lawful rescue, finance, ONA-operation and Concurso 36/2012 exit architecture conceived as an alternative to the control route attributed to the Acosta Matos perimeter. The issue is not whether the firm originated every adverse act. It is whether, while knowing the possession, access, security, mixed-title, funding and implementation risks, it failed to activate, update, preserve or transfer material safeguards called for by its mandate and billing; whether those failures deprived the client side of a real and substantial opportunity to prevent or materially contain identifiable parts of the adverse outcome; and whether its later enforcement steps against Matkator contradicted the original protective purpose. The client side has since worked to reverse those outcomes.

FINITE CRIMINAL ROUTE

DP 748/2026 is not the civil action.

Narrow criminal object

The question is whether the executing court was asked to act on a materially false or incomplete composite appearance concerning Matkator’s debt and debtor status, finca 8584’s identity and value, hotel integration, the need for verification and the beneficiary of adjudication or cession.

Threshold still to prove

It requires an exact deception or equivalent material omission, knowledge and fraudulent intent, causal judicial error, a prejudicial patrimonial resolution or disposition, loss and improper benefit. Deficient performance or disputed billing does not itself constitute procedural fraud.

Controlled status

The complaint was filed on 4 February and expanded on 16 March. The 24 March provisional dismissal was challenged by reconsideration and subsidiary appeal. The 8 June prosecution report sought partial allowance because the dismissal’s specific reasoning was insufficient, while also calling for clearer ordering and delimitation of the facts; a synthesis was filed on 26 June. The 21 August prosecution Decree refers to judicial ratification on 16 July. The signed decision of that date remains a public-corpus gap.

DP 748 must test a confined criminal hypothesis. The civil action must audit the full mandate and account without using the criminal process as an evidential shortcut.

LA LAGUNA · ETJ 163/2020

The remate assignee: leading hypothesis, identity still unproved.

The client side’s position is that an intended recipient connected to the Acosta Matos perimeter is the leading concrete hypothesis and the overwhelmingly dominant one requiring verification, because the enforcement reserved cession to a third party and preserved recourse against further Matkator assets within the same physical, operational and economic hotel chain whose consolidation is attributed to that perimeter. Gil Marer's assessment is that the probability of an unconnected assignee or ultimate beneficiary is close to zero. That is an attributed probability assessment—not a proved legal identity—and can be confirmed or excluded through defined records.

Reservation of cession

The request for adjudication with reservation of cession to a third party appears in the controlled record.

Documented

Finca 8584, its title and its place within Sun Park are identified; integration, possession, independent use and current value require technical evidence.

Mixed

The same hotel, control, operation and value-capture chain supports a connected Acosta Matos hypothesis that is not an abstract possibility.

The person who would receive or received the remate, and their beneficial owner, is not publicly identified.

Unproved

Court certification, instructions, KYC/AML, source of funds, beneficial ownership, side agreements, Registry entry, possession and later transfers.

Obtain
Controlled formulation: the record proves reservation of cession and preserves the claimant's near-zero probability assessment for an unconnected beneficiary; it does not yet name the assignee. Until the decisive records are obtained, “Acosta Matos is the assignee” will not be published as a verified identity. Nor should that open identity be used to neutralise a concrete hypothesis supported by convergence of asset, control, operation, finance and benefit.
