The causal thesis, without substituting defendants

Improved position: Cuatrecasas was retained and billed within a lawful rescue, finance, ONA-operation and Concurso 36/2012 exit architecture conceived as an alternative to the control route attributed to the Acosta Matos perimeter. The issue is not whether the firm originated every adverse act. It is whether, while knowing the possession, access, security, mixed-title, funding and implementation risks, it failed to activate, update, preserve or transfer material safeguards called for by its mandate and billing; whether those failures deprived the client side of a real and substantial opportunity to prevent or materially contain identifiable parts of the adverse outcome; and whether its later enforcement steps against Matkator contradicted the original protective purpose. The client side has since worked to reverse those outcomes.

FINITE CRIMINAL ROUTE

DP 748/2026 is not the civil action.

Narrow criminal object

The question is whether the executing court was asked to act on a materially false or incomplete composite appearance concerning Matkator’s debt and debtor status, finca 8584’s identity and value, hotel integration, the need for verification and the beneficiary of adjudication or cession.

Threshold still to prove

It requires an exact deception or equivalent material omission, knowledge and fraudulent intent, causal judicial error, a prejudicial patrimonial resolution or disposition, loss and improper benefit. Deficient performance or disputed billing does not itself constitute procedural fraud.

Controlled status

The complaint was filed on 4 February and expanded on 16 March. The 24 March provisional dismissal was challenged by reconsideration and subsidiary appeal. The 8 June prosecution report sought partial allowance because the dismissal’s specific reasoning was insufficient, while also calling for clearer ordering and delimitation of the facts; a synthesis was filed on 26 June. The 21 August prosecution Decree refers to judicial ratification on 16 July. The signed decision of that date remains a public-corpus gap.

DP 748 must test a confined criminal hypothesis. The civil action must audit the full mandate and account without using the criminal process as an evidential shortcut.
