What would materially change or narrow Por Derecho’s present view?

  • A complete primary 2013 AC source and definitive-text record resolving the €737,338.85 CEXP item and its legal treatment.
  • Delivery receipts, LexNET/docket entries and reasoned orders showing how the 2015–2018 CEXP/productive-unit submissions were received and dealt with.
  • The exact primary withdrawal/archive act, instruction and appeal/finality chain for PP 1041/2017, plus complete PH122→CAM assignment economics and any contemporaneous estate-interest analysis.
  • A contemporaneous title/perimeter reconciliation explaining the transition from fragmented ownership to the whole-project representations and later award/operation.
  • The complete Series F/G application, idoneidad decision(s), AEAT report scope and source-and-use evidence reconciling eligible expenditure and any other support.
  • Final or later procedural records from ICAM/CCACM/Tenerife capable of changing the currently published professional-conduct status.
  • Primary records that contradict, contextualise or defeat any published inference. Corrections should receive equivalent prominence.
