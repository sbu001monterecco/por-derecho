The 2022 Adjudication: Reconstructing the Offer, Debt, Deed and Accounts

Review of the primary sources corrects several earlier formulations and narrows the issue to verifiable questions: what was offered, what the Court authorised, what debt was used as consideration, how the EUR 400,000 premises line was accounted for and what the final insolvency accounts show.

Active reconstruction · not a judicial finding

Scope limit. This page does not claim that one proceeding can deliver “radical nullity of everything”, or that every downstream act automatically falls. The adjudication, insolvency accounting, land-register entries, third-party title, professional responsibility and any criminal or supervisory dimension require their own analysis, parties and procedural routes.

Six corrections that change the focus

EUR 13,168,082.02

has an exact arithmetic bridge

The 21 February 2022 deed itemises six principal-and-interest components across two mortgages. It is therefore unsafe to describe the figure as arbitrary or mathematically unexplained.

Debt

serving as consideration

The deed presents the figure as debt providing non-cash consideration for the transfer. That does not by itself prove a cash price or a liquid insolvency surplus.

EUR 5,186,061.79

approximate offered waiver

The 15 January 2018 offer gives this approximate figure. It does not match the EUR 6,102,697.60 calculated in later working papers.

159

mortgaged apartments

Protocol 457 of 2022 concerns the 159 mortgaged properties. The claim that it also re-transferred the 2018 premises is not established by that deed.

EUR 400,000

separate transaction line

The 18 May 2021 order separates adjudication of the mortgaged properties from direct sale of the unencumbered properties for EUR 400,000. Their accounting and registration must be reconstructed separately.

EUR 9,052,251.69

2016 classification figure

The 2016 definitive texts classify this amount as specially privileged credit. That classification does not automatically answer the amount payable or extinguished in 2022.

The calculation recorded in the deed

The six components reproduced in protocol 457 add exactly to EUR 13,168,082.02. The legal question is no longer whether a calculation exists, but whether every component was legally due, covered, recognised or authorised and correctly applied in the liquidation.

Document chain and unresolved questions

Offer · 15 January 2018

Dación at EUR 7,065,384.42, separate sale of unencumbered assets for EUR 400,000 and future recording of the waiver of the differential.

Order · 18 May 2021

Adjudicates the mortgaged properties under the plan and separately approves direct sale of the unencumbered properties for EUR 400,000.

Deed · 21 February 2022

Formalises the dación of 159 apartments, uses EUR 13,168,082.02 as debt-consideration, declares the debt extinguished and requests mortgage cancellation.

Land-register position not yet reconciled. A May 2022 simple note examined in this review still displayed both mortgages. A notarial cancellation request must not be conflated with effective cancellation property by property. Complete historic and current certificates are required.

Questions requiring documentary or judicial answers

  • Which court decision supports each of the six components of the EUR 13,168,082.02 debt?
  • What principal and interest were legally payable in February 2022 under the applicable insolvency and transitional rules?
  • Was the deed notified to the Court within the five days stated in it, and what review followed?
  • Where was the EUR 400,000 booked: receipt, retention, refund, set-off or application to another line?
  • What do the final account, liquidation balance and conclusion order record?
  • Which entries were cancelled or maintained for each property, and which later right-holders must be joined to any proceeding?
  • Which issues had already been raised, decided or potentially affected by finality, preclusion or time bars?

How this intelligence should be used

1. Reconstruction and accounts

Obtain the complete court file, the credit calculation as at 21 February 2022, the EUR 400,000 ledger, the five-day notice and the final accounts.

2. Precise relief

Only then seek determination, accounting correction, integration of an actually proven surplus, or partial ineffectiveness of a specific element shown to exceed what was authorised.

3. Separate perimeters

Address land-register correction, Matkator or third-party rights, professional responsibility and criminal, administrative or supervisory tracks separately.

Proving a surplus. A surplus does not arise automatically by subtracting a debt from an appraisal. The amount realised or legally attributed in the transaction must be proved and compared with the legally payable debt after every component has been reconstructed.
