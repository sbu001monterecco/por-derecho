The central issue is no longer a recalculation of interest: it is whether the transaction implemented retained the identity of the transaction authorised

The January 2021 judicial sources allow figures that had previously been conflated to be separated: recognised insolvency credit, mortgage liability, the threshold imposed on a third-party bidder, the separate sale of non-mortgaged assets and any later notarial consideration. The reconstruction must trace each concept from offer and plan through deed, Land Registry and liquidation accounts.

Active reconstruction · not a judicial finding

Scope limit. A documentary or accounting mismatch does not by itself prove nullity, fraud, surplus, bad faith or liability. The more precise question is: which act authorised each change in legal or economic function between offer, competitive publicity, adjudication, deed and accounting?

What the January 2021 sources now allow us to state more precisely

EUR 3,079,104.66 → EUR 3,182,000

the EUR 102,895.34 movement has a documentary explanation

The signed 25 January 2021 order reproduces the Provincial Court default-interest amount and later daily accrual until the mortgage-liability cap was reached. This explains the arithmetic movement; it does not by itself determine its classification as finally recognised insolvency credit.

EUR 13,168,082.02

mortgage subtotal within the competitive threshold

The 29 January 2021 court notice adds EUR 11,941,921.02 for the first rank and EUR 1,226,161.00 for the second. It is a primary figure within the conditions for an improved bid; any later legal function must be proved rather than assumed.

EUR 400,000

a separate component

The same notice separates EUR 400,000 for assets not subject to the special privilege. Later orders quoted in the 2022 appeals continue to describe a separate direct sale of premises and pools/solarium for that amount.

EUR 14,713,880.31

quantified conditions for a third party

This is the sum of both mortgage components, the EUR 400,000 and EUR 1,145,798.29 in community fees, before then-unquantified taxes and costs. It must not automatically be converted into CAM's price or recognised credit.

EUR 14.8m

the competition ceased to be hypothetical

A third-party bidder documented an acquisition proposal dated 8 February 2021 for EUR 14.8 million covering the identified property perimeter. The existence and content of the proposal are source-located; its procedural filing, sufficiency, funds and outcome still require documentary closure.

EUR 9,052,251.69

credit figure used in reconstructions of the definitive texts

It must be tied back to a certified copy of the definitive texts. The key discipline is not to conflate recognised credit with mortgage liability, the third-party threshold or any debt later set off in the deed.

The competition was real; its outcome still needs reconstruction

A strengthening fact for the public record. The court notice did not open a merely theoretical possibility: a third-party bidder documented a EUR 14.8m proposal. That permits an objective inquiry into how the proposal was tested against the published conditions and why the process ultimately ended with CAM's proposal.

The website deliberately anonymises the third-party bidder's identity. For the relevant insolvency question, its procedural and economic function is enough: a third party produced a proposal above the quantified threshold. The court filing, authority and funds evidence, judicial communications, 18 May 2021 attendance and exact procedural treatment still need to be obtained or closed out.

Do not pre-judge the result. A EUR 14.8m offer does not by itself prove that the third party met every requirement or should have received the adjudication. It does make the competitive process a material, verifiable control point.

Authorised transaction versus later implementation

Offer / Plan · 2018

The working documentary record reconstructs a dación of mortgaged properties, a separate EUR 400,000 sale of non-mortgaged assets and a waiver clause whose exact wording must be tied back to the original.

Definitive authorisation · 2021

The 2022 appeals reproduce the 18 May 2021 order as approving the proposal contained in the plan: apartments by dación in payment of privileged credits and premises/pools by a separate EUR 400,000 direct sale.

Deed · 21 February 2022

Current evidence status: later party reconstructions attribute EUR 13,168,082.02 to the deed as “value of consideration”. The exact binary/certified deed, full protocol and clause remain priority primary evidence before that characterisation is presented as verified.

The identity question. If a number born as a condition for a third party later appears with a different economic or extinguishing function, the act producing that change must be identified. Numerical coincidence is not a substitute for the legal bridge.

What remains needed to close the chain

  • 21 February 2022 deed: complete/certified copy, protocol, annexes and exact clauses on consideration, set-off, extinction and cancellation.
  • Definitive orders: signed originals of 18 May 2021, 15 October 2021 and 26 January 2022, with service and finality.
  • 2021 and 2022 testimonios: full contents and notarial/Registry use; the 325/646-page counts are not published as fact unless verified.
  • Interest: CAM certificate/calculation taking EUR 3,079,104.66 to the EUR 3,182,000 cap, including days, rate, service and challenge.
  • Third-party bidder: procedural filing, authority/funds evidence and the minute or recording of the 18 May 2021 licitation.
  • EUR 400,000: bank evidence, ledger, effect of the 2019 non-validation, any refund/retention/set-off and final treatment.
  • Waiver: exact original clause and any later act preserving, modifying or extinguishing it.
  • Final accounts: complete bridge from credit and interest through dación, payments, cancellations and any residual.

How this strengthens the position without overstating it

1. It changes the centre of gravity

From “were the interest figures miscalculated?” to “was exactly the authorised transaction implemented, with the same legal function for each figure?”. That is a more durable documentary question.

2. It adds a real market-control point

The anonymous third-party EUR 14.8m proposal allows the published threshold, actual competition, outcome and estate benefit to be compared without turning the bidder into a public protagonist.

3. It makes the thesis falsifiable

The page states which documents could confirm or refute a mismatch: orders, testimonios, deed, interest calculation, bank records, Registry and final accounts. Any actor can therefore supply the missing bridge.

Proportionate relief. First reconstruct the accounts and correspondence between judicial title and implementation. Only then can counsel assess accounting correction, integration of a proven surplus, partial ineffectiveness, retroaction or another remedy.
