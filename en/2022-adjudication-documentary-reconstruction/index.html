The central issue is no longer a recalculation of interest: it is whether the transaction implemented retained the identity of the transaction authorised

The signed 25 January 2021 order, the 29 January court notice and deed no. 457 dated 21 February 2022 now allow recognised insolvency credit, mortgage liability, the threshold imposed on a third party, the debt stated as consideration for the dación and the separate sale of non-mortgaged assets to be compared directly. The decisive issue is the documentary bridge between each function.

Three primary sources materially sharpen the analysis

EUR 3,079,104.66 → EUR 3,182,000

the EUR 102,895.34 movement has a documentary explanation

The signed 25 January 2021 order reproduces the Provincial Court default-interest amount and later daily accrual until the mortgage-liability cap was reached. This explains the arithmetic movement; it does not by itself determine its classification as finally recognised insolvency credit.

EUR 13,168,082.02

the same figure appears in two different contexts

The 29 January 2021 notice derives it as the sum of the two mortgage components required from a third-party better bidder. Deed no. 457 later uses EUR 13,168,082.02 as the debt serving as consideration for the dación. The numerical identity is now verified; the legal bridge between those functions and the lawfully set-off insolvency credit remains to be demonstrated.

EUR 400,000

the premises line remains separate

The notice separately states EUR 400,000 for assets outside the special privilege. In the reviewed operative and judicial-recap portions of deed no. 457, the dación concerns the mortgaged properties; those sections do not expressly re-execute the premises/pools sale or reconcile the 2018 cash payment.

EUR 14,713,880.31

quantified conditions for a third party

This is the sum of both mortgage components, the EUR 400,000 and EUR 1,145,798.29 in community fees, before then-unquantified taxes and costs. It must not automatically be converted into CAM's price or recognised credit.

EUR 14.8m

the competition ceased to be hypothetical

A third-party bidder documented an acquisition proposal dated 8 February 2021 for EUR 14.8 million covering the identified property perimeter. Only the bidder's name is anonymised in the public record; the bid, its terms and every related fact remain visible. Procedural filing, authority/funds and treatment at the licitation still require documentary closure.

5 calendar days

the deed creates a verifiable downstream trail

Deed no. 457 requires the insolvency administrator to communicate it to the Mercantile Court within five calendar days and sets out cancellation requests. The next control point is the actual court filing, order/mandamiento and Registry implementation.

The competition was real; its outcome still needs reconstruction

A stronger control point. The court notice did not open a merely theoretical possibility: a third-party bidder documented a EUR 14.8m proposal. That permits an objective inquiry into how the proposal was tested against the published conditions and why the process ultimately ended with CAM's proposal.

The website anonymises only the third-party bidder's name; the bid, its terms, procedural treatment and every related fact remain visible. For the relevant insolvency question, its procedural and economic function is enough: a EUR 14.8m proposal existed against then-quantified components totalling EUR 14,713,880.31, before additional taxes and costs that were not yet quantified. The signed 18 May 2021 Auto establishes the procedural outcome—nonappearance and nondeposit followed by definitive approval of CAM's proposal—but not the contents of the missing appearance record or every downstream condition. The third party's court filing, authority, proof of funds, judicial communications and the appearance minute/recording remain to be closed.

Do not pre-judge the result. A EUR 14.8m offer does not by itself prove that the third party met every requirement or should have received the adjudication. It does make the competitive process a material, verifiable control point.

Authorised transaction versus notarial implementation

Offer / Plan · 2018

The documentary record reconstructs a dación of mortgaged properties, a separate EUR 400,000 sale of non-mortgaged assets and a waiver clause whose wording and effect must be reconciled against the later acts.

Definitive authorisation · 2021

The complete signed five-page E044 / Auto 164/2021 of 18 May 2021 definitively approved CAM's proposal after the third-party bidder's nonappearance and nondeposit. That approval remained subject to finality, lawful debt, deed, EUR 400,000, costs, reporting, cancellation, Registry and accounting steps that must be proved separately.

Deed no. 457 · 21 February 2022

Primary source recovered: 76 pages. The deed transfers the described mortgaged properties to CAM and states EUR 13,168,082.02 as the debt serving as consideration for the dación. It also requires communication to the court within five days and sets out downstream cancellation requests. The legal correctness of the amount and actual Registry/accounting consequences must be proved separately.

The identity question is now more concrete. It is no longer disputed whether EUR 13,168,082.02 appears in the deed: it does. The issue is which title turns the competitive-threshold figure into debt legally usable as consideration for the dación, how that reconciles with the definitive texts and what consequence followed for the estate.

The deed also separates what it proves from what remains open

It proves

The deed's identity, protocol 457, date, appearances, conveyance of the described properties, stated debt consideration and the communication/cancellation obligations and requests.

It does not prove by itself

That every credit component was legally correct and finally privileged, that community/tax obligations were actually paid, that the Registry cancelled exactly as requested or that the estate had no residual.

Its existence does not automatically cure

The separate 2018 premises/pools transaction that was not judicially validated in 2019. The later title, entry and economic treatment that may have regularised that line must be identified.

How this strengthens the position without overstating it

1. It replaces an inference with a notarial fact

EUR 13,168,082.02 no longer depends on a later summary: the deed verifies it as debt serving as consideration for the dación. That makes the title-and-classification question materially more concrete.

2. It adds a real market-control point

The EUR 14.8m third-party proposal, with only the bidder's name anonymised, allows the published threshold, actual competition, outcome and estate benefit to be compared without turning the bidder into a public protagonist.

3. It separates order from implementation

The existence of an order, deed or Registry entry is not used as an automatic substitute for the next evidential link. The chain is tested act by act and property by property.

Proportionate relief. First reconstruct title, debt, accounts and the correspondence between authorisation and implementation. Only then can counsel assess accounting correction, integration of a proven residual, partial ineffectiveness, retroaction or another remedy.
