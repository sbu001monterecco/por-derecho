STANDING NOTICE · VERSION 20 AUGUST 2026

The source of money and professional services is part of the evidence too.

This notice sets a public standard for verification, preservation and traceability for conduct connected with the assets, rights, works, public support, financing, income and proceedings documented by Project Sun Rock / Por Derecho.

Before acting: verify · preserve · document · decline or escalate where appropriate.

Who this notice addresses

According to their actual function, it addresses funders, investors, beneficiaries, operators, administrators, purchasers, transferors, insurers, auditors, lawyers, tax advisers, consultants, experts, valuers, intermediaries, payment institutions, custodians and bodies granting, supervising or certifying public support or funds.

Money and value

Finance, investment, grants, subsidies, RIC, ERDF, operating income, professional fees, guarantees, insurance, fruits, surplus and transfers of value.

Assets and authority

Properties, third-party rights, insolvency-estate assets, possession, works, licences, contracts, community resolutions, deeds, judicial certificates, registrations and powers.

Professional services

Mandate, client identity, beneficial ownership, counterparties, conflicts, independence, confidentiality, scope, supervision, billing and files.

Public or regulated decision

Eligibility, expenditure, aid cumulation, employment, valuations, due diligence, conditions, waivers, verification and audit trail.

Six minimum checks

1

Identity and beneficial ownership

Who instructs, who pays, who receives, who controls and who obtains the final economic benefit.

2

Title and authority

Which asset or right is affected, who owns it, who may consent and which contemporaneous document authorises the act.

3

Source, destination and purpose

Where each euro comes from, what it funds, which account and contract support it and which asset, work, invoice or job it finances.

4

No overlap

Whether the same asset, cost, invoice, job or value was presented to more than one funder, fund, authority or instrument, and under which cumulation rule.

5

Mandate and conflicts

Which professional entity acted, in what capacity and under which former-client, confidentiality, independence and information-barrier controls.

6

Preservation and escalation

Preserve files, metadata, communications, conflict searches, KYC, approvals, waivers, invoices and records; stop or escalate when source or authority is unresolved.

What this notice does not allege

Providing services, receiving or transferring funds, granting public support, operating an asset or appearing in this record does not prove wrongdoing, knowledge, participation or responsibility. This notice does not replace individual notice, a legal demand, a complaint, a judicial decision or an audit conclusion. Nor does it turn a discrepancy into misconduct.

Any attribution requires individual evidence of duty or power, knowledge, act or omission, causation, loss, applicable rule and remedy. A complete documentary explanation may confirm, qualify or resolve a concern.

Right of response, correction and documentation

Any affected person or entity may identify a factual error, provide a primary source, explain its role or perimeter, document controls applied and request a correction. Verifiable material corrections should receive equivalent visibility and remain preserved under version control.
