What was sent. What came back. What remains.

Fourteen core records consolidate key communications and bounded technical-record references concerning public bodies, judicial-administration routes and statutory professional corporations shown or discussed in the accountability architecture. Each separates competence, traceable events, evidential limits and one finite outstanding action. A professional-regulation supplement updated to 25 August 2026 maps ICAM, CCACM, ICALPA and ICATF without merging their files or competences.

How to read the register

An institutional mark now points to this evidence record—not to an implied relationship.

Registration, receipt, rejection, referral, opening, closure and appeal are different procedural states. A filing proves what was submitted and when; a response proves only what it says. Neither volume nor silence establishes the merits. Public bodies and statutory professional corporations are classified by their distinct competence, not treated as interchangeable.
