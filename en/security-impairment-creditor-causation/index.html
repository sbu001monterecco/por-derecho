Security, impairment and causation

From formal security to net enforceable position. This framework avoids two opposite errors: saying the mortgage automatically disappears when the asset deteriorates, or treating formal survival as if it neutralised damage attributable to the creditor itself or to actors whose conduct can legally be attributed to it.

Control rule

A surviving security right and an unimpaired enforceable position are not the same proposition. First identify the real security right and holder; then prove challenged conduct actor by actor; then quantify what was impaired; only then assess effects on damages, good faith, compensation, enforcement, the insolvency estate, the funded exit and, where applicable, criminal responsibility.

Two planes that must not be collapsed

REAL SECURITY / PROPERTY

Registered mortgage, properties or units, real-estate value and legally applicable extensions.

OPERATING / FINANCING VALUE

Access, operation, customers, bookings, revenue, contracts, reputation, DD, valuation, replacement security and closing capacity.

BRIDGE

They are legally distinct but may be economically interdependent. That relationship must be proved, not presumed.

Evidence matrix

QuestionEvidence neededLimit
What security existed?Title, registration, debt, holder and date.Debt ≠ ownership; mortgage ≠ operation.
Who did what?Act, actor, capacity, instructions, mandate/control and attribution.Relationship or benefit ≠ attribution.
What was impaired?Before/after condition, operation, revenue, access, value, DD and replacement security.The hotel business is not automatically the legal object of the mortgage.
What caused what?Condition-by-condition closing evidence, lender decision and contrary explanations.Chronology ≠ causation.
What is the net position?Debt, value, damages, fruits/revenue, compensation, restitution and enforcement effects.There is no single answer without the legal route and proved facts.
Is there a criminal consequence?Offence, conduct, authorship/participation, intent or negligence where applicable, damage and causation.Criminal allegation ≠ criminal finding.

Primary legal basis

Mortgage Law arts. 109–110: extension of the mortgage to improvements and indemnities on the statutory terms. Art. 117: legal significance of deterioration making security insufficient; that statutory case concerns owner-caused deterioration and does not mechanically govern creditor conduct. Civil Code art. 7: good faith and prohibition of abuse of rights, including damages and measures preventing persistence of abuse. Criminal Code art. 263: a criminal gate for damage to another's property when all elements are proved. None of these rules by itself turns the Sun Park hypothesis into a conclusion.

Falsifiable Sun Park hypothesis

existing creditor/security → legally attributable conduct → impairment of property or the operating/financing package → lower value or verifiability → DD/replacement security/closing affected → later position relied upon by the creditor. Every arrow needs its own source.

The hypothesis weakens or fails if there is an independent lender decision predating the impairment, evidence that access and operation were not material to closing, independent insufficiency of replacement security, a non-closing cause unrelated to the challenged conduct, or no legal attribution to the relevant creditor.
