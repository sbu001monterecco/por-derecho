Security, impairment and causation.

A mortgage may formally subsist while leaving open what a creditor can netly enforce if conduct legally attributable to it is proved to have caused or contributed to impairment of the asset, the operating business or replacement financing.

Two objects that must not be collapsed

Real security / property and operating / financing value are not legally identical. In Sun Park the second category may include access, the CEXP/operating structure, bookings, revenue, contracts, reputation, due diligence, valuation and the ability to offer security to an incoming financier. Economic importance to the hotel does not by itself make an item mortgaged property.

The framework therefore requires separate proof of: formal right → act → attribution → impairment → causation → quantum → legal consequence.

What the statutes say—and do not say

RuleRole in the analysisLimit
Mortgage Law 109–110Extends the mortgage to accessions, improvements and specified indemnities.Does not make the whole hotel business mortgaged property or decide liability for impairment.
Mortgage Law 117Shows that loss of property value can trigger protection of the security.Its case is deterioration through the owner's fraud, fault or will; it is not the specific rule for creditor-caused impairment.
Civil Code 7Good faith and abuse of rights; where conditions are met, compensation and measures against persistence of abuse.Does not presume abuse from exercising a security right.
Civil Code 1101 / 1902Potential contractual or tort liability bases where their respective elements are proved.The relevant obligation/conduct, fault or intent where required, damage and causation must be established.
Civil Code 1195–1196 / TRLC 153Set-off requires concrete conditions and insolvency imposes its own limits.A damages claim does not automatically reduce a secured claim.
Criminal Code 263Criminal gate for damage to another's property when its elements are met.Does not itself prove damage, authorship, intent, attribution or liability in Sun Park.

Falsifiable application to Sun Park

Gil Marer's attributed hypothesis is that the sequence should be tested as one system: existing creditor/security → alleged material-control change → access/operation affected → asset or operating value damaged or de-rated → weaker DD, valuation or replacement security → funded exit made materially harder → later reliance on non-completion, deficiency or reduced value. Chronology alone does not prove that chain.

The hypothesis weakens or fails if sources show, for example, that financing would have failed for an independent reason, the financier did not depend on the affected access/value/security, a no-close decision predated the impairment, the asset was already insufficient for unrelated reasons, or the relevant conduct was not legally attributable to the creditor invoked.
