Provincial Public Prosecution Office of Las Palmas · DI 273/2013 → DP 332/2014

An official accusation does not turn its hypothesis into truth.

It is documented that prosecutor Tomás Fernández de Páiz signed a criminal complaint against Gil Marer and Patricia Domínguez on 27 December 2013. Gil alleges that the act adopted a frame supplied by the insolvency administrator and shifted onto the incoming perimeter an economic and operating crisis formed partly before December 2011. This page tests—and limits—that allegation proposition by proposition.

Controlled conclusion

The supportable allegation is causal inversion and defective temporal attribution—not “everything happened before us.”

Gil alleges that the complaint criminalised an inherited emergency and privileged the insolvency administrator's frame without a visible reconciliation by date, actor, capacity and knowledge. The located record warrants independent audit of that allegation. It does not by itself prove knowing falsity, capture, collusion or an offence by an individual prosecutor.
What the record supports

Inherited frame + suspicion directed at the incoming perimeter

The complaint incorporates 2008–2011 ownership, sales, debt and Community/operation conflict, but centres suspicion on the 6 February 2012 agreement and alleged later non-payment/non-collection.

Necessary correction

The central operative events did not all predate arrival

The agreement, operator transition and disputed collection conduct occurred after arrival. They require separate examination; concealing that would weaken, not strengthen, the challenge.

Outcome and applicable law

Law 2/2023 · art. 36.2

It does not retroactively turn the 2013 complaint into retaliation

For later acts, protected-informant status, a qualifying report/disclosure, disadvantage and causal connection must each be proved. This page does not automatically attribute liability under that law to any prosecutor.

Official framework: Organic Statute of the Public Prosecution Service · Law 2/2023. The links verify the framework; characterising individuals requires the file and actor-specific proof.
