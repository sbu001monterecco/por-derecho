Holding 001

Inspection and preservation

Partially allowed reconsideration only to replace the reasoning; maintained denial of filing 7,299. The court could not identify ownership of photographed interiors.

Adverse, limited reasoning: it regarded common-area rubble as causing no damage beyond a removable aesthetic effect, did not attribute the start of common-area works to CAM, and found the sale delay not attributable to the court or AC.

Not established: absence of damage throughout the estate, a complete property map or the result of a later responsibility claim.
