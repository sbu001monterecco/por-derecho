OPEN CALL · WHOLE-CLAIM CONTROL · 9 SEPTEMBER 2026

Cuatrecasas: come on the record.

Project Sun Rock invites Cuatrecasas Legal, S.L.P. to state publicly, clearly and documentably whether it has used, is using, or intends to use the four Matkator promissory notes, Cambiario 1048/2019, ETJ 163/2020, any adjudication, remate cession, assignment or related recovery instrument to obtain or transfer economic value or control over the Aweswell/Sun Rock investment perimeter for the benefit of an adverse party, a connected person or any undisclosed third party.

Our allegation, not a finding. We allege that the instrument-and-recovery architecture may have created a non-transparent side door into the former-client investment perimeter through Matkator—a route we describe metaphorically as a potential Trojan-horse mechanism. Work principally documented to Aweswell/LPB later appears in four Matkator pagarés and judicial enforcement against Matkator, while Cuatrecasas has expressly sought the faculty to cede the remate to a third party. We have not proved a clandestine agreement, an improper assignee, misuse of confidential information, a common plan with Acosta Matos/CAM/HNT, procedural fraud or any criminal offence. Cuatrecasas is invited to deny, correct, explain or document each proposition.
Why this matters. The documented rescue work placed Cuatrecasas on the Aweswell/LPB side of a lawful alternative to the adverse control-and-consolidation route alleged by Project Sun Rock. Our position is that later affirmative enforcement against Matkator may represent a progressive inversion of that protective purpose. The exact legal scope of each retainer remains matter-specific; a fee claim, pagaré or enforcement act is not improper merely because it is adverse to a former client.

The statement we invite Cuatrecasas to make

If accurate, Cuatrecasas can put the following on the record:

They may adopt it, qualify it, reject it, or replace it with their own precise statement. Silence is not treated as guilt.

This page therefore describes Matkator as a separate company within the investment perimeter and does not assume an unproved parent/subsidiary relationship.

Important legal boundary

The absence of a located Matkator invoice or engagement does not by itself defeat a pagaré or prove misattribution: an autonomous instrument, guarantee, assumption or other valid accommodation may create liability. The repository therefore asks for the instrument cause, authority and accounting bridge rather than assuming the answer.

Collection correction: what the sources do and do not permit us to say

Superseded proposition: “Cuatrecasas never demanded Aweswell.” Current emails contradict that categorical statement. Cuatrecasas pursued payment/settlement with Gil/Aweswell and chased LPB invoices.
Still open: this review has not located a separate judicial action against Aweswell in the UK or Spain for the same fee universe, a distinct formal demand served on the hotel as an operating unit, or proof that Cuatrecasas lodged/proved its own fee claim as creditor in Concurso 36/2012. Those are evidence gaps, not proof of non-existence.

Concurso 36/2012: presence is verified; creditor proof is not

Cuatrecasas was plainly present in the LPB insolvency as counsel for the Aweswell/LPB side and filed/handled insolvency work. In May 2020 it withdrew from Aweswell representation because of unpaid fees. The present controlled search has not located proof that Cuatrecasas itself communicated, proved or sought recognition of its own fee credit as a creditor in Concurso 36/2012. The correct repository question is therefore whether such a creditor filing existed and, if so, how it reconciles with the later two-claim/pagaré structure.
