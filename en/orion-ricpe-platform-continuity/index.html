ARCHITECTURE CLARIFICATION — DISTINCT BRANCHES, DOCUMENTED CONNECTIONS

The analysis distinguishes two interconnected branches: the Sun Park/MYND asset, title, financing, public-support and operating branch, and the later Orion formation, capital, financing, management, related-party and market-admission branch.

They concern distinct legal persons, assets and transactions. They must not be presented as unconnected: the record documents RICPE’s founding and financing relationship with Orion, AGM’s management role, and ownership and governance connections involving Pamalexsha and the Acosta Matos perimeter.

These connections belong within the unitary analysis. They do not, without further evidence, establish that Sun Park assets, income, financing or public support flowed into Orion. Any such transfer, guarantee, collateral use or other economic benefit must be traced specifically.

Orion’s incorporation in June 2023 prevents attributing earlier Sun Park acts to Orion itself. It does not erase the earlier roles of individuals or entities subsequently connected to it. Any allegation concerning concealment, conflicts, misleading disclosure or enabling conduct must be tested against each actor’s knowledge, duties, decisions and conduct, not inferred automatically from association.

Actor-specific inquiry: FMMM within the connected branches

How did FMMM enter the Pamalexsha/AGM/Orion perimeter after acting as a paid property administrator in the Sun Park/Community environment?

FMMM’s professional trajectory remains a specific inquiry within the broader documented RICPE, AGM, Pamalexsha and Acosta Matos connections. Each actor’s mandate, knowledge, duties and conduct must be reconstructed separately; personal association alone does not prove coordination or liability:

Role and remuneration

Appointment, contract, period, fees, powers, document custody and services performed for Sun Park/Community.

Duties and conduct

What he had to advise, warn, disclose and protect; what he knew about ownership, insolvency, Community, security, works, finance and conflicts; what he actually did.

Later transition

When the role ended, how he entered Pamalexsha/AGM/Orion, who introduced or appointed him and whether the chronology bears on independence, incentives or conflicts.

Professional formulation: determine whether FMMM adequately discharged his paid duties of advice, diligence, warning, information and protection in relation to Sun Park, and establish how and under what circumstances he moved into the later perimeter. “Failed miserably” is not used as a substitute for proof.
