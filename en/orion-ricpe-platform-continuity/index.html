PLATFORM → NEW VEHICLE · CONTINUITY ≠ MONEY FLOW

Orion Rental SOCIMI is not unrelated to RICPE: RICPE incorporated it. That does not prove Sun Park or MYND funded it.

This page fixes an essential distinction. The controlled 2023 material places RIC Private Equity Investment Partners, SCR, S.A. as the founder of Orion Rental SOCIMI. That relationship establishes institutional platform continuity. It does not, by itself, establish a transfer of revenue, public support, financing or value from Sun Park / MYND Yaiza.

CONTROLLED POSITION · 15 AUG 2026

Supported in the corpus: RICPE incorporated Orion on 16 June 2023 with €60,000 and appears initially as sole shareholder; after a later 2023 capital increase its interest fell below 1% according to the controlled audited material.

Not established: that MYND Yaiza revenue, Series F/G financing, GC/836/P06 support, EU-cofinanced expenditure or disputed asset value funded Orion.

What “platform continuity” means

Platform continuity can exist where a later vehicle reuses or evolves existing infrastructure: investors, governance, professionals, RIC expertise, fundraising processes, controls, financing channels, advice or institutional track record. That continuity can be entirely lawful.

Public question: what part of RICPE's capital relationships, investor base, governance, advisers, management, controls, RIC experience and institutional credibility carried into Orion, and what records demonstrate that Orion's funds and security were independent of Sun Park/MYND?

Why Orion matters to the RICPE investigation

RICPE cannot be understood only as a vehicle that funded isolated hotel projects and stopped there. Its own controlled material presents Orion as a later extension of its activity. That makes it relevant to reconstruct whether the same gatekeepers, investors, conflict procedures, advisers and capital channels were reused.

The relevance is one of governance, institutional continuity and financial traceability. It does not make Orion the beneficiary of any earlier alleged wrongdoing and does not automatically project 2023 relationships backwards into 2018–2022.

Three explanations must remain open

A · Independent later project

Orion may be economically independent of Sun Park/MYND despite having RICPE as founder.

B · Legitimate platform reuse

It may share know-how, investors, professionals or RIC infrastructure without receiving MYND value.

C · Identifiable value bridge

A loan, guarantee, dividend, fee, refinancing or transfer may exist. This remains an investigative hypothesis until primary evidence proves it.

What this page does not establish

It does not establish conspiracy, fraud, diversion of grants, double funding, EU-funds use in Orion, money laundering, misappropriation, automatic common control or an earlier common plan. Nor do later family, professional or corporate relationships retrospectively prove joint action at Sun Park.

The pressure is documentary: RICPE founded Orion. Produce the capital, governance, management and financing chain showing what continuity existed and what continuity did not.
