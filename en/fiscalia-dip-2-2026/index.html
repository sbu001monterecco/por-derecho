The documentary contradiction

What the Decree said, and what the court record already showed.

The highlighted Spanish phrases are the precise core of the error. The comparison carefully distinguishes filing an appeal from its admission and eventual determination.

DIP 2/2026 Decree · 2 MAR check

Fiscalía's wording

«También se ha comprobado que contra el auto resolutorio de ambas reposiciones no consta interpuesto recurso alguno —al menos, hasta la fecha de la consulta (02/03/2026)—.»

Translation: “It has also been verified that no appeal is recorded as having been filed against the order deciding both applications for reconsideration—at least as of the date checked (2 March 2026).” The Spanish original controls.

«La discrepancia del denunciante con dicha resolución, que, insistimos, habrá devenido firme al no haber hecho uso oportunamente del recurso de apelación, no puede servir de base para considerar que estemos ante una prevaricación judicial.»

Translation: the Decree said the decision would have become final because the right of appeal had not been exercised in time. The Spanish original controls.

The second statement appears immediately before the conclusion that no offence was established and the proceedings were closed. It was therefore not a purely incidental observation.

Court record predating 2 MAR

What was already officially recorded

28 January 2026 · Las Palmas Provincial Court, Fourth Section · RPL 3319/2025. The order directed: “With the foregoing appeal filing, form and register the appeal file…”, identifying Aweswell Limited as appellant.

18 February 2026 · Mercantile Court. The order recorded receipt of the “communication of the filing of an appeal against the order dated 11/11/2025” and directed electronic transfer of the proceedings to the Fourth Section.

Limited conclusion: before 2 March, an appeal had been filed, registered and was being processed. That does not prejudge final admission, outcome or merits; it does contradict the statement that no appeal was recorded as filed.

Contextual explanation

What it establishes and why it matters

  1. The appeal predates the closure. It did not come into existence with the 11 March communication: the court had documented it on 28 January and 18 February.
  2. Filing is not outcome. The records establish that the appeal was filed, registered and processed; they do not decide final admissibility or how the appeal should be determined.
  3. The Decree's wording was materially inaccurate. It did not say admissibility was uncertain: it said no appeal was recorded as filed and inferred finality from failure to appeal.
  4. The premise formed part of the closure reasoning. Correcting it required an express assessment of whether it altered the conclusion, even if other reasoning might independently support that conclusion.
  5. The 11 March registration proves institutional notice. It shows that Fiscalía formally received the update; by itself it does not establish who read it, whether it was placed on the file, or whether any correction or reconsideration followed.
  6. The error does not prove intent. The objective contradiction warrants quality control and traceability; it does not by itself prove bias, prosecutorial misconduct, collusion or external influence.

Evidential limits

A firm correction that stays within the evidence.

The record supports stating

That by 2 March the appeal was already documented in official court records; that the Decree stated otherwise; that this premise contributed to the closure reasoning; and that the contradiction was formally communicated on 11 March.

The record alone does not support stating

That the error was deliberate; that the signing prosecutor previously knew every court document; that a particular individual read the communication; that the appeal had to succeed; or that corruption or external influence has been proved.

Editorial rule: contemporary document before inference; an explicit distinction between filing, admission and outcome; and a right of reply for any person or institution providing authentic material capable of completing or correcting this sequence.
