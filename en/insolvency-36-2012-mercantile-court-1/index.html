Three levels of knowledge

1 · Direct judicial

Filed submissions, hearings, rulings reproducing allegations, reports and communications formally incorporated into the case.

2 · Administrator / reporting duty

Facts known or verifiable by the Insolvency Administration are not automatically attributed to the judge. The record should identify when they were reported or when the risk later reached the court directly.

3 · External/later

RICPE, CNMV, funds, tax and later operation may show continuity or consequence, but not historic judicial knowledge without proven incorporation.

Responsibility rule

An omission by itself does not establish a criminal offence. Its potential relevance lies in how earlier knowledge may contextualise specific later rulings and their consequences.

Master question: for each material risk, which identifiable judicial act connected knowledge to effective control of the Insolvency Administration and protection of the asset before the next difficult-to-reverse step?

The insolvency perimeter is itself a supervision test

LPB was the debtor declared in Insolvency 36/2012; that declaration did not make the whole Sun Park complex insolvent or place it in the estate. The proceeding arose from LPB’s voluntary filing under asserted cash-flow and imminent-enforcement pressure. It concerned LPB’s estate and did not by itself determine the solvency, ownership or operating status of CEXP, Matkator, Aweswell, other owners or the hotel as a whole. The Court had to protect and liquidate LPB property without creating title by spillover over independent rights. The institutional question is twofold: whether what was inside the estate was sufficiently protected and whether insolvency-originated acts produced material effects outside it without equivalent title. Institutional defence preserved

The judge could lawfully disagree with LPB/Aweswell, rely on the Administrator, consider a measure unnecessary, be constrained by an appeal, or conclude that a third party failed objective requirements. A reconciling ruling or record may exist outside the corpus. The decisive source is the certified case file, not the absence of a document from Por Derecho's archive.

Strict separation of routes

DP 1956/2026 is not the forum in which Por Derecho seeks to determine any eventual responsibility of the Mercantile judge. That route concerns the Insolvency Administrator's own acts, omissions, premises, implementation and accounting. The potential criminal significance of specified judicial rulings and conduct was raised through a separate complaint presented on 18 June 2026 and its later amplification, registered or linked by the Decanato under daily reference no. 24; that reference is not a NIG, Diligencias Previas number or formal case number.

Separately, DI 169/2026 and CGPJ Appeal 286/2026 form the institutional/supervisory route. The Permanent Commission/Inspection may consider court functioning and institutional handling; jurisdictional appeals review rulings; LAJ/court-office acts have their own perimeter; the Public Prosecution/TSJ route alone can assess criminal relevance. No route proves another route's hypothesis merely by existing.

Interface rule: separate procedures; distinct objects and legal standards; connected knowledge; limited common evidence; coordinated preservation; individual attribution; no automatic joinder, collective culpability or waiver. A judicial ruling does not by itself absorb the Administrator's own factual inputs, figures, recommendations, implementation or accounting; the Administrator's conduct does not predetermine judicial responsibility.
