Mercantile Court No. 1: distinguish what was known, what was decided, and what lay outside each ruling.

The review is directed at the court and proceeding, not at personalizing an accusation against an individual judge. Names are used only where necessary to attribute a signed judicial act.

26 June 2018: a verifiable knowledge point

The order in Insolvency 36/2012 records LPB's appeal against approval of the liquidation plan and the request to suspend realization of immovable assets. It reasons that, if the appeal succeeded, the conditions of the transfer in payment to Construcciones Acosta Matos S.A. for the listed properties would be substantially different, and therefore suspends realization of those properties. The remaining rulings stayed unchanged and liquidation could continue for the other estate assets.

The order is signed by Alberto López Villarrubia as Judge. That supports attribution of the specific judicial act; it does not support an inference that every external fact was known or within that order's scope.

24 October 2019: later judicial control

The primary order identified in the record concludes that a specific ob rem unlinking and sale had been carried out without the required prior judicial authorization and refuses later authorization or validation. That is material to reconstructing the Court's control capacity, but it is not by itself a criminal finding and does not automatically determine every downstream consequence.

Institutional questions the record should answer

  1. Which documents on title, asset perimeter, Matkator, RIC, financing and exploitation were actually in the court record before each 2020–2022 decision?
  2. Which later ruling reconciled the 2019 non-validation decision with any subsequent act materially dependent on that situation?
  3. Which issues were expressly raised and decided, which were rejected on procedural grounds, and which were outside scope?
  4. What protective measures were legally available before steps with difficult-to-reverse consequences, and which were used?
  5. What complete record was transmitted to the Provincial Court for each appeal?

Reasonable defence preserved

The Court decides matters that procedurally come before it within the insolvency case and is not required in every order to determine all collateral civil, criminal, corporate or third-party disputes. An unresolved issue cannot fairly be labelled ignored unless it is shown to have been properly raised, documented and within the decision's scope.

What this page does not establish

It does not establish judicial misconduct, corruption, deliberate bias or collusion. Its purpose is narrower: traceability between document, judicial knowledge, competence, ruling and consequence.
