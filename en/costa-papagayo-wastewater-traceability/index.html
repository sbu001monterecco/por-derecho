COSTA PAPAGAYO · SANITATION · DOCUMENTARY TRACEABILITY

An infrastructure question that needs the complete record.

The Costa Papagayo wastewater treatment plant appears in public administrative sources. What still needs to be verified is the documentary chain: title, rights, contributions, operating duties, and the relationship of any entity or unit to that infrastructure.

The precise question

What do the documents say about the wastewater plant and who held which right or duty?

The answer cannot come from an isolated reference, a commercial name or a party’s assertion. It requires the reconciliation of title, planning and water records, agreements, any urban conservation entity where relevant, and operational and cost records.

What must stay separate.

Infrastructure, land title, accommodation units, economic contributions, operation and maintenance responsibility may or may not align. Each category requires its own evidence.

  • Title or real right in the plant and its component elements.
  • Title to linked plots, registered properties or units.
  • Any contribution, levy, charge or economic right.
  • The person or entity authorised to represent a community or urban entity.
  • Operation, maintenance, connection and use of treated water.
  • The relationship of all of the above to separate administrative acts or proceedings.

Verification request

The responsible route is to request specific documents from each record holder.

The request should respect personal-data limits and legally protected secrecy. Where appropriate, a redacted version, index, certificate of existence, or identification of file and access route will be enough.
