Do not conflate amounts

Different numbers, different meanings

Loan principal, maximum mortgage liability, valuation, refurbishment investment, RIC investment, subsidy and total project finance are different magnitudes. This page does not claim that the project's wider €36.7m reconstruction is a CajaSiete loan.
