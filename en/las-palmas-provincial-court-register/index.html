Rule for new proceedings

When another Provincial Court proceeding is found, it will link to PD-SP-I-0044 as the navigation parent and to the exact section as competent organ only when the source identifies it. Where the section is not yet known, the known parent court is preserved and an express gap is opened; the specific organ is not guessed.
