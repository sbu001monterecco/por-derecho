Formal warning in 2021 and supervisory activity later acknowledged by CNMV. In 2026, supervision, infringement-reporting and public-information access remain separate channels.

No public sanction or intervention naming RICPE was located in the bounded public review. That does not prove the absence of confidential supervisory work.

Finite question: who approved the 2020 ownership / purchase / no-charges representations, what title evidence supported them, and what correction was made when RICPE’s own July 2021 record showed a materially different position?

Documents, not speculation

The tension is resolved by the native video, brochure versions, title/encumbrance records, minutes, due diligence, approvals and investor corrections.

Supervision ≠ public outcome

CNMV has acknowledged supervisory activity. The public question is what corrective or investor-protection outcome can be identified and lawfully disclosed.

Right of reply open

Boundaries: registration ≠ admission; acknowledged supervision ≠ sanction; documentary divergence ≠ intent; association ≠ responsibility; and a later outcome does not retrospectively prove the accuracy of an earlier representation.
