MATERIAL UPDATE · 27 AUGUST 2026

ONA: from the sabotage hypothesis to the institutional-protection question

The public reading no longer requires readers to assemble separate dossiers. The funded exit, 7 June, private attribution and institutional response are now presented as one auditable chain.

Limit: institutional collusion or a criminal organisation is not presumed. The 2018 provisional criminal dismissal and its confirmation on appeal remain mandatory adverse evidence. The new layer asks what was protected and investigated; absence of a particular step is not treated as proof of complicity.

The new chain

Private actors
what they did and knew
IA
what was protected or authorised
Court
what it knew and decided
Prosecution
what evidence was acquired and tested
Outcome
title, finance, refurbishment, MYND

The Montelanza/Molina → Community → CAM/Acosta Matos continuity is framed as functional, patrimonial and governance continuity to reconstruct, not inherited criminal liability.

The controlling question is now: if a funded alternative was materially developed, who knew of it, who acted on its critical dependencies, who had capacity to protect it, and which investigative steps were taken to prove or reject an unlawful displacement before the later economic outcome was consolidated?
