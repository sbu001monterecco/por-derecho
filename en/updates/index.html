NewUnitary title controlWebinar · OB REM · JSP

The “under study” label does not cure the on-camera claims; the OB REM chain and JSP units now operate as independent controls

11 November 2020 webinar: the dossier transcribes José Acosta’s statement that the three complexes had been bought with own funds, belonged to the company and were unencumbered, together with fixed Sun Park particulars: 220 apartments, €12m, works in Q1 2021 and funding by the end of 2021. The June brochure’s stage caveat was not carried into those categorical claims. RICPE’s July 2021 certificate —54 CAM units, LPB assets still in the insolvency estate, 18 third-party units, no LOI and no comprehensive due diligence— and the February 2022 LPB deed materially contradict them.

OB REM: the order of 24 October 2019 records that deed 2,150 separated and sold 31 LPB properties during the stay, without prior judicial authority, and that the Court did not validate the operation. Cancelling the registered linkage did not extinguish the loan: it removed the separate-sale barrier while CAM continued to enforce the claim.

JSP / Monte Lanza units: the reconstructed schedule of 61 properties is published and Sun Park’s own Judgment 89/2014 is applied. That judgment made the individual title of 18 owners prevail over possession, keys and tourist operation; for the same reason, Community authority, unitary operation or an LPB award could not reach a Matkator, Thompson or other third-party property without a valid unit-specific title. The 8,499/8,500 Registry jump is now framed as a standalone alarm with precise demands of the private actors, insolvency administrator, Court and Public Prosecutor.

Attributed public characterisation: Gil Marer reports the OB REM sequence and later use of “Community” authority as patrimonial displacement, disloyal administration and, if sufficient documentary or procedural deception is established, procedural fraud. It is an express criminal allegation requiring investigation; it is not presented as a criminal judgment.

NewProvenance and authorityCommunity · Cabildo · insolvency · RIC/ERDF

“The Community” no longer operates as a shortcut to authority

The main journey now contains a visual stop: a person signing as president or administrator, or a record attributed to an owner community, does not by itself prove a current appointment, resolution, quorum, mandate, scope over each unit or the owner’s consent. The chain no longer permits a jump from that label to whole-hotel control and then to adjudication, licence, tourism classification, RIC, ERDF, incentives, finance or income.

The documentary sequence separates three matters. Cabildo’s 10 March 2026 rejection did not determine title or authority. Its 7 April access resolution identified six tourism files concerning all units, holder/name changes, refurbishment and classification, but their existence does not validate the premises used. The 18 June communication formally demanded author, signature, office, mandate, exact community, units, verification, effect, funding, incentives and preservation; it establishes the claimant’s alert, not Cabildo acceptance or a merits decision.

Precise frame: the site does not say that every Community defect is automatically criminal. It alleges possible repeated propagation of absent, exceeded or unproved authority with proprietary and public effects. Every recipient must produce its documentary bridge and the check it performed.

NewLawWhistleblowers · judicial protection · insolvency

A protected report and a court filing are different; the duty to react remains verifiable

The review separates two legal planes. The 2021 filings entered the insolvency case, met opposition and were later held judicially irrelevant: there was a procedural response, not simple silence. That does not identify a substantive reconciliation of title, third-party units, promotion and estate risk, or locate any Article 40.1 LEC referral to the Public Prosecutor or Article 82 TRLC supervision report.

The current Law 2/2023 regime protects qualifying reports made with reasonable grounds to believe the information true, prohibits obstructing or delaying follow-up and protects public disclosure in defined circumstances, including a low prospect of effective treatment because an authority may be in collusion. Canary Islands Decree 91/2024 permits up to six months only for special complexity and requires immediate referral to the Spanish or European Public Prosecutor where appropriate. Failure to respond within that regime would be a verifiable procedural failure; it would not prove collusion by itself.
