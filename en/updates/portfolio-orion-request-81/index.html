Institutional status · 22 August 2026 · Portfolio Stock Exchange

ORION / Portfolio: Request 81.

Portfolio has procedurally acknowledged the new evidential submission concerning the existing ORION RENTAL SOCIMI whistleblowing matter. Its support/intake system created Request 81 and states that it is under review. This public record deliberately separates intake from any merits finding.

Confirmed by received communicationPortfolio's intake/support system created Request 81 and states that it is “under review”.
Not yet confirmedNo merits decision, formal reopening/extension, preservation confirmation, Board circulation, senior institutional owner or CNMV coordination has yet been confirmed.
Reading boundary. “Under review” is reported here only as an intake/procedural status. It does not mean Portfolio has accepted any allegation, formally reopened its earlier investigation, placed the matter before its Board, or reached any adverse finding against ORION or any person.

What was notified on 22 August

The communication was framed as new evidence within the existing protected file, not a disconnected new complaint. It asked Portfolio expressly to preserve the informant's dual capacity: a directly and indirectly economically affected party — including through Aweswell Limited, a UK-incorporated foreign investor in Spain — and, separately and cumulatively, an informant / alertador / whistleblower.

The submission asked for a documentary review of the material sufficiency of ORION disclosure: what information existed, who knew it, what reached ORION's Board and advisers, what reached Portfolio, what was communicated to investors, and what related-party, conflict, due-diligence and preservation controls were applied.

Patrimonial and cross-border dimension

Portfolio was expressly put on notice of the allegation of real patrimonial, business and cross-border harm affecting Aweswell Limited, its Spanish companies and the Sun Rock / Sun Park investment perimeter. This is the affected party's position to be determined through the competent processes; notification does not amount to Portfolio accepting it.

Institutional escalation requested

The submission asked that the material be brought to the full Board of Directors and to Legal, Compliance, Risk and senior management; that the 2024–2025 whistleblowing file and the ORION admission/disclosure file be preserved; that a senior institutional owner be identified; and that Portfolio determine what communication to shareholders, investors or international stakeholders is legally required or institutionally appropriate.

Current status: those steps have been requested but have not yet been confirmed.
