Related context · independent decisions

Two workstreams that must never be confused.

The historic controversy and the future investment discussion may both concern Lanzarote, but they require different evidence, teams, procedures and decisions. Neither should be used to purchase, delay or predetermine the other.

Workstream A · legacy resolution

Sun Park / MYND Yaiza: close a difficult chapter correctly

The Sun Park history is long, contested and supported by a substantial public evidential record. This letter does not ask either institution to accept our conclusions without examination. It asks for a practical route to complete the record, identify what each competent body can answer and resolve matters that documentary clarity can resolve.

Objective: resolution where possible; lawful continuation through the proper procedures where disagreement remains.

  • Create a file-by-file documentary matrix.
  • Distinguish verified facts, party assertions, inferences and open questions.
  • Preserve all proceedings and rights while pursuing good-faith resolution.

Workstream B · active future project

Montaña Roja: pursue the preferred route responsibly

Sun Rock is examining and actively pursuing Montaña Roja as its preferred current route for a new hotel in Playa Blanca. Due diligence still determines the complete transaction and rights perimeter, the current planning route, technical feasibility, project economics and the appropriate capital structure. No acquisition, planning approval, committed financing or construction decision is announced by this letter.

Current direction, subject to all conditions: a high-quality, non-residential hotel intended for unitary hotel operation, with only those complementary commercial, service and public-facing uses compatible with the hotel, the site and the applicable planning framework.

  • Complete and reconcile the private transaction and rights perimeter.
  • Confirm the ordinary planning, tourism, environmental and infrastructure route.
  • Prepare the technical, operating and capital case in parallel with the transaction.

Institutional safeguard

No exchange of past for future.

Clarity protects the institutions, the project parties and the public. The historic record and the future investment workstream must remain legally, evidentially and procedurally independent.

What this letter does not do. It does not offer future investment as consideration for the treatment of Sun Park/MYND Yaiza files. It does not make resolution of those files conditional on institutional support for Montaña Roja. It seeks no shortcut, waiver, predetermined conclusion or preferential treatment. The legacy route must depend on its own documents, facts, law and competent procedures; the future route must depend on its own acquisition, planning, tourism, environmental, technical, financial and public-interest merits.
Rights preserved. Nothing in this constructive invitation waives any allegation, defence, administrative request, appeal, proceeding, recovery right or request for evidence preservation. The detailed public record remains available on its existing pages.
