The institutional question

Will RSM independently reconstruct, preserve, test and explain the professional and organisational perimeter placed before its Committee?

The test of a sound review is not whether RSM agrees with the reporter. It is whether the record can show what was examined, what was excluded and why, what sources were preserved, how independence was managed and what reasoned outcome was reached.

What RSM has already done properly

Institutional credit where due. Francisco J. Remiro Hernández, Head of Ethics & Independence, engaged directly; expressly guaranteed confidentiality and discretion; directed the matter into the formal ethics channel; the case was identified as NNR4-1025C2F66; RSM later stated that the Committee was continuing to analyse the documentation and that the additional material supplied on 5 June was being considered; it also communicated a concrete expected window for conclusions.

Recognising those steps does not prejudge the result. It raises the standard for the next stage: a traceable conclusion that addresses the perimeter actually raised.

Six questions the outcome should be able to answer

Clarification requested

1. Preservation

Have relevant San Telmo/RSM legacy documents, correspondence, case files, conflict records and other materials needed to reconstruct matters within RSM's legitimate perimeter been preserved?

Clarification requested

2. Scope

Is the review limited to one person's formal status, or does it examine professional, organisational and information relationships where materially relevant?

Clarification requested

3. Independence

Is the review structurally separated from people or offices that may have had a connection to the matters being examined?

Clarification requested

4. Continuing effects

Does the review consider only historic conduct, or also representations, document custody or consequences that may have continued after integration?

Clarification requested

5. External cooperation

Will RSM preserve and provide relevant material in response to any lawful, properly framed request by a competent body — including, within their respective remits, ICALPA/DIP 80/2026, judicial or prosecution bodies connected with DP 1901/2026 and, if its status changes, DP 1956/2026, regulators such as CNMV, or competent public/EU-funds control functions?

Clarification requested

6. Professional-responsibility perimeter

If a professional-responsibility issue were ever established in relation to conduct within the historic professional environment under review, does RSM regard it as solely personal to the professional, within an RSM/legacy San Telmo entity, or belonging to another identifiable professional entity?

What is — and is not — being asked. RSM is not being asked to support the reporter's allegations or adopt the position of ICALPA, prosecutors, a court, CNMV or another body. The narrower question is: what is the correct professional perimeter, will evidence be preserved, and will the relevant entity cooperate with legitimate requests?

What a useful institutional decision could look like

A · SubstantiatedExplain facts, scope and remediation.
B · Partly substantiatedSeparate what is confirmed from what is not.
C · Not substantiatedExplain the basis and evidence used.
D · Outside perimeterIdentify the precise organisational or legal boundary and its basis.
E · Referral / cooperationIdentify any issue requiring treatment by another competent body.

The transparency rule: a conclusion may agree or disagree with the reporter. What should be avoided is replacing a traceable decision with silence or an unexplained boundary of competence.
