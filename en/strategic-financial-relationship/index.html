Strategic Financial Relationship

The objective is not to find one lender for one transaction. It is to build a first-class, long-term financial relationship around the Sponsor family, Aweswell Limited as the legal UK HoldCo/Sponsor, the Sun Rock hospitality and investment group/platform, and individually ring-fenced hotel ProjectCos—while keeping every pool of capital independently governed and underwritten.

Relationship architecture

Keep the relationship integrated. Keep the risks separable.

A sophisticated financial institution can understand the whole relationship without collapsing legal entities or credit risks into one another. The family layer, Sponsor/HoldCo, platform and ProjectCos can share a relationship while retaining the governance, security and underwriting discipline required for each mandate.

Three independent relationship cases

A bank should not need one thesis to justify the whole relationship.

The strength of the architecture is that each lane can make commercial sense on its own. A relationship chair can coordinate the institution; individual desks and capital pools still decide independently what they are authorised and willing to do.

Credit and governance discipline

A relationship is not a cross-guarantee.

Relationship depth should improve execution and information flow, not erase the legal and economic boundaries that make future assets bankable. The objective is coordinated coverage with disciplined underwriting.

Independent ProjectCos

Each hotel should be independently structured, governed and underwritten, with its own uses, sources, cash flows and security package.

No automatic cross-collateralisation

Avoid unnecessary guarantees, pledges or security links that could impair another ProjectCo's future financeability.

Future scale is not today's collateral

The €125–200m gross hotel asset scenario is context only. An early facility must stand on the issuer, covenants, repayment rights, controlled accounts and security actually granted at that time.

One institution, independent decisions

A private bank, corporate bank, real-estate desk, private-credit fund or co-investment vehicle may each reach a different decision. Relationship coordination does not imply universal investment approval.

Recovery remains separate

Sun Park recovery, damages, fruits and other contingent rights are not added to the public hotel asset range and are not ordinary Montaña Roja debt-service support.

Ordinary lawful project route

Montaña Roja must be acquired, planned, authorised, financed and delivered on its own transaction, technical, economic and public-law merits.

Restricted relationship layer

The information a relationship team needs is not the information the public website should publish.

A serious private-wealth and corporate-banking process requires verified information on current assets, liabilities, guarantees, liquidity requirements, reserves and expected liquidity events. That material belongs in restricted relationship documentation with appropriate confidentiality—not in public Git.

Restricted financial map. The private workstream separates liquid assets; controlled private assets; entrepreneurial/business value; contingent or recovery interests; liabilities; guarantees and security; family and corporate liquidity requirements; expected future liquidity events; desired family reserve; future investable AUM; and Sun Rock reinvestment capital. Unresolved recovery claims are not treated as cash or recognised current NAV.
