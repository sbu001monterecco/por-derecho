Lourdes / MYND incident: requests to control Sun Park's Google Business Profile

This dossier deals only with requests which Google associated in 2021–2022 with Lourdes Castillejo / MYND and mynd.hotels@gmail.com. The separate 2019 episode involving sunpark264@gmail.com is published on its own page and linked as a related incident, not treated as the same event.

What Google documented

28 October 2021Google notified an ownership request for the Sun Park profile associated with Lourdes Castillejo, role “Business owner”, using mynd.hotels@gmail.com.
24 February 2022A further notification again shows Lourdes Castillejo, now as “Employee”, using the same Gmail account.
20 June 2022Preserved material records another request as “MYND Hotels, Employee”, again linked to mynd.hotels@gmail.com.
What this proves — and what it does not.

The notifications prove the association made by Google's system between those requests, names, roles and account. They do not yet prove who was physically using the device, who controlled the account, who gave the instruction, which IP was used, what documents were supplied or whether Lourdes personally authorised every use of her name.

Contemporaneous correspondence in February 2022 expressly kept open an alternative possibility: a third party may have used Lourdes's name without her consent. That possibility remains open and should be resolved through native provider records and direct evidence.

Finite questions

  • Who created and administered mynd.hotels@gmail.com?
  • Who submitted or authenticated each request?
  • Which entity was represented on each date: MYND, Canarian Hospitality, HNT, CAM or another entity?
  • What title, authorisation or supporting document was supplied to Google?
  • Which permissions were granted or refused and which profile fields were changed?
  • Which devices, IP addresses, sessions and internal instructions can identify the actual actor?
  • If Lourdes did not submit one or more requests, who used her identity?
