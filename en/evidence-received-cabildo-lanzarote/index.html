Title boundary

Tourism files can establish what was requested, supplied, checked and registered. They do not by themselves establish ownership of every horizontal-division finca. The December 2022 segregation deed transfers a defined CAMSA economic unit and states a value of €10,213,382.82; its property annex is a defined CAMSA schedule, not a blanket statement that HNT owned every Sun Park finca.

Fincas 8584 and 8588 remain examples within a wider unit-by-unit recovery perimeter. The schedule's omissions are a documentary-scope issue only; fresh Registry evidence is required before stating a current 2026 ownership position.
