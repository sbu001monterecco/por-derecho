ACTS AND OMISSIONS

Do not collapse an affirmative communication into an allegation—or silence into proof.

DOCUMENTED AFFIRMATIVE COMMUNICATION

17 March 2021

The preserved record shows a 1:26 voice note followed by a short written message attributed to Íñigo de Luisa. That is an affirmative communication in the factual chronology.

Alleged professional/legal significance: the client side relies on the communication, together with the 8 March transmission and the later record, when asking what Cuatrecasas understood, retained, escalated or did next. Calling it a legally culpable “commission” is a characterisation requiring separate legal analysis.

ALLEGED OMISSION / NON-ACTION

The evidential chain to test

Documented notice → response/action presently located or not located → applicable duty and scope → alleged omission/non-action → causation → alleged downstream consequence.

The absence of a response in one mailbox, page or dataset does not prove that the firm took no action. The repository should upgrade an omission allegation only after the relevant file, handover, internal escalation and contrary evidence have been sought.

METHOD / PUBLICATION BOUNDARY

What would strengthen—or falsify—the client-side theory.

  • The complete Cuatrecasas engagement, scope, conflict, withdrawal and handover file by client and workstream.
  • The preserved 8 March 2021 message and attachments, with available transmission metadata.
  • The original 17 March voice-note file and any complete transcript or surrounding thread.
  • Any internal forwarding, risk, conflict, knowledge, preservation or escalation records responsive to the 2021 package.
  • Contrary evidence showing advice, referral, warning, escalation or action that is not visible in the client-side record.
  • A causation analysis separating what Cuatrecasas could lawfully affect from decisions made independently by courts, insolvency office-holders, Acosta Matos/CAM, RICPE, public authorities or other actors.

Right of correction: substantiated contrary documents should be linked into the same chronology with equal prominence. Silence is not admission; association is not participation; later benefit does not by itself prove earlier agreement or professional causation.
