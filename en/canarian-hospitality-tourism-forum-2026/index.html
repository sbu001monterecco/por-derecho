One forum. Different documented relationships.

A November tourism meeting brings several distinct lines into view: Canarian Hospitality's public forum, reported Acosta Matos ownership, an older Ashotel/PwC professional circular, and World Travel Market reporting. The proper test is documentary identity, capacity, date and conduct — not guilt by association.

Evidence boundary: An invitation, professional association, social-media reaction, trade-fair interview or shared hospitality venue does not establish attendance, common knowledge, improper benefit, conspiracy, an offence or territorial jurisdiction. This page reports source-supported roles and open questions, not findings of wrongdoing.
