The legal and operating architecture

One relationship. Separate underwriting per layer.

The platform identity is Sun Rock. The legal Sponsor/HoldCo is Aweswell Limited. Individual hotel projects are intended to be ring-fenced in Spanish ProjectCos and must be independently diligenced, financed and controlled.

01 · Sponsor / HoldCo

Aweswell Limited

The UK company leads the Sponsor relationship, holds or controls the relevant group patrimony subject to verification, and is the proposed issuer/borrower for the HoldCo liquidity programme.

02 · Group platform

Sun Rock

Sun Rock is the hospitality and investment platform identity. It is not being presented as a separate legal borrower merely because the platform name is used in the programme.

03 · Asset perimeter

ProjectCos

Each acquired or developed hotel should have its own title, accounts, contracts, operating case, security perimeter, governance and financing analysis. No ProjectCo asset is pledged by implication alone.

Ownership and balance-sheet statement. As represented by management at the status date, the HoldCo is fully privately owned and currently free of outside capital, outside equity and outside debt. This is a current management statement for initial discussion, not an audited balance-sheet certification; institutional verification is part of the process.

The immediate capital request

Proposed Sun Rock Loan Notes.

The immediate working assumption is a UK-law HoldCo instrument issued by Aweswell Limited. The jurisdiction may change after legal, tax and regulatory review. The note programme is proposed, not yet constituted or issued.

Issuer / borrowerAweswell Limited, the UK Sponsor/HoldCo. Sun Rock is the platform identity, not an automatic legal issuer.
PurposeImmediate HoldCo liquidity for transaction execution, diligence, deposits or controlled acquisition steps, corporate readiness and selected investment opportunities, including the Montaña Roja route.
InstrumentProposed Sun Rock Loan Notes or an equivalent secured HoldCo note instrument, initially contemplated under English law and subject to definitive documentation.
EconomicsA fixed coupon and a variable or participating coupon component are contemplated. Rate, reference measure, caps/floors, payment dates, cash/PIK treatment, seniority and waterfall remain subject to negotiation.
Security objectiveThe commercial objective is a secured, asset-backed HoldCo package over relevant Aweswell patrimony and, where legally available and agreed, permitted future acquired project interests. The enforceable package must be defined, perfected and prioritised in definitive documents.
DrawdownCapital may be arranged in one or more tranches, with availability and drawdowns linked to agreed conditions, verified milestones, sources and uses, reporting and lender/investor approvals.
Verification boundaryNo asset is collateral merely because it is under discussion. Title, ownership, existing security, corporate powers, solvency, ProjectCo rights, senior-lender rights and intercreditor arrangements must be checked before reliance.
Security is proposed, not implied. The phrase “asset-backed” describes the intended structure of the negotiated HoldCo instrument. It does not represent that a blanket charge, mortgage, pledge or other security has already been granted or perfected.

How the capital fits the transaction

HoldCo liquidity is not target-company share financing.

The purpose is to make the separation explicit. Aweswell’s proposed HoldCo liquidity is a Sponsor-level funding route. Any acquisition of land, shares, rights or project assets must be funded and documented through lawful, separately underwritten sources.

1

Capitalise the Sponsor

Arrange HoldCo liquidity for Aweswell’s execution capacity and agreed Sponsor obligations.

2

Control the route

Pursue the acquisition and control package for Montaña Roja through separately documented transaction steps.

3

Ring-fence the ProjectCo

Place the hotel asset and project economics in a Spanish vehicle with its own diligence and accounts.

4

Arrange project finance

Seek direct real-estate or development finance only against the verified ProjectCo opportunity.

5

Stage seller consideration

If agreed, deferred or milestone-based seller consideration is documented separately and made compatible with senior finance.

6

Draw against evidence

Development capital is drawn against certified progress, cost-to-complete and agreed controls.

Compliance principle. No financing is intended to be advanced by a target company, or otherwise structured to finance the acquisition of its own shares. HoldCo capital, acquisition/control capital and ProjectCo finance remain distinct underwriting questions.

Transaction in view

Montaña Roja · Yaiza · Lanzarote.

Montaña Roja is the preferred current route for a new hotel in Playa Blanca and is under active execution work. It is not represented as completed or financeable on assumptions alone.

Current project objective

Hotel-led, high-quality destination project

The current direction is a four-star or greater hotel, modelled at approximately 300 or more units or suites, with complementary commercial and service spaces. Final scale, design, operator, planning route, ownership and capital structure remain subject to verification and agreement.

Current execution position

Transaction and capital work in parallel

Commercial, legal, planning, technical and financing workstreams are advancing in parallel. No acquisition, planning approval, operator appointment or committed project financing is announced on this page before it exists.

Potential seller mechanics

Deferred and milestone-based consideration

A negotiated acquisition may include deferred consideration, staged milestone payments, seller notes or equivalent arrangements. These are potential transaction terms, not current liabilities or commitments, and must be reconciled with senior finance, security and ProjectCo governance.

Potential future financing

ProjectCo draws after verification

Development or whole-loan capital would be considered only after the project perimeter, title, planning path, technical scope, complete cost, operating case, equity and security package are sufficiently verified.
