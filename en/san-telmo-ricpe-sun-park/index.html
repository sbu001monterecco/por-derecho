Same hotel. Parallel professional lives.

A source-led bridge between the insolvency life of Sun Park and the contemporaneous investor/financing life connected to RIC Private Equity.

Editorial boundary. This page identifies documented roles, direct source statements and finite reconciliation questions. Why this cannot be treated as mere media proximity

The source records a client/investor-introduction statement by a San Telmo partner in direct discussion with RICPE's Director General, followed by project/promoter discussion including Acosta Matos. Separately, Sun Park had already been publicly presented inside the RICPE tourism-investment portfolio. The result is a concrete same-asset reconciliation problem: the hotel's insolvency life and investment life were proceeding in parallel.

Contemporaneous January 2022 correspondence with criminal/compliance counsel also records that the 2021 video directly mentions the Sun Park project and highlights the significance of participation by the law firm in which the insolvency administrator was a partner.
