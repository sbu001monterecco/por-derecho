3. What the image’s “same asset” shorthand means

Same hotel complex and connected project perimeter—not one undivided legal asset. LPB was the debtor in Concurso 36/2012; the whole Sun Park complex was not automatically the insolvency estate. The shorthand does not erase Matkator or third-party title and does not assert that every unit formed part of LPB’s estate.

This is a request for traceability, not unrestricted disclosure of client identities or privileged communications. Process, date, scope, responsibility, custody and verification can often be demonstrated through redaction, certification or independent review.
