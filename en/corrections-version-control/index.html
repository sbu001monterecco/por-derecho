Correcting the record does not weaken the case: it identifies precisely what can be said, what was superseded and what remains open.

This page preserves the material corrections that control over drafts, memoranda, earlier pages and broader formulations. A correction does not erase history: it explains what changed, why it changed and where the current public formulation is maintained.

Rule: primary documents and issue-specific legal validation prevail over summaries, internal drafts and later analysis. “LIVE_VERIFIED” means that a publication was deployed and read back from the public edge; it does not mean that its allegations were independently or judicially verified.

Version control and prior use

Not established

Procedural control

The novelty of each fact or argument must be compared with the court file, earlier submissions, decisions, knowledge and applicable time rules.

Privacy: the versions, emails, recipients, identifiers and hashes remain in private evidence systems. This page publishes only the provenance conclusion needed to interpret the history correctly.

How corrections are applied

  1. The new or more complete source is recorded and compared with the earlier formulation.
  2. Factual error, legal-characterisation change, evidential limit and wording improvement are distinguished.
  3. The controlling page receives the current formulation.
  4. Supporting pages link to the correction or display a superseded-version notice.
  5. The correction is applied in English and Spanish and verified on the public site.
