A creditor chain is not a clean slate. It is a sequence of title, knowledge, decision, conduct and benefit.

The Sun Park record must identify not only who held the credit, but who controlled the decisions, what information was available, what alternatives were rejected, what each actor did after notice and how the credit was ultimately converted into control and title.

CONTROLLED LEGAL POSITION

“Lender-of-record liability” is a forensic organising framework, not one freestanding Spanish cause of action. But the absence of one global cause does not erase responsibility. Upstream retained obligations, defects affecting the credit, direct downstream conduct, mandate and ratification, insolvency gatekeeping, unjust enrichment, universal succession and civil liability arising from independently proved crime must each be tested against the same reconstructed chain.

THE STRONGEST CURRENT DEVELOPMENT

The record now supports a serious case for judicial determination of an indirect credit-to-control-to-title appropriation architecture. The case does not depend on alleging that every transfer was unlawful or that a classical pacto comisorio clause has already been found. It asks whether creditor-side decision control, rejected exits, CAM's acquisition, a disputed valuation, pre-title control, Insolvency Administrator gatekeeping and the 2022 dación operated together without genuine value and surplus protection.

Controlled conclusion

No actor receives a clean slate merely because the credit changed hands or a later order formalised title. The legal analysis begins with the true credit and follows knowledge, decision authority, direct conduct, institutional gatekeeping, benefit, causation and succession through every node.

The strongest current case is not “everyone inherited everything.” It is that distinct actors occupied distinct but connected positions in a credit-to-title architecture and must answer for the rights they acquired, the decisions they controlled, the acts they performed, the benefits they received and the incremental harm legally attributable to them.

Evidence-led publication. No final civil, professional, insolvency, regulatory or criminal liability finding is asserted. Complete deeds, mandates, decisions and corrective or exculpatory material are invited.

One reconciliation for the alleged multiple lives of the credit

Gil Marer and Aweswell allege that the economic package originated by Caja Insular de Ahorros de Canarias / La Caja de Canarias was split and instrumentalised across Bankia administration/enforcement, Insolvency 36/2012, Arrecife Mortgage Enforcement 90/2012 and the Bankia → SAREB → Promontoria Holding 122 B.V. (PH122) → CAM chain, culminating in an alleged credit-to-control-to-title conversion. They further allege that materially false or inflated documents or data may have enabled an unjustified benefit. The proposition does not make the sequence a crime or transfer liability between entities.

Individual capacity

Caja Insular/BFA/Bankia/CaixaBank are tested in the corporate/product lane; Bankia/SAREB/PH122/CAM in the asset lane. Haya is a documented servicer/interlocutor. Cerberus is context only until ownership, mandate, instruction, control, ratification or benefit is proved.

Two forums that must not be merged

Insolvency 36/2012 and Arrecife EH90 require reconciliation by asset, balance, date, function, payment and cancellation. The located EH90 claim concerns the second facility; Protocol 457 implements the 159-property insolvency dación and does not itself prove satisfaction or cancellation of EH90.

End-to-end ledger

The control must connect €839,441, €857,373.81, €9,052,251.69, €12,251,446.21, €13,165,832.36 and €13,168,082.02 without treating them as addends. Each figure needs source, date, concept, interest, security, payment, assignment, set-off, extinguishment and recovery.

Falsity: concrete proof

The exact document, original and comparator; author; alteration, simulation or false attribution; knowledge, intent, use, induced disposition and benefit/harm are required. An authentic deed may contain disputed recitals without itself being false. For a private actor, CP 392 incorporates only CP 390.1.1º–3º, not mere untruthful narration under 390.1.4º; CP 393 first requires a proved false document and then, knowing its falsity, presentation in court or, for other use, also a purpose to harm.

Innocent explanation and boundary. The published evidence does not establish NPL fraud, documentary/notarial falsity, a false signature, alteration, notarial knowledge, collusion, a common plan, double recovery or any actor's crime. “Multiple lives,” “split credit” and “NPL fraud” are not freestanding offences. CP 250.1.7 requires evidential/procedural fraud, judicial error and an economically prejudicial resulting decision; CP 259–261 must be applied under the pre- or post-1-Jul-2015 text and their debtor/purpose limits. Legal-person criminal liability exists only from 23-Dec-2010 and for offences with an express corporate-liability provision; CP 130.2 presupposes that responsibility and a listed corporate event, without transferring natural-person intent or guilt or deciding civil/product liability. Variations may reflect dates, interest, caps, scope, instruments, transcription or procedural functions; assignments, discounted NPL purchase, enforcement, dación and adjudication can be lawful. Discount and later recovery do not prove fraud. The burden of substantiating a concrete infringement lies with the party asserting it and, in criminal proceedings, with the prosecution; no person accused must prove innocence.
