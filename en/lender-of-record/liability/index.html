A creditor chain is not a clean slate. It is a sequence of title, knowledge, decision, conduct and benefit.

The Sun Park record must identify not only who held the credit, but who controlled the decisions, what information was available, what alternatives were rejected, what each actor did after notice and how the credit was ultimately converted into control and title.

CONTROLLED LEGAL POSITION

“Lender-of-record liability” is a forensic organising framework, not one freestanding Spanish cause of action. But the absence of one global cause does not erase responsibility. Upstream retained obligations, defects affecting the credit, direct downstream conduct, mandate and ratification, insolvency gatekeeping, unjust enrichment, universal succession and civil liability arising from independently proved crime must each be tested against the same reconstructed chain.

THE STRONGEST CURRENT DEVELOPMENT

The record now supports a serious case for judicial determination of an indirect credit-to-control-to-title appropriation architecture. The case does not depend on alleging that every transfer was unlawful or that a classical pacto comisorio clause has already been found. It asks whether creditor-side decision control, rejected exits, CAM's acquisition, a disputed valuation, pre-title control, Insolvency Administrator gatekeeping and the 2022 dación operated together without genuine value and surplus protection.

Six separations that prevent both impunity and overstatement

Credit / personal liability

The credit and accessories may travel. Every personal obligation of the transferor does not automatically travel.

Defect / new wrongdoing

A defect affecting the credit may remain relevant. A later holder also answers for its own post-notice conduct.

Holder / servicer / sponsor

PH122, Haya and Cerberus roles require mandate, authority, instruction, ratification and benefit evidence.

Creditor / Insolvency Administrator

Private desire to acquire did not substitute for the statutory office and judicial gates required to affect LPB's estate.

Assignment / universal succession

PH122→CAM was a credit assignment; CAM→HNT was a different corporate operation described as universal succession.

Sequence / causation

Duty, power, knowledge, act, implementation, intervening causes, damage and remedy must be proved actor by actor.

Damage must be incremental and allocated

Ring 1 · proximal

Asset or equity diminution, identifiable surplus, rents/fruits in a proven wrongful period, direct damage and necessary corrective costs.

Ring 2 · foreseeable counterfactual

Lost refinancing, convenio exit, identified hotel-operation period and finance costs—subject to financeability and probability proof.

Ring 3 · high-proof

Long-term platform, brand expansion, future developments and wide multi-year projections. These do not lead the case.

Liquidity/default, owner conflict, Administrator and judicial decisions, title problems, independent finance committees, market risk and mitigation must all be tested as possible intervening causes.

Controlled conclusion

No actor receives a clean slate merely because the credit changed hands or a later order formalised title. The legal analysis begins with the true credit and follows knowledge, decision authority, direct conduct, institutional gatekeeping, benefit, causation and succession through every node.

The strongest current case is not “everyone inherited everything.” It is that distinct actors occupied distinct but connected positions in a credit-to-title architecture and must answer for the rights they acquired, the decisions they controlled, the acts they performed, the benefits they received and the incremental harm legally attributable to them.

Evidence-led publication. No final civil, professional, insolvency, regulatory or criminal liability finding is asserted. Complete deeds, mandates, decisions and corrective or exculpatory material are invited.
