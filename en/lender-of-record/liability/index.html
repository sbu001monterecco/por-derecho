A change in lender of record does not create either a clean slate or an automatic transfer of every historic liability.

The Sun Park record requires a more exact analysis: what credit and security moved; what defects or defences may affect that credit; what obligations stayed upstream; what each later actor knew; what it then did in its own capacity; and what incremental harm can legally be attributed to that conduct.

CONTROLLED LEGAL POSITION

“Lender-of-record liability” is used here as a forensic organising label. It is not presented as one freestanding Spanish cause of action. Assignment, direct fault, contract, mandate, responsibility for others, corporate succession, restitution and criminal civil liability each have separate elements, parties, limitation rules and remedies.

The six separations that control this record

1. Credit versus personal liability

The assigned credit and legally included accessory rights may travel. A transfer does not, without more, move every personal contractual or extra-contractual liability of the transferor.

2. Defect versus new wrongdoing

A defect or defence affecting the credit may remain relevant to an assignee. Separately, a later holder may incur responsibility through its own conduct after acquisition or notice.

3. Owner versus servicer

Ownership of the credit, servicing, mandate, employment, decision authority and ratification must be proved separately.

4. Group versus legal person

A commercial or investment perimeter does not itself erase separate corporate personality or establish group-wide liability.

5. Assignment versus succession

The 2022 CAM→HNT segregation stated to operate by universal succession is a different legal event from an ordinary credit assignment.

6. Factual sequence versus legal causation

Showing that one event followed another is not enough. Duty, fault, material causation, legal attribution, intervening causes, mitigation and damage must be proved.

Foreseeability, remoteness and incremental loss

Ring 1 · proximal

Asset or equity diminution, identifiable surplus, rents/fruits during a proven wrongful period, direct physical harm and necessary corrective costs.

Ring 2 · foreseeable counterfactual

Lost refinancing, convenio exit, a defined hotel-operation period, operator arrangements and financing costs—only with financeability and causal proof.

Ring 3 · high-proof / remote

Long-term platform, brand expansion, future developments, generic reputation/capital-raising effects and wide multi-year projections.

The damages model begins with Ring 1 and builds outward. It does not begin with one global figure and assign the full 2008–2026 history to the last entity in the chain.

Intervening-cause control. Liquidity/default, owner conflict, insolvency-administration and judicial decisions, title/ob-rem issues, independent financier committees, market risk and mitigation must all be tested.

Controlled conclusion

The presently responsible formulation is not that every downstream entity inherited everything done before it. The stronger and testable architecture is a network of legally distinct questions: upstream retained obligations; defects and defences affecting the assigned credit; each later actor's own conduct after acquisition or notice; proven servicing/mandate links; and the separate consequences of the CAM→HNT universal-succession operation.

Evidence-led publication. No civil, professional, concursal, regulatory or criminal liability finding is asserted. The record expressly invites complete deeds, schedules, decision papers, explanations and exculpatory or corrective material.
