20 October 2017 · Civil Code art. 1535 · deed and price not produced

Contested credit, withheld deed and a lost opportunity to object.

The issue is not only who bought the credit. It is whether PH122 and Construcciones Acosta Matos documented a different or inflated debt, whether the insolvency administration and court allowed that figure to take effect without effective contradiction, and whether withholding the complete deed and price deprived LPB of a timely opportunity to challenge it and exercise its rights.

Public position

Contemporary documents establish a material dispute predating the assignment. Cristo A. Suárez Pimentel's 24 June 2016 legal report and 12 July 2016 formal demand specifically challenged balance, enforceability, partial releases, double counting and financial clauses.

We contend that the failure to provide the complete PH122–CAM deed, individual price and debt calculation in time created a decisive asymmetry: those who made or validated the transaction knew its contents; LPB could not test or oppose them on equal terms before substitution and disputed figures were allowed to claim procedural effect.

Constitutional issue: the sequence creates an objective appearance of favourable treatment, institutional bias and deficient effective judicial protection. This is an evidential conclusion open to contradiction, not a final criminal conviction.

Responsibility and duty to explain

Promontoria Holding 122 B.V.

As assignor and active insolvency participant, it should have known the history, admitted amounts, enforcement and 2016 objections. It must explain which balance it sold, what adjustments were made, at what price, and why LPB did not receive the complete deed before procedural effects were claimed.

Construcciones Acosta Matos, S.A.

As assignee and direct beneficiary, it had to prove title, scope, price and balance without enlarging the credit. Non-voluntary production followed by a disclosure order supports an inference of deliberate opacity requiring a documentary answer.

Insolvency administration

It knew of the quantified objections by July 2016. It must explain what it verified, how it reconciled the amounts, why it supported substitution without providing LPB the essential documents, and what it did to protect contradiction, the estate and LPB's claims.

Insolvency court

It was required to safeguard hearing, equality of arms, reasons and credit control. Refusing full production while presuming AC verification, despite inconsistent figures and the buyer's direct interest, feeds an objective appearance of favouritism and failed judicial protection requiring full-record review.

Cumulative effect

The sequence must not be atomised: prior dispute + opaque assignment + divergent figures + facilitated substitution + delayed or denied access + lost opportunity to object. Together these matters justify investigation into whether a civil appearance of undisputed debt was manufactured and used to defeat redemption, reinforce liquidation and transfer value and control to CAM.

Legal precision and missing proof

Article 1535 treats a credit as litigious from the answer to the claim concerning it. The material case is strong; the strict technical classification requires identification, before 20 October 2017 and for each loan, of the claim, opposition or insolvency incident disputing existence, enforceability or amount and the response joining that issue.

The presently missing procedural document does not erase the documented dispute or the duty to explain non-production and inconsistent figures. It prevents us, for now, from publishing as settled fact that both credits indisputably met every Article 1535 requirement.

Standing documentary demand

  • Complete PH122–CAM deed and every schedule.
  • Total and allocated price, payment date and proof of payment.
  • Loan-by-loan reconciliation between deed, final insolvency texts, enforcement and substitution.
  • Pre- and post-assignment PH122/Cerberus/Haya–CAM–AC communications.
  • Claim, answer, opposition or insolvency incident pending on 20 October 2017.
  • AC verification report and the complete grounds for declining production to LPB.

Status: documented allegations and open questions. Rights of reply and correction and the presumption of innocence are preserved. Criminal responsibility is determined only by a court.
