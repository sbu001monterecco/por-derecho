Credit chain · financial package · alleged overreach

Lender of Record.

This page separates the mortgage credit from the disputed financial package, then tracks how the lender position moved from Caja Insular de Ahorros de Canarias through later holders to Construcciones Acosta Matos, S.A. It also records where the credit ended and where separate product-liability, estate and operating questions remain alive.

See the 2008 sale and lender convergence

LPB concurso*: limited estate, repeated overreach

A limited LPB concurso was repeatedly used, or allowed to be used, as if it were authority over the whole hotel, the whole ownership perimeter, the CEXP/exploitation layer, Matkator/third-party units, works, finance and later hotel operation.

On this site, "LPB concurso" means Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. and the estate in Concurso 36/2012 only. It does not mean that the whole hotel, all Sun Park owners, the CEXP/exploitation layer, Matkator units, third-party units, works, finance or later hotel operation were placed under that proceeding.

Required public formulation: identify "LPB and its estate in Concurso 36/2012", the relevant date, procedural phase, exact power and supporting record, while expressly separating the rest of the hotel and exploitation perimeter.

01

Assignable mortgage credit

The supported chain is treated as a date-specific sequence: Caja Insular de Ahorros de Canarias (La Caja de Canarias); Bankia, S.A.; Sociedad de Gestión de Activos Procedentes de la Reestructuración Bancaria, S.A. (SAREB); Promontoria Holding 122 B.V.; and Construcciones Acosta Matos, S.A., NIF A-35079342.

The exact originator and every assignment step must be pinned to the signed deeds, notices and court substitutions.

02

Disputed financial package

The disputed package is a separate liability and causation lane: first mortgage, floor clause, EUR 5 million swap/cobertura, EUR 405,000 pledged deposit, EUR 850,000 second mortgage/refinance, default notices, execution filing and auction pressure. Valencia PO 1859/2023 against CaixaBank, S.A. sits in this lane; trial is set on the located record for 28 January 2027 at 10:00.

The working thesis is that the package caused or materially contributed to the alleged default, the execution threat and the defensive LPB concurso.

Timeline

From lender to claimant, and from claimant to alleged control narrative

2008 - original Sun Park finance

Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. acquired the majority Sun Park ownership position with finance recorded internally as a La Caja de Canarias loan. The original deed must confirm the exact corporate lender and the later succession route into Bankia, S.A.

2008 to 2012 - signed financial-package terms

The first mortgage was for EUR 8.6 million. The signed EUR 5 million risk-management contract ran from 7 July 2008 to 7 January 2012. Its client-pay leg used Euribor-minus-spread terms and 5.15%/4.95% bands below stated triggers, netted against Euribor 3M received by LPB. The contract says LPB would bear a higher financing cost below those bands. Suitability, disclosure, linkage and causation remain contested.

2010 - second mortgage and pledged deposit

A second mortgage/refinancing facility of EUR 850,000 and a EUR 405,000 pledge were put in place. Aweswell's pleading and expert say the pledge was consumed immediately and only EUR 445,000 was effectively available. CaixaBank's expert disputes proof and economic treatment. Actual account entries and reciprocal restitution remain to be reconciled.

January to June 2012 - execution pressure and defensive concurso

On 21 May 2012, LPB's external advisers reported that a Bankia mortgage auction of 166 LPB apartments had been fixed for 22 June. They identified refinancing, a voluntary suspension by Bankia, or a voluntary concurso declared before the auction as the three available routes. On 31 May they described filing as urgent.

LPB alleged actual insolvency. The 6 June declaration order recorded approximately EUR 21.60 million of assets and EUR 10.19 million of liabilities, declared LPB only, retained LPB management subject to intervention, and notified the mortgage-execution court. The evidence-led framing therefore treats enforcement as the immediate trigger while testing finance, trading, governance, receivables and operational interference separately.

2012 to 2014 - SAREB credit acquisition and onward sale

The case record says Bankia transferred the mortgage credits to Sociedad de Gestión de Activos Procedentes de la Reestructuración Bancaria, S.A. The working position is that the mortgage credit moved, while the swap/cobertura liability and mis-selling claim remained a separate Bankia/CaixaBank responsibility lane unless the assignment documents prove otherwise.

April to July 2014 - SAREB receives the perimeter and rescue file

SAREB received a valuation report and draft sale-mandate, framework and standstill documents. They described LPB's units as part of a hotel activity subject to tourism, community and management restrictions and proposed suspension of enforcement, a convenio and controlled sale. SAREB said the steps appeared generally appropriate subject to formal documents and price, and invited drafts. No signed standstill or binding restructuring has yet been located.

December 2014 - Project Meridian and Promontoria Holding 122 B.V.

Public reporting states that Cerberus Capital Management, L.P. won SAREB's Project Meridian, a EUR 133 million hotel-sector loan portfolio secured by 26 hotels in Spain. The Sun Park record identifies Promontoria Holding 122 B.V. as the acquiring vehicle for the relevant credit. The asset schedule, price and debtor notices must be produced and reconciled with the court record.

Public source: CoStar Finance report on Project Meridian.

20 October 2017 to February 2018 - Construcciones Acosta Matos, S.A. becomes assignee and recognised successor

The located notarial testimony identifies Promontoria Holding 122 B.V. as assigning both mortgage credits to Construcciones Acosta Matos, S.A., NIF A-35079342. Arrecife recognised procedural succession on 4 January 2018 and the concurso court substituted Construcciones Acosta Matos, S.A. on 8 February. A creditor position was not ownership of 100% of Sun Park or authority over CEXP, other owners or the whole operation.

2018 onward - alleged instrumentalisation

The LPB concurso, mortgage credit and auction posture were allegedly used by later actors as if they supplied wider authority over the hotel, its commercial exploitation, works, third-party ownership, investment narrative and later operation. That alleged overreach is separate from, but causally connected to, the Bankia/CaixaBank financial-package lane.

21 February 2022 - dación and credit application

The located deed record identifies Construcciones Acosta Matos, S.A. as recipient of enumerated LPB properties and states EUR 13,168,082.02 consideration. The complete finca, valuation, payment and distribution reconciliation remains required.

18 August 2023 - Arrecife EH 90/2012 ends

Decree 500/2023 named Construcciones Acosta Matos, S.A. as executing successor and terminated the mortgage enforcement for supervening loss of object after dación, mortgage cancellation and satisfaction. Any claimed surviving right must now be identified separately; EH 90/2012 cannot accurately be described as a continuing enforcement.

2022 to 2024 - Hotel New Trend and later CAM successors

Official notices record segregation of a Sun Park economic unit from Construcciones Acosta Matos, S.A. to Hotel New Trend, S.L.U., followed by its total demerger and extinction. A genuinely new same-name Construcciones Acosta Matos, S.L. arose with an effective operations date of 15 December 2023. The item-level allocations of any residual rights, liabilities and procedural powers have not been produced.

2023 to 2027 - the separate CaixaBank liability lane

Aweswell seeks annulment of the swap and linked 2010 instruments, alternatively damages, in Valencia PO 1859/2023 before JPI No. 27. CaixaBank disputes standing, scope, preclusion, time bars, breach, causation and quantum. Trial is set on the located court record for 28 January 2027 at 10:00; no merits judgment has been located.

Current credit status The supported mortgage-credit chain reaches Construcciones Acosta Matos, S.A., the 2022 dación and the 2023 satisfaction-based termination. That does not extinguish separate product-liability, estate-accounting, third-party title, exploitation-income or actor-specific damages questions.

Working conclusion

The lender-of-record chain now has an evidential end point: the 2022 dación and the August 2023 termination of EH 90/2012 after cancellation and satisfaction. The disputed financial package follows a separate path into the live Valencia case. Neither path decides the viability of the whole hotel or grants authority over CEXP, third-party property or later operation.

Possible responsibility may be concurrent, successive or contributory. The conduct of one actor does not absolve a predecessor, assignee, servicer, adviser, insolvency office-holder, institutional decision-maker or later operator of its own acts, omissions and state of knowledge.
