Origin of Concurso 36/2012 · disputed default premise · direct-market response

Banking origin and lender of record.

The first question is no longer only who later held the mortgage credit. It is whether the 2011–2012 default, acceleration, enforcement and auction premise was accurate and complete; whether the resulting voluntary insolvency filing was a properly informed protective decision; and how a proceeding limited to LPB later affected a wider hotel, ownership and trading perimeter.

Public status — pending and contested: the present Valencia claim is described here as the proceeding against the successor institution to the 2011–2012 lender. Its present defendant is deliberately not identified in full at this stage. No liability finding is asserted.

What this page does—and does not say

Documented record: LPB faced mortgage enforcement and an auction over 166 LPB apartments fixed for 22 June 2012; LPB sought voluntary insolvency protection on 4 June 2012; and only Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. was the debtor in Concurso 36/2012.

Group position / party allegation: the banking relationship may have been treated as in default on a mistaken or incomplete basis; the advice to use voluntary insolvency as the defensive response may, viewed with the fuller record now available, have been wrong, negligent, reckless or materially incomplete; and later efforts to conclude, narrow or reverse the process did not receive effective institutional support.

Unresolved questions: the exact debt then due and enforceable, the account and grace-period treatment, the accuracy of the enforcement application, the information supplied to the local enforcement court, the scope and quality of the professional advice, and the legal significance of later acts or omissions by the insolvency administrator and court.

01

LPB was the debtor

“LPB insolvency” means Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. and its estate in Concurso 36/2012. It does not mean that Sun Park as a whole, every owner, the hotel operation, the Community of Exploitation, Matkator or all third-party rights were placed in insolvency.

02

The wider perimeter remained separate

Matkator was not the debtor. The Comunidad de Explotación del Complejo Sun Park was a separate operating structure and was not automatically absorbed into LPB’s estate. The community-of-owners layer, individual units, bookings, customers, goodwill and distribution systems also require separate legal and evidential analysis.

Controlled chronology

From acquisition and banking pressure to a defensive filing

The chronology is deliberately staged. It does not compress a changing ownership position, a disputed banking relationship and the filing decision into one unsupported event.

2008 — acquisition finance and disputed financial package

LPB’s majority Sun Park position was financed through the original Canary banking perimeter. The controlled record identifies a mortgage relationship together with a floor clause, swap or cobertura, pledged funds and later refinancing. The mortgage debt and the disputed financial-product conduct are related factually but are not automatically the same legal claim.

Late 2011 to early 2012 — staged Aweswell entry from the prior Multimatrix position

Aweswell entered LPB’s ownership and control structure during late 2011 and early 2012. Corporate publication material records a sole-shareholder change dated 3 January 2012. The exact economic, corporate and management stages should be stated separately rather than described as one undated purchase.

2011–2012 — disputed treatment as in default

The group now questions whether the lender’s classification of the relationship as in arrears or default accurately reflected the grace period, account closures, amortisation sequence, available funds, the linked financial package and the amount lawfully due. That is a pleaded and evidential question, not a concluded fact.

15 May to 22 June 2012 — enforcement and auction track

The preserved court record includes a mortgage-enforcement notification and auction diligence dated 15 May 2012. The controlled source register records an auction over 166 LPB apartments fixed for 22 June 2012. The present review asks whether the executing party and the enforcement court were working from a correct, complete and legally enforceable account of the debt.

4 June 2012 — voluntary insolvency as a defensive measure

LPB entered voluntary insolvency before the scheduled auction. The immediate filing trigger must be distinguished from the wider causes of distress: disputed banking conduct, pre-existing owner conflict, operational fragmentation, lost trading confidence and the limits of the information and advice available at the time.

After filing — attempts to conclude or narrow the process

The group’s position is that, once it sought to reverse, conclude or confine the defensive procedure, the insolvency machinery did not support an effective exit. This remains an allegation requiring a dated map of every application, report, response, order, omission and appeal.

Current Valencia proceeding

Ordinary Proceeding 1859/2023-9 is pending before Court of First Instance 27 of Valencia. Project Sun Rock records the trial as listed for January 2027. The proceeding tests the successor institution’s responsibility within the banking and financial-product lane; it does not by itself decide every concursal, community, title, accounting or extraconcursal issue.

Two accounting and liability lanes

Do not merge the Valencia claim with the concursal credit reconciliation

A

Banking and financial-product lane

This lane examines the original loan relationship, floor clause, swap or cobertura, pledged funds, refinancing, account administration, default notices, acceleration, enforcement and causation of the defensive filing.

B

Concursal credit and accounting lane

A separate question concerns the amounts later asserted by experts, recognised in the insolvency, transferred through the credit chain and ultimately used in enforcement, dación, cancellation and satisfaction. A numerical discrepancy may matter to the concurso without determining the separate banking claim.

Required reconciliation

The evidence should bridge the original principal, ordinary and default interest, financial-product charges, payments and pledged funds, the recognised concursal credit, every assignment, the enforcement amount, the €400,000 transaction, the dación value, cancellations, costs, cash movements and any surplus. A later title or satisfaction entry does not retrospectively validate every antecedent calculation or act.

Later lender-of-record chain

Reopening the origin does not erase the later credit sequence

The mortgage-credit chain, the disputed financial-product lane and later control or title assertions remain separate propositions. Each transfer must be proved by its own deed, notice and procedural substitution.

2008 — original Canary lender perimeter

The controlled record places the original LPB mortgage finance within the Caja General de Ahorros de Canarias / La Caja de Canarias perimeter, followed historically by Bankia, S.A. The original deed and succession documentation remain the primary source for the exact legal lender at each date.

2013 — SAREB credit acquisition

The case record identifies Sociedad de Gestión de Activos Procedentes de la Reestructuración Bancaria, S.A. as the later acquirer of the mortgage credit. What moved with the mortgage and what remained within the original banking and financial-product responsibility lane must be determined from the assignment documents, not assumed.

December 2014 — Project Meridian / Promontoria Holding 122 B.V.

Public reporting identifies Cerberus Capital Management, L.P. as the successful bidder for SAREB’s Project Meridian hotel-loan portfolio. The Sun Park record identifies Promontoria Holding 122 B.V. as the vehicle later holding the relevant credit. The asset schedule, acquisition price, servicing file and debtor notices remain required.

Public background source: CoStar Finance report on Project Meridian.

October 2017 to 2018 — Construcciones Acosta Matos, S.A. enters as creditor

The controlled record places the Promontoria-to-CAMSA credit transfer in this period. A creditor position—even a specially privileged or enforcing position—was not in itself ownership of 100% of Sun Park, authority over the Community of Exploitation, authority over Matkator or a licence to operate the whole hotel.

2022 onward — formal title, Hotel New Trend, S.L. and operation

The later adjudication, dación, mortgage cancellation and CAMSA-to-Hotel New Trend succession or segregation route must be reconciled with the earlier credit, possession, works, income and third-party-rights record. Later title cannot answer every antecedent question by itself.

Operating business—not only real estate

Minority interference and the direct-to-market response

The origin story also includes a trading business whose value lay in guests, repeat customers, bookings, call handling, digital distribution, sales processes and goodwill.

Group position: the Monte Lanza/Molina minority-dissident perimeter materially disrupted unitary hotel operation and helped make conventional tour-operator distribution difficult. Faced with a property affected by internal ownership and operating conflict, Gil Marer and the Aweswell management team developed a route to market that was less dependent on traditional tour operators.

Independent support in the business record: contemporaneous emails, booking and payment records support the existence of direct reservations, a substantial resident and repeat-customer base, call-handling and conversion scripts, sales reporting, customer reactivation campaigns, presentations and negotiations with external operators.

Causation limit: those records establish that a direct-market platform existed and had economic substance. They do not yet prove that every tour operator declined solely because of the internal conflict, or quantify the precise share of loss attributable to each minority actor, lender, adviser, administrator, judicial act or later private actor.

Assets of the trading platform

Reservations, customer data, repeat relationships, telephone and digital sales architecture, payment records, channel relationships, staff know-how, brand, goodwill and forward bookings must be traced separately from registered units and common areas.

Why the perimeter matters

Damage to that platform could affect Aweswell, the exploitation structure, Matkator and other non-debtor interests even where the formal insolvency jurisdiction concerned LPB. The existence of a concurso does not automatically convert all resulting harm into a claim belonging only to LPB’s estate.

Read the separate Community instrumentalisation record, including the owner-conflict chronology and the distinction between LPB, the Community of Exploitation, the community of owners and individual proprietors.

Institutional responsibility

Active decisions and passive non-protection must be tested separately

Question Correct public framing Evidence needed
Did the insolvency administrator or judge formally control the whole hotel? No. Their formal role arose in LPB’s proceeding. Responsibility outside the estate cannot be assumed from a general label. Specific orders, reports, authorisations, objections, notices and estate-perimeter rulings.
Can acts or omissions still have affected non-debtors? Yes, potentially. The issue is whether a specific act, approval, silence after notice, accounting treatment, failure to protect or failure to correct foreseeably enabled harm beyond LPB. Duty, knowledge, legal capacity, timing, causation and the available corrective measure for each event.
Does later creditor title cure the earlier record? No automatic conclusion follows. Creditor status, practical control, formal title, authority over third parties and lawful exploitation are different propositions. Assignment deeds, substitutions, possession records, Community authority, operating contracts, title entries and income accounting.
Was the original filing necessarily wrong? Not established. The present allegation is that the decision may have been induced by an inaccurate banking premise and incomplete advice. Reasonableness must be tested using the information actually available in 2012. Native bank records, enforcement file, professional advice file subject to privilege review, board/shareholder decisions and contemporaneous alternatives.

Preserved first-hand evidence item

Gil Marer’s account is that Ron Coggle stated, in front of two lawyers from Cuatrecasas, Gonçalves Pereira, S.L.P., that he would close the hotel. This remains a party statement requiring confirmation of date, place, exact words, context and all persons present. It is not presented as an independently adjudicated finding.

Questions now requiring a documentary answer

Was LPB genuinely and correctly classified as in default in 2011? What exact amount was due, on what date and under which instrument? Were the acceleration, execution and auction application accurate and complete? What did Multimatrix, Aweswell, LPB, the lender and the advisers each know? Was voluntary insolvency the only reasonable protective option? What later applications sought conclusion or reversal, and how did the administrator and court respond? Which acts or omissions enabled a limited LPB proceeding to affect the Community of Exploitation, Matkator, the direct-sales platform and other extraconcursal interests?
