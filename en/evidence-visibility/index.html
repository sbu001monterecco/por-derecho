Mandatory evidence-object triad

Preserve it. Search it. Show it.

01

Native source

Preserve the original file or identify its controlled external custodian. Record filename, provenance, size, pages or duration, SHA-256, variants and exact-file limitations.

02

Searchable text

Publish native extraction, transcription or OCR, with the method and limitations. Add the source language and a labelled translation where material.

03

Evidence images

Render source-derived full-page images in order. Thumbnails, crops and highlights may supplement the full page but cannot replace it.

Redaction

Public protection without destroying custody

Where full display is inappropriate, the unredacted original remains preserved in its controlled layer. A distinct public derivative is created, flattened, fingerprinted, reviewed and linked to its own images and searchable text.

Redaction is a derivative process—not deletion, not silent editing, and not evidence replacement.

Carries every unresolved source, image, OCR, translation and redaction gap into the next thread.

Lifecycle

Completion is record-specific

  • SOURCE_PENDING — identified, but not yet held in repository custody.
  • SOURCE_PRESERVED — native or controlled connected source fingerprinted.
  • TEXT_EXTRACTED — searchable source text exists.
  • IMAGE_RENDERED — source-derived visual evidence exists.
  • REDACTED_PUBLIC_DERIVATIVE — approved public derivative and images/text exist.
  • LIVE_VERIFIED — public routes and assets independently read back.

A package is never called visually complete while an in-scope item remains pending, partial or blocked.
