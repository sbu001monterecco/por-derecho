CONTEMPORANEOUS NOTICE · FILING · EXTEMPORANEITY

Three questions must never be collapsed into one

1 · Created

Was the analysis in existence at the relevant time, and is its date/version reliable?

2 · Transmitted

Who received it or the underlying warning, by what channel, and is receipt evidenced?

3 · Filed

Was it formally incorporated into the relevant section/piece of Concurso 36/2012?

4 · Timely?

Only if the applicable deadline and actual filing/service date are established can “extemporaneous” be tested.

Significance of contemporaneous notice

The 10 May 2018 draft records that LPB had repeatedly notified the insolvency administration of its disagreement with the valuation and its intention to obtain independent analysis. Because the report predates the 7 June 2018 physical-control event, it is important ex-ante source evidence that valuation/liquidation concerns were being documented before that event. But the statement inside the draft does not, by itself, prove the content, date, recipient or receipt of each underlying notification. Those must be matched to emails, filings, acknowledgements or other primary transmission records.

May versus June 2018: the author's later filing recollection

In July 2023 Espejo wrote that the May 2018 economic note appeared not to have been filed, whereas the June 2018 economic note was filed and referred to the relevant data. For repository purposes the correct statuses are therefore:

  • May note: author-reported apparently not filed; not “late” or “extemporaneous”.
  • June note: author-reported filed; not yet “court-confirmed filed” until matched to the docket entry, receipt or certified index.

Rule for “extemporaneous”

Use the term only where the record establishes (a) the procedural act and applicable deadline, (b) the actual filing/service date, and (c), where relevant, the court's treatment or procedural consequence. Not filed ≠ filed late. Later discovery ≠ late filing. A contemporaneous document ≠ automatic court notice.

Source-integrity controls

  • Name control: David Espejo Navarro. Search typo variants such as “Expejo” and “Esperjo”, but normalize the public index to the verified name.
  • 11 June cover discrepancy: preserve “32/2012” as printed; explain that the body/context concerns 36/2012. Never silently alter the source.

  • Client input versus expert opinion: briefs, chronology notes and instructions supplied to the expert remain a different evidence class from eXW-authored analysis.
  • Filing status: author recollection, lawyer transmission, filing receipt, docket incorporation and judicial citation are separate evidence grades.
  • Privacy and reliance: no private contact details, fee material, family information, confidential strategy or restricted full-report reproduction on the public page.
Evidential limit. Expert analysis is evidence, not adjudication. It may support or undermine a factual/economic proposition, notice chronology or methodology challenge; it does not by itself establish misconduct, knowledge, causation, liability or criminal intent by another actor. Those propositions require their own sources and legal tests.
