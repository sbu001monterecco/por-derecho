PROVENANCE · FILING · TIMELINESS

Four questions must never be collapsed

1 · Created

Did the analysis exist, and are its date, authorship and version reliable?

2 · Transmitted

Who received it, by what channel, and is receipt evidenced?

3 · Filed

Was it formally incorporated into the relevant piece and under which annex number?

4 · Timely?

What deadline applied, and when was the document actually filed or served?

Contemporaneous notice in May 2018

The draft records that LPB had communicated valuation objections and an intention to obtain independent analysis before the 7 June physical-control event. It is ex ante evidence that the issue was being documented; it does not by itself prove the content, date, recipient or receipt of each underlying notice.

May versus June: clarification and an unresolved document match

In July 2023 Espejo recalled that the May economic note appeared not to have been filed and that the June note was filed. That recollection is provenance evidence, not a substitute for the certified docket.

Unresolved document match: it has not yet been established that the retrospectively mentioned “May economic note” is exactly the 10 May 2018 valuation/liquidation draft.

Rule for “extemporaneous”

Use the term only when the act, applicable deadline and actual filing/service date are established. Not filed ≠ filed late. Discovered later ≠ late filing. A contemporaneous document ≠ automatic court notice.

INFERENCE BOUNDARY

What the sequence establishes—and what it does not

It establishes

Existence of defined items, signature of four finals, transmission, selection of two annexes, a procedural role and identified judicial use.

It does not establish

That every recipient knew every conclusion, that every report was filed, or that every report had effect across every legal track.

It does not support an inference of

Alteration, illegality, knowledge, causation, responsibility or criminal intent from a version, access or recording discrepancy alone.

Hearing recording. Later correspondence mentions three files and, on the durations stated, a raw difference of approximately 42 minutes between the wall-clock interval and total file runtime. A recess could explain some or all of it. A later request for Espejo to review the recording also initially omitted the recording attachment. No manipulation, deletion or incompleteness is alleged without originals, metadata, hashes and/or a certified court copy.

Open evidence and custody questions

  1. Whether the expert obtained and reviewed the exact court-file versions before giving evidence.
  2. A certified audiovisual index or complete court copy and a chronology of recesses or off-record periods.
  3. Location and review of the final LPB appeal as regards the access/recording issue.
  4. A complete matrix of versions, custody, annexing, filing, judicial citation and SHA-256 hashes for each material report.
  5. Independent authentication of any precise historic VIDISPIR link before it is published as verified.
  6. Durable custody of originals that may currently exist only as working copies.
