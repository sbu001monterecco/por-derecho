The mandatory July 2021 reconciliation point

The signed internal certification of 20 July 2021 described 262 properties: 54 attributed to CAM, 190 to LPB and 18 to third parties; no signed LOI; comprehensive due diligence not yet started; and a transaction that could still fail. That document is a mandatory control point against the earlier webinar wording.
