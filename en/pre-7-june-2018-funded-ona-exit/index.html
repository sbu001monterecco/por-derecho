CONTROLLED CUTOFF · CLOSE OF 6 JUNE 2018

Before 7 June 2018: the funded ONA exit was already in execution

The record shows a coordinated attempt to combine a professional operator, bridge capital, a EUR 26 million acquisition route, staged security, professional due diligence and a supervised exit from Insolvency Proceeding 36/2012.

Executive finding

No unconditional cash drawdown or completed court exit is proved by 6 June. But a multi-route package was already in execution: the ONA/Clubotel lease and suspensive annex had been signed; the Lagune/Elaia own-funds route existed; EG/Dentons were working a marked bridge term sheet and diligence; Stoneweg had been met and security prepared; Cuatrecasas and experts had assembled the file; and specialist insolvency counsel had received the court record.

Meaning of “funded exit”

The architecture was intended to produce and deploy sufficient funds. The phrase does not mean every condition was satisfied, money was drawn, or the Court had completed the exit.

Unresolved mechanics matter.

The 4 June court order was being dealt with on 7–8 June and the debt calculation was challenged or clarification sought. The exit amount and mechanism must not be described as settled by 6 June.

The 7 June divide

The possession change described in the contemporaneous management record occurred immediately after the signed ONA package and while the bridge, acquisition, security, diligence and court-exit routes were active. The chronology requires route-by-route causation analysis; chronology alone is not proof of illegality, obstruction, liability or criminal conduct.

The 12 June Stoneweg/Varia offer and August term sheet are continuations of pre-existing workstreams. They rebut the idea that finance was invented only after 7 June, but do not retroactively prove an unconditional facility on 6 June.
