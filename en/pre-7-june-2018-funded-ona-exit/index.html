Owners’ initiative · documentary cutoff: 6 June 2018

The funded-exit work preceded the takeover.

The owners were pursuing several financing and acquisition routes, an ONA operating agreement and coordinated legal implementation before 7 June. These were successive and parallel efforts to pay the liabilities of Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. (LPB), recover corporate autonomy and preserve a viable operating hotel.

Gil Marer’s stated case: the ONA-led funded exit was fully in place and was sabotaged by the adverse parties. This reader places that allegation beside the dated record. ONA/Clubotel was the operating anchor; the owners initiated the work and external financiers occupied a separate role.

Security could be delivered in stages

The January instrument directly records the sequencing. The absence of an already-completed post-exit mortgage does not, by itself, show an absence of financing or interim collateral.

  1. Protect the advance

    Arrange the applicable share pledges, receivable or available-asset security and controlled payment process.

  2. Coordinate payment and exit

    Establish the payable liabilities and the court/administrator steps for satisfaction and conclusion.

  3. Complete later security

    Deliver the agreed LPB property security once the exit makes it available.

This is the documented transaction design. Gil’s position is that it could be performed. Each route’s actual approvals, delivery and outcome remain separately traceable. Read the complete security sequence.

LPB’s concurso was not the whole hotel

LPB was the insolvent property-owning company within a mixed-ownership complex. Other owners’ assets, Community governance and hotel-wide exploitation were distinct layers. Calling the whole hotel or its entire operation “in insolvency” collapses those distinctions. See the ownership and operating perimeter.

The record supports sustained commercial interest and specific concerns about access, protection and legal certainty. It does not establish that every financier withdrew for the same reason. Subsequent events must be tested against this existing work, not used to erase it.
