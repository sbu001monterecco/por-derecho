Criminal-first hindsight analysis of the €400,000 sale

Gil Marer’s stated conclusion, with the benefit of hindsight, is categorical: he alleges that the sale was a crime and that its consideration became illicit proceeds of that alleged crime. That is the complainant’s prosecution thesis for Fiscalía to investigate—not a criminal judgment presently in the record.

The requested legal route is to investigate whether disposing of estate assets in this way, at this price and through this process caused estate prejudice and amounted to disloyal administration under Article 252; if a predicate offence and the money trail are proved, the disposition, recovery and any knowing downstream use must then be examined under the confiscation rules in Article 127 and Article 301. Gil alleges actual knowledge, or alternatively conscious avoidance, by Parrilla—not from receipt alone, but from the cumulative professional and transaction chronology set out below. The primary responsibility allegation remains directed at the Administrator who controlled the disposition and estate account, reported paying himself and authorised the later counsel payment.

ACTÚA was not a safe substitute for operational reality

The €489,361.96 plan figure for the 31-property branch came from the ACTÚA framework requested by CAM. The recovered ACTÚA materials say occupation was unknown, acknowledge that the valuer did not enter 100% of the complex, attribute the “at least 61” unrefurbished-unit figure to information supplied by others and apply a uniform 57.14% physical depreciation to the functional unit. Only three of its 13 photographs are clear apartment interiors and none is visibly tied to a finca, block or access provider. GESVALT’s contrary work treated the 262 properties as a single hotel operation and assessed approximately 44 apartments as poor while the remainder were usable.

Gil’s allegation is that ACTÚA was materially false or non-representative on multiple levels and that the Administrator either knew, or consciously disregarded obvious reasons to know, that it could not safely support this disposal. Gil characterises the latter possibility as wilful and criminal recklessness. The present record proves the methodological warnings and the existence of contrary professional material; knowledge, deliberate disregard, the applicable offence elements and criminal purpose are matters for investigation.

The reception/main entrance and access, premises, pools and solarium are functionally central to the hotel. GESVALT’s unitary approach supports examination of hotel-wide loss when those components were separated. It does not mean that the legal standalone value of the 31 fincas automatically equals GESVALT’s €26.4 million whole-hotel valuation. The allegation is not centred on arithmetic. Even if a professional fee remained due, Gil/Patricia say the Administrator crossed the line by entering the lawyer–client fee relationship while controlling disputed estate liquidity, linking a payment assurance and a purported “peace” to the Parrilla→Jiménez transition, and benefiting—together with CAM’s downstream perimeter—from reduced resistance to his decisions. The Administrator’s reports disclose the €400,000 account/liquidity sequence, his self-payment and the later Parrilla payment. Gil directly confirms that the sale money was the only source then available and therefore alleges that the €26,750 was a downstream application of illicit proceeds of the alleged sale offence. A matching bank debit has not yet been recovered, so the exact €400,000→€26,750 trace remains a combination of the Administrator’s disclosed sequence and Gil’s testimony rather than an independently completed bank trace. That distinction does not erase the payment mismatch; it defines the document Fiscalía should obtain.

Knowledge allegation: Gil alleges that Parrilla actually knew, or consciously avoided knowing, that he was receiving money derived from the alleged unlawful sale and that the payment formed part of the Administrator’s strategic intervention. He is not pleaded as a chance recipient: the basis is cumulative—specialist insolvency mandate; formal warnings and instruction conflicts in 2016–2017; the 2018 sale challenge; the 2019 non-convalidation; his fee record; and the 2020 “peace”/payment-assurance handover—not receipt alone. Specialisation does not establish mens rea by itself, but “he merely received it” does not answer the pleaded direct exposure. No criminal judgment presently establishes knowledge, participation or an Article 301 offence; invoice, authority, account debit, disclosure and restitution remain compulsory production.
