Conflict, procedural good faith and possible crime: element tests, not labels

LEGAL PROFESSION · ART. 51

The Spanish General Statute requires abstention where former-client information or knowledge from other matters could improperly benefit a new client to the former client’s detriment, and applies the rules across the firm as a whole.

BOE · General Statute of the Legal Profession

LEC · ART. 247

Participants in litigation must act in procedural good faith; courts can reject procedural fraud/abuse and refer potentially professional misconduct to the relevant professional body.

BOE · Civil Procedure Act

PENAL CODE · ART. 250.1.7

Procedural fraud requires manipulation of evidence or analogous procedural fraud causing judicial error and an economically prejudicial decision. This is an element test, not a present finding.

BOE · Penal Code

RICPE: recorded conflict disclosure does not end the inquiry

Later audited accounts record that Acosta Matos disclosed a conflict, abstained from certain deliberations/votes and that a Control Unit process produced a favourable report. That is counter-evidence and must remain visible.

Gil Marer alleges that the control was merely formal or inadequate. Testing that allegation requires the underlying conflict register, minutes, Control Unit report, real recusal mechanics, access records, communications, origination/negotiation history and the information disclosed about Sun Park ownership, insolvency and due diligence.
