Unitary reconstruction · primary-record test

Five documentary bridges that must exist if the present outcome is lawful.

This page does not ask the reader to accept one complete allegation. It isolates five conversions of authority, debt, control and value and asks for the primary record that lawfully connects one state to the next.

Method: proposition → located evidence → strongest contrary explanation → missing controlling record → revisable conclusion. A missing record is not proof that it never existed.

1 · Community / CEXP → successor hotel-operation authority

Located tension: the 2011 Community record treats hotel exploitation/tourism as outside ordinary Community competence; a separate CEXP governance record still exists in 2017; the 4-Feb-2022 Owners’ Community record identifies José Daniel Acosta Matos as president of the Owners’ Community and purports to approve project/licensing/unified-operation matters; HNT later becomes the hotel economic-unit vehicle.

Produce: the valid CEXP dissolution, replacement, succession or transfer instrument; the legal basis for any Owners’ Community exercise of hotel-operation authority; and the instrument by which HNT/current operator acquired exploitation rights distinct from title or corporate segregation.

Boundary: the reviewed source does not establish that José Daniel Acosta Matos was president of CEXP.

2 · CEXP maintenance-copy compliance → Monterecco/Pink possession or operation

Located evidence: the strongest controlled May-2012 material attributes the relevant copies to CEXP maintenance and the obligation to CEXP rather than Monterecco/Pink/LPB.

Produce: notarial act, signed receipt, attendees and capacities, exact key/unit schedule, and the appellate document showing how a CEXP maintenance-copy compliance act became evidence of Monterecco possession, operation or acceptance in its own capacity.

Boundary: the identity of the individual physical deliverer is secondary and remains controlled by the primary receipt/notarial record.

3 · Banking obligation → valid default / acceleration → Mortgage Enforcement 90/2012

Located tension: the second facility fixes its first principal instalment on 1-Jan-2012, while the 29-Nov-2011 Bankia demand accelerated the full €839,441 capital and the later initiating filing alleged principal-amortisation non-payment from July 2011.

Produce: complete monthly debt certificate, native statements, notice/service and cure record, contractual acceleration basis, and the complete EH 90/2012 docket.

Strongest defence: interest, swap, cross-default or another contractual trigger may supply a lawful explanation; the chronology alone does not decide validity.

4 · Recognised credit → €13,168,082.02 → satisfaction / title / residual

Located correction: several earlier arithmetic anomalies have been resolved. Repeated numbers can perform different legal/accounting functions.

Produce: one transaction-level single-satisfaction ledger from the original facilities through Bankia, SAREB, PH122, CAM, Protocols 869/870, Protocol 457, EH 90/2012, the separate €400,000/31-finca branch, cancellation, dación/adjudication and any residual or surplus.

Boundary: repeated amounts do not prove double recovery, document falsity or unjust enrichment without satisfaction and accounting evidence.

5 · Developed ONA / Clubotel funded exit → precise reason it did not close

Located evidence: the record supports a developed multi-route funded-exit architecture, including operating, finance, creditor-payment/consignation and later Clubotel participation work.

Produce: exact financing available, conditions precedent, security package, creditor denominator, required court/administrator/creditor cooperation, closing timetable and the actor-specific point at which each condition failed.

Boundary: signed, approved or conditional finance evidence is not the same as completed drawdown.
