Reading rule. Primary judicial text → condition or limit → later event → evidence of compliance → unresolved question.

LPB estate ≠ 262-finca functional valuation ≠ all Sun Park ≠ productive unit ≠ exploitation rights.

A judicial authorisation, clarification, deed, registry entry and liquidation account perform different functions. One does not automatically prove the next. Asset perimeter: what the word “hotel” cannot prove

159 LPB fincas

The mortgaged branch is enumerated in the plan, approval and Protocol 457. The judicial and notarial route is proved; the complete registry/cancellation pack and final debt reconciliation are not.

Matkator 8584 and 8588

Historic notes identify Matkator; both fincas are omitted from the examined inventory/approval/deed lists. ACTÚA valued them within a broader functional perimeter, but valuation transfers no title. No judicial transfer bridge has been identified.

29 premises + 2 pools/solarium

A separate EUR 400,000 branch. A 2018 deed, 2019 refusal to validate, 2021 re-approval and omission from Protocol 457 mean title and money require their own chain.

Operation, keys and business

No examined operative text retrospectively transfers CEXP/Community rights, licences, furniture, clientele, bookings, pre-title income or operation of the entire complex. Each right requires its own contract, consent, title or administrative act.

The 2018 → 2022 control gap

The 26 June 2018 order stayed realisation; it did not grant material control. The 24 February 2021 order recorded conflicting positions about possession, works and mixed title but granted CAM no authority over the entire complex. Protocol 457 stated delivery on 21 February 2022 for its schedule.

Current result. For each transition in access, keys, security, works, operation and income between June 2018 and the 2022 deed: NO PRIMARY JUDICIAL AUTHORISATION YET IDENTIFIED. This does not exclude valid non-judicial authority; it requires that authority to be identified and linked to the corresponding asset, actor, date and economic benefit.
