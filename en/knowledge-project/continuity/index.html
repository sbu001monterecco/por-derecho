What is preserved

Reusable intelligence, not conversational dependence.

The continuity layer preserves the One Asset / One History thesis, evidential categories, canonical source/proposition model, ledgers, anti-duplication strategy, preliminary corpus/token ranges, staged processing programme, publication firewall, book architecture, Fundación Por Derecho/Havidia boundaries, audience/resource pathways, execution gates and unresolved work.

It is designed so a future researcher can recover the project's operating model without treating prior AI prose as evidence.

Public / private boundary

Continuity does not require publishing protected material.

Privileged advice, litigation strategy, private personal data, confidential correspondence, protected sources, reserved tax information, unredacted audio and other restricted material remain outside the public GitHub continuity pack. Where a task depends on them, a future thread must search the appropriate connected private source.

Deletion gate

A source conversation is not disposable until a fresh thread can recover the work.

The project's deletion standard requires zero critical reusable-intelligence gaps, at least 99% recovery of material architecture/decisions, intentional and source-recoverable private exclusions, successful START_HERE retrieval, merger to main, and verification of the live public pages.

This is a material-intelligence continuity standard, not a claim that every conversational sentence has been copied.
