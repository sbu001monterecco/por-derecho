This page is

A guide enabling the file to be audited from beginning to end.

It organises the published award, general scheme requirements, expected production and dependencies with RICPE, RIC, European Union co-financing, property and employment.

This page is not

An assertion of an irregular subsidy or proved duplicate payment.

Coexisting instruments create a reconciliation requirement. They do not prove overlap, non-compliance, improper payment or intent without the cost, payment, aid and verification file.

THE CASE IN 7 MINUTES

Six gates an examining or control officer should be able to reconstruct.

1 · application

Who applied for what

Beneficiary, group, real sponsor, location, memorandum, timetable and exact hotel project.

2 · asset

Title and availability

Which units, common areas, rights and works formed the investment; what remained outside and under which primary source.

3 · finance

Own resources and other sources

Self-financing, RICPE/RIC, banking, suppliers, other aid and treatment of related-party transactions.

4 · execution

Eligible cost and payment

Contract, supplier, invoice, line item, asset, date, bank payment and exclusion of ineligible cost.

5 · employment

What the 60 means

Person, employer, registration, period, hours/FTE, net creation or maintenance, outsourcing and retention.

6 · outcome

Verification, settlement and closure

Interim/final conditions, inventory, inspection, payment, modification, non-compliance, sanction, correction or recovery.

CONTROL FRAMEWORK

The general scheme already contains concrete questions.

Viability and self-financing

The Regulation requires technical, economic and financial viability and generally at least 25% self-financing, subject to the rules applicable to the specific file.

Start of investment

The application must precede the start of investment and the first firm commitment affecting the project, subject to the applicable confirmation and rules.

Assets and related parties

Eligible material assets must be identifiable; related-party transactions require the treatment and, where applicable, express authorisation provided in the file.

OPEN KIMONO · BEST PRACTICE AND ITS OPPOSITE

A public-aid file should be explainable without relying on institutional trust.

Positive model

Open and reproducible Regional Incentives control

  • Beneficiary, group, asset and perimeter identified.
  • Requirements and applicable rule version fixed.
  • Other aid and finance reconciled before and after award.
  • Invoice, payment, asset and eligible cost linked.
  • Employment proved by person and period.
  • Modifications, inspections and decisions reasoned.
  • Issues and exculpatory evidence preserved.
Warning

The opposite practice

  • Award treated as payment and final compliance.
  • Unitary hotel project without a property/right map.
  • Different instruments used to avoid cost reconciliation.
  • Employment repeated in headlines without population or period.
  • Related transactions without an authorisation or valuation trail.
  • File closed without inventory, payment, verification or compliance decision.

Boundary: this comparison does not assert that GC/836/P06 contains every negative deficiency. It defines the documentary test that can confirm, correct or reject the concern.
