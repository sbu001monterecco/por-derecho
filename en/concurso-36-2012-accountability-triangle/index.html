Continuity and attribution method

Each connection requires: source → date → actor/capacity → identifiable act or omission → demonstrable knowledge → applicable duty/power → response → effect → lawful alternative explanation → procedural state. A link helps readers find and compare material; it does not itself prove the content of the arrow.

Mandatory states: verified primary fact · verified procedural identity/relationship · party position · analytical inference · context · open reference. New evidence must update the node, its edges, related pages, search, the Master Register or overlay, and the handover as one change unit.
