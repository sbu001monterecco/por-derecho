This is not only a pending-action list. It is a public call to the offices that hold the competence and the file.

The ledger also serves continuity internally, but its primary public function is to identify which authority must produce, explain or reconcile each record. Where responsibility is split between two or more offices, the full chain is shown.

Public rule

No authority is asked to answer for another. We identify the office that processes, the body that decides, the agency that issued the binding report, and the authority that manages or controls the public funding.

Public call

These offices are not being asked to adopt Project Sun Rock's hypothesis. They are being asked to do what their competence and records allow: identify the file, produce the existing document, explain the scope of the decision, and state formally when a document does not exist or belongs to another authority.

Right of reply: if any authority or entity supplies documentation that closes or corrects a question, the ledger will be updated with equivalent prominence.
