The most useful contrast: ACTÚA is approximately 63% below GESVALT's 2017 RICS value and 62% below GESVALT's June-2018 ECO value. The gap alone does not prove fraud because purposes/methods differ; the contemporaneous GESVALT critical report matters because it also identifies concrete defects in data, depreciation and physical-condition assumptions.

The ACTÚA discontinuity is not merely numerical

57.14% applied across the complex

The 1 March 2018 critical report states that ACTÚA used the same 57.14% depreciation for all apartments rather than assessing each property separately.

≈44 units in poor condition

The same report states that only approximately 44 units were in poor condition, with the remainder usable.

Inactive company ≠ abandoned hotel

The liquidation plan refers to the cessation/inactivity of LPB. That corporate proposition must not silently become “the whole Sun Park hotel was empty or unusable.”

Why the images may become self-undermining evidence

Project Sun Rock's hypothesis is that if ACTÚA's negative photographs geolocate mainly to a limited group of closed units—particularly the Blocks 4–5 / dissident-perimeter hypothesis—the images would cease to prove general abandonment and instead demonstrate physical segmentation of closure. The test is falsifiable: photo → apartment → block → owner/controller → operating status on 15 January 2018.

This page does not call ACTÚA “proven fraud.” It records a Project allegation of a materially misleading valuation narrative alongside the objective fact that a contemporaneous professional counter-report identified specific methodological and physical-condition defects.

The registry “crime scene”: all 262 properties

The horizontal-property universe contains 220 apartments + 40 commercial/service units + 2 solarium/pool properties. The map does not replace the Registry; it connects title to physical location and chronology.
