ONA / Clubotel: the funded-exit sequence before and after 7 June 2018

The documents place ONA / Clubotel La Dorada, S.L.'s proposed acquisition and operating role inside a coordinated strategy to determine the debt properly payable, finance payment or consignation to creditors and enable Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. to exit Proceeding 36/2012. The record does not fit a simple description of ONA as an isolated rival bidder.

Limit: documented facts, attributed contemporaneous statements, allegations and criminal-investigation hypotheses remain separate. No unadjudicated criminal guilt or jointly adopted witness statement is asserted.

Before 7 June

  1. April 2018: the project file contains an ONA LOI and business plan.
  2. 26 April: Carlos Sanz asked the Insolvency Administrator for offer terms, the amount to improve and deadline.
  3. 27 April: the Administrator did not provide that commercial information and directed Carlos to formal appearance and a request through the court.
  4. 6 June: ONA industrial-lease documentation is in the record. The operating/acquisition and financing architecture therefore materially existed before the 7 June event.

After 7 June

  1. Financing and exit work continued, so causation must be proved component by component.
  2. In September 2018 Carlos Sanz, Daniel Irigoyen, Cuatrecasas, Juan Tomás Parrilla and the LPB/Aweswell side worked on quantifying what was required to exit the proceeding.
  3. Daniel Irigoyen contemporaneously reported that he had told the judge financing was available to exit by payment/consignation to creditors.
  4. Cuatrecasas correspondence considered removing a subtle ONA reference from one particular filing.
  5. In 2019 Clubotel La Dorada, S.L. sought formal appearance through Daniel Irigoyen and challenged the refusal; its formal interest was framed as acquisition of Sun Park and access to procedural information.

Criminal-investigation lens

act/omission → knowledge → duty or transaction dependency → causal effect → intent indicators → benefit/effect → contrary evidence → proof gap. For private actors, the question is whether there was knowing, intentional unlawful interference with the funded exit. For the Administrator and judge, duty, knowledge, causation and legally relevant mens rea must be proved; adverse outcomes alone are insufficient.
