Proceeding 36/2012 · ONA / Clubotel · visual reconstruction

A funded exit was already in motion. The criminal question is whether it was deliberately sabotaged.

The record places ONA / Clubotel La Dorada inside a coordinated architecture to quantify the debt, finance payment or consignation to creditors and take Luchy Playa Blanca out of the insolvency proceeding. That architecture materially existed before 7 June 2018 and continued afterward.

Evidential limit: “deliberate sabotage” is used here as a criminal-investigation hypothesis, not as a conviction or adjudicated fact. Attribution is made act by act and actor by actor, separating documents, attributed testimony, inference, allegation and proof gaps.

Working criminal formulation: possible deliberate sabotage of a funded exit

Hypothesis: once a materially developed funded exit existed, specific private acts and institutional acts or omissions may have been used — with knowledge of the transaction and its critical dependencies — to deliberately frustrate, weaken or increase the cost of that exit, impair access, diligence, possession, operational continuity or procedural certainty, and preserve or shift practical advantage toward the adverse perimeter.

The investigation must determine whether this was a sequence of lawful decisions or mistakes, or whether communications, instructions, coordination patterns, benefits, concealment or misuse of authority can prove knowing unlawful interference. Chronology and later benefit are contextual indicators; alone they do not prove criminal intent or a collective criminal agreement.

Attribution: who sits in the chain and what can presently be said

DIRECT EXECUTION ATTRIBUTION

Laura Acosta Matos

The 7 June dossier records sworn judicial testimony attributing operational instructions during the material takeover to Laura Acosta Matos. That supports a direct factual place in the execution chain according to that witness. It does not by itself prove criminal intent or full knowledge of ONA.

DO NOT ATTRIBUTE THE PHYSICAL ACT WITHOUT PROOF

José Daniel Acosta Matos

The same testimony stated that José Daniel Acosta Matos was not present that day. Investigation must separate physical presence from possible prior knowledge, instructions, coordination, later role and benefit. The physical execution of 7 June should not be attributed to him without additional evidence.

INSTITUTIONAL ACT TO TEST

Insolvency Administrator

The record places him as recipient of Carlos Sanz's April approach; the 7 June dossier records the 18 May security/access preservation initiative and his later explanation concerning access to LPB premises and broken locks if necessary. The criminal question is whether he knew the limits and the funded exit and whether any act or omission deliberately facilitated an excess of authority or private advantage. Formal redirection or a poor decision alone is insufficient.

JUDICIAL CONTROL TO RECONSTRUCT

Magistrate-Judge

No order granting CAM possession of the whole hotel has been located in the reviewed record. Decision by decision, the inquiry must reconstruct what the court knew about financing, material control and appearance, what protective action was legally available, and what was done or omitted. Any criminal hypothesis requires duty, knowledge, causation and legally relevant mens rea; an adverse ruling is not a crime.

EXECUTION CHAIN

Community / security / material executors

Contracts, orders, incident reports, CCTV, access logs, keys, communications and the exact scope of the security mandate must identify who converted preservation or limited-access authority into material control, if that occurred, and under whose instructions.

FUNDED-EXIT SOURCES / WITNESSES

ONA / Clubotel · Carlos Sanz · Daniel Irigoyen

Their principal function on this page is evidential: conditions, financing, closing dependencies, required access, communications with the Administrator/court, concrete impairment and later continuity. Cuatrecasas and other advisers must be analysed by their own conduct and separate sources, not by mere participation in the transaction.
