Proceeding 36/2012 · ONA / Clubotel · visual reconstruction

A funded exit was already in motion. The criminal question is whether it was deliberately sabotaged.

The record places ONA / Clubotel La Dorada inside a coordinated architecture to quantify debt, finance payment or consignation to creditors and take Luchy Playa Blanca out of the insolvency proceeding. That architecture materially existed before 7 June 2018 and continued afterward.

Evidential limit: “deliberate sabotage” is a criminal-investigation hypothesis, not a conviction or adjudicated fact. Attribution is act-specific and actor-specific, separating documents, attributed testimony, inference, allegation, contrary evidence and unresolved gaps.

Working criminal formulation: possible deliberate sabotage of a funded exit

Hypothesis: once a materially developed funded exit existed, specific private acts and institutional acts or omissions may have been used — with knowledge of the transaction and its critical dependencies — to deliberately frustrate, weaken or increase the cost of that exit, impair access, diligence, possession, operational continuity or procedural certainty, and preserve or shift practical advantage toward the adverse perimeter.

The investigation must determine whether this was a sequence of lawful decisions or mistakes, or whether communications, instructions, coordination, benefits, concealment or misuse of authority can prove knowing unlawful interference. Chronology and later benefit are contextual indicators; alone they do not prove criminal intent or a collective agreement.

Attribution: who sits in the chain and what can presently be said

DIRECT EXECUTION ATTRIBUTION

Laura Acosta Matos

The 7 June dossier records sworn judicial testimony attributing operational instructions during the material takeover to Laura Acosta Matos. That supports a direct factual place in the execution chain according to that witness. It does not by itself prove criminal intent or full knowledge of ONA.

DO NOT ATTRIBUTE THE PHYSICAL ACT WITHOUT PROOF

José Daniel Acosta Matos

The same testimony stated that he was not present that day. Physical presence, prior knowledge, instructions, coordination, later role and benefit must be separated.

INSTITUTIONAL ACT TO TEST

Insolvency Administrator

The record documents receipt of Carlos Sanz's April approach and places the IA in the May security/access pathway. The criminal question is whether he knew the limits and the funded exit and whether any act or omission deliberately facilitated excess authority or private advantage. A poor decision or formal redirection is not enough.

JUDICIAL CONTROL TO RECONSTRUCT

Magistrate-Judge

No order granting CAM possession of the whole hotel has been located in the reviewed record. Decision by decision, reconstruct what the court knew, what protection was legally available and what was done or omitted. An adverse ruling is not a crime.

EXECUTION CHAIN

Community / security / material executors

Contracts, orders, incident reports, CCTV, logs, keys, communications and the exact security mandate must identify who converted preservation or limited access into material control, if that occurred, and under whose instructions.

FUNDED-EXIT SOURCES / WITNESSES
