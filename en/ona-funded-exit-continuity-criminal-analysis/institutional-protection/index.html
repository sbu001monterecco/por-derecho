Institutional protection · Proceeding 36/2012 · ONA / Clubotel · 7 June 2018

Who acted, who knew, who had to protect, and who investigated?

Reverse engineering does not end with identifying who obtained or exercised material control. If a funded exit existed that could be tested as a route to pay or consign creditors, the next question is what protection the Insolvency Administrator, the Court, the prosecution service and other authorities provided while assets, rights, evidence and competing exit routes could still be preserved.

Controlling limit: this ledger does not presume a criminal organisation, institutional collusion or collective guilt. Montelanza/Molina, the later Acosta Matos perimeter, private actors, the Insolvency Administrator, the Magistrate-Judge and prosecution authorities are assessed by their own conduct, date, knowledge, capacity, duty, causation and contrary evidence. The 2018 provisional criminal dismissal and its confirmation on appeal remain mandatory adverse evidence.

The chain should no longer be read in fragments

The working hypothesis is a functional continuity to test, not inherited criminal liability: Montelanza–Molina property/governance roots → later accumulation of CAM/Acosta Matos positions → material control → need for insolvency/judicial protection → investigative response → later economic outcome.

1 · private perimeterMontelanza / Molina → CAM / Acosta Matos

Reconstruct patrimonial, corporate, office-holder, knowledge and functional continuity without presuming criminal continuity.

2 · 7 JuneMaterial control

3 · protectionIA + Court

What they knew, what they could protect and which measures they adopted, rejected or left unresolved.

4 · investigationProsecution / Judicial Police

Trace the later result entity by entity without treating later benefit as retroactive proof of intent.

Institutional attribution must answer four questions

PRIVATE ACTORS

What did they do and what did they know?

The public record already contains actor-specific attributions around 7 June. The next layer is knowledge of ONA, title/possession limits, prior communications, instructions, security, keys, access, due diligence and later benefit.

Lawful alternative valid exercise of credit/title rights, legitimate preservation and independent private decisions.

INSOLVENCY ADMINISTRATOR

What did he protect, authorise or fail to correct?

The record documents receipt of Carlos Sanz's April approach and places the IA in the May security/access pathway. The native scope of authority must be compared literally with execution and with preservation of estate value and exit alternatives.

Lawful alternative reasonable insolvency administration, necessary security, competence limits and formal routing of third parties.

COURT

Where was judicial protection?

Decision by decision, reconstruct what the court knew about material control, ONA, financing, appearance, extra-insolvency assets and protective measures; what was requested, what was decided and with what effect.

Lawful alternative legitimate judicial discretion, procedural limits, missing legal prerequisites or insufficient proof at the relevant time.

PROSECUTION SERVICE

What was actually investigated?

The question is not why prosecutors did not charge. It is which concrete investigative steps were taken to prove or reject instructions, excess of authority, knowledge of the funded exit, causation, communications, flows, benefit and possible institutional conduct.

Lawful alternative insufficient indicia, reasoned closure, different territorial/material competence or later evidence not available at the time.

The question institutions should be able to answer

If a funded alternative was materially developed, the record should allow these questions to be answered with documents rather than assumptions:

  • Who knew of the exit, and from when?
  • Who gave each material instruction?
  • What title authorised each access, exclusion or intervention?
  • Who knew LPB did not equal the whole of Sun Park?
  • What did the IA do to preserve value and alternatives?
  • What concrete protection was requested from the Court?
  • What did the Court decide and what remained undecided?
  • What evidence did prosecutors obtain?
  • Were communications, CCTV, logs, phones and banking material preserved?
  • Was the functional Montelanza/Molina → Community → CAM/Acosta Matos relationship reconstructed?
  • Was ONA examined as a possible target/motive of interference or merely as a failed transaction?
  • Was the later economic outcome traced entity by entity?

Absence of protection or of a particular investigative step does not prove complicity. But where notice, potential capacity to act and a material result coexist, the institutional response should be reconstructable and auditable.
