Scale Capital · institutional underwriting

Platform capital and project capital solve different problems.

First Capital and Scale Capital are capital roles, not fixed investor identities or rigid ticket bands. First Capital is the faster-liquidity role; Scale Capital is the larger or longer-horizon institutional/private-bank/private-credit role. Actual amount, tenor, structure and economics are counterparty-specific. Platform and ProjectCo capital can form part of one relationship, but they are not interchangeable and one does not automatically support the other.

Conversion path

From an email to an investable decision.

A positive first response is the beginning of underwriting, not the end of fundraising. The process becomes progressively more specific only as mandate, fit and information rights are established.

1 · Public credibility review

Initial email plus this public institutional architecture.

2 · Substantive response & routing

The institution identifies whether a relevant principal-capital, credit, equity or relationship owner exists.

3 · Senior management call

Confirm mandate, asset stage, capital type, jurisdiction and the milestones required to proceed.

4 · Controlled private disclosure

Confidentiality/NDA where appropriate, followed by a concise tailored pack rather than indiscriminate data-room access.

5 · Preliminary underwriting

Transaction perimeter, sources & uses, business plan, planning, operator route, security, Sponsor equity and downside cases.

6 · Indicative interest

Structure, information requests, preliminary parameters or a reasoned decision not to proceed.

7 · Full DD & approvals

Legal, technical, financial, tax, KYC/AML, investment/credit committee and execution workstreams.

8 · Term sheet / LOI / offer

Only a documented instrument from an authorised counterparty should be treated as terms or an offer.

9 · Definitive documents & closing

Conditions precedent, funds flow, security, governance and post-close reporting become operative only when documented.

Underwriting discipline

Build value from the hotel economics upward.

The institutional case is intended to be constructed from verifiable operating and asset assumptions rather than a promotional headline recovery value.

Operating case

Keys/category → occupancy → ADR/RevPAR → revenue → GOP/EBITDA → stabilisation.

Asset / development case

Acquisition basis → capex/development cost → contingency → operator/brand route → comparable evidence → valuation methodology.

Capital case

Sponsor equity → senior/project capital → defined support → security/cash control → downside → refinancing/exit.

No double counting. Public incentives, RIC-compatible capital where lawful, grants, senior debt, seller/deferred funding, Sponsor equity and other capital must be reconciled through one complete sources-and-uses model. Eligibility is never assumed merely because a programme exists.
