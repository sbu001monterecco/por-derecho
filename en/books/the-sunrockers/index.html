The SunRockers

Before it was a legal battle, it was a place people belonged.

An illustrated and oral-history book about Sun Park Living in Playa Blanca: longer stays, friendship, participation, activity, reinvention and the people who repeatedly returned. It recovers the human life of the place before later legal and financial history came to dominate its public memory.

The place

Playa Blanca before the case file

The book returns to Sun Park as lived experience: terraces, pool, conversations, activities, repeat stays, friendships and the proposition that a longer stay could become participation rather than passive tourism.

The memory project

Guests, staff and returning voices

The archive is only one side. The finished book is designed to collect memories, photographs and contrary as well as affectionate recollections from people who were actually there, creating a richer record than any later dispute could provide.

Ethos

Life can get larger, not smaller

The SunRockers idea connects community, friendship, contribution and later-life possibility. Adam J. Jackson’s The Ethos is treated as a philosophical reference in that wider conversation, not as an endorsement of this book or the legal project.
