Book 2 · AI and documentary capability

Law-mower Man

They had the files. He found a way to read them all.

A nonfiction exploration of what changes when one person, after years of legal and documentary complexity, gains AI-assisted capacity to search, compare, connect and keep questioning the entire record. The machine does not supply the truth. It changes the economics of finding, testing and remembering evidence.

The central question

What changes when an individual can finally interrogate the whole record?

The shift is one of capability and information asymmetry. AI can help search enormous archives, resolve entities, align chronologies, detect contradictions and keep questions alive across documents that no single human could economically reread in full every time.

The discipline

Memory without automation of judgment

AI is treated as an assisted memory and research layer, not an automated judge. Provenance, source verification, hallucination risk, adverse evidence and human legal judgment remain essential.

The transformation

Buried → searching → mapping → connecting → testing → remembering.

Sun Park / MYND Yaiza provides the lived laboratory, but the book's wider subject is what happens when the economics of documentary investigation change for individuals, lawyers, journalists and institutions.
