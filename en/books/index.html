Gil Marer · publishing programme

Books in publication · coming shortly

One lived history is generating several different books: human, technological, institutional, community and financial. Each has its own reader promise. All are being developed against the same source discipline.

Working titles and publication sequence may evolve. “In publication” means actively being developed for publication; it does not imply that a retail release date or preorder has already been announced.

Flagship · in publication

Reason to Believe

One Asset. One History.

Sun Park, Playa Blanca, the SunRockers, the rupture, MYND Yaiza and the attempt to reconstruct one continuous human, economic and institutional history while its ending is still being written.

Book 2 · in publication

Law-mower Man

What Happens When AI Reads the Whole Case?

A nonfiction exploration of AI-assisted legal memory, information asymmetry and what changes when one person can keep asking questions across millions of words and fragmented proceedings. Working title pending clearance.

Book 3 · in publication

The SunRockers

Before it was a legal battle, it was a place people belonged.

An illustrated/oral-history direction for Sun Park Living: longer stays, friendship, activities, reinvention, memories and the human ethos of Playa Blanca. Publication depends in part on image, memory and consent clearance.

Book 4 · in publication

Justice in Pieces

When one history becomes many files.

A broader institutional investigation into effective judicial protection, courts, prosecution, insolvency, procedural fragmentation, institutional memory and the Canary Islands/Las Palmas research question — tested comparatively rather than assumed.

Book 5 · special situations · in publication

Special Situations

The underworld of claims trading, defaults and distressed opportunity.

A professional-investigative book about the market that appears when someone else cannot pay: NPLs, distressed claims, servicing, loan sales, enforcement, insolvency, special-situations capital and the conversion of default into investment opportunity.

Sun Park provides one laboratory — including the Bankia → SAREB → Meridian → Promontoria/PH122/Cerberus → later-creditor route — but the book must expand beyond a single case and distinguish legitimate distressed investing from disputed or abusive conduct.

One publishing system

Different books. One evidence discipline.

The books are not five retellings of the same dispute. Reason to Believe carries the human story; Law-mower Man the AI/legal-memory discovery; The SunRockers the community memory; Justice in Pieces the institutional question; and Special Situations the distressed-credit and claims-trading market.

The public Knowledge Project remains the source-control counterpart. Allegations are not converted into findings merely because they become narrative material.
