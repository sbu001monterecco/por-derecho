Book 6 · working title

4 Green Houses, One Red Hotel

One hotel. Years of advice. A former adviser becomes an enforcing creditor. Show the chain.

A document-led examination of professional advice around Sun Park: what was accepted, billed, known, done, left undone, communicated, left uncommunicated and handed over—and what changed when Cuatrecasas later pursued its own claimed fees against Matkator through La Laguna proceedings connected to the same physical hotel reality.

LinkedInWhatsAppEmail

The title is a literary provocation, not a finding. The manuscript distinguishes documents, party allegations, legal hypotheses, contrary evidence, defences and unresolved proof. The full manuscript remains private.

The central inversion: the firm was engaged inside a rescue, finance, insolvency and asset-protection perimeter. It later became claimant and executant against an entity and property within that same hotel perimeter. That transition does not establish wrongdoing. It means that the later fee enforcement cannot responsibly be understood without reconstructing the earlier mandate and knowledge.
Public book architecture updated · 31 August 2026

The evidential spine

The client did not hire a file. The book will not investigate one.

  1. MandateWho retained which Cuatrecasas entity, for what work and on whose instructions?
  2. KnowledgeWhat was known about possession, control, financing, title, Matkator, LPB and Concurso 36/2012?
  3. Act or omissionWhat protection, warning, implementation or evidence-preservation step existed—or was missing?
  4. CommunicationWhat was told to the client, funders, successor lawyers and courts, and what remained silent?
  5. Fee inversionHow did invoice, payer, pagaré, debtor, title, execution, asset and possible cession connect?
  6. Cause and remedyWhat changed because of a proved act, and what is the strongest innocent explanation?

No guilt by chronology: an adverse result, later benefit, silence, a shared asset or the existence of another claim against the same firm does not prove negligence, fraud, collusion, intention or causation. Every link requires its own source, duty, decision, counterfactual and loss.

The six-part professional audit

Acts, omissions, silence, enablement and the later attempt to recover fees.

What the firm did

The record includes real professional work, advice, filings, financing activity and protective intervention. The book rejects the simplistic claim that nobody did anything.

What may have been omitted

The client side alleges that unitary risks were treated through fragmented workstreams and that material safeguards, updates, implementation or evidence preservation may have been absent.

What was communicated—or not

The inquiry separates express advice, warnings, negotiation, silence, handover and later court representations. Silence matters only where a communication duty and material consequence can be proved.

What may have been enabled

“Enablement” is a causal allegation, not association. It requires proof that a defined professional act or omission materially facilitated or aggravated an identifiable outcome pursued or enjoyed by another actor.

Fees due or disputed

The book does not assume that no fee was due. It demands the complete chain: client, instructor, beneficiary, invoice, payer, pagaré, debtor, judicial title, asset, adjudication, possible cession and final beneficiary.

Later affirmative enforcement

Cambiario 1048/2019 and ETJ 163/2020 require an act-by-act reconstruction: service, notice, former-client conflict, asset identity, valuation, opposition to verification, adjudication and any cession decision.

A finite criminal-procedural question

Procedural fraud is a hypothesis to prove—not a publicity label.

The client side has advanced a confined estafa procesal hypothesis in DP 748/2026. The book may report and test that hypothesis. It will not state that Cuatrecasas committed an offence unless a competent process establishes the necessary elements.

Material appearance

Was the executing court asked to act on a materially false or legally incomplete appearance concerning debt, debtor, service, property identity, integration, value, verification, adjudication or cession?

Knowledge and intent

Who knew the relevant facts, what omission was legally material, and is there evidence of fraudulent purpose rather than error, disputed advice or ordinary enforcement?

Judicial and patrimonial effect

Did the alleged conduct cause judicial error and a prejudicial patrimonial resolution or disposition; who lost; and who obtained or expected an improper benefit?

Boundary: disputed billing, deficient professional performance, an adverse court order or later third-party benefit does not by itself satisfy this chain.

Controlled formulation: until transmission and reliance are evidenced, the book describes a directional cross-proceeding effect requiring investigation. A defence-side use of DP 748 material to seek protection in ETJ 163/2020 proves that those 2026 proceedings were connected by current counsel; it does not prove that Cuatrecasas created La Laguna in order to manipulate Concurso 36/2012.

Publication strategy

Maximum accountable pressure. Minimum evidential waste.

The project’s media strategy is to make finite, source-controlled questions impossible to ignore—not to replace proof with volume. Five releases organise the work:

The professional lifecycle. Mandate, billing, knowledge, advice, action, omission, communication and handover.
The Matkator inversion. The transition from rescue adviser to claimant and executant against a company and property inside the same hotel reality.
The fee and instrument chain. Client through final beneficiary, with every missing document visibly marked.
The La Laguna–Las Palmas map. Documented links, missing transmission and reliance, and the strongest non-fraud explanation.
The institutional answer tracker. Questions asked, documents produced, right of reply, corrections, professional-body status and court outcomes.

Right-of-reply rule: every material release should show the primary source, evidential status, strongest Cuatrecasas defence, missing proof, finite questions, response status and correction history. A non-response may be reported as a non-response by a stated date; it is not an admission.
