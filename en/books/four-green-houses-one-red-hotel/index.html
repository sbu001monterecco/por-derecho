Book 6 · working title

4 Green Houses, One Red Hotel

Property. Advice. Consequence.

A document-led examination of professional advice around one hotel already in a red-alert situation, and of the later transition from adviser to creditor-enforcement actor. The title is a literary provocation, not a finding of professional misconduct.

LinkedInWhatsAppEmail

The manuscript distinguishes documented acts, allegations, professional opinions, counterarguments and unresolved questions. Full manuscript remains private while the evidence is developed.

The central question

What is the real cost of professional advice?

The book tests mandates, knowledge, advice, action, handover, later enforcement, causation and loss without converting an adverse result into negligence by assumption.

Evidence discipline

The strongest defence belongs in the book too.

Every criticism is tested against what the advisers knew at the time, the scope of their retainer, reasonable tactical alternatives and the possibility that later outcomes would have occurred anyway.
