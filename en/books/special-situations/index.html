Book 5 · distressed credit

Special Situations

What happens to your debt when someone else sees your default as an investment opportunity?

A professional-investigative book about the market that begins where ordinary borrowers often stop seeing: NPL sales, claims trading, servicing, distressed credit, enforcement, restructuring, insolvency and special-situations capital. It asks who buys the claim, why, at what economics, who services it, and when a debt position can become a route to collateral, control or underlying asset value.

LinkedInWhatsAppEmail

“Hidden market” or “underworld” describes opacity to ordinary readers, not criminality. The book distinguishes legitimate distressed investing, hard bargaining, disputed conduct and proven illegality.

The invisible transaction

Your liability can be somebody else's asset

A defaulted obligation can be sold, discounted, serviced, restructured or enforced. The incentives of originator, purchaser, servicer, borrower, insolvency estate and collateral holder may then diverge sharply. The book makes that market legible to non-specialists without demonising the market itself.

Sun Park laboratory

Follow the credit chain

The Bankia → SAREB → Project Meridian → Promontoria Holding 122 / Cerberus perimeter → later-creditor history is one case-study route. It must be sourced precisely and expanded with Spanish, EU and international comparisons before it can support broader conclusions.

For investors, owners, lawyers, journalists and students: discount, servicing, enforcement strategy, collateral, recovery economics, information asymmetry and the point at which a credit investment may alter control of the underlying asset.
