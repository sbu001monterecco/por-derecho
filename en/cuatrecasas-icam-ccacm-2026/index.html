The appeal's procedural boundary

CCACM formally opened 193/2026, served the interested parties and received later material. On 26 June it said a submission would be joined to the file but recalled Article 118.1 of Law 39/2015: there is no indefinite reply chain, and facts or documents that could have been produced earlier may be left out.

Consequence: this page is a decision aid, not a new filing. Any future submission should answer an institutional request or identify a genuinely supervening, dated, essential fact that was not previously available.

CCACM may uphold in whole or part, dismiss or reject as inadmissible. If it identifies a formal defect and should not decide the merits, it may order retroaction to the point of the defect.

B · Partner decision or supervision

Identify who instructed, authorised, supervised or ratified own-account recovery, opposition to suspension/expert review, 70% adjudication, reiteration and reservation of assignment.

Partner status does not prove authorship.

C · Professional firm

Firm responsibility is not automatic. It requires concurrent responsibility, action for the firm's account and benefit through administrators or instructed representatives, or directly imputable conduct.

The correct object is the system applied here: client, conflict, secrecy, barriers, access, approvals and preservation.

Eight finite questions also capable of exonerating

Client and perimeter. Who was treated as client, instructor, beneficiary, invoiced entity, expected payer, note issuer and judicial debtor?
End of engagement. When and for which matters did the relationship end, and what resignation, transfer or handover proves it?
Former client. What conflict analysis preceded recovery and enforcement?
Secrecy and access. What information was available, to whom, and under what barriers or team segregation?
Decision chain. Who instructed, authorised or supervised each 2024–2026 ETJ step?
Adjudication and assignment. What review preceded the 70% adjudication and assignment reservation?
Governance. Was ethics, risk, compliance, management or equivalent consulted, and is there a record?
Exculpatory account. What documents establish the credit basis, correct debtor, team independence, barriers and approvals?

Proportionate institutional options

CCACM · 193/2026

Decide on the existing record; assess whether the limitation archive rested on a sufficiently delimited object; and, if a formal defect exists, partly uphold and order retroaction for preliminary information. No need to decide debt, ETJ merits, damages or crime.

ICAM · preliminary information

ICAM states that a complaint may be archived, sent to preliminary information or lead to a disciplinary file. The Statute permits preliminary information and gives the registering Bar competence over professional firms. The check can be limited to the eight questions.

Tenerife · territorial acts

If it received 1487/26, it can delimit court acts carried out in its territory and the individual conduct of those signing or directing them, without automatically assuming Madrid or professional-firm modules.

Coordination · avoid a vacuum

Territorial competence should allocate the examination rather than evaporate it. A reasoned decision can assign each module, preserve dates and explain exclusions.

Spanish authority and institutional support

Regulated professional firm

The General Statute subjects lawyers and professional firms to discipline; the registering Bar exercises professional-conduct and sanctioning competence over the firm.

General Statute · Articles 41, 119–120 and 128

Conflict, secrecy and collective practice

A former-client matter requires abstention where secrecy is at risk or prior knowledge may create improper advantage; conflict rules apply to the group and secrecy survives the engagement.

General Statute · Articles 22 and 51

Safeguarded preliminary information

It determines whether proceedings should start; it is not a sanction and must coexist with hearing, contradiction and defence rights.

ICAM · Professional conduct

Appeal and retroaction

Law 39/2015 limits late material, requires hearing for new facts and permits retroaction for a formal defect.

Law 39/2015 · Articles 118–119

Professional-firm inspiration without collective accusation

Cuatrecasas publicly describes a Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct applying to partners, lawyers and staff, subscription every three months, information-security policies, compliance structures and an Ethics Communication Channel. That does not prove how controls worked here; it supports a neutral question:

How was the firm's published governance architecture applied to this matter? The answer may exonerate, narrow or confirm the need for further examination.

As a non-binding comparison, the English regulator requires firms to maintain governance, systems, controls and records demonstrating compliance, with manager accountability for those systems. It is not Spanish law; it is only a model for questions and traceability.

Explicit exculpatory gate

The examination should close or narrow if records show that Matkator was correctly the debtor; the note basis and authority were clear; no material secrecy risk existed; sufficient conflict analysis, effective barriers and an independent team were in place; later steps were ordinary consequences of the title; adjudication and any assignment were properly authorised without a connected third party; and no former-client information was used to gain improper advantage.

An examination capable of exonerating is more institutional than a communication designed only to confirm an allegation.
