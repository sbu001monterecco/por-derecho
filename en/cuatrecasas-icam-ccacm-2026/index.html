ICAM / CCACM 2026: what was alleged, archived, appealed and what actually remains open.

This page does not present bar complaints as proof of misconduct. It reconstructs the documentary sequence, distinguishes the bodies involved, and separates receipt, archive, appeal, territorial referral and merits determination.

Reading rule: a complaint is an allegation. An archive based on limitation, non-professional character or territorial competence is not a substantive exoneration. A pending appeal does not mean the complaint will succeed. A “RECIBIDO” proves receipt, not admissibility or success.

The 493/26 precedent changes how 1566/26 should be described

In the first chain, ICAM's portal generated 493/26 on the same day the appeal against 434/26 was filed. The registered document was unequivocally an administrative appeal, and that challenge was later transferred and became CCACM 193/2026.

The most precise formulation for the second chain is therefore:

1487/26 → stamped appeal RE-007622/26 → technical intake 1566/26 → CCACM forwarding still to be certified.

1566/26 should not, without further proof, be described either as a second merits complaint or as an already opened CCACM file. The 493/26 precedent makes a technical-intake function plausible, but the decisive proof remains the forwarding certificate and any CCACM docket number.

Unreconciled institutional chronology

A documentary tension should be preserved without converting it into an accusation.

ICAM 434/26 · 6 March

The order describes the relevant professional relationship between the group and the firm as 2018–2020.

ICAM 1487/26 · 1 July

When analysing the 6 March 2026 LinkedIn message, the order says the lawyer-client relationship had ended “more than seven years” earlier.

Read literally, the formulations do not align without further explanation of which relationship, engagement or perimeter each order was describing. The record therefore labels this an unreconciled institutional chronology, not evidence of bad faith.

Tenerife referral and separate Canary routes

The 1 July order directs that the decision and a copy of 1487/26 be sent to the Tenerife Bar. No territorial file number has yet been located that proves receipt of that referral.

That route must remain separate from ICALPA DIP 79/2026 and DIP 80/2026, and also from the earlier 2647-ICATF reference, which arose from a prior consultation/channel and must not automatically be described as the territorial file resulting from the 1487/26 referral.
