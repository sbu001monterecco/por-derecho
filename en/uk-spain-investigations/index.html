01 / Case anatomy

Three overlapping realities, separate legal questions.

Sun Park, now commercially presented as MYND Yaiza, is the central physical asset. The question is how the rights and responsibilities around that asset changed—and whether particular actors had the power, knowledge and lawful basis for their acts.

01 · PROPERTY

Ownership, insolvency and possession

Which registered properties belonged to LPB's insolvency estate, which to other owners, and which rights authorised access, control, works and later transfers?

02 · BUSINESS

Operating rights, income and contracts

Which actual entity could contract with customers, collect income, pay costs or claim loss at each date? Hotel revenue and group investment are not the same as an individual claimant's recoverable damages.

03 · FINANCE

Debt, investment and institutional scrutiny

How did creditor positions, valuations, investor disclosures, financing and public-sector communications interact with underlying property and commercial records?

Entity separation is central. AWESWELL LIMITED, Luchy Playa Blanca, Matkator, historical operating/CEXP arrangements, other holders and individual customers cannot be merged into one plaintiff or one economic loss. A common document may matter to several routes without giving each claimant the same entitlement.

03 / Investigative questions

Where serious financial-crime investigation can add value.

The programme has working positions and a substantial record. A specialist's independence would be demonstrated by testing the strongest lawful alternatives as hard as the allegations themselves.

01 · Follow the rights before the money

Map dated legal ownership, community/insolvency authority, creditors, receivables, assignments and account beneficiaries before testing who gained or lost from each transaction.

02 · Reconcile financial and investment representations

Compare contemporaneous business and investor descriptions with property files, valuations, actual finance, investment-vehicle governance and available supervisory correspondence.

03 · Establish the UK connection event by event

Identify original UK contracts, communications, booking transactions, actual payment/refund data and their holder, location, knowledge and net consequence; do not infer UK jurisdiction from nationality or business branding.

04 · Turn a large corpus into reliable evidence

Source-version control, provenance, relevant interviews, contradictory decisions, missing originals, translations, privilege screening and disclosure-ready indexes are as important as developing a compelling theory.

High-stakes institutional questions require particular care. Regulatory notice, refusal, court delay or an influential commercial relationship does not establish collusion. Allegations must be tested person by person and institution by institution, including the statutory power, actual knowledge, contrary evidence and applicable standard.

05 / Evidential discipline

Four statuses. No shortcuts.

Source files, court outcomes, claimant allegations and investigative hypotheses do different work. They should remain labelled distinctly throughout a professional assessment.

Documented actA verified communication, filing, payment screen, deed or court act establishes its own content and date—not all proposed inferences.
Attributed allegationA stated position requiring actor-specific duty, knowledge, intent, causation and legal classification.
Contrary outcomeAdverse orders, legitimate creditor/title reliance, client refund and alternative explanations must be retained, not buried.
Open discriminatorA particular original record, transaction, witness answer or legal ruling that could materially strengthen or defeat the proposition.
