PROSPECTIVE PROJECT · PLAYA BLANCA · DUE DILIGENCE

A new hotel. A wider local opportunity.

We are examining a prospective investment at Montaña Roja in Playa Blanca. No acquisition, planning approval, financing commitment or construction decision is announced. Subject to satisfactory due diligence and all required approvals, the ambition is a high-quality new hotel with complementary commercial and public-facing space and measurable local value.

The proposition under examination

Hospitality, commerce and social value designed together.

The present concept is intentionally described at a high level. The final programme, scale, design, operator, ownership and capital structure cannot be represented as settled before the relevant diligence and approvals are complete.

01 · Hotel

A new high-quality hospitality asset

A ground-up hotel proposition that responds to the site, the applicable planning framework, operational feasibility and Lanzarote’s long-term visitor economy.

02 · Commercial space

A public-facing complement

Retail, food, service and other commercial uses that can serve guests and the wider area, with opportunities for local participation where feasibility and fair selection permit.

03 · Local compact

Benefits that can be tested

A project framework that defines how employment, training, procurement, resource use, accessibility and public-realm outcomes will be measured if the project advances.

These are ambitions under examination. They are not a planning application, investment commitment, construction programme or guarantee of delivery.

Conditions before commitment

The project must earn the right to be announced as executable.

A credible future page must show what has been verified, what remains a party assertion, what is an inference, what is a proposal and what remains an open question.

Private diligence · transaction and rights

Establish the complete perimeter

Confirm title, corporate authority, project and intellectual-property permissions, administrative positions, third-party rights, liabilities and the documents required for a clean and enforceable transaction.

  • No public assumption that every relevant right is held by one party.
  • No public transaction structure before binding documents exist.
  • No migration of confidential price, deposit, commission or ownership data to this page.
Public-law diligence · planning and delivery

Confirm the ordinary route

Establish the current planning framework and the applicable tourism, environmental, infrastructure, water, sanitation, energy, mobility, accessibility and building requirements with the competent administrations.

  • No assumption that historic reports or approvals remain effective.
  • No request for preferential treatment or a predetermined result.
  • No final capital commitment before the delivery route is credible.
Controlled disclosure. The existence of active due diligence may be public. The identities, economics, negotiation positions, private legal advice and unverified compensation theories within that diligence remain private unless separately authorised and independently verified.

Proposed Lanzarote social-value compact

A framework to be designed before promises are made.

If the opportunity reaches an investable and deliverable stage, the project should adopt a transparent compact with a baseline, feasible targets, responsible owners, measurement dates and public reporting.

01 · People

Employment, training and progression

Assess construction and operating roles, apprenticeships, vocational partnerships, management development and accessible pathways for Lanzarote and Canary Islands residents.

02 · Enterprise

Local suppliers and public-facing opportunity

Develop a fair procurement and tenant-engagement approach for suitable local food, retail, services, maintenance, technology, creative and visitor-economy businesses.

03 · Place

Water, energy, access and landscape

Test resource efficiency, climate resilience, accessibility, mobility, landscaping and public-realm benefits against technical evidence and the applicable public processes.

No job count, procurement percentage, investment amount or environmental performance figure is stated until feasibility supports it.

Institutional dialogue

Ask the right questions before committing the capital.

We seek an ordinary, transparent pre-application and technical dialogue with the competent teams of the Cabildo de Lanzarote and Yaiza Council. The purpose is to identify the applicable route and evidence—not to obtain an informal approval or negotiate the treatment of unrelated files.

The Montaña Roja dialogue is independent of the Sun Park/MYND Yaiza legacy workstream described in the open letter.
