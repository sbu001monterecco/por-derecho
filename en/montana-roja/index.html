The project direction

A hotel-led project designed for long-term operation.

The current direction is a high-quality, non-residential hotel project intended for unitary hotel exploitation, with only those complementary commercial, service and public-facing uses that are compatible with the hotel, the site and the applicable planning framework. Final scale, design, operator, ownership and capital structure remain to be established through the ordinary workstreams.

01 · Hotel

One serious hospitality proposition

A ground-up hotel project shaped by the site, current planning law, operational feasibility, market demand and the long-term visitor economy of Lanzarote.

02 · Complementary uses

Useful where they strengthen the hotel

Food, retail, service and public-facing uses may form part of the project where they are operationally useful, commercially sensible and lawfully compatible with the hotel.

03 · Local value

Benefits that can be measured

Employment, training, procurement, accessibility, resource use and public-realm outcomes should be measured against evidence if the project advances.

This page states the direction being actively pursued. It does not represent that the final design, planning entitlement, operator, financing or transaction has already been completed.

Execution due diligence

The objective is clear. The route must be proven.

Due diligence is not being used to decide whether Sun Rock wants to establish another hotel position in Playa Blanca. That strategic objective is already active. The work determines whether Montaña Roja can be the route, what must be acquired or controlled, the current legal and planning position, the executable project, its economics and the appropriate capital structure.

Illustrative project capital stack

Capital changes as the project moves from acquisition to development.

This is a public architecture, not a financing term sheet. It shows the intended order and role of capital without publishing lender terms, seller economics, leverage, pricing, security values or committed amounts.

01 · Sponsor capitalAweswell Limited · legal Sponsor/HoldCo

Equity and defined Sponsor support from the legal group layer, coordinated through the Sun Rock platform and sized for the actual transaction and lender requirements. No unlimited parent support is announced.

02 · Senior acquisition capitalBank / institutional lender

Third-party senior financing at the acquisition perimeter, expected to rank ahead of deferred seller consideration and to use controlled closing mechanics.

03 · Deferred seller considerationSeller Project Loan Notes / equivalent

Milestone-driven deferred consideration, structurally subordinated to senior finance and designed so genuine milestone delay does not automatically create a project default.

04 · Development capitalProjectCo financing when the project is financeable

Later construction/development capital sized to the verified planning, technical, cost and operating case. Its security and intercreditor structure must be compatible with the acquisition stack.

Execution roadmap

From GREEN go-forward status to an operating hotel.

The workstreams are deliberately parallel. Transaction, rights, financing, planning and technical preparation should advance together, while each irreversible commitment remains conditional on the evidence needed for that stage.

  1. GREEN · nowActive pursuit

    Strategic decision, transaction work, diligence and capital preparation proceed.

  2. 01 · PerimeterVerify what is being acquired

    Corporate, title, rights, liabilities, project documentation and the complete economic perimeter are established.

  3. 02 · Heads & capitalAgree the commercial architecture

    Heads of terms, controlled cash mechanics, senior funding route and deferred seller consideration are aligned subject to diligence and finance.

  4. 03 · ControlSign / satisfy conditions / complete

    Closing occurs only when the agreed transaction perimeter, funding and conditions precedent are deliverable.

  5. 04 · Project proofPlanning, technical, product & operator case

    The ordinary current route, feasibility, capex, programme and operating model are proven sufficiently for development underwriting.

  6. 05 · BuildDevelopment finance & construction

    ProjectCo capital is committed and drawn against the verified development plan and agreed controls.

  7. 06 · OperateOpen, stabilise and refinance

    The hotel enters unitary operation, establishes sustainable cash flow and can then be assessed for long-term refinancing or recapitalisation.

02 · Enterprise

Develop a fair procurement and tenant-engagement approach for suitable local food, retail, services, maintenance, technology, creative and visitor-economy businesses.
