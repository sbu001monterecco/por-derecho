PREFERRED ROUTE · PLAYA BLANCA · ACTIVE PURSUIT

A new hotel. A route we are actively pursuing.

Sun Rock is examining and actively pursuing Montaña Roja as its preferred current route for a new hotel in Playa Blanca. The intention to move forward is clear; the transaction and delivery route must still be proven. Aweswell Limited, Sun Rock's existing UK HoldCo, is acting as Sponsor while the commercial, legal, planning, technical and capital work advances in parallel. No acquisition, planning approval or committed project financing is announced before it exists.

The project direction

A hotel-led project designed for long-term operation.

The current direction is a high-quality, non-residential hotel project intended for unitary hotel exploitation, with only those complementary commercial, service and public-facing uses that are compatible with the hotel, the site and the applicable planning framework. Final scale, design, operator, ownership and capital structure remain to be established through the ordinary workstreams.

01 · Hotel

One serious hospitality proposition

A ground-up hotel project shaped by the site, current planning law, operational feasibility, market demand and the long-term visitor economy of Lanzarote.

02 · Complementary uses

Useful where they strengthen the hotel

Food, retail, service and public-facing uses may form part of the project where they are operationally useful, commercially sensible and lawfully compatible with the hotel.

03 · Local value

Benefits that can be measured

Employment, training, procurement, accessibility, resource use and public-realm outcomes should be measured against evidence if the project advances.

This page states the direction being actively pursued. It does not represent that the final design, planning entitlement, operator, financing or transaction has already been completed.

Execution due diligence

The objective is clear. The route must be proven.

Due diligence is not being used to decide whether Sun Rock wants to establish another hotel position in Playa Blanca. That strategic objective is already active. The work determines whether Montaña Roja can be the route, what must be acquired or controlled, the current legal and planning position, the executable project, its economics and the appropriate capital structure.

Private diligence · transaction and rights

Establish the complete perimeter

Confirm title, corporate authority, project and intellectual-property permissions, administrative positions, third-party rights, liabilities and the documents required for a clean and enforceable transaction.

  • No public assumption that every relevant right is held by one party.
  • No public transaction structure before binding documents exist.
  • No migration of confidential price, deposit, commission or ownership data to this page.
Public-law diligence · planning and delivery

Prove the ordinary route

Establish the current planning framework and the applicable tourism, environmental, infrastructure, water, sanitation, energy, mobility, accessibility and building requirements with the competent administrations.

  • No assumption that historic reports or approvals remain effective.
  • No request for preferential treatment or a predetermined result.
  • No final project commitment before the delivery route is credible.
Controlled disclosure. The existence of active work and due diligence may be public. Identities, economics, negotiation positions, private legal advice and unverified compensation theories remain private unless separately authorised and independently verified.

Project readiness & capital path

Prepare the project and the capital architecture together.

The project is being prepared as an executable transaction, not as a concept that waits until the end to ask how it will be financed. Commercial, legal, planning, technical and capital work are intended to progress in parallel so that a viable project can move efficiently from agreement to delivery.

01 · Sponsor

Aweswell Limited · existing UK HoldCo

Aweswell is acting as Sponsor: providing leadership, governance, professional resources and the corporate framework from which the project can be advanced. This page does not announce any unlimited guarantee, share pledge or security package.

02 · Project structure

Ring-fenced execution

If the transaction proceeds, the hotel is expected to sit within an appropriately ring-fenced project structure so that acquisition, development costs, financing, operating cash flows and project governance can be independently understood and underwritten.

03 · Capital

Prepare for the full capital requirement

Capital preparation is already part of the development work. The objective is to make the verified project capable of attracting the acquisition, development and operating capital it requires, with each provider able to assess a clear project rather than a collection of unresolved assumptions.

Intended sequence. Sponsor → transaction control → project vehicle → verified project → sponsor / structured equity capital → senior project finance → construction → hotel operation → stabilisation / refinancing. The final structure will depend on the actual transaction, project economics and professional advice.

No public offer or financing commitment is made by this page. The objective is to prepare the project and its capital architecture together, so that financing becomes a route to execution rather than an issue first considered after a transaction has been agreed.

Proposed Lanzarote social-value compact

Measure value before making promises.

If the opportunity reaches an investable and deliverable stage, the project should adopt a transparent compact with a baseline, feasible targets, responsible owners, measurement dates and public reporting.

01 · People

Employment, training and progression

Assess construction and operating roles, apprenticeships, vocational partnerships, management development and accessible pathways for Lanzarote and Canary Islands residents.

02 · Enterprise

Local suppliers and public-facing opportunity

Develop a fair procurement and tenant-engagement approach for suitable local food, retail, services, maintenance, technology, creative and visitor-economy businesses.

03 · Place

Water, energy, access and landscape

Test resource efficiency, climate resilience, accessibility, mobility, landscaping and public-realm benefits against technical evidence and the applicable public processes.

No job count, procurement percentage, investment amount or environmental performance figure is stated until feasibility supports it.

Institutional dialogue

Advance the ordinary route while the transaction advances.

We seek an ordinary, transparent pre-application and technical dialogue with the competent teams of the Cabildo de Lanzarote and Yaiza Council so that the applicable planning, tourism, environmental, infrastructure and delivery requirements can be understood early and addressed properly.

The purpose is to prepare a credible project, not to obtain an informal approval, preferential treatment or a predetermined result. The Montaña Roja workstream remains independent of the Sun Park/MYND Yaiza legacy workstream described in the open letter.
