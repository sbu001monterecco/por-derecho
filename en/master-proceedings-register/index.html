Master Proceedings Register

This page maps the legally distinct judicial, prosecutorial, administrative, regulatory, tax, professional-body, public-funds and related procedural tracks identified in the Project Sun Rock / Por Derecho record. It is designed to show how the tracks intersect without pretending that they are one case.

What inclusion means. A row is an index entry, not a finding of wrongdoing, not proof that an authority accepted the merits, and not necessarily a separate formal proceeding. Read TRUE, FALSE and UNVERIFIED together with the record class and source status. Open gaps remain open.

Why this register exists

The project’s position is multitrack: insolvency, property and possession, criminal complaints, Fiscalía files, tax, tourism and municipal records, professional regulation, public-funds scrutiny, banking and appellate routes can affect the same underlying asset and chronology while remaining legally distinct. The truth-seeking function is to make those distinctions visible, connect documented parent/child and referral relationships, and expose missing primary evidence rather than filling gaps by inference.

The canonical operational source remains archive/PROCEEDINGS_MASTER_REGISTER.csv. This page is a public projection of selected fields. It deliberately does not render internal notes, private source anchors or strategy fields, and rows explicitly marked internal/private are excluded.

Three record states

TRUE · distinct file/proceeding supportedFALSE · supporting reference, not a separate proceedingUNVERIFIED · plausible reference, primary confirmation still missing

Source status and the open-reference-gap field are part of the record, not footnotes to be hidden.

Use it with the storyline, not instead of the evidence

The master register is a navigation layer. A material proposition still requires the underlying filing, decision, institutional original, registered receipt or other appropriate source. The procedural spine is especially useful for reverse-engineering a decision back to the filing that preceded it, the represented party and professional lineage, and any appeal or follow-up.
