60-second orientation

The case, before the detail.

Sun Park is a hotel complex whose title, control, operation, financing and income are reconstructed across insolvency, property, Community and commercial records. The central task is to separate what the documents prove, what is alleged, what institutions decided, and what remains unresolved.

Open RecoveryOpen Evidence

1 · Fragmented rights

The record distinguishes insolvency-estate assets, extra-insolvency property, Community authority and hotel-operation rights rather than treating the complex as one undivided legal asset.

2 · Control and operation

The investigation asks how access, possession, works, hotel operation and income changed over time, and what authority supported each step.

3 · Institutional decisions

Each court, administrator, regulator and professional body is assessed by what it received, verified, decided and preserved—not by association alone.
