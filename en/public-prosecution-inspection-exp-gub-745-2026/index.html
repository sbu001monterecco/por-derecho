INSTITUTIONAL UPDATE · 26 AUGUST 2026 · SOURCE-TO-DECISION CONTROL

Fiscal Inspection archives Exp. Gub. 745/2026; the registered filing permits a finite test of the stated reasons.

The Decree notified on 26 August describes the communication as “confusa e incoherente”, invokes Article 66.1(c) of Law 39/2015 and says no irregularity attributable to a particular prosecutor in a particular matter was specified. The 2/3 August registry record, however, contains express scope delimiters, identified proceedings and numbered requests. This page does not convert that tension into a finding of unlawfulness: it records what each source says and the routes that remain open.

Exp. Gub. 745/2026 · archived · administrative route ended according to the Decree

Deadline control. The Decree itself states that there is a one-month optional administrative reconsideration route or, alternatively, a two-month contentious-administrative appeal to the Supreme Court. If reconsideration is filed, Law 39/2015 bars the contentious appeal until reconsideration is expressly resolved or deemed dismissed. Parallel clarification, access, preservation or document-format requests do not by themselves suspend those periods. Judicial calculation must also address Article 128 LJCA, under which August does not run for the ordinary contentious-appeal period.

3 · IMMEDIATE OPTIONS

Parallel actions, with one principal fork that is not parallel.

A · Optional reconsideration — 1 month

Seek revocation/reopening or, alternatively, a formal cure opportunity. Main grounds: the actual structure of the filing; identified proceedings; the purpose of individual attribution; Article 68 against the Article 66 framework selected by the Decree; and a request for a specific explanation of what was supposedly unintelligible.

Interaction: once reconsideration is filed, the contentious appeal cannot be filed until it is expressly resolved or deemed dismissed.

B · Direct contentious appeal — 2 months

Alternative to reconsideration, before the Supreme Court's Contentious-Administrative Chamber as indicated by the Decree and LJCA. Counsel should decide this before the period expires. Article 128 LJCA provides that August does not run for the ordinary filing period, save the special fundamental-rights procedure.

C · Ad cautelam cure/clarification — immediate

File a short matrix without admitting that the original was unclear: proceeding → concrete act → functional person to identify → alleged irregularity or question to verify → requested step. Ask that it be incorporated into Exp. Gub. 745/2026 and expressly reserve review rights.

D · Full file and copies — immediate

Request the complete index/file, responsible unit, procedural acts and material used for the decision, relying where applicable on the interested party's right to access and copies under Article 53.1(a) Law 39/2015.

E · Native machine-readable document — immediate

Request the native/searchable PDF, electronic-signature or verification information and an explanation of the production route for the rasterised notification copy. Preserve the received file exactly as received and keep OCR as a separate derivative.

F · Preservation — immediate

Repeat, in finite terms, preservation of proceedings, institutional email, attachments, headers, metadata, allocation records and ATLANTE/GEISER/RedSARA traces linked to the Inspection object. Do not imply unproved destruction or alteration.

Recommended parallel architecture. Keep four simultaneous tracks — (1) review choice: reconsideration or direct contentious appeal; (2) ad cautelam cure/clarification; (3) access + native machine-readable copy; (4) preservation — and record each receipt. Tracks 2–4 must not be treated as suspending track 1.
