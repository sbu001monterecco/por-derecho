DEBT · VOTING · ACCESS · SECURITY · MAINTENANCE · LIQUIDATION

Gil Marer accuses five actors of de facto administration and the Administrator and judge of enabling the outcome.

The controlling allegation identifies Francisco Mario Matos Matas, Antonio Cogolludo Rojas, Shaila María Cogolludo Ramos, José Daniel Acosta Matos and Laura Patricia Acosta Matos as a coordinated de facto or shadow-administration structure by 2018 at the latest. It attributes affirmative enabling acts and omissions to the insolvency administrator; and decisions, refusals, delay and omissions to Magistrate-Judge Alberto López Villarrubia that allegedly preserved the mechanism and sabotaged or frustrated a developed, finance-backed exit.

Active forensic investigation · not a declaration of guilt

GIL MARER’S DIRECT CRIMINAL ALLEGATION · NOT A JUDICIAL FINDING

Shadow administration inside and outside the insolvency’s recorded perimeter

Gil alleges that the five actors coordinated debt and voting, access and security, keys, maintenance, works and valuation, information, insolvency strategy, availability of finance and exit, operation, income, title and outcome over Luchy Playa Blanca’s patrimonial and business sphere and the integrated hotel platform. He alleges that the insolvency administrator did not merely omit action: he intervened through emails, meetings, requests, authorisations, decisions, implementation, adoption and ratification. He further alleges judicial decisions, refusals, closures, delay and omissions, characterised by Gil as knowing judicial prevarication under the legally applicable forms.

Boundary: this is a direct criminal accusation, not a conviction. Every function, act, duty, state of knowledge, intent, causal contribution and benefit requires individual proof; Matkator and third-party assets do not thereby become LPB estate assets, and the financeable exit retained outstanding conditions.

Proof rule. “De facto administrator”, “shadow administrator”, creditor in possession, delegated estate manager and necessary cooperator are not synonyms. Function, duration, autonomy, authority, knowledge, benefit and effect must be proved person by person and asset by asset.

What has been located

  • Incidents 07 and 08 pre-date the 2016 definitive texts.
  • 20 January 2017: formal Article 97 bis modification of the Community pre-insolvency claim to contingent/no own amount.
  • 15 February 2018: formal Promontoria-to-CAM holder substitution without increasing the recognised EUR 857,373.81 and EUR 8,194,877.88 amounts.
  • 23 July 2019: the Provincial Court fixed liquidation/better-bid effects and stated that it was not modifying the definitive texts.
  • 2021: later figures operated as better-bid conditions, not as an identified express amendment of the definitive list.
Current conclusion. No post-liquidation insolvency incident has been located that finally re-quantifies the Community debt, enlarges the EUR 9,052,251.69 privileged total or converts every later interest component into definitive insolvency credit. This means “not located”, not “non-existent”, pending the complete certified court index.

The more accurate present description is possible functional substitution or category collapse: reserve figures, mortgage responsibility, better-bid thresholds and consideration may have operated as though they were definitive debt, although they did not perform the same legal function. Later material was filed and contested; clandestinity is therefore not proved.

Documented coordination versus unproved collusion

Security 2018

Administrator request → Community approval → private access implementation. This is a documented coordination chain. The defence is necessary preservation of an inactive and deteriorating property.

Debt 2019–2021

Community certificate/spreadsheet → Administrator filing/certification → judicial better-bid condition. Accuracy, non-duplication and sufficient verification remain to be proved.

CAM opposition

CAM relied on Community decisions and Administrator participation to defend access and technical measures. This is open procedural alignment, not criminal collusion by itself.

Exit negotiation

Contemporaneous communications also show open negotiation, interest-limit discussion and contact facilitation. This is mandatory counterevidence against a total secret-conspiracy theory.
