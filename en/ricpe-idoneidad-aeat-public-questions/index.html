€1,598,849.32 for refurbishment: what exactly was financed, and which authorisation covered it?

We do not ask the reader to guess. We ask the authorities and entities holding the file to show the file and reconcile the chronology publicly.

The central documentary question

The public and regulatory material currently located attributes to MYND Yaiza a Canary Islands Government idoneidad declaration dated 1 December 2022; a €1,598,849.32 Series F associated with project/refurbishment/transformation; and a later €4,974,853.78 Series G associated with job creation.

Question: if the hotel refurbishment had already been substantially carried out by the time of the December 2022 financing, what works, invoices, liabilities, reimbursement of eligible expenditure, bridge financing or other authorised category did the €1,598,849.32 actually finance?

Second question: did Decree 224/2022 and the AEAT binding report of 17 November 2022 cover Series F and Series G together, or was there another application, report, amendment or idoneidad declaration?

Evidential boundary: we do not state that the financing was irregular or that authorisation was absent. We state that the exact scope of the authorisation and exact use of funds should be documentarily verifiable.

One publicly identified idoneidad; two reported phases

Series F

€1,598,849.32

Hotel MYND Yaiza project / refurbishment, remodelling or transformation according to the corporate and regulatory materials located.

Series G

€4,974,853.78

MYND Yaiza · job creation.

The administration should establish which of these possibilities is correct:

  1. One file, AEAT report and decree covered both phases.
  2. Each phase had a separate authorisation or report.
  3. A later amendment, extension or supplementary act existed.
  4. No second act was necessary because the first legally covered both phases.

Who should answer what

Canary Islands Council of Government

What instruments, amounts, entities, purposes and conditions were declared suitable by Decree 224/2022? Series F, Series G or both? Were later acts adopted?

Vice-Ministry of Finance / Tax Policy Service

Publish or identify the complete file index, original RICPE application, project memorandum, proposed instruments, AEAT referral, report received, resolution proposal, annexes, amendments and monitoring.

AEAT

What project, amount, instrument, use and timetable did the binding report of 17 November 2022 assess? How were the refurbishment component and later employment component treated?

CNMV

What information concerning the idoneidad, AEAT report, use of funds, conflicts and two share series appeared in regulatory and investor-facing material?

RICPE / Board / Control Unit

Explain and document the precise use of Series F and Series G, subscription dates, drawdowns, participatory loans, invoices, work certificates, bridge financing and internal controls.

Hotel New Trend / CAM

Identify which costs or payments each line funded or refinanced and how they were separated from other aid, finance, community works or public incentives.
