Help preserve the record. Help close a temporary capacity gap.

What this is really about

A legal and public-interest matter, not a technology experiment.

The repositories exist so the work can be checked, corrected and continued without turning serious allegations into unsupported conclusions.

01Spain

Insolvency, patrimony and recovery

At the centre is Concurso 36/2012 and the former Sun Park hotel in the Canary Islands. Gil Marer alleges that 7 June 2018 marked a material-control pivot during a funded exit/rescue process. The repository expressly treats that as an allegation requiring actor-by-actor proof, not a judicial finding.

02Germany

FTI / Meeting Point evidence and estates

The documentary record later intersects with Club Sei, Meeting Point and FTI. The controlled inquiry concerns rights, receivables, project costs, disclosures, custody of evidence, creditor recovery and the correct debtor or estate. It does not assert that Sun Park caused FTI's insolvency.

03Europe

Public money and financial interests

The work separately tracks Spanish RIC tax treatment, regional incentives, SEPI support, possible FEDER/ERDF questions and other public-finance routes. They are not treated as one fund. OLAF, EPPO or other EU routes are relevant only where their specific legal and evidential thresholds are met.

Why the public-interest dimension is real

Private injury and public-interest reporting can coexist.

The project keeps those capacities separate. Gil says he is both an injured party pursuing recovery and a reporting person raising wider questions where public resources, institutional handling or EU financial interests may be affected.

Whistleblower / reporting-person work

Gil states that he has reported matters since 2020–2021 and invokes only the protection that may apply communication by communication under Spain's Law 2/2023, Directive (EU) 2019/1937 and, where the required nexus exists, Germany's Hinweisgeberschutzgesetz and other applicable regimes. No blanket immunity is claimed.

Public-finance accountability

The questions include how public support, tax incentives, regional aid, possible EU co-financing and restructuring information were represented, monitored and reconciled. Receipt of public money is not evidence of wrongdoing; the point is to preserve the records needed to test the facts.

Access to justice and asset recovery

The work asks where evidence is held, how value moved, which estate or person has the claim, what remains recoverable and which authority can lawfully act. It seeks preservation and proportionate scrutiny, not collective guilt or indiscriminate seizure.

Why help is useful now

A resource imbalance can become an evidential imbalance.

Gil alleges that the displacement of patrimony that is itself part of the recovery case has also reduced the resources available to defend, investigate and recover that patrimony, while adverse parties and institutions have substantially greater professional and financial capacity. That allegation remains subject to proof; the practical resource gap is immediate.

01

High-volume evidence

Thousands of documents, messages, court acts, corporate records and cross-jurisdictional relationships must be kept source-controlled and internally consistent.

02

AI is already heavily used

Gil reports that he is using the available capacity of his paid ChatGPT plan and additional paid usage. He describes AI as already embedded in document review, chronology, translation, contradiction testing, source control and repository governance.

03

Continuity matters

When capacity runs out, the risk is not merely inconvenience. It is fragmentation: lost corrections, repeated review, entity confusion, weak provenance or unsupported causal jumps in a legally sensitive record.

04

A possible bridge, not a certainty

Gil presently expects that material recoveries may be achievable within approximately twelve months or sooner. That is an expectation, not a representation or guarantee; timing, amount and outcome remain uncertain.

Reciprocity, without pretending certainty

This does not have to be a one-way favour.

The present request is for bridge capacity during an unusually resource-intensive phase. If recovery succeeds, the economic position changes.

For service providers

Successful recovery would allow greater normal paid consumption of AI, professional, storage, hosting, research and other services that are currently capacity-constrained.

For grant supporters

Gil's stated intention is to pay forward the benefit where practical—by supporting comparable public-interest, whistleblower-resilience or access-to-justice work once recovery restores resources.

For recoverable support

Where a supporter requires repayment, that can be explored privately and professionally. Repayment would depend on the actual terms agreed and actual recovery; this public page creates no debt or entitlement.

Independence and legal boundaries

Help the capacity. Do not buy the answer.

Support must strengthen the integrity of the work, not compromise it.

  • No supporter receives a guaranteed legal, editorial or evidential conclusion.
  • No allegation becomes a finding because a supporter agrees with it or funds work around it.
  • Adverse, exculpatory and contradictory material remains part of the record.
  • Private evidence, privileged material and personal data are not exchanged merely because someone offers support.
  • Any grant, donated service, deferred fee, repayment arrangement or other material support should be documented under the law and professional rules applicable to the parties.
  • This page is not a securities offering, crowdfunding campaign, litigation-investment offer, promise of recovery or guarantee of repayment.
