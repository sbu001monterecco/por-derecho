What ONA adds to the criminal analysis.

ONA provides a documented counterfactual: operator, plan, valuation, due diligence, finance routes, security and a payment/consignation strategy existed. Loss of access, control, income, information or security can therefore be measured against a real alternative rather than aspiration alone.

What it still does not establish.

It does not by itself establish a completed offence, coordination or intent. Knowledge communications, condition ledger, debt figure, certified docket, benefit flows and contemporaneous reasons for every blockage remain required.

  5. Loss of hotel, business, rents, control and later appreciation.
  6. Loss to LPB, Aweswell, stakeholders, financiers, ONA/Clubotel and creditors who could have been paid.
