ONA/Clubotel was not a rival bidder: it was part of the funded exit

This dossier places in one sequence the pre-7-June ONA documents, the access sought by Carlos Sanz, the finance architecture, the work of Daniel Irigoyen and Cuatrecasas, Clubotel's personation and the criminal proof that must be required actor by actor.

Controlled conclusion.

Clubotel La Dorada, S.L./ONA participated as intended operator and potential acquirer within an alliance coordinated with the LPB/Aweswell perimeter and its advisers. The wider objective, documented in the exit correspondence, was to fix the legally payable amount, obtain sufficient finance, pay or consign the recognised creditors and conclude Insolvency Proceeding 36/2012. Clubotel's personation formalised its acquisition and information interest; it must not be rewritten as though that pleading alone promised payment of the final list of claims.

GIL MARER'S CRIMINAL HYPOTHESIS

The question is not whether there was an offer: it is who prevented its conversion into payment, exit and security

Gil alleges a criminal continuum in which the five private actors used parallel governance, debt, possession and operating control; the Insolvency Administrator acted or omitted acts necessary to protect and implement the exit; and the judge, with knowledge of the package, provided no effective protection or route. No conviction or adjudicated guilt is published.

Mandatory test for each person: act or omission → capacity/duty → actual knowledge of the ONA package → dependency affected → contemporary reaction → measurable loss → alleged intent → benefit/destination → alternative explanation → missing proof.
