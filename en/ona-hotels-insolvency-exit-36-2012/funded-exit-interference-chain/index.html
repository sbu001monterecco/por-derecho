ONA funded exit: the missing chain between financing, the hotel facilities and the 24 October 2019 order

The documented exit was not designed around breaking the hotel into isolated liquidation pieces. It combined ONA/Clubotel as operator, funding, payment or consignation of creditors, conclusion of the insolvency proceeding and later refinancing of an intact operating hotel asset.

ALLEGATION ATTRIBUTED TO GIL MARER

Direct and active sabotage of the funded exit

Gil Marer alleges that commissions and omissions of the Insolvency Administrator enabled the CAM/private-actor perimeter and knowingly degraded the conditions needed to pay the debt, conclude the insolvency, preserve the productive unit and permit genuine third-party competition.

Boundary: several underlying acts and effects are documented or were later judicially found; criminal intent, concert and counterfactual certainty of closing are not a conviction or final judicial finding.

Exit architecture

ONA / Clubotel operator → Stoneweg/Varia + capital routes → reliable debt figure → payment/consignation → conclude Concurso 36/2012 → mortgage/refinance → unitary hotel operation

Transaction

FEB 2019
Documented

ONA remained active

24 OCT 2019
Judicial finding

The court confirms the structural defect

The order refused validation of the separate sale. It held that the AC's conduct contradicted the liquidation-plan order and the Provincial Court's interpretation because the disposal was unitary and should have been stayed as a whole. It added that third parties would not improve the offer if the unencumbered hotel installations were excluded and that the operation had been completed without prior judicial authorization and only later reported to the court.

Why the 2019 order is structural self-corroboration

Before: warning

In December 2018 a lawyer working on the exit contemporaneously warned that separating the facilities damaged both payment/conclusion and future competitive bidding.

After: judicial finding

In October 2019 the court independently held that the disposal was unitary and that stripping the unencumbered installations impaired realistic third-party improved offers.

What it does not prove alone

The order did not declare criminal intent or a criminal agreement with CAM. That layer requires actor-specific evidence.
