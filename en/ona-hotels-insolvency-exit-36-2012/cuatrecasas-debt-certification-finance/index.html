Cuatrecasas knew and worked within the ONA architecture

The September 2018 record does not show Cuatrecasas as an outside observer. Its professionals participated in debt quantification, submission strategy, security and closing work together with Carlos Sanz, Daniel Irigoyen and the LPB/Aweswell side.

Insolvency work

  • Final claims texts and debt figure.
  • Post-petition and privileged claims.
  • Draft request for certification/update.

Especially probative fact.

Within the drafting chain, a Cuatrecasas professional agreed to remove a subtle reference to ONA from one submission. That does not remove ONA from the project: it shows that ONA was known and within the strategy even though it was deliberately not named in that particular document.
