Documented fact.

The April 2018 → 2019 personation sequence shows continuity between the access route required by the AC and ONA/Clubotel's later formal attempt. The reason for directing personation must be compared with the later opposition to that route.

Attribution control.

Carlos's own contemporaneous lane is now preserved in the ONA documented fact matrix. His emails establish transaction purpose, information dependency and the need for a reliable debt figure; they do not make him a co-author of Gil Marer's later criminal theory.

Gil Marer allegation.

Gil says this was not neutral procedure but an early instance of gatekeeping which, combined with the absence of a reproducible debt figure, loss of control and opposition to Clubotel, helped frustrate an exit capable of paying creditors.

Criminal limit.

The April response alone proves neither crime nor intent. Criminal relevance requires proof of duty, later knowledge of the package, absence of lawful justification, effect on finance/personation, continuity with other decisions and any benefit to the adverse perimeter.
