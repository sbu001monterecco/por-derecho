allegation / hypothesis

Deliberate sabotage

The proposition that the AC deliberately frustrated the funded exit in order to enable CAM/private-actor objectives remains a serious, testable allegation. Corroborated objective events do not replace proof of knowledge, causation and intent.

DP 1956/2026 and DP 1901/2026: separate procedure, intertwined evidence

DP 1956/2026 remains, on the latest control, formally instituted and provisionally dismissed at the initial threshold without the requested finite evidential investigation. There was no trial or substantive merits determination; it should not be described as a merits exoneration following investigation.

DP 1901/2026 concerns the private-actor/CAM side. The latest located primary judicial material is the 12 July 2026 Providencia referring admission to the Public Prosecutor for five days. No later signed terminating decision was located.

The two routes are procedurally separate. Their relationship is evidential: private conduct can explain why AC acts/omissions mattered, while the exercise/non-exercise of insolvency powers can explain how private outcomes acquired effect. No formal accumulation is asserted and neither proceeding proves the other.

EUR 350,000: the real proof gap stays visible

The contemporaneous 24 January 2018 email records EUR 350,000 — “Intermediación/Comisiones (3%)”, but that email does not identify the AC as recipient. Patricia's later factual declaration records her recollection that JDAM attributed the amount to the AC for intermediation/management concerning acquisition of the credit from Promontoria.

Current classification: serious witness-supported allegation + contemporaneous corroborative email; payment not proved. Two targeted Gmail searches on 3 September 2026 did not recover independent banking/payment proof. The gap requires banking, invoices, accounting, contracts, tax records, professional/conflict records and CAM/AC records. It must not be rewritten as “Borja was paid EUR 350,000 to negotiate with SAREB.”
