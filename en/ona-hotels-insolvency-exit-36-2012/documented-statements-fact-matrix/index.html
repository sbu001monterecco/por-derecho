Before 7 June.

ONA transaction materials, Carlos Sanz's access request and the Clubotel operating instrument all pre-dated the material-control event. The criminal relevance alleged by Gil is therefore not retrospective invention: the alternative transaction existed before the event that he says impaired access, operation, information, income and collateral.

After 7 June.

The route continued through finance work, the judge/AC presentation, September debt-and-payment coordination, 2019 novation/guarantee/conclusion work and Clubotel personation. Continued work does not prove that 7 June caused failure, but it permits the effect of the event and later institutional decisions to be tested against a real counterfactual.

Gil Marer's criminal inference — separately attributed.

Gil alleges that the five identified private actors, together with affirmative acts or legally relevant omissions of the Insolvency Administrator and judicial acts/omissions of the Magistrate-Judge, formed a continuum that frustrated a creditor-paying exit, degraded its security/operating dependencies and enabled the adverse control outcome to consolidate.

The required proof test is actor-specific: act/omission → capacity/duty → actual knowledge → dependency affected → contemporaneous effect → loss → alleged intent → benefit/destination → alternative explanation.

No adjudicated guilt.

Later control, adjudication, finance or economic benefit can be investigated as outcome evidence; it does not automatically prove an earlier criminal agreement. Family, corporate or professional association does not transfer knowledge or intent.
