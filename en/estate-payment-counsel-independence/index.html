00 / CRIMINAL-PROSECUTORIAL SPINE

Five questions before civil, mercantile or insolvency remedies.

The organising question is not whether a lawyer could ever be paid from estate funds. It is whether a sequence of actor-specific acts, omissions, control points, communications and economic relationships warrants criminal investigation for interference with an already-developed exit and the independence of LPB’s defence.

1 · ObjectWas there a real economic route to impair?

ONA/Clubotel, Stoneweg and Cuatrecasas records place a materially developed operating, DD, term-sheet and insolvency-exit architecture before 7 June 2018. Closing was not guaranteed.

2 · PivotWhat changed on 7 June?

Contemporaneous messages record the asserted CAM/Community possession event and immediate complaints, photographs/videos and requests for judicial protection. Authority, actors and criminal intent remain separate questions.

3 · ControlWho controlled value and information?

The later €400,000 CAM transaction, the LPB estate account and the AC’s payment/reporting role create a source-and-control question. The 2019 nonconvalidation and later legal developments remain part of the contrary record.

4 · LeverageWhat happened around counsel?

May–June 2020 records show AC/JTP fee discussions, intermediation and a “peace” sequence; the client side expressly rejected Parrilla’s final proposal on 12 June. A later AC report records €26,750 paid.

5 · Criminal proofKnowledge, purpose, effect, benefit?

Prosecutorial proof requires source, authority, communications, knowledge, any condition or reciprocal act, causal effect on defence/exit, and resulting benefit or harm. The page does not substitute chronology for those elements.

Criminal first means element first, not offence-label first. Civil, mercantile and insolvency law remain essential because they determine title, authority, estate treatment, duties and remedies. Here they operate as evidential predicates, counterevidence and remedy context. The first reading order is actor → act/omission → knowledge → purpose → causal effect → benefit/harm → legal characterisation.

Who paid LPB’s lawyer?

01 / SEQUENCE

Exit → sale → defence → fee

The ONA/Clubotel operating terms were signed on 6 June 2018. The owner side had developed buyer and finance routes, with closing steps still open. The administrator separately sold facilities to CAM on 28 November 2018 and reported a buyer cheque into an LPB account he controlled. LPB sought the deed through a filed request on 10 January 2019, and the court refused convalidation on 24 October. The administrator’s 21 July 2021 paid-credit report lists €26,750 for former LPB counsel Juan Tomás Parrilla as paid in July 2020.

Gil’s unitary allegation: a functional adverse mechanism, not legal identity. Gil alleges that, in the decisive 2018–2020 window, private-purchaser interest, the AC’s control of estate information, assets and payments, and the separate judicial protection/remedy gate operated together in practical effect to neutralise LPB’s independently instructed representation during the owner-funded exit and its immediate defence after 7 June 2018. Anti-fragmentation / domino rule: the fee episode is downstream, not the origin. The May 2018 correspondence records ONA bringing Stoneweg to Cuatrecasas to advance the transaction, discuss due diligence and term-sheet work, and coordinate the concurso route; the 7 June correspondence records immediate complaints and evidence preservation in response to CAM’s asserted possession; and the 12 June 2020 fee chain records an express rejection of Parrilla’s final proposal and a move toward an insolvency incident. The 2020 rejection directly proves rejection of that proposal. Gil’s further position—that it forms one downstream act of resistance to an alleged wider mechanism that had already impaired the ONA-led funded exit—is an attributed unitary inference, not a claim that the 12 June email by itself proves every upstream act or criminal element. This does not say that the actors became one legal person, that the judge was a payer or payee, or that association alone proves culpability. It is a joined causal case: each actor’s notice, duty, power, act or omission, knowledge and effect must be tested individually, and any coordinating mechanism must be proved with primary sources. Counsel’s protective work remains part of the record; the allegation concerns material windows and mechanisms of effect.
02 / PROSECUTORIAL AND PROFESSIONAL INQUIRY

Why the payment relationship matters

The concern would remain if the amount were smaller or larger. Each question below requires its own evidence and legal assessment.

Pressure on the local representative

Gil characterises the exchange as part of repeated misuse of professional leverage and undue pressure. He states that Patricia, his partner and local representative in the Canary Islands, experienced the exchange as threatening and felt particularly vulnerable while handling the matter locally on his behalf, including the dimension he identifies of doing so as a woman, against the background of the representation failures, omissions, acts and refusals he attributes to Parrilla. Her 28 May reply is retained only as evidence for that alleged pressure and information asymmetry. It is not treated here as consent, waiver, ratification, negotiation, fee allocation or authority.

Claim-holder versus payee

Contemporaneous advice described the insolvency-cost claim as belonging to client-side funders rather than automatically to the lawyer or procurador. Reconcile that position with the fee allocation, invoice, IGIC treatment, service and authority.

Loyalty to LPB

A lawyer’s instructions and judgment should serve LPB. Determine whether payment discussions with an actor adverse to LPB influenced the advice, litigation choices or silence.

Administrator’s dual position

The AC controlled estate payments while LPB and the AC had opposed positions in litigation. That structure calls for scrutiny of his legal basis, motives and safeguards.

Estate property and creditor priority

A charge to the estate affects LPB and creditors. Identify the services, invoice, classification as a claim against the estate and approval or challenge route.

Incentive and dependence

The prospect of payment can matter before cash changes hands. Fix each proposal and decision date and test whether counsel expected money controlled by the AC.

Timing and protective work

Compare payment discussions with each dated request, appeal, warning and missed opportunity concerning the ONA exit and hotel facilities; retain counsel’s protective work.

Adverse beneficiary

CAM bought the facilities; the AC reported receipt into LPB’s controlled account. Investigate any connection among buyer benefit, administrator decisions and counsel fee without assuming a direct CAM-to-lawyer payment.

Confidentiality and instructions

Check whether privileged strategy, client instructions or settlement positions reached a party whose interests diverged from LPB’s; no breach is established by a payment entry.

Disclosure and professional independence

Test what counsel disclosed about the payer, fee basis, communications with the administrator and any conflict affecting independent judgment. Patricia’s response is expressly excluded as proof of consent or validation; the relevant questions are counsel’s duties, conduct, disclosure and the objective payment record.

Causal loss

Identify how a specified act or omission changed a defined opportunity to protect the hotel as a unit, challenge the sale or pursue the developed exit; do not assume the exit was guaranteed.

Judicial treatment

Judicial protection and remedy are a distinct causal gate. Test exact notice, power, request, signed act or omission and effect. The judge’s own acts have a separate evidence lane; no record here shows that he ordered or received the fee.

Criminal and professional inquiry

If evidence shows dishonest diversion of estate funds, a bought change of loyalty, collusion or purposeful concealment, the elements and individual knowledge should be tested. A fee entry itself does not establish bribery or a crime.

Proof boundary

The 2018 buyer cheque and the reported July 2020 fee are distinct entries. The matched bank debit and credit, invoice, fee mandate, IGIC treatment, actual services, earlier incentive, each actor’s knowledge and causal effect remain to be verified. This reader does not use Patricia’s 28 May response to supply client authority, consent, waiver, ratification or validation. LPB could in principle lawfully pay its own lawyer from estate funds; the issue is whether this particular arrangement met the duties and benefited LPB.

04 / FALSIFICATION + PRODUCTION

What would narrow the thesis, and what a prosecutor should obtain.

Evidence that would narrow or falsify material parts

  • A complete bank reconciliation showing the €26,750 came from unrelated, independently available lawful liquidity rather than the alleged source chain.
  • A contemporaneous, independently informed LPB authority approving the specific post-transition payment after full conflict/source disclosure.
  • Complete AC–JTP communications showing ordinary fee administration with no condition, “peace” linkage, information exchange or professional leverage.
  • ONA/Stoneweg records showing the exit lacked material financing/closing viability before 7 June or that the 7 June event did not impair relevant closing assumptions.
  • Actor-specific records showing the relevant person lacked knowledge of the exit or of the alleged downstream consequence.

Priority prosecutor production

  • Purchaser debit, LPB account credit, complete Santander/estate statements, AC remuneration debits and the €26,750 debit/recipient credit.
  • Parrilla invoice(s), IGIC, fee mandate, claim-recognition authority, services and estate ledger.
  • Complete AC↔Parrilla and AC↔incoming-counsel communications, including the 1 June “peace” sequence and any CAM-related intermediation.
  • ONA/Clubotel/Stoneweg DD, term sheet, financing, closing conditions, security assumptions and communications delivered to or known by the AC/court.
  • 7 June access/keys/security instructions, Community act/minutes, complaints, images, witness records and person–act–time reconstruction.
Hostile-reader acceptance rule:

If a contrary source closes a link, the unitary theory must narrow at that link. If a source strengthens a link, it may strengthen only that link and dependent propositions. Neither fragmentation nor evidential overreach is permitted.
