THE CENTRAL ALLEGATION · WITHOUT DILUTION

The alleged criminal threshold was crossed before or, at the latest, at the hearing.

Gil Marer and Aweswell Limited allege that the 12 March 2019 Fiscal opinion was, at minimum, gravely reckless and may already have been criminal. They allege that, following the CNMV, AEAT and insolvency-court alert chain concerning Acosta Matos/RICPE, Ricardo de Mosteyrín Sampalo had crossed the criminal threshold before or, at the latest, through his participation at the 25 July 2023 hearing.

The accusation is not limited to passivity: Gil alleges active instrumentalisation of the Calificación against Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. (LPB), the persons affected by the Calificación and especially Gil as reporting person/alertador, together with the creation or maintenance of an institutional shield for the Acosta Matos/RICPE perimeter.

Gil and Aweswell further allege that the later prosecution position and conduct have caused or contributed to continuing patrimonial, procedural and reputational harm within the Aweswell perimeter. Loss must be allocated by claimant, right, date, causal mechanism and amount without double counting.

Proof boundary. Judgment 163/2023 is materially adverse and under appeal. Ministerio Fiscal attendance is documented; personal identification of Ricardo at the hearing is Gil's direct evidence and does not replace the official appearance record. Personal knowledge of each source, exact oral acts, offence elements, causation and individual responsibility remain open. Those gaps do not erase the allegation; nor do they permit it to be published as proved guilt.

ONE STORY · FOUR STAGES

From alleged gross recklessness to continuation of the shield.

12 MAR 2019

Grave recklessness—or worse

The two-page Fiscal opinion sought 15 years, damages and deficit liability. Gil places it, at minimum, in grave recklessness; the evidence then available must also be tested against a possible criminal threshold.

2020–JUL 2023

Accumulated notice

RICPE/CAM, CNMV/AEAT, register 918/2021 and the July 2021 RICPE/CAM certification form the notice chain. Institutional incorporation is not automatically personal reading.

25 JUL 2023

Threshold crossed

Gil's position is that the Fiscal had crossed the criminal line before or, at the latest, through the hearing: maintenance, adoption, reaffirmation, use or knowing failure to correct.

2023–PRESENT

Opportunity to correct

Later alerts, use of the Judgment in the elEconomista episode and the 2026 decisions/referrals are analysed as continuity, notice and opportunity to neutralise harm—not as the late birth of the criminal allegation.

WHAT IS REQUIRED NOW

A traceable response, not another fragmented absorption.

1 · Preserve and produce

Complete file, index, allocation, referrals, received corpus, signed report, decisions, access traces and any review or correction.

2 · Answer actor by actor

Keep the institution, offices, leadership and individual signatories separate. Office alone does not transfer knowledge or intent.

3 · Correct instrumental use

State whether the 2019–2023 position is maintained, corrected, narrowed, investigated or referred, and what prevents continuing use as a shield.

4 · Protect the reporting person

Test each communication and detriment under Law 2/2023 without converting that Law into an automatic offence or excluding it through a simplified chronology.

5 · Prevent incremental harm

Identify lawful preservation, investigation, accounting, restitution and non-aggravation measures while judicial and recovery routes remain open.

6 · Provide real contradiction

Produce the institutional reason and contrary evidence. The correction and right-of-reply route remains open with equivalent visibility.
