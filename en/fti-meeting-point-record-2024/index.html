The FTI/Meeting Point record does not begin with the January 2025 media episode.

The located emails show that FTI Touristik logged an external report as reference 2024-94, requested information for a compliance interview and that, in later correspondence involving Rödl & Partner, ownership documents and possession-recovery proceedings were requested. On 3 January 2025 a confirmation and preservation request was sent to Lars Creutzmann, copying the FTI compliance and insolvency perimeter.

Exact scope: the record establishes notice, receipt, a route for verification and requests for documents. It does not establish that FTI, Meeting Point, Lars Creutzmann or the legal contacts accepted the allegations, knew of a particular illegality, or incurred liability.

The point of tension

A commercialisation question could not be closed by a sentence without the documents the threads themselves required.

The January 2025 media trace preserves one position attributed to Meeting Point, that it had not commercialised the hotel, and another attributed to Laura/CAM, that it had. Those attributions still require authentication against the native records of the speaker.

Public product and distribution

Already published sources place Club Sei Lanzarote in the Meeting Point/FTI portfolio or commercial environment and at Calle Janubio 3. They establish public presentation and offering, not by themselves the contracting party, inventory, completed bookings or authority.

FTI record · 2024

The review requested title and possession

The compliance and Rödl correspondence requests ownership data, a land-registry extract and recovery proceedings. This confirms that those questions were material to the review, not that recipients knew of or approved any conduct.

Media response · 2025

Two accounts still to authenticate

The attributed conflict is not presented as a confession. Its function is to show why contracts, listings, bookings, invoices and payments are necessary before either a denial or an admission can become a complete answer.

The useful public contradiction is narrow: a classification judgment in LPB's Concurso 36/2012 did not determine who negotiated, authorised, commercialised, invoiced or received revenue from Club Sei Lanzarote. Nor could it replace the documents that FTI and its legal contacts had requested to investigate title and possession.
