Group governance · professional perimeter

Current Advisers to the Group

A controlled, role-based view of firms and professionals that are current advisers or pre-approved advisers to the Group. Adviser status is kept separate from adverse-party and third-party roles elsewhere in the record.

Status matters

What “pre-approved adviser” means

A pre-approved adviser is a professional firm with which the Group, an affiliate and/or one or more of the Group’s principals has previously completed relevant client-acceptance, KYC and/or onboarding processes. The relationship history may also include a prior assignment, proposed assignment, pre-assignment or engagement discussion, depending on the entity and matter. There is presently the possibility of a new or renewed engagement.

Pre-approved does not mean currently engaged. It does not represent that the firm is presently instructed on a particular matter or carrying out an active workstream. Nor does it waive or predetermine any fresh conflict check, client/matter acceptance, KYC refresh, engagement-letter process or other internal clearance that the relevant firm, office or legal entity may require before accepting a new instruction.

Group tax position

Compliance, governance and lawful dispute resolution

A tax dispute is not an admission of liability and a tax authority’s contested position is not presented here as a final determination while lawful review remains open.

The Group’s tax-governance position is to identify and meet applicable tax and filing obligations in the relevant jurisdictions, to obtain appropriate professional advice, and to use the available administrative and judicial review mechanisms where the Group or an affiliate disputes an authority’s legal or factual position.

Classification safeguard

Adviser roles are evidence-controlled

Repository and website classifications distinguish current adviser, pre-approved adviser/potential new engagement, historical adviser, prospective adviser/onboarding in progress, third-party professional, adverse-party professional and role under verification. When the evidence is uncertain, the default is role under verification, not adverse.

Adviser-status notice: this page describes relationship status for transparency and continuity. It is not a representation that every listed firm advises every Group entity or principal, nor that a pre-approved adviser has a live mandate or has completed the fresh clearances required for a new matter. Tax-position material describes contested processes and is not tax advice.
