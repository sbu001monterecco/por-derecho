Professional onboarding & conflicts reader

A bounded public starting point for a law firm, bank, auditor or other regulated professional reviewing the Group for KYC, conflicts or matter acceptance. It is designed to identify the correct legal capacities and route the reviewer to the relevant public record without requiring the reviewer to reconstruct the entire dispute from the homepage.

Step 1

Identify the client and capacity before screening names.

Connected economic interests do not merge legal persons, claims, liabilities or engagement capacities.

Step 2

Read adviser status separately from mandate status.

Pre-approved is a Group relationship classification, not a substitute for a professional firm's own acceptance procedures.

Pre-approved adviser records prior relevant client-acceptance, KYC and/or onboarding history involving the Group, an affiliate and/or one or more Group principals, with potential for a new or renewed engagement. It does not mean a live mandate or active workstream, and it does not waive fresh matter-specific conflict checks, client/matter acceptance, KYC refresh, engagement-letter or other internal clearance.

For Fieldfisher, the public presentation is deliberately firm-level, with relationship emphasis on the Spanish and UK businesses within the wider international platform. Specific individual lawyers are not listed on the public adviser page.

Step 4

Prospective transaction counterparties require a confidential current list.

The public website and repository are not the authoritative matter-opening form for an unannounced financing, investment, acquisition or other prospective transaction.

Current or contemplated lenders, banking groups, investors, sellers, co-investors, funds, advisers and transaction vehicles may change during mandate formation. The professional firm should therefore obtain the current confidential counterparty and affiliate list from the instructing channel before completing conflicts clearance.

Public or repository material may contain historical or public-safe transaction-development references. Those references are not a complete current conflict list, do not prove a live dialogue or mandate, and must not be treated as authority to contact, disclose to or act against a named organisation.
