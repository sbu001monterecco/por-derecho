How to read this

Every visible relationship should resolve to an identifiable act or question.

The public layer does not turn hypotheses into facts. DOCUMENTED denotes source-controlled support; DISPUTED marks a documentary tension; OPEN EVIDENTIAL GAP identifies a known missing primary item; and ANALYTICAL QUESTION identifies an inference still requiring proof.
