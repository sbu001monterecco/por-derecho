Understand the status

A green receipt and an open investigation are not the same thing. Each event has a procedural state and an independently assessed evidence state.

01 · Procedural status

Draft → electronically filed → registered → received by competent office → assessed → decision. Only recorded steps count.

02 · Evidence status

Official award, recorded inspection and dated opinion are sources. Payment, personal knowledge, intentional wrongdoing and causation require further proof.

The three filing routes

Follow the authority’s actual competence—not the order in which the documents were written. Every route retains its own receipt, legal test and next action.

What this map must never imply

  • That a €3.44m awarded grant was actually paid or unlawfully obtained.

Three different legal instruments

A single fact can be relevant to multiple authorities, but each statutory route has a separate triggering condition.
