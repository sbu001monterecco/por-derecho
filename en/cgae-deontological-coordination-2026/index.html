NATIONAL DRAFT · 22 SEPTEMBER 2026 · NOT SENT

Consejo General de la Abogacía Española: a national consultation without replacing first instance.

The national request starts from an express rule: RS-001022/2026 already stated that CGAE does not hear ordinary complaints against lawyers at first instance. This communication therefore seeks no sanction or merits decision on DIP 79/2026 or DIP 80/2026. It seeks criteria, coordination, consistency and an inter-Bar route for issues now touching Las Palmas, Tenerife, Madrid and the Canary Council.

Scope: preliminary information and the ordinary three-month period · complainant rights under the 2024 Regulation · competence conflicts · territorial coordination · professional independence · external-contact handling · preservation and traceability.

1. Why CGAE has its own distinct question

Not first instance

CGAE is not asked to decide whether Rodríguez-Batllori, Ramírez de Prada or another professional committed misconduct.

Deontological consultation

The Professional Deontology Commission publicly lists among its functions answering deontological consultations from individuals, Bars and institutions, and unifying criteria.

Inter-Bar competence

The same Commission examines competence conflicts between Bars and channels initiatives from Bars and autonomous councils. That is distinct from first-instance discipline.

2. Event triggering the national consultation

DIP 80/2026 was opened by ICALPA on 27-May-2026. The 2024 Disciplinary Procedure Regulation for the Spanish legal profession provides that preliminary information should not last more than three months save exceptional circumstances. As at 22-Sep-2026, the located record contains opening, rapporteur and many later entries, but no communicated archive, disciplinary opening, suspension, transfer or explanation of exceptional extension.

Separately, the professional concerned filed a September 2026 opposition before the Provincial Court expressly naming ICALPA among the institutions approached by Gil Marer and using that institutional activity within his defence narrative. It is published only as proof of awareness of the Bar route, not proof of a specific ICALPA notification.

The national question is not “why has he not been sanctioned?” It is: what uniform criteria apply when preliminary information exceeds the ordinary period, what must be documented as exceptional circumstances, what rights does the complainant have, and what coordination mechanism applies when the same evidence matrix touches several Bars?

4. Prepared national email text

Subject: Request for national/inter-Bar deontological criteria and coordination — 2024 disciplinary regulation — DIP 80/2026 / Canary Council / ICATF / ICAM

For the attention of the Consejo General de la Abogacía Española and, in particular, the Commission or body responsible for Professional Deontology:

My name is Gil Marer. I write in my own name and in defence of the legitimate interests of Aweswell Limited and Matkator, S.L.

I expressly start from CGAE response RS-001022/2026, which stated that the Consejo General does not hear ordinary complaints against lawyers at first instance. I am not asking CGAE to replace ICALPA, ICATF, ICAM or the Canary Council, or to anticipate a sanction or merits decision concerning any professional.

This communication raises only national and inter-Bar questions of procedure, coordination and deontological criteria.

The immediate event giving rise to this consultation is ICALPA DIP 80/2026, preliminary information opened on 27 May 2026 concerning D. Francisco de Borja Rodríguez-Batllori Laffitte. The 2024 Disciplinary Procedure Regulation for the Spanish legal profession provides that preliminary information should not last more than three months save exceptional circumstances. As at 22 September 2026, the record received by us contains the opening, identification of the rapporteur and numerous later registry entries, but we have not located a communicated decision archiving the matter, opening disciplinary proceedings, suspending or transferring it, or explaining an exceptional extension.

We do not infer internal inactivity, protection of the professional or any irregularity from that absence. Precisely for that reason, we seek a verifiable institutional criterion on the applicable procedural framework.

There is also a traceability dimension. In his September 2026 opposition before the Provincial Court in the appeal concerning his removal as Insolvency Administrator, the professional concerned expressly lists ICALPA among the institutions approached by this side. That filing proves awareness of the existence of the Bar route, but not how that awareness arose or what internal act ICALPA performed.

In parallel there are: ICALPA DIP 79/2026; communications and traceability requests before ICATF; a Tenerife territorial referral ordered by ICAM in 1487/26 whose local receipt we are still seeking to certify; and a regional communication prepared for the Consejo Canario de Colegios de Abogados.

Accordingly, and with full respect for the competence of each territorial body, I ask CGAE for:

1. general criteria on the duration of preliminary information under the 2024 Regulation and the way exceptional circumstances justifying extension beyond the ordinary period should be documented;
2. clarification of the complainant's participation and appeal rights during preliminary information and against its archive;
3. general criteria on suspension or coordination where related judicial proceedings exist;
4. identification of the mechanism applicable where competence overlaps or conflicts arise between Bars;
5. national good-practice criteria on independence, conflicts, recusal and traceability of materially relevant external contacts with rapporteurs or investigators;
6. guidance on the proper relationship between a territorial Bar, an autonomous council and CGAE where a deontological issue has a recurrent, systemic or inter-Bar dimension; and
7. assignment of a reference and internal referral of this consultation to the competent Commission/body if the general contact route is not the final internal destination.

I do not seek access to confidential deliberations or information that would prejudice the defence rights of any professional. Nor do I request a finding of responsibility. The purpose is to obtain procedural and coordination criteria that distinguish clearly between receipt, preliminary information, disciplinary proceedings, suspension, archive, appeal and territorial coordination.

I will attach or link the parallel Canary Council communication and a short index of the necessary institutional references, avoiding a mass documentary filing unless specific material is requested.

I would be grateful for written acknowledgement, a reference number and identification of the body or service responsible for this consultation.

Yours faithfully,

Gil Marer
for himself and in defence of the legitimate interests of Aweswell Limited and Matkator, S.L.
