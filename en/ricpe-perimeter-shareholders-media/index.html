Sun Park / MYND Yaiza chronology inside RICPE

2019–2020 · formation and fundraising

RICPE was incorporated in January 2019, CNMV-registered in October, and early 2020 press said it was studying six hotel projects including one in Lanzarote.

20 July 2021 · internal RICPE certificate

It recorded 262 properties: 54 CAM, 190 LPB and 18 third parties; a future/conditional CAM acquisition; unsigned LOI; and full due diligence not yet started.

2022 · financing and opening

RICPE's public MYND Yaiza page identifies Hotel New Trend as operator and Canarian Hospitality as management, and records rehabilitation financing plus job-creation funding.

21 May 2026 · Series B capital reduction

The shareholders' meeting approved a €2,020,714.80 reduction and full cancellation of Series B with repayment to its holders.

17–27 Aug 2026 · whistleblowing alert and closure

The ethics-channel certificate records acceptance/assignment, a request for the 20 July 2021 certificate marked “Not shown to the reporting person”, and closure without an internal investigation.
