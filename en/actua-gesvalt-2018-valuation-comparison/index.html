One hotel, four valuation products, and the €400,000 branch that became a “golden trigger”.

The record does not support treating the reception, service premises, pools, gardens and solarium as expendable leftovers. ACTÚA itself described the 262-title complex as one functional unit and the 31 titles as common operational equipment. GESVALT valued the hotel as one exploitation. This page publishes the source documents and separates fact, professional opinion, inference and Gil Marer’s criminal allegation.

ACTÚA’s internal control: it says fincas 8653 and 8654—the pool and solarium areas—are a sine qua non for tourist operation and that their value had already been computed in the apartments. That is difficult to reconcile with treating the 31-title branch as a self-contained, non-duplicative measure of the economic loss caused by separating control of those assets.

Why the branch was worth more than a standalone price

The central issue is control, not merely the sum of registry-unit values. The branch contained the entrance/reception, food-and-beverage and service areas, pools, gardens and solarium. ACTÚA described those assets as common operational equipment; GESVALT treated the properties as one hotel exploitation. Gil’s position is therefore that the hotel’s business value collapses without secure control of the assets required to receive guests and operate it.

This does not make €26.4m the automatic legal value of the 31 titles. It does mean that €489,361.96 cannot safely be treated as a complete measure of prejudice without analysing loss of control, bargaining power, competition, funded-exit feasibility and the value of the remaining 231 titles after separation.

The “golden trigger”: the practical effect began in 2018, not only in 2022

“Golden trigger” is Gil Marer’s forensic and economic characterisation, not a statutory term or judicial finding. It describes a transaction that gave CAM an early operational-control position and a €400,000 valuation/transaction anchor that was later reused in the liquidation structure.

The practical advantage was not created for the first time in 2022.

Judgment and implementation are different questions. The 24 October 2019 order had a real effect: it refused convalidation and restored the competition route. But it was not a self-executing global unwind and does not itself prove refund, reconveyance, Registry cancellation, tax correction or estate-ledger restoration. The defensible finding is not that the judge made an empty gesture; it is that the later chain implementing all those consequences has not been located. The affirmative 2021/2022 reuse of the same branch and the same €400,000 makes that implementation gap material.

Gil’s alleged mechanism: detach the operational choke points; obtain early control; leave the non-convalidated deed without a located completed unwind; then carry the same branch and price into the later sale structure. He asks Fiscalía to investigate whether that sequence was deliberate disloyal administration and whether the Administrator’s knowledge of ACTÚA’s functional-unity language and the contrary GESVALT material establishes knowledge or conscious disregard.

Cajasiete remains an open production lane: Gil states that CAM raised approximately €1m against the locales shortly after the €400,000 acquisition. No mortgage deed or current certified Registry extract proving lender, amount, date and charged titles has yet been located in the controlled public bundle.

Consequences for the Administrator and Juan Tomás Parrilla

The Administrator

The Administrator treated the 31-title branch as outside the suspension affecting the mortgaged apartment fincas; LPB challenged that construction, and the court later refused to convalidate the deed because prior authority was absent. As controller of the disposition, account and estate reporting, the implementation question bears directly on his conduct: refund, reconveyance, Registry, tax and ledger evidence must be produced, and any continuing control or economic advantage after non-convalidation must be determined.

He also reported the €400,000 becoming liquid and paying his own remuneration; the located record does not complete the unwind; he later reported paying Parrilla €26,750; and he stood to face less effective resistance if owner-side representation weakened. Gil alleges that this was an over-the-line intervention in the lawyer–client relationship linked to a false offer of “peace”, not merely a fee-arithmetic dispute.

Juan Tomás Parrilla

Gil alleges actual knowledge, or alternatively conscious avoidance, by Parrilla concerning the alleged criminal origin and strategic use of the money. Parrilla is not pleaded as a chance recipient with no connection to the file. The allegation rests cumulatively on his specialist insolvency mandate; the July 2016 proposed burofax warning of alleged Community-organ fraud; the June 2017 conflict over a further burofax; the 2018 valuation, control and liquidation disputes; LPB’s challenge to Protocol 2150; the 2019 non-convalidation while he remained counsel; his own written fee position; and the 2020 route in which an Administrator contact combined “peace”, payment assurance and the handover to incoming counsel.

Professional specialisation does not establish mens rea by itself, but “receipt alone” is not an answer to this allegation because receipt alone is not its basis: direct exposure to the entire sequence is. That is the knowledge proposition Gil asks investigators to test and that the documentary chronology can support, subject to full criminal proof.

The legal conclusion is not predetermined: Article 301 still requires proof of its knowledge or serious-negligence elements, and no criminal judgment presently establishes them. Gil calls on Fiscalía and ICALPA to test the allegation by obtaining the invoice, account debit, estate ledger, authority, disclosure, communications and any restitution record.
