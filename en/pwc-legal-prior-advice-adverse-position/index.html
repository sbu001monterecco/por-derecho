The “switching sides” allegation is published — and tested

Project allegation

Project Sun Rock alleges that PwC-linked professionals who previously advised and received detailed information from the Sun Park/Luchy/Matkator perimeter later occupied, or may have occupied, a materially adverse position in connected matters. The Project regards that potential transition as particularly serious if any later mandate concerned the same assets, Community, debt, operation, insolvency or Calificación.

It is not yet presented as an established professional breach. The exact later mandate, adverse client, subject matter, conflict check, consent/waiver, communications, use of earlier information and concrete act must be produced. Carlos Saavedra’s later status as a witness does not by itself equal adverse representation.

The conflict test that must be answered

1 · Prior engagement

Who exactly was the client? Matkator, Luchy, Gil/Patricia, the operating Community or another perimeter? What did the engagement cover?

2 · Information

Which minutes, contracts, payments, accounts, debts, litigation, licence, operation and insolvency information did each professional receive?

3 · Later position

Whom did each professional later advise or represent, in what matter, with what consent and what use was made of the earlier knowledge?
