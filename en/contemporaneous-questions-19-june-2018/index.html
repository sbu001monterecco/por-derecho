CORRECTIONS THE SOURCE CANNOT TURN INTO FACT

Two controls must travel with the historic questions.

QB-COR-001 The 19-Jun-2018 text calls FMMM JDAM’s “cousin”. FMMM stated on 20-Jul-2018 that José Daniel Acosta Matos was not his cousin and that there was no family relationship. The site does not adopt kinship without an independent primary source.
QB-COR-002 The text asks about a 17-May-2018 meeting. The located canonical ACTA is dated 18-May-2018. The 17-May wording is preserved literally but not converted into fact; any separate 17-May meeting remains open.
QB-COR-003 The document was sent to a lawyer “for your consideration”. Counsel replied “I am analysing it” and sent a different draft of questions for Gil. The 251-question bank is therefore not described as filed, adopted or actually put in court.

U08 · COURT AUTHORITY

U09 · VALUATION

U10 · CREDIT FINANCE

U11 · 7JUNE

Were you present on 7-Jun-2018? If not, what did you know beforehand and what did you instruct, approve, adopt, reject, correct or reverse afterwards?

U12 · EXPLOITATION EXIT

What did you know about CEXP, Pink/LPB/Matkator, ongoing operation, guests/workers and the ONA-funded exit before acting?

U13 · DOWNSTREAM USE

Which 2017–2018 document or result was later reused in title, adjudication, works, financing, RIC/RICPE, operation or income, and by whom?

U14 · CUSTODY CONTRARY

Identify the native documents in your custody and also produce contrary or exculpatory evidence that limits, corrects or rebuts the allegations.
