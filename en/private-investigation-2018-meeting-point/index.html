Contemporaneous evidence · 8 May 2018

Before 7 June, a private investigation was already examining a hotel/Meeting Point connection.

A first-phase private investigation dated 8 May 2018 examined the Sun Park conflict and contained a dedicated section on a hotel executive whose professional history included Meeting Point and Labranda. The identity is withheld from this publication; the investigator's identity and professional accreditation and the complete unredacted material are retained for competent authorities where lawfully required.

What this page proves: that the professional connection was already under investigation before the 7 June 2018 change in material control. What it does not prove: that the executive participated in unlawful entry, represented a particular Meeting Point entity at Sun Park, or that a Club Sei contract already existed.

Why the date matters

This is not a reconstruction created after Lava Verde or Club Sei.

The report predates 7 June 2018, the public Lava Verde presentation and the Club Sei Lanzarote announcement. Its significance is chronological: it shows that a possible bridge between hotel preparation and a professional with Meeting Point/Labranda background was already being examined before those later milestones.

Responsible anonymisation

The identity is not necessary for the documentary question to be public.

Project Sun Rock does not publish the investigator's name or personal information here, and it does not reproduce the complete private report. Identity, accreditation and the unredacted original are preserved for provision to courts, prosecutors, police, regulators or other competent bodies where legally appropriate.

The hotel executive is also anonymised in this extract. The purpose is to direct attention to verifiable records — access, capacity, communications and project preparation — rather than turn an investigative lead into a personal accusation.
