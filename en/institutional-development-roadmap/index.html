Capital architecture

Two financing asks, one Sponsor strategy.

Ask A — HoldCo capitalisation: Aweswell liquidity and execution capacity. Ask B — ProjectCo finance: acquisition/development/operating capital for the verified hotel. Mixing them weakens underwriting; showing their relationship strengthens it.

SponsorAweswell Limited

Governance, equity support, liquidity and transaction leadership.

HoldCo capitalRunway / liquidity

Diligence, advisers, deposits/control, pipeline and execution capacity.

Project vehicleSpanish ProjectCo

Ring-fenced asset, costs, contracts, debt and operating cash.

Capital stackMultiple sources

Sponsor equity + optional vendor/structured capital + senior debt.

DeliveryDevelopment facility

Milestone/certification draws and cost-to-complete discipline.

OpeningOperating liquidity

Pre-opening, working capital, DSRA and FF&E/lifecycle reserves.

StabilisedPermanent capital

Refinance, recapitalise, hold/sell and recycle permitted equity.

Recourse is separate from security.

  • Project security: mortgage/real-estate security, ProjectCo equity, accounts, receivables, insurance, material contracts and shareholder loans as applicable.
  • Full-recourse option: payment, completion, cost-overrun, equity/carry support and separate Sponsor-level security where agreed.
  • Negotiating discipline: stronger Sponsor support should buy better economics, leverage, tenor, certainty or reserve treatment—not be given away without value.
No automatic cross-collateralisation.
Other hotels, future assets and unresolved recovery rights are not ordinary ProjectCo collateral unless later expressly, lawfully and deliberately granted.

Governance · oversight · ethics

Every material decision has a proposer, verifier, approver and record.

The framework follows institutional principles rather than relying on personalities. No person should be able to originate a material transaction, approve it, release the money and verify the outcome alone.

Aweswell Board

Strategy, risk appetite, Sponsor commitments, conflicts, material financing and irreversible capital gates.

Investment / FID Committee

Independent challenge of underwriting, downside, planning, TDC, capital stack and exit/repayment.

ProjectCo governance

Ring-fenced contracts, bank accounts, budgets, debt, procurement, distributions and related-party approvals.

Independent assurance

Legal/tax counsel, technical/QS, valuation, feasibility, audit/accounting and lender advisers as applicable.

Financial controlMoney never moves on narrative alone.
  • Approved budget + sources/uses.
  • Four-eyes bank mandates.
  • Certified construction draws.
  • Cost-to-complete and cash forecast.
  • Variance thresholds and escalation.
Conflicts + ethicsInterests are disclosed before decisions.
  • Conflicts and related-party register.
  • Abstention where appropriate.
  • Procurement policy / tender evidence.
  • Anti-bribery, gifts and inducements policy.
  • Whistleblowing and no-retaliation process.
Compliance + dataRestricted information stays restricted.
  • KYC/AML and beneficial ownership.
  • Sanctions/PEP screening.
  • Source of funds/wealth where applicable.
  • Cyber, privacy and access logging.
  • Public/private publication controls.
Status disciplineEvery investor/lender-facing statement is labelled by substance: current fact, planned structure or conditional future state. “In discussion”, “mandated” and “committed” are not interchangeable.

Hotel revenue & lockbox

Commercial success becomes lender-visible cash only after reconciliation.

A tour-operator or guest payment is not automatically available debt-service cash. Refunds, taxes, commissions, merchant deductions and legally protected amounts are reconciled first; the verified HotelCo-owned receivable then enters the secured collection architecture.

Base waterfall logic

  1. Statutory / legally senior protected amounts.
  2. Payroll and essential hotel OpEx.
  3. Agreed operator / brand / distribution obligations.
  4. Senior interest and principal.
  5. DSRA replenishment.
  6. FF&E / lifecycle capex reserve.
  7. Other agreed secured obligations.
  8. Permitted distributions.

Two-hotel rule: each hotel keeps its own receivables, lockbox and waterfall unless a later lawful financing deliberately provides otherwise.
