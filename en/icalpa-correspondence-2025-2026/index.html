The preventive warning about independence was already made to ICALPA

On 10 June 2026, in separate DIP 79/2026 and DIP 80/2026 communications, Gil Marer did not allege that pressure or interference had already occurred at ICALPA. He identified a preventive structural-risk hypothesis and requested reinforced safeguards for rapporteur independence and file integrity.

Source-controlled formulations already used: “objective risk of structural pressure and need to protect the rapporteur”; possible “informal approaches”, “third-party calls” and “non-procedural contacts”; and a request for the “maximum appearance and reality of independence, confidentiality, traceability and non-interference”.

The request also asked how any attempted contact, approach or pressure would be logged; what route a rapporteur or employee should follow after an external communication; and whether abstention, substitution or reinforced internal controls existed if an incident could affect the appearance of impartiality.

Future use: at Canary Council level, this should be framed as a documented preventive continuation: appointment of a conflict-cleared rapporteur with actual and apparent independence, willingness to pursue finite evidence, and institutional backing to resist any informal approach that may arise. No assertion is made that such an approach has occurred.

DIP 80/2026: temporal control and visibility asymmetry

22 September 2026. 273 days have elapsed since the initial competence enquiry of 23-Dec-2025, 218 days since the formal complaint registered on 16-Feb-2026, and 118 days since DIP 80/2026 opened on 27-May-2026.

Current rule: the 2024 Disciplinary Procedure Regulation for the Spanish legal profession preserves preliminary information but provides that it should not last more than three months save exceptional circumstances. The reform also strengthened complainant participation and appeal rights.

The reviewed mailbox contains the opening, rapporteur and multiple later registry entries. No later communication has been located archiving DIP 80, opening disciplinary proceedings, ordering suspension or transfer, or explaining an exceptional extension. Absence of a located communication does not prove no internal act exists.

New external event: in his September 2026 opposition before the Provincial Court, the professional concerned expressly names ICALPA among the institutions approached by Gil Marer. That proves awareness of the existence of the ICALPA route; it does not prove how he learned of it, what he was served with or what internal act the Bar performed.

Open question: if DIP 80 remains preliminary information, what exceptional circumstances justify exceeding the ordinary period and what was the latest material procedural act?
