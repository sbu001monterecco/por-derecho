The document-answerable question

For every material testimony, DIOR or certification in Insolvency 36/2012, can the Justice Administration produce the complete chain: request → decision → certified content → expression of finality → service → delivery → external use → current effect?

If the chain is fully regular, certified production can close the issue. If there is a material divergence, it can be isolated by document, date, person and effect. What should not remain is a space in which no institution assumes responsibility for checking the official record.

2018: a concrete worked example

  1. The 26 June 2018 order suspended realisation of the mortgaged properties in the terms stated by that order while an issue was pending before the Provincial Court. This page does not say that the entire liquidation phase was literally suspended.
  2. On 17 October 2018 the Insolvency Administrator requested testimony of the orders of 16 April and 26 June 2018. The located request does not state a specific external purpose.
  3. On 22 October 2018 a court-office act signed by LAJ Águeda Reyes Almeida ordered, among other matters, delivery to the Administrator of the requested testimonies.
  4. On 28 November 2018 the Administrator and CAM formalised the €400,000 branch of the transaction before a notary.
  5. The available record attributes to the 24 October 2019 order a refusal to validate that separate sale after the fact. The exact wording and consequences must be controlled by the complete primary order.

No allegation is made that the LAJ necessarily knew the later notarial use or that issuing a testimony was unlawful. The supervisory question is precisely what was issued, what it contained, what purpose was recorded, who received it, how it was used and what—if anything—the court office did after later developments.

Not just insolvency harm: three simultaneous planes

Insolvency plane · LPB

Estate value, income, realisation, restructuring/refinancing alternatives, payments, causes of action and current routes to restoration or conclusion.

Extra-insolvency plane · Matkator / third parties

Property, title, possession, access, works, exploitation, income, community burdens and rights that were not automatically part of LPB's estate.

Cross-border plane · Aweswell / foreign capital

Foreign shareholder/investor value, capital, financing, recovery, professional costs and remedies crossing Spain's border.

The originating events may be historic while alleged effects on title, possession, hotel exploitation, income, financing, public incentives or restrictions remain current. Causation and quantum must be proved asset-by-asset and right-by-right.

External life of judicial-office instruments

The investigative hypothesis is limited: instruments generated inside Insolvency 36/2012 may have acquired documentary portability outside it—at notary, Registry, corporate, investor, financing, administrative or public-incentive level. This does not establish that any LAJ knew, intended or caused every downstream use.

The audit should therefore follow: originating decision → request → office instrument → service/delivery → external use → later transaction/representation → current legal/economic position.

Where a documented bridge exists, compare the judicial title/availability position with the material held by Intervención General, SNCA / EU-funds authorities, CNMV/RICPE and other relevant public bodies. Public financing or incentives do not prove irregularity; the question is whether asset, title and availability representations matched the rights actually available.

What DP 1901/2026 can verify — and why the routes remain separate

DP 1901/2026 remains a public criminal-verification node →

The criminal route and the LAJ supervisory route do not replace one another. A criminal investigation may assess criminal relevance within its remit; the LAJ hierarchy can obtain, preserve and certify Justice Administration records within its own functions. A certified LAJ result may confirm, reject or qualify factual premises relevant elsewhere.

The strongest institutional defence should be tested, not suppressed

It may be that every testimony was correctly issued, every relevant party properly served, every expression of finality legally justified, external use was solely the responsibility of the recipient, no LAJ knew of any improper use, and no court-office instrument exceeded the judicial decision it certified. That explanation should not be rejected in advance. It should be demonstrable from the official record.

What this page does not establish

It does not establish prevarication, forgery, cooperation, collusion, favouritism, criminal liability or extra-procedural knowledge by any LAJ. It does not establish that all current hotel value, financing or public support is damage to LPB, Matkator, Aweswell or third parties. Registration of the three supervisory filings does not itself prove that a preliminary or disciplinary inquiry has been opened.

Public message to the three LAJ supervisory levels

To the TSJ Canarias Government Secretariat, the Las Palmas Provincial Coordination Secretariat and the national Secretariat-General:

The three institutions now have formal notice of the same evidential corpus and the same finite documentary questions.

We do not ask you to share our conclusions. We ask you to produce the official record capable of confirming, correcting or rejecting them.

What testimonies were actually issued? What did they contain? To whom were they delivered? What finality was certified? Who was served? How were the instruments later used? What effects did they produce inside and outside the insolvency proceeding? What happened when those effects or uses later became known?

If everything was regular, a certified reconstruction can demonstrate it. If it was not, the divergence can be identified and the competent response determined.

The request is more basic than accepting our interpretation: an identifiable authority should assume responsibility for finding out what happened in its own records.
