LEGAL PRACTICE · 1995–2014 · SUN PARK

Álvaro Campanario Hernández: public chronology and documented Sun Park role

This record joins a bounded historical search to the Sun Park file. Official sources establish legal publications in 1995 and 2004 and an insolvency appointment in 2009; internal records establish representation and Community activity from 2009–2011. Inferences about motives, friendships or a prospective LPB appointment are not established.

Search variants: Alvaro Campanario · Álvaro Campanario. They do not replace the canonical identity.

Documented

Documented

ICALPA identifies him as an author in 1995 and 2004; the BOE records an appointment as insolvency administrator by order of 19-Mar-2009. The Sun Park file preserves a 27-Apr-2009 email and meeting/litigation records from 2011–2014.

Attributed account

Attributed account

The declarant from Gil's perimeter says he acted for dissenting owners, negotiated a fixed rent and was central to the 2011 meeting sequence. She also infers that his insolvency work may have related to an expectation of appointment in LPB.

Open proof

Open proof

No evidence has been located that he sought appointment as LPB's insolvency administrator, was a friend of the appointee, knowingly misled the court or entered an unlawful agreement. Powers, the full client file, the rental letter and the appeal file remain outstanding.

READING METHOD

One chronology, three evidence lanes

This record does not convert professional proximity, a shared corporate office or chronological succession into a mandate, knowledge, conflict, agreement or responsibility. Every proposition retains its date, source and limit.

Publication boundary

Official and internal facts are presented as documented facts. The declarant's statements are expressly attributed. An allegation is not a ruling; an open question is not an insinuation of guilt.
