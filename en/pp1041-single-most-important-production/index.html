P0 EVIDENCE DEMAND · PP 1041/2017 · THE DECISIVE BRIDGE

The production capable of deciding who controlled the loss of LPB's disclosure and insolvency-exit route.

PP 1041 is not a side issue. It is the point where assignment economics, control of LPB's representation, the Insolvency Administrator's duties, CAM's benefit, the loss of an exit route and the potential criminal characterisation meet in one recoverable evidential file.

THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT PRODUCTION

The certified PP 1041/2017 file together with the Insolvency Administrator's internal authority, instruction and estate-interest record.

This production can establish—or disprove—who caused the withdrawal filed in LPB's name, whether there was valid authority, what the decision-maker knew, whether the act served the estate, whether the attribution to LPB was true, what contact or alignment existed with the beneficiary perimeter, and whether the loss of disclosure and a financed insolvency exit was deliberate and foreseeable.

It is the evidential fulcrum between a professional-strategy explanation and Por Derecho's allegation of an unauthorised, conflicted and deliberately harmful institutional act that enabled the CAM/Acosta Matos credit→control→title route.

What the present record already fixes

19 DEC 2017
The court opened a functioning disclosure route.

LPB obtained admission of preliminary proceedings seeking the PH122→CAM deed, price, payment and costs. Admission did not decide Article 1535 on the merits; it made the transfer economics judicially obtainable.

25 JAN 2018
The Insolvency Administrator directly changed control of LPB's representation.

The formal mandate of the lawyer pursuing that route was terminated and future LPB action was placed behind an Administrator authorisation gate.

5 MAR 2018
A withdrawal was filed in LPB's name.

CAM did not oppose and the disclosure route ended. The decree records the procedural result; it does not itself reveal the internal instruction, authority, purpose or estate-interest analysis.

13 MAY 2024
The court confirmed that the proceeding remained archived because of that withdrawal.

The lost route therefore had a durable and outcome-relevant procedural effect.

Why this production can decide the case

1 · Authority and authorship

It can identify who instructed, drafted, signed, authorised and filed the withdrawal—and whether that person had power to bind LPB.

2 · Truth or false attribution

It can show whether the filing accurately reflected a competent LPB decision or whether LPB's name was used to present another actor's decision as its own.

3 · Duty, conflict and estate interest

It can reveal whether the Administrator contemporaneously compared continuation against abandonment, identified conflicts and documented a benefit to LPB and the estate.

4 · Knowledge and criminal purpose

It can show what was known about transfer price, redemption, debt reconciliation, CAM's position, ONA, financing and the likely consequence of ending disclosure.

5 · Coordination and beneficiary

It can identify communications, instructions, ratification or alignment between the Administrator-controlled representation and CAM or the Acosta Matos perimeter.

6 · Causation and remedy

It can connect the act to the loss of information, negotiation, redemption, debt satisfaction, insolvency exit, estate value and the later credit-to-title outcome.

Its criminal significance

If the certified file shows that a person without valid authority caused a withdrawal to be filed in LPB's name, knowingly presented the act as LPB's decision, concealed the true decision-maker or induced a court to terminate a valuable route, the evidence may support investigation of procedural deception, documentary or procedural falsity, disloyal administration, participation or necessary cooperation, and deliberate frustration of estate recovery.

If it instead shows valid contemporaneous authority, independent advice, a reasoned comparison of alternatives, a measurable estate benefit and no coordination with the beneficiary perimeter, it may materially weaken or defeat those criminal inferences.

The file is decisive because it can move the case in either direction.

Its civil, commercial and insolvency significance

Validity of representation

Authority can affect whether the abandonment was validly attributable to LPB and what corrective procedural route remains available.

Administrador Concursal responsibility

The record can establish breach of duty, conflict, causation, professional liability, removal or other insolvency consequences.

Loss of redemption and negotiation

Transfer economics could affect debt settlement, redemption, appeal, refinancing and the value of LPB's position.

Pacto-comisorio architecture

The destruction of disclosure and alternatives may form part of the alleged indirect credit-to-control-to-title mechanism.

Damages and lost opportunity

The file can connect one institutional act to the loss of a developed ONA/finance insolvency-exit route.

Restitution and unwinding

If a criminally caused act became an indispensable bridge, dependent later acts, estate value and current benefit must be tested for restitution or accounting.

No later decree can substitute for the missing internal record

A decree recording that a withdrawal was requested in LPB's name proves the procedural event and its result. It does not, by itself, prove:

  • who made the decision;
  • whether the decision-maker had authority;
  • whether LPB validly consented;
  • whether the Administrator acted independently and in the estate's interest;
  • whether the act was coordinated with or requested by the beneficiary;
  • whether the likely loss of redemption and exit was known and intended.

Those questions live in the certified native file and the Administrator's internal record.

Production protocol

Certified and complete

Produce the entire docket, index and native records—not selected extracts or summaries.

Native metadata preserved

Preserve signatures, timestamps, transmission headers, document hashes and access history.

Privilege handled transparently

Any withheld material should be identified in a privilege log with date, author, recipient, subject and legal basis.

Chain of custody

Record source, custodian, export method, certification, hash and every later handling event.

No silent deletion

Preserve email, messaging, document-management and filing-system records, including deleted-item logs and backups.

Corrective material included

Exculpatory, contradictory and estate-benefit evidence must be produced with the inculpatory material.
