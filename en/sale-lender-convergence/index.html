One hotel. Several legal perimeters.

The essential correction

What “LPB concurso*” actually means

Documented fact. The 6 June 2012 order concerned Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. and LPB's estate. It recorded assets of approximately EUR 21.60 million and liabilities of approximately EUR 10.19 million. LPB nevertheless alleged actual insolvency under the inability-to-pay test.

The accurate shorthand is: LPB's asset-surplus, cash-flow insolvency filing, made under imminent auction pressure. It was not a declaration that Sun Park as a whole, all owners, CEXP or the hotel-exploitation layer were insolvent.

The 2012 hinge

A defensible filing decision is not the same as a whole-hotel insolvency

21 MAY
Three routes identified

LPB's external advisers reported the scheduled auction and identified refinancing, Bankia's voluntary suspension, or a concurso declared before auction. This is contemporaneous evidence of imminent enforcement pressure.

31 MAY
Filing described as urgent

The advisers said an early filing was needed for a declaration capable of suspending the auction, while recognising that declaration was not guaranteed.

1-6 JUN
LPB applies; the court declares LPB

The filing alleged actual insolvency. The order accepted the statutory test on the documents presented, retained LPB management subject to intervention, notified the execution court and asked whether the mortgaged LPB assets were affected to business activity.

AFTERWARD
Exit efforts require their own record

The group says it sought to reverse, end or successfully exit the concurso from an early stage. Each proposal, refusal, delay and decision should be indexed separately rather than compressed into a single blame narrative.

Legal precision: asset surplus and cash-flow insolvency can coexist. The order shows what the court accepted from the filed material; the full application bundle is needed to assess every factual premise. No unverified “three-month notice” proposition is relied upon here.

Responsibility without absolution

Different duties at different times

Possible responsibility may be concurrent, successive or contributory, subject to evidence and law. A later actor's conduct does not erase an earlier act; an earlier wrong does not remove a later actor's duty to verify.

Independent corroboration protocol

Eight questions for every actor

  1. What did you receive, when and from whom?
  2. What did you verify independently?
  3. What decision did you make and who authorised it?
  4. What did you pass to a successor, servicer, office-holder or court?
  5. What was omitted, disputed or corrected?
  6. Which contemporaneous records support your account?
  7. What preservation and custodian searches were completed?
  8. What corrective or restorative action do you now propose?

This is a structured corroboration method, not a presumption of guilt. Silence is not admission. The public register should say only: “No substantive response received by [date].”

Recovery is wider than LPB property

Each right and loss must be assigned to its actual holder. Property value, rent, operating profit, enterprise value and shareholder diminution must then be reconciled to prevent gaps or double recovery.
