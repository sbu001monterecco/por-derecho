One hotel could concentrate several economic histories without becoming one legal person or one insolvency estate.

LPB property, bank finance, hotel operation, owners’ governance and later credit assignments converged around Sun Park. The evidential task is to reconstruct where they intersected and where they remained legally distinct.

Reading rule

Convergence does not mean identity. A financial decision affecting the same platform does not automatically make the creditor owner of the hotel, LPB’s insolvency the insolvency of every owner, or the operator owner of the properties.

Four perimeters that must remain separate

1 · Property

LPB held a majority real-estate position, not 100% of Sun Park. Matkator and other owners retained their own rights.

2 · Credit

Loans and security created creditor rights. Being mortgagee or assignee did not, by itself, equal ownership or operating authority over the whole complex.

3 · Operation

CEXP and later Monterecco/Pink appear in a distinct operating/tourism layer. Spanish Monterecco Sun Park, S.L. → Pink Canary Services, S.L. is a name continuity, not a successor company.

4 · Insolvency

Insolvency 36/2012 concerned LPB and its estate. Its powers did not automatically absorb the property, income, contracts or rights of the other perimeters.

The sequence that makes the convergence visible

2008 · LPB majority property and finance

LPB’s majority Sun Park position and its financing formed the patrimonial starting point. The exact composition must remain property-by-property; the complex was always mixed ownership.

1 December 2011 → 14 February 2012 · change of LPB shareholder

Monterecco Sun Park Limited acquired the LPB shares from Multimatrix; later came the management decision, Spanish publicity deed, Registry presentation and BORME record. Monterecco Sun Park Limited was later renamed Aweswell Limited: the same UK company, not a transfer to a successor.

28 February 2012 · commercial platform before insolvency

The viability plan already described Summers Villages, Sun Park Living and a proposed tour-operator lane. The business and direct-distribution architecture therefore pre-dated the insolvency proceeding.

15 May → 22 June 2012 · enforcement and auction

The primary Mortgage Enforcement 90/2012 diligence schedules 158 properties and fixes auction for 22 June. That pressure explains the urgency of the defensive insolvency filing without deciding the whole banking or business causation question.

4–6 June 2012 · Insolvency 36/2012

The court received and declared LPB’s insolvency before the auction. The proceeding reached LPB, not the whole hotel or CEXP, Matkator, Aweswell or other owners.

2014 → 2017 · credit transfer

The creditor position passed through SAREB and Promontoria Holding 122 B.V. to the 20 October 2017 assignment to Construcciones Acosta Matos, S.A. Each transfer requires its own title, balance and scope.

2017–2018 · credit, access and control begin to overlap

CAM’s entry as credit assignee coincides in time with a phase involving access, security, owners’ governance and later material control that must be reconstructed from each actor’s own acts. That coincidence does not automatically convert the credit into authority over the whole hotel.

2022–2023 · later title and end of enforcement

Later adjudication/dación and the eventual termination of Mortgage Enforcement 90/2012 are later stages. Later title does not retrospectively authorise every earlier act of control or commercialisation.

The 2012 hinge: three questions, not one

Why was the filing made?

Enforcement with an imminent auction is the documented immediate trigger. The filing also followed earlier viability and restructuring work.

Was LPB insolvent?

The order recorded assets above liabilities, but the historical test concerned regular payment of due obligations. Asset value and liquidity must be analysed separately.

Who caused the crisis?

No single figure resolves it. Financial products, payments, default, governance, operation, receivables, interference and later decisions belong to distinct causation lanes.

What could the insolvency proceeding control?

LPB and its estate, according to each procedural phase and order. Any practical reach into other rights requires an independent documentary basis.

The lender chain does not erase origin responsibility — or automatically create it

Each later credit holder may have its own duties to verify what it acquired, for what amount and on what documentation. But assignment does not automatically make the assignee responsible for every earlier act by the bank, nor does it absolve earlier actors of their own conduct.

Later convergence: investment, public support and operation do not automatically validate the earlier premise

When the same asset later appears in RICPE, RIC, regional incentives, ERDF and MYND, the traceability question is what each new gatekeeper checked about ownership, availability, perimeter and authority. Later formalisation, finance or public support does not automatically resolve earlier patrimonial disputes.

Equally, an earlier dispute does not justify concluding automatically that every later finance, incentive or operation was unlawful. Each layer requires its own file.
