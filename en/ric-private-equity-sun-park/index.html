Control point · provenance before appearance

The name “Community” is not an authority audit.

This is the bridge the reader must stop and test. A document apparently coming from an owner community, or a person presenting as its president or administrator, does not by itself establish appointment, resolution, scope, representation of third-party units or power to produce the requested consequence.

Reading rule: never accept “the Community authorised it” as a conclusion. Require the exact community, organ, current appointment, meeting notice, quorum, voting-entitled owners, resolution and minutes, mandate, units covered, each third party’s title or consent, and the verification performed by the recipient.

The chain that must be proved

An appearance of authority cannot become legitimacy merely by circulating.

Downstream repetition can amplify an unverified premise. Every arrow requires its own independent, contemporaneous documentary bridge.

Legal conclusion applied to this record

Article 3 of Spain’s Horizontal Property Act permits the true owner to dispose of an individual unit. A valid CAM acquisition of a particular property would therefore establish ownership only of that property and its inseparable common share: it would not transfer LPB, Matkator/Thompson units, common assets or the hotel as one business; nor would it alone authorise works, occupation, hotel operation or representation over another owner’s property. Articles 7 and 17 constrain acts affecting the structure, common elements, constitutive title or by-laws.

The strongest evidential formulation is not “CAM owned nothing”.
