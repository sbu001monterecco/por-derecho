The control thesis

One chain: source, authority, control, capital and benefit.

The unitary question: how did a legally fragmented hotel asset, subject to insolvency proceedings and third-party rights, come to be presented as an acquired, unencumbered and investment-ready Acosta Matos project; which controls did it pass; when was that picture qualified or corrected; what new evidence permitted its re-entry; and who ultimately obtained the operating platform, finance, support, income and value?

RIC is not a peripheral third party in this record. Its materials introduced Sun Park into a regulated investment perimeter and described financial, legal, fiscal, risk, executive and Board filters. The public question is what it received, independently verified, communicated and preserves.

Ten finite checks

What a documented response can resolve.

  1. Identity, Board resolution and appointment date of the System Manager.
  2. Independence, resources and historic relationships of the handler or provider.
  3. Registration and preservation of every communication and attachment received.
  4. Exclusion of examined persons from control of scope and evidence.
  5. Complete, unfiltered delivery to the President and non-conflicted Board.
  6. Individual interest, abstention and report-reliance declarations.
  7. Existence, authorship, date, qualifications and circulation of the four described reports.
  8. Appointment and mandate of a genuinely independent external investigator.
  9. A reasoned, minuted Board decision and remediation measures.
  10. Whether information supplied to shareholders, investors or authorities requires correction.

Public basis: RIC’s management team, published Board, Ethics Channel and Spanish Law 2/2023.
