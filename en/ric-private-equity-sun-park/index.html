The control thesis

One chain: source, authority, control, capital and benefit.

The unitary question: how did a legally fragmented hotel asset, subject to insolvency proceedings and third-party rights, come to be presented as an acquired, unencumbered and investment-ready Acosta Matos project; which controls did it pass; when was that picture qualified or corrected; what new evidence permitted its re-entry; and who ultimately obtained the operating platform, finance, support, income and value?

RIC is not a peripheral third party in this record. Its materials introduced Sun Park into a regulated investment perimeter and described financial, legal, fiscal, risk, executive and Board filters. The public question is what it received, independently verified, communicated and preserves.

“Predatory inclusion” · attributed theory

How outsiders can become collateral damage and a narrative human shield.

Gil Marer's allegation The term describes placing a disputed asset inside an investor, adviser and public-control perimeter before title and authority are clarified. The alleged mechanism has four steps: (1) a presentation creates an expectation of investment and regulated legitimacy; (2) investors and administrations acquire economic, tax or reputational exposure; (3) unwinding the project imposes correction, repayment and other costs on outsiders; and (4) that exposure may become practical pressure to consolidate the property outcome anticipated by the presentation. Investors are collateral damage because they bear correction risk, and a narrative human shield because their presence may be invoked to make challenge appear more costly.

Boundary: the record establishes Sun Park's inclusion in the presentation and the later title contradiction. It does not yet establish a specific RIC allocation to Sun Park, an investor's applied tax advantage, causal reliance, loss, coordination with the court or administrator, or fraudulent intent. “Investment and tax fraud”, “procedural fraud” and “insolvency fraud” remain alleged legal characterisations that must be proved element by element.

Established

Investor presentation; purchase and encumbrance statements; fragmented title acknowledged in 2021; design and outcome of the bid process; limited scope of the 2022 deed.

Missing for the investment/tax theory

Specific investor and line; subscription; disbursement; loan or use; RIC benefit actually applied; reliance, loss, correction and causation.

Hypothesis requiring investigation

That adjudication was pursued to “make real” the brochure's anticipated picture, or that administrator, court and advisers acted to legitimise it. The sequence permits the question; it does not decide the answer.

Attributed conclusion under adversarial test

Legitimate editorial intervention, strategic use of an order, or sabotage?

Gil Marer alleges that the sequence—counterparty warning, request to wait, incompatible accounts and delivery of an adverse order that stopped an already prepared piece—functioned as sabotage of publication. The direct record supports saying that the piece stopped after the order was received and that a prior request to wait was made. It does not yet support stating as fact who alerted CAM, who sent the order, whether Meeting Point coordinated with anyone, or that Romera lacked independent editorial judgment. El Economista, Romera, CAM and Meeting Point are invited to identify the speakers, communications and their complete account.
