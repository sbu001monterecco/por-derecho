RICPE: rejection, substantive compliance and circulation of the alert

Gil Marer's position as an alertador/informante and injured party: I allege that the handling of this alert is materially non-compliant and is operating as a barrier keeping relevant information from independent board scrutiny, shareholders and investors, and competent public authorities. My allegations of non-compliance and concealment are submitted for documentary testing, not presented as judicial or supervisory findings of guilt.

The question is not whether RICPE must accept my conclusions or determine third-party property litigation. It is whether it independently and diligently examined its own conduct, and who received, assessed, preserved or decided not to pass on each relevant item. A personal interest in recovering loss does not, by itself, erase a report's public-interest dimension.

Why the alert is not exhausted by my private loss

Pages 1–3 classify the report under financial matters, public revenue and money laundering and raise corporate governance, conflicts, due diligence, investor information, valuation and financial traceability relating to Sun Park/MYND Yaiza. They ask for project presentations to be reconciled with the internal July 2021 certification and its use in the insolvency proceedings. They request examination of HNT's investment criteria, security and financing, eligible expenditure and employment commitments. Those are questions about RICPE's controls, not merely a claim for compensation.

The source itself separates private RICPE investment, the RIC tax incentive, regional incentives and European/FEDER funding. Coexistence does not establish a single pool, double funding or fraud. Examination needs to identify each transaction, asset, title or right, cost, beneficiary, decision and competent authority. Proximity does not turn Sun Park into an Orion asset or transfer liability to every director, lender or adviser.

My criticism is that the third-party-dispute classification may leave questions about RICPE's own conduct unanswered: which title and diligence file supported financing; what changed between the conditional study and approval; who assessed conflicts; what investors were told; and which control checked the use of funds. RICPE may contest an indication's relevance or sufficiency, but that assessment should be testable against the particular document and corresponding reason.

What the allegation of non-compliance means here

RICPE's public ethics-channel statement distinguishes compliance alerts from ordinary complaints and announces confidentiality, security and good-faith anti-retaliation safeguards. Its Compliance Unit is described as a permanent internal body evaluating and reporting on compliance and system effectiveness. My challenge compares that stated function with what the certificate actually permits a reader to verify. This review has not obtained the complete contemporaneous internal protocol or admissibility file.

A specific document was requested; its specific assessment is not identified. The 19 August request identifies RICPE's own certification. The questions are whether it was available, which version was obtained, whether someone examined it before closure, who did so and why it failed the applied threshold. Requesting a copy may legitimately check a version; the mere existence of a certification does not prove an offence. But a general insufficiency conclusion does not let an outside reader reconstruct that analysis.

Responsible officer and conflicts. The resolution attributes closure to the person responsible for the system and ends without naming the natural-person decision-maker. That does not prove there was no valid appointment or make the decision automatically invalid. It leaves open who drafted, reviewed, approved and executed closure and whether they were independent of people named in the alert. Ithikios's technical involvement is not an independent merits investigation.

Visibility and preservation. The 19 August visibility annotation and the access-history table without entries on page 6 raise traceability questions. They do not conclusively prove intentional suppression, absence of internal logs or destruction of records. The native events, notifications, permissions and retention policy are needed, including evidence contradicting my hypothesis.

Legal test and limits. Law 2/2023, articles 5, 6, 8 and 9, addresses effective handling, identified responsibility, independence and diligent processing; an external platform does not displace that responsibility. This is not an obligation to open a full investigation into every account, and a maximum response period is not a minimum investigation duration. The external-channel procedure in article 18 must not automatically be imposed on this internal channel. The certificate does not itself establish a missed deadline.

Reporting-person status: a material legal qualification. Articles 2, 3 and 35 require the relevant material scope, employment/professional context and reasonable grounds, not conclusive proof. However, article 35.2.a expressly excludes information contained in inadmitted communications from the protection described there. That cannot be omitted, nor can protection be guaranteed by calling myself an alertador. The validity of the inadmission, application of that exclusion and the sector-specific regime require independent assessment. Challenging closure does not confer immunity to publish anything.

The concealment allegation: recipient by recipient

Gil Marer alleges possible containment of information that defeats useful circulation and, where knowledge and purpose are proved, deliberate concealment. The certificate does not establish who received or did not receive information inside or outside RICPE. Lack of confirmation to the reporting person is not proof that no onward transmission occurred. Intentional concealment requires an identified actor, known information, specific duty, act or omission, recipient, date and demonstrated effect.

The board and independent oversight

“Independent board” here expresses a demand for non-conflicted scrutiny; it does not certify that every director is independent. Which non-conflicted body supervised the risk and channel operation, what summary did it receive, and what did it decide? The system officer's independence also protects against instructions from the board itself. This does not require giving every director the complete confidential case file. A minimised risk report may permit meaningful oversight while respecting confidentiality.

Shareholders and investors

The question is whether verified risks were assessed for materiality to investment decisions and whether disclosure or correction was required. Not every allegation must be circulated in full to investors. The existence of an alert, verification of its contents, a material risk and a specific disclosure obligation are different propositions. The requested evidence is the materiality analysis, information versions and circulation trail—not an assumption that each investor was ignorant or entitled to the channel's personal data.

CNMV: preserve the action that is already documented

The public CNMV verification route distinguishes supervisory communications from public-information access. The existing register identifies the 27 August acknowledgement of the 20 August communication (PD-SP-EVT-0175) and the 4 September decision, outgoing reference 2026149422, extending the public-access request 2026114903 by one month because of volume and complexity (PD-SP-EVT-0177/0178: decision and notification). Accordingly, this analysis does not assert complete CNMV ignorance, or describe the extension as an access refusal or supervisory closure.

The remaining questions concern what RICPE itself communicated, which duties it considered, what each unit received and what the authority examined. Notice supplied by the reporting person does not itself establish RICPE's compliance; an acknowledgement does not establish investigation or acceptance of the allegations. The official CNMV reporting channel has its own scope and requirements.

Other affected public authorities

The GC/836/P06 traceability dossier and public-authority reconstruction distinguish AEAT/RIC, regional and State incentive functions and FEDER controls. Receipt and the particular duty must be established for each office, not inferred collectively from a diagram. Article 9.2.j of Law 2/2023 addresses referral to the prosecution service for indicatively criminal facts and to the European Public Prosecutor where EU financial interests are affected. This requires assessment of facts and competence, not automatic referral of every commercial dispute to every authority.

The records that would resolve the dispute

The request is for a verifiable index, proportionate access or certification where legitimate restrictions apply: the officer's appointment and delegation; conflict checks and recusals; permissions and access, allocation, visibility and notification logs; the inventory and versions of documents examined; reasons connecting each RICPE-specific issue to a source and threshold; authors and approval of closure; preservation and lawful retention; minimised reporting to non-conflicted oversight; investor materiality and correction assessments; and assessment and records of any regulatory or criminal referrals that were required.

Preservation does not mean indiscriminate or perpetual retention of personal data. It must be reconciled with articles 26 and 32 and applicable evidential obligations. Nor is wholesale publication of sensitive logs requested. An index, dates, functions, certifications and severable documents can make accountability possible without unnecessary disclosure.

The project's unitary criminal hypothesis is retained, but it requires individual evidence of participation, knowledge, contribution and causation. Neither a private-channel closure nor professional connections establish fraud, concealment offences, collusion or collective guilt. RICPE and the people mentioned may provide contrary evidence, explain actions not visible in the certificate or correct facts; corrections must be linked with equivalent prominence.
