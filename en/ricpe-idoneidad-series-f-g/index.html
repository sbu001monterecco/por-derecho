Series F, Series G and Decree 224/2022: let the record show what each institution authorised and what each euro financed.

This page does not publish a finding of fraud or illegality. It publishes a concrete timing and documentary question, the legitimate explanations that may exist, the records capable of resolving it, and the authority competent to answer.

Did Decree 224/2022 and the AEAT binding report of 17 November 2022 expressly cover Series F — €1,598,849.32 for Hotel MYND Yaiza — Series G — €4,974,853.78 for employment creation — both, or was there a second/amended authorisation?

Four open scenarios — none is presumed

1 · One file covered both phases

The application, AEAT report and final act should then identify the two phases, instruments, amounts, timetable and authorised uses.

2 · Separate authorisations existed

The application, binding report, resolution/decree, date, amount, instrument and file for each phase should be identified.

3 · The approval was amended

The amendment, date, reason, scope and file should be identified, showing what changed from the original approval.

4 · No second act existed

The competent authority and RICPE should identify the legal/documentary basis on which each series and use was treated as covered by the existing authorisation.

Public sources and legal framework

Council of Government · 1 Dec 2022 · F.O.D. 25RD 1758/2007 · art. 15Official RIC procedure · Canary Islands

The official Council of Government agenda for 1 December 2022 records, as F.O.D. 25, approval of the draft decree declaring the suitability/idoneidad of the financial instruments referred to in an application submitted by RIC Private Equity Investment Partners, S.C.R., S.A. Article 15 of RD 1758/2007 requires the application to identify who will carry out the project, describe the project and RIC requirements, its timetable and the characteristics of the instruments; it also requires a binding AEAT report. The Canary Islands procedure identifies the Tax Policy Service as processing body and the Council of Government as deciding body.

Series F/G figures and the hotel-opening date derive from RICPE's audited 2023 accounts controlled in the RICPE/CNMV dossier. The full Resolution 28/2026 and appeal contain personal data and are not reproduced here unredacted; their procedural effects, references and necessary metadata are recorded.
