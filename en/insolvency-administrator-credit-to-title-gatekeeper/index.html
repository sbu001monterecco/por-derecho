Why the PP 1041 bridge is potentially case-changing

Direct causation

The 25 January act directly ended the mandate of the lawyer pursuing disclosure. It was not merely an event occurring before the later filing.

Asymmetry of proof

LPB had to litigate for the transfer economics; CAM's recognition relied in part on the verification attributed to the Administrator.

Counterfactual value

Price and payment information could have supported redemption, debt reconciliation, negotiation, appeal and a financed exit, even if Article 1535 was ultimately disputed.

Strongest present allegation

Por Derecho alleges that the Administrator-controlled office did not merely fail to preserve LPB's route: it became the essential institutional bridge through which information and alternatives were removed, CAM's creditor position was operationalised, disputed valuation and control were allowed to harden, and the eventual dación became progressively difficult to reverse.

This allegation is stronger than a negligence case but remains dependent on primary proof of authority, instruction, knowledge, purpose, conflict, benefit and causal contribution. It is not an adjudicated criminal finding.

The strongest defence

The Administrator may say that he acted within statutory liquidation powers and under judicial supervision; that the Article 1535 theory lacked merit and was rejected; that changing counsel served cost, strategy, control or conflict management; that ACTÚA was professional; that ONA and finance were conditional; that material-control disputes belonged to other owners or proceedings; that the dación was judicially authorised; and that no surplus existed.

Those propositions must be tested against the native authority, decision, valuation, competition and accounting files. Later orders do not themselves supply the missing contemporaneous reasoning.
