Cristo Ayose Suárez Pimentel — this is a protection and traceability route, not a complaint against him

Express position: Por Derecho/Aweswell regards Cristo as historic project-side counsel and makes no professional-conduct allegation against him here. The purpose of the 21 August 2026 communication is to recover evidence about pressure directed at him by the Insolvency Administrator and to certify whether the threatened Bar referral ever became an actual institutional action.

The source chain confirms that on 25 January 2018 the Insolvency Administrator said Cristo's conduct would be reported to the Bar “for the corresponding purposes”, specifically terminated his professional relationship with LPB, requested venia, required him not to act, warned of possible judicial action and instructed the procurador not to file papers without IA authorisation. On 4 February 2023 Cristo recovered that email and described it as the one relating to the “complaint to the Bar and the other threats”.

What remains open: no registered complaint, preliminary information, hearing, file, decision or closure record by ICATF against Cristo has been located in the source mailbox. Certification is therefore the objective. If a Bar action existed and produced a professional or reputational effect, the purpose is to reconstruct it and give Cristo the traceability needed to understand it and, where the certified record warrants it, correct or rehabilitate any consequence affecting him.

Boundary: the threat and specific mandate termination are documented. It is not yet asserted as fact that the IA's conduct was legally unlawful, that a complaint was actually filed, that the Bar sanctioned Cristo, or that the pressure alone caused the later discontinuance of DP 1041/2017.
