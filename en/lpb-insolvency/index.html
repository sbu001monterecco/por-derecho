Concurso 36/2012 · cash flow · estate perimeter · causation

LPB Insolvency*.

In June 2012, Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. sought and obtained a voluntary concurso while facing an imminent mortgage auction. The asterisk matters: LPB was a majority but not sole Sun Park property owner, and the proceeding did not place the whole hotel, CEXP, Pink, Matkator, Aweswell or other owners into insolvency.

Read the evidence-led answer Follow the lender chain

Current status: Bankia's enforcement is documented as the immediate filing trigger. The linked-product causation case remains contested in Valencia PO 1859/2023; trial is set on the located court record for 28 January 2027 at 10:00. No merits conclusion is drawn.

The short answer

Three decisions, three separate tests.

A lender's enforcement, a director's protective filing and a court's declaration are not the same act. Each must be tested against the information, duties and alternatives at that date.

01 · Bankia

Immediate trigger; wider responsibility contested.

The announced 22 June 2012 auction explains the timing of LPB's filing. Whether the swap, pledge, refinancing, notices and enforcement were properly sold, administered and causative remains disputed. The complete Arrecife default and acceleration file is still required.

02 · LPB filing

Defensive does not mean false.

LPB had very low recorded cash and severe negative working capital while also showing assets above liabilities. Under the 2012 cash-flow test, those facts can coexist. On the current record, requesting the concurso was a defensible protective decision, not a proved reckless filing.

03 · Court order

Not shown to have been legally wrong.

The court accepted the statutory threshold on the documents filed. Its reasoning did not publicly reconstruct every due debt or available source of cash, but the asset surplus alone does not invalidate the declaration. The complete petition and annexes remain the decisive control.

Bounded conclusion The present record supports a real LPB liquidity crisis and a defensive filing under execution pressure. It leaves open who caused or aggravated that crisis, whether it could readily have been cured, and whether later actors used the limited proceeding beyond LPB's estate.

The asterisk

What every insolvency reference must preserve.

These are not qualifications added for style. They prevent a corporate proceeding from being misdescribed as authority over a multi-owner hotel and its distinct operating layers.

  • 1. One debtorOnly Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U., NIF B35998582, entered Concurso 36/2012.
  • 2. Majority, not 100%The filing described 159 of 220 accommodation units, 29 of 40 premises and pools/solaria. LPB did not own every Sun Park property.
  • 3. Estate is not hotelThe proceeding reached LPB's assets and rights. It did not automatically absorb Matkator or other owners' properties or income.
  • 4. Exploitation is separateCEXP, Pink and other contractual, tourism, on-site, invoicing and income-recipient roles require their own date-specific evidence.
  • 5. Asset surplus can coexistThe 2012 test concerned regular ability to pay due obligations. It was not a balance-sheet-only test.
  • 6. Trigger is not sole causeThe auction explains the filing date. Finance, trading, governance, receivables and operational interference remain separate causation tracks.
  • 7. Office is not universal controlThe insolvency administrator's powers concerned LPB according to the procedural phase; they did not create title or authority over every owner, unit or business.
  • 8. Later judgment is not finalThe 2023 culpable-calificacion judgment is materially adverse and must be disclosed. The located record shows an appeal and no authenticated appellate disposition by the audit cut-off.

Signed contract and live claim

What the financial-package record now establishes.

The signed terms correct parts of the voice recollection while leaving the legal dispute fully open.

17 June 2008 · EUR 5 million risk-management contract

The signed schedule records coverage from 7 July 2008 to 7 January 2012 with quarterly net settlements. LPB paid Euribor 3M minus a spread in specified periods or 5.15%/4.95% fixed bands below stated triggers, and received Euribor 3M. The contract states that below the fixed bands LPB would bear a higher financing cost for that period than without the operation.

27 May 2010 · EUR 850,000 second mortgage and EUR 405,000 pledge

Those amounts are documented. Aweswell's pleading and expert say the pledge was consumed immediately and only EUR 445,000 was effectively available. CaixaBank's expert disputes proof and the claimed economic treatment. The cash entries, post-concurso payments and reciprocal restitution remain to be reconciled.

May to June 2012 · enforcement and filing

Contemporary advice records a Bankia auction fixed for 22 June and identifies refinancing, a voluntary Bankia stay or a declared concurso as available routes. The report that execution was filed the day after a grace period ended remains unverified pending the complete EH 90/2012 docket and notices.

The LEC text in force in early 2012 did not impose a universal three-month default threshold: article 693 allowed total acceleration after a missed instalment where that consequence was agreed and registered. The general three-month threshold arrived in May 2013. Later 12/15-month protection has a defined natural-person and residential scope, so it is not a universal rule for LPB's corporate hotel mortgage. This correction does not establish that Bankia complied with LPB's deed, registration, demand, amount, notice or good-faith requirements.

2023 to 2027 · Valencia PO 1859/2023

Aweswell seeks annulment of the swap and linked 2010 instruments, with subsidiary damages. CaixaBank disputes standing, scope, preclusion, time bars, breach, causation and quantum. The 6 November 2025 court diligence set trial for 28 January 2027 at 10:00 before JPI No. 27 Valencia.

Rate correction The remembered “4.8% to 5.8% on top of the mortgage” description is not used as a verified LPB contractual fact. The signed schedule contains 5.15% and 4.95% bands, subject to triggers and netting against Euribor 3M. Overall cost must be calculated from actual loan and settlement records.

2012 legal test

Liquidity, accuracy and perimeter decide the description.

Current restructuring concepts cannot be projected backwards. In 2012, actual insolvency meant inability regularly to meet due obligations; imminent insolvency had no fixed three-month forecast in the statutory text then applied.

Documented

What the order recorded

LPB alleged actual insolvency. The 6 June order recorded approximately EUR 21.60 million of assets and EUR 10.19 million of liabilities, declared LPB only, retained its management subject to intervention and notified the mortgage-enforcement court.

Still required

What decides accuracy and avoidability

The certified petition and annexes; aged due debts; unrestricted bank balances and pledges; daily cash forecasts; collectible CEXP income; committed bridge finance; default and auction documents; and the first proposals to terminate or successfully exit the proceeding.

Official controls: Ley 22/2003 in the 2012 historical wording; LEC article 693 in the pre-filing wording; Law 1/2013; Law 5/2019 article 24; current consolidated TRLC. Date-specific application, contractual requirements, case law and transitional rules require Spanish counsel.
