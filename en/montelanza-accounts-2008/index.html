Why this matters to the 360° reconstruction

Economic origin

The filing fixes a 17 June 2008 transaction, asset exit and cessation of Montelanza's activity, providing a contemporaneous checkpoint for the economic history preceding LPB's insolvency architecture.

Physical asset

The notes record major 2008 disposals of tangible fixed assets and create a source for reconciling what left Montelanza against later Land Registry units and fragmented ownership.

RIC tax history

The notes contain historic Canary Islands Investment Reserve materialisation. That is Montelanza's own tax-history node and must not be conflated with later RIC/RICPE financing of MYND Yaiza.
