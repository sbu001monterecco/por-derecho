Transfer is not repayment.

A holder sale needs an eligible willing buyer, price, formalities and register/assignment mechanics. It changes the creditor; it does not extinguish the issuer’s debt. Succession follows applicable formalities. A Note exchange likewise requires its own closing and reconciliation and does not create cash or reset maturity automatically.
