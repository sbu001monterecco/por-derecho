The first-money proposition

Simple enough to understand in one meeting.

The first investor should not need to underwrite the entire future programme. The facility is intended to stand on its own, with a defined security package, controlled deployment and contractual repayment mechanics.

01

Commit

A defined amount is committed under definitive bilateral documentation.

02

Keep control

Undrawn cash can remain with the investor rather than sitting as unrestricted operating cash.

03

Draw selectively

Only approved utilisations are funded, with documentary support and permitted-use controls.

04

Secure

The agreed security and founder-support package is put in place subject to legal validity and perfection.

05

Take out

Targeted cash repayment within 24 months from refinancing, replacement capital, realised proceeds or other lawful sources.

Proposed downside architecture

Security is specific, documented and enforceable—or it is not counted.

The definitive package is intended to capture the maximum agreed legally available protection without describing disputed, future or unperfected rights as present collateral.

01

100% AWESWELL share pledge

Proposed pledge over all issued AWESWELL shares: Patricia as registered holder, with Patricia and Gil joining in respect of their beneficial interests, subject to definitive English-law documentation.

02

AWESWELL corporate security

Security over agreed eligible AWESWELL assets, receivables, controlled accounts and proceeds, with asset-by-asset validity, priority and perfection controls.

03

Founder support

Personal Guarantee and Integrity Undertaking, kept legally distinct from corporate security and from any separately perfected founder collateral.

04

Cash-control discipline

Drawn money may move through controlled accounts, escrow or direct approved-payee mechanics. Undrawn commitments do not become debt merely because they exist.

Important: “secured” does not mean risk-free. Security can fall in value, rank behind superior claims, take time and money to enforce, or prove insufficient to repay the whole investment.

The intended exit

A larger financing can replace first capital without leaving a gap.

Institutional refinancing is one intended take-out route, not a promise. No bank, private-credit manager or other institution is represented as committed unless and until definitive documentation says so.

Intended repayment sources

  • Larger institutional / private-credit refinancing
  • Replacement private or strategic capital
  • Realised and legally available recovery proceeds
  • Asset / project refinancing or other lawful financing
  • Available distributable cash, where permitted

What is not assumed

  • No institution is treated as committed before it signs
  • Contested claims are not presented as current cash
  • Programme authority is not outstanding debt
  • A 24-month target is not a guaranteed exit
  • Future capital is not counted twice as repayment and security
