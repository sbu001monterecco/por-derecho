One transaction · two parallel lanes

Bridge first. Institutional programme in parallel.

The bridge is designed to stand on its own as valid secured debt. SRLN-2026 is the scale and take-out route, not a condition to the validity of first-money funding.

A

FIRST MONEY

Committed Secured Delayed-Draw Bridge

Capital is committed but remains with the lender until an approved utilisation. Each draw is documented, reconciled and linked to permitted purposes, with direct-to-payee or controlled-account settlement available where agreed.

  • Commitment and utilisation separated
  • Interest only on funded amounts
  • Share pledge and corporate security package at closing
  • Founder support and controlled-cash mechanics
  • No dependence on future Note issuance for validity
B

SCALE / TAKE-OUT

SRLN-2026 Private Note Programme

A controlled institutional note framework intended to refinance or migrate eligible Bridge utilisations after the relevant corporate, legal, tax, valuation, security, settlement and registration conditions are satisfied.

  • Programme authority is not debt
  • Notes arise only on funded, validly closed utilisation
  • Authoritative register and settlement reconciliation
  • Security continuity subject to definitive documentation
  • Private distribution only to legally eligible recipients

Proposed downside architecture

Security is built around control, not a headline.

These protections are proposed transaction terms and become effective only when the definitive documents are executed and any required perfection steps are completed.

01

100% share pledge

Proposed pledge over the issued shares of AWESWELL LIMITED, with Patricia signing as registered holder and both Patricia and Gil joining in respect of their beneficial interests, subject to definitive English-law documentation.

02

Corporate security

AWESWELL security over agreed eligible corporate assets and receivables, with asset-by-asset validity, priority and perfection controls.

03

Founder support

Personal Guarantee and Integrity Undertaking, kept legally distinct from company security and from any separately perfected founder collateral.

04

Controlled cash

Undrawn money can remain with the investor. Drawn funds may move through controlled accounts, escrow or direct approved-payee mechanics rather than unrestricted operating cash.
