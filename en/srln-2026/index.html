First Capital · €500k–€5m

Short-horizon capital that can stand on its own.

The first-money investor should not have to underwrite the entire future programme. The intended structure is a secured delayed-draw bilateral facility with controlled deployment and a target cash take-out within 24 months.

01

Commit

A defined amount is committed under definitive bilateral documentation.

02

Keep control

Undrawn cash can remain with the investor instead of sitting as unrestricted operating cash.

03

Draw selectively

Only approved utilisations are funded, with documentary support and permitted-use controls.

04

Secure

The agreed security and founder-support package is established subject to validity and perfection.

05

Take out

Targeted cash repayment within 24 months from refinancing, replacement capital, realised proceeds or other lawful sources.

Scale Capital · €5m–€50m+

Larger capital without forcing one instrument on every mandate.

Scale Capital can be structured around the actual counterparty mandate: standby / revolving availability, private credit, structured debt, preferred or participating capital, strategic equity or another institutionally appropriate HoldCo structure.

01

Standby / delayed draw

Commitment can exist before utilisation. Undrawn capital need not become debt or unrestricted cash.

02

Private credit / structured debt

Senior, whole-loan, subordinated, preferred or hybrid structures can be tested against actual security, cash flows and mandate.

03

Preferred / strategic equity

Longer-duration capital can participate at HoldCo or another agreed level without being forced into the first-money debt format.

04

Refinance / scale

Larger capital can refinance First Capital, fund acquisitions, support new projects or establish recurring Sponsor capacity.

First Capital is designed not to become an intercreditor trap.

A bona fide refinancing that pays the first investor the amounts then contractually due should be capable of closing through simultaneous funds-flow, release / re-ranking and replacement-security mechanics under the definitive documents.

Shared downside architecture

Security is specific, documented and enforceable—or it is not counted.

Both capital routes start from the same discipline: identify the legal owner, define the secured obligation, perfect the available collateral, control cash, and never describe disputed or future value as present collateral.

01

100% AWESWELL share pledge

Proposed pledge over all issued AWESWELL shares: Patricia as registered holder, with Patricia and Gil joining in respect of their beneficial interests, subject to definitive English-law documentation.

02

AWESWELL corporate security

Security over agreed eligible AWESWELL assets, receivables, controlled accounts and proceeds, with asset-by-asset validity, priority and perfection controls.

03

Founder support

Personal Guarantee and Integrity Undertaking, kept legally distinct from corporate security and from any separately perfected founder collateral.

04

Cash-control discipline

Drawn money may move through controlled accounts, escrow or direct approved-payee mechanics. Undrawn commitments do not become debt merely because they exist.

Important: “secured” does not mean risk-free. Security can fall in value, rank behind superior claims, take time and money to enforce, or prove insufficient to repay the whole investment.
