Capital roles

First Capital and Scale Capital are functions, not fixed investor classes.

An eligible HNW principal, family office, private bank, private-credit provider or institution may fit according to the role, mandate and negotiated transaction—not because of a public ticket band.

01

First Capital

Faster liquidity and flexibility. Amount, tenor, draw conditions, security, repayment and economics are negotiated privately with the actual eligible counterparty.

02

Scale Capital

Larger or longer-horizon capital. The counterparty may use its own terms, credit process and documentation, including a refinance or replacement of earlier capital.

03

Instrument flexibility

The programme is designed to support an appropriate private note, facility or other negotiated structure without forcing one bilateral lender or one immutable format.

04

Cash discipline

Agreement is not cash. Commitment is not cash. Undrawn availability is not cash. Funding becomes liquidity only when validly drawn and received.
