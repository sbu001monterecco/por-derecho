From the 2011 Community intervention to the present.

A criminal-first reconstruction of Gil Marer's allegations and the Por Derecho corpus. The question is not whether an adverse sequence “looks criminal”, but where — if anywhere — actor-specific evidence moves beyond a civil, Community, insolvency or commercial dispute and can support reasonable suspicion of deliberate unlawful conduct.

Governing boundary: “hijacking”, “takeover”, “instrumentalisation”, “conflict”, “disloyalty”, “fraud” and “criminality” are used only as attributed allegations, investigative hypotheses or legal classifications to be tested unless a competent source has established the specific proposition. Association, sequence and benefit do not by themselves prove agreement or intent.
DocumentedMinutes, judgments, registers, controlled emails and dated facts.
AttributedStatements by Gil or other witnesses, preserved as statements.
InferenceCausal or knowledge hypothesis requiring additional evidential links.
Open evidenceFinite questions capable of confirming, limiting or refuting an allegation.

Starting point

2011 must be read in two stages.

This reconstruction avoids treating “2011” as one rhetorical event. 2 February records the governance/litigation intervention; 22 June records the debt, vote and authority mechanism. Their possible causal relationship is an investigative question, not an automatic conclusion.

2 February 2011

Governance + litigation

The current control places Álvaro Campanario Hernández representing a substantial part of the later claimant perimeter, acting as secretary of the meeting, explaining pending litigation and proposing the Community's submission/settlement position. Officers were then elected and litigation powers authorised.

Proves: role continuity, knowledge opportunity and participation in the legal/Community architecture. Does not prove: debt design, knowing falsity or a criminal plan.

22 June 2011

Debt → vote → authority

The controlled minutes record LPB with a much larger represented coefficient, but a separate list of those treated as entitled to vote because Community obligations were considered current. That voting perimeter approved debt/recovery, lawyers and administration/maintenance measures involving Pamanil.

Proves: the documented mechanism existed. Open: validity of debt, regularity of exclusion, who calculated what, and whether anyone knew a material proposition was false.

Important correction. Campanario was secretary of the 2-Feb-2011 meeting; that must not be confused with the person elected as Community secretary for the subsequent period.

Thread thesis. The defensible criminal test is entrusted power/duty → verified knowledge → act or omission within practical capacity → patrimonial effect → possible benefit → evidence of intent or a defensible explanation. Civil or insolvency adversity alone cannot supply the missing criminal links.

Mens rea

Do not jump from notice to agreement.

1Mistake / competing interpretation
2Notice of dispute
3Knowledge opportunity
4Actual knowledge strongly supported
5Deliberate continuation despite knowledge
6Improper purpose, concealment or concert supported by evidence

The criminal inquiry becomes stronger only when it can move through this ladder with actor-specific documents, not through proximity, adverse outcome or later benefit.

Loss rule. A seemingly better exit opportunity does not by itself make the course ultimately followed disloyal administration. The criminal question is whether evidence shows that an actor knew of a materially superior estate outcome and deliberately frustrated it for an improper purpose.
