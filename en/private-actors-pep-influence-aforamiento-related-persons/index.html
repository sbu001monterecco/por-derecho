DUE DILIGENCE · LEGAL CATEGORIES · EVIDENCE BEFORE LABELS

Are any material private actors PEPs, specially related persons, aforados—or actually shown to influence public officeholders?

This is a verification question, not an accusation. Wealth, business prominence, public funding, access to institutions, a favourable decision or a corporate relationship does not by itself answer any of those legally different questions.

Current controlled position: the bounded public-source scan performed on 20 August 2026 did not locate a qualifying public office for José Daniel Acosta Matos, Laura Patricia Acosta Matos or Francisco Mario Matos Matas. That is not a certification that they have never been PEPs/PRPs. The historical-office, family/close-associate, influence, aforamiento and insolvency-related-person tests remain separate and source-led.

Six questions that must not be collapsed

1 · PEP / PRP

Spanish AML law uses persona con responsabilidad pública. The question is whether the person held a qualifying important public function, with exact office and dates.

2 · Family / close associate

A family member or allegado of a verified PEP can trigger enhanced AML diligence. That is not the same thing as being the officeholder.

3 · Access / influence

Contact, access, a claim of access, an intervention and actual causal influence are different evidential levels. None may be silently upgraded.

4 · Aforamiento

Special criminal jurisdiction arises from a qualifying office/status and the law in force—not from money, reputation or connections.

5 · PER in insolvency

Persona especialmente relacionada is debtor-specific and date-specific. For LPB’s 2012 insolvency, historical insolvency law must be tested before current TRLC shorthand.

6 · Corporate related person

Article 231 LSC persona vinculada is a corporate-conflict category. It is not a synonym for PEP, PER or political influence.

Criminal-law limit: Spanish Criminal Code arts. 428–430 concern conduct materially beyond acquaintance or access. In the private-person route, the law addresses actual influence over an official/authority through a personal relationship in order to obtain a resolution capable of generating economic benefit. This page does not say that threshold has been met by any named private actor.

PER and “related person” need their own clocks

For Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. / Concurso 36/2012, the starting question is the historical Article 93 of Ley 22/2003 applicable to the relevant 2012 insolvency facts and any transitional rules—not a retrospective application of the current TRLC label. The historical corporate-debtor test included specified qualifying shareholders, de iure/de facto administrators, liquidators, general attorneys, group relationships and a defined presumption for certain credit assignees/adjudicatees within the statutory pre-declaration period.

PER is not generic. A later creditor, unit owner, Community participant, promoter or investor-platform director does not become a “persona especialmente relacionada” with LPB merely because that later role is commercially close to the asset. The exact debtor, relationship, credit, dates and statutory route must be identified.

Separately, Article 231 LSC defines persons linked to company administrators for corporate-conflict purposes. Orion’s own disclosure materials are useful here because they expressly identify related-person relationships around JDAM and FMMM. Those disclosures are evidence of a corporate related-person perimeter—not evidence of PEP status, aforamiento, political protection, PER status or wrongdoing.

Aforamiento is not influence

The audit asks whether any relevant person actually held an office/status that changed criminal jurisdiction at the relevant date. Current LOPJ Article 57 identifies a defined set of national and judicial officeholders. Autonomous rules must be checked in their historical version. The Canary Statute was substantially reformed in 2018, so an old special-forum proposition cannot simply be carried into a current analysis.

No shortcut: a private businessperson is not aforado because they know politicians, meet officials, participate in publicly financed projects or claim access to a judge.

What would change the current status?

PEP / PRP

An official appointment/election/cese record identifying a qualifying function and dates—or reliable evidence of a statutory family/known-close-associate relationship to an identified PEP.

Influence

Authenticated communications or official records showing an intervention directed to a decision-maker, its content, timing, decision and benefit nexus; equally, records excluding or innocently explaining the claimed channel.

PER / aforamiento

For PER: LPB ownership/administration/credit chronology and the historical statutory route. For aforamiento: the actual office/status, dates and jurisdictional rule in force.
