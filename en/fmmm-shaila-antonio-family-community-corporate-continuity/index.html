WHY A DEDICATED PAGE

The significance is continuity, not kinship alone

1 · Community chronology

FMMM appears in the controlled Community/Pamanil record by 2011; Shaila and Antonio appear in the same documentary environment by April 2014. Their dates and capacities are kept person-specific.

2 · Shared documentary environment

The 8 April 2014 communications record places FMMM and Shaila together in the Pamanil / Community communications perimeter. Antonio appears in the Community/Pamanil representation lane two days later. That proximity is a research fact, not a finding of common intent.

3 · Later corporate structure

The repository's audit of the 7 November 2024 Orion Documento de Emisión records an expressly disclosed FMMM–Shaila–Antonio family/corporate architecture around Pamalexsha and related companies. The correct question is what each later role proves, not whether later ownership can be projected backwards.

4 · A contemporaneous investigative grouping

Private 2018 working material already examined FMMM, Antonio and Shaila together. Such advocacy material proves that the questions were being raised at the time; it does not establish that the allegations were true.

Controlled chronology

2011
FMMM / Community-Pamanil lane. The controlled register records Francisco Mario Matos Matas in administrator functions by 22 June 2011. The source-reported title does not itself establish lawful appointment, term, mandate or later authority.
2014
Documentary intersection. A communication dated 8 April records FMMM and Shaila within the Pamanil / Community communications perimeter; Antonio is documented in the Community / Pamanil representation lane by 10 April.
2016
Pamalexsha appears. The Community file contains a Pamalexsha administration-services budget. This supports a later service-provider/documentary transition; it does not prove universal legal succession from Pamanil.
2018
The hinge: authority · voting · title · security · access. The public investigation must identify, act by act, who held what title or power, who communicated or instructed, what property or Community right was involved, and what primary document supports the proposition. This is the point at which a family diagram must never substitute for attribution.
2024+
Later corporate convergence. The Orion disclosure audit records the family relationship and later Pamalexsha / Noalpa / AGM / Orion interests. Those later facts are relevant to chronology, disclosure, custody and conflict questions; they do not retrospectively prove a 2011–2018 common plan.
