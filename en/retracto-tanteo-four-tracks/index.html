DO NOT CONFUSE · CREDIT ≠ UNITS ≠ CO-OWNERS ≠ EXPLOITATION

There is no single “retracto” at Sun Park.

The proper analysis separates four legal tracks. One is documented as judicial proceedings concerning the PH122→CAM credit assignment. A second is documented as a 2018 legal workstream concerning unit transfers. The other two require transaction-specific proof of genuine pro-indiviso ownership or an independent contractual, tourism, exploitation or insolvency-estate right.

Control rule: no right is presumed merely because a unit later entered the CAM perimeter. Identify the finca, seller, buyer, ownership structure, deed, registration/knowledge, potential right-holder, deadline and action taken.

Day Zero Before Day Nine

A demand does not by itself convert an undetermined controversy into a won forfeiture defence. On the Article 1535 track, Por Derecho requires identification and proof of the credit, transfer, assignee, debtor and the precise demand for payment relied upon as the trigger. LO 1/2025 Article 7 now adds a potential caducity-suspension mechanism through a qualifying MASC where the right remains alive; its specific application to the nine-day period is marked COUNSEL VALIDATION REQUIRED. Criminal overlap is analysed under Article 40 LEC, not as an automatic freeze.

Credit · Article 1535 · PP 1041/2017

Object: the PH122→CAM assignment of mortgage credit. LPB sought the deed, price, payment and costs to assess possible Article 1535 relief. This track did not concern sales of individual apartments or locals.

RTB

Unit-rights legal workstream · May–June 2018

It is documented that counsel analysed a possible “tanteo/retracto legal de LUCHY” concerning units acquired from minority owners by a third party, contemporaneously written as “CAM?”. The workstream is direct evidence; a valid right is not presumed.

RTC

Co-owner retracto · Civil Code Articles 1522–1524

Potentially relevant where an outsider purchases an undivided share in the same common property. Article 1524 provides a nine-day period from Registry registration or, failing that, actual knowledge of the sale.

RTD

Contract · tourism · single-operation · insolvency

The registered statutes, horizontal title, 2008 arrangements, CEXP, single-operation rules and insolvency instruments may create rights or obligations independent of statutory retracto.

Legal correction concerning Canary Law 5/1999

Counsel investigated, among other possible bases, paragraph 4 of the sole transitional provision of Canary Law 5/1999, which had created a preferential-acquisition mechanism linked to the single-operation principle.

However, Law 2/2000 altered that regime, and Constitutional Court judgment 28/2012 records that the paragraph had been repealed and declared it unconstitutional and void. It therefore cannot be published as an operative statutory basis in 2018.

The correct question is whether each transaction engaged another valid basis: Articles 1522–1524, contract, CEXP/single-operation obligations or a right belonging to LPB/the insolvency estate.

Horizontal-property boundary

Civil Code Article 396 is a critical control: sale of an independent flat or local does not give owners of the other flats/locals a statutory tanteo or retracto merely by virtue of that horizontal-property ownership. Owning different Sun Park apartments is therefore not enough for Article 1522.

The decisive question: was an independent private unit sold, or was an undivided share of the same common property sold? Only the latter structure opens the co-owner-retracto analysis.

Aweswell / LPB / Matkator / CEXP: keep standing separate

Economic harm can be analysed at platform level, but legal standing cannot. Each transfer must identify the actual right-holder: LPB, Matkator, Aweswell, CEXP or another person. Matkator does not acquire a retracto over every Sun Park sale merely because it owns other units; Aweswell does not automatically inherit LPB's rights merely as shareholder.

The registered statutes do reinforce a separate proposition: Sun Park was configured for tourist exploitation as a single unit, with continuity into successive titles and a distinct exploitation-community structure. That architecture must be analysed separately as a potential source of exploitation obligations or rights.

Insolvency administrator: causation must be proved afresh

For PP 1041, C5 documentary evidence establishes that the insolvency administration caused the end of Cristo's specific formal LPB mandate. That does not prove the administrator cancelled possible unit-level rights.

For each finca reconstruct the right-holder, administrator knowledge, control of the right, legal advice received, deadline, decision/omission, estate benefit, loss and beneficiary.

Loyalty test: if a valid right existed and was within administrator control, what did the administrator do to preserve it—or what contemporaneous estate-interest analysis explains why it was not worth preserving?

PP 1041 procedural correction

The 15 February 2018 Commercial Court order provided a 20-day appeal under Article 97 bis.2 of the then-applicable Insolvency Law. Any prior repository wording describing that order as carrying a five-day reposición is superseded.

Keep separate: the 5 Mar 2018 PP1041 Decree carried a five-day review remedy; the 13 May 2024 ordering act carried a five-day reposición.

Unitary question

Between late 2017 and mid-2018, while individual Sun Park property interests were moving toward CAM, what valid protective, acquisition, co-owner, exploitation or estate rights actually existed for each transaction; who held them, who controlled them, when did the deadline run, and what happened to each right?

That question may confirm a legitimate sequence of independent transactions, or reveal a repeated direction of lost optionality and convergent benefit. The conclusion must emerge from the title-and-rights chain, not replace it.
