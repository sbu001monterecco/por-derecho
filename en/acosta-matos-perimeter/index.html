JSP / Sun Group / Montelanza: the origin makes the 8503–8507 block exceptional

JSP is not treated here as a peripheral third party. Public sources place José Sánchez Rodríguez —who founded JSP with his father José Sánchez Peñate— at the origin of the Sun Group tourism development in Playa Blanca. A former manager of the five complexes attributed to Sánchez the drive behind the design and construction of Sun Park, Sun Royal, Sun Beach, Sun Island and Sun Tropical, grouped under Sun Group.

The documentary continuity matters. In 2008 the Canary Islands official bulletin identified Montelanza, S.L. as holder of the Hotel Apartamento Sun Park; a contemporaneous sector report recorded Montelanza’s June-2008 transfer of Sun Park ownership and operation while retaining Sun Royal and Sun Tropical-Island, the latter through its subsidiary Inversiones Turísticas Playa Blanca. In 2017, JSP shareholders holding more than 5% formally demanded explanations about the sale of Sun Park, the majority interest in Explobeach and the majority interest in Inversiones Turísticas Playa Blanca.

“Near subsidiary / cuasi-filial” is Project Sun Rock analytical shorthand, not a corporate-law conclusion. The connection is not based on a share table alone: the 2015 BORME identifies José Sánchez Rodríguez as a director of Montelanza, alongside Anastasio Molina López and José Vicente Llinares Dorta. In addition, an internal reconstruction based on mercantile data places JSP at 26.82% of Montelanza —the largest individual holding identified in that working sheet— alongside other companies and persons in the historical perimeter. The combination of Sun Group development, Montelanza title/operation, shared governance and JSP shareholding warrants reconstruction of capital, votes, directors, agreements and coordinated action; it does not by itself establish a formal subsidiary or direct ownership above 50%.
