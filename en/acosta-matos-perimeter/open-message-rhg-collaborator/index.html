5 · Partly anonymised open message

To the professional or collaborator who recognises their involvement

This message does not presume that you acted improperly. Your identity is deliberately omitted while there is insufficient primary basis to individualise your role publicly. The immediate question is documentary: what function did you perform, for which entity, on what dates, under what mandate and with what information?

If you participated — directly or indirectly — in a professional, contractual, financial, technical, brand, franchise, operating, refurbishment, diligence, introduction, approval or communication relationship involving any of the following perimeters:

  • Radisson Blu Resort Lanzarote;
  • Canarian Hospitality or a connected operating entity;
  • Grupo or Construcciones Acosta Matos;
  • Las Coronas de Teguise;
  • RIC Private Equity;
  • Hotel New Trend / Sun Park, currently marketed as MYND Yaiza;
  • Insolvency 36/2012, LPB, Matkator or the Community/CEXP;

you are asked to preserve immediately, within the applicable legal and professional limits, the records capable of resolving the following questions.

Requested preservation

  • engagement letters, proposals, matter codes, contracts, amendments and billing;
  • emails, messaging, calendars, meeting notes, calls, shared documents and metadata;
  • conflict, independence, KYC/UBO, acceptance, AML, risk and information-barrier records;
  • documents concerning ownership, title, availability, Community authority, works, licences, operator, finance, source of funds and beneficial owners;
  • legal, technical, tax, financial, brand, franchise or due-diligence reports received or produced;
  • access records for matters, data rooms, DMS, email or platforms and any retention/deletion policy applied.

Finite questions

  1. Entity and function: for which legal person did you act and what was your role, mandate or professional relationship?
  2. Dates and scope: when did your involvement begin and end, and which asset, hotel, project, financing or counterparty did it cover?
  3. Instruction and remuneration: who requested, approved, instructed and paid for your work?
  4. Information received: what documents did you receive concerning title, fragmented ownership, Insolvency 36/2012, LPB, Matkator, third parties, Community/CEXP, works, operation or finance?
  5. Independent verification: what checks did you perform and which exceptions, doubts, reservations or conditions did you document?
  6. Overlap: was there any earlier or later engagement within an adverse or overlapping perimeter, and what conflict/confidentiality control applied?
  7. RHG: was any representation or report dependent on those facts transmitted to RHG, a franchising entity, the owner, the operator or their advisers?
  8. Correction: if any premise was incomplete or inaccurate, when was it identified, who was notified and what action followed?

A documentary response or precise correction within ten working days will permit it to be incorporated before any later institutional expansion.

A short response stating that this message does not concern you and, if known, identifying the correct entity or function is also useful. Every substantiated correction or materially different explanation will be incorporated with equivalent prominence.

Silence will not be represented as an admission. The record will show only that an opportunity for preservation, clarification and response was offered.
