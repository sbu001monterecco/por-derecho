Sun Park: 2018 ob rem deed, court decisions and registry position to 2022

Judicial refusal to validate the 2018 deed and divergent historical entries for individual properties require joint examination with the later decisions and titles.

Documented facts, court rulings, allegations and open questions are separate. Chronology cannot establish concerted action, crime or damages. Corrections and contrary sources are welcome.

Outstanding evidence and questions

  1. Obtain full literal certificates for all properties; a historical extract does not establish present title.
  2. Reconstruct entry 704 / Diary 54, references, qualification, notices, corrections, expiry and re-presentation.
  3. Test the release conditions of the ob rem link and its stated 17 July 2024 limit: the date alone proves neither automatic cancellation nor current status.
  4. Reconcile the 2018 €400,000 with payment records, estate ledgers, possible refunds, set-offs and accounts.
  5. Compare the 2021 decisions, 2022 deed, judicial certificates, cancellation instruments and actual registered effect.
  6. Separate debt, amount discharged, charges, price, alleged surplus and damage estimates; do not assume an amount is legally established.

Later decisions and alternative legal explanation

The 18 May 2021 order definitively approved CAM's proposal, and the 15 October 2021 order dismissed challenges to it. These are material contrary decisions and must not be omitted. The 2019 refusal alone proves neither registry nullity, restitution, intent nor criminal responsibility; later approval does not automatically settle every earlier question either.

Source hierarchy and protection

Full originals have been reviewed of the 24 October 2019 (six pages), 12 May 2020 (two pages) and 18 May 2021 (five pages) orders, historical simple registry extracts, and a 282-page judicial compilation containing deed 2150 from PDF page 27. The compilation is not a 282-page deed. Simple extracts are not literal registry certifications. Neither original private files nor professional correspondence are reproduced here. The non-validation order of 24 October 2019 is separate from a standing decision on the same date.
