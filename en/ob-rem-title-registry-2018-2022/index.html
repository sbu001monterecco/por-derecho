Legal distinctions and contrary evidence

The 2019 refusal to validate does not establish that the Registry was actually corrected. Nor does the 2021 judicial approval automatically establish a cure of every earlier consequence. Informational extract, title, possession, credit and accounting are distinct evidence categories.

The historical ob-rem link also had release conditions tied to mortgage cancellation or subrogation with mortgage-holder consent, and a temporal reference to 17 July 2024. The entire term and its property-specific operation must be examined.

Questions requiring documentary answers

  1. Which registered entry and title support each transfer or cancellation?
  2. What is the complete presentation, qualification, lapse and possible re-presentation history of entry 704/Diario 54?
  3. What order, deed or warrant, if any, corrected the consequences of the 2019 refusal?
  4. How were the €400,000, any refund and the later transfer accounted for?
  5. Which finality, standing, limitation and third-party protection issues should counsel assess?
