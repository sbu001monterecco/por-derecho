This was not a passive servicing inbox.

The primary correspondence proves that LPB's restructuring proposals entered a functioning creditor-side decision channel: they were analysed against the debt holder's criteria, rejected by “the creditor,” and routed through Cerberus and Bankia interlocutors.

STRONGEST SUPPORTED CONCLUSION

The issue is no longer whether the workout reached the creditor perimeter. It did. The unresolved issue is who decided, under what mandate and committee authority, on what information, for what reason, and with what foreseeable effect on LPB's ability to refinance, exit insolvency or preserve estate value.

Naming control. The source-supported holder is Promontoria Holding 122 B.V. / PH122. “PH11” is not used because the controlled record does not presently establish it. “Bankia external” is a forensic working label for the upstream Bankia/originator lane; it is not a legal entity or statutory category.

The correspondence that fixes knowledge and decision routing

8 APR 2015

The proposal entered a weekly decision forum

Haya stated that the proposal had been analysed during a weekly conference. A one-year standstill did not fit the debt holder's criteria; the remaining options required more detail on amounts, timetables and proposed investors.

What this proves: actual evaluation against creditor criteria. What it leaves open: participants, principal, committee authority, documents considered and reasons.

18 MAY 2016

Haya communicated a creditor rejection

Cuatrecasas had presented a proposal as destined for Haya or Cerberus. Haya first requested a signed borrower version and supplied its delivery address; the next day it stated that the terms had been rejected by “the creditor” and that execution was no longer needed.

What this proves: a rejection decision existed and travelled through Haya. What it leaves open: the identity and authority of the decision-maker, the reasoning and whether alternatives were compared.

11–19 MAY 2017

One operational interface, two distinct legal lanes

LPB sent Haya the Bankia current-account/SWAP items, PH122's two mortgages, prior Bankia and PH122/Cerberus offers, the convenio and viability plan, and the stated UK proposal of €5 million immediate payment plus terms for the balance. Haya identified the Cerberus interlocutors and separately undertook to provide the Bankia contact.

What this proves: direct knowledge of the credit and workout architecture and operational routing of both lanes. What it does not prove: common legal personality or automatic cross-liability.

“Bankia external”: the upstream lane that must not disappear

Origination and products

Original mortgage/refinancing terms, SWAP or hedging product, pledged funds, disclosures and linked obligations.

Account and enforcement

Payments, settlements, charges, account application, default, acceleration, enforcement and instructions.

Transfer and retention

What moved to SAREB and PH122, what remained personal to the originator/successor, and how the Valencia proceeding affects the lane.

A later assignee does not automatically inherit every personal Bankia liability. But the assigned credit remains dependent on its true originating amount, payments, defences and security.

Attribution without hiding behind missing contracts

What is already attributable

  • Haya's own emails and acts belong to Haya.
  • PH122's holder status and economic position belong to PH122.
  • Bankia's upstream conduct remains in the Bankia lane.
  • CAM's post-acquisition conduct belongs to CAM.

What requires the missing mandate

  • Cerberus-level instruction or control.
  • PH122 committee membership and decision-maker.
  • Whether Haya had authority to decide or only communicate.
  • Ratification, benefit and responsibility for another actor's conduct.
The evidential demand: identify the principal, servicer, employer, committee, decision-maker, instruction, information pack, reasons, alternative analysis, ratification and benefit for every 2015–2017 decision.

Controlled conclusion

By April 2015 the LPB workout was being analysed in a creditor-side weekly forum. In May 2016 Haya communicated a creditor rejection. In May 2017 Haya identified the Cerberus interlocutors and separately undertook to provide the Bankia contact.

The burden has shifted. The relevant actors should now produce the mandate and decision record explaining who exercised power over the credit, why the proposed exits were rejected and whether the resulting loss of refinancing or estate value was foreseeable and attributable.

Status: primary correspondence supports knowledge and decision routing. It does not by itself prove unlawful purpose, group-wide liability or a criminal agreement. Corrective and exculpatory primary records are invited.
