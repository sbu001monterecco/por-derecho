PUBLIC-SOURCE REVIEW · 27 OCTOBER 2026

Bankia / CaixaBank and Cerberus: match each issue to the competent review.

The historic commercial-credit chain requires three separate assessments: the original lender's conduct and retained obligations; the conduct and authority of each later holder, servicer or adviser; and the jurisdiction of the body asked to review that conduct. A sale of a credit, corporate succession and a servicing mandate are different events. Each requires its own documents and applicable law.

Exact company, dated capacity. Companies House identifies Cerberus European Servicing Ltd, company 08388731. That registration identifies a company; it does not establish its authority over PH122, its role in a particular LPB decision, or an FCA permission. The wider Cerberus name must not substitute for the legal entity whose acts are being examined.

Banco de España · customer conduct

Its complaints procedure is open to legal persons using supervised banking services, subject to eligibility and prior complaint requirements. A commercial company still needs an actor-specific review of standing, dates and the relevant banking conduct.

Banco de España · supervisory information

The external reporting channel concerns possible breaches by supervised entities or unauthorised reserved activities. It requires a supported regulatory issue. The Bank expressly distinguishes this channel from individual product or service complaints.

ECB · prudential supervision

The ECB breach-reporting route covers EU prudential banking law within its competence, including relevant governance and internal controls. Its platform excludes consumer-protection and anti-money-laundering implementation matters. A private loan dispute does not establish a prudential breach.

CNMV · investment services

The investor-complaints route is relevant only where the product, service and entity fall within its remit. Any swap-related issue needs that classification first. CNMV reports are nonbinding and do not award damages; an identified investment-service issue does not transfer the entire mortgage dispute to CNMV.

United Kingdom · entity and activity

The FCA's published perimeter places much commercial lending, including lending to limited companies, outside its remit. A UK company address or a group's regulated affiliate does not establish FCA jurisdiction over this Spanish commercial credit. The exact entity, activity, period and permission must be tested.

United States · securities-law nexus

The SEC's tips framework concerns possible violations of US federal securities laws. A route involving an investment adviser needs facts connecting the identified entity and conduct to that framework. The Cerberus brand or a Spanish debt assignment alone does not establish that connection.

Company compliance and legal responsibility

Cerberus describes its European servicing platform publicly. That description provides organisational context; a case-specific review still needs the actual principal, mandate, decision authority and record custodian. Internal compliance review, regulatory jurisdiction and adjudicated liability each require their own basis. No group-wide responsibility follows from a shared name.

Historical and judicial limits. Under Order ECC/2502/2012, Article 10, same-matter litigation and issues reserved to judicial proof or damages assessment can exclude the financial complaints procedure. Article 12 makes its final reports nonbinding. Historic events require their own limitation and admissibility analysis. The map above is not a finding that any actor breached a duty or that a particular complaint is admissible.

The correspondence that fixes knowledge and decision routing

8 APR 2015

The proposal entered a weekly decision forum

Haya stated that the proposal had been analysed during a weekly conference. A one-year standstill did not fit the debt holder's criteria; the remaining options required more detail on amounts, timetables and proposed investors.

What this proves: actual evaluation against creditor criteria. What it leaves open: participants, principal, committee authority, documents considered and reasons.

18 MAY 2016

Haya communicated a creditor rejection

Cuatrecasas had presented a proposal as destined for Haya or Cerberus. Haya first requested a signed borrower version and supplied its delivery address; the next day it stated that the terms had been rejected by “the creditor” and that execution was no longer needed.

What this proves: a rejection decision existed and travelled through Haya. What it leaves open: the identity and authority of the decision-maker, the reasoning and whether alternatives were compared.

11–19 MAY 2017

One operational interface, two distinct legal lanes

LPB sent Haya the Bankia current-account/SWAP items, PH122's two mortgages, prior Bankia and PH122/Cerberus offers, the convenio and viability plan, and the stated UK proposal of €5 million immediate payment plus terms for the balance. Haya identified the Cerberus interlocutors and separately undertook to provide the Bankia contact.

What this proves: direct knowledge of the credit and workout architecture and operational routing of both lanes. What it does not prove: common legal personality or automatic cross-liability.

Controlled conclusion

By April 2015 Haya said the LPB workout was being analysed in a weekly forum. In May 2016 Haya communicated rejection by “the creditor.” In May 2017 it identified interlocutors described as Cerberus and separately undertook to provide the Bankia contact.

Por Derecho retains the burden of substantiating its assertions and, in criminal proceedings, the prosecution must prove guilt. The mandate and decision-file request is a finite production request to identify who exercised power over the credit, why proposed exits were rejected and whether legally attributable harm resulted. Non-production does not reverse the burden, is not an admission and does not require anyone to prove innocence.

Status: primary correspondence supports receipt, communicated evaluation and routing. It does not itself prove Haya decision capacity, Cerberus ownership/control, unlawful purpose, group-wide liability or a criminal agreement. Corrective and exculpatory primary records are invited.
