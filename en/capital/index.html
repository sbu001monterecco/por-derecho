05 · Programme architecture

Staged programme capacity, with the final ceiling subject to NewCo Board, counsel and PMR freeze.

The programme ceiling is a financing framework. It allows multiple Series, investor mandates and project stages without treating unused capacity as debt or cash.

First Capital

Anchor / execution lane

A smaller first-capital tranche can provide controlled liquidity, deposits, diligence and execution capacity. The historic £1m–£5m discussion is an execution lane, not the scale of the overall programme.

Scale Capital

Institutional issuance

Larger private-credit, private-bank, family-office or institutional Series can refinance earlier capital, fund acquisitions/development or establish recurring programme capacity.

Future Series

Multiple uses, one governance framework

Different Series may serve different assets, maturities, currencies or investor groups while maintaining one controlled issuer, register, disclosure and governance architecture.

07 · Control framework

Designed for institutional scrutiny.

The programme is intended to separate Sponsor risk, issuer risk and project risk instead of obscuring them. The same discipline applies to cash, security, disclosure and future hotel value.

Cash control

Committed capacity is not cash; undrawn capital is not debt. Funded cash can move through approved utilisation, controlled settlement, escrow, lockbox or direct-payee mechanics as documented.

Security discipline

Only legally available, documented and where required perfected collateral is counted. Future hotel value and unauthorised cross-collateralisation are excluded.

Governance & reporting

Board authority, conflicts, four-eyes controls, independent advice, register integrity, covenant/reporting calendars and status discipline are built into the programme design.

Legal system, jurisdiction & effective remedies: Montaña Roja is a Spanish investment and project, with UK Sponsor and financing layers. Spanish law provides constitutional and statutory protections for effective judicial protection, defence, independent adjudication and reasoned decisions, but those protections do not guarantee a favourable, rapid or collectible outcome. English-law financing terms cannot displace mandatory Spanish law or forum for Spanish property/security, corporate/insolvency or public-law matters. Service, interim relief, recognition and enforcement between England and Spain are analysed separately. Foreign-investment registration supports compliance and evidential continuity; it is not immunity, State backing, collateral or an assumed investor-State arbitration right. Historic Sun Park / MYND Yaiza disputes remain separate and are not presented as a prediction of Montaña Roja's judicial treatment.
