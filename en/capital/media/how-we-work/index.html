Experience → discipline → better projects

How we work.

Long-term credibility is not a slogan. It is the cumulative result of clear authority, disciplined capital, traceable decisions, measurable outcomes and a willingness to preserve contrary evidence and correct mistakes. Years of complex hospitality, financing, property and institutional experience have made these standards non-negotiable for the next phase of Sun Rock.

Our values should be demonstrated in the record: what we approve, what we refuse, what we measure, what we preserve, what we disclose and how we act when something goes wrong.

01 · Title & rights

Know exactly what the project controls.

Property, shares, licences, intellectual property, operator rights, access and third-party interests are mapped before they are relied upon. A hotel brand or operating reality never substitutes for the underlying legal perimeter.

02 · Authority

Know exactly who can decide and sign.

Board authority, powers, community or shareholder approvals, conflicts and delegations are documented before commitments are made. Representation is proved rather than assumed.

03 · Capital

Every euro has a source, use and owner.

Sponsor capital, ProjectCo debt, public support, investor money, operating cash and recovery claims remain separately identified. No double counting, hidden cross-collateralisation or unsupported value substitution.

04 · Public support

Audit-ready from day one.

Where lawful public support is used, eligibility, invoices, payments, assets, jobs, other aid, modifications, indicators and final verification are connected in one reproducible trail. Awards are not treated as proof of final compliance.

05 · Technical delivery

Approved design, actual works and changes must reconcile.

Technical documents, permits, instructions, variations, completion records and as-built reality are version-controlled. Material changes leave an authorised, dated explanation.

06 · Employment

Jobs are people, periods and careers—not a headline number.

Employment commitments are measured by employer, role, FTE, period, retention, training and progression. The objective is durable skilled work and a stronger hospitality labour market.

07 · Environment & infrastructure

Measure impacts before making claims.

Water, energy, waste, mobility, biodiversity and infrastructure capacity are baselined and monitored. Regeneration projects use science, lawful permissions and survival/outcome measures rather than decorative ESG claims.

08 · Decisions

Important decisions should be reconstructable later.

Material decisions identify the evidence considered, alternatives, responsible person, conflicts, advice, decision and follow-up. Silence, referral or receipt is never misdescribed as substantive approval.

09 · Evidence

Preserve what helps and what hurts.

Contrary evidence, adverse decisions and uncertainty stay in the record. The purpose of diligence is not to manufacture confidence; it is to make confidence deserved.

10 · Counterparties

Disagreement does not suspend professionalism.

Contracts, notices, cure processes, escalation routes and settlement channels are defined early. We preserve rights firmly while keeping commercial dialogue possible wherever lawful and useful.

11 · Communication

Say only what is true at the time.

“Pursuing” is not “acquired”; “reviewing” is not “approved”; “capacity” is not “cash”; a conversation is not a mandate. Public statements are versioned and corrected when better evidence arrives.

12 · Leadership

Stewardship means leaving the system stronger.

The standard is not whether a project can get through a process. It is whether investors, employees, guests, suppliers, institutions and the destination can understand what happened and reasonably trust the way it was done.

Why publish this? These standards are forward-looking Sun Rock commitments. They do not ask readers to accept any historical allegation against any third party. The lesson taken from complex past experience is simpler: future projects should be easier to audit, easier to finance, easier to govern and harder to misunderstand.

Build trust before asking for it.

For institutional, media or strategic conversations about the next phase of Sun Rock, start with the public layer and move into controlled diligence only where appropriate.
