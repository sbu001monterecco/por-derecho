Investing in the next chapter.

Sun Rock is preparing for a new phase of long-term hospitality investment: disciplined private capital, high-value hospitality and lasting local value in Lanzarote. This hub provides public-safe context for media, institutions, capital providers and other stakeholders without converting corporate communications into a securities solicitation or an announcement of milestones that have not yet occurred.

Current public positioning

Long-term capital and destination value can reinforce one another.

“A good hospitality investment should work for its capital providers and leave substantial value in the destination where it operates. Those objectives are not opposites. Done well, they reinforce each other.”

Sun Rock's public direction is future-facing: invest, build and operate for the long term; support higher-value tourism; create durable employment and skills; strengthen local procurement and the wider visitor economy; improve resource efficiency; and explore credible environmental and community regeneration alongside hospitality growth.

Quote-safe core facts

What can be said now.

Sponsor

AWESWELL LIMITED is the existing UK Sponsor/HoldCo. Sun Rock is the hospitality and investment platform identity. Individual hotel assets are intended to remain ring-fenced through appropriate ProjectCos.

Hospitality direction

Sun Rock is actively pursuing further long-term hospitality investment in Playa Blanca, Lanzarote. The strategy is quality-led and operating-led rather than growth for the sake of adding beds.

Capital discipline

Projects must stand on their own transaction, planning, technical, operating and financing merits. Public support, where lawful and appropriate, is supplementary rather than a substitute for financeable project economics.

Local value

Employment, training, local and Canary procurement, visitor expenditure outside the hotel, accessibility, resource efficiency and wider destination outcomes are intended to be measured as the programme advances.

Investment climate

The programme supports a simple principle: long-term capital goes where it can invest with confidence, and durable projects are strongest where capital providers, institutions and local communities can prosper together.

Current stage

Specific acquisitions, planning approvals, operator appointments, financing commitments, construction starts and securities issuance are not described as completed before they actually occur.

Disclosure discipline

Corporate news is not a securities offer.

Use “pursuing”, “preparing”, “working toward” or “intends” where the relevant milestone remains conditional. Do not convert strategy into a completed transaction.
Do not describe a lender, investor, arranger, exchange, operator or public authority as committed or supportive without a separately verified basis. Conversations, introductions and review do not equal mandate or approval.
Do not present programme capacity, management scenarios or future hotel values as current cash, NAV, security, committed financing or guaranteed forecasts.
Keep the new-project capital campaign separate from Sun Park / MYND Yaiza recovery, enforcement and litigation funding. Historic matters may provide background but do not finance the campaign merely because they exist.

Financial-promotion boundary. This page is informational corporate/media material. It is not an offer or solicitation of securities, investment advice, a subscription facility, a credit commitment, a representation of LSEG approval, or a guarantee of return. Investment-specific discussions remain subject to recipient eligibility, lawful communication, definitive documentation, due diligence and closing.
