Sun Park / JSP / Monte Lanza

Were Multimatrix’s purchase options frustrated?

Gil Marer’s stated belief and allegation: JSP and Monte Lanza frustrated Multimatrix’s ability to exercise the options through which it intended to acquire 100% of the hotel’s 262 fincas, alongside acts and omissions of separately identified non-JSP dissident interests associated with the Monte Lanza / Molina perimeter.

The question is not simply whether an option expired. It is whether identifiable conduct prevented exercise or completion, and whether subsequent positions relied on that obstruction.

This is not an adjudicated finding. JSP, Monte Lanza, individual shareholders, non-JSP dissidents, later purchasers and the two Communities are not one legal person. Each alleged act, duty, knowledge, causal contribution and benefit requires its own proof.

Source review and clarification: 5 September 2026. Criminal/prosecutorial-first investigation, with separate civil, corporate, property and procedural questions.

Before the sale, sold, retained: three different states

The 26 May 2008 operating contract states that Monte Lanza owned 40 commercial premises and two pools and operated 220 apartments with their owners’ consent. It does not state that Monte Lanza owned all 262 fincas. E09

The accounts describe a 17 June 2008 corporate sale for €1,368,000, parallel apartment sales by certain shareholders, and a separate arrangement over eleven unsold premises with a stated endpoint of 31 December 2010. The corporate amount is not the established price of the entire hotel. E01

The intended whole-hotel acquisition must be separated from the property actually conveyed, the rights retained by each owner, and the original options binding each seller. JSP’s disclosed 26.82% equity interest in Monte Lanza at the 2008 and 2012 year ends is not ownership of 26.82% of the hotel or evidence of majority ownership. E01 · E02

The documentary comparisons that matter

The purchaser-side expectation

Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. (LPB)’s statements to 30 September 2009 report 29 acquired commercial properties plus two pools, use until end 2010 of eleven premises still belonging to Monte Lanza, and a purchase described for December 2010. These are reviewed statements, not the original option or a full audit. They support investigating the promised completion, not assuming its legal terms. E07, p. 13

Deliveries to shareholders during 2010

Monte Lanza’s later memorandum reports that certain premises were delivered to certain shareholders in repayment of earlier advances. Which properties, which recipients, when, and subject to which existing rights? The comparison with the eleven-unit arrangement is essential. The memorandum does not identify JSP as a recipient or prove a conflicting disposal. E02, p. 23

The accounting explanation is not the legal answer

The option-related reclassification narrative must be matched to the €24,000 reserve/tax movement, receipt, contract, notices and any release or refund obligation. Neither a ledger entry nor a stated expiry proves that exercise was unobstructed or forfeiture lawful. E02, pp. 23,26–28

Correction: the 2016 conditional deed is recovered

The recovered copy of 18 November 2016, protocol 2026 concerns 8499/local 3 and 8500/local 4—not 8498. It provides for 8499 to the Community and 8500 to the other creditors. E08, pp. 1–13

Two express suspensive conditions remain to be proved: acceptance by all named creditors and approval by Monte Lanza’s general meeting. Recovery of the copy does not establish fulfilment, registration or a later Community-to-CAM sale.

The 18 April 2017 extracts still name Monte Lanza. Finca 8498 has a different trail: presentation 348/Diario 48 dated 2 June 2016. That is a presentation date, not the established deed-execution date or a completed transfer. E03–E05

Eight points that must remain open and visible

P1 · What exact options existed?

Recover the original instruments, all amendments, exercise requirements and seller-by-seller schedules. Trace Victory Properties, Multimatrix and LPB without assuming they were the same beneficiary. E10

P2 · Who owned what, and when?

Map pre-sale property, completed conveyances, retained units and subsequent acquisitions. Do not classify later purchasers or every non-LPB owner as an original defaulting seller. Ownership comparison

P3 · What actually prevented completion?

Identify each actor’s duty, notice, refusal, withheld document or other act, its date and the closing step it blocked. Test buyer readiness and funding, the causal counterfactual and lawful explanations, not chronology alone.

P4 · Which 2010 transfers overlapped?

Match the units, recipients, debts, valuations, approvals and deeds to the eleven-unit schedule, including any consent, release or surviving rights. 2010 account

P5 · How did use and governance affect value?

Test access, facilities, income, debt, votes and representation against particular records. Note 7’s facility and lease descriptions do not prove actual obstruction; its overlapping unit 11 references also need reconciliation. E07, p. 15

P6 · What did each non-JSP dissident do?

Identify the owner/entity, representative, contract or resolution, and act or omission separately. Voluntary membership of the Comunidad de Explotación (CEXP), dissent or non-adherence alone is not breach. Contrary record

P7 · Did the conditional adjudication complete?

Obtain all acceptances, corporate approval, completion and title records, then any separate onward disposition and consideration. Do not treat the recovered deed as a complete Community/CAM chain. Deed correction

P8 · Where did the value and rights go?

Reconcile option receipts, shareholder repayments, liquidation quotas, JSP’s explanations and later estate treatment. Verify successor standing, loss and action-specific limitation before claiming a live recovery remedy. O01

The strongest contrary evidence must travel with the allegation

The 2008 minutes describe CEXP participation as voluntary. The existing canonical reconstruction preserves the adverse AP 89/2014 title/possessory result, LPB’s adverse procedural history and the distinct Bankia/financing, operating and market explanations. None may be erased to make the alleged seller-side mechanism appear proved or the sole cause. E10 · Canonical historical control

An omission requires an identified duty and ability to act. A shared surname, representation at a meeting or company association does not transfer knowledge, intention or liability.

Legal issue: obstruction is not neutral expiry

Good faith, breach and prevention of a condition merit instrument-specific analysis under Civil Code articles 7,1101,1119,1256 and 1258. Article 1119 does not automatically revive or exercise an expired purchase option. Establish the legal nature of the right and the conduct first. Any criminal classification needs separate historical elements and actor-specific proof. Official Civil Code
