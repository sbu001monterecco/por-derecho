In 2017, JSP shareholders demanded more explanations about the Sun Park sale

The official notice identifies a concrete documentary trail: the shareholder request, earlier meetings, management’s explanations and the records showing what was sold, what remained outstanding and where the value went.

The new document: BORME-C-2017-7368

The agenda supplement records a request received on 3 August 2017 from shareholders representing more than 5% of the capital. The meeting was scheduled for 5 September, with a second call the following day. The notice is dated 7 August and was published on 10 August 2017, page 8556. S01

Item Five requests fuller explanations concerning the Sun Park Complex sale and the majority of Explobeach shares, expressly referring to explanations already given at previous general meetings.

The investigative consequence is specific: the notice itself identifies earlier explanations. Their minutes, annexes, questions and answers should be recovered. Their contents must not be assumed.

What it does not prove: this is neither a conveyance nor meeting minutes. It does not establish the Sun Park sale date or price, a transfer of all 262 properties, the purchaser of each right, or a crime.

Decisive correction: 26.82% is not a direct majority

The executed review of Monte Lanza’s 2008 and 2012 accounts and the certified June 2009 meeting extract records JSP at 26.82%; in 2009 this is 59 of 220 company shares. Those shares are not the apartments. This dossier relies on the derivative control of that executed review; the original sources retain their separate evidential status and custody. S06, P4

This corrects the earlier direct-majority hypothesis at those dates. Any indirect, contractual or de facto control requires different evidence. A majority among those present at one meeting also does not by itself establish permanent corporate control.

Sale, residual option and intermediate disposals

The May 2008 operating contract distinguishes 220 apartments belonging to their respective owners from 40 commercial premises and two pools owned by Monte Lanza. The accounts describe an option exercised in June 2008 and another over eleven unsold premises; later accounts describe sales in 2009 and transfers to shareholders in 2010 against debts or advances. S06, P1–P3

Gil Marer alleges that the intended whole-hotel acquisition was frustrated. Test that allegation against seller mandates, signed options, exercise attempts, obstacles, conveyances, releases and payments. Retained title and an accounting reference to expiry do not, alone, establish either a valid release or a crime.

Monte Lanza: corporate winding-up in 2015–2018

The 2015 notice records Monte Lanza’s voluntary dissolution and Atilio Rodríguez Beltrá^ as liquidator. It also records cessations of José Manuel Romero Domínguez, José Vicente Llinares Dorta, Anastasio Molina López and José Sánchez Rodríguez. Monte Lanza’s extinction was registered on 9 October 2018. S02 · S04

The extinction deed, final balance, operations report, shareholder list and liquidation allocations are key records for checking corporate ownership and the destination of value. This winding-up must not be confused with JSP’s insolvency beginning in 2021.

JSP and Celgán: 440/2021 is a separate legal and temporal track

The official notice identifies José Sánchez Peñate, S.A. and Celgán, S.A. in insolvency 440/2021, declared on 21 July 2021, historically before Commercial Court no. 2 in Las Palmas. It names judge Guillermo Fernández García, Praxis de Reestructuraciones y Concursal, S.L.P. as insolvency administrator, and Miota & Alonso Asociados, S.L.P. as delegated auxiliary. These are separate identities. S05

Rafael Franco Miranda, Fidel Blasco Miota, Román García-Varela Iglesias and the exact capacity of procurador Francisco Javier Pérez Almeida require the original edict and subsequent changes. The reviewed reproduction does not certify current offices. S07

The question is what direct property, shares, liquidation entitlements, unpaid price, receivables or causes of action belonged to JSP and how they were treated in the inventory and accounts. A subsidiary’s estate is not assumed to be its shareholder’s, and 2021 appointees are not presumed to have directed 2015–2018 acts. JSP’s final conclusion and extinction have not been verified.

Priority documents and traceability

First: the August 2017 request, September minutes and explanations from earlier meetings. Second: 2008 sale and option instruments, 2009–2010 disposals and Monte Lanza’s liquidation. Third: the 2016 deed’s acceptances and corporate approval, later titles and consideration. Fourth: the inventory, reports, liquidation, final accounts and current status of 440/2021.

KPMG Auditores, S.L. appears in JSP auditor-reappointment notices from 2018 and 2020. Establish the financial years, scope and records custody; appointment does not prove review of the disputed transactions. S04 · S08

A private request is not a compulsory disclosure order. Preservation, access, disclosure and evidential use are distinct steps. Any investigation of communications should identify existing data, custodians and specific periods through the appropriate lawful route, without presuming illicit conversations.
