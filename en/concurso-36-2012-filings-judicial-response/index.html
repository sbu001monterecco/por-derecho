7 June 2018 → estate → productive-unit continuity

Active estate

The test includes secured-credit/bid amounts, competitive offers, preservation, recoveries, AC accounting, disposition and the later €2,098 treasury endpoint—not merely who ultimately received the property.

Productive-unit continuity

Contemporaneous material describes an occupied hotel with activity, maintenance, partial renovation and a live funded operating/exit route. Any effect of June access/control on customers, security, investor diligence, refurbishment and continuity must be proved as a causal chain.

Physical condition

9 July 2018 photographs show visibly green/discoloured pool water, residue/staining, accumulated leaves/debris and neglected-looking common areas. They prove visible condition, not who caused it.

Damage/destruction thesis. The documentary record supports investigation of active-estate erosion, productive-unit continuity loss and external harm. Attribution of the whole loss to named persons remains an allegation unless proved by primary evidence and, where applicable, competent decision.

The alleged “overreach of the concurso”

Concurso 36/2012 was LPB's insolvency proceeding, not an insolvency proceeding over the whole Sun Park complex. Each effect therefore requires a title-and-authority test.

PerimeterInside LPB by default?Overreach question

Pink / operating contractsNot automaticallyContracts, utilities, operator appearance and tax effects are not absorbed merely by LPB's insolvency.
Other owners / CommunityNoSecurity/common-area administration does not itself create title over apartments or universal hotel control.
Rule. The overreach thesis does not assume illegality. It asks where a power valid within LPB's insolvency perimeter allegedly produced, or was used to produce, effects on non-LPB property, possession, operation, contracts or third-party rights, and what exact authority supported each effect.
