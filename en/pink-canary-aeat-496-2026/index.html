What this record does not establish

The reviewed order does not establish a tax offence, fraud, validity or invalidity of the underlying assessments, entitlement to repayment, final liability or the outcome of PO 496/2026. It also does not convert the separate Sun Park / RICPE / AEAT notice history into this litigation.

The correct distinction is between the AEAT notice and registration history, the Pink / AEAT contentious proceeding, and separate provenance questions about third-party statements. Evidence may overlap, but the lanes, objects and standards do not become identical.
