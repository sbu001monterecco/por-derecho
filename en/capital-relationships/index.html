Who may find the conversation relevant.

The objective is a small number of serious relationships, not broad distribution. Relevance depends on the actual person, organisation, role, jurisdiction, mandate and capacity—not a label alone.

01 · Principal capital

Owner-led and institutional decision-makers

Potentially relevant conversations can include single-family offices, hotel-owning families, hospitality principals and institutional or professional private-capital decision-makers able to assess a long-term Sponsor relationship or a specific hotel asset.

02 · Strategic capital

Asset and hospitality experience matters

The useful counterparty understands some combination of hotels, real estate, development, operating risk, governance, financing and patient capital. No one is treated as suitable merely because they are wealthy or experienced.

03 · Trusted introductions

The reader may be the bridge, not the investor

Lawyers, private bankers, corporate-finance professionals, hotel owners and trusted business principals may know an appropriate decision-maker. An introduction does not create authority, mandate, endorsement or entitlement to receive restricted materials.

Controlled private process

Seven gates before investment-specific material.

The public website stops at the doorway. A serious capital process then becomes progressively more specific only where the counterparty, jurisdiction and legal route justify it.

01Identity and role

Who is the person or organisation, who is the decision-maker, and what role are they actually performing?

02Jurisdiction

Where is the recipient, entity and relevant activity located, and which legal/regulatory perimeter applies?

03Principal or intermediary

Determine whether the person invests its own capital, represents another principal, advises, arranges or only introduces.

04Conflicts and purpose

Confirm the proposed conversation, relevant conflicts and whether the relationship is capital, strategic or both.

05Communication basis

Establish the appropriate lawful basis and recipient classification before investment-specific promotion or documents are supplied.

06Confidentiality

Use proportionate confidentiality and information controls before restricted financial, legal or transaction material is opened.

07Controlled diligence

Move only an aligned party into focused source review, term discussion, KYC/AML, approvals and definitive documentation.

Closing ruleNo shortcut from website to funding

No enquiry, introduction, NDA or management call is an investment decision, allocation or financing commitment.

What may be discussed later

Instrument follows relationship and diligence.

The current strategy is broader than any single historical “Loan Note” concept. Depending on the actual transaction and legal advice, a private discussion could examine HoldCo structured capital, preferred or participating capital, a private Loan Note or comparable instrument, and separately financed hotel ProjectCos. No such instrument is represented here as final, approved, available or offered.

Aweswell HoldCo / Sun Rock group-platform

A governed group-level relationship around Sponsor liquidity, corporate finance and platform development. Aweswell is the legal HoldCo/Sponsor; Sun Rock is the commercial group/platform identity. Rights and protections follow the actual contracting entity and documentation.

Ring-fenced ProjectCo

Asset-specific capital for a hotel that stands on its own title, transaction perimeter, complete cost, operating case, approvals, security and financing structure.

Strategic / co-investment relationship

A principal may contribute capital, hotel capability, operating experience, relationships or a combination, subject to a separately agreed role and transaction.

Material-status rule. Historical or superseded financing drafts are not current offers and must not be used as present terms. Current private materials, if any are shared, must carry their own date, status, recipient boundary and reliance limitations.

Non-negotiable boundaries

The gateway must never become a retail product page.

Trust comes from precise status, risk separation and verifiable protections—not from high-return language, borrowed regulatory prestige or an implied promise that future assets already secure present capital.

Public capital firewall

  • No public coupon, target return, minimum subscription, allocation or application form.
  • No “invest now”, “subscribe”, “reserve notes” or retail investment funnel.
  • No downloadable private investment memorandum or term sheet from the public website.
  • No bank, adviser, lawyer, trustee, exchange or regulator presented as endorsing an investment unless an exact documented capacity genuinely permits that statement.
  • Future platform scale is context, not present collateral.
  • Unresolved Sun Park recovery, damages or fruits are not presented as current cash, recognised NAV or ordinary Montaña Roja debt-service support.
  • Montaña Roja must stand on its own transaction, planning, technical, economic and financing merits.
  • Any specific financing remains subject to legal, regulatory, tax, diligence, KYC/AML, approval and definitive-document requirements.
