Commercial traceability · Sun Park → Lava Verde → Club Sei

Control and marketing before title—and the alleged causal path to adjudication.

Construcciones Acosta Matos publicly presented Hotel Lava Verde at C/ Janubio 3 as a 279-room project; Meeting Point Hotels/FTI announced Club Sei Lanzarote; consumer-facing offers and physical pilots followed before the January–February 2022 LPB adjudication and deed threshold. Gil Marer and Project Sun Rock allege that this was not mere chronology: the disputed June 2018 exclusion allegedly disabled operation, income and the funded-exit route, enabled project access and pre-title marketing, and created operational, informational, valuation and competitive asymmetries that materially caused, conditioned and enabled the later adjudication.

Criminal-causation allegation, not a judgment: the cumulative record supports investigation of that mechanism. It does not itself establish an advance agreement, any actor's criminal intent, the exact Meeting Point contracting entity, completed bookings or revenue, or a judicial finding of causation. Those propositions require actor-specific communications, contracts, access records, accounts and the complete adjudication file.

One location, several commercial identities

The allegation is causal, not merely chronological.

The public and documentary chain must be read alongside the material-control and funded-exit records: alleged exclusion and loss of operation/income → practical project access, pilots and pre-title marketing → asymmetric information, valuation and competition → later adjudication and downstream benefit. Each arrow is a testable proposition, not an automatic finding.

  1. 7 JUN 2018

    Disputed material control

    The takeover dossier documents a broken coded entrance, chains or padlocks, security and exclusion. Project Sun Rock maintains that from that date the Community/security/CAM-aligned perimeter had practical control of access to the complex. That position is not the same as title to every unit.

  2. 31 JAN 2019

    Hotel Lava Verde

    Acosta Matos places the project at C/ Janubio 3, describes rehabilitation of a four-star tourist complex, identifies Construcciones Acosta Matos S.A. as owner, states 279 rooms and names José D. Acosta Matos as architect. Its “Operated by” field is left blank.

  3. 1–2 FEB 2019

    Club Sei Lanzarote enters the FTI / Meeting Point announcement

    Trade press reported that FTI-owned Meeting Point Hotels would convert three existing hotels into the new Club Sei brand, including Club Sei Lanzarote. The reporting did not then disclose which Lanzarote property was involved.

  4. 2019–FEB 2020

    A pilot had already been executed

    A direct supplier response preserved in the evidence record identifies Sun Park with the establishment the supplier knew as Lava Verde and confirms manufacture and installation of a pilot room months earlier. The record does not by itself identify the contracting party, exact unit, payer or legal basis for access.

  5. 21 JAN 2020

    Public commercial offering

    An archived Loveholidays capture shows “Club Sei Lanzarote”, Playa Blanca, offered to consumers with a holiday price. The capture proves online commercial presence; it does not prove the hotel opened, a booking completed or revenue was received.

  6. MAR–JUN 2020

    Four pilots and project-wide planning

    A contemporaneous commercial meeting note reports a visit to the complex at CAM’s invitation, four supplier pilots and a request to price a selected model for large-scale replication. The same note opens a possible Meeting Point witness/source route. It remains a contemporaneous account of an oral conversation and requires direct testimony and records.

  7. 27 OCT 2020

    Club Sei rollout postponed

    Touristik Aktuell reported that FTI postponed implementation of the Club Sei concept because of the pandemic and set no new date. This is important contrary evidence: announcements and listings must not be converted into a claim that the Lanzarote hotel actually opened or traded under the brand.

  8. 24 FEB 2021

    The court record still described fragmented ownership

    In Concurso 36/2012, CAM stated that it owned 54 apartments and that the complex also contained LPB and third-party units. Aweswell alleged works and security-blocked access. That procedural picture requires an explanation of what “owner” meant in a 279-room hotel-project presentation.

  9. JAN–FEB 2022

    Later formal threshold and alleged causal result

    The adjudication and deed affecting the LPB perimeter came after the exclusion, funded-exit failure, Lava Verde, pilot and Club Sei milestones. Gil Marer alleges those earlier conditions materially produced and enabled the outcome. Their exact causal and finca scope remains open and must stay separate from Matkator and other third-party property.

  10. 2024–2025

    Preservation and 357/2024

    The controlled archive contains compliance and preservation communications directed into the FTI/Meeting Point perimeter before and during the restructuring period. Later proceeding 357/2024 matters because it may be a custody or disclosure location for contracts, inventory, reservations, claims and contingencies — not because the proceeding itself proves concealment or judicial impropriety.

The discrepancy requiring records

279 rooms in the project presentation. 54 apartments in CAM’s judicial position.

The figures are not necessarily like-for-like until each document’s definitions are known. That is exactly why a finca-to-unit-to-room map and the project’s title basis are required.

Public presentation · 2019

“Owner”: CAM · 279 rooms

The Lava Verde page presents an integrated hotel project at Janubio 3. It does not explain which properties make up the 279 rooms or what third-party rights are included.

Court position · 2021

CAM told Commercial Court 1 that it owned 54 apartments and acknowledged LPB and third-party units coexisted within the complex.

Finite question

What did “owner” mean?

Room inventory, Land Registry records, authorisations, owner agreements, operating contracts and the material supplied to designers, operators, distributors and investors are required.

No false statement is inferred from the numerical difference alone. The page identifies a material discrepancy between an integrated hotel-project presentation and a more limited judicial ownership position that must be reconciled through primary records.

Access converted into project execution

The pilot confirms physical work before formal acquisition of the LPB assets.

The question is not simply who designed a room. It is who could access, measure, commission, install, show and plan renovation of a hotel whose title remained fragmented and whose LPB assets remained in insolvency.
