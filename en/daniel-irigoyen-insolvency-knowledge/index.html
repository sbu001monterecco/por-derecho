Daniel Irigoyen: his role in the funded exit

Daniel Irigoyen was a career magistrate and commercial-law specialist historically recorded on voluntary leave, acting in this transaction as a lawyer and legal coordinator. His role connected investor, shareholder, LPB and hotel operator. It should not be reduced to “ONA’s lawyer”, or confused with the insolvency judge or a financier.

Why it matters: his documentation connects the commercial exit with knowledge and action in the insolvency process. It enables examination of what was presented, requested and answered, without automatically attributing Gil Marer’s criminal conclusions to him.
