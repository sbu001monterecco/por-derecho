Daniel's communications evidence what he reported professionally and may authenticate mandate, documents, chronology, interlocutors and purpose. They do not replace the certified docket, turn a conversation into a court ruling, or make Daniel a co-author of Gil Marer's later criminal theory.

Relevance to Gil Marer's attributed criminal hypothesis.

This source makes the counterfactual testable: the ONA/Clubotel alternative existed before 7 June and remained active afterward. Investigators can therefore ask, actor by actor, who knew of the alternative, what power or duty they had, which decision or conduct affected access, operations, debt, security, collateral or personation, and what contemporaneous effect followed. The existence of a better opportunity does not itself prove sabotage, conspiracy, judicial misconduct or disloyal administration.
