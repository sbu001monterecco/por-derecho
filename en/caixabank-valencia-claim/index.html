LIVE CLAIM · BANKING ORIGIN · RECOVERY

CaixaBank Valencia claim: a live recovery route beside—not inside—the later lender-in-possession chain.

The controlled record separates two connected questions. One is the pending claim against CAIXABANK, S.A. arising from the historic banking and financial-product relationship. The other is the later mortgage-credit, enforcement, possession and title chain through Bankia, SAREB, Promontoria Holding 122 B.V., Construcciones Acosta Matos and the subsequent hotel-title perimeter. They must be reconciled, not collapsed.

Administrador Concursal · witness origin

CaixaBank requested Borja's testimony; Aweswell adhered

The later court shorthand must not erase the sequence documented contemporaneously by counsel.

A 30 October 2024 email from counsel Javier Sixto records that the testimony of Francisco de Borja Rodríguez-Batllori Laffitte, the Administrador Concursal in Concurso 36/2012, was admitted at CaixaBank's request and that Aweswell adhered to that request. Later court citations therefore describe him procedurally as testigo propuesto por la parte actora y demandada.

The controlled reading is not “both parties independently chose the AC.” It is: CaixaBank requested/proposed Borja; Aweswell adhered; the later both-sides wording records the resulting procedural posture.

Gil Marer's account that the adhesion was defensive and driven by concern about procedural prejudice is preserved as client-reported context; an express contemporaneous counsel source stating that rationale has not yet been located.

No inference by label. The witness-origin sequence does not prove that Borja's eventual testimony will favour CaixaBank, nor bias, collusion, conflict, improper coordination or false evidence.

Calificación and recovery

Why the live banking claim matters without rewriting the past

The materially adverse first-instance Calificación record must be reconciled with a live third-party recovery route connected to the same economic history. If the banking claim succeeds in whole or in part, the result may affect net-loss and recovery analysis. It does not presently establish liability and it does not prove that patrimonial loss was caused exclusively by the bank.

Chronology matters. Sentencia 163/2023 is dated 28 September 2023. CaixaBank's expert report describes the present claim as filed on 13 October 2023. This later proceeding therefore cannot, by itself, prove that the Fiscal in 2019 or the first-instance judge before 28 September 2023 knew of this particular lawsuit. Earlier knowledge of the underlying banking dispute or recovery potential must be proved from earlier sources actor by actor.
