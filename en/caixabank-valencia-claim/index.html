The recognised insolvency credit and Mortgage Enforcement 90/2012 must be reconciled by legal function, property, payment and effect. €839,441 (capital demand), €857,373.81 (EH90 claim), €9,052,251.69 (recognised insolvency credit), €12,251,446.21 (PH122 certificates), €13,165,832.36 (Protocols 869 + 870) and €13,168,082.02 (stated threshold/consideration) are figures with different dates and functions. They must not be added or treated as one debt without a source–date–concept–payment–cancellation ledger.

Proof of falsity or inflation

The hypothesis requires the exact document, original and comparator; author and capacity; alteration, simulation or false attribution; knowledge and intent; use; induced reliance or disposition; and resulting benefit or harm. An authentic deed may record disputed party recitals without the instrument or the notarial act thereby being false. For a private actor, CP 392 incorporates only CP 390.1.1º–3º, not mere untruthful narration under 390.1.4º; CP 393 first requires a proved false document and then, knowing its falsity, presentation in court or, for other use, also a purpose to harm.

Complete boundary and innocent explanation. The published record does not establish NPL fraud, documentary or notarial falsity, false signatures, physical or digital alteration, notarial knowledge, collusion, a common plan, double recovery or criminal liability by any actor. “Split credit,” “multiple lives” and “NPL fraud” are not freestanding offences, and unjust enrichment is principally civil. Procedural fraud under CP 250.1.7 requires evidence manipulation or analogous procedural fraud, error by the judge or court and a resulting decision prejudicing economic interests; an accounting discrepancy is not enough. CP 259–261 changed on 1-Jul-2015 and retain actor, conduct and purpose limits: CP 261 does not cover every disputed credit submitted after the insolvency declaration. Legal-person criminal liability operates only from 23-Dec-2010 and where the Code expressly provides it; it cannot be projected retrospectively onto 2008 or May 2010. CP 130.2 presupposes an otherwise applicable legal-person responsibility and a listed corporate event; it does not transfer natural-person intent or guilt or decide civil/product liability. Differences may lawfully reflect dates, interest, mortgage caps, instruments, scope, transcription or procedural function; an assignment, discounted NPL acquisition, enforcement, dación or adjudication may be regular. Discounted acquisition followed by recovery is not fraud by itself. The hypothesis can advance only if complete accounting and original instruments exclude those explanations and satisfy the elements of a specific offence.

Why a “double floor” is alleged

The mortgage floor prevented the full E12m fall from reaching the loan while the swap required 5.15% or 4.85% and returned declining E3m. On the €5m covered slice, the drain increased when the loan rate had already stopped falling. E12m/E3m, partial notional and 2012 expiry added basis, amount and duration risk.

Why it was not a maximum cap

Above the 5.55% barrier, the described structure delivered about 0.15 points of relief; it did not impose a hard ceiling on mortgage cost. Partial amount and shorter tenor matter to effectiveness and explanation, but do not alone prove an improper purpose.

CaixaBank's position

Its expert says the written contract warned of negative settlements and uses a 17-Jun-2008 forward curve to derive a 0.516% sum of annualised favourable differentials. Aweswell disputes that this is a full cash/options valuation and what scenarios were meaningfully explained before signature.

TIN, APR/TAE and all-in cost

TAE is not “TIN plus every product.” The second deed states TAE 3.717%; no first-loan TAE is invented. A useful expert model should separately reconstruct ex-post loan, floor, swap, fees, pledge and refinancing cost without mislabelling an unregulated sum as TAE.

HYPOTHESIS REJECTED AS A PRESENT FACT

The swap did not legally replace the bank's provisions, regulatory capital or minimum reserves

No per-loan cash pot

Credit impairment coverage was an accounting adjustment; own funds applied at institution/group level to risk-weighted exposures; and minimum reserves were based mainly on bank liabilities. None was a cash escrow separated for this mortgage.

What can be investigated

Swap settlements could worsen LPB's liquidity and default risk without releasing any bank prudential duty. Testing internal purpose or pricing requires credit classification, collateral/LTV, RWA/capital, impairment, risk approval, valuation and product/hedge-desk records.

No responsible numerical comparison yet

Alleged €400,000/€800,000 receipts cannot be compared to a supposed bank “reserve” using the €8.6m nominal. Even the 8% capital rule depended on risk weights and internal data; it was not automatically 8% of the loan.

Knowledge of falling rates

No evidence establishes a secret Banco de España warning. The ECB held 4.00% on 5-Jun-2008, raised to 4.25% on 9-Jul and first cut on 15-Oct. Any bank-foreknowledge allegation needs internal documents; the later collapse cannot be treated as known in June.

Administrador Concursal · witness origin

CaixaBank requested Borja's testimony; Aweswell adhered

The later court shorthand must not erase the sequence documented contemporaneously by counsel.

A 30 October 2024 email from counsel Javier Sixto records that the testimony of Francisco de Borja Rodríguez-Batllori Laffitte, the Administrador Concursal in Concurso 36/2012, was admitted at CaixaBank's request and that Aweswell adhered to that request. Later court citations therefore describe him procedurally as testigo propuesto por la parte actora y demandada.

The controlled reading is not “both parties independently chose the AC.” It is: CaixaBank requested/proposed Borja; Aweswell adhered; the later both-sides wording records the resulting procedural posture.

Gil Marer's account that the adhesion was defensive and driven by concern about procedural prejudice is preserved as client-reported context; an express contemporaneous counsel source stating that rationale has not yet been located.

No inference by label. The witness-origin sequence does not prove that Borja's eventual testimony will favour CaixaBank, nor bias, collusion, conflict, improper coordination or false evidence.

Calificación and recovery

Why the live banking claim matters without rewriting the past

The materially adverse first-instance Calificación record must be reconciled with a live third-party recovery route connected to the same economic history. If the banking claim succeeds in whole or in part, the result may affect net-loss and recovery analysis. It does not presently establish liability and it does not prove that patrimonial loss was caused exclusively by the bank.

Chronology matters. Sentencia 163/2023 is dated 28 September 2023. CaixaBank's expert report describes the present claim as filed on 13 October 2023. This later proceeding therefore cannot, by itself, prove that the Fiscal in 2019 or the first-instance judge before 28 September 2023 knew of this particular lawsuit. Earlier knowledge of the underlying banking dispute or recovery potential must be proved from earlier sources actor by actor.
