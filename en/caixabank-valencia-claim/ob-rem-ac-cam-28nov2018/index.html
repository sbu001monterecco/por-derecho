28-NOV-2018 · LPB REPRESENTED BY THE INSOLVENCY ADMINISTRATOR ↔ CAM

OB REM: an integrated property/mortgage link was extinguished to permit a direct transfer to the creditor perimeter.

The significance is functional. Registry wording reproduced in Aweswell's 2019 opposition described specified properties as linked so they should not be sold separately and described the distributed mortgage as one loan for payment allocation. On 28 November 2018 LPB, acting through the Insolvency Administrator, and CAM declared the OB REM link extinguished as part of a direct sale.

Boundary. The deed, Registry treatment, Administrator's March 2019 filing and Aweswell's opposition document a real dispute. This page does not state that the transaction was criminal. Any criminal characterization requires proof of authority, knowledge, purpose, loss, benefit and any required concert.

CAM's dual role and the institutional gateway

CAM as buyer

Receives the properties sold directly.

+

CAM as creditor/right-holder

According to the Administrator's later position, CAM also held the mortgage-credit/right position whose consent made release possible.

Institutional question: LPB was not acting through an ordinary autonomous director. The Insolvency Administrator was exercising LPB's liquidation/representation powers. The forensic question therefore includes what estate-interest, authority, conflict, valuation and alternative analysis justified using that power to carry out this transaction with CAM.

POR DERECHO ALLEGATION · CRIMINAL-FIRST · NOT A FINDING

Functional enabling-agent hypothesis

Por Derecho alleges that the OB REM transaction should be compared with other occasions on which the Insolvency Administrator controlled a decisive LPB gateway and the outcome materially favoured the CAM/Acosta Matos perimeter. The allegation is that the office was disloyally instrumentalised as a functional enabling gateway within a credit→control→title architecture detrimental to LPB and its perimeter.

“Functional agent” is an investigative label, not proof of contractual agency or criminal concert. A legal conclusion requires actor-specific proof of power/duty, knowledge, act or omission, intent where required, causation, loss, benefit and any necessary coordination.

Correct use: recurrence does not prove guilt. It identifies records to compare for a common instruction, logic, conflict or independent estate-interest justification.

Strongest innocent explanation

CAM may say it had capacity to consent to release as holder of the associated mortgage right; the Administrator may say the liquidation plan permitted sale of properties outside the suspension, the €400,000 transaction benefited the estate, and judicial validation cured a Registry/formal issue.

The evidence-led response is to demand the contemporaneous authority, valuation, estate-interest comparison, funds flow, interaction with the suspension, final accounts and any side agreements — rather than presuming those explanations true or false.
