Criminal-law lens · public evidence map

Does the evidence confirm, narrow or defeat one continuing enterprise?

This map tests the unitary thesis through finite questions and primary evidence capable of confirming, narrowing or defeating it; it does not assume the answer.

Attributed criminal theory · not a judicial finding. Gil Marer and Aweswell allege one continuing economic-criminal enterprise, advanced through successive adoption and divided functions. This describes a factual and investigative theory: it does not itself prove a statutory criminal organisation or group under Criminal Code Articles 570 bis or 570 ter, an original pact, any person’s participation or guilt. Each actor and each offence require separate proof of their historical elements, conduct, capacity or duty, knowledge, intent where required, causal contribution and contrary material. This is only a factual allegation of connection: it does not characterise the conduct as a continuing or permanent offence or alter completion, any participation window or limitation period for any specific offence.

Attribution rule. For any person or entity, the minimum sequence is: act → capacity/authority → authorship or instruction → knowledge → intent where legally required → use/reliance → prejudice → benefit → causation → correction or continuation after notice. Relationship, chronology, benefit, institutional error or an adverse decision, standing alone, do not prove concertation.

Adversarial control

Contrary evidence should sit beside the thesis, not be hidden at the end.

Control example: Provincial Court Order 804/2018

It is materially adverse: it confirmed provisional dismissal, treated much of the controversy as civil, questioned the criminally protectable possession asserted for CEXP and relied on the insolvency administrator's account. It must be published that clearly.

At the same time, it did not thereby adjudicate every later title, tax, RIC/RICPE, 2022 implementation, CAM→HNT segregation, Matkator/third-party right, current-operation or civil, tourism, Community and Registry issue.

Required page pattern: strongest contrary evidence → strongest innocent explanation → what record could narrow or defeat the inference → what genuinely remains open.

Final rule

The website should become more verifiable, not more accusatory.

The purpose of this architecture is to let prosecutors, judges, investigators, professionals, media and affected parties find the exact proposition, its source, contrary evidence and the missing discriminator quickly. A documented correction that narrows the thesis has the same methodological value as a confirmation.
