Multitrack judicial failure must be proved by sequences, not adjectives.

This map places every filing, warning or contradiction beside the judicial/LAJ/prosecutorial response actually located and beside the asset, claim, hotel or productive-unit life that continued afterward. The investigative thesis is that institutional fragmentation may have produced a practical neutralisation or shielding effect; it does not present capture, criminal coordination or unproved judicial intent as fact.

Reading rule. An adverse decision may be legally wrong without being criminal; an apparent omission may be a corpus gap; and a formally valid act does not by itself determine third-party perimeter, economic causation or a separate criminal question.
