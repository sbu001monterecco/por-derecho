ASSET CLASSIFICATION

What a hotel finca within Sun Park/MYND Yaiza actually is.

Private registered title

A legally distinct title compartment with its own owner, acquisition chain, charges and disposition rights.

Inseparable rights

Quota and co-ownership in common elements, installations and services, together with governance rights and duties.

Operating node

Space included, or capable of inclusion, in one hotel operation covering reception, services, inventory, bookings and revenue.

Authority node

Owner title and consent are relevant to the operator’s enabling title and lawful inclusion of the space.

Economic and data node

Physical room, PMS, marketing, invoices, revenue, works, cost and valuation may depend upon the title.

Evidential and restitution anchor

The finca supports standing to identify, inspect, access, account, restore, separate, restitute or compensate.

Canonical formula: registered title + common rights/quota + regulated hotel dependency + owner authority + possession/revenue/data + evidential and remedial platform.

WHAT IT IS NOT

Eight errors that fragment or misdescribe the object.

Not merely an “apartment”

A historic label does not create an autonomous hotel business where access, reception, services, guests and revenue depend on the hotel.

Not merely today’s room

Number and configuration may have changed; title remains the reference for reconstructing space and rights.

Not an automatic operator share

Finca ownership does not make the owner a shareholder of HNT, Canarian Hospitality, CAM or the operating company.

Not LPB estate property by proximity

A Matkator finca does not enter Concurso 36/2012 because it is in the same hotel or group.

Not automatically transferred with “the hotel”

A corporate transaction, productive-unit transfer or management contract does not transfer third-party title without sufficient instrument and authority.

Not extinguished by transformation

Demolition, fusion, division, renumbering or hotel use do not automatically extinguish the finca or its claims.

Not proof of consent by itself

Unity of exploitation or majority control does not replace evidence of the applicable owner enabling title and authority.

Not criminal by definition

Purchase, operation, adjudication or integration may be lawful. Criminal relevance requires act, knowledge, deception or omission, causation, prejudice and beneficiary.

ACQUISITION / APPROPRIATION

“Consensually or otherwise” requires classification, not a global conclusion.

T-A · verified consensual acquisition

Deed, authority, consent, object, consideration and registry chain confirmed.

T-B · disputed consent

A formal transaction exists, but information, authority, pressure, object, price or validity is disputed.

T-C · alleged non-consensual integration

Title remains elsewhere while possession, works or exploitation by the hotel perimeter is alleged.

T-D · judicial disposition

Auction, adjudication, cession or registration whose regularity and effect require reconstruction.

T-E · represented corporate/productive inclusion

A transaction is said to include the asset or right; schedules, exclusions and registry execution must be tested.

T-F / T-G · open or common

Unproved history, or common/service/operational asset that must not be confused with private title.

Rule. A valid purchase may matter to accumulated control and terminal-beneficiary analysis without being criminal. A deed alone does not prove authority over neighbouring titles, past revenues, common elements, works or third-party consent.

“FINCA ON FINCA”

Title-on-title injury: controlled meaning.

The alleged use of control obtained through some fincas, common elements, Community power, reception, operator, debt, procedure, Registry or regulation to empty another finca of physical identity, access, economic utility, voice, revenue, evidence or remedy, and then convert the material result into an apparently consolidated legal position.

1 · Absorption

Works, division, fusion, renumbering, access and physical room.

2 · Exploitation

PMS, bookings, invoices, revenue and operator control.

3 · Procedure

Debt, claim, enforcement, adjudication, cession and Registry.

4 · Normalisation

Corporate, tourism, finance, aid, tax and public records.

Not a standalone offence. Each layer must be translated into the relevant civil, corporate, insolvency, administrative or criminal wrong and proved actor by actor.

CLAIMANT’S CRIMINAL POSITION

Procedural fraud alleged as completed; adjudicative status kept separate.

Position preserved without dilution

Gil Marer and the affected Aweswell–LPB–Matkator perimeter maintain that a completed procedural fraud occurred in the Cuatrecasas/Matkator La Laguna proceedings. They also allege concert, participation or cooperation, concealment or regularisation, and use of that route as an instrument contributing to, protecting or making harder to reverse the procedural and insolvency fraud they attribute to Concurso 36/2012.

Public classification: party position / completed offence alleged. This page contains no judicial finding of guilt.

Procedural fraud

Requires particularised procedural fraud or manipulation, judicial error, resolution and economic prejudice. The party maintains completion; the criminal court has not declared it.

Conspiracy/proposition

Require agreement or invitation and resolution concerning a particular offence; converging benefit or sequence alone is insufficient.

Participation/“enablement”

Must be classified as joint authorship, instrumentality, inducement, necessary cooperation, complicity or a later autonomous offence.

Concealment

A specific post-offence category for a non-author/non-accomplice; it is not a universal label.

“Insolvency fraud”

The exact act and offence must be identified: procedural fraud, insolvency offence, false document, disloyal administration, misappropriation, obstruction, prevaricación or another route.

Adverse evidence

Valid debt, lawful procedure, sufficient disclosure, independent judicial reasoning and verified consensual acquisition must also be preserved.
