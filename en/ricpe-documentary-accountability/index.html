2020 versus 20 JUL 2021: the documentary contrast requiring explanation

2020 · investor-facing picture

The 4 June material places Sun Park in RICPE's project table associated with CAM and identifies CAM as the project company/title-holder. The 11 November webinar presents Sun Park as an approximately 220-apartment project to be transformed into a four-star hotel, within communication also describing controls, advisers and due diligence.

Those statements prove what was communicated; they do not automatically prove the legal truth of each expression.

20 JUL 2021 · RICPE's own record

The Enrique Guerra certification preserved in the corpus describes 262 properties: 54 CAM, 190 LPB and 18 third-party; conditions reform on CAM becoming owner; and records absence of the LOI and complete due diligence, plus prior project withdrawal.

CAM filed that certification in Insolvency 36/2012 on 21 July 2021.

The question is not “was there deception?” as a pre-decided conclusion. It is: what changed between the 2020 communication and the 2021 record, who knew it, what was corrected for investors/CNMV/advisers, and which record later allowed reactivation?

2021–2022: the capital route revived before the full documentary implementation had closed

The corpus places a RICPE capital/investment decision linked to Sun Park on 29/12/2021 and later review in early 2022. That sequence must be reconciled with:

26/01/2022 adjudication → 14/02/2022 testimony/finality episode → 21/02/2022 deed → Registry → CAM→HNT restructuring.

The evidential question is which exact record — or combination of records — moved the project from future ownership / incomplete due diligence to a position treated as sufficient for investment, finance, transformation and later administrative support.

Temporal proximity does not prove causation, and issuance of a court-office instrument does not prove knowledge of its eventual use before RICPE, the notary, Registry or funding authorities.

RIC capital: documented total and a discrepancy requiring reconciliation

The controlled dossier attributes to RICPE's audited 2023 accounts Series F/G financing to HNT/MYND Yaiza totalling €6,573,703.10. Other preserved project/prospectus materials contain a different component split.

No version is silently selected. Until native audited accounts, every prospectus version, agreements, approvals, Control Unit records, drawdowns and payments are compared, the total is cited with attribution to the controlled source and the exact split remains open.

The controlled audited material also describes the RIC procedure as involving Canary Government suitability decrees preceded by binding AEAT reports. That fiscal/investment gate is not, by itself, a civil-title warranty over every property or right.

RIC is not the subsidy, and CNMV supervision is not project approval

RICPE / private-RIC capital

Reconstruct origin, approval, beneficiary, security, conflicts/abstentions, due diligence, conditions, drawdown and use.

CNMV · supervision

SCR status adds a regulatory framework; it does not mean CNMV certified Sun Park title or verified the material truth of each prospectus/DFI. CNMV itself states content responsibility lies with the vehicle/manager.

AEAT + Canary Government · RIC

These are fiscal/suitability gates within the RIC mechanism. The facts represented and the exact scope of each decision must be obtained, without turning tax suitability into a general ownership guarantee.

Rule: RIC capital, the regional incentive and EU co-financing are distinct layers. Their convergence requires reconciliation of beneficiary, title/availability, perimeter, eligible costs, other finance and controls. It does not establish double funding, fraud, improper payment or EU-budget loss.

Three consequence planes that cannot be atomised inside LPB

Insolvency · LPB

Estate, value, realisation, income, credit/payment accounting, exit alternatives, restitution/reintegration/damages and present capacity to reverse insolvency consequences.

Extra-insolvency · Matkator / third parties

Title and Registry, possession/access, works, hotel use, fruits/revenue, community burdens, financing/development capacity and remedies over rights that did not automatically enter LPB's estate.

Cross-border · Aweswell / foreign capital

Foreign-investor value/control, inbound capital or financing, recovery, refinancing, legal/accounting/tax/enforcement costs and remedies crossing Spain's border.

The foreign-investor cross-border plane is distinct from EU financial interests. Both may coexist; neither proves the other.

Serial reliance: the institutionalisation question

A chain may institutionalise a premise without agreement among participants. A notary may rely on testimony; a Registry on a deed; RICPE on a due-diligence package; a RIC authority on investment representations; an incentives authority on beneficiary declarations; an operator on the title or contract it receives.

The correct pressure is not “everyone collaborated.” It is to ask what each gatekeeper independently re-checked against the original properties, rights and authority once RICPE's own July-2021 documentation had materially narrowed the earlier picture.

Ongoing alleged harm, not a historical episode

MYND Yaiza is currently marketed and operated. That operation is not itself unlawful and does not turn all hotel revenue into damage. It does require an audit of whether current use, restriction, value or revenue relates to disputed assets/rights; whether such rights still support project finance or value; and what restitutionary, Registry, accounting, administrative or damages remedies remain open for LPB, Matkator, Aweswell or third parties.

Historical act ≠ extinguished consequence. The analysis reaches present title, Registry position, possession/access, operation, revenue, value, public-support conditions and remedies.

Reasonable defence preserved

An SCR may legitimately study a project subject to future acquisitions, have a promoter-linked director while applying appropriate conflict controls, reasonably rely on advisers and finance only when it considers its conditions satisfied. It may also later found Orion without any transfer of MYND value. Version or figure differences may exist without falsity or loss.

That is precisely why native accounts, minutes, conflict/abstention records, due diligence, agreements, tax files and bank traceability are the best answer.

What this page does not establish

It does not establish investor fraud, subsidy fraud, double funding, money laundering, falsification, automatic shared knowledge, conspiracy, CNMV approval of Sun Park, a civil-title warranty by AEAT/Canary Government, or a Sun Park/MYND→Orion money flow.
