This documentary accountability request is directed to RICPE because of its own governance, Control/Compliance and investor-protection responsibilities. It does not ask the Board to defend CAM, HNT, AGM, Orion or any individual; it asks for preservation, independent examination, correction and a governance decision.

2020 → 2021: the first reconciliation

CNMV places José Acosta Matos on RICPE’s board from 4 Nov 2019. The 4 Jun 2020 investor material must be compared with RICPE’s internal 20 Jul 2021 certification: 54 CAM properties, 190 LPB and 18 third-party, CAM acquisition future/conditional, no signed LOI and full due diligence not yet started. CAM filed that certification in Insolvency 36/2012 on 21 Jul 2021.

Question for the Board: who introduced the investor project described as a participating loan of up to €2m within an approximately €11m Lanzarote-apartment investment, what part was Hotel Sun Park, what related-party/conflict disclosure was made, who abstained and who authorised progression?

What valuation made the project financeable?

RICPE should produce the valuation it used: assets/properties valued, assumed owner, valuer, date, methodology, encumbrances, resulting value, security and underwriting use. It must be reconciled with the approximately €9.77m January-2018 valuation already identified in the historical record and any value CAM supplied to Insolvency 36/2012.

We do not treat ordinary bank LTV practice as a legal rule binding RICPE. The narrower question is: if the capital/funding proposition was around €12m, what value, equity, additional security or financial structure supported that figure?
