RIC Private Equity Investment Partners, SCR, S.A. (RICPE): what did the platform know when Sun Park was being presented?

This page separates RICPE statements, promoter statements, court documents and later events. It does not presume fraud, collusion or liability merely because a project was financed or presented.

Core question

Can RICPE identify, date by date, what it understood about effective hotel/unit ownership, CAM's legal position, LPB/third-party assets, adjudication status and due-diligence conditions when Sun Park was introduced, maintained or updated in investment materials?

  1. Who proposed and approved Sun Park internally.
  2. What title/ownership was verified in 2019–2021 and through which records.

  4. The exact function and recipients of the 21/07/2021 certification.

  8. RICPE's present correction/right-of-reply position.

Reasonable defence preserved

An investment company may describe a project subject to future acquisitions, approvals or due diligence and may legitimately rely on promoter/adviser information. A simplified or later-outdated description does not itself prove deception. Text, date, recipient, condition and actual knowledge must be compared.

What this page does not establish

It does not establish investor fraud, money laundering, misappropriation, falsification, regulatory breach or automatic shared knowledge between RICPE, CAM, HNT, MYND or individuals.
