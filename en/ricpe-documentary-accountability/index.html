This page separates RICPE statements, promoter documents, insolvency events and later effects. It does not presume fraud or collusion. It asks whether a legally fragmented title/availability picture was properly corrected and verified before passing through RIC capital, regional incentives and current MYND Yaiza operation.

The audit extends to extra-insolvency rights of Matkator/third parties, the cross-border position of Aweswell/foreign capital, and current effects of use, revenue, value, financing and public support. Each alleged harm or benefit must be proved asset by asset and right by right.

Three planes RICPE cannot reduce to LPB's insolvency

Insolvency

LPB value and causes of action, liquidation, income, payments, realisation and current remedies.

Extra-insolvency

Matkator and third parties: property, access, works, use, exploitation, fruits, burdens and value of rights outside LPB's estate.

Cross-border

Aweswell/foreign investor: value and control, inbound financing/capital, recovery, costs and remedies crossing Spain's border.

An insolvency proceeding over LPB does not turn the entire physical/economic Sun Park perimeter into insolvency-estate property or extinguish direct third-party/cross-border rights that must be proved separately.

Functional instrumentalisation: the documentary-reliance question

The working hypothesis does not require RICPE to have known of any earlier irregularity. It asks whether a title, testimony, expression of finality, deed or representation born in another context entered due diligence and was treated as sufficient to finance a broader perimeter.

A chain can institutionalise a premise without agreement among participants. The audit should therefore identify what record each RICPE function received, who verified it, against which independent source, and what happened when later information narrowed or contradicted the earlier picture.

Ongoing alleged harm, not a closed episode

MYND Yaiza currently operates and is marketed commercially. That is not itself unlawful and does not turn all revenue into damage. It does require an audit of whether use, restriction, value or revenue from disputed assets/rights persists; whether such rights continue to support the project; and what restitutionary, Registry, accounting, administrative or damages remedy remains open for LPB, Matkator, Aweswell or third parties.

Historical act ≠ extinguished consequence. The analysis must reach the present legal and economic position.

Reasonable defence preserved

An SCR may legitimately study a project subject to future acquisitions, rely on advisers/promoters and finance only when it understands conditions to be satisfied. Version or figure differences can exist without falsity or loss. That defence is real and should be visible.

That is precisely why native accounts, minutes, due diligence, contracts and drawdown traceability are the best answer.

What this page does not establish

It does not establish investor fraud, subsidy fraud, double funding, money laundering, falsification, automatic shared knowledge or liability by association among RICPE, CAM, HNT, MYND, officials or advisers.
