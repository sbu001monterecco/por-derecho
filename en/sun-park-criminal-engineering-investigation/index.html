LIVE INVESTIGATION · ARCHITECTURE · ENABLERS · BENEFIT · IRREVERSIBILITY

Was there one continuing enterprise and, if so, how was it adopted, enabled, formalised, financed and protected?

The forensic engineering works forwards and backwards through an alleged sequence of private, insolvency, judicial, procedural, notarial, land-register, financial and supervisory functions.

ATTRIBUTED CRIMINAL THEORY · NOT A JUDICIAL FINDING · POR DERECHO CENTRAL ALLEGATION. Gil Marer and Aweswell allege one continuing economic-criminal enterprise, advanced through successive adoption and divided functions. The theory does not itself prove a statutory criminal organisation or group under Criminal Code Articles 570 bis or 570 ter, an original pact, any person’s participation or guilt. Each actor and each offence require separate proof of their historical elements, conduct, capacity or duty, knowledge, intent where required, causal contribution and contrary material. This is only a factual allegation of connection: it does not characterise the conduct as a continuing or permanent offence or alter completion, any participation window or limitation period for any specific offence.

Control rule. An order, procedural office act, deed, registry entry, appraisal, audited account, regulated vehicle or subsidy does not by itself prove the upstream premise. For each formalisation the audit asks who originated the premise, who verified it independently, who knew of the contradiction and what benefit the formal act protected.

Independent titles and final decisions

Infringement hypotheses: element by element

No global accusation is published. Each hypothesis requires objective conduct, knowledge or intent, participation, causation and exculpatory evidence.

Procedural or judicial deception

Materially false or incomplete representation, capacity to produce a patrimonial decision, knowledge and individual contribution.

Documentary or accounting falsity

Knowingly inaccurate certificate, minute, account, title, debt, ownership, expenditure, employment or later use.

Punishable insolvency or frustration

Concealment or diversion of value, artificial liability inflation, improper preference, incomplete records or frustrated recovery.

Disloyal administration or unauthorised disposition

Use of assets, fruits, revenue or rights without authority and contrary to duties of loyalty or estate protection.

Obstruction, coercion or retaliation

Access restrictions, pressure, intimidation, interference with advisers/witnesses or evidence after a concrete alert.

Market, aid and funds

Materially misleading title, charges, conflicts, costs, works, employment, funding accumulation or eligibility.

High thresholds. Judicial prevarication, corruption, money laundering or coordinated participation require evidence of the subjective element and the specific link; an adverse decision, professional relationship or corporate succession is not enough.
