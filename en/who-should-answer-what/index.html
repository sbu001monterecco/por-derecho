DOCUMENT-LED ACCOUNTABILITY MAP

One strong question for each function. One file capable of answering it.

Different institutions must answer different questions. This map separates remit, knowledge and proof so that neither institutional over-attribution nor fragmentation between competences can erase a common factual matrix.

Cross-cutting rule: received ≠ examined ≠ verified ≠ decided ≠ corrected. Relationship ≠ identity. Platform continuity ≠ money flow. Historical act ≠ extinguished consequence.

Neither a “group” label replaces individual proof nor corporate separation erases documented relationships. Each connection must be proved by date, entity, office, document, capital and effect.
