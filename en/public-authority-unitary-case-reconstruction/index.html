SHARED FACT

A question does not cease to matter to your institution because another authority also needs the answer.

Example: title on a material date. It may independently matter to the insolvency, a municipal file, tourism, RICPE/CNMV, public funding and—if a materially inaccurate representation was knowingly used—to a criminal investigation. The legal consequence is not the same in every lane; the underlying fact may be shared.

The system is intended to let each institution mark its own part as verified, not verified, contradicted or outside competence, rather than rebuilding the same fact from scratch—or assuming it was resolved—in every file.

DUAL COMMAND

A civil irregularity does not automatically become a crime; formal validity does not automatically close a separate criminal question.

Civil / commercial / insolvency / administrative track

Validity, competence, title, authority, credit, procedure, effectiveness, appeal and remedy.

Criminal / investigative / forensic track

Objective act, actor, contemporaneous knowledge, representation or omission, document used, recipient, reliance, benefit, harm, pattern, proven coordination and alternative explanation.

Actor-specific discipline. A company, family, law firm or institution does not share knowledge or intent merely because its members are connected. Coordination needs its own evidence. An adverse or disputed decision by an official or judge is not by itself criminal evidence.

SOURCE OF TRUTH

Proposition → evidence → dependency → revisable conclusion.

Canonical IDs

UNIT, OWN, VOTE, ACTA, AUTH, DEBT, AC, JUD, PUB, TXN, FUND and EVID prevent each thread from creating its own version of the facts.

Mandatory contradiction search

Every adverse proposition must actively search for evidence that weakens or exculpates. What survives that review is more useful than a one-sided accumulation of material.

Visible corrections

Statuses change when better evidence appears. Corrections should preserve the earlier statement, the source that caused the change and downstream pages requiring revision.

CORRECTION / RIGHT OF REPLY

The fastest way to weaken a conclusion is to provide a better source.

People, professionals and institutions mentioned in the record may provide documents, context or corrections. A material documentary response should itself be treated as evidence: it may confirm, limit, contradict or supersede an existing proposition. The methodological objective is a system capable of changing its mind when the evidence changes.

Permanent question: what proxy, deed, recalculation, due-diligence record, waiver, ruling or contemporaneous communication would require us to modify this conclusion? If it exists, it should be sought and displayed.
