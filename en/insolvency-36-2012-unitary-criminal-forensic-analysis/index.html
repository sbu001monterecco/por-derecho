Criminal-law boundary. This is an investigative and prosecutorial theory. It preserves direct criminal allegations where they exist, but it does not present intent, criminal agreement, judicial wrongdoing, fraud, falsity, disloyal administration, insolvency offence or criminal organisation as adjudicated facts. Every legal characterisation must be proved person by person, document by document and under the law applicable at the date.
