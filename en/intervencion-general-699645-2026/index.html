NEW INSTITUTIONAL RESPONSE · 19 AUGUST 2026

The General Comptroller clarifies what it did — and did not — say about Sun Park / MYND Yaiza.

SALIDA 699645/2026 expressly narrows the reach of the 11 June response. The Office maintains that RIC suitability is not itself a Canary Islands regional subsidy and that its own audit remit is limited. But it adds a critical traceability point: the earlier response cannot be used as a general certificate that no other aid, incentives, EU/ERDF funding, payments, certifications or administrative files existed.

Document: General Outgoing Registration 699645/2026 · electronically signed by Miguel Francisco del Pino Sánchez, Vice-Comptroller General, on 19/08/2026 at 14:35 · verification NDE shown: RP001-000rJcefH+oVjHc+7kUw5ScHQ==.

“It does not contain, and cannot be interpreted as, a general certification that any aid, subsidy, regional incentive, European financing, payment, certification or administrative file other than those examined in that reply does not exist.”

— faithful English translation of the General Comptroller’s statement on SALIDA 497011/2026.

This materially corrects an overbroad reading of the June communication. The safe conclusion is no longer “there were no other funds”; it is: the General Comptroller was not certifying that question and says each possible instrument must be pursued before the body with substantive competence.

What the new response confirms

1 · Limited remit

The Office describes its role as internal economic-financial control of the Canary Islands public sector through legally prescribed control files and plans. It does not claim a general power to investigate prospectively every aspect of the asset.

2 · RIC is a specific lane

It maintains that the suitability declaration for RIC financial instruments is not, by itself, a regional-government subsidy and requires the competent Finance authority plus a binding AEAT report.

3 · Other funding remains a separate documentary question

The response itself lists regional incentives, public aid, European funds, ERDF/FEDER funds, tax obligations, RIC-linked instruments and local-authority actions as matters to be established through the relevant competent bodies.

4 · No merits ruling

The letter expressly says it does not prejudge the truth of the facts, the existence of irregularities, responsibility of persons/entities/public bodies, or rights and remedies available before other competent bodies.

What it does NOT prove

It does not, by itself, prove a specific double-funding event, a particular ERDF payment, an unlawful subsidy or civil/criminal/administrative liability. It is a statement about competence and documentary scope.

For the same reason, it cannot safely be presented as an “administrative clearance” of the project or its participants. The Office itself says it lacks the competence and objective basis to establish irregularity or responsibility in the manner requested.

The documentary consequence

The evidence exercise now has to proceed body by body and instrument by instrument.

RIC / suitability

Government of the Canary Islands, Finance Vice-Ministry and AEAT: application, project memorandum, binding report, Decree 224/2022, instruments, amounts, timetable and authorised uses.

Regional incentives

Identify the file, granting authority, beneficiary, eligible investment, certifications, payments, controls and any recovery/correction measures.

ERDF / EU funding

Separate the documented ERDF/FEDER reference from the still-open questions of amount, certified expenditure, percentage, payments, verifications and compatibility with other sources.

Regional/local grants and aid

Seek each file from its managing/granting body. The absence of a control file at the General Comptroller is not the same thing as the absence of a grant file elsewhere.

Access and confidentiality: the Office’s position

The response argues that a third party or complainant does not, merely by that status, become an interested party in a financial-control file, and it invokes statutory reservation/confidentiality rules governing control information.

Legal boundary: this site records that as the General Comptroller’s legal position in this letter. The document is not a court judgment universally resolving every possible access right under transparency law, whistleblower protection, criminal procedure or other sector-specific regimes.
