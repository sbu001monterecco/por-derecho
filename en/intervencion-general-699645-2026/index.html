Mandatory interpretive correction

It is inaccurate to say the General Comptroller confirmed that “there was no public funding”. What it confirmed is only that a RIC suitability declaration is not, by itself, an autonomous subsidy or direct Canary treasury payment and that the Office does not participate in fiscalising it.

The August letter says the June reply cannot be used as a general nonexistence certificate for other aid, subsidies, regional incentives, European or ERDF funding, payments, certifications or separate files.

Result: questions concerning GC/836/P06, ERDF, payments, employment, Series F/G, other support and possible overlap remain open and must be resolved through native files. This does not prove that any such instrument existed, was paid or was irregular.

Access, SIINF and separate routes

The letter argues that complainant or third-party status does not by itself confer access to a possible financial-control file and invokes reservation and confidentiality. This page records that institutional position; it does not present it as a universal court ruling on transparency, whistleblower protection, criminal procedure or other sector-specific rights.

SIINF is for objective indications connected with the activity or functioning of Canary autonomous-administration bodies. It does not replace access requests concerning RIC, regional incentives, ERDF, tax, local or judicial files.
