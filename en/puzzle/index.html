Predatory inclusion · attributed theory, finite proof test

Gil Marer's position: later people, entities, professional reputation, capital or institutional resources may allegedly be drawn into a structure after unresolved legacy exposure or non-disclosure, thereby strengthening it. The site does not infer that later participants knew, concealed, breached duties or joined wrongdoing. The test is legacy exposure → material fact/disclosure reason → actor-specific knowledge → omission/misleading presentation → new actor/resource entry → reliance/enabling effect → benefit/causation. A later actor's lack of knowledge can be exculpatory of that actor's intent.
