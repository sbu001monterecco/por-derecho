Immediate legal alert

Do not confuse historic Article 93 with current TRLC Article 93.

Here, Article 93.2.2 and Article 93.3 refer to the former Law 22/2003 on Insolvency, in the version temporally applicable to Insolvency 36/2012. Current TRLC Article 93 concerns a different subject. For a credit transfer after the insolvency declaration, the sharper classification control is not historic Article 93.3 alone but also Article 97.4.4 of Law 22/2003 — now reflected in TRLC Article 310.2.4 — and only if primary evidence first establishes that the later creditor legally qualified as a specially related person.

Decisive timing control: the published chronology places CAM's entry as assignee in October 2017, after the 2012 declaration. Article 93.3's rebuttable presumption concerned acquisition within the two years before declaration, so it is not by itself the natural rule for that transfer. The post-declaration substitution rule and the assignee's legal status must be tested, not assumed.

Immediate action 01

Audit credit classification at every substitution.

Finite request: obtain certified copies of every application, Insolvency Administrator submission, order and creditor-list entry documenting Bankia/SAREB → Promontoria Holding 122 B.V. → CAM, showing exact date, amount, security and legal classification before and after each substitution.

  • Determine whether the later creditor met any Article 93 test at the legally relevant date, including de jure/de facto administrator, liquidator, general attorney or group relationship.
  • If Article 93.2.2 is advanced, prove real functions, powers, instructions or intervention in LPB's management/disposition; creditor, buyer, material possessor or beneficiary status is not enough by itself.
  • Keep Article 93.3 separate: it has its own timing requirement and requires a credit belonging to a specially related person.
  • Require the documentary explanation for why classification was retained or changed when the creditor changed.

Target result: a documented binary answer — applicable or not; if applicable, how it was treated; if not, what factual or legal precondition was missing.

Immediate action 02

TRLC Article 82: request information, not a disguised historical judgment.

An Article 82 request should be narrow and operational. Before filing it, check the Article 83 consequence and overlap with routes already open. The purpose is defined production, preservation, accounting reconciliation and protection of any continuing insolvency-estate interest, not to decide historic civil or criminal responsibility through a supervision request.

  • Complete estate general ledger/journal and bank statements for relevant periods.
  • Reconciliation: credit → substitutions → dation/adjudication → two separate EUR 400,000 trails → Community liabilities → fruits/income → final balance.
  • Delivery/restoration inventories, keys, access, works and affected spaces.
  • Documentary support for each accounting treatment still capable of verification, plus native-file preservation.
  • Status and support for final accounts/accounting and any later correction, preserving the historical date of each act or omission.

Minimum proof per link: primary source, date, author, recipient, authority invoked, available neutral alternative, patrimonial consequence and the evidence capable of breaking the chain. Functional convergence is not coordination without actor-specific proof.

Immediate action 05

PwC, Grant Thornton and RSM: preserve, do not accuse.

Recent correspondence shows that professional-source preservation has already been activated. The correct next step is controlled traceability and proportionate requests for records capable of fixing chronology, knowledge, custody, conflict, escalation or reporting routes. A professional firm's involvement is not by itself evidence of complicity or validation of conduct.

  • retainer/engagement and scope;
  • independence/conflict checks and client/perimeter changes;
  • contemporaneous correspondence and meeting notes;
  • file custody, transfer, production and closure;
  • internal risk/ethics/reporting escalations and native metadata limited to relevant periods.

Operational control: do not duplicate notices already sent; record acknowledgments, deadlines and substantive response or non-response before any further escalation.

Immediate action 06

Two EUR 400,000 trails, accounts, fruits and title: mandatory reconciliation.

The two EUR 400,000 references must remain separate unless a primary source proves identity. For each: date, payer, payee, account, purpose, ledger entry, contractual/notarial support and final-account treatment. Apply the same discipline to fruits/income, Community charges and every property transferred or used.

Required output: one money–credit–title–possession–use reconciliation capable of exposing any unexplained amount or asset without converting a gap into a criminal conclusion.

Immediate action 07

Certified files and strict separation of routes.

Obtain and compare certified records of the relevant insolvency proceedings, DP 1956/2026 and its appeal chain, private-actor proceedings and the specific judicial-accountability modules. Evidence may overlap; one route does not prove another.

  • IA: entrusted power → knowledge → act/omission → estate harm/accounting → correlated advantage.
  • Private actors: actor → document/representation → knowledge → recipient/decision → benefit → harm.
  • Judicial route: exact order → material on record → legal alternative → reasoning/supervision → effect. Error or adverse outcome is not, without more, judicial criminality.

What changes after the rescan

The strongest formulation is not “everything proves a common plan.” It is: every link can and should be subjected to a finite evidential test, and a missing documentary bridge at a specific point must remain identified as missing rather than filled by inference. The immediate objective is to obtain the documents capable of closing or destroying the hypothesis.
