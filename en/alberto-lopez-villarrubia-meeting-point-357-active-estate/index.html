Civil, insolvency, institutional and criminal analysis

What is wrong—or requires explanation.

1 · Verifiable protection

The neutral source was refused

The 24-Feb-2021 order refused inspection and expert valuation despite recording works, debris and blocked-access allegations. This left no neutral property–photo–damage–value baseline. The law places conservation on the administrator but allows court assistance; allocating competence does not by itself show supervision was effective.

2 · Truncated productive unit

Property is not operation

LPB was the only debtor and the hotel was mixed-owned. That made it necessary to separate and value LPB properties, CEXP participation/contracts, operation, revenue, goodwill, rescue capacity and claims. Gil alleges that this productive layer was disabled and not effectively protected.

3 · Control and market before title

Club Sei is not mere chronology

Commercial sources support pre-title preparation and presentation of Club Sei Lanzarote at the same location. Gil alleges that an unidentified Meeting Point/FTI legal person was a potentially injuring actor in that mechanism. The brochure, pilot and offer do not prove contract, opening, bookings, revenue, authority or crime.

4 · Appearance in 357

The attribution needs authentication

It is wrong to convert three publications into a proved personal signature; it is also wrong to ignore them. The signed original and allocation history are required. Ordinary substitution, support, reassignment or a register error could explain the names.

5 · Conflict

Factual overlap is not an automatic ground

LOPJ Article 219 requires a specific ground such as direct/indirect interest or prior participation in the same or a related matter. Deciding another insolvency is not enough. The CAM–Club Sei–Meeting Point link, personal knowledge and decisional effect must be proved before alleging a mandatory recusal.

6 · SEPI

No personal notice duty is proved

SEPI documents EUR 31 million for Meeting Point. No current record traces it into Sun Park, proves omission from FASEE/357 or creditor harm, or establishes a personal judicial duty to notify SEPI. The proper inquiry is into loan terms, debtor disclosures, SEPI monitoring and the Auren file.

Attributed criminal thesis: Gil Marer^ and Aweswell Limited^ allege that control/exclusion, loss of operation and rescue capacity, works/pilots and pre-title marketing created the information, valuation and competition conditions that caused or conditioned adjudication. They allege that Club Sei^ was the commercial layer of a potentially injuring actor in the Meeting Point perimeter. This is not a criminal judgment and does not prove that any of the three debtors committed a crime. Criminal attribution to a judge requires duty, knowledge, objectively unjust action, intent and causation—not an adverse ruling or chronology alone.
