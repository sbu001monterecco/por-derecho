PWC CANARIAS · CARLOS SAAVEDRA & 2015–2016 TEAM · PUBLIC PROFESSIONAL RECORD

What PwC Canarias was asked to do — and what its surviving records can still answer.

The surviving primary record establishes a professional legal-advisory relationship involving Landwell-PricewaterhouseCoopers Tax & Legal Services, S.L., Matkator/Luchy Playa Blanca and the Sun Park perimeter. The public question is narrower than an accusation: what was the exact mandate, which professionals received which material, what work product was created, what was communicated to the insolvency administrator, and what records remain?

Evidence-led · entity / scope / preservation questions open

Editorial boundary. This page distinguishes documented professional involvement from later responsibility. It does not allege that PwC, Carlos Saavedra or another team member caused later Sun Park events, breached professional duties, switched sides, misused confidential information or bears liability. Those propositions require separate act-by-act evidence.

EVIDENCE GAP

The stated appendix is not in the located copy

The invoice says “See appendix”, but the preserved copy presently located contains only the covering letter and invoice page. The appendix describing the billed work has not yet been recovered. Later correspondence shows the invoice remained pending; therefore the record supports an invoiced professional relationship, not the previous site's stronger phrase “paid legal adviser”.

What the record supports

Knowledge, mandate and breach are three different questions.

A · DOCUMENTED PROFESSIONAL EXPOSURE

What PwC was shown or worked on

The source family supports exposure to Community/CEXP accounts and expenses, resolutions, registry/property material, payment/debt questions, operating/contracts material, proposed challenges and contact with the insolvency administrator. Attribution remains person-by-person.

B · MANDATE

What PwC was professionally required to do

The invoice proves legal advice but its missing appendix means the complete formal scope is still open. The engagement letter, terms of business, matter-opening record and full work-product index are priority retrieval items.

C · RESPONSIBILITY

What is not established by A or B

Receipt of information and professional participation do not establish negligence, conflict breach, concealment, causation or responsibility for later events. Any such proposition requires the exact duty, later act/omission, evidence, consequence and causal bridge.

LATER-POSITION THEORY

A separate unresolved hypothesis

The Project has previously questioned whether PwC-linked professionals later occupied a materially adverse position. The current evidence establishes Carlos's 2022 witness role, not an adverse mandate. No public finding is made here of a “switch”, conflict breach or misuse of confidential information.
