8 · Criminal relevance versus PwC culpability

The underlying Sun Park continuum may require criminal investigation without predetermining PwC responsibility.

The Project alleges that disputed Community authority, debt-based voting exclusion, records/minutes, access/control measures, private-unit/title displacement and subsequent transaction/funding representations cannot responsibly be analysed only as disconnected civil, horizontal-property, tourism and insolvency disputes. If falsity, absence of authority, knowing use and patrimonial consequence are established, criminal-law questions may arise.

PwC control: prior knowledge is strongly documented. Criminal complicity by Carlos or any PwC professional is not. That would require a later same-asset mandate or act, actual knowledge/access, concrete assistance or legally sufficient omission, material causation and the mental element required by the relevant offence.
