PUBLIC CASE SNAPSHOT

What the UK judgment establishes—and what it does not.

Identity

Claimant: Edgeworth Capital (Luxembourg) S.à r.l.
Defendant: Cuatrecasas Gonçalves Pereira LLP.
Case: BL-2024-000237 / [2025] EWHC 1014 (Ch).

Judgment record

Allegation

The judgment records a pleaded €213 million professional-negligence and breach-of-retainer claim concerning alleged failure to advise before a June 2019 transaction on the effect of two early-2019 Spanish Supreme Court decisions. The defendant denies the claim.

Allegation + denial

Decision

The Court permitted specified amendments and, subject to a compliant statement of truth, dismissed the strike-out application. It did not decide duty, breach, causation or loss on the merits.

Procedural only

Later status

A June 2026 press report says a costs and case-management conference occurred in April 2026 and the matter was moving toward disclosure. The underlying sealed case-management order is not in this public corpus.

Entity, SRA and forum firewall

The LLP named in the Edgeworth judgment cannot be treated as the responsible defendant in another matter merely because the same brand or London office appears. Any civil, insurance or SRA route must identify the precise legal person, retainer, office, lawyer status, work, invoice, act, governing law and alleged breach. No UK court action is being pursued. Any SRA consideration is a distinct, secondary and jurisdiction-dependent regulatory question; it is not litigation, a damages forum or a substitute for the Spain-led merits analysis.

Official checks: SRA Solicitors Register and SRA overseas and cross-border practice rules.
