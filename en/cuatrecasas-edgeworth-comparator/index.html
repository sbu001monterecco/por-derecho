INDEPENDENT MATTERS · EXACT 2019 JURISPRUDENCE · SEPARATE EVIDENCE

Two independent client matters. The same 2019 Supreme Court jurisprudence. Different alleged failures.

Edgeworth's pleaded case alleges failure to advise. The independent Aweswell / Sun Rock record is different: it shows that STS 112/2019 was supplied directly to Cuatrecasas, its asserted economic significance was explained, and the judgment was later included again in a live refinancing / insolvency-exit package. The legitimate comparison is therefore not whether one case proves the other, but how material law was identified, communicated and converted into client protection.

Controlling boundary: Edgeworth Capital (Luxembourg) S.à r.l. v Cuatrecasas Gonçalves Pereira LLP, BL-2024-000237 / [2025] EWHC 1014 (Ch), is a separate English High Court claim involving different parties, transaction, retainer, causation and alleged loss. Cuatrecasas denies the Edgeworth claim. The May 2025 judgment was procedural and made no merits finding of negligence, breach, causation or loss. The Aweswell / Sun Rock allegations are likewise disputed and unadjudicated. Nothing on this page transfers liability or findings between the matters.

THE TWO 2019 SUPREME COURT DECISIONS

The jurisprudential bridge can now be identified precisely.

STS 112/201920 February 2019 · ECLI:ES:TS:2019:521Underlying insolvency incident: 3000334/2014. The decision addressed recognition and treatment of mortgage-secured interest within the insolvency creditors' list.
STS 227/201911 April 2019 · ECLI:ES:TS:2019:1222Underlying insolvency incident: 11/2015. It refined the treatment of post-declaration interest, including the distinction between remunerative and default / moratory interest.

The public Edgeworth allegation identifies Spanish matters 3000334/14 and 11/2015, decided in February and April 2019. Those identifiers map to STS 112/2019 and STS 227/2019 respectively. An independent June 2019 Clifford Chance briefing treated the pair as potentially having serious practical consequences in insolvency finance.

AWESWELL PRIMARY CHRONOLOGY

The Sun Rock record is a knowledge-and-implementation chronology.

31 MAY 2019

Cuatrecasas was preparing to discuss the LPB insolvency with a prospective financier and itself identified the interest calculation as disputed and likely to reduce the amount required. Aweswell then sent STS 112/2019 directly to the firm and explained the client's asserted economic consequence. Cuatrecasas acknowledged the importance of the clarification.

11 JUN 2019

Aweswell again attached STS 112/2019 in an email to Cuatrecasas concerning Fiduciam, a proposed Article 176 insolvency exit, blocked proof-of-funds mechanics and the security package. Cuatrecasas acknowledged the material and continued advising on how security could be structured while LPB remained in insolvency.

4 NOV 2019

LPB's reposición expressly invoked STS 227/2019 and Article 59.1 LC against the treatment of post-insolvency default interest. The filed material was subsequently forwarded to Cuatrecasas. This is a later procedural route and should not be collapsed into the pre-June STS 112 chronology.

16 JAN 2020

In the separate Talleres Jinámar matter, the same Mercantile Court treated the February / April 2019 Supreme Court jurisprudence as new and applicable when resolving an actual mortgage-interest dispute. That decision is a practical comparator, not proof that LPB was procedurally identical or entitled to the same result.

What the Aweswell record establishes: STS 112/2019 was not merely available in the abstract. It was placed directly into the Cuatrecasas client / financing record, its asserted economic consequence was explained, and it was supplied again while the firm remained engaged on the financing and security architecture. What this does not establish by itself: breach, negligence, causation, loss, deliberate conduct or the merits of the Edgeworth claim.

What the UK judgment establishes—and what it does not

The official judgment identifies BL-2024-000237 / [2025] EWHC 1014 (Ch), the pleaded allegation and denial, and the procedural decision. It does not determine negligence, breach, causation or loss on the merits.

Two independent proof chains

Edgeworth's evidence is not relied upon as evidence of the Sun Rock/Aweswell case. Separate records stay separate. No pattern or propensity inference. No shared intent, common plan, borrowed damages or institutional culpability is inferred from the existence of two disputes.

The UK legal route is not being pursued

Sun Rock/Aweswell is not pursuing UK legal proceedings. No UK court action is being pursued, and no UK firm is currently instructed for such proceedings. Any SRA consideration is a distinct, secondary and jurisdiction-dependent regulatory question.
