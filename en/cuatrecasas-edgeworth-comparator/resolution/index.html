THE THREE BOUNDARIES

What Edgeworth does not prove

It does not prove negligence, a pattern, shared facts, shared intent, equivalent damages or liability in the Sun Rock/Aweswell matter.

What must be examined here

Client and responsible entity, scope of retainer, material legal developments, advice and warnings, implementation and protection work, handover, causation and claimant-specific loss.

What remains open

A structured institutional discussion capable of narrowing or resolving the dispute while the documentary record and each party’s rights remain preserved.

2019 Supreme Court issue: the Sun Rock/Aweswell record may raise a potentially related 2019 Spanish Supreme Court question. No identity with the two decisions pleaded in Edgeworth—and no overlap in duty, use or causation—is asserted unless established from primary files.

Right of reply: Cuatrecasas, Edgeworth and any affected participant may identify an error, provide an omitted primary source or clarify procedural status. A verified correction will be incorporated with equivalent prominence. Non-response proves nothing.
