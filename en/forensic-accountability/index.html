05 / P0 EVIDENCE

Six questions that could change the story

Each has a finite original-document test. The result may corroborate, narrow or disprove the claimant’s theory.

P0.1Title and exploitation

Which finca-specific ownership, contracts, mandates and operating rights existed at each date?

Decisive evidence: Land Registry, deeds and CEXP/Owners’ Community agreements
P0.2RICPE suitability

What did Decree 224/2022 cover: Series F, Series G, both, or a later amendment?

Decisive evidence: File 02/2022, AEAT report 17 Nov 2022, annexes and amendments
P0.3Actual use of funding

Who received each drawdown, when, for what use and against which invoices?

Decisive evidence: Loan agreements, bank statements, invoices, works certificates and ledgers
P0.4Regional aid

Was the grant award actually paid; which eligible costs and employment commitments were verified?

Decisive evidence: GC/836/P06 individual file, payment orders and audits
P0.5Decision and competence

Which officeholder or statutory body signed or received each record and under what power or duty?

Decisive evidence: Decisions, signatures, delegations, delivery registers and minutes
P0.6Contrary findings

What do Sentencia 163/2023, appeals and access decisions actually hold?

Decisive evidence: Full judgments, appeal status, parties’ responses and administrative records
