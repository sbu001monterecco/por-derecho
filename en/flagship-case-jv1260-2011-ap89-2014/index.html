TEST CASE

The question is not merely who won the eviction claim.

The institutional question is which facts entered the court, how Monterecco entered litigation already underway, what each judicial level actually decided, and which propositions may later have been reused.

Documented

A real proceeding at two judicial levels

The file is identified as eviction-for-precario verbal proceedings 1260/2011 before JPI no. 4 Arrecife. The appeal proceeded as Roll 793/2012 and culminated in Judgment 89/2014 dated 13 January 2014.

Documented with limitation

The extension against Monterecco was claimant-driven

The 23 February 2012 pleading records that, after learning of a change in exploitation, the claimant side expanded the action against Monterecco Sun Park, S.L. The exact legal character of that step must be fixed from the complete pleading and the court order that handled it.

Keystone evidence

18 units against a hotel operated as one economic whole

The current extraction identifies an 18-unit claimant-side perimeter. The reconstruction task is to map each property, owner, 2008 obligation, participation or withdrawal from hotel exploitation, and litigation position.

Critical limit

88.96% does not mean universal authority

The judgment says the Comunidad de Explotación “was constituted 88.96% by the apartments” belonging to LPB. That wording does not equate to rights over the 18 units or a valid property-community majority at any later date.

TWO DECISIONS · ONE FILE

Por Derecho does not use the appeal judgment as a substitute for the first-instance record. The reconstruction must show which allegation, evidence and issue reached each court.

Level 2 · Provincial Court

Roll 793/2012 · Judgment 89/2014

Holding: the Court allowed the claimants' appeal, partially reversed the first-instance judgment and ordered the eviction of CEXP and Monterecco from the claimants' 18 units. It imposed first-instance costs on Monterecco and made no special appellate costs order.

Adverse ratio: it found that Monterecco possessed the apartments and keys, held the extension against it justified, and applied veil lifting because of the close CEXP–Monterecco–LPB relationship in this dispute.

Limit: it did not decide title or authority over the rest of the mixed-ownership complex, later Community governance, the 2018 events, Insolvency 36/2012 or criminal responsibility.

PARALLEL COMMAND

The case is analysed civilly and criminal-forensically at the same time.

No civil irregularity automatically becomes a crime; no procedural formality by itself closes a separate criminal question if evidence independently establishes representation, knowledge, use and consequence.

Track A · Civil / possession / procedure

Registered title · consent · exploitation · standing · basis of possession · effect of the February-2012 cession · admission of Monterecco · scope of each judgment.

Track B · Criminal / investigative / forensic

Act or representation → documentary reality → knowledge → material omission/manipulation if any → judicial understanding → decision → benefit/harm → causation → alternative innocent explanation.

Labels are used to investigate, not to accuse.Fraudulent contrivance, procedural fraud, procedural fraud offence or documentary falsity can only be advanced if the necessary elements are reconstructed actor by actor. The flagship case must also be capable of concluding that the hypothesis fails.

PROVISIONAL CONCLUSION

Flagship because it can change the reading of the wider system — in either direction.

If the file establishes ordinary litigation and a properly founded extension, that conclusion will be preserved. If it establishes a misleading procedural equivalence, a material omission or later use broader than what was decided, each link and actor will be traced separately.

Exit ruleThe objective is not to discredit a judgment because it was adverse. It is to reconstruct exactly what was requested, what was proved, what was decided, what was not decided, and how the decision was used afterwards.
