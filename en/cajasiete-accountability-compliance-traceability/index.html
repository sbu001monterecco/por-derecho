CajaSiete: who decided, what did the bank know, and what trace remains?

Three documentary layers — finance/security over Sun Park–MYND Yaiza, investment and access relationships around Orion, and handling of Communication 6 — now have to be reconciled as one governance, conflict, compliance and traceability question. No single layer proves crime or automatic liability; their convergence requires an independent, attributable review.

3. The institutional-liability question is finite and testable

No presumption of guilt is required. The test is whether CajaSiete's own financial/corporate exposure required a different independent review; whether inadmission prevented examination of facts relevant to the bank's own relationships; who took that decision; what each function knew at each date; and what the bank did after notice.

DecisionWho classified, proposed and approved the 28 April inadmission and 18 September review/reaffirmation?

4. The law requires an owner of responsibility

LAW 2/2023 · ART. 8

System Officer

The governing body appoints a natural person responsible for the Internal Information System; if a collegiate body is used, one member must be delegated management and investigation powers. Appointments/removals are notified to the competent authority.

Consolidated BOE text

LAW 2/2023 · ART. 26

Information register

Obliged entities must keep a confidential register of reports and resulting internal investigations. It is not public; the public question here is decision ownership and traceability, not indiscriminate access to protected data.

LAW 2/2023 · ART. 32

Data, access and retention

The statute limits access and provides for deletion/anonymisation under conditions. Deleting personal/report data and erasing responsibility traceability are not the same proposition.

5. Escalation to identifiable governance

CajaSiete's own published composition allows the issue to be directed to identifiable governance and control functions without assuming that any listed person participated in the historic events or channel decision.

Internal competence published by the bank itself. The Audit & Risk Commission regulation assigns the Commission regulatory-compliance oversight, investigation of irregular conduct and third-party complaints and, expressly, responsibility for the Ethics Channel and decisions on files arising from valid reports that have undergone investigation. It provides for technical support in the investigation phase from the Ethics & Conduct Committee and Internal Audit. Communication 6 can therefore be escalated to an identifiable, auditable governance function; a generic channel signature does not exhaust that accountability architecture.

6. Actions requested from the Board, Audit & Risk, Compliance and Internal Audit

  1. Governing Board: confirm receipt and assign an institutional owner for this review, insulated from relevant commercial exposure.
  2. Audit & Risk Commission: open a governance review of Communication 6 and the 18 September response, recording conclusions and remedial measures in minutes.
  3. System Officer / Compliance: confirm whether the RSII is an individual or collegiate body; confirm appointment notification to the competent authority; identify at least the function/person with decision responsibility or state the specific legal basis for withholding identity.
  4. Conflicts: document what conflict check occurred before inadmission and whether recusal/reassignment was considered under the 10 December 2025 assurance.
  5. Internal Audit: verify the audit trail for receipt, access, classification, assessment, decision, communications, retention, anonymisation and any deletion.
  6. Risk: reconstruct relevant CajaSiete exposures concerning Sun Park/HNT/MYND, Orion and related perimeters by instrument, date, borrower, collateral, valuation, approver, related parties, drawdown and repayment.
  7. Legal: identify the internal procedure/version applied to Communication 6 and the basis for each inadmission ground and any destruction/anonymisation.
  8. Preservation: lawfully retain metadata, logs, minutes, conflict checks, reassignments, decisions and cross-references; this does not ask the bank to retain data whose deletion is legally mandatory.
  9. Response: issue one attributable, documented response distinguishing facts, confidentiality limits, decisions and open points.
