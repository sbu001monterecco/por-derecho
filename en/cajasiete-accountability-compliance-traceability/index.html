3B. Four lawful exits: every one needs a record

EXIT 1

The controls worked

CajaSiete can document what it verified, who verified it, what conflict was ruled out, which body decided and why. That outcome should be reflected faithfully in the public record.

EXIT 2

It received incomplete or incorrect information

Identify what was missing or wrong, when it was detected, its impact and what correction/escalation follows. That does not automatically make the bank responsible for information it received.

EXIT 3

Routing or control failed

Identify why information did not reach the proper function, preserve the trail and correct the process. A routing failure does not by itself establish intent.

EXIT 4

No further action is required

CajaSiete can take that position, but should identify the competent function, the basis applied and the conflict control supporting it. A generic signature does not replace that explanation.

Control design, documentation and correction. No function is asked to accuse another. Each function records only what it did, what it received, what conflict it assessed and what it escalated. Compatible answers reinforce each other; incompatible answers become objectively testable audit or supervisory questions.

4. Law 2/2023 requires a System Officer and an identifiable management architecture

LAW 2/2023 · ART. 8

System Officer

The governing body appoints a natural person responsible for the Internal Information System; if a collegiate body is used, one member must be delegated management and investigation powers. Appointments/removals are notified to the competent authority.

Consolidated BOE text

LAW 2/2023 · ART. 26

Information register

Obliged entities must keep a confidential register of reports and resulting internal investigations. It is not public; the public question here is decision ownership and traceability, not indiscriminate access to protected data.

LAW 2/2023 · ART. 32

Data, access and retention

The statute limits access and provides for deletion/anonymisation under conditions. Deleting personal/report data and erasing responsibility traceability are not the same proposition.

The bank says a System Officer exists

CajaSiete states publicly that it has appointed a Responsable del Sistema Interno de Información and that its channel managers include the Technical Criminal Compliance Unit.

5. Escalation to identifiable governance and control functions

CajaSiete's own published composition allows the issue to be directed to identifiable governance and control functions without assuming that any listed person participated in the historic events or channel decision.

Internal competence published by the bank itself. The Audit & Risk Commission regulation assigns the Commission regulatory-compliance oversight, investigation of irregular conduct and third-party complaints and, expressly, responsibility for the Ethics Channel and decisions on files arising from valid reports that have undergone investigation. It provides for technical support in the investigation phase from the Ethics & Conduct Committee and Internal Audit. Communication 6 can therefore be escalated to an identifiable, auditable governance function; a generic channel signature does not exhaust that accountability architecture.
