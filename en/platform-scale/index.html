The scale proposition

Two operating hotel positions create a different financial relationship.

The public model deliberately separates future hotel operations from damages and contingent recovery rights. It asks a narrower question: if a new approximately 300-unit five-star hotel reaches stabilised operation and Sun Rock lawfully recovers sufficient control or economic rights in the existing Sun Park / MYND Yaiza perimeter to consolidate its operating economics, what scale could the resulting hospitality platform represent?

Relationship architecture

One institutional relationship. Separate risks and pools of capital.

The scale thesis is stronger when the structure prevents the relationship from becoming a blended balance sheet. Family wealth, Aweswell as the legal Sponsor/HoldCo, Sun Rock as the commercial group/platform and each ProjectCo can create separate reasons for an institution to engage; none obliges the others.

Private wealthFamily / private wealth

Realised liquidity, custody, investment, lending and wealth planning. Unrealised recoveries are not current AUM.

HoldCo / SponsorAweswell Limited

Treasury, Sponsor capital, corporate finance and governance over rights actually controlled.

Group / platformSun Rock

Commercial hospitality and investment identity for strategy, origination, operating know-how, banking relationships and capital recycling. It is not presented as the legal parent or automatic borrower.

AssetsRing-fenced ProjectCos

Acquisition, development, debt and security underwritten hotel by hotel, avoiding unnecessary cross-collateralisation.

Underwriting rule: one relationship does not imply that every desk, vehicle or pool of capital will invest. Each mandate and asset retains its own approval, documentation, security and risk.

Why a long-term financial relationship becomes rational

One relationship. Several independently governed pools of capital.

The financial opportunity is larger than one acquisition loan. A two-hotel operating Sponsor can generate recurring requirements across corporate banking, wealth management, project finance, private capital, refinancing and future liquidity events. Each mandate remains legally separate and independently underwritten.

Corporate banking

Operating wallet

Accounts, treasury, payments, payroll, merchant acquiring, guarantees, working capital, FX and day-to-day banking for HoldCo and ProjectCos.

Project capital

Asset-by-asset underwriting

Sponsor equity, co-investment, preferred capital, acquisition finance, development finance, construction debt and stabilised refinancing.

Private banking & wealth

Future liquidity destination

Distributions, refinancings, disposals and realised recoveries can create investable family liquidity requiring custody, diversified investment and long-term wealth planning.

Real estate

Repeated transaction flow

Valuation, debt, equity, operator selection, capex, asset management and eventual recapitalisation or sale across individually ring-fenced hotels.

Advisory

Complexity made governable

Corporate structure, M&A, capital markets, tax, succession and cross-border execution become recurring needs as the platform matures.

Next assets

Capital recycling

Retained operating cash flow and realised liquidity can become Sponsor capital for additional hospitality opportunities rather than ending with one completed hotel.

Early private capital

Future scale explains the opportunity. Present protections must still carry the risk.

A smaller early-stage private loan or structured-capital instrument may be economically modest relative to the intended platform, but it must never be sold on future scale alone. The investor or lender must be able to understand the current issuer, use of funds, repayment route, covenants, controlled accounts and security actually granted.

What exists today

Aweswell Limited as UK HoldCo / Sponsor; active Montaña Roja transaction work; a wider hospitality origination programme; live banking and capital dialogues; and a separately pursued recovery perimeter. None of that is automatically equivalent to perfected security.

What future scale can add

More operating cash flow, a deeper refinancing market, larger Sponsor equity, recurring bankable activity and future liquidity events. That context can make early capital commercially intelligible without pretending it is present collateral.

Publication boundary

The platform thesis is stronger when the uncertainties stay visible.

Sun Rock does not need to inflate the present to show a meaningful future. The discipline is to separate current facts, future scenarios and contingent recoveries so that a sophisticated reader can see both the opportunity and the work still required.

This page does not state: that Montaña Roja has been acquired; that approximately 300 units are approved; that Sun Park / MYND Yaiza is presently controlled by Sun Rock; that €125–200m is today's balance sheet or NAV; that €40–58m revenue or €10–18m EBITDA is forecast or guaranteed; that any damages claim has been recognised as an asset; that any bank, investor or lender has committed capital; or that future platform scale is current collateral.
