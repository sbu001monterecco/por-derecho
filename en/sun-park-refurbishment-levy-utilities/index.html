FIVE TRACKS · 2008–PRESENT · PRESENT→ORIGIN AND ORIGIN→PRESENT

The 2022 adjudication did not turn the past into a blank page.

This reconstruction contrasts an asserted clean start after adjudication with the pre-title record of control, project activity, works and fragmented ownership; places beside it the Community, licence, tourism, finance and opening processes that moved in parallel; and asks what each decision-maker actually verified.

Active forensic investigation · not a finding of guilt

POR DERECHO ALLEGATION · NOT A JUDICIAL FINDING

Por Derecho says the Acosta Matos perimeter did not arrive in 2022 at a blank asset, but at a material, commercial and project position created before formal title. The hypothesis is that 2022 processes may have formalised or normalised that position. It must be proved or rejected act by act; a coordinated criminal operation is not presumed.

Reading rule. The current result does not prove its own origin. A licence does not manufacture title; a Community minute does not authorise another owner's property; a later deed does not automatically ratify earlier works; and concurrent proceedings do not establish unlawful agreement.

Two accounts that cannot substitute for one another

ACCOUNT TO TEST

The strongest clean-start case

CAM may argue that a defined perimeter was received through court order, deed and registration; earlier activity was security, survey, maintenance, pilot or contingent planning; the final project dates from January 2022; and each later permission, funding instrument and right had an independent legal basis.

Evidence required: the source, date, asset, consent, independent process and third-party protection for each proposition.

Licence, title and Community authority are different questions

Municipal licence

Who applied, in what capacity, with what project, units and title? A licence does not determine private rights by itself.

Community authority

Which meeting, notice, vote, majority, conflict and absent-owner process approved structure, façade, common elements or expenditure?

Property-by-property title

Who owned and could consent on the exact date? LPB's insolvency did not automatically include Matkator or third parties.

Works actually performed

Survey, pilot, maintenance, internal refurbishment, demolition, structure, façade or full project? Compare plan to as-built condition.

An approximately €4.467 million levy should leave a trace

Resolution and budget are not final cost. The required chain is:

resolution → budget → allocation → owner demand → collection/advance → contract → invoice → IGIC → certificate → payment → variation → final account → insolvency treatment → later funding or aid use.

The approximately €3,260,416.81 attributed to LPB must be read with title, voting, conflict, accounting and estate issues. The present absence of a complete reconciliation is a production gap, not proof of misappropriation or falsity.

Criminal relevance: the threshold still to be proved

The hypothesis is whether the 2022 convergence was lawful independent regularisation or conscious formalisation of an earlier position lacking sufficient authority. The criminal threshold requires, actor by actor:

objective act → exact false or unlawful proposition → knowledge/intent → use and recipient → error or dependency → benefit → loss → causation.

Not proved on this page: criminal sanitisation, agreement, knowing falsity, procedural fraud, necessary cooperation, public-office wrongdoing or collective guilt.

What the strongest defence may prove

The defence may produce valid Community authority, materially distinct projects, lawful internal works, urgent preservation need, independent public files, complete accounting, correctly allocated utilities, reasonable due diligence, autonomous later rights and third-party protection.

Por Derecho's answer must be document, date, property, vote, invoice, payment, knowledge and consequence—not adjectives.
