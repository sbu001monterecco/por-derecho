ONE CAUSAL SPINE · FIVE EVIDENCE LANES · 2008–PRESENT

Do not separate the refurbishment from the 7 June 2018 takeover.

Work planning, pilot rooms, demolition, works, licences, the Community levy and opening must not be analysed as self-contained civil episodes. They belong in one reconstruction: living hotel and funded exit → prior preparation → force-based control of access → exclusion and interruption → deterioration and physical transformation → project and investors before title → 2022 formalisation → current operation and benefit.

Active forensic investigation · not a finding of guilt

POR DERECHO'S CENTRAL ALLEGATION · NOT A JUDICIAL FINDING

Por Derecho alleges that the 7 June 2018 material takeover shifted practical control of the hotel, frustrated or impaired a live but conditional ONA/Lagune/funded exit, damaged LPB's active estate and the productive hotel unit, and enabled later transformation, commercialisation and formalisation for the Acosta Matos/HNT/MYND project. Each act, state of knowledge, loss and causal link must be proved actor by actor.

Anti-atomisation rule. Property-by-property proof remains mandatory because it identifies owner, authority, victim, loss and remedy. But it cannot be used to break economic and criminal-forensic causation. A licence, invoice, Community minute or apparent basis within one unit does not by itself explain how access was obtained, which operation was displaced, which project was prepared or how the act was later used.

7 June 2018: the central rupture

What the controlled record contains

Force on the coded access or locks, chains/padlocks or replacement cylinders, uniformed control of entry and continuing exclusion of the onsite responsible person appear in testimony and contemporary communications. No order granting CAM possession of the whole mixed-ownership hotel has been located.

Why this is not a locksmith dispute

Control of entrances determines who can operate, maintain, inspect, show, refurbish, contract and finance a hotel. The question is whether limited security authority became practical control over LPB, extra-insolvency property, guests, staff, information and the exit platform.

The provisional 2018 criminal dismissal is an adverse procedural outcome that must remain visible. It did not create whole-hotel CAM title or determine the lawfulness of every later work, project, representation or consequence.

The works: a possible physical execution phase of the alleged takeover

It is not enough to ask whether one window, bathroom or room could be refurbished in civil law. Every work package requires simultaneous reconstruction of:

Local right

Property/common element, owner, possession, Community vote, project, licence, contractor, invoice, payer, date and as-built condition.

Source of control

Who supplied access and keys, under what authority, from when, who was excluded and whether the work was possible without the 7 June rupture.

Unitary function

Real preservation, independent unit work, or part of physically/commercially replacing the ONA/Aweswell platform with Lava Verde, Club Sei, RICPE and MYND.

Downstream effect

How it changed configuration, inventory, value, appearance of availability, costs, levy, finance, aid, licensing, opening or difficulty of restitution.

There is no analytical presumption of neutrality. The labels “security”, “maintenance”, “pilot”, “internal work” or “refurbishment” do not decide function. Nor is every work declared criminal: lawful independence or criminal function must be proved at the relevant node.

Active estate and productive unit: different but causally inseparable

LPB's active estate

It comprises LPB assets and rights within Insolvency 36/2012. The inquiry must measure lost possession or income, deterioration, works/costs, reduced value, lost causes of action and frustration of a better realisation or exit.

The productive hotel unit

Sun Park functioned economically through LPB, Matkator and other owners' assets, common infrastructure, operation, guests, staff, data, goodwill and commercial relations. The insolvency estate did not own everything, but capture or destruction of the platform could damage the estate and extra-insolvency rights simultaneously.

Do not use the insolvency boundary to atomise the economic event. Do not use economic unity to erase separate title.

From pre-title transformation to 2022 formalisation

A later deed, registration, licence, Community minute, tax approval, financing or opening may create prospective legal effects. It does not automatically prove that earlier access, works, hotel availability, costs or representations were lawful. Each 2022 process must identify which fact it independently checked and which it inherited from another file.

The approximately €4.467m levy—including approximately €3,260,416.81 attributed to LPB—must be followed from resolution and allocation through contract, invoice, IGIC, certification, payment, final account, insolvency treatment and later use in financing or aid.

Criminal threshold and boundaries

For every person or entity the reconstruction must prove:

objective conduct → authority invoked → knowledge of title/insolvency/ONA → representation or omission → recipient and dependency → control or benefit → loss → causation → contrary explanation.

Only then, where supported, may the inquiry examine unauthorised dispossession/control, damage, impairment of the active estate and productive unit, frustration of rescue or exit, punishable insolvency, disloyal administration, procedural deception, documentary falsity, knowing facilitation or coordination.

Not established on this page: a proved criminal takeover, collective guilt, certain ONA closing, quantified destruction of the entire estate or criminal knowledge by an institution merely because it received or formalised documents.
