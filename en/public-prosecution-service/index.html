Canonical institutional register · control 2 September 2026

Public Prosecution Service: one institution, separate offices, separate files, separate events.

This is the canonical entry point for reconstructing what was filed, to which office, which external reference it received, which prosecution file was opened or cited, and which reply, decree, referral, notice or acknowledgement came back. A Por Derecho ID is never replaced by the REGAGE/RedSARA number: both are preserved and linked.

Hierarchy

From state level to the area prosecution office

The generic Spanish Ministerio Fiscal retains PD-SP-I-0002. Central offices, the Canary Islands autonomous-community prosecution office, provincial offices and the Arrecife area office retain their own immutable PD-SP-I identities. The European Public Prosecutor's Office is displayed as a separate supranational branch.

Numbering

Our ID and the official ID are different layers

PD-SP-R identifies a prosecution file in the canonical register; PD-SP-EVT identifies each filing or act; REGAGE…, DI, DIP, EG, ST and NIG remain external official references. The ^ marker remains reserved for confirmed identities, not every document transport.

Responses

Every located act keeps its own event ID

A decree, notice, acknowledgement and email transport are not collapsed. Where the source supports it, each retains a unique PD-SP-EVT and is linked to its prosecution file. Where a source is missing, the gap stays open: no response is invented.
