MEDIA · VERIFICATION → DOCUMENT RECEIVED → DECISION NOT TO PUBLISH → TRACEABILITY

elEconomista was preparing a Sun Park publication. Javier Romera received a court order and said that, “with this”, they could not publish it. Who sent what, what was said about its effect, and what was verified?

The direct email establishes an editorial decision linked to a document received. It does not by itself establish pressure, censorship, the sender’s identity, or whether any legal interpretation communicated was correct or incorrect. This page asks for the native-source chain.

What can presently be supported

16–17 January 2025

Journalistic material concerning Sun Park was supplied to Javier Romera. The preserved exchange indicates that before publication he wanted to review judicial decisions and have the relevant points properly verified.

20 January 2025

Romera wrote that “we have been sent this order”, concerning the insolvency classification and a disqualification, and that “with this we cannot publish it”. This proves receipt of a document and the communicated decision; the email itself does not identify the sender.

Three voice notes

The documentary record identifies three WhatsApp audio files associated with CAM, Meeting Point and non-publication. They still require native preservation, hashing, controlled transcription and authentication before their contents can be attributed as fact.

Statements attributed to third parties

Contemporaneous summaries attribute certain statements to Laura Patricia Acosta Matos and the Meeting Point environment. They are verification material, not authenticated written statements by those interlocutors.

10 February 2021 precedent

elEconomista published an article by José L. Jiménez —not Javier Romera— about Canary capital, RIC and hotel renovation. It stated that RIC Equity had investment authorisations in operations developed by Acosta Matos and carried comments from Enrique Guerra. This is prior coverage context, not proof of knowledge of the 2025 events.

Discipline: contact with a newsroom ≠ improper influence. Sending a ruling ≠ falsehood. A decision not to publish ≠ censorship. What can be verified is the documentary sequence; legal and editorial assessments require the complete sources.

The core question

Who sent elEconomista which judicial decision, from what account and in what capacity; what statement accompanied it about finality, scope and effect; what did the newsroom verify; and what verifiable fact explains the ultimate decision not to publish?

OPEN MESSAGE

To Javier Romera, elEconomista director Amador G. Ayora, and Editorial Ecoprensa, S.A.

Javier Romera was editor-in-chief during the January 2025 exchange and was later appointed deputy director. We treat him here as a journalist and limited custodian of communications he directly perceived, not as an expert or as a route to unrelated confidential sources.

We do not seek general internal editorial deliberations or confidential sources. We seek authentication of specific external communications, records received, metadata, and factual correction of this chronology if incomplete.

Eight finite questions

  1. Who contacted Javier Romera or elEconomista between 14 and 21 January 2025 about the Sun Park publication, and in what stated capacity?
  2. Was the request to wait, not publish temporarily, not publish permanently, correct the proposed article, or simply verify it?
  3. Who sent the judicial order referred to in the 20 January email, from what account, with what message, headers and attachments?
  4. What, if anything, was said about the order’s finality, appeals and effect on ownership, control or legitimacy concerning Sun Park?
  5. What verification did the newspaper undertake regarding finality, appeals and legal scope before deciding not to publish?
  6. What version did the newspaper receive concerning CAM/Meeting Point and any commercialisation of the hotel?
  7. Are the messages, emails, call records, three identified voice notes, contemporaneous notes, attachments and necessary metadata preserved?
  8. Does Javier Romera or Editorial Ecoprensa wish to correct, complete or contextualise this chronology publicly?

What this page does not allege

We do not state that Laura Patricia Acosta Matos sent the order until the native transmission proves that point. We do not allege coercion or censorship. We do not claim elEconomista was obliged to publish. We do not turn a later complaint by this side into a judicial finding. Primary evidence that contradicts this account should result in correction.

Outstanding source work

Key gaps include the original transmission of the order with headers and attachments; authentication of the three voice notes; the certified judicial version and its finality/appeal status at the relevant time; and the final status of later proceedings concerning disclosure of that order.

Right of reply

We expressly invite Javier Romera, elEconomista and Editorial Ecoprensa to provide a documentary correction. An ordinary editorial explanation —for example, that the proposed story no longer met the newspaper’s verification standard after receipt of the order— belongs here with the same prominence as the question.
