Cabildo de Lanzarote: what did “all accommodation units” mean in the file?

A tourism-operation file can show what documentation was used to describe the operating unit. It does not by itself alter ownership, real rights or title to individual units.

Defined files

The 13 August 2026 request in file 614/2026 seeks implementation of Decree 2026-2735 and access to files 3671/2022, 498/2020, 547/2020 and 21219/2021, with priority for 3671/2022.

Core question

When Tourism Planning dealt with “all accommodation units”, which list of fincas/units was before it, who asserted title or authority over them, what verification was performed, and how were Matkator or other third-party units treated?

  1. Complete files with annexes and metadata.
  2. Unit/finca lists and plans.
  3. Powers, community minutes or title documents relied on.
  4. Internal technical/legal reports.
  5. Communications with Yaiza and the Canary Government.
  6. Final decisions, conditions and reservations.

Rule: unitary tourism operation and ownership are distinct legal categories.
