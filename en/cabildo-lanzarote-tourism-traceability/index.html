Cabildo de Lanzarote: from the “all units” request to HNT/MYND

The recovered April 2026 disclosure now permits a source-based reconstruction of the 2022–2023 transition. Tourism records show what was requested, supplied and registered; they do not by themselves alter or conclusively establish title to every independent finca.

What changed in the evidence position

Cabildo supplied 3671/2022 in April 2026 as part of a six-ZIP production together with 2984/2023, 17070/2023, 19995/2023, 7206/2024 and 4887/2025. The 17 September 2026 Decree 6999 separately delivered 498/2020, 547/2020 and 21219/2021. Accordingly, the current question is not whether 3671 was ever supplied, but whether the institutional record is complete, traceable and current.

3671/2022 · “all accommodation units”

Initial scope

The Sun Park Owners' Community sought restoration of the tourism record to the historic 220 units / 660 places. Cabildo recorded the then-RGT position as one star, 159 units / 477 places, with the Community as exploitation holder.

Authority and title requirement

Cabildo's March 2022 requirement expressly distinguished authority for common-area reform/conservation works from authority for the tourism-unit modification. It required applicant authority, an updated unit/finca plan and list, deeds for own units, and owner-issued enabling title for the remaining units.

August submission

The applicant later asserted enabling titles for 206 units / 618 places and supplied a matrix, titles and owner authorisations. Those are applicant-supplied documentary representations unless a later Cabildo act establishes the extent of independent verification.

Yaiza's mandatory report

Cabildo sought Yaiza's prior municipal compatibility report. Yaiza's definitive Exp. 5944/2022 report concluded that the proposed one-star / 220-unit / 660-place configuration did not comply with the then-current Plan General: AB8 required hotel use of at least three stars and listed 318 places.

Open issue: the presently accessible extracted set does not establish the final Cabildo disposition after that adverse municipal input. The result must not be inferred as either an approval or refusal.

2984/2023 → 17070/2023: two materially different steps

February 2023 · 2984/2023

HNT material sought MYND Yaiza as a four-star, 220-unit / 660-place hotel and requested holder/name changes. Cabildo responded that the registered position remained Sun Park, one star, 159 units / 477 places, Community as exploitation holder, and required property title plus—if pursued—a provisional four-star classification request.

No subsequent compliance filing or final act appears in the disclosed 2984 corpus. A formal withdrawal or final outcome is therefore not invented.

October 2023 · 17070/2023

The later file instead framed the change as holder/operator and commercial denomination while preserving one star, 159 units / 477 places. The executive summary instructed that places and category were not to be modified.

RGT inscription 2023-T15093, 18 October 2023, records Hotel New Trend, S.L. as holder and MYND Yaiza as commercial name for H-35-3-0000034. Cabildo then transmitted the registration evidence to Yaiza and to the applicant.

The December 2022 segregation deed: what it proves and what it does not

The deed used in the 2023 files records a segregation of a defined CAMSA economic unit to HNT and values the segregated patrimony at €10,213,382.82. Its property annex is a defined CAMSA schedule. It is not treated as a blanket statement that HNT owned every independent Sun Park finca.

The schedule has omissions at individual horizontal-division positions, including positions associated in the working unit/finca crosswalk with fincas 8584 and 8588. That is a documentary-scope observation only, not a current Registry conclusion. Fresh Registry evidence is required before stating the present 2026 ownership position, and 8584/8588 remain examples rather than the recovery denominator.

Connected administrative transformation

2020/2021 Cabildo baseline → 3671/2022 all-units request → 2984/2023 HNT/segregation change → 17070/2023 holder/name modification → 19995/2023 project material → 7206/2024 activity/works completion → 4887/2025 301-unit/four-star configuration.

The next reconstruction layer is the unit-by-unit crosswalk: Registry → Catastro → historic Sun Park number → owner → Community authority → Yaiza works/legalisation/occupation → Cabildo tourism inclusion → HNT/MYND operator/tourism status.

That crosswalk must remain evidence-led. Possession of information by one Cabildo department is not automatically attributed to every Cabildo official or unit.

17 September transparency position

Decree 2026-7000 is treated as a scope/transparency decision, not a merits finding about Sun Park. The intended Comisionado challenge accepts that transparency law need not make Cabildo conduct a criminal investigation, financial audit or bespoke forensic reconstruction; it focuses on the failure to sever and process identifiable requests for pre-existing Cabildo records.

Only one new reclamación is controlled for that decision. No separate substantive reclamación is presently controlled against Decree 6999 for 3671, because 3671 was already delivered in April.
