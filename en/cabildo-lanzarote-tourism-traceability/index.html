3671/2022 · “all accommodation units”

Initial scope

The Sun Park Owners' Community sought restoration of the tourism record to the historic 220 units / 660 places. Cabildo recorded the then-RGT position as one star, 159 units / 477 places, with the Community as exploitation holder.

Authority and title requirement

Cabildo's March 2022 requirement expressly distinguished authority for common-area reform/conservation works from authority for the tourism-unit modification. It required applicant authority, an updated unit/finca plan and list, deeds for own units and owner-issued enabling title for the remaining units.

August submission

The applicant later asserted enabling titles for 206 units / 618 places and supplied a matrix, titles and owner authorisations. Those remain applicant-supplied documentary representations unless a later Cabildo act establishes the extent of independent verification.

Yaiza report and direct HNT notice

Yaiza's definitive Exp. 5944/2022 report concluded that the proposed one-star / 220-unit / 660-place configuration did not comply with the then-current Plan General. Cabildo communicated the report and hearing step in March 2023. HNT electronically accessed its 27 March 2023 notification that same day.

Open issue: the complete six-ZIP corpus does not contain a later final merits resolution disposing of 3671. The file must therefore not be narrated as finally approved or finally refused on the present record.

The December 2022 segregation deed: what it proves and what it does not

The deed used in the 2023 files records a segregation of a defined CAMSA economic unit to HNT and values the segregated patrimony at €10,213,382.82. Its property annex is a defined CAMSA schedule. It is not treated as a blanket statement that HNT owned every independent Sun Park finca.

Fincas 8584 and 8588 remain examples within the wider unit-by-unit recovery perimeter. Any omission from the deed schedule is a documentary-scope observation, not a current Registry conclusion. Fresh Registry evidence is required before stating the present 2026 ownership position.
