GC-CIV-003 · DP 1041/2017 · CRIMINAL-FIRST · PRIMARY SOURCE

Who took control of LPB's procedural voice before the retracto route was discontinued?

This module is not a conviction. It presents a Gil Marer actor-specific criminal investigative hypothesis against a documentary test: what occurred, who intervened, what procedural effect followed, what potential private benefit must be investigated, and which evidence is still needed to confirm or refute intent, concert or abuse of authority.

19-Dec-2017LPB obtains an Auto requiring CAM to exhibit the 20-Oct-2017 credit-assignment deed and its economic terms.
18/23-Jan-2018CAM is cited/required; the LAJ records a positive SCNE result.
25-Jan-2018The AC/liquidator requests venia, communicates termination of LPB's professional relationship and instructs that no further filings be made without his express consent.
25-Feb-2018The later Decree records CAM filing 1222/18 as consenting/not opposing LPB's discontinuance.
5-Mar-2018Decree 000139/2018 records LPB discontinuance, art. 20.3 LEC dismissal and suspension of the 25-Apr hearing.

GIL MARER'S PUBLIC POSITION

Standalone first; unitary pattern second.

Gil Marer says the sequence should be investigated as a possible standalone criminal act: the insolvency administrator/liquidator's intervention in LPB's representation and control of filings inside a procedure LPB had obtained to force CAM to disclose the price, payment date and costs of a credit assignment, followed by a formal LPB discontinuance to which CAM consented and by termination of the diligence.

Gil also says the episode must not be fragmented. He places it within a before-during-after-concurso reconstruction and alleges that it may supply contextual evidence of a broader pattern of collaboration, enablement or instrumentalisation functionally favourable to the private perimeter he historically identifies as Molina / Montalanza-Molina → Acosta Matos. That continuity is an investigative thesis: contextual links do not replace proof of contact, knowledge, order, agreement, intent or individual benefit.

As to effect, Gil alleges LPB lost effective control of a route seeking economically sensitive information for its retracto while CAM — the party required to produce it — later consented to discontinuance. The present evidence permits that hypothesis to be formulated strongly; it does not yet permit a statement as proven fact that the AC personally filed the discontinuance, acted on the order of any named Acosta Matos person, or participated in a criminal concert.

What is proved

  • The diligence sought the 20-Oct-2017 deed, concrete price, payment date and costs.
  • LPB was applicant; Construcciones Acosta Matos, S.A. was respondent/required party.
  • CAM was cited and opposed the diligence.
  • The 25-Jan communication proves AC/liquidator intervention in LPB's professional representation relationship and control over further filings.
  • Decree 139/2018 proves that LPB requested discontinuance as a party, that CAM did not oppose/consented, and that the matter was dismissed.
  • The Decree suspended the then-pending 25-Apr-2018 hearing and was reviewable within five days.

What must not be invented

  • Who materially drafted, signed or filed LPB's discontinuance.
  • The exact authority or power basis relied on by the AC for replacement/procedural control.
  • What occurred on the originally scheduled 19-Feb date or why a 25-Apr hearing later existed.
  • Whether CAM had already supplied all or part of the deed/equivalent information.
  • The complete text of CAM filing 1222/18.
  • An order, pact, payment or coordination between the AC and any specific private individual.
  • Criminal intent, corruption, procedural fraud/insolvency fraud or another offence as a judicial conclusion.
  • Finality of the 5-Mar Decree.

Benefit and causation hypothesis to test

The potential benefit is not framed as “CAM automatically won”. It is framed falsifiably: if the economic information had not already been supplied through an equivalent route, discontinuance ended the judicial mechanism LPB had obtained to compel CAM to produce it. That consequence may be material to retracto, the real economics of the assignment and LPB's exit/recovery strategy. Investigation must establish what information was already available, what LPB could have obtained if the route continued, and who made the decisive decisions.

Actor separation. GC-CIV-003 identifies CAM at entity level as the opposing party and records its consent to discontinuance. This file by itself does not prove which person in the Molina/Montalanza-Molina/Acosta Matos perimeters instructed, knew of, participated in or personally benefited from the discontinuance. Each such attribution needs its own source.
