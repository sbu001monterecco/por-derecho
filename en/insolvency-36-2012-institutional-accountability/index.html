One causal reconstruction, not four accusations

ADMINISTRATOR → JUDICIAL DECISION → LAJ/COURT-OFFICE INSTRUMENT → NOTARY/REGISTRY/IMPLEMENTATION → CURRENT PATRIMONIAL EFFECT → REMEDY

Every arrow is a proposition that must be proved. Multiple actors appearing in one chronology does not establish unlawful coordination.

Objective: restore assets and rights where a lawful route exists; account for fruits and income; compensate proven harm where restoration is impossible; attribute responsibility only according to demonstrated causal contribution.
