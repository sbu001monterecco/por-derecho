Insolvency administrator: knowledge → duty → act → open question

Central role

The administrator appears throughout estate custody/realisation, court reporting, culpability and disputed 2018–22 transactions.

Primary judicial controls

The June 2018 order references suspension in the form proposed by the administrator; the October 2019 order records an unlinking/sale carried out by the administrator without prior judicial authorisation and refuses later validation.

Missing matrix

For each access, transfer, sale, dación or title representation: what power existed, what was known about LPB/Matkator/third parties, what was reported, and what protective or opposing act followed?

Fair defence. An insolvency administrator has statutory functions and management discretion within a court-supervised process. An error, disputed judgment or even an unauthorised act does not by itself prove corruption, collusion or intent to harm.

Las Palmas Provincial Court · Section 4

RPL 2523/2025

Culpability appeal

LexNET confirms proceedings before Section 4 and a September 2025 request for information/certification concerning the culpability section and participation by creditors/the prosecution service.

RPL 3304/2025

Administrator-removal appeal

A 9 June 2026 order scheduled study/vote/judgment for 7 July 2026 and identified the panel for that act. The scheduling document does not establish the later outcome.

RPL 3319/2025

Accumulated appeal

Order 223/2026 of 20 July 2026 accumulated RPL 3319/2025 with 3304/2025 for joint decision.

Fair defence. An appellate court is not an unlimited de novo investigation. Review depends on the appealed decision, the transmitted record and procedural rules for later evidence.
Appellate question: does each appeal contain the full material needed to decide its actual issue, and what procedural route exists for later facts that materially affect the premise under appeal?
