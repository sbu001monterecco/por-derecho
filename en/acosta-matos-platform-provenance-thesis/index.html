3. What investigating “100%” means

It does not mean mechanically adding asset values, loans, turnover, subsidies, RIC and EU funding. It means not excluding a benefit or substitution route merely because the connection is not a direct bank transfer.

Forward

Original right/asset/opportunity → displacement/control → pre-title commercial use → representation → operator/investor/professional → contract/finance → income/fee/equity/asset → reinvestment → present holder.

Backward

Later asset/relationship → actual decision inputs → track record/right/information/reputation used → authority at the time → causal contribution → independent lawful inputs.

A direct cash-transfer gap does not by itself answer opportunity, goodwill, reputation, data, access, mandate or financing-capacity questions. But chronology alone does not prove causation.
