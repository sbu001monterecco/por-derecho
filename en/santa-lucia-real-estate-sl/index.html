PROPERTY COMPANY · PRIETO–ANTONIO BRIDGE

SANTA LUCIA REAL ESTATE: the official corporate bridge between Prieto and Antonio

The official incorporation jointly records Juan Carlos Prieto Puente, Antonio Cogolludo Rojas and Atilio Alberto Maddonni Citara as several administrators. Changes in 2021 and 2025 date and limit their offices. The company does not itself prove a relationship with Sun Park, NOALPA, PAMALEXSHA or Esteban López Noriega.

Search variants: Santa Lucia Real Estate, S.L. · Santa Lucía Real Estate · Inversiones Santa Lucía. They do not replace the canonical identity.
