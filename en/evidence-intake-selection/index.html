LIBRARY · GOOGLE DRIVE · GMAIL · CUSTODY-FIRST SELECTION

What should be published in full, in part, as an excerpt—or withheld.

This register selects evidentially useful materials while preserving custody, privacy, privilege, version identity and source hierarchy. Native signed filings, receipts, contemporaneous communications and source-derived images are prioritised. Generated analyses remain context and never replace primary evidence.

Completeness boundary. The connected Library was enumerated and searched, Gmail attachments were reviewed selectively, and Google Drive was searched through its connected mount. Recursive Drive listing is not supported, so this is a prioritised legal-evidence selection—not a claim that every byte in Drive was read. Duplicate filename/size families remain one candidate family until hashing proves distinct variants.
