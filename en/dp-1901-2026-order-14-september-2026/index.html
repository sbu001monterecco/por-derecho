Contextual, interconnected critique bounded by the available evidence

The order must be read together with DIP 2/2026, the daily-reference-24 judicial complaint, the Control 21 private-actor complaint, RPL 3304/3319 and E.G. 745. The critique below identifies verifiable tensions and production questions; it does not convert them into findings of guilt.

1 · Proceeding identity: Ref. 21 → DP 1901 is contemporaneously corroborated; the later Ref. 24 association remains unexplained

The order says DP 1901 was opened for alleged judicial prevarication. The source-by-source reconstruction now also establishes that the 18 June judicial complaint and the 25 June private-actor complaint were autonomous filings; that the 25 June judicial supplement expressly belonged to daily reference no. 24; and that on 9 July private-actor material was physically tendered under DP 1901/2026, Plaza 6. The official 12 July direction then linked that same DP to DIP 2/2026. The collision is no longer a generic inference, but the initiating document, certified allocation trail and any joinder/reassignment/segregation act remain missing; the site records Ref. 21 → DP 1901 as the complainant’s firsthand chronology, contemporaneously corroborated by the 25 June procuradora email and the 9 July operational mapping; the official initiating/reparto event and later Ref. 24 association mechanism remain unproduced.

2 · Competence: if the object was judicial prevarication, the TSJ route needs an explanation

The LOPJ assigns the Civil and Criminal Chamber of the High Court of Justice the investigation and trial of criminal cases against judges and magistrates for offences committed in office in the autonomous community, unless jurisdiction belongs to the Supreme Court. If DP 1901 was in substance deciding an allegation against the Mercantile judge for judicial acts, the record should explain why an ordinary Investigation Section decided it or what competence/allocation treatment occurred. The question does not itself establish nullity.

3 · The appeal statement mixes personal identity with corporate appellants

The order says the decisions could have been appealed by “him” and that there is no record he did so. The controlled record shows appeals by Aweswell Limited and Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. —RPL 3319/2025 and RPL 3304/2025— against the removal decision. If the sentence refers only to Gil personally, its relevance to the criminal theory needs explanation; if it describes the status of the underlying orders, it needs reconciliation with the existing corporate appeals.

4 · “No minimum evidential basis”: we now know more about what was tendered, not what was actually examined

The order says the allegations lack a minimum objective/evidential basis. It is now documented that an autonomous judicial package existed, that it had a separate supplement, and that on 9 July private-actor material was tendered under DP 1901/Plaza 6. This makes it essential to distinguish physical tender from electronic incorporation, transmission to Fiscalía and actual review. The decisive question remains what documentation actually formed the DP 1901 file and what corpus reached the Fiscal before the 29 July report. Without a certified index and the prosecution report, the sufficiency assessment cannot be audited against the complete denominator.

5 · The 29 July prosecution report exists, but its reasoning remains unavailable

The order establishes three points: a Public Prosecution report exists, it is dated 29/07/2026, and it sought dismissal. Missing are the author, full text, assignment, source corpus, transmission index, any review/instruction and treatment of the DIP 2 appeal correction. Without those sources it is impossible to know whether the Fiscal understood DP 1901 as Control 21, as daily reference no. 24/DIP 2, or as a combined file.

6 · Final dismissal and evidential-insufficiency language: two planes worth separating

The order invokes Article 637.2 LECrim —the fact is not criminal— and orders final dismissal. At the same time its reasoning repeatedly stresses lack of a “minimum” objectively testable/evidential basis, language closer to the evidential-insufficiency plane that the LECrim distinguishes from final dismissal. The critique does not say Article 637.2 was necessarily unavailable; it asks for clearer reasoning separating legal non-criminality from insufficiency of the material before the court.

7 · The private-actor lane cannot silently disappear under judge-only reasoning

If DP 1901 contains Control 21 private-actor material, an order reasoned exclusively in terms of judicial prevarication does not show whether that lane was separated, referred, joined or left without its own disposition. The request is not to assume the order dismissed Control 21; it is to obtain the initiating document, full docket and allocation map before attributing that effect.

8 · Order date, notice date and remedy deadlines are separate facts

The order is dated 14 September and prints remedy routes/deadlines, but no deadline should be fixed from the decision date alone. The electronic service/access record is an independent source that should be preserved and controlled.

Boundary: these criticisms concern procedural identity, competence, evidential denominator, the appeal premise and clarity of the dismissal basis. They do not prove that the DP 1901 judge, the 29 July Fiscal, the Mercantile judge or any private actor acted criminally or in coordination.
