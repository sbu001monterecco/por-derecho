Joan Cruz Nuez^

Hotel executive with documented professional history at Meeting Point and HKI Hotels. His name appears in a Sun Park photographic package dated 1 March 2018. ORIGINAL CAPTURE · HKI HOTELS · 25 NOVEMBER 2019

The HKI Hotels website displayed “Sun Park (Lanzarote)” as a project.

original capture recoveredURL visiblevisible date 25/11/20192018 Wayback: open

Documented fact. The recovered original is titled Screenshot 2019-11-25 16.38.58.png. The address bar shows hkihotels.com/web/proyectos/en-lanzarote/; the “PROYECTOS” page lists “Hotel Alyssa Suite (Lanzarote)” and, immediately below, “Sun Park (Lanzarote)”. The capture's own taskbar shows 25/11/2019, 16:38.

These images are not a later reconstruction of the website. They were recovered as original screenshot files from the Project archive. The source presentation “Comercializacion Club Sie Lanzarote y Sun Park (1-2-fly y HKI 2018-2019)” was created on the same 25 November 2019 and preserves these captures.

What it proves — and what it does not. The capture establishes that, in the preserved website state on 25 November 2019, HKI Hotels publicly presented Sun Park (Lanzarote) within its projects section and the same website presented Joan Cruz within its team. It does not yet fix the first publication date, nor prove by itself a contract, mandate from LPB/Matkator/CEXP, insolvency or judicial authorisation, payment, investment, ownership or legality. Nor does it automatically backdate the listing to 1 March or 7 June 2018.

Investigative consequence: the evidence reduces the issue to finite, testable questions: when did Sun Park first appear on HKI Hotels; who commissioned or authorised the listing; what services were provided, to whom, under what documents; and how — if at all — does that connect with the 1 March event, the 7 June takeover and later operation/commercialisation?
