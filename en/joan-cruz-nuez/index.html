Hospitality executive with documented professional and corporate history across Meeting Point, LABRANDA and HKI Hotels, and later BLUESEA/Meeting Point integration work. The recovered evidence archive also contains original 25 November 2019 HKI Hotels website captures showing “Sun Park (Lanzarote)” under “PROYECTOS” and Joan Cruz on HKI Hotels' team page.

Central boundary: the relevance is continuity, not automatic attribution. Later corporate and commercial links make those questions more important; they do not answer them.

WHY THIS NODE MATTERS

The question is no longer a single encounter; it is a longer owner–operator–developer chronology.

The combined public record supports investigating Joan Cruz as a potential knowledge witness / continuity node because his Meeting Point/LABRANDA history overlaps a documented Acosta Matos hospitality relationship before 2018, the Sun Park evidence in March 2018, HKI's later public presentation of Sun Park, and his post-FTI return to Meeting Point work under BLUESEA.

ORIGINAL CAPTURE · HKI HOTELS · 25 NOVEMBER 2019

The HKI Hotels website displayed “Sun Park (Lanzarote)” as a project.

original capture recoveredURL visiblevisible date 25/11/20192018 Wayback: open

Documented fact. The recovered original is titled Screenshot 2019-11-25 16.38.58.png. The address bar shows hkihotels.com/web/proyectos/en-lanzarote/; the “PROYECTOS” page lists “Hotel Alyssa Suite (Lanzarote)” and, immediately below, “Sun Park (Lanzarote)”. The capture's own taskbar shows 25/11/2019, 16:38.

These images are not a later reconstruction of the website. They were recovered as original screenshot files from the Project archive. The source presentation “Comercializacion Club Sie Lanzarote y Sun Park (1-2-fly y HKI 2018-2019)” was created on the same 25 November 2019 and preserves these captures.

What it proves — and what it does not. The capture establishes that, in the preserved website state on 25 November 2019, HKI Hotels publicly presented Sun Park (Lanzarote) within its projects section and the same website presented Joan Cruz within its team. It does not yet fix the first publication date, nor prove by itself a contract, mandate from LPB/Matkator/CEXP, insolvency or judicial authorisation, payment, investment, ownership or legality. Nor does it automatically backdate the listing to 1 March or 7 June 2018.

Investigative consequence: when did Sun Park first appear on HKI Hotels; who commissioned or authorised the listing; what services were provided, to whom, under what documents; and how — if at all — does that connect with the 1 March event, the 7 June takeover and later Lava Verde/Club Sei operation or commercialisation?
