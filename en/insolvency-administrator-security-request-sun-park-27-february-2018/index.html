27 FEB 2018 · IA → COMMUNITY PRESIDENCY

Request to convene a meeting to consider hiring security

The communication records LPB’s insolvency administrator asking the Community president to convene a General Meeting to consider a security service. It is not a court order, Community resolution or proof that a later measure was approved or implemented within that scope.

Open public-safe PDF Open Insolvency corpus

What it proves

A direct request to convene a meeting, with the stated purpose of preventing access by unauthorised persons and preventing deterioration and improper use of common and private areas.

What it does not prove

It does not identify “unauthorised persons”, property-by-property scope, a valid resolution, contract, payer, operational instructions, lock changes, key-taking, possession, operation or criminal agreement.

Mandatory limit: proximity to 7 June makes the instruction chain a proper investigation target; it does not by itself prove that the sender ordered every later act.

Operative text — controlled English translation

Subject: NOTICE OF GENERAL MEETING — SUN PARK OWNERS’ COMMUNITY Date: Tuesday, 27 February 2018, 10:16:06 WET Dear Antonio, I am writing to you in your capacity as President of the Sun Park Owners’ Community and in my capacity as insolvency administrator of Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U., an entity which holds the majority of participation quotas in the said Community, asking you to convene a General Meeting for the purpose of adopting a resolution to hire a security service that prevents access to the complex by unauthorised persons and thereby prevents deterioration and improper use of the common and privately owned areas. I remain at your disposal to discuss any matter you consider appropriate. Yours faithfully, Borja Rodríguez-Batllori Laffitte Lawyer
