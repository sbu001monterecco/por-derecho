SUN PARK · 27 FEB 2018 · INSOLVENCY ADMINISTRATION → COMMUNITY PRESIDENCY

The insolvency administrator asked for a meeting to be convened to consider hiring security.

The document fixes a concrete institutional route before 7 June: request to the Community presidency, General Meeting and potential hiring resolution. It must be read literally. It is not, by itself, an order to take possession, change locks or exclude workers, guests or rights-holders.

Preventing access by “unauthorised persons” and preventing deterioration and improper use of common and privately owned areas.

It does not mention breaking locks, placing chains, replacing cylinders, taking keys or excluding any defined category of person.

Chain to complete

Notice, attendees, authority, quotas, votes, resolution, contract, payer, provider instructions and property-by-property implementation.

Controlled English translation of the operative text

Subject: NOTICE OF GENERAL MEETING — SUN PARK OWNERS’ COMMUNITY Date: Tuesday, 27 February 2018, 10:16:06 WET Dear Antonio, I am writing to you in your capacity as President of the Sun Park Owners’ Community and in my capacity as insolvency administrator of Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U., an entity which holds the majority of participation quotas in the said Community, asking you to convene a General Meeting for the purpose of adopting a resolution to hire a security service that prevents access to the complex by unauthorised persons and thereby prevents deterioration and improper use of the common and privately owned areas. I remain at your disposal to discuss any matter you consider appropriate. Yours faithfully, Borja Rodríguez-Batllori Laffitte Lawyer

The source’s statement concerning the majority of quotas is translated as part of the document and is not independently adopted as fact. The Spanish source transcript preserves the visible typographical forms and marks them with sic.

Why it matters—and why it must not be overstated

Link to private actors

The sender selected the Community presidency and a Community resolution as the route for hiring. Notice, attendee authority, voting, contract and provider instructions therefore become necessary documents in the implementation chain.

Link to 7 June

The communication predates the material access and security shift. Proximity makes comparison necessary, but does not by itself prove that every 7 June act was ordered or authorised by the sender.

Mandatory limit: a request to convene a meeting and consider security is not a general authority to appropriate the complex, affect non-estate assets, perform locksmith acts or exclude anyone without verifying title, mandate and perimeter.

Later contrast

Statement about the meeting

A later statement placed the hiring of security at the 18 May meeting, while also distinguishing that request from cylinder changes and chains, which it attributed to Community resolutions. The original notice, minutes and contract must test that account.

Implementation attribution

Material acts

Statements concerning broken locks, chains, replaced cylinders and exclusion of the on-site caretaker/manager belong to a different source and phase. Attribution must remain actor-specific.

Finite documentary production

  1. Native 27 February email/RFC822, complete headers and custody path.
  2. Notice, agenda and delivery records for the 18 May 2018 meeting.
  3. Owner list, quotas, debts, proxies, attendees, abstentions and votes.
  4. Minutes, audio, notes, drafts and certification of security resolutions.
  5. Quotes, contract, provider, payer, invoices, account charged and approvals.
  6. Operational instructions: authorised persons, properties, access points, keys, hours and escalation.
  7. Daily logs, incidents and communications with the insolvency administration and Guardia Civil.
  8. Records separating preventive security from locksmith work, possession and hotel operation.
