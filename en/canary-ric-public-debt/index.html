CANARY GOVERNMENT · TREASURY · RIC · RICPE · HNT / MYND YAIZA

Not zero connection. Not the same money either.

RIC-eligible Canary public debt and RICPE private finance are different routes for materialising the same statutory tax incentive. The first raises money for budgeted public investment; the second channels private capital into business projects. The Acosta Matos connection is documented in the RICPE→HNT route. It has not been established that proceeds from the 2024 or 2025 public bonds funded HNT, MYND Yaiza or any Acosta Matos entity.

Established connection The same RIC regime and the same public-policy purpose of converting tax reserves into investment located in the Canary Islands; the private route also documents €6,573,703.10 of Series F/G finance lent to HNT.
Unproved bridge The record contains no euro-by-euro allocation from Government bonds to HNT. The 2024–2025 chronology also cannot be the source of the 2022–2023 HNT loans.

WHY THE GOVERNMENT RAISES THESE FUNDS

One instrument performs two functions at once.

Investor function

Materialise a RIC allocation

A taxpayer that has allocated RIC needs a legally qualifying investment within the applicable time and conditions. The public bond provides one route. The offer was open to natural and legal persons; RIC eligibility is a feature of the security, not proof that every subscriber used it for tax purposes.

Government function

Finance public investment at a lower coupon

The Government receives principal, pays coupon and repays nominal. The 2024 AFI memorandum explains that the investor's financial-tax return can permit a lower coupon and “ease the Canary Islands' financing burden” while proceeds finance eligible public investment.

Public-policy function

Turn tax relief into territorial capital

The design links a private tax benefit to investment located in the Canary Islands. In the public route, the destination is public budgets; in the RICPE route, capital moves indirectly into qualifying private projects.

Direct answer: the Government is not raising money to “fund RICPE”. It borrows for public investment and uses the bond's RIC eligibility to reduce its financing cost. Demand above supply shows appetite for that asset; it does not reveal taxpayers' identities or the destination of their other investments.

The remaining hypothesis: an entity within the same perimeter could appear both as beneficiary/borrower in the private route and as subscriber or RIC originator in another route. That dual position is not presumed. It is tested by matching Series F/G registers, bond applications, NIFs, beneficial ownership, RIC returns and bank movements.

REVERSE-ENGINEERED REDIGEST · REPOSITORY + WEBSITE

What the Treasury production adds—and what remains separate.

Adds

2022 authorisation planning

The 2022 request contemplated loans, bonds and a possible RIC issue with term, nominal value, coupon and Article 27.4.D.4 use. This tranche does not prove that issue was executed, subscribed, settled or allocated.

Separates

Ordinary, private-placement and RIC debt

The 2022–2025 files include ordinary loans and private placements serving other purposes, including maturities and reimbursements. They must not be merged with public RIC-eligible issues.

Does not close

Identity and beneficiary flow

It provides no subscriber or beneficial-owner bridge to Acosta Matos and no Treasury source-and-use trail to HNT. The access production is also partial and announces later tranches.

Limited institutional overlaps: AFI is technical coordinator of the public issues; it does not appear as a RICPE/HNT actor in this corpus. Cecabank appears in the ordinary-debt universe and, in separate RICPE sources, as depositary. A shared provider or financial infrastructure does not prove a shared transaction, money flow, knowledge or responsibility.

REDACTIONS, ALLOCATION AND FALSIFIABLE PROOF

An absence of names is not converted into an absence of connection.

Dissociated subscribers

The production does not permit public reconstruction of the complete subscriber list, NIFs, allocations and beneficial ownership. That leaves the Acosta cross-match open; it does not confirm it.

2025: €80,000 / €100,000

The published Order fixes an initial minimum allocation of €80,000. An operational draw/allocation record refers to €100,000. This may be a drafting inconsistency or a later operational rule; version, authority, communication and actual application must be reconciled.

Staged access

The letter dated 28 August 2026 partially implements Resolution 154/2026: it delivers 2022–2025 files and announces successive production of remaining records after review and dissociation.

No-inference rule: a drafting difference, dissociated data, institutional coincidence or oversubscribed offer does not prove preferential treatment, connected subscription, diversion, double funding or criminality.

WHAT CAN PROVE OR REFUTE THE BRIDGE

The closure list is finite.

  1. Complete 2024/2025 application and allocation register, lawfully dissociated or produced to a competent authority.
  2. NIF/beneficial-owner cross-match against Acosta Matos persons and entities.
  3. RICPE Series F/G share register and subscription documents.
  4. RIC returns for each investment originator, preserving tax secrecy and proper process.
  5. RICPE→HNT contracts, bank accounts and drawdowns.
  6. HNT accounting and source-and-use by invoice, work, job and period.
  7. Complete suitability file: application, AEAT report, Decree 224/2022, annexes and amendments.
  8. GC/836/P06 file: declared other aid, eligible cost, payments, employment, verification and closure.
  9. 2025 reconciliation of the €80,000 published minimum allocation with the €100,000 operational reference.
  10. Remaining documents promised under the staged Resolution 154/2026 production.

Closure test: if NIF matching excludes the Acosta Matos perimeter, the possible public-subscription bridge closes. If there is a match, it opens only the next question: amount, capacity, beneficial owner, RIC origin, timing and presence or absence of any onward flow. A match by itself proves no irregularity.
