The full ACTÚA report changes the test: uniform 57.14% depreciation, occupation “unknown” and a mixed photographic record.

The primary source is now located: the first 46 pages of a 492-page source contain the ACTÚA valuation. Real deterioration is shown, but so are usable common areas, reception and people present. The forensic issue moves toward sample representativeness and uniform extrapolation to the whole complex.

Methodological correction: the public theory should no longer be “ACTÚA showed only negative Blocks 4–5 photographs.” The stronger question is whether the inspection material justified describing the whole complex categorically as poorly maintained/not renovated and applying the same 57.14% to the apartment stock, or whether additional representative evidence existed but has not yet been recovered.

What ACTÚA says about its own assignment

The report identifies Construcciones Acosta Matos S.A. as requester, describes the purpose as advice to determine the stated reasonable value, and treats the 262 registry properties as one functional unit. It describes the complex as not renovated and in poor conservation/maintenance, adopts 20 years of physical age and calculates replacement cost new of about €12.190m.

Its update method produces a real-estate NPV of about €10.143m, but ACTÚA selects net replacement cost as the final value: €9,776,003.13. The model includes about €1.585m initial adaptation investment and starts its first model year without operating revenue.

Safeguard: these differences and internal tensions are expert-analysis material. They do not by themselves prove fraud, intent or legal invalidity.
