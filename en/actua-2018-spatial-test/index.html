The confirmed architectural map gives exactly 44 apartments in Blocks 4 + 5: Block 4 = 16; Block 5 = 28. That coincidence with GESVALT's “≈44” makes those blocks a photo-geolocation priority. It does not prove that they were the 44 units meant by the expert and it conflicts numerically with the contemporaneous two-block/61-apartment statement.

Publication rule: Blocks 4–5 remain a high-priority spatial hypothesis. Proof requires photo → apartment → block → registry property → owner/controller → operating status at 15/01/2018.

What is documented about ACTÚA

GESVALT's critical expert report identifies ACTÚA file 00405-18-00 Z-0 and records that the visit/valuation was made at Acosta Matos's request to determine the stated reasonable value of Sun Park. GESVALT criticises purpose/methodology, hotel-rate inputs, uniform depreciation, the physical-condition description and land/reversion value. Its conclusions state that the valuation supplied by Acosta Matos was not suitable for adequately calculating the complex's market value and that no detailed property-by-property depreciation assessment had been made.

The Liquidation Plan used ACTÚA values: €9,776,003.13 for the whole complex; €6,607,551.87 for LPB's 159 apartments; €489,361.96 for 31 commercial/pool-solarium titles; and €7,096,913.83 in total for LPB real estate.

This is not a criminal finding. Project Sun Rock alleges a materially misleading representation. The narrower proved position is that a contemporaneous professional expert report identified concrete defects in purpose, inputs, depreciation and physical reality, and the ACTÚA value entered the CAM / Liquidation Plan pathway.
