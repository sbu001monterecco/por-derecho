PP 1041: the causation that can now be stated

C5 · end of Cristo's formal mandate

The 25 January 2018 communication documents that the insolvency administration ended Cristo's professional relationship with LPB, requested transfer of representation in PP 1041 and imposed an authorisation gate over future LPB action. No voluntary resignation has been located.

Withdrawal · attribution remains narrower

The 5 March decree proves LPB withdrew and the file recorded Borja as LPB's lawyer. It does not by itself prove who internally instructed, drafted, signed or filed the withdrawal.

The question is no longer “how did PP 1041 end?” That is documented. The question is who decided LPB should withdraw, why, under what authority, on what estate-interest analysis and with what economic effect.

Insolvency administration and Court: different proof thresholds

Insolvency administration

In PP 1041 there is direct documentary causation for the end of Cristo's formal LPB mandate. Moving from that fact to duty breach or liability requires the applicable power, estate interest, damage, economic causation and alternative explanation.

Commercial Court

The order with an 8 February 2018 body date —judge signature 9 February, LAJ signature 14 February and later 15 February label/notification— recognised CAM and rejected the litigious-credit theory, but also corrected amounts in LPB's favour. Comparing that route with the disclosure ordered by Court of First Instance No. 2 supports an intervention/deference asymmetry inquiry, not a presumption of improper bias.

Accountability ladder

1 · Repeated effect

Supported. The CAM perimeter repeatedly appears as beneficiary of material transitions.

2 · Specific representation causation

Supported in PP 1041. The insolvency administration directly caused the end of Cristo's formal mandate in that workstream.

3 · Possible duty breach

Investigable hypothesis. Requires duty, power, deviation from estate interest, damage and causation.

4 · Crime / influence / prevarication

Not a finding. Requires additional legal and subjective elements, actor by actor.

5 · Concerted action

Highest threshold. Requires communication or agreement; aligned outcomes are insufficient.
