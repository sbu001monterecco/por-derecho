Cuatrecasas and Sun Park: a documentary reconstruction of the professional lifecycle.

This public record reconstructs Cuatrecasas' involvement in the Sun Park / Aweswell / Luchy Playa Blanca / Matkator perimeter and separates documented facts from propositions that still require evidence. The central issue is not to turn a fee claim into an automatic accusation, but to understand the transition from adviser to the perimeter to enforcing creditor against Matkator.

Evidence discipline: inclusion here does not imply wrongdoing. Possible professional responsibility, judicial decisions, the debt, and any assignment of the auction award are separate planes. Privileged material stays outside the public layer; this page publishes traceability and verifiable questions.

Control question: was ETJ 163/2020 solely fee recovery, or could it, in context, affect an asset position that the prior mandate knew and had been engaged to protect?

The public record does not answer this conclusively. The answer requires the complete Cambiario 1048/2019 and ETJ 163/2020 files, billing/promissory-note ledger, conflict analysis, and the identity — or negative certification — of any assignee or final beneficiary.

AP4 / Control 24: separate plane, professional connection

The working dossier also raises whether a figure used as a third-party improvement threshold later operated as a firm economic premise. That issue primarily belongs to the judicial and insolvency record. The Cuatrecasas question is different: whether the firm identified, modelled, preserved and handed over the economic problem — recognised credit, mortgage liability, interest, threshold, cash/credit equivalence and result for the estate — within a rescue mandate.

This page does not assert that Cuatrecasas caused the court's reasoning, colluded with CAM/HNT/MYND/RIC, or that any connected third-party assignee has been proved.
