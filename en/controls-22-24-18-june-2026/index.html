Continuity rules

Do not degrade states

Prepared ≠ filed. Filed ≠ allocated. Allocated ≠ opened. Provisional dismissal ≠ final merits determination.

Do not merge people

An Administrator act is not attributed to the judge; a judicial ruling does not absorb the Administrator's factual inputs, figures, implementation or accounts; a shared beneficiary or source does not prove concert.

Primary source first

Stamps, receipts, signed rulings, NIG/IUP, certified testimony, deeds and accounting control over filenames, drafts, chat memory or internal summaries.

Thread continuity

A successor thread must load current main, canonical registers and the handover before modifying these identities. Unknown status cannot become certainty through repetition.

Unitary reading without collective attribution

Por Derecho treats Control 21 / DP 1901/2026 as the private-actor layer; Control 22 / DP 1956/2026 as the Insolvency Administrator's own-act layer; and Control 24 as the judicial-act/ruling layer.
