PwC, Grant Thornton and RSM/San Telmo: why their files matter to prosecutors without turning professional proximity into guilt.

The professional communications of 18–19 August 2026 do not replace earlier complaints and do not themselves prove coordination, information transfer or an offence. They identify something more concrete: independent custodians, matter files, systems, metadata and controls capable of confirming or disproving same-asset hypotheses.

Precision boundary. Providing services, introducing clients, retaining information, appearing in a file or maintaining a professional relationship does not prove wrongdoing. The prosecutorial relevance lies in identifiable and potentially perishable sources.

What is genuinely new

Any new communication must therefore identify exactly what emerged later and what additional measure follows.
