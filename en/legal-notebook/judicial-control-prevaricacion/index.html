Reading rule

The comparator opens the question. Only the Sun Park record can answer it.

A complaint against another judge does not make a separate allegation true. Its more limited value is that criminal scrutiny of judicial office in a hotel-control dispute is a legally cognisable reporting subject and follows a route distinct from CGPJ discipline. Serious analysis must then return immediately to each case's own evidence.

Does not prove

Guilt by proximity

No same judge, same parties, shared mechanism, common network or institutional pattern is asserted. No verified overlap supports such a conclusion.

Does permit

A testing method

Reconstruct knowledge, jurisdiction, available remedy, decision or omission, legal explanation and patrimonial effect before applying a criminal label.

Negative control

Losing is not prevaricación

Even intense disagreement is insufficient where a resolution remains within a legally defensible interpretation. Criminal injustice and the mental element require their own proof.

Attributed position: Gil Marer maintains that specified acts, omissions and decisions of Magistrate-Judge Alberto López Villarrubia, taken as a complete sequence, cross the threshold requiring criminal investigation. This is his formally advanced allegation under his responsibility; it is not a conviction or judicial finding of guilt.

Spanish Criminal Code · Articles 446-449

Four offences; four different evidential tests.

The record should not be forced into all of them. Classification must follow facts.

CP 446

Knowingly unjust resolution

Requires: resolution, injustice in the criminal-law sense and knowledge.

Sun Park: the allegation relies on prior knowledge, authority limits, alternatives and a sequence of effects.

Decisive gap: exclude a legally defensible interpretation and prove the knowledge-decision link.

CP 447

Gross negligence or inexcusable ignorance

Requires: a manifestly unjust resolution caused by that qualified degree of fault.

Sun Park: a possible alternative classification, not a substitute appeal.

Decisive gap: manifest injustice, seriousness of departure and causation.

CP 448

Unjustified refusal to adjudicate

Requires: a specific matter that had to be decided and refusal without legal cause.

Sun Park: only engaged by identifying application, duty and refusal.

Decisive gap: a general claim of insufficient protection does not suffice.

CP 449

Malicious delay

Requires: delay caused to secure an illegitimate purpose.

Sun Park: chronology may open the question.

Decisive gap: delay alone does not prove illegitimate purpose.

Correct route: under Article 73.3(b) LOPJ, a TSJ Civil and Criminal Chamber deals with specified criminal cases against judges and magistrates for acts committed in office within the Autonomous Community, unless the Supreme Court has jurisdiction. Articles 405-410 LOPJ address judicial criminal liability. CGPJ discipline does not determine these elements.
