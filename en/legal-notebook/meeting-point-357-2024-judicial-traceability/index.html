Documentary investigation · Meeting Point Spain · proceeding 357/2024

Two judicial names. One proceeding. An explanation still missing.

Archived Public Insolvency Register copies attribute a 24 October 2024 decision to Alberto López Villarrubia and an 18 February 2025 decision to Guillermo Fernández García, both in Meeting Point Spain proceeding 357/2024 before Las Palmas Commercial Court 3. The succession may have an ordinary administrative or judicial explanation. The register alone does not supply it. A separate investigation into Sun Park / Lava Verde / Club Sei now adds a second question: whether the debtor or its perimeter held material commercial records and how those records were preserved or disclosed.

Public Insolvency Register

The minimum chronology is fixed. The explanation is not.

Both copies were issued on 7 March 2025 and retain the same debtor, court and proceeding number. The “judge” field changes from one decision to the next.

  1. 18 SEP 2024

    Opening or initial notice

    Later notices refer to a decree of this date in the negotiations procedure under articles 583 and following. The signed decree and allocation record are requested.

  2. 24 OCT 2024

    Extension of effects

    A notice published on 29 October attributes to Alberto López Villarrubia the decision extending the effects of the negotiations notice. The register field does not by itself prove who personally signed the original.

  3. 18 FEB 2025

    Appointment of expert

    A notice published on 19 February attributes to Guillermo Fernández García the decision appointing Auren as restructuring expert.

  4. 7 MAR 2025

    Archived copies

    Both register notices were issued on the same date. The form itself warns that the register cannot guarantee the integrity of all data received from its source.

Inference control

What is observed is not evidence of irregularity.

It establishes

Successive register attribution

Two names appear on different acts under the same proceeding number, court and debtor over a five-month sequence.

It does not establish

Signature, reason or impropriety

It does not show who signed each original, or prove substitution, support, reassignment, data error, coordination or misconduct.

It can be resolved by

Orders and allocation trail

The signed originals, allocation register and any substitution, secondment or reassignment decision can close the question routinely.

Publication rule: “two judicial names appear successively” is a documentary observation. “Two judges acted improperly” would be a conclusion these notices do not support.

The commercial connection that changes documentary relevance

357/2024 may be a custody and disclosure node for Sun Park / Club Sei.

The relevance is no longer limited to the two “judge” fields. Acosta Matos publicly presents Hotel Lava Verde at C/ Janubio 3 as a 279-room project; Meeting Point Hotels/FTI announced Club Sei Lanzarote in 2019; a February 2020 capture offered it commercially in Playa Blanca; and a direct supplier source linked Sun Park to Lava Verde and confirmed physical pilot work before the 2022 LPB adjudication. The exact Meeting Point contracting entity remains unidentified.

Project

Lava Verde

CAM appears publicly as “owner” of the 279-room project. In 2021, however, CAM told the insolvency court it owned 54 apartments within a complex containing LPB and third-party units. The difference requires title/perimeter evidence, not an automatic conclusion of falsehood.

Brand / distribution

Club Sei / Meeting Point

Trade sources place Club Sei Lanzarote within Meeting Point Hotels/FTI. The contract, inventory, bookings, payments and specific legal entity remain unproduced. FTI also postponed Club Sei implementation in October 2020.

Why a public answer matters

Meeting Point is a traceability point, not a link in a chain of guilt.

The identities make an ordinary procedural explanation especially useful: Alberto López Villarrubia appears in Sun Park’s judicial history; Guillermo Fernández García delivered Inparsa Judgment 61/2025. That institutional crossover does not connect the merits and does not establish a common network.

Finite document request

The judicial question needs four documents; the Sun Park question needs a second package.

1

18 SEP 2024 decree

Complete signed copy, decision-maker and initial allocation record.

2

24 OCT 2024 decision

Signed original to test whether the register attribution matches the author of the act.

3

18 FEB 2025 decision

Original Auren appointment with signature, jurisdiction and procedural background.

4

Substitution or allocation decision

If there was a change of office-holder, support, secondment or register correction, its date and scope.

5

Article 583 notice and annexes

Debts, litigation, claims and contingencies, including any Sun Park/Club Sei reference.

6

Auren file

Acceptance, mandate, information requests, records received, report and workpaper index.

7

Contract and inventory

Meeting Point/FTI entity, contracts, allotments, PMS/channel-manager, OTAs, bookings, invoices and Sun Park/Lava Verde/Club Sei flows.

8

2024 preservation

What the FTI/Meeting Point perimeter received, who preserved/reviewed it and what was transferred into the Spanish proceeding or expert file.

Corporate precision: FTI Touristik, the German parent of the tourism group, must not be confused with FTI Consulting, an independent advisory firm. Shared initials do not evidence a relationship.
