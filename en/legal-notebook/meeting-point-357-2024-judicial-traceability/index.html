Documentary investigation · three Meeting Point debtors · proceeding 357/2024

Three debtors. One proceeding. A partly resolved judicial trail.

Signed Auto 97/2025 records a joint article 583 filing by Meeting Point Hotelmanagement (Canaries), S.L.U., Meeting Point Spain, S.L.U. and Meeting Point Investment, S.L.U. on 23 July 2024, deemed communicated on 31 July. It also records the 23 January 2025 request for Auren and appoints Auren on 18 February under Guillermo Fernández García's electronic signature. The earlier 24 October register attribution to Magistrate-Judge Alberto López Villarrubia, holder of the then Commercial Court No. 1 of Las Palmas de Gran Canaria, in an act of the then Commercial Court No. 3 still needs the signed act and allocation or substitution trail. A separate Sun Park / Lava Verde / Club Sei question concerns what commercial records any debtor held and how they were preserved or disclosed.

Inference control

A corrected chronology is not evidence of irregularity.

Established

Three debtors and signed Auren order

Auto 97/2025 fixes the joint applicants, initial dates, expert request and signed appointment.

Not established

24 October signature or impropriety

The register attribution does not prove who signed that original, why names differ, or any coordination or misconduct.

Still needed

24 October original and allocation trail

The signed act and any substitution, support, secondment, reassignment or correction record can resolve the remaining question routinely.

Publication rule: the signed 18 February order closes one gap; it does not convert the earlier register-name sequence into evidence of wrongdoing.

The commercial connection that changes documentary relevance

357/2024 may be a custody and disclosure node for Sun Park / Club Sei.

The relevance is no longer limited to two register fields. Acosta Matos publicly presents Hotel Lava Verde at C/ Janubio 3 as a 279-room project; Meeting Point Hotels/FTI announced Club Sei Lanzarote in 2019; consumer-facing captures offered it commercially; and a direct supplier source linked Sun Park to Lava Verde and confirmed physical pilot work before the 2022 LPB adjudication. The exact Meeting Point contracting entity remains unidentified.

Project

Lava Verde

CAM appears publicly as “owner” of the 279-room project. In 2021, however, CAM told the insolvency court it owned 54 apartments within a complex containing LPB and third-party units. The difference requires title/perimeter evidence, not an automatic conclusion of falsehood.

Brand / distribution

Club Sei / Meeting Point

Trade sources place Club Sei Lanzarote within Meeting Point Hotels/FTI. The contract, inventory, bookings, payments and specific legal entity remain unproduced. FTI also postponed Club Sei implementation in October 2020.

Why a public answer matters

Meeting Point is a traceability point, not a link in a chain of guilt.

The identities make an ordinary procedural explanation especially useful: Magistrate López Villarrubia appears in Sun Park’s judicial history; Guillermo Fernández García delivered Inparsa Judgment 61/2025. That institutional crossover does not connect the merits and does not establish a common network.
