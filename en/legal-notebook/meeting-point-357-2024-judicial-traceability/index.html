Inference control

What is observed is not evidence of irregularity.

It establishes

One signed later order; one earlier register notice

The February Auren appointment and its signer are verified from the court order. The October attribution is verified from a register notice.

It does not establish

Reason or impropriety

The record does not establish why different judicial names appear on different acts, or prove substitution, support, reassignment, data error, coordination, conflict or misconduct.

It can be resolved by

October original and allocation trail

The signed October decision, allocation register and any substitution, secondment, reassignment or register-correction decision can close the remaining question routinely.

Publication rule: “different judicial names appear on different acts” is a documentary observation. “A judicial conflict or misconduct occurred” would be a conclusion the current records do not support.

The commercial connection that changes documentary relevance

357/2024 may be a custody and disclosure node for Sun Park / Club Sei.

The relevance is no longer limited to the two “judge” fields. Acosta Matos publicly presents Hotel Lava Verde at C/ Janubio 3 as a 279-room project; Meeting Point Hotels/FTI announced Club Sei Lanzarote in 2019; a February 2020 capture offered it commercially in Playa Blanca; and a direct supplier source linked Sun Park to Lava Verde and confirmed physical pilot work before the 2022 LPB adjudication. The exact Meeting Point contracting entity remains unidentified.
