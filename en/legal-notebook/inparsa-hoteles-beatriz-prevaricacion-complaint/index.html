Legal analysis · Inparsa / Hoteles Beatriz · restructuring · prevaricación

When can an insolvency dispute become a criminal question?

Judgment 61/2025 approved a restructuring that changed control of Inparsa over the shareholders’ opposition. A CGPJ complaint followed and, according to La Razón, the Canary Islands High Court provisionally screened a prevaricación complaint against the judge. None of that establishes guilt. It requires us to distinguish a commercial disagreement, a procedural irregularity and a potentially criminally unjust decision.

Facts, accounts and limits

Four layers that must not be collapsed.

The force of this matter does not depend on adjectives. It depends on what the court decided, what each party alleges, what later procedural step has been reported and which primary record remains unavailable.

Judicial document

Approval of the plan

Judgment 61/2025 of 18 September 2025 approved the Blantyre/Meru plan. It describes support from 89.9% of affected financial debt, debt measures and a capitalisation that would allocate approximately 71% of the equity to the plan proponents.

Inparsa allegation

Solvency, maturity and valuation

Inparsa has publicly challenged insolvency, debt maturity, the valuation relied on, treatment of financial evidence and the protection it received. These are a party’s allegations, not findings in a criminal judgment.

Opposing position

Viability and creditor support

Blantyre has said that the debt had matured, Inparsa was insolvent, the valuation was valid and the plan was the viable alternative supported by the large majority of financial creditors.

Later reporting

CGPJ and criminal complaints

Media reported Inparsa’s CGPJ complaint. On 13 February 2026, La Razón reported provisional admission or screening of a prevaricación complaint by the TSJC. We have not located the primary TSJC order or a later public outcome that would justify describing a more advanced status.

Exact status: preliminary admission or screening asks whether there is a basis to investigate. It does not find that the judge made a knowingly unjust decision, prove intent or confirm Inparsa’s economic account.

Spanish Criminal Code articles 446–449

A debatable ruling is not prevaricación merely because it is debatable.

The published case-law threshold requires an objectively unjust decision outside any reasonable or defensible legal interpretation, together with the required mental element. The criminal question is not simply whether another outcome was preferable.

1 · Decision

Identify the exact act

Date, terms, jurisdiction, application decided and executable consequences. A broad criticism of proceedings does not replace the allegedly unjust decision.

2 · Objective injustice

Exclude a defensible interpretation

The case must explain why the decision cannot reasonably fit the law or evidence, not merely why one party was harmed.

3 · Knowledge

Prove what the decision-maker knew

Traceability is central: documents filed, date of notice, warnings, funded alternatives and judicial responses.

4 · Mental element

Do not infer intent from outcome

Economic scale or an irreversible control transfer may justify scrutiny, but it does not alone prove knowledge of injustice.

A limited legal bridge

Inparsa does not prove Sun Park. It makes a method of investigation visible.

The matters involve different debtors, parties, mechanisms and judges. The defensible connection lies in the questions an independent reviewer must answer when commercial proceedings produce a difficult-to-reverse transfer of value or control.

Agenda for lawyers and journalists

Six questions capable of changing the account.

TSJC

What does the primary order say?

Its text, date, chamber, scope and later status are needed—not only media characterisation.

Valuation

Which expert reports did the court see?

Subject, cut-off date, debt assumptions, comparables, sensitivities and responses to objections.

Alternatives

Was there a funded offer?

Amount, terms, proof of funds, procedural timing and express reason for acceptance or rejection.

Remedy

What protection remained effective?

Stay, adversarial process, appeal and practical ability to prevent an irreversible effect.

Knowledge

What was filed and when?

The chronology of submissions and responses matters more than retrospective reconstruction.

Challenge

What is the other side’s explanation?

Evidence must be capable of refuting the thesis. Proponents and advisers are expressly invited to correct the record.
