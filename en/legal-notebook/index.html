Editorial proposition

Not a list of accusations. A testable legal record.

The notebook begins where a headline normally ends: with the order, the valuation date and scope, the route by which debt became control, the treatment of funded alternatives, the distinction between estate and non-estate rights, and the procedural status of every challenge.

Document first

Primary law before commentary

Judgments, court orders and official records are prioritised. Press and professional analysis are identified as such and never silently upgraded into findings.

Competing accounts

Each side remains visible

Creditor, shareholder, debtor, administrator and court positions are separated. Provisional admission of a complaint is not guilt; rejection of one argument does not answer every valuation question.

Finite questions

Corrections can close the gap

Every note ends with specific missing records or procedural outcomes. A sourced correction is incorporated with a visible date and scope.

Disclosure: Project Sun Rock is directly interested in the integrity of insolvency, valuation and hotel-control processes in Las Palmas. Shared reporting agenda

The questions that connect these notes—without conflating them.

Valuation

Which value, at what date and for what purpose?

Enterprise value, secured-asset value, equity value and liquidation value are not interchangeable. Comparables and assumptions must travel with the number.

Competition

Was the market genuinely tested?

Time, deposits, minimum uplifts, access to data and treatment of funded exit offers determine whether formal competition could produce maximum value.

Control

What legal bridge moved value or authority?

Debt enforcement, debt-to-equity conversion, asset sale and operational possession are different routes. Each needs a traceable legal and accounting bridge.

Perimeter

What belonged to the estate?

A process concerning one debtor cannot silently absorb third-party property, contractual rights or operating authority outside the estate.

Judicial protection

What remedy was available—and used?

The test is knowledge, competence, available remedy, decision or omission, implementation and effect, not merely whether a party lost.

Outcome

Which challenge is actually pending?

A complaint, provisional screening, reconsideration request, appeal and final judgment have radically different legal significance.
