Corporate note · page 23

The company’s own memory links the 2008 sale to cessation of activity and exploitation by the purchaser.

Controlled primary-source proposition. Page 23 states in substance that on 17 June 2008 a EUR 1,368,000 sale took place in parallel with apartment sales by certain shareholders; that this meant cessation of the company’s activity; and that the transferred assets were placed at the purchaser’s disposal for exploitation.

This proves what Monte Lanza stated in its deposited accounting material about that economic transition. It does not by itself prove the title chain for every unit, authority for later Community/CEXP acts, the identity or knowledge of every participant, or fraud, intent or criminal liability.
