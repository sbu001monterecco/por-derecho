INSOLVENCY 36/2012 · 2021 ALERT → IMPLEMENTATION → CONSEQUENCES

From alert to irreversibility.

This map does not treat a later consequence as proof of earlier judicial knowledge or intent. It separates three planes: what was communicated, what insolvency treatment can be identified, and what happened later. The institutional question is what control broke — or lawfully explained — the chain before title, Registry effects, works, finance and operation made reversal materially harder.

Central limit. The 2021 RICPE material reached the proceeding, was opposed by CAM and the Insolvency Administration, and was later acknowledged as admitted but treated as irrelevant in January 2022. The question is therefore not “the judge never saw the alert”, but what substantive treatment it received as a title, estate-protection, anticipatory-commercialisation and supervision issue.

THE RESULT DOES NOT ANSWER THE ORIGIN

Consolidated title, physical transformation, capital/incentives and integrated operation are later consequences. They do not by themselves prove either legitimacy or illegitimacy of each earlier step.
