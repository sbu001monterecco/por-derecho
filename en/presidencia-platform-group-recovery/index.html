The allegation and the decision requested

Gil Marer alleges an organised, coordinated and continuing criminal mechanism involving the de facto and substantively inequitable displacement and instrumentalisation of the group's business platform. The allegation connects the loss of its rights, operations, opportunities and financing capacity with use of that foundation to obtain commitments, investment, parallel or successive financing, mandates and benefits for others.

The requested outcomes are a responsible unit, preservation, objective legal assessment, reconstruction of administrative reliance and proportionate measures within each body's powers. The Government is not asked to declare guilt or reverse judicial decisions.

  3. The 2008 whole-hotel transaction: distinguish intended agreement, completion and alleged breaches.

  5. Community and adoption: decisions, functions and documentary reliance, not names alone.
  6. Capital and successive uses: commitments, financing, security, mandates and benefits.
  7. Continuing injury: original harm, aggravation and new losses allocated by holder.
  8. Public interest: title, tourism, RIC/RICPE, incentives and European connection without conflating instruments.
  9. Review channels: DP1901/DI22 facts and traceability gaps at their proper evidential weight.

  12. Urgency and third parties: proportionate prevention, bona fide protection and separate procedures.
