CLASSIFICATION SECTION · HEARING · EVIDENCE CONTROL

What evidence was scheduled, and what was actually heard?

The issue can no longer be reduced to “some witnesses attended and others did not”. Court documents show a materially broader formal programme, initially reserved for two days. The judgment, however, records the hearing as held and completed on 25 July 2023. Por Derecho is reconstructing the difference person by person and document by document.

5 April 2023The Court formally scheduled the hearing for 25 and 26 July and ordered specific interrogations/witness arrangements.
25 July 2023All three audiovisual components currently preserved correspond to this date.
Judgment 163/2023Its tenth factual antecedent says the hearing was held on 25 July and, after evidence and submissions, the case was left for judgment.

READING RULE

Scheduled does not mean testified

Prepared→Admitted / scheduled→Citation ordered→Attended→Called→Testified→Addressed in judgment

This page does not convert an internal question list into judicial admission, or a summons into testimony. Nor does it treat a person as absent merely because no named remote-video tile is visible.

24 JUL 2023 · ACCESS TO EVIDENCE

An expert contemporaneously recorded a problem opening the filed evidence CD on the day before the hearing

David Espejo Navarro wrote directly to court personnel on 24 July after a telephone call he said he had just received from the court. His email records that he had been told that certain documents on the CD previously filed in the proceedings could not be opened, including expert evidence produced by his side. He added that the extremely short notice made it impossible to re-supply the documentation and that he did not know how many files were affected.

This is a contemporaneous professional communication and is concrete documentary evidence of a reported accessibility problem. It does not, by itself, prove what the Judge personally reviewed, when he reviewed it, or what effect the incident had on the judgment. Those questions require the evidence index, any cure/replacement actions, the hearing record and an actor-specific evidence trail.

EXPERT-EVIDENCE TRACEABILITY

The 24 July incident has a documented prehistory going back to 2018

A separate reconstruction now follows David Espejo's economic and financial expert work from the 2018 professional engagement, through signed versions prepared for the 2019 classification opposition, circulation across successive advisers, later ratification work and the 2022 CAM-offer assessment, into the 2023 hearing.

Its central question is testable: what was created, what each adviser received, what was filed, what reached the record, what was technically readable, what was explained and what was later addressed or preserved on appeal.

Por Derecho does not claim that there is a “missing 26 July hearing”. The order reserved that day, but the judgment itself says the hearing was completed on 25 July. The precise question is how the evidence formally scheduled for 26 July was disposed of.

OUR POSITION AND OBJECTIVE

Reconstruct the evidence that actually existed and was heard

The defence had assembled an evidential architecture spanning accounting, tax advisers, economic/financial experts, hotel operations, dealings with the insolvency administration and other sources. Our objective is to determine what evidence was formally admitted or scheduled, what was accessible, what was actually heard, what was not, why, what the Court demonstrably heard, and how that evidential record was treated in Judgment 163/2023 and the pending appeal.

If the reconstruction shows a material irregularity, it should be visible in documents, summonses, recordings and rulings. If it shows that evidence was withdrawn, not served, duplicative or properly excluded, that should also be recorded.
