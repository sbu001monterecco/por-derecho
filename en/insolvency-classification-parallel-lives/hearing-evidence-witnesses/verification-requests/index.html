25/26 JUL 2023 HEARING · VERIFICATION REQUESTS

What we ask to be certified, explained or verified

These requests turn broad concerns into finite checks. They do not ask the CGPJ to reverse Judgment 163/2023 and do not replace the appeal. They separate judicial merits from hearing administration, evidence accessibility, record integrity, witness service and institutional functioning.

1 · COURT / LAJ

What we ask the judicial office to certify

1. Complete certified minute. The minute/record of the hearing and any continuation, suspension, recess or ruling affecting the 25/26-Jul programme.
2. Complete audiovisual index. Identification of every official component and confirmation whether anything exists beyond the three preserved files.
3. VIDEO_02→VIDEO_03 interval. Explanation of the approximate visible-clock gap 11:18:50→11:58:22.
4. End of VIDEO_03. Confirmation whether approximately 12:23:21 is the true official end or whether final submissions/another component are missing.
5. Attendance and participation. Certified identification of Judge, LAJ, prosecution, insolvency administrator, counsel, parties, witnesses, experts and remote endpoints.
6. Person-by-person status. For each scheduled person: admission, summons, service, attendance, calling, testimony and, if not heard, the exact act disposing of that evidence.
7. 26 July programme. The precise act by which day-two evidence was advanced, waived, not served, excluded, declared unnecessary or otherwise disposed of.
8. Arrecife judicial requests. Requests, acceptance, returns, service, incidents and any cancellation of scheduled Arrecife videoconferences.
9. Filed evidence-CD incident. Which files could not be opened on 24 July, when the problem was detected, who communicated it and what cure was attempted.
10. Actual accessibility before judgment. Whether affected files were ultimately opened/replaced and what evidence index was actually accessible when the hearing began and when the case was left for judgment.
11. Representation and defence. Counsel withdrawal, notice, substitution, adjournment/remote-participation requests, objections, reservations, nullity points and rulings.

2 · PROVINCIAL COURT

What we ask the appellate record to establish

12. Record transmitted. Complete index of the record sent with the appeal from Judgment 163/2023.
13. Audiovisual record. Whether the AP received all official components, any 26-Jul disposition and the complete hearing minute.
14. Accessibility incident. Whether the CD problem, any remediation and the affected documents are in the appellate record.
15. Service and effective defence. Whether the AP received witness-service, appearance and representation history and the Article 24 issues raised.
16. Supplementation. Whether missing material triggered a request for or receipt of a supplemental record.

3 · CGPJ / INSPECTION

What we ask to be institutionally verified

17. Alzada 286/2026. Confirm whether REGAGE26e00069061338 and all five files were incorporated and what body actually examined them.
18. Material examination. Distinguish presented/registered/routed material from material actually examined at DI 169/2026 and appeal stages.
19. Newly reconstructed hearing evidence. Incorporate the 25/26-Jul matrix and determine whether it affects any prior court-functioning/supervision issue.
20. 24-Jul incident. Determine whether the reported inability to open filed evidence warrants a functioning/Inspection inquiry independent of the merits of the judgment.
21. Two-day programme. Verify through the minute, service returns and rulings how the formal 26-Jul programme was disposed of.
22. Audiovisual integrity and defence. Consider obtaining the complete record to verify completeness and effective-defence allegations.
23. Competence classification. State which issues are jurisdictional merits, court functioning/Inspection, potentially disciplinary, LAJ/office matters, or outside CGPJ competence.
24. Inspection Service. Confirm whether any report/referral exists concerning Insolvency 36/2012 and, if not, whether the newly recovered functioning evidence warrants referral.
25. Auditable answer. Give a reasoned issue-by-issue response to the non-merits points rather than collapsing the whole package into disagreement with judicial rulings.

EXPRESS BOUNDARY

What we are not asking the CGPJ to do

We are not asking it to annul or correct Judgment 163/2023, decide ownership, quantify debts, determine insolvency culpability or substitute for the Provincial Court. Nor do we presently state as established fact that the Judge suppressed witnesses, manipulated the recording, refused to read evidence or deliberately denied a defence. Those propositions would require actor-specific proof not presently established.

Current proposed route.

The proportionate route is to supplement Alzada 286/2026 with the newly reconstructed evidence and, where appropriate, seek a focused court-functioning/Inspection review. A wholly new disciplinary complaint should be reserved for new, personal and disciplinarily cognisable facts, or a later decision leaving these functioning facts unexamined.
