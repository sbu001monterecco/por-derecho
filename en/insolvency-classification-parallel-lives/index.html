THE STARTING POINT

What is a culpable insolvency classification?

The Spanish concept is the classification of the insolvency proceeding as culpable (calificación culpable). It is not, by itself, a criminal conviction and it does not mean that a person has been found guilty of a crime.

In an ordinary insolvency, this track asks whether the insolvency of the debtor company was fortuitous or whether legally relevant intentional or grossly negligent conduct generated or aggravated it. It may identify affected persons or accomplices and carry serious personal and patrimonial consequences.

In simple terms, the central question is:

“Was this company's insolvency fortuitous, or did legally relevant conduct by particular persons generate or aggravate it?”

Its normal purpose is not automatically to determine ownership of third-party assets, physical control of an entire mixed-ownership complex, authority to market that complex, what title was represented to operators or investors, or what later checks were required for RIC, regional incentives or ERDF/FEDER funding.

Temporal regime: Judgment 163/2023 states that former Insolvency Act Article 164.1 applies because of the date on which the classification section was opened. Insolvency Act 22/2003 (BOE).

GIL MARER'S ALLEGATION

“They lied about what I did and did not do.” How Por Derecho tests that allegation

Gil Marer alleges that material accusations made about his conduct were false and, where the relevant actor demonstrably held the contrary documents or had themselves participated in the contrary event, knowingly false.

This page does not ask the reader to accept that conclusion on assertion. It places each accusation beside the contemporaneous document, the later judicial rejection or narrowing, the appeal ground and the actor-specific knowledge record.

The evidential threshold is simple: contradiction + knowledge. A false statement can be shown from documents. A knowingly false statement additionally requires evidence that the speaker knew the contrary fact when making it.

The test capable of confirming, narrowing or defeating retaliation

1 · Subject matter and personIdentify which acts or omissions fall within Article 2 and whether Gil obtained the information in his professional context as shareholder, director or connected person.
2 · Protectable reportTest reasonable grounds, channel, recipient and Article 35 requirements, including the temporal extension in the Sixth Additional Provision.
3 · KnowledgeSeparate CNMV/AEAT receipt, incorporation into the judicial file, and personal reading or knowledge attributable to each actor.
4 · Detriment and causationIdentify the alleged professional, patrimonial or reputational disadvantage and whether it occurred because of reporting, not merely after reporting.
5 · Article 38.4 presumptionOnce a compliant report and detriment are reasonably shown, the statute presumes retaliation and requires a justification based on grounds unrelated to the report.
6 · Independent explanationPreserve the 2018 stay, 2019 non-validation, adverse findings actually upheld and every record capable of refuting the hypothesis.

Essential limit: chronology and presence justify investigation but do not by themselves prove which annexes each actor read, the precise oral position maintained by the prosecutor, that the hearing or judgment occurred solely because of reporting, or that the Administrator, Fiscalía, Court and CAM acted by agreement. Article 36.2 defines retaliation; Article 38.4 creates only a rebuttable presumption after a compliant report and detriment are reasonably shown. One procedural life inside the insolvency. Several parallel lives outside it.

While LPB's insolvency followed its court process, other layers of the same complex continued or acquired different legal and economic lives. The investigation must identify, layer by layer, which asset or right was used, who had authority to use it, and what information later reached operators, investors and public authorities.

The rule of this page

Truth is not made safer by making it weaker. Material contradictions should be stated plainly. So should serious adverse findings. The protection against overstatement is source discipline, not euphemism.

Por Derecho therefore separates: what is documented; what the first-instance Judge found; what that same judgment rejected; what is alleged on appeal; what contrary knowledge can be attributed to each actor; and what remains an inference about intent or motive.

This page makes identified evidence-based allegations of materially false, selective or internally contradictory representations where the source record permits them. It does not present unadjudicated criminal, disciplinary or corrupt intent as an established fact.
