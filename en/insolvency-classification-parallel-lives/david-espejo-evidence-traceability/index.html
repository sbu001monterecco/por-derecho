David Espejo: from professional expert work to the question of what evidence actually reached each decision-maker

This record does not assume that expert evidence is correct because it comes from a trusted professional, or that a procedural incident necessarily amounts to a material irregularity. It reconstructs a testable chain: what work existed, who received it, what was intended for filing, what entered the record, what could be opened, what was explained or ratified, and what was later addressed in judgment or appeal.

READING RULE

The evidential chain consists of distinct states

Created→Signed→Received by adviser→Included in pleading→Filed→Court-readable→Explained / ratified→Addressed→Preserved on appeal

Por Derecho does not collapse one state into the next. Receiving a report does not prove filing; filing does not prove technical accessibility; accessibility does not prove consideration; and silence in a decision does not itself establish cause or intent.

INDEPENDENCE AND CHRONOLOGY

The professional engagement is documented before the later personal relationship

Recovered records place meetings, strategy, fieldwork, reports and professional billing from April 2018. That chronology matters to independence: the documented origin of Espejo's involvement is a professional expert engagement. The later personal relationship is not used as a substitute for technical verification of his conclusions.

Por Derecho tests each expert conclusion against its sources, calculations, assumptions and later documents. Personal or professional trust does not turn a conclusion into a proved fact.

ADVISER CHAIN

The question is not merely who knew Espejo, but what knowledge survived each professional handover

Records already reconstructed connect Espejo's work to different advisers at different stages: procedural use with Juan Tomás Parrilla; circulation of economic material during the Cuatrecasas period; an express introduction to Daniel Jiménez/SLJ in May 2020 so incoming counsel could understand his work inside and outside the insolvency; later interaction with Luis Miguel López and Cristo Suárez Pimentel; and preparation for ratification before the 2023 hearing.

The full audit must distinguish, for each adviser: received → reviewed → incorporated → filed → handed over. No professional breach is attributed without reconstructing mandate, file and act.

2020–2022 · BRIDGE TO OTHER TRACKS

RIC/CNMV, marketing and the CAM offer

The professional relationship did not end with the 2019 opposition. Later records identify review of RIC-related material, discussion of a possible CNMV contact, FITUR attendance to gather information about marketing of the hotel and, in 2022, a specific economic assessment of the CAM offer.

These references are published as evidence of the existence and scope of work, not as automatic validation of a thesis about RICPE, CAM or any third party. Substantive cross-links belong in those dossiers once each proposition can be tied to primary evidence.

24–25 JULY 2023

From the expert history to evidence accessibility

The final stage of this chain leads into the already documented 24 July 2023 access incident and the hearing the next day. Espejo recorded in writing that, after a call he attributed to the Court, he had been told that certain documents on the filed evidence CD could not be opened, including expert material from his side.

This documents a reported accessibility incident; it does not itself establish what the Judge read, when, or what effect the incident had on Judgment 163/2023.
