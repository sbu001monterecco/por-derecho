What title and availability premise did each gatekeeper verify as Sun Park became a financeable, publicly supported and operating project?

This page submits a chain of functional instrumentalisation, documentary institutionalisation and serial reliance to verification. It does not presume unlawful agreement, fraud, shared knowledge or collective action. It asks what each actor checked against the underlying properties, rights and original records.

The issue does not end inside Insolvency 36/2012.

A document or premise born in a proceeding limited to LPB may acquire operative life in notarial practice, Registry, corporate records, RIC investment, RICPE, tax controls, regional incentives, EU co-financing and current hotel operation. Each transition should be independently reconstructable.

Institutionalisation can occur through serial reliance, not necessarily coordination

A testimony may be used before a notary; a deed before the Registry; a title package in due diligence; due diligence in an investment decision; beneficiary declarations in an incentives application; and an operator may rely on the title or contract it receives.

Serial reliance: did each recipient actually return to the original title, property, authority and right, or rely on documentation produced or accepted by the preceding link?

Lack of re-verification is not presumed. That is why the underlying files are requested from each gatekeeper.

Historical act ≠ extinguished consequence

MYND Yaiza is marketed today. That does not make its operation or all revenue unlawful or recoverable. It keeps open a precise question: which asset/right generates each current use, revenue, value or financing capacity; who owns it; what title authorises exploitation; and what remedy remains if a divergence is proved.
