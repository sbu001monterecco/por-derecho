How could a documentary premise about Sun Park travel through insolvency, private capital, public incentives and current hotel operation?

This page submits a documentary-portability and institutionalisation chain to verification. “Instrumentalisation” is used functionally: a document, certification or legal position can become an instrument for later reliance. It does not presume unlawful agreement, common intent, fraud or shared knowledge among actors.

The issue can no longer be reduced to “what happened inside Insolvency 36/2012”.

The question is whether instruments and premises born in a proceeding limited to LPB acquired external operative life and supported, directly or indirectly, title, corporate restructuring, RIC investment, regional incentives, a current public representation of European Union co-financing and commercial exploitation over a wider material perimeter.

Not only LPB: three consequence/harm planes must be audited

1 · Insolvency · LPB

Estate value, realisation, income, credit/payment accounting, refinancing or exit alternatives, causes of action, restitution and present ability to conclude or reverse consequences of the insolvency process.

2 · Extra-insolvency · Matkator / third parties

Title and Registry position, possession/access, works, hotel use, fruits/revenue, community burdens, financing/development value and remedies concerning property or rights that did not automatically enter LPB's estate merely because they were physically or operationally connected to Sun Park.

3 · Cross-border · Aweswell / foreign capital

Foreign shareholder/investor value and control, inbound capital or funding, recovery of investment, refinancing, professional/accounting/tax/enforcement costs and causes of action or remedies crossing Spain's border.

The foreign-investor cross-border plane and the EU-financial-interest plane are legally distinct. Both may coexist; neither proves the other.

Ongoing alleged harm: the clock does not stop in 2018, 2022 or 2023

An originating act can be historic while its consequences remain live. The audit must look at today's title/Registry position, possession and access, physical works, hotel exploitation, rooms/spaces used, fruits and revenue, financing/security capacity, created value, public incentives subject to compliance, community obligations, restrictions on third parties, stranded/impaired foreign capital and remedies still open.

The official MYND Yaiza website currently markets rooms, services, restaurants and other hotel products. That documents current commercial operation. It does not convert all hotel revenue or value into claimant damage. It requires a more precise answer: which asset or right generates each current use, revenue or value, who legally owns it and what title authorises its exploitation.

Temporal model: originating act → documentary instrument → external reliance → capital/support/operation → present position → continuing or remediable consequence.

“Instrumentalisation” without a conspiracy theory

A chain may institutionalise a premise even if not every participant knows its origin or shares an intention. A notary may rely on testimony; a Registry on a deed; an investor on a due-diligence package; an incentives authority on beneficiary representations; an operator on the title or contract it receives.

That is why public pressure is directed to independent verification: if each link correctly checked its premise, the documentary chain can prove it. If a premise broadened, simplified or shifted as it moved between legal contexts, the exact point can be identified.

No shared knowledge is attributed by this sequence to the LAJ, Court, Insolvency Administrator, CAM, HNT, RICPE, CNMV, funding authorities or MYND.

What this page establishes — and what it does not

It establishes as a verifiable object: that the project passed through insolvency, external title acts, a RIC perimeter, financing attributed to RICPE, a regional-incentive file and a current public EU co-financing statement, ending in current commercial hotel operation.

It does not establish: conspiracy, RIC fraud, subsidy fraud, double funding, falsification, money laundering, EU-budget loss, criminal liability of officials or private actors, or that all current value/revenue belongs to LPB, Matkator or Aweswell.

The evidential demand is harder to evade than a general accusation: asset by asset, right by right, document by document, euro by euro and date by date.

Public message

We do not ask any institution or investor to accept our theory.

We ask each one to identify the title, availability and perimeter it verified before producing the next effect: testimony, deed, registration, financing, incentive, certification, co-financing or exploitation.
