THE PROFESSIONAL GATEWAY

Five separate decisions. No automatic promotion.

Firm-wide assessment is separate from conflicts screening, a retainer and independent legal analysis. F0–F4 below describe procurement only; they are not the prosecution's criminal-evidence G-tests.

  1. F0

    Verify the institution

    Identify the actual regulated legal entity, institutional intake route and published practice. Research alone does not prove available capacity.

    24 candidates recorded
  2. F1

    Confirm capability & fit

    Ask firm management for actual England & Wales prosecution leadership, civil recovery capacity, a Spain-qualified legal bridge, availability and limitations.

    4 approached; 0 firm offers confirmed
  3. F2

    Clear conflicts securely

    Only after the firm identifies a secure route and the owner approves a minimal client/adverse-party schedule. Decisions are firm-specific and scope-limited.

    No clearance reported publicly
  4. F3

    Agree an independent mandate

    Separate client authority, merits/jurisdiction review, scope, supervision, fees and written acceptance. No relationship is implied by an enquiry.

    0 new engagements verified
  5. F4

    Permit controlled evidence access

    Provide a narrowly scoped, logged, access-controlled evidential index only after authorisation and appropriate professional safeguards.

    Private case room remains restricted
FOUR WAYS TO SUPPORT THE WORK

Fit is assessed across a firm, not by one individual's interest.

A

Integrated team

An available English prosecution/recovery lead and suitably qualified Spanish-language or Spanish-law colleagues.

B

Established counterpart

An English lead firm working with an available, established Spain-qualified lawyer or firm.

C

Coordinated specialists

A properly supervised cross-border group of legal, language and forensic specialists.

D

Bounded contribution

A realistic specialist role or independent referral when a complete mandate cannot responsibly be staffed.

PUBLIC TRANSPARENCY / PRIVATE DUE PROCESS

What is published — and what is protected.

Open to public scrutiny

  • The size of the assessed firm universe and dated aggregate first-contact activity.
  • The selection criteria, staged governance and A–D delivery models.
  • The difference between a preparatory draft, a sent enquiry, capacity confirmation and engagement.
  • Corrections and future changes after source verification and public-release review.

Not part of this public page

  • The names of firms merely approached or under confidential consideration.
  • Individual contacts, exact emails, historical mandates, conflicts results or private commercial terms.
  • Applicant and adverse-person schedules, original evidence, draft prosecution papers or private data-room URLs.
  • Any assertion that the proposed criminal case has passed independent legal or court tests.
Why not name the firms?

Approaches are exploratory professional enquiries, not endorsements, refusals, client relationships or findings about any firm's conduct. Publishing identities or correspondence prematurely could mislead readers and interfere with proper confidentiality and conflict screening.

Legal and evidential distinction

The underlying matters include disputed allegations and contemplated legal proceedings. A working private-prosecution draft is not a filed prosecution or issued summons. This programme's progress is not proof of jurisdiction, an offence, fault, damages or recovery. The rights and reputations of all parties remain subject to applicable law and fair process.
