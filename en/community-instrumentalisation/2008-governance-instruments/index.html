Documentary reading

What the located instruments support.

Every proposition below is limited to the content and date of the stated instrument.

1987 Owners' StatutesThe located Article 6 records a tourism-exploitation purpose for the complex as a single unit, subject to a stated exception for local-storage premises and a qualified-owner decision to change the purpose. The instrument concerns the property-community layer: individual properties, common elements, participation and owners' organs.
2008 CEXP materialThe located records identify an April 2008 collective-origin meeting and a 26 May transfer from Montelanza to CEXP, stated to take effect on 27 May 2008. CEXP is recorded as accepting operation and administration in its own name, with operating responsibilities and administrative change-of-holder steps.
The source-level consequenceThe Owners' Community and CEXP cannot be treated as interchangeable merely because both concern the same physical hotel. A claim, payment, authority, vote, contract, proceeding or public filing must identify the actual legal body, capacity, property scope and date.

No overclaiming

What the documents do not decide.

Publication is not a legal adjudication.

Not a debt judgment

No Costa Papagayo liability is determined here.

The instruments do not identify the legal debtor, quantify an enforceable balance, show a valid invoice, prove receipt of payment, or prove a later settlement.

Not universal authority

CEXP's located origin is not a universal mandate.

It does not by itself prove that all units, non-participating owners, common areas or later acts fell within the same authority.

Not an insolvency extension

LPB's Concurso 36/2012 is not expanded by reference.

The insolvency perimeter must be established from the relevant court and estate documents; it does not automatically absorb CEXP, the Owners' Community, Matkator or third-party owners.

Not a criminal conclusion

A discrepancy must be proved, not assumed.

The source set supports targeted questions about authority, debtor identity and disclosure. It does not establish falsification, collusion or criminal conduct.
