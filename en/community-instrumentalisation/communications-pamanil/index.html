What would prove or disprove performance?

People

Employment contracts, payroll, Social Security, rosters, supervisor logs and insurance.

Work and supplies

Work orders, access logs, photographs, consumables, tools, water/electricity contracts and supplier invoices.

Money and authority

Signed contract, valid resolutions/proxies, bank statements, tax returns, invoices, payments and beneficiary mapping.

Criminal-law boundary. For each alleged act, the record must identify actor/principal, duty and capacity, falsity, knowledge, intent, reliance, causal effect, benefit or harm and the strongest lawful explanation. Missing records or an invalid resolution do not automatically establish fraud.
