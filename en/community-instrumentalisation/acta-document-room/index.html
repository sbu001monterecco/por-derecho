Nineteen located ACTA families are digitised and posted—with every remaining historical gap visible.

For every located control copy, this room now provides page-sequenced public text, a rendered text-edition PDF, an irreversibly redacted source facsimile and redacted source-page images. The layers remain separate so that OCR is never presented as certified transcription and a received copy is never presented as the official minutes book.

No completeness by label

What “complete public-safe package” means here.

Completeness is about this public edition, not completeness of the official Community archive or authenticity of the copy received.

Required componentPass conditionWhat it does not prove
Public-safe text or explicit redaction markerEvery line that can be published is presented in sequence; where privacy requires a full-page redaction, the edition preserves its position and explains that decision rather than revealing private OCR. Illegible text is not completed by conjecture.That the received copy is the official book or that its statements are true or valid.
Public text-edition PDFThe controlled edition is rendered as a readable PDF after deterministic page, count and privacy checks. Two wholly withheld packages contain page markers, not public OCR.That the PDF is a facsimile, unredacted original, certified copy or line-by-line verified transcription.
Rendered edition pagesThe thumbnail list and public-edition page count agree, with no unexplained publication-page gap.That the thumbnails reproduce the visual appearance of the native ACTA.
Redacted source facsimile and source-page imagesEvery received source page is visually represented in order after document-level privacy review, with any removed region logged.That the received copy is the official book or that unlocated annexes and variants do not exist.
Integrity manifestSource locator, received-copy hash where available, size/page metadata, transformations, redactions, variants and open reconciliations are recorded.An uninterrupted forensic chain of custody or official certification.

Variant rule: a readable package remains “partial” when another recorded byte variant, page-count variant, annex set or signed-book version has not been reconciled. Public redaction must protect non-essential personal data while preserving the sequence and governance-relevant wording.
