Sun Park / MYND Yaiza: follow the structure and the funds, layer by layer.

The relevant sequence is not one transaction. The record must separate the statutory RIC from RIC Private Equity, the 2020–2021 investor presentation phase, the later CAMSA-to-Hotel New Trend segregation and subsequent public-support files, including GC/836/P06.

Evidence boundary. The coexistence of private financing, RIC/idoneidad, regional incentives or possible EU funding does not prove double funding, fraud or illegality. The verifiable question is whether each layer can be reconciled by date, beneficiary, title, eligible cost and source/use of funds.

1. The chain that must be reconstructable

Reading rule: a later corporate reorganisation does not retrospectively prove title, authority, availability or eligibility at an earlier date.

2. RIC is not RIC Private Equity

RIC

Reserva para Inversiones en Canarias. A statutory tax mechanism with its own legal requirements and administrative records.

RIC Private Equity

A separately named regulated investment vehicle. Its name does not make it the statutory RIC mechanism and does not imply CNMV endorsement of each underlying project.

Why the distinction matters

The chronology must separate materialisation/investment, ownership, project financing and public aid/incentives.

3. Documentary chronology

2019–2021 · investor phase

The repository controls RIC Private Equity as the vehicle used in the investor-facing phase. Sun Park / the future MYND Yaiza project appears in project material before the later 2022 segregation into Hotel New Trend.

2020–2021 · project and works

The evidence architecture contains investor-facing material and a physical reform chronology. The question is what rights, costs, dates and availability were represented at each stage.

The BORME segregation record reproduced in the source corpus identifies Construcciones Acosta Matos, S.A. as the segregating company and Hotel New Trend, S.L.U. as beneficiary of the hotel economic unit for transformation and later operation as MYND Yaiza.

2022 onward · public files

The repository separately controls Decree 224/2022, the RIC/idoneidad route, RICPE Series F/G and regional-incentives file GC/836/P06. The exact relationship between costs, aid, employment, payments and any EU/ERDF component requires the primary files.

4. GC/836/P06: what the file should establish

Beneficiary and availability

Applicant, beneficiary, project holder and the title/availability document relied upon.

Eligible cost and finance

Eligible budget, start dates, financing, declared other aid, invoices and proof of payment.

Control and closure

Certifications, inspections, payments, employment commitments and checks, closure, corrections or recovery.

Open question, not allegation: if EU/ERDF financing existed, identify the operation, programme, percentage, managing/intermediate body, certified expenditure, verifications and any corrections. Until then, this page does not state that HNT/MYND received EU funds.

5. Treasury and Internal Audit: custody map, not verdict

Resolution 90/2026 of the Canary Islands Treasury and Financial Policy Directorate-General, reviewed in the source corpus, granted access to the requested framework and stated the absence of formally approved protocols of the type requested within that Directorate-General, relying instead on the identified normative framework. The formulation must remain narrow: it does not mean that procedures, controls or records did not exist.

The later Internal Audit sequence, culminating on 19 August 2026, similarly delimited its remit and clarified that its earlier response was not a general certificate that no other aid, regional incentives, EU/ERDF financing, payments, certifications or files existed.

Practical consequence: each authority should identify the native file and competent custodian. The objective is to reconcile sources and uses, not replace files with inference.
