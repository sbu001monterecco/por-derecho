Separate responsibilities. One connected economic and evidential reality.

This record explains why the professional issues linked to Sun Park / MYND Yaiza should no longer be read only as isolated bilateral complaints. It keeps every responsibility, Bar and file separate while preserving common evidence and the inter-Bar dimension.

Canonical rule: separate responsibility does not mean separate reality. Individual professional responsibility remains actor-specific; common evidence and economic context may require coordinated preservation and institutional treatment without collective attribution.

1. What the reader is asked to understand — and what is not asserted

Yes

A common professional matrix

Different lawyers, firms, administrators, advisers and professional bodies appear at different points around the same asset, insolvency, Community structure, operation, finance and litigation. The analysis must identify what each person knew and did while preserving common records.

No

No automatic collective attribution

Membership of a firm, family, Bar, transaction or economic environment does not transfer knowledge, intention or responsibility. An opened Bar file does not prove misconduct; a later professional relationship does not prove conflict; a territorial referral does not decide the merits.

Evidential boundary: this record does not find conspiracy, criminal organisation, professional corruption, bribery, misuse of confidential information or guilt by any person or firm. Those characterisations require actor-specific evidence and, where applicable, a competent authority's decision.

4. Why the economic context matters to professional ethics

Sun Park was not merely a collection of land-registry units. The project record treats it as an economic platform: assets and rights, hotel operation, income, commercial relationships, financing capacity, insolvency position and the capacity to attract investment. Gil Marer/Aweswell's thesis is that substantial parts of that platform were progressively displaced and later incorporated into an Acosta Matos-linked economic perimeter through distinct roles performed by CAM, HNT, Canarian Hospitality, RICPE and the MYND Yaiza operation.

That setting can generate mandates, fees, finance, transactions, litigation, audit work, advice and institutional decisions. The professional question is therefore not whether everyone who touched the matter became part of a common plan; it is what each professional knew, what duty applied, what they did or omitted, who paid, what source of funds existed, what conflict was assessed and what effect followed.

Mandatory financial separation: RIC, private investment, loans, regional aid, tax incentives and FEDER/other European funds are distinct legal and economic tracks. They are not treated as the same money without documentary traceability.

6. What is being asked at regional level

Coordination without collective liability

Determine whether the matters should remain procedurally separate while using a common preservation, competence, referral and traceability framework.

Competence and abstention

Identify the competent organ and participation, abstention or recusal safeguards where review concerns a constituent Bar's handling.

Common evidence

Prevent the same source from losing context when it appears in different files; retain authenticity, provenance, contradiction and limits.

National route

Clarify which questions may or must reach CGAE and by what mechanism, while respecting that CGAE does not ordinarily replace territorial Bars at first instance.
