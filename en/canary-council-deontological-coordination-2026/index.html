Separate responsibilities. One connected economic and evidential reality.

This record explains why the professional issues linked to Sun Park / MYND Yaiza should no longer be read only as isolated bilateral complaints. It keeps every responsibility, Bar and file separate while preserving common evidence and the inter-Bar dimension.

Canonical rule: separate responsibility does not mean separate reality. Individual professional responsibility remains actor-specific; common evidence and economic context may require coordinated preservation and institutional treatment without collective attribution.

1. What the reader is asked to understand — and what is not asserted

Yes

A common professional matrix

Different lawyers, firms, administrators, advisers and professional bodies appear at different points around the same asset, insolvency, Community structure, operation, finance and litigation. The analysis must identify what each person knew and did while preserving common records.

No

No automatic collective attribution

Membership of a firm, family, Bar, transaction or economic environment does not transfer knowledge, intention or responsibility. An opened Bar file does not prove misconduct; a later professional relationship does not prove conflict; a territorial referral does not decide the merits.

Evidential boundary: this record does not find conspiracy, criminal organisation, professional corruption, bribery, misuse of confidential information or guilt by any person or firm. Those characterisations require actor-specific evidence and, where applicable, a competent authority's decision.

5. Why the economic context matters to professional ethics

Sun Park was not merely a collection of land-registry units. The project record treats it as an economic platform: assets and rights, hotel operation, income, commercial relationships, financing capacity, insolvency position and the capacity to attract investment. Gil Marer/Aweswell's thesis is that substantial parts of that platform were progressively displaced and later incorporated into an Acosta Matos-linked economic perimeter through distinct roles performed by CAM, HNT, Canarian Hospitality, RICPE and the MYND Yaiza operation.

That setting can generate mandates, fees, finance, transactions, litigation, audit work, advice and institutional decisions. The professional question is therefore not whether everyone who touched the matter became part of a common plan; it is what each professional knew, what duty applied, what they did or omitted, who paid, what source of funds existed, what conflict was assessed and what effect followed.

Mandatory financial separation: RIC, private investment, loans, regional aid, tax incentives and FEDER/other European funds are distinct legal and economic tracks. They are not treated as the same money without documentary traceability.

This communication does not ask the Consejo Canario to merge legally distinct disciplinary matters, to predetermine misconduct, or to decide criminal, insolvency or judicial liability outside its competence. It asks for a clear regional institutional route for connected professional-conduct questions that are already distributed across more than one territorial Bar and that share a substantial evidential and economic context.

The context is important. Our position begins with the Aweswell / LPB / Matkator / Sun Park business platform and an alleged staged displacement of practical control beginning in 2017–2018. The evidence we have preserved does not jump from that period to the later 2022 adjudication/formalisation: it includes a 31 January 2019 Acosta Matos web capture presenting “Hotel Lava Verde”, at the Sun Park address, among “Hoteles propios”; 2019–2020 pilot-room and whole-hotel refurbishment activity; 2020 CAM / Meeting Point project discussions; and the November 2020 RIC Private Equity investor presentation of Sun Park before HNT / MYND formalisation. Gil Marer’s present provenance allegation is that 100% — not a lesser percentage — of the later asset, income, commercial-relationship, reputational/institutional-access and capital-access position of the Acosta Matos / RICPE-linked perimeter derives directly or indirectly from that displaced platform. I do not ask the Consejo Canario to accept that proposition as proved. It is the reporting party’s causal/provenance allegation, and each asset, income stream, relationship, professional act and recovery route must be independently tested. Nor do I ask the Council to infer wrongdoing from employment, investment, professional advice, public support or institutional relationships. The concern is that the later accumulation of legitimate jobs, investment, operating income, professional mandates and public interfaces can create economic embeddedness and moral-hazard incentives that make independent historical scrutiny more, not less, important.

We therefore ask the Canary Council not to decide DIP 80 on the merits in place of ICALPA, but to identify what regional coordination/accountability mechanism can certify: (i) whether DIP 80 remains preliminary information; (ii) whether an archive or formal disciplinary file exists; (iii) whether the same-facts criminal-concurrence rule has been applied or considered; (iv) the date/nature of the rapporteur's latest substantive act; and (v) the next expected procedural step. We do not request confidential third-party representations; we request status, date, route and traceability.

There is also a preventive procedural-integrity issue that we already raised expressly with ICALPA on 10 June 2026. At that time we did not allege that pressure or interference had occurred inside the Bar. We identified a structural risk: a file connected with large economic, professional and institutional interests may attract informal approaches, third-party calls, attempts to learn the rapporteur’s preliminary view or other non-procedural communications. We therefore requested reinforced safeguards of independence, confidentiality, traceability and non-interference. Consistently with that position, we now ask that any rapporteur, investigator or responsible person appointed by the Canary Council be conflict-cleared in advance, independent in fact and appearance, genuinely willing to pursue the necessary finite enquiries, and given sufficient institutional backing to resist any informal contact or pressure that may arise. If any materially relevant external approach occurs, we ask that it be logged and that any representation or document enter only through a formal, traceable and contradictable channel. This is a preventive request designed to protect the Council and the rapporteur; it does not assert that such contacts have occurred.
