Francisco de Borja Rodríguez-Batllori Laffitte: court appointment and limits

The 6-Jun-2012 order court-appointed him as insolvency administrator of Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. in Insolvency 36/2012. He is a court-appointed, judicial-adjacent professional: he is not a private actor in the Community network and was not the judge. The attributed friendship or coordination with Álvaro Campanario lacks located independent proof.

Search variants: Francisco de Borja Rodríguez-Batllori · Rodríguez-Batllori Laffitte. They do not replace the canonical identity.

READING METHOD

Publication boundary
