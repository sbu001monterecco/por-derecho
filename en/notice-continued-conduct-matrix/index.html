NOTICE · INFORMATION · LATER CONDUCT · CAUSATION · PROOF

One matrix. Separate actors and capacities.

This matrix tests whether a proved notice or information state preceded a later act or omission. It does not treat chronology, professional association, corporate succession, adverse outcome or shared interest as proof of knowledge, coordination, intent or criminal liability.

Required discipline: notice must be dated and attributed to the correct legal person and capacity; an omission requires a proved duty and ability to act; contribution, causation and benefit remain separate; every allegation appears beside an innocent or lawful explanation and the documents needed to decide.
