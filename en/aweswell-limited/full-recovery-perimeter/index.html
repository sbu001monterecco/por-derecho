Aweswell Limited: the filed £1 is not the value of the business.

This page separates the statutory carrying presentation from the directors' asserted economic and recovery perimeter. The latter includes, subject to successful recovery and proof, the asserted full unencumbered ownership of Sun Park / MYND Yaiza, the hotel business/platform, attributable hotel income and all legally recoverable damages, restitution and compensation.

£1 carrying presentation ≠ asserted full economic/recovery value
Canonical directors' position: Aweswell Limited and its directors do not attribute a £1 or zero economic value to the Sun Park / MYND Yaiza business, hotel, income or recovery perimeter. Their stated position is that the full economic value of the group business and recovery rights remains to be established, recovered, measured and recognised incrementally as title, control, income, quantum and recovery certainty are proved in and out of court.
Accounting boundary. This page does not state that the full asserted recovery perimeter is already recognised as net assets in the filed 2025 accounts. Property, earned income, receivables, restitution and damages follow different recognition and measurement rules. Later recognition must be supported item by item.

Recognition bridge from £1

Identify the asset or right.

Exact finca, business asset, income stream, receivable, restitution right or damages claim.

Establish the reporting entity and title/control.

Aweswell, LPB or Matkator; registry, contracts, judgments, ledgers and control evidence.

Measure reliably.

Independent valuation, reconstructed accounts, hotel-income records and expert quantum analysis.

Apply the correct recognition threshold.

Ordinary asset recognition where applicable; higher certainty for contingent recoveries and compensation.

Recognise or disclose incrementally.

Maintain an auditable bridge from the £1 filed presentation to each later recognised amount.

Use in proceedings and correspondence

The distinction applies whenever the accounts are cited in the concurso, calificación, civil, criminal, tax, enforcement, regulatory, professional-accountability, lending or investment contexts.

A third party should not use the £1 line to assert that the directors valued the hotel at £1, admitted zero economic value, waived ownership or income, admitted no loss, capped damages or accepted insolvency/culpability.

Conversely, the directors' asserted full recovery perimeter must remain labelled as a recovery/economic position until each component is independently established and recognised under the applicable rules.

Return to the filed-accounts record

Evidence-first presentation. Official register facts, filed directors' statements, independent accounting analysis and asserted recovery positions are kept separate.
