Locked interpretation rule

£1 is a carrying presentation—not a valuation conclusion.

The figure cannot fairly be re-described as the value of the hotel, the group, its property, income, creditor rights, damages or recoveries.

The £1 reported by Aweswell Limited is the directors’ provisional, prudential and non-valuation carrying presentation under constrained reporting conditions. It is not a waiver, abandonment, admission, estoppel, recovery cap, insolvency finding, culpability finding or judicial determination.

No automatic balance-sheet uplift

03

UK accounting analysis

How value can be recognised correctly.

FRS 102 requires recognition to be assessed item by item. General recognition requires probable future economic benefits and a cost or value that can be measured reliably. Where a reasonable estimate cannot be made, non-recognition may be accompanied by disclosure and later recognition when the criteria are met.

Property and business assetsProve entity-level title or control, then classify and measure.

Reconcile each finca/right to Aweswell, LPB or Matkator; establish consolidation treatment; classify as property, plant and equipment, investment property, a financial asset or another class; support cost, revaluation or fair value with the appropriate evidence and independent valuation.

Hotel incomeRecognise earned, attributable and reliably measurable income in the correct entity and period.

Income allegedly received or retained by another actor requires a legally grounded receivable, restitution, account-of-profits or damages analysis. Forecast future hotel income is not booked wholesale, although it may support an authorised valuation model.

Damages and restitutionDisclose probable claims; recognise only when the required certainty is reached.

Contingent assets are not recognised until inflow is virtually certain. A probable but not virtually certain inflow is disclosed when practicable. Compensation for lost or impaired property is recognised only when virtually certain.

Later accountingUse the correct mechanism: correction, estimate change, revaluation or later-period recognition.

A later award or recovery is not automatically a restatement of 2025. Amended accounts for the same period must relate to the original reporting date; later events normally enter the period in which recognition conditions are met.

Evidence bridge required before a material uplift

1
Right and reporting entity.

Identify the exact property, receivable, income stream or claim and the entity that owns or controls it.

2
Title and transaction reconciliation.

Registry records, acquisition documents, court orders, ledgers and intercompany funding must reconcile.

3
Professional measurement.

Independent property/business valuation, reconstructed accounts, hotel income records and expert quantum analysis.

4
Probability and certainty.

Apply the ordinary recognition threshold or, for contingent recoveries and specified compensation, the virtually-certain threshold.

5
Auditable bridge from £1.

Show every recognised increment by asset, entity, period, measurement basis and source.

Technical clarification about the filed FRS 102 citations

The supplied PDF is preserved unchanged. Its discussion of prudence refers to FRS 102 paragraphs 17.21–17.23. In the January 2022 edition, those paragraphs concern useful life and depreciation methods. The general prudence principle is paragraph 2.9; recognition and reliable measurement are addressed in 2.27–2.32; contingent assets in 21.13 and 21.16; and property measurement in Sections 16 and 17.

Paragraph 2.9 permits caution under uncertainty but expressly does not permit deliberate understatement or bias. This clarification is a repository technical note, not an alteration of the Companies House document.

Third-party misuse and correction rule

Any use of the £1 figure must include its provisional, prudential and non-valuation context. Statements such as “the hotel was valued at £1”, “the group admitted it had no value”, “there were no damages”, “rights were waived” or “Companies House confirmed a £1 value” are not supported by the filing merely because £1 appears on the balance sheet.

  1. Preserve the exact statement, author, date and forum.
  2. Quote the full filing qualification and distinguish carrying amount from market value, recoverable amount and claim recognition.
  3. Request a written correction where the qualification was omitted or the figure was converted into a substantive valuation conclusion.
  4. Escalate to legal and accounting advisers where the use affects lending, concurso, calificación, tax, enforcement, regulation or reputation.

Misunderstanding is not automatically bad faith. Intent must be evidenced.
