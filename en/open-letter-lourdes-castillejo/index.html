OPEN LETTER · 16 AUGUST 2026

Lourdes Castillejo: who asked to control Sun Park's digital identity?

Your name appears in a documented sequence of requests concerning Sun Park's Google Business Profile during the transition toward MYND. I do not treat it as proved that you personally made every request. That is exactly why I am asking you to help clarify what happened.

This letter asks verifiable questions. It is not a finding of guilt.

If you acted under legitimate corporate instructions, explain who instructed you and what title was relied upon. If someone else used your name, say so. If you know relevant facts learned in a professional context, lawful reporting routes exist when their requirements are met.

What is documented

28 October 2021Google associates an ownership request for Sun Park with Lourdes Castillejo, role “Business owner”, using mynd.hotels@gmail.com.
24 February 2022A further preserved request again shows Lourdes Castillejo, now as “Employee”, using the same account.
20 June 2022Preserved material records another request as “MYND Hotels, Employee”, again linked to mynd.hotels@gmail.com.

That documents the association made by Google. It does not yet identify the physical user, the controller of the account, the author of instructions, the IP address used, the documents supplied or the entity that authorised use of your name.

There is a caution that we ourselves recorded in February 2022. The possibility was expressly considered that a third party may have used your name without your consent. That possibility should be resolved through evidence, not assumption.

What I am asking you to clarify

  • Were you the person who submitted or authenticated the October 2021 and February 2022 requests?
  • Who controlled mynd.hotels@gmail.com and who could access it?
  • Which entity were you representing on each date: MYND, Canarian Hospitality, Hotel New Trend, CAM or another entity?
  • Who instructed you and what ownership, authorisation or operating documents were used?
  • What access was obtained and what profile name, website, telephone, photographs, status, links or categories were changed?
  • Were historical Sun Park digital assets migrated or reused?
  • If you did not make one or more requests, who used your name?
There is another related incident, but it is separate.

The June 2019 sunpark264@gmail.com episode — a customer email and a Google recovery clue compatible with @acostamatos.com — has its own dossier. I do not allege that you knew about that episode or that the two incidents had the same author.

This is no longer only a Google question

The later MYND Yaiza chronology is separately documented across layers involving RICPE investment and RIC, regional incentives and FEDER. Those layers must remain legally distinct.

The Lourdes / MYND incident matters as a question of provenance and due diligence: what asset and commercial platform were being represented, who could represent continuity to customers, what title existed and what information later reached investors, managers, financiers or public authorities. A Google request does not prove that later funding or incentives were improper.

If you know the chain, you can help clarify it

I am not asking you to adopt our interpretation. I am asking for precision: what you did, what you were asked to do, what you were told, what you knew, what you did not know and what records can verify it.

If you hold relevant information, use a lawful and competent route.

Spain's Law 2/2023 protects, within its requirements and limits, certain people who report specified infringements learned in a work or professional context and prohibits retaliation in the circumstances provided by law.

I am not asking you to steal documents, enter systems without authorisation, unlawfully copy databases or give me company secrets. Preserve intact any originals you already lawfully possess, do not alter metadata and seek independent advice if needed.
