CRIMINAL-FIRST · GIL MARER THESIS · NOT A CONVICTION

AC intervention → control of LPB's procedural voice → discontinuance

The new primary-source set prevents GC-CIV-003 from being reduced to “it was archived”. The actor-specific, falsifiable question is: what authority did the insolvency administrator/liquidator invoke to terminate LPB's professional relationship and condition further filings in a diligence whose purpose was to compel CAM to disclose the price, payment date and costs of the credit assignment, and how did that control lead into a formal LPB discontinuance to which CAM consented?

Gil Marer's public position. Gil Marer says this sequence requires criminal investigation, first as a possible standalone act of procedural instrumentalisation: an AC intervention in LPB's representation and procedural control followed by termination of the retracto/information route LPB had obtained against CAM. He also says it must be examined as part of a unitary before-during-after-concurso sequence in which, on his allegation, acts and omissions by different actors operated in a manner functionally favourable to the private perimeter he historically identifies as Molina/Montalanza-Molina and, later, Acosta Matos.

Gil alleges the practical consequence was contrary to LPB's objective of obtaining the economic documentation of the assignment and potentially favourable to CAM because the judicial mechanism designed to obtain it ended. The present record proves the documentary sequence and procedural effect of the discontinuance; it does not yet prove criminal intent, concert, personal authorship of LPB's discontinuance filing, or an instruction by any named private-perimeter individual to the AC.

Documented

  • LPB obtained an Auto requiring CAM to produce the assignment deed and its economic terms.
  • CAM was cited/required and opposed the diligence.
  • On 25-Jan the AC/liquidator communicated termination of LPB's existing professional relationship, requested venia and instructed no further filings without his express consent.
  • Decree 139/2018 records LPB's discontinuance, CAM's consent and dismissal; the 25-Apr hearing was suspended.

To prove or exclude

  • Who drafted, signed and filed LPB's discontinuance and under what authority.
  • What happened on 19-Feb and whether CAM had already provided some or all of the information.
  • The exact contents of CAM filing 1222/18.
  • Any communication, instruction, agreement or coordination between the AC and private-perimeter persons.
  • Intent, unlawful purpose, causal prejudice and individual private benefit.
  • Service, review and finality of the 5-Mar Decree.
