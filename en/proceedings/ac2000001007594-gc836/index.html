Why repetition must be tested atomically

Dynamic

Payment/liquidation, employment maintenance, incidents, corrections and recoveries may generate later records.

Effective delivery

A favourable decision does not by itself prove which annex was actually made available.

Separability

Partial repetition should not contaminate the remainder.

Index and custody

Existing indexes, metadata and search/referral records are not a request for a new audit.

Property and criminal context

Gil Marer alleges that rights/assets belonging to or asserted by LPB, Matkator and Aweswell may have been incorporated, represented or economically leveraged inside the Sun Park/MYND Yaiza perimeter without their knowledge or consent, and that the same asset later became relevant to private financing, RIC/RICPE, GC/836/P06 and advertised FEDER support. This is a hypothesis to prove or disprove from records, not a published criminal conclusion.

CTBG is not asked to decide fraud, asset stripping, falsification, civil title, insolvency liability or corruption. It is asked whether access can be blocked as globally repetitive without showing what was requested, decided and effectively delivered.

Employment: 60 jobs against the ERTE/ERE history

The 60-job condition should be read against the labour history of the same hotel: ERTE/ERE, dismissals and Social Court proceedings. The reconstruction asks who was employer/operator, who took decisions and what material influence was exercised by the Community/PAMANIL/PAMALEXSHA/FMMM perimeter and later CAM/HNT/RICPE/MYND.

Boundary: actor continuity alone does not prove that a person criminally caused the historic workforce loss or that later job creation was fraudulent.

Cadastre: did any alarm reach the system?

For 5137001FS1953N, follow-up should ask —without requesting protected identities— whether complaints, objections, rectification requests, internal alerts, protected-reporting communications or referrals existed concerning title, units, representation, alteration or availability. Request only non-identifying institutional metadata.
