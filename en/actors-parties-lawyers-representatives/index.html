A1–A8 · CONTINUITY OF THE DEFENCE · CONCURSO 36/2012

Our-side counsel: work, pressure on the defence and documented effect

This table records the verifiable sequence for lawyers and firms acting for Gil Marer, Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. (LPB), Aweswell Limited or the related perimeter. Gil Marer and Patricia Domínguez contend that acts and omissions of the Insolvency Administrator (IA) interfered, directly or cumulatively, with that defence. Their position is not presented as a judicial finding. Each mandate is told in its full context: accepted and billed scope, IA conduct, the professional response or limit, the result, and any later deterioration or transition. A formal reason for withdrawal belongs in that history, but does not by itself exhaust the IA’s prior effect or the effectiveness of the mandate.

Precision rule: the 19 February 2020 providencia granted the prior procedural licence required by Article 215.2 of the Criminal Code; it did not decide whether calumny or insult occurred, and does not prove that a complaint was later filed, admitted or upheld. Its procedural character does not erase the documented practical effect on the defence team. Describing the initiative as “abusive” is Gil/Patricia’s express position, not a disciplinary or criminal determination.

Two connected tracks, without merging proof and allegation. On the asset track, the record documents an active finance-backed exit, suspension of realisation of the mortgaged apartments and the IA’s position that the premises branch fell outside that suspension. On 28 November 2018 the IA conveyed 31 properties—reception, premises and pools/solarium—to CAM for €400,000, against an ACTÚA appraisal of €489,361.96. The IA later reported that the cheque had become effective and that he had paid his own remuneration; the 24 October 2019 order held that the deed had been executed without judicial authorisation and refused convalidation. On the defence track, the May–June 2020 Parrilla–Jiménez transition included a promise of “peace”, possible withdrawal of an action, assurance of payment to outgoing counsel and the procurator, praise of Parrilla and presentation of IA/CAM positions. Gil/Patricia call that second track a “false peace” and a possible perverse incentive intended to fracture trust and influence the handover. They do not present bribery as proved: bank statements and the estate ledger have not yet established that the later €26,750 Parrilla payment came specifically from the €400,000.
