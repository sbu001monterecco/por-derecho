Material actors are named in full. Responsibility is attributed act by act.

This register prevents initials, corporate changes or professional titles from obscuring who acted. It also prevents the opposite error: inclusion here does not automatically make anyone responsible for unlawful conduct.

Public rule: verified full name on first reference; initials only afterwards. Lawyer for a party ≠ automatic co-author. Witness ≠ adverse counsel. Challenged judge or public official ≠ private actor. Protected private witness identities remain reserved.

This table records the verifiable sequence for lawyers and firms acting for Gil Marer, Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. (LPB), Aweswell Limited or the related perimeter. Gil Marer and Patricia Domínguez contend that acts and omissions of the Insolvency Administrator (IA) interfered, directly or cumulatively, with that defence. Their position is not presented as a judicial finding: documented fact, the client’s contemporaneous account, the professional’s response and still-disputed causation are kept separate.

Precision rule: refusal of documents, judicial authority to bring a complaint, client-decided substitution and professional withdrawal are different events. The 19 February 2020 order authorised a possible complaint; it does not prove that one was filed, admitted or upheld. Describing the initiative as “abusive” is Gil/Patricia’s express position, not a disciplinary or criminal determination.
