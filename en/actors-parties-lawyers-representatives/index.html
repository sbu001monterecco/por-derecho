This register prevents initials, corporate changes or professional titles from obscuring who acted. It also prevents the opposite error: inclusion here does not automatically make anyone responsible for unlawful conduct.

Public rule: verified full name on first reference; initials only afterwards. Lawyer for a party ≠ automatic co-author. Witness ≠ adverse counsel. Challenged judge or public official ≠ private actor. Protected private witness identities remain reserved.
