Why this page exists

An employment-only answer no longer closes the perimeter.

The record contains three classes of information that need to be reconciled: a commercial relationship acknowledged by Grant Thornton Spain with a partner company located in the Canary Islands; public professional presentations and use of Grant Thornton-associated infrastructure; and a 2020 record in which Grant Thornton Advisory expressly knew the insolvency administrator's name and the CAM perimeter before receiving confidential Aweswell/Sun Park information.

Transparency standard requested. A professional-services firm may legitimately distinguish between employee, partner, contractor, collaborator, Senior Advisor and independent professional. That is precisely why a traceable answer should identify the legal entity, relationship type, scope of work, systems used, supervision, conflict controls and responsibility perimeter, rather than stopping at “not employee/not partner”.

Potential professional responsibility

Spanish Law 2/2007 makes the act, capacity and entity material.

Article 11 of Law 2/2007 provides, for social debts arising from professional acts, joint and several responsibility of the professional company and the professionals—members or not—who acted, subject to the applicable contractual or extra-contractual rules. Additional Provision 2 extends the regime to certain forms of collective professional activity under a common denomination.

This is not a finding of shared liability. The statute does not automatically make Cuyás Abogados, Grant Thornton Canarias, Grant Thornton Spain or GTIL liable for an insolvency administrator's acts. It remains necessary to prove, act by act, that relevant professional conduct was carried out by, for, through or within the professional perimeter of the entity or collective body said to bear responsibility.

Publication and recovery test: document → professional act → capacity → legal entity → authority/supervision → duty → damage → causation → applicable liability rule → evidence gap.
