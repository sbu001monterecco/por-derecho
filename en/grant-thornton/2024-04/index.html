Statement-quality issue

“Does not work for the Firm in any capacity” is broader than “not employee/not partner”.

On 21 December 2024, GTIL stated, on behalf of its legal team, that Grant Thornton Spain had confirmed Francisco de Borja Rodríguez-Batllori Laffitte was neither partner nor employee and “does not work for the Firm in any capacity”. The same message said the Firm was taking action to have allegedly misleading LinkedIn information amended.

On 8 January 2025, GT Spain's own Internal Information System added a material fact: Rodríguez-Batllori provided services on a commercial basis to a Grant Thornton partner company in Spain located in the Canary Islands.

Limited conclusion. This record does not show that the December statement was deliberately false. But unless “the Firm” and “any capacity” are explained at legal-entity level, the categorical wording is potentially materially incomplete and capable of misleading a reader about the later-acknowledged professional/commercial relationship. A high transparency standard calls for an express correction or reconciliation.

Potential professional responsibility

Shared responsibility is framed as a testable legal hypothesis, not a conclusion.

Spanish Law 2/2007 provides for joint and several responsibility of a professional company and the professionals who acted in respect of certain debts deriving from professional acts, and extends the regime to certain collective professional activity under a common denomination. Applying that regime here first requires proof of the professional act, capacity, entity, authority/supervision, damage and causation.

Control rule: branding, an email domain or office address does not establish liability. It does justify requiring the reconstruction: document → professional act → capacity → entity → authority/supervision → duty → damage → causation → applicable rule.
