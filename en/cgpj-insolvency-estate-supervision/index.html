INSOLVENCY 36/2012 · ASSET PROTECTION · ADMINISTRATOR SUPERVISION

What did judicial supervision do when the principal asset needed protection before the outcome became irreversible?

This page does not claim that the judge was the day-to-day manager of the hotel or that every Administrator omission is attributable to the Judge. It reconstructs a narrower question: when a risk reached the Administrator or Court, what reporting/control duty or power existed, what verifiable response followed, and what happened next.

The correct institutional relationship

Insolvency estate → Administrator conservation duty → judicial supervision → specific information where needed → liquidation decisions → final accounting and traceability.

Current insolvency legislation states that the Insolvency Administration is subject to supervision by the insolvency judge and allows the judge to require specific information on matters connected with the case. It also provides for removal for just cause and, in the current wording, serious failures of diligence, impartiality or independence. Historical events must be judged under the law in force at each date; the current provisions are used only to describe the institutional supervision architecture.

Limit: supervising the Administrator does not mean replacing him in daily management. But it also does not make the court irrelevant once a risk enters the record and a specific information, conservation, inspection or control measure is requested.

Statutory framework: TRLC Article 82, judicial supervision; TRLC Articles 100–103, removal, replacement, accounts and appeals. The applicable version and transitional rules must be checked for each act and proceeding.

Seven nuclei that should be read together

1 · June–September 2018

Funded exit, material control of the hotel, need to preserve and quantify. Daniel Irigoyen is a contemporaneous professional source for testing what was explained, requested and reflected in the court file. An exit proposal must be assessed against its conditions, funding and actual ability to complete; it is not presented as a completed sale.

2 · Debt / threshold

Recognised debt, interest, improvement threshold and amount ultimately absorbed are distinct categories. The control question is which act connects each one.

3 · €400,000 / non-validation

The 2019 ruling requires this reconstruction to compare its operative terms with what followed: ownership, possession, money, inventory, accounting and any new title or restitution. The legal consequence cannot be inferred from the label “non-validation” alone.

4 · Physical and operating condition

If alteration, blocked access or deterioration is alleged and expert/judicial inspection is refused, identify the reasons for refusal and what evidence, if any, supported the decision on the disputed fact. Do not assume that every refusal required a substitute expert or inspection.

5 · Administrator supervision

What he knew, what he reported, what the Court required, and what alternative control existed when removal or protective measures did not succeed.

6 · 2021 competition

ONA/third-party bidder are assessed through material equality: access, information, perimeter, time, solvency, deposits and comparability of heterogeneous consideration.

7 · 2022+ implementation

Adjudication, court-office acts, deed, cancellation of charges, Registry, post-deed communication and final accounts should form a single reproducible chain.

DOCUMENTARY COMPARISON · NO PRESUMPTION OF IRREGULARITY

What was authorised and what was actually implemented

Reliance on a judicial authorisation requires identifying what it decided and the information on which it proceeded. The relevant comparison is between its terms and later implementation: properties and rights, consideration, payments, accounting and effects on third parties. The authorisation is not disregarded; nor is its scope enlarged to answer questions it did not decide. Any alleged departure must be established through the corresponding documents and assessed against the contrary explanation.

Three verification fields for every row

Official record: document, date, entry/receipt and recipient establishing what entered the file; the existence of private correspondence alone does not prove judicial knowledge. Identifiable response: act, signatory, date, scope and service, or a documented search still without a result; “not located” does not mean “non-existent”. Competent route: obtaining/certifying the record, inspection of functioning or duties, judicial appeal, or liability through the appropriate route. Unverified fields remain open and are not completed by inference.

An identifiable institutional request, not a substitute judicial appeal

The institutional request seeks a verifiable account of the receipt, allocation, transmission and response to specific risks and requests, and identification of matters within the receiving body's powers. Challenges to the legal correctness of judicial decisions belong to the applicable judicial remedies. A governance or inspection request does not replace them, automatically suspend their deadlines or itself alter what was decided.

The LOPJ distinguishes inspection of functioning and compliance with duties from review of the judicial interpretation and application of law. That distinction permits precise requests for records and responses without converting supervision into an indiscriminate appeal. The factual relationship between private conduct, the Administrator's conduct and judicial acts is preserved; knowledge, competence and responsibility are individualised.

The double perimeter question

Was what belonged to the estate protected sufficiently?

LPB and its property/rights were inside the insolvency. Preservation and value of the productive/hotel unit could be affected by access, works, security, income, possession and loss of competitive value.

Did limited insolvency authority project too far?

Matkator and other owners, third-party rights and certain operating positions were not automatically absorbed. An act originating in the insolvency could have material effects outside the estate without creating title over those assets.

Economic interdependence does not merge legal ownership. Alleged harm is traced separately through LPB's estate, operating rights and other affected entities, while explaining how interference with one component could affect the hotel business as a whole. Gil Marer and the affected entities maintain their position as directly interested and affected parties reporting significant ongoing harm to the business and assets of AWESWELL LIMITED, a UK investor in Spain. Separately and concurrently, Gil maintains his stated informant/whistleblower capacity under potentially applicable frameworks, including Spain's Ley 2/2023. Each basis of standing and protection requires its own foundation; the personal informant capacity is not automatically attributed to every entity.

Where relevant, rights concerning public patrimonial liability under Articles 32–37 of Ley 40/2015 are reserved, separately from the specific regimes concerning the administration of justice. This reservation, an access request or an informant communication is not itself a formal compensation claim or evidence that a limitation period has been interrupted.

What this file does not establish

It does not establish judicial prevaricación, corruption, collusion or disciplinary responsibility. Nor does it assert that missing supervisory records do not exist. Its function is to enable and request verifiable institutional reconciliation: if the act exists, identify it; if no action was legally required, explain why; if the risk belonged elsewhere, classify and route it correctly.

Editorial clarification of 28 September 2026. It does not alter historical pleadings, file documents with any authority or turn externally preserved material into admitted evidence. Existing procedural-utility and evidence-status map.
