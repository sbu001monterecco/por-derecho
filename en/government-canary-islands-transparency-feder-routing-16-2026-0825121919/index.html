OFFICIAL ADMINISTRATIVE NOTICE · TRANSPARENCY · FEDER / REGIONAL INCENTIVES

Government of the Canary Islands routes the Sun Park / MYND Yaiza transparency request to the competent Regional Incentives body.

On 26 August 2026, the Government recorded and routed transparency file 16/2026-0825121919, expressly describing it as a request concerning the FEDER operation publicised at Hotel Sun Park, subsequently marketed as MYND Yaiza.

WHAT THE NOTICE ESTABLISHES

Administrative description and competent-body routing

The notice states that the request entered the Public Information Unit of the Ministry of Finance and Relations with the European Union on 25 August 2026, registration number 1772826/2026. It says that the requested Regional Incentives information was not held by that department and that, under Article 44.1 LTAIP, the request was forwarded to the Directorate-General for Economic Promotion and Diversification, attached to the Ministry of Economy, Industry, Trade and the Self-Employed.

Institutional route documented by the notice:
Government of the Canary Islands → Finance / EU Relations → Directorate-General for Economic Promotion and Diversification → Regional Incentives / FEDER inquiry → Hotel Sun Park → MYND Yaiza.
