Control 24: criminal complaint / notitia criminis concerning acts and rulings of the judge in Insolvency 36/2012.

The located controlling package is addressed to the Civil and Criminal Chamber of the High Court of Justice of the Canary Islands and concerns Magistrate-Judge Alberto López Villarrubia. Its own cover defines it as a written complaint and notitia criminis, not a formal querella or formal appearance.

Later official status: pending primary confirmation

Status control: “Control 24” is a material/handwritten presentation reference. It is not, by itself, a NIG, Diligencias Previas number, allocation, opening, admission, dismissal or formal TSJC case number. Por Derecho does not assign any of those states without a primary official source.

Architecture of the filed package

Principal document

A 31-page direct criminal complaint within the 79-page unified package.

It asks for documentary verification before a criminal conclusion and preserves lawful alternative explanations.

Selected annexes

They include material on the 2018 funded exit, 2018–2021 rulings, the 2019 non-validation, procedural communications and tender/adjudication decisions.

Several annexes are extracts and the complaint itself asks for complete certified testimony.

Five documentary nuclei in Control 24

1 · Funded exit and knowledge

The question is not whether the Court was required to approve a proposal. It is what formally reached the court, what useful act followed, what figures were required and what measure preserved a payment/deposit route before the asset and opportunity changed materially.

2 · Debt, threshold and res judicata

The recognised claim must be distinguished from figures used for a third party to improve the dación proposal, and any later act giving each amount a final function must be located.

3 · OB REM operation and non-validation

The 28/11/2018 deed, later validation request and 24/10/2019 ruling require reconstruction of any restitution, regularisation, new title, accounting entry or Registry effect that actually followed.

4 · Tender, competition and adjudication

Evidence should allow comparison of publicity, access, time, deposit, solvency, object, economic comparability and treatment of ONA/Ottawa/third parties, without assuming any third-party interest was a closed offer.

5 · Identity of creditor/bidder/adjudicatee

Company names must be attributed to the exact legal person in each ruling, deed, offer, entry or act. A common brand or surname does not merge separate legal persons.

Evidence rule

A contradiction, adverse ruling or apparent omission does not by itself establish judicial prevaricación. The analysis requires a specific ruling or duty, legally qualified injustice/arbitrariness, knowledge, causation and testing of alternative explanations.

Supplement of 25 June 2026

The supplement was framed to join the 18 June complaint. It particularly refines the alleged change of function of certain figures: from amounts used to set a third-party improvement threshold to amounts later treated as final or binding. It does not assert that no reconciling act exists; it asks for that act to be located and certified.

It also requests an economic/documentary reconciliation distinguishing recognised claim, mortgage liability, interest, competitive threshold, consideration, extinguished/waived debt and any surplus.

Date traceability: Control 24 originates on 18/06/2026; the supplement's actual entry date is 25/06/2026. The reported administrative joining does not replace the second date.
