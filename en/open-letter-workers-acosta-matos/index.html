OPEN LETTER · 16 AUGUST 2026

To everyone who works or has worked at Construcciones Acosta Matos and Acosta Matos Group companies

If you know a fact, an instruction, an account, a file, a meeting, a decision or an explanation that could help establish what happened, I am not asking you to choose a side. I am asking you not to destroy the truth and to use the lawful reporting routes available to you.

Employment is not responsibility.

Working for a company does not make a person responsible for decisions taken by its owners, directors or managers. Nor do we assume every employee knows something relevant. But anyone who does know should understand that lawful reporting channels exist and that Spain's Law 2/2023 provides protection against retaliation when its requirements are met.

My name is Gil Marer. For years I have assembled and submitted documentation concerning Sun Park, Construcciones Acosta Matos and later business and financial structures appearing in this record. This letter has a specific purpose: to address people who were inside those organisations and may know matters that outsiders can only reconstruct in part.

An explanation confirming a fact matters. An explanation disproving or correcting it matters too. What is needed is verifiable evidence.

A concrete example: sunpark264@gmail.com

On 6 June 2019, a former Sun Park customer received an email from SUN PARK <sunpark264@gmail.com> telling him to remove his belongings from the complex because it would shortly be refurbished. The customer forwarded the message to the official address and explained that he and others had written their email addresses on their luggage because they had been instructed to do so.

A Google account-recovery screen for sunpark264@gmail.com was preserved that day. Google's own interface displayed the partially masked clue ros••••••@aco••••••••.com.

The evidential boundary matters. The masked string is provider-generated data and is compatible with the domain acostamatos.com. It does not reveal the full email address, identify a person named Rosa, or by itself prove who created the account, who controlled it at any given time or who wrote the customer email. During the contemporaneous check a complete address under that domain was manually typed into the form; that value was user input and must not be treated as information confirmed by Google.

To anyone who can explain that recovery route

To any current or former worker, system administrator, marketing employee, adviser or director who knows why Google displayed that recovery clue:

I am not saying that any named person sent that message. I am not saying CAM or any particular person created or controlled the account. The source does not yet support that attribution.

I am asking something simpler and more important: if you know what recovery address was actually configured, who administered the account or why the clue was compatible with acostamatos.com, go to a competent authority and explain what you know.

Who configured the account? Who held its credentials? Who decided to use the name SUN PARK? Where did former customers' contact data come from? Who had access to the luggage or its inventory? Who instructed contact with those customers? What emails, devices, backups or records can establish a lawful explanation or an irregularity?

If there is an innocent explanation, that explanation also deserves to be documented. If someone knows different facts, that matters too.

To every employee: if you know something, you can report it

Spain's Law 2/2023 of 20 February protects, within its scope, people who obtained information about specified infringements in a work or professional context. It includes employees and can include former employees, the self-employed and people working for contractors, subcontractors and suppliers. Protection requires, among other things, reasonable grounds to believe the information was true when reported and within the law's scope.

The law expressly prohibits retaliation, including threats and attempted retaliation. Reporting channels can allow anonymous reports and the informant's identity must be protected under the statutory regime.

The internal system is the preferred route when the matter can be addressed effectively and the informant considers there is no risk of retaliation. It does not have to be exhausted before a person can report directly through an external channel.

Law 2/2023 in the Spanish Official Gazette ↗ · External channel of the Independent Authority for the Protection of Informants (AIPI) ↗

What you can do now

  1. Preserve. Keep intact originals that you already lawfully possess or may lawfully access: emails, messages, calendars, files, minutes, invoices, orders, photographs or logs. Do not alter metadata, delete material or “clean” devices.
  2. Write a factual chronology. Separate what you personally saw or heard from what others told you. Record dates, people, systems, filenames and where the original can be found.
  3. Use the internal reporting channel or Internal Information System if one exists and you consider it safe and effective. Law 2/2023 requires an internal system in various cases, including private entities with 50 or more workers. This page does not publish an Acosta Matos URL because we have not currently verified a specific public corporate reporting channel.
  4. Go directly to the competent external channel. The AIPI operates a secure and confidential external channel, including anonymous reporting. Exact competence can depend on territorial scope; a regional authority may be competent where the matter is confined to one autonomous community.
  5. If the facts may constitute a crime, report them to the National Police, Guardia Civil, Public Prosecutor or the competent court. Law 2/2023 itself provides for immediate referral to the Public Prosecutor when reported facts may indicatively constitute a criminal offence.
  6. If the information specifically affects EU funds or the Union's financial interests, say so expressly. Additional specialist national or European channels may apply; the receiving authority should assess competence and referral.
  7. If you fear retaliation, document it from the beginning. Preserve messages, changes of duties, threats, disciplinary measures or other harm you believe is connected with reporting, and seek independent advice.
  8. If you may personally face liability, speak to an independent lawyer before making a statement. Law 2/2023 protects qualifying informants, but it is not a general criminal immunity and it does not make criminal acquisition of information lawful.
What you should not do

Do not enter accounts or systems without authorisation. Do not take devices. Do not unlawfully copy databases. Do not manufacture evidence. Do not coordinate stories. Do not destroy or alter originals. Do not disclose unnecessary personal data. Reporting does not require committing another infringement.

If an internal channel exists, this is what it is for

An internal information system is not supposed to identify who “spoke” so that they can be punished. The law requires confidentiality, security and effective handling. Where an entity is required to have an internal system, its governing body is responsible for implementing it, and information about reporting routes must be clear and accessible under the applicable regime.

This letter therefore also asks Construcciones Acosta Matos and every company described as part of the Acosta Matos Group that is legally required to maintain such a system: what is your Internal Information System, who is responsible for it, and how can a current or former worker use it today without fear of retaliation?

I am not asking for loyalty to me

I am not even asking you to agree with our interpretation of the record. I am asking for loyalty to the facts.

If an instruction was lawful, say so. If an account had an ordinary explanation, document it. If somebody else made a decision, identify the source that proves it. If something we have published is wrong, produce evidence that corrects it. And if you witnessed conduct you believe an authority should know about, do not assume silence is your only option.

The Sun Park record illustrates why apparently small details matter: a recovery clue, a customer email, an instruction, an access log or a file version can help establish who knew what, when, and with what authority.

Anyone with direct knowledge: take the lawful step

My invitation is explicit. Go to the Police, Guardia Civil, Public Prosecutor, competent court, the external informant-protection channel or, where safe and effective, your company's internal channel. State only what you know. Provide only what you lawfully possess. Ask for a record of the report. Keep the receipt or communication code. And if your position is sensitive, obtain independent legal advice.

The point is not to help me. The point is to get evidence to a place where it can be preserved, tested and, where appropriate, investigated.

Gil Marer
