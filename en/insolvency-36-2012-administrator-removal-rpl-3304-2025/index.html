EXECUTIVE FINDING

Two appellants, one controversy

Aweswell Limited (PD-SP-O-0001^) initiated the removal application. Luchy Playa Blanca, S.L.U. (PD-SP-O-0002^), the insolvent company itself, also and independently challenged the refusal. LPB is not an informal supporter: it is an appellant in RPL 3304/2025. After accumulation, both challenges converge procedurally in that roll.

This page separates established procedural fact, party allegation, inference/reconstruction, appellate contention and unresolved responsibility. Criminal or intentional allegations are not presented as adjudicated facts.

What Aweswell actually requested

The application sought, among other relief, removal under article 100 TRLC, a replacement appointment under article 101 TRLC, rendition of accounts, notice to the Public Insolvency Register, communication of accounting material to AEAT and communication/referral to the Public Prosecutor where pleaded. These are remedies requested, not remedies already granted or facts adjudicated.

The controlled first-instance orders decided standing and did not comprehensively adjudicate the seven pleaded factual blocks one by one. “The application was rejected” cannot be converted into “the seven factual grounds were judicially found false.”

Seven pleaded factual grounds

Accounts / rendition of accounts

Alleged prolonged failures in annual-account preparation, filing and accounting traceability.

Financial risk / swap

Alleged inaction or obstruction concerning the 2008 financial-risk contract and a possible estate claim.

Credit-assignment price / retract

Alleged insufficient access to the true price and full terms of the credit assignment and resulting strategic consequences.

Dación en pago / fiscal valuation

Allegations concerning valuation, perimeter, authority and estate protection in the transfer/adjudication transaction.

Fragmentation of the hotel operating unit

Alleged failure to protect the integrated hotel operation and third-party rights outside the estate.

Remuneration

Allegations concerning remuneration and interest; materially connected to, but procedurally separate from, the fee route and RPL 421/2026.

Opacity / conflicts

Alleged information asymmetries, relationships and conflicts said to affect transparency and independence.

LPB also appeals

LPB filed its own challenge to the refusal. Its appeal is RPL 3304/2025. That means the insolvent company itself is challenging the first-instance decision. It does not automatically validate every Aweswell allegation, but it prevents the matter from being portrayed as an extinguished unilateral Aweswell initiative.

The Master Register distinguishes GC-APP-005 / PD-SP-R-0011^ for LPB and GC-APP-006 / PD-SP-R-0012^ for Aweswell. GC-APP-007 / PD-SP-R-0013 is a continuity family reference, not a third appeal.

The judicial organ is also part of the contested history

This cannot be presented as a dispute isolated between Aweswell and the Insolvency Administrator. Judicial decisions, alleged omissions, timing and treatment of the removal application form part of the appealed procedural itinerary. They must be separated into: what the Administrator did or allegedly failed to do; what Aweswell requested; what LPB challenges; what the court decided; what reasoning or acts are challenged; and what remains for the Provincial Court.

Legal qualification: including judicial conduct does not presume criminal guilt or replace a judicial determination of responsibility. It prevents treating the court as an external observer when its decisions and procedural treatment form part of the documentary sequence under appeal.
