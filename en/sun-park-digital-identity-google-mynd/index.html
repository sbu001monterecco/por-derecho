Sun Park: customer email, a recovery clue compatible with @acostamatos.com, and later MYND requests

The preserved record now supports a more precise digital chain: a Gmail account presenting itself to Sun Park customers in 2019; a Google-generated recovery clue for that same account compatible with the acostamatos.com domain; and several ownership/access requests concerning Sun Park's Google Business Profile associated with Lourdes Castillejo or MYND in 2021–2022. The connection warrants technical investigation. It does not yet establish a common author or controller.

The digital chronology

6 June 2019 · `SUN PARK <sunpark264@gmail.com>` writes to a customer

A preserved copy records a message sent under the name SUN PARK from sunpark264@gmail.com. It asks a historical customer to remove belongings from the complex because Sun Park would shortly be refurbished.

The historical operation's reply challenges the legitimacy of that Gmail address on the same day and asks that the information be preserved. The customer explains that he and others had written their email addresses on their luggage after being instructed to do so. This creates questions about source of contact data, custody of belongings, recipients and authority to speak as Sun Park.

Limit: the preserved copy establishes the content and address displayed in the preserved exchange; technical attribution of the sender requires the native message, headers and provider data.

6 June 2019 · Google displays the recovery clue for `sunpark264@gmail.com`

In a preserved Google account-recovery capture for that account, the system asks the user to confirm the recovery email address previously added and displays this partially masked pattern:

ros••••••@aco••••••••.com

Fact: that masked string is shown by Google in the preserved screen. Inference: the pattern is compatible with the domain acostamatos.com. Limit: it does not reveal the complete address or identify the person or entity that created, controlled or used the account.

During the contemporaneous check a full address under the acostamatos.com domain was typed into the form. That was input by the person conducting the test and must not be confused with a value confirmed by Google.

2019 · a second alleged message family remains without a native source

The complainant identifies another communication said to have stated that the Sun Park business had ceased and to have given instructions for collecting belongings. The native message has not been located in the controlled corpus.

We therefore do not publish that wording as an authenticated document. It remains a retrieval route.

28 October 2021 · Google Business ownership request

Google sent a direct notification to the existing Sun Park profile holder. The request was associated with Lourdes Castillejo, role “Business owner”, and mynd.hotels@gmail.com.

The notification proves the association made by Google's system. It does not yet identify the physical user who submitted the request.

24 February 2022 · second request

A further preserved notification again shows Lourdes Castillejo, now as “Employee”, using the same Gmail account.

Contemporaneous correspondence of 25 February expressly records an alternative possibility: someone else may have used her name without consent. That possibility remains open.

20 June 2022 · `MYND Hotels, Employee`

Preserved Google material records another ownership request associated with MYND Hotels, role “Employee”, again using mynd.hotels@gmail.com.

The chain that can now safely be stated

2019 → 2021–2022: verifiable functional continuity.

sunpark264@gmail.com → Sun Park customer → Google recovery clue compatible with @acostamatos.com → Lourdes/MYND + mynd.hotels@gmail.com → requests to control the Sun Park Google Business Profile

The connection matters because both source families concern the ability to speak as Sun Park, reach its customers or control its public digital representation. The 2019 recovery clue also introduces a technically verifiable link requiring confirmation with the Acosta Matos perimeter.

But that chain does not yet prove common authorship. It does not establish that CAM controlled the account, that any named person sent the message, that Lourdes knew about the 2019 episode, or that Canarian Hospitality, HNT, MYND and CAM automatically shared instructions or intent.

What a technical investigation should obtain

  • Google confirmation of the exact recovery email configured for `sunpark264@gmail.com` on 6 June 2019 and its change history.
  • Lawfully obtainable creation, recovery, session, IP and device data for `sunpark264@gmail.com` and `mynd.hotels@gmail.com`.
  • Native RFC822/EML and complete headers for the 2019 message and any related campaign.
  • Request identifiers for the October-2021, February-2022 and June-2022 Google Business requests.
  • Permission and edit history for the Sun Park profile: name, website, telephone, photographs, categories, status and links.
  • The represented legal entity and documentary authority supplied for each request.
  • Account-administration and instruction records held by CAM, HNT, Canarian Hospitality and MYND, keeping those entities legally distinct.
  • The source — to be determined, not presumed — of any historical customer list, luggage-contact data, PMS or reservation information used for contact.

Finite questions for Lourdes

The open letter to Lourdes Castillejo now also incorporates this 2019 evidence. It does not presume she knew about it. It asks whether she can identify who controlled mynd.hotels@gmail.com, who instructed the Sun Park profile requests, which entity was represented and whether she knew of sunpark264@gmail.com or a recovery route linked to the acostamatos.com domain.

From digital identity to the economic chain

Sun Park was not only real estate. The continuity record concerns customers, reputation, data, bookings, commercial relationships, physical access, project preparation, commercialisation and later finance and investment.

This dossier therefore connects to the material-control chronology, the Acosta Matos perimeter, the RICPE trail and the RIC, regional-incentives and FEDER chain.

The connection means that provenance of the asset and commercial platform may matter to later due diligence, investment or finance. It does not mean that a digital irregularity, if proved, automatically renders later funding irregular.
