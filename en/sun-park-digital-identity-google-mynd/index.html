Sun Park's digital identity also changed — but exact authorship remains open

The preserved record contains two distinct source families: a Gmail account presenting itself to customers as Sun Park in 2019, and several ownership/access requests concerning Sun Park's Google Business Profile associated with Lourdes Castillejo or MYND in 2021–2022. They can be investigated together as a commercial digital-identity control question. They cannot simply be merged into a finding of common authorship or collusion.

The digital chronology

6 June 2019 · `SUN PARK <sunpark264@gmail.com>`

A preserved copy of an exchange with a former customer records a message sent under the name SUN PARK from sunpark264@gmail.com, asking the customer to remove belongings because the complex would shortly be refurbished.

The customer's reply records that he and others had written their email addresses on their luggage after being instructed to do so. This creates questions about the source of contact data, custody of belongings, recipients and authority to speak as Sun Park.

Limit: the preserved copy provides a concrete evidential route, but technical attribution requires native mail, headers and provider data.

2019 · a second alleged message family remains without a native source

The complainant identifies another communication said to have stated that the Sun Park business had ceased and to have given instructions for collecting belongings. The native message has not been located in the controlled corpus.

We therefore do not publish that wording as an authenticated document. It remains a retrieval route.

28 October 2021 · Google Business ownership request

Google sent a direct notification to the existing Sun Park profile holder. The request was associated with Lourdes Castillejo, role “Business owner”, and mynd.hotels@gmail.com.

The notification proves the association made by Google's system. It does not yet identify the physical user who submitted the request.

24 February 2022 · second request

A further preserved notification again shows Lourdes Castillejo, now as “Employee”, using the same Gmail account.

Contemporaneous correspondence of 25 February expressly records an alternative possibility: someone else may have used her name without consent. That possibility remains open.

20 June 2022 · `MYND Hotels, Employee`

Preserved Google material records another ownership request associated with MYND Hotels, role “Employee”, again using mynd.hotels@gmail.com.

August 2022 onward · dispute and need for the complete history

Later party communications describe objections to profile changes, a suspension for activity regarded as suspicious and later restoration/closure events. Establishing what actually happened requires the provider's complete history: requests, decisions, permissions, edits, suspensions, restorations and the accounts that executed each action.

What connects 2019 with 2021–2022?

Function, not already-proved authorship.

The 2019 episode concerns the ability to communicate with former customers as “Sun Park”. The 2021–2022 requests concern the ability to control a Google asset representing Sun Park to the public. Both may affect name, location, reputation, reviews, photographs, calls, enquiries, bookings, traffic and goodwill.

That supports a coordinated investigation of commercial digital identity. It does not support automatically attributing shared knowledge or intent to Canarian Hospitality, MYND, Hotel New Trend, CAM or Lourdes, or claiming that the same person created or controlled both accounts.

The MYND Gmail account: why it matters

Repeated use of mynd.hotels@gmail.com in business-profile requests matters because a non-corporate mailbox may have a different ownership, recovery, custody and audit trail from an account under a corporate domain. The question is who administered it and under whose authority.

It is not a presumption of wrongdoing. Businesses and teams use Gmail for innocent reasons. It becomes evidentially relevant here because the same account repeatedly appears in requests directed at obtaining control of a disputed business profile.

What would resolve the issue

  • Lawfully obtainable creation, recovery, session, IP and device data for `sunpark264@gmail.com` and `mynd.hotels@gmail.com`.
  • Native RFC822/EML and complete headers for the 2019 message and any related campaign.
  • Request identifiers for the October-2021, February-2022 and June-2022 Google Business requests.
  • Permission and edit history for the Sun Park profile: name, website, telephone, photographs, categories, status and links.
  • The represented legal entity and documentary authority supplied for each request.
  • Account-administration and instruction records held by CAM, HNT, Canarian Hospitality and MYND, keeping those entities legally distinct.
  • The lawful or unlawful origin — to be determined, not presumed — of any historical customer list or data used for contact.

From digital identity to the economic chain

Sun Park was not only real estate. The continuity record concerns customers, reputation, data, bookings, commercial relationships, physical access, project preparation, commercialisation and later finance and investment.

This dossier therefore connects to the material-control chronology, the Acosta Matos perimeter, the RICPE trail and the RIC, regional-incentives and FEDER chain.

The connection means that provenance of the asset and commercial platform may matter to later due diligence, investment or finance. It does not mean that a digital irregularity, if proved, automatically renders later funding irregular.

A person whose name appears can clarify a great deal

That is why we have addressed a specific open letter to Lourdes Castillejo. If she submitted the requests, she can explain the instruction and title. If she did not, she may be able to establish that someone else used her identity. Either answer is materially important.

We also maintain an open letter to current and former workers explaining lawful reporting and why employment is not responsibility.
