RECOVERY FIRST · ACCOUNTABILITY IN SERVICE OF RECOVERY

Recover before punishing.

Our objective is to restore the lawful patrimonial and economic position of LPB, Matkator, Aweswell and other affected Group interests; recover assets, rights, income and value where a lawful route exists; and obtain compensation for proven harm that cannot be reversed.

Restitution where possible. Compensation where not. Individual responsibility only to the extent primary evidence establishes a causal contribution.

The question organising the whole programme

WHAT MUST BE PROVED → CORRECTED → RESTORED → ACCOUNTED FOR → COMPENSATED?

The investigations concerning the Insolvency Administrator, the Court, the LAJ/court office and private actors are not autonomous punitive ends. They are separate nodes in one patrimonial reconstruction: what belonged to whom, what happened, through which mechanism, what consequence continues today, and what remedy remains available.

Seven recovery objectives

1 · Preserve

Protect LPB, its causes of action and recoverable value; keep Matkator and third-party extra-insolvency rights separate where the evidence requires it.

2 · Restore

Recover property, title, possession, access, control, corporate rights and other interests for the correct holder where a lawful route exists.

3 · Reverse effects

Test continuing judicial, documentary, notarial, Registry, administrative and economic consequences of historic acts.

4 · Account

Reconstruct credit, interest, transactions, income, fruits, exploitation, costs and any surplus or displaced value.

5 · Damages

Quantify only causally provable loss: assets, fruits, income, investment value, financing effects and recoverable costs.

6 · Individual causation

Duty/power → verified knowledge → act/omission → implementation → consequence → remedy.

7 · Accountability by proof

Keep judicial remedy, civil/insolvency liability, discipline, inspection and criminal routes separate. None is presumed from another.

What this strategy does not presume

Adverse outcome ≠ misconduct · judicial error ≠ prevarication · testimony issued ≠ improper use · chronology ≠ causation · missing document in our corpus ≠ non-existence · group label ≠ common legal identity or intent.
