Architecture as a documentary node: JDAM, the 2022 visado and the chain beyond the hotel.

This record asks whether architectural documentation may have become —with or without the professional's knowledge, which remains open— a technical-legitimacy layer within a property, insolvency, municipal, tourism, investor, finance, tax, public-support, operational and judicial sequence.

Evidential boundary. No crime, professional breach, falsehood, lack of habilitation, professional coordination or public-authority participation is declared. Documented facts, source statements, Project Sun Rock allegations, inferences and open questions are separated. Each actor requires act-by-act proof.

Institutional status at 20 August 2026. COALZ registered entries 26/008230, 26/008474 and 26/008476; the last was passed to its Board, as were the earlier materials. COAGC placed primary disciplinary competence in Lanzarote, said specified data did not match its records, retained ordinary membership data and reported no Sun Park/MYND documentation. The reviewed record does not establish a merits decision, a formally opened disciplinary case or a later explanation of the discrepancy.

1. The conversion chain requiring verification

Apparent authorityProfessional commissionProject and visadoAdministrative useLater benefit / reliance

The issue is not that architecture coexisted with investment or works. It is whether professional records capable of generating administrative and commercial confidence rested on verified premises concerning client, representation, consent, title and perimeter.

Investigative rule: the later result —opened hotel, registration, finance, support or operation— does not retrospectively validate the authority or perimeter invoked when the commission began, the project was prepared or documentation was filed.
