Work in progress: what Cuatrecasas said had been worked, what had not been invoiced, and what later became a collection instrument.

In ordinary billing language, WIP is not yet an issued invoice. It is work recorded, accrued or valued internally. This provisional track keeps that layer separate from estimates, proformas, final invoices, promissory notes and cash.

Control rule: WIP or estimated work cannot be counted as an issued invoice or as cash paid. A later proforma, pagaré or judicial claim must identify the work it absorbs before the same economic value can safely be reconciled across instruments.
