Work in progress: what Cuatrecasas said had been worked, what had not been invoiced, and what later became a collection instrument.

In ordinary billing language, WIP is not yet an issued invoice. It is work recorded, accrued or valued internally. This provisional track keeps that layer separate from estimates, proformas, final invoices, promissory notes and cash.

Control rule: WIP or estimated work cannot be counted as an issued invoice or as cash paid. A later proforma, pagaré or judicial claim must identify the work it absorbs before the same economic value can safely be reconciled across instruments.

The “around €400,000” statement

On 15 June 2020 Collections stated two components: €136,830.72 of unpaid bills plus VAT, and €161,738.75 of work carried out plus VAT, then said “we might be talking about around four hundred thousand pending euros.” The two stated components total €298,569.47 before VAT.

Basis still unclear. A purely mechanical 21% uplift would produce €361,269.06, not exactly €400,000. That calculation is shown only to expose the reconciliation gap; it is not an assumption that 21% was the correct VAT treatment of every component. Any other interest, cost, tax, WIP or rounding component must be sourced.
