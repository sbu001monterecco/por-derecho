CONTROLLED POSITION

The appeal is stronger if it is narrower.

The court’s core objection

The 16 July order maintains provisional dismissal and treats the complaint as lacking a sufficiently exact act, property and minimum criminal indicium, with much of the dispute belonging in the civil enforcement.

The proposed appellate answer

The 25 June synthesis followed the 8 June prosecution report and attempted to isolate finite acts and checks. The appellate complaint is that the later order does not sufficiently explain why that cure still failed.

The unresolved merits issue

Knowledge is not the same as deception. The record still needs to identify a concrete false factual representation, evidence manipulation or material omission capable of causing judicial error, if any.

CORRECTIONS THAT CONTROL

Transparency requires publishing what weakens the case as well as what supports it.

Debt discussions did occur

Direct debt-resolution communications with Gil/Aweswell exist in 2020 and 2022. The record should not say Cuatrecasas never approached them.

Invoice client is not the whole debt analysis

2019 billing to Aweswell/LPB makes the original instruments important; it does not itself defeat a valid autonomous Matkator promissory-note obligation.

25 April limits concealment theory

The filing is evidence of knowledge and also evidence that the controversy was put before the execution court. Knowledge plus enforcement is not automatically procedural fraud.

No completed assignment is asserted

The current controlled record proves a reserved right, not the identity of an assignee or a completed transfer.

Exculpatory gate: if certified records show that material facts were already before the court, that Cuatrecasas had not yet received a warning at the relevant stage, or that no materially misleading representation was made, that result should be recorded and used.
