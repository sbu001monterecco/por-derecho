Founder · investor · platform builder

Gil Marer and the group behind Project Sun Rock.

A long-term entrepreneurial and Canary Islands hospitality history, a substantial recovery programme, and a disciplined search for clean new hotel and resort projects.

The entrepreneur

Founder-investor and hospitality platform builder.

Gil’s entrepreneurial career spans more than three decades across UK property, technology-enabled businesses and Canary Islands hospitality. His strongest documented operating chapter is the Sun Park/Summers Villages platform built in Playa Blanca from 2011 until the loss of material control on 7 June 2018.

In 2011, Aweswell entered the Sun Park hotel-and-business perimeter. Gil then led owner-level strategy, business development, funding and counterparty relationships, commercial structuring and coordination between the United Kingdom and Spain.

The team developed Sun Park Living and Summers Villages: a differentiated proposition combining flexible short and extended stays, direct reservations, specialist distribution and an active guest community. Contemporary material described a 220-apartment proposition and approximately 50 employees at the outset. Around the property, the team built direct customer acquisition, repeat and referral demand, a dedicated website, booking and property-management technology, activities and community programming. The model attracted specialist media attention and was conceived for replication beyond one property.

Gil tends to work where conventional categories overlook value: connecting an asset to a customer proposition, an operating system and a practical transaction route. Today that means originating and reconstructing Canary Islands opportunities, bringing principals and advisers together, and working to secure projects through diligence, negotiation, capital and signed agreements—or rejecting them when the evidence does not support proceeding.

The group in context

Connected by purpose; separated by law and function.

“Project Sun Rock” is a working name. It does not erase the boundaries between the holding company, affected companies and rights, the historical operating platform, the recovery record and any future project company or joint venture.

Current holding-company perimeter

Aweswell Limited

The UK holding company through which the 2011 sponsor position and current group strategy are coordinated. Aweswell can contribute only assets, shares, contractual rights or proceeds that it legally owns and is permitted to use; disputed recovery value is not presented as present cash.

Historical operating platform

Sun Rock · Sun Park Living · Summers Villages

The property, hospitality product, community, direct-demand, technology and specialist-distribution system developed around Sun Park. The operating intelligence and archive remain relevant; the historical hotel operation and commercial relationships are not represented as continuing uninterrupted.

Recovery and public record

Project Sun Rock · Por Derecho

The evidence-led programme pursuing the best supported combination of restoration, accounting, equivalent value, replacement assets and damages across the correct claimant perimeters. This website is an accountability record, not a court, fund, law firm or registered foundation.

Future strategy

Ring-fenced new projects

Hotel, resort and genuinely hotel-adjacent opportunities are originated and pursued through project-specific diligence and proposed SPV/JV structures. Unless expressly stated otherwise, pipeline opportunities remain unacquired, non-exclusive and outside Aweswell’s control until signed documents and closing.

The legal matter in context

Significant, but not the whole identity.

The loss of material control on 7 June 2018 and the insolvency, title, exploitation, valuation, financing and public-process disputes that followed interrupted the founding hotel and wider platform. The allegations and procedural status are documented elsewhere on this site.

What recovery means

The programme seeks the best evidence-supported outcome for each proper claimant and asset layer. Property, hotel exploitation, sponsor rights, distribution, customers and wider platform value must not be collapsed into one undifferentiated claim or counted twice.

What it does not mean

Recovery is not presented as a guaranteed outcome, current cash, automatically available collateral or a condition of working together on new projects. New capital and assets should be protected primarily at the relevant ProjectCo, asset and controlled-account level.
