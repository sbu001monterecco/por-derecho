Anti-fragmentation and anti-inflation rules

Formal ≠ email

RedSARA/AGE is one formal event class; a SENT email is a different institutional communication. They are linked but never counted as the same type of event.

DRAFT ≠ SENT

Drafts are preserved for provenance and version comparison, but they do not prove transmission and are excluded from delivery denominators.

Duplicate ≠ new substance

Materially repeated transmissions keep every native locator, but require duplicate_of and controlling_version before substantive-event counts are increased.
