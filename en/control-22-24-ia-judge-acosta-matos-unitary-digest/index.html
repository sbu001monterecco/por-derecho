MAXIMUM HYPOTHESIS ≠ GUILT.

This page takes the evidence to its investigative ceiling while identifying exactly what remains necessary to turn convergent effects into an attributable criminal offence. It does not declare disloyal administration, prevarication, corruption, co-authorship, influence-peddling or conspiracy as proven facts.

The question joining the record

CAM obtains recognition, control or consolidation; LPB loses information, protection, control, competitive neutrality or recovery options. At each step, where is the equivalent benefit for the estate and who documented that this was the best outcome for LPB?

The force of the analysis does not depend on asserting a secret agreement. It begins with something more testable: the repeated distribution of effects. Only then does it ask whether the repetition is explained by independent lawful decisions, poor administration, insufficient supervision or concerted facilitation.

Investigative ceiling by actor

INSOLVENCY ADMINISTRATION

8/10

Possible systemic disloyal-administration pattern

Ceiling, not probability. The maximum hypothesis asks whether entrusted insolvency powers were exercised, withheld or left uncorrected in a way knowingly contrary to LPB's patrimonial interests, with effects repeatedly favourable to CAM.

To cross the criminal threshold: exact power/duty, knowledge, excess or breach, quantified patrimonial harm, causation and the required subjective element.

COMMERCIAL COURT JUDGE

7–8/10

Possible knowing selective supervision and non-neutralisation

The maximum hypothesis is not “LPB lost rulings”. It is whether specific resolutions or omissions, read against the knowledge and supervisory powers then available, were objectively unjust within the relevant criminal threshold and maintained CAM-favourable outcomes.

Counter-record: the 15/02/2018 order corrected amounts for LPB and judicial reliance on the insolvency administration may be legitimate.

CAM PERIMETER

6–7/10

Recurring beneficiary; participation remains to prove

Continuity of benefit supports investigation of knowledge, request, inducement, cooperation or agreement. Benefit alone does not make CAM a co-author or cooperator.

The figures are evidence/hypothesis ceilings used to orient investigation, not guilt percentages or procedural forecasts.

IA + Judge: functional convergence

CEILING 7/10

What can now be said

At different points, acts, reports or omissions by the insolvency administration and judicial decisions, deference or failures to neutralise converged in effects preserving or strengthening a CAM-centred position, while LPB lost rights, information, control or options for which no compensating estate benefit is currently identified.

NOT PROVED

The missing jump

No direct evidence has yet been located that the IA and judge agreed to favour CAM, that CAM induced either actor, that there was consideration, influence-peddling, bribery or a predetermined award.

Hypothesis worth testing: were these convergences the sum of independent explainable decisions, or was there concerted facilitation that can be reconstructed through communications, instructions, shared knowledge and concrete departures from ordinary process?

Conspiracy: strong optics, insufficient proof so far

CURRENT CEILING 3–4/10

The repetition of the same beneficiary across different mechanisms creates serious coordination optics and justifies examination of communications, instructions and relationships. It does not substitute for proof of an agreement and common resolved purpose to execute an offence.

The responsible public expression is therefore “possible concerted facilitation to investigate”, not “proved conspiracy”. The hypothesis can rise rapidly if direct evidence of agreement, inducement, necessary cooperation, influence or shared purpose appears; without it, it remains open.

The strongest counter-case

A serious investigation must try to defeat this alternative explanation: the insolvency administration exercised discretion in good faith and relied on court orders; the judge legitimately relied on the IA and decided legally different applications; Article 1535 was weak; CAM lawfully pursued its interests; Sun Park's fragmentation created unavoidable conflict; and there are restoration, cost-benefit or reconciliation records not yet held by Por Derecho.

The case becomes materially stronger if the certified record cannot produce those explanatory bridges.

Conclusion

The maximum sustainable theory today is not “proved conspiracy”. It is that the record now contains enough independent episodes to support a strong investigation of a repeated CAM-favourable effect pattern, possible non-neutral or disloyal insolvency administration, possible selective/non-neutralising judicial supervision and possible concerted convergence that can only be confirmed or rejected by closing the documentary bridges identified above.
