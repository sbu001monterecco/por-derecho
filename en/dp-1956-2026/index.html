What it establishes — and what it does not

It establishes

A provisional procedural status

The controlled record documents that DP 1956/2026 was opened and that the order communicated on 21 July simultaneously terminated the proceeding and ordered provisional dismissal.

That status should be published as exactly that: provisional dismissal.

It does not establish

It is not a merits judgment

Provisional dismissal does not turn the underlying allegations into established facts, but neither should it be described as an acquittal, a free dismissal or a final determination that the alleged events did not occur.

Editorial rule: this page does not publish or summarise private legal advice concerning later procedural choices. Status and possible legal consequences are explained only from public legal sources and controlled procedural material.

Public legal rule

Provisional does not mean immutable.

Article 641 of Spain's Criminal Procedure Act governs provisional dismissal. Public guidance from the Spanish State Prosecutor General distinguishes that status from free dismissal and explains that, where the legal requirements are met, new data or evidence may permit an application to reopen a provisionally dismissed proceeding. Reopening is not automatic: it depends on the new material, the applicable legal requirements and a decision by the competent judicial body.

Practical consequence: preserving evidence today does not imply guilt and does not predict reopening. It means avoiding the loss of material that may need to be assessed later if a competent authority lawfully requires it.

The useful question

What would actually be new and material?

More communications or repetition of an allegation are not enough. Any later change of status would need sufficiently concrete and legally relevant material. Categories that could require reassessment, without implying that any one would be sufficient, include:

Later judicial decision

A later ruling that establishes or materially clarifies a concrete act, duty, loss or causal relationship relevant to the perimeter already examined.

New primary document

A document not previously in the proceeding that helps individualise knowledge, instruction, authority, accounting treatment, patrimonial destination or consequence of a concrete act.

Finding by another competent body

A professional, regulatory or control decision establishing a specific factual proposition. The mere existence of another file is not enough.

Production by professional custodians

Records held by professional firms or other custodians that provide new evidence about relationships, communications, files, conflicts, instructions or documentary traceability.

Three finite documentary clusters

The useful perimeter should remain concrete. The working record identifies three document families for reconciliation, without presenting any of them as automatic proof of an offence:

  1. 2018–2019: the 28 November 2018 transaction, the later request for validation and the 24 October 2019 order that declined validation, together with unresolved registry, patrimonial, banking and accounting consequences.
  2. 25 January 2022: the Insolvency Administrator's report and annexes, tested against the documentation actually in the insolvency proceeding.
  3. 21 February 2022: deed protocol 334 and documentary reconciliation of the economic figures, including the separate EUR 400,000 component and its ultimate treatment in the estate accounts.

Boundary: identifying those clusters does not authorise a general or prospective investigation of the Insolvency Administrator's entire professional activity. The test is document → individualised act → duty → consequence → explanation.

Preservation and cooperation

The question for any custodian is simple.

They are not being asked to support an accusation. They are being asked to preserve documents within their perimeter, identify the correct entity or function and cooperate with any lawful, properly framed request from a competent authority, including a future request if the procedural status of DP 1956/2026 were to change.

The legal possibility of later reopening does not mean there is currently an application to reopen or that any future application would be granted.
