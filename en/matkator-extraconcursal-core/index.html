The private chain must leave abstraction.

This does not state that DP 1901/2026 has found liability. It identifies the control point where the private and extraconcursal hypothesis must be confirmed or excluded through primary sources.

  1. 01Limited positionA limited ownership, credit or Community position is not authority over all properties, rights or income.
  2. 02Apparent documentary authorityMinutes, census, vote, debt, certificates, offices, powers and Community banking must be checked at origin.
  3. 03Material controlSecurity, keys, locks, access, exclusion, measurements, pilots, projects and works.
  4. 04Whole-hotel presentationThe hotel is presented commercially, technically and financially as a complete project.
  5. 05Transfer and operationReconstruction of CAM-HNT, Canarian Hospitality/MYND, contracts, operator and inventory.
  6. 06MonetisationReservations, income, financing, incentives, value, accounts, accounting and final beneficiary.
