SNCA: the issue is no longer whether there is a generic EU-funds reference, but what was actually verified about the asset receiving support.

The SNCA/IGAE route should distinguish alert, verification, referral and result. Fraud is not presumed. The question is what source was checked and whether control reached the real ownership/availability of the transformed perimeter, the beneficiary, eligible costs, other financing sources and affected extra-insolvency rights.

What a funds-control review cannot simply assume

The incentive file does not exist in a vacuum. The asset comes from a perimeter involving LPB's insolvency, fragmented ownership and third-party rights. The corpus also preserves a July 2021 RIC certification describing 54 CAM properties, 190 LPB properties and 18 third-party properties while placing the principal acquisition and full due diligence as future/conditional at that time.

The anti-fraud question is not that fragmentation automatically invalidates support. It is whether the GC/836/P06 application and control correctly identified:

  1. which properties/rights actually formed the eligible project;
  2. what title and availability HNT or its perimeter held over each;
  3. which Matkator/third-party assets or rights remained outside;
  4. which works/costs related to owned, common or third-party assets;
  5. what was stated about litigation, insolvency and title/availability conditions;
  6. what independent verification was performed before award, payment, certification or closure.

RIC, regional incentive and EU support are not the same thing

The controlled dossier attributes to RICPE's audited 2023 accounts total Series F/G financing for HNT/MYND Yaiza of €6,573,703.10. The published GC/836/P06 subsidy is €3,440,914.20. MYND currently states EU co-financing through Regional Economic Incentives.

These figures are not automatically added or characterised as improper finance. They should be reconciled agreement by agreement, invoice by invoice and source by source: RIC capital/loans, subsidy, co-financing, bank/supplier finance, payments, eligible costs and own funds.

There are also preserved RIC/project materials with a tranche split that does not exactly match the split used in the controlled audited-account reconstruction. That discrepancy requires native sources; it does not prove irregularity.

Not only EU financial interests: extra-insolvency and cross-border consequences

LPB · insolvency

Support/control may intersect with value, works, assets and causes of action in the insolvency process, but that process does not define the whole Sun Park perimeter.

Matkator / third parties · extra-insolvency

If a third-party unit, right, access, work, installation or income stream materially entered the transformed project, the corresponding title or consent must be identified. No such inclusion is presumed.

Aweswell / foreign investor · cross-border

Loss/restriction of value, control, financing, recovery or remedies of a foreign investor is legally separate from the EU-financial-interest question and requires its own audit.

Why the issue is current

MYND Yaiza is currently marketed as an operating hotel. Therefore, if a title, availability or funded-perimeter divergence remains unresolved, potential consequences can continue through operation, revenue, asset value, compliance conditions, third-party rights and remedies.

Historical act ≠ extinguished harm. The control chain should reach the present position and distinguish legitimate present value/revenue from any consequence actually proved to derive from another person's rights.

What this page does not establish

An alert is not proof of fraud. A subsidy is not proof of irregularity. EU co-financing is not proof of EU-budget loss. Coexistence with RIC capital is not proof of double funding. Fragmented ownership is not proof that a supported cost affected a third party.

The public pressure is documentary: if control was sufficient, identify what was checked. If the chain cannot be reconstructed, identify the missing record and the authority responsible for producing it.
