OPEN KIMONO · BEST CONTROL AND WARNING

Protecting the Union’s financial interests requires an open trail, not a black box.

Positive model

Good SNCA / ERDF practice

  • The alert is preserved with attachments and real status.
  • Competence and referral are documented.
  • Operation, beneficiary and expenditure are identified.
  • Invoice, payment, certification and indicator are connected.
  • Other aid and finance are reconciled.
  • Contrary evidence can close or narrow the hypothesis.
  • Correction and recovery, where applicable, remain traceable.
Warning

The opposite practice

  • Channel used as a mailbox without status or custodian.
  • Referral treated as investigation or solution.
  • ERDF plaque used as substitute for the file.
  • Award used as proof of final eligibility.
  • Separate instruments used to avoid an invoice table.
  • Employment declared without persons, period or FTE.
  • Secrecy or silence converted into a merits answer.

Boundary: the comparison does not assert that SNCA, IGAE, DGFE or the regional authority engaged in every negative practice. It defines a public standard the file can meet, correct or fail.
