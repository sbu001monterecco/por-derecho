PROVINCIAL PUBLIC PROSECUTOR'S OFFICE OF SANTA CRUZ DE TENERIFE · 21 AUG 2026

Administrative File 95/2026: procedural closure and a documented difference of scope.

This record preserves the submitted email, the received notification, the controlled transcription of the Decree and its complete English translation. It separates what the Public Prosecutor decided, what the submission actually requested and what remains without a documentary answer.

Control rule: the Decree closed Administrative File 95/2026 on a procedural and investigative-competence rationale. It did not expressly find that the supplied documents were false, irrelevant, exculpatory or insufficient.

1 · PROCEDURAL RESULT

What was opened

An administrative/governance file, registered as File 95/2026, to process the 21 August communication.

What was closed

The administrative file itself. The Decree did not reopen Preliminary Proceedings 748/2026.

Stated reason

The Office regarded the matter as already judicialised in DP 748/2026 and said that it could not open new prosecutorial investigation proceedings or order the requested steps outside the judicial file, even though that file was archived.

Exact effect: this is an adverse routing decision. It is not an individual merits determination concerning each document or requested investigative step.

2 · DOCUMENTED DIFFERENCE OF SCOPE

The communication was not limited to seeking reconsideration within DP 748/2026.

The Decree summarises the submission principally as a request to review the Public Prosecution Service's position of 8 June. That purpose appeared in the email, but it did not exhaust its content.

4 · THE WIDER TENERIFE PROSECUTOR PERIMETER

The Decree itself refers to DIP 7/2026, DIP 12/2026 and DIP 20/2026. Recovered primary documents from DIP 20/2026 establish a separate file concerning a reported RIC hotel-investment offer, possible false data or misleading advertising and possible falsification or Public Treasury offences, transferred to the Provincial Public Prosecutor's Office of Las Palmas.

Limit: the exact contents of DIP 7/2026 and DIP 12/2026 remain pending primary-source recovery. No unverified subject is attributed to those files.

7 · CONTROLLED PUBLIC FORMULATION

The objection is not that the Public Prosecutor “rejected the evidence.” It is that the Decree applied a route focused exclusively on DP 748/2026 to a communication that also requested preservation, association with earlier prosecutorial records, separation of files and finite checks distinct from reconsideration of the 8 June report.

The outstanding institutional question is documentary: what was preserved, in which file, before which body and by what decision each request was—or must be—resolved.
