CRIMINAL-EVIDENCE MAP · NOT A VERDICT

What each link could mean criminally if it is proved.

The map does not start from “many irregularities.” It starts from concrete acts: make a representation, know a contrary fact, reuse the representation, obtain an effect, and attribute the use to an individual with knowledge and intent. Only then is a possible Spanish criminal-law classification tested.

Rule. Association, family relationship, office, presence, chronology, later benefit or membership of the same company do not prove authorship or agreement. Every arrow needs a source and every offence needs its own elements.

From document to possible offence.

2 · CRIMINAL-LAW FAMILIES

Legal hypotheses, not convictions.

CP 390 · 392 · 393

Documentary falsity / knowing use

Was a material document altered or simulated; did a private person commit falsity in a public/official/mercantile document; or was a false document knowingly presented or used?

Key proof: original, author, version, signature, metadata, essential false proposition, knowledge and concrete use.

CP 248 ff.

Fraud / possible procedural dimension

Was there sufficient deception causing error and a disposition/effect with patrimonial prejudice and profit motive?

Not enough: an inaccurate statement. Deception, error, act/effect, prejudice and intent must connect.

CP 252

Disloyal administration

Did someone with powers to administer another's assets exceed or breach those powers and thereby cause patrimonial harm?

Key proof: source and limits of power, knowledge, conduct, practical capacity and causal harm.

CP 257

Frustration of enforcement

Was a patrimonial disposition or obligation undertaken to prejudice creditors or obstruct foreseeable enforcement?

Key proof: asset, disposition, timing, knowledge of creditor/enforcement and purpose.

CP 259 ff.

Insolvency offences

While actually or imminently insolvent, was a specifically criminalised act committed affecting assets, accounts or creditors?

Not enough: an insolvency irregularity or disputed patrimonial decision.

CP 570 bis / ter

Criminal organisation / group

Was there a stable/coordinated group with divided tasks for committing offences?

Not enough: many actors, business ties, family links or connected chronology.

LECrim 262 · CP 408

Institutional knowledge and duty to act

LECrim 262 regulates reporting duties linked to office/profession/trade; CP 408 requires intentional failure by an authority/public official to promote prosecution of known crimes.

Not enough: an adverse response, non-intentional omission or competence disagreement.

Official text: Spanish Criminal Code · BOE · LECrim · BOE. Application depends on the provision temporally applicable to each alleged act.

4 · PROCEEDING MAP

The same evidence performs a different function in each route.

AN querella 2023

Accusatory antecedent: shows that falsity/fraud/disloyal-administration/investment theories were already pleaded. It does not prove them.

Las Palmas complaint 25 Jun 2026

Reframes the theory around non-circular provenance and measures capable of fixing authorship, authority, use and benefit.

DP 1901/2026

Current private-actor/CAM verification lane. The controlled providencia proves referral to prosecutors on admission, not a merits finding.

Insolvency 36/2012

The same records may matter to title, control, estate value, fruits, decisions and duties without the insolvency case automatically deciding criminality.

Cabildo / Yaiza

Shows what was presented, required and administratively effected. These authorities do not decide criminal guilt.

RIC / AEAT / GC/836 / CNMV

Allows comparison of what hotel perimeter, financing, title, investment and status was represented in other institutional circuits and when.
