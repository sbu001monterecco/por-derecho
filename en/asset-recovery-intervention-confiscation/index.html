PRESERVE FIRST · TRACE THE VALUE · DECIDE THE MERITS AFTER

The remedy must not arrive after the value has gone.

The Sun Park / MYND Yaiza record is now large enough to pose a different question. Not merely who did what in the past, but what assets, income, rights, records and metadata are being preserved today so that a future judicial, prosecutorial, regulatory or administrative decision can still be effective?

One economic chain can cross several legal persons and several administrative files. The files may be separate. The value chain is not.

Follow the value, not the label.

A later company, restructuring, registration, refinancing, refurbishment, operator or hotel brand is a later event. It does not by itself establish illegality. It also does not, by itself, extinguish the traceability of what came before.

Original asset / right→Possession / control→Corporate layer→Financing→Works→Hotel operation→Revenue / fruits→Refinancing→Distribution / substitute value→Current holder

Administrative fragmentation is not a defence to economic traceability. The institutional task is to identify which authority controls which part of this chain, which statutory power it can use, and what has actually been preserved.

The powers already exist.

Criminal judicial intervention

Penal Code art. 33.7(g). Judicial intervention can reach a whole legal person or defined installations, sections or business units. The investigating judge may order it provisionally during the investigation. The statute gives the intervener access to premises and information.

Primary law: Código Penal, art. 33.7(g).

Seize early to keep confiscation effective

Penal Code art. 127 octies. Property, means, instruments and proceeds can be seized or attached and deposited by judicial authority from the first investigative steps in order to preserve the effectiveness of confiscation. Equivalent-value recovery is separately contemplated by art. 127 septies.

This is a preservation mechanism, not a declaration that any identified downstream asset has already been adjudged criminal proceeds.

BOE ↗

CNMV intervention and substitution

Ley 22/2014 art. 102 expressly imports the Ley 35/2003 intervention/substitution regime for SCR, SICC, SGEIC and depositaries. Under arts. 72–75 of Ley 35/2003, exceptional-gravity cases can lead to intervention or provisional substitution, designated interveners/administrators, access to offices/books/documents and control of subsequent corporate acts.

The immediate case question is the exact regulated status of the RICPE vehicle, manager and depositary and whether the statutory threshold is engaged.

ORGA — preserve and administer value

The Oficina de Recuperación y Gestión de Activos is an auxiliary body of the Administration of Justice with statutory functions of locating, recovering, conserving, administering and realising assets within its criminal-law remit. Productive value need not simply be allowed to dissipate while proceedings continue.

ORGA does not itself decide guilt or create the underlying judicial restraint.

RD 948/2015 ↗

Structures without legal personality

Penal Code art. 129 extends specified consequences to qualifying enterprises, organisations, groups and other entities without legal personality. The legal nature of every Community/CEXP/owners' structure must be established first; they are not interchangeable.

Tax, incentives and EU funds

AEAT, Canary finance/control bodies, granting authorities and IGAE/SNCA have different competences. The common question is transversal reconciliation: RIC → RICPE/HNT financing → works and eligible costs → regional incentives → FEDER/EU dimension → invoices, payments and certifications.

Successive approvals cannot substitute for identifying which authority independently verified the underlying facts each one assumed.

The demand is finite.

For every competent authority, answer seven questions.

  1. What asset, income stream, right, account, document or dataset is within your competence?
  2. What preservation, intervention, supervision, seizure, administration or recovery power applies?
  3. What factual/legal threshold activates it?
  4. Which primary evidence has been examined against that threshold?
  5. What has actually been preserved or restrained, and since what date?
  6. Which other authority controls the next part of the value chain?
  7. If no preservation measure is considered justified, state the reason and the record on which that decision rests.

The burden has shifted from visibility to action. Project Sun Rock is not asking for confiscation by proclamation. It is demanding that an eventual lawful recovery not be made worthless by institutional delay, fragmented files or the movement of reachable value while merits are still being decided.
