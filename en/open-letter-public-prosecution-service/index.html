When the allegations reversed, did the scrutiny reverse too?

A documented reconstruction of what Spain's Public Prosecution Service knew, what it did, what changed later and what remains unanswered. We do not ask readers to adopt our theory; we ask for a process whose independence, scope and reasoning can be verified.

What independently verifiable scrutiny did the reversed hypothesis receive?

The question is not whether different complaints had to produce the same outcome. It is whether the direction of scrutiny could change when the direction of the allegations changed.

Two causal explanations that should be capable of comparison

2013 PROSECUTION THEORY

Was value improperly displaced from LPB?

The complaint constructed a criminal hypothesis around the LPB–operator relationship, rent/consideration, corporate structure and possible prejudice to LPB and creditors.

2014 COUNTER-NARRATIVE

What caused or aggravated LPB's economic weakness?

Gil/Patricia's complaints focused on Community governance, voting exclusion, debt attributed to LPB, hotel-wide operating costs, unitary exploitation, access/keys and minority-owner interference.

Is there a document showing that the prosecution service genuinely compared these two explanations of the same economic deterioration?

The standard: knowledge → response → consequence

1. KNOWNWhat document proves the institution received it?
2. REQUESTEDWhat investigative step, review or preservation was sought?
3. RESPONSEWhat did prosecutors actually do?
4. UNRESOLVEDWhat remained outside the decision or unverified?
5. AFTERWARDSWhat occurred later, and what causal link—if any—can actually be demonstrated?

“No response identified in the reviewed record” does not mean “no response existed”. “Happened afterwards” does not mean “was caused by”.

One number that requires a caveat

97 registrations do not mean 97 investigations

The reconstructed institutional register documents dozens of submissions to prosecution bodies, including a 97-entry matrix for December 2025–June 2026. That demonstrates breadth of notice/registration, not that every annex was personally read or that every matter received substantive investigation.

RECEIVED ≠ EXAMINED ≠ INVESTIGATED ≠ DECIDED ON THE MERITS.

What this message does not ask readers to ignore

Prosecutors considered there was a sufficient basis to bring the 2013 complaint. The 2014 complaints used highly accusatory language and some legal characterisations may have been weak or contestable. Prosecutors are not obliged to perform every investigative measure requested. Territorial referrals can be legally correct. There is a materially adverse first-instance insolvency culpability judgment under appeal. And later adverse decisions do not themselves prove bias, capture or collusion.

Our question survives those concessions; it does not depend on concealing them.

The public thesis

We do not assert as established fact that the Public Prosecution Service was “captured”. We ask whether an early allocation of credibility, repetition of inherited frames, fragmented files and territorial return could produce functional instrumentalisation even if no individual intended that outcome.

The strongest institutional answer would not be a simple denial. It would identify the review, record, investigative measures and reasoning through which the system can show that it corrected its own frames when the evidence changed.

Formal open letter

Read the full message

We do not ask for a favourable outcome; we ask for a verifiable process

This letter does not ask the Public Prosecution Service to believe our account. It asks it to test our account and to test with the same rigor the accounts, conduct and documents of every other relevant actor.

We do not say every complaint must proceed, every requested step must be performed or every territorial referral is improper. We ask that “the same facts” be tested by date, actor, conduct, asset, injured party and evidence; that LPB, Matkator, the Community and operators not be conflated; and that earlier decisions not turn their factual premises into propositions immune from later evidence.

We do not ask prosecutors to believe us. We ask them to scrutinise us rigorously—and to scrutinise those we identify with the same rigor.

The first frame and the reversal of roles

In December 2013 the Public Prosecution Service filed a criminal complaint against Gil Marer and Patricia Domínguez arising from DI 273/2013. The primary reconstruction now published shows an AC→prosecution chain and a theory mixing 2008–2011 architecture with later 2012–2013 acts; it is therefore audited by actor, date, capacity and evidence, without treating the complaint as truth or claiming that everything predated arrival. In March 2014 those same accused parties submitted an extensive documentary counter-narrative to the Arrecife prosecution office concerning Community governance, minority owners, debt, voting, exploitation and costs. That file was archived in May 2014. In 2018, while the earlier route remained alive, Gil/Aweswell complained about conduct attributed to the insolvency administrator, CAM and other actors.

Can the Public Prosecution Service demonstrate that when the direction of the allegations reversed, the direction of scrutiny reversed with it?

New evidence, new events and distinct perimeters

“Same facts”, “civil/insolvency” and “already judicialised” can be legally correct categories, but they require demonstration. A 2022 transaction could not literally have been investigated in a 2019 archive. Matkator property does not automatically enter LPB's insolvency estate because it sits within the same complex. Community authority over common functions does not itself establish authority over every private space.

Relationship between facts is not identity of facts.

Territorial competence and reconsideration

Territorial competence may legitimately explain many referrals back to Las Palmas/Lanzarote. But deciding which office is competent does not necessarily answer whether later evidence and contradictions received cumulative and independent assessment.

Unity of action should not become unity around an uncorrected factual premise. Hierarchical dependence should not mean hierarchical inheritance of error.

What we ask

We ask for an identifiable and documented review of the complete route; an identity matrix before any renewed “same facts” conclusion; certified reconstruction of DI 248/2018; the final DP 332/2014 order; primary review of the 25 April 2019 rent expert report; identification of treatment after the DIP 2/2026 correction; the prosecution report and subsequent order in DP 1901/2026; follow-through after GUB 86/2026; express separation of LPB, Matkator, Community and operator perimeters; and an answer to the cumulative institutional question.

An invitation to contradict us with documents

If an investigative step we have not located exists, identify it. If a referral included substantive examination, identify it. If facts we regard as distinct are legally identical, explain the identity by comparing their elements. If a contradiction was corrected, incorporate the correction.

We do not want to preserve errors because they favour our position. We want the record to be more accurate than any competing narrative.

The final question

Can prosecutors demonstrate that evidence arising after an archive was examined as new evidence when it truly was new?

We seek no privilege and no immunity. We seek legality, objectivity, impartiality, traceability and verifiable reconsideration.

The strongest answer to a concern about capture or instrumentalisation is not a declaration that it does not exist. It is a process through which that can be verified.
