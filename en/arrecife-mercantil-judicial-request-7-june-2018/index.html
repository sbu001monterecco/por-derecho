What “exhorto” means

An exhorto is a formal court-to-court judicial-assistance request: one court asks another court to perform a judicial act or supply records/information within the requested court's sphere. It is not an informal party email and it is not a lawyer writing privately to a judge.

Legal/factual significance here: it establishes an institutional communication channel between Juzgado de Instrucción nº2 de Arrecife and Juzgado de lo Mercantil nº1 in Concurso 36/2012 before the alleged 7 June physical event. Because the request originated on 2 May, the later 11 June reply cannot be assumed to have addressed the takeover unless the missing reply says so.
