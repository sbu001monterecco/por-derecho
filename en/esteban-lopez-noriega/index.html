LEGAL PRACTICE · COMMUNITY · MILLAN AND MINERS

Esteban López Noriega: Community presence from 2011 and an official professional-company link

The 22-Jun-2011 minutes already place him as a lawyer invited by the Community administrator. Later sources present him as a Community-side lawyer and the BORME professionally links him with Juan Carlos Prieto Puente in MILLAN AND MINERS. The chronology prevents describing his entry simply as post-2012.

Search variants: Esteban Noriega · Esteban Noruega · Esteban Muriega. They do not replace the canonical identity.
