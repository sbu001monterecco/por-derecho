Unitary digest · bidirectional procedural map

How the proceedings connect

The record is not one proceeding. It is a set of legally distinct judicial, prosecutorial, insolvency, administrative, tax, regulatory, professional and public-funds tracks that can intersect around the same chronology, evidence, credit, assets and alleged harm. This map is designed to make those intersections understandable without erasing their legal boundaries.

How to read the lines. A direct procedural relationship is shown only from an explicit canonical parent/link/review field. A context or convergence bridge explains why separate files may need to be read together. Context does not mean joinder, coordination, knowledge, wrongdoing or liability; equally, a separate docket number does not establish factual, evidential, patrimonial or causation independence.

Institutional completeness test

If one of these files is being decided, reviewed or supervised

This record does not ask any court, prosecutor or supervisory body to pretend that separate proceedings are one case, and it does not ask for a predetermined result. The narrower question is methodological: can a decision in one legally separate file be complete if a materially relevant part of the same asset, credit, insolvency administration, evidence, control history or alleged-harm chain sits in another procedural silo?

Jurisdictional boundaries do not eliminate the need for institutional memory. A legally correct view of one file can still be materially incomplete if evidence necessary to understand it has been left in another file. The burden is not to collapse the cases. It is to identify their intersections accurately, expose contradictory premises where they exist, and keep the proof status of every proposition visible.

Formal-procedure boundary. This public map is not an extra-record filing, appeal, complaint or personación. Material relied upon in a live proceeding must be put before the competent organ through the lawful procedural route. The map's role is transparency, traceability and cross-record verification.

Audiencia Provincial de Las Palmas

Three appellate objects. One concursal record to test.

Calificación: RPL 2523/2025.

AC separation/removal: RPL 3304/2025, with RPL 3319/2025 recorded as accumulated/linked within that separation-removal track.

AC fees/remuneration: RPL 421/2026.

These are not one legal appeal. They are, however, three appellate objects arising from the same Concurso 36/2012 estate and the same insolvency administration, with overlapping chronology, evidence, the Administrador Concursal's role, the productive unit/hotel and alleged patrimonial consequences.

The institutional question is not whether they share one roll number—they do not. It is whether any one can be understood completely without recognising the material record sitting in the other two.

Read each appeal against the other two

Calificación: can the culpability narrative be evaluated without understanding the disputed administration whose removal was sought and whose remuneration is separately challenged?

Removal/separation: can the conduct relied upon there be assessed without the chronology, evidence and patrimonial consequences that also bear on calificación and remuneration?

Fees: can remuneration be treated as wholly detached from the administration, work, consequences and disputed acts for which removal was sought?

These are cross-reading questions, not findings that one appeal legally controls another and not proof of misconduct.

For any institutional reader

Before accepting the isolated file as the whole relevant record

What exactly was before this organ? What materially connected proceeding, event, asset, credit or evidence family existed elsewhere at the same time? Was that connection formally communicated or otherwise procedurally cognisable? Does another file proceed from an inconsistent factual premise? What disappears if this file is read alone?

The strongest safeguard against an erroneous narrative is not another assertion. It is the ability to move proposition → source → proceeding → response → consequence → related proceeding, and then backwards again.

No conclusion is requested here. The request is methodological: do not decide the part without first testing whether the whole materially changes the part.

Building the map from the canonical register…

The map is navigation, not the evidence itself

A material proposition still needs the underlying filing, decision, institutional original, registered receipt, ACTA, communication or other appropriate source. Open reference gaps remain visible because uncertainty is part of the procedural record. NOT LOCATED is not DID NOT EXIST. The purpose of this map is to help a reader discover the correct next node, understand why it may matter and distinguish what is documented, alleged, inferred, contradicted or still unresolved.
