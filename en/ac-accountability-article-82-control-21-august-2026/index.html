PROCEDURAL CONTROL · 21 AUGUST 2026

Article 82 is for supervision, not historical absolution

The current control does not treat a broad Article 82 TRLC application as an automatic next step. Its residual value is operational: finite production, preservation, accounting or continuing protection of the insolvency estate, after checking the effect of Article 83 and the pending appellate, criminal, qualification and responsibility routes.

Why the distinction matters

A supervision request should not be converted into a substitute merits forum for historic conduct. If a historical issue is already being litigated, preserved or is better determined in a dedicated removal, responsibility or criminal route, the default control is not to invite a duplicative Article 82 determination.

Non-erasure rule

Later production, correction, restitution, improved accounting or a new explanation may reduce continuing harm and may become relevant evidence. It does not erase the original act or omission, the knowledge available at the time, prior contrary notice or the patrimonial consequence. Equally, a later correction is not itself proof of earlier guilt. Each event must remain dated and assessed in the competent proceeding.

Accountability remains route-specific

  • DP 1956/2026: AC-specific knowledge, entrusted powers, concrete acts or omissions, estate harm/accounting and any actor-specific criminal relevance.
  • DP 1901/2026: private-actor, Community and CAM-linked questions concerning authority, title, control, debt, valuation and transaction representations.
  • Judicial-accountability lane: exact judicial acts, record knowledge, supervision and procedural traceability, kept separate from AC and private-actor conduct.

Common evidence may connect routes; one proceeding does not prove another.

Judicial firewall

Judicial approval of an act does not by itself transform the AC's factual premises, calculations, professional recommendation, material implementation, signature, custody, restitution duties or subsequent accounting into purely judicial conduct. Conversely, an adverse judicial ruling cannot be attributed to the AC as criminal conduct without an actor-specific evidential bridge.

Current evidential priority

The priority is primary production rather than additional narrative: complete DP 1956 and appellate files, assignment/notarial records, AC ledgers and final accounts, the two distinct EUR 400,000 trails, the distinct financing chains, and property-by-property title/possession treatment.

Public-language control

This project seeks competent findings of responsibility or culpability where the evidence and law justify them. It does not present criminal guilt, corruption, conspiracy, misappropriation or judicial criminality as established without a competent finding.
