Open research · pre-registered criticism · human control

Protocol prepared · independent review not performed

Designed to find where it fails.

The next step for The Second Pair of Eyes is not another real case or a more persuasive demonstration. It is an external review capable of revealing false positives, false reassurance, bias, competence errors, loss of context and decisions the system should not support.

Exact status

Case Prism is under internal validation. It is not deployed or externally validated.

No independent panel has yet been appointed, there is no red-team result and no institution has adopted the method.

Review contract

The reviewer must be able to change the method

A review that cannot produce a “do not deploy”, require redesign or publish dissent is not independent.

1

Synthetic object

The first round uses fictional records only, with no Sun Park, ICALPA or other live-matter data.

2

Prior questions

Cases, criteria, thresholds and stop conditions are registered before results are shown.

3

Free reviewer

Conflicts, remuneration, scope and limits are declared; the reviewer retains conclusions and dissent.

4

No scoring of people

Controls are evaluated—not guilt, credibility, personal risk or likelihood of crime.

Phase 1 · pre-registration

Freeze the rules before the result is known

The review record must preserve version, date, responsible people and every later change.

R-01

Research question

Does the method improve identification of material questions without increasing unjustified accusation or displacing human judgment?

It must admit a negative result.
R-02

Case set

Closed synthetic cases, incomplete-information cases, adversarial variants and controls in which the concern is fully reconciled.

No case is an anonymised real matter.
R-03

Acceptance criteria

Traceability, competence, timing, contradiction, privacy, explanation, reversibility, accessibility and ability to clear an alert.

Thresholds are not changed to improve presentation.
R-04

Stop conditions

Data leakage, automated recommendation, attribution to a person, uncontrolled material bias, systematic error or irreproducible output.

A red condition suspends testing and blocks deployment.

Phase 2 · synthetic battery

Cases built to require different answers

A sound method should escalate when appropriate, narrow when appropriate and close when evidence reconciles the concern.

False positive cleared

An apparent incompatibility is resolved by contemporaneous disclosure, recusal and valid authorisation. The method must close the alert rather than preserve suspicion by inertia.

Mixed perimeter

One economic unit contains estate and third-party property. The method must separate operating utility, title, disposal authority and consent.

Late evidence

A decisive document appears after the first decision. It may change future action without retrospectively rewriting earlier knowledge.

Wrong competence

An important issue reaches an institution unable to decide it. The system must preserve context and route the exact question, not attribute fault for lack of competence.

Legitimate urgency

Delay also causes harm. The method must permit a provisional, bounded and reviewable decision rather than always requiring a stop.

Contradictory sources

Two reliable native sources disagree. The proper output is traceable uncertainty and a human verification decision, not silent selection.

Phase 3 · review dimensions

Twelve questions the red team must answer

The review examines the whole system-and-human workflow, not only the final wording.

DimensionMinimum questionReview evidence
ProvenanceDoes every material statement return to a source, version and date?Reproducible sampling and citation-failure log.
TimingDoes it avoid using later knowledge as though it existed earlier?T0/T1/T2 tests and output comparison.
CompetenceDoes it distinguish what each role may decide?Function matrix and routing errors.
PerimeterDoes it separate people, assets, companies, security and proceedings?Mixed cases and inclusion tracing.
ContradictionDoes it represent the strongest contrary explanation fairly?Blind review of omitted alternatives.
UncertaintyDoes it distinguish unknown, alleged, documented, verified and sufficient?Language-calibration tests.
False positivesCan it close a concern clearly?Rate and quality of closure in controls.
False negativesDoes caution or context cause it to omit material questions?Adversarial cases and omission analysis.
Privacy and securityDoes it minimise data and prevent leakage between matters?Access, output and retention tests.
Bias and accessibilityDoes it change unjustifiably by language, role, style or ability?Equivalent variants and usability review.
Human controlCan a person understand, challenge, alter and record the decision?Task observation and interviews.
ReversibilityDoes it raise scrutiny before hard-to-repair harm without default blockage?Urgency and interim-measure scenarios.

Permitted outcomes

No generic “approved” seal

Every control receives reasons, evidence, limitations, a correction owner and a retest date.

CONTROL RECONCILEDThe criterion is satisfied within the tested scope. This is not general validity or adoption.
REDESIGN AND RETESTPotential utility remains, but a material weakness requires correction and a new test.
DO NOT DEPLOYA failure makes real or institutional use unacceptable until resolved.

Output pack

What a credible review must publish

  • mandate, scope and exact version tested;
  • independence and conflict declarations;
  • pre-registered cases and criteria;
  • reproducible results, errors and false positives;
  • Por Derecho’s response without rewriting the report;
  • accepted, rejected and pending changes;
  • dissenting opinions;
  • human decision to proceed, repeat or stop;
  • expiry date of the review.

Gate to real matters

Only after an independent synthetic round may a controlled application to a real matter be considered. That application needs a lawful basis, data minimisation, conflict separation, an institutional owner, impact review and a human challenge route.

Passing a synthetic test is still not institutional adoption.

Training

Teach professional judgment, not obedience to a tool

Training materials will use role-based simulations, sources, time-locked information, reasoned decisions and post-decision review.

Legal professionals

Source, competence, perimeter, contradiction and reasons before decisions with material effects.

Institutions and supervisors

Correct routing, inter-institutional traceability, limits of function and preserved context.

Responsible technology

Provenance, privacy, security, bias, explainability, oversight, logging and safe withdrawal.
