Fragmented truth.

Separate responsibility. Integrate evidence. Complex matters often have to be divided among different courts, authorities, companies and legal questions. That separation can be entirely lawful. The risk arises when the relationship between the fragments disappears.

The breadth paradox

The whole can be too broad to investigate; the pieces can be too small to reveal the whole.

The answer is not one super-authority. It is traceable relationships, finite handoffs and human decisions within competence.

Evidential recognisability

Sometimes the evidence is the relationship.

Document A may be accurate. Document B may also be accurate. The material question may still be what happened between A and B: timing, reliance, contradiction, adoption, omission or consequence.

Keep boundaries

Different legal persons, proceedings, dates and competences remain distinct. Context does not erase jurisdiction.

Preserve relationships

Where meaning depends on a source-backed connection, the relationship is recorded rather than lost when files are split.

Human consequence

A relationship alert does not decide illegality, misconduct, guilt or liability. A competent human decides what follows.

Synthetic test

Five variants, different answers.

A useful method must distinguish innocent compartmentalisation from harmful information loss and from genuinely evidenced coordination.

Benign compartmentalisation

Different descriptions are reconciled by timing, legal perimeter and independent verification. The concern must close.

Procedural atomisation

No one misleads, but no decision-maker sees the relationship needed to understand the fragments. The repair is a finite shared-fact handoff.

Record fragmentation

A relevant annex or notification does not travel with the file. Provenance and restoration are examined before blame.

Audience switching

A private actor uses materially different perimeters for different audiences. The differences require reconciliation, not an automatic finding of deception.

Coordination evidence

Only actor-specific communications, instructions or a comparable concrete mechanism justify escalation to a coordination hypothesis.

Clearance matters

If additional evidence restores the relationship or shows the apparent conflict was false, the alert must be removed or downgraded.

The Second Pair of Eyes

No seventh check.

The locked sequence remains Source → Authority → Perimeter → Contradiction → Consequence → Reversibility. Evidential recognisability is an overlay, especially across perimeter, contradiction and consequence.

Its purpose is to make a fragmented record more understandable without making it more accusatory.
