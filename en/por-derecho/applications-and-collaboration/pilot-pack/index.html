Collaboration ladder

Seven levels, each reversible

The institution chooses how far to go. No stage commits it to the next.

1. Observe

Review the public methodology and safeguards.

2. Correct

Identify a factual, procedural, legal or competence error.

3. Red-team

Try to demonstrate bias, false positives, competence drift or a missing contrary explanation.

4. Synthetic pilot

Apply the method to a fictional or anonymised file without real case data.

5. Independent live application

Por Derecho applies the method publicly to its own matter without speaking for the institution.

6. Institutional pilot

Only after express institutional acceptance and agreed governance.

7. Adoption

Recorded only when the institution expressly confirms it.

Neutrality test

The method must be able to say “the concern is resolved”

A control methodology is defective if it can only escalate. A pilot should deliberately include a scenario in which the second check removes the initial concern.
