One methodology.
Many matters.

Por Derecho is developing The Second Pair of Eyes as a reusable method for reconstructing records, separating fact from inference, surfacing contradiction and making the next human decision visible. The method can be applied to a live matter without becoming an accusation engine, and institutions can test it without handing over confidential files.

Application ladder

Separate application, pilot and adoption

Readiness is never presented as institutional endorsement. Each level needs different evidence.

Reference implementation

A real public case demonstrates the method, with uncertainty, counter-evidence and institutional limits visible. No official adoption is claimed.

Next live-case application

The same pre-existing method is applied to a second case. This tests whether the methodology is reusable rather than engineered to reach one conclusion.

Synthetic institutional pilot

A Bar or Council can test the workflow on a fictional or anonymised scenario, without importing real confidential files or delegating human judgment.

Institutional adoption

This state is recorded only when the institution itself expressly confirms use, commissioning, piloting or adoption. Readiness is never called adoption.

Ways to collaborate

Six modes, from observation to institutional pilot

None requires an institution to accept Por Derecho's conclusions.

Observe

Inspect the public method without participation or institutional identification.

Correct

Identify factual, procedural, temporal or competence error for versioned correction.

Red-team

Search for false positives, bias, missing counter-evidence and stronger alternative explanations.

Synthetic pilot

Test a fictional or anonymised case without real-case personal data.

Live application

Apply public pre-existing rules to a real matter with enhanced privacy and no substitution of roles.

Institutional pilot

The institution tests the method under its own governance; only the institution can later confirm adoption.

Technical partners

Implementation support belongs in its own lane.

A document-intelligence or BPM partner can help test ingestion, OCR, classification, semantic research, anonymisation and workflow gates without adopting Por Derecho's conclusions or receiving live confidential evidence in the first stage.

Separation rule

  • Technical assistance is not institutional endorsement.
  • Pilot readiness is not adoption.
  • Shared methodology is not shared evidence.

Methodological contract

“Do not trust our conclusion. Test our method.”

An institution, professional or affected person can improve the model by correcting a proposition, completing an unknown state, supplying documented contradiction or raising a competence objection. Every accepted correction should preserve the prior version, date, reason and source.

Institutional boundary

  • Por Derecho is an initiative in formation; it is not presented here as an already registered foundation.
  • The method is not a disciplinary body, court, prosecution service or appeal route.
  • It does not automate closure, initiation, sanctions, suspension or final decisions.
  • It does not presume guilt or score credibility.
  • Cases do not share evidence merely because they share a methodology.
  • “Pilot-ready” never means “adopted by the institution”.

Reusable standard

What every application must be able to answer

The tool fails if it merely makes an accusation look more sophisticated.

1 · What we know

Sources, versions, evidence states and facts already verified.

2 · What is alleged

Separated from established facts and inference.

3 · What is missing

Material unknowns, who could certify them and what follows if they remain unavailable.

4 · What contradicts it

The strongest contrary explanation and potentially exculpatory material.

5 · Who decides

Competence and the next human gate, without institutional drift.

6 · What would change the view

The evidence or decision that would resolve, weaken, strengthen or close the point.
