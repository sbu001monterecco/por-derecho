Formation · integrity · conflict control

Working draft · 20 August 2026

Governance before growth.

This page converts a foundation intention into a formation and control pack that can be challenged publicly. These are not executed statutes, adopted resolutions or registered instruments. They are design instructions for review by Spanish legal counsel, a notary, the relevant Protectorate and the independent people who may eventually accept real responsibilities.

Exact legal status

Por Derecho remains an initiative in formation.

There is not yet a registered Foundation, constituted Board of Trustees, independent committee, scientific council, accredited programme or institutional authorisation for use.

Formation pack

Eight instruments before execution

Each instrument must be legally validated, adapted to the applicable framework and approved by those who formally accept responsibility.

Draft

1 · Purposes and public-benefit theory

Preventive procedural integrity, documentary traceability, education, research, access to justice and responsible technology without invading public competences.

Not a substitute for the statutory purposes required by law.
Draft

2 · Statutes and mandate rules

Object, seat, scope, Board, terms, appointment, removal, decisions, delegations, accounts, amendment and destination of assets.

Legal, notarial and registry validation required.
Draft

3 · Independence charter

Recusal, conflicts, funding, donors, property, founder, related litigation, publication and prohibition on influence over outcomes.

Must bind trustees, staff, reviewers and collaborators.
Draft

4 · Method charter

Evidence states, provenance, contradiction, strongest defences, corrections, human control, AI limits and review of hard-to-reverse decisions.

A support tool; never an automated decision-maker.
Draft

5 · Data and security policy

Minimisation, lawful bases, role-based access, encryption, retention, incidents, rights, vendors and no silent re-use.

Data-protection impact assessments where required.
Draft

6 · Publication and correction policy

Source, response, proof state, pre-publication review, versions, withdrawal, rectification and separation of research, litigation and public communication.

Visible correction is a function, not a defeat.
Draft

7 · Funding and resource acceptance

Donor diligence, restrictions, gifts, conditional funding, segregated accounts, related-matter funds and rejection of material control.

No resource buys a conclusion or position.
Draft

8 · Property and heritage charter

The seat, if acquired, serves the mission; it does not control the institution. Ownership, use, conservation, funding and governance remain distinct.

The house is not the Foundation.

Matter related to a founder, trustee or funder

Quarantine and independent-decision protocol

Disclosing a conflict does not cure it. It must change who can access, frame, fund, decide and publish.

Declare and classify

Identify the personal, financial, professional, litigation, family or reputational interest and its exact reach.

The declaration is recorded before the material decision.

Information quarantine

Separate the record, permissions, funding, advice, communications and team.

Access only for a documented need.

Recuse

The connected person does not lead the review, select the conclusion or vote on the merits.

They may submit evidence as an identified party subject to contradiction.

Appoint an independent lead

Written mandate, defined competence, checked conflict and freedom to close, correct or decline publication.

The founder has no veto over the conclusion.

Apply the double test

Strongest favourable case, strongest contrary case, missing evidence, competence, proportionality and reversibility.

No response is not an admission.

Record and review

Decision, reasons, dissent, review date, funding, access and evidence that would change the result.

Publication only after legal, privacy and security control.

Funding, property and donors

No asset may control the method

Independence can be lost through material dependency as well as an express instruction.

Resources without outcome purchase

Conditions determining findings, silence, persons examined, publications or appointments are rejected. Legitimate restrictions are documented and compatible with the general purpose.

Segregated accounts and records

A related matter does not use general funds without independent, traceable authority. Personal litigation costs are not presented as Foundation activity.

The house is not the Foundation

San Bernardo may provide permanence and capacity if acquired and brought into use. It gives no seller, funder, owner or donor influence over method, appointments or conclusions.

Formation route

From draft to valid instruments

  1. opinion on legal form, territorial scope, competent Protectorate and purposes;
  2. map of founders, endowment, assets, funding and beneficiaries;
  3. specialist review of statutes and charters;
  4. selection, diligence and acceptance by independent trustees;
  5. financial, accounting, tax, employment, technology and insurance plan;
  6. execution, registration and lawful registrations;
  7. first action plan and external-evaluation timetable;
  8. publish the new status only when documented.

Language control

Until the legal acts are completed and evidenced, the site will continue to say “initiative in formation”. “Board”, “committee” and “council” describe proposed bodies, not constituted ones.

This draft does not replace legal, notarial, tax, accounting, employment, planning, heritage, data-protection or insurance advice.
