Learn to look twice without turning an alert into an accusation.

Por Derecho tests its methodology first on fictional records. Each simulation must be capable of identifying a material issue, preserving a lawful explanation, clearing a false positive and leaving the decision with a human professional.

The neutrality test

An integrity tool is not credible if it only confirms concerns. It must also be able to conclude that a record is reconciled or that the initial hypothesis is unsupported.

No laboratory case is an anonymised real matter. The facts, documents, names, figures and decisions are pedagogical constructions.

Case Prism

One economic asset. Several legal perimeters.

The commercial objective of preserving a unified operation may be legitimate. The question is whether title, authority, mortgage, insolvency, operation and commercial presentation actually reach the same thing.

Time-locked record

The user sees only the information available at each point. Later evidence is not projected backwards onto the first decision.

Separate competences

The insolvency practitioner, court, prosecution service where relevant and professional reviewer see the record through different functions.

Non-binary outcomes

The concern may be reconciled, the transaction may be restructured, or the risk may remain open.

Boundary with the live matter

Test first. Apply later, only if it withstands criticism.

The Case Lab contains no people, companies, documents, figures or decisions from Sun Park. Its purpose is to fix neutral rules before any future use on a real record is considered.

Conditions before crossing that boundary

Rapid comprehension by a neutral visitor · genuine ability to clear concerns · visible human control · correct institutional competence · external specialist review.
