Stable methodology

Six checks for decisions with integrity

The tool does not label people or decide legality or guilt. It asks verifiable questions and preserves the human response.

SourceWhich document supports the proposition and which version it is.
AuthorityWho could act and under what power at that date.
PerimeterWhich assets, people and proceedings are actually reached.
ContradictionWhich material evidence points in another direction.
ConsequenceWho gains, who loses and which effect becomes embedded.
ReversibilityWhether a later remedy could genuinely repair the harm.

Two symbols · two functions

Institutional method and record review

The two icons follow a deliberate visual grammar: one identifies the method; the other marks a legally significant documentary check.

Master mark

Two eyes + scales. Used on methodology, education, research and oversight pages.

Micro-marker

Document + eye. Placed beside a sentence, source or point requiring concrete reconciliation.

Example: this marker does not mean that a breach exists. It means that source, authority, perimeter, contradiction, consequence or reversibility require an identifiable second check.

Technical partner route

A first implementation conversation should use synthetic material.

The live Sun Park matter is the founding stress test. A technical partner does not need to evaluate its merits to help design document ingestion, OCR, classification, source-grounded research, anonymisation, workflow gates and auditability for the Por Derecho method.

Boundary for partners

  • No existing technical partnership is claimed.
  • No confidential live evidence is needed for the first scoping discussion.
  • The system must not produce automated decisions, guilt scores or credibility scores.
  • Technical readiness does not imply institutional adoption.

Research and education

A specialism, not a day-one alternative for “everything”

The initial proposition focuses on AI, procedural integrity and oversight of high-responsibility decisions—not replacing schools of legal practice or general programmes.

Universities

Research, clinics, microcredentials, postgraduate work and independent evaluation.

Professional schools

Specialist modules that can integrate into existing accredited education.

Professionals

Simulations for insolvency practitioners, judges, prosecutors, court officials, lawyers, experts and supervisors.

Responsible technology

Provenance, privacy, explainability, bias, false positives and auditability.
