How it works

A complex record, a structured second review.

The demonstrator shows how an AI layer can ask questions and preserve warnings without replacing the judgment of the responsible professional.

Status: educational prototype. It does not determine legality, invalidity, crime or responsibility. The data are fictional and used only to explain the method.

Alpha File · liquidation

Synthetic case

Facts supplied

  • An insolvent company owns 60% of a property operated as one economic unit.
  • A non-insolvent third party appears as owner of the remaining 40%.
  • A unitary sale is proposed to maximise value and accelerate liquidation.
  • Cadastre, Land Registry and two contractual annexes describe area and easements differently.

Proposed action

Authorise the joint sale on the present record.

Legitimate objective: maximise value for the estate while respecting third-party rights and the legal framework.
Second Pair of EyesAI-assisted second review
AI in service of human decisions
01SourceOriginal sources are identified, but a native copy of one annex is still missing.Reconcile
Check version, date, signature and the relationship between deed, registry extract, cadastral certificate and easement annex.
02AuthorityThe legal bridge enabling a joint sale without the co-owner’s consent is not yet visible.Material inconsistency
The review does not conclude that the sale is invalid. It requires the title, order, mandate or consent allowing the process to reach the 40% outside the insolvency estate.
03PerimeterAnnexes and easements sit inside the economic transaction, but ownership is not unambiguously delimited.Verify further
Distinguish estate asset, third-party asset, common area, use right, easement and accessory element before defining the object of sale.
04ContradictionLand Registry and Cadastre disagree on area and boundaries; the commercial report uses a third description.Material inconsistency
The difference may have a lawful explanation. It should be recorded before presenting the asset as one legally available unit.
05ConsequenceThe transaction may shift risk to the third party, buyer, estate and the professional who authorises or reports.Relevant risk
Identify who receives the price, who loses use or title, which protection the buyer receives and which contingency remains in the estate.
06ReversibilityA completed sale to a third party may be costly and materially difficult to unwind.High irreversibility risk
The less effective a later remedy would be, the stronger the need for prior reconciliation, reasons and preservation of the warning.

AI asks questions. The decision remains human.

No option is preselected.
The tool preserves the warning; the competent person decides what to do and why.
Decision trail
  • Review opened: six dimensions checked; no automated decision exists.
✓ An alert can also be resolved when the record is reconciled.
Neutral closure example.

A supplementary deed predating the proposal is produced, registered and signed by the third party. It expressly authorises the joint sale and defines allocation of the price. The authority alert changes to “issue reconciled”. The tool does not accuse: it also records why a concern is no longer supported.
