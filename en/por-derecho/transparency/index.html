Phase 1 transparency release · 25 August 2026

Transparency before authority.

Por Derecho is an initiative in formation. It is not yet a registered foundation, law firm, public authority, complaints service or deployed judicial system. It arose from experience in a matter in which its founder has direct interests. This page states who presently speaks, what is operating, what remains unverified and what is protected.

Current reality

  • Founder-led initiative.
  • No independent governing body has been constituted.
  • No institutional adoption, accreditation or endorsement is claimed.
  • No automated legal decision or direct AI-to-publication route is permitted.

Evidence and publication

Publish enough to verify; withhold only for a stated and proportionate reason

The original or best controlled source remains authoritative. Public summaries, OCR, embeddings and AI output are derivatives.

Acquire and classify

Identify source, authority, matter, version, date and lawful access before analysis.

External similarity does not create matter membership.

Test the proposition

Separate documented fact, attributed allegation, inference, official outcome and material contrary evidence.

Chronology does not prove intention.

Protect what must remain private

Assess privilege, personal data, source safety, copyright and evidential integrity.

Retrievability never means publishability.

Human publication decision

State what the source establishes, what it does not establish and what evidence would change the view.

No automatic model-to-publication route.

Correct visibly

Material changes retain date, reason, affected page and whether the conclusion changed.

Correction is a function, not a defeat.
