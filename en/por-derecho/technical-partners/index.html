Technical scoping route

Build the method before touching the live file.

Por Derecho is ready for a technically informed scoping discussion with document-intelligence, BPM, anonymisation, search and responsible-AI implementation partners. The first useful conversation is not a legal merits meeting. It is a systems-design discussion about how a protected record can be ingested, searched, cited, reviewed and governed without delegating human responsibility.

Boundary: Por Derecho is an initiative in formation. This page does not claim any existing partnership, pilot, funding, deployment, institutional adoption or endorsement.

Why this route exists

Give technical partners the right door.

The Sun Park matter is the founding stress test. The partner conversation should focus on a reusable, synthetic-testable method rather than asking a technology provider to evaluate disputed facts.

Separate the layers

Live legal record, Por Derecho method, foundation governance and technical implementation must remain distinct even when they link to each other.

Start with synthetic material

A pilot can test the architecture without sharing confidential documents, personal data, privileged material or live-case evidence.

Keep humans accountable

AI can retrieve, classify, compare and ask. It must not decide truth, guilt, liability, sanction, closure or professional credibility.

Technical fit map

Capabilities Por Derecho needs.

This is the capability map for an initial AI document-research and implementation discussion.

Document ingestion and preservation

Bulk intake, deduplication, version control, chain-of-custody metadata, file-family handling and evidence-state labelling.

OCR, extraction and classification

Scanned PDFs, signatures, dates, amounts, parties, authorities, proceedings, deadlines and document-type classification.

Semantic research with source references

Natural-language research over the corpus with exact citations, source snippets, uncertainty markers and refusal to answer beyond the record.

Anonymisation and publication controls

Public/private separation, redaction, pseudonymisation, protected training material and no accidental leakage into public pages.

Workflow gates and audit trail

Human review queues, escalation, correction, second review, sign-off, deadline tracking and auditability of AI-assisted steps.

Security and data residency

EU processing, access controls, privilege boundaries, model-use constraints, local embedding options and clear treatment of sensitive records.

Synthetic pilot

Legal memory and evidence integrity.

The first pilot should prove the workflow, not the legal case.

Prepare synthetic corpus

Fictional or heavily anonymised records with dates, competing versions, missing authority and reversible/adverse outcomes.

Run document intelligence

OCR, classification, metadata extraction, entity/date/amount/proceeding detection and source normalization.

Research with citations

Ask legal-record questions and require grounded answers, contrary sources, missing evidence and confidence boundaries.

Human gate

A person accepts, corrects, rejects or escalates each output. The system preserves the reason, source and version.

Meeting design

What the first call should decide.

The right first meeting is a technically informed scoping session, not a demo request and not a legal argument.

QuestionPurpose
What corpus shape should a synthetic pilot use?Define file types, languages, metadata and adversarial test conditions before architecture.
Which functions belong in search/RAG, BPM workflow and human review?Prevent a single AI layer from becoming an uncontrolled decision system.
How should anonymisation, redaction and public/private boundaries be enforced?Make it technically impossible to confuse partner testing with public publication.
What audit trail is required?Show every prompt, retrieval, source, correction, reviewer and final human gate.
What would a paid scoping workshop or limited POC require?Move from conversation to defined deliverable without claiming partnership or adoption.

Implementation boundary

AI assists the record. It does not become the authority.

The technical partner’s role would be to help design, implement, test or review controlled document-intelligence workflows. It would not be to endorse the live Sun Park position, decide a professional complaint, provide legal advice or validate Por Derecho as a foundation.
