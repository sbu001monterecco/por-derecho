Method · source discipline · human gates

Reusable control method

The Second Pair of Eyes.

The method exists to make difficult legal-record decisions more traceable before harm becomes irreversible. It is not an accusation engine, appeal body, disciplinary authority or automated decision system.

Use rule: Do not trust the conclusion. Test the source, authority, perimeter, contradiction, consequence and reversibility.

Stable method

Six checks before a decision hardens.

Each check must identify the source, the uncertainty and the next human decision. The method fails if it only makes an allegation look more sophisticated.

SourceWhich document, version and evidence state supports the proposition.
AuthorityWho could act, decide, certify or represent at the relevant date.
PerimeterWhich assets, people, time periods and proceedings are actually reached.
ContradictionWhat material evidence points elsewhere or weakens the proposed view.
ConsequenceWho gains, who loses and what practical effect becomes embedded.
ReversibilityWhether a later remedy could genuinely repair the harm.

AI boundary

What AI may and may not do.

The method can be AI-assisted only when the system preserves human authority, source traceability and correction.

Permitted assistance

  • Ingest and classify documents.
  • Extract dates, entities, amounts, authorities and proceedings.
  • Find source-backed answers and contradictions.
  • Build chronology, gaps and review questions.
  • Route items to human review and preserve audit trail.

Prohibited outputs

  • No automated decision.
  • No guilt score, credibility score or person-risk score.
  • No sanction, closure, professional finding or legal-liability decision.
  • No inference presented as established fact.
  • No institutional adoption claim without express written confirmation.

From founding stress test to transferable method

The live matter explains why the method exists. It does not control the method.

Sun Park remains the founding stress test. The Second Pair of Eyes must be capable of being tested on synthetic and contrary scenarios where the concern is weakened, corrected or closed. That is why partner and institutional conversations should begin with a synthetic pilot, not confidential live evidence.
