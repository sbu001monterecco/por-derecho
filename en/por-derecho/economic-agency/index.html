Foundational pillar

SI — Superintelligence of Effective Judicial Protection and Property Rights

SI — Superinteligencia de la tutela judicial efectiva y de la protección del derecho de propiedad

Effective judicial protection and property rights are foundational to this programme, not merely instruments for financial gain.

The intended intelligence supports evidence preservation, timely protection, independent examination and lawful remedies, including when concerns relate to judicial, prosecutorial, court-administration or supervisory acts or omissions. Official accounts and the owner's account must both be tested against their sources. Neither institutional authority nor agreement with the owner substitutes for evidence.

Keep the current legal effect of an act separate from questions about its factual premises. Identify the correct right-holder, property or interest, actor, capacity, timing, contrary evidence and competent review route. This is a method for examining concerns, not a finding about any person or institution, and not permission to disregard orders or procedural requirements.

The eight review cases below make this foundation testable. The versioned contract preserves the detailed scope, legal-orientation references, limitations and change history.

The principal, not the professional silo

The capacity to understand, choose, mobilise and act.

Economic agency means understanding one's true position, choosing a course, mobilising the right resources and acting effectively. The ambition is an integrated institutional capability: judgment, memory, coordination and execution that support the economic principal rather than remaining fragmented across advisers and systems.

Know and protect

Preserve the record, establish what it supports, expose contrary evidence and identify whose rights or value may be affected.

Create and finance

Connect enterprise-building, operating judgment, reporting and financing readiness. Recovery is one purpose, not the limit of the future.

Act and remember

Turn authorised decisions into verifiable action and retain what happened. A draft, an attempted act and a confirmed result are different states.

On our side in purpose. Independent in judgment.

The intended intelligence must challenge our assumptions, narrow unsupported claims and recommend a different course when the evidence requires it. More agreement, more documents or more connected agents is not a substitute for better judgment.

AI asks. Humans decide. Distinct people, companies, proceedings, permissions and responsibilities remain distinct. Reasoning does not grant itself authority to send, file, spend, disclose or publish.

A necessary separation

The founders' private economic programme and Por Derecho's public-interest methodology are institutionally distinct. The founding experience may generate questions; it must not control conclusions. Por Derecho remains an initiative in formation, not a constituted independent institution.

Eight rights-protection review cases

Acceptance scenarios to execute and record—not completed performance tests.

  1. SI-RIGHTS-01: Compare the exact submission with the response; detect a substituted request, allegation, person or reference without inventing the cause.
  2. SI-RIGHTS-02: Trace repeated accounts to their original sources; distinguish independent examination from circular corroboration.
  3. SI-RIGHTS-03: Identify the correct right-holder, threatened interest, timing and potential irreversible harm; obtain competent review of protective steps.
  4. SI-RIGHTS-04: Examine independence and conflicts where an implicated body reviews the concern; assess legally available routes without treating receipt as resolution.
  5. SI-RIGHTS-05: Preserve contrary evidence, revise unsupported owner propositions and propagate corrections.
  6. SI-RIGHTS-06: Keep legal effect and factual reliability separate, retaining compliance, challenge and deadline requirements.
  7. SI-RIGHTS-07: Verify actual custody, filing or review receipts; distinguish preparation, transmission, receipt, examination and outcome.
  8. SI-RIGHTS-08: Reopen affected checks after source correction or loss of access; preserve history, permissions and explicit gaps.

A definition that must earn its continued use

Review the evidence. Review the wording. Review the review.

A bounded recursive audit follows material changes from their sources through the definition, translations and affected pages. It seeks the strongest contrary reading and checks whether earlier corrections actually propagated. It stops with a recorded disposition or explicit blocker—not an endless loop or an automatic green badge.

RETAIN · CLARIFY · REVISE · INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE. These are possible conclusions, not predetermined answers. The review may recommend replacing the phrase itself. Material revisions require owner adoption and the normal publication controls.

The review contract provides for weekly Monday-morning checks and reconsideration when material objectives or evidence change. A scheduled review is not evidence of a completed audit.

Review contract

Read current sources and the last accepted definition; define a finite changed-source scope; test real objectives; seek contrary evidence and hidden dependence; check both excessive institutional deference and owner-confirmation bias; follow the source-to-definition-to-rights-pillar-to-translation-to-page chain in both directions; test proposed corrections; and audit earlier coverage, source independence and correction propagation. Stop with a disposition or explicit blocker for every scoped item.

Concept accuracy, source custody, repository acceptance, deployment, live readback and demonstrated performance receive separate findings.
