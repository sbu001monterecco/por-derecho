How to read the three scopes

Inside a proceeding

A Fiscal report, position or communication recorded within a judicial or other non-Fiscalía file.

Outside proceedings

A complaint, communication, decree or response in a distinct Fiscalía intake, investigation, governmental or inspection file.

Cross-file bridge

One event expressly references both categories. The bridge records that reference only; it does not prove joinder or shared merits.
