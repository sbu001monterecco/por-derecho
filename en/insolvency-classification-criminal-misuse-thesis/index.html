INSOLVENCY 36/2012 · CULPABILITY · INVESTIGATIVE THESIS

Possible criminal misuse of proceedings: five documentary signals, one demanding test.

The current record supports a serious, document-based investigative thesis. Por Derecho calls it the criminal-misuse thesis. It is not a conviction, does not attribute collective guilt and cannot replace investigation by the competent authorities.

CRIMINAL-MISUSE THESIS · DOCUMENT-BASED · NOT A FINDING

The chain must be examined as a whole.

The material point is not one adverse decision in isolation. It is the cumulative sequence: a broad insolvency accusation, exceptionally severe Fiscal adoption, institutional reuse in DI 248, selected judicial adoption, and a possible beneficial effect for the opposing private perimeter.

Proof boundaryThe sequence does not by itself prove common design, personal coordination, knowing falsity, criminal intent or purposeful benefit. Every element must be proved actor by actor and proposition by proposition.
Materially excessive Administrator allegationsThe culpability report advanced a broad package. Judgment 163/2023 rejected or narrowed several branches and did not award the requested insolvency-deficit liability.
Exceptionally severe Fiscal opinionTwo pages requested 15 years' disqualification, damages and deficit liability, with very limited visible individual reasoning and an internal textual defect about who allegedly aggravated insolvency.
Institutional circularity in DI 248The adverse Fiscal culpability position was later invoked when Gil/Aweswell's complaint against the AC/CAM perimeter was archived. The two prosecutors and their acts remain separate.
Selected judicial adoptionThe judgment accepted some adverse propositions, rejected others and left causation and reasoning tensions challenged in RPL 2523/2025.
Possible private benefitIt is necessary to investigate whether the chain produced direct or indirect benefit for the Acosta Matos perimeter, without treating advantage, proximity or aligned results as automatic proof of agreement or offence.

ACTOR-BY-ACTOR TEST

The thesis advances only if it passes this matrix

ElementVerifiable questionWhat it prevents
Act or omissionWhat exactly did each person do, sign, decide, reproduce or fail to do?Guilt by association.
Source and dateWhich primary document fixes the proposition, and when?Unsupported retrospective reconstruction.
Knowledge and authorityWhat did the actor receive or know, and what could or should that actor lawfully do?Automatic institutional knowledge.
Contradiction and responseWhat contrary evidence existed, reached the actor and was treated in what way?Turning disagreement into a lie.
Causation, harm and benefitWhat decision or effect followed, and who obtained what specific benefit?Inferring agreement from aligned outcomes.
Alternative explanationWhat error, procedural limit, legal interpretation or reconciling record provides the strongest defence?An unfalsifiable thesis.

APPEAL FIREWALL

RPL 2523/2025 is not the forum for deciding this criminal thesis

The Provincial Court should decide the appeal only on the procedurally usable record, pleaded grounds, adversarial process and law. This publication is not addressed to the Court, does not introduce extra-record evidence and does not seek agreement with Por Derecho's thesis. Any criminal, civil, regulatory or disciplinary investigation has a different authority, object and standard.

An adverse appellate decision would not prove offence or capture; a successful appeal would not prove the criminal thesis. What the appeal legitimately requires is independence, real engagement with the grounds and sufficient reasons.

CONTRADICTION, CORRECTION AND REPLY

The contrary record belongs on the page

Judgment 163/2023 contains materially adverse findings and they are not hidden. Fiscalía was entitled to disagree with the DI 248 complaint and was not required to perform every requested step. The Judge could assess evidence differently, reasonably rely on the Administrator or consider steps inadmissible. The 2018 provisional dismissal affirmed on appeal concerning the access/control change remains relevant contrary evidence; it neither closes later new facts nor makes the present thesis true.

Right of reply and correction. Every named person or institution may provide a decision, certified index, file, exculpatory document, alternative explanation or verifiable correction. Silence will not be treated as admission. A substantive correction will receive equivalent visibility with its date, source and scope preserved.
