What this page means

Critical representation, not advertising

The pieces are deliberately fictional. They may use exaggeration, irony, advertising composition and double meaning to formulate questions of public interest. Formal fiction does not license an invented identity, hotel, figure, corporate role, legal relationship or source.

A network of relationships, not common ownership

A person, firm, brand, manager, financier, adviser, media outlet or asset appearing in one composition does not prove common ownership, shared knowledge, endorsement, coordination or responsibility. Each visible relationship needs its own type, date, source and boundary.

Central boundary: the ^ marker confirms canonical identity inside CAEPR; it does not confirm an allegation or turn a professional relationship into participation in another act.

MANDATORY CONTINUITY

Every controlled event ends with an action list

Under this control, a material event is not complete merely because it has been narrated. It must produce a finite EVENT_DIGEST_ACTIONS showing what was checked, what was repaired and what remains open. The eleven mandatory lanes are:

  1. Current main / routing: reconcile `main` and the startup controls.
  2. Repository / GitHub digest: search date, reference, file/proceeding, actors, aliases, source, asset and relevant PR/commit history.
  3. Identity ^ audit: enumerate all people/entities and check ID, legal form, aliases, homonyms and ^ state.
  4. Source / provenance / contrary record: find the strongest source and retain contrary or limiting evidence.
  5. Chronology / causal bridges: connect before-event-after without turning chronology into intent or causation.
  6. Proceeding / filing / authority: check filed documents, annexes, registrations, responses and procedural use as separate objects.
  7. Asset / money / role: separate title, equity, debt, finance, RIC, mandate, management, brand, works, payment and benefit.
  8. Interlink / discoverability: verify incoming and outgoing links with stable IDs.
  9. Public / private / publication: distinguish repository, deployment, live website and private/connected sources.
  10. Repair / validate / verify: fix safe defects, run relevant checks and verify the live route if published.
  11. Next actions / checkpoint: leave a finite status list and a restart point outside chat.

No synchronisation assumption: GitHub is the public continuity layer, but Google Drive is not presumed to be a continuous sink; historical chats are not presumed imported; a Gmail/Drive source discussed in chat is not presumed to exist in Git; and a temporary generated file is not presumed preserved.

Sources, corrections and right of reply

Visual pieces must allow the reader to travel back from the compressed headline to the source, ID, role and boundary. A correction does not erase the previous artefact: it identifies it, explains what changed and links the current version. People and entities mentioned may provide documents or contrary context for incorporation into the record under the appropriate evidence state.
