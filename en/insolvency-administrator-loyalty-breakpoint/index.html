CROSS-CUTTING FORENSIC QUESTION · 2008 → PRE-CAM → CAM → LIQUIDATION

At what point, if any, did the insolvency administration stop acting loyally for LPB?

The question does not begin with Acosta Matos. It begins earlier: with the fracture of the 2008 sale and operating architecture, the later private-party disputes and the Community/debt/access machinery that existed before CAM entered the picture. The next question is whether the insolvency administration merely administered that conflict or progressively validated, protected or functionally implemented the position of parties adverse to LPB.

The hypothesis Por Derecho requires to be investigated: if a loyalty breakpoint existed, was it a single moment or a gradual transition — from tolerating an adverse private architecture, to validating it, to allowing CAM later to adopt and monetise it?

1. A structured conflict existed before CAM

The group position reaches back to the 2008 transfer between Monte Lanza and Multimatrix and the continuity of the hotel business as a single economic unit. The controlled project record preserves the case that a minority part of that architecture remained outside or frustrated full implementation of the sale/operating structure.

The recorded 11 June 2016 meeting provides an important control point: Juan Carlos Prieto Puente appears for Molina-family interests and Francisco Mario Matos Matas as Community administrator for those interests, opposite LPB/PwC/Simó/Cristo advisers. The meeting expressly discusses the 2008 operating structure, Community charges, debt, access, governance and the position of the insolvency administration.

Boundary: that meeting establishes a pre-CAM private adverse perimeter and concrete disputes. It does not by itself prove that the insolvency administration represented, obeyed or colluded with that perimeter.

2. The proper test is to identify the “loyalty breakpoint”

Central question: when did an administrator decision, if ever, stop being explainable as neutral insolvency administration — even if debatable or harmful — and begin showing conflict, dependency, functional alignment or conduct incompatible with the estate interest?

Before CAM

Did the administrator investigate debt, votes, access, exploitation and predecessor-party documentation with equivalent rigour? Or were some private-party positions progressively accepted, protected or converted into insolvency reality?

When CAM arrives

Does CAM enter as an independent third party or adopt an already-existing adverse architecture? What did it know about the earlier disputes? Who supplied information, access, position or continuity?

Administrator + CAM

Which contacts, reports, permissions, silences or decisions show coincidence, deference, negligence, conflict or knowing cooperation? Classification must be act-specific.

Benefit to LPB

Where an administrator act materially benefited an adverse perimeter, what measurable benefit did LPB or the estate receive? If there was one, it should be documentable.

3. The continuity that must be proved or disproved

2008 SALE / OPERATION → FRACTURE AND PRIVATE CONFLICT → COMMUNITY / DEBT / ACCESS → ADMINISTRATOR ACTS OR OMISSIONS → CAM ENTRY → CREDIT / CONTROL → JUNE 2018 → LIQUIDATION / ADJUDICATION → HNT / MYND / LATER FINANCE

This chronology does not prove a common agreement. The investigation must distinguish historical succession, opportunistic benefit, knowing adoption of a predecessor architecture, functional cooperation and — at the highest evidential level — direct instructions, representation, agreement or knowingly coordinated action.

4. Retracto is the most sensitive test episode

Two workstreams must remain distinct: the Article 1535 / PP 1041 credit-assignment route concerning PH122→CAM, and the later unit/property-level tanteo-retracto or preferential-acquisition rights.

For PP 1041 it is documented that Cristo Suárez Pimentel initiated the LPB route; on 25 January 2018 the insolvency administration directly terminated his specific formal LPB mandate and placed future action behind an administrator-authorisation gate; and the 5 March 2018 decree records that LPB requested withdrawal, CAM did not oppose and Borja was listed as LPB's lawyer. The precise internal decision, instruction, drafting and filing chain behind the withdrawal remains open.

The validity question is unavoidable: if a material conflict or functional alignment with adverse interests already existed before the administrator took control of LPB's legal representation, what legal effect should be given to interventions affecting retracto, tanteo, representation, withdrawal of proceedings and other LPB protections?

5. Global nullity does not follow automatically

Por Derecho requires each post-breakpoint act to be examined according to its legal nature: conflict or loss of independence, duty breach, excess of authority, invalidity or ineffectiveness, rescission/reversal, patrimonial liability, restitution, unjust enrichment, material procedural indefension and — where constituent facts exist — possible criminal significance.

The question is not “is everything void?” The proper question is: if the office-holder controlling or driving an act was already materially conflicted, which acts depended upon that power and what specific legal consequence follows?

6. Evidential scale for the breakpoint

L0–L2

Adverse effect / recurrence

One harmful decision or repeated same-beneficiary outcomes do not prove representation or collusion.

L3

Conflict indicia

Role overlap, information dependency, missing abstention, asymmetry, contradiction with estate interest.

L4

Strong functional alignment

Repeated conduct difficult to reconcile with a loyal explanation, combined with knowledge, capacity to act and no identified LPB benefit.

L5

Direct proof

Instructions, representation, agreement, undisclosed agency or knowingly coordinated joint action.

Control conclusion: Por Derecho does not publish as fact that the insolvency administrator was the representative of Molina-family interests or CAM, or that collusion is established. It publishes a harder, testable question: what was the first act at which loyalty to LPB ceased to be a sustainable explanation, and what consequences does that breakpoint have for everything the administrator did afterwards?
