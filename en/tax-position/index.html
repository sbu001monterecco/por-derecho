Group governance · tax

Group Tax Position

A concise public statement of how the Group approaches tax governance, professional advice and disputed tax positions. It separates compliance objectives from the lawful right to challenge an authority where facts or law are contested.

Tax governance

Principles applied across relevant entities and jurisdictions

This statement is deliberately framed as a governance position, not as an audit opinion or a representation that every Group entity has identical tax residence, obligations or advisers.

Applicable obligations

The Group's position is to identify and meet tax, reporting and filing obligations under the laws applicable to the relevant entity, transaction, asset and jurisdiction.

Professional advice

The Group uses appropriately qualified legal, tax, accounting and other professional advisers where specialist advice is required. Adviser status is separately controlled on the Current Advisers page.

Risk and documentation

Material tax positions should be documented, capable of professional review and supported by the underlying commercial and legal record.

Authorities and review

The Group may engage with tax authorities through the applicable administrative and judicial processes. Where it disputes an authority's position, it may use the lawful review, appeal, nullity and court mechanisms available.

Important distinction

Compliance and dispute are not opposites

A taxpayer can seek to comply with applicable law and still disagree with a tax authority over jurisdiction, facts, legal characterisation, procedure, evidence or the validity of an administrative act. A pending challenge is not an admission that the authority's position is correct, and the existence of a dispute should not be presented as a finding of wrongdoing.

Spain · open dispute resolution

Pink Canary Services / AEAT

Contested and unresolved.

Current documentary position: Pink Canary Services, S.L. is party to an open contentious-administrative process involving Spain's Agencia Estatal de Administración Tributaria (AEAT). The legal-team record identifies Procedimiento Ordinario 496/2026 before the Audiencia Nacional, Sala de lo Contencioso-Administrativo, Sección 7ª.

The current working draft dated 1 September 2026 seeks to extend the pending judicial appeal so that it also covers AEAT's express resolution of 29 September 2025, which rejected Pink's request for a declaration of nullity. The draft states that the judicial challenge had already been brought against the prior presumed rejection and seeks to make the later express resolution part of the same court process.

Status control: as at 28 August 2026, this repository treats that 1 September document as a legal-team draft unless and until filing is independently confirmed. The existence of the underlying pending proceeding is separate from the filing status of that draft.

Public benchmark

Why the statement is structured this way

Long-term family-owned investment and property groups commonly separate tax governance, risk, adviser use and dealings with tax authorities from the detail of individual disputes. Project Sun Rock follows that disciplined architecture while limiting its statements to positions supported by the Group's own current record.

Tax notice: this is a public governance statement, not tax advice, a tax-residence certificate, an audit opinion or a representation on the final outcome of any dispute.
