ATTRIBUTED PUBLIC POSITION

This page sets out a public invitation by Gil Marer, expressed in his distinct capacities as a businessperson and philanthropist, a directly affected party and an alertador/reporting person. The request is that each institution examine its own part of the record, preserve evidence, explain decisions and correct what the evidence and the competent legal process establish should be corrected.

Six verbs. One standard.

PRESERVE

Keep the record intact

Files, submissions, metadata, communications, allocation records, decisions and referral trails should be preserved in accordance with law.

VERIFY

Return to primary sources

Test propositions against native records, rulings, entries and registers; do not turn a chain of references into a presumption of truth.

EXPLAIN

Make reasoning traceable

Where the law permits, leave enough traceability to show what was received, examined, referred and why a decision was taken.

CORRECT

Rectify established errors

If a premise, datum, route or act is objectively shown to be wrong, correct it through the appropriate legal channel.

ROUTE

Competence without fragmentation

Refer matters to the competent body while retaining the documentary connections needed to understand sequences that may cross files and territories.

ACCOUNT

A checkable response

Institutional accountability means a reasoned, reviewable response compatible with independence, legality and impartiality.

The map: three places, different responsibilities

CENTRE OF GRAVITY

Las Palmas de Gran Canaria

This is the principal focus of the invitation because the documentary core concerns proceedings, judicial bodies and the Las Palmas Provincial Prosecution Office centred there.

LANZAROTE

Arrecife

Within the Prosecution Service, the Arrecife de Lanzarote–Puerto del Rosario Area Prosecution Office is a differentiated unit under the Las Palmas Provincial Office and covers the Arrecife judicial district.

TENERIFE

San Cristóbal de La Laguna

La Laguna sits within a different territorial perimeter: the judicial district is listed within the area served by the Tenerife Provincial Prosecution Office, seated in Santa Cruz de Tenerife.

Two accountability routes that must not be confused

Judges and courts

Judicial independence requires a clear distinction between judicial review and governance, inspection or discipline. The substance of a judicial decision is challenged through the remedies provided by law. The CGPJ has inspection and oversight powers over the functioning of courts, but inspection is not an instruction on how a judge must decide an individual case.

CGPJ · Court inspections · LOPJ · inspection and oversight

Public Prosecution Service

The Constitution organises the Ministerio Fiscal under principles of unity of action and hierarchical dependence, subject to legality and impartiality. That structure is different from judicial independence and allows institutional responsibility and escalation to be mapped without conflating functions.

What this page does not say

It does not identify guilty parties.
A complaint, filing, appeal or hypothesis is not a finding of proved fact.
It does not turn notification into investigation.
Received, examined, investigated and decided are different states.
It does not impose collective responsibility.
Institutional or professional proximity does not transfer knowledge, intention or liability.
It does not seek interference with rulings.
Inspection and discipline do not replace judicial remedies or authorise direction of a judge's decision.
It does not depict real people.
The two figures in the image are fictional and purely symbolic.
It does not erase contrary evidence.
Responses, dismissals, adverse rulings, corrections and exculpatory material must remain visible.

The invitation

Clean up what needs cleaning. Correct what needs correcting. Preserve what needs preserving. Explain what should be explained. And rise to the standard required by law and public service.

The purpose is not indiscriminate disparagement of judges, prosecutors or institutions. It is to ask for a traceable, non-fragmented and legally competent reading of the matters reported, together with a willingness to correct errors where they are established.
