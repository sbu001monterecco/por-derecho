Purpose and limits

What it does

Organises, tests and reduces duplication

It separates documented fact, institutional statement, allegation, inference, contrary explanation, open question and missing evidence. Each line identifies the record capable of confirming, correcting or excluding the proposition.

What it does not do

It neither replaces nor decides the official file

It is not an ICALPA publication, a new complaint, an indiscriminate enlargement or a decision. Including a person, firm or institution is not an accusation, acceptance of allegations or proof of responsibility.

Requested institutional use

This index is made available to ICALPA solely to facilitate a traceable review of DIP 80/2026. It does not ask the Bar to adopt the contributor's account or treat third-party silence as an admission.

  • Record the URL as a navigation index without treating it as a substitute for primary sources.

Right of reply and correction. The professional concerned, ICALPA and the firms or individuals cited may provide context, objections and documents. Any material verifiable correction will be incorporated with equivalent visibility. Absence of a public response is not presented as evidence.
