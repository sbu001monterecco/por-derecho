DIP 80/2026 · REVERSE AND FORWARD RECONSTRUCTION · INSTITUTIONAL CONTAINMENT

The question is no longer only what happened in the insolvency. It is whether office-derived powers became the bridge that made a private patrimonial route operational.

This review works backwards from MYND Yaiza/HNT/RICPE and forwards from LPB, Matkator, the hotel operating unit, the Community and the acquired credit. It identifies which acts depended on the Insolvency Administrator, what verifiable benefit reached the estate, what loss may have followed and what contemporaneous record should explain the decision.

Essential boundary. This page is not an ICALPA publication, decision, expert report or finding of guilt. A criminal complaint, the opening of DIP 80/2026, a professional relationship or absence of a public response does not prove an offence, breach, unmanaged conflict, coordination or responsibility. Criminal elements belong to the criminal authorities; professional conduct belongs to ICALPA within its lawful competence.

Institutional urgency. The opening act is established. What has not been located is a later communication identifying the latest act, the professional's hearing, a pending verification, a reasoned suspension or the selected route. This page does not assert an automatic three-month deadline for completing preliminary information. It asks for the more basic and verifiable answer: identify the clock, the file, the act performed and the path to a reasoned decision.

1. Executive conclusion: the office-instrumentalisation hypothesis

Hypothesis requiring proof or rejection. Powers entrusted to the Insolvency Administrator to represent LPB, inform the Court, preserve the estate, verify credit and value, assess alternatives, liquidate, implement and account may have operated — through acts or omissions that must be individually identified — as a conversion layer through which disputed private premises acquired procedural, patrimonial, registry, financing and operating effect.

Disputed credit, Community authority and control → use or non-use of office powers → weakened LPB defence and information routes → uncontrolled material and economic control → valuation and liquidation → award/dation → HNT financing and MYND normalisation → increasing irreversibility and third-party involvement.

The sequence does not itself establish a criminal plan. Its value is that it creates a falsifiable test: if each decision had a lawful basis, reasonable estate benefit, independence, sufficient information and a contemporaneous record, the records should demonstrate it. Where they do not exist or do not reconcile duty, decision and outcome, the professional-conduct and potentially criminal questions intensify.

2. The source-of-power test

Positive test

What could CAM do itself?

Defend its claimed credit, submit evidence, make applications, bid and seek judicial decisions. Those acts must be assessed by reference to their own authors and elements.

Differential test

What required the office gate?

Control or terminate LPB's representation, withdraw LPB proceedings, select what alternatives reached the Court, report for the estate, protect property and income, implement liquidation and render accounts. Those capacities came from the office, not CAM.

Mandatory sequence for each episode:
OFFICE POWER → LEGAL SOURCE → INFORMATION AVAILABLE → DUTY AND PURPOSE → ACT OR OMISSION → PRIVATE OBSTACLE REMOVED → ESTATE BENEFIT → PATRIMONIAL CONSEQUENCE → CONTEMPORANEOUS REASON → STRONGEST CONTRARY EXPLANATION → DECISIVE MISSING EVIDENCE.

4. Criminal modules: hierarchy and controls

Primary provisional hypothesis

Disloyal administration · Criminal Code article 252

The focus is authority over another's property, possible infringement or excess and patrimonial loss. Power, duty, act or omission, mental element, loss and causation must each be established.

Conditional

Procedural fraud

Requires a deliberate deceptive mechanism or manipulated evidence, judicial error and a harmful patrimonial decision. Legal error, forceful advocacy, a non-deceptive omission or adverse result is insufficient.

Conditional · counsel validation

Professional harm · Criminal Code article 467

Requires identification of the interest entrusted to the lawyer, manifest harm, the act or omission and the intentional or serious-negligence route. Application to the dual lawyer/insolvency-office role requires careful analysis.

Conditional

Documents, secrecy and participation

Each requires the exact document or information, author, knowledge, use, contribution and consequence. Professional proximity, shared branding or final benefit does not replace those elements.

Criminal-control rule. No category is established through rhetorical accumulation. Every proposition must admit a real contrary explanation and identify the evidence capable of excluding it. Where the evidence is absent, the correct status is “pending verification”, not “proved”.

6. ICALPA's clock and institutional risk

85

days from the opening agreement

From 27 May to 20 August 2026. This is not automatically expiry or unlawful delay. It makes it reasonable to require the phase, latest act, hearing, outstanding verification and decision date.

Decisive legal fact

Notification to the professional must be certified

The Statutes require notification of the opening and connect that notification with limitation. Without the date and status of his representations the clock cannot be correctly assessed.

Controlled causal proposition. ICALPA did not cause the historical events and silence does not prove protection or complicity. Timely, finite and reasoned action could nevertheless have reduced foreseeable risks of evidence loss, continuing contested representation, widening professional involvement, continued reliance on disputed premises and normalisation of unexplained practice. The absence of a communicated route has not contained those risks.

The problematic signal to the profession is not a reasoned archive or a decision adverse to the complainant. The risk arises when opened preliminary information, repeatedly supplemented and expressly pressed for a route, remains without a visible account of what was checked, what remains and how it will be resolved. That creates moral-hazard, public-confidence and institutional-reputation risk.

Economic reservation. This review does not presently quantify or attribute additional economic loss to ICALPA's handling. That issue is expressly reserved. The current identified risk concerns evidence preservation, professional governance, institutional confidence and reputation.

7. Institutional intervention requested now

  1. Immediate acknowledgment and routing to the competent body, rapporteur, Dean and Governing Board.
  2. Current phase, date and nature of the latest act and ownership of the next decision.
  3. Exact date on which opening was notified to the professional, status of his representations and whether that step is complete, without requesting protected content.
  4. Official index of material actually incorporated, separated and deduplicated.
  5. A reasoned route: archive; minor-infringement route; formal disciplinary proceeding; finite checks; or reasoned suspension/coordination with a specific criminal route.
  6. For every outstanding check: exact fact, document, custodian, request and completion date.
  7. Preservation or identification of custodians in the PwC/Landwell, Grant Thornton/Cuyás and San Telmo/RSM perimeters within the Bar's competence.
  8. Dean/Governing Board oversight if a decision cannot yet be taken.
  9. A status and route response within five working days, or a dated reasoned programme leading to it.

No outcome is demanded. A reasoned archive after proportionate and adversarial checking is legitimate. So are defined checks, a grounded suspension or formal opening where appropriate. What is no longer sufficient is a phase without identified ownership, clock or communicated explanation.
