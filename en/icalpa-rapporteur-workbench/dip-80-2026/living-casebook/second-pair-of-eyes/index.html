One material question, six checks

“Second check open” does not mean the concern is correct. It means an identifiable reconciliation still has to be closed before a merits inference.

Definition of 100%

No material unknown remains hidden

Completeness does not require possessing every possible document. It requires every material proposition to be either verified or explicitly identified as unknown, with likely source, acquisition route, effect if unavailable and a human gate.

1. Identify

Fact, act, omission, date and duty.

2. Test

Primary source and strongest contrary explanation.

3. Resolve or keep open

Clear the concern or define the finite remaining check.

4. Decide

The competent human authority reasons the next step.
