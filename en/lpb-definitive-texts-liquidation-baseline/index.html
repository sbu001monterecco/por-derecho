Which definitive text governed LPB's liquidation?

The documentary answer is not one PDF. It is a chain: the 2013 report and inventory, the 2016 filing and definitive creditor list, the February 2018 substitution of the credit holder, and a liquidation plan expressly referring back to the inventory made definitive.

Primary-source promotion · reviewed 20 August 2026

Boundary. This page reconstructs the documentary starting point. It does not conclude that every later act is void, that a liquid surplus exists, that every later interest component is necessarily improper, or that a drafting inconsistency proves unlawful conduct. Each consequence requires its own source, parties, law, finality, time limits and proof.

The operative chain

15 January 2013 · Insolvency Administration report

The report under former Article 75 LC contains the active-mass inventory, passive analysis and the Administration's qualifications concerning documents and valuation. It is the baseline later modified, not a document silently displaced.

28/29 April 2016 · definitive texts

The Administrator states that the results of incidents and challenges were incorporated into the inventory, creditor list and reasoned report, and that the earlier report was elevated to definitive status except where expressly modified.

28/29 April 2016 · separate definitive creditor list

The creditor schedule identifies each claim, classification and change. The total specially privileged credit is EUR 9,052,251.69.

8 February 2018 · change of holder, not automatic enlargement

The Court recognises Construcciones Acosta Matos, S.A. as holder of the two claims previously attributed to Promontoria: EUR 857,373.81 and EUR 8,194,877.88. The assignment changes the holder at those amounts; it does not by itself turn all mortgage caps or later accruals into definitive insolvency claims.

2018 · liquidation plan

The plan itself says that the assets to be realised are those included in the inventory annexed to the Administrator's report and made definitive, and that plan valuations start from that inventory subject to the updated appraisal and properly recorded later changes.

Which inventory entered the liquidation?

According to the report made definitive, the plan identifies the following LPB real-estate perimeter:

159

Mortgaged properties identified by Registry number in the plan.

29

Premises

Including reception and service/maintenance areas described by the plan.

2

Pool-and-solarium properties

Separate properties treated with the unencumbered asset group.

Property boundary. This is LPB's inventory. It does not by itself turn Matkator or third-party properties, or every operating, access or common-area right, into insolvency-estate assets.

The plan contrasts a definitive real-estate inventory value of approximately EUR 15.375 million with a 2018 updated appraisal allocating EUR 7,096,913.83 to the identified LPB real-estate assets. A valuation change does not by itself alter the legal identity of a property or determine the payable debt or a surplus.

Six quantities that must remain separate

1 · Inventory

Which assets and rights entered the estate and at what reference value.

2 · Insolvency classification

Which claim was recognised, for whom and with what privilege: here, EUR 9,052,251.69.

3 · Mortgage responsibility

Registered caps for principal, interest, costs and accessory items.

4 · Payable debt

The amount legally due on a specific date after applying insolvency law.

5 · Non-cash consideration

Debt that a deed says was extinguished or set off in exchange for a transfer.

6 · Realisation outcome

Cash, set-off, waiver, costs and any surplus finally recorded in the accounts.

Arithmetic identity or difference between documents does not establish that the amounts perform the same legal function.
