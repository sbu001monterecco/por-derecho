BOUNDARY THAT MUST NOT BE ERASED

LPB is in insolvency. That does not turn the entire hotel into the estate.

Inside

Patrimony, rights, claims, receivables and fruits belonging to LPB, together with acts that lawfully fall within Insolvency 36/2012.

Outside or requiring separate authority

Matkator and third-party property and rights; also any whole-hotel access, possession, operation, works, income or control requiring specific title, consent or order.

Overreach test

For every act: holder → authority → order → actor → knowledge/objection → response → benefit → harm. A global invocation of “the insolvency” is not enough.

CAUSATION TO PROVE, NOT A JUDICIAL CONCLUSION

Active estate, productive unit and extra-insolvency harm

Active estate

The published thesis is cumulative diminution/destruction of LPB's recoverable value: assets and rights, fruits, occupation, deterioration, credits, overpayments, rescue opportunities and costs. Each item requires holder, period, actor, causation and quantum.

Productive unit

Kept separate from the real-estate inventory: operation, reservations/data, contracts, revenue, licences, services, goodwill and the hotel's ability to continue. Alleged destruction or disruption requires proof of who held each component.

Extra-insolvency harm

Matkator was not the debtor. Its units and direct losses must not be absorbed into LPB. The same applies to proprietary rights of other holders and autonomous losses outside the insolvency.

Alleged overreach

The point is not that the insolvency has no effect; it is that its effect has a perimeter. The question is whether it was used or treated as authority for acts, assets, control or operation requiring an independent legal basis.

7 June 2018 as the hinge. The investigation must connect before and after: access authority → material control change → alleged closure/non-operation and income loss → frustration of funded exits → preservation or loss of assets/fruits → later adjudication and transformation. That causal chain remains an evidential thesis, not a final judgment.
