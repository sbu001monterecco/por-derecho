First, where is the separate disposition of the private Ref.21 subject and its supplement? Second, what happened to Ref.24’s own wider pre-2025 nucleus: financed exit, credit, OB REM, bidding/adjudication and entity identity? If the order concerned only the judge complaint, identify the private complaint’s actual file and decision. If another competent file already examined them, produce it and narrow the non-treatment allegation accordingly.

Why this matters to recovery

The papers sought documents held by counterparties, banks, Community custodians, digital providers and administrations. Those records may connect authority, funding decisions, recipients, losses and assets. A defect in handling can affect access to proof and effective relief; the particular effect must be demonstrated, not assumed.

Map claimant loss, recipient gain and presently reachable assets separately. Whole-wealth investigation does not declare all wealth criminal proceeds. The group’s property, creditor, funding, CEXP and wider commercial interests are not all contained in LPB’s insolvency estate.

Fiscal report, E.G.745 and judicialisation

The 29 July report exists as a dated act seeking archive according to the September order. Its author, assignment, exact documents and reasoning remain required. The 15 September DI22 decree separately maintains archive on judicialisation grounds; it does not identify DP1901 as the relevant proceeding on its face.

The risk to test is circular reliance: one route declines because another allegedly covers the facts, while that other route may have a different subject. Preserve actual decisions and lawful alternative explanations. Do not transfer authorship from DI22 to the July report, or treat a limited archive as universal clearance of aid, title or financing.

The practical addition to the Presidencia requests

Within its powers, request the entry/scan/index/association history, exact referrals, competent holder of each subject, records of preservation and the Government’s own reliance and decisions. Judicial merits and attachments remain with the competent authorities. The separate SAIP later seeks existing handling records; it does not replace the urgent communication or preserve every deadline.
