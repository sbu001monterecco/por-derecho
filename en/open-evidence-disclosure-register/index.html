Open Evidence & Disclosure Register

Project Sun Rock / Por Derecho publicly identifies the records that can confirm, narrow or disprove its current reconstruction. We invite courts, public authorities, regulators, professional advisers, corporate custodians, lenders, insolvency actors and other lawful record-holders to help close those gaps.

This invitation is symmetrical. Evidence that supports our current account is useful. Evidence that contradicts, exculpates, contextualises or corrects it is equally useful. If reliable primary evidence shows that our public record is wrong, we intend to correct the public record visibly.
Transparency does not mean surrendering litigation strategy. We do not publish privileged advice, witness preparation, tactical sequencing, deadline calculations, settlement positions, private contacts, draft pleadings, confidential provider locators, native private email bodies, third-party personal data or security-sensitive preservation methods. This page is not a subpoena, filing, complaint, appeal, waiver or merits finding.

P5 · Our public transparency commitments

  1. Move material corrections into canonical sources. We commit to reducing reliance on runtime-only overlays so crawler-visible/static sources carry the current controlled wording.
  2. Five Documentary Bridges as public gateway. We will use the five finite primary-record bridges as the main institutional entry point rather than escalating rhetoric.
  3. Show the strongest defence beside each gap. Each major bridge should state the strongest contrary explanation, missing controlling record and what would change if that record is produced.
  4. Publish quantitative ledgers only when reproducible. The credit/satisfaction and claimant/recovery ledgers will be published only with reproducible source tables and calculations.
  5. Refresh operational truth without rewriting history. Current-state metadata should be regenerated through the prescribed workflow; historical observations remain historical evidence.
  6. Keep CAEPR caret denominators scope-specific. Every identity-coverage claim will state its exact denominator; a specialist/local result will not be promoted into global completion.
  7. Reconcile stale PRs proposition by proposition. Later corrections control; stale branches will not be wholesale-merged over newer source boundaries.
  8. Keep adverse evidence visible. Material adverse judgments and contrary records will remain visible beside the allegations and appeal/correction record.
  9. Search native mail/Drive for contrary evidence too. Native Phase-B acquisition will be question-led and will search exculpatory and contradictory evidence on equal terms with supportive material.
  10. Preserve thread continuity audits. Material thread conclusions will continue to be written into durable repository controls so chat deletion does not erase operational reasoning.
