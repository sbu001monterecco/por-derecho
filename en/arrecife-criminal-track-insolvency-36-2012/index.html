What the architecture establishes

Notice is plural; proof remains bounded.

This chronology defeats a simplistic “no notice unless one LexNET receipt is found” model, but it does not allow the opposite overstatement.

Controlled / supportable

  • A formal Arrecife → Mercantil exhorto lane existed.
  • Arrecife later records receipt of a Mercantile response.
  • The 7 June allegations and urgent-protection request are contemporaneous.
  • Separate AC and Irigoyen knowledge/notice lanes require actor-specific audit.
  • Counsel correspondence records an instruction/draft/communication issue.

Not established by this page alone

  • The missing Mercantile response’s wording or annexes.
  • That a takeover-specific Mercantile LexNET filing did or did not occur.
  • The exact information orally supplied to the judge or AC on 13 June.
  • Criminal intent, prevaricación, collusion, capture or any actor’s guilt.
  • That every alleged physical act was unlawful or adjudicated as a crime.
[CONTENT MISSING] means the underlying content has not been located and must not be reconstructed by inference. LEXNET RECEIPT NOT LOCATED means no matching receipt is controlled at present; it does not mean that no filing existed.

Criminal-prosecutorial / forensic first

Test each actor against the same evidentiary sequence.

The dry-law procedural classification runs alongside this test; it does not replace it.

actor → notice/knowledge → legal duty or power → act/omission → alleged deception/concealment → procedural effect → patrimonial effect → beneficiary/harm → subjective element → counter-evidence → missing proof → later implementation

Judge / court

What did the court receive, when, through which formal or actual-notice route; what could it legally do; what did it decide or omit; and what later implementation followed?

Insolvency administrator

Once actual knowledge is evidenced, what did the AC verify, report, request, protect and account for regarding access, possession, security, income/fruits, third-party assets and funded exits?

Counsel / intermediaries

Reconstruct instruction → acknowledgement → advice → draft → deadline → filed receipt → court treatment → consequence. Preserve work done; do not infer motive from a missing receipt.
