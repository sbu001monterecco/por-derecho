PRIMARY JUDICIAL RECORD · 25 JUNE 2018

What the sworn witness record establishes — and where it stops.

The controlled 32-page court attachment records that Miguel Ángel Barreiro Cano promised to tell the truth. In his statement he placed a person he called “JOAN” at Sun Park in February or March 2018 together with a person he called DANIEL. He described measurements of the buffet dining room, night bar and entrances using a plan; another occasion when “DANIEL Y JOAN” showed the hotel to three men; and two further visits by “DANIEL Y JOAN”.

Do not over-read this source. The statement itself does not identify “JOAN” by surname, does not state what entity he represented, does not prove an operator contract, and does not determine whether any February/March access was authorised or unlawful. The court-file folio numbering has also not yet been reconciled page-by-page to the later 18/19-page photographic compilations.

Limiting and contrary evidence travels with the corroboration

The same sworn record says the witness did not know JOAN's role, describes the relevant visit with three other men as being in common areas and says no damage was caused during that visit. It also separately states that before 7 June the witness had not seen José Daniel Acosta Matos force a door cylinder and that José Daniel Acosta Matos was not present at the 7 June event.

Those limits matter. The combined chronology supports investigating knowledge, evaluation, measurements, project preparation and the identity/capacity of the participants. It does not by itself establish force, unlawful access, a particular contract, fraud, conspiracy or criminal common purpose.
