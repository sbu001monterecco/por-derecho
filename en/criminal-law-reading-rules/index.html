PUBLIC CRIMINAL-LAW SAFEGUARD

Evidence first. Individual attribution. No collective guilt.

This rule governs the public reading of Por Derecho’s criminal-law material. It does not decide whether any offence occurred, who is responsible, or which remedy will ultimately be available.

A sequence is not a plan; a relationship is not responsibility; receipt is not endorsement; an adverse decision or legal error is not criminal intent.

Individual attribution is indispensable

Required bridge

For a criminal hypothesis, the record must separately test act, legal capacity or authority, authorship or instruction, knowledge, intent where required, use or reliance, prejudice, benefit, causation, and any correction or continuation after notice.

No transferred intent

A corporate relationship, later title, regulated status, professional contact, receipt of a report, commercial benefit or chronology does not transfer knowledge or intent from one person or entity to another.

Separate legal perimeters

LPB is not the whole hotel; the Owners’ Community is not CEXP; CEXP is not Monterecco/Pink; creditor position is not whole-hotel title; material control is not title; later title is not retroactive authority.

Contradiction first

Every serious inference must state the strongest contrary evidence, the strongest innocent explanation, the missing discriminator and the source that could narrow or defeat it.

Procedure, recovery and standing remain separate

Prepared → sent → delivered → registered → joined to file → admitted → investigated → decided are distinct statuses. No stage is implied by an earlier one. Criminal preservation, civil restitution, insolvency accounting, registry correction, regulatory supervision, tax/funds reconciliation and transparency access are separate routes with separate competent authorities.

No asset, income stream or holder is described here as criminal property, a criminal beneficiary or a bad-faith recipient without the primary legal finding. Tracing preserves questions; it does not predetermine confiscation. LPB, Aweswell, Matkator, CEXP, individual owners, operators and third parties may have different rights, losses and standing.

Correction and source safety

Authentic, documented correction receives equivalent visibility. Public pages are source indexes and finite questions; native records, confidential communications, personal data, protected witnesses, legal advice and unredacted material remain outside the public repository.
