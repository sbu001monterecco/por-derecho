One alleged organised operational unit — narrowly defined.

This page visualises Gil Marer / Project Sun Rock's attributed case theory that five identified private actors, the Sun Park Community organ, the Insolvency Administrator and the judicial-supervision layer converged functionally around specified Sun Park acts and outcomes. The visual frame is an investigative proposition to be proved edge by edge, not a finding of collective guilt.

5 private actors 1 Community organ 1 Insolvency Administrator 1 judicial layer 5 later continuity nodes\n 8 witness / custodian nodes
Definition used on this page.

Organised operational unit means only an alleged functional convergence of distinct actors, an organ and institutional control points around identified documents, authority claims, access/security events, insolvency acts and consequences. It does not mean one legal person, one company, an adjudicated conspiracy, a criminal organisation, automatic shared intent, or that every participant knew or directed every act.

The narrow frame

Functional convergence, not transferred liability.

The diagram puts the requested actors and control points in one frame because the allegation concerns a linked sequence. The frame itself proves nothing. Every connector has to survive actor-specific tests of capacity, authority, knowledge, act or omission, intent where legally required, causation, benefit and the strongest lawful alternative.

Publication discipline

What would justify strengthening—or dismantling—the map.

A serious organised-unit hypothesis must be falsifiable. A missing bridge is not filled by repetition; a contrary source is not hidden because it weakens the theory.

Strengthen an edge only with a bridge.

Identify the actor, capacity, date, native document or act, authority claimed, sender/recipient, knowledge, instruction or adoption, effect, causation, beneficiary, lawful alternative and what happened after notice. Where the proposition concerns criminal intent, association or sequence is insufficient.

Break an edge when the evidence breaks it.

Publish a lawful mandate, contrary instruction, lack of receipt, independent decision, corrective measure, missing causal link, different contracting entity, temporal impossibility or other exculpatory record at the same level of visibility as the allegation.

Keep later entities later.

RICPE, Lava Verde, Club SEI, MYND Yaiza and Orion are not silently converted into members of an earlier alleged unit. Their relevance depends on the precise later representation, transaction, governance, funding, due-diligence, disclosure or benefit question being tested.

Right of reply and correction.

Any identified person or entity may provide a correction, exculpatory document, lawful explanation or reply. Silence is not admission. A correction should preserve the earlier source, what changed and the date of change.
