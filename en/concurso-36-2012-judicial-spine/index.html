Three ledgers needed to close the chain

1. Order → Asset

For every finca: previous owner, inclusion or exclusion from LPB's estate, mortgage status, treatment in each order, deed 457, Registry result and any later transfer. Matkator and third-party property cannot be merged by narrative into LPB's estate.

2. Order → Money

Every figure must retain its legal function: credit, mortgage cap, competitive threshold, consideration, cash, cost or surplus. A repeated number does not automatically retain the same legal function.

3. Order → Implementation

For each authorization: what was ordered, conditions, who had to act, what proves compliance, when, which deed/mandate/Registry/bank/accounting entry implemented it, and what remained unresolved.
