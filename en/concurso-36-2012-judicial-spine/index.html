What the court actually authorized and how it became the transaction registered in 2022

This register avoids compressing the 2022 result into one generic “adjudication order”. It reconstructs each act separately and requires a documentary bridge between judicial authorization, the legal/economic function of each figure, deed, Registry and accounts.

Central rule. Recognized insolvency credit ≠ mortgage liability ≠ third-bidder threshold ≠ debt stated as consideration for the dation ≠ registry value ≠ eventual surplus.
Method correction. Do not use “the 26 January 2022 adjudication order” as shorthand for the whole sequence. Auto 164/2021 definitively approved CAM on 18 May 2021; two 15 October decisions dealt with separate branches; the two 26 January 2022 Autos were clarifications; and deed 457 was executed on 21 February 2022.

Three ledgers needed to close the chain

1. Order → Asset

For every finca: previous owner, inclusion or exclusion from LPB's estate, mortgage status, treatment in each order, deed 457, Registry result and any later transfer. Matkator and third-party property cannot be merged by narrative into LPB's estate.

2. Order → Money

Every figure must retain its legal function: credit, mortgage cap, competitive threshold, consideration, cash, cost or surplus. A repeated number does not automatically retain the same legal function.

3. Order → Implementation

For each authorization: what was ordered, conditions, who had to act, what proves compliance, when, which deed/mandate/Registry/bank/accounting entry implemented it, and what remained unresolved.

Document priority

  1. Stamped third-party proposal, powers, funds, service and bond evidence.
  2. Complete minute or recording of the 18 May 2021 appearance.
  3. Applications, oppositions and service for the 15 October 2021 and 26 January 2022 pairs.
  4. 2021-2022 testimonios, requests, service and notarial/Registry use.
  5. Post-deed court communication, mandate and Registry implementation.
  6. Complete accounting bridge, including EUR 400,000 and any surplus.
