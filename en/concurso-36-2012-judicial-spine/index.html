What the 2013 insolvency-administrator report adds

A mixed cooperation record

The administrator called documentary delivery “not acceptable” because of delay and omissions. The same report says the representative attended every requested meeting, relations were “cordial and fluid” and there was a cooperative disposition. A source-faithful criticism preserves both propositions: documentary delay is not total refusal to cooperate.

Value, liabilities and trajectory require explanation

The administrator provisionally estimated EUR 16,752,575.32 in assets against EUR 9,469,157.93 in insolvency liabilities, with EUR 15,375,462.70 attributed to the real estate. This is not an adjudicated solvency finding: several entries and supporting records were unverified. It does require later reports and accounts to explain the trajectory of value, income and exit possibilities into liquidation.

Perimeter and future action

The report addresses 159 apartments, 29 premises and four pools after describing the sale of 12 of the 171 apartments initially acquired. It does not establish LPB ownership of all 262 properties in the mixed complex. It also announced rescission, recovery and possible criminal-action review; authority, implementation and outcome must be located before effectiveness or omission is asserted.

Official-request status. The 20-Aug-2026 agreement in Exp. Gub. 38/2026 answered and archived the TSJ supervisory lane, directing the requested index and testimony chains to article 234 LOPJ. An administrative appeal receipt and registry-processing confirmation are located; no superior decision is located. No response or certified production from the other two 15-Aug lanes is located.

Three ledgers needed to close the chain

1. Order → Asset

For every finca: previous owner, inclusion or exclusion from LPB's estate, mortgage status, treatment in each order, deed 457, Registry result and any later transfer. Matkator and third-party property cannot be merged by narrative into LPB's estate.

2. Order → Money

Every figure must retain its legal function: credit, mortgage cap, competitive threshold, consideration, cash, cost or surplus. A repeated number does not automatically retain the same legal function.

3. Order → Implementation

For each authorization: what was ordered, conditions, who had to act, what proves compliance, when, which deed/mandate/Registry/bank/accounting entry implemented it, and what remained unresolved.
