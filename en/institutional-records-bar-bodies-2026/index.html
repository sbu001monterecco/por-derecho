IDENTITY CONTROL · 25 AUGUST 2026

Four bodies. Four competences. No automatic equivalence.

This record supplements the general institutional map and locks the separation between ICAM, CCACM, ICALPA and ICATF so future work does not collapse identities, competences or procedural status.

Rule: filing, receipt, referral, preliminary information, archive and appeal are distinct states. The existence of a professional-regulation route does not prove misconduct. An acknowledgement proves receipt only.

DIP 79/2026 and DIP 80/2026 are distinct preliminary professional-control tracks with different objects and persons concerned. They must not be folded into 193/2026 or treated as one chain with ICAM.

Locked rule: CCACM is Madrid. ICATF is Tenerife. ICALPA is Las Palmas. Acronyms must never be inferred from thematic proximity.
