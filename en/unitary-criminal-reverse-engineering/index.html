ATTRIBUTED CRIMINAL THEORY · NOT A JUDICIAL FINDING. Gil Marer and Aweswell allege one continuing economic-criminal enterprise, advanced through successive adoption and divided functions. The theory does not itself prove a statutory criminal organisation or group under Criminal Code Articles 570 bis or 570 ter, an original pact, any person’s participation or guilt. Each actor and each offence require separate proof of their historical elements, conduct, capacity or duty, knowledge, intent where required, causal contribution and contrary material. This is only a factual allegation of connection: it does not characterise the conduct as a continuing or permanent offence or alter completion, any participation window or limitation period for any specific offence.

Criminal case and defence

Strongest prosecution

Strongest prosecution: creditor decisions, suppression of disclosure or exits, insolvency authority exercised against LPB, disputed value, control before title, unreconciled debt, competition and surplus, and preservation of benefit in HNT/MYND formed connected bridges. Every proposed offence separately requires its own elements in force at the historical date, authorship or participation, mental element and causation; deception, disposition, loss or benefit are imported only where that specific offence requires them.

Strongest defence

Strongest defence: no ex ante pact; the dación was judicial and post-default; Article 1535 was rejected; ACTÚA was professional; ONA/funds were conditional; the third party may have failed requirements; control may have been authorised; later titles and third parties may be independent; no common plan or intent has been adjudicated.
