HISTORIC OWNERSHIP/OPERATION · OPEN IDENTITY BRIDGE

Montelanza / MONTE LANZA: Sun Park perimeter and outstanding tax-ID/sheet bridge

BOC uses Montelanza, S.L., CIF B35279850, for Hotel Apartamento Sun Park; BORME uses MONTE LANZA SOCIEDAD LIMITADA, sheet GC1339, when publishing dissolution in 2015 and extinction in 2018. Context suggests continuity, but spellings are not normalised without a certificate linking tax ID and sheet.

Search variants: Montelanza, S.L. · MONTE LANZA SOCIEDAD LIMITADA. They do not replace the canonical identity.

Documented

Documented

BOC links Montelanza and its tax ID to Sun Park. Internal minutes place it as owner/entity and in historic operation. BORME publishes dissolution and extinction of the separated MONTE LANZA spelling.

Attributed account

Attributed account

The project position attributes to Montelanza an owner/operator function at the root of the 2008 dispute. It is not presented as a sale of the company, a common breach or the sole cause of insolvency.

Open proof

Open proof

A tax-ID/sheet certificate, historic contracts, unit-by-unit schedule and complete transfer/operation file remain outstanding. It must not be confused with Construcciones Montelanza 06, S.L.

READING METHOD

One chronology, three evidence lanes

This record does not convert professional proximity, a shared corporate office or chronological succession into a mandate, knowledge, conflict, agreement or responsibility. Every proposition retains its date, source and limit.

Publication boundary

Official and internal facts are presented as documented facts. The declarant's statements are expressly attributed. An allegation is not a ruling; an open question is not an insinuation of guilt.
