READER ROOM · PERMANENT COMMISSION · APPEAL 286/2026 / DI 169/2026

The question is not whether one ruling was wrong. It is what supervision protected the estate before each irreversible step.

This reader room reconstructs one institutional question: knowledge → supervisory duty/power → request or risk → identifiable response → consequence → later opportunity to correct. It begins with the adverse CGPJ decisions and does not ask the Council to revisit the jurisdictional merits of court rulings.

Preparing this page for a possible institutional review or visit does not mean that any visit is scheduled or that the CGPJ cooperates with or endorses this publication. The institutional mark identifies the body only.

THE ORIGINAL THESIS · RESTORED

Protection of the insolvency estate and supervision of the Insolvency Administrator are the spine, not a side issue.

The communication that originated DI 169/2026 asked whether judicial supervision over the principal asset in Insolvency 36/2012 and over the Insolvency Administration had been exercised sufficiently. Appeal 286/2026 developed the same question: not to recalculate debts or replace appeals, but to identify what control acts linked known risks to preservation, information, liquidation and the final patrimonial result.

KNOWN RISK → ADMINISTRATOR MUST REPORT/PRESERVE → JUDGE CAN SUPERVISE/REQUIRE → RESPONSE → PATRIMONIAL STEP → WAS IT RECONCILED?

Current insolvency legislation confirms that the Insolvency Administration is subject to judicial supervision and that the judge may require specific information. For 2018 events the law then in force must be applied; the current rule is used only as a systemic confirmation of the supervisory relationship, not retroactively.

THREE LEVELS OF KNOWLEDGE

The judge is not attributed everything known by the Administrator or everything that happened outside the insolvency file.

LEVEL 1

Direct judicial knowledge

Filed submissions, hearings, rulings reproducing allegations, formally transmitted reports and documented acts of the court.

LEVEL 2

Administrator knowledge / reporting duty

Facts known or requiring verification by the Insolvency Administration. They are attributed to the judge only where the timing and method of reporting can be identified, or where the risk later reached the court directly.

LEVEL 3

External or later context

RICPE, CNMV, tax, funds, later hotel operation and other tracks may establish continuity or consequence, but not historical judicial knowledge unless incorporation into the case is shown.

PERIMETER

The insolvency had to protect LPB without turning LPB into all of Sun Park.

Inside the estate

Insolvency authority required inventory, preservation, information and liquidation of LPB property/rights. The supervisory question becomes stronger where deterioration, exclusion of access, material control change or economic use of the asset is alleged.

Outside the estate

Matkator, third parties, operating rights and other titles did not automatically enter LPB's insolvency. The paradox to test is whether property under judicial protection was insufficiently protected while limited insolvency-derived authority produced material effects beyond the estate.

Locate the act distinguishing and connecting principal, interest, classification, improvement threshold and debt extinguished/absorbed. Numerical difference ≠ irregularity; absence of an identified reconciliation = documentary question.

TITLE BRIDGE

SEPARATE MODULE

Appearance of impartiality / LPAM does not substitute for proof of supervision.

Allegations about communications or a personal relationship remain separate. No intimate friendship, influence, partiality, corruption or responsibility is established. The institutional question is only whether the individualised material and proposed official sources warrant a finite check or a specific explanation of why such a check would be unnecessary.

POSSIBLE INSTITUTIONAL OUTPUTS

The page does not demand a predetermined conclusion.

A reasoned answer may: maintain the jurisdictional boundary if it explains why every nucleus lacks a governmental or disciplinary dimension; distinguish and refer court-functioning issues to the Inspection Service; return a defined disciplinary module to the Promotor where Article 608 LOPJ permits; separate LAJ/court-office matters; or leave criminal characterisation exclusively to the Public Prosecution/TSJ route. None of those steps itself proves responsibility.

Institutional defence preserved: rulings, reports, procedural constraints, restitution acts, urgency reasons or technical explanations may exist outside Por Derecho's corpus. If identified, they must be incorporated and the classification must change.
