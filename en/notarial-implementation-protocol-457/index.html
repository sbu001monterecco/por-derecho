Protocol 457 formalised a transaction; it does not by itself answer whether every component was legally payable.

The notarial audit must separate documentary authentication and formalisation from matters reserved to the Court, the parties, the Insolvency Administration and the Land Registry.

Evidential limit. The deed proves what the appearers declared and formalised and which documents were invoked. It does not by itself prove that every debt component was payable in insolvency, that every property was correctly included or that the requested cancellation was actually registered.

Strongest notarial defence

The notary may maintain that the deed relied on facially valid testimonies and decisions, that the appearers declared the debt and transaction, that insolvency payability was for the Court and parties, and that qualification and entry were for the Land Registry.

What this page does not establish

It does not establish that the notary invented the figure, knew of any falsehood, joined any coordination, knowingly exceeded the notarial role or incurred civil, disciplinary or criminal liability. For a private actor, CP 392 incorporates only CP 390.1.1º–3º: a materially false party recital faithfully reproduced in the deed does not by itself satisfy CP 392. CP 393 additionally requires an already-false document and then, knowing its falsity, presentation in court or, for other use, a purpose to harm. Each hypothesis requires the complete protocol, incorporated documents, communications and applicable law.

Insolvency 36/2012 and Arrecife EH90: distinct instruments

Protocol 457 implements the 159-property insolvency dación. It is not, by itself, a certified liquidation, satisfaction, termination or cancellation of Arrecife Mortgage Enforcement 90/2012. The located enforcement claim concerns the second facility and claims EUR 857,373.81 plus EUR 257,000 provisional; the complete file, allocations, payments, termination decisions and cancellation entries remain outstanding.

Separate EUR 400,000 branch. The transaction concerning 29 premises and 2 pools/solarium—31 properties—belongs to a documentary branch distinct from the 159-property dación. It must not be automatically folded into, added to or allocated against the Protocol 457 consideration.

This separation does not establish double recovery, NPL fraud or falsity. The strongest lawful explanation is that each instrument served a distinct function, date, balance and procedural perimeter and that the complete record reconciles them. Gil Marer and Aweswell's contrary allegation requires document-, balance-, use-, disposition-, benefit- and harm-specific proof for each actor.
