Protocol 457 formalised a transaction; it does not by itself answer whether every component was legally payable.

The notarial audit must separate documentary authentication and formalisation from matters reserved to the Court, the parties, the Insolvency Administration and the Land Registry.

Evidential limit. The deed proves what the appearers declared and formalised and which documents were invoked. It does not by itself prove that every debt component was payable in insolvency, that every property was correctly included or that the requested cancellation was actually registered.

What is currently documented

21 February 2022

The deed formalises a dación concerning 159 listed mortgaged properties.

EUR 13,168,082.02

The deed reproduces six principal-and-interest components adding exactly to that amount and describes it as debt serving as consideration for the transfer.

Requested effects

It declares the debt extinguished, requests mortgage cancellation and contemplates notification to the Court within five days.

Finite questions

  1. Which authentic copies, testimonies and statements of finality were produced?
  2. Which document contained or justified the six components of EUR 13,168,082.02?
  3. Does the deed reproduce the judicial authorisation literally or add a further economic characterisation?
  4. What title and charge verification was carried out for the 159 properties?
  5. What reservations were recorded concerning Matkator, third parties or unencumbered assets?
  6. Which cancellations were requested and what evidence shows that they were later made?
  7. When, how and with which schedules was the deed notified to the Court?

Strongest notarial defence

The notary may maintain that the deed relied on facially valid testimonies and decisions, that the appearers declared the debt and transaction, that insolvency payability was for the Court and parties, and that qualification and entry were for the Land Registry.

What this page does not establish

It does not establish that the notary invented the figure, knew of any falsehood, joined any coordination, knowingly exceeded the notarial role or incurred civil, disciplinary or criminal liability. Each hypothesis requires the complete protocol, incorporated documents, communications and applicable law.

Priority evidence

  • Complete certified copy of the protocol and schedules.
  • Judicial documents produced and their issuance chain.
  • Debt liquidation or certificate delivered to the notary.
  • Indexes, communication and receipt of the court notification.
  • Registry presentation, qualification and resulting entries.
