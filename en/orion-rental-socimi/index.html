Orion Rental SOCIMI: the later corporate node that binds legacy actors to a wider board, manager, investors and market infrastructure.

Orion Rental SOCIMI is relevant to the Sun Park reconstruction because its public corporate structure places two actors already documented in the earlier Sun Park record — Francisco Mario Matos Matas and José Daniel Acosta Matos — inside a later listed-company board, while Orion's management is publicly identified as AGM Canary Asset Management and RIC Capital presents Orion as a RIC-backed investment project.

Temporal firewall. Orion was constituted in June 2023. It therefore cannot be treated as a participant in the 2011–2022 Sun Park events. Nor does board membership, investment, audit, market listing or professional service establish knowledge of, participation in, or responsibility for earlier alleged misconduct. Orion matters here as a later governance, disclosure, due-diligence and reputational-continuity node.

GIL MARER'S EXPRESS ALLEGATION / ANALYTICAL THESIS

“Predatory inclusion”: reputation and resources as a shield and a sword.

Gil Marer's words and thesis: the alleged repeat modus operandi is one of “predatory inclusion”: existing actors, after what Gil alleges was “clandestine non-compliance” accompanied by “chronic deliberate concealment” and “non-disclosure”, bring existing and new individuals and entities into a wider structure and cause their reputation, institutional credibility, capital, network, professional standing, muscle power and resources to be lent to the perimeter. In Gil's formulation, that can turn a new actor — even one not proved to know the underlying history — into a “forced collaborator” and into both a “shield and a sword”, an “illicit shield and an illicit sword”, protecting and advancing what he describes as the whole scheme or “house of cards.”

Attribution boundary. Those expressions are Gil Marer's allegations and analytical language. This page does not state as fact that Orion, its directors, investors, auditors, advisers, Portfolio, Euroclear or any other new participant was coerced, deceived, failed a legal duty, concealed information or knowingly joined an illicit scheme. The proposition is testable only actor by actor and disclosure by disclosure.

Why Orion is probative of the question, not the answer

The significance is structural. Two legacy actors from the Sun Park chronology appear in a later vehicle whose board includes additional established businesspeople and whose management, investment and market architecture extends the network well beyond the original dispute. That makes a finite due-diligence question unavoidable: what was disclosed to whom, when, by whom, and what did each recipient independently verify?

THE DISCLOSURE / DUE-DILIGENCE TEST

The “shield and sword” thesis only survives if the missing disclosure bridges can be proved.

1 · Legacy fact
What history existed?

Identify the exact Sun Park proceedings, complaints, judgments, appeals, ownership disputes, Community records, RIC-related questions and actor-specific allegations that existed before each Orion appointment, investment or market step.

2 · Disclosure
What was actually disclosed?

Board packs, conflicts registers, related-party files, subscription documents, investor memoranda, management contracts, legal opinions, audit correspondence, market disclosures and diligence requests must show the information supplied or omitted.

3 · Knowledge
What did each person know?

FMMM and JDAM must be tested against their own earlier documented roles. Enrique Guerra, Víctor Martí Gilabert and every other director must be assessed separately. Shared board membership cannot substitute for individual knowledge.

4 · Reliance / reputational effect
Was another actor's standing used?

Test whether investors, directors, advisers, auditors, public bodies or market infrastructure were invoked as reassurance, validation or leverage in another transaction or public narrative — and whether they had the material facts necessary to give that reassurance knowingly.

5 · Benefit and causation
What did the inclusion enable?

Trace capital, guarantees, finance, access to RIC, subsidies, contracts, permissions, market admission, publicity and counterparty confidence. A benefit must be linked to the alleged omission rather than inferred from chronology alone.

6 · Innocent alternative
What if the wider board knew nothing?

That possibility remains live. If a new director, investor or provider was not told material background and did not independently know it, the evidence may support Gil's concept of a “forced collaborator” as an unwitting reputational/resource layer — but it would not justify accusing that new actor of intentional wrongdoing.

Repository rule: “predatory inclusion” is not proved by networking density. It requires a sequence: legacy exposure → duty or practical reason to disclose → material omission or misleading presentation → entry of a new actor/resource → reliance or enabling effect → actor-specific benefit/causation. If those bridges are absent, the criminal or dishonest inference must be rejected or reduced.

The new actors are not presumed collaborators.

Orion's board publicly includes Víctor Martí Gilabert, Ángel Medina Padrón, Francisco López González, Anil Partap Harjani, Oscar Díaz Melián, Jesús Díaz Quintana, Manuel Padrón Padrón, Francisco Mario Matos Matas, Enrique Guerra Suárez and José Daniel Acosta Matos. The existence of a common board is a governance fact. It is not evidence that the other directors knew of or endorsed Gil's allegations concerning Sun Park.

That distinction is central to the thesis itself: if “predatory inclusion” occurred, some later participants may be relevant precisely because their reputation or resources were allegedly recruited without full disclosure. Their board packs, conflict checks and diligence records are therefore potentially exculpatory as well as probative.

Right of reply and correction. Orion Rental SOCIMI, AGM Canary Asset Management, RIC Capital, every director, investor, auditor, adviser, Portfolio, Euroclear and every person or entity mentioned may provide corrections, contrary evidence or exculpatory documents. Silence is not admission.
