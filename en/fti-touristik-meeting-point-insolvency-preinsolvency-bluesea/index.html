Las Palmas · distinct proceeding

357/2024: a joint three-debtor negotiation, not the German insolvencies.

Signed Auto 97/2025 identifies Meeting Point Hotelmanagement (Canaries), S.L.U.; Meeting Point Spain, S.L.U.; and Meeting Point Investment, S.L.U. as the three joint applicants/debtors under articles 583 et seq. of the Spanish insolvency law. The Spanish proceeding is a restructuring and potential records-custody node distinct from the German debtors.

  1. 23 JUL 2024

    Joint filing

    Auto 97/2025 records that all three debtors filed the communication of negotiations on this date.

  2. 31 JUL 2024

    Communication deemed made

    The same signed order records a diligence deeming the communication made. The 18 September decree is therefore a later procedural event, not the beginning of 357/2024.

  3. 24 OCT 2024

    Extension of effects

    The Public Insolvency Register attributes the act to Magistrate-Judge Alberto López Villarrubia in the then Commercial Court No. 3. Register attribution does not by itself establish who personally signed the original or prove allocation or substitution.

    The same three debtors requested appointment of AUREN REESTRUCTURACIONES SLP as restructuring expert.

  5. 18 FEB 2025

    Electronically signed Auto 97/2025, issued by Guillermo Fernández García, appoints Auren for all three debtors.

Public support · separate financing question

SEPI authorised €31 million for the Meeting Point operation—not €56 million.

SEPI's 27 June 2022 release announces €31 million for the group-level Meeting Point operation: a €20.7 million participating loan and a €10.3 million ordinary loan. SEPI's 2023 annual accounts supply the entity allocation: €10.7 million participating to Meeting Point Hotelmanagement (Canaries), €10 million participating to Meeting Point Spain and €10.3 million ordinary to Hotelmanagement. The often-repeated €56 million is the arithmetic total of two separate authorisations—Meeting Point €31 million plus BLUESEA €25 million—not one Meeting Point instrument.

Annual-account allocation: Hotelmanagement €21 million (€10.7m participating + €10.3m ordinary); Meeting Point Spain €10 million participating.

Partial repayments reported

€3.09m + €5.3m

SEPI later reported partial repayments in 2022 and 2023. Their subtraction from €31 million is not a certified current balance because interest and later movements remain unreconciled.

Separate operation

BLUESEA · €25 million

BLUESEA's separate authorised support was later renounced. It must not be presented as drawn Meeting Point finance or merged into one €56 million Meeting Point loan.
