# Lanzarote open letter — two-track publication control

**Control date:** 27 August 2026  
**Repository:** `sbu001monterecco/por-derecho`  
**Public status:** approved for bilingual publication

## Published architecture

The Lanzarote institutional invitation is organised around two independent workstreams:

1. **Resolve the past:** a constructive route for documentary completion, institutional traceability and lawful resolution of the legacy questions concerning Sun Park, now operated as MYND Yaiza.
2. **Build the future:** a carefully qualified public introduction to a prospective Montaña Roja investment workstream, presently subject to legal, corporate, planning, technical and financial due diligence.

The division of competences between the Cabildo de Lanzarote and the Ayuntamiento de Yaiza remains express throughout the publication.

## Controlling safeguards

- Future investment is not offered as consideration for the handling of any historic file.
- Resolution of historic files is not conditional on support for Montaña Roja.
- No preferential treatment, shortcut, waiver or predetermined outcome is requested.
- The Montaña Roja pages do not announce an acquisition, agreed transaction, financing, planning approval, tourism authorisation, building licence, operator appointment, compensation entitlement, subsidy or construction start.
- Social-value commitments are qualitative until feasibility supports publishable baselines and targets.

## Confidentiality control

Private correspondence was used only to understand the workstream and control public claims. No private correspondence or attachment is published through this update. The public pages exclude private identities, prices, deposits, exclusivity proposals, commissions, beneficial-ownership information, seller circumstances, negotiation positions, private compensation estimates and legal advice.
